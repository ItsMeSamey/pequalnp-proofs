# Three-port normalization and linear label transfer

Index the three sides of a triangle T so side i is opposite terminal i. For a binary cycle c and cocycle d, record p in F2³/<111> from c restricted to T and q in the even plane from d restricted to T. A child whose original elements have disjoint c,d supports has p·q=0 by orthogonality. Write D=I+J, so D takes any word to its even representative modulo 111 and is the identity on the even plane.

In a rooted K4 with boundary triangle T, let u,v be cycle and cocycle words on its three spokes. Vertex parity gives u=Dp. Potentials at the three terminals relative to the central vertex give q=Dv. Conversely these are the only conditions for a cycle and cocycle on K4, with the boundary cycle representative free up to 111. A usable completion requires u AND v=0. Every p·q=0 boundary has such a completion: try both v=q,q+111; if u has weight two, q is constant on those two positions since u·q=0.

A rooted minor on the original triangle and the three surviving spokes can supply a family of baseline completions. Fix a linear section lifting each minor cycle to the child by adding contracted elements and each minor cocycle by adding deleted elements. These sections exist by the standard restriction descriptions of binary cycle and cocycle spaces of a minor, or by solving the corresponding linear equations. The added supports are disjoint from one another. Use the raw minor cycle (T=u, spokes=u), and the raw minor cocycle (T=Dv, spokes=v). The former is defined on the even plane; the latter on F2³. Composing the two sections with the child's linear label maps and ignoring virtual triangle labels gives linear maps of u,v. Extend the u map from the even plane to F2³ by any linear extension, and assign these two maps as spoke weights. Optionally choose a cycle section sending the triangle cycle to the actual unlabelled triangle, so that using any raw representative of p has exactly the same label.

For a graphic parent, replacing an ordinary boundary triangle by this K4 baseline gadget is a graph triangle-to-star replacement. For a cographic parent represented with its slot a vertex star, delete the degree-three vertex and join the three neighbors by the opposite-index triangle. The dual exchange yields the identical abstract cycle/cocycle coordinate map. Directly, the three cut bits at the deleted star determine the differences Dp among neighbor potentials, and the cycle degrees q at the neighbors can be balanced by any new triangle pattern v with Dv=q. These local operations commute on edge-disjoint slots; in the cographic graph their distinct removed star centers cannot be adjacent. Additional parallel edges should retain distinct labels.

For each exceptional child retain its original virtual triangle and enumerate one of its table entries (p,q,l). Pin its three cocycle bits to q. Among representatives of p choose one disjoint from q (choose the smaller admissible representative, for example). Such a representative exists because q is even and p·q=0. Pin the cycle bits to that representative. The parent cycle can always be normalized to the chosen word by adding the cycle T: it changes no other coordinates, has zero original label and is available also in the torso after transformations on other slots. The parent/output slot can be conditioned the same way. Put zero original label on these slots, and XOR the exceptional child labels into the residual target. Six coordinate readout bits per retained slot suffice if the basic solver supports linear label targets but has no explicit pinning interface. The extra coordinates are local to one torso query; subtree messages retain their original label dimension.

For any realizable assembly with t original target label bits, fix one baseline completion with the same boundary for each child and compare its label to the realized label. If more than t children use distinct-from-baseline choices, some nonempty subset of their differences sums to zero. Replacing that entire subset preserves the label and all boundary data. Iteration leaves at most t exceptional children. The baseline choice need not be canonical across boundary states.

The standalone check cographic_check.py enumerates both cycle and cocycle spaces of two-slot graphic and cographic parents, transforms the first slot and retains a pinned representative at the second. Keeping every ordinary edge and every spoke bit in the observed tuples tests all linear labels simultaneously. The exact tuple sets agree (76 graphic, 58 cographic). The graph representation with all cographic decomposition slots as vertex stars is a separate decomposition hypothesis, not something this local check constructs.
