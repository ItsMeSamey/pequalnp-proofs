# Exact aggregation of quadratic syndromes over a finite chain ring

Let f in F₂[t] be monic irreducible of degree d, let k,b,h be positive, and set R=F₂[t]/(fᵏ), N=dk. The input gives affine binary equations A on the (b+h)N coordinates of (X,Z). Sum over X in Rᵇ and h columns Yⱼ in Rᵇ, requiring Zⱼ=Σᵢ XᵢYᵢⱼ and (X,Z) in A. This count is deterministic polynomial time in the explicit coordinate description. The number of independent syndrome bits can grow with the input. Even with k>1 and varying multiplication ranks, only k+1 strata are needed.

Write Iᵥ=(fᵛ) for 0≤v≤k and B(v,w)=|A ∩ (Iᵥᵇ × I_wʰ)|. The answer is

    2^(bhN) B(k,k)
    + Σ_{v=0}^{k−1} 2^[h((b−1)N+dv)] [B(v,v)−B(v+1,v)].

For X in Iᵥᵇ outside Iᵥ₊₁ᵇ, its entries generate Iᵥ. Indeed, at least one entry is fᵛ times a unit. The map Yⱼ ↦ X·Yⱼ has image Iᵥ of size 2^(N−dv) and constant fiber size 2^((b−1)N+dv). The columns are independent conditioned on X. Subtracting the two affine counts removes the next left-hand stratum while keeping the right-hand ideal fixed. X=0 contributes the first term.

**Explicit coordinates and certificates.** Represent a ring element by the coefficients of 1,t,…,t^(N−1). Multiplication is carryless polynomial multiplication followed by division with remainder by fᵏ. Membership in Iᵥ means that the remainder modulo fᵛ is zero, including at v=k. Build the binary matrix of this remainder operation from its values on all monomials of degree below N. Each B is zero for an inconsistent system and 2 to the binary nullity otherwise. Counts have at most bhN+bN+1 bits. If a separate list of quadratic syndromes is supplied, a proposed invertible binary input coordinate change, output readout and offsets can be verified by expanding each coordinate in multilinear degree at most two, reducing squares by x²=x, and comparing coefficients. All coordinate arrays are explicit. Recognition without a supplied certificate is a separate problem.

**Two extensions.** An extra factor (−1)^Q(X,Z) for binary quadratic Q can be included by replacing every B with its signed affine sum. Parametrize the affine subspace by free bits and eliminate interacting pairs, using Σ_{a,b}(−1)^(ab+au+bv)=2(−1)^(uv) for affine forms u,v in the remaining bits. A remaining nonconstant linear phase sums to zero. Alternatively, restrict each Yⱼ to a given translate of one common R-submodule D of Rᵇ. Subtract its translate contribution X·offsetⱼ from Zⱼ by a binary-linear coordinate change. For generators gᵢ of D, define Hᵥ by X·gᵢ ∈ Iᵥ for all i. Use Hᵥ in place of Iᵥᵇ in the intersections and use per-column fiber |D|/|Iᵥ| on each nonempty exact stratum. Hₖ can contain nonzero X, all giving zero shifted products with |D| choices for each column. Compute |D| by binary elimination on all tʲgᵢ for 0≤j<N. The fiber formula applies only to nonempty strata.

**Balanced packet wires.** At the supplied legal root packing of program.md, select identical full packets, one distinguished row and its matching live column in each, pin other columns to zero, and sum other rows freely. The exact transfer with row y and column x is K=((1,1−w),(0,w)), w=N₀/T, where N₀=C(T)/C′(0), T=2L+1. With complementary rails, K[y,x] K[1−y,1−x]=w·1[y=x]. The two-row equality test is exact over the rational coefficient ring and after reduction at the evaluation precision. Impose the complementary encoding in original 0/1 endpoint tensors. For a bipartite factor graph of Boolean variable vertices and bounded-arity gate-relation vertices, assign a packet pair to every incidence. Each variable tensor allows the two uniform complementary-rail encodings of that variable. Each relation tensor permits exactly its satisfying tuples on the first rails and their complements on the second rails. Use identical slot labels on each side of every selected pair. For a bounded-degree implementation copy high-fanout bits through binary trees of deterministic equality gates. Unused packets contribute one with free rows and zero columns. The active components therefore give wᵉ times the ordinary nonnegative integer count when there are e incidences. A positive integer weight is obtained for each satisfying assignment after removing the common scalar. All tensors here have integral entries zero or one. No assumption about entrywise positivity of W is needed.

Compile ring multiplication and affine equations by their explicit binary AND/XOR circuits and pin the checked output. Every satisfying assignment to (X,Y) has exactly one valid internal trace. The original Boolean output count is at most 2^((b+bh)N); removing wᵉ recovers it exactly whenever p_t−e·v₂(w)>(b+bh)N. At this node v₂(w)=a+1. Use the polynomial padding in program.md with its input-size parameter enlarged to dominate the trace, incidence count and bit bound. This certifies realizability for the constructed layout. The algorithm for extracting a count from the scalar divides by the known power of two first, then inverts the remaining unit at the reduced precision. Signed phase weights in the extension can instead be recovered by subtracting two nonnegative counts with opposite output pins.

Run `python chain_ring.py` in this directory. It checks 40 affine systems against exhaustive original assignments, including inconsistent and unconstrained readouts, f=t and f=t²+t+1, and verifies the four complementary-rail transfer entries at L=8. The tests exercise the baseline count and local wire. The submodule and phase extensions above are algebraic derivations.

**Using the syndrome-flow realization.** In the [balanced packet construction](../tensor-expander/result.md), each logical equality has scalar gₑ(T) at *every* deformation value T, because its two copies have the same polynomial preparation and opposite rails. On a connected logical graph with E edges and V vertices, one independent incidence-flow layer per binary syndrome contributes 2^(E−V+1) solutions exactly when the sum of its local predicates is zero. When the verified aggregate predicates on the affine primary coordinates are the chain-ring readouts above, multiply the chain-ring count by that factor for each layer and by the product of the known packet-pair scalars for all wires. Affine copies with unique extension preserve the primary count when distributing monomials across local predicates. At an evaluation where this scalar is nonzero with known valuation, guard bits must cover its valuation, the flow multiplicity and the bit length of the desired integer. At evaluations where it vanishes, the forward scalar identity still holds. The direct bounded-arity gate construction above avoids needing a particular flow layout.
