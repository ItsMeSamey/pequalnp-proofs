# What the sum-free certificate gives for the standard SAT lifts

Fix a common reference z. A signed OR clause is the section z=false of NAE(literals,z); global complementation restores z=false from any solution. In the incidence cycle space of the lifted formula, selections on original occurrences have even counts at each original variable. The selected reference port for a clause is forced to the parity of its original selections. Global reference parity follows automatically. A clause is touched iff its original selector vector is nonempty; the extra reference is unmarked. Thus the lifted phase equals the previous original OR phase (the original Fourier factors −s contribute a globally even count of minus signs). The sum-free test for this direct transformation is exactly the ordinary test, including the pure-free collapse.

For exact-3CNF the conventional split uses NAE(a,b,h), NAE(NOT h,c,z) with private h per clause. It is equisatisfiable, without any claim of preserving exact multiplicity. Let A,B,C,Z be the four external selected bits, with even total. The two h ports carry the same bit H=A+B=C+Z. If no external port is marked, neither pair of clauses is touched; with a pair on just one side only that side is touched. For one mark per side H=1, so both subclauses are touched and the marked negation of h contributes the missing sign. When all four ports are marked H=0, both subclauses are touched and the phase differs by one from the original single touched gate. Therefore the split adds exactly ABC to the original phase, independent of the split order. In particular it agrees with the OR phase for selections supported on at most two original variable classes (the 3 distinct original variables in each clause are essential).

The pure-free collapse proof for the ordinary certificate only uses one-variable negative cosets and their pairwise overlaps; the cubic correction is always zero there. Deleting original pure clauses embeds the smaller selection space by zeros and preserves the condition. Thus, after this generic split, a passing original formula reduces by pure peeling to components consisting of two complementary clauses. The split quadratic degree and the NAE witness algorithm do not extend this certificate to arbitrary hard 3SAT instances through these standard reductions. This does not constrain other encodings or different certificates.

## Arbitrary existential tree gadgets, even with private variable fanout

Assume each replacement gadget is a connected acyclic incidence network of signed NAE gates on private Boolean variables, with each external bit appearing at exactly one dangling gate port. There are no fixed constants and no private variables shared between gadgets. Assume the projected boundary relation is the intended single signed NAE on at least two distinct external bits. Fibers need not be equinumerous.

Consider the Steiner tree connecting the dangling boundary leaves. If a Steiner variable h had at least three boundary-bearing branches, fix the forbidden uniform boundary assignment in the intended literal coordinates. Each branch restricts h to a subset of {0,1}. Their intersection is empty; at most two subsets witness emptiness (use one rejecting 0 and one rejecting 1). A boundary flip in a different branch would leave the contradiction, whereas any single external flip of a forbidden NAE word must be accepted. Therefore every internal Steiner variable has tree degree two. Off-tree attachments at variable h admit both values of h by satisfiability of the gadget and global complement symmetry. An off-tree private branch at a Steiner gate similarly supplies both values for the free gate port. This makes the gate vacuous for all other port values. Deleting it would factor the projected boundary relation into at least two nonempty proper boundary groups, impossible because a single NAE has full projection on every proper subset but is not the full relation. Thus only degree-two private wires and NAE gates matter for pairs of externally selected ports. No off-tree incidences occur in an even internal flow with zero boundary there.

Gauge-flip entire NAE gates along the reduced tree so each private wire is seen with opposite signs at its two ends. Recursive elimination by ∃h NAE(A,h) AND NAE(NOT h,B) = NAE(A,B) identifies the induced boundary signs with the intended signs up to one global complement. For an even selection of exactly two boundary leaves, the unique internal parity routing is their path. If it encounters r gates then it crosses r−1 private wires, each contributing one negative mark. Thus its total phase is 1 plus the two boundary negative marks, identical to the direct single NAE. The zero selection also agrees. Selections on at most two original variable classes yield only these 0- or 2-port boundary patterns after adding the reference bit. Consequently the pure-free collapse for this sign certificate holds for any such tree replacement, even if its topology and polarities were chosen after inspecting the formula. Cyclic gadgets, fixed constants, shared internal state and alternate certificates remain outside the result.
