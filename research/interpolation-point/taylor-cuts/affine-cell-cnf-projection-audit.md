# CNF truth, independent fibers, and switch witnesses

For clause-falsifying affine cells B_i on joint variables, initialize a dictionary with the ambient cell of weight one. At step i retain each old entry (H,c) and add −c at H∩B_i; discard empty intersections, merge equal affine cells using a canonical augmented row basis, and drop zero weights. Induction gives the exact zero-one predicate ∏(1−1_{B_i}). The work is polynomial in the explicit input and the sum of the intermediate dictionary sizes. A small final support does not bound that sum. Existential projection must follow formation of this truth predicate: with a private bit x, both the violation and the truth of the single clause (x) project onto the full exterior.

Under a linear or affine map π, pushing a weighted cell H=a+V forward under counting measure gives the cell πH weighted by c times |V∩ker π_lin|. Thus the projected CNF multiplicity is nonnegative, and its positive support is the desired existential relation. A minimum linear interface for multiplicity serves as an upper bound on the interface of this support. Exact thresholding to a zero-one mixture can require separate work. In particular the polynomial signed-mixture algorithm for multiplicity alone does not establish a polynomial minimum-support-interface algorithm on arbitrary overlapping projected cells.

For f=Σ c_i 1_{H_i}, the primal energy on an affine constraint D is Σ_{i,j}c_i c_j |H_i∩H_j∩D|. It is positive iff f is nonzero somewhere in D. Starting from positive energy, split by each remaining independent direction in the fiber and retain a positive half; only one half needs testing since energy is additive. At most dim(D) queries lift an actual point in the nonzero support. A CNF truth mixture certifies an original witness there. Nonzero-support projection of a genuinely signed message uses the pushforward of f²; the square of the pushforward may lose both lifts of f(x)=(−1)^x. If different microscopic witnesses cancel before f is formed, even its squared energy detects only surviving message support. A witness interpretation then needs a separate noncancellation proof or a lift relation. For several factors, private fibers must be independent conditional on the shared interface before separately projected masses can be multiplied. For example x=0 and x=1 both project positively when x is eliminated separately, although they have no joint witness.

The read-only C6 two-switch NAND test has accepting trajectory counts 2,1,1,0 on 00,01,10,11 respectively. Its final rotation is forced and its two choices of first rotation accept exactly when y=0 and x=0 respectively. Pin y=0 in each of r disjoint tests. Their joint acceptance predicate is universal while the model multiplicity ∏(2−x_i) has a unique maximum at the zero vector. Its translation-period space is zero, hence its minimum linear interface rank is r. This comparison concerns the indicated rotation multiplicity. Conversely for r disjoint unpinned NAND tests the exterior acceptance set has odd size 3^r; any nonzero translation period would pair all its points. Its Boolean interface therefore has full rank 2r. Efficient witnesses and small local truth descriptions need not yield a low-rank global interface.
