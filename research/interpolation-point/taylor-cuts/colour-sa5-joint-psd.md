# A joint ternary level-five SA and pair-PSD witness

For a simple 6-regular graph of girth at least 21 use the squared radius-two Gram construction in colour-joint-sa3-sdp.md. In real orthonormal coordinates set a_v to 6 at v, −3 at distance one, 1 at distance two, and add √12 in a private orthogonal coordinate. Then T_uv=⟨a_u,a_v⟩/132 is PSD with diagonal 1 and distance numerators −66, 25, −6, 1, and 0 respectively at distances 1, 2, 3, 4, and farther. The pair probability for a particular ordered equal colour outcome is (1+2T_uv)/9. A particular unequal ordered outcome then has probability (1−T_uv)/9. The centered one-hot pair moment matrix is T⊗(I₃−J₃/3)/3. Thus positivity follows from an explicit Gram representation, without a numerical eigenvalue check.

Retain the ambient distances 1 through 4 within each query on at most five vertices and merge larger distances into one far symbol. The retained pattern restricts exactly on deleting a query vertex. A connected t-mark component is spanned by the union of shortest paths for a proximity spanning tree. This union has at most 4(t−1) edges and is acyclic. An alternative shortcut of length at most four would yield a cycle of length at most 4(t−1)+4≤20, impossible. After trimming unmarked leaves this is a tree with at most five leaves. Enumerate its marked types and take disjoint unions with all cross distances far. The counts of metric types at sizes 0 through 5 are 1, 1, 5, 20, 135 and 995.

The file colour-sa5-r4-psd-orbits.txt assigns a nonnegative rational weight to each particular proper ordered palette assignment, keyed by the metric and equality orbit as in the earlier cutoff-three construction. Improper assignments have zero mass. The checker regenerates the metric types and verifies normalization, marginal closure under deletion, and each prescribed two-vertex mass exactly. Site and palette symmetry then identify the remaining two-vertex entries. Both relaxations therefore use the very same pair moments, for every vertex subset of the host. The PSD assertion concerns the one-hot moment matrix of the constant and first-degree variables; it does not assert a higher-order PSD moment matrix.

The standalone verifier check_colour_sa5_r4_psd.py regenerated all types, checked all 26,132 assignment orbits and 418,126 named-colour marginal identities including improper parents, and confirmed all prescribed pair probabilities exactly.
