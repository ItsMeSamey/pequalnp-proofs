# Three pinned vertices and neutral principal pivots

Let T be three named vertices of a simple graph with binary adjacency A. Feasibility means an induced even vertex set containing T. It is equivalent to the following finite list of labelled Schur complement queries: choose disjoint neutral subsets P and U, |U|≤2, with A[P] nonsingular (empty is allowed). After eliminating P from A[P∪T∪U], the submatrix on U is zero, the columns from U to T are distinct nonzero even-weight vectors and the graph on T∪U has all degrees even. U can be guessed in O(n²) choices. The choice of P remains unrestricted.

Proof. Choose a minimal feasible S and I=S minus T. For v in ker A[I], a zero terminal boundary A[T,I]v would allow its support to be removed, preserving induced degrees of every retained vertex. Its terminal boundary has even weight since the all-even equations on I yield 1_T^T A[T,I]v=1_I^T A[I]v. Thus the kernel dimension is at most two.

Delete neutrals outside S. Edge pivot at adjacent neutral vertices u,v is implemented by local complementations at u,v,u. The local witness map at u changes only x_u to 1+x_u+(Ax)_u. Under this complementation a neighbor's adjacency sum changes at i by 1+x_i (when updating x as well). Applying the three maps shows the edge pivot replaces x_u,x_v by (Ax)_u,(Ax)_v respectively, without changing the remaining indicator. For a fully selected even witness, both become zero; delete them. The remaining graph is the Schur complement of the two-site block and is still fully selected and even. Repeating until the residual interior has no edge produces a maximum-rank invertible principal block P; the surviving U has size dim ker A[I]. The Schur boundary on survivors maps their free coordinates into terminal parity by extending each vector uniquely to ker A[I] using the eliminated equations, so still has independent columns in the even-weight plane. They are distinct, nonzero pair vectors, as claimed. The inverse pivot transformations and zero extensions construct a witness from any listed pattern. Equivalently, one may finish by local complementing and deleting each survivor. These operations toggle only its pair of terminal neighbors. Terminal names never move and all pivots use eliminated neutrals.

Unrooted fixed-target pivot-minor recognition, for example https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04656, permits a change of surviving labels and a different pivot schedule. Full graph LC equivalence between two supplied graphs does not eliminate an unknown P. Neither theorem directly decides the displayed query.
