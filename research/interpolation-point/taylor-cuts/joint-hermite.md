# Tapered recovery from full shifted Appendix D polynomials

Write L=2^a, α=2L, d=M(2L−1), 1≤u≤a/2. The active grid consists of t in [−L,L) with i=v₂(t)<u. Set S=Σ_{i<u}(u−i)/2^i, A=(d+1)/S, n_i=ceil(A(u−i)/2^i). Use the ordinary integer points j=2^i(2r+1), 0≤r<n_i. There are between d+1 and d+u points. The corresponding t is the unique grid representative of j modulo α, and F(j) follows by evaluating the entire integral shifted polynomial at (j−t)/α. The source computes these full polynomials before extracting Hermite data. Truncated jets alone are insufficient for this argument.

In the weight at zero, factors from shallower shells contribute valuation zero and deeper shells contribute +(l−i)n_l. Same-shell factors contribute −(n_i−1)−v₂(r!(n_i−1−r)!). The negative valuation of each weight is consequently at most 2(n_i−1)−Σ_{l>i}(l−i)n_l. Ceiling rounding can only improve the upper estimate 2 A(u−i)/2^i−Σ_{l>i}(l−i)A(u−l)/2^l = A G(u−i)/2^i, where G(k)=6−(2k+6)/2^k. Hence p+ceil(6A/2^i) bits at each active center suffice for p origin bits. This works coefficientwise over the unramified extension and truncated V ring. Multiplication by rational weights and division by their powers of two is performed before final reduction. The O(d+u) weights have polynomial bit size (nodes O(d+u), unnormalized products O((d+u)log(d+u)) bits). Full shifted interpolation at d+1 residue-separated lifts per center and subsequent polynomial evaluation have polynomial overhead in d,L,extension degree,truncation and precision. This retains the cost of the source's recursive calls at those lifts.

At N_i of order M2^i/L the guard contributes O(M/√u) to sqrt(N_i p_i), in every shell. Base precision adds O(sqrt(N_i p)); the full source recursion and other traversal terms still apply. This is a precision theorem and does not improve the published asymptotic time guarantee.

## Simultaneous ambiguity in the grid-integral polynomial module

Replace A by A₀=d/S and the ceilings by floors m_i. The normalized product P(T)=∏_{i,r<m_i}(1−T/(2^i(2r+1))) has degree ≤d and P(0)=1. At an inactive t all normalized factors have integral shifted coefficients and unit constant. At t=2^i(2b+1) active, the exact minimum valuation of the shifted coefficients is
E=m_i + Σ_{j=1}^{a−i} #{0≤r<m_i : r≡b mod 2^j} − Σ_{l>i}(l−i)m_l.
This follows by multiplicativity of polynomial Gauss content, or reduction modulo two after dividing each factor by its content. The residue counts sum to at least v₂(m_i!)−m_i/2^{a−i}. Consequently E≥A₀G(u−i)/2^i−2−log₂(m_i+1)−m_i/2^{a−i}. Under the source growing parameters (a≥2u, d=M(2L−1), u→∞), the error is uniformly little-o of A₀/2^i. Indeed the last-term relative error is at most (u−i)2^{i−a}, and the logarithmic term is negligible even at the smallest shell target. Thus P has integral shifted coefficients everywhere, and indistinguishability from zero forces at least one guard Ω(d/(u2^i)) even when all shifted coefficients are supplied. Multiplying P by 2^{p−1} gives the corresponding p-bit exact-recovery witness. This matches the per-shell precision charge on the whole grid-integral module. It is not a Boolean packet lower bound or a computational-time lower bound.

## Exact finite-instance optimality test

Represent F=Σ c_j(T/α)^j. Origin integrality says c_j are 2-adic integers. For each t and j the shifted coefficient is Σ_{k≥j} binom(k,j)(t/α)^{k−j}c_k. Multiply this row by 2^D for D=(a+1)d to obtain integer row B. Prescribe difference precision κ=0 for unobserved coefficients and κ equal to the observed precision otherwise. All zero-transcript ambiguities satisfy Bc=0 modulo 2^{D+κ} rowwise. Compute an integer kernel basis for [B | −diag(2^{D+κ})] by Smith reduction and project its first d+1 coordinates. The gcd of the first coordinates of the resulting basis has 2-valuation exactly the recoverable origin precision. The integer congruences capture arbitrary 2-adic vectors at finite precision by approximation, and the matrices are rational so extension and V coordinates separate. Inhomogeneous Smith reduction gives a representative for a consistent measured transcript. Matrix dimensions O(Ld) by O(Ld+d) and bit size O(D+d log(d+L)+max κ) give polynomial bit complexity with polynomial-bit Smith/Hermite algorithms.

## Boolean packet ambiguity

Use full Boolean pinned packets and P₀=2^u. Assign the 2P₀ words b(y)=c xor <h,bin_u(y mod P₀)> equally often (assumes 2P₀ divides the packet count). Pin input and output to the same b, giving F(0)=1. For every active t exactly M/4 rows have b(x)=1 and b(x+t)=0. Any allowed input y in such a row has t+x−y nonzero mod P₀. In its Cauchy weight expanded at t+αz the vanishing factor contributes αz/(t+x−y); the remaining normalized factors have integral coefficients, with unit derivative ratio. Thus every such row contributes a factor 2^{a−u+2}z. All active shifted polynomials vanish modulo 2^{(a−u+2)M/4} and to order M/4 simultaneously. An identically zero Boolean network gives the opposite origin decision with the same data below those thresholds. The stronger tapered module example above still lacks a Boolean realization.
