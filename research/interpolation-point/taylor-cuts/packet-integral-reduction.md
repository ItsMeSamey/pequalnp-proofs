# Physical/shared packet ideals and three separate transfer statements

All packets here are identical full packets of length L=2^a. Set D=2L−1, d=LD, κ=C'(0)^L, and R=Z_(2)[U,V] with its usual degree grading. For a degree-at-most-d word f write its normalized binary form as κ V^d f(U/V). These forms have coefficients in the two-local integers. Let I be the ideal generated by the resulting physical words with common Boolean input X and independently chosen output bits Y. Equivalently, for fixed X allow each row to contribute either its shared row sum or 1 (the transformation between these and the Bernoulli row factors is integral). Let J use arbitrary rowwise choices 1 or a physically accessible single W_{y−x}. Then I⊂J; the degree-d pieces are the corresponding cleared modules. Denote the unscaled dehomogenized n-packet spans by P_n and R_n, respectively. Neutral packets allow padding.

## Lemma. Equal valuation minima

Use the valuative criterion for the integral closure of ideals in Noetherian domains. It suffices to check discrete valuation rings in the fraction field containing R. Fix such a valuation v, set T=U/V, and remove the common v(κ V^d) from both minima. If v(T)<0 all T−d have the same valuation and a single selected input slot attains each row's minimum. If v(2)=0 and v(T)≥0, all W_d are integral (their cancelled-denominator polynomials have integral coefficients at v); the all-neutral product is optimal. Otherwise set e=v(2)>0, ρ=max_{s∈S}v(T−s)≥0 and h=min(a,ceil(ρ/e)). Choose one input slot representative of each residue class modulo 2^h, i.e. X has precisely those selected bits. Every row's full offset interval is L consecutive integers. If ρ≤ae, the offsets attaining ρ are precisely those congruent to a maximizing s modulo 2^h. For strict fractional ρ/e this follows by the ultrametric inequality; at integer depth another offset in that class cannot have a larger valuation than the global maximum. Thus exactly one selected offset attains every row's full maximum, including h=0. If ρ>ae, each interval has exactly one offset in the class modulo L and that offset has valuation ≥ae, strictly larger than all others; in this case all slots are selected. Distinct comparisons use v(n)=e·v₂(n) for nonzero integers. With identical row numerators c=C(T)/C'(0), the unique maximizing denominator valuation prevents cancellation in that row sum, so its valuation equals the minimum of the individual W_d. Each row can choose that sum or the neutral 1. The resulting physical-span product attains v(J); hence v(I)=v(J). This covers ramified DVRs as well as the horizontal characteristic-zero case. By the valuative and reduction criteria J is integral over I and I is a reduction of J. [Huneke–Swanson, *Integral Closure*, valuative and reduction criteria](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~iswanso/book/SwansonHuneke.pdf)

## Proposition 1. Fixed-L transfer, no numerical reduction-number bound required

For some r=r(L), J^{r+1}=I J^r, hence J^n=I^{n−r}J^r for n≥r. Taking precisely the degree nd component removes all nonconstant coefficient multipliers; cancelling κ^n gives R_n=P_{n−r}R_r (products of modules mean their linear spans). Every single relaxed packet can be represented using L physical packets, one per row kernel or neutral. Consequently R_n⊂P_{n+r(L−1)} with no coefficient multiplier. This is a fixed-L statement without quantitative information on r.

There is also a same-packet-count conclusion for all sufficiently large n with a fixed-L scalar loss. Both physical and relaxed one-packet rational spans contain polynomials of leading degrees 0,1,d−1,d. The first two follow by integer-grid interpolation using the constant and the cardinal W_s (the unavailable left endpoint is supplied by the constant). For the latter two use the full-row products for singleton inputs at slots 0 and 1. They have identical nonzero leading coefficients and their difference has degree exactly d−1: after stripping the common c^L, the difference of the next Laurent coefficients is L. Thus n physical rational factors attain every leading degree through nd for n≥d−2: fixing k choices of high factors gives the interval [k(d−1),k(d−1)+n]. Distinct leading degrees span. Pick r'≥max(r,d−2); the two modules at r' are full lattices in the same rational space, so 2^B R_{r'}⊂P_{r'} for finite B=B(L). The reduction then gives 2^B R_n⊂P_n for all n≥r'. This neither estimates B(L) nor proves same-count inclusion for the finitely many smaller n.

## Proposition 2. Explicit small overhead and a scalar loss

R is a regular Noetherian ring of dimension three, since Z_(2) is a DVR and polynomial rings over regular Noetherian rings are regular. In particular every localization at a maximal ideal is regular local of dimension at most three. Lipman–Sathaye Theorem 1″ applies with J as their ideal and I its reduction; it holds without an equicharacteristic hypothesis. Their parameter δ is the supremum over the relevant localizations of analytic spread minus one minus a nonnegative integer; analytic spread is at most local dimension, so δ≤2. Therefore J^{n+2}⊂overline(J^{n+δ})⊂I^n for n≥1. [Original theorem, pp. 203–204](https://www.ms.uky.edu/~sohum/ma561/affgeom/BrSkThm_Lipman_Sathaye.pdf)

For g∈R_{n+2}, the homogenization κ^{n+2}g_hom belongs to J^{n+2}. Extracting degree (n+2)d in I^n writes it as a combination of n-packet physical binary forms κ^n P_hom with coefficients of homogeneous degree 2d in R. Set V=1 and cancel κ^n. Every remaining coefficient of degree ≤2d is a 2-local combination of monomials, each a product of at most 2L ordinary polynomials of degree ≤D. Each such factor is in the one-packet physical span: on the 2L grid sites its values are integral, all nonzero cardinal diagonal values are 2-local units, and the constant handles the missing offset −L by first matching the value there. Single W_s is obtainable by a singleton input, one active row and neutral others. Thus

    κ² R_{n+2} ⊂ P_{n+2L}.

The net overhead is (2L−2)L row slots and the scalar loss is 2L v₂(C'(0))=O(L²). With L≤s^{1/1000} both are o(s). This establishes a numerical transfer for signed linear combinations of genuine shared Boolean words. It can consume all the target precision if only one origin bit is requested. It is not a physical Boolean decision lower bound.
