# An unavoidable pole of the signed scalar under every relabeling

Let E be even and assign the occurrence wires any permutation of 0,...,E−1 in an original CNF whose variables all have even occurrence degree. Use both ports of each occurrence and the paired-Cauchy Gaussian deformation. At a primitive 2E-th root ξ, 1+T^(2d) vanishes precisely for d=E/2 among positive distances below E. The zero is simple. All singular covariances are the SAME function, Γ_(E/2), independent of which endpoint of a wire is used. The graph of these singular pairs on the actual 2E ports is a disjoint union of E/2 copies of K_{2,2}, one per unordered pair of exponents at distance E/2.

A Wick term can have pole order at most E. Attaining E forces the selection of all ports and a perfect matching made exclusively of singular edges. There are exactly 2^(E/2) such perfect matchings and all have the same leading product. The coefficient of the all-port monomial in the product of actual centered gate polynomials is nonzero: every signed OR has a nonzero top coefficient, and each even-arity COPY has top coefficient 2^(1−arity). The leading Laurent coefficient is exactly the all-port coefficient times 2^(E/2) times Res_ξ(Γ_(E/2))^E, up to the nonzero raw normalization. Consequently the pole order of the fully summed signed scalar is exactly E. The reduced rational denominator contains Φ_(2E)^E, of degree E φ(2E). This argument sums the actual gate signs and all candidate highest-order terms; no inference from a support bound is needed.

The connected SAT and UNSAT instances in paired-cauchy-ternary-contact.md have E=36N, all degrees either 4 or 12. For N a power of two, φ(72N)=24N, so their actual rational denominators have degree at least 864N² under every permutation. The high-contact fixed-layout proof and the large true denominator are independent conclusions. In particular an UNSAT origin can have a nonzero leading pole. This prevents reconstruction based on a universally polylogarithmic *actual denominator degree* on these preprocessed inputs. The pole by itself gives no lower bound on deciding the value at zero or on recurrence-based compact representations.
