# Exact shell reductions and support examples

For the October quantitative contraction see https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex . Here M is the real wire count, L the packet size, p the required shell precision, e=4 ceil(log2 s), and u the taper parameter. All layouts below are admissible choices of the paper's packing. None is asserted to be the unique outcome of its greedy ties.

## Root Frobenius interpolation

At modulus 2^p the unramified coefficient ring has Frobenius automorphism sigma. Construct sigma(U) as the unique Hensel lift of U^2 mod 2 that is a root of the defining polynomial. This need not equal a global number-field automorphism. Independently lift interpolation points as roots of x^(2^e)-x and hold the nilpotent coefficient variable V fixed. Differences of points with distinct residues are units. An integer-root tensor has every creation lift fixed, so each orbit yields e evaluations from one recursive call. There are at least 2^e-e 2^(e/2) elements of full degree, enough for the degree M(2L-1). The number of expensive evaluations per shell becomes ceil((M(2L-1)+1)/e), plus polynomial arithmetic. General children must be checked at their actual lift precision. No generic improvement of the exponential order follows.

## One direct jet per path

Replace an interpolation scalar at a designated call by Z in the coefficient ring modulo Z^q, q=ceil(3M/u)(u-i). Crossing denominators after removal of their known powers of 2 have unit constant term. Every crossing coefficient carries Z and its original 2-adic weight. Discard mode tuples beta with |beta|>=q or sum w beta>=p. Child descriptions are immutable as in the source; subsequent interpolation variables are evaluated at ordinary scalars, so each ring element carries a single extra factor q in its dimension. Hermite recovery and canonical coefficient lifts act coordinatewise. A dense jet element has e ell q p bits. This preserves polynomial space at one marked node per path, and removes the need for M(2L-1)+1 separate scalar solves there. For k=6M/D colors per weight and A=sqrt(kp), the count is bounded by exp(O(min(A,q(1+log^+(A/q))))): bound the logarithm by 2A sqrt(Li_2(z))+q log(1/z) and choose z near min(1,(q/A)^2). Only shells with u-i=o(sqrt u) get a smaller exponential order. Low shallow-shell budgets remain.

## Odd shell aggregation

Write c_(x,b)=2^(a+1+b) C_x d_x^(-b), with d_x=t-y0+x+2L zeta odd and congruent to 1 mod 2, C_x integral. At normalization weight v let h=p-v. Indices b with identical min(b,h) and residue mod 2^(h-1) have identical normalized row coefficients and source moments sum_j j^b X_j mod 2^h. For each tile and class use one mode with scalar tag H(S)=sum_b S^(a+1+b). A macro tuple gamma receives the integer coefficient [S^v] product H(S)^gamma. Ordinary powers count the ordered row-factor assignments in the source product. Restore 2^v afterward. For fixed c<1 and h<=c log2 L, there are O((M/L)(h+2^h)) class modes. Degree is <=M, yielding exp(o(M/sqrt(u))) tuples for those sectors. The statement concerns these sectors only.

## Positive-unit actual support and aggregation

Take M/2 disjoint positive unit clauses on distinct variables. Each contributes a unit row and a free variable row, joined via a binary subdivision equality. At power-of-two sizes balance every ordered pair of labels equally; choose all constrained rows in unlike-symbol packets at coordinate zero (available fraction 63/64 for each column label). Pair constrained/free slots of the same column label. At t=1,zeta=1, a positive fraction of the O(M/L) target tiles each receive k=Omega(L) constrained rows. Let B=floor(c L/(u log L)) for small fixed c. Choose each beta_b (1<=b<=B) independently between zero and floor(k/(2B)); fill remaining degree with beta_0, using only beta_0 in other active tiles. The sum over all row bits makes free rows constant one, and the chosen maximal degree forces every constrained row to use a crossing. After common row factors and powers of two, the term is positive, since all-one equality-column bits give a positive summand. Each row denominator is odd and <=4L. Multiplying by product d_x^B clears all odd denominators, and the numerator is <=(4L)^(MB)(B+1)^M 2^M L^((B+1)M). Including the extracted powers gives valuation O(M(B+1)log L)<p for sufficiently small c, since p>=24ML/u. This gives exp(Omega(M log u/u^2)) genuinely nonzero tuples at the root, where log L=Theta(u). The formula has a unique solution and can be simplified at origin. More strongly, on a different legal aligned layout each packet of unit rows is paired slotwise with an identical-position packet of free rows, giving F(T)=(sum_X product_x(W(T)X)_x)^(M/(2L)). Thus even the deformed scalar can be calculated with 2^L poly work per sample on that layout. Vanishing-support enumeration and algebraic aggregation are different tasks.

## Source-local affine rows

For a physically present lifted indicator H y=b over GF(2), define c=b+H_unmarked z at the shifted Boolean values of unmarked ports. Its mixed derivative on distinct marked ports K equals zero if c is outside the image of H_K or if the all-ones row on K is outside its rowspace. Otherwise choose lambda H_K=1_K. The derivative is 2^(|K|-rank H_K) times (-1)^(|K|+lambda c). This is an integer identity by summing a character over an affine coset. It does not authorize transferring root implications to shifted ports. For a concrete four-slot packet comprising a unit x, the two COPY2 ports, and a unit not-x, with subdivision pairs (0,1) and (2,3), the root contraction is zero. At t=1,zeta=0 the shifted contraction is 2c(1-c+c^2), c=-(L+1)/(L-1), nonzero mod 4.
