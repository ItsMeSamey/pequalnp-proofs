# Positive matching quotients at the Boolean origin

Group whole clause and COPY gadgets. Their conditional signed sums are nonnegative: at each port assignment each satisfying internal Boolean pattern contributes the same positive scalar. For disjoint regions with h internal logical bits enumerate their boundary tables in O(2^h poly(input)) time per region. A supplied elimination ordering of induced width t alternatively costs O(2^t poly(input)). Retain one Boolean representative per nonzero entry.

Every nonnegative rational two-port table admits a nonnegative weighted matching realization. Reorient port bits to obtain entries (z,b,a,k) in order 00,01,10,11, k>0 and zk≥ab: choose a corner of the larger diagonal product, or any nonzero corner if both vanish. All-zero certifies zero. With 1 indicating external occupancy, give terminals monomer weights b/k and a/k and their mutual edge weight (zk−ab)/k², with overall scalar k. Direct enumeration gives the specified table. Complementing a wire uses a forced degree-two vertex. Tested all 624 nonzero tables over {0,1,2,3,4}. All weights have polynomial bit length in the table size.

At every fixed surrounding configuration the signed sum is preserved and the new interior absolute mass equals its absolute signed sum. Both positive and negative masses therefore decrease weakly. Lightest magnitude may change, so the minority-to-lightest parameter K is measured on the reduced graph. The resulting running time is table-construction cost plus O((K+1)poly(input)) for zero testing. For witnesses pin surviving logical wires by self-reduction, then recover a saved representative for each region. Decisions fixing internal signed gadget edges are not Boolean restrictions.

NAE3 has an exact nonnegative gadget even with three ports: connect all terminals to two centers by unit edges. Only the second center allows a monomer of weight 2. One free terminal gives a single weight-2 completion, two free terminals give two unit completions, all others give none. This encodes 2 NAE. Signed NAE with each variable occurring twice consequently has K=0 after replacement, on arbitrary topology, despite exponentially many opposing terms in the default OR-pair encoding. The decision class is covered by known matching-realizable degree constraints (Shao and Živný, ISAAC 2023).

A cubic three-edge-connected bipartite OR/COPY incidence graph has no nonempty proper region with at most two boundary edges. An unbounded-size two-port region may encode arbitrary #F in its table: add (u∨v∨x) for an original variable x and set both ports true. COPY3 violates matching-support exchange. No unrestricted SAT speedup follows.
