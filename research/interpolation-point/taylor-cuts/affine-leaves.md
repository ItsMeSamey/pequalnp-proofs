# Exact integration and obstructions for affine Boolean leaves

An affine code can have a compact description and still resist a single baseline query. For an exact-cover instance of n triples on 3k elements, impose Ax=1 modulo two, with A the element/triple incidence matrix. Impose one ordinary popcount fiber equal to k modulo a prime exceeding n. Each element now has odd positive coverage and the total coverage is precisely 3k. Hence every element is covered exactly once, and the fiber count is parsimoniously the exact-cover count. Across enough primes this recovers the exact answer. This is a baseline-rank-one, moment-order-zero obstruction to a general affine-leaf integration rule. It concerns an exact algorithm across the primes, not any assertion about hardness modulo a particular fixed prime.

For a positive criterion, let variables range over a nonempty affine coset a+C and let p be odd. For a Boolean polynomial f of total degree at most d, the inclusion of the coset is 2^(−codim C) times the sum over w in the dual code of (−1)^(w·(x−a)). The uniform-cube sum of f times a character of weight >d vanishes: each multilinear monomial omits some coordinate in the character. Thus when dual distance exceeds d, the coset sum is exactly the uniform-cube sum scaled by 2^(−codim C). A supplied or efficiently enumerable complete list of the lower-weight dual words gives the corresponding exact correction for smaller distance. The computation of the surviving weighted uniform-cube sums is charged separately.

For β odd-prime residue constraints, their indicator on F_p equals the product of 1−(B_j x−h_j)^(p−1). For moments through degree q of ρ linear forms, reduce each form exponent as a function on the prime field, obtaining total-degree bound min(q,ρ(p−1)). Low-weight cutoff d=β(p−1)+min(q,ρ(p−1)) therefore suffices. Alternatively if all baseline constraints touch only s coordinates the Boolean indicator has a multilinear representation of degree at most s regardless of p; replace β(p−1) by the smaller of it and s. Additional leaf polynomial factors require accounting for their own Boolean degrees. When p>q the usual truncated exponential or integer moment convolution computes surviving cube moments for bounded β,ρ, in p^O(β)(q+1)^O(ρ) times polynomial overhead.

A complementary method is the parity-check trellis. With variable order fixed, after separating the first j columns of A from the rest, the feasible partial syndromes form a translate of an intersection of two linear images of dimension λ_j=rk(A_pre)+rk(A_suf)−rk(A). A DP retains partial syndrome, β baseline residues and the truncated moment polynomial. It costs 2^O(max λ_j) p^O(β)(q+1)^O(ρ) times polynomial overhead. This supplies exact integration for many large leaves at arbitrary precision without a promise of short dual-word enumeration.
