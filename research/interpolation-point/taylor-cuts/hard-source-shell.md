# Mode support on an NP-hard source class

Start from exact-three distinct-variable SAT with occurrence at most four ([Tovey Theorem 2.3](https://cedric.cnam.fr/~bentzc/INITREC/Files/CB11.pdf)). Introduce P_i,N_i,t_i with P_i XOR N_i XOR t_i=0. Link successive guards with t_i XOR a_i XOR b_i=0 and t_(i+1) XOR a_i XOR b_i=0. Express each parity equation by four ternary clauses with odd negative count. Add one all-positive clause (t1 OR t2 OR t3), forcing every equal guard to one. Each original clause becomes an all-negative triple on its false rails. Every original solution has exactly 2^(n-1) extensions.

The output is connected, has no pure variable or binary clause, and each variable has degree 4 through 13. Every nonempty set of variables touches at least 4|S|/3 clauses, ruling out matching autarkies. A parity block precludes every syntactic Horn renaming. Simplifying its encoding may recover the input formula, and no resistance to general preprocessing is asserted.

Use the companion [connected support proof](connected-shallow-support.md). At the normalized zero interpolation coordinate, the local q are nonpositive. An OR with odd negative count has nonnegative unmarked value and derivative sign (-1)^k or zero at order k. An all-positive OR has the opposite sign at every order, including zero. Thus the exceptional row only changes the common sign. Ensure its strict witness by moving one designated real port to a full packet within its existing ordered class. If bit-zero symbols differ, mark this port with order-zero crossing mode in addition to other marks. Otherwise place it at an even bit-zero copy slot; its real successor then makes q strictly negative at all-one columns. The swap is possible for every source low-variance label allocation: each class has M/D_arr+o(M/D_arr) real slots, but globally nonfull packets contain o(M/D_arr) slots. Equal-symbol classes allocate half the padded slots to even positions. This existence statement permits the swap; it does not cover every unmodified placement.

There are 3(3n-2) one-negative parity clauses. The number of total literal occurrences is 12(3n-2)+3m+3, and the source wire count M is at most four times that. Since m<=4n/3, the density of eligible negative row ports exceeds 1/64 by a constant, giving the required heavy tiles after excluding nonfull and equal-symbol slots. Every variable has degree >=4, so its trivalent equality tree contains d-2 COPY3 vertices. Their total number is at least half the literal occurrences and thus at least M/8. Hence actual multilinear row degree is <=7M/8; after clearing the O(MB) denominator degree the standard grid still has a constant positive fraction of good points. The companion's numerator-height bound and mode multiplicities now give exp(Omega(M log u/u^2)) surviving true contributions at a common node of every full grid, for both YES and NO source inputs. This is a lower bound on this specific enumeration strategy. The reductions do not show that every solver needs that time.
