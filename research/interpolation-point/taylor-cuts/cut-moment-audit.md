# Directional moment transfer

## Restoration obstruction for a fixed cut

Suppose k distinct paired bits are seen across a cut at T=0 in one direction. Choose a witnessing row for each bit. Keep the same-packet pairs, or color packets red and blue and keep the distinct-packet pairs with red witness slot and blue other slot. Some choice retains at least k/5 witnesses. Restrict the cross matrix to their columns and corresponding rows. It is block diagonal by packet, and each origin block is an identity matrix of dimension h<=L. Its determinant at T=1+2LZ is an integral polynomial of degree at most h(2L-1). At Z=-1/(2L) it equals one. Thus its minimum coefficient valuation is at most (a+1)h(2L-1), below p_t by the source M>=L D_arr>=L^2. Divide out the minimum to get a nonzero residue polynomial. Its fraction of roots in a full M(2L-1)+1 source grid is at most h/M. On all other sites the block has h nonzero Smith factors. Such ranks add over good blocks even when their determinant product vanishes modulo 2^p. Expected lost total rank at a uniform grid point is at most L/M times the number of retained witnesses. Thus at least a fraction 1-2L/M of the ordinary shallow-shell grid has actual rank at least k/10. This works for each fixed cut, regardless of how the legal packets were packed. A library of o(M/L) fixed tree cuts can be handled simultaneously by a union bound. It does not certify high minimum rank over all adaptively chosen trees.

At a true scalar sample form W(T)J, with J duplicating each paired bit. Assign original row predicates and independent bits to leaves of a binary tree. On every cut compute Smith generators of the two actual cross row modules over the unramified scalar chain ring. Keep the powers of two in generator rows; solve divisible pivots by exact division of representatives, not inversion of nonunits. Containment of restrictions in parent and sibling modules follows because each generator is an actual raw-row combination. An auxiliary nilpotent coefficient ring may fail to be a chain ring; the Cauchy matrices here are scalar.

Store the incoming polynomial coefficients of a cluster conditional sum with each outgoing monomial as weight. Incoming degree is bounded by D_S, sum of local predicate degrees; outgoing degree by D-D_S. Substitute sibling outgoing and parent incoming forms using the verified maps and multiply. Agreement on attainable incoming values suffices. Time and table space are polynomial factors times (D+1)^O(r) for maximum directional generator count r. Recompute indexed coefficients over unit-Vandermonde grids at merges for polynomial space and (D+1)^O(rh) time on height h.
