# Ternary palette decision on signed one-hot sources

The adapter recognizes synchronized one-hot graph-color instances and checks every returned bit assignment on the original signed clauses and parity equations. Unrecognized inputs or residuals beyond the state cap can return UNKNOWN.

Within each edge-sharing triangle component the ordered colors of an initial edge force each third vertex. A contradiction precludes a solution. Edges outside triangles are two-site frames. Every consistent frame is of the form c(v)=t_i+s_i a_i(v), with a fixed local labeling a_i(v) in GF(3), a translation t_i in GF(3), and s_i nonzero. Equate colors at shared vertices. Gaussian elimination removes the translations exactly and permits back-substitution. The residual Hs=0 asks for a full-support ternary kernel vector, a classical finite-field critical problem. Perfect matchings between triples of distinct local colors supply additional sound signed orientation equations by the parity of derangements in S3.

Zero syndrome columns may take either nonzero sign. At least two proportional nonzero columns attain the entire common syndrome line, since two independently chosen nonzero ternary scalars sum to all of GF(3). Quotient that line and record the linear correction needed for reverse substitution. A residual rank r needs at most 3^r states per sign, carrying one partial witness per state. This is polynomial for logarithmic residual rank, with no bound required on the number of peeled columns. These steps preserve feasibility, not uniform counts.

A missing target has a short certificate whenever the residual kernel has a coordinate forced zero, or four restricted coordinate functionals representing the four projective directions in a two-dimensional dual subspace. Every kernel assignment then sets at least one of the four coordinates to zero. The solver also recognizes inconsistent prism orientation cycles. On a twisted triangle ring and a compiled 42-vertex graph, orientation inconsistency proved UNSAT before the state cap or pencil test. The four-column raw matrix separately exercises the true pencil check.

A dedicated prism solver additionally reduces all translations to one three-valued disequality per base edge. A 2-degenerate base can always be extended greedily after the parity check. A supplied triangle partition is polynomially checkable. Automatic ambiguous partition discovery has a separate cap. Neither parser solves all graph instances: ordinary graph three-coloring embeds in ternary nonzero edge differences with cycle-basis constraints, and ternary-chirality-graph.md realizes arbitrary homogeneous ternary systems as ordinary graphs with source witnesses.
