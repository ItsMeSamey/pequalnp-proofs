# Growing module defects via deterministic port sampling

Use the loopless connected port macrograph and ternary sparse target polynomial from sparse-translate-port-matching.md, with m≥1 links. Assume all module vertex deletions leave the macrograph connected, the consistent parity code has free dimension r, and each incident edge uses a distinct physical port. Put L=ceil(log₂(2m+2)). At each module i put d_i=number of ports, b_i=number of nonport vertices, and h_i=maximum degree of its internal simple complement. Suppose d_i≥128L and b_i+d_i/4≥2h_i+2. No h_i-dependent enumeration is necessary. Require known or verified affine total ternary gain in the selected ports as in the earlier theorem.

If P is identically zero on the code, return NO. Otherwise compute the translate basis for P, with D≤(m+1)² retained shifts. Use ε=2^(−6L). Choose q a power of two with q≥max(2,r/ε) and q<2 max(2,r/ε) unless equality, and enumerate z=(Tr(a b^j))_{0≤j<r} for all a,b∈F_q (b^0=1). For a nonzero latent character indexed by c, sum over a first: its expectation is zero unless Σ_j c_j b^j=0 and one otherwise, by nondegeneracy of the trace pairing. The nonzero polynomial has at most r−1 roots, so absolute bias is at most ε. Every translate obeys the same bound. A defining irreducible of degree log₂q can be found by exhaustive candidates and polynomial-time irreducibility tests, with polynomial cost in q. For r=0 use the one empty center. This is the standard finite-field small-bias construction.

At module i set X to the sum of its d_i signs (+1 on unselected ports). Under the full uniform code these signs have the prescribed full parity marginal. Every proper subset of the d_i bits is uniform, so the 2L-th moment agrees with independent signs. In expanding the moment, only tuples with every index repeated evenly survive; pairing the 2L positions bounds their number by (2L−1)!! d_i^L≤(2L d_i)^L. In the small-bias translated list, the absolute difference in expectation per monomial is at most ε when its latent character is nontrivial and zero otherwise. Therefore the moment is ≤(2L d_i)^L+ε d_i^(2L). The probability of fewer than d_i/4 unselected links is at most
(8L/d_i)^L+4^L ε ≤2·2^(−4L).
Union bound over ≤2m modules and ≤(m+1)² translates gives probability strictly less than one. At least one enumerated center simultaneously leaves ≥d_i/4 ports unselected everywhere in every retained translate. Its residual graphs have even order ≥2h_i+2 and hence perfect matchings. Some retained translate hits P≠0. Try actual centers/translated codewords, construct matching completions, and replay the original witness. No oracle for modular graph matching or list of missing patterns is needed for the endpoint-sum case.

Here r≤m and q=O(m(m+1)^6), so the q²-center enumeration is polynomial in ordinary input size with uniform exponent independent of h_i, albeit impractically large. This is a separate theoretical sampler from the explicit-event implementation. Local infeasibility is possible: forbid all internal edges among a set of h_i+1 ports while retaining all others. Leaving only two of these ports at a module with no nonports produces an even nonmatchable pattern that occurs in the macro parity code. Positive feasibility on a translated pattern is certified by constructing the actual matching.
