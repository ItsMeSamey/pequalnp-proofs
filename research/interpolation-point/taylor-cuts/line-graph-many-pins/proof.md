# Any fixed number of induced-even pins on simple line graphs

Let H be a finite undirected simple graph and K its k required edges. In its line graph the degree at a selected uv is deg_F(u)+deg_F(v)−2. Thus positive degrees in each connected component of the selected root edges F have a common parity. Let F_o consist of the odd components of one witness, K_o=K∩F_o and K_e=K minus K_o. Every vertex incident to F_o is disjoint from all even-component supports.

**Odd compression.** Start with S=K_o. A defect is a vertex of positive even degree in S. In the residual graph of edges F_o minus S, the odd vertices are exactly the defects together with the vertices of F_o outside the current active vertex set. If a defect exists, its residual component contains a second odd vertex. Take a shortest path to any other odd residual vertex. Every internal vertex already has positive odd degree in S: a fresh vertex would have been an earlier odd endpoint. Add all path edges to S. Interior parities are unchanged; the starting defect is repaired; the endpoint is either a repaired defect or a newly included vertex of degree one. No new defect is introduced. Repeat. The result contains all required odd edges, has only odd positive degrees and uses at most d extra vertices beyond the endpoints of K_o, where d is the initial number of defects. Every initial defect has degree at least two, so d≤|K_o|≤k. Compression stays inside F_o and preserves disjointness from all even components.

**Algorithm.** Guess K_o, reject shared endpoints with K_e, and enumerate sets U comprising all endpoints of K_o and at most d additional vertices outside the endpoints of either pin class. In H[U] find an extension of K_o making every vertex of U odd. In H−U find an extension of K_e making every vertex even, including unused vertices of degree zero. Each is an ordinary binary boundary problem: remove forced edges, toggle parities at their ends, and process a spanning forest from leaves to roots. Accept when both succeed, returning their union. The two supports lie on disjoint vertex sets. Conversely, odd compression supplies a guessed U containing the compressed odd solution while the original even part remains available. Search takes 2^k n^{k+O(1)} deterministic time and polynomial space. This is polynomial for every fixed k. Simple line graphs without supplied roots can first be recognized and inverted by the algorithm cited in the three-pin paper.

In particular this covers four pins forming two vertex-disjoint two-edge paths in H even in the same ambient component. For a mixed solution with one odd two-edge path only its central vertex is defective, so one extra vertex suffices on that branch. The prior factor method remains polynomial even for unbounded k under its localization promise. The exponent here depends on k. The displayed line degree identity requires H simple.
