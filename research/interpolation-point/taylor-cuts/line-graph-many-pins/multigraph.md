# Fixed pins in the simple line graph of a supplied loopless multigraph

Distinct root edges are adjacent once when they have at least one common endpoint. Let t_uv be the selected multiplicity of a parallel bundle. A selected uv edge has line degree deg_F(u)+deg_F(v)−t_uv−1: its t_uv−1 selected parallel neighbors were counted at both endpoints. Write p_v for the desired total-degree parity. Each active bundle must satisfy t_uv ≡ 1+p_u+p_v modulo two. Zero multiplicity imposes no equation.

Guess the parities at every endpoint of a forced edge, at most 2k vertices. For each compulsory bundle prescribe the smallest count t at least its number of forced IDs with the indicated parity. If t exceeds bundle size reject this guess. Include the forced IDs and any required extra ID of that bundle. Only parity of this count affects any incident degree. For an optional bundle between unequal-parity vertices, discard all selected edges: their count is even. Between equal-parity vertices any active optional bundle has odd count; retain just one edge. After these operations the parity contributions of all bundles are encoded by ordinary simple edges within a common-parity class. The compulsory even-multiplicity bundles cross between the classes but make zero parity contribution.

Let T be the guessed odd compulsory endpoints, including vertices incident only to even compulsory bundles. In a witness use the previously proved odd compression on the simple within-odd skeleton, starting with its required odd skeleton edges and declaring *all* T vertices active initially. An initial defect is any T vertex of even current degree, including zero. Every T vertex has odd final skeleton degree. Thus odd vertices of the residual skeleton are precisely the defects and the as-yet-unseen final support vertices. Adding shortest residual paths repairs at least one defect per new vertex, just as in proof.md. At most |T|≤2k fresh vertices suffice and none was a guessed even endpoint.

Enumerate these fresh vertices together with T to form U, forbidding guessed even endpoints. Run the ordinary odd boundary extension on the simple skeleton induced on U and the even extension on its complement. All compulsory equal-parity bundles must occur as edges in the corresponding extension; compulsory unequal bundles lie across this partition and are absent from both extensions. Restore the required bundle multiplicities and choose an arbitrary original ID in each used optional bundle. Every final vertex degree has its assigned parity. The line-degree equation now verifies evenness at every selected ID, including each member of a compulsory crossing bundle. Conversely the compressed witness yields a successful guess. This takes 2^{2k} n^{2k+O(1)} time with input-polynomial overhead, where n counts root vertices; edge ID processing is polynomial in the total multiplicity. The theorem assumes a supplied loopless multigraph root.
