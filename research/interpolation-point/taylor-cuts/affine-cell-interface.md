# Finding a linear interface for a signed sum of affine cells

Given rational weights and explicitly specified affine cells C_i=a_i+L_i in F_2^d, let f(x)=sum_i w_i 1_{C_i}(x). Remove inconsistent cells by elimination. There is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm to return the unique smallest dual subspace U such that f depends only on the evaluations of U. This counts cancellations exactly. Zero and constant functions have U=0.

With the unnormalized Walsh convention, the transform is F(z)=sum_i w_i 2^{dim L_i} (-1)^{z·a_i} 1_{L_i^perp}(z). For any affine filter Q compute E(Q)=sum_{z in Q} F(z)^2. For pair i,j intersect Q with both normal spaces. An empty intersection contributes zero. On an intersection b+D, the character with exponent a_i+a_j sums to zero unless a_i+a_j annihilates D, when it sums to (-1)^{b·(a_i+a_j)}2^{dim D}. Multiply by the two weighted cell sizes and sum all ordered pairs. Gaussian elimination suffices. Clear input denominators by their product once; with total weight encoding length B the integer sum has O(B+d+log(m+1)) bits, including intermediate signed sums. The scaling of energy is the square of the clearing denominator.

Start U=0. If E(all)=E(U), stop. Otherwise maintain a filter Q starting at all with E(Q)>E(Q intersect U). Successively subdivide by each coordinate and retain the first half with this strict inequality. Add the resulting singleton to the basis. Positivity and additivity ensure it is outside U and has nonzero transform. After at most d insertions U spans the support. Orthogonality and Walsh inversion show that the set of translation periods equals U^perp, proving minimality. The algorithm uses O((d+1)^2) energy queries, each requiring O(m^2) polynomial-size linear systems. A verifier can alternatively test each basis frequency individually and compare the final two energies. Enumerating the quotient table separately takes 2^{dim U} evaluations of the original mixture on representatives.

As a separation from syntactic row span, partition a hyperplane by the first nonzero bit of a basis of its free coordinates, with one additional cell for their all-zero vector. These d disjoint cells collectively use normal directions spanning the entire ambient space, although their indicator sum has a one-dimensional interface.

If messages f and g on the same ambient space have minimal spaces U,V, condition on evaluations of W=U intersect V. The remaining evaluations of bases extending W inside U,V are independent under a uniform input. Consequently, with unnormalized fiber masses f_beta, g_beta, the joint sum is 2^{dim W-d} sum_beta f_beta g_beta. Two explicit mixtures also contract directly by pairwise intersections; semantic interface discovery can instead provide a compressed table for subsequent reuse.

For mere support of overlapping cells, the same recognition problem is coNP-hard. From a 3-CNF take the cell t=1 plus the unmodified falsification cell of each clause (with t free). The union is always nonempty and is constant as a Boolean predicate exactly when the CNF is unsatisfiable. Every cell has at most three fixed coordinates. A multiplicity message for these cells meets the input model of the algorithm; converting it into its union indicator is a different operation.
