# Projecting explicit forbidden syndromes through private normal directions

First solve the hard XOR equations and work in independent affine seed coordinates. Each clause's falsity equations become an empty set of points, the whole chart, or a coset of a linear kernel. Discard inconsistent falsity events and use a canonical reduced row basis for every remaining normal space S. Group equal spaces and deduplicate their forbidden binary evaluation tuples D. An empty clause in the chart forbids the unique rank-zero evaluation. Any group containing all 2^dim(S) evaluations is immediately inconsistent.

For one group let V be the span of every other live scope, T=S∩V and q=dim(S)−dim(T)>0. Extend a basis of T to a basis of S with q private rows. Convert each listed forbidden tuple into a shared and private part. Replace this group by a prohibition on exactly the shared tuples whose number of distinct forbidden private parts equals 2^q. No unlisted private word needs enumeration. If no shared tuple is blocked, discard the group. Merge any resulting group with the same shared scope by unioning its forbidden tuple sets. At T=0 the only possible alternatives are discarding the group and detecting inconsistency.

Every evaluation on V extends to the full space of seed assignments. Conditioned on it, all 2^q private tuples are possible, since the private rows are independent modulo V. All other constraints have fixed values. Consequently the new predicate on V agrees with existential elimination of the old private dimensions. Each step reduces the joint live normal rank by q. At most d successful steps are possible for a seed space of dimension d. Projection cannot increase the total number of listed forbidden tuples. Computing intersections, changes of basis and per-scope histograms is deterministic polynomial work in d and the explicit clause input. This extends the single-forbidden-point and bounded-arity eliminations of affine-private-kernel.md without an exponential-size truth table.

To recover a witness, save each step's exterior and private basis and its dictionary of prohibited private words by shared evaluation. Starting from any feasible residual seed, reverse the steps. Keep its evaluations on V unchanged. For its current shared value, choose the smallest nonnegative integer missing from that dictionary bucket. Testing integers from zero onward stops after at most one more than the number listed, even at unbounded q. A Gaussian system combining the preserved exterior evaluations and new private evaluations has a solution by independence. The restored group is satisfied, every previously restored requirement belonging to the current exterior is preserved, and induction recovers a full original solution. Finally undo the hard XOR chart and replay the original formula.

The implementation accepts an externally supplied residual seed in chart coordinates, checking it against the residual. Otherwise it uses the grouped optimal rank-drop/exception solver on the remaining forbidden flats. One auxiliary per distinct live normal, fixed to that form by a linear equality, lets the existing signed-CNF backend read these residual constraints without enumerating any width-dependent table. If the residual is empty a seed of zero suffices before lifting. This establishes a decision and witness algorithm. The number of extensions on a surviving fiber is 2^q minus its forbidden count, which may depend on the shared value. The reduced predicate alone cannot be used as an exact counter.

For a connected separation take m clauses (g∨u_i∨v_i) on one common bit and disjoint private pairs. Before the prepass the optimal conditioning-plus-exception cost p is m. A nonignored group S_i has rank three, so its intersection with the conditioning space W must have dimension at least two. Modulo the common direction g it therefore contributes at least one independent dimension supported on its own private pair. Summing over nonignored groups lower-bounds dim W by their number; each ignored singleton group costs one. Ignoring all attains equality. Each private projection eliminates its complete group, leaving an empty residual of cost zero. The original model count is 4^m+3^m, with differing multiplicities for g=1 and g=0.

The checker exhaustively compares every surviving fiber on 240 small forbidden-flat systems and validates original witnesses on 140 signed CNF/XOR instances. Additional controls cover an 80-dimensional private fiber, a fully blocked shared tuple merging into a contradiction, the connected and disjoint growing-cost families, and a residual requiring the existing backend.
