# Labelled edge templates for ordinary matching

Fix a vertex bipartition. Each template on one side has ordered endpoint lists A,B, endpoint labels a(u),b(v) in F3^j, and an offset c. For every distinct u in A,v in B there must be an edge of label a(u)+b(v)+c, whose original ID is stored. Every internal edge gain between two endpoints must be represented in some orientation. Templates may overlap. These conditions can be verified by scanning all endpoint pairs and comparing incidence triples against the original edge list.

For N vertices and h total templates, enumerate all nonnegative multiplicity vectors of sum at most floor(N/2), numbering binom(floor(N/2)+h,h). If left and right internal totals are p,q, require 2(p−q)=|left|−|right|. Remove every internal edge. For each template occurrence on either shore create two new vertices on the opposite shore. Connect the first to A with labels a(u)+c, the second to B with labels b(v). Retain original crossing edges. The resulting bipartite graph has at most 2N vertices. A perfect matching uses each occurrence at distinct endpoints, assigning it a validated original edge with the same combined gain. This reconstructs an original matching. Conversely, label every internal edge of any given matching with a compatible template, then wire its two endpoints into any unused occurrence in the corresponding multiplicity instance. Counts can differ. Testing all instances gives deterministic target decision and original witness in the bipartite solver time multiplied by the multiplicity count and polynomial overhead. For h=o(N) the count is exp(o(N)), since log binom(floor(N/2)+h,h) is O(h log(1+N/h)). Automatic templates on an internal support with t classes of identical labelled neighborhoods under pairwise transpositions: for each unordered pair of classes including identical classes, include one template for each present gain. Recognize transpositions by checking against all third vertices (internal edge is unchanged by swapping). The classes are equivalence classes since automorphisms form a group and conjugating (v w) by (u v) gives (u w). This gives at most 3^j t(t+1)/2 templates on that side. An arbitrary labelled crossing graph is allowed. The bipartition is supplied; automatic recognition here refers to the templates for that cut.
