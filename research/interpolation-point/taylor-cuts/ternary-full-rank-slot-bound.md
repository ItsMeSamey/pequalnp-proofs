# Sharp all-target threshold for full-rank slots

Let 2b independent slots each have gain set A_j affinely spanning F3^b, b≥1. Their sumset is all F3^b. Translate each slot by a concrete baseline so it contains 0. Choose b actual alternatives v forming a basis in that slot. In the group algebra over F3, write X^g for a basis element, I the augmentation ideal, and y_i=X^{e_i}−1. This ring is the truncated polynomial algebra with y_i³=0, so I^{2b+1}=0. Modulo I² the linear class of X^v−1 is sum v_i y_i, so inside the actual group-algebra span of a slot one can take a linear combination of its differences with any specified linear part y_i. Use two distinct slots for each coordinate. The product of the resulting 2b elements is exactly the product of y_i²: replacing even one factor by its quadratic error puts the term in I^{2b+1}. Since (X^{e_i}−1)²=1+X^{e_i}+X^{2e_i}, the product is the sum of all distinct group basis elements with coefficient one. Each nonzero target coefficient requires at least one original choice from every slot. Reinstating baselines translates the full group to itself.

This bound is attained by 2b−1 copies of {0,e_1,...,e_b}. To sum to (2,...,2) each unit must be selected at least twice. In a matching-port assignment, maximize the number of full affine rank slots with an ordinary weighted bipartite matching (plus compulsory contributions). On reaching 2b, find a concrete original matching at any target by group-sum DP on just b+1 witnessed gains in each selected slot; all other slots use baselines. Cost O(3^b b²) after matching witnesses and variation spans are available. This uses one of b+1 actual choices per slot, unlike binary switches with one available difference. The group-algebra proof does not require a signed or fractional matching witness.

More generally choose 2b distinct occupied slots and one concrete difference in each. If the corresponding linear forms have nonzero product in the truncated ring, the same argument makes this fixed collection of binary toggles cover all targets. On a fixed assignment one can find such a product if present by storing, for each degree k≤2b after scanning a prefix of slots, the linear span of products from k distinct prefix slots. Update with unchanged products or one token from the new slot; after each update keep an independent basis drawn from actual products with witnesses. The entire graded dimension sums to 3^b. This is a sufficient test and can fail for a genuinely full sumset.

For a fixed ordered coordinate basis let J_e denote the reduced-basis pivot indices of D_e. If distinct slots can be allocated two per coordinate i with i in J_e, choose for each slot a signed linear combination whose linear part starts at i with coefficient one. Only both pivot-1 slots can supply y_1 squared; thereafter both pivot-2 slots must supply y_2 squared, and induction forces every pivot choice. The top coefficient is one. Search over unknown assignments by guessing those 2b slots and testing ordinary perfect assignment completion. If none passes, Hall gives a nonempty coordinate set T with fewer than 2|T| occupied slots whose pivots intersect T in each assignment: any obstruction on individual duplicate demands also obstructs all duplicates of its underlying coordinates. Enumerate T and its exceptional occupied slots, forcing selected optional edges and excluding other edges touching T. All remaining slots have intrinsic dimension at most b−|T|, so their exact profiles need only lower-dimensional local queries; the bipartite assignment still uses the ambient rank. There are L^{O(b)} distinguished guesses up to factors depending on b. Exceptional slots can still require the ambient rank.

Actual matching sharpness for b≥2: complete graph on 4b−2 vertices with b+1 parallel labels {0,e_1,...,e_b} on every pair. Each matching uses 2b−1 pairs, hence its profile is the entire group except (2,...,2). Every proper linear projection is full and no nonzero translation preserves the full profile. The proof uses the classical augmentation-ideal method associated with Olson, https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-314X(69)90021-3; priority for this slot formulation is not claimed.
