# Nonenumerative layer transfer and a checkable long-flip catalogue

In the Cayley-layer high-girth brick from cayley-chord-brick-audit.md each cyclic layer has w=|Γ|/k matched pairs, since the layer homomorphism is onto. Apart from the two added chords, graph edges remain within one layer or join adjacent layers. Keep the first layer and the four chord endpoints, and sweep consecutive pairs of layers. This supplies a path decomposition of width O(w). Standard edge-introduction matching DP tracks matched status of active vertices and the accumulated ternary gain, requiring each forgotten vertex already matched. Original-edge traceback gives witnesses. For arbitrary labels the time and storage including traceback are 2^{O(w)}3^{O(b)}poly(N). As w≤p³ and N=2kw=8p^C w, one has w=O(N^{3/(C+3)}). The decomposition is explicit for the construction; a separate discovery cost is needed on arbitrary hidden layouts. The obstruction itself forces indispensable flips of size Ω(N^{C/(C+3)}) at every matching while ordinary girth is Ω(log N).

An independent general method handles graphs with polynomially enumerable primitive long cycles. In the bipartite double cover of the simple support, retain chordless cycles whose projection repeats no original vertex. Such projections are exactly the simple even cycles having no chord between vertices at odd cyclic distance: only edges between opposite layers of a lifted cycle can be its chords. A chord between vertices of the same original parity on the cycle cannot be a lifted chord. Every simple even cycle either belongs to this catalogue or its flip factors into two shorter actual flips using an odd-distance chord. Thus the catalogue connects all perfect matchings and remains sufficient under induced vertex deletion, as a chord between surviving cycle vertices cannot disappear. Choose one canonical occurrence per endpoint pair in original multigraphs, use only these choices on the long cycle and include individual parallel two-edge moves. Any physical cycle flip can first normalize its matched occurrences to canonical edges and then restore desired IDs afterwards.

Charge all L induced cycles of the double cover, including discarded nonsimple projections. Output-sensitive algorithms enumerate them in poly(input)(L+1) total time; see https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.7610 . An advertised polynomial cap may be enforced by a conservative polynomial-in-cap timeout, returning UNKNOWN if the enumeration has not completed (total output-sensitive runtime alone need not bound the first L outputs of an instance with much larger output). With F≤L+O(m²) physical templates the finite-difference method decides nonvanishing of any degree-d polynomial on perfect matching incidence vectors in (F+1)^{O(d)}2^{O(d)}poly(input), allowing unrestricted cycle length. Actual original witnesses are recovered. Orthogonal tests against already discovered lifted rows consequently construct the entire degree-d moment span via ordinary linear elimination for fixed d in this class. The explicit catalogue may be exponential on other graphs.

Even when this catalogue is exponential, a degree-aware catalogue of polynomial cardinality exists for fixed d. All differences of lifted matching vectors lie in the span of witnessed differences across actual cycle flips, by path telescoping. At most sum_{i≤d} binom(m,i) concrete flip pairs suffice to span these differences, irrespective of their support lengths. No uniform procedure to construct such a small spanning list on arbitrary graphs follows from this dimension bound.
