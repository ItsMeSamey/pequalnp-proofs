# Occupied-site Newton modes in the October packet contraction

At one packet and one crossing tile, let J be the distinct real column positions j. An empty tile contributes nothing. Write H=2^{i+1}, d_x=T−y0+x, c=C(T)/C'(0). Its exact rational kernel is c/(d_x−Hj) before reduction, as in eq:tensor-mode-expansion. All the denominators d_x−Hj have the same valuation v_x≤i. Choose j0,...,jm−1 from J greedily minimizing the 2-adic valuation of the product of the differences to previously chosen sites. Let δ_k be that minimum (δ_0=0), P_k(j)=∏_{l<k}(j−j_l). The source modes are integer linear forms Σ_{j in J} (P_k(j)/2^{δ_k})X_j, for k<m. All earlier sites have numerator zero. The exact Newton coefficient multiplying mode k in row x is

    c H^k 2^{δ_k} / ∏_{l=0}^k(d_x−H j_l).

Indeed the Newton divided difference of the reciprocal kernel is c H^k divided by this product, by telescoping. Interpolation at these m sites is exact irrespective of polynomial degree. Equivalently the interpolation remainder has P_m(j) as a factor. Each coefficient has valuation v(c)−v_x+k(i+1−v_x)+δ_k, at least 1+k+δ_k at active precision (if c=0 the crossing vanishes). Use the minimum across real crossing rows for each mode weight, omit empty row families and modes beyond precision, and keep all packet group and child port assignments unchanged. Polynomial bit complexity follows from at most m factors of O(log L) bits in P_k and from stripping known powers of two before unit inversions. Canonical edge entry lifts in the recursive child remain valid because the resulting values agree in the parent residue ring. Restrict the recursion to the truncated expansion before reducing precision as in the source.

An alternative baseline, useful independently of the greedy valuation, uses binom(j,k) on the entire tile. Re-expanding ordinary power modes by Stirling numbers of the second kind supplies extra valuation v₂(k!) in addition to 1+k. When occupied sites all lie in one residue class mod 2^h, use j=j0+2^h u and re-expand in u. The improved weights are at least 1+(h+1)k+v₂(k!) and only finitely many u sites occur. In the extreme singleton case only the constant mode survives. This enhancement saves parent work when many crossing sites share low binary digits. It assumes no favorable occupancy is guaranteed by bounded source degree.

For any chosen bases with weights w_l, replace the source bound by the exact template product ∏_l(1−x^{w_l})^{-1} and sum degrees below p_t, at unchanged child sizes and precision. The actual mode count and weight distribution govern improvement. Without an input-dependent profile the factorial gain alone changes constants and does not improve the general exponent's order. Decision-only zero testing can reuse this exact arithmetic transformation without changing its correctness.
