# Private peeling followed by exact weighted rank-drop

First apply count-preserving sparse private-normal elimination on the hard XOR domain. The caller's peel_rank_cap controls whether the remaining span is small enough for enumeration. Otherwise, work on independent evaluations of that span. Give each distinct residual normal an auxiliary evaluation bit with a defining XOR. Every residual assignment has exactly one extension to these bits.

A positive-default factor with only zero-valued exceptions becomes one ordinary hard clause per exception plus a scalar. A zero-default factor with a single positive tuple becomes a scalar times pins. Expand each remaining factor as its default plus the sum of its exceptional tuple indicators weighted by actual-minus-default differences, omitting zero coefficients. The resulting exact signed sum consists of counts of the hard CNF under additional XOR pins, times the accumulated scale. soft_terms_cap limits the number of expansion terms before they are run; flat_cap limits rank-drop certificate discovery per term. Any missed term leaves the count unknown. An optional source_flat_cap enables direct grouped rank-drop on the original input as a fallback. These are parameter caps, not wall-clock budgets.

A signed term alone does not give a witness. Given positive total mass, recover successive residual coordinates by summing the entire signed expression on each candidate zero half. If that half has mass zero, its sibling is positive. Additional pins cannot increase the hybrid optimum: restriction projects the old conditioning span to no larger dimension, and none of the old groups increases residual rank under that span. Groups of equal projected normal span are jointly resolved or jointly exceptional; merging high-rank groups costs no more than their previous total since 1+sum t_i≤product(1+t_i). After recovering all coordinates, reverse the private trace and XOR chart, and replay the original constraints. Every definite return therefore gives an exact count; UNKNOWN carries no count.

A strict mixed control uses four leaf bits y_i, two hubs u,v, clauses (u∨y_i),(v∨y_i), one clause (u∨a), and two disjoint binary clauses on fresh pairs. The disjoint pairs yield scale nine; eliminating a leaves weights 1 or 2 depending on u. The rank-six residual needs two signed terms. With peel rank cap zero and rank-drop cap two the composition returns 9(32+4)=324. Direct source rank-drop misses cap two; soft-term cap one also misses. Complementing u through a fresh XOR tie and imposing the equivalent guard preserves 324. Forbidding every hub pair gives an unsatisfiable control. The selected-occurrence examples yield (unmarked model count, marked certificate count) equal to (0,0) and (2,6); the portfolio counts the marked assignments when given that encoding. An independent checker additionally verified a tied source with one model and three marked paths.

The reference checker compared 180 seeded CNF+XOR inputs at two cap schedules with brute-force original counts and witnesses. A generous fallback forced a complete decision in the second schedule. The run used the shared protected light-verification lock, 15-second timeout, niceness +15 and 153600 KiB address-space limit. An independently written checker compared a further 28 calls, including direct fallback and both cap boundaries.
