# Genuine SAT/UNSAT collision jets can coincide to high order

For N variables indexed 0,...,N−1 use two occurrences each: a free unit at exponent i and an anchor unit at exponent N+i. Thus all occurrence labels are contiguous and all matched copies use the corresponding occurrence label. Select nonempty disjoint A,B⊂[0,N) with identical sums of i^j for 0≤j≤H. In the satisfiable formula both units on variable i are negative on B and positive elsewhere. In the unsatisfiable formula flip the free unit on every i in A∪B while retaining anchors and all COPY2 gates. The satisfiable origin count is exactly one; the other is zero. Duplicated clauses and unit propagation make both instances easy; the claim concerns the sufficiency of scalar jet data with a supplied labeling.

Use the collision-jet mode representation, writing Z_i for c_i sum b_i^d G_d. Pass to the series field with coefficients Q(G_0,...,G_H). For R_i=(1+Z_i)/(1−Z_i), the constant R_*=(1+G_0)/(1−G_0) is independent of i. The z^j coefficient of log(R_i/R_*) is polynomial in i of degree at most j. Indeed all occurrences of i enter through (1+z)^i=exp(i log(1+z)); substitute w=i log(1+z) into the single universal power series defining this logarithm. Only powers w^d for d≤j contribute. The zeroth power-sum equality gives |A|=|B| and cancels R_*. All other equalities give product_{i∈A} R_i / product_{i∈B} R_i=1 modulo z^(H+1). Thus the two free-unit products agree through order H as polynomials in ALL Gaussian symbols. Multiplying the identical anchors and COPY2 gates, and then taking Gaussian moments, preserves this agreement. There is no assumption of independence among blocks.

For existence consider all 2^N subsets. Their vectors of integer power sums have at most product_{j=0}^H (1+N^(j+1)) possibilities, less than 2^N for H=c sqrt(N/log N) with sufficiently small positive c and large N. Remove the intersection of two distinct subsets with equal vectors to obtain nonempty A and B. This yields indistinguishability at H=Ω(sqrt(N/log N)), including any fixed power of log N. Explicit examples at N=2^(H+1) split indices by parity of binary digit sum: the signed exponential generating polynomial product_{j=0}^H(1−x^(2^j)) has a root of order H+1 at x=1, yielding equal moments through H. This result allows formulas or other algorithms to inspect the CNF, choose a different variable labeling, or use other evaluation points; it only disproves decision from these low-order scalar jets alone.
