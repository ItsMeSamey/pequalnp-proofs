# Matching-first decision caching with an affine integer programming test

For n≥5 take two cycles and one Boolean matching bit a_i per column. Cycle edges carry three-bit words. Top legal words S are the cube minus 000,001,111. In decimal encoding use σ=(2 3)(4 5 6) on S and transitions t_out=σ^(a_i+c_i)(t_in), where c_0=1 and other c_i=0. Bottom legal words encode 0 through 5 with transition u_out=u_in+a_i modulo 6. At each vertex include one width-seven clause for each forbidden full local corner. There are 7n bits and O(n) clauses. A closed bottom cycle requires Σa_i=0 modulo 6. The composite top permutation is then σ, a derangement. Hence the system is unsatisfiable.

Call a full matching assignment good if its sum is 0 modulo 6. Every assignment on the first n−5 coordinates has a good extension, since the final five bits can supply any weight from zero through five. Thus there are at least 2^(n−5) good endpoints.

**Integer marginal feasibility.** At a good endpoint place weight −1 at each of the two compatible top tuples on the 2-orbit and +1 at each of the three on the 3-orbit, independently at each vertex. The weights sum to one and the incoming and outgoing marginal on each shared block agrees, since the maps preserve the orbit partition. Choose any closed bottom solution and place unit mass on its local tuples. These witnesses lift to every prefix on the path by putting the omitted matching coordinates at their good completion values. They satisfy the affine integer programming relaxation of the vertex relations and induce witnesses for individual clauses by marginalization. A nonnegative rational witness with positive mass on all compatible top tuples uses the uniform distribution on five states; a uniform mixture of six closed bottom solutions has the same full-support property. The integer witnesses can therefore be supported within this rational witness even when one first enforces basic LP support.

**GF(2) localization.** At a good endpoint each of the five legal top pairs has mass 1, yielding total 1 in GF(2). Use a closed bottom solution with unit mass. Local expectations agree on overlapping blocks and constants, and hence define a GF(2) linear functional on all squarefree monomials by assigning arbitrary values off the union of local monomial sets. The functional takes 1 to 1 and annihilates every forbidden-corner indicator. For each legal top corner it takes value 1 on the corner indicator; for each legal bottom corner choose the initial bottom state to hit it. Thus in each scope the corner indicator is in the ANF row space exactly when forbidden. All ancestors of good endpoints pass the row-space constant test by restriction.

**Signed isomorphisms.** The degree-seven projection of the row space at a partial stage with matching bits left is the coordinate span of the surviving seven-bit scopes. Its variable incidence classes identify the remaining matching bits as singleton classes, by the horizontal-twin argument in decision-cache-signed-prism.md with three horizontal twins per class. Signed substitutions induce coordinate permutations in top degree. At a full endpoint the degree-six projection recovers all scopes. If an arbitrary endpoint space is signed-isomorphic to a good endpoint space, its actual forbidden corners map into actual forbidden corners by the good-space membership test. Every endpoint has the same total number of forbidden corners, n(64−5)+n(64−6), and the map of scoped corners is injective. Therefore it maps the actual forbidden lists bijectively. In particular it identifies the bottom cycle by projection size six and preserves the existence of a closed bottom solution. Any endpoint in a good endpoint's signed-row-space class is good. Raw signed-CNF sharing also preserves this property.

On the top, the omitted words have three distinct pairwise Hamming distances 1, 2, 3, so a signed bit permutation that preserves S fixes each omitted word individually. It fixes 110 as the bitwise complement of 001. The local predicate that (110,110) is legal recovers a_i=0 at each ordinary top vertex, since 110 belongs to the 3-cycle. At vertex zero the predicate is always false. The top cycle is intrinsically identified by the five-word projection size. Its marked pattern recovers an endpoint assignment up to 2n dihedral choices and two possibilities for the exceptional bit. A good endpoint type has at most 4n assignments.

At a prefix, the set of attainable endpoint space types is invariant under signed-row-space isomorphisms because they preserve the unassigned matching set and commute with restriction after matching values are correspondingly signed. Intersect this set with the types of good endpoints. A state having any type in this intersection admits a genuinely good completion by the preceding argument and therefore passes both pruning tests. The intersection is the union of those for the two children of every matching-variable split. Induction on remaining matching bits proves that exhaustive staged search visits every good endpoint type despite intermediate cache hits or infeasible-branch pruning. Consequently there are at least 2^(n−5)/(4n) endpoint types for decision-only signed-row-space or signed-CNF caching with these tests. This concerns the specified matching-first method. The global modular condition and the top permutation decide the entire family directly.

The finite checker check_prism_aip.py verifies the stabilizer, orbit weights, global unsatisfiability, assignment lower bound, and mark multiplicities for n=5 through 13.
