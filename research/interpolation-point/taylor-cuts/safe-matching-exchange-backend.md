# Native safe edge exchanges

The solver takes a dictionary of square-free source-edge masks and GF(3) coefficients. Powers of an incidence variable can be reduced to one on the Boolean cube. At a k-matching node it obtains a maximum matching, truncates to k for a baseline, and checks the polynomial there. For positive slack it scans pairs of original edge IDs e,f whose endpoint deletion leaves matching number at least ν−2. For a completion R of size k−1, the two lifted endpoints are R+e and R+f. In the difference, terms supported entirely on the residual cancel; only monomials containing exactly one of e,f and otherwise supported on surviving edges contribute. Each such term loses a variable. An actual nonzero witness at the difference leaf supplies an original matching at one of the two endpoints.

The positive-slack safe-exchange connectivity theorem justifies a negative result if all these differences vanish. A recursive step reduces size by one, degree by at least one and slack by at most one. Thus at initial slack s≥d all nonconstant nodes have positive slack, while at s≥d−1 a tight nonconstant node can only be affine. The positive-slack linear case reduces directly to ordinary matching on the residual because its differences are constant. The maximum number of nodes is at most (1+binom(m,2))^d for m original edge IDs; ordinary matching, polynomial restriction and original-ID reconstruction at each node take polynomial overhead. With s≥d−1 a supplied polynomial affine nonvanishing oracle gives the same fixed-degree polynomial clock. No such oracle is implemented internally for arbitrary tight instances.

At smaller slack, a tight node evaluates actual size-k matchings by a least-vertex branching enumeration with maximum-matching feasibility pruning. There are at most binom(m,k) candidate size-k edge sets per tight node; without a cap this part can be exponential. The brute cap counts evaluated tight-fallback matchings globally and can yield UNKNOWN. Polynomial work per enumerated candidate follows because each unpruned branch extends to a solution, there are at most n levels, and each node has at most m+1 children. All returned witnesses are checked against original edge IDs.
