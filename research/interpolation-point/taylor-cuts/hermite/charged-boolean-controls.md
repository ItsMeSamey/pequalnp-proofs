# Charged Boolean packet witnesses

An integral-span identity is not automatically a pair of Boolean network instances. The following construction uses only nonnegative Boolean vertices at the parent call and charges every selector connection. It applies to a designed legal packet placement, not automatically the paper's particular deterministic tie-breaking or its direct SAT-to-network conversion.

**Protected logical selector.** Represent one bit by the two words 10 and 01 on two rails. Route each rail in a separate packet, with the communication wire in one real slot, all other columns pinned to zero and all other row factors free. For the active slot the deformed value is exactly w(T)X with w=W₀. At both endpoints allow precisely the two one-hot words. If their words disagree, a row one is requested on a zero column, so the transfer is zero. If they agree, the selected one contributes w and the zero rail contributes one. The logical link is exactly w(T) times an undeformed equality. Extra real free/pin-zero fillers in this arrangement integrate to one. The identity is over the rational polynomial ring, before any precision reduction.

**Two Boolean networks.** At L=2 put a=W₀, b=W₁ and c=W₋₁. For a single full two-slot data packet, there are physical pinned-column, Boolean row-monomial responses 1,a,b,c,ab,ac,(a+b)(a+c). The signed combination with coefficients 27,12,9,27,74,90,−40 is (T²−1)³. Choose a local nine-bit index for 279 multiplicity labels: the first 239 are positive, the final 40 negative. On the column side test that the label is valid and clamp the two data bits to the corresponding column input. On the row side, the same label selects the required Boolean row monomial. Share its nine bits by protected links. Give the row vertex two additional protected links carrying a running parity; impose s_i=s_{i−1} XOR [label is negative]. For adjacent vertices attach their parity links to one column equality, with an initial parity-zero pin and final observer imposing even or odd. The two resulting networks differ only in the terminal observer. All truth tables are fixed bounded-arity Boolean predicates. Each label and parity state has exactly one allowed two-rail codeword. Therefore

    F_even(T) − F_odd(T) = W₀(T)^(11k) (T²−1)^(3k).

There are k full data packets, 11k protected logical links and 22k rail packets. With one filler in each length-two rail packet this is 46k real wires before optional balance padding; the largest row vertex has arity 2+2×(9+2)=24. W₀(0)=1, so the two origin counts differ by (−1)^k. On the active odd residues t=±1, W₀(T)=−(T+2)(T²−1)/2 has coefficientwise two-adic valuation exactly two after T=t+4z. The last factor has valuation 9k. Thus the networks have identical entire jets at both nodes modulo 2^(31k) despite opposite origin parity. Their counts need not distinguish SAT from UNSAT. For fixed r,L the finite label-pair classes can be filled out by additional free-row/zero-column edges and balanced groups at constant factor overhead. This construction yields a linear precision lower in physical wire count for this designed Boolean tensor family. It does not transfer the uncharged 9k+1 optimum from the original pin module to networks on the original 2k wires, and fixed L is not the asymptotically growing packet scale of the published schedule.

At L=4, another example needs no global selector at all. Fix X=0110 (slot zero the low bit) and use the Boolean row predicate (Y₀ XOR Y₁) AND (Y₂ XOR Y₃). Its two disjoint row factors give origin one. On the odd shell the complete jets have minimum valuation five (the isolated Y=X pin has valuation three); k independent copies are therefore indistinguishable from zero modulo 2^(5k). One can verify the per-packet conclusion from the first twelve scaled coefficients and the higher-order divisibility: each order-j coefficient of W(t+αz) is divisible by 2^j because its positive-order factors α/(t−q) are even. The same bound holds after forming any network of integral local tables. A stronger signed local difference −(Y₀−Y₁)(Y₂−Y₃) at this X has value one at the origin and gives (W₂−W₀)(W₋₂−W₀). Each difference acquires an extra factor two over the common C(T)/C′(0) at odd t, giving total valuation eight. Encoding its sign across repeated packets requires a parity observer or other explicitly charged coupling.
