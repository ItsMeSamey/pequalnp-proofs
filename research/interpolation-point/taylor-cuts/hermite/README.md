# Joint Hermite data at small packet sizes

For each L=2^a, use the paper's grid S={−L,…,L−1}, α=2L, h=ceil(3M/u), and q_t=h max(0,u−v₂(t)) at nonzero t. The input jet records the coefficients of z^j in F(t+αz), for j<q_t, at common precision modulo 2^P. The target here is F(0) modulo 2. All numbers below are exact small-case results; no asymptotic inference about the SAT-generated network follows from them.

For an integral module generated by polynomials f_k, form the jet matrix A with columns indexed by f_k and target row b_k=f_k(0). The target is determined on all integral coefficient vectors if and only if 2^(P−1)b is in the row span of A modulo 2^P, by the Smith form over this finite principal ideal ring. The script checks membership by picking a least-valuation entry, normalizing its unit, eliminating the corresponding target coordinate and removing its column. The remaining ambiguity in the pivot multiplier annihilates the whole pivot row because its entries are divisible by the pivot's two-power. Binary search gives the least uniform precision. All generator jets are computed directly from the cancelled numerator product of W_d and its common denominator, retaining 120 bits; tested thresholds stay below this cap.

On products of exactly M grid generators W_d, include d=−L as well as all legal slot differences to describe the envelope generated by physical one-slot forms together with constants. This is valid for the mathematical envelope because the complete Lagrange cardinal basis sums to one and each scaling at its own node is a two-adic unit. It does not realize an arbitrary aggregate envelope element in a physical M-wire packet. With constants included, least P is 5,10,14,19 for L=2,u=1,M=1,…,4 respectively; 5,9,13 for L=4,u=2,M=1,…,3; 25 for L=4,u=1,M=2; and 7,13 for L=8,u=3,M=1,2. Restricting instead to homogeneous legal slot differences, without adjoining 1, changes these thresholds to (3,5,7,9), (3,5,7), 12, and (4,7), respectively.

In the physical single-packet response model, fix all Boolean column inputs to X and vary one Boolean row tensor on all L slots. Each column of the jet matrix is now the response of a point mass at one Boolean row word Y, and the target is the row tensor entry at X. Smith membership handles arbitrary integral row values. For Boolean row entries the script instead enumerates every one of the 2^(2^L) possible tables in Gray order; identical jet signatures with different target bits are exact counterexamples. At L=4,u=1,X=1111 (slot zero is the least significant bit), the integral module requires 27 bits but Boolean tables need two. For X=0101 the thresholds are 24 and 12. At L=4,u=2,X=1111 they are 9 and 2. These finite Boolean calculations demonstrate that the linear span need not capture all source restrictions. They do not model every root CNF vertex or a whole active schedule.

Pin words alone can underestimate the required precision. At L=4,u=1,X=0110 the isolated point mass at Y=X becomes detectable at four bits. Yet Boolean tables with support bitmasks 0x350 and 0x530 have identical retained jets modulo 256 and disagree at X. Exhaustive comparison gives nine bits as the least uniform precision for all Boolean tables at this X. Thus there are exact inter-word cancellations beyond a single pin's content.

Cost caveat for ordinary joint interpolation: for q_t integer samples t+αj, 0≤j<q_t, split their original-value Lagrange weights by residue class. At point (t,j) of valuation i, the arithmetic precision loss in its individual origin weight is −v₂(E_t(0))+(q_t−1)(a+1−i)+v₂(j!)+v₂((q_t−1−j)!), with E_t as in the tapered Hermite lemma. Alternatively, selecting every integer j≤N except multiples of 2^u lowers maximum precision, but at the deepest active shell J=floor(N/2^u)≈M L/2^u while D_i=2L/2^u; the source proxy sqrt(M J/D_i) is Θ(M). For monotone shell multiplicities with total degree coverage, q_0=Ω(M). Some shell before q falls below q_0/2 has q_i−q_{i+1}=Ω(M/u), making the cross-class loss at least (D_i/2)(q_i−q_{i+1}). That class has Ω(M) required evaluations and odd local arguments among consecutive samples, not merely the uniquely cheap ζ=0 evaluation. This accounting does not settle nonmonotone, source-sensitive, or nonlinear joint reconstruction.
