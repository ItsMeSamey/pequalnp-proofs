# Physical packet products and two-bit Boolean recovery

For fixed packet column X, let F(T) be the Boolean multilinear extension of A evaluated at W(T)X. At t=−1 every L=2^a≥2 satisfies W(−1)=−P, where P shifts right with a leading zero. Also αW'(−1)=2(P+E) modulo four, where E maps the last column bit to the first row. Indeed C'(−1)/C'(0)=−1. For an off-shift slot difference d, W'_d(−1)=1/(1+d); multiplication by 2L gives valuation at least two except for d=L−1, where it equals two. The derivative at d=−1 is 1/L by the logarithmic derivative of the remaining factors. Put b=PX and let h0,h1 be the first two scaled jet entries. Expanding multilinearly modulo four gives h0+h1=A(b)+2 X_last Δ₀A(b) mod 4: interior first differences from W(−1)=−P cancel those from the derivative. For Boolean-valued A, A(b) equals the low bit of h0. Therefore the value of A on the cyclic rotation of X is the parity of (h0+h1+(h0 mod 2))/2. This gives origin parity in particular if X is all ones or A is invariant under rotation. No bound on L is needed.

Exact counterexample after column summation: at L=2 take A=1[Y=00]. Let the first column observer select only binary word 01, or the second select words 00 and 10, numbering slot zero as the low bit. Both aggregate jets (h0,h1) equal (2,2) mod 4, but the origin counts are respectively zero and one. For two pinned packets, the product of their two spill row bits likewise has zero first-order jets even when both rotated spill bits are one. Exact aggregation of the shifted target can condition on each packet's spill bit, clamp that row input, and negate its other formal row inputs before evaluating at W(−1)=−P. More directly, the circular shift P+E connects only low-bit-local groups including its wrap link. Neither computation restores the identity wiring on arbitrary tensors.

For k full L=2 packets, the integral module spanned by all products of physical pin responses requires precisely P=9k+1 bits in the uniform two-node jet schedule to determine its value at the origin modulo two. Denote the three legal one-slot functions by a=W₀,b=W₁,c=W₋₁. Using the three nonzero pinned columns, linear row tests and the row monomial Y₀Y₁ shows the one-packet module contains 1,a,b,c,ab,ac,a²+bc. An exact polynomial identity is

    (T²−1)³ = 27+12a+9b+27c+34ab+50ac−40(a²+bc).

Thus its k-th power belongs to the k-packet module, takes odd value at zero, and both F(±1+4z) are divisible coefficientwise by 2^(9k). This lower bound applies to all linear combinations of physical pin products, not just an individual pin or root CNF.

Conversely all polynomials here have degree at most 6k. Write F=E(D)+T O(D), D=T²−1, with deg E≤m=3k and deg O<m. Set U=D/8. On each branch T=±1+4z the change z↦±z+2z² has invertible linear coefficient and integral formal inverse. From the input jets one can therefore compute F(±sqrt(1+8U)) up to order m. Half-sums and half-differences followed by unit division recover E(8U), O(8U) modulo 2^(P−1). For the even coefficients below degree m this gives sufficient accuracy even after dividing by 8^j. At degree m subtract the odd contribution from the positive branch. Since the odd polynomial has no coefficient of degree m and every nonconstant coefficient of sqrt(1+8U) is divisible by four, its degree-m contribution is known modulo 2^P. The top even coefficient can now be divided by 8^m to recover parity. Summing the recovered values at D=−1 yields F(0) modulo two. The source retains 6k coefficients per node, which includes the required terms through degree m. This proves the upper at P=9k+1 and it was independently checked.

The executable multiple.js enumerates all physical local pins and compresses their coordinate vectors over Z/2^120 in the product of the truncated node jet rings, retaining origin as a separate coordinate. Packet products use componentwise truncated convolution. Least-valuation pivot row operations retain generators in their original coordinates; each resulting generating set has size at most the number of coordinates. For L=4,u=2 and one/two full packets, the least uniform precision was 17/35, and the least possible max per-shell scaled budget max_i 2^i P_i was 26/51. At L=4,u=1 the uniform thresholds were 50/99. At L=2 the numerical values 10,19,28,37 agree with the theorem. A streamed implementation of the general local enumeration needs ≤4^L poly(s,B) bit operations per packet and polynomial space given precision cap B; the tiny checker stores the pin columns for transparency. These are not claimed optimal on Boolean-CNF inputs.

Precision convention: E and O may have rational coefficients that are not individually two-adic integers. Recover each even coefficient up to additive error in 2Z₂ after dividing its known scaled coefficient by 8^j, then sum the rational lifts at D=−1 before extracting the parity of their integral total. The top coefficient need not have a separately defined parity.
