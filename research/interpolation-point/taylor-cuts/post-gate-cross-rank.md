Pair each primary packet with an identical-layout packet of free COPY1 rows. Their slotwise paired column bits express the native subdivision COPY2 of one variable per primary row occurrence. After summing Y, COPY1 gives 1, OR2 with arguments q,s gives s+(1−s)q, and units give q or 1−q. For a group cut pair each of 32 A symbols with one of 32 B symbols. For each pattern of the remaining coordinates make clusters of six packets: crossing primary P having coordinate-zero pair (A,B), dormant duplicate D, one free twin for each, local primary Q having (A,A) at coordinate zero, and its free twin. Let q be the parity at which P rows have symbol A and columns B. Other coordinate conventions agree, so the P,Q complete row labels match at parity q, and P,D match everywhere. For b unequal other coordinates, the P template has 2^(b+1)H/L packets and Q has 2^b H/L. This fits the required 4:2 allocation. Leftover templates are paired units/free twins.
