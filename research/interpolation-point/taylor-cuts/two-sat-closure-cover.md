# Vertex-cover parameter for signed 2-SAT with arbitrary XORs

Start from any satisfying Boolean baseline of the 2-CNF, reject if there is none, and complement bits to make the baseline zero. In this chart, implications have unary antecedents, and the remaining nontrivial clauses are negative exclusions. Form the feasible singleton implication closures P₁,…,P_N, collapsing equal closures. Every model is the union of its true variables' closures. Conversely each union satisfies all implications. A union violates a unary or binary negative clause only if a single closure already violates it or if the union of two selected closures does. Single closures were verified, so it suffices to exclude incompatible pairs. The baseline and these data are provided by the [closure preparation routine](/workspace/scratch/compression/two_sat_generator_interaction.py).

In the reduced XOR coordinate space let λ_i denote the syndrome of toggling variable i, and shift the target by the baseline. For generator selection bits q_j its union has i-th bit OR_{j∈S_i} q_j, where S_i={j:i∈P_j}. Discard empty supports and zero weights. Combine equal supports by XORing their vector weights; a combined zero removes even a large apparent interaction. Construct a graph on generators with every forbidden pair as an edge and a clique on every distinct surviving support. Let τ be its minimum vertex-cover size. Using successively larger budgets and branching on either endpoint of an uncovered edge finds a minimum cover in O(2^τ polynomial(input)) time: the sum of binary search-tree sizes for budgets zero through τ is geometric.

Enumerate all 2^τ patterns on the cover. Reject selections containing a forbidden pair and remove outside generators conflicting with a selected cover vertex. Outside the cover there are no forbidden pairs. A surviving support meeting a selected cover vertex contributes its weight as a constant; any other support has at most one available outside generator. Consequently all remaining syndrome conditions are binary linear equations. Gaussian elimination with tagged columns recovers one outside selection, if one exists. The union of the chosen closures, XORed with the baseline, is checked against the original CNF and XOR rows. Enumeration and cover discovery each cost O(2^τ polynomial(input)), additively. The exponent is independent of XOR rank. Distinct generator selections can represent one model, so this establishes decision and witness recovery only.

The [implementation](/workspace/scratch/compression/two_sat_closure_cover.py) and [checks](/workspace/scratch/compression/two_sat_closure_cover_check.py) cover a rank-24 connected conflict star with τ=1, a case where identical toggle neighborhoods cancel completely, and a connected star where raw multi-XOR SCC hull peeling stalls but one cover variable decides infeasibility.
