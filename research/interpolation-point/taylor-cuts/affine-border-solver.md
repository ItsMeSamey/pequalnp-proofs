# Quadratic flat avoidance in polynomial time per polynomial-size clique basis

The input is a list of forbidden affine subspaces over F₂, each of codimension at most two after normalization. Expand each consistent codimension-two flat's violation indicator as the product of its two satisfaction indicators. Multilinearize with x_i²=x_i. It is zero on every feasible assignment. Gaussian elimination in descending graded lexicographic monomial order gives identities replacing pivot pairs by smaller squarefree monomials of degrees zero, one or two. Among squarefree monomials of equal degree this order compares the integer masks numerically, with higher coordinate indices more significant. Unit constraints and any additional consequences need only be checked at the end.

Let H have an edge for each nonpivot pair and let C be its family of cliques, including singletons and the empty clique. Enumerate C by repeatedly extending a growing clique to higher-numbered common neighbors. The enumeration has polynomial delay and can stop at a declared bound. Only the distinct squarefree masks x_i c for c in C require reduction. Initialize the value of each clique to its unit basis vector and process the other border masks in increasing graded lex order.

For a border mask z=x_i c outside C choose a pivot pair iv with v in c, and put q=c without v. Each constant or linear term t of its substitution contributes the already known reduction of tq. For each lower pair ah of that substitution, take the reduction of aq first. For every clique c' occurring in that result, add the reduction of hc'. Accumulate all additions over F₂. Each lookup is a clique or border. To verify the order, temporarily use the ordinary, unmultilinearized graded lex order. By induction c' is at most the ordinary product aq. Thus hc' is at most haq, which is strictly smaller than ivq=z by multiplicativity of this order and the strict pivot rule. Replacing any positive exponent by one can only decrease degree. This also proves inductively that each stored reduction uses only cliques at most its own input. Every rewrite preserves evaluation on every genuine model; neither confluence nor independence of the clique monomials modulo the ideal is assumed.

For each variable assemble its multiplication table from these reductions. A model x has the nonzero evaluation vector of all clique monomials (its constant entry is one). This vector is simultaneously a left eigenvector with eigenvalues x_i. Starting with the full evaluation space, intersect with the two candidate eigenspaces of each multiplication table in succession, keeping the variable prefix. The two children of a space have disjoint intersection; the sum of their dimensions is at most their parent's. Thus each level contains at most |C| live prefixes. The tables need not commute. Finally check the candidate prefix assignments against all original nonvacuous flats, which removes spurious candidates. Every genuine model survives, and each verified assignment is counted only once.

With input length L the algorithm takes poly(L,n,|C|) time and space. A budget refusal asserts only that this chosen graph has more cliques than permitted. If H is triangle-free then |C|=1+n+|E(H)|, even if H has a large matching or large vertex cover. The bound concerns this coordinate and monomial order. It implies no unconditional polynomial bound on an arbitrary flat family.
