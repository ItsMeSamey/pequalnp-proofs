# Exact minimum of additive signed-cell costs on a supplied tree

First solve the global affine equations. An inconsistent system is infeasible; otherwise substitute a bijection x=x₀+Kt from free seed bits to feasible assignments in every original cell. For each leaf cost determine its minimum dual interface U_i on this restricted space by the [Walsh-energy algorithm](/workspace/scratch/compression/affine-cell-interface.md). For a supplied binary tree put S_v equal to the sum of descendant interfaces, O_v equal to the sum of all other leaf interfaces, and W_v=S_v∩O_v. The table at v gives, for every functional on W_v, the minimum sum of its leaf costs over extending evaluations on S_v.

At a leaf extend a basis of W_i to a basis of U_i. A linear right inverse realizes any chosen basis evaluations as one seed. Other choices differ only by the annihilator of U_i and therefore yield the same leaf value. Pin each W_i pattern, pull every signed cell back along this section, then minimize on the free U_i coordinates with the [affine-edge cache](/workspace/scratch/compression/affine-edge-cache.md). Save an attaining evaluation of the entire leaf basis.

For children L,R, enumerate each functional on J=W_L+W_R; use its restrictions to W_L and W_R to look up the two child values. Also W_v⊆J: for w=l+r in the outside span, l is in S_L∩(S_R+O_v) and r is in S_R∩(S_L+O_v). Each candidate sum updates the minimum at its W_v restriction. This enumeration tests the full child-boundary consistency. In particular S_L∩S_R lies in both W_L and W_R, so child minimizers for a candidate agree there and glue to one functional on S_L+S_R. Conversely any evaluation giving a parent solution induces an enumerated functional on J. These two implications prove the recurrence. At the root the boundary is zero. Replay stored choices down the tree and jointly solve all winning leaf evaluations by Gaussian elimination, then lift the seed into the original coordinates. Independent seed bits need no enumeration for minimization.

For fixed explicit rational inputs, preprocessing of spans, cell restrictions, Walsh energies and bases takes polynomial work. Write j_v=dim J at an internal node and w_i=dim W_i at a leaf. Join work is polynomial(input) times Σ_v 2^j_v, storing one record for each of 2^dim W_v keys per node. Leaf work is Σ_i 2^w_i times a per-fiber affine-cache cost, or more precisely the sum of those costs over its fibers. That cache can take exponential time in the number of free leaf coordinates. With at most P representatives per order layer and polynomially many cell terms it takes polynomial(input,P) per fiber. The implementation processes leaf caches one at a time, and retains their selected evaluation only. Tables can require exponential space in the boundary dimensions. Finding an optimal tree is outside this algorithm.
