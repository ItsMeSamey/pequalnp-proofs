# One-sided matching witnesses for additive ternary labels

Assign every input edge its own variable, even for parallel edges. Work over a binary field of even extension degree containing a primitive cube root of unity w. For each character u of the b-dimensional ternary label group, put X_e w^{u·g_e} in both off-diagonal matrix entries of edge e, summing entries for parallel edges. Its Pfaffian is the sum of the perfect-matching monomials weighted by their characters: signs disappear in characteristic two. Sum the 3^b Pfaffians after multiplying by w^{−u·target}. Character orthogonality kills wrong labels and gives 3^b=1 in this field on the desired label. Thus the formal result is nonzero exactly when a target matching exists, since distinct edge sets have distinct monomials.

At n vertices its total degree is n/2. Independently sampling each edge weight uniformly from a field of size q≥max(4,n) gives miss probability at most n/(2q) on positive even-order instances, by polynomial identity testing. The implementation takes the smallest even field degree achieving the size bound and constructs an irreducible modulus deterministically. Independent rounds multiply the failure bound. The empty perfect matching is handled by the unit empty Pfaffian. A zero result is reported as UNKNOWN, including on true negatives. SAT carries original edge IDs for direct deterministic checking.

Extraction fixes the initial evaluation and picks one remaining vertex. The current polynomial value is a sum, over incident edges, of that edge's weight times the residual evaluation after deleting the two endpoints and subtracting the edge's label. A nonzero sum has a nonzero summand. Continue on one such restriction until the matching is complete. This needs no additional random choices. The time per round is 3^b times a polynomial in the explicit graph and label sizes, and streaming the characters takes polynomial space. The probability guarantee requires independent uniform weights; arbitrary supplied weights retain soundness of positive witnesses only. The method applies to witness decision and gives no exact matching count.

The checker compares 68 filtered evaluations and extractions with direct edge-set monomial sums. For two parallel, equally labelled edges it enumerates all 16 uniform GF(4) weight pairs: exactly 12 succeed. Equal weights give a genuine cancellation; an independently chosen second evaluation can still recover an edge.
