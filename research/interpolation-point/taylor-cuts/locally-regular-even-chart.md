# An all-even graph-state chart through native regular-matroid markers

Give a simple graph with adjacency A binary syndrome labels of dimension h. The all-even decision asks for an induced even subgraph S with a specified label sum. Form the block space (x,y) with one two-bit block per vertex, symplectic pairing x·y'+y·x' and graph Lagrangian L_A={(x,Ax)}. Let Q(w) count the nonzero blocks of w modulo two. Locally Q(u,v)=u+v+uv, so Q polarizes to the displayed symplectic form. Its restriction to a Lagrangian is linear. There is a unique affine coset c+L_A satisfying ⟨c,v⟩=Q(v) for every v in L_A, by nondegeneracy and L_A=L_A^perp.

On a simple undirected graph, x·Ax=0, giving Q(x,Ax)=(1+A1)·x. Hence c=(0,1+A1) works. The full-support words of c+L_A correspond exactly to all-even vertex sets: take S where x is zero. At these indices, the second bit (Ax+1+A1)_i equals 1 iff (A 1_S)_i=0. For unselected vertices full support holds from their first bit. The label equation is an affine linear score on the word since 1_S=1+x.

Suppose a supplied product of invertible binary 2-by-2 block maps, optionally permuting sites, takes L_A onto L_D for a simple bipartite graph D with cross matrix B. Such block maps preserve zero, Q and the symplectic pairing. Therefore the full-support words map bijectively between the two canonical affine cosets. On the target coset all original syndrome forms pull back to affine linear scores on its free first coordinates, hence to affine linear scores on its selected vertex bits.

For selected bits (u,w) on the two shores of D, an even induced subgraph is exactly a disjoint cycle (Bw,w) and cocycle (u,B^T u) for the binary matroid [I|B]. Each target syndrome row uses only the nonbasis coordinates of the cycle and the basis coordinates of the cocycle. Arbitrary constants in the transported affine score shift the target. If [I|B] is regular, the fixed-marker theorem in research/regular-markers/README.md gives a deterministic polynomial-time decision and witness at fixed h. The inverse block transformation gives the initial witness. All transformations, the fundamental representation and equality of the two Lagrangians can be verified by binary linear algebra; the result assumes that a suitable block transformation is available. This covers the identity transform as a special case.

For initial local marks a, toggle the off-diagonal clique on the marked vertices and append the check a(S)=0, as in affine-nae-two-label-controls.md. The chart applies if the resulting all-even instance has such a supplied regular representation. The circle-graph matching construction instead handles arbitrary local marks directly on its input chord graph.
