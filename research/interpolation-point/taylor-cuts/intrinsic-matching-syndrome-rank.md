# Affine dimension of attainable matching syndromes

On a loopless graph with ternary vector edge labels, find one perfect matching M0 or return infeasible. The empty graph has only the empty matching. In a nonempty graph subtract the original label of M0 from every edge incident to one fixed vertex. The new label of every matching equals its old label minus the base label.

Let L be the translated edge-label matrix. Compute a basis for the ternary span of incidence vectors of all perfect matchings using Lovász's deterministic polynomial algorithm over a fixed finite field. The image D under L is exactly the linear span of attainable differences. Thus d=dim D is intrinsic and computable in polynomial time. If the translated target lies outside D return UNSAT with a linear separator. Otherwise Gaussian elimination finds d coordinate positions injective on D; project all edge labels and the translated target onto these coordinates. Full and projected target equality agree for all matchings. Check any witness on the original instance. Group-character methods cost O*(3^d), with unchanged random-miss behavior. Two parallel edges of labels 0,1 show that the affine hull may contain an unattainable target.

A second way to construct the span uses Silina's polynomial algorithm for a perfect matching lattice basis of actual matchings. Delete disallowed edges by forcing individual edges and testing for perfect completion. For each matching-covered component extend local basis matchings by the base on other components. Every global matching difference is a sum of local differences. In each component the degree equation forces the integer coefficients of a matching expressed in the lattice basis to sum to one. Parallel edges may be simplified to one representative for each endpoint pair, adding all differences of parallel allowed edge labels, witnessed by single-pair swaps.

This yields deterministic avoidance of a supplied affine subspace whenever the attainable affine hull is not contained in it. Delete edges while the hull still certifies some matching outside. An inclusion-minimal resulting edge set must be a single such matching. Avoidance of one forbidden residue does not in general decide a prescribed ternary residue.

For bipartite graphs contract the base edges, directing each unmatched edge from its left endpoint's pair to its right endpoint's pair and subtracting the base label at its tail. A simple directed cycle is an actual alternating flip. Parallel alternatives give loops. Within each strongly connected component choose paths P_u root→u and R_u u→root. Decompose every closed walk P_u+(u,v)+R_v and P_v+R_v into simple directed cycles. These span the circulation space: weight the first walks by arc flow and subtract the second walks with total incoming flow at v. Every auxiliary root path cancels by conservation. Their gains therefore generate exactly D, giving polynomially many concrete single-flip witnesses.

Lovász's fixed-field theorem is quoted as Theorem 20 in https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2023.28 . The lattice-basis construction is https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03863 .
