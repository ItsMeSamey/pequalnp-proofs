# Compatible level-three colour marginals and a one-hot SDP

Let the host graph be simple, 6-regular, and of girth at least nine. This fixed girth can be imposed on the previous random regular family with positive limiting probability. Write e_v and e'_v for mutually orthonormal coordinate bases of two copies of real vertex space. Define

    a_v = 6 e_v − 3 Σ_{dist(v,u)=1} e_u + Σ_{dist(v,u)=2} e_u + √12 e'_v .
    T_uv = ⟨a_u,a_v⟩/132.

The matrix T is positive semidefinite and has diagonal one, since the squared norm is 36+6·9+30+12. At distances 1, 2, 3, 4 and larger than 4 its respective off-diagonal numerators are −66, 25, −6, 1 and 0. Girth ≥9 ensures that each pair at distance at most four has a unique path of length at most four and that all radius-two overlaps expand as in the 6-regular tree: at distance one the overlap gives −36−30, at distance two it gives 12+9+4, at distance three it gives −3−3, and at distance four only the midpoint contributes.

Set p_uv=(1+2T_uv)/3 at distinct vertices. These numbers are equality probabilities in [0,1/2], and vanish on edges. Use uniform single-vertex colours. Give each ordered equal-colour outcome probability p_uv/3 and each ordered unequal-colour outcome (1−p_uv)/6. For a triple with pair probabilities a,b,c give the all-equal equality partition probability t=max(0,(a+b+c−1)/2); give the three partitions having exactly one equal pair respective masses a−t,b−t,c−t; give the all-distinct partition mass 1−a−b−c+2t. These sum to one and are nonnegative: for example (a+b+c−1)/2≤a because b+c≤1. Conditional on the partition, inject its classes uniformly into the three palette values. It follows that every triple projects to exactly the prescribed pair distribution. No improper edge is ever selected. This is an actual ternary level-three Sherali–Adams family on all vertex subsets of size at most three.

Let X_vi be the formal one-hot indicator. Then E[X_vi]=1/3 and the centered second-moment matrix is (1/3)T⊗(I₃−J₃/3), which is positive semidefinite. Together with the means this gives a PSD moment matrix including the constant coordinate. For u=v it enforces one-hot consistency, and for adjacent u,v the diagonal-colour pair entries vanish. The pair moments of this SDP are exactly those realized by the triple marginals above. Thus even their simultaneous imposition is feasible. In a Boolean lifted-clause convention local nonnegativity checks follow when all involved literals live on at most three graph vertices; the naming of levels can shift with the width of the base constraint. No higher-order PSD matrix or arbitrary-level Sherali–Adams feasibility is asserted.

### A separate universal level-four Sherali–Adams witness

Every graph of girth at least five admits level-four ternary probability tables for proper 3-colouring. For |S|≤4, the induced graph is a forest. Use colour-sa4-orbits.txt, in which a row gives the probability of a *particular ordered palette assignment* as a function of the isomorphism type of the induced forest and the partition of its vertices by equal colours. Improper assignments have probability zero. The key is the lexicographically smallest pair (sorted edge list, sorted partition into sorted vertex lists) over all vertex permutations; the first component of the file key gives the vertex count. There are only 32 entries. Their nonnegative values are rational with denominators dividing 36. The empty assignment has mass one. For every labelled forest on one through four vertices, every omitted vertex and every colouring of the rest, the three extensions sum to the listed probability for the induced subforest. The standalone checker check_colour_sa4.py verifies all 4,306 such identities over the rationals. Iterating deletions gives normalization and all cross-scope consistencies. This establishes level four on every graph in the degree-six family. It uses different pair marginals from the PSD witness: all nonadjacent pairs are independent in this table. Simultaneous level-four feasibility with that SDP would require a common witness.

### Exact obstruction to the same ansatz at five vertices

A normalized family of tables on *every* forest of size at most five cannot depend only on the induced forest and the palette equality partition. In fact, the obstruction needs no nonnegativity. The supplied integer certificate colour-forest-sa5-obstruction.json lists 15 instances of the one-vertex marginal identity. Each entry specifies its integer multiplier, vertex count, forest edges, omitted vertex, and ordered colouring on the remaining vertices. Denote an orbit weight by q. For each entry the equation is the sum of q over the three proper extensions minus the q of the restriction, equal to zero. The integer-weighted sum of these 15 rows cancels every orbit except the empty assignment with coefficient one. Their joint consequence is therefore q(empty)=0, contradicting normalization. The independent standard-library checker check_colour_forest_sa5_obstruction.py verifies acyclicity, legality, canonical orbit identifications, and the coefficient sum exactly. Even a palette-asymmetric normalized real solution depending only on the induced forest would give an invariant one by averaging over the six palette permutations. Graph-specific tables may depend on vertices' surroundings or on their mutual distances outside the induced forest; this certificate does not address those possibilities.
