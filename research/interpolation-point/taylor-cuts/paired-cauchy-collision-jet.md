# Joint contraction at the covariance collision

Work with z=T−1 and truncated rational series. Put b_i=(T^(2a_i)−1)/(T^(2a_i)+1) and c_i=2T^a_i/(1+T^(2a_i)). The paired covariance equals c_i c_j/(1−b_i b_j). Every b_i is divisible by z. Replace each centered port by c_i sum_{d=0}^H b_i^d G_d with independent standard Gaussian symbols. By geometric summation all covariances agree modulo z^(H+1), and the Wick pairing polynomial therefore agrees for every actual OR/COPY gate product.

Multiply local polynomials and take independent Gaussian moments (zero on odd exponents, (k−1)!! for even k). Store the exponent of G_0 separately, bounded by the number M of ports. Discard positive-mode exponent vectors whose weight sum d k_d exceeds H, by z-adic divisibility. The remaining count is the cumulative partition number, exp(O(sqrt(H))). Schoolbook polynomial multiplication, even squaring this number of states, costs exp(O(sqrt(H))) poly(M,E,H) bit operations for integer labels 0 through E−1. This applies to arbitrary signed native gates. High-arity COPY can be accumulated as a sum of two products. Keep all gate normalizations and the raw factor 2^E, where E counts paired occurrences.

For bit cost, each term has at most M+H factors of 1/(1+(1+z)^(2a_i)), counting multiplicity after removing overweight monomials. Expand each factor as one half times a geometric series in (1−(1+z)^(2a_i))/2. At order H all coefficients have common denominator dividing 2^(M+2H), before the gate normalizations of at most 2^M. Numerator heights are polynomial in M,E,H by truncated binomial expansion: at most H positive-order factors and each derivative binomial coefficient through order H has polynomial bit size. Logs of term multiplicities are polynomial, including ≤M! for Gaussian moments. Exact rational arithmetic avoids division by nonunits when a dyadic precision is requested. Full recovery from the common rational denominator in paired-cauchy-deformation.md has the bound H=O(ME²); polynomial-time low jets alone do not achieve that recovery.

A concrete check uses (x) AND (NOT x). Its centered positive and negative units use Z1,Z2, and COPY2 uses Z3,Z4. The two paired exponents label {1,3} and {2,4}. Within each pair the covariance is 1, between pairs it is r. The complete unnormalized Gaussian scalar is 4 E[(1+Z1)(1−Z2)(1+Z3Z4)]/8=−r². At exponents 0,1 it equals −4T²/(1+T²)². In particular its negative collision value and its zero origin both occur on a genuine contradictory input.
