# A reciprocal Cauchy covariance with exactly the paired SAT origin

Give each occurrence e a distinct integer in [0,E−1] and give its two ports the same integer. Off-diagonal covariance on a pair at distance δ is 1 if δ=0 and 2T^δ/(1+T^(2δ)) otherwise. This is regular at zero. The origin consists of independent paired coordinates of variance one. Applying the Gaussian functional to the multilinear centered OR/COPY gate polynomials reproduces the Boolean sum after multiplication by 2^E because no coordinate at zero is used more than twice. For real T>0 the covariance is positive semidefinite, as 1/(x_i+x_j) is the Gram kernel of exp(−x_i t) on the positive line. Scale row i by sqrt(2)T^a_i, x_i=T^(2a_i).

For M ports and k=floor(M/2), a common denominator for every Gaussian partial matching is D=product over δ=1,...,E−1 of (1+T^(2δ))^k. Its degree is kE(E−1), and D(0)=1. Each rational matching term is proper or constant, so the cleared normalized total has degree at most the same bound. Multiply by 2^M before taking expectation to clear local substitutions and restore the 2^E count multiplier afterward. At even integer samples D is odd; working to p+max(0,M−E) bits suffices for this known power-of-two scaling. Interpolation from even samples can lose additional precision. Exact rational interpolation on degree+1 ordinary positive integers avoids that issue: integer entry and factorial moment bit lengths and the Vandermonde arithmetic all have polynomial height for E,M polynomial. Polynomial evaluation time nevertheless requires a full gate-scalar evaluator.

A single pure subset hafnian at positive T is computable by the Pfaffian–hafnian formula. Perturb all equal nodes by independent slopes i times one formal parameter z, retaining the original row scalings. The ratio is regular at z=0 by the identity. Clearing all pair denominators uses degree O(M²) in z. Polynomial interpolation of the cleared Pfaffian and extraction of the common vanishing order avoids division by a 2-adic nonunit. One can work throughout over rational numbers with the sqrt(2) row scalings combined as 2^(|S|/2).

Five ports already rule out representing all principal subset hafnians by absolute principal Pfaffians of one real skew matrix, the required input for a direct principal minor summation. At any positive T pairs fix |Aij|=Γij>0. On i<j<k<l, equality in the triangle inequality requires signs s_ij s_kl=−s_ik s_jl=s_il s_jk. Change vertex signs so s_1i=1. For 1<i<j<k this forces s_jk=−s_ik=s_ij. Triples 234 and 235 imply s24=s25=−s23; triple 245 implies s25=−s24. This contradiction also allows an arbitrary overall sign for each subset. The special Cauchy ratio evades the contradiction by a subset-dependent product denominator. An all-gate algorithm using another mechanism is open. Ordinary OR3 has nonzero constant and singleton coefficients after centering, hence fails the single-parity necessary condition for a planar matchgate in this basis. Even after projecting total parity, two ternary ORs have nonzero coefficients on every even subset of their six ports. This makes the all-minors obstruction relevant to native gates; it does not exclude scalar cancellations, lifted representations, or other bases.
