# Effective zero-loss transfer through special-fiber regularity

This is a third argument, independent of the quantitative Briançon–Skoda tradeoff in packet-integral-reduction.md. Adopt its ideals I (physical) and J (relaxed), its d=L(2L−1), and A=Z_(2). Set B_n=(J^n)_{nd}, B_0=A, V=I_d. This standard graded A-algebra B already has a qualitative degree-one reduction V, so the quotient of the abstract special fiber B_k=B/2B by the image of V is finite-dimensional. Reducing the ambient form coefficients need not compute B_k.

Every B_n is finite free over the DVR, being finitely generated and torsion-free inside binary forms. Hence its special-fiber dimension equals its rational rank. For n≥d−2 it equals nd+1: leading degrees 0,1,d−1,d in the physical span give all k(d−1)+[0,n] by choosing k high factors; the union is [0,nd]. Thus H(n)=dn+1 throughout this range.

Put c=(d−1)(d−2)/2 and R=d+c. For n≥R, the degree-n Macaulay expansion of dn+1 consists of d terms binom(n+1−j,n−j) for j=0,...,d−1 followed by c terms binom(h,h) for h=n−d down through n−d−c+1. Raising gives d(n+1)+1, exactly H(n+1). Consequently the lex ideal with the same Hilbert function has no new generator in degree n+1 by the lex shadow formula. Its generators thus have degree ≤R. The stable-ideal resolution gives regularity of the lex ideal ≤R. The lex Betti bound in arbitrary characteristic yields reg(B_k)≤R. [Macaulay](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-26.1.531) · [Eliahou–Kervaire](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(90)90237-I) · [Pardue](https://doi.org/10.1215/ijm/1255985937)

Extend k faithfully to an infinite field. The image of V avoids containment in each associated prime outside the irrelevant maximal ideal, because B_k/(V) has finite length. Choose a linear form from V avoiding their finite union and repeat in the quotient. After dim B_k=2 filter-regular choices the quotient is Artinian. Quotienting by a filter-regular linear form does not increase regularity (use the multiplication exact sequence and its finite-length annihilator inside irrelevant torsion). The Artinian quotient vanishes above R, hence its further quotient B_k/(V) does too. Faithful field extension descends this vanishing. Nakayama on B_{R+1}/VB_R gives B_{R+1}=VB_R in the original lattice. Multiplication propagates equality.

Cancelling common denominator powers yields R_n=P_{n−R} R_R for n≥R, writing R_n and P_n for unscaled relaxed and physical word spans. Using one physical packet for each row of a residual relaxed word gives R_n⊂P_{n+(L−1)R}. For smaller n, use direct expansion and neutral padding. The bound is O(L^5) extra full packets or O(L^6) slots with no scalar loss. Reserve this overhead in advance within a fixed budget. At L≤s^{1/1000}, M=Θ(s), it is o(M). The relaxed mask witness thus gives an integral combination of genuine shared Boolean pin products with origin one and the same simultaneous divisibilities. This is an additive-reconstruction obstruction, not a single Boolean formula witness.

## Consequence for additive reconstruction budgets

For a fixed budget of P full packets, reserve (L−1)R packets and take the known relaxed masks in the rest. If a≥2u, u→∞, and L^6=o(M) with M=PL, their product has origin one and every coefficient at each active t has valuation at least floor((M−O(L^6))/u)D_i/2. The physical envelope transfer preserves this single polynomial. Therefore a universal additive origin decoder correct on all true pin words needs at least one node or coefficient above that precision threshold, even though the jets come from the same polynomial. The all-crossing Cauchy minor bound in packet-joint-taper.md at this possibly arbitrary active node implies at least Ω(M/√u) jointly weight-feasible modes for literal linear-mode enumeration under collision packing. The passage through a linear combination is valid for an additive map on the observed residue groups that agrees on every pin word; it does not assert that a nonlinear algorithm on individually restricted CNF instances inherits this cost.

Independent algebra audit approved the full identical-packet proposition, including the abstract special fiber, characteristic-two regularity, descent and the zero-loss slot count. At a fixed finite precision K, a Z_(2)-linear witness can also be converted into a literal signed integer combination: replace each 2-local coefficient by an integer representative modulo 2^K. The true origin and all integral local coefficients remain unchanged modulo 2^K. The remaining integer origin discrepancy is a multiple of 2^K; add its negative times the neutral word, which is an integer sum of legal pinned words, to restore origin exactly while preserving all local residues.

For reconstruction at a fixed precision, convert the 2-local coefficients to signed integers at enough precision for the target calculation, then use a neutral physical word to correct the value at the origin. The transfer concerns linear combinations of full identical packets.
