Cassandra, Littman and Zhang used exact pruning of value vectors in [Incremental Pruning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.1525). Hansen and Bowman developed [improved vector-pruning algorithms](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/hansen20a.html). The accompanying construction represents a vector indexed by suffix assignments through signed affine cells and computes its inner products without enumerating those assignments. The runtime theorem uses rational linear programming. The finite checker enumerates small supports.
