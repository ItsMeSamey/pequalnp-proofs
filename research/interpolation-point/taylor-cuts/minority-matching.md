# Mixed-sign matching zero test at the Boolean origin

Discard zero activities. Enumerate original monomer–dimer configurations of each sign with polynomial delay. At the least residual vertex branch over its monomer and incident dimers. Check feasibility with the required residual sign by adding one dummy per vertex, an edge to its dummy for each allowed monomer, and a positive clique on all dummies. Every original configuration with t dimers has (2t−1)!! dummy completions, so use this augmentation for feasibility alone. The deterministic Correct Parity Matching oracle is due to [El Maalouly, Steiner and Wulf](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2023.28). Pruning before traversal gives polynomial delay and space.

Let P and N be the absolute masses of the two signs and w the smallest nonzero configuration magnitude. An empty graph of configurations gives zero immediately. Let K=min(P,N)/w. Alternately generate the two streams until one ends. Add terms from its opponent until the latter exceeds the completed total or exhausts. Before first exhaustion each stream has issued at most floor(K)+1 configurations. If the exhausted side had smaller mass, its opponent crosses the total within floor(K)+1 emissions overall, or exhausts. Otherwise the remaining stream is itself the lesser-mass stream. The algorithm decides equality of masses in (K+1)poly(input) time without computing w or K. Partial sums have polynomial bit length. A 4,096-case check compared stream decisions on pairs of six-element weighted sublists against full summation.

At an assignment-preserving Boolean-origin encoding with signed fiber sum equal to a common positive scalar times its truth indicator, fixing external logical wires deletes terms. Both masses decrease and the minimum surviving magnitude increases. Thus K cannot increase and standard prefix self-reduction returns a witness with at most n additional calls. With the original w fixed, the sum of the two child minority masses divided by w is at most the parent bound. The lexicographic pair of this bound and the remaining prefix length decreases on every recursive descent.

For the default uncentered truth-table OR3 gadget, each invalid fiber has distinct positive and negative completions (local weights +3 and −1 after clearing its two-denominator). In an exact 3-CNF with distinct variables per clause some assignment falsifies at least m/8 clauses by averaging. Fix all external ports accordingly. Choose two opposite-sign completions independently on each false OR3 and fix one nonzero completion elsewhere, including all consistent COPY tensors. Each sign therefore has at least 2^(ceil(m/8)−1) original matchings. This forces exponential K for this encoding, including satisfiable and unsatisfiable inputs. Local completion choices also form a parity cube: primitive individual gadget occupancy probes cannot partition it into sign-homogeneous leaves with fewer than 2^t leaves when t clauses are false. This concerns the prescribed representation and oracle.
