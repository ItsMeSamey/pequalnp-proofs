# Testing a common layer for affine flats of rank two

After Gaussian preprocessing, let D be the dimension of the active normal span. Fix any direct decomposition into r binary two-dimensional spaces B_i and a complementary space C. A polynomial test decides whether W=C⊕(⊕L_i) meets every rank-two input normal plane P_e, where L_i is a line of B_i. A positive result gives exact counting and witness recovery in 2^{D−r}poly(input) time and polynomial space, for arbitrary affine offsets. The normal decomposition can come from a maximal direct family of input planes or from pairing arbitrary coordinate basis elements.

For each gate e choose basis vectors p1,p2 of its plane in the dual. The two columns of A_ie are the coordinates in the chosen basis of B_i of the projections of p1,p2 along the direct decomposition. Thus A_ie maps gate coefficients into basis-plane coordinates. Introduce line labels at gates and basis planes. For a chosen gate coefficient direction v≠0, p=v1 p1+v2 p2 is nonzero. The condition p∈W is exactly A_ie v∈L_i for every i, since C is included whole. A zero block imposes no constraint. An invertible block transmits the label by a permutation on the three nonzero directions. Propagate permutations along a spanning tree of each invertible-block component. Check each of the three root values against every remaining edge and discard incompatible root values. Empty domains reject.

A rank-one block is satisfied exactly if the gate label is its kernel OR the basis label is its image. Translate each endpoint equality to the corresponding root-label atom or false. Use these as binary positive clauses and add pairwise negative clauses excluding distinct root labels for the same component. An ordinary Boolean 2-SAT model may leave roots unlabeled. Complete each such root by any allowed value. Every positive clause already contained a true atom, and exclusivity is preserved. This includes clauses on a single root and singleton domains. A total solution clearly satisfies the Boolean encoding. Thus this is an equivalence for the fixed decomposition, without searching all decompositions.

For each assignment on a basis of W (dimension D−r), inconsistent forbidden flats disappear, a whole forbidden flat kills the fiber, and every other residual exclusion is one hyperplane because W meets its original normal plane. Enforce the complementary linear equations and use Gaussian elimination to count and recover witnesses. Fibers are disjoint. Multiply by 2^{d−D} for inactive original coordinates if these were removed.

When all nonzero blocks are invertible, the criterion is that each support component's cycle permutations have a common fixed projective point. A nontrivial transposition qualifies. For example take three coordinate planes on three binary two-bit blocks plus (u,u,0), (0,v,v), and (w,0,Tw), with T swapping coordinates. All six planes are pairwise disjoint. The last three span rank five: their dependencies correspond to the line fixed by T. Consequently no ambient change yields a doubled binary presentation, in which every subset has even rank. The diagonal lines in all three coordinate planes meet every gate, so eight Gaussian fibers suffice for arbitrary offsets. Generic conditioning may find other layers when this particular decomposition fails.
