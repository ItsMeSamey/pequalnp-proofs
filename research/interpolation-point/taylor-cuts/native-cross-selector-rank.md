# The odd-shell native transfer has large explicit additive rank

Use the pinned October Appendix D, https://github.com/openai/math/blob/42907c9e999cc00d6abe7a50bfc1023250edd1b9/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex#L398-L522 . At the odd shell i=0 and any unit ζ let T=t+2Lζ, h=C(T)/C′(0), and p=p_t. Then v₂(h)=a+1 for L=2^a. In a crossing parity tile all T+x−y are units. Its transfer is K(Y,X)=Π_x ℓ_{Y_x}(q_x), q_x=Σ_y h X_y/(T+x−y), ℓ₀=1−q, ℓ₁=q. The source mode L_b=Σ j^b X_y on y=y₀+2j has coefficient valuation a+1+b. The parent retains Σ(b+1)β<p. The following bound concerns the actual kernel before applying the specific row tensors.

At the fixed-density root with paired subdivision columns there is a bipartition of the 64 group symbols for which the modular kernel flattening needs 2^{Ω(M/√u)} additive generators. This applies to explicit bases representing the whole transfer, including standard-face or border representations meant to work for all physical row words. It is not a lower bound on the contracted SAT scalar.

By the published collision bound, for A=r^{2a} label classes the load of bit-zero-equal symbols is at most M/r+sqrt(2AMΔ/r)≤M/r+M/sqrt(rA), using M≥2A²Δ and r=64. At most 6M/L parity tiles exist. Tiles containing fewer than L/32 real slots hold fewer than 6M/32 ports altogether. Thus Ω(M/L) tiles have Θ(L) real positions and unequal row/column symbols. A random split of symbols retains a quarter in expectation oriented from the source side B0 to the target side A0. In each retained packet there is exactly one B0 tile. The same real slot positions give A0 rows crossing to it. Each logical column bit appears at most twice, both endpoints sharing its source group.

Let k=floor(εL/√u) for a sufficiently small constant ε; asymptotically k dominates a. Sample the Ω(M) logical bits meeting retained tiles with probability k/L. Conditional on a particular endpoint being selected, its tile occupancy has expectation at most k+2. Its sum of v₂(y−y′) over other selected positions has expectation O(k+a): its mate contributes at most a, and summing valuations against an entire length-L interval costs O(L) by counting multiples of each power of two. Remove bits whose occupancy or sum at either retained endpoint exceeds a large absolute constant times k. Conditional Markov and union bounds leave Ω(Mk/L) survivors in expectation. Trim to R=Θ(Mk/L), pin all other bits zero, and choose one representative retained endpoint per surviving bit. Every retained data packet has d≤Ck selected positions and each position's total difference valuation is at most Ck.

For each such packet take 4Ck uniform distinct samples from its ≥L/32 real A0 rows. Conditional expected summed difference valuation is O(k) per sampled row by the same interval calculation. Some choice has at least half its points below a large O(k) threshold. Keep them. At least 2d distinct rows are now available, every retained row has total valuation O(k), and this holds also in every subsequent subset.

For any remaining subset I of d rows and all d data columns J, inverse entries of the Cauchy matrix 1/(T+x−y) are, up to signs,
  Π_{v∈J}(T+x−v) Π_{s∈I,s≠x}(T+s−y) / [Π_{s∈I,s≠x}(s−x) Π_{v∈J,v≠y}(y−v)].
Restoring the factor h costs a+1. Thus there is a common B=a+1+O(k) for which 2^B X_y is an integral linear combination of these d actual row forms, for ANY d remaining reservoir rows. Clear the powers in the fraction field before reducing; odd denominators are units. Formally distinct columns can subsequently be identified when they carry the same bit. To isolate the product of s≤d representative bits within one packet, expand one at a time. Each partial summand uses only one physical row per previously recovered bit. For that summand select any d fresh reservoir rows and use its own inverse for the next bit. There are enough rows since at most d−1 are in use. Multiplying across packets gives 2^{B|S|} times every squarefree product on a subset S of the R bits as a sum of multilinear products of DISTINCT physical row forms. The total charge BR=O(ε² ML/u) is below p−1 for small ε since p≥24ML/u and k≫a.

Each distinct-row product is a sum of actual selector rows after fixing those Y bits to one and summing all the other bits with ℓ₀+ℓ₁=1. To project the full group-cut flattening, set the non-B0 source bits zero and marginalize non-A0 output bits; these are side-local operations. Thus the row module contains 2^{p−1} times all 2^R Boolean squarefree monomials. They are residue-field independent by Boolean zeta inversion. Over a finite principal chain ring a module generated by d elements has socle dimension ≤d by invariant factors. Project any auxiliary nilpotents to zero first. This proves the claimed generator lower bound. Over the fraction field the same proof, taking a small constant sample fraction and disregarding precision, gives rank 2^{Ω(M)}.

For the explicit weighted source-moment module, let P_j(z)=Π_{i≠j}(z−i) on the d selected local j positions. Its coefficient combination of L_b equals P_j(j)X_j. From the scaled moments 2^{a+1+b}L_b one obtains 2^{a+d+v₂(P_j(j))}X_j, with b≤d−1. Products on any subset have total cost O(R(k+a))<p and use at most d factors per representative tile. The same socle lower bound applies before evaluating target coefficients, independent of monomial order.

The published odd-shell upper order exp(O(M/√u)) and the threshold H=ceil(s/floor(sqrt(log₂s))) thus cannot be uniformly reduced merely by explicitly materializing an additive basis of the uncontracted transfer. Gate-dependent sums, circuits or nonlinear encodings can still be smaller. check_cross_selector_rank.py verifies every inverse on three small exact packets and every small Boolean word/target product, including a duplicated logical column. The result does not assume all individual recursive child scalars are nonzero.
