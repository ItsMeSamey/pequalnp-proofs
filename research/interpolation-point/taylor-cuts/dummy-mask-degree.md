# Exact degree of the direct dummy-edge mask

For every s≥0 take the original graph H=K_{8+2s} and k=4+s. Select two disjoint four-vertex blocks. Within each name two different perfect matchings, interpreted as bit 0 and bit 1. Label a single edge exclusive to the bit-1 matching in each block by one over F₃, and all other original edges by zero. Pair the remaining 2s original vertices into s disjoint edges e_i. Append s pairwise-disjoint dummy edges d_i on fresh vertices, so maximum cardinality increases from k to k+s.

Consider the actual k-matchings choosing the two four-block options a,b and choosing e_i or d_i independently according to a bit z_i that is one for d_i. Every original or new edge indicator is affine on this Boolean (s+2)-cube, including those identically zero. The k-matchings which themselves are perfect matchings of H with gain two meet the cube at the single assignment a=b=1 and all z_i=0. A polynomial nonzero precisely on these target matchings must on the cube have a nonzero alternating mixed difference of order s+2, since only one summand is nonzero. Its total degree is therefore at least s+2, over F₃, or over any field for a polynomial recognizing this same fixed support. The direct target quadratic times the s exclusion factors attains this bound over F₃. Similarly, a union of individually sound lower-degree nonvanishing filters cannot cover this corner: any filter witnessing it has the same singleton-support obstruction on the cube.

This concerns recognition of the original accepted matchings as a subset of the enlarged matching domain, with the stated direct witness interpretation. It says nothing about algorithms returning other lifted witnesses, computing only global existence, or using multiple tests with cancellation. The original graph in the obstruction is itself a simple complete brick, and the displayed original target is attainable.
