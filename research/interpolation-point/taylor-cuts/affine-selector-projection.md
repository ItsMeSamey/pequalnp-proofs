# Eliminating an affine selector clique

A verified signed clique on w_i=s_i·x+b_i forbids w_i=w_j=1 for every distinct pair. Gaussian elimination tests the p+1 possible selector words 0,e_1,…,e_p. Distinct feasible words give disjoint fibers of codimension rank(s_i). Decision takes the disjunction of their cofactors and exact counting adds model counts; an exactly-one parity identity excludes the zero arm.

The reference finder considers each direction in an input plane as a pivot. Collect directions sharing its plane and occurring in at least two distinct plane scopes. Try the pivot with this set and with one possible extra neighbor deleted. Verify endpoint bad-bit consistency on every pair. The clean clique-plus-bridge family with p≥5 and at most one high-incidence exterior neighbor per padding direction is recovered after arbitrary invertible affine coordinate change. Completeness for general cluttered neighborhoods is not required for sound use.

For supplied or discovered forms let S=span(s_i). Seek a projection π:U→S with π|S=id. Each plane P with P∩S=0 must satisfy π(P)=0. For P∩S=span(h) and a second plane normal b, require π(b)∈span(h). These are linear equations on the projection entries. On success T=ker π is a common exterior complement. Test S-only exclusions on each word. A mixed gate vanishes or imposes one equality on b+π(b)∈T. The remaining gates give a single exterior CNF G and at most p+1 affine cells C_i, one per feasible arm. On exterior points the decision predicate is G AND the union of C_i, while the model multiplicity equals 1[G] times the number of cells containing the point. Retain equal cells with their separate multiplicities. If each cell consists of one affine equality, the union excludes the single flat where all equalities fail. An unrestricted cell makes the union universal, and zero cells yield the empty union.

In the parity-selector four-colouring family every proper core colouring activates exactly two of the three nonzero edge functionals on each forest edge. Its p=3sg arms contribute a constant 2sg for g forest edges with s copies. Thus the selector projects away even though it has no constant witness and its full affine hull has dimension p−1. The general executable returns exterior gates, affine cells and original-coordinate lift for every recognized complement.
