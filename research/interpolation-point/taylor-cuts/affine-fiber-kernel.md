# Local minimum-width kernel

Group forbidden flats with identical homogeneous normal space V of rank j. In a common basis of V, the excluded outputs form a set B in F₂^j. For each b in B, find the greatest dimension s_b of any affine flat contained in B through b. Replace the original exclusions by the union of these at most |B| flats pulled back to seed coordinates. Exact width is j−min_b s_b. Every replacement lies inside B and covers its chosen point, proving equivalence. Conversely every exact union must cover each b by a piece contained in B. The lower bound even allows arbitrary original seed normals, because projecting any forbidden seed flat of codimension h to the j outputs gives codimension at most h. Every such projection must be contained in B.

For fixed width k the construction takes 2^{O(k²)}poly(input) time by enumerating lists of at most j independent directions through each point, testing the resulting flat against B. If a group contains at least two distinct exclusions, each point belongs to a two-point line and the rank drops by at least one. Thus paired parallel rank-three exclusions admit a dimension-preserving rank-two kernel. For pairs on disjoint triple scopes the shared-wedge conversion requires one auxiliary per scope, while this reduction needs none. Gaussian restriction can expose new pairs inside recursion. Apply only strictly rank-lowering group rewrites, with duplicate removal. Number of clauses never increases and total rank strictly decreases, giving a polynomial number of passes at fixed k.

Here k bounds the original input ranks. Skip empty B and return contradiction when B is the whole output space. The optimality statement is local to this predicate considered alone. A seed flat x+L of codimension h projects to dimension dim L−dim(L∩ker π) ≥ (d−h)−(d−j)=j−h.
