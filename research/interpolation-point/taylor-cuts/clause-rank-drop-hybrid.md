# Optimal rank-drop and exceptional flats

Solve the hard XOR rows and parameterize their affine space by d independent coordinates. Each clause's falsification is now an inconsistent system (drop it), the whole chart (zero models), or an affine flat with normal space S_i. In ungrouped mode duplicate clauses count as separate exceptions. Grouped mode combines equal normal spaces at this initial stage and deduplicates identical forbidden evaluations. A group of t distinct forbidden evaluations has cost b_i=ceil(log₂(t+1)); a singleton costs one. Its members are disjoint.

Minimize dim W plus the total cost of groups still having normal rank above one modulo W. Choose a violating nonignored group of residual rank r. Either charge its exception cost or include one of the 2^r−1 hyperplanes of dimension r−1 in its residual normal space in the extension of W. Every final solution which avoids this exception must contain such a hyperplane in its quotient by the present W, because only at most one of those r dimensions may survive. For nonzero mask m a basis of its hyperplane is e_j+m_j e_q for j≠q, where m_q=1. Enumerate the hyperplanes only when r−1 fits the budget. The implementation maintains the annihilator of W and complementary directions via the existing rank-drop backend.

The number of leaves is bounded by 4^b in a trial of budget b, since 4^(−b_i)+(2^r−1)4^(−(r−1))≤1 for r≥2. Node depth and cost per visited branch add polynomial overhead. Iterative deepening to first success finds p, the exact optimum, in O*(4^p). If a decision to ignore eventually proves redundant on that path, its final W certifies the smaller actual budget and completeness ensures discovery by the appropriate trial. A cap miss returns a missing count separately from count zero.

The default additionally prunes the exception branch whenever r−1≤b_i. Starting from any solution that paid for that group, adjoin any current residual hyperplane at dimension cost at most r−1, eliminating the group's exception and never increasing other residual ranks. Thus some solution of no greater budget is represented among the hyperplane branches. Rank two now has only three unit-cost children. At r≥3, bound the remaining children by one unit-cost exception and 2^r−1 children costing r−1. Set beta=(1+sqrt(29))/2≈3.19258. Then beta²=beta+7 gives equality at r=3. For larger r the ratio (2^(r+1)−1)/(2^r−1)≤15/7<beta, so 2^r−1≤(beta−1)beta^(r−2) persists. Each branch's normalized beta-potential sums to at most one. The default search therefore uses O*(beta^p), with the same counting bound below. The optional prune=False reproduces the four-base algorithm for checks.

For a fixed W enumerate its 2^dimW fibres. On each remove inconsistent forbidden events, reject if one fills the fibre, and convert every rank-one forbidden event to its opposite linear equality. For every remaining group multiply by (1−sum_f 1_f) over its compatible forbidden flats; this equality uses their disjointness. Expand each group into its empty selection or a single selected event. Every signed summand is the size of a linear intersection, zero or two to its free dimension. Across fibres there are at most 2^p such summands, with polynomial arithmetic overhead. On the first positive fibre determine each witness bit by evaluating its zero half with the same inclusion-exclusion and prefix equations. If that mass is zero, the other half retains the entire positive mass. Undo the affine charts and verify the original CNF and XOR rows.

A connected separation uses m≥3 clauses (x∨y_i) and one clause (x∨z_1∨...∨z_m). Choose W=span(x) and ignore the last clause, giving p=2. No budget-one choice suffices, as a line cannot suppress the last clause and ignoring a single exception at W=0 misses other high-rank clauses. Pure conditioning costs m (include x and m−1 z-coordinates; the last clause gives the matching lower bound), while W=0 costs m+1 exceptions. Its exact count is 2^(2m)+2^m−1.

Four distinct forbidden points on six bits have ungrouped optimum four and grouped optimum three. Any W of dimension less than five leaves all four normal ranks above one, while suppressing them all costs five. Grouping uses five signed alternatives, compared with sixteen. For independent copies the optima add: the normal spaces are in direct sum, and dim W≥sum_i dim(W∩S_i). Thus the raw expansion improvement repeats at exponential scale. Component factorization offers another solver for this particular family.

The protected checker compared 280 random instances in both conventions, including 140 comparisons per convention to exhaustive subspace optimization. It also checks budget misses, boundary clauses and both separation gadgets.
