# Connected shallow-shell support

The following proof uses the fixed-density [source translation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-reduction.tex) and the [quantitative contraction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex).

Take a simple connected cubic bipartite incidence graph with n clauses and n variables. Give each occurrence in one perfect matching negative sign, all others positive. The source has M=6n real wires, n OR3 and n COPY3 row vertices. Every variable appears in both signs, and the incidence graph can expand. Decision for these dual-Horn instances is easy.

At t=1, i=0, a negative row port is one of M/6 marks. Only 1/64 of padded slots have equal symbols, and the variance bound implies nonfull packets carry o(M) slots. Hence Omega(M/L) full unlike tiles have k=Omega(L) available marks. Set B=floor(cL/(u log L)), c small. In each dense tile choose beta_b independently in [0,floor(k/(2B))] for b=1,...,B and fill remaining k with beta_0. This gives exp(Omega(M log u/u^2)) root candidates.

Remove 2^(a+1+b) Z from each selected crossing coefficient before specializing at the formal interpolation coordinate Z=0. The rest is A(Z)/d_x(Z)^(b+1), where d_x=1+x-y_0+2L Z, y_0=x mod 2, and A=C(1+2L Z)/(2L Z C'(0)). At zero d_x is positive odd and A(0)=-(L+1)/(L-1). Baseline local forms q are zero or this negative number times a shifted Boolean bit. The derivative on marked ports K of the one-negative OR has sign (-1)^|K| or zero at q<=0, by its product of falsification literals. The unmarked value is positive. The COPY derivative is product of untouched q's plus (-1)^|K| times product of the untouched (1-q)'s; it has the same sign or is zero. Thus all allocations have the same sign. Taking all paired columns one and marking precisely the available negative clause ports gives a nonzero term. Untouched literals in each marked clause are positive.

Clear odd denominators at zero by (L-1)^O(M) and product d_x(0)^(B+1). There are at most 2^(M/2)(B+2)^M assignments and allocations, each moment is at most L^(B+1), and |q|<=3. The nonzero numerator has O(M(B+1)log L) bits; restoring the extracted powers of two still gives valuation below p_t>=24ML/u for sufficiently small c.

Clear the formal denominators by product d_x(Z)^(B+1); these are units at every sample. Divide the numerator by its minimum coefficient valuation, which is at most its valuation at zero. Its reduction is now nonzero. Every local W and normalized A has degree at most 2L-1. Source row degree is 5n=5M/6 since COPY3 has degree two. Thus the cleared degree is at most (5M/6)(2L-1)+M(B+1)=(5/6+o(1))M(2L-1). A positive fraction of ANY full source interpolation grid of M(2L-1)+1 distinct residues avoids its roots and the zero residue. At those points the original beta contribution survives modulo p_t. Averaging gives a single point retaining a constant fraction of the tuples. The argument permits any layout satisfying the source common-padding variance construction but does not address shorter, custom interpolation schedules or aggregation of nonzero tuples.
