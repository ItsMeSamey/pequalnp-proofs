# A quadratic correction learned on the near-perfect layer

Let H have a perfect matching, n=2k>0, and let Q be quadratic over F₃ on original edge incidence. The scalar target polynomial is Q=1−(g−t)². Form a dictionary of quadratics certified zero on all original perfect matchings. It may include each affine identity of perfect-matching incidence times every affine basis monomial; derive the identities by finite-field matching span. Add each x_e x_f whose pair of IDs cannot extend to a perfect matching. More strongly, for each edge e, multiply by x_e the full affine annihilator of the e-present family and by (1−x_e) the affine annihilator of the e-absent family. The empty branch has every affine identity. Each dictionary entry vanishes on the original perfect layer. A simple subdictionary is h_v times affine forms, where h_v=1 minus the sum of incidences at v.

Get a witnessed full degree-two evaluation span of (k−1)-matchings using safe-exchange-slack.md, since slack is one. Solve for a dictionary combination J agreeing with Q on this span, hence the entire near-perfect layer. Failure is inconclusive. On success append one disjoint dummy edge and test Q−J, using original coordinates, on size-k matchings. This again has slack one. A matching using the dummy restricts to an original near-perfect matching and gives zero. A matching omitting it is an original perfect matching and Q−J equals Q. Thus a nonzero result returns actual original target IDs and a zero result proves absence. Complexity is polynomial with the classical finite-field linear matching-span algorithm; handle zero vertices directly. No universal fit claim is made.

The K₃,₃ labels ((1,0,2),(1,2,0),(0,0,0)) give gain zero on even permutations and one on odd permutations. The even and odd triples have the same incidence sum, so this binary function has no rational affine lift. Yet on all 18 near-perfect matchings the h_v times affine dictionary has F₃-rank 17, unchanged when the missing-target polynomial for two is appended; the correction works. The independent checker check_dummy_layer_corrections.py covers 370 target-free normalized words on seven named hosts and 1,424 words on 32 sampled cubic hosts. No failures in this finite evidence. The YES controls in dummy-mask-degree.md, including the high-girth refinement, fit no quadratic extension-by-zero at one dummy, even with arbitrary valid quadratic identities. The latter controls do not settle missing-target coverage.
