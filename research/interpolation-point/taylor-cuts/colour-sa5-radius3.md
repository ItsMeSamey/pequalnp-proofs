# Ternary level-five Sherali–Adams for graph 3-colouring at girth sixteen

Every simple graph of maximum degree at most six and girth at least sixteen admits a system of nonnegative, consistent distributions on proper 3-colourings of all sets of at most five vertices. The construction uses ambient distances, giving a different family of marginals from the earlier induced-forest tables. In particular the non-three-colourable degree-six witnesses can be chosen with this fixed girth by the positive limiting probability of having no cycle of lengths three through fifteen.

**Metric types.** For an ordered query S, retain the unordered pairs at ambient distances 1, 2, or 3, recording the distance; all other pairs have a common far symbol. This operation commutes exactly with deleting a query vertex. For each connected component of the proximity graph (retained pairs), take shortest paths for the edges of a spanning tree of its t query vertices. Their union is connected and contains at most 3(t−1) edges. Since t≤5, it contains no cycle. Any pair of queried vertices in this tree joined by an ambient path of length at most three has the same distance already in the tree: a different path of that length and the tree path would contain a cycle of length at most 3(t−1)+3≤15. Conversely all tree distances at most three are valid ambient paths. Trim unmarked leaves. The resulting tree spans its query vertices, has degree at most six, at most 3(t−1)+1 vertices, and every leaf is marked. Different proximity components have no cross distances to record. This argument does not require their underlying paths to be disjoint.

**Finite rational tables.** Enumerate nonisomorphic trees of between t and 3(t−1)+1 vertices with maximum degree six. Mark every leaf and choose further vertices to get t marks. Record only distances through three. Include the one-point and empty types, and close under disjoint union (all cross distances far). After relabeling, this gives respectively 1, 1, 4, 12, 59, and 293 types on zero to five marks. Some excess types would do no harm; the tree argument ensures coverage. For each type and every proper assignment, canonicalize the sorted list of triples (u,v,d), u<v, by lexicographic minimum over mark permutations. Break graph ties by minimizing the sorted partition of marks into equal colours. The file colour-sa5-r3-orbits.txt gives the probability of each *specific ordered assignment*, not the total mass of the orbit. Improper assignments have mass zero.

There are 6,690 entries, all nonnegative rationals, including 720 zeros. The empty mass is one. The program check_colour_sa5_radius3.py independently regenerates the metric types and checks, over the rationals, every legal-parent marginal identity for every deleted mark and assignment to the others. It verifies 105,139 identities; an improper parent has only improper extensions. It also confirms every supplied row is used. Thus iterated restriction gives all lower marginals and normalizes each distribution. Across all queries in a host, the restricted ambient type is exactly the actual type of the smaller query. This proves the claimed SA feasibility, irrespective of a global colouring. The certificate was obtained by an orbit LP followed by exact elimination on the positive support; the proof checker does not use an LP solver.

The result applies to the ternary CSP formulation and Boolean lifts whose checked families of literal events collectively touch at most five host vertices. It supplies neither consistency on six-vertex sets nor positive-semidefiniteness of these particular pair moments. Ciardo and Živný give primary precedent for the general failure of fixed-level Sherali–Adams to solve approximate graph colouring; their construction uses line digraphs. Its existential result does not directly imply the fixed-degree, fixed-girth universal table proved here. [The Sherali–Adams Hierarchy for Promise CSPs through Tensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02478).

**Arbitrary host degree.** The trimmed tree has at most five leaves because each leaf is a query vertex. Its maximum degree is at most five regardless of ambient degree: each branch of an h-valent vertex ends at a distinct leaf. The enumerator's degree-six filter therefore loses no needed trimmed tree. The witness applies to every simple graph of girth at least sixteen, including those with unbounded degree. The independent incremental tree enumeration in check_colour_sa5_r3_independent.py verified 125,386 exact named-palette marginal equations including improper restrictions and confirmed full orbit coverage.
