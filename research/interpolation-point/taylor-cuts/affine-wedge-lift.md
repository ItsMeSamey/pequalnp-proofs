# Sharing quadratic products across affine triples

Given affine seeds of dimension d, each rank-three forbidden flat has a normal space V_i in the dual. In the binary exterior square of that dual take Q_i=Λ²V_i. Let t be the minimum dimension of a linear subspace intersecting every Q_i nontrivially. A deterministic search in 7^t poly(input) finds a t-dimensional such subspace W generated by decomposable wedges. Start at zero and inspect a missed Q_i. Branch on adjoining each of its seven nonzero vectors. For any target solution containing the current span, one of those seven maintains containment. Every nonzero bivector on three dimensions is decomposable: the seven distinct two-dimensional subspaces give distinct nonzero bivectors, exhausting all possibilities. Iterative deepening sums a geometric series.

For each selected generator u∧v, add y=(u·x)(v·x). Enforce this with two forbidden rank-at-most-two flats, excluding u·x=0 and y=1 simultaneously, and excluding u·x=1 and y+v·x=1 simultaneously. This gives exactly one extension for each seed. For an input flat choose a∧b in W∩Q_i and complete a,b to a basis a,b,c of V_i. Shift the three affine outputs so that the excluded triple is zero. The function (1+a(x))(1+b(x)) is affine in the enlarged coordinates on the graph of the auxiliary products: the degree-two squarefree coefficients of each product form its wedge, and by construction the appropriate sum of generator products cancels every degree-two coefficient. Calculate all remaining affine coefficients by expansion modulo x_j²=x_j. Exclude the simultaneous equations (1+a(x))(1+b(x))=1 and c(x)=0 by using this affine representative. Keep lower-rank constraints. The reduction is parsimonious and uses d+t unrestricted seed bits, 2t new constraints, and one replacement per rank-three constraint.

Together with affine-cover-decision.md the total decision/witness time is polynomial in input times the sum of 7^t and (3/2)^{d+t}2^{o(d+t)}. A supplied decomposable basis or a greedy construction removes the search charge, with its actual dimension substituted for t. Against the direct deterministic (7/4)^d base, search wins asymptotically at fixed t/d strictly below both log(7/4)/log(7) and log(7/4)/log(3/2)−1. No general 3-SAT improvement follows. For ordinary triples in a linear hypergraph, the three coordinate-pair positions of one triple are disjoint from those of every other triple. Nonzero witnesses from these disjointly supported Q_i are linearly independent, forcing t≥m; selecting one wedge per triple attains equality. This lower bound concerns the specified exterior-square formulation. For any number of triples sharing a common pair, t=1.
