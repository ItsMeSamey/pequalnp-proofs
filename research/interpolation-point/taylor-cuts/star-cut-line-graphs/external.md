# Unbounded external graph of bounded cut-rank width

In proof.md the selected subset of J contributes only its signature g=Σ w_i z_i. Fix the guessed root interface p_Q. A selected J vertex i must have internal selected degree w_i·p_Q. Its forced pins must be included. Given a binary tree of subsets for J (root the full vertex set, leaves singletons) whose cuts all have binary adjacency rank ≤t, compute every feasible g and a realizing subset in 2^{O(h+t)} poly(|J|) time. The number of vertices or pins of J is unrestricted.

For a tree node X, let A denote J adjacency. Enumerate incoming shifts b in the image of A[X,complement X] on binary vectors, and outgoing shifts c in the image of its transpose. Both spaces have dimension ≤t; they can be represented by full vertex bit vectors. A table entry (b,c,g) is attainable precisely when some choice on X includes its required pins, has outgoing parity c and label sum g, and each selected i has internal selected degree plus b_i equal to w_i·p_Q. Leaves test their two choices directly.

At a split X=L∪R guess two realized child entries and a parent incoming b. Require b_L=b restricted to L plus c_R restricted to L, and analogously b_R=b restricted to R plus c_L restricted to R. These restrictions to a sibling exist since it is in the child's complement. Record the outgoing vector (c_L+c_R) restricted to the exterior of X and XOR the two label sums. The implied shifts on each child belong to its enumerated incoming space: extend any realization of b on the parent exterior with the selected sibling vector. The same equations prove conversely that each combined pair supplies a valid parent witness. At the root b=c=0 and the stored attainable signatures are exact. Backpointers restore choices on J. With one of these g values in place of the enumerated apex sum, the root compression proof applies unchanged. Enumerate p_Q and g, giving overall 2^{O(h+t+k)} n^{2k+h+O(1)} time, with input-polynomial factors. The exponential dependence on |J| in the arbitrary-graph version disappears.

A supplied cograph cotree specializes directly: track incoming uniform parity b, selected cardinality parity s and signature g. For disjoint union both children inherit b; for join each child's b is toggled by the opposite child's s. The same leaf and backpointer rules apply.
