# One additional arbitrary cut-parity score

In the star-cut algorithm, an extra independent output bit can prescribe the parity of selected root edges across any supplied cut λ, without requiring that cut be supported on Q. The bit is a score only and does not toggle any adjacency. Guess the class-1 and class-0 score split subject to their XOR being the target. When each boundary solve has fully specified vertex degree parities, its own cut score is already determined by their dot product with the vertex indicator λ.

Fix the original class-1 witness F, and obtain a compressed class-1 solution S by the repair procedure. Keep every declared vertex, including isolated exceptions. If S and F give different cut scores, R=F minus S has cut score one. Every declared vertex has even residual degree; every fresh support vertex has odd residual degree. Pair all residual half-edges at each declared vertex and leave all half-edges at fresh vertices unpaired. This partitions R into closed trails on declared vertices and trails with fresh endpoints and declared interiors. A cut has zero weight on closed trails, while an open trail has cut weight equal to the XOR of its endpoints' λ values. Thus one trail has two differently labeled, hence distinct, fresh endpoints. Add it to S. This preserves degree parities of declared vertices, adds exactly two new ordinary vertices of odd degree, and restores the score. The path may revisit a declared vertex; its parity remains unchanged. The enlarged class-1 support stays disjoint from the original class-0 solution, which continues to supply its original score.

Consequently enumerate up to d+2 fresh vertices in place of d; a scored witness is returned exactly when feasible. No statement about arbitrary edge labels follows from this endpoint argument. For the bounded-degree variant with one score correction, its extra trail might be a single edge between two fresh vertices detached from the prescribed forest. To enumerate that case, seed one arbitrary fresh vertex (a polynomial factor) and grow one more vertex adjacent to it, or, if the trail has declared internal vertices, grow both endpoints from the declared support.
