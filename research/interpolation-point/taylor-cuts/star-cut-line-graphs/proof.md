# Pinned induced-even sets under star-cut perturbations of a line graph

Supply a simple loopless root H, an ordered subset Q of h root vertices, and a symmetric binary h×h matrix B. For each root edge e let φ(e) be its incidence vector restricted to Q. Starting with L(H), toggle adjacency between distinct e,f exactly when φ(e)ᵀBφ(f)=1. Also allow a additional vertices inducing an arbitrary supplied simple graph J. Vertex i of J has a binary h-vector w_i and sees exactly the root edges e for which w_i·φ(e)=1. Pins may lie anywhere. The algorithm is polynomial for fixed k,h,a, where k counts forced root edges.

Guess selected J-vertices z and the degree parities p_Q on Q. Reject guesses omitting a forced J vertex. Summing the restricted incidences of the selected root edges gives p_Q, so a selected J vertex requires (A_J z)_i+w_i·p_Q=0. At a selected root edge e, toggles contribute φ(e)ᵀBp_Q+φ(e)ᵀBφ(e), the latter term removing the self contribution. Over the binary field with symmetric B this term equals diag(B)·φ(e). Adjacent selected J vertices add (Σ_{z_i=1}w_i)·φ(e). Extend c=Bp_Q+diag(B)+Σ_{z_i=1}w_i by zeros away from Q. A selected uv now requires p_u+c_u=p_v+c_v because its original line degree has parity p_u+p_v. Write b_v=p_v+c_v. On Q both b and p are known; elsewhere b_v equals degree parity.

Guess which root pins belong to class b=1. Prescribe their endpoints in class 1, other root pins' endpoints in class 0, and all Q vertices in their known classes. Reject incompatible prescriptions. Start with class-1 pins S and declare all prescribed class-1 vertices active even when isolated. Ordinary active vertices require degree odd; Q vertices require guessed p_v. Let d be the number of declared vertices where current degree differs from target. Then d≤2k+h.

In a witness restrict to its class-1 edges F, retaining declared isolated vertices. The odd vertices of the residual F minus S are precisely the current wrong declared vertices and the fresh vertices of F. Every fresh vertex is ordinary with odd final degree. From a wrong vertex take a shortest residual path to any other odd residual vertex; one exists by component parity. Internal vertices are already declared and keep their current parities. At the end either another mismatch is repaired or a fresh vertex is declared with degree one. Each added path repairs at least one mismatch and adds at most one fresh vertex. All changes remain in the original class-1 region. Declared class-1 vertices with desired degree zero may stay isolated.

Enumerate up to d fresh vertices outside the prescribed sets. On their union U with the original class-1 set, force the class-1 pins and solve the binary boundary with parity one on ordinary vertices and p_v on exceptions. On H−U force class-0 pins and require even degrees on ordinary vertices and p_v on exceptions. Spanning forests after subtracting forced edges solve both boundaries. Every acceptance gives the guessed p_Q, correct transformed classes, and even induced degrees in the original graph, including the selected J vertices. Compression guarantees completeness. Runtime is 2^{a+h+k} n^{2k+h+O(1)} with polynomial space. Bounded-degree rooted forest enumeration applies with at most 2k+h roots and at most 2k+h fresh vertices.

Deletion distance to multigraph line graphs can be unbounded already for a=0 and h=2. Take two disjoint m-edge root stars and Q their centers. For B the all-ones matrix the toggle deletes both internal spoke cliques and adds every edge between them, producing K_{m,m}. For m≥4, deleting fewer than m vertices leaves both shores nonempty and one shore has at least three vertices, yielding an induced claw. Deleting an entire shore suffices. Every multigraph line graph is claw-free since each edge's neighbors are covered by its two endpoint cliques. This theorem uses a supplied representation; recognition of a hidden perturbation is open here.
