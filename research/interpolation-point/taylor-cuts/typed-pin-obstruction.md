# A connected SAT control excludes reciprocal typed gauges for bounded-occurrence 3-SAT

Use an occurrence basis B at the variable and B^{-T} at the clause, with its type determined by occurrence sign, variable occurrence number and ordered clause port. The construction also preserves the degree, clause arity and entire incident sign profile at the designated variable. Matrices may be independently selected for each type and freshly chosen for the input. Assume transformed factors are nonnegative ordinary matching signatures, even up to independent nonzero scalars. The scalar qualification also permits complex bases.

Fix a mixed trivalent target variable, with occurrences +,+,− in that order, each at clause port one. For each of its three types exhibit two disjoint open contexts elsewhere in the same input. Its variable-side context forces the raw value bad (the external literal false). Its clause-side context forces the raw value good. The context signatures are positive multiples of the indicated unit vectors before transforming. All internal reciprocal changes cancel in contraction, so B e_bad and B^{-T} e_good are individually scalar multiples of nonnegative vectors. Their ordinary bilinear pairing is zero. Their supports are nonempty and disjoint, hence both are singleton in dimension two. Relabel each transformed wire so B e_bad is supported at zero. Invertibility ensures (B e_good)_1 is nonzero. No sign or phase assumption about its other entry is required.

The external signature of the target COPY, also when implemented by an internally contracted tree of trivalent COPY vertices, has support contained in 2^P union 2^N, where P and N are its positive and negative port sets. Both maximal words P,N occur with nonzero coefficient from a unique value of the original variable. This support cannot be realized by nonnegative weighted matching. Given two feasible matching configurations, the alternating component at a differing port changes that port and at most one other external one (represent monomers by private stubs). Starting from P with |P|=2 and trying to activate the unique port in N necessarily creates a forbidden word. Multiplication by any nonzero overall constant cannot alter a support. Thus the stipulated local catalogue fails on this single satisfiable control.

For the exact width-three, maximum-occurrence-four class use Tovey's 13-clause forcing gadget (Theorem 2.3, https://cedric.cnam.fr/~bentzc/INITREC/Files/CB11.pdf). It forces y false with three occurrences of y, four occurrences at most for all variables, and 61 extensions of y=false. A fourth occurrence serves as an external literal. To force the cut occurrence's bad value, select an opposite-signed one of the remaining occurrences of its variable; make the other two literals of that clause false with independent forcing gadgets. Every other clause on that variable gets private neutral literals. To force the exterior clause's favorable value, make both other literals false by the same construction. Each pin context closes independently with neutral exterior clauses, so no contradictory pins are imposed simultaneously. Connect these closed fixtures and a neutral target star by fresh transparent bridge clauses on exterior anchors only. The executable check_catalog_pin.py builds a connected planted control with 163 variables, 183 ternary clauses, three distinct variables per clause and at most four occurrences per variable. It exhaustively checks Tovey's fixed pin, verifies the planted assignment, degree and connectivity. External occurrence indices are assigned by input order and are preserved by the appended bridges.

Taking disjoint unions with this fixed satisfiable control preserves Tovey's NP-hard decision problem. Connectivity can also be retained: for each input component replace one (a∨b∨c) by (a∨b∨y) and (¬y∨c∨z) with fresh z forced false using the same gadget. This preserves satisfiability, all old occurrence budgets, and leaves y with two spare occurrences. Chain these y's and an exterior control anchor via bridge clauses (u∨v∨fresh), each with a fresh variable that can always be set true. The old control pin regions stay separated as before. Direct preprocessing may discard the controls or change the encoding; this theorem concerns the stated port catalogue before such transformations. Instance-specific wire IDs, transformed blocks with different native interfaces, and exponentially many adaptive branches are outside its scope. A similar construction for mixed 2/3 width and maximum occurrence three uses the binary pin pair (ℓ∨u),(ℓ∨¬u) to force a literal with one slot spare.
