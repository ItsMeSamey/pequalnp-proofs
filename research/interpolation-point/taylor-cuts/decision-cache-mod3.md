# Fixed-order decision caching on an inconsistent flow system

For n≥3 take directed top and bottom n-cycles and direct matching edges from top to bottom. Each cycle edge carries an element of Z/3 encoded by 00,01,10. Each matching edge carries a single Boolean bit, embedded as 0 or 1 in Z/3. Choose vertex charges summing to 1 modulo 3. At each vertex impose outgoing flow minus incoming flow equal to the charge, requiring both trits to use legal codes. For each forbidden local 5-bit tuple include its width-five corner clause. This explicit bounded-occurrence CNF has 5n bits and O(n) clauses. Summing all balance equations proves unsatisfiability.

Branch on matching bits first in a prescribed order, using named variables and fixed polarity. Once all those bits have assignment a, a vertex relation on its two incident trits consists of three pairs (t,t+k), where k is determined by the charge, orientation and corresponding matching bit. In particular the three pairs project bijectively to each edge's legal codes. Each forbidden pair of binary codes yields its own four-bit corner clause.

**Resistance to GF(2) row reduction.** Let V_a be the row span of the residual ANFs of the clause-violation indicators. At each vertex take the sum in GF(2) of point evaluations at the three legal pairs. These linear functionals on squarefree monomials agree on every overlap of two scopes: a shared trit has each legal code once, and the empty support has mass 3=1. Define a single linear functional L_a on the full monomial basis by these prescriptions, assigning zero elsewhere. It has L_a(1)=1 and kills V_a, so the constant polynomial 1 never appears in the reduced span.

If a and b differ on a matching bit, choose one endpoint. Its locally legal pairs under a and b are disjoint translations. The indicator of one pair legal under a is an original clause indicator after restriction under b, so belongs to V_b. But its value under L_a is 1. This proves V_a ≠ V_b for all distinct assignments. Distinct same-depth prefix rows cannot merge either: extending the two restrictions by identical remaining matching bits would otherwise give identical full-restriction row spaces by linearity. Earlier contradiction by constant elimination would likewise survive every extension. There are no ordinary units or empty clauses in this prefix. No state at different prefix depths can share a raw residual CNF: the union of supports contains exactly the remaining bits. For row spaces the support union is intrinsic to the space and again every remaining bit is essential in at least one forbidden corner indicator. Therefore a fixed-order Boolean-decision DFS with canonical labeled cofactor keys, or exact RREF keys and constant pruning, expands at least 2^n states, even with unbounded cache. This cost is 2^(N/5) for N Boolean variables. The result assumes no additional early solving rules.

**Symmetries and closures.** The proof distinguishes named row spaces. Keys modulo signed variable renamings, substitutions of trit symbols by arbitrary permutations, ideal closure, or semantic equivalence require separate analyses. All fully restricted functions are identically false. A global modular-flow solver detects the contradiction immediately, and degree-bounded local consistency strengthened along the two cycles can exploit their small width. GF(2) multiplication of input rows may also enable additional deductions. This is a method lower bound for a specified order and stopping policy. A simpler width-three GF(2) flow on the same prism gives the analogous classic labeled syntactic obstruction, but sums of its corner indicators already yield a GF(2) constant contradiction at the root.

**Resolution conversion with provenance.** Suppose an ordered decision DAG queries a fresh variable per path and each false sink identifies a particular original CNF clause falsified by every path reaching it. Let C_v consist of the negations of queried literals common to every root-to-v path. At a sink the named clause is contained in C_v. Weaken it to C_v. For an edge from v that assigns literal l, C_child is contained in C_v ∪ {¬l}: paths reaching the child include all paths to v followed by this edge. Weaken the two child clauses to the corresponding supersets and resolve on the query variable. The root clause is empty. This gives a regular resolution-with-weakening refutation with O(number of nodes) clause lines using unrestricted weakening, or O(N times number of nodes) individual literal additions. This proof needs path validity and uniform original-clause labels. Merging cofactor identities may erase the names of clauses giving the same empty residual. A DAG merely accepting or rejecting the Boolean function of an unsatisfiable instance can be one sink.

Beame, Impagliazzo, Pitassi and Segerlind formalize several distinct formula-caching proof systems. Their Theorem 4.6 proves at least 2^(n−2) nodes for FC^WSR refutations of GT_n. This is a primary precedent for decision-only caching lower bounds. Its parameter and inference rules should be specified when citing it.
