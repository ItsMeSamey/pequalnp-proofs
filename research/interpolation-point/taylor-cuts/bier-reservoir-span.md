# Low-degree evaluations on independent sets with a supplied reservoir

Given ANY finite graph, let N be a supplied independent set of size k+s. The task is to test whether a polynomial of degree d is nonzero on an independent k-set, and to return an original witness or the whole degree-d evaluation span. If k<d just enumerate the k-sets. For k≥d use the following deterministic condition. For EVERY independent T outside N with t=|T|≤d, set N_T=N minus the neighbors of T and require
|N_T|−(k−t)≥d−t.
In particular if each outside vertex has at most λ≥2 neighbors in N then s≥(λ−1)d suffices. All conditions are checked in n^{O(d)} time without a maximum-independent-set oracle.

The evaluations on independent k-sets having at most d vertices outside N span the entire degree-d space, over every field. We prove the equivalent annihilator statement. Multilinearize and write any polynomial vanishing on that small family as a sum of x_T q_T(x_N). Process T in increasing size. Terms with nonindependent T vanish identically, as do terms containing any neighbor of T in N. Put r=d−t, p=|N_T|, and k'=k−t. By hypothesis min(k',p−k')≥r. On the Boolean cube of the p remaining inside coordinates, the integral Bier basis at homogeneous level j≤r expresses the degree-j part as a linear combination of
x_R binom(|X|−|R|,j−|R|)
where R ranges over ballot subsets, |R|≤j (for sorted R_i use R_i≥2i). Indeed each such expression is the sum of all squarefree degree-j monomials containing R. Integral unimodularity allows reduction in any characteristic. Here and below binomial coefficients are first evaluated on integers, never via division in the field.

Whenever x_T=1 on a feasible k-set, write q for the number of chosen outside vertices beyond T. Its inside cardinality is k'−q. The integer-valued function binom(k'−q−|R|,j−|R|) has an integral Newton expansion in binom(q,h) for h≤j−|R|, by finite differences. Use generalized integer binomials if the top argument is negative; x_R vanishes on such assignments. On the Boolean domain binom(q,h) equals the sum of degree-h monomials on the outside vertices not in T. The replacement preserves the degree bound and is an identity after multiplication by x_T on every feasible k-set.

The new terms still having exactly the outside support T are x_T times a combination of ballot monomials x_R (aggregate coefficients for each R across all homogeneous levels). On the branch with precisely T outside, every k'-subset of N_T is feasible. Earlier smaller groups were removed, other groups of the same size vanish on that branch, and strict supersets also vanish. Therefore this combination vanishes there. The ballot monomials through degree r are independent on the k' slice: for k'≤p/2 they are a subset of the Bier basis on that level. For k'>p/2 complement the variables; the ballot family is closed under subsets, and expansion of x_R in complement monomials is triangular with unit diagonal up to signs. Apply lower-slice independence at p−k'≥r. Hence the aggregate coefficient of every retained x_R is zero. Only strict supersets of T survive the rewrite. Repeating this finite induction to outside degree d leaves zero globally. The layer basis used here is Theorem 8 of Plaza and Xiang, Resilience of ranks of higher inclusion matrices, https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08124 .

To produce a finite list of actual witnesses, enumerate T of size ≤d and one reference k'-subset of N_T. Include all such k'-subsets within d replacements of that reference. The evaluation rows span the full uniform-slice fiber because any subset farther than d has d+1 disjoint bijective replacements toward the reference. On their Boolean cube the order-(d+1) alternating sum of any degree-d polynomial vanishes, reducing to subsets closer to the reference. Thus at most n^{O(d)} original sets suffice. Evaluate any supplied polynomial or row-reduce for the complete feature span. No nontrivial arithmetic on Bier basis vectors is needed by the constructor.

The sufficient bound is sharp for the restriction to at most d outside vertices. Take d disjoint K_{λ,2} with N consisting of the λ-vertex shores, a maximum independent set. Let k=d+1, so s=(λ−1)d−1. Pick one vertex in each opposite shore, forming T of size d. The degree-d monomial x_T vanishes on every independent k-set with at most d outside vertices, since T blocks all of N. Yet adding the other vertex of one opposite shore realizes x_T=1. For λ=2 these conflict graphs are line graphs of disjoint C₄s, so the same direct-list threshold s=d is sharp even for matchings. This does not limit the separate recursive construction using other witnesses at s=d−1. A missing promise gives no general negative answer.
