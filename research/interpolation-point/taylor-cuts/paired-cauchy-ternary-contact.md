# Connected exact-3CNF high contact

Use N≥3 and equal-power-sum disjoint A,B as in paired-cauchy-contact.md. Make one free and one anchor pin on x_i, with those SAT and UNSAT signs. Replace each signed pin b by all four clauses (b OR ±y OR ±z), with y,z private to that pin. At each cyclic index i, for j=i,i+1 append (x_i OR u_j OR v_j) and (NOT x_i OR NOT u_j OR NOT v_j). Setting u_j=0,v_j=1 satisfies every connector. The four pin clauses force b. Thus the first formula is satisfiable and the other unsatisfiable.

The incidence graph is connected. There are no tautologies, duplicates, strict subsumptions, units, binary implications or pure variables. Each private variable and connector variable has four occurrences, and each x_i has twelve. Every nonempty variable set S therefore touches at least 4|S|/3>|S| clauses. This excludes matching autarkies. Consequently unit/pure/tautology/subsumption deletion, binary implication SCC preprocessing, matching-autarky peeling and usual component decomposition make no progress. Bounded-width resolution can recover each pin, and this is not claimed hard for general solvers.

Use consecutive blocks of N exponents, with exponent kN+i on occurrence template k at index i. There are 36 templates (12 clauses per index with 3 slots each), and the two native ports sharing an occurrence use its exponent. Only four free clause factors per index change. Set t=log T and Z_a=sech(at) sum_{d=0}^H tanh(at)^d G_d truncated at order H. For free-pin signs ± let P_i^± be the product of the actual four clause truth polynomials in their 12 separate port forms. Its order-j coefficient has degree ≤j in i, because every individual port label is affine in i. Both constant coefficients are nonzero polynomials in G_0 independent of i (at G_0=0 every clause is 7/8). In the field of rational functions in the Gaussian symbols the normalized log of the ratio P_i^+/P_i^- exists. Its order-j coefficient remains a polynomial in i of degree ≤j by multiplication of coefficients on compositions of j. Equal power sums kill the sum of logarithms through H and equal cardinality cancels the constant ratio. The entire differing factors therefore agree through H. Multiplying all common anchors, connectors and actual COPY polynomials and taking Gaussian expectation preserves the agreement. This argument compares complete products and does not substitute Boolean pin equivalence into deformed nodes.

The resulting Boolean origins differ, and for this template labeling the jet contact is Ω(sqrt(N/log N)) with constant formula-size overhead. For K arbitrary prescribed permutations, the 12 varying free-clause exponents per index need not be affine in i. Pigeonhole simultaneous sums of all monomials of degree ≤H in those 12 labels. Each coordinate has O((H+1) log N) bits; their number is K binomial(H+12,12). If K binomial(H+12,12)(H+1)log N=o(N), the same SAT/UNSAT formulas fool all K prescribed permutations by the same logarithmic-product argument.

## Adaptive deterministic scalar queries

Allow arbitrary two independent N-bit sign strings for free and anchor pins. The original formula is satisfiable exactly when the strings are equal. Suppose a deterministic algorithm sees the size, layout conventions and earlier replies, and chooses a new permutation for an order-H scalar-jet query. Alice holding the free string and Bob holding the anchor string can simulate this query. For each of the four clause templates of their own pin type, communicate the sums of a_1^r a_2^s a_3^v over indices with positive pin sign for all r+s+v≤H. Negative-class sums follow from the public full label list. Zeroth moments give cardinalities. In t=log T, the normalized log of one clause has order-j coefficient homogeneous of degree j in its three wire exponents, with universal rational-function coefficients in the Gaussian symbols for each sign pattern. Consequently these transmitted integer sums reconstruct the entire product of each party's actual factors through H, including the nonnormalized constants. Common guards and COPY tables are public. Local computation in a communication lower bound is unrestricted: both parties derive the same final Gaussian expectation and hence every future query.

There are O((H+1)^3) moments per party, each O((H+1)log(E+1)) bits. Any exact deterministic equality protocol must communicate at least N bits: each accepting transcript's rectangle contains at most one diagonal input pair. Therefore q scalar queries require q(H+1)^4 log(E+1)=Ω(N). This covers deterministic adaptive relabeling chosen from earlier scalar replies, with the exponents permuting [0,E). It leaves algorithms that inspect the formula syntax when choosing a layout, as well as randomized bounded-error algorithms, outside its scope. It is a query lower bound, not a superpolynomial running time lower bound.
