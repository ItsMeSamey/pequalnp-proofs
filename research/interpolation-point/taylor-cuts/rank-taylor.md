# Cut-rank Taylor messages

At fixed baseline tuple on disjoint Boolean groups, let a local factor depend on its own bits and rational linear measurements of all bits. Its z^k coefficient has degree at most k in incoming statistics. For each cluster A, take a basis of all demands from inside restricted to the outside bits (incoming rank), and a basis of all outside demands restricted to the inside bits (outgoing rank). Use original independent rows as bases. Exact elimination constructs all basis changes.

For coefficient order k keep a polynomial in incoming coordinates y of total degree at most k, separately for each monomial moment of outgoing coordinates of degree at most q−k. Semantically each entry sums the inside factors' product over inside assignments weighted by the specified outgoing monomial. For siblings A,B, each A incoming coordinate is a linear combination of parent incoming and B outgoing coordinates; the reverse holds independently. Each parent outgoing coordinate is a linear combination of both child outgoing coordinates. Expand these substitutions and the desired moment using multinomial convolution. At k=k_A+k_B, desired parent degree m≤q−k requires outgoing moments on A of degree at most m+k_B≤q−k_A, and similarly for B. All required coefficients therefore already exist. Charge actual leaf integration. For bounded incoming and outgoing ranks ρ, the cost per node is (q+1)^O(ρ) times polynomial input and precision overhead. Polynomial substitution is implemented with coefficient arrays, not a list of all choices per factor. Rational basis maps are quotients of minors of polynomial-bit raw matrices. Semantic messages in these original-row bases have polynomial bit length for polynomially many explicit polynomial-bit factors, polynomial precision and polynomial-bit baseline leaf terms: moments have degree at most q, and denominators of original input coefficients and basis coordinates admit common polynomial-bit multiples. Individual contracted sums at each merge have polynomial bit lengths by using these semantic bounds and reducing fractions.

Dense correction example: prefix rows L_i=Σ_{j≤i}2^j x_j and P_i(1+z)=zL_i+z²(x_i+L_i). The original variable is restored at T=0. Negative unit clauses at t=1 have simple zero baseline and full joint correction rank, and the final expanded factor induces a clique on x. Contiguous cluster interfaces have both ranks at most one. The prefix statistic has exponentially many attainable values.

For a connected irredundant high-treewidth original CNF, replace each vertex i of a path by a block of b bits and each path edge by all b² binary negative clauses between blocks. Each clause is essential (turn on its two endpoints only); the graph contains K_b,b and has treewidth at least b. Replace each block by its OR flag with weights 1 or 2^b−1. This preparatory transformation preserves the count; open-neighborhood signatures or supplied blocks suffice. Interpolate on flags using the same dense prefixes. Assign each edge factor 1−P_iP_{i+1} to group i. Outside demands on an interval see a common interval sum and at most a constant number of endpoint exceptions; incoming demands see a common earlier prefix and at most a constant number of endpoint variables. The two ranks are bounded regardless of block count or block size. The entire polynomial has degree at most 4 times the number of path edges, so one full jet suffices for this illustrative family. This is a structured preprocessing and interpolation construction, not a claim about arbitrary optimized October layouts.

A universal separated linear interface can require binom(q+r,r) dimensions. On the two sides let each of r coordinates u_i,v_i be the population count of q fresh bits. The coefficient-respecting factor (1+z u·v)^q at z=1 gives a positive diagonal monomial kernel for all degree-at-most-q monomials. Those monomials are independent on the grid {0,…,q}^r by ordinary interpolation, so the grid matrix has exactly the stated rank over the rationals. This does not give a time bound for any individual count.
