# Overlapping exact quotas in reservoir coordinates

Use the hereditary conflict model of bier-quota-reservoir.md, with actual inside items partitioned into independent unit pools N_i. More generally, outside item v may have a vector a_v of signed integer consumptions. Its selection changes the remaining exact reservoir counts in every coordinate at once. Given an outside subset T, define k'_i(T)=k_i−Σ_{v∈T}a_iv and p_i(T) as the number of reservoir options in pool i compatible with all of T. For each hereditary-feasible outside T of size t≤d require the two-sided margin d−t≤k'_i(T)≤p_i(T)−(d−t) for every pool. Do not omit an invalid small quota value on grounds of monotonicity when negative consumptions are allowed. For nonnegative vectors one may instead use the weaker margin from the previous theorem and ignore quota-overflow T which no superset can repair.

Then all degree-d evaluations on actual quota solutions are spanned by the same n^{O(d)} list: choose at most d outside options and at most d−t within-pool replacements total from one reference completion. In the previous proof there is no need to discard degree j_i>k'_i in the signed case, since every j_i≤d−t≤k'_i. On a completion containing T each q_i is the weighted sum Σ_{v outside T}a_iv x_v, rather than an unweighted count within a block. For every h, binom(q_i,h) equals the coefficient of u^h in
∏_v [1+x_v ((1+u)^{a_iv}−1)].
Interpret negative powers as truncated integral power series. Each factor's nonconstant term is divisible by u, so the coefficient is a Boolean polynomial of degree at most h, with no constant term when h>0. Even if several resources involve the same outside variable, multiplication and multilinearization cannot increase the total degree. Thus Newton expansion preserves the degree budget and pushes nonconstant terms to strict outside supersets, as required. The remaining product-slice independence and reference-neighborhood construction are unchanged. Actual sampling needs no expansion of a binomial power; all cardinality arithmetic is polynomial in the encoded bit lengths. Standard finite-field polynomial evaluations are on feasible items only.

Given a quota matrix, use distinct column signatures of reservoir items as columns of B. If they are rationally independent, solve Bk=rhs and Ba_v=signature(v) for every outside item. Integral solutions certify the above representation. Multiple consumption need not disappear when there is a fixed total-cardinality row: with reservoir types 'neither', 'A only', 'B only', the column for a physical edge in A∩B has coordinates (−1,1,1) in this reservoir basis. This simultaneously enforces the total number selected, the count in A and the count in B.

For graph matching, take conflicts from shared endpoints and N itself a physical matching; every graph conflict contains at most one reservoir edge. Every enumerated solution is thus a true matching with its original edge IDs. Under a fixed k quota this stable-margin theorem needs spare matching capacity and does not cover the unrestricted perfect layer. For an explicit perfect-matching realization of the fixed-k problem append |V|−2k independent ports adjacent to every original vertex, with auxiliary edges neutral and excluded from the original quotas. Any matching of size k extends via bijecting its exposed vertices to these ports, and every augmented perfect matching arises this way. For variable k, a complete neutral reservoir clique of |V| new vertices instead extends any original matching, pairing its leftover new vertices internally.
