# Two-face certificates for prescribed ternary matching gain

Use original matching incidence columns, normalized by a leading 1. A sufficient promise is quadratic generation of the perfect-matching toric ideal. More generally assume on each edge-deletion face that the rational kernel of these columns is spanned by relations a+b=c+d among matching columns. Quadratic toric generation implies this hereditary property: clear denominators, split any integer relation into two positive factorizations of the same multiset, then connect these by quadratic Markov moves. Deleted edges always have total multiplicity zero.

Normalize the other two residues affinely over F₃ to 0 and 1. When the target is absent, let f denote this integral binary label. In every quadratic relation f(a)+f(b)−f(c)−f(d) is zero modulo 3 and has magnitude at most two. Hence f annihilates all generating relations over Q. It follows that f is rational affine on matching incidence. No move catalogue is needed.

Build a rational affine basis of actual perfect matching incidences by ordinary rational linear optimization (test annihilator basis forms by extrema, adding off-span witnesses). Return any target witness obtained. If all basis labels agree, test global constancy by ternary matching span; if the test fails the proposed negative certificate is inconclusive. Otherwise fit a rational affine c to the observed binary labels. Coefficient lengths are polynomial by determinants of binary minors. Test that the minimum and maximum c values over matchings are exactly 0 and 1; call their faces F0 and F1. Any matching outside Fi uses either one edge excluded from that entire face, or two edges crossing an odd cut tight on Fi. Indeed a face is defined by the original inequalities tight throughout it. The defining matching inequalities are nonnegativity, degree one and odd-cut lower bounds. An integral matching outside the face violates at least one tight equality. Pinning either the single excluded edge or two crossing edges thus excludes all of Fi.

For each i enumerate all at-most-two edge sets and retain feasible pin restrictions avoiding Fi, tested by ordinary weighted matching extrema. On each retained restriction require c identically 1−i by ordinary extrema and normalized gain identically 1−i by finite-field matching span on the residual with the forced gain added back. These scans cover all matchings: the two faces are disjoint and all matchings outside each are covered. Success certifies a genuine NO on every graph. Empty graph and no-perfect-matching cases are direct. If target absent under the promise the fitted c is f everywhere, hence all certificate checks succeed.

For witness extraction, when the initial certificate is inconclusive, visit edges in order and delete one if the remaining subgraph still fails the certificate; any actual target witness returned immediately suffices. Under the hereditary promise certificate failure is equivalent to existence, so a minimal such subgraph is itself a target matching. Verify its original IDs. Outside the promise return UNKNOWN when extraction fails. Every YES/NO is sound and runtime polynomial with ordinary weighted matching and Lovász's linear matching-span algorithm over F₃. No toric-generation recognition assumption is needed to run this three-outcome algorithm.

K3,3 supplies a precise limitation. Its sole incidence relation over Q or F₃ equates the three even and the three odd permutations (solving edge equalities forces coefficients constant on each sign with opposite signs). A binary label zero on even permutations and one on odd is thus realizable by edge labels over F₃, yet it violates the rational relation. This is a target-free input with no two-face affine fit.

For integral costs, a matching M with cost OPT+k has at most 2k pins making it optimal. Repeatedly exclude the current optimum face by one or two edges of M until M is optimal. Each exclusion raises the integer optimum by ≥1. Testing all pin sets of size ≤2k therefore recovers an actual witness for exact level OPT+k, or proves absence of the level. Applying this from either endpoint of a supplied integer lift gives full ternary decisions at all levels in m^{2 floor(W/2)+O(1)}poly time for lift width W. On K_{6k}, take k pairs of triples A_i,B_i, cost zero inside any A_i and one elsewhere. Reference M joins A_i bijectively to B_i. Its optimum gap is k. Fewer than two pins in a gadget permit its two free cross edges to reroute internally, improving cost by one. Two pins per gadget suffice, proving sharpness on simple complete bricks.
