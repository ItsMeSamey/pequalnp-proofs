# Concentrating unresolved gains across an unknown assignment

Fix a feasible near-barrier port pattern. A bipartite perfect assignment selects one gain set per chosen assignment edge. Additional compulsory occupied factors may be present. Gains of the occupied factors are independently realized. Discard empty optional factors and reject empty compulsory ones. Write b≥1 for vector dimension and s=ceil(log2(4b)). In each slot e obtain a baseline g_e and at most b actual concrete differences spanning the variation D_e. In the matching application this is polynomial by the finite-field matching-span theorem, affine-subspace avoidance with edge-deletion witness reconstruction, and separate testing of local endpoint options. No prescribed-gain membership is required for this step.

Temporarily fix an assignment. Let W be the total token span of the remaining occupied slots, with d=dim W. Stop at U=W if d=0 or if s bases can be selected using at most one concrete token per slot across all bases. Otherwise apply the matroid intersection rank formula to the partition matroid (one per slot) and the union of s copies of the represented vector matroid. By the matroid union rank formula, there exist Y⊆X⊆E with
r_part(E minus X)+|X minus Y|+s rank(Y)<sd.
Fewer than s(d−rank(Y)) slots have some token outside span(Y), since each such slot either has a token outside X or uses an element of X minus Y. Remove these slots as exceptional. The residual span dimension falls to at most rank(Y)<d. Telescoping gives ≤s(b−u) removed slots at termination, where u=dim U. All nonexceptional slots have D_e⊆U. Their su distinct reserved slots exhibit the s bases. The optional toggles on these bases cover all U by the Holder bound in ternary-additive-bases-refinement.md. Thus retained profiles sum to exactly one affine coset.

To find a successful unknown assignment, enumerate U, at most s(b−u) exceptional occupied slots, and su reserved occupied slots with their concrete tokens grouped in bases. Force indicated optional edges and reject conflicting assignments and invalid bases. Require every reserve to have direction inside U. Reject unguessed compulsory slots whose direction is not contained in U; exclude all such unguessed optional slots. Query each exceptional factor for its possible gains only in F3^b/U, keeping original witnesses. Group-sum DP combines the exceptional lists. All other slots have one quotient baseline label. Use labelled bipartite matching in the quotient on the residual assignment (including the shifts from forced choices), then correct the aggregate error by the reserved toggles inside U via subset-sum traceback. Completeness follows by applying the per-assignment lemma to any original solution. Soundness follows by assembling actual independent slot witnesses.

For explicit input size L the number of guesses is at most 3^{O(b²)} L^{O(b log(2b))}: enumerate subspaces by ordered vector bases; at most sb slots are exceptional or reserved; ordering the latter encodes their grouping. Per guess, multiply polynomial overhead and 3^{O(b)} by the actual clocks for exceptional membership in rank b−u and bipartite assignment in that rank. Local edge options and optional parallel labels are included in L. A single nonbipartite factor can still require unrestricted prescribed matching.

Minimizing the number of flexible occupied factors over feasible assignments is one ordinary minimum-cost bipartite matching with compulsory contributions added. If that minimum exceeds sb, no branch with U=0 is feasible, so all unresolved ordinary matching queries strictly drop gain dimension. More generally a candidate U is impossible if minimum occupied count with D_e not contained in U exceeds s(b−u). If the strict-drop test holds recursively at unresolved nonbipartite nodes, quotient nesting lasts at most b levels. Decomposition and pattern discovery costs, branching and leaf solvers remain part of the total clock. Counting is not asserted.
