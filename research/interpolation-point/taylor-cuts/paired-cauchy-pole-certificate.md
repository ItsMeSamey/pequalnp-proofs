# Formula-aware sufficient stopping rule

Apply any sound preprocessing first. Equisatisfiability suffices for decision; exact counting needs multiplicity-preserving reductions. Build the native OR/COPY network on the remaining formula, with distinct integer occurrence labels. For each cyclotomic factor Φ_m of 1+T^(2d) for a positive label distance d, make a graph on active gate ports (delete COPY1 ports). Give an edge weight 1 precisely if m divides 4d but does not divide 2d, where d is the endpoint label distance. All other edges have weight zero, including d=0. Let u_m be the maximum ordinary matching weight. Pairing edges of every centered gate term form such a matching, and all individual denominators are squarefree in characteristic zero. Thus D(T)=product_m Φ_m(T)^u_m clears every term and the whole signed scalar. Each term is bounded at infinity, so the cleared polynomial has degree at most h=deg D. D(0)=1.

Compute the full gate-product collision jet through this certified h and multiply by D modulo (T−1)^(h+1). That residue is now the full polynomial, which can be evaluated at zero and scaled to the integer count. Graph construction and exact matching are polynomial for polynomially bounded labels. Compute cyclotomic polynomials by dividing T^k−1 by proper-divisor factors; their coefficient bit sizes and that of D are polynomial in degree since all roots have modulus one. This gives a verifiable formula-specific bound on the vanishing order at T=1 for a satisfiable residual (its origin count is nonzero), and a polynomial decision and count rule whenever h=O(log²(input length)). Connected components may be handled separately and relabeled locally. No assumption that this h bounds all inputs polylogarithmically is justified.

The one-clause OR3 on once-occurring exponents 1,2,3 with signs +,+,− has normalized scalar (7+Γ_distance2)/8: the two distance-one terms cancel and the candidate Φ4 pole is absent. Conversely n positive unit clauses on contiguous exponents give a Θ(n³) total bound if the network is retained whole. For n/2<d<3n/4 the Φ_(4d) positive-weight edges are exactly the n−d disjoint pairs at distance d, hence u_(4d)=n−d. Summing (n−d)φ(4d) yields Ω(n³) from the mean order of the totient, and the former common denominator gives O(n³). Preprocessing immediately solves this control. It limits the pole estimate rather than the scalar evaluation complexity.

Stronger scope restriction: the same Θ(E³) bound holds on EVERY unreduced original CNF with all E occurrence labels contiguous and nonempty clauses. Select only the clause endpoint of each occurrence, choosing the constant term at every COPY. Every centered OR truth polynomial has nonzero coefficient on every subset. Hence every matching of the E distinct clause ports is a supported term, including each distance-d matching used in the lower bound. The support-only certificate is consequently a residual-size bound on this common label scheme. A structural improvement here would require detecting cancellations between terms.
