# One trellis for all boundary minima of a sparse Walsh score

First solve the hard XOR pins and parameterize their affine solution space by independent binary coordinates x. Inconsistency gives an empty output table. Substitute in every score and boundary form. Absorb affine offsets into signed rational Walsh coefficients, merge identical frequencies, discard zero coefficients, and move the zero frequency to the constant. Let A have the s surviving distinct frequency masks as rows, with rank r. The score for Y=Ax has one univariate cost per bit Y_j, namely c_j for zero and −c_j for one. Its allowed vectors are exactly ker H for any basis H of ker Aᵀ used as rows. This uses s−r checks.

Let Bx+b be the original boundary, of linear rank w. Some of its information may be independent of Y. For fixed Y the linear boundary values differ precisely by B ker A. The linear tests on Bx that ignore that ambiguity are given by c annihilating B ker A. Select such checks whose cB form a basis of row(B) intersect row(A); call its dimension t. For each selected c solve dA=cB and collect the d as rows of D. For every attainable boundary b+beta, the exact compatible frequency vectors are

    H Y = 0,      D Y = (c beta)_c.

To see sufficiency, start with any preimage for Y. The mismatch to beta belongs to im B and vanishes on the selected tests. All annihilators of B ker A induce linear combinations of these tests on im B; hence that mismatch belongs to B ker A and can be corrected without changing Y.

Let M=[H;D], columns m_j, and V the span of the standard basis vectors in the final t output positions. Start syndrome zero and process one physical column at a time, choosing Y_j∈{0,1}, adding m_j Y_j and the corresponding scalar cost. Merge equal syndromes by minimum, storing one attaining prefix. Discard a new syndrome outside span(unprocessed columns)+V. At layer j the surviving states are exactly span(processed columns) intersect (span(unprocessed columns)+V): membership is necessary, and every vector in the intersection has both a realizing prefix and a completing suffix to some virtual target. Its realizing prefixes pass every earlier pruning test by that same completion. Therefore the DP runs in polynomial overhead times sum_j 2^lambda_j, with lambda_j the dimension of this intersection. Keeping only the current layer and one length-s witness per state gives polynomial overhead times the largest layer in working space. Constructing the explicit output adds 2^w entries and corresponding time and output storage. The combined bound is O*(2^(s−r+t)+2^w), hence O*(2^(s−r+w)). The computed witness Y need not be unique. For each attainable beta solve simultaneously Ax=Y, Bx=beta and undo the Gaussian chart to return an optimum original word. Distinct original boundaries can share a target and a Y.

For three affine literals L_1,L_2,L_3 the indicator that NAE fails is (1+sum_{i<j}(−1)^(L_i+L_j))/4. All-equal literals give one; otherwise one pair agrees and two differ, giving zero. This identity is valid for repeated, constant or complementary literals. The zero-minimum boundary entries give precisely the feasible NAE fibres and supply witnesses. This is an optimization/decision table, not a count of satisfying models.

The adjacent Python implementation and brute checker include fractional weights, exact cancellations, boundary offsets and dependencies, inconsistent pins, and independent boundary directions. They compared 910 complete tables and 2,580 optimum witnesses through dimension six. A separate 21-frequency example with seven independent parity checks has peak trellis size two at the chosen order.
The syndrome-state construction uses classical minimal linear-code trellises. See [Forney, Codes on Graphs](https://ocw.tau.edu.ng/courses/electrical-engineering-and-computer-science/6-451-principles-of-digital-communication-ii-spring-2005/video-lectures/lecture-17-codes-on-graphs/).
