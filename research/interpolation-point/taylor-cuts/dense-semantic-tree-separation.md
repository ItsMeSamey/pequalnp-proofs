# A two-leaf semantic tree with small computed leaf hulls and exponential physical-coordinate hulls in every order

For every sufficiently large even n, set m=n−2. There are linearly independent vectors u_1,...,u_m in F₂^n such that, among the index masks of weight one or two, no two different masks give row sums that are simultaneously zero on n/2 coordinates. Equivalently, for each pair of such different masks λ,μ the number of coordinates j for which (λU)_j=(μU)_j=0 is strictly less than n/2, where U has the u_i as rows.

**Existence and recognition.** Choose every entry independently and uniformly. For each fixed pair of masks their two outputs are independent uniform bits in each column, since the distinct nonzero masks are linearly independent over F₂. Their common-zero count is Bin(n,1/4); the probability it reaches n/2 is exponentially small. A union bound covers O(n⁴) pairs. The expected number of nonzero left-kernel vectors is (2^m−1)2^(−n)<1/4, so full row rank and all pair tests hold with probability bounded below by a positive constant. Rank and every pair test are polynomial-time verifiable.

There is also a deterministic polynomial-time construction for sufficiently large n. Fix entries column by column, and within a column bit by bit, minimizing the conditional expectation of the sum of the number of nonzero left kernels and the number of bad pairs. For a bad-pair probability count common zeros among the completed columns, determine the common-zero probability of the partially fixed column by solving two binary equations on its unread bits, and use a Bin(remaining columns,1/4) tail. For the kernel expectation let K be the left kernel of the completed columns and K₀ its subspace of vectors with zero coefficients on the unread rows of the current column. Vectors in K outside K₀ pass the current column with probability 1/2. In K₀ count those annihilating its already fixed part by another dimension computation. Thus the expected number of vectors surviving the current column is (|K|−|K₀|)/2 plus that last count; subtract the zero vector and multiply by 2^(−number of future columns). These conditional expectations have polynomial-bit rational representations. The initial expectation is below one and the final score is integral, hence zero.

**Global residual hull.** Take the signed-affine-cell score f(x)=sum_i 1[u_i·x=0] (positive unit weights already suffice). For any order of the physical n bits split it into prefix A and suffix B of equal sizes. If two row projections onto B are equal, their pair-sum mask vanishes there; if one projection is zero its unit mask vanishes there. At most one such relation exists by construction. Discard the zero row or the two rows of the colliding pair if needed. Every remaining B projection is nonzero and occurs exactly once among *all* m rows.

The rank of the projections onto A of all original rows is at least m−|B|=n/2−2. Deleting at most two rows leaves at least t=n/2−4 independent A projections. Choose t such rows. For fixed prefix x_A, the normalized Walsh coefficient of its residual at any one of the corresponding B frequencies is exactly (−1)^(u_i|A·x_A)/2; no other row contributes at that frequency. Independence makes the projected residual point set *exactly* the 2^t vertices of a cube. Its convex hull is therefore a linear image of the entire physical residual hull with 2^t vertices. The physical residual hull itself has at least this many vertices in every physical variable order. This argument does not allow a global change of linear coordinates; indeed such a change also makes the example easy.

**Two leaves and no duplicated private coordinates.** Split the rows into two sets of nearly equal size. On each leaf retain its sum of parity indicators. Their semantic frequency spans have rank Θ(n) individually, and are in direct sum, so the fork interface is zero. To inflate the *syntactic* normal interface without changing either function, replace just one summand in each leaf by a disjoint partition of its cell according to the first nonzero evaluation among the other leaf's row basis, plus the all-zero case. These cells are all consistent because the combined rows are independent. Their sum is the original summand, yet each syntactic leaf now uses all m normal directions. The partition uses only existing ambient coordinates and at most O(n) cells per leaf. Thus its raw normal interface has dimension m, direct enumeration using either leaf's semantic rank alone costs 2^Θ(n), and the global hull in every physical coordinate order is exponential.

Both leaves admit a polynomial-time local hull calculation after recovering their charts. Traverse frequency coordinates with a binary prefix tree, retaining a prefix exactly when its affine filter Q has positive Walsh energy sum_{z in Q} F(z)^2. For a signed cell mixture this energy is exactly computable by polynomially many affine intersections and character sums, as in affine-cell-interface.md. If s frequencies are nonzero there are at most s live prefixes per level, hence at most 1+2ds queries in d ambient dimensions (one query when s=0). At singleton leaves evaluate the signed coefficient directly. The actual spectra here consist precisely of frequency zero and the listed independent leaf rows; exact cancellation removes all artificially introduced normal directions. No hidden row basis or oracle for the initial unrefined presentation is required. Use these independent frequencies as coordinate bits on the leaf. Assigning a prefix only adds a scalar to the unassigned portion of the score; the residual hull has at most two vertices, and in fact the leaf extremum is attained by optimizing the independent parity evaluations separately. The two optimized words are compatible on the empty interface and lift by Gaussian elimination to actual assignments, with two physical free directions. The displayed separation is within the signed-cell optimization model, including its Gaussian elimination step; it does not assert hardness or forbid a different single global affine chart from working.

**Fork dimension audit.** Let A,B denote child semantic spans and O the outside span after any hard affine equations have been solved in one common coordinate system. Write X=A∩(B+O), Y=B∩(A+O), W=(A+B)∩O. Then X+Y=W+X=W+Y. For example any y in Y has y=a+o with a in A and o in O, giving o in W and a in X; and any w in W decomposes into terms in X and Y. In addition X∩Y=A∩B. Enumerate evaluations of X+Y, or for a fixed parent W evaluation enumerate its extensions by dim(X+Y)−dim W free binary coordinates. These give both child boundary words. Every optimizer pair at such words agrees on A∩B, hence the two linear evaluations glue and extend through the ambient space. This gives a total fork enumeration exponent dim(X+Y), saving dim(A∩B) over independently enumerating all child pairs. If some parent evaluations are prescribed, enumerate only the corresponding affine fiber; infeasible prescribed values contribute no configuration.
