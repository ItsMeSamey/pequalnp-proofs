# The complete nonorigin grid of zero flags can be independent of satisfiability

Use the minimum conditional-expectation stack of native-odd-grid.md, genuine copies first and B negative-unit tails. Encode an arbitrary exact-3SAT instance on n variables by two rails x_i,y_i per variable and one positive unit g. For each i use positive binary x_i∨y_i and negative ternary ¬x_i∨¬y_i∨¬g. Replace each original clause by the negative triple on its three false rails. Exactly one rail per pair must hold, so the transformation preserves satisfiability and counts.

With m old edges in the encoded network, the lowest nonzero coefficient of Z(z) is [z^(n+1)]Z=2^(m−1). All n+1 positive clauses have zero constant and linear term the sum of incident subdivision bits. Every negative clause, equality and unary variable has constant one. The linear Boolean sums concern disjoint bits, since an old edge has at most one clause endpoint. Summing the product over uniform bits gives 2^m times (1/2)(2/2)^n, as claimed.

Z(z)/z^(n+1) is integral with power-of-two constant. By the rational root theorem any nonzero rational root has reduced numerator dividing this constant. For t=1,…,L−1, h_t has odd numerator and |h_t|>1, by the binomial ratio in the stack note. Thus every positive integer node, including every even one, has Z(h_t) nonzero irrespective of satisfiability.

For 1≤t≤B all native stack and outside factors are nonzero. In particular 2−h_t and 1+(1−h_t)^d for d=0,1,2 can vanish only for h_t=2, which is not an odd 2-local unit. For t>B an entire genuine layer is routed outside the slots, so its positive unit vanishes. For t<0 the first genuine layer is entirely unmatched. The exact rational grid zero flags therefore have the input-independent pattern F(t)=0 iff t<0 or t>B, for all nonorigin nodes. At t=0 the zero flag is precisely SAT decision on the constructed CNF. At any requested positive t≤B the native modular value is nonzero as well, by the signed height reconstruction bound in the stack note: outside bits read by a positive number of rows contribute odd units even at even t. Inactive even nodes do not have stipulated native precision. This obstruction to deciding SAT from the zero flags allows other predicates, derivatives and packings.
