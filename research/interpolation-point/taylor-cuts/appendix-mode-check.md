# Tests at the actual recursive packet interface

In the quantitative contraction appendix, lines 390–493, the node expansion sums mode exponent tuples at total weighted degree below p_t. Summing old Boolean row ports first gives T multilinearly extended to its deformed row arguments h_i: sum_Y T(Y) times the product of interpolation selectors equals sum_S c_S product_{i in S}h_i, with c_S the Boolean Möbius coefficients. This identity uses no division. Each mode token must occupy a distinct target row. For its occupied rows D_v at vertex v, every contributing term has extra valuation at least min_{S containing D_v}v₂(c_S). A tuple vanishes if its source-mode weight plus the minimum aggregate vertex charge over eligible token placements is at least p_t. Charge the cost of computing or lower-bounding that assignment minimum. Old entries must use the source's creation-tagged canonical lifts; identities holding only at an earlier modulus need not survive. For instance the canonical integer lift of a+b modulo two is the Boolean XOR polynomial a+b−2ab. Local Möbius coefficients can be recomputed from the tagged entry routine. Even polynomial-space streaming through subsets of a degree-d tensor is within 2^O(d) times the entry cost. Ordinary OR clause tensors have top coefficient ±1, hence zero extra charge for every subset. In the source's sparse reduction the other side consists of binary subdivision-equality tensors, 1−h₁−h₂+2h₁h₂. Activating both ports gains one additional bit and activating only one gains none, at most M/2 extra bits overall. No uniform asymptotic improvement is shown.

Additional dilation t+α2^dζ gives an extra d bits per selected nonlocal row coefficient from the simple t-root at unit ζ. Undoing that additional substitution while retaining the same unrestricted jet through order q_t−1 costs d(q_t−1) bits on its highest coefficient. At odd nodes q_t=ceil(3M/u)u≥3M, while at most M rows activate. Thus the gain alone cannot remove an eligible tuple when recalibrating precision to preserve the existing jet. This does not rule out a more specialized reconstruction or a tighter treatment of the existing precision slack.

Merely requesting decision at root precision one leaves the published p_t=p+8hD_t+8M(a+1) guard. A separate randomized finite-field test is available: weight each original Boolean variable by an independent tag α_i in an extension field K of size at least 2n when that bit equals one. The multilinear tag polynomial is nonzero exactly when SAT holds; a random evaluation detects it with probability ≥1/2. When applied to the prime-field baseline method, only scalar weights use K; baseline fibers still take values in the prime subfield. A nonzero scalar yields a witness by retaining a nonzero branch while fixing each variable. This is not deterministic derandomization or a proven unrestricted improvement over the October bound.
