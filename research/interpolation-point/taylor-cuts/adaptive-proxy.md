# Discovering a directional tree for a high-precision proxy

## Unavoidable packet on individual-atom trees

A packet type with unequal symbols in all a coordinates has Hpad packets. The global hole count divided by Hpad is at most D_arr^2(sqrt(2 Delta/M)+L/M)=o(1), so one such packet is full. Its row labels are all different, and its column labels are all different. It therefore contains one row port from each of L distinct old predicate atoms and one slot from each of L distinct paired bits, each mate outside the packet. Their matrix in WJ is exactly full Cauchy W.

Any binary tree of those distinct atoms admits a cut balanced on the 2L distinguished leaves. One direction contains an r-by-r Cauchy minor with r>=L/3. At any nonzero residue lift on any active shell, normalization valuation is a+1. Each of the two Vandermonde factors has difference-valuation sum at most rL/2, and the denominator valuations are nonnegative. Thus total minor Smith exponent sum is <=r(a+1+L). For tau>=2(a+1+L), at least half the Smith factors survive modulo 2^tau, giving directional rank >=L/6 for EVERY congruent proxy/lift and tree, even if chosen separately at the interpolation point. At p_t>(a+1)L^2 the minor determinant itself survives and the rank is >=L/3. This is a representation-specific obstruction for individual demanded port values. It does not prohibit packing many atoms into one differently evaluated leaf, or simplified and nonlinear predicate representations.

At an actual CNF root sample write A=W(T)J in the scalar ring O/2^p. Let A0 be a supplied or computably chosen matrix congruent to A modulo 2^tau (tau>=1); coordinate truncation is one default choice. Fix its integer-coordinate lift in K=Q[U]/f, rather than assuming finite-ring addition commutes with lifting. Over K represent the graph code by [I | A0^T], grouping all outputs for one source predicate and retaining one group per paired input bit. On each union of groups the rank-connectivity function equals the sum of the two directional cross ranks, by eliminating the identity pivots. It is a symmetric integer submodular function with value zero at the empty set. [Korhonen and Oum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04756) find a branch decomposition of width at most r or certify rejection in 2^O(r^2) times polynomial oracle work. Exact K ranks are polynomial-bit via rational multiplication matrices in the power basis, since f is irreducible over Q. This applies to grouped leaves without splitting predicates. For matrices integral over the local DVR, finite-ring Smith rank cannot exceed this lifted rank, so the directional moment evaluator applies at full target precision. Rejection only concerns this chosen lift.

Write A=A0+2^tau E at the working precision, by exact division on divisible representatives. Multilinearity in the distinct row-port arguments gives a Taylor sum over j distinct corrected ports. Each correction picks one input bit from E x, contributes its coefficient and pins that Boolean bit to one. Pinning the same variable repeatedly has no effect. Evaluate the mixed derivative of each affected old predicate with these bits fixed, over the base A0. There are at most sum_{j<=k} binom(M,j) N^j terms, k=floor((p-1)/tau) and N independent bits. Higher terms vanish. The full cost per scalar is polynomial bit factors times 2^O(r^2)+(M+N+1)^O(r+k). For fixed r,k this is polynomial space as well, and search can use a separate decomposition at every sample. Larger r,k require accounting for discovery memory and the moment tables. The recognition theorem needs explicit bounded-degree root predicates; recursively created packet tensors require a separate cost bound.

The sum of the two finite-ring generator counts itself need not be submodular. Over Z/4 take rows (1,2), (1,0), (2,0). For cuts {row0,row2} and {row2,column0}, the values on the two cuts, intersection, and union are 1,1,1,2. Thus substituting generator counts directly in the submodular branch-width theorem is unjustified.
