# Relational clause lemmas and acyclic gate extensions

This proof format augments source clauses with previously checked relational lemmas. IDs are their append positions, starting at zero. A bit step names two earlier clauses, a positive pivot, a new clause C, and the explicit pins falsifying every literal of C. The verifier checks the pins against C. If they conflict, C is a tautology. Otherwise they must make a predecessor false or restrict the two predecessors respectively to the unit pivot and its negation. Both assignments to the sole remaining interface bit are then impossible, establishing C. Only an earlier, literally empty clause can close the proof, and no later records are allowed. IDs can be reused as premises, giving a DAG.

An extension introduces the next unused positive variable with a NOT, AND or OR on previously active signed literals. Its two or three defining CNF clauses are generated by the verifier. Every earlier assignment extends uniquely through the definitions. Thus refutation of the extended formula refutes the original source. Existing and future names cannot be used as extension outputs and inputs, respectively.

The translator accepts explicit indexed resolution and extended-resolution records, with left positive pivot and right negative pivot. Swap predecessor orientation or consecutively renumber fresh names when importing another convention. Write the ordinary resolvent as C. If its falsifying pins are consistent and leave the pivot free, both predecessors reduce to opposite units. If C retains a pivot sign from a tautological antecedent and fixes the pivot, a predecessor is already false. This proves exact replay for ordinary resolution, including tautologies. An empty input clause can close immediately. The interface rule and gate extension are additional proof rules and are not a simulation result for the prior frozen source-subtree checker.

Translation adds a copy of the derived clause for its pins, constant many fresh gate clauses per extension and O(log M + log V) bits per referenced clause ID or pivot for M lines and V variable names. Thus explicit-resolution literal-occurrence size grows by a constant factor apart from standard index encoding. If the source resolution format stores only parent references and pivots, materializing width-w resolvents and pins costs O(w log V) additional bits per step. Verification is polynomial in explicitly supplied source and proof length; neither a short proof nor efficient proof search follows. The implementation is a translator for the documented record format, not a parser for DRAT, LRAT or arbitrary external proof dialects.

The control suite checks a refutation in which a fresh negation variable actually participates in producing the terminal empty clause, all 256 subsets of the eight nonempty non-tautological clauses on two variables, exact definitional semantics on signed inputs, and mutations involving pins, forward references, freshness, and the terminal clause.
