# Orientable surface with unsupplied face cover

Assume a connected cellular embedding in an orientable surface of genus g. Retain the demanded vertices and their incident face vertices in the radial incidence graph. A cover of size k radius-two dominates this graph, since every retained face is incident to a demand and every demand is adjacent to a selected face. In each component the selected centers can be linked by hops of length at most five, because their two-balls cover a connected graph. Reject diameter greater than 5k−1. Every surviving component has treewidth O((g+1)k) by bounded-genus local treewidth. A constructive single-exponential constant-factor treewidth approximation and nice-decomposition DP use face selection flags and demand coverage flags, checking each demand on forget. Minimize cost and recover faces by backtracking; sum component optima. The runtime is 2^O((g+1)k) poly(N). Try increasing budgets to find the minimum. Empty support needs no search. [Layered separators](https://users.monash.edu/~davidwo/papers/LayeredSeparator.pdf); [terminal face cover FPT](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-025-00782-x).

Add the face ghosts and use the paths of planar.md. Primal cuts are the dual even subgraphs of zero mod-two homology, since they bound unions of dual faces. Decorate the dual by disk parity gadgets and edge connectors as before. Choose a reference matching representing the empty even subgraph independent of numeric weights. The symmetric difference with another matching traces selected dual edges in the strips between the vertex disks; all other differences in the disks do not affect its homology. Calibrate each surface Kasteleyn Pfaffian sign on this reference term. The relative sign of a matching in class h is then (-1)^q(h) for a quadratic refinement. Twisting orientations by H¹ adds all 2^(2g) linear characters. Their average preserves class zero and cancels every other class. Twisting the ghost paths changes weights without changing orientations. Thus 2^b Pfaffians suffice for b=2g+max(0,k−1), evaluated at p+b bits before averaging at precision p. When k=0 restore the free global flip. Retain zero-weight graph edges for the embedding/orientations. Reference calibrations use orientation signs alone. The positive-rational dyadic reconstruction and signed integral jet treatment in planar.md carry over. Overall running time is 2^O((g+1)k) poly(N) plus 2^b poly(N,p+b,bit lengths,jet size). [Cimasoni–Reshetikhin](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0608070).

Independent exhaustive torus replay: on the 3×3 square torus each of the four homology sectors has 256 closed walls, only the 256 in class zero are cuts of the 512 spin assignments. This validates the topological projection; it does not benchmark Pfaffian orientations.
