# What one Appendix D interpolation value can encode

At a depth-zero source call with a supplied legal packing, take t=1 and the permitted interpolation residue ζ=1. Write T=2L+1, N=C(T)/C′(0); the source formula gives W_xy(T)=N/(T−y+x) and v₂(N)=a+1. Use ℓ=P₀=1 and ordinary Boolean indicator tensors. The two claims below concern this prescribed scalar modulo the source's node precision p_t.

**A decision reduction.** Normalize a nonempty width-three CNF on n used variables and m clauses; empty clauses and trivially true formulas can be handled separately. For bounded degrees one may replace each variable occurrence by a distinct copy on a cycle of implications, which permits exactly the two uniform settings and uses at most three occurrences per copy. Remove repeated or complementary literals within clauses in the usual way. Choose x=0 and distinct positive even slots y_j for variables j. Use m identical full packets. In packet i set distinguished bit Y_x=1. Sum every other row freely, using l₀+l₁=1. At slots y_j for occurrences in clause i keep the column bits live; pin every other column bit to zero. For a fixed j, all of its live ports have the same full column-endpoint label. Join them in a 0/1 original tensor permitting precisely the two lists of literal truth values induced by assignments to j. All unselected packets have pinned-zero columns and free rows, so their factor is one.

For each assignment the product of the m real packet factors is N^m times the product over clauses of the sum of 1/d_j for its true literals, with d_j=T−y_j+x positive and odd. All terms after division by N^m are nonnegative. For each clause clear its common odd denominator ∏_{j in clause}d_j; its resulting integer is at most 3(3L+1)^2. The sum over assignments after clearing denominators is a nonnegative integer at most 2^n[3(3L+1)^2]^m, positive precisely when the CNF is satisfiable. Consequently precision greater than m(a+1)+n+m log₂(3(3L+1)^2) preserves that criterion. The source precision p_t=P₀+8 ceil(3M/u)L+8M(a+1) suffices. At zero W(0)=I and each packet's row x reads a pinned-zero column, so F(0)=0 for every formula in this construction.

Here is explicit polynomial padding for the published parameters. Put u=ceil(log₂(2(n+m+1))), s=M=2^{u/ε}, with the paper's fixed reciprocal-integer ε. Then λ=u/ε, L=2^{2u} and D_arr=r^{2a}=L^{12}. Fill each ordered-label class with exactly M/D_arr matching edges; this multiplicity is a multiple of L and much larger than m. The full all-unequal unordered pair type therefore yields M/D_arr identical full packets. The balanced padding gives exactly M/r ports of each coordinate-symbol on each side. M meets the active root and degree conditions asymptotically; finite parameter exceptions can be treated separately. Joining column endpoints of the same label leaves every per-label port load unchanged. The entire padded network, packing and Boolean tables are constructible in polynomial time in n+m with a very large fixed exponent. This is thus a many-one NP-hardness result for testing NONZERO at this supplied deformed evaluation. It places no hardness requirement on computing F(0) on these instances, nor on the layout selected by an implementation's greedy tie-breaking. No SAT lower bound follows.

**Exact programmable masked permanent.** Instead give each of m packets one pinned row at common x and a column j slot y_j allowed only when A_ij=1. For every j, enforce exactly one among its allowed X_ij using one original tensor; pin unused ports to zero. If any column is empty its permanent is already zero. There are m active ports, and a nonzero contribution requires at least one in every packet. Thus this point is per(A)∏_j W_{x,y_j}, since every contributing assignment gives one allowed perfect matching. The factor has valuation m(a+1) and a computable odd unit part. As p_t−m(a+1)>log₂(m!), its residue recovers the entire 0/1 permanent by removing that power of two and inverting the unit part.

Explicit bounded-degree tables suffice. To reduce arbitrary bipartite degrees, replace a vertex of degree d≥1 by a path on 2d−1 vertices, with one incident original edge attached to each odd-position socket. Exactly one socket must take an external matching edge by counting the two color classes of the internal path. For every such choice the remaining two even paths match uniquely. This replacement preserves the permanent while giving maximum degree three and polynomial size. The supplied root oracle is #P-hard for exact evaluation by the usual perfect-matching counting theorem. This does not assert a parsimonious reduction from SAT decision to existence of a perfect matching.
