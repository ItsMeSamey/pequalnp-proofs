# Recognizing semantic cut rank with labelled graph decompositions

For all-negative 2CNF, models are independent sets. For X independent subset S, its residual on an unconstrained Y is the Boolean monomial enforcing zero on N_Y(S). Distinct neighborhoods give independent monomials by subset zeta inversion. On a c-label expression for G[X], track occupied labels and neighborhood unions for independent subsets in the FINAL induced graph at each subterm. At disjoint union, final cross-child edges are uniform by current child-label pair: they all arose from later operations, and labels cannot split. Inspect one representative pair in the final graph, or propagate future edge flags. Merge compatible masks and neighborhood unions. Apply relabelings to masks; edge-addition operations no longer need enforcement. Stop beyond w neighborhoods: each partial independent set extends globally with every omitted X vertex false. Cost ≤4^c(w+1)^2 poly(N). As-built early abort is unsound: before an edge joins two X vertices with separate singleton Y neighborhoods there are four unions, after it only three.

For arbitrary G[Y], distinct cross-neighborhoods of independent X subsets correspond to distinct rows (test singleton Y assignments), but need not be linearly independent. With expressions for both shores list representatives until either side exceeds 2^w distinct neighborhoods or finishes, then eliminate the compressed compatibility matrix. A rank-w Boolean matrix has at most 2^w rows by pivot projection. The following improvement avoids even 2^w enumeration.

**Basis algorithm with true-only triggers and signed conclusions.** Assume all-negative X feasibility after known flips, and only cross implications x⇒literal(y). The suffix Q_Y may have signed binary constraints, supplied with a labelled decomposition whose final inter-child forbidden bit relations are uniform by label pair. An ordinary clique-width expression suffices for unsigned graphs, while signed internal tables require edge-typed expressions or verified relation uniformity. As functions on Sat(Q_Y), each prefix residual is a product of pin indicators. For each X occupancy mask keep a linearly independent basis selected from actual pin predicates. At a compatible join all pairwise products of the two bases span the full family by bilinearity; taking products just unions pin lists (a conflict is the zero function). Merge compatible mask collections and repeat row elimination after every candidate or small batch. Every partial residual is an actual full residual by assigning zero outside the partial X subset; an observed rank>w certifies rejection. The zero padding uses true-only triggers and hereditary X feasibility. For signed Q_Y a pin-list count >w is not evidence of excessive rank: on a∨b, the four functions 1,a,b,ab have rank three.

To reduce L candidate pin rows, compute their column span over Sat(Q_Y) on the Y expression. Per labelled subterm store a column-vector basis separately for each profile of seen spin values per label (≤4^c). Leaf vectors record the L pins' compatibility with the chosen bit, including internal unary tests. At union combine only child occupancies compatible with the final cross-child constraints; their column vectors combine coordinatewise because each candidate is a conjunction of pins. Products of basis vectors span the products by bilinearity. Sum the compatible spaces, then reduce. Root basis across occupancies determines all row relations and permits returning original independent candidate pin rows. Dimension is ≤L throughout; use any field. With candidate insertion one at a time L≤w+1, X masks and mask pairs cost 2^O(c_X) and each Y oracle 2^O(c_Y)poly(w,N). This gives threshold recognition and exact rank when ≤w in 2^O(c_X+c_Y)poly(w,N) field operations. Select actual columns as bases when rational bit growth matters: entries then are bits and elimination intermediates have polynomial bit bounds. With only one controlled shore and arbitrary other internal constraints, the transposed hardness result of semantic-hardness.md applies.

**Whole-graph layout discovery for all-negative simple graph formulas only.** At any cut restrict to the empty and all singleton independent assignments on each shore. Subtract the empty row and column to expose [1] and −C, C the cut adjacency matrix. Thus semantic rank≥1+rank_F C. If optimum semantic width ≤w, ordinary binary graph rankwidth ≤k, where k=w−1 for Q or F₂ (nonzero mod-2 minor gives nonzero rational minor), and k=2^(w−1) for odd fields (at most that many distinct binary C rows). [Oum’s constructive approximation](https://mathsci.kaist.ac.kr/~sangil/pdf/2006approxfull.pdf) returns a tree of ordinary graph cutrank O(k+1) or certifies graph rankwidth>k in FPT time. For cutrank a, neighborhood unions are supports of row spaces spanned by ≤a of ≤2^a different rows. At most (2^a+1)^a unions, and hence this many nonzero semantic rows, arise. This is the established connection to [Boolean-width](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030439751100418X). The returned approximation guarantees a semantic-width bound of 2^O((k+1)^2) on promised instances without a supplied layout; a larger-than-requested output does not disprove the exact semantic width target. Fixed-cut recognition hardness allows internal shore graphs of unbounded global width.
