# Adaptive layouts under Boolean semantics

A semantic cut matrix is the 0/1 truth table indexed by full assignments on the two shores. Cofactors are submatrices, up to removal of fixed-coordinate labels, so their ranks cannot increase when additional variables are fixed. For a supplied menu of layouts a semantic depth-d branching certificate therefore gives a shared selector of size at most 2^d−1 by taking the union of all queries. Each full assignment to this union extends a root-to-leaf restriction and uses its certified layout. Conversely a shared selector can simply be queried. With a per-cofactor rank verifier, exact depth-d discovery tests the current leaf or tries each remaining variable and requires success in both children: (2n)^d times the verification cost. A supplied decision tree takes only its size times the verifier cost. Satisfying counts combine by summing branches with their unary weights; weights of fixed variables must be included exactly once.

**Rank threshold two.** Over Q or odd characteristic, a Boolean matrix has rank ≤2 iff it has at most two distinct nonzero rows OR at most two distinct nonzero columns. For two independent basis rows, if coordinates with their patterns 10,01,11 all occur, any other binary row is αu+βv where α,β and α+β must all be bits. The only nonzero possibilities are the two basis rows. Otherwise there are at most two distinct nonzero columns. The converse is immediate; the assertion fails over F₂ where u+v may be a third binary row. For 2CNF and a fixed cut, check for three distinct nonzero semantic rows in polynomial time: introduce three prefix assignments, separate accepting suffix witnesses for each, and an additional suffix assignment for each unordered pair. Guess which of the latter pair accepts and one rejected clause on the other side (at most (2m)^3 branches). Impose all accepting copies of the formula, and pin all bits in each designated reject clause to falsify it. The resulting formula is still 2CNF. A feasible branch is exactly the required distinction certificate. Repeat on the transpose. Over F₂ one can alternatively guess an invertible Boolean 3×3 table, 3 prefix and 3 suffix assignments, and a violated clause for every zero, yielding polynomial time also (larger fixed exponent). Similar fixed-threshold minors give XP for general w. For arbitrary 3CNF even width-two semantic recognition at one cut is coNP-hard: conjoin a formula on the left with two independent cross-cut equalities; its cutrank is zero if unsatisfiable, four otherwise.

For a known tree of bounded semantic rank w, weighted counting for 2CNF is constructive: retain a representative assignment per distinct residual Boolean function at each tree node and its aggregated unary weight. Equivalence of two representative residual 2CNFs is tested by implication in both directions: falsify one clause and test the other formula for satisfiability. Combine representative pairs, test resulting residuals for equivalence, then merge. This is valid because Boolean functional equivalence survives plugging in any sibling or outside values. At most 2^w distinct binary rows occur by pivot-column projection. In particular for width two at most four residual rows need be propagated per cut. A finite menu and depth-d witness may use different trees per leaf.

**Closure.** After feasible conditioning, the prime pair incompatibilities between remaining individually unforced coordinates are induced from the original binary closure. An inclusion-minimal partial assignment that cannot extend has size ≤2: project out its complement by resolution, which preserves width two. Thus an incompatible pair plus pins must contain a conflict of size two, which can involve only the claimed endpoints because each is compatible with all pins separately. Indirect *forcing* still occurs. For example (¬z∨x) and (z∨y) entails x∨y; either choice of z forces one endpoint. In contrast, raw syntactic crossing matrices of binary clauses simply lose selected rows/columns independent of the chosen bit values, so any successful adaptive path supplies a shared selector for raw cutrank. These three notions (raw, prime, semantic truth table) differ.

**Exact separation even for binary CNF.** Take the chain x1≤...≤xn, encoded by n−1 binary clauses, and a variable tree with odd/even indices at its root cut. A feasible cofactor has exactly one contiguous free segment between its last forced zero and first forced one; all other bits are fixed, and with ℓ free bits it has ℓ+1 threshold assignments. When ℓ≤2w−1, each cut has some shore containing at most w−1 free bits, hence at most w distinct threshold projections, so every rank ≤w. For ℓ≥2w, fix outside 2w consecutive free positions consistently. Their alternating root shores each have w+1 threshold patterns; the matrix has its diagonal and one neighboring diagonal equal to one and no other nonzeros. Its rank is w+1 in every field. Thus a shared selector must hit each length-2w interval and every such hitting set works; the minimum is floor(n/(2w)). An adaptive leaf can cover at most 2w thresholds; median-position binary queries achieve this bound with exact optimal depth max(0,ceil(log₂((n+1)/(2w)))). This provides a linear-versus-logarithmic gap for w=1 or 2 without long clauses. Other tree choices can change the w=2 optimum.
