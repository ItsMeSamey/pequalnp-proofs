# Full-precision direct projection for planar two-product tensors

Expand the two product terms per vertex into a spin. Contract each edge conditional on both endpoint modes into a binary table Q. For positive rational entries write q=Q00, x²=Q10 Q01/(Q00 Q11), t²=Q10 Q11/(Q00 Q01), u²=Q01 Q11/(Q00 Q10). Positive square roots give Q(a,b)=q x^[a≠b] t^a u^b. Accumulate H_v from h_v(1)/h_v(0) times all row-end t and column-end u factors. The constant C is the product of q and h_v(0). Zero-field is H_v²=1, an exact rational test. The cross-ratio uniquely fixes positive x and the endpoint entries fix t,u. Edge biases may cancel at a vertex. Fields with H_v²≠1 can be covered by faces.

No multiquadratic compositum is required. The product D of denominators of every input entry is a common denominator for the partition. With B total input bits each assignment has absolute weight at most 2^B and D≤2^B. Round each radical to a positive dyadic with relative error 2^(-4(B+n+ceil(log2(m+n+2))+2)); C stays rational. Each summand uses at most m+n factors. Product relative error and summing over 2^n assignments yields absolute error below 1/(2D). Compute the partition of the rounded model exactly and round after multiplying by D. Intermediate signed sums are exact. Computing H as a single square root of its rational square keeps bit complexity polynomial.

For signed, zero or jet entries use supplied symmetric edge tables or a checked decomposition. In a connected plane graph with k>0 supplied covering faces add one ghost in each and assign every field once via a spoke weighted h_v(0),h_v(1) for equal,unequal. Fix the first ghost to zero. Assignments with this anchor correspond bijectively to primal cuts, equivalently dual even subgraphs. For every other ghost project onto even cut parity on its path to the anchor. Fourier expansion uses 2^(k-1) ordinary weighted cut sums, flipping the unequal weights along selected paths. Divide by 2^(k-1) with that many extra binary digits in the exponent of precision, i.e. k-1 guard bits, on consistent integer polynomial lifts. Components without ghosts contribute twice their cut sum. This treats zero and signed weights directly.

A planar gadget for ternary even parity consists of three external vertices forming a triangle and a central vertex adjacent to each with unit weight. Assign triangle edge weights 1,0,0. Deleting any two ports leaves a single matching, deleting none gives weight one and odd deletions give zero. Chain gates using unique prefix parities to realize an arbitrary even gate in cyclic order. A length-three path joining ports encodes arbitrary weights on selected/unselected edges: outer-edge weights w1,1 and middle weight w0. FKT computes the weighted perfect matching sum. Lift jet coefficients and use degree-bounded integer polynomial interpolation or a division-free Pfaffian. The common sign of oriented matchings is determined from one matching of the underlying unweighted graph.

Failure controls: a triangle of equality edges with two positive pins assigned to distinct faces has unrestricted augmented partition 2, pinned partition 1. Naively dividing by four misses the two-ghost insertion. On a cube with a positive nonunit field at each vertex and symmetric unit interactions no single face covers the field support. This diagnoses the test, without implying hardness of the cube.
