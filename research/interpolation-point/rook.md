# Full partial-matching polynomial of aligned Cauchy packets

Use m fully occupied packets of a repeated legal type as in program.md, distinct distinguished row slots x_i and aligned distinct column slots y_j. Pin inactive columns, freely sum other rows and neutralize unused packets. Use distinguished unary entries [1,1+V], exact coefficientwise canonical lifts even at P₀=1; ℓ≥m+1 retains every order. Column j's m bits are constrained by one genuine exactly-one or at-most-one Boolean tensor. For an unmarked numeric alternative use [1,2] at P₀≥2 (the multiplicity of a private Boolean flag b subject to b⇒Y). Degrees m≤L≤Δ fit the supplied packing.

The row sum is 1+V Σ_j W_ij X_ij, W_ij=N(T)/(ρ_i+σ_j), ρ_i=T+x_i and σ_j=−y_j. In the product expansion chosen rows pick distinct columns. For a specified h-matching the unchosen m−h columns have independently K=m possible placements (or m+1 for at most one). Thus F(T,V)=Σ_h V^h K^(m−h) N(T)^h R_h(C), where R_h is the sum of all h-edge matching products of the complete Cauchy array C_ij=1/(ρ_i+σ_j). This gives the full packet scalar; no diagonal projection is needed. For numeric row entries [1,2] evaluate the V-polynomial at one before any jet truncation.

At an integer T>2L append k copies of a virtual row and column, both from a positive parameter R larger than all real parameters in magnitude. Their edge weights are a_i=1/(ρ_i+R), b_j=1/(R+σ_j), c=1/(2R). Write S_j(R)=Σ_{|I|=|J|=j}a_I b_J per(C[complement I,complement J]), taking products on subscript sets. The augmented permanent is Σ_{j≤k}(k!)² c^(k−j) S_j/(k−j)!, since the two injections into the dummy sets contribute (k)_j² choices and remaining dummy pairings contribute (k−j)!. Solve the triangular system k=0,...,m over Q. With D_R=∏(R+ρ_i)∏(R+σ_j), the polynomial D_R S_j has degree ≤2(m−j) and its coefficient of that degree is R_(m−j)(C). Recover all these coefficients by sampling 2m+1 distinct R.

To evaluate an augmented permanent with coincident parameters, perturb the dummy row/column parameters to R+i s and R+j s with positive distinct indices on each shore. At positive s all row parameters and all column parameters are separately distinct. Use det(C∘C)/det C over Q. Multiply by the product of all entry denominators; this gives a polynomial in s of degree ≤(m+k)². Interpolate its value at zero, then divide by the nonzero denominator product there. [Borchardt identity, Singer](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v11i1r48).

Reconstruct F over Q[T,V] from m(2L−1)+1 integer T samples (degree bound from original W). All three interpolation parameters have polynomial magnitude and all loops have polynomial length; rational denominator products of polynomially many polynomial-size or polynomial-bit factors have polynomial bit size. Determinants have order ≤2m, and permutation sums add O(m log m) bits beyond cleared products. Use fraction-free rational elimination for the triangular system. Multiply by N^h before specializing to singular T. Then shift/evaluate the reconstructed polynomial at source T=t+αζ and reduce the integral shifted coefficients at p_t in the prescribed ring (equivalently retain sufficient guard bits for pre-cleared denominators). No factorial, confluent determinant, or nonunit is inverted in a truncated 2-adic ring. Arbitrary relifted intermediate tensors must independently satisfy the actual lifted entry identities.

A genuine counterexample to simply masking Borchardt is the availability matrix [[1,1,0],[0,1,1],[1,0,1]] on three packets/columns. Its diagonal and three-cycle contribute positive products A and B at T>2L. The masked permanent is A+B, whereas the masked squared-determinant ratio is (A²+B²)/(A+B). Such a mask is installed by pinning corresponding column slots zero; at top occupancy the unused-column multiplicity plays no role. This is a counterexample to that proposed identity, not a lower bound for every mask. For arbitrary masks the earlier program.md gives a separate #P-hard intermediate.

## Single determinant for the full packet scalar

A shorter direct algorithm replaces all virtual-border work above:

    det(z C + C∘C)/det C = Σ_h z^(m−h) R_h(C).

To prove it work over the rational function field in independently variable row and column parameters. At a hyperplane ρ_i+σ_j=0 the numerator has at most a double pole and the Cauchy determinant in the denominator a simple pole. Its quotient has at most a simple pole; cofactor expansion shows its residue equals the analogous determinant quotient on deletion of i,j. The rook polynomial has precisely the corresponding residue, since only matchings using that edge contribute, leaving a matching of the smaller array with the same z exponent. Apply induction. Other candidate poles from division by the Cauchy determinant occur only at equal row or equal column parameters, by the explicit Cauchy determinant formula. The numerator is alternating there, so those divisors cancel. Thus the difference is a polynomial in the ρ,σ parameters coefficientwise in z. At each z^(m−h) it is homogeneous of degree −h under uniform scaling, hence zero for h>0. At h=0 both sides have leading coefficient one. The case m=0 starts the induction.

Homogenization gives F(T,V)=det(K C + V N(T)(C∘C))/det C, without dividing by V. At safe rational T, det C is nonzero and independent of V. Compute the numerator in Q[V] (or interpolate its degree-m polynomial) and then reconstruct in T as above. This is the preferred algorithm, with the confluent proof providing a second derivation. No priority claim is made for the underlying rational identity.

An actual equality-tensor obstruction: take two packets with distinguished rows pinned to one, two aligned live columns, and impose X_ij=x_j across packets by ordinary Boolean COPY tensors. Summing over the two variable bits gives per(W)+2W_11 W_21+2W_12 W_22. The repeated-column terms survive at safe T and at t=ζ=1 with the same-parity slot choices and adequate source precision. COPY does not enforce the exactly-one premise. This explicitly refutes substituting SAT variable equality into the matching determinant without further correction.
