# Ordered cutrank and arbitrary clauses

For binary clauses use the SCC algorithm in twocnf.md with w the largest cutrank of the supplied topological order. Runtime is 2^O(w³)poly(N). To find an order use [Nagamochi, Theorem 1](https://www.amp.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/tecrep/ps_file/2012/2012-004.pdf) on rank A[U,V−U] plus (w+1) times the number of entering precedence arcs. Its width-w prefixes are exactly feasible ideals of bounded rank. The rank term is submodular because the span of (e_i,A[:,i]) for i in U has dimension |U|+rank A[V−U,U]. Subtract a modular function and restrict to unions of SCCs. The theorem gives deterministic n^(2w+O(1)) recognition with polynomial space, an XP guarantee.

Multiply transition weights by all translated unary weights in the SCC over a commutative semiring. Track the XOR sum of the SCC markers, each the XOR of its original variable columns, correcting the target for complements and fixed variables. The overhead is 2^s where s is the aggregated binary rank.

For any CNF with supplied variable order, build at each cut the binary matrix M[P,Q] recording which past/future falsifying literal patterns form a crossing clause. A surviving prefix's residual forbidden patterns are the support union of selected rows. With rank w there are at most 2^w distinct rows and a support union is determined by at most w spanning rows. Store each residual set directly, restrict it at the next variable, reject a forbidden empty pattern and append newly crossing clauses at their first variable. The resulting weighted count costs 2^O(w²+s)poly(N). The SCC layout recognition does not imply order recognition for this second parameter. Related work: [precisely satisfiable sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6485).

Cubic-only rank cannot suffice: each antipodal NAE triple contributes xyz+(1−x)(1−y)(1−z), whose cubic part vanishes, yet 2-coloring linear 3-uniform 4-regular hypergraphs is NP-complete. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03395). Independent checks verified the quadratic identity on 6,400 assignments.
