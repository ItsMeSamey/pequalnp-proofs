# Transfer through the interpolation algorithm's parity tests

Take a simple constant-degree n-vertex edge expander G. For each edge uv use the clause (¬x_u OR ¬x_v). The Θ(n) clauses form an antichain. As σ tends to zero the sparsification threshold M tends to infinity, since Γh≤σM. Every nonempty proper core of a binary edge clause occurs at most Δ times. The root is therefore a leaf for large n.

Each edge violation is a distinct squarefree quadratic monomial x_u x_v over F₂. The permanent Tanner row matrix has kernel minimum nonzero weight at least m/P. Since P is polylogarithmic and m=Θ(n), every edge column is nonzero for large n. Choose a permanent row f(e) containing each edge. No other edge produces the same quadratic, so both endpoints lie in that row factor's scope. Appended candidate rows preserve all permanent rows.

The sparse counter restricts ≤n/√log₂ n high-incidence variables S. On the all-zero branch no constant-one equation can appear, and every free-free edge remains a distinct monomial in its permanent row. All surviving equations are sums of homogeneous quadratics. Every remaining edge therefore still belongs to a nonzero factor. If the algorithm takes its exhaustive fallback, it already visits 2^n assignments.

At a point with r≥4 on n'=n−|S| free variables, each group has size ≤2n'/r≤n/2. Sum the original edge-expansion inequality over groups. Edges meeting S contribute ≤Δ|S|. Thus at least (ηn'−Δ|S|)/2=Ω(n) free-free edges cross groups. Each has at least one endpoint mismatching the target of its permanent row f(e). For each free variable choose its incident edge factor with largest mismatch count across all points. Its original graph degree is bounded by Δ, hence the selected variables have Ω(nQ) total mismatches, even when distinct edges use the same row. Isolated vertices can be omitted. This is the premise of layout-group-budget.md. For r<4 a largest group already has Ω(n) variables.

If R/Q is bounded below, the actual precision budget d_H=kR(m_test+2(ℓ−1)) with ℓ=n+1 gives q=floor(d_H/Q)=Ω(n) and hence 2^{Ω(n)} correction pairs. Otherwise the same-point streaming obstruction applies. Varying the layout alone inside the specified interpolation pipeline therefore cannot produce polynomial time on this family, even after pruning every unattainable measurement tuple. This leaves open different preprocessing, aggregation of measurement fibers or correction sets, and the sharper separate algorithm in the revised manuscript. The formula has a straightforward satisfying assignment; its model count is the number of independent sets of G.
