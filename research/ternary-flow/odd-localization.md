# Odd-characteristic scalar conservation localizes near a fixed marker

Consider native binary guards x_i(b_i XOR XOR_{j in N_i}x_j)=0. Absorb self coefficients into b_i so i is absent from N_i. Let V_i be the Boolean 0/1 violation indicator interpreted in an odd-characteristic field K. Suppose F=sum_i lambda_i V_i depends only on coordinates in T. Then F has the same representation after discarding all rows except those centered in T or among its outgoing neighbors. The retained row supports are contained in T together with its direct neighbors. An exact scalar conservation certificate for a fixed bounded-width marker in bounded-degree guarded syntax therefore certifies a bounded-size inconsistent subsystem.

Every Boolean function has a unique multilinear polynomial over K. Let c_i=lambda_i(-1)^b_i. In the polynomial

    lambda_i V_i = (lambda_i/2) x_i (1 - (-1)^b_i product_{j in N_i} (1-2x_j))

the coefficient of x_i x_j for j in N_i equals c_i. The coefficient of x_i x_j x_k for distinct neighbors j,k equals -2c_i. Call row i active if c_i is nonzero. For every pair {i,j} not contained in T the coefficient of x_i x_j vanishes. Thus an arc i→j from an active row in such a pair requires a reverse arc and c_j=-c_i.

Suppose an active i outside T has a neighbor j outside T and a different neighbor k. Both links must be reciprocal and c_j=c_k=-c_i. Any arc between j and k would require reciprocity and c_j=-c_k, since j is outside T. This is impossible in odd characteristic. Thus the coefficient of x_i x_j x_k equals -2c_i from row i alone, contradicting its vanishing. Both i and j consequently have precisely each other as outgoing neighbors. No other active row can contain either bit, by the same pair condition. Their total contribution is independent of i,j since F is, and evaluates to zero at i=j=0. Discard both rows. An active outside vertex with no neighbors contributes at most a multiple of its own variable, and no other active row can involve it. Its contribution is therefore zero. All remaining active outside vertices have a neighbor in T and all their neighbors in T. These links are reciprocal, so their centers occur among the outgoing neighbors of T. Rows centered in T involve only direct neighbors. This proves the assertion.

For a single marker with Boolean violation function V_M on T, a refutation identity 1=lambda_M V_M+sum_i lambda_i V_i has F supported as above. For every assignment on the surviving coordinates this identity still holds. Hence the bounded subsystem is inconsistent. Its variable count is at most |T|(1+max_i |N_i|); it has a correspondingly bounded-degree Boolean PC or SA refutation by exhaustive truth-table partition. No charge variation or gadget uniformity is required.

As a second corollary, if F is affine in all inputs, its every pair and triple coefficient vanishes. Applying the same reciprocal-link and triple argument globally shows each active row either has no neighbors or belongs to a mutually linked pair whose vertices have no other outgoing neighbors. Affine odd-field conservation from scalar sums of actual native failure indicators involves only these local parts. Nonconstant multipliers and inequalities require separate analysis.
