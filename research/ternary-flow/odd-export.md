# Odd-order Tseitin after clamping a local configuration

Orient a connected graph G on n vertices, with charges b in F3 of nonzero total and boundary map B from edge flows to vertex charges. Use symbols in F3 and Boolean one-hot bits per edge. For |S|<hn/2, sample the marginal on S of a uniform flow satisfying all charges except a defect at any vertex in the giant of G−S. Choice of defect leaves the law unchanged by a path translation. These distributions project consistently by nesting. All assertions below concern the direct one-hot Tseitin presentation; transfer to a different guard encoding requires a degree-bounded simulation and a witness for any auxiliary coordinates.

Let c have positive mass on S. The system with z_S=c has no GF2-PC refutation of degree d (Boolean one-hot, polynomial presentation of local parity with bounded scope) whenever removing |S|+3(d+1)+Δ edges always leaves a giant of size >n/2, and Δ≤d+1 or the Fourier degree budget is enlarged to Δ. Here is a direct proof, avoiding an expansion-pruning lemma.

Extend scalars to GF4 and use ω of order three. The functions χ_a(z)=ω^(a·z), for a in F3^E, form a basis. Every one-hot bit expands in frequencies on its own edge. Degree-d polynomials have Fourier supports on frequency vectors with at most d nonzeros. Set t=max(d+1,Δ). Require |S|+3t+Δ<hn/2. Group frequencies of weight at most t by their coset modulo im(Bᵀ)+F3^S. For a pair a,a' in one block choose a−a'=Bᵀu+w with w supported on S. Normalize u to be zero on the giant of G minus S and the support of a−a'. This is possible because u is constant there. Label the pair by ω^(u·b+w·c).

Any other normalized decomposition differs by (v,−Bᵀv) with Bᵀv supported on S and v zero on the giant of G−S: it is constant there, and vanishes at a vertex of that giant by nesting. A full defect flow z extending c exists with its defect in this giant, so v·b−(Bᵀv)·c=0, where the second pairing is on S. The label is therefore well defined. For three degree-t frequencies, delete S and the union of their three supports (at most |S|+3t edges). The common giant is nested in every pair giant. The two summed potentials vanish there, hence also on the appropriate pair giant for their sum, since the sum is constant on that pair giant. This proves the product law for labels.

Map each basis character to a vector on its block with scalar coefficient its label relative to a fixed representative. This linear map sends 1 to a nonzero vector. Its kernel is spanned by pair differences weighted by pair labels, separately within each block. Multiplication by any single edge character translates all frequencies, preserving group differences and labels. Thus when translating a polynomial of Fourier weight at most d, its kernel membership is preserved, using t≥d+1 on the target. Multiplying by a one-hot bit is a linear combination of such translations. Formal PC multiplication starts with polynomial degree at most d−1, or uses the usual non-increasing degree distinction before multilinearization; under either ordinary rule all intermediate translations fit this budget.

The one-hot domain axioms vanish identically as functions. A clamped bit equality vanishes in the map by the pair labels for frequencies differing on its clamped edge (take u=0). For a vertex star, group the local Fourier expansion by translates of multiples of Bᵀ1_v. Within each orbit these differ locally by the phases dictated by b_v: the potentials j1_v are normalized since the giant avoids v when the nonzero star is removed. Any function vanishing on the correct star has zero phase-weighted coefficient sum per orbit: this is Fourier independence after restricting the characters to the charge hyperplane. Distinct local orbits that coalesce in the larger quotient cause no difficulty. This proves the claimed robust PC lower bound.

# All-equation export for the direct one-hot presentation

Export each original GF2-PC consequence as the direct forbidden-assignment CNF on its support, with clauses interpreted as sums of mismatch literals ≥1. For level-k SA condition on its multiplier of ≤k literals by refining to full symbols on S, |S|≤k. The charge distributions define all necessary moments. Conditional on one positive-probability refinement c, any nonbridge edge in G−S is uniform by translating its cycle by an arbitrary group element, which keeps c and the defect fixed. This argument works for any one-edge marginal, using a defect in the giant after the further deletion. Every mismatch literal of such an edge therefore has expectation at least 1/3. Three such occurrences already give clause validity, even on repeated edge scopes.

Suppose there are fewer than three such occurrences. Assume G contains two edge-disjoint spanning trees. Removing S followed by all B' bridges of G−S produces q blocks. At least 2(q−1) original edges cross these blocks, and at most |S|+B' cross. Since B'≤q−1, also B'≤|S|. Thus the entire clause is on at most 2k+2 edges together with the conditioning, and has a joint marginal there. Each configuration in its support retains the PC lower bound proved above (substitute |S|≤2k+2 in the bound). If any configuration falsified this clause, clamping that configuration in the original consequence derivation gives a refutation, since the polynomial consequence is a nonzero constant under the clamp. This contradiction proves the inequality pointwise in this joint marginal. Averaging proves SA feasibility for all exports simultaneously, including addition of the original true domain axioms and literal box bounds.

Precisely, it suffices that 2k+2+3max(d+1,Δ)+Δ<hn/2, with k+1 also inside the local law. The result concerns arbitrary quadratic GF2-PC consequences when d=2 and applies to general degree d as well. The direct one-hot Tseitin system does not itself supply a deficiency-one guarded binary presentation. To obtain bounded-degree multigraph expanders carrying the two trees, add the edges of two Hamiltonian paths as separate parallel-edge layers to a bounded-degree expander.

# General bounded-local Boolean decoding; no spanning trees needed

Let each network bit be a Boolean function of at most w incident or otherwise specified source edges (support need not be geometrically contiguous). Assume that every original axiom after this substitution has bounded edge scope and vanishes on the local law there. Let a PC derivation use degree d. In the Fourier proof replace the character-weight budget by max((d+1)w, largest decoded axiom scope, w, Δ). A multiplication by a decoded bit is a linear combination of translations by characters of weight at most w; translation again preserves pair differences exactly. It suffices to allow an extra w in the budget when degree is charged after Boolean reduction.

For a local function f on a set J within the Fourier budget that vanishes throughout the conditional positive support on J given z_S=c, its Fourier transform has zero normal form. Indeed choose a defect in the giant of G−(S∪J) and a full flow extending c with that defect. The admissible joint values on J are the projection of this affine space of flows. Two characters on J have equal restrictions up to scalar precisely when their difference lies in im(Bᵀ)+F3^S (orthogonal complement of the flow directions vanishing on S). Their scalar ratio equals the normalized pair label: the potential is zero at the chosen defect by nesting. Hence canceling coefficients in the normal form is exactly Fourier cancellation on the projected affine space. Relative to GF4 the characteristic does not collapse this independence because the group order is a power of three. In particular any number of additional bit pins that are individually certain in their conditional local marginals all vanish in the same map; no simultaneous distribution on all their supports is required.

For SA, refine any multiplier assignment to full edge values c on at most kw edges. A mismatch literal on at most w edges has either zero conditional probability or probability at least 3^(−w), because its local conditional law is uniform on an affine projection of dimension ≤w. If at least 3^w literal occurrences have positive mismatch probability their expectation sum suffices. Otherwise reveal all edge scopes of these occurrences, augmenting S by at most w(3^w−1) edge values. For each positive-mass refinement, all previously zero mismatch events remain zero by nested projection. If every newly revealed literal also matched its forbidden value, every assignment pin specified by the exported clause would be individually certain in the same conditional Fourier map. Add them all as axioms to the PC derivation. From a derived polynomial false on these pins one obtains 1 algebraically at degree at most max(d,1): telescope each monomial against its evaluation, using monomial multiples of the pin equations. All axioms, including the new pins, vanished in that map, contradiction. Consequently each refinement has at least one mismatch almost surely and averaging proves the clause inequality. This argument also justifies simultaneous SA feasibility for all exports in the arbitrary bounded-local decoding setting.

For instance require kw+w(3^w−1)+3t+Δ<hn/2, where t is the enlarged character budget above. All constants other than k,d depend only on the fixed arities. This is a conditional transfer theorem for deficiency-one guarded networks: an explicit locally valid bounded-scope decoding from odd-order Tseitin into the network is required.
