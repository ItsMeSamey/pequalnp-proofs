# Physical construction at the charged-input floor

The expression compiler in ShoreCompiler.lean uses the list-of-gates compiler and the wire simulation from the project's PEqualNP.Library.Helpers.TableauExpr. ShoreConstruction.lean specializes that compiler to binary monotone expressions. An expression with k leaves emits exactly k−1 gates. Its output may be an original raw wire when k=1. The finite-family OR constructor uses no identity gate for an empty summand.

ShoreBankCover.lean gives a sufficient partition certificate: nonempty terms are universal row literals, universal column literals and rectangles with both shores nonempty. Their costs in leaves are one per universal literal and the sum of shore cardinalities for each rectangle. The certificate transfers to the original physical representation when these terms cover exactly the matrix and their weight equals its charged-input count.

ShoreComponents.lean constructs that decomposition from the matrix itself. The distinct nonempty residual column sets index the boxes. The row shore of a box is the fiber of its column set among nonuniversal charged rows. Equal-or-disjointness makes the column shores disjoint. Counting each fiber and the disjoint union of the column shores shows the total leaf cost is exactly the charged count. The hypotheses include an original positive cell and an original negative cell. The positive cell ensures there is an output term; the negative cell makes every universal flag charged. Empty residuals, singleton blocks and raw outputs are covered by the same construction.
