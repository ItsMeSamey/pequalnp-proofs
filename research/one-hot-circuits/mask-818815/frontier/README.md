# Monotone fan-in-two size of 4×6 promise mask 818815

With truth-bit index 6r+c the four row masks are 63,57,7,3 and the six column masks are 15,13,5,3,3,3. Each row has a 1 where another row has a 0, and likewise for each column. If its indicator were unused, its opposite-shore-fixed input would be coordinatewise ≤ every other input on that shore, so monotonicity would force its pattern to be a subset of every other pattern. Thus every one of the ten raw indicators must be used. This test uses distinct raw indicators even for the three equal columns.

At most nine binary gates connecting all ten indicators to one observer have zero spare fanout: each gate combines two inputs into one and there must be at least nine merges. Hence such a circuit has exactly nine gates, all gates and raw inputs have one outgoing occurrence, and the network is a read-once formula.

frontier.cpp directly examines all 24 original promise cells. Its start state contains the ten labeled-indicator truth masks. Each branch takes two available functions and computes their conjunction or disjunction. Consuming them at their last use, or retaining either for later, describes every live topological order. A frontier of k live values needs ≥k−1 remaining mergers, so retention is impossible at the nine-gate cap. The same retention options permit positive controls at larger caps. A constant or already available truth function is unnecessary in a least-size circuit by propagation or substitution. An indistinguishable positive and negative cell at a frontier can never subsequently be separated. For each positive cell the code intersects a 24-bit candidate mask of negative cells with each equality condition; this enforces all 135 pair distinctions without a 64-bit pair ceiling. Identical sorted frontiers and budgets have equivalent completions.

Baseline call arguments are mask, binary budget and starting flags. Flags 0..3 are rows, 4..9 columns, and optional bit 10 is the OR of raw columns 3,4,5. Raw starting mask 1023 and budget 9 give UNSAT; the same executable recovers a 10-gate physical witness. Independent comparison on quotient starting mask 1151 gives UNSAT at 7 and SAT at 8, with two gates to construct the merged column indicator. Only the direct raw search and raw witness are needed for the size-ten conclusion. Compile with a C++17 compiler, replay the four keys in result.log, and run verify.py for scalar validation of the positive witnesses and the essential-flag test.
