# The monotone two-input gate minimum for mask 818815 is ten

On the promise that exactly one row and one column are active, the neighborhoods of rows 0 through 3 are {0,1,2,3,4,5}, {0,3,4,5}, {0,1,2}, and {0,1}. The ten-gate upper circuit accepts the disjunction of row 0, column 0, row 1 with a column in {3,4,5}, column 1 with a row in {2,3}, and row 2 with column 2. Its physical gate list appears in baseline10.json and Mask818815Upper.lean.

For the lower bound, an unread row in a monotone circuit would have a neighborhood contained in every row neighborhood. Indeed, the only read raw already true on its incident edge is the column flag. Turning on any other row preserves acceptance. No row neighborhood is contained in the intersection {0}. Likewise no column neighborhood is contained in the common intersection {0} of all column neighborhoods. Thus all ten raw wires must occur in the output cone.

An output cone with ten source wires and at most nine binary gates must be a read-once tree with exactly nine binary gates. This follows by counting edges in the connected underlying graph; repeated uses or a lower-arity gate would leave too few distinct edges. Restrict all other raw flags to zero, retaining rows 2 and 3 and columns 1 and 2. Each of these four singleton inputs is rejected: its stripe has a zero on the original promise and the circuit is monotone. The pairs r2c1, r2c2 and r3c1 are accepted, and r3c2 is rejected. Constant folding leaves a read-once AND/OR tree; every retained flag is essential since it participates in an accepted pair whose deletion leaves a rejecting singleton.

If the root were OR, each required positive pair would have to lie in one child, since if split, both children see at most one active flag. Those three pairs form a connected graph on all four flags, so no partition into two nonempty children works. If the root were AND, each positive pair would have to span the partition, since its other child would otherwise be zero. The three positive edges force the unique partition into rows versus columns. The positives r3c1 and r2c2 then require the row child to accept r3 and the column child to accept c2, contradicting the missing pair. The reasoning places no conditions on the two same-shore pairs.

The formal lower proof in L_meta_23368.lean adapts the published L_meta_10189 finite read-once separation checker to four rows, six columns, the new mask, and zero spare gates. Both source files import PEqualNP.Defs.
