For any k ≥ 1, form one cluster of eight guard variables g_j and k clusters of eight private variables x_ij, with j modulo eight. In each cluster z include all 16 implications z_j → z_{j+d}, d=1,2. For each private cluster i add a bridge x_i0 → g_0 and a target clause (x_i0 ∨ x_i3). This is connected 2-CNF of size O(k).

To restrict the attainable violation vectors, set all guards true and all variables in private cluster i equal to 1−t_i. Every implication is satisfied. The k target clauses have violation indicators exactly t_1,...,t_k, independently. Thus any real polynomial in the clause-violation indicators that represents satisfaction on all assignments restricts to ∏(1−t_i) on the Boolean cube. Its unique multilinear representative has nonzero full monomial, so its degree is at least k. Multilinear reduction cannot increase degree.

All supports are distinct pairs, so there are no units, duplicates or subsumptions. Every variable has at least two occurrences of either polarity from internal implications, and the incidence graph has minimum degree two. For each literal of any clause, two internal implications contain its complement with distinct other variables. At least one of these variables differs from the original clause's second variable. Resolving there yields a non-tautological clause, so the original clause is not blocked. No listed elimination can take a first step. Equivalence substitution is outside this claim. The restriction is a coordinate face of the attainable violation vectors, using a diagonal subcube of the variable assignments.

An actual input coordinate face gives a smaller example. Use two disjoint eight-variable guard clusters A and B with the same internal implication edges, and k individual private variables y_i. For each i add the four implications A_0 → y_i, A_1 → y_i, y_i → B_0, y_i → B_1, plus the target (y_i ∨ A_2). Fix A entirely false and B entirely true; all guards and bridges are satisfied, and the target violations are 1−y_i. This has k+16 variables and 5k+32 clauses. The two distinct complement witnesses property still holds for every literal: guards use their internal implications, and y_i uses its two incoming or two outgoing implications. Thus the same connectedness and elimination guarantees apply, and the exact polynomial degree is at least k even on a coordinate face of input assignments.
