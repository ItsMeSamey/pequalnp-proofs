For n divisible by 4 take the lexicographically first 40-tuple of permutations of [n] whose undirected union graph joins every two disjoint n/4-sets by an edge. Discard loops and duplicate edges. This explicit exhaustive construction has maximum degree D ≤ 80. It exists because a uniform permutation maps no element of a fixed n/4-set into another fixed disjoint n/4-set with probability at most (3/4)^(n/4). Independent permutations and the union bound give failure probability at most 3^n exp(−40n/16)<1.

Use the monotone 2-CNF with clause u ∨ v for each edge. In any cut with each side at least n/3, greedily select a crossing edge and remove its endpoints and their neighbors. Each step removes at most 2(D+1) vertices. While both sides retain n/4 vertices a further edge exists. Thus every such cut has an induced crossing matching of size t ≥ n/[24(D+1)].

Take C to contain exactly the crossing clauses. Set all nonmatching variables true. All nonmatching clauses of C now hold. For the matching clauses set the left endpoints false and let the right endpoints vary freely. This realizes the coordinate face of the violation vectors on which matching bits vary independently and all other bits vanish. Hence a polynomial representing satisfaction of C in its violation bits has degree at least t: on that face the unique multilinear representative is the product of (1−c_i). A univariate polynomial testing whether the total number of crossing violations is zero likewise has degree at least t. With both endpoints free the crossing satisfaction matrix contains the t-fold tensor power of [[0,1],[1,1]], of rank 2^t over the reals.

Optional conditioning: fix at most n/[48(D+1)] variables S with no fixed zero-zero edge. Exclude N[S] during matching selection at a cost of at most n/48 vertices. If the cut is balanced on the remaining variables, each side initially has at least (n−|S|)/3. The bound on matching size becomes ((n−|S|)/3−n/48−n/4)/[2(D+1)], still linear. Set all other unfixed nonmatching variables true; fixed zero vertices have no matching neighbors. All clauses off the matching are satisfied. Restrictions with a fixed zero-zero crossing edge instead give a constant zero satisfaction function.

This proves an exact degree barrier on every balanced cut of the original variables for these particular violation coordinates. It does not imply a runtime lower bound. The permutation search need not be efficient.

The same graph directly obstructs the adaptive component criterion. Answer every query true. At most b vertices disappear and the residual monotone formula stays satisfiable, so no sound refutation can close this branch. If every remaining component had at most 2n/3 vertices and b<n/12, one can group them into two vertex sets of at least n/4 each. If some component has size ≥n/4 take it as the first group; the rest have size at least n/3−b. Otherwise accumulate whole components until the group has size between n/4 and n/2, leaving more than n/4. No edge joins the groups, contradicting the construction. Thus even b growing linearly below n/12 cannot certify a balanced decomposition in the adaptive model of hidden.md.
