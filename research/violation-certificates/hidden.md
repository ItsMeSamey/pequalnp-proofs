# Locating a bounded-degree interaction without its cut

Fix clause width k and degree d. Suppose some partition X|Y with both shores of size at most 2n/3 has a crossing formula C with satisfaction indicator g equal to one minus a degree-d polynomial in its nonempty clause-violation monomials. Composing with literal products gives assignment degree q≤kd. If q>0, g depends on at most t=q·2^(q−1) variables by Nisan–Szegedy. For q=0 take the empty support. Indeed total influence is at most q by Parseval, and each relevant variable has influence at least 2^(1−q), since its nonzero discrete derivative has degree at most q−1. A nonzero multilinear polynomial of degree j is nonzero on a fraction at least 2^(−j), by induction using a nonzero derivative and pairing its two extensions.

**Recovering an equivalent factor.** Enumerate candidate supports of size ≤t and all their Boolean truth tables g. Form D from all clauses of F implied by g. For each accepting support pin a clause must contain a satisfied pinned literal, after tautology removal. Fit 1−g by nonempty violation monomials of D through degree d using ordinary unweighted squared residual. Mixed moments with g are computed by summing over support pins and intersecting clause-falsification cubes. Zero residual certifies equivalence: g implies all clauses by construction, and whenever all clauses in D hold every feature vanishes, forcing g=1. For the true g, D contains C and the known-to-exist crossing polynomial is an available fit. All clauses outside D lie on their original shores. Group primal components outside D, including isolated variables, into balanced shores by subset-sum on component sizes. The true guess passes, and any accepted guess yields a valid factorization. Recovered clauses may differ from the original crossing set.

**Direct counting decomposition.** Enumerate supports S of size ≤t and all pins. Accept each branch if all residual components have at most 2n/3 variables or if a degree-d linear combination of the residual formula’s nonempty violation monomials equals one identically. Test the latter by unweighted squared residual; it certifies unsatisfiability. An empty clause is immediate. Accept S only if all pins pass. At the true support, accepting pins for g clear every crossing clause. Rejecting pins specialize the hidden polynomial to an identically-one refutation over the residual clauses. A satisfied clause makes its feature zero; a fully falsified clause is already an empty-clause certificate.

The direct test may instead enumerate adaptive binary branching trees of depth at most b=k²d using the same two leaf certificates. Try variables recursively, requiring both children to pass. A witness tree exists: choose a remaining crossing clause and query all its at most k variables per round. Every monomial of maximum positive assignment degree meets this clause, since its falsifying pin gives constant zero whereas a disjoint top-degree coefficient would survive. Each round drops the degree until g is constant. A constant-true leaf clears the crossing clauses. A constant-false leaf has the specialized degree-d refutation. The witness proof can use the unknown cut; verification and search do not. The search uses (2n)^{O(b)} polynomial-cost leaf tests. See fixedwidth.md and hidden-cut-audit.md for the depth bound and a distinct one-sided promised variant.

At each successful tree sum across pins, multiplying component counts, with isolated free variables included. For a full polynomial algorithm assume every nontrivial connected restriction that can arise admits such a balanced witness. At any round, component variable counts summed across all descendant leaves total at most 2^b times the parent size. Depth is O(log n), so total variable mass examined is polynomial for fixed b, even if nodes split into unboundedly many components. Terminal empty instances may be charged to their parent. A single good interface gives only a reduction to its sides.

On a bounded-degree monotone edge expander, the all-ones path of any fixed-depth search leaves a satisfiable formula and a connected component larger than 2n/3 for sufficiently large n. Otherwise the removal of a constant number of vertices gives a balanced separation up to a constant number of vertices, contradicting edge expansion and bounded degree. Thus the adaptive criterion also has an explicit obstruction. See barrier.md for a quantitative crossing-degree obstruction.

The scripts verify.py, structural_verify.py and hidden_verify.py test 7,652 formula-degree pairs, 16 structural parameter pairs and 1,450 eligible formula-cut pairs respectively. The last script checks factor recovery at the true g, equivalence, component balancing and exact counting on four variables.

[The reference counter](adaptive.py) returns an exact count when it finds a certified tree and returns None when its search budget proves insufficient. Its finite suite compares all accepted counts with direct enumeration.
