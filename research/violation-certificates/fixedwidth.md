# A shallow decision tree under the fixed-width promise

Suppose the satisfaction indicator of a width-k CNF is given pointwise by a polynomial of degree at most d in the clause-violation bits, or is promised to admit one. Substituting a product of at most k literal indicators for each violation bit and multilinearizing gives exact real degree t ≤ kd in the assignment bits. This implies a junta on at most t·2^(t−1) relevant variables for t ≥ 1, by Nisan and Szegedy. Chiarelli, Hatami and Saks improve the asymptotic bound to O(2^t). [Nisan and Szegedy](https://doi.org/10.1145/129712.129757) · [Chiarelli, Hatami and Saks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08564)

A constructive bound tailored to CNF gives assignment-query depth at most kt ≤ k²d. At a nonconstant node choose any remaining non-tautological clause C and query all its variables. Every highest-degree monomial intersects C. Indeed, assigning C's variables to falsify it forces the whole function to zero. A degree-t monomial disjoint from C would survive this restriction with its coefficient unchanged, since contributions from strict supersets require degree greater than t. Fixing C's variables in any way therefore lowers the degree by at least one. Repeat with the restricted formula. Empty clauses and formulas with no remaining clauses are immediate leaves. If t rounds have elapsed, evaluate one arbitrary extension to obtain the constant value. A constant-zero node encountered earlier can continue harmlessly to the cap. Remove tautologies before choosing clauses.

Thus a known degree cap yields deterministic SAT decision and exact counting in 2^(k²d) poly(input size) time on the promised class, without computing or receiving the certificate. For counting, weight each accepting leaf by two to the power of its number of unqueried variables. Query depth here means assignment bits for evaluation of the fixed function. The SAT algorithm explores the tree. A formula outside the promise can return an incorrect result under this cap.

The proposed sharper depth kd fails. Take clauses (x ∨ y), (¬y ∨ z), (¬z ∨ ¬x). Their violation events are pairwise disjoint since each pair forces conflicting values on their shared variable. Hence satisfaction equals one minus the sum of the three violation bits, giving k=2 and d=1. The satisfying assignments are 011 and 100. Starting at 011, flipping any one variable changes the answer, so any deterministic evaluator must read all three bits on this assignment. Conjoining r disjoint copies multiplies their degree-one certificate polynomials, giving certificate degree at most r. At a satisfying assignment all 3r variables are sensitive, yielding depth 3r > 2r.

A simpler independent SAT algorithm on the same promise evaluates every assignment of Hamming weight at most kd. A multilinear polynomial of degree t vanishing on all assignments of weight at most t has zero coefficients by Möbius inversion. This alternative takes n^{O(kd)} time and also needs no supplied certificate.
