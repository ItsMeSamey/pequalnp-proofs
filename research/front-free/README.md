# Charging uses of erased sources

Let a connected output cone have g binary gates and d distinct source wires. Its undirected multigraph has cycle rank g−d+1, since there are 2g operand edges and g+d vertices. For a set D of used source wires, write t for their total fanout, counting operand occurrences in the output cone. Set all D to zero and assume that the restricted labels still require a nonconstant output. The specialized circuit can then be simplified to at most g−t gates.

To see this, first delete the D source vertices from the undirected cone. The remaining graph stays connected because every remaining vertex still has its directed path to the output. This deletion reduces cycle rank by exactly t−|D|. Propagate zero through AND gates by edge and vertex deletions. At an OR whose remaining operand is b, contract the edge from b to that OR, preserving the edges to its consumers. Drop dead gates. These are graph minor operations, so the rank of the final connected cone cannot increase. Its source count d' is at most d−|D|. Its gate count satisfies g'−d'+1 ≤ g−d+1−t+|D|, hence g' ≤ g−t. Distinct surviving initial signals can remain distinct during the charge, even when a subsequent sample projection identifies their columns.

Apply this to the 13 residual primitives that vanish on positives at points 7 through 12. The five-point core and symmetric two-gate point deletion give a lower bound of 21 on the six-high-point restriction. Any candidate residual circuit with 33 gates therefore has **at most 12 operand occurrences** of these 13 vanishing primitive wires. Six of these occurrences are forced by singleton differences for raw points 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. A seventh is forced by the difference {1,20}. If raw point 1 is absent, differences {1,20} and {1,21} require both other vanishing wires, so at least eight occurrences are forced. That branch requires at least 29 residual gates and at least 30 raw gates after adding the front. More generally every additional vanishing-source occurrence beyond seven raises this branch-specific bound by one.

Run `python3 verify.py ../seed-obstructions` to check the vanishing source list and private differences on the pinned matrix.

## Raw suffix omitting w54

If the supplied front wire w54 is unused, every one of w13 through w18 must occur separately. The six positive-negative bit pairs (0,52), (1,52), (2,52), (3,53), (4,54), (5,55) are distinguished on the entire remaining 44-input interface only by w13, w14, w15, w16, w17 and w18, respectively. These six lines vanish on all high-point residual positives. The six mandatory first-group raw point literals and the two-wire cut {raw point 1, w26} remain mandatory in this full interface. Indeed their positive rows contain no front lines, so adding those line columns cannot add another separating bit.

The vanished raw coordinates are exactly w0 through w6, w13 through w18 and w26 through w31. Thus at least 13 operand occurrences disappear before the 21-gate high-point core, giving at least 34 raw gates. At budget 34 there can be exactly 13 such occurrences. If w1 were absent, the cuts {w1,w26} and {w1,w27} would force both low ORs, exceeding the allowance. Consequently w0 through w6 and w13 through w18 are each used exactly once, and none of w26 through w31 is used. This concerns reads inside the output cone. A 34-slot raw suffix meeting the lower bound has no room for dead gates outside it.

**The 13 forced uses feed 13 distinct OR gates.** Work with the already forced available inputs w0 through w25 and w32 through w43. For each of the 13 compulsory vanishing inputs x, the verifier finds a positive-negative pair distinguished only by x whose positive has every other compulsory vanishing input zero. First set those other twelve inputs to zero. The fanout charge removes at least twelve gates. The private pair still requires x. Since x is used once, if its original gate was AND, that AND must survive this first simplification. Its other operand cannot gain a dependence on x under zero substitution, and the circuit still requires x. The AND is not the output, since other high-point positives have x false. Erasing x now removes the AND and one consumer. That would remove at least fourteen gates before the 21-gate core, contradicting the 34-gate budget. Thus x enters an OR.

For every pair of compulsory inputs x,y, at least one has a private negative containing the other. A shared entry OR would cease to distinguish that private pair, and there is no other use of the private input. Hence the entry gates are distinct.

For each front line w13 through w18 its private negatives collectively cover every other remaining primitive. Its entry OR cannot therefore use a primitive partner. Nor can the partner depend only on points or only on lines. A useful pure-side operand must be true at a singleton of that side, since all actual positives have just one point and one line. By monotonicity a private negative containing that singleton would activate the partner, contradicting separation. A partner false on all positives can be replaced by zero, saving the gate, contradicting the established size minimum. Thus **each front-line entry OR has a mixed partner**. The same finite check shows that w2 also needs a computed partner: its only otherwise possible primitive partners are compulsory wires, which cannot share an entry OR.

Each of the six mixed partners is false on all raw negatives private to its front line. Projecting this private negative family to the six-point core dominates 32 of its 33 maximal negatives, which the verifier checks. In coordinates with six point bits followed by seven suffix line bits, the single possible exceptions for w13 through w18 are respectively 122, 1063, 1063, 4125, 122, 4125. Each exception has one active line and the four high points outside that line. The partner therefore rejects all but possibly that one high negative. It cannot itself be any compulsory entry OR, since an entry OR is true at the singleton of its disappearing operand whereas the partner is false on every pure singleton. All other gates survive the 13 deletions in a minimum 21-gate high core, so each front-line partner has nonempty high-positive support.

The [22-gate six-point core](../six-point-core/README.md) raises the front-free branch lower bound to 35. Thus any 34-gate raw solution must use wire 54.
