# Partial rectangle filters with arbitrary point preprocessing

Let a polynomial-size monotone circuit consist of a monotone combiner of filters, each an OR of products of a pure point factor and a pure line factor. The point factors have a shared arbitrary monotone binary DAG; the line factors have shared OR preprocessing. Suppose all flags are accepted, at least a fixed positive fraction of the original maximal negatives are rejected, and the combiner has zero certificates of size b=o(q/log³q) on its rejecting assignments. Then s=Ω(Nq/(log q)²).

Normalize only the pure point gates: iteratively replace a current gate by one whenever its current point singleton support has fractional geometric line cover exceeding M=K log q. By fractional-line-cover-pinning.md choose K large enough that at most o(1) of the original maximal tests change somewhere along the sequence. All positives survive. Fold point constants internally while allowing symbolic constant inputs to the preexisting labeled products, keeping the original outer combiner.

By capped-false-pair-matching.md some still-rejected maximal negative has every current filter with false matching at least m=ceil(q log³q) equal to one. Choose a zero certificate among the zero filters there. The selected at most b filters jointly accept every flag, or that flag would agree with the zero certificate. Each has a false-pair vertex cover of size <m. Delete the union of their covers, removing at most bm=o(N) vertices and at most (q+1)bm flags. The OR of all selected products has precisely the induced incidence graph on singleton pairs. No assertion about larger masks is needed. No constant-one point factor can occur in a product with a surviving singleton-active line factor since every surviving line has surviving nonincident points. Drop all products with an empty singleton factor for this singleton-profile construction.

Reverse the shared line OR computation as in threshold-rectangle-supergraph-obstruction.md, seeding each distinct selected product by its point factor. This costs at most s gates together with the retained point DAG. Each surviving line output has exactly its induced star as point singleton support. Every new reverse OR, including intermediate binary unions, has singleton support contained in a geometric line: choose any surviving raw line ancestral to the corresponding line node, and every contributing point seed also reaches that line's readout. The old point gates have singleton support contained in their normalized support even after restriction, hence fractional cover ≤M. Thus every wire of the constructed DAG has fractional cover at most max(1,M).

The line-star outputs have total support size at least N(q+1)−(q+1)bm. Apply rich-line-physical-or-cost.md to these actual wire supports. The constructed circuit needs Ω(Nq/M²) distinct OR gates, so the original circuit has at least that many gates by the size comparison. Query-side ANDs remain outside the transpose.
