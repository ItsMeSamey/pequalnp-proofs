# A bounded cost for paying a repeatedly used physical gate only once

Use the hypotheses and notation of scalar-four-leaf-cherry.md (q≥4). Select any k distinct original internal gates G. For each g, form C[g←1] by replacing every outgoing use of the physical g by one and evaluating the original root. Build the cofactors above the common original forward/reverse circuit: reuse unchanged wires, fold constants, and share identical changed gates if desired. Let X_O and X_A count additional physical OR and AND rows in this one cofactor arena before degree truncation. No additional copy of unaffected portions is needed. The sums over g of gate counts in the strict output-descendant cones of g are always valid easy upper bounds. The arena may also use other verified cofactor implementations; proof lengths must then be witnessed in those implementations.

Count a flag if it has a native ≤4-leaf proof OR if for at least one g there is a native proof of the original local F_g with at most two raw occurrences and a proof on the same raw flag of C[g←1] in the arena with at most two raw occurrences (zero allowed). This class places no bound on original root-proof multiplicity in the latter case. Its size satisfies

45O+83A−44N+22 +22X_O+18X_A+10k

as an upper bound. In particular if all cofactors have available implementations with zero added rows, even k≤s leaves this bound at most 93s. This is a sufficient structural criterion; it does not assert that an arbitrary circuit or its minimum can meet the criterion.

Proof. For every input F_g AND C[g←1] implies the unmodified scalar: if F_g=1, pinning it to one leaves the physical evaluation unchanged. Append these product checks to the common four-leaf construction, using the same exact linear and quadratic layers. Each new OR in the arena adds at most one pure OR and one quadratic routing OR; each new AND adds at most one bottom AND. For each g add at most two routing ORs for joining the local linear and quadratic versions of the two operands, then one top AND. When an operand is the constant one, use a top AND with the same nonconstant remaining operand on both slots (this is allowed and costs one gate); zero operands are discarded. A nontrivial included product cannot be identically one because it is sound on the empty negative. All these truncated terms are sound and cover every flag counted through a cofactor. The typed bound 18P+4R+18B+2T in scalar-and-depth-two.md (typed accounting detailed in scalar-four-leaf-cherry.md) therefore adds at most 22X_O+18X_A+10k. The original four-leaf terms and their direct-root two-leaf exceptions are charged once in the original budget.

Controls. The proper-star construction already covers all flags by two-leaf proofs, requiring G empty. In the common occupancy example G includes the single mixed occupancy gate. On a flag this gate has a two-leaf witness and replacing all its outgoing uses by one recovers the ordinary star computation, with a two-leaf proof on each flag. With receivers p AND G followed by their star AND, the cofactor needs at most N fresh row products and N−1 final ORs, regardless of the number of uses. More generally, replacing repeated copies of a gate by one also removes every all-one conjunction downstream; proof-tree path multiplicities never enter the arena charge. The signed-matching separation with cliques on the two shores is outside this plane promise: it accepts same-shore pairs forbidden in the proof of the typed depth-two bound. No claim of an unrestricted physical lower bound follows merely from large repetition.
