# Mixed signs on both sides of a flag

Choose O on g in an order-q projective plane. Select a≥1 free points on g excluding O and b≥1 free lines through O excluding g. These two sets are mutually nonincident. Zero every neighbour of the free vertices and permit arbitrary independent signs on the free vertices. For a,b≤q−2 all residual two-by-two masks still obey the original sparse promise. The zero point set has bq+1 elements and zero line set aq+1, disjoint from their respective free sets. Thus d=(a+b)(q+1)+2 variables are removed. The remaining degrees are q on surviving boundary points/lines, and q+1−a or q+1−b away from them. The minimum degree k=q+1−max(a,b) suffices in the induced lower bound of restriction-flow.md. At q401,a=b=25 this leaves 302304 vertices, k=377 and ≥603008 residual gates. A loss of 43417 gates therefore suffices, or a surplus of 3213 above 2d. It is a target, not an unconditional guarantee.

For a=b=h set all free signs to zero. Each side loses D=h(q+1)+1 variables; under a random flag and random free sets each raw input's deletion probability is p=D/N. For two distinct same-side variables it is jointly D(D−1)/(N(N−1)) by two-transitivity of the Desarguesian plane. For opposite incident variables it is E/(N(q+1)), where E=1+2h(q+1)+h²q: O sees h+1 deleted lines, the h free points each see q+1, and the hq other zero points each see h+1. Nonincident opposite variables are jointly deleted with probability (D²−E)/(N(N−q−1)). Use the maximum J of these three probabilities; the transitivity assumptions hold at the prime-field order considered here.

**Raw-bearing ANDs.** Let U count all direct raw input-use slots and A1,A2 count total fanout of ANDs with one and two raw inputs. Normalize away duplicate raw-pair gates, repeated inputs and absorptions. At any gate a direct raw parent yields a zero trigger, and a raw-bearing AND parent contributes its one or two raw operands. A direct versus AND slot cannot overlap (absorption). Two distinct AND parents can overlap in at most one trigger, else identical raw-pair ANDs. Summing the resulting distinct trigger set sizes therefore gives at least U+A1/2+3A2/2. Within slots there are A2 raw pairs. Between two slots of sizes i,j, at most ij≤(i²+j²)/2 pairs, hence in all at most (U+A1)/2+3A2 pairs. Bonferroni gives an expected number of simultaneous constant-parent removals at least

    (p−J/2)U + (p/2−J/2)A1 + (3p/2−3J)A2.

At q401,h33 a loss of 56281 suffices, certified if U≥4N and A2≥54091 regardless of A1. This conditional bound constrains near-optimal circuits.
