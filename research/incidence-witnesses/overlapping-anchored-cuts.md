# Exact overlap of anchored scalar cuts in the ordinary star circuit

Let N=q²+q+1, E=N(q+1). Choose a perfect matching l↦r_l in the incidence graph. It exists by Hall's theorem, since a set of lines has (q+1) times its cardinality in edges, and each neighboring point receives at most q+1 edges. Build the full-cube scalar circuit as an OR of the N products of query l and its point star. For d≥2 and t≥0 with d+t≤q, compute the point star by starting with an OR tree on d points and successively adjoining one point per gate. Place r_l last. Let S_l,j be the prefix on d+j points, and set G_j={S_l,j: all l} for 0≤j≤t.

On a native flag (p,l), the distinguishing path against n_l (query l and the complement of l as payload) is unique. Only the l product fires, its query is high on n_l, and at every point OR only the child containing p fires. Thus at stage j exactly N(d+j) flags cross rich pure gates, while N(q+1−d−j) flags bypass. Every point belongs to B_j because the matching flag for it always bypasses. Its one physical raw-to-final-star wire accounts for that flag at every stage. There are no mixed or thin crossings.

The producer count for G_j is exactly N(d+j−1) OR gates, so rich-covered flags attain O_up,j+|G_j| exactly. These antichains are pairwise disjoint and can be scheduled as disjoint layers even with a strict topological order. Their union has only N(d+t−1) producer ORs, compared to the sum N[(t+1)(d−1)+t(t+1)/2]. Each later layer adds precisely N ORs and covers precisely N new flags. The entire correct circuit has E+N−1 gates and no internal fanout at all. This distinguishes disjoint crossing gates or ordering intervals from disjoint charged producer cones.

## One physical bypass carries every flag of its point through all stages

Choose k pencil lines through o, 1≤k≤q−1. Let A be their union, |A|=1+kq. Compute all flags with point in A using one ordinary point-star product per p∈A. On all N−k other lines take a line-star product restricted to points outside A. These have positive remaining degree: an unchosen pencil line has q points outside A and a nonpencil line has q+1−k. OR all products together. The incidence products are partitioned, so the total gate count is E+|A|+N−k−1. The sole raw use of each p∈A is its input to the point-star product.

Choose d+t≤q+1−k with d≥2, and form the same disjoint prefix cuts across only the N−k restricted line stars. For every one of the |A|(q+1) flags of A, at every cut the only distinguishing path uses its point's single raw wire. In particular the query OR of the point-star product cannot be chosen at its AND, since it also fires on n_l. Each stage has (N−k)(d+j) rich crossings, with exact producer count (N−k)(d+j−1), and the nested union pays only for the largest prefix. For k≈q/2 and d,t≈q/4, a constant fraction of all flags takes these mandatory point bypasses through Θ(q) cuts. The construction is on the full cube. It addresses addition of overlapping cut charges and makes no optimality assertion about the circuit.
