# Charging degree shortfalls individually

Let n denote the number of surviving inputs, d_x the degree of a surviving input x, and assume minimum degree at least two and same-side codegrees at most one. In restriction-flow.md cap port weight at an arbitrary integer k≥2. The private sibling serving x accepts d_x distinct raw singletons, hence supplies at least min(k,d_x) units. Write Δ for the sum of (k−d_x)_+ over surviving inputs. The private-sibling lower bound becomes kG−Δ; the upper bound s'+1+(k−1)e₀ stays valid. With the original t and δ this gives (k+1)δ≤2n−t+Δ. In particular s'≥2n−1−(2n+Δ)/(k+1).

At q=401 independently retain every vertex with probability one half. Then E[n]=N and E[Δ]=N times E[(k−B)_+] for B binomial with 402 trials and success probability one half. Each vertex survives independently of its own neighbor count. On the event some original vertex has surviving degree below two, use the trivial bound R≤s. Its probability is at most 2N·403·2^−402. Arguing by contradiction with s≤L−1, good samples satisfy R−2|D|≤L−4N+(2n+Δ)/(k+1). For bad samples the same bound holds with an additional 4N. Thus the unconditional expected hurdle for credits above 2|D| is L−4N+N(2+E[(k−B)_+])/(k+1) plus at most 4N·2N·403·2^−402.

Under the sole-use AND compensation hypothesis, the separate expected root and sole penalties are bounded by (3/4)^N and 2N·2^−403. At k=178 all corrections together give a hurdle below 3447.324. A total of 3448 credits from other AND disagreements, shared-wire zero probabilities and any excess raw occurrences suffices.

A structural zero certificate Z for a shared gate guarantees expected fanout credit at least (f−1)2^−|Z|; overlapping certificates at that gate may be evaluated by inclusion-exclusion. At an AND a disjoint pair consisting of a zero certificate Z for one operand and a positive certificate C for its other operand earns at least 2^−(|C|+|Z|). Forward and reverse orientations represent incompatible events, so their lower bounds add. Choose each AND's contribution once; the fanout credits are a separate term even at those same gates.
