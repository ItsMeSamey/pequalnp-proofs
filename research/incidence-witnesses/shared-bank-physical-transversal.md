# An actual transversal removes the bank-width cutoff

In the layered scalar model of shared-bank-sparse-product.md allow arbitrary product width t, both producer layers shared. Use the globally marked flags of shared-bank-outsider-petals.md, ≤4O in number. Assign each remaining flag to one sound product accepting it. Fix one such flag (p,l). The banks not containing p all contain l. If any has only queries through p, its query support must be {l}, since a larger support would globally mark the flag. The dual point witness at l always exists by unrestricted payload, and is likewise singleton if unmarked. Such flags remain isolated in their assigned products.

Suppose instead that every bank not containing p has an outside query. Their outsider sets must have transversal number greater than q: otherwise selecting the transversal with payload {p} activates all banks on a legal negative. For EACH such bank choose a physical OR path from raw l to it, and take the first OR h along that path whose query support has ≥2 labels. One input has query support {l}. Because the flag is globally unmarked there is an outside query z_h in the support of h. For this fixed flag choose just ONE z_h per DISTINCT h. Every bank selecting h contains z_h, so the set of chosen z_h IS A TRANSVERSAL of every offending bank, irrespective of overlap. Thus at least q+1 distinct first-growth OR gates were required by this flag. Globally each OR has at most two singleton line labels at its operand slots and each label has q+1 possible points, so the total number of unwitnessed unmarked flags is at most 2O. This price is independent of width or fanout. All probes have at most q queries.

In private AND product cones the remaining assigned isolated flags across all terms are at most 2A by distinct bank-entry slots, yielding E≤6O+2A for ARBITRARY width. For general shared AND cones and log₂t<q write b=min(t,4(log₂t)²). The induced-matching transversal proof applies to min(m,q) pairs and yields b per term even with t>q. Consequently E≤6O+bA and E≤6O+2A+bF, where F counts excess AND readers including one terminal per designated product, as in shared-bank-branch-excess.md.

These prove an unrestricted-width Ω(Nq/log²q) lower bound within this layered model as q grows: if s≥Nq/100 it is already true, otherwise t≤2N+s because each distinct bank is a raw or a bottom OR. Thus t=O(q³) and log₂t<q for q>100 (for example 2N+Nq/100<q³ there, and 3log₂q<q). Private AND cones meet the official Nq/100 directly. Arbitrary interleaving of the AND and OR producer layers remains outside this proof. The sharp q+2 control in shared-bank-outsider-petals.md illustrates why selecting arbitrary one-per-bank outsiders would have failed.
