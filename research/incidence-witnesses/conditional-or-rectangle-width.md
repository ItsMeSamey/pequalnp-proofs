# Boolean rectangle width on earlier query backgrounds

Fix C and suppose the original unrestricted-payload scalar circuit has s≤CN physical gates. Write N=q²+q+1. A positive fraction of the expected rich mixed OR charges described below must occur at physical ORs whose conditional point-by-line Boolean matrices have rectangle cover number at least c_C log q, for some c_C>0. The matrices are evaluated before the next random block. Rectangle cover number means the minimum number of all-one combinatorial rectangles covering the binary support.

## Assignment and entropy

Take J≥32e(C+1) a fixed integer, and expose J consecutive disjoint blocks of d=floor((q−1)/(J+1)) random query lines, inside a random ordered (q−1)-set as in earlier-mixed-receiver-tradeoff.md. At each stage write V for the previous prefix and W for the prefix including its new block. The final unhit points have expected size (1/e+o(1))N. Use the one-orientation-per-flag assignment in that note. The rich-feeder removal costs at most 2sJ flag-stages. Across the J stages, loss of earlier acceptance at V+p+ℓ costs at most 2s(q+1) flag-stages by monotonicity and uniqueness of the orientation's point. The earlier-query-hot bound costs in expectation O_C(sJ[q^(5/6)+N/(d log q)])=o(JNq). Thus the expected retained mass is Ω(JNq) for the stated fixed J; a fraction tending to one is unnecessary.

A retained assignment has an oriented AND and its point p, with the sibling h true on V+p+ℓ and false on W+p. There are at most 2s groups per stage, one per orientation, and its assigned lines ℓ are distinct. For a group of size h₀ trace the h computation separately for each assigned ℓ. Define the row of a signal a at earlier V and point p to be S_{a,p}={l outside V : a(V+p+l)=1}. Put D=ceil(q^(2/3)). Stop a trace when ≤D of this row's labels are incident with p. Every visited gate rejects W+p. At an AND choose one rejecting input deterministically, independent of the label in this group; it is true on every traversing positive. At an OR take one deterministic true input on the positive, and both inputs reject W+p. A reached raw has at most one accepted incident label because it rejects W+p. For each assigned label record the bits identifying the OR choices until stopping. A given bit string determines the entire path and whether it has stopped, independently of the label, so distinct strings form a prefix code. At most D assigned labels have the same string. Mean bit length is at least log₂(h₀/D) by the entropy bound. Each bit charges one physical triple (p,ℓ,OR) at the stage. Each flag has only one assigned orientation, and one directed path per flag cannot revisit a physical OR, even in a reconvergent DAG. Thus the triples counted are distinct within the stage. Different stages are separate experiments in the count, and no claim of globally disjoint triples is needed.

Summing h₀ log₂(h₀/D) and applying convexity over ≤2sJ groups and then over the random choice gives expected charge Ω_C(JNq log q). Every charged row at its physical OR meets the pencil of p in >D labels and misses the entire new random block.

## Conditional rectangle charge

For a fixed V and physical OR a, use the earlier binary matrix with rows p outside the covered points of V satisfying a(V+p)=0, columns l outside V, entries 1[l∈S_{a,p}]. This matrix is fixed before the new block is sampled, although a later trace need not be. Suppose its Boolean rectangle cover has size ≤r. Fix such a cover as a function of V alone, and denote its column sets by B₁,…,B_r. In each charged row, its support is the union of the pools of the rectangles containing that row. The entire support misses the new block because a(W+p)=0. With K=floor(D/(2r)), pools containing <K lines through p together account for <rK≤D/2 incident labels. The whole row contains >D such labels. Consequently its entire incident count, and hence its actual triple charges, is at most twice the sum of incident counts in its pools containing ≥K lines through p. These individual pools miss the block.

For a fixed pool of m labels, when m≤K²/2 the points incident with ≥K pool lines have at most 2m total incidences with the pool. Indeed, selecting K lines at each of j such points gives j≤m(K−1)/(K²−m)≤2m/K by codegree and Cauchy. The union of their incident pool-line sets has size ≤m, and duplicate occurrences cost at most binom(j,2) since two points have at most one common line. The avoidance probability given V is ≤exp(−md/(N−|V|)). For larger pools bound their incidences by N(q+1). Conditional expected charge to a gate of cover size ≤r is thus at most
```
O(rN/d) + 2rN(q+1) exp(−K²d/(2N)).
```
Rank-zero matrices receive no charge. For r≤log q and fixed J the tail is negligible since K≫q^(2/3)/log q and d=Θ_C(q).

Summing the conditional estimate over s gates and J stages bounds the expected contribution of all ORs of cover size ≤r by O_C(JNrq). Taking r=floor(c_C log q) with sufficiently small positive c_C leaves a positive fraction of the entropy charge on the larger covers. In particular, at some stage one physical OR has such a conditional Boolean width. Rank-one point-assisted copies of a common query pool are included in the low-width bound even if they are never active on query-only inputs. The conclusion measures conditional rectangle width and does not supply a superlinear circuit lower bound.
