# Dense contaminating branches with a common scalar checker

Use odd prime orders tending to infinity and the flag-disjoint paired-packet family from cold-deficiency-paired-packet-packing.md. Put N=q²+q+1, a=binom(q,2)+2, P0=[at least a points active], L0=[at least q queries active]. Then P0 AND L0 implies true incidence F on the full cube. Indeed q distinct lines cover at least q(q+1)−binom(q,2) points by the two-term inclusion-exclusion lower bound; at most binom(q,2)+1 points remain outside.

For a fixed filler line l* let T denote its exact star (the line raw times the OR of its q+1 incident point raws). Drop packets using l*, and their twins. One may choose l* so the lost fraction is at most 14/N by averaging the seven query labels of each packet and its twin. Complete all omitted flags in the donor by ordinary point terms. Make A from an ordinary full line-star circuit by feeding every raw x through x OR (P0 OR T). Make B from the point-term paired donor, feeding every raw x through a single shared B-branch proxy x OR (L0 OR T), in lieu of its previous proxies. Use separate branches and put the final scalar AND above their outputs. The branches equal F OR P0 and F OR L0, so the final output is F on the full cube. Thresholds are single-shore circuits. For example a Batcher sorting network (binary OR/AND comparator, pad then fold constants) costs O(N log² N) additional gates, hence total size Θ(Nq) on this family. See https://doi.org/10.1145/1468075.1468121 .

Schedule the T star and pure-query threshold before B, next the paired B trees, and only afterward the A point threshold and completion collectors. At every retained singleton query, the L0 output and T are zero on all singleton point masks and at its maximal native negative. Pure-query gates cannot merge point blocks at a fixed query, and the pure-point subtree for T can contribute at most one line singleton point on any other query: two of its distinct payloads on l* cannot also lie on that query. Thus none of these dependencies merges two retained points before the paired trees. The original isomorphic packet merges persist with their distinct twin charges. Extra raw-read slots in threshold/T computations cannot supply changing paths to these B joins: every such path passes through a threshold or T output that is unchanged on the paired tests. A and the B completion have no path back into the selected joins. Hence t retained packets give 17t nonexempt events with at most 2N eligible raw slots and Ω(Nq) aggregate deficiency asymptotically.

No port of cold-gate-inclusive-payers.md in this entire circuit certifies one of the chosen flags. At zero query, an A point bank has exactly its q+1 original point singletons (P0 has no singleton and T vanishes); at zero payload its pure query support is empty. The A query operand owns its query l at zero points, but the opposite point bank accepts the entire off-line payload because it fires P0 there (q²≥a). In B the point operand owns just its point p on zero queries, but its opposite query bank accepts all off-pencil queries because their q² labels fire L0. A packet query bank has ≥2 incident labels at each of its nine points, so cannot itself own a singleton query in the opposite direction. A B completion bank with a single query can certify only its uncovered flag and hence no selected assigned flag. Neither root operand has any singleton support on either pure shore. The only mixed AND in the filler T certifies flags on its excluded query. Pure-shore threshold gates cannot certify any flag by a two-shore port. Every row product in either branch is true on all q+1 flags of T, and the final root accepts all flags. A pure-shore AND either accepts no native flags or at least the q+1 through one accepted singleton. Consequently no AND accepting at most q flags can pay a selected event. At the original selected B product the unexposed bank already activates on a legal off-pencil pure mask of q queried lines.

Any zero-deletion leaving at least a point raws makes A accept a pure independent negative regardless of deleted queries. Any such deletion leaving q query raws makes B accept a legal pure independent negative regardless of deleted points. Therefore salvaging either unmodified branch by raw zero restrictions followed only by positive star additions requires ≥N−a+1 deleted points for A, or ≥N−q+1 deleted lines for B. Likewise pinning A at the final AND via the sufficient original-maximal-negative condition of minimum-guard-query-deletion.md needs ≥N−q+1 deleted lines: for any surviving q-set Q, A is zero on (outside(Q),Q). Pinning B by that condition is impossible on the empty query. The construction escapes the small-deletion certificate, not arbitrary semantic simplification. Both donors admit the original F before their substitutions; unswitching A's raw proxies and deleting B, for example, gives a strictly smaller native solution. No gate-minimality assertion is made.
