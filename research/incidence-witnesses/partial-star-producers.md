# Partial-star producer rank

Fix all query lines to zero, with unrestricted point payload. Let O denote the number of ORs in the simplified baseline, at most s−N by fork-publication/fork-transfer.md. For each line x, a signal rejecting its full point complement is x-safe. Such a signal has singleton support contained in x.

Maintain a component partition of the points of every line. Scan the gates topologically, uniting the points in every x-safe support. For an AND some x-safe parent already contains all singleton points of its output, so no new union is needed. For an OR both parents are x-safe and their nonempty supports are already internally connected, hence at most one union succeeds. A successful union entails that the output accepts two singleton points, determining x uniquely. Thus the sum of the graphic ranks of all resulting partitions is at most O. A nontrivial component of k points uses rank k−1≥k/2; at most 2O flags belong to nontrivial components across all lines. This proof applies even with arbitrary gate sharing and any number of designated outputs.

Let D count dirty sole-read query lines x with exact forced-gate star V_g, as in fork-publication/fork-transfer.md. In an accepting forced proof on a point singleton p in x, mark the g leaves (at least one since the baseline output is zero). At an AND with one marked child the unmarked child's signal accepts p in the baseline: its selected proof used no marker. At least one such sibling rejects the full complement of x. Otherwise splice baseline proofs of all these siblings at the complement into the marked tree, contradicting exactness of V_g. This remains valid when the physical gate IDs repeat.

Outside the nontrivial components such a sibling has singleton support exactly {p}. Each such physical AND operand can witness at most the q+1 flags at p. Therefore at least D−floor(2O/(q+1)) distinct AND operand ports of singleton baseline support are required. At q=401 and s≤646424, at most 970442 flags lie in nontrivial components and at least D−2414 thin ports are required. The same port can serve an entire pencil, so this does not close the unrestricted lower bound.
