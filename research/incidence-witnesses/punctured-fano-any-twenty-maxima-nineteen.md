# Nineteen gates even after deleting one maximal negative

A binary monotone circuit accepting sixteen flags and rejecting any twenty of the twenty-one maximal negatives of the twelve-site punctured Fano instance requires at least nineteen gates. Fanout and depth are unrestricted.

Take a minimum live circuit. The nineteen-maxima theorem already gives eighteen gates. At eighteen, the twelve essential inputs give exactly seven fanout units above one. The first-fork results punctured-fano-almost-full-forks.md and punctured-fano-equality-cold-tree.md apply after one omission: no two singleton-hot raws share a first fork, the other raws are independent, and no cold raw first branches at a hot owner's fork. Thus at least six hot owners are necessary. If there are only six distinct forks, the opposite shore has unique paths, contradicting punctured-fano-clean-shore-nineteen.md, which needs only singleton and cross-pair tests. There are therefore exactly seven distinct forks, each of fanout two.

Seven hot owners are excluded by punctured-fano-seven-fork-clamp.md under the directed separation that survives every one-row omission. Its read-capped clamped formula proof covers all sixteen five-cold configurations. Hence exactly six hot owners fill one shore, and one unowned fork X remains. Let S be the cold inputs first branching at X. An empty S again contradicts the unique-shore lower bound. For nonempty S, punctured-fano-unowned-fork.md excludes every size at least three and all but sixteen (omitted row, size-two S) cases per shore. A singleton of degree two is impossible: zeroing it leaves a five-leaf residual with unique paths and at most seventeen gates, whereas five-cold-eighteen.md requires eighteen. Degree-three singletons and the remaining pairs are excluded by punctured-fano-stray-pin.md, which saves two and five distinct physical slots respectively, against residual lower bounds of seventeen and fourteen. This exhausts size eighteen.

Consequently a full-sample minimum circuit with an AND root, one of whose parents rejects at most one maximal negative, requires at least twenty-one gates: the other cone costs at least nineteen, and the nonraw correcting parent and root supply two distinct further slots by the AND-path single-use lemma in punctured-fano-root-interfaces.md. The unrestricted size-twenty question remains open.
