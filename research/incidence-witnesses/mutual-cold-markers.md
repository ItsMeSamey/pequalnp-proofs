# Removing reciprocal cold-witness overlap

Use the full point-domain line-normalized circuit of cold-warm-aggregate.md at order 401. Its H remaining sole-read query lines begin at ANDs. For each x, Uₓ(P) is its first fork's value when x is the sole active query; P_J is all points outside the union of J. Every Uₓ accepts all points on x. A singleton-cold owner has Uₓ(P_{x})=0; fix its witness to {x}. For the other sole-read owners draw an arc x→y, with y any different query line, if Uₓ(P_{x,y})=0. Owners without outgoing arcs are globally warm. Owners with outgoing arcs are dirty, since Uₓ(P_{x})=1; their downstream single-fork forced output Vₓ is the exact star. Distinct dirty owners have distinct first forks.

Choose one outgoing arc per owner with any, minimizing the number m of selected directed two-cycles. Subject to this maximize the sum over two-cycles of the larger endpoint's first-fork index in a fixed topological ordering. Edit every globally warm owner and the later endpoint x of each selected two-cycle {x,z}. Call all other sole-read owners charged. Every bad neighbor y of such an edited endpoint x is an unedited dirty owner, is cold with x as its own chosen witness, and has a strictly earlier fork than x. For y=z this follows by choice. For a different y, redirecting x to y would remove the two-cycle with z. Minimality forces creation of a two-cycle with y, so y already chooses x. This excludes nonowners, fixed singleton owners, globally warm owners and any vertex in a different selected two-cycle. If y had a later fork than x, the switch would improve the secondary objective.

Apply the warm marker edit to this enlarged edited set: set each marked raw input to zero in its original private path and inject it by OR at its fork. Every marked owner is dirty with a distinct fork, giving one saved slot per edited marker and preservation of positive singleton flags. On a maximal negative with active queries J, |J|≤2, if every active edited owner is warm on P_J, the usual monotone comparison with the original circuit proves rejection. Otherwise J={x,y} for one newly edited two-cycle endpoint x and a bad neighbor y as above. The only active edited owner is x. The earlier unedited fork of y cannot see x's injection. With x's raw suppressed, this earlier fork has value U_y(P_J)=0, as when y is switched off by monotonicity. Other inactive markers act identically. Every path from y goes through its unedited fork, so switching y off leaves the edited output unchanged. Now the result is precisely Vₓ(P_J)=0: x's raw is suppressed, x alone is forced at its fork, and all other query inputs are zero. Thus all tests on arbitrary payloads with at most two queried lines remain correct, including tests with an unedited query.

For each charged owner trace its cold-star trie using the chosen witness, as in cold-warm-aggregate.md. Each yields ≥3106 eligible physical OR/point pairs. A physical OR charged for x and y would have more than two singleton points on both lines. Its support is contained in the union of each owner's at-most-two chosen witness lines. Thus x and y must have chosen one another, impossible since one endpoint of every such pair was edited. Consequently W≥3106C, where C is the number of charged owners and M=H−C the number edited.

Apply the single-booking inequality M+αW>E(h)−1 in cold-warm-aggregate.md. If H≥3383 then h=52 has E=3383 and α·3106=64766312/64803606, so it succeeds for C≤1737. At h=53, E=3387 and α·3106=66011818/64803606, so it succeeds for C≥161. These ranges overlap. H=3382 with C=182 fails every integer h in this envelope (including no pins); this only asserts optimality for that numeric test. Thus H≤3382 in any below-target circuit in the stated line-normalized form.

Combined with the independently obtained sparse point bound H_P≤9709 on the bilateral normal form (and preservation of line use counts through the point hoist), F≤1613+3382+9709=14704 there. The point-side cold definition uses bounded witness masks and is not needed to prove the line result.
