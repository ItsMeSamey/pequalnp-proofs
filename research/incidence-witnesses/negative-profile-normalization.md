# Joint physical gate savings on a common family of negative extensions

Take a live binary monotone incidence DAG and a family of legal independent inputs such that every required three-by-three independent mask is contained in one family member. Each raw bit varies: its singleton requires a member with that bit, and a neighbor requires a member without it. Label gates C if always zero on the family, H if always one, and M otherwise. The root is C; raws are M. Let c_i,h_i count gates with i same-color operand occurrences, b_C,b_H count middle gates receiving a C or H, and e_CH,e_HC count opposite-color wires.

A middle gate with a C input must be OR with its other input middle. A middle gate with an H input must be AND with its other input middle. Every C gate with no C inputs is an AND of two middle inputs; an OR that always rejects has two C inputs, and an AND with an H input has the profile of its other input. Dually an H source is an OR of two middle operands. A C-to-H edge always enters an OR whose other input is H; an H-to-C edge always enters an AND whose other input is C.

Force all H gates high in the original circuit, then evaluate only the middle network with C gates zero, forwarding middle boundary gates as identities. Retain all c_0 source ANDs and OR their outputs. On each family member the middle values are unchanged and the retained sources all reject. Monotonicity covers every negative below a family member. For every required positive, the preliminary high forcing can only increase the original output. The first C gate firing in that preliminary evaluation cannot have any C operands (a C OR has only C inputs, and a firing C AND cannot have an earlier false C operand). It is a retained source, and suppressing its earlier C gates does not change its inputs. This also proves c_0≥1 if positive tests exist.

The final OR costs c_0−1, so savings are at least Δ=c_1+c_2+b_C+b_H+h_0+h_1+h_2−c_0+1. Direct physical operand counting gives F_C=c_2+b_C+e_CH−c_0+1 and F_H=h_2+b_H+e_HC−h_0, where F sums original excess fanout of nonoutput gates of that color. Thus Δ=F_C+F_H+2h_0+(h_1−e_CH)+(c_1−e_HC)≥F_C+F_H+2h_0. All counts concern slots of the same original DAG.

Below target at q401 one has Δ≤1616 by dominating-theta.md. On the arbitrary-point circuit one choice is all T of at most three lines together with every point outside their union; it includes point signals not coverable by three lines as H, and clean mixed-only signals rejecting every such negative as C. Other common cofinal families may capture pair-only H signals. On a bilaterally normalized DAG all extensions must meet its narrower promise. This is a conditional joint budget, not a closure of the q401 gap.
