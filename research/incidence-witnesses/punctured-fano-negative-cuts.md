# Blocking cuts on punctured-Fano negatives

For a legal negative n let T_n count true internal gates, b_n count ANDs with exactly one true operand, and τ_n be the minimum size of a vertex cover avoiding n. Force every true signal on n to one. This removes T_n gates and bypasses b_n distinct additional gates, preserves rejection at n, and only increases acceptance of flags. The residual needs at least τ_n−1 ORs: from the false root route one false parent per AND and both parents per OR. The reached raw leaves give a zero certificate covering all flags, disjoint from n, and the connected cone needs at least its leaf count minus one ORs. An OR of a minimum cover attains this one-negative bound. Therefore s≥T_n+b_n+τ_n−1. Every directed path from a true gate to the false output hits a one-true AND, but many paths may share it.

For a nonempty family F one can first force all signals true throughout F and bypass the one-common-true AND receivers outside that set. In the remainder, any fixed orientation of every AND (one retained operand per gate) yields a single raw zero certificate. Incompatible negatives, whose union contains a flag, cannot share a certifying orientation. Thus at least ceil(log2 χ) residual ANDs are required in addition to max(τ_n−1) ORs, where χ is the chromatic number of the incompatibility graph.

All 21 maximal negatives are pairwise incompatible. For each n, precisely b_n global AND orientation bits must choose the false parent at one-true gates; arbitrary choices of the other bits still route a valid zero certificate. These sufficient orientation subcubes are disjoint. Thus sum_n 2^(-b_n)≤1 and
    2^s ≥ sum_n 2^(T_n+τ_n−1).
Seventeen masks have τ=8 and four have τ=7. If s≤20, the mean T_n weighted by 2^(τ_n−1) is at most 20−log2(2432)=8.752072487. Every pair of maximal negatives must have opposing forced bits at some physical AND. If L_g,R_g are the two opposite one-true classes, sum_g |L_g| |R_g| ≥210. This measures capacity of physical gates, not separate gates per path. The checker, routing argument and quantitative bounds were independently verified; no unrestricted threshold-input assumption occurs.
