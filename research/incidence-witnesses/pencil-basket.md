# Near-full pencil basket at order 401

Use the same bilateral normalized live DAG, legal masks, and fanout notation as blocker-basket.md. Let L be any line and choose B of b≤396 of its 402 points, or use the dual construction. The same bound F_C+E_D−H_B≤1616 holds for each such B if s≤646424.

For b>0 choose distinct x,y on L outside B and Y through y other than L. Then x and Y survive D=B∪N(B), and x is off Y. All matched arms (p,xp), p∈Y, survive except (y,L). Every surviving point loses at most b lines, every surviving line loses no points. In the first theta cleanup remove surviving points of L other than x and surviving lines through y other than Y. The exceptional y retains Y. Every removed point off Y other than x on L sees at most one line through y (L itself), and every removed line through y other than L loses at most one point of L. All other surviving vertices retain degree at least 402−b−1≥5. Each noncore remaining vertex has a neighbor in the theta (its join to x or intersection with Y). The two elimination stages and theta cost of blocker-basket.md now apply with 401 arms, giving 2n−4 on the residual. The physical cold normalization and sole-use compensation are identical; extending a residual 4-by-4 mask by B occupies at most 400 positions on either shore. The b=0 case uses the full theta.

Here is a constructive selection test. Pick a carrier L and a positive k≤396, and average over uniform k-subsets of L. For each gate g let r_g(L) count points of L which individually block g on all legal negative extensions. Put h(t)=1−choose(402−t,k)/choose(402,k). Each gate receives the credit f_g−1 with probability at least h(r_g(L)). Write e_v=(f_v−2)_+. The expected raw credit is exactly
  k/402 times the sum of (f_p−2) over p on L, plus e_L, plus k/402 times the sum of e_Y over all lines Y other than L.
Indeed Y≠L is deleted exactly when its unique intersection with L is chosen. Thus the sum of this raw term and the weighted gate-hit probabilities is ≤1616 for each carrier individually in a subtarget circuit. A larger computed sum certifies one violating basket, which can also be selected deterministically by conditioning successive draws on their exact hypergeometric hit probabilities. This computes a certificate when the blocker incidence data are given. It does not show the threshold is reached on every circuit.

Geometry independently audited by /root/incidence/audit. Original uncapped point masks are only required for the preceding bilateral normalization transfer; all masks here obey that transfer's inherited caps.

## Simultaneous incident carriers

Fix a flag O on L. Select a points of L other than O and b lines through O other than L, with 1≤a,b≤396. No selected point is on any selected line. Take x on L other than O and the selected points, and Y through O other than L and the selected lines. Only (O,L) is a broken arm: Y loses just O and each lost line through x must be L. Every surviving point lies off the b chosen lines and loses at most a lines through point pins. Every surviving line dually loses at most b points. The first cleanup deletes surviving points on L and lines through O except the poles. Its removed points are on no removed line, and vice versa, since O and L have already disappeared. Each other point loses at most one further line, and each other line at most one further point. Every deleted nonpin keeps a neighbor: outside O each point sees at most one selected line and at most a joins to pins, while O sees b selected lines and L; the dual treats L. Adjoining B to a residual four-by-four mask uses at most 400 on either shore. Thus the physical bound holds for up to 792 pins in one basket. Independently audited by /root/incidence/audit.

For a uniform flag and independent uniform subsets on its two carriers, a gate's singleton blockers hit with conditional probability one minus the product of the two hypergeometric avoidance probabilities, using 401 candidate positions per carrier. The weighted sum of hit probabilities is a lower bound on expected cold excess. Each basket deletes exactly a+bq+1 points and b+aq+1 lines (the pencil union counts its center once and is disjoint from selected points, with dual for lines). Symmetry makes the two deletion marginals uniform. At a=b=396 the expected raw term is (159193 E−396 H)/161203 where E sums all raw excess and H counts all sole reads. All terms refer to one experiment.
