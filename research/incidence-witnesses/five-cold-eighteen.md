# Exact eighteen-gate bound for the five-cold residual

Under the five-cold hypotheses of [the lattice proof](five-cold-lattice.md), the exact minimum is eighteen gates. Repeat that proof with seventeen gates in the budget. The four mergers and five private ANDs leave eight slots. Since the four triple stars force five or more hot-only gates, at most three additional useful unary ANDs remain. Enumerate j from zero through three with hot budget 8−j and the same relaxation of nested filters. For each of the four labelled degree-two deletions, the numbers of candidate tuples surviving the cardinality bound are 1, 224, 1796 and 99 respectively. The hot-support closure with its budget of auxiliary masks rejects every tuple. The executable check-five-cold-eighteen.py asserts all four exclusions.

The usual formula realizes the bound on the full incidence predicate. Form four triple neighborhoods with two hot ORs per triple and the fifth pair neighborhood with one OR. Conjoin each neighborhood with its cold site and join the five terms by four OR gates. This uses 9+5+4=18 binary gates. In particular the two-gate pinning proof for a clean six-leaf shore now requires at least twenty gates before pinning.
