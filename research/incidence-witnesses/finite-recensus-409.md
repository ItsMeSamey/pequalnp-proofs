# Finite residual census for prime order 409

Use source-groups-408.md with q=409, N=167691, contrary allowance k=15096 over 4N−4, and original bilateral bound S₀=37001. Actual deletions leave n sites and total T=2N−n deleted; z denotes spent surplus. The protected theta and marks guarantee minimum degree three.

## Temporary two-by-two bound

For a graph of codegree at most one and minimum degree at least three write D_d=Σ_v max(0,d−deg(v)). Every circuit accepting the flags and rejecting the independent two-by-two masks has size s≥2n−1−floor((2n+D_d)/(d+1)). In the weighted-port proof of residual-peel-census.md all structural arguments use only degree three, including choosing witnesses when a common neighbor was deleted. With weights capped at d, each nonbad local raw demands min(d,deg(v)) from its private sibling. Total demand is at least d(n−B_bad)−D_d. The same outgoing bound gives (d+1)(2n−1−s)≤2n+D_d−(e−B_bad). No temporary peel is necessary.

For D deleted sites on one shore, the original opposite-shore lost degrees X_v have sum cD and variance sum qD(1−D/N), c=q+1, by uniqueness of joins. Put μ=cD/N. For h>μ, at most qD(1−D/N)/(h−μ)² sites have X_v≥h, while Σ(X_v−h)_+≤qD(1−D/N)/(4(h−μ)) by the pointwise squared bound. Summing across both shores costs at most the same bound at D=T as long as h>cT/N: the ratio (1−D/N)/(h−cD/N)^j increases with D on this range for j=1,2 and h≤c. The bounds increase with T. Thus low(e), the number of surviving sites of degree ≤e, is bounded by floor[q T(N−T) N / ((c−e)N−cT)²], or 2N if the unsquared denominator is nonpositive. For d with positive gap, D_d≤floor[qT(N−T)/(4((c−d)N−cT))]. Minimize the resulting integer upper bound P on 2n−s over the indicated grid of d.

## High-degree sole AND census

Take r=65 and retain independent masks of size 2r+2 per shore. On one query shore track just sole AND-first owners of degree >2r. Their first-fork functions U_x accept all surviving neighbor singletons and have a common size-s product extractor as in residual-peel-census.md; that proof works in the absence of a common surviving join. A set X with x∈X and |X|≤2 is bad for x if no payload of ≤r outside its union of neighborhoods activates U_x. Owners with bad {x} use this singleton witness. Each remaining owner with a bad pair chooses a bad partner y, possibly an untracked vertex. Owners with neither are globally warm. Minimize the number of directed selected two-cycles and then maximize the sum of their later first-fork indices. Edit globally warm owners and the later endpoint of each selected two-cycle as in mutual-cold-points.md.

Every edited x has an off-star U_x witness of size ≤r. Its forced fork with all queries suppressed rejects every off-star test of size ≤2: otherwise the native computation with x alone on the union of that payload and the off-star witness would accept a negative. Its fork cannot coincide with that of another tracked owner y, because forcing the common fork accepts each neighbor singleton of y, including one outside x. For an edited reciprocal owner x each bad partner y is an unedited tracked owner choosing x, with earlier fork: redirecting x would otherwise destroy its reciprocal pair without replacement, or improve the secondary objective. If a negative with active x includes such a bad partner, erase y. Its earlier fork is zero on the native only-y test by its reciprocal cold witness, and cannot see the later injection of x; no other edited owner is active. This leaves the already rejected forced-x test. On every other two-by-two negative, augment its payload by ≤2 warm witnesses of size ≤r avoiding its active queries; the original negative already activates all active injected forks there. Positive flags persist and disjoint AND-first paths each save ≥1 gate by the suppression-and-injection proof in residual-peel-census.md.

Each unedited owner has a chosen bad witness X. In the common extractor mark gates with support contained in its union of neighborhoods and >2 singleton neighbors of x. The root is marked. Its connected marked ancestor region has ≥r gates: otherwise its ≤r boundary wires that are thin on x miss some neighbor since deg(x)>2r. Every boundary wire active there has an outsider singleton; at most r outsiders jointly dominate that neighbor at the root, violating coldness. If a gate is charged by unedited x and y, its >2 neighbors of x contained in the witness union for y imply x is in y's chosen X: an outsider has at most two neighbors in that union. Similarly y is in x's chosen X. Thus the owners chose each other, impossible with both unedited. A three-way or larger overlap contains such a forbidden pair. The unedited count is consequently ≤floor(s/r) on this shore. The temporary baseline 2n−P bounds the edited count by s−2n+P. Applying separate experiments to the two shores gives total high-degree sole AND count at most C=2(k−z+P−4)+2 floor(s/r).

## Reset budget and finite check

Reset after each 768 fresh surplus units. At a reset hoist all current sole ORs of degree above b*=2r+2+floor(k/768)+1=152, including geometrically protected sites when eligible. Keep all geometric protections. The starting symmetric promise is ≥q−1; starting with allowance b* and dropping one per reset leaves ≥2r+2 throughout. Skipped sole ORs stay below the degree cutoff at all later stages. Between resets at most 1536 new sole ORs can appear, and the initial soles are all AND-first. As usual a zero-propagation surplus costs at most one newly affected owner per unit and other savings at most two. Protect unmarked current sole ORs in addition to the permanent sites. Their count B is at most both I=2z−m and low(b*)+1536, where m counts literal deletion rounds. If a single simplification spends a large block, reset before the next count.

Total sole count S≤min(S₀+I,low(b*)+1536+C): above degree b*, every sole is a tracked AND or a new sole OR. The unprotected sole AND count u≤min(S−B,low(2r)+C). Total excess G≤S+k−z−3. Use the source-groups-408.md inequality. The largest possible B remains adverse after substituting the minimum bound on u: it is adverse both on the constant branch and on the branch u=S−B. Geometric protection needs only H=q−9 because batches have width at most eight, separately from the reset degree cutoff.

The accompanying check-finite-recensus-409.py uses width 256 boxes in z and m and width 5000 boxes in T. It uses the largest T in each box for geometry and degree loss and the smallest T for n in the temporary baseline and s in the census. Earlier deletion rounds occupy distinct surplus levels, so the sum of the largest eligible caps bounds T. A trial cap between 3 and 8 always succeeds; the maximum needed was 6 and the total cap sum was 88022. Thus the iteration outspends k while the theta baseline remains, proving s≥ceil(167691·409/100)=685857 at order 409.
