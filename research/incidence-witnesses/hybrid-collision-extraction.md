# Compiling narrow and broad singleton collisions together

Use the scalar circuit, orientations, and sparse star lower bound Nq of collision-cut-cost.md. Query support S(t) is syntactic. A collision is an AND whose parent supports intersect in I_t. Fix integer d≥2, d<N. Narrow collisions have union width ≤d, and k other collisions are broad. Put r=2k+1. Then

Nq ≤ (1+d/2+r(d+1))s + d/2+r(d−1) + (2k²+k−d−1/2)N.

Let H comprise the narrow gates and all their gate ancestors, h=|H|, v=s−h. The output is outside H since its support has size N. Every member of H has width ≤d. Let b be the number of distinct H exits and e the number of exterior raw query labels. Every nonoutput exterior gate sends at least one wire into another exterior gate, so b≤min(h,v+1−e). Let c count H gates with a purely query parent and b_p count purely query H exits. Trace a chosen successor from each of the N−e exclusively internal raw query labels, stopping at the first mixed H gate or pure exit. A pure merger can decrease the number of paths by at most one. A mixed endpoint takes at most one pure operand. Paying the mergers and mixed endpoints from c and pure endpoints from b_p proves c+b_p≥N−e.

Compile the whole zero-query baseline using ≤s−N/2 gates, since distinct query raws touch at least N/2 different gates, all forwarded at that baseline. Cache specialized values of H gates on their supports at total cost ≤d(h−c). A pure parent at a fixed singleton is constant. Elsewhere reconstruct each target as baseline OR a delta supported on its syntactic labels.

For each broad AND, in topological order, reconstruct the delta rows of its two operands. Record their AND J, supported on I_t. Finally reconstruct the output. A target in H or a raw input is immediate from the cache or the input. For an exterior target, seed it with reverse weight one. At an OR copy each weight to both operands. At an AND multiply by the baseline of the sibling and propagate. Join contributions to a common operand by OR. An exterior pass costs ≤3v+1−b−e, from ≤2v products and ≤v+1−b−e merges counted by outgoing fanout excess on the exterior gates and inlets. Exterior payload inputs have zero delta. For each label, combine the raw query weight, weighted H exit specializations on their supports, and weighted earlier J terms on their intersections. A cached specialization may include its baseline since its OR with that baseline is exact. Exit terms cost ≤d(2b−b_p) per pass. Exactness follows by distributing the AND of the two baseline-OR-delta pairs. Only an overlapping pair of delta supports requires the extra product J. The output baseline is identically zero.

Creating broad products costs kN. A product of broad gate i contributes to at most twice the number of later broad gates plus one final pass. Each contribution costs at most 2N, giving at most 2k²N extra. Thus before maximizing the cost is at most s−N/2 + dh + r(3v+1+(2d−1)b−e) −d(N−e) +(2k²+k)N. Substitute the bound on b. The maximum in h occurs at the join (s+1−e)/2, since the left slope is d+r(2d−4)≥0 and the right slope d−r(2d+2)<0. The remaining coefficient of e is d/2−r(d−1)≤0. This gives the displayed result. Cache and baseline are constructed once across all passes.

At q=401 this excludes every circuit of size ≤646424 for the (k,d) values in check-hybrid-collisions.py. With no narrow gates a separate pass costs ≤3s−4N+3 by the AND and excess bounds in query-collision-localization.md, excluding up to ten collisions of arbitrary width. Unrestricted q=401 remains open.

## Disjoint broad scopes

If the full query supports of the broad gates are pairwise disjoint, take r=3 and replace the broad bill by 3N for any number of broad gates. Seed all exterior left operands with weight one simultaneously, and do likewise for the right operands. A seed overrides any other accumulated weight by constant one, so the pass needs no additional gates for seeding. An operand inside H or a raw input is read directly. On a requested label in I_g, all other targets in its batch have syntactic delta zero. Broad ancestors with nonempty query support cannot feed another broad gate by disjointness. Thus the two batched responses give the exact operand deltas on the required labels without jumping through any other broad gate. The products and final weighted contributions total at most 3 times the sum of the intersection widths, at most 3N. The bound is consequently that for k=1, including the q401 exclusion at d=29, independently of the number of broad gates.
