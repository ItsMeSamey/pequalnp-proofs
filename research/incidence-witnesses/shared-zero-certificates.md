# Shared internal gates that vanish under biased deletions

Start from the bilateral sole-use normal form, and delete every raw input independently with probability p. For each shared internal gate g choose any zero certificate Z_g, a set of inputs which, when set to zero, forces its output to zero. The nonnegative shared-gate term in zero-flow.md has expectation at least sum_g (f_g-1)p^|Z_g|, except a possible independent penalty of one for the output. Certificates for different gates may overlap arbitrarily. Every AND other than the reserved sole-use slots may add the probability of disagreement of its parents. If Z forces one parent to zero and disjoint C forces the other to one, this probability is at least p^|Z|(1-p)^|C|. Opposite orientations at the same AND are disjoint events. Raw occurrences above the fanout-two baseline also add p per excess occurrence after reserving the private compensation.

For N=161203, L=646425 and any integer k>=2 the sufficient credit hurdle is
    L-4N + 2N(1-p)(2+E[(k-B)_+])/(k+1) + 4N epsilon + 1 + 2N p^403,
where B is Binomial(402,1-p) and epsilon=2N Pr[B<=1]. Indeed the random surviving vertex count has expectation 2N(1-p), survival is independent of a vertex's own incident-neighbor count, and the bad-event and private compensation bounds follow from soft-degree.md and sole-use-compensation.md. We conservatively budget one entire credit for the output.

Taking p=15/16 and k=19 makes this hurdle strictly less than 3863.701. Therefore fanout excess 4397 supported on internal gates each having a two-input zero certificate suffices, as does fanout excess 6475 on gates with certificates of size at most eight. These are sufficient conditions in the hoisted architecture, with no hypothesis on raw uses, rich stars or AND motifs. They can be combined with AND disagreement and with different certificate widths by summation. They do not guarantee that an arbitrary small circuit has such a profile.

## Dense balanced flag deletions

Use the balanced restriction with all free signs zero. At h=q-1=400 there is no positive-sign reserve to spend. One residual axis point x and the q off-axis points on the one retained pencil line Y survive; symmetrically Y and the q lines through x different from the axis survive. Each nongeneric pole x,Y has degree q, and all other vertices have degree two, with their mutual flag matching determined by intersection. In particular the residual weighted flow applies at k=2 with 804 surviving vertices and gives 1071 gates. The removed population is D=160801 per shore. Every lost sole-use private AND is paid by a one-zero credit except possibly the at most 2h=800 free vertices, since every other deleted vertex has a surviving neighbor (a nonfree removed point on a free pencil line sees the surviving line through x and that point; for points on the axis the surviving-neighbor calculation distinguishes the free axis points and the center O). The center O sees the retained pencil line Y. The same properties hold dually. Thus 646425-1071-4D+800=2950 additional credits suffice.

Every given raw variable survives with probability 402/N by symmetry, so any zero certificate of size at most w is entirely removed with probability at least 1-402w/N. No independence assumption is needed. The shared fanout excess thresholds at w=2,8,50,100,300 are 2965,3011,3371,3931,11713 respectively, even without any total raw fanout hypothesis. At h=399 the corresponding residual bound is 2411, the added-credit hurdle 3216, and the per-gate lower bound is 1-804w/N.
