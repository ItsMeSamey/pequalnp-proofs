# Earlier mixed receivers after random point and query restrictions

Fix c>0, a projective plane of order q, N=q²+q+1, and a binary monotone circuit of size s computing existence of an incidence on arbitrary point masks and masks of at most q lines. Logs are natural except in the entropy bound. Put a=exp(−c), T=⌊√log q⌋, d=⌊(q−1)/(T+1)⌋. Independently select each point into R with probability c/q. Let U be the lines avoiding R, M=|U|. Draw an ordered list of q−1 distinct members of U, with full set W. Use T consecutive increments of length d, denoting their preceding and ending prefixes by V and W_i. Let X consist of points outside R and off W.

Conditional on p outside R, its retained degree into U is binomial with parameters q+1 and (1−c/q)^q, since pencil lines have disjoint remainders. Chernoff and a union bound give degree (a+o(1))q uniformly for all such p with high probability. Also |R|=o(N) with high probability; counting flags gives M=(a+o(1))N. Condition hereafter on such a choice with uniform vanishing errors. For each p outside R, avoidance of its pencil by W has probability exp(−1+o(1)) uniformly, by the hypergeometric product. Changing one sampled line by a swap changes |X| by at most 2(q+1). The exposure martingale (couple completions by transposition) therefore gives |X|=(1/e+o(1))N with high probability. Each p in X retains all its U-neighbors outside W. The total number of available flag-stages is (a/e+o(1))TNq with high probability.

Pin R to one and restrict queries to U. At each stage the three tests are the positive W_i+p+l and negatives W_i+p, W_i+l+(X outside l), all with R added. They are legal since every query misses R. Apply the monotone chain trace of near-unit-feeder-intervals.md on this fixed X. Discard at most 2s distinct flags across the chain admitting a rich traced point feeder (cost at most 2sT assignments). Assign every remaining flag-stage to one oriented AND with point feeder g and receiver h. Each orientation owns one point over its entire assigned history, h(W_i+p)=0 and h(W_i+p+l)=1. In evaluations below R is implicit.

Discard assignments with h(V+p+l)=0. The monotone change on that fixed pair occurs at most once across the disjoint increments. Each orientation owns at most (a+o(1))q lines, so total loss is at most 2s(a+o(1))q.

For each stage discard assignments with h(V+l)=1. Conditional on V and R, define every gate's shifted singleton-line support on U outside V. The proof in earlier-query-hot-feeder-entropy.md applies to these supports: the prefix-code cost is at least H log₂(H/(2sD)) for H discarded flags, D=⌈q^(2/3)⌉. A charged OR rejects W_i+p, so its pre-increment support of size m avoids a uniform d-subset of U outside V. For m≤D²/2 the rich pencils together contribute at most 2m flag charges per physical OR by the two-line intersection bound. Larger supports contribute at most N(q+1) charges on avoidance. Thus expected cost per OR is O(M/d)+N(q+1)exp(−D²d/(2M))=O(M/d). This bound is simultaneous over assignments conditional on the actual increment, even though X uses future queries. Jensen and splitting at s q^(5/6) give E[H]=O(s(q^(5/6)+M/(d log q))). Constants are absolute for each fixed c and all sufficiently large q.

Consequently if s=o(N√log q), discarded assignments total o(TNq) with high probability by Markov. There remain (exp(−c−1)+o(1))TNq assigned flag-stages whose receiver has h(V+p+l)=1 and h(V+p)=h(V+l)=0. If instead s≤c₀ N√log q for a sufficiently small fixed c₀=c₀(c)>0, the transition loss divided by TNq is at most (2a+o(1))c₀, and the expected entropy loss divided by TNq is O(ac₀)+o(1). Markov gives positive probability of retaining at least (a/(2e))TNq by choosing c₀ small enough. The latter constant-fraction assertion is existential.

For both regimes, with high probability all stages and all physical h,p rejecting W_i+p have at most 10(M/d)log q candidate lines l in U outside V with h(V+p+l)=1. Indeed the candidate set, fixed given V,R,p,h, must miss the entire increment, with probability at most q^(−10) beyond this threshold. A union bound over sNT choices suffices. At some single stage there are Ω(Nq) distinct retained flags with O(q(log q)^(3/2)) candidate lines per receiver and point. By discarding orientations serving fewer than κNq/s flags, for sufficiently small κ depending only on c, one retains Ω(Nq) flags and Ω(N) distinct points. For s of order N√log q the resulting per-orientation multiplicity is Ω(q/√log q). The witness concerns receiver behavior on the pinned background R+V; it supplies no unrestricted superlinear lower bound.
