# Thirty-six physical excess gates from iterated dense restrictions

At the start of a round apply the simultaneous sole-OR-spine hoist of sole-or-hoist.md to any current sole-use raws starting in OR. It preserves independent masks through b_i=38−i sites per shore and all singleton flags. The original test proof uses only b_i+1 sites per shore, codegree one, and degree greater than b_i. Thus the literal hoist does not decrease any raw's use count, and it does not increase the gate count. Sole raws in this form have a single AND reader. If any dead or redundant gate is present, an elementary simplification saving at least one gate completes the round without a raw deletion. In particular a strict size decrease due to hoisting itself is also an available surplus round. One may prune and simplify further, tracking all decreases.

Consequently the number S of current sole raws is at most 9843+2(2T+35)≤11497. Literal hoists never add sole raws, and net gate loss from hoists is nonnegative, so it can safely be included in this upper bound on the cumulative number of non-hoist eliminations.
