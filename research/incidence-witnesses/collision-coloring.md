# Colored broad-gate passes

List broad ANDs in original topological order. For gate j write I_j for its two child scopes' intersection and U_j for their union. A reverse pass seeded at all left (or all right) targets of a group produces the OR of the individual baseline transfer weights. These weights do not use any jump values and may be computed before all jump reconstruction. Reconstruct jumps in gate order, not in color order; cycles of colors incur no extra pass.

Color the graph joining j and k when I_j meets U_k or I_k meets U_j. Within a color, each label requested at j is absent syntactically from all other targets. A nonzero increment source on that label carries the label in its syntactic scope, so it has no path to an unintended target. The group's linear transfer therefore has no cross-talk on that request. Separate left/right colorings with arm-specific scopes also suffice. Disjoint intersections alone do not suffice.

For an exact-incidence counterexample choose three lines l1, la, l2 with b=l1∩la distinct from u=l1∩l2. Choose p on l1 outside {b,u} and r on l2 different from u. Use broad AND parent pairs (l1 OR la, (l1 AND p) OR b) and ((l2 AND r) OR u, l1 OR l2). Each of the two gates certifies incidence; OR both with a DNF for all remaining flags. The repeated query labels are {l1} and {l2}. On query l1 with empty point payload, both broad gates vanish, but the pooled left parent is one from the first gate and the pooled right parent is one from the second. The resulting false jump reaches the output. Both broad ANDs have incomparable children.

For point payloads only the maximal negative M_l consisting of all points outside each query line l needs to be protected. All pooled computations are monotone in payload, and on positives they dominate the originals by induction because every desired target is included in its group. For each target j compute every grouped arm's truncated bit on M_l using the exact original jumps indexed strictly before j, plus ordinary raw and boundary corrections. When preceding jumps have been reconstructed exactly on M_l, the computed group arm bit is exactly the OR of these truncated bits over its seed set. For j's own arms the truncation omits no actual predecessor. Therefore require the product of the pooled left and right bits to agree with the true J_j at all required j,l. This certifies the exact-jumps invariant by induction without imposing a color order. It is necessary for that invariant, though masked wrong jumps can still give the right final output. The same test with full rather than truncated bits is sufficient.

When a true jump is zero, a failed product test is witnessed by j, a gate i contributing a left one, and a gate k contributing a right one. Thus with aligned left and right classes the semantic admissibility condition is a hypergraph coloring with forbidden subsets of size at most three, including repeated indices. Demanding that each pooled arm itself stay exact is stronger. For the dual direction, full disjoint line sets may be outside the sparse promise; test instead at all allowed maximal disjoint payloads, or require an implication throughout the disjoint downset.
