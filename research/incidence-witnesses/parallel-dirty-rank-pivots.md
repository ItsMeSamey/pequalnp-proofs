# Many tie-independent high endpoint ranks under one common negative

In PG(2,q), q≥3 a prime power, choose m between 1 and floor(q/2), a set I of m affine x-coordinates, and an injection f from I to its complement. Use all mq points p=(x,y) in those columns as pivots. For each use the construction of dirty-joint-rank-pivot.md with L0 horizontal through p, a_p=(f(x),y), d_p the vertical line of a_p. For its q−1 other working lines use just the affine nonzero slopes through p, with partner ui=Li intersect d_p. Q_p is the OR of the q ordinary nonvertical queries through p and d_p. All other special gates are as in the single pivot. Each gadget has its own physical gates and common inputs are only original raws.

All designated flags are disjoint across gadgets. Each pivot is the point of its own q nonvertical flagged lines. At a partner point with x coordinate f(x), each nonzero-slope line fixes exactly one original pivot on that line in column x; the horizontal and vertical flags of that partner are the two special flags of the single pivot at the same y. Columns I and f(I) are disjoint. Each individual term accepts on native singleton inputs exactly its designated flags, as can also be checked directly from Ti and X. Use one ordinary unshared point-star formula covering the remaining flags. The same per-gadget argument guarantees full-cube soundness. Every native flag is covered.

In each pivot's square on its q−1 working flags all other terms fail on the positive rows. The proper-neighborhood negatives for those flags have L0 active and exclude both p and a_p. The single-pivot argument gives J−I, rank q−1, at its shared g_p and its indicated p input pin for every tie rule. The only shared internal signals are the g_p and K_p. Select all pivot points as payload and precisely the m vertical lines of columns f(I) as queries. This is a legal independent mask and activates all the shared gates. Each shared gate misses some flag and accepts two endpoint-disjoint flags, preventing replacement by a single raw by the positive-signature test. Pinning a single g_p instead to either of its own operands still causes a false output on the pure inputs from the single pivot. This does not prevent more global refactoring.

The residual formula uses E−mq(2q+1) remaining flags and N−mq nonempty point stars: pivots keep their vertical flag; every point in f(I) has no remaining flag; all other points retain their flags. Its size is therefore E+N−1−mq(2q+2). Each gadget uses q+3 gates for Q_p, p OR a_p, g_p and K_p; 4(q−1) for its Ti; 4 for X; q−1 ORs within its own collector. Joining mq gadget outputs with the residual formula adds mq gates. Total size E+N−1+(4q+1)mq. With m=floor(q/2), linear-in-q raw-pin ranks occur at approximately N/2 distinct sites, but the construction pays linear-in-q physical arms per pivot. The ordinary smaller full-cube formula shows that global size minimality is not established for this representation.
