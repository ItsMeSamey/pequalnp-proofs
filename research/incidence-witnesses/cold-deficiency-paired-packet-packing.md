# Packing sharp cold-pool obstructions into one circuit

Work in PG(2,q) for odd prime powers q≥29. Write N=q²+q+1 and E=N(q+1). Use the physical merger and two-shore eligibility definitions of cold-pool-two-shore-matching.md. A packet is a projective image of the labelled affine square template in cold-deficiency-seventeen.md. The nine points consist of four vertices, the center and four directions at infinity. The seven lines are the four sides, two diagonals and the infinity line. Their respective selected point counts are 3,3,3,3,4,4,4. The footprint consists of all 24 incidences between these selected labels. Each image retains its template labelling.

Choose flag-disjoint packet images greedily. A uniformly random projectivity maps each fixed template flag uniformly onto the E plane flags. Thus the fraction of images intersecting any one fixed footprint is at most 24²/E, by a union bound. When a maximal collection has been chosen its forbidden fractions cover all images, so there are at least E/576 packets. On a fixed query line at most floor((q+1)/3) packed packets can use the line since their footprints are disjoint. Greedily pair packets sharing no selected query label until this is impossible. If any packet remains, all remaining packets intersect its seven selected lines in the sense of sharing a label, so at most 7 floor((q+1)/3) remain. Write t for the number of participating packets, counting both members of each pair. Then t≥E/576−7 floor((q+1)/3). This bound is asymptotic and may be vacuous at small orders.

Represent every raw signal x in the construction through one physical gate x OR K, where K is the AND of an incident point-query pair and one further distinct point. Build K directly from raws. Its extra raw slots cannot reach any event along a changing path: K is zero on every singleton flag, both corresponding one-shore deletions, and every singleton-query complement anchor. At an anchor either its query is absent or the incident point is absent. K implies exact incidence on the entire cube.

For each pair use the bijections on both point labels and query labels induced by composing the first packet’s inverse template labelling with the second packet’s template labelling. These bijections preserve all 24 footprint incidences. For each selected point in each packet build its term as its point proxy AND the OR of its own incident selected query proxies in that same packet. Form nine cherries, each the OR of the terms for corresponding template points in the two packets. Join these nine by a binary OR tree. After building all pair trees, OR their outputs with ordinary flag products on proxy labels for every uncovered plane flag. Take the common topological order with all packet trees finished before any completion or output collector. When K is false this is a partition of the full incidence DNF. When K is true incidence already holds and all proxies are true. The output therefore computes exact incidence on the full cube.

Fix a selected line in a packet. In its paired tree every twin term is zero on tests with that singleton query because the query label sets are disjoint. For its singleton-point tests, each of its incident selected points activates precisely its own term. The subtree collecting any set of cherries is cold at the line’s complement anchor. Only selected incident sites can activate it on singleton points. Neither query-only calculations nor individual product terms induce mergers. Cherries also induce none on this query. Earlier packet trees on the same query have touched disjoint point sites by footprint disjointness. In the current tree a join therefore merges on this query exactly when both children contain at least one of its selected incident sites: their internally connected groups have no previous cross-child merger. This reasoning also holds for the corresponding twin query, with precisely the same occupied template leaves. Hence every such address is charged on at least two different labels. The line contributes its selected point count minus one events, for 17 distinct nonexempt events per packet. Different pairs have distinct addresses and, within a pair, the two packets' event labels are disjoint.

Within each packet choose actual witnesses at each merger from the two incident child groups. The selected events on a line connect all its incident sites in a tree, so every selected site on every line appears as some witness. All nine point labels and all seven query labels occur. Each supplies a changing path via its proxy raw slot, using the zero-query versus singleton-query test for the query endpoint. No other point witnesses are active, and the extra reads into K cannot be on a changing path. Thus each packet sees exactly 16 distinct eligible slots for 17 events. Its deficiency is exactly one by the lower bound excluding all deficient subsets of size ≤16 in cold-deficiency-seventeen.md. The 17t events together see at most 2N slots, giving aggregate deficiency at least 17t−2N. Some point occurs in at least 9t/N packets and some line in at least 7t/N. Transitivity permits moving either one to a prescribed label of its shore. All estimates concern the displayed circuit, with no gate-minimality assertion.
