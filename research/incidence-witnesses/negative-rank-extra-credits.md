# Which activated shared gates add to the raw zero-flow charge?

Fix an independent set P of point and line inputs, set its neighbors to zero, and permit every bit in P either sign. The residual incidence function on surviving raw bits is independent of those signs. Let z_g mark gates evaluating zero with P all zero and all surviving raw inputs one. Let u_g mark gates accepting P alone. Thus a z mark certifies a constant-zero wire at the lower phase and a u mark a constant-one wire at the upper phase. Take the standard low-phase gate savings R0 and define K to consist of gate slots neither of whose parents is z-marked, for which the gate or at least one parent is u-marked. At least R0+|K| slots can be deleted simultaneously. In topological order maintain a signal between its low- and high-phase functions on each residual input. Usual zero-parent simplification if a z-marked parent exists satisfies these bounds. Otherwise one can set a u-marked gate to one, set an OR with u-marked parent to one, or bypass an AND with u-marked parent to its other operand. Each replacement stays inside the interval. At the output both extreme functions coincide on the required masks. Count gate slots before optional further simplification. This gives a union-of-slots accounting valid even for overlapping or nested gates.

Consequently every shared AND marked u gives at least one credit in addition to raw uses and private sole-use AND compensation: if it is z-marked its own excess f-1 is at least one in R0, separate from its own exactly-one-zero term if any. Otherwise neither parent is z-marked and its own slot belongs to K. An activated shared OR has the same guarantee unless exactly one parent is z-marked and its gate is not z-marked. Other K destinations may still count in this case. Extra ANDs or fanout credits previously explicitly charged must not be charged twice. This conditional bound measures architecture beyond semantic rank alone.

## Exact incidence obstruction

Pick R on a line L0 and a point O outside L0. Select m<=q points p_1,...,p_m of L0 other than R. Let S be the OR of all q+1 line variables through R. Make the prefix gates g_i=S OR p_1 OR ... OR p_i. For distinct lines M_i through O avoiding R, put T_i=O AND M_i. To any exact ambient incidence circuit append the guarded terms T_i AND g_i in an output OR. All additions imply a genuine flag, so the resulting circuit is exact. The guard and prefix of each arm are incomparable, and the arm is individually observable within the added collector at p_1,O,M_i. The entire collector is redundant relative to the ambient exact circuit. It costs q+4m gates, with no assumption about the ambient architecture.

For P_j={O,R,p_m,...,p_(m-j+1)} all new T_i remain zero even in the high phase because every M_i is zeroed. The S-parent is zero in the low phase. Every newly activated prefix has a raw p_i that is already low-phase zero; its downstream prefix, if any, also has a low-zero p_(i+1), and its guard consumer has a low-zero T_i. Slots affected by opposite-phase activation had therefore already simplified at the lower phase. The added gadget yields no extra constant-parent slot from the high-phase signs, at any stage, compared with the corresponding zero phase. The shared prefixes i<m have fanout two and zero width q+1+i; in reverse order their values give a triangular rank m-1 on the nested negative inputs. Thus even linear zero width and linear rank cannot unconditionally be counted as extra slots beyond zero flow. Stronger implications require a condition such as minimality or the distribution of gate types and surviving parents.

For a,b<=30 pinned points/lines use the explicitly bilaterally hoisted circuit. Every deleted nonpin raw vertex has a surviving neighbor: opposite pins remove at most b inputs of the opposite shore and each same-side pin removes at most one additional neighbor by codegree one, totaling at most a+b<402. Every surviving vertex keeps degree at least 402-max(a,b), since it has no neighbor among the opposite pins. Thus private AND reimbursements yield at least 2|D|-a-b baseline slots aside from the following additive credit E: all shared low-zero fanout excess, all nonprivate exactly-one-low-zero ANDs, the distinct outside-baseline K slots, and any direct raw fanout exceeding two on deleted variables. The disjoint-cone count gives |D|>=403(a+b)-binom(a,2)-binom(b,2), since separate unions of neighborhoods lose at most one per same-side pair. With n=2N-|D| and k=402-max(a,b), the sufficient E threshold is 646425-4N+1+floor(2n/(k+1))+a+b. Its maximum using this n upper bound for all 961 pairs (a,b) is 3310, attained for the estimate at (0,30) or (30,0). This conditional criterion is independent of the total raw fanout and may be applied to an early dirty rank stage.
