# Shared OR banks and dirty conjunctions on the original plane promise

Suppose the physical scalar circuit has the following layered description. A binary OR DAG over raw point and line variables supplies banks (allowing raw inputs as banks). A binary AND DAG takes these banks as its leaves. A binary OR collector takes designated product outputs. Both producer layers permit arbitrary fanout, and product outputs can themselves be reused inside other products. Each designated product T has at most t distinct bank leaves after flattening AND, regardless of repeated occurrences. Assume 2≤t≤q. Trim zero or vacuous terms; an individual raw or nonempty bank alone cannot be a sound output term since raw singletons are negative. Thus the number of distinct product outputs is at most the physical AND count A. Write O for the physical OR count. Each product output is sound since the scalar collector is OR. There is no soundness assumption on any proper AND fragment or OR bank.

For a native flag (p,l) accepted by product T, fix payload {p}. Some bank of T not containing p has its entire query support inside star(p). Otherwise, in every bank not containing p choose one nonneighbor query; at most t≤q selected queries together with p make T true on an independent mask. This includes the case all banks contain p, by taking no queries. The witness bank contains all lines paired with p by T. Dually for l, evaluating the complement of l with just query l forces a bank not containing l whose point support lies inside l; this bank contains every point paired with l by T.

Call a flag paid if any chosen witness for it has ≥2 query lines or ≥2 points on its corresponding shore. Across all products the total number of paid flags is at most 4O, as follows. For one point p project all point raws to zero in the original OR bank DAG. Each query support of size ≥2 contained in star(p) has a binary OR construction within that star. Fold empty and unary forwarding after projection (also tied inputs if desired). In each resulting bank cone, follow its support down to one-label frontier wires. Every label in the bank belongs to a frontier slot of a gate whose support has size ≥2 in star(p); each physical such gate has at most two input slots. Therefore the union of all these banks contains at most twice the number of these physical OR gates. Gates with two distinct lines in their support cannot be charged to any other point by uniqueness of joins in the dual plane. This counts ≤2O flags for the query projection across all points; the point projection gives ≤2O more. Projected binary gate IDs are inherited from the original physical graph and never charged twice on the same shore.

Assign each remaining flag to one product accepting it. Both its witnesses have singleton support, so it is the only edge at either endpoint in that product's native accepted-flag graph. The assigned remaining flags are a matching. Each needs a bank whose query support is its singleton line, hence h≤t assigned flags per product. In a private product forest with no AND gate belonging to two designated product cones, sum h≤2A since each product with k≥2 distinct leaves uses ≥k−1 private AND gates. This gives E≤4O+2A even when t grows up to q.

With arbitrary product sharing there is an additional logarithmic bound. Among the h matching flags form a conflict graph on flags, joining two if one of their crossed point-line pairs is incident in the original plane. If the induced plane graph on the h points and h lines has e edges, it has at most h(1+sqrt(4h−3))/2 edges by counting point pairs on lines, and the conflict graph has ≤e−h edges. A random ordering retains vertices earlier than all neighbours as an independent set of expected size Σ 1/(deg+1) ≥h/(1+average degree) ≥h/sqrt(4h−3). Thus it contains a matching of size m≥sqrt(h)/2 with NO cross incidences in the original graph. For each of the 2^m transversals selecting one endpoint of each such flag, the selected endpoints are independent and use ≤m≤h≤t≤q queries. A sound T rejects every transversal via a false bank. Every bank of T hits every one of the m singleton positive flags. Such a bank can be false on at most one transversal: the selected endpoint must avoid its support on every flag, uniquely fixing the choices if any are possible. Therefore 2^m≤t, giving h≤4(log₂ t)².

Combining gives E=N(q+1)≤4O+min(t,4(log₂ t)²)A. In particular width≤100 implies Nq≤100s at every order q>100; arbitrary sharing at both producer layers is allowed. Width≤q implies s=Ω(Nq/log²q) on unbounded orders in this class. The proof neither constrains the number of raw occurrences at banks nor counts occurrences of a shared AND as gates. It does not cover an OR gate mixing products below a later AND except when an independent exact structural rewriting supplies such a layered realization at a proven cost.
