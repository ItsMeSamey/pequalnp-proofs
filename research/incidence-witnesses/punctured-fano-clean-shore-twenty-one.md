# Exact bounded-promise cost when one shore has unique paths

Suppose each raw on one shore of punctured Fano has a unique path to the output. The other shore has arbitrary fanout; depth and gate types are unrestricted. On the original two-hot, two-cold promise the minimum is twenty-one gates. The row-star construction uses ten hot ORs, six private ANDs and five collector ORs.

Call the unique-path shore cold. A binary cold merger cannot be AND. Choose a leaf y in one arm and a leaf z in the other, with incident flags p+y and q+z. On p alone the root rejects. When y is added, its unique path forces this merger to toggle; hence the z-arm already passes on p. The y-arm likewise passes on q. Both arms pass on the pure hot input p+q. All downstream side inputs needed on p+y are independent of y by the unique path property and still pass on p+q. This contradicts rejection on at most two hot raws. Therefore all five mergers are ORs.

As in five-cold-lattice.md, incomparability forces one private unary AND for each leaf before its first merger. Their hot-only operand footprints contain the associated stars, which connect all six hot sites; at least five hot gates are required. In a candidate of size at most twenty, the five mergers and six designated private ANDs leave at most nine gates for the hot computation and extra unary gates. If j extra unary ANDs have nonfull singleton-hot support, then j≤4 and there are at most 9−j hot-only gates. Each such mask contains a whole star.

For a given star s at cold leaf c, and a pair T of distinct hot nonneighbors, c+T is a legal negative. If every extra AND filter on the path has singleton-hot support meeting T, they all pass on T, and the designated private filter must reject T. In particular its singleton support U is disjoint from T. To relax the tree, test the sufficient premise that every enumerated extra support containing s meets T. This permits additional extraneous filters to help, while full-support extras pass automatically. Every U contains s.

For each multiset of j extra supports the checker chooses an admissible U for each leaf. All these supports together must be produced with at most 9−j hot gates, starting at six singleton masks and applying union/intersection. It saturates required masks producible from current supports, then branches on one other new producible mask per available helper. Each distinct nonsingleton requirement costs a gate. Saturation preserves completeness since these required masks must be computed anyway. Enumerating both shore orientations returns 1,93,672,56,0 cardinality-feasible tuples for j=0 through 4, respectively, none realizable. All tests used by the proof have at most two hot and at most one cold active raws, apart from the stated promise for the matching upper. The unrestricted twenty-gate decision remains open.
