# A linear-overhead pinned rectangle with no one-parent gate resolutions

Use the residual construction of pinned-fresh-rectangle-obstruction.md. For definiteness take q≥7 and 4≤r=q−h≤q. There are r surviving pencil rows T through O, A consists of their points other than O, and U consists of T and all q² nonpencil lines. Every p∈A has all its q+1 neighbors in U. We construct a circuit of size at most the ordinary row-star size plus O(N) in which B=(OR A) AND (OR U) creates |A|(q+1) positive flags and |A||U|−|A|(q+1) nonflags. Every gate in the A×U decoder is indispensable against both single-parent resolutions on the actual residual tests. Global minimality is not asserted.

**Sites and reserved flags.** Take a non-O point e on a pencil row outside T. Take three distinct nonpencil lines m,m₁,s through e, so their pairwise intersections are outside A. Choose distinct J,t₀ in T. Put a=s∩t₀, b=m∩J, and J*=(J−{O}) together with the points W_t=m₁∩t of all other t∈T. This meets every line of U, while each A-point lies on a line of T. The intersection J*∩m is just b.

Use the two anchors (a,t₀),(b,m) and their enlarged terms Hₗ,Hₚ from the preceding note. For every t∈T−{t₀} write Z_t=x_a OR t, so Hₗ is the AND of t₀ and all Z_t. For every p∈J*−{b} write V_p=m OR x_p, so Hₚ is the AND of x_b and all V_p.

Choose different z₀,z₁ on m in two distinct T rows other than J. For every t≠t₀ choose a point d_t∈t−{O} distinct from j_t=s∩t, from m∩t and from m₁∩t. For p∈J*−{b} choose z=z₀ if p∈J, and z=z₁ otherwise. Put g_p=pz. All g_p belong to U: if the join is a pencil line then it is the common row in T. They are all distinct, since within each family p lies on a line not containing z and a common line of the two families would have to equal m, which contains no such p. None equals s.

Add the following terms, with brackets denoting actual parent order. The displayed two-operand OR signals are shared physically between the indicated terms.

    D_t = [x_(d_t) AND Z_t] AND G_t,     G_t=t OR s
    E_t = x_(j_t) AND G_t

Their singleton flags are respectively (d_t,t) and the pair (j_t,t),(j_t,s). For every p choose a U-line l_p through p differing from g_p and every line already assigned to that point by the preceding t-terms. There are at most three exclusions. Add the dual terms

    D_p = [l_p AND V_p] AND G_p,         G_p=x_p OR x_z
    E_p = g_p AND G_p.

Their singleton flags are (p,l_p) and the pair (p,g_p),(z,g_p). All reserved flags listed, including the anchors, are distinct. For the only potential cross-family collisions, z lies on m and is neither j_t nor d_t; a point p on J has g_p≠J,s, while a point p off J is W_t and hence neither j_t nor d_t. The anchors cannot occur among the additional flags: a is on s in row t₀ and not in J*; b is excluded from the p-terms, and all g_p differ from m. Within each family the rows for t are distinct and the dual g_p are distinct. The selected l_p cannot cause another collision since p is neither z₀ nor z₁.

Let C be the OR of Hₗ,Hₚ, all four sets of helper terms and the ordinary row-star terms on all A×U flags not yet reserved. Omit empty row terms. A nonflag acceptance of D_t requires the true flag (a,s), and one of D_p requires the true flag (z,m). The two-point or two-line terms E are honest stars. The only additional acceptances in C beyond incidence are the pure guards all T or all J* in the anchors. They are harmless when both shores are nonempty. Thus B AND C computes incidence on A×U on the full cube, including when the original h pins are applied. All requests outside A can be handled by the ordinary row stars. The extra helper terms and their shared filters cost O(q), and B costs O(N).

**Single-parent resolutions.** Each top-level term in C has at least one unique singleton flag; its OR-collector edges cannot be dropped. Every raw in either pool of B has a flag, every literal in an honest remaining row has one, and the guard OR G_t or G_p has separate private flags at its E star. Within a Z_t, omitting x_a loses the anchor (a,t₀), and omitting t loses (d_t,t). Within V_p, omitting m loses (b,m), and omitting x_p loses (p,l_p). All these are real flag losses in the entire residual decoder.

Every AND factor in Hₗ is necessary: to exhibit a negative with only the t factor failing, take query T−{t} and a point on t, including the case t=t₀ for the direct factor. Both shores are active. In Hₚ use X=J*−{p} and a query that hits J* only at p, including p=b. A point p=W_t off J uses t; p=b uses m. For p on J, all other J points rule out just the line J, and the at most r−1 off-J representatives forbid at most r−1 further lines through p. Since q+1>r, a private line through p exists and belongs to U. Therefore removing any AND subtree in either anchor produces a legal false positive.

For D_t write P=x_(d_t) AND Z_t. Replacing P by x_(d_t) falsely accepts d_t with query s. Replacing P by Z_t or D_t by G_t falsely accepts query t with a point in another A-row. Replacing D_t by P falsely accepts {a,d_t} with a U-query missing both. In each case B is true. The dual witnesses for D_p are explicit: replacing its inner AND by l_p falsely accepts z with query l_p; replacing that inner AND by V_p or D_p by G_p falsely accepts p with a query missing p. Replacing D_p by its inner AND falsely accepts {m,l_p} with a point in A on neither query. These choices exist for q≥7,r≥4, since a U-line is available off any two sites, and each of m,l_p covers at most q points of A. The required query sets have size at most two. Each honest E star or nonempty ordinary row star also requires both operands: choose a point and a nonincident query with only the retained shore's operand true. For an ordinary row term its query set is contained in its row neighborhood. For E_p, to retain its point operand alone choose either p or z with an unrelated query; to retain g_p alone use an off-line point. The analogous witnesses apply to E_t.

Finally, replacing B by its query operand falsely accepts T and no A-points, while replacing B by its point operand falsely accepts J* and no U-queries. Dropping B at the final AND accepts either pure example, and dropping C accepts a nonincident A×U pair. All witnesses are compatible with the original h pinned lines: they use at most r free queries, and every selected A-point avoids the pins. This gives single-parent irreducibility and shared nonraw reconvergence at O(Nq) total physical cost for the specified pin. It does not determine the minimum cost.
