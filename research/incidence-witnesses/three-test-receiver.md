# Sample-wide receiver replacements

Use one common original sample: all singleton incident flags as positives and all independent masks with ≤3 points and ≤3 lines as negatives. At an AND mark a bypass if one operand's accepted-negative set is contained in the other's; forward the operand with the smaller set. At an OR mark a bypass if one operand's accepted-flag set contains the other's; forward the operand with the larger set. Count each original gate only once.

Apply all replacements in topological order. On negatives the new values stay at most the old: the selected AND operand originally equaled the gate there, and selecting an OR operand is always at most that OR. On positives the new values stay at least the old: AND projection can only increase, and the selected OR operand originally equaled the OR there. Thus the rebuilt circuit is correct on this same sample. Its size is at least 4N−4 without assuming sole-use normalization: retain a full dominating theta and eliminate all other raws in one batch by the three-by-three to two-by-two lemma; each has degree q+1 and a retained neighbor. The number of marked physical gates is therefore at most δ=s−(4N−4), at most 1616 under the q401 subtarget. This separate inequality cannot simply be added to the cold ledger.

Each independent three-by-three mask belongs to a three-pole core at q401. Choose L avoiding the at most three selected points and the at most three intersections of selected lines. Choose X containing all intersections of those lines with L, choose O on L outside X, and choose rows covering all selected points. Supplement unused positions arbitrarily. The same construction with six poles embeds two arbitrary three-by-three masks in one physical core: avoid at most six points and 15 pairwise line intersections when choosing L. The masks individually retain independence; their union need not be independent. Thus every unmarked AND admits two opposite negative disagreements observable in a common six-pole core, and every unmarked OR exhibits both orientations of positive flag disagreement. This does not equate witness multiplicity to physical savings. Independent audit confirmed simultaneous replacements, original-sample lower bound and three-pole coverage.
