# Owner-to-tap induced matching as the sampling parameter

Use exactly the physical gates, active eligible owner set E, and marked tap convention of many-tap-donor-or-dag.md (SHA-256 d2dd63659ce14073e72feb028ee7d96011bdc9e8d89f0f9d5798f2ab27fc066e). Form the bipartite graph with E on the left and the physical marked vertices on the right; adjacency is designated reachability, so each right neighborhood is A_v. Let d be its maximum induced matching size.

The class of unions of the A_v has VC dimension exactly d. If a set of k rows is shattered, each singleton trace must occur, so each of the k rows has a tap containing it and none of the other k. These k row-tap edges form an induced matching. Conversely any induced matching supplies singleton traces on its chosen rows; arbitrary unions realize all subsets there, including the empty union.

If d=o(q/(r+1)), choose t so d=o(t) and t=o(q/(r+1)). Since the root tap covers E, d≥1 and t tends to infinity. For iid uniform sampling of t rows, the empirical measure converges uniformly over the union class with vanishing error in probability. For example the symmetrized expectation of the maximum absolute deviation is bounded by twice the Rademacher maximum over at most (et/d)^d distinct sample traces (Sauer's bound for t≥d). The exponential-moment bound for bounded Rademacher sums gives expectation at most 2√[2(log 2+d log(et/d))/t], tending to zero even when d/t tends to zero slowly. Repeated sampled rows do not enlarge the number of traces. These are bounds on the class conditional on fixed sample rows in the Rademacher step; no independence assumption between taps is needed.

Deleting all endpoints of donor conflicts still loses o(1) empirical mass in expectation. Therefore one sample has both vanishing uniform discrepancy and vanishing removed fraction. The union of ALL unhit tap neighborhoods has true mass o(1); the full-path physical accounting of the earlier note removes k distinct good row ANDs and at least k−1 distinct collector ORs, with k=(1−o(1))|E|. The same residual gives s≥(4+2α−o(1))N without any restriction on the number of taps.

Quantitatively, for any fixed α>0 and fixed gap η>0, if s≤(4+2α−η)N and |E|≥αN along a family of growing orders, then d=Ω_{α,η}(q/(r+1)). Otherwise a subsequence with d(r+1)/q→0 would satisfy the preceding lower bound and contradict the fixed gap. Without a fixed coefficient gap, the little-o implication alone does not provide a uniform positive induced-matching constant.
