# Removing sharing through components with private variables

Use the producer-rooted read-once components of anchored/README.md. A live variable v is the sole local ordinary leaf of a nonoutput component whose exit evaluates to one on v alone. All other variables are called nonlive; let H be their count. Only nonempty producers with live variables can have a nonzero singleton support, and then that support is precisely their own variable. Every incidence neighbor of a local variable is exterior, as established by the OR-LCA lemma.

The following construction retains internal sharing only through producer components without ordinary local variables. Replace the exit supplied to the other components as follows.
* A live producer supplies its raw ordinary variable.
* Any other nonempty producer supplies zero.
* Empty producers still supply their original formula evaluated on the replaced entering signals.

The replacements are monotone lower approximations on every input: if v is selected, its live producer's exit is one, and if v is absent the replacement is zero. For each nonempty nonoutput component add a detector: evaluate its local formula at these replaced inlets with all its own ordinary variables set to zero. The scalar result is the OR of all detectors and the substituted output component with all its own ordinary variables set to zero. The resulting circuit takes only live variables. Each raw leaf occurrence is an original port emitted by a live producer. Nonempty producers never feed their computed detector values into another component, so only empty producer exits can fan out among the internal gates.

On any positive incidence whose two endpoints are live, the two owning producers' replacements are exact because their local variables are selected. If any other nonempty producer's actual bit differs from its replacement, choose the first in topological order. Its replacement is zero and its actual value is one, and no local variable of that producer is selected. All its replaced inlets equal the actual inlets (empty computations are unchanged on matching predecessors), so its detector accepts. Without such a producer, all inlets reaching the output are exact. All output-local variables are nonlive and therefore unselected on this assignment, so the output computation accepts.

Consider any live edge-free input (P,T) with at most q−1 entries on each side. The substituted output is zero by lower approximation and correctness. For a detector in C, remove all C-local variables from (P,T), an operation that leaves its inlets unchanged: no local variable influences an entering port. The detector is bounded by the actual exit on the resulting input. Choose any locally owned variable v. All q+1 neighbors are exterior and there is a neighbor y that avoids all the remaining selected vertices of v's side, because v was removed, each other same-side vertex shares at most one neighbor with v, and at most q−1 were selected. Augment the input by y. It is still edge-free, and it has at most q lines. If the exit of C were one, this legal negative would dominate the accepted (v,y) on the exit and every outside input, which is impossible. Thus every detector vanishes on the required negatives.

## Counting with bounded sharing of empty components

Let F be the total number of outgoing port occurrences of live producers; then F≤(2N−H)+e=s+1−H. Let M≥1 bound the number of directed port paths from the exit of each empty producer until their first entrance into a nonempty component or the output component. Parallel port uses count separately. All such target components are terminals. If there are no empty producers, take M=1. The restriction does not bound the number of paths or uses of nonempty producers. In particular arbitrarily many internal nonempty gates may each have unbounded fanout.

Unfold only the empty producer components. Each live raw port entering a terminal contributes at most one leaf occurrence. A live raw port entering an empty producer component contributes at most M leaf occurrences because that component is a tree and its exit has at most M routed paths to terminal formulas. Thus the detector OR, after constants are folded, is a formula with at most MF ordinary leaves.

Also (q+1)H≤MF. Each nonlive variable has a distinguished port-only AND sibling that accepts every one of its q+1 neighbor singletons (see anchored/weighted.md, including its inactive-owner extension). They are disjoint subtrees within the terminal components. Trace these selected siblings down through empty producers, cutting at every nonempty producer. Each cut nonempty port has singleton weight either zero or one, and only live producers can contribute one. Every unfolded empty subtree obeys subadditivity of singleton support. The total of all sibling weights is therefore bounded by the number of unfolded live leaves, at most MF by the very same path counting. This calculation uses the unpruned terminal forest, so it does not require every such sibling to survive pruning in the detector OR.

Start with any perfect matching of the regular bipartite incidence graph; it exists by Hall's theorem. Remove its edges, then remove all nonlive vertices. At least Nq−(q+1)H test edges remain in a bipartite graph J of maximum degree q. Although J need not be induced, the proper-neighborhood negative for each e=(p,l), with points Adj_J(l) minus p and lines Adj_J(p) minus l, is independent even in the original plane. Any cross incidence would complete a 4-cycle through e. Both sides have at most q−1 variables. Thus the detector formula passes every positive and every proper-neighborhood test required for J.

The formula must have at least |E(J)| leaves by the Karchmer–Wigderson proper-neighborhood rank argument from formula-leaves.md. For clarity, at each v the output-v matrix on its incident edge square is all ones except t_v diagonal zeros, where t_v counts edges whose diagonal return is the other endpoint. For degree ≥2 its rank is at least t_v. For degree one, at least one occurrence is required by essentiality (positive edge vs rejected opposite singleton), so it still has ≥t_v leaves. This covers arbitrary degree profiles. The ranks are bounded by the number of occurrences since each occurrence traces a disjoint rectangle. Summing t_v gives |E(J)|.

Consequently Nq ≤ 2MF ≤ 2M(s+1−H). In particular when H≥1 and M≤50 the desired bound Nq≤100s holds at every order. If H=0 the stronger formula bound Nq≤MF≤M(s+1) applies, implying the same conclusion for M≤50 and s≥1. With no empty producers we have Nq≤2(s+1−H); thus arbitrary internal sharing through components owning ordinary variables still costs at least Nq/2−1 gates.
