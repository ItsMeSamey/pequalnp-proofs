# Ten-copy lift with punctured-pencil exceptions

Let q>100 be an integer plane order. A monotone binary circuit H of size s is required to accept every singleton flag. On maximal negatives (X_Z,Z) with exactly q distinct query lines it must reject whenever Z is not a punctured pencil (q concurrent lines from a pencil of q+1). No rejection guarantee is required on a punctured pencil or on any smaller query set. A circuit of size at most 10s+9 then computes exact incidence on arbitrary point masks with at most q selected lines.

Two-color the query lines so that each pencil contains at least two lines of each color. Under independent fair colors the chance that a particular pencil fails is 2(q+2)/2^(q+1). By a union bound over N=q²+q+1 pencils the failure probability is below one for q>100 (already below one at q=101, and the bound decreases thereafter). In particular a monochromatic set of q distinct lines cannot be concurrent.

Use the five point blocks of five-copy-maximal-negative-lift.md. Each has at least q distinct disjoint filler lines, among which one can choose three not concurrent. In each affine quadrant there are at least floor(q/2) outside horizontal and at least floor(q/2) outside vertical lines; two outside horizontals and one outside vertical are not concurrent. For the enlarged small/small quadrant these same outside lines still avoid all its infinity points. For the block consisting of the two horizontal and vertical infinity points, take all q lines of a third direction and one line of a fourth direction. This supplies q+1 lines disjoint from the block and a nonconcurrent triple. The needed directions and counts exist at the stated orders.

For every block B and color i, give a copy of H the selected points in B and the selected lines of color i, setting every other raw to zero. OR the ten outputs. Every flag survives in one copy. For an independent original mask (P,Y), consider any copy and let Y' be its selected lines. If |Y'|=q, it is monochromatic and not concurrent, so Z=Y' is permitted. If 2≤|Y'|<q and Y' is concurrent at O, choose a filler avoiding O; such a filler exists by the nonconcurrent triple and cannot already be in Y'. Add it, then pad with arbitrary fresh fillers to size q. A nonconcurrent Y' of size below q needs only this padding. If |Y'|≤1, add the entire nonconcurrent triple; the resulting set has at most four lines and can be padded to size q. At every padding step the available pool of at least q fillers suffices even when some original queries were themselves fillers. The extra lines are only comparison labels for the input to the original H and may be of either color.

In all cases Z is a nonconcurrent q-set extending Y', and P intersect B lies in X_Z by independence and filler avoidance. This copy's input is dominated by a required negative of H and therefore rejects. Each copy costs at most s and nine binary ORs suffice. In particular any full-promise lower bound L implies s≥(L−9)/10 for this punctured-pencil exception model. The coloring proof and block construction use no Desarguesian assumption.
