# Five cold leaves after a degree-two pin

Take the punctured-Fano incidence graph (delete one incident point and line in Fano order two), and then delete either degree-two cold vertex. Keep the other shore as six hot inputs. Suppose the five remaining cold inputs each have a unique path to the circuit output, and all four binary mergers of those paths are OR gates. If hot singletons reject and the hot/cold pairs realize precisely the remaining neighborhoods, the circuit needs at least seventeen binary gates.

For a cold leaf with star A, the singleton-hot masks of the hot operands of all unary AND gates on its path intersect to A. If all pass, setting the cold leaf to one forces the output to one along its OR mergers and unary gates. If one fails, the cold leaf cannot change the rejecting hot-singleton baseline. Each leaf needs a private unary AND before its first merger. Otherwise its star contains the star of any leaf arriving from the sibling branch, since it passes every filter above the merger whenever that sibling leaf does. This contradicts incomparability of the five stars.

Designate one private AND per leaf. Each designated hot operand contains its star. The four three-element stars cover the six hot variables and form a connected hypergraph by intersection. Consequently the hot gate cones connect all six hot variables in their underlying undirected graph: each hot variable in a singleton support has a path to the relevant hot operand. A binary gate can join at most two previously disconnected components. Thus at least five hot gates are necessary.

With sixteen gates, subtracting the four mergers and the five designated ANDs leaves at most seven slots for all hot gates and extra unary gates. Let j count only useful additional unary AND filters, discarding any whose hot singleton support is full. Every other extra AND on a cold path has a support G containing the star of each leaf beneath it. Thus j≤2, and the hot support budget is at most 7−j.

The checker enumerates all multisets of j nonfull masks each containing some requested star. For each star A, intersect all the enumerated masks that contain A, obtaining I (full if none). A designated hot operand U for this leaf satisfies U∩I=A. Indeed U contains A, and every member of U outside A must be rejected by some actual additional filter on its path, one of the masks included in I. This allows filters from unrelated branches to help and therefore tests a superset of realizable configurations.

For each choice of one such U per star, let R be the distinct U and G masks. Each is nonraw since it contains an entire star. If |R|≤7−j, allow up to 7−j−|R| additional hot masks. Starting with the six singleton masks, repeatedly adjoin all producible required masks by pairwise union or intersection. If required masks remain, branch over the choice of any other producible nonempty new mask, spending one helper. This exhausts every topological support computation: a producible required mask can always be made available immediately, and an empty or already present mask cannot generate a new mask absent a further new operand.

For each of four labelled pins (two per shore), respectively 1, 224, and 27 tuples survive the cardinality check for j=0,1,2. No tuple is attainable. Run `python -B check-five-cold-lattice.py` to replay.
