# Tie-independent pivot rank with no jointly safe shared coalition

For q≥2 choose p on L0, a distinct point a on L0 and d through a missing p. For each other pencil line Li (1≤i≤q) choose a point ui on Li other than p. Symbols are raw selectors. Define Q as OR of all queries through p and the extra query d, g=(p OR a) AND Q, K=p OR L0. Define Ti=(g OR ui) AND (K OR ui) AND Li and X=g AND (a OR L0) AND (K OR d). Use the OR of these terms and an unshared point-star formula covering all flags except the two per Ti (p,Li),(ui,Li) and three per X (p,L0),(a,L0),(a,d). Use binary gates.

Only g and K are internally shared. Both miss some flag and both activate on the legal nonedge {p,d}; no nonempty shared coalition is jointly safe. Both accept endpoint-disjoint native flags, so neither meets the positive-side requirement for a raw-proxy replacement. The ordinary completion is a forest of formulas with one final collector and uses no intermediate gate of the special terms.

Ti is safe: unless ui is present its acceptance uses g,K and Li, hence p is present or both a,L0 are present. X is safe: if L0 is present it meets p or a from g. Otherwise a must be present, and K OR d forces p or d; d meets a and in the p case every query in Q meets p or a. The omitted flags reach their designated terms. This proves full-cube correctness. Replacing g by its left operand accepts the pure input {p,a} through X, while replacing it by Q accepts pure {L0}. Every OR operand in Q, p OR a, K and the Ti and X clauses is used on a native flag that loses its designated term if that operand is omitted.

For ei=(p,Li) and its proper-neighborhood negative Si, restrict to 1≤i,j≤q. Only Ti accepts ei. On Sj, g=0, Q=1, K=1 because L0 is active, Li is present iff i≠j, and ui is present iff i=j. On the diagonal the only false Ti clause is Li, so KW returns that line without visiting g. Off diagonal the only false clause is g OR ui, whose only true operand on ei is g. In g only p OR a is false on Sj and its only true operand on ei is p. Thus every memoryless tie rule gives J−I on this q-square at g and at that physical p pin. The rank is q.

The ordinary completion costs E+N−2q−5 for E=N(q+1): E−2q−3 flag slots and N−2 joins of N−1 nonempty point stars. Q, p OR a, g and K cost q+4, the Ti cost 4q, X costs four, and the final joins cost q+1. Total E+N+4q+4 is only 4q+5 above the standard point-star circuit. The example is globally nonminimum; it refutes only inference from shared-gate dirtiness, incompleteness or absence of jointly safe coalitions to bounded ranks.
