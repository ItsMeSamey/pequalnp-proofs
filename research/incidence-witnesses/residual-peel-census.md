# Repeated sole-owner census on a punctured projective plane

Consider monotone binary AND/OR circuits on the incidence graph of a plane of order q, with N=q²+q+1 vertices per shore. We use the protected iteration of protected-dense-signature-401.md. At a counting stage let n count surviving sites, z the surplus already spent, and k=floor(cN) the contrary initial allowance above 4N−4. Then physical size s≤2n−4+k−z. The fixed dominating theta and three-neighbor invariant maintain the baseline 2n−4 on the actual circuit. Assume total sites deleted in the iteration are ≤βN for some fixed β<1. Constants below can depend on β. The temporary experiments in this note never change that circuit.

## Residual two-by-two lower bound

For a codegree-one bipartite graph on v vertices with minimum degree d≥3 on both shores, any circuit accepting the singleton flags and rejecting independent two-by-two masks has size at least
```
2v−1−floor(2v/(d+1)).
```
Here is the weighted-port argument, including the missing-join case. Cut at shared signals (including raw signals) to obtain read-once components with ordinary local raws and incoming ports. Count a shared raw as a one-local zero-gate producer. All paths from an ordinary local raw to the exterior pass through the component exit. Any two local raws x,y specialize the final output to their OR. For same-shore raws choose one neighbor of each and turn those vertices on; at most two opposite vertices suffice, even if x,y have no common neighbor. For opposite-shore raws choose a neighbor of each so the two chosen neighbors are nonincident, distinct from x and y: choose a neighbor of x other than y, then a neighbor of y other than x avoiding that first vertex (at most one further exclusion by codegree one). With x,y initially absent the chosen input is independent. Toggling either gives a flag. Thus their least common ancestor is OR. An incident local pair would also specialize to AND on the empty background, impossible. In particular all neighbors of a local raw are exterior.

A component containing a local raw x has singleton support of size at most one at its exit: if some other raw a activates the exit, choose a neighbor y of x such that a+y is independent. This is possible by codegree and d≥3. The outside on a+y dominates that on x+y, and the exit is true there, a contradiction. Write B for the number of nonoutput components with a unique local raw. Each of the other v−B local raws x has a private AND before its first common ancestor with another local raw, or before the output in a single-local output component. Otherwise x alone forces that ancestor, which is OR; a neighbor of another local y in the opposite child that is not a neighbor of x witnesses the contradiction, by comparing x plus that neighbor against y plus that neighbor. In the single-local output case x alone would be accepted. The port-only sibling tree at the private AND must activate on every neighbor singleton, because otherwise the x path is blocked. These sibling trees are disjoint.

Give each signal weight min(d,number of activating singleton raws). This is subadditive at either gate. Write e=s−v+1 for total fanout excess excluding the output (prune dead gates and fold constants first), and e₀ for excess of producers containing no local raw. Every shared producer of fanout f offers f outgoing port weights. Subtract the exit weight once for each empty producer, which itself consumes at least that weight from incoming ports. The disjoint sibling trees consequently give
```
d(v−B) ≤ v + e + (d−1)e₀,       e₀ ≤ e−B.
```
Indeed nonempty producers have exit weight at most one and their number is at most v. Writing a=e−B and Δ=2v−1−s yields v−B=Δ+a and (d+1)Δ≤2v−a, as asserted.

Take d=floor(q^(1/2)). Starting with the current surviving graph, repeatedly peel any vertex of degree ≤d. During this temporary procedure the number peeled is O(q), uniformly over stages. Otherwise consider the first j=ceil(Cq) peeled vertices for a sufficiently large constant C. Each had ≥H=q+1−d original neighbors in the union of original deletion sites and those j vertices, of size T≤βN+j. Choose H per vertex. The chosen sets pairwise intersect in at most one (opposite-shore sets are disjoint). Cauchy gives j≤T(H−1)/(H²−T)≤(β/(1−β)+o(1))q, contradiction. The remaining graph is nonempty and has minimum degree >d. Zeroing the peeled variables of any proposed two-by-two circuit and applying the previous inequality gives size ≥2n−O(q+N/d)=2n−o(N). Peeling is used only for this lower bound. The census below uses the unpeeled circuit; all but O(q) vertices already have degree >d there.

## Sparse warm/cold census for one shore

Choose r=floor(q^(1/4)). At a census suppose the circuit is correct on all independent masks with ≤2r+2 sites per shore, and on all singleton flags. Disregard O(q) owners of degree ≤d and all protected sole-OR owners, which number o(N) by the reset rule below. For each remaining sole raw x on the query shore the first reader is AND. Follow its private path to the first shared signal or output gₓ. Define Uₓ(T) as gₓ with x alone active on its shore and opposite payload T. It accepts all surviving neighbor singletons: toggling x at each such singleton changes the output, so the entire private path must toggle.

All the Uₓ, on payloads of size ≤r, have one common circuit of size at most s. At zero queries each gate of each tracked path is zero on such T: append any surviving neighbor of its owner, then toggle that owner to create a positive from an independent mask. The path must toggle there, hence its baseline was zero on T by monotonicity. In the common zero-query circuit therefore fold all these path gates to zero. Along a private path, when x is turned on, an OR propagates x's current value unchanged (its sibling baseline is zero), and an AND multiplies by its sibling baseline. Once two tracked paths meet, they cannot first meet at an AND: on a flag of one owner the other incoming signal is unchanged from zero-query baseline since the active owner's path has not yet reached it. No common surviving neighbor is required. To compute all path products with one multiplication per tracked AND, process the union of paths backwards, propagating the product of downstream sibling factors to each predecessor. A tracked path stops at a fork or output and merged paths have the same remaining suffix. At an AND there is at most one tracked predecessor, so at most one replacement AND is needed. At an OR copy the suffix unchanged to each predecessor. The outputs at the tracked raws are the desired products. This costs at most the folded gate count, including arbitrary sharing of baseline siblings.

An owner is cold if for some set X of at most two surviving query vertices containing x, every payload of ≤r labels avoiding all neighbors of X has Uₓ=0. Choose one such X. In the common product circuit mark every gate whose singleton support lies in the union of those neighborhoods and contains >2 neighbors of x. Uₓ's output is marked, since an outsider singleton would contradict coldness. If its connected marked ancestor region has a<r gates, there are ≤a+1 boundary signals by binary fanin and connectedness. Those with ≤2 neighbors of x cover at most 2r<d neighbors. Select an uncovered neighbor y. Every boundary signal active on y is heavy, unmarked, and so accepts some outsider singleton. These ≤r outsiders jointly dominate y on every boundary signal, making the root true and contradicting coldness. A physical gate can be charged to at most two distinct cold owners: its >2 neighbors of any such owner could not lie in another owner's chosen union unless that heavy owner belongs to the chosen X, by codegree one. Thus at most 2s/r=o(N) owners are cold.

Every other tracked owner x is warm. For each negative query set J of size at most two containing x there is a witness Tₓ of size ≤r avoiding J that activates Uₓ. The forks of distinct warm owners x,y are distinct: at zero queries force their putatively common fork to one. The forced output accepts each singleton neighbor L of y because it dominates the flag of y downstream. Choose L outside x's star by codegree and large degree. Warmth gives a witness Tₓ avoiding x. With x active the original circuit on Tₓ∪{L} has that fork true. Its output dominates the forced zero-query computation on L, hence accepts this independent input, a contradiction.

In a temporary experiment suppress each warm raw along its private path and inject it by OR at its fork. The distinct warm paths are disjoint because merged private paths end at the same fork. Each begins with AND. When the path includes an OR, its first AND and its first subsequent OR are both folded by the zero substitution, paying at least two gates before adding at most one OR. If it contains only ANDs, its zero path folds entirely and the injected fork becomes its raw, saving at least one. For an active warm flag, setting its own fork true suffices; other injections have zero raw, and any exterior computation at that flag is maintained because all paths from the suppressed active raw went through that fork. Unmarked flags are likewise preserved. On a negative with queries J and payload T, both of size ≤2, extend T by the witnesses of the ≤2 active warm queries. The resulting independent payload has size ≤2+2r and makes every active injected fork originally true. Induction bounds all edited signals by original ones there; monotonicity then gives rejection on T. The edited size is therefore at least 2n−o(N) by temporary peeling. The number of warm owners is at most s−2n+o(N)≤k−z+o(N).

Repeat the temporary experiment separately in the opposite direction using the same pre-edit circuit. This proves that the total sole count S≤2(k−z)+o(N), uniformly. The two edits do not need to commute.

## Epochs and gate count

Set block=ceil(N^(3/4)). After each full block of cumulative surplus, before the next counting round perform a literal simultaneous sole-OR hoist for all such owners not in the permanent geometric protection. Start with symmetric independent-mask capacity b=2r+2+ceil(k/block)+2; the initial bilateral normalization has symmetric capacity at least q−1. Each reset consumes at most one unit of capacity: to prove an off-spine sibling zero on an independent mask, append a neighbor of the spine owner avoiding the other selected vertices, then toggle the owner. Codegree and degree larger than b suffice. Distinct spines do not merge since each off-spine sibling rejects every other singleton. Move their siblings to ORs at the output, bypassing each spine by its raw. The number of gates does not increase. Positive flags survive by taking the earliest lowered signal, whose original true sibling is now included at output. Literal resets create no new sole ORs outside the permanent set; other raw uses are preserved. Charge any subsequent pruning separately. There are at most floor(k/block) resets. Reserve permanently all geometrically marked sites and three neighbors as they first reach degree ≤b+max(t)+2, in addition to the theta. Because b=o(q), their number is O(q) by the same Cauchy calculation. Thus throughout, permanent sites plus newly arising sole ORs number p=o(N). Census can be performed at each counting stage directly: the still-available promise always covers 2r+2. Alternatively a census at the latest epoch changes the upper estimate by O(block) before the next reset. Sole ORs are excluded from the warm/cold experiments.

For clarity, the local token inequality from protected-asymptotic-surplus.md is
```
(n−p)(t−7) > (3t−1)u + (t+1)(6G+8p+2),
```
where u≤S counts unprotected sole AND owners and G≤S+k−z−3 is fanout excess. If it holds, an elementary gate simplification or a batch of ≤t zero deletions earns one unit beyond the two-per-deleted-raw baseline. All real operations preserve the remaining promise except the explicitly budgeted resets. With S≤2(k−z)+o(N) the leading sufficient condition is (2−T/N)(t−7)>(24t+16)(c−z/N), with uniform o(1) error. For c=149/2500 split [0,c] in 100 equal surplus intervals and take the integer caps in check-recurrent-census-stages.py. Each previous deletion round occupies a distinct prior surplus level. Thus the actual T/N is bounded by the integral of the caps plus O(1/N). The script checks both endpoints of each affine inequality exactly and bounds T/N by 9983/10000+o(1), strictly below one. Positive minimum margin absorbs all uniform errors. Producing more surplus than k contradicts the theta baseline. Consequently liminf s/N≥4+149/2500 for this scalar promise.
