# Simultaneous negative activation of equally behaving shared signals

In a size-minimum binary monotone scalar circuit for the incidence promise, take any nonempty antichain G of shared nonroot internal signals, each with at least two distinct physical gate consumers. Suppose all members have the same values on all native positive flags. There exists a single promised independent input on which every member of G is one. The witness may depend on the antichain. This imposes more than separate acceptance of possibly incompatible negatives at each shared signal.

Proof. If there were no common negative, pin all outgoing uses of G to zero, while preserving their original upstream cones. Flags where the gates are all zero are unaffected; all other flags make every member one. The conjunction of the retained members appended by one OR restores every lost flag and rejects all negatives. This costs at most k=|G| gates. Denote the set of distinct direct gate consumers by F, of size f. All f are removed upon constant folding, and none can be needed to preserve the selected gates by incomparability. If f>k this contradicts minimality. Otherwise at least 2k distinct gate/consumer incidences and at most 2f force f=k, with two distinct G operands at every consumer. In particular k≥2, as a singleton selected signal has at least two consumers. Every consumer in F now computes zero, including OR consumers. Some consumer in F is not the original root and therefore has a downstream consumer by trimming. Its immediate successor is outside F, whose input slots are already occupied by G, and outside all retained ancestor cones by incomparability. This additional row is removed by folding the zero input. Thus at least k+1 rows disappeared, again a contradiction.

In fact equality of flag signatures can be replaced by the weaker requirement that every native flag lost by the double-use-zero operation makes all members of G one. Hence every jointly negative-rejecting antichain of shared gates in a minimum circuit must lose some flag at zero on which at least one selected signal was originally zero. This is a condition on the original scalar DAG and its promised tests, not an assumption on internal row receivers or unfolded proof multiplicities.
