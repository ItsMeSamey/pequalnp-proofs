# Exact seventeen gates after pinning a degree-three cold site

Take the punctured-Fano singleton-hot and hot/cold pair tests with one degree-three cold site removed. If each of the five remaining cold variables has a unique path to the output, the exact minimum is seventeen gates. This requires no assumption on the merger types. The upper bound uses two hot OR gates for each of three triples, one for each of two doubletons, five private ANDs, and four OR mergers.

For the lower bound assume sixteen gates. Each cold pair sharing a hot neighbor must meet at an OR merger, since that hot singleton is rejected and adding either of the leaves activates the output. Only the two doubleton leaves have disjoint neighborhoods. Thus an AND merger can only form a cherry of these two leaves. If every merger is OR the [five-leaf lattice relaxation](five-cold-lattice.md) excludes all eight labelled pins. Here the three remaining triples cover and connect all six hot inputs, so five or more hot-only gates are necessary. The replay check-five-cold-triple-or.py examines 1, 308, and 35 relaxed tuples for zero, one, and two extra filters per pin.

At an AND cherry, each of its two input branches needs a unary OR: otherwise that branch stays zero with just the other cold leaf selected, forbidding its neighborhood. Designate one OR on each branch. Each of the other three leaves has a private unary AND. A sibling ordinary leaf must pass all filters above their first merger; if the sibling subtree consists solely of the cherry, either cherry leaf must likewise pass every such filter. Since no ordinary star contains another leaf's neighborhood, a private AND is necessary. The designated hot operands of the three ordinary leaves contain their triple stars. Their cones connect the six hot variables, so at least five hot gates are necessary. After paying the four mergers and five designated unary gates there are at most two spare unary gates in the sixteen-gate budget.

If neither spare is inside the two cherry branches, the AND gives (y OR U) AND (z OR V). On hot singletons the activated y term is transmitted wherever V passes, filtered by any subsequent common AND masks. The rejecting baseline ensures no other hot additive can create an output. The z term similarly starts with support U. Exchanging the designated supports thus gives exactly the same relaxed feasibility test as the all-OR case.

If there are two spares inside, the three ordinary triple supports are unfiltered and at most five hot gates remain. Closure under binary union and intersection from the six singleton masks, with the three triples required, finds no such five-gate computation. If there is one spare inside either branch, there may be one effective outside filter G or none. Other unary ORs above the cherry or on ordinary paths play no role on the tests, by baseline rejection. In the no-filter case allow six hot gates; when G is present allow five. For each ordinary triple A enumerate private operands U such that U=A or U∩G=A. Include all U and the optional G as required hot masks. Use the same closure search, permitting unrestricted auxiliary masks within budget and continuing to enumerate them even after the requirements are available.

On the extended cherry branch write (a,b) for its baseline and active singleton-hot masks. With one designated OR and one extra unary gate, the possibilities using hot operands u,v are (u∪v,full), (u∩v,v), and (v,u∪v). The other branch is a single OR with some operand w. Let W be full or G according as the optional outside filter misses or covers the cherry. The two transmitted stars must therefore be a∩W and b∩w∩W, in either order, with a∩w∩W empty by baseline rejection. The executable check-five-cold-triple-cherry.py exhausts these possibilities. Each pin gives 27 candidate hot states counted with their parameter choices and none realizes the cherry. Both replays together take less than two seconds on the test machine.
