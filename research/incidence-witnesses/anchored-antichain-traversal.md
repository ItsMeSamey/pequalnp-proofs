# Anchored negative traversal at a physical cut

On native flag e=(p,l), compare the scalar to n_l consisting of query l and every point outside l. The only raw whose bit is larger on e is p. Follow a distinguishing path: at an OR choose a high operand on e, and at an AND choose a low operand on n_l. From a distinguishing gate these preserve both properties. Thus for ANY antichain G of intermediate gates, either some such traversal bypasses G all the way to p, or it encounters a g with g(e)=1 and g(n_l)=0. Fix one traversal per e. Write B for the point labels reached without encountering G, and let M be the distinct flags whose selected crossing g rejects the bare point p. Such a g also rejects the bare query l by monotonicity since n_l contains it, so the crossing is genuinely mixed on this native pair.

For all other covered flags the crossing g accepts bare singleton p. Its bare point support is contained in l since it rejects n_l, and it rejects the entire bare point complement of l. Let I denote the point labels singled out by crossings with bare support exactly {p}. For crossings with ≥2 support points, their line ownership is unique by plane codegree one. For each l assign each distinct covered p to one crossing at l; intersecting raw labels across the assigned gates do not multiply this count. With k_l distinct crossing gates and d_l assigned points, the zero-complement token routing of mixed-trace-receiver-charge.md costs at least d_l−k_l physical ORs in their common producer DAG, distinct for different l. This uses actual shared upstream gates and the same all-query-zero bare simulation for every crossing: n_l suppresses the bare complement by monotonicity. Hence, writing O_up for distinct OR producers in the union of the cones of G and E=N(q+1),
E ≤ (q+1)|B union I| + |M| + O_up + |G|.
No receiver semantics have been assumed. The inequality is a dichotomy for every original scalar DAG, not a proof that its raw-bypass or singleton-channel cases are expensive. The mixed flags are counted distinctly, not charged to distinct gates. A single mixed feature can separate the singleton-query sample cheaply while accepting other negatives.

More generally compare e to the maximal payload at any ≤q queries Y containing l. Again the only upward raw discrepancy is p. The same physically distinguishing traversal must cross G at a signal rejecting that actual negative or bypass to p. No line uniqueness conclusion for rich crossings under this larger Y is asserted.
