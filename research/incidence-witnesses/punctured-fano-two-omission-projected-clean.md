# Nineteen gates for 198 pairs of omitted maxima

Start with the twelve inputs and sixteen flags of punctured Fano. For all 198 two-maxima omissions satisfying the direction and separation conditions of punctured-fano-eighteen-deletions.md, a circuit accepting the flags and rejecting every other maximal negative has at least nineteen binary monotone gates. This also reproduces the bound for every single omission.

Assume a minimum eighteen-gate circuit. As in punctured-fano-any-twenty-maxima-nineteen.md, the exact fanout ledger provides seven units. The guarded first-fork conditions force at least six distinct singleton-hot owners. With only six forks a cold shore is clean, requiring at least nineteen gates already on the singleton/pair tests. Seven hot owners are impossible by the seven-fork clamp: all of its ordered witnesses are among the starting conditions, including hot-hot singleton isolation. This leaves six hot owners on one shore and one unowned fork X of fanout two. All cold raws meeting a fork meet X first. The empty group is already clean.

For a nonempty set S of cold raws first meeting X, use either the direct singleton/mixed-degree pin argument or the projection of punctured-fano-unowned-fork.md. In the projection, force the two readers' X inputs to zero and OR the original signal X into the scalar output. After simplifying both original readers, the new circuit costs at most seventeen gates. Every flag still accepts. The checker retains certified negative maxima as in the unowned-fork note.

The relocated circuit has a clean cold shore. For c in S its only path up to X was unique, and its sole surviving extension from X now leads directly to the new terminal OR. For any c outside S the original path to the old output was unique and did not pass through X. Deleting wires and bypassing simplified gates cannot create a second such path, and c cannot reach the new X input. If some c became inessential, its required flag and the retained hot singleton would already contradict one another.

Whenever every singleton and every opposite-shore nonflag is covered by the certified maxima, apply the clean-shore nineteen-gate lower bound immediately. If just one cold site lacks coverage of some nonflag, pin it to zero. Its flag and a hot singleton ensure essentiality, so at least one gate disappears. The remaining five cold sites still have unique paths, exact hot/cold pair neighborhoods and rejected hot singletons. Both the degree-two and degree-three five-cold results require at least seventeen gates, contradicting the remaining budget of sixteen.

Run check-fano-projected-clean.py. Over both shore orientations the one-omission proof uses the old projection in 2,362 profiles and a direct pin in 284. Among direction-supported two-omission profiles, 20,868 use the old projection, 3,304 a direct pin, 704 projected exactness, and 72 the projected single-site pin. These alternatives exhaust 198 × 2 × 63 choices. The twelve other double omissions fall outside the original directional assumptions.
