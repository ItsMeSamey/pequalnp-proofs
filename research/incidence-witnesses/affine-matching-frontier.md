# Exact raw projections on each unrestricted affine matching slice

Use the affine part of a projective plane with an affine parallel class defining q point blocks P_x of size q (its parallel lines' affine points). For each of the other q directions, use the q affine lines of that direction as a block L_a. All P_x and L_a are disjoint within their respective shores. Each restriction P_x × L_a induces a perfect matching by unique affine intersection. This holds in every projective plane after choosing its line at infinity, including nonclassical planes.

Set all raws outside one selected pair of blocks to zero. On these 2q free bits, the scalar circuit computes the matching on the full cube: every line subset has size ≤q, so the original promise rejects every independent mask and flags force every positive by monotonicity. A wire in this restriction is semantically mixed if it depends on a point and a line input. From the mixed output trace backwards through mixed wires, stopping at one-shore or constant parents. This constructs an actual set of distinct mixed gates with a unique designated output, allowing arbitrary sharing. The incoming boundary wires from one shore can be treated as inputs to the remaining monotone circuit even if their original computations passed through mixed gates.

For p∈P_x compare point masks {p} and P_x minus {p}, fixing the line mask to the singleton matched line of p. The first output is one, the second zero. Since line-only and constant boundary wires do not change and the remaining mixed circuit is monotone, at least one point-only boundary wire is high in the first mask and low in the second. Its restricted function on all inputs is exactly the raw p, by monotonicity and one-shore dependence. Distinct p require distinct boundary wires. Reversing shores gives a distinct line-projection wire for each line in L_a. Thus every restricted mixed cone has at least 2q distinct projection wires and at least as many incoming slots. With k mixed gates in the cone, at least k−1 more slots connect internal mixed gates to a mixed reader: each nonoutput mixed gate lies on a retained path to the output. Consequently k≥2q−1.

Across q² block choices this gives ≥q³ wire–block incidences for exact point projections and ≥q³ for exact line projections. It gives ≥q²(2q−1) mixed-gate–block incidences. A physical gate may occur under multiple restrictions. If the output is an OR of structurally block-local modules and boundary terms that vanish on the affine block slices, then each appropriate module must itself compute the full matching: other modules see pure or empty masks and the global OR rejects all such masks. In that architecture no physically mixed gate belongs to two modules, even if pure preprocessing is shared. Thus ≥q²(2q−1) distinct mixed gates are necessary there, and q AND plus q−1 OR per isolated matching attains the per-module count. The architecture restriction is necessary for this last addition of gate counts.
