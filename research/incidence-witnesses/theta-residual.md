# Exact size of the dense pencil residual

At h=q−1 the balanced flag restriction leaves a bipartite theta graph with nonadjacent poles x,Y and q paths x−lᵢ−pᵢ−Y. Its monotone binary incidence function has exact circuit size 4q, already on the two-by-two sample for q≥2.

The expression consisting of the OR of all pᵢ AND (Y OR lᵢ), together with x AND (ORᵢ lᵢ), has 4q gates. For the lower bound retain x,Y and the pair p₁,l₁. The other 2q−2 raws will be deleted. For each deleted raw v with exactly one use initially at an OR, follow its initial private OR spine to the first non-OR consumer or fork, including a branching OR as the exit. Its off-spine sibling signals do not depend on v. None accepts any other generic singleton u: take a neighbor y of v such that {u,y} is independent and y≠u. For same-shore u the private neighborhoods {pole,matching neighbor} distinguish them, and for opposite-shore u use the other neighbor if necessary. The independent assignment u+y then turns positive when v is added. If its sibling accepted u the spine exit would hide this change. For the same reason every such sibling rejects each retained pole singleton: for same-shore pole u use the opposite pole y neighboring v, and for opposite-shore pole use the other neighbor. The test uses at most two raws of either shore. These singleton rejections also show that the hoisted spines cannot merge. Additionally every such sibling rejects {x,Y} since adding v to this independent pair immediately gives a hit.

Hoist all those spines as in sole-or-hoist.md: replace their exits by the corresponding raws and collect all off-spine siblings by OR at the output. This does not increase size. All positive singleton flags survive by the earliest-changed-signal argument. The retained four singleton negatives and the pole-pair negative survive since every appended sibling already rejects them and bypasses only decrease the other old signals. On the literal hoisted circuit every deleted raw has at least two uses or a sole private AND as first reader. Initially sole AND readers remain AND readers; an occurrence transferred from another variable's removed OR spine is retained in the appended collection. In particular no deletion of an unused gate is necessary before the count.

Set all 2q−2 deleted raws to zero and retain all four others. The restricted function depends on all four and passes the three positive pair tests forming x−l₁−p₁−Y, the four singleton negatives, and the negative pair (x,Y). It requires at least four gates on those tests alone. Three binary gates with four essential raws make a read-once formula. At a root OR, each accepted pair would lie within a single child (since the singletons reject), contradicting connectedness of the positive-pair graph. At a root AND each accepted pair instead crosses the children, as every child is zero at the empty input. Thus the split is the unique bipartition. Each child's two singletons are accepted in it, so the forbidden cross pair (x,Y) would be accepted.

Evaluate the deletion phase with the four retained raws set to one. Each deleted sole raw v has an AND sibling true at that phase: its flag with the surviving neighboring pole is positive, its pole singleton is negative, and at its sole AND that sibling is independent of v. These private compensating ANDs are distinct, as none can have two deleted raw operands and a surviving sibling. The restricted output is nonzero. Zero-flow conservation now counts at least two removable slots per deleted raw: two raw uses when available, or its one use plus the exactly-one-zero credit for its compensated AND. This is disjoint from the at least four residual gates. The original size is at least 2(2q−2)+4=4q.

## Application at order 401

In the bilateral hoisted normal form the dense balanced restriction deletes 160801 raws per shore and yields this exact 1604-gate lower bound. Every deleted variable except the 400 free pins on either shore has a surviving neighbor. Only sole free pins can lose the standard private compensation. Their expected number is 400(H_P+H_L)/161203 by symmetry. With H_P≤6461 and H_L≤3382 the target extra-credit hurdle is therefore at most

    646425−4·160801−1604 + 400·9843/161203 = 1641.423864… .

Any combined expected extra credit of at least 1642 suffices. Distinct available terms are fanout excess of low-zero shared gates, raw-use excess above two on deleted inputs and nonprivate exactly-one-zero ANDs. A shared gate with a zero certificate of w raws contributes its excess with probability at least 1−402w/161203. For w equal to 2, 8, 10, 50, 100, 200 and 300, sufficient corresponding total excesses are respectively 1650, 1675, 1684, 1876, 2187, 3275 and 6517. These are thresholds on the actual gate profile.
