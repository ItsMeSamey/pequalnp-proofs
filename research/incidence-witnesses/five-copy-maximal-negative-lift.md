# Five copies lift exactly-q maximal negatives to the full sparse promise

Let H be a monotone binary circuit of s gates on the point and line inputs of any projective plane of order q≥2. Suppose H accepts every singleton flag and rejects (X_Z,Z) for every q-element line set Z, where X_Z comprises all points outside its union. Then a monotone binary circuit of at most 5s+4 gates accepts exactly the inputs containing a flag whenever at most q lines are selected, with no restriction on the point payload.

It suffices to cover the point universe by five blocks B, each disjoint from at least q distinct geometric lines. Compute an OR of five copies of H, retaining only point inputs of their own block, with all query lines available to each. A positive input contains a flag with its point in some block; that copy accepts by monotonicity. On an independent (P,Y) with |Y|≤q, choose q−|Y| fresh filler lines disjoint from B: at least q−|Y| of its q available fillers are outside Y. The enlarged query set Z has size exactly q. Every point of P intersect B avoids both Y and all fresh fillers. Thus the input of this copy is coordinatewise below (X_Z,Z), where H rejects, and the OR rejects. Each copy may use a different Z in this proof; no filler is added to the physical circuit. Substituting zero outside each block and folding constants costs no gates. Four final binary ORs suffice.

Fix an infinity line, so its removal gives an affine plane with q+1 parallel directions and q lines per direction. Choose distinct horizontal and vertical directions. In each family partition its q affine lines into a small set of floor(q/2) and a large set of ceil(q/2) (designate the halves arbitrarily if equal). Every affine point belongs to the quadrant defined by its two sets. Initially each of the four affine quadrants misses at least 2 floor(q/2) distinct projective lines from the two outside families and also misses the infinity line. This gives 2 floor(q/2)+1≥q fillers. Enlarge the small/small quadrant by all infinity points except the horizontal and vertical directions. The outside lines there have exactly those two excluded infinity points, so they remain disjoint from this block. Their number is 2 ceil(q/2)≥q without using the infinity line. The fifth block contains just the two excluded infinity points. All q lines of any third parallel direction miss this block. A third direction exists even at q=2. These blocks partition all projective points.

This reduction applies to exact rejection of every q-line maximal negative. A distributional error estimate alone cannot be substituted for that hypothesis. Any lower bound L on the original sparse promise implies s≥(L−4)/5 for these maximal-negative tests.
