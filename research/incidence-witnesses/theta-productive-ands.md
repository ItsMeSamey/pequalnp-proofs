# Original AND gates with few accepted flags

Let A_g be the original singleton incident pairs activating g and W_g the event that g accepts a residual independent negative on a uniformly chosen full theta. Every warm AND survives in the untrimmed middle network, since only warm OR boundaries are bypassed. Its rebuilt value on each positive is at most its original value. If W_g holds but no member of A_g survives, g belongs to the positive-dead gates in theta-positive-dead.md. No cold source is counted.

Each original flag (p,l) occurs in exactly 3q² of Nq² theta choices. The cases x=p, Y=l and the matching arm contribute q² each. The last chooses one of q other Y through p and one of q other x on l. Thus additional expected physical credit beyond the source-cover ledger is at least

    sum over original AND g of max(0, Pr[W_g] − 3|A_g|/N).

This counts fanout-one gates too. It is a lower bound on part of the positive-dead savings, never an additional charge for the same gates. The corresponding OR bound uses the event that both operands are warm. Independent audit by /root/incidence/audit verified the original AND transfer and all flag counts.
