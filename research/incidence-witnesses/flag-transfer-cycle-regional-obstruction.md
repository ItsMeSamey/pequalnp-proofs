# A locally irredundant formula hiding all query-pool gates from regional marks

In PG(2,q), q≥2, order all N=q²+q+1 points cyclically as p_i with no three consecutive collinear. For existence, identify points with the scalar classes of nonzero elements of GF(q³) and take successive powers of a multiplicative generator α. The vectors 1,α,α² are independent over GF(q), so multiplying by any α^i gives the required cyclic triples, including wraparound.

Let b_i be the line through p_i,p_{i+1}. Its predecessor b_{i−1} is distinct. Form a private binary query OR Y_i of the q lines through p_i other than b_{i−1}. Form the term

T_i = (p_i∨p_{i+1}) ∧ (p_i∨b_i) ∧ Y_i

and OR all terms in a binary tree. No internal gate is shared. The exact full-cube function is incidence: any accepting term contains either p_i with an incident query or the auxiliary flag (p_{i+1},b_i). Every native flag (p_i,l) activates exactly one term. If l=b_{i−1} it activates T_{i−1}; otherwise it activates T_i. In particular the successor does not also cover a flag already assigned to its predecessor. All accepting parses have at least three raw leaves, so the two-leaf envelope is empty. Every flag still admits a three-leaf parse, and this example does not defeat the four-leaf bound.

Forwarding any single gate to either parent breaks correctness on the original promise. The two guard ORs in T_i each need their exceptional literal for the private flag (p_{i+1},b_i); each needs its p_i arm for some assigned line other than b_i. Every query OR branch has a private flag of owner p_i, and each outer OR loses an assigned flag if either child is dropped. Omitting the first, second, or last product factor respectively accepts the legal negatives consisting of b_i alone; p_{i+1} and any eligible line different from b_i; or p_i alone. Forwarding any AND deletes at least one such factor and cannot restore rejection. Thus even at q=2 the formula is irredundant under every single-parent forwarding.

Under the one-line projection used for regional-cold-or-packing.md, every query-pool node is constant as a function of the payload. If reached on the cold trace it must be false and therefore empty-support, so it is never marked. Across ALL line regions the marks lie among at most 3N−1 gates (two guard ORs per term and the outer ORs). Every feasible set of regional weights therefore has total mass at most (3N−1)/q, and for H empty even the exact weighted number of distinct paid ORs is at most 3N−1. For q≥300 this cannot meet Nq/100. The circuit costs N(q+4)−1 gates: each term uses q−1 query ORs, two guard ORs and two ANDs. An ordinary star formula costs N(q+2)−1, so this is a control within 2N additional gates of that explicit formula, not a lower bound on actual minimum size.
