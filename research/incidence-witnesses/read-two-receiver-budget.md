In the bilateral DAG of dominating-theta.md let E count raw uses above two, and T count nonsole inputs all of whose readers are AND. For any deleted such x pick a retained neighbor y. Comparing pure y with flag x+y changes the output. The earliest changed raw reader has its other operand already true on y (all other change must pass through such a reader). Hence that reader has exactly one low-zero operand at the theta restriction. Its unique zero operand identifies its deleted owner, so neither another deleted owner nor a sole-private compensator charges that AND. The credit adds to raw surplus and low-zero internal fanout. The marginal deletion probability is 160800/161203. Below target this gives (160800/161203)(E+T)≤1616, hence E+T≤1620. With H≤8316, at least 322406−8316−1620=312470 raw variables have exactly two uses including an OR. For a mixed read-two x with OR sibling B and AND sibling A, additionally charge x whenever it is deleted, a surviving singleton a≠x activates B, and a surviving neighbor y of x is independent of a. On a+y the OR reader is true. Adding x changes the output, so its AND reader must change, and A was already true on a+y since the other channel did not change. The resulting exactly-one-zero AND is distinct across all types of deleted owners by its unique zero input.

For an exactly-twice x with both readers OR, every two alternative singleton witnesses a,b≠x of the two siblings must form an incidence edge. Otherwise choose a neighbor y of x adjacent to neither (at most two neighbors excluded, by codegree one). The independent mask a+b+y then activates both readers while dominating x+y outside raw x, forcing the output true. Both are actual monotone evaluations, including for nested readers. Consequently, if both alternative sets are nonempty they form a complete incidence biclique, and one side has size at most one.
