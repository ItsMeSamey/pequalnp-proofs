# Two-cold marker depth and pinning credit

Use cold-marker-transfer.md and fork-pinning-267.md at order 401. For each 2-cold line x take its chosen J and the complementary-zero extraction cone, with only one rejecting parent traversed at each reached AND. For each of its 402 singleton labels choose a path down through accepting children until the first wire with at most two singleton points on x. Unfold the union of these paths and suppress unary occurrences. Every leaf represents one or two assigned labels since the stopping wire is thin; all branch nodes are true binary ORs of the physical baseline. Distinct occurrences carrying a common label along its path cannot use the same physical gate, since the original circuit is acyclic.

Let D be the sum over labels of depths in the compressed trie. Split all two-label leaves into a binary fork, yielding a full binary tree on 402 individual labels at a cost of at most 402 in total depth. The minimum external path length for 402 leaves is 3508. Hence D≥3106. At a branch, a visiting label p is eligible when both operands accept some point of x distinct from p, as in the 267-point pinning theorem. If ineligible, the child assigned p carries no other assigned label and must be a leaf after compression. Each such singleton leaf accounts for at most one ineligible label at its parent; there are at most 402 overall. Thus at least 2704 distinct eligible (gate,p) pairs are certified for this x.

For any physical OR charged on x its singleton support is contained in the union of at most two lines (it rejects the chosen complementary payload) and has more than two points on x (the traversal stopped at all thinner gates). Therefore a physical OR is charged for at most two x. Choose, for each gate, the x for which its number of eligible certified pairs is larger, retaining at least half the total. As in fork-transfer.md, these physical ORs and their singleton witnesses survive the literal initial spine hoist used in the pinning proof. The resulting sum of eligible counts for one chosen line per gate is at least 1352 times the number of 2-cold lines. Since 2258·1352≥3052338, 2258 such lines suffice for the target. In particular any below-target circuit has at most 2257 2-cold sole-read lines. The warm size credit then implies that 5471 dirty sole-read lines with an AND on their private paths suffice.
