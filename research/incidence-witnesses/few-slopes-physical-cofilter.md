# Shared physical incidence checking for r occupied slopes

Let 1≤r≤q=2^k. A binary monotone circuit over PG(2,q) preserves every incidence flag and is exact on arbitrary payloads and query sets supported on at most r ordinary affine slopes. Arbitrary vertical and infinity queries may occur simultaneously. Size O(N r² log² q); construction is nonuniform. There is no limit on the number of labels per occupied slope.

Take m=2r bins and h=ceil(3r log₂(2q)) independent random maps of the q slopes to [m]. For any fixed r-set S and a∈S, the probability that a shares its bin with another member of S is at most (r−1)/m<1/2 per map. There are at most rq^r choices of S,a, so there exists a family such that every a is isolated in S by one of its maps. Every smaller S extends to an r-set.

For each hash i and bin j, run the ordinary-slope shear from parallel-slope-physical-cofilter.md, reading every point and only query labels in the bin. Let C_ij be its output. For each slope a compute its activity A_a, then output the OR over a of A_a AND the intersection over i of C_{i,h_i(a)}. Add all projective boundary incidences exactly once. A genuine affine flag at a makes every chosen bin accept, so the circuit preserves all positives at any query weight. On a negative, each active a has an isolated bin for at least one hash where the exact single-slope calculation rejects on the full payload. Inactive a are disabled by A_a. Each bin costs O(N log q) gates; h*m=O(r² log q). The remaining physical aggregate costs O(qh+N). The result is o(Nq) when r=o(sqrt(q)/log q), with arbitrary numbers of labels per occupied slope.
