# Physical scalar charge at binary AND-depth two

Let O and A count the binary OR and AND gates of one monotone DAG on projective-plane points and lines. Suppose it rejects every independent input with arbitrary payload and at most four queries (so the original cap suffices for q≥4). It need not accept all flags. At binary AND-depth at most two, the number of accepted singleton flags is at most 22O+20A. Unlimited fanout is permitted. Fold constants. The geometric properties used below are bipartite codegree at most one on each shore and at least two nonneighbors per vertex.

**Proof skeleton.** On an accepted singleton flag (p,l), choose an accepting proof. Above the first AND T the path uses only OR gates. A path ending at a raw instead would accept a forbidden singleton. Thus T is itself safe: its acceptance implies output acceptance. Below each of its two input slots, the proof chooses one path down through ORs, stopping as soon as it reaches a pure OR/raw cone or an AND. Call the signal at this stop the module. For an AND module its two operands are pure OR/raw cones. For a pure module its own cone is the one operand pool. Each pool is exactly an OR of the raws in its support, including a singleton raw as a degenerate case. The resulting at most four pools are clauses. Their conjunction implies T and hence the output. Each clause contains p or l.

For any such skeleton there is a clause excluding p, containing l, whose nonempty query marginal is contained in the pencil of p. Otherwise choose p for clauses containing p and one off-pencil query for each remaining clause. This would accept a legal independent input with at most four queries. Dually there is a clause excluding l, containing p, whose point marginal lies on l: choose l for clauses containing it and one off-line point for each remaining clause in the contrary case. Call these the query and point witnesses.

**Exceptional flags.** At most 2O flags have any pure OR/raw pool with query marginal of size ≥2 that contains their query and lies within their own pencil. For each p project the union of all such cones to the query shore, discarding zero supports and contracting singleton supports to the corresponding raw label. Each weak component of these retained cones has ≥2 raw labels, since all retained gates and raws feed some selected output. If u distinct labels occur, h gates and c components give 2h≥h+u−c and c≤u/2, whence u≤2h. The h physical ORs have at least two query labels within p's pencil, which identifies p uniquely by codegree. Sum over p. The dual bound is also 2O. Discard these ≤4O flags. For every remaining flag the selected point witness has point marginal exactly {p} and excludes l; the query witness has query marginal exactly {l} and excludes p. They are distinct. If both occur in one module, that bottom AND accounts for at most two ordered flags, since either orientation of its two fixed operands identifies both labels. Discard at most 2A such flags.

The two witnesses now lie in opposite modules of T. Orient the proof so the point witness belongs to module G reached below operand g and the query witness to module H reached below operand h.

**Low false degree.** For a signal v let R(v) be the points x for which v accepts at least two distinct nonincident singleton pairs in row x. Let C(v) be the analogous set of lines with at least two distinct false singleton pairs in their column. If p is outside R(G), G cannot accept p alone, since at least two lines avoid p. Its point-witness clause accepts p alone. Thus G is a bottom AND and its other clause must accept l and reject p. Every query in the latter's marginal S forms an accepted pair with p. Hence S contains l with at most one member outside p's pencil. If |S|≤3, the physical G accounts for at most six such flags: there are two choices of the point witness identifying p, and at most three query labels in the opposite operand.

For |S|≥4, all such labels across all p account for at most 6O flags. For each p take the union of all relevant query-projected pure OR cones. In each backward trace stop when support size is ≤3. Every unstopped gate has ≥4 labels with at most one outside the pencil of p, which identifies p uniquely (two pencils intersect in at most one line). Every label in a selected pool appears below an input slot of an unstopped gate whose upstream support size is ≤3. Thus at most 2·3 labels per unstopped gate are needed across all selected pools. This gives 6O+6A for the low-row case. By duality l outside C(H) costs another 6O+6A. The dual reasoning uses only the two-site false-pair relation.

**High false degree.** If T=g AND h is safe, then R(g) times C(h) is contained in incidence. For p in R(g) choose distinct false-query witnesses b₁,b₂; for l in C(h) choose distinct false-point witnesses x₁,x₂. If p misses l, choose x_i missing b_j, possible by codegree. The independent input with points p,x_i and queries l,b_j turns both operands on. This is impossible. By monotonicity along OR paths R(G)⊆R(g), C(H)⊆C(h). For the remaining flags, p∈R(G) and l∈C(H). For each physical T and orientation its nonempty product R(g) times C(h) is a star; choose one center once, including a fixed choice for a single edge.

For a point-centered star p, C(h) lies in p's pencil. Trace each assigned l from h down its chosen OR path to H. Along this path C(v) contains l and lies in that pencil. If |C(h)|=1, charge the single possible flag to the oriented T. Otherwise trace until the first transition from |C(v)|≥2 to size one, or end at H with |C(H)|≥2. A transition charges l to a slot of a physical OR with |C(v)|≥2. Such a v identifies p uniquely, and the chosen slot identifies l, so at most two flags per OR are charged in this way. A terminal H with |C(H)|≥2 also identifies p uniquely. Its query witness is one of at most two fixed operand clauses if it is a bottom AND, or its own single clause for a pure OR module. Each candidate singleton query marginal identifies at most one l. A raw cannot terminate with ≥2 high-false columns: a point raw has none and a query raw has only its own column. Consequently terminal charges cost at most 2A+O. The same counting for line-centered stars costs another 2O for transitions and 2A+O for terminals. Root charges in the two center cases combined cost at most 2A, since each T has only two orientations and a single chosen center per orientation. The high-degree total is at most 6O+6A.

The sum is 4O+2A+12O+12A+6O+6A=22O+20A. In particular a depth-two circuit accepting all flags has size at least N(q+1)/22. No statement here bounds circuits having only two AND *runs* but arbitrarily many binary ANDs per run.

**Unrestricted DAG.** For each signal compute three versions allowing respectively zero, one and two binary ANDs on each input-to-output path of an accepting proof. Raws are the same at every level. An OR takes its operands at the same level. An AND at level zero is constant zero and at each positive level takes its operands one level lower. The level-two output accepts exactly those inputs admitting a proof of AND-depth at most two; its values are bounded above by the original output. Thus the number of distinct flags admitting such a proof in any original correct circuit is at most 66O+40A by the same theorem. The complement counts flags on which every accepting proof contains a path with at least three ANDs.
