# A sequence of 67 shared-gate eliminations on independent tests

In a projective plane of order 401 set N=161203 and k=402. Assume a live binary monotone circuit of at most 646424 gates passes all incidence flags and all independent masks of size at most 400 on each side, as needed here. The full sparse promise suffices. On bilateral restricted promises the positive tests after superimposing independent pins must also be correct; the stated flag and negative tests ensure that by monotonicity. The procedure below must find 67 successive shared gates accepting a tested independent mask, even if there are arbitrarily many shared gates rejecting every such test at their respective rounds.

At a round let z bound the number of positive pins on each side. All positives pinned so far form an independent set. Their neighbors are fixed to zero. At least m=N−kz vertices per side are outside the zero neighbors. Already pinned vertices can be included in those counts: all their incident edges have their other endpoint fixed zero, so they are isolated in the surviving bipartite graph. Pick m vertices on each side and find the maximum number E_d of edges of a subgraph J of maximum degree d. Every surviving flag can be used as a positive test. Constants from pins are propagated and dead logic pruned. For each edge of J test its proper-neighborhood negative (the other incident vertices at both endpoints) and the two singleton negatives. The free mask is independent by four-cycle exclusion, uses at most d−1 vertices per side for d≥2, and avoids all old pinned neighbors. The tests are legal if z+d−1≤400.

If every shared gate rejects all these negative inputs, cut all its outgoing occurrences, inserting zero. Retain the formula fragment for each shared gate and for the output and disjoin them all. No gate is duplicated inside the fragments and the new output ORs introduce no variable leaves. An accepting flag still activates a fragment: the earliest originally true shared gate has the right values on every inlet, or the original output does if no shared gate was true. All fragments reject each tested negative by equality with the original evaluations there. The resulting formula has at most s+1 raw leaves: each raw occurrence belongs to at most one fragment and a live binary circuit has at most s+1 raw occurrences. Star rank plus singleton essentiality then requires at least E_d+ν(J)≥(1+1/d)E_d leaves. If this exceeds 646425, some shared gate accepts a tested independent mask. Pin its free selected inputs to one and all their neighbors to zero. This fixes the gate to one. The new per-side pin cap is z+d−1. Before unary rewiring remove the constant gate, losing one shared slot. During a subsequent unary bypass at most one new parent becomes shared, and that can only happen on removal of another shared slot. The total shared count therefore strictly decreases at each successful step, although identities can change. This proof makes no gate-level claim on the two-line maximal negatives.

## Degree-capped edge count

Give each source-to-point and line-to-sink arc capacity d and each incidence arc unit capacity. Integral maximum flow equals E_d. The cut indexed by a point set of size a and a line set of size b has capacity d(2m−a−b)+e(a,b), where e counts plane incidences between those actual sets. Cuts with a+b≤m cost at least dm. Otherwise write a+b=m+u. Uniformly,

    e(a,b) ≥ F(m,u) = [kmu−sqrt(qmu(N−m)(N−u))]/N.

Indeed centered mixing from MMᵀ=qI+J gives the same expression with a,b in place of m,u. For fixed a+b≤N put v=ab. The positive part of kv−sqrt(qv(v+N²−N(a+b))) is nondecreasing in v: divide by v>0 to get an increasing function of v, then multiply its positive part by v. The smallest product for sizes bounded by m is mu. When a+b>N, apply the preceding argument to the complements, whose size sum is below N and whose product is at least (N−m)(N−u). Restore the term k(a+b−N) to the edge count and to the bound. This proves the claimed F lower bound on every relevant cut.

Starting at z=0, the following run-length pairs give degree and number of repetitions: (4,27), (5,13), (6,8), (7,5), (8,3), (9,2), (10,2), (11,1), (12,1), (13,1), (14,1), (17,1), (20,1), (28,1). At each step take target floor(646425d/(d+1))+1. Every integer u from zero to m has d(m−u)+e≥target, as verified by squaring the exact displayed mixing inequality when needed in check-terminal-dirty.py. The final per-side pin cap is 366. This is a structural obstruction for hypothetical below-target circuits and does not prove the unrestricted size lower bound.
