# Original shared guard cost from just one off-pencil donor

Suppose a physical monotone circuit computes incidence on arbitrary point payloads with at most q queries as an OR over rows p AND H AND C_p with a single common guard H. Count the h physical gates in the ancestor cone of H, including H, irrespective of their reuse by any cofilter or row. Assume some donor p has C_p(empty,K)=1 on a set of t distinct lines not containing p. For the stated asymptotic take t≤r=O(1) and q sufficiently large.

Retain every point outside the union of K other than p, and every line neither through p nor in K. Set all other inputs of H to zero. A retained flag still activates H because every positive singleton flag requires H. For an independent retained mask (A,Y) with |Y|≤q−t, the extension (A union {p},Y union K) is independent and has at most q queries. There the cofilter and donor raw are both active, so H must reject. Monotonicity gives rejection already on (A,Y). The restricted guard is thus a circuit for the residual incidence graph, and its physical size after folding is at most h. No copy of the guard or cofilter is charged.

The retained graph has n≥2N−(t+1)(q+2) vertices, since at most t(q+1)+1 points and q+1+t lines were removed. Every retained point has q remaining neighbors, losing only its line to p. Every retained line has at least q+1−t remaining neighbors: it avoids p and meets each template line only once. Set d=min(q,q+1−t). The two-by-two induced weighted-flow lemma of restriction-flow.md / residual-peel-census.md gives, for d≥3 and q−t≥2,
    h ≥ 2n−1−floor(2n/(d+1))
      ≥ 4N−2(t+1)(q+2)−1−floor(4N/(d+1)).
In particular h≥4N−O((r+1)q). The conclusion concerns a single actual original cone, not a resulting cost divided by a replication factor.

There is a second proof of the same asymptotic by the batch lemma of two-stage-theta.md. For q−t≥3 and d≥3, choose t+1 distinct lines through p and keep all retained points on these lines. Every retained line meets the spokes in distinct points and at most t such intersections were removed, so it has a kept neighbor. Choose z,z' such that p,z,z' are noncollinear and keep all surviving lines through z or z'. Every surviving point x≠p is outside pz or outside pz', so the join to the corresponding center is well-defined, avoids p, and is not a template line since x avoids K. Thus it has a kept neighbor. Add the two endpoints of one retained flag to ensure a nonzero residual output. At most (t+3)(q+1)+2 vertices are kept. Deleting all other raws in one batch saves at least two per deleted raw: each had degree greater than two and retains its kept neighbor, and the three-by-three initial tests suffice for the two-by-two output. This also yields h≥2n−2((t+3)(q+1)+2), without any probabilistic domination bound.

If the full physical circuit has t_out additional actual gates outside the union of H's ancestors, its size equals h+t_out. There is no automatic lower bound here on t_out from the number of syntactic rows or cofilters; gates in shared cones are counted only once.
