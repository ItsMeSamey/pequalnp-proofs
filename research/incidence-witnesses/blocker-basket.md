# Physical budget for an independent basket of blocking tests

Work at q=401 in the fixed live bilateral normalized DAG, with every sole-read input directly feeding a private AND, and assume size s≤646424. Let B be any independent set of b≤199 point and line vertices combined. Put D=B union its graph neighborhood and restrict all raws in D to zero. Every surviving vertex loses at most b neighbors. For a surviving point, for example, it lies off all pinned lines, and each pinned point deletes at most one neighbor line through it; the dual is identical. A deleted vertex outside B also keeps a neighbor: codegree one bounds loss to same-shore pins by their number, with at most b more original pinned opposite-shore vertices in total. Choosing one surviving point x and a surviving line Y not through x is possible since each shore has more than q+1 survivors.

The natural q+1 matching pairs are p on Y and line xp. At most b pairs are bad because a point or line in the pair failed to survive. No point of B is on surviving Y and no line of B goes through surviving x. Thus each line in B rules out at most one point of Y and each point in B at most one line through x. Keep the two poles x,Y and every surviving good pair. If j≤b pairs are bad, first remove surviving points other than x on the j bad lines through x and surviving lines other than Y through the j bad points on Y. A removed point lying on Y retains Y, and a removed line through x retains x. Each other removed vertex has at least q+1−b−j≥4 neighbors outside this batch, since it meets each of the j opposing lines or points at most once. Remaining nontheta vertices likewise have degree at least four; the retained poles have at least q+1−j neighbors. Each remaining noncore point is on a good line through x, and every noncore line meets Y in a good point. Apply the two-stage elimination of two-stage-theta.md, from four-by-four tests to three-by-three then two-by-two. The resulting theta on k=q+1−j matched arms has size 4k from theta-residual.md. Thus the restricted graph on n=2N−|D| raws requires at least 2n−4 gates on the four-by-four tests.

In the original DAG designate as cold all gates and raw inputs always zero on the FOUR-BY-FOUR independent tests of the residual graph at D=0. All deleted raw bits are cold; all surviving raws can be selected separately and are not. In particular an original gate vanishing on all legal independent extensions of B is cold: adjoining B to each such residual negative stays independent, uses at most b+4≤203 per shore, and cannot decrease any value. The cold normalization from negative-profile-normalization.md counts at least U_D+F_C+c_1 removed slots (only use the cold color; deleted C raw inputs are fixed at 0). Here F_C is original excess at cold internal nonoutput gates, c_1 the number of internal cold gates with one cold operand, and U_D all original raw uses of D. The same proof works by counting the raw cold wires in the cold fanout identity. The c_0 ANDs are reconstructed on these restrictions; there is an original positive flag with D=0. For every deleted sole-read raw outside B the private AND accepts a surviving partner at its sibling, so that sibling is not cold. These distinct slots contribute to c_1.

Writing H_B for sole-read pins in B and E_D for raw uses beyond two in D gives U_D+c_1≥2|D|−H_B+E_D. With the restricted 2n−4 bound this proves F_C+E_D≤1616+H_B. All credits use one actual restriction and one DAG, independent of path multiplicities. This does not imply additivity for different, incompatible baskets B or the full order-401 target.
