# Exact gate count for the subdivided star

Let F_m = cd OR the union over i of a_i d and a_i b_i. Its minterm graph is a tree. Write A for OR_i a_i and B for OR_i(a_i AND b_i). The scalar binary monotone circuit (A OR c) AND (d OR B) uses 3m+1 gates for m>=1, since B implies A. With all b_i on and c,d off, each singleton a_i switches both final operands from zero to one.

For the lower bound, F_0=cd requires one gate. In an irredundant circuit for F_m fix i. There are distinct gates P directly reading a_i and Q directly reading b_i. Otherwise both essential inputs have just the same unique direct gate, and the output must be symmetric in them, whereas switching them with d=1 and all other inputs zero changes F_m. Neither gate is the output: neither variable can force a constant output at either setting.

Set a_i=0 and set b_i to the controlling constant for Q, zero for AND or one for OR. The residual function is F_(m-1). Both P and Q simplify away. If Q has a consumer other than P, that gate simplifies as well, since it reads a constant. Otherwise P reads Q and a_i, both fixed, and hence its consumer also simplifies. Such a consumer exists by irredundancy and differs from Q by acyclicity. In either case at least three gates disappear. Induction gives 3m+1, even allowing unrestricted internal sharing.

This is an induced restriction in every projective plane of order q for any m<=q. Choose d as a line and a_1,...,a_m,c as distinct points on it. For each i choose b_i as a line through a_i other than d. Its intersection with d is exactly a_i. At m=q the common collision witness uses q data lines, and separate witnesses use only b_i and a_i. If the original promise permits at most q lines, all inputs of the restricted function except assignments with every selected line on are automatic; by monotonicity these assignments are also forced to the specified value whenever a query point is present. With no points present their value could differ. The optimality proved above is for total F_m; m<=q-1 keeps every restricted input within the line-count promise.
