# Aggregate bound on sole-read lines at order 401

For a live binary monotone circuit correct on arbitrary points and at most 401 lines, apply the simultaneous line-only raw OR-spine hoist before counting sole-read queries. This keeps the full domain: for a negative without the line x, append a point of x missed by all at most 401 selected lines. The enlarged mask is still independent, while adding x is necessarily positive by monotonicity from the incident pair, even if the enlarged positive exceeds the line cap. A sibling on x's first OR spine must therefore be zero on the original negative. The simultaneous bypass and appended sibling disjunction preserve all positive flags by the earliest-changed-signal argument of sole-or-hoist.md. The literal transformation costs no gates, preserves liveness, and every remaining sole-use query now first enters an AND: uses of a spine exit transfer to the raw, including occurrences transferred into the appended disjunction. Gates with duplicate operands may be retained as binary occurrences. In what follows H counts these remaining sole-use queries in the normalized circuit. The conclusion does not bound the pre-hoist H of a different architecture.

Assume q=401 and N=161203. Classify these H markers at t=2 as in cold-marker-transfer.md, writing C for the cold count and M=H−C for the warm count. Every warm marker is dirty, since an upstream-exact marker is cold on its own line's complement. All warm paths start with an AND. The warm bypass therefore saves at least M gates before further pruning, producing a circuit correct on arbitrary points and at most two queried lines. The first-fork paths edited are disjoint. For each marker the paid saving comes from the initial AND run plus its first subsequent OR if there is one, with at most one OR paid to reinsert the raw marker. Without a subsequent OR the initial AND run disappears and insertion is a wire.

## Cold-star weight

For each cold x use its complementary-zero traversal from cold-marker-depth.md. Pick one path per point singleton on x, stopping at the first wire supporting at most two points of x. After unary suppression the routed full binary tree has leaves with one or two assigned labels. Every branch is a physical baseline OR with more than two supported points on x. If b leaves have two assigned labels, split each of these leaves into two singletons. The total depth of the resulting 402 labels is the original sum D plus 2b. A full binary tree on 402 leaves has minimum external depth 3508, obtained with 110 leaves at depth eight and 292 at depth nine. Hence D≥3508−2b. A visiting label is ineligible at an OR only if one operand accepts no other singleton on x; the routed child then has exactly that one assigned label, making it a singleton leaf after suppression. At most 402−2b singleton leaves can be charged. The eligible count for that cold line is thus at least 3106, since the two b-dependent terms cancel. A label cannot visit a physical gate twice by acyclicity.

Every charged physical OR rejects the complementary mask for a union of at most two lines, so its singleton support lies in that union. Only its two covering lines can each contain more than two of those points. For each physical OR retain the line giving the larger eligible label count; across all ORs the resulting sum W is at least 1553C. The zero-query baseline computation adds only ANDs in extracting the upstream guards. Every charged OR thus corresponds to a physical original OR, and its two original operands have the routed point-singleton witnesses.

## Preservation and single booking

In the warm bypass, suppressing a raw line has no effect on pure point assignments where that raw is already zero. Reinserting it by OR at the fork has no effect there either. All retained signals and the inputs to retained OR slots preserve their point-singleton evaluations. The first subsequent OR used in paying for a warm splice has an initial-AND-run operand zero on every pure point singleton. It consequently cannot have contributed an eligible pair. The other paid slots are ANDs. If a formerly eligible gate becomes dead and is pruned, its removal gives a size saving separate from the M already booked. Record the gate at its first disappearance only. An eligible OR cannot itself forward merely by folding a zero operand, since both its operands have point-singleton witnesses. After pruning, hoist only query-line OR spines again on the two-line residual domain. The same adjoining-point argument establishes this domain without increasing size. Bypassed ORs now agree with their raw query on point singletons, so no eligible slot is on such a spine. If any further unused slots are pruned, apply the same first-disappearance bookkeeping. No point-spine hoist is required.

## Pinning arithmetic

Use fork-pinning-267.md with a variable h: pin h points on a random line, zero all their neighboring lines, and permit either sign for pins. There are |D|=402h+1 deleted raws including pins; n=2N−|D| free variables have minimum degree k=402−h for h≥1. All deleted nonpin lines have a surviving neighbor. Each sole-use deleted line's private AND compensates the raw-use deficit; at most h uncompensated pins remain. Extra eligible OR slots are high-forced with neither operand low-zero and add to those low-phase savings. The residual weighted-flow bound uses at most two lines and two further points, inside the arbitrary-point two-line promise at both pin phases. Each retained eligible pair has probability at least α=h·401/(161203·402) of contributing. The contribution αd_g of a single removed eligible gate is less than one because d_g≤402 and h is 104 or 105. Its separately booked pruning saving therefore dominates its lost expectation. Thus it suffices that
    M+α·1553 C > E(h)−1,
    E(h)=646425−4N+1+floor(2n/(k+1))+h.
The strict comparison suffices since the actual total savings is integer for each pin choice.

Assume H≥3594 and use M=H−C. The common denominator for α·1553 is 64803606. At h=104, |D|=41809, n=280597, k=298, floor(2n/299)=1876, and E=3594. The numerator is 64766312. Thus H−37294C/64803606>3593 whenever C≤1737. At h=105, |D|=42211, n=280195, k=297, floor(2n/298)=1880, and E=3599. The numerator is 65389065. Hence H+585459C/64803606>3598 whenever C≥443. These ranges cover every nonnegative C. Therefore H≥3594 implies s≥646425, and any subtarget circuit has H≤3593 in the indicated line-normalized form.
