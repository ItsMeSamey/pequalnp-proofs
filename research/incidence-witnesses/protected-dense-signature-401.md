# A 646425-gate lower bound for scalar order-401 incidence

Let n₀=322406 raw sites. Suppose an original circuit has size at most 2n₀−4+k, k=1616. Use the bilateral normalized circuit of mutual-cold-points.md with at most S₀=9843 sole raws, all feeding private ANDs. It accepts all singleton flags and rejects all independent three-by-three masks. Use no further hoists. Actual operations are literal zero restrictions or gate-decreasing all-domain simplifications.

## Geometry

Permanently protect the 806 sites of a fixed full dominating theta K. Every site outside K has a plane neighbor in K. Maintain at least three surviving plane neighbors at every surviving vertex. The current circuit on n sites then costs at least 2n−4: delete the remaining noncore sites by the two-stage-theta.md batch lemma from three-by-three tests and use the exact two-by-two theta bound. This final hypothetical batch may rehoist sole raws.

Use at most 1617 rounds of at most t=24 raw deletions each, always avoiding protected raws. When a surviving site first drops to at most 26 neighbors, mark and permanently protect it and three surviving neighbors. Before crossing this threshold it has at least 27 neighbors and after a batch at least three, since at most 24 were deleted. Previous marks retain their three neighbors. Monitor sites protected for other reasons also. For each mark choose h=376 already-deleted neighbors. These subsets intersect pairwise in at most one, including opposite shores where they are disjoint. With T≤38808 total deletions and j marks, site multiplicities have sum jh and squared sum at most jh+j(j−1). Cauchy gives j≤T(h−1)/(h²−T), so j≤141. Marks add at most 564 protected raws.

Before each round permanently protect every current sole-OR raw. Therefore every deleted sole raw v has an AND reader. Its sibling is independent of v and accepts every current plane-neighbor singleton by the singleton-versus-flag test. At least three such neighbors survive every contemplated batch, even for a deleted owner, since each unprotected vertex began the round with degree at least 27.

## Reader accounting without further hoists

At the all-other-raws-true mask of a batch D, keep high code even if subsequently unreachable. The literal zero-flow count of planted-theta-credit.md pays 2|D| eliminations, plus deleted raw fanout above two, internal zero-gate fanout excess, and all one-zero ANDs other than the distinct compensations of deleted sole raws. Their siblings are high by the surviving-neighbor test. Trim any previously dead code beforehand. Physical size decreases beyond 2|D| are surplus. Separate pruning or gate-decreasing all-domain simplifications pay their entire gate loss as surplus. Cumulative surplus is at most k under the assumed original size because the surviving baseline is always 2n−4.

For each occurrence of a surviving raw x in the live pre-batch circuit, either its slot persists at a high gate with two high operands, its gate is a one-zero AND with high operand x, or its gate is a high bypassed OR with x its unique high operand. In the last case follow one outgoing occurrence through high bypassed ORs until reaching a surviving slot at a two-high gate or a high-operand slot at a one-zero AND. There was no dead code originally. A path ending instead at a bypassed output would make the whole residual equal x, impossible on the singleton/flag tests. Different starting raw occurrences cannot coalesce on these forwarding chains since every bypassed OR has a unique high operand. A terminal compensated AND is impossible: its high operand would reduce to the lone raw x, whereas its sibling accepts three distinct surviving neighbor singletons. Thus the total of surviving raw use-count decreases, counting only positive decreases per raw, is at most the number of surplus one-zero ANDs. Slots in newly dead high code still count; subsequent pruning is separate.

A pre-batch sole-AND raw that survives the batch retains its AND reader: zero restriction cannot forward an AND to a surviving raw, and if the whole reader were zero the raw would be inessential despite its remaining neighbors. Thus new sole raws or new sole-OR raws after zero propagation are covered by the above occurrence losses. For each separate elementary all-domain simplification or trimming, at most two labels per eliminated gate can suffer a raw-use loss or lose their former sole AND reader, since they must occupy original operand slots of eliminated gates. This covers bypassing a redundant gate, dropping a duplicate input in an associative OR tree by bypassing its OR, and trimming. Initial dead pruning is a surplus operation on the literal normalized start. Therefore at most 2k sites newly require sole-OR protection, S≤S₀+2k=13075 for the number of sole raws, and P≤806+564+2k=4602 for the protected surviving raws.

## A round always pays extra

Take an elementary simplification as above if one is available. Otherwise suppose no deletion set D of at most 24 unprotected raws pays extra. Every such set meets the geometry conditions. The zero-flow ledger forces all shared internal gates high on these dense masks, and the two operands of every AND agree there except possibly at a designated AND of an unprotected sole raw. All unprotected raws have fanout at most two. Protected raws are fixed high. A designated AND equals its owner's dense signature because its sibling accepts ≥27 neighbor singletons. All other ANDs have identical operand signatures, including those reading protected raws.

As in strict-dense-signature.md, every signal is constantly true on these dense masks (large) or the OR of a unique nonempty set of ≤24 unprotected labels (small). An OR unions signatures, promoting 25 or more labels to large. Small internal gates cannot branch. At the earliest small nondesignated AND the operands expand as ORs of ordinary raws and designated tokens. Their identical label sets cannot include a sole raw's token, since its unique producing AND would need to reach both operands without a small branch. Thus both operands are identical full-cube raw ORs, giving an elementary simplification. All nondesignated ANDs therefore have two large operands.

Write E for raw fanout above two and F for internal fanout excess, excluding the output. The physical size identity gives F+E≤S+k−3≤14688. Include the P protected raws as large source vertices in the large-induced DAG. Each internal source is an OR of two small operands supplying at least 25 token labels. Small OR trees cannot share gates, each ordinary unprotected raw has at most two uses, and each designated private token has at most one use. Thus there are ≤2n₀/25 internal sources. The sum of fanout excess of the included vertices is at most F+P+E, since a protected raw contributes at most one plus its excess above two. Counting large-to-large slots as in strict-dense-signature.md bounds nodes with two large operands by 2n₀/25+F+E+2P. Write U≤S for the number of designated ANDs. Total AND count A≤U+2n₀/25+F+E+2P.

Flatten OR trees at every AND and shared OR output. The number of tops is ≤1+F+2A−U, since each designated AND has a slot occupied by its raw. Every unprotected twice-read raw feeds two ORs: nondesignated ANDs cannot take a small raw, and a designated sibling is large. Its occurrences belong to distinct groups or a duplicate raw can be dropped by a gate saving. Each such raw x has one group with at most two ordinary unprotected labels. Otherwise pick one distinct-from-x label per group such that the chosen labels do not form a flag, possible by codegree one. Append a surviving neighbor of x avoiding conflicts with at most two same-shore labels. The resulting independent negative has at most three selected on either shore and saturates both OR groups; adding x cannot change the output but supplies a flag. This also applies when the groups are nested. Thus at least (n−S−P)/2 OR groups are required. The conflicting bounds are

    (322406−38808−13075−4602)/2 = 132960.5,
    1+13075+3·14688+4·322406/25+4·4602 = 127132.96.

The second number bounds 1+F+2A−U. Hence a simplification exists or a set D of at most 24 unprotected raws pays at least one extra physical slot. Take it and update protection. All actual reductions preserve the three-by-three tests and all remaining singleton flags. After 1617 rounds the cumulative surplus exceeds k=1616, while the surviving 2n−4 baseline still holds. This proves the claimed 646425 bound with no circuit-profile hypothesis.
