# A cyclic mixed witness that detects cancellation

For q=s² in characteristic 2 let K=F_s and T=vF_s with v outside F_s. For active affine points p1,p2 and active ordinary query lines l1,l2, write e(p,l)=y−ax−b. Accept if the two diagonal residuals belong to K and the off-diagonal residuals belong to T. Witnesses may repeat. Append the exact contributions of vertical queries, infinity queries and direction points. This is an explicit monotone function: an OR over legal four-label terms, with tabulated field conditions. There is no claim that this direct enumeration is cheap.

Every genuine flag passes by repeating the point and the line. On an affine punctured-pencil maximal negative from bilinear-trace-maximal-negative.md all four ordinary residuals are nonzero products −t_i s_j. The product of the diagonal entries equals the product of the off-diagonal entries. But F_s^* and v²F_s^* are disjoint, since in characteristic 2 an element whose square lies in the subfield already lies in it. Thus the cyclic check rejects that whole negative, including its projective boundary. Every acyclic trace witness with nontrivial kernels admits compatible neighbors on the same negative.

Two nonparallel ordinary active lines and all affine points off them form a surviving legal negative. The map assigning a point its two residuals is affine bijective, so take p1 and p2 having residual pairs (1,v),(v,1). This applies separately to every choice of distinct subfield cosets if the two diagonal entries use any nonzero element in the first coset and the two off-diagonal entries any nonzero element in the second. Thus the AND of all such unanchored square checks still accepts this one negative. On a singleton payload or a singleton query, in contrast, repeated-side equality forces one pair to have residual in K intersect T={0}. Exactness on these controls does not imply exactness on general independent masks.
