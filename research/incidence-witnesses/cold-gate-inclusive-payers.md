# Paying cold mergers from physical products and their guarded banks

Use the distinct designated flags of cold-pool-two-shore-matching.md. A point-owned port is an oriented pair (u,v) of operands of one physical AND where u's accepted point singletons at zero queries form precisely {p}, and v at zero points rejects the query mask of ALL lines outside the pencil of p. It certifies (p,l) whenever v accepts singleton query l with zero points. The off-pencil predicate is a check on the actual circuit, including its extension outside the native query cap. It is not inferred from the promise. Exchange shores for line-owned ports. Certified flags activate their physical product by monotonicity.

For t point-owned ports covering a set D of distinct flags, |D|≤O+t, where O is the number of original ORs. Freeze all points to zero and reuse this ONE physical baseline for all selected ports. For each used owner OR together its selected v wires. With j owners this costs at most t−j ORs. Each resulting output rejects its entire off-pencil mask and accepts its chosen singleton lines. Such a mixed multioutput circuit needs at least |D|−j physical ORs: separately for each owner process wires cold on that owner's off-pencil mask in topological order with singleton lines initially separate. Every nonempty actual accepted singleton set of a cold gate is contained in one current component. A cold AND forwards any cold operand since that operand contains the whole gate support; a cold OR merges once if its two nonempty operand supports lie in distinct components. Any such physical OR accepts two distinct line singletons, identifying that unique owner since all its accepted singleton lines lie on the owner's pencil. If an output accepts d chosen lines they must end in one component, requiring at least d−1 merges. These charged ORs are distinct across owners. Comparison with O+t−j yields the claim. It also holds for line-owned ports on a separate baseline.

Exempt singly charged original OR events (≤O). Choose point- and line-owned ports for disjoint sets of remaining designated flags, optionally requiring each product to be upstream of its charged original OR. Write t_P,t_L for the numbers of ports used, each ≤2A. Next remove events whose distinct flags are accepted by any of b chosen original AND gates each accepting at most k native singleton flags (≤kb events). Match the remaining events to actual changing raw slots, with deficiency d. On a trimmed circuit s=O+A this gives
    Nq ≤ 3O + t_P + t_L + kb + (s+1) + d.
An empty port selection needs no baseline charge O. Overlapping physical cost categories in this formula are explicitly charged separately. No general bound on d follows.

In the paired-packet construction each point term multiplies a point proxy p OR K by a bank of packet query lines incident to p (the query raws may themselves use y OR K proxies). The actual marker vanishes at both zero points and zero queries. The bank rejects the entire off-pencil mask at zero points, independently of the collector code and of all other packets. Thus every one of the 17t packet events is paid within |D|≤O+t_P using at most nine physical point-term ports per participating packet. This applies to arbitrary fanout inside banks. Actual minimum circuits are not assumed to contain this port structure.
