# Two-point off-line witnesses with nearly all query lines already present

Let b=ceil(q/sqrt(log q)) and first choose uniformly q−1−b lines V. Write X for the points not hit by V. For each l outside V consider |X∩l|. Conditional on l outside V, each point on l is missed with probability η=C(N−1−q,q−1−b)/C(N−1,q−1−b), independent of its identity. Thus the conditional mean is η(q+1), with η=e^(−1+o(1)). Exchanging one sampled line changes the count by at most one, since each sampled line hits l only once. Expose the random list without replacement. Two alternative values at one step can be coupled in the remaining sample by a transposition, changing the final count by at most one. The bounded-difference martingale tail and a union bound therefore give |X∩l|=η(q+1)+O(sqrt(q log q)) for all l outside V with probability tending to one. Double-counting the flags on X then gives |X|=(1+o(1))ηN.

Fix such a V. Sample points R inside X independently with probability θ=(log q)/(4q), and set ρ=(1−θ)^(η(q+1))=q^(−1/(4e)+o(1)). The chance each l outside V avoids R is uniformly (1+o(1))ρ by the preceding error estimate. Conditional on p∈X remaining outside R, the other surviving sites on different lines through p are disjoint. Its number d_p of available query neighbors is a sum of independent Bernoulli variables with mean (1+o(1))qρ. Concentration uniformly over p gives d_p=(1+o(1))qρ. The resulting line set U consists of non-V lines avoiding R. Every l∈U contains η(q+1)+O(sqrt(q log q)) points of X, all outside R. With |R|=o(N) whp, a second flag count gives |U|=(1+o(1))Nρ.

For each h,l let the family of corrections consist of the 1- or 2-point subsets S⊆X outside l for which h(V+l+S)=1. The Bernoulli pin hits all eligible preselected families of ceil(100N/log q) disjoint corrections, and all families of ceil(24q) singleton corrections, by the same q^(−6) bounds as disjoint-offline-point-witnesses.md, now conditional on V. Fix an R satisfying these properties and the degree conclusions.

Draw b further lines W' uniformly in U; test with the original pins R,V and final query W=V∪W'. Each p outside R in X survives with probability C(|U|−d_p,b)/C(|U|,b)=1−o(1), since b d_p/|U|=o(1). The expected residual flags consequently number (1/e+o(1))Nqρ. For the point-owned receivers apply the entropy charge with initial tests R+V+l, final rejecting input R+W+p, and support tested in U. A charged OR's support avoids W', giving expected O(|U|/b) charge per OR for d=ceil(q^(2/3)): the large-support remainder N(q+1)exp(−d²b/(2|U|)) is negligible. If the circuit size is O(N), at most O(N qρ/sqrt(log q)) flags in expectation can use h(R+V+l)=1, by the Jensen inequality and qρ=q^(1−1/(4e)+o(1)). At most 2 #ORs further flags can use rich feeders. Therefore on some final draw, (1/e−o(1))Nqρ flags require point-owned complementary ANDs for which every eligible h has h(R+V+l)=0. On that flag h still accepts the full flag and the dense off-line negative at its terminal receiving AND.

In particular, relative to V with just b queries left to add, such a wire h has no corrections on the singleton query-plus-V alone, fewer than ceil(24q) correcting singleton points in X outside l, and a point transversal of size at most 2 ceil(100N/log q) for all correcting one- or two-point subsets. The guarantee does not extend automatically to a fixed predetermined V with highly nonuniform surviving line degrees or to arbitrary width corrections.
