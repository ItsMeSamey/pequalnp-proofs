# Four raw occurrences in an arbitrary mixed scalar circuit

Let q≥4, N=q²+q+1, and let a trimmed binary monotone scalar incidence circuit have O OR gates, A AND gates and s=O+A. Let D4 be the native flags admitting a proof with at most four raw leaf occurrences in any tree shape. Then

|D4| ≤ 45O+83A−44N+22 ≤ 83s.

In particular the original Nq/100 target holds throughout every circuit offering a read-2 proof for every flag (at most two occurrences of each of its two raws, thus ≤4 total). In a strict counterexample more than 17 percent of flags must require at least five occurrences in every proof. No depth bound, pre-existing addressed receivers, or no-sharing condition is assumed.

**Contexts.** Evaluate each original wire F_g on the original raw input. Compute every root context K_g simultaneously: seed the root with one, copy along reversed ORs, multiply by the other original operand at reversed ANDs, and sum contributions at each wire using OR. A proof of F_g and a proof of K_g splice into a root proof with one distinguished F_g occurrence, even if the context contains other uses of the same physical wire. Conversely removing any one occurrence in an unfolded proof yields a context proof whose leaves are exactly the remaining raw occurrences. This common forward/reverse circuit contains at most 3A ANDs and U=O+s−2N+1 ORs: reverse merges use at most 2s−(s+2N−1) binary ORs. Fold constants (including multiplication by one); this preserves the existence of a proof with no more occurrences of the activated raws. A context folded to constant one has an empty original proof: in the reverse computation with a constant-free forward DAG, this requires an OR-only path to the original root.

**Small-degree extraction.** For the remaining forward/reverse circuit compute at each wire its linear part L (proofs with one raw occurrence) and quadratic part Q (proofs with exactly two occurrences). For a raw use itself and zero. At each OR take separate unions, at each AND take zero and the product of its two linear operands. Fold only constants, retaining nonconstant tied products as ANDs. Thus all linear pool ORs total at most U, all quadratic routing ORs at most U, and all quadratic sources are at most 3A physical ANDs of pure linear pools. A nonzero quadratic signal has a bottom AND in its ancestry. All formulas denote acceptance by proofs of the indicated sizes; no algebraic cancellation is used.

A proof with 2–4 raw occurrences has an AND occurrence g with exactly two leaves beneath it and at most two leaves outside: take a deepest binary branch in the AND/leaf skeleton. F_g is an original AND and its selected proof is quadratic. Include a top product Q(F_g) AND (L(K_g) OR Q(K_g)) for each original AND g with a nonzero, nonconstant context. Combine all products by OR. The context covers any outside proof of size one or two. When K_g folded to one the flag already has a two-leaf proof at the original root, so charge these separately via |D2|≤s. If a context proof shrinks under constant folding it either retains one/two leaves or disappears into the constant-one case. Every new product is sound because it implies F_g AND K_g, hence the original scalar. It therefore rejects all original negative tests even when only a subset of flags passes.

**Typed gate count.** The selected witness circuit has at most P=U physical pure linear-pool ORs, R=U+A routing ORs between AND levels (allowing one linear/quadratic join per g), B=3A bottom products, and T=A top products. Pure context signals forward to their linear pool, so they introduce no new pool OR gate. Outer scalar aggregation ORs play no role in the following ledger. The typed form of scalar-and-depth-two.md is that at most 18P+4R+18B+2T flags survive the safe top products. To see the count in that proof directly: its large exact-pool exceptions use 4P, same-module cases use 2B, low-row and low-column cases use 12P+12B, high-degree transitions use 4R, high-terminal pure modules cost 2P and bottom modules 4B, and orientations of high-root products cost 2T. A transition follows a routing OR before reaching a fixed bottom or pure module, never continuing inside a pure pool. Thus the categories here implement exactly those paths.

Adding s for the omitted constant contexts gives s+22U+60A =45O+83A−44N+22. For q≥4 this is at most 83s. The established 43-gate Fano four-occurrence control concerns q=2 and is also numerically compatible; proper-star refactorings have two-leaf proofs.
