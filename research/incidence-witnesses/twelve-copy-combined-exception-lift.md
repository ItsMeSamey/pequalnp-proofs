# Simultaneous removal of compact complements and arbitrary exceptional masks

Combine the hypotheses of ten-copy-large-uncovered-lift.md and twelve-copy-arbitrary-exception-lift.md as follows. In any plane of sufficiently large order suppose H accepts all flags. Among q-query maximal masks whose uncovered point set has size ≥q√q−q, H may fail on at most exp(q/100) arbitrarily chosen masks E. Every other negative test, including all smaller query cardinalities and every compact complement, may also be exceptional. Twelve restricted copies of H and eleven OR gates suffice for the full original promise.

Use the six blocks of the twelve-copy proof. In each rectangular block take as filler pool all outside horizontals and verticals; the largest single parallel group has at most 2q/3+O(1) members. In the sixth block take ⌈11q/20⌉ lines from each of two other directions. Each pool F has at least q+q/15 members and contains fewer than 0.7q lines from any pencil. By a union bound a fair two-coloring simultaneously caps each color's load in every E-set at 3q/4 and in every pencil at 2q/3.

Fix a copy and independent input, with its n retained monochromatic queries Y'. Complete by fillers to q lines. If a center has load greater than L=⌊4q/5⌋, exchange one removable filler through it for one unused filler off it. The removable label exists by the 2q/3 cap on Y'; the pool has more than (1+1/15−0.7)q off it, while the current completion has fewer than q−L. Repeat to reach L; no second center can exceed q−L+1 while this is done. If n>3q/4 the resulting completion avoids E. Its maximum pencil load m≤L gives ≥q√q−q uncovered points by the geometric estimate in the ten-copy proof.

If n≤3q/4, this completion contains at least q/4 removable fillers. Choose a fixed set of u=⌊q/25⌋ unused pool members, available since |F|≥q+q/15. Replace any u removable members by this fixed set. The resulting completions are distinct and number at least binom(⌈q/4⌉,u)≥2^u>exp(q/100) for large q. None has pencil load above L+u≤0.84q; this too implies at least q√q−q uncovered points for sufficiently large q. Choose a completion outside E. In all cases the retained payload lies in the uncovered set, so H rejects. Each flag survives some copy. No physical extension inputs are needed.
