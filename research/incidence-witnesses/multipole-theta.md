# Expanded theta restrictions that see every independent two-by-two test

Fix an incident O on L. Choose r≥2 distinct points X on L other than O, and r lines R through O other than L, with r≤q. Retain points X together with all points on R, and lines R together with all lines through X. The core has n=2[r(q+1)+1] vertices, comprising on the point shore the r poles, O, and rq generic points. Pole degrees are q+1, O has degree r+1, and each generic point has degree r+1 (its own row and r distinct joins to X). The edge count is r(r+2)q+2r+1.

Choose x in X and Y in R. Every vertex outside the full theta at x,Y has a neighbor in it. Remove all extra vertices from the outer core in one batch: each has degree greater than two and a theta neighbor. The elimination lemma in two-stage-theta.md buys two gates per removed raw going from three-by-three independent tests to two-by-two tests. The inner theta costs 4(q+1). Thus the outer core requires at least 2n−4 gates. The cold normalization and positive-dead pruning use the outer core’s three-by-three independent masks and its own positive flags. The integer source cover has t≤r(r+2)q+2r+1. Uniform choices in PG(2,q) retain each raw with probability [r(q+1)+1]/N and each flag with probability [r(r+2)q+2r+1]/[N(q+1)] by projective transitivity.

At r=2 every independent two-point two-line mask occurs in some core for q≥3. For p,p' and l,l', choose L avoiding p,p' and l∩l'. The union of three pencils has at most 3q+1 lines, strictly less than N for q≥3. The intersections x1,x2 of L with l,l' differ. Choose O on L outside x1,x2, and use the lines Op,Op' as rows, supplementing if they coincide. Thus gates agreeing on the independent three-by-three tests of every expanded core agree on all original independent two-by-two masks (smaller masks also embed). This does not cover all larger masks by itself.

At order 401 the two-pole experiment retains 805 per shore versus 403 on the full theta. It has 3213 flags instead of 1206. Its physical baseline remains 2n−4; expected raw credit decreases by 402 E/N and the source-cover cap rises by 2007. These are separate experiments. The r=2 geometry, exact probabilities, coverage and elimination were independently verified by /root/incidence/audit.
