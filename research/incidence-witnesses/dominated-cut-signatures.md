# What the scalar DAG must retain when a negative dominates a cut signature

For an antichain G of k trimmed nonroot internal gates let L be the actually lost flags after the common outgoing-use-zero cut. For each flag e let P_e be its subset of active sites in G. Let N range over inclusion-maximal realized activation signatures on legal negatives (obtain them from maximal independent payloads). Write D for those e in L whose P_e is contained in at least one such N. These are precisely the lost flags no monotone repair from G alone can accept while rejecting every negative. For any other flag the conjunction of its whole P_e rejects every negative and accepts that flag; any activation of a signal-only repair on a dominated flag persists to its dominating negative.

**Lower bound for bypassing raw channels.** In the actual output-side DAG stop the backwards exploration at any member of G, treating it as an input. Let B be the distinct raw sites still reaching the output without meeting G. For every e=(p,l) in D and every realizing dominating negative n, some endpoint of e absent from n belongs to B. Indeed the G coordinates on e are bounded by those on n. Unless such a raw is in B, all remaining raw inputs on e are also bounded by those on n and the scalar monotone output cannot separate them. Equivalently the usual positive/negative traversal follows a physically distinguishing path ending at such a raw without crossing G. Use any actual dominating negatives for this step, including ones whose signatures are nonmaximal; only signature recognition of D needs the maximal family.

Collect into F all forced raw endpoints: force p if some dominating n contains l, and force l if some dominating n contains p. All F belong to B. B must additionally vertex-cover the dominated flags with neither endpoint already in F. Thus
|B| ≥ |F| + matching(D restricted to vertices outside F).
The one physical output-side DAG contains all k cut input sites (by antichain and trimming) and at least |B| original raws. With binary fanin it needs at least k+|B|−1 physical gates by edge counting; gates not in its backwards cone are additional. This assertion does not sum possibly overlapping entire producer cones. For an entirely point-only antichain the empty-query/all-points negative dominates every flag and contains its point, forcing the original query raw of every lost flag to bypass G.

**Pricing the separable flags on the same cut.** Choose any collection of nonempty signature terms J_a⊆G, none contained in a realized maximal negative, covering every flag in L outside D (the full signatures of those flags always suffice). Let u be the number of retained sites in the union and t the total term sizes. The OR of their conjunctions appended to the residual root costs at most t gates and every dominated flag remains missing. Append a star repair for D costing ≤|D|+matching(D) including its join. If G consists of shared sites in a minimum circuit, the physical savings imply
f+k+1_{f=k} ≤ u+t+|D|+matching(D),
where f counts distinct direct consumers. Empty families or D add zero repair gates. The star term represents a cost upper bound for actual raw repair, not extra physical gates known to be necessary. The bypass bound above is a complementary lower bound for physical input channels on the original cut.
