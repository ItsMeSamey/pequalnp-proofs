# Sharp smallest Hall-deficient cold-pool event family

Use the eligible-slot graph of cold-pool-two-shore-matching.md, including BOTH actual operand point witnesses per merger. Let S be Hall deficient, e=|S|, k its distinct query lines and m the distinct chosen endpoint points. Each of those m+k raw labels supplies an eligible slot, so m+k<e. For every l, the selected actual edges in its merger tree form a forest and expose at least e_l+1 different incident point labels. Thus the number I of incidences between these m points and k lines is at least e+k. Define J(n,t)=n*a*(a−1)/2+(t mod n)*a with a=floor(t/n). Convexity and unique joins/meets require J(k,e+k)≤m(m−1)/2 and J(m,e+k)≤k(k−1)/2. The following exhaustive integer loop leaves no pairs for e≤16 and only (k,m)=(7,9),(8,8) for e=17.

    for e in range(1,18):
        for k in range(1,e):
            for m in range(1,e-k):
                t=e+k
                if t<=m*k and 2*J(k,t)<=m*(m-1) and 2*J(m,t)<=k*(k-1):

The (8,8) alternative cannot occur. Some line contains r≥4 of the eight chosen points. On the remaining seven lines at least 25−r−7=18−r incidences are outside it. Therefore J(7,18−r)≤(8−r)(7−r)/2; each integer r=4,...,8 violates this bound.

For actual sharpness take the Desarguesian plane at prime order 29. Start with the affine square A=(0,0), B=(1,0), C=(0,1), D=(1,1). Choose the nine points consisting of these four, their center H=(1/2,1/2), the horizontal and vertical infinity points and the two diagonal-direction infinity points. Choose the seven lines consisting of the six joining lines of the square and the infinity line. The four sides meet the point set in three sites each, the two diagonals in four each and the infinity line in four, for I=24. The seven selected queries therefore provide I−7=17 tree edges on these nine points.

Build a point-star formula for the FULL plane with nine distinguished point terms grouped in a subtree. Pair each of the nine with its own distinct auxiliary point term in a cherry, choosing the auxiliary points outside all seven selected lines. Then join these nine cherries by a binary tree, and afterwards join all remaining point terms. For each auxiliary point, forbid all seven selected lines and, for the auxiliary point paired with distinguished p, additionally forbid all at most eight lines joining p to another distinguished point. These at most 15 lines cover at most 450 points in the plane of 871; previously used auxiliaries forbid at most eight more. Thus each cherry's line contains no other distinguished point. Every joining address of at least two cherries is then noncollinear. Its two groups of point stars have at least two different lines joining a point in one group to a point in the other (else their union would be collinear), and hence this OR address is multiply charged in the full-plane cold-pool construction.

For each selected query l, no auxiliary cherry point is on l. In the nine-cherry subtree the exact singleton contributions under l are precisely the distinguished points on l. Its merges among those selected sites number |l intersect P|−1, and all their point witnesses are drawn from the nine. At these gates there are 17 nonexempt events altogether. To ensure one eligible query slot per selected label, choose a native true flag K on an unselected query and an unselected point and feed each selected query to all its uses through a single proxy (y_l OR K). The marker does not use selected raw labels. This monotone circuit still computes exact incidence on the full cube: if K=0 it agrees literally, if K=1 the underlying flag already makes incidence true. Choose separate physical K pins, not new uses of one of the nine designated point raws. Keep the original single physical raw slot per distinguished point. At all seven query anchors K vanishes. For other singleton-query anchors K also vanishes since the point of K is outside its query's complement. At a flag under the marker query some additional gates may now become true, but never false. To retain multi-charge status by exactly the ordinary specializations for every query, choose K with an additional distinct unselected point conjunct. It still implies a native flag, and is identically zero at every singleton-point test and at every one-query anchor. Consequently all selected mergers and all their second charge labels are exactly as in the point-star formula.

The 17 events see at most nine eligible point slots and seven eligible query slots. Hence this family is physically Hall deficient. It has deficiency exactly one, since a smaller deficient subset was excluded and each set of sixteen events is matchable. This upper construction is an exact native circuit with sharing; it makes no assertion about global gate minimality. Its seven query proxies can be removed jointly. In every deficient 17-event family the physical raw-slot neighborhood necessarily has size sixteen with exactly one eligible slot per each of the nine point and seven query labels. This constrains eligible changing paths on the selected tests, not all physical reads of these labels.
