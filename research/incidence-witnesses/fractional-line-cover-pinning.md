# Geometric concentration of pure point singleton supports

Let A consist of points whose pure singleton makes a physical gate one. Let M be its fractional covering number by geometric lines. Finite LP duality gives nonnegative point weights totaling M and weight at most one per line. On a uniform maximal q-line negative the gate is zero with probability at most (N+1) exp(−M/28).

First sample each line independently at rate q/N. The weight V hit has mean at most 3M/4 since (1−q/N)^(q+1)≥1/4. Removal of coordinate i loses weight d_i≤1; Σd_i≤V because only uniquely covered points contribute. Entropy tensorization as in capped-false-pair-matching.md gives log E exp(λV)≤λ E V/(1−λ/2), since Σd_i²≤V. At λ=1/4 this gives Pr[V=M]≤exp(−M/28). A gate zero on the complementary input requires every supported point covered, hence V=M. Conditioning on exactly q sampled lines costs at most N+1.

For s≤q^D and fixed caps a,b, use a threshold K log q large enough depending on D,a,b and any desired fixed error exponent. Iteratively pin to one any gate above this threshold, also allowing the false matching triggers of iterative-false-matching-normal-form.md. Each edit's failure probability is bounded on the same uniform original maximal negatives. The sum over s edits remains smaller than the extension mass of any independent (a,b)-mask. The final circuit retains all flags and all such negatives. A threshold ω(log q) instead gives superpolynomially small error. The point-shore constraint uses the asymmetry of the promise.

Each remaining support lies on at most 2M geometric lines together with at most 2M² further individual points. Choose an optimal fractional cover u of total M. For a line l with k>2M supported points, sum their covering constraints. This gives k≤k u_l+M−u_l since each other line hits l at most once. Thus u_l≥1/2, so at most 2M such lines exist. Remove their points. Every line then contains at most 2M residual points and summing residual cover constraints gives a residual count at most 2M². Empty supports need no cover.
