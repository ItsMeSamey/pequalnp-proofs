# Negative witnesses at a last private switch in a selected gate minimum

Among all gate-minimal circuits for the original plane promise select C minimizing Σ_e c_e as in alternation-switch-charge.md. Consider a marked OR g=A∨B whose only physical reader is an AND h=g∧K with distinct inputs. Assume h has no AND descendant. Its consumers, if any, are in the final OR collector. Let C[h←1] denote the old output when every use of h (or the output if h is final) is forced to one.

There must exist a same-query maximal negative n with B(n)=1, K(n)=0 and C[h←1](n)=1; the same holds with A in place of B. Suppose there is no such negative for B. Delete the two old gates g,h and replace their output by h'=(A∧K)∨B, two binary gates, redirecting all old h slots. On all assignments h'≥h. A newly accepted maximal negative would have h'=1,h=0, hence B=1,K=0, and the forced old output would accept. This was excluded; monotonicity extends rejection to every original negative. The native flags persist. Since g was private, no external value is lost. The new implementation has at most the original size.

No old OR acquires a new AND descendant: every ancestor of A or K already led to h. The replacement OR has no AND descendant. In converting an old native proof, if it visits h, its old selected operand at g is consistent throughout the proof DAG. For choice A use the replacement A∧K; for choice B use just the old B proof. All new OR cost is zero; old ORs contributing to the new proof were already in the old one. External AND/OR choices and any already-shared child proofs can be retained. Therefore for every e, c'_e≤c_e, with c'_e≤c_e−1 if a minimizing proof visits h, as the marked g disappears. In particular some flag must require h: otherwise pinning h to zero loses no flag, preserves all negatives and removes an actual gate, contradicting minimum (for final h every flag requires it). There is strict improvement in the chosen secondary objective, contradiction. Any further trimming only removes gates.

This prices a switch using actual legal maximal negatives and an equal-cost physical edit. The two witnesses may coincide or be reused at other switches. The statement does not bound the total number of switches in arbitrary minimum circuits.
