# All flags can require large read under coordinated within-term parent forwarding

Let t≥1, q+1>2t, and choose any cyclic order of the N points of the plane. Each owner p receives t exceptional flags, one on each of the next t distinct points a_j. Choose its exceptional line b_j through a_j avoiding p and all other exceptional points of this owner, and different from all earlier exceptional lines already assigned at a_j. This excludes at most t+(t−1) lines out of q+1. Every point a is used as an exceptional point exactly t times, on distinct lines. Write Y_p for the OR of its other q+1−t incident lines. Thus p's retained flags together with the t exceptional flags assigned to each owner partition all flags.

At owner p take r=t+1 products on pairs (p,Y_p) and (a_j,b_j). Encode their OR by its full monotone CNF: every one of the 2^r clauses chooses one literal from each pair. Use binary trees and private copies of every occurrence, including Y_p. OR the N owner terms. By distributivity the full-cube function is precisely incidence. On each native flag exactly one pair in its assigned term has both coordinates true; in fact every other pair has BOTH coordinates false on that input, because the chosen auxiliary lines avoid the owner, and the auxiliary vertices/lines are distinct. On an exceptional flag its line lies outside Y_p. Consequently every accepting parse has 2^(r−1) occurrences of EACH endpoint, including after descending to the unique selected raw in a Y_p OR. No other term accepts that flag. With t=2 every flag requires eight leaves. Increasing t gives unbounded repetition along growing plane orders.

Even simultaneous forwarding of arbitrary gates to either parent within ANY ONE complete owner term, leaving everything else fixed, cannot save a gate while preserving the promise. A surviving clause must accept every flag assigned to this owner: the other terms are fixed and reject each of them. Each of its literals is the sole true literal on the appropriate assigned flag. Every surviving Y_p must still accept all the owner's retained incident queries. Thus no OR subtree in a surviving clause can be pruned. If an AND forwarding removes a clause, choose the opposite coordinate in every pair and set only these coordinates to one. If Y_p must be one, use an allowed query avoiding all selected auxiliary points (at most t forbidden lines among q+1−t options). The selected points and queries are independent: the auxiliary lines miss the owner and other auxiliary points, and no pair has both coordinates. At most r≤q query lines are used. This negative falsifies only the deleted clause, hence all surviving clauses accept it. Every nontrivial edit thus fails. Forwarding an outer collecting OR alone also loses a private flag.

The formula has no shared internal gates and uses N[2^(r−1)(q−t)+r2^r]−1 gates. On the singleton-line regional cold-OR tests every query-pool OR is constant, so marked ORs can only be among the N−1 collecting ORs and N·2^r(r−1) clause ORs. For fixed t this gives O(N) globally distinct cold marks even though all flags are missed by any parse bound below 2^r. Global factoring of the prime CNFs is a cost-decreasing rewrite, so the construction is explicitly not a gate minimum. The irredundancy assertion concerns coordinated parent-forwarding within one owner, not arbitrary multi-gate equivalent replacements.
