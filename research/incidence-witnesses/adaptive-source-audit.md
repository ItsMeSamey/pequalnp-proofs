# Source-cone accounting for 67 shared gates at order 401

Consider a 401-plane and a binary monotone circuit with at most s=646424 gates, correct on every incidence flag and every independent input of at most 400 entries per side. Start with the live normalized circuit and iteratively choose a topologically earliest shared internal gate. Its current internal ancestor cone, including the chosen gate, is a formula of ℓ leaves and ℓ−1 distinct original internal slots. The raw leaf labels may repeat. Every gate inside has no route outside except through the chosen gate. When that gate is fixed to a constant the whole cone can therefore be discarded. During unary rewiring a gate of fanout f transfers at most f−1 extra uses to its surviving parent. If f≥2 a counted shared gate is removed and at most one new parent becomes shared. If f=1 no gate becomes shared. Thus every iteration strictly decreases the count of shared internal gates even though identities of shared slots can change. For t iterations and u the sum of their ℓ, the final formula has at most s−u+t+1 leaves.

Keep a subgraph of good positive flags and the independent set of previously pinned raw inputs. Measure lost edges in units of 402. At a source cone choose j≥1. If an independent free set S of at most 2j vertices makes the cone true, pin S to one and all remaining neighbors to zero. This costs at most 402|S| edge units, including all edges incident to the pins, which also touch their zeroed neighbors. Independence must be checked in the original incidence graph, not just the surviving good flags. Earlier pinned neighbors have already been zeroed, so extending the pin set preserves independence. Every retained flag stays positive by monotonicity.

Otherwise set the gate to zero, discarding the good flags it previously accepted. If there are a such flags, choose the j+1 most populated of 402 disjoint matching classes on these flags. Their subgraph has at least a(j+1)/402 edges and maximum degree j+1. All its proper-neighborhood negatives are independent free sets of at most 2j vertices and are rejected by this cone. Both opposite-endpoint singleton tests are also rejected since j≥1. Thus the formula star-rank argument gives at least E+matching-number leaves on this subgraph, including degree-one vertices by singleton essentiality. Consequently ℓ≥a(j+2)/402. The semantic edit only lowers the output on all inputs, and all good flags unaccepted by this gate persist.

For B=ℓ/8+1206 choose j=floor(B/804). Then 1≤j≤102 on the size range here, so j+1 matching classes exist. The pin branch costs at most 804j≤B edge units. The zero branch costs at most ℓ/(j+2)≤B since ℓ=8B−9648. For j≥6 this is immediate, and for 1≤j≤5 use (6−j)B≤804(6−j)(j+1)≤9648. Reserve 402 units per actual pin even if the number of lost edges is smaller. Hence with C=u/8+1206t the number H of pins satisfies H≤C/402 and the surviving edges number at least 402(N−C), where N=161203.

Suppose t≤66. Since u≤s+t, C≤160407.25 and H≤399. From the surviving edges choose the most populated d=401−H matching classes. They supply at least d(N−C) good edges and degree at most d. Their proper-neighborhood negatives superimposed on all pins use at most H+d−1=400 coordinates per side, and positive and singleton tests use at most H+1≤400. The negatives stay geometrically independent. Semantic zero edits cannot cause new positives on them. By the same star rank and matching argument, the residual formula has at least (d+1)(N−C)≥(402−C/402)(N−C) leaves.

The excess of this lower bound over the formula leaf budget is at least

    (402−C/402)(161203−C)+8C−9649t−646425.

The quadratic is minimized at C=159795.5 and the constant term is worst when t=66. Its value there is 1436.999378… >0. This contradiction proves that any such circuit needs at least 67 shared internal gates. The statement is a conditional obstruction for circuits below the target size, not an unrestricted size lower bound.
