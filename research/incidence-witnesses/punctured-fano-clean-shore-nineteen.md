# A clean shore of six unique paths costs nineteen gates

Assume each of the six raw sites on one shore has a unique path to the output, every hot singleton rejects, and all hot/cold pairs realize the punctured-Fano neighborhoods exactly. The other shore may have arbitrary fanout. At least nineteen binary gates are necessary.

The common-neighbor argument in punctured-fano-equality-cold-tree.md permits at most one AND among the five binary mergers; it would join the two degree-two cold leaves. When every merger is OR, each leaf's neighborhood is exactly the intersection of hot masks at unary ANDs on its path. Bypassing unary OR gates to their cold-dependent operand therefore preserves all required pair positives; all negatives persist by monotonicity. The path intersection formula does not require preserving larger masks. After these bypasses each leaf has a private AND before its first merger by incomparability. Pinning either degree-two leaf to zero eliminates its private AND and also the first merger OR, since the intervening unary gates are AND. 

With an AND cherry each of its two pre-merge branches contains a unary OR: otherwise this branch could never pass when only the opposite leaf is activated. Pin one of the two cherry leaves to zero, eliminating at least its first reader. If fewer than two gates have been removed, the other leaf's pre-cherry OR is still present. All remaining binary cold mergers are OR, the pinned arm having become hot-only. Thus that surviving unary OR can be bypassed as above, supplying a second distinct removal.

Both cases leave five essential cold leaves with unique paths and all-OR mergers, still correct on the required singleton and pair tests. By five-cold-lattice.md they cost at least seventeen gates. This proves the claim. It generalizes to any circuit with these exact tests regardless of its behavior on other rows.

In the small AND-root correction regime with dense parent of size eighteen, the ledger has total fanout surplus seven inside its cone. If the retained negatives pass all first-group and directed hot/cold separation conditions of punctured-fano-eighteen-deletions.md, six or fewer forks would force a clean shore, now excluded. Consequently there are exactly seven distinct branching wires, each of fanout two within the dense cone. Their subsequent reads by the correction are not counted in this ledger.
