# A minimum formula with every raw read twice

Take five points on an arc in PG(2,401), cyclically ordered. The five points and their five side lines are called centers. Also take all five diagonal lines, and the five points at which two nonadjacent side lines meet. These extra vertices are centerless. No diagonal contains one of the extra points: its two defining sides contain four of the five arc points, so a joining line through that intersection containing any one of those four arc points must be one of those sides. Each extra point is on precisely its two chosen sides; three concurrent sides would include two adjacent ones whose arc vertex is on no other side. All ten point labels and all ten line labels are distinct. This induced C4-free graph has 30 flags. The ten centers each have degree four and the ten centerless vertices each have degree two.

Orient the 10-cycle of centers cyclically, and direct every remaining edge from a center to its centerless neighbor. Every center has three outgoing edges and one incoming edge. Take the OR, over the ten centers v, of v AND the OR of its three outgoing neighbors. This full-cube formula represents exactly the oriented flags. It has 39 gates, exactly two occurrences of every raw input, zero AND gates with two raw parents, and no internal sharing. Every centerless raw has two OR readers. Each center has one OR reader and one AND reader. The displayed formula has the least possible gate count among all binary monotone formulas on the restriction.

Indeed, the attachment graph joining centers to centerless vertices is 2-regular, so it contains a perfect matching. The center set is therefore a minimum vertex cover of size ten. In the usual Karchmer–Wigderson star-rank argument, each vertex v of degree at least two needs at least its number of outgoing diagonals plus an indicator that it receives a diagonal. Vertices receiving diagonals cover the edges. Summing the rank bounds gives at least |E|+τ = 30+10 = 40 leaves. All rank witnesses are permissible with q=401. The argument proves optimality among formulas; smaller general circuits with internal sharing are not ruled out.

This realizes the architecture at 4N−1 gates on an induced restriction with N=10 variables per shore. It does not give an upper bound for full projective-plane incidence or for restrictions retaining degree 402.
