# Exact upstream forks and one simultaneous reverse pass

Consider a live binary monotone circuit on a plane of order q, N=q²+q+1, with arbitrary point masks and at most q queried lines. A query line x has a single outgoing occurrence. Follow its unique path until the first internal gate g of fanout at least two, using the output if it occurs first. Put all other lines to zero. For payload T let U_x(T)=g(x=1,T), and let V_g(T) be the output with all lines zero and g forced to one. Because all paths from x pass through g, the exterior is a unary monotone function of g at each fixed T. Its zero cofactor is zero (no greater than the output at zero query). The star of x is therefore U_x AND V_g. Both factors contain all x-neighbor singletons. At the complementary point mask one factor must vanish; that factor agrees with the exact star on every payload by monotonicity. If two lines share the same first fork they have the same V_g containing both stars, hence both U factors are exact. For every sole-read x the zero-query baseline g(0,T) vanishes identically: at all points present the output changes from zero to one with x, so g changes; use monotonicity downward.
 
Compute the entire zero-query baseline once, using at most s−N gates. Follow one chosen outgoing path from each raw query through pure-query gates to a first mixed consumer. Pure gates can merge at most two paths, and each mixed terminal accepts at most one pure operand, so the number of distinct such gates is at least N. Each folds at baseline. The output depends on both shores. On the unique path up to g, the product of the baseline siblings of the AND steps is exactly U_x, by distributing gates with one changing operand and using g(0,T)=0. Computing these products uses no new OR. Thus all upstream-exact stars are computed with only the ORs of the shared baseline.
 
At each of these outputs trace backwards gates whose value on the full complementary mask is zero. At an AND choose any complementary-zero child; it retains all singleton points accepted at the parent. At an OR both children are complementary-zero; route each accepted singleton through a child accepting it. Stop at a single supported point. Every traversed gate has its ACTUAL singleton support contained in the line, since every other point is in the zero mask. A branch on two chosen singleton paths has support at least two. It must be a physical original OR since the extractor added only ANDs; baseline forwarding never changes point-mask evaluations. Distinct lines can never charge the same such gate. In the unforwarded original each of its two operands has the selected singleton supports of the corresponding compiled operands. Apply the trie estimate of star-trie.md.
 
When the pinning proof first hoists sole-use OR spines, the off-spine operands reject every independent singleton. Every bypassed OR hence equals its spine raw on each pure singleton. Induction over all surviving original gates preserves their pure singleton values, including those of each replaced operand. No removed spine OR can have support size two. Therefore all previously certified physical OR slots and the singleton pairs proving their eligibility survive in the unsimplified hoisted circuit. No assertion of full-payload equality after hoisting is needed.
 
For the separate one-pass inequality, let H count sole-use query lines and F be the sum of fanout minus one over live nonoutput internal gates. For lines whose U is not exact, V is exact and their fork is distinct. In one reverse pass seed the output with one. Copy weights through OR, propagate through AND after multiplying by the baseline sibling, and OR contributions at each internal gate. The extra OR cost is at most F. The resulting weight W_g is at most V_g by splicing any one path to the output. It accepts a point singleton whenever there is an accepting intervention proof with just one use of g. Patch each incident singleton missed by W_g by OR-ing its raw point into this output; let B count these flags. All exact original H stars now share at most s−N+F+B OR gates. Each demands q distinct binary OR branches by the same complementary-zero tracing argument; charges are disjoint between stars. Consequently Hq≤s−N+F+B. This statement concerns singleton interventions and arbitrary point masks only.
