# One-sided 267-point pinning at order 401

Let q=401, N=q²+q+1=161203, and L0=ceil(Nq/100)=646425. Consider a live binary monotone circuit for incidence on arbitrary points and at most q selected lines. Constants, if present, are simplified first. Apply the single simultaneous sole-variable OR-spine hoist of sole-or-hoist.md to the whole circuit, before any pinning. Its size does not increase. As in sole-use-compensation.md the new circuit is correct at least on masks with at most q points and at most q−1 lines: for a negative without the spine raw x, choose a neighbor of x avoiding the selected same-side vertices, using codegree one and the strict bound below q+1. Adding this neighbor leaves a legal independent mask for the *original* circuit, but then adding x makes it positive. This proves the bypassed off-spine siblings vanish on these negatives. Monotonicity and the earliest-changed-gate argument preserve all singleton flags. Each remaining sole-use raw now enters a private AND whose other parent accepts every neighbor singleton.

**Which original gates count.** The hoist really changes the circuit. However, every bypassed spine signal agrees with its replacing raw on each pure singleton: its off-spine siblings reject all singletons by the same extension argument. By topological induction all retained old signals still agree with their original singleton evaluations. A gate with at least two different accepted singleton labels cannot itself be a removed spine gate, since each spine signal accepts only its own raw singleton. If one of its operands was a removed spine signal, the new operand has the same singleton evaluations. Thus OR slots and singleton witnesses counted below can be certified in the original circuit, with no need for a second hoist on the restricted circuit. Use the literal simultaneous construction, without extra post-hoist optimization that could erase the named slots.

Choose a uniformly random plane line L and a uniform h=267 subset P of its points. Pin P, set every neighbor line to zero, and allow the pins either sign. The deletion set D at the lower phase comprises both P and all lines meeting P. The h pencils have only L in common, so |D|=h+(1+hq)=107335 and n=2N−|D|=215071. Every free point off L loses exactly h neighbor lines; a free point on L loses just L. Every free line retains all its points. Hence residual minimum degree is k=q+1−h=135. Each deleted nonpin is a line and still has a surviving point, since it has q+1 points and at most h pins. The residual output is nonconstant. For all residual tests involving at most two points and two lines the lower and upper pin phases compute the same residual incidence: no surviving line meets a pin, and even the upper phase uses at most h+2 points and two lines.

Let z mark wires zero when all D are zero and the free raws are one, and let u mark signals true on P alone. Removing gates with a z-parent costs R0 slots. In the usual counting identity for zero-flow.md, R0 equals raw uses of D plus all low-zero internal fanout excess plus the number of ANDs with exactly one low-zero operand. The fanout term is nonnegative (live circuit, nonzero output). Counting two uses per deleted raw incurs deficit at most one per sole-use raw. For every sole-use deleted nonpin its private AND contributes an exactly-one-zero credit, since its other operand accepts a surviving neighbor. These ANDs are distinct because their other operand accepts a full star. The h pins themselves cost at most h total deficit. Thus R0≥2|D|−h.

Additionally remove the slots K of the counted ORs that have u=1 and neither operand z-marked. These are disjoint from R0. A simultaneous implementation processes gates in topological order, keeping each signal between its evaluations at the lower and upper pin phases on every free assignment. A low-zero operand permits ordinary simplification at the lower extreme (zero at AND, bypass at OR), and pin-forced OR slots without low-zero parents may be set to one. Both choices retain the interval invariant. At the output the extremes agree on the required tests. Apply the induced weighted-flow lower bound to the residual circuit, namely s_res≥2n−1−floor(2n/(k+1)). This uses only the residual two-by-two domain, C4-freeness and minimum degree; same-shore OR specialization can select one private neighbor per vertex. Consequently
    s ≥ 4N−1−floor(2n/(k+1))−h+|K|.
Here floor(430142/136)=3162. Thus |K|≥L0−4N+1+3162+267=5043 suffices.

For each distinct OR g select a line m on which it has at least two point-singleton witnesses. Count a point x on m only if g accepts singleton x and each operand accepts a point singleton on m different from x. Let d_g count these eligible x. Count each physical gate with just one selected m. If L is different from m and L meets m at x in P, g is high-forced while both operands accept surviving point singletons. Thus g contributes a K slot. Each eligible x gives probability (q/N)(h/(q+1)), and the events for different x on m are disjoint. Therefore
    E|K| ≥ (267·401)/(161203·402) sum_g d_g.
In particular sum_g d_g ≥ ceil(5043·161203·402/(267·401))=3052338 suffices.

For each OR-only exact star cone on line m, route one path per singleton to its own raw leaf, unfold the path union and suppress unary occurrences. The full binary tree on 402 leaves has total leaf depth at least 3508 (110 leaves at depth 8 and 292 at depth 9 minimize). Every branch occurrence contributes its label multiplicity as gate-point pairs. A label x that is ineligible must occupy a singleton branch since both children are used; charge it to that compressed leaf child. There are at most 402 charges. Each routed label visits a physical gate at most once. Each counted physical gate supports at least two point singletons, all lying on m because the cone is OR-only and exact on point singletons. Different lines share at most one point and so cannot share such a counted gate. Thus 983 distinct exact stars give at least 983·3106=3053198 eligible pairs, above threshold. The statement concerns circuits with this aggregate architecture and does not prove the unrestricted order-401 bound.
