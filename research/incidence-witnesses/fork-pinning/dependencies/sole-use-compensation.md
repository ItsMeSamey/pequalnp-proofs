# Paying the raw-fanout deficit after simultaneous OR hoists

Use the single simultaneous spine transformation in sole-or-hoist.md before any two-line-only specialization. Its proof on arbitrary points and at most q selected lines preserves at least the domain of at most q selected points and q-1 selected lines. To see the reserve is sharp for this rewrite, let x be a point and let B require q fixed lines all missing x. Replacing x by x OR B preserves the original incidence function on all promised inputs, since at B=1 none of those q lines tests x. Hoisting B accepts a negative consisting solely of these lines.

All induced weighted-flow tests require at most two points and two lines. Same-side local variables specialize to OR by setting one neighbor of each; opposite-side variables use one additional point and one additional line. Singleton comparisons, incident local AND restrictions and off-path sibling tests obey the same caps. Hence all-zero restrictions of the hoisted circuit still satisfy the necessary domain, even though the arbitrary-point two-line normalization does not follow from the bilateral hoist.

Let H count raw variables of fanout one in the hoisted circuit. Each directly feeds an AND, and the other operand does not depend on x. At every incident singleton flag x+y the output must depend on x, so the other operand is one on the pure singleton y. These ANDs are distinct: an AND joining two raw inputs cannot have even one of them as a full-star sibling. The number U of raw operand occurrences is at least 4N-H.

Under independent deletion of each raw input with probability p, each private AND has exactly one zero parent whenever x is deleted and at least one of its 402 neighbors survives. This event has probability p(1-p^402). Thus the two leading zero-flow terms pU and expected exactly-one credits together have lower bound 4pN-H p^403. Each private slot is disjoint from the raw-pair ANDs, ANDs of two raw ORs, and ANDs with a raw-OR-pair parent: its raw operand and full-star sibling can be neither a raw-OR pair nor another raw input. These complementary credits remain fully additive. The fanout contribution can cost at most one on the bad degree event, already budgeted in zero-flow.md.

Consequently all the half-deletion thresholds 15805, 31609 and 63217 in zero-flow.md hold for the corresponding motifs of the jointly hoisted circuit irrespective of total raw uses. At worst the previous strict surplus comparison must also pay 2N/2^403, which preserves every stated integer threshold. With balanced flag deletion every deleted vertex except the 2h free vertices still has a surviving neighbor, so the private ANDs deterministically cancel the deficit due to sole-use deletions except those free vertices. Extra possible raw fanout above two is beneficial in expectation.

For a one-sided alternative, minimize gate count on arbitrary points and at most two lines, and hoist line-raw spines only. This retains the full domain: on a negative without x, at most two selected lines cover at most two points of x, so some point of x can be appended without adding a line. The resulting circuit is still minimum, and consequently every gate satisfies two-line normalization (otherwise a gate can be removed). Let H_L count sole-use line inputs and U_L raw line uses. Each such line gives a full point star. Delete only lines independently with probability 1/2. No pure point is deleted, so every edited genuine rich OR has two nonzero parents and gives a full extra credit when at least one of its witnessing lines is deleted. For one label this probability is 1/2, for two labels 3/4, at least 3/8 per label. Thus E[R] >= U_L/2+3W/8-1, and W>=200H_L.

Only the N point degrees can fail k=138 in this one-sided restriction. With epsilon=N times Pr[Bin(402,1/2)<138], the same score calculation as zero-flow.md gives a sufficient surplus
    (U_L-2N)/2+3W/8 > L-4N+1+3N/139+(L-1)epsilon.
The right hand side is below 5101.987. As U_L>=2N-H_L, H_L>=69 is sufficient without any raw point-use hypothesis. The bounds are conditional on measurable parameters of the appropriate normal form; q401 remains open.
