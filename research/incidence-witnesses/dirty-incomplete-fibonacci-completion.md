# Aggregate Fibonacci proofs with every shared internal gate dirty and incomplete

Use the plane, proxies U,V,W,A,B, recurrence h_i, exits e_i, and three special flags of flag-live-fibonacci-endpoint-reads.md. Let E be the remaining edge set obtained by removing the three special flags and all t matched flags f_i, 1≤i≤t. Choose z on b₀ other than x and a₀; it lies off l. Build two physically separate ordinary star formulas on the same raws and proxies, using a point-star formula for M₁ on E∪{(z,l)} and a line-star formula for M₂ on E∪{(a₁,b₂)}. Both added pairs are nonedges. Fold each extra entry into its own star's inner OR. All ordinary formula gates have fanout one, even where formulas would otherwise share a partial OR. Keep the original D=U AND V and the original W; use S=D OR M₁ as both base seeds and T=W OR M₂ as terminal selector. The scalar output ORs the t exits and h_t AND T.

On every independent input the proxies agree with their raws, and S can fire only in the presence of l and at least one of x,z. If S is false, induction kills all h_i on that input because each fresh pair is incident. When S is true, every a_i is absent since each lies on l, and b₀ is absent since both x and z lie on b₀. This kills the exits, W, and the M₂ ghost, so the terminal is false. Every E flag activates both completions, hence the terminal. The special flags activate S and W, and each matched flag activates its exit. Monotonicity gives full-cube correctness.

The complete list of possibly shared internal gates is U,V,W,A,B,S and h₁,…,h_t. Each of U,A,W accepts the pure point a₀, and each of V,B accepts the pure line b₀. Each misses f₁. The nonedge (x,l) fires S and all h_i. At each f_j both completions, D and W are false and the proxies A,B are zero. Only h_j fires there: below j all predecessors are false, above j both fresh raws vanish and the recurrence takes consecutive predecessor ANDs. Thus S misses every f_j and each h_i misses every other f_j. All internally shared gates are dirty on legal masks and miss at least one native flag. The complete circuit is flag-live: ordinary ghost-entry ORs can be placed along a chain starting with a genuine neighbor, so all ordinary gates fire on a genuine row/column flag; every bank gate fires on a special, matched or ordinary flag. No gate dedicated solely to an isolated ghost pair is needed.

At every flag avoiding x,l and every a_i,b_i, all bank raws vanish, both completions are true, all exits false, and every proof of the terminal uses exactly F_t copies of M₁ and one of M₂. Every such copy requires exactly its two real endpoint leaves; neither proxy fires. Thus every proof has exactly 2(F_t+1) leaves on at least (N−2t−4)(q+1) flags, N=q²+q+1. Both anchor raws still have precisely two physical readers. The unshared completion formulas each cost |E|+N gates, since all N rows or columns have nonzero remaining degree and one extra entry was added. The bank costs 5t+5, plus two gates for S,T, giving 2N(q+2)+3t+1 overall. The ordinary full-plane point-star construction costs N(q+2)−1 gates. In particular the example survives the stated shared-gate filter while allowing global size improvements. The first ghost strictly enlarges S over D on the independent pair (z,l), and the second strictly enlarges T over W on (a₁,b₂). The displayed completion can nevertheless delete both ghost entries without changing the scalar output, since all genuine flags remain covered.
