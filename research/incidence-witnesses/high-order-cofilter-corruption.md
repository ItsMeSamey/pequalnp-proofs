# A linear physical source of dense labeled corruption

Let q be an odd prime, N=q²+q+1, and use affine points and the q nonvertical slope classes of affine lines in PG(2,q). Take q sufficiently large. Choose for every slope a a fixed subset Y_a of ceil(q/2) parallel lines. The Y_a are disjoint sets of labels, each covering at least half the affine points. A single signal Q_trigger=OR_a AND_{l∈Y_a} l costs O(N) gates.

An O(N)-gate monotone estimate of the number n of selected affine points can be fixed in advance. Pad the q² input bits by zeros to a power of two and merge in a balanced tree. At height j≥1 round the sum of the two child estimates down to the nearest of 0 and powers (1+1/[100(j+1)²])^i, i≥0, not exceeding 2^j. A positive estimate is always at least one. Store its unary comparisons with the O((j+1)³) possible positive thresholds, together with constant comparisons when needed. For each comparison at a node, OR the ANDs of child threshold bits whose indicated lower values have sum reaching that comparison, including lower zero. This computes the rounded comparison exactly. There are polynomially many gates in j per node and O(N/2^j) nodes at height j, hence O(N) gates in total. The estimate is an underestimate and is at least n divided by the product of the height factors, which is less than 1.02. Test the estimate against 0.7q², using the stored threshold immediately above if necessary. This test implies n≥0.7q² and accepts all n≥0.9q² for large q.

Apply this estimator to the inputs p AND Q_trigger. Call its verdict E. It requires Q_trigger and at least 0.7q² affine points, and accepts whenever a trigger Y_a is selected and at least 0.9q² affine points are active. Whenever E=1 some actual incidence exists, indeed at least 0.2q² distinct selected points meet the selected trigger. Every new gate beyond raw point/query leaves is zero on a singleton point-query pair: the query trigger ANDs have at least two distinct line leaves and the point inputs to the estimator are gated. Fold constants and simplify unary leaf cases. These added signals do not create large pure singleton pools or large false-pair graphs.

Take native cofilters C_p with exact star semantics on payload {p}, in any exact OR-of-rows completion as in cofilter-conflict-cleaning.md. Replace every C_p by C_p OR E. This costs O(N) gates including one new OR per head. Native tests and all independent tests are unchanged, and outputs grow only on already-positive inputs. Every global explanation graph for the new cofilter errors, even one using nonincident pairs, has Ω(Nq) edges. To see this, suppose m≤Nq/20. Some Y_a has at most m/q≤N/20 incident J-edges. Choose an affine owner p on one of the omitted parallel lines. Activate Y_a and choose as external payload S every affine point except p and the J-neighbors of Y_a. Then S contains at least 0.9q² points for large q and J misses S×Y_a. The native cofilter rejects Y_a at {p}, whereas the new cofilter accepts at {p}∪S. The test uses ≤q−2 queries. The argument applies even if earlier errors already existed. In particular for starting pure exact query stars the only new corruptions are high-order: they require at least ceil(q/2) queries and a constant fraction of all points.

## True flags versus arbitrary explanatory pairs

Begin with pure query star heads, so there is no other corruption. Choose two distinct affine anchors u,v and a fixed affine query l₀ missing both. In each slope choose Y_a to avoid l₀ and the lines through u or v; enough lines remain. Additionally require u,v,l₀ for E. This leaves the truth and cost properties above. Two nonincident pairs now explain every corrupted native-negative test: l₀ is selected and at least one of u,v belongs to the external payload rather than being the owner itself. Thus J={(u,l₀),(v,l₀)} suffices.

Any J restricted to actual incidence still needs Ω(Nq) edges. Choose Y_a as before and add l₀. The additional line contributes at most q+1 J-neighbors, and neither anchor can be a J-neighbor of any selected line. Choose p outside these lines and different from both anchors, and take all remaining J-free affine points other than p as S. The augmented E activates and no J edge lies in S×(Y_a∪{l₀}). Its query count is still ≤q−2 for large q.

Accordingly a large mandatory explanation size can be caused by a single physically inexpensive detector on high-order already-positive inputs. Conversely a tiny nonincident explanation can conceal a large minimum explanation restricted to actual flags. Neither example gives a small circuit for all native stars or a smaller exact completion.
