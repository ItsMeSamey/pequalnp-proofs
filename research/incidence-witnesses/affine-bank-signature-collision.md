# Parallel queries collide at every pivot

In the first shared-column-rank-pivots.md construction let H_x=F∨v_x. Replace G_x at row-product uses by G'_x=G_x∧H_x, retaining the outer guard H_x. Each guarded row is unchanged on the full cube by conditioning on H_x. At a proper test S_(p,L_j), v_x=1, so the added conjunct is true. A forced off-diagonal route into G'_x must continue through old G_x and down to the original p pin. Thus rank q persists simultaneously at all q² distinct affine site pins under every tie rule.

Compare a flag (p,L) and independent mask (p,L') with L' a different line parallel to the nonvertical L. All affine column occupancies, original G_z, infinity-point signals, slope ORs Y_j, common Q and vertical query inputs match. But H_x is one on the flag and zero on the negative for x the column of p. With all infinity points absent, the updated unguarded row collector C'_x is one on the flag and zero on the negative. At the maximal negative for the single query L', the donor and H_x are still zero. The only active slope is i and its infinity point is absent because it lies on L', hence C'_x is still zero. Consequently no monotone recipe over this dictionary meets the directional specification on flags and maximal negatives: its required zero there forces a zero on (p,L'). For arbitrary Boolean recipes the impossibility is for the specification testing all independent negatives. An unrestricted nonmonotone recipe tested only on maximal negatives could behave differently on (p,L').

The collision holds for every pivot. Admitting G'_x restores the original algebraic factorization. This is a fixed-dictionary obstruction on an explicit circuit, without minimum-size assumptions.
