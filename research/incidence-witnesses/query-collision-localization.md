# Localizing repeated query reads at AND gates

Fix the line shore as queries. Write LS(h) for the set of raw query labels with a syntactic path to h. For every AND g let I_g be the intersection of its two parents' query supports. Let D contain those ANDs with a nonempty I_g, and H their inclusive gate-ancestor union. In addition to the reverse-single-read.md construction, cache h(P,{l}) for each affected h and l in its LS. Process gates forward, using the zero-query baseline for parents not mentioning l. This costs at most the sum of |LS(h)| over H. It suffices to cache l only when it is required by some downstream overlap in I_g; this optional restriction can be propagated backward.

For every g in D and l in I_g append W_g(P) AND g(P,{l}) to the output for l; W_g is the zero-query reverse context. Its presence implies original acceptance at that singleton query, even with downstream shared copies, by the context path and its unchanged baseline sibling proofs. A true positive at a queried flag admits a backward trace to a changed child at OR, and to a changed child with baseline-true sibling at AND until reaching either the raw query (recognized by the original reverse term) or a first AND where both children are false at baseline and true on the flag. In the latter case l belongs to I_g and the complete context W_g is true. Thus all stars are computed. The cost is bounded by
    Nq <= 2s+2A-2N+1 + sum_(h in H)|LS(h)| + 2 sum_(g in D)|I_g|.
The same proof is valid in the opposite orientation on the original sparse sample. If |LS(g)|<=d for all g in D, every ancestor in H has width at most d, giving Nq<=(4+3d)s-2N+1. In particular d<=32 gives Nq<=100s at all orders, with no bound on sharing or gate depth. A bound on overlap size alone, without the cache demand cost, is insufficient for this deduction.

If only overlap gates of width <=d are patched this way, append the missed singleton flags at one gate each. A below-target q401 circuit consequently has at least 322408 flags per orientation missed when d=32. With d=15 at least 33290032 are missed per orientation, thus at least 1776458 require a larger-support split in both orientations. With d=8 the both-orientations count is at least 28926266. The first split can be a different gate in the two orientations. Both independent reviewers audited this construction.
