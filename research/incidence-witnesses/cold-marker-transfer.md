# Few cold markers and simultaneous sparse interventions

Work with the original scalar circuit, arbitrary point masks and at most q selected lines. For a sole-read query line x follow its unique directed path to its first fork gₓ (or output). Write Uₓ(P) for gₓ on point payload P and with x its sole active query. It accepts every singleton on x. As in fork-publication/fork-transfer.md, the Uₓ share a zero-query baseline with at most O≤s−N physical OR gates, plus extracted AND gates only: gₓ's zero-query baseline vanishes and the single changing input up to the fork is propagated by the product of baseline AND siblings. A dirty marker has inexact Uₓ; its forced-fork output Vₓ at zero query is the exact point star of x. Distinct dirty markers have distinct first forks.

Fix 1≤t≤q. For a set J of lines put P_J equal to all points outside their union. Call x t-cold if some J with x∈J and |J|≤t has Uₓ(P_J)=0. This includes upstream-exact x; restrict the count to dirty x if desired. Then

    # t-cold ≤ floor(t O / (ceil((q+1)/t)−1)).

For each cold x choose one such J. In the extractor trace from Uₓ through gates rejecting P_J and accepting more than t singleton points on x. At each traced AND choose one rejecting parent: its actual singleton support contains that of the AND. At each traced OR both parents reject P_J. Expand only parents meeting the heaviness threshold. Each boundary wire has at most t points on x, including raw wires. The boundary supports collectively cover all q+1 root points by the containment property of the chosen AND and union property of the OR. In the reached physical DAG with a distinct OR gates and h distinct unaryized AND gates, the boundary has at most 2a+h−(a+h−1)=a+1 distinct wires by connectedness. Therefore a≥ceil((q+1)/t)−1. All charged ORs are physical baseline gates. Each rejects P_J, hence its entire singleton support lies in the union of at most t lines. There are at most t lines on which this particular support contains more than t points: each such line must be in every covering J. Thus any physical OR receives at most t charges over all selected x, even when witnesses J differ.

For q=401,s≤646424,N=161203 one has O≤485221. For t=2 at most 4852 lines are cold, and for t=3 at most 10944 are cold. No small positive certificate of the dirty guard is assumed.

For a subset X of noncold dirty markers of size at most t, forcing all their forks to one at zero query gives precisely the OR of their stars on all payloads. Each single-fork star is a lower bound. On P_X the true circuit with queries X activates every forced fork by monotonicity from Uₓ(P_X)=1; the zero-query forced computation is dominated by that true negative. All disjoint payloads are subsets of P_X. More generally a separate arbitrary set J of at most t active queries likewise activates, on P_J, every marked fork whose marker belongs to J.

## Size credit on private paths with an AND

Select any set of noncold dirty markers whose paths through their first forks each contain an AND. These private path gate sets are disjoint, since two merging private paths would have the same first fork. If a path starts with OR, hoist the initial OR prefix, ending immediately before its first AND: bypass the prefix by its raw x and append its off-path siblings to the global output disjunction as in sole-or-hoist.md. The total initial cost does not increase. Only marked prefixes are hoisted. Old gate values weakly decrease before the output append. Every appended sibling is originally zero on an independent input with query set J, |J|≤t≤q−1, even for arbitrary point masks: turn off x, add a point on x avoiding the at most |J| other lines, and compare before/after adding x. If that sibling were one then its spine exit hides the sole raw's change. The enlarged masks have at most q queries. The usual earliest-change argument at a singleton flag shows that the appended output with hoisted prefixes preserves all positive flags.

Now replace each selected raw x by zero in its private path. At the exit gₓ inject x by OR with its resulting computed value, including all other modifications topologically. For a maximal negative (P_J,J), every injected x in J had original gₓ=1 by noncoldness, even if hoisting changed its computed value. Every edited old signal is therefore bounded by its original value on this negative, and the appended siblings are bounded by their original zero values. At a flag with active query unmarked, all splice inputs are zero and the value agrees with the hoisted circuit. At a marked flag, suppressing the sole active raw only affects its private path before its fork; the injected fork value one dominates its old hoisted value, so the hoisted output still accepts. No appended detector branches off the interior of that marked private path: between prefixes of the selected distinct-fork markers such branching would merge their original paths at an OR and force the same first fork. The raw x on a newly hoisted path first meets an AND. Constant-zero propagation removes its initial AND run and its first later OR, if one exists, freeing at least two slots before paying at most one to reinsert x. If no later OR exists, the entire private path folds to zero and reinsertion is just a wire. Thus at least one slot per selected marker is saved. This edit has been proved on all ≤t-query negatives and all positive flags; it leaves every raw variable available to the residual circuit.

At q401 use t=2. The full two-by-two-domain weighted-flow bound on the residual circuit is 4N−1−floor(4N/403)=643211. Saving 3214 slots therefore suffices for the target 646425. In particular 8066 distinct dirty markers with an AND on their pre-fork paths suffice, since at most 4852 are cold. This conditional credit does not cover dirty markers whose private path consists solely of ORs.
