# Quantitative antichain tests for distinct flag signatures

In a trimmed minimum binary monotone plane-incidence circuit select k incomparable nonroot gates, each with at least two distinct consumers. Write f for their number of distinct direct consumers. Cutting all outgoing uses to zero removes at least f+1_{f=k} rows and f≥k (shared-signature-common-negative.md). If only j of the chosen gates are retained for repair another k−j original rows disappear by incomparability. Let L be the actually lost flags. Denote by P_e the chosen gates true at flag e, and by P_Y those true at the maximal independent payload for query set Y. There are M=sum_{i≤q} binom(N,i) such masks; they dominate all promised negatives.

**Almost-common negative.** If every e in L misses at most h of the k signals, some maximal negative misses at most b ln(bM), where b=h+1. Otherwise color the k sites independently uniformly with b colors. For each negative and color the probability its zero sites miss the color is at most exp(−|zero sites|/b). A union bound gives a single coloring for which every negative has a zero in each color. Every lost flag activates a whole color because it has fewer than b holes. OR of within-color conjunctions restores every lost flag, rejects every negative, and costs ≤k gates including the residual-root join. Colors are nonempty by the negative condition. This contradicts the ≥k+1 gate savings. In particular one ≤q query set hits all but b ln(bM) of any banks B_i whose intersection with Y is necessary for i to fire on a negative. Banks can overlap arbitrarily. At h=0 the previous exact common-negative theorem is stronger.

**Unrestricted activation profiles.** Write beta=max_Y |P_Y|/k and alpha_e=|P_e|/k. For any positive integers b,t with bM beta^t<1 and d=f+k+1_{f=k}−2bt>0,

(1−bM beta^t)d ≤ 2 sum_{e in L}(1−alpha_e^t)^b.

Independently sample b blocks of t sites with replacement. The OR of their conjunctions is sound with probability at least 1−bM beta^t and misses each lost flag with probability (1−alpha_e^t)^b. In a sound trial retain only those ≤bt different selected signals that were drawn; constructing and appending the DNF costs at most bt gates. For the residual missed flags E' add a star repair at cost ≤|E'|+vertex-cover(E')≤2|E'| including its join: assign each flag to one covering vertex, OR the distinct neighboring raws at each used vertex, intersect its own selector, OR all products into the output. Empty E' costs zero. The physical cut savings plus minimality imply d≤2|E'| on every sound trial, giving the formula by multiplying by the sound indicator and taking expectation. One common cut and one actual block family are used in each trial. M may be replaced by the number of inclusion-maximal negative activation signatures. No existence of an adequately large, coherent antichain in every circuit is assumed.
