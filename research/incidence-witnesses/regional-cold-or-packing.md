# Regional marking on original singleton-line projections

Fix just query line l to one, other query lines to zero. Trace the scalar output backwards on the negative with every point outside l active. At an OR trace both parents; at an AND choose a false parent. Stop at wires of specialized point-singleton support size ≤1. Every reached wire rejects that complement; all its singleton supports lie inside l. The union of frontier supports covers l since an OR takes union and each chosen AND child contains its parent's support. There are ≥q+1 distinct frontier wires. Connected-graph edge counting makes the number of frontier wires at most one plus the number of physical ORs branched at, even with unrestricted reconvergence and tied slots. Denote these physical ORs C_l. A reached wire cannot be constant one. This uses the original one-line negatives; the dual dense-line complement is outside the promise.

Weights w_l≥0 with Σ_{l:g∈C_l} w_l≤1 at every physical gate certify s≥q Σ_l w_l. This permits ≥Nq regional mark occurrences, with shared gates paid only once. If weights of total N/100 exist the target follows at every order; multiplicity ≤100 suffices.

Even absence of internal fanout need not produce these weights. In the exact formula OR_p [p AND (OR of its incident queries)] with private query cones and binary collection tree, singleton-line specialization leaves q+1 point terms live. Every point term has singleton support at most one and is a stopping wire. All ORs charged in every region therefore lie in the same N−1 collection gates, whatever the grouping. Summing the gate capacities gives Σ_l w_l≤(N−1)/q for EVERY permitted collection of traces. The formula has no shared internal gates. Forwarding any individual binary gate to either input breaks promise correctness: each query OR or collector OR loses a private flag; replacing a point product by either input accepts a pure negative. Global gate minimality is not asserted.

The Θ(Nq) query-producer work lies entirely outside the regional marks. Thus bounded physical fanout and single-gate irredundancy do not yield low regional overlap. A minimum-circuit argument must charge the hidden producer gates or find other compatible regions.
