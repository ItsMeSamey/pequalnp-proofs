# Omission sets retaining the eighteen-gate lower bound

For up to four omitted maximal negatives keep all sixteen flags. The proof in punctured-fano-twenty-maxima.md extends provided (i) each pair of potential hot sites remains distinguishable by swapping labels on a singleton background, (ii) for each ordered opposite-shore nonincident pair x,y some retained maximum contains x and a neighbor of y, and (iii) every nonincident opposite-shore pair is contained in some retained maximum. Condition (i) ensures at most one hot label per first branch. The induced-P4 and singleton restrictions survive any four omissions by punctured-fano-almost-full-forks.md. Therefore at seventeen gates there are exactly six hot labels spanning one shore, each with its own first fork. Condition (ii) and the positive incident pairs prevent cold contamination of these first forks; condition (iii) identifies every leaf neighborhood exactly. The cold-tree and hot-gate counting proof then applies verbatim.

Independent checker review verifies that all three conditions hold for 198 of 210 two-row omission sets, 1062 of 1330 three-row sets, and 3441 of 5985 four-row sets, as well as all singleton omissions. Failure of a condition leaves that case undecided. Source: check-fano-eighteen-deletions.py.
