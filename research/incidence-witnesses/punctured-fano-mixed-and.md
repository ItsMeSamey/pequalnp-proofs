# Warm mixed ANDs in a 20-gate candidate

Use the common original sample of punctured-Fano independent two-by-two negatives and all 16 positive flags. A cold gate vanishes on all these negatives; other gates and all raws are warm. Suppress cold operands in the warm network, bypass its warm boundary ORs, retain the c0 cold ANDs with two warm operands as terminals, and collect them by OR. By negative-profile-normalization.md the size falls by at least F_C+c1, with F_C the original nonoutput cold fanout excess and c1 the number of cold gates with one cold operand.

A rebuilt source is safe if its operand cones contain no warm AND with ancestry on both shores. In a safe cone, a signal active on a flag already activates on one endpoint singleton: pure gates see only their own shore, and mixed gates are ORs. Each productive safe source therefore has nonempty singleton supports on both operands. These must be disjoint and on opposite shores, since a shared singleton or same-shore pair would trigger a forbidden negative. No mixed warm signal with bicolor singleton support can reach a productive safe source parent, as every subsequent gate on that path is mixed and hence OR, preserving both colors.

Suppress singleton-silent gates in those cones using their rebuilt signatures. They reject every flag and their removal only decreases negatives. In the remaining circuit a mixed-ancestry OR with unicolor support receives surviving signals of that color alone. Its simplified realization has only that shore as raw ancestry; original warm ANDs in the cone already have pure ancestry. Thus no shared gate needs duplicating to obtain separated products.

If the safe sources cover all 16 flags, this construction yields a separated circuit no larger than the original, which requires 21 gates by punctured-fano-separated-products.md. Consequently every size-at-most-20 candidate has a flag with no accepting safe source, and contains a structurally mixed AND firing on a required negative. This follows from the physical circuit, independent of any solver return.

Quantitatively retain all productive safe sources; let d count all other discarded sources, including unproductive ones. Let h count distinct remaining warm gates outside the retained source cones, excluding every previously removed gate and all collector/source gates. If E is the set of uncovered flags, assign its edges to a minimum vertex cover of size τ(E) and build a star product per group. A group of j flags needs j gates before aggregation. The new collector costs c0−d+τ−1 instead of c0−1; even if no safe sources remain E contains all flags. This builds a separated circuit of size at most s−F_C−c1−2d−h+|E|+τ(E). Thus for s≤20,
    |E|+τ(E) ≥ 1+F_C+c1+2d+h.
Independent audit confirmed the purity reduction and quantitative physical ledger.
