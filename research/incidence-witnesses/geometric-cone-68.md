# Geometric source-cone threshold

Refine size-sharing-interpolation.md at q=401 by writing a=799.75. For 3<=j<=90 a pin of at most j points and j lines loses at most a j edge units. If a shore has fewer than j pins the bound 804j-402 suffices. With j pins on both shores each zero-neighborhood has size at most 402j-(j-1). At least 402j-binom(j,2) lines pass through the pin points. Count their intersections with the j pin lines: any pair of pin lines meets in a point outside the point pins, so at most j lines counted from the point neighborhood pass through that meet. Consequently cross-incidences between zero-neighborhoods save another j²(1-(j-1)/402) units. The total saving is at least 4.25j throughout this range.
