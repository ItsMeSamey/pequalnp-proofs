For the 21-feature projected care table, the monotone operand of a sole
inversion requires at least 25 binary gates.

Let H and L be the forced-one and forced-zero operand rows. For each
comparable descending target pair (a,b), monotonicity before and after
the sole inversion forces a in L and b in H. Keeping only minimal H and
maximal L retains all constraints. The 21 singleton differences establish
that all 21 features occur in the useful operand DAG.

For a useful DAG with g binary gates, n inputs and one output, the sum
of (fanout-1) over all vertices except the output is g-n+1. Split at every
vertex of fanout at least two, keeping it as the output of a region and
replacing each outward use with a separate formal leaf. This yields at
most g-n+2 regions, each a read-once formula on its local input leaves
and donor references. Order regions topologically. Each original input
belongs to exactly one region, including singleton regions when an input
itself has multiple uses.

For h in H choose a consistent 1-certificate trace, taking both inputs
at AND, one true input at OR. For l in L choose a consistent 0-trace
dually. Let P_h and N_l be traced original variables. P_h hits h minus
l for every l; N_l hits h minus l for every h. Within each region traced
in both assignments, the positive and negative proof trees share at
most one leaf: at every common internal node exactly one child can be
shared. Hence at most one member of P_h intersect N_l is local to any
given region.

If P_h reaches a local input of region t and N_l reaches a local input
of the same region, its output is true at h and false at l. Since this
output depends monotonically only on inputs in t or earlier regions,
some feature in h minus l must lie in a region numbered at most t.

precone_region_sat.py 5 --ordered encodes exactly these necessary
conditions with five regions. Color each feature by its region. The
'pos' and 'neg' variables select traced inputs. 'same' is forced true
for each pair in one region and disallows joint trace intersections of
size two. 'true' and 'false' indicate a trace visits a local variable in
a region. The last region's ordering constraint is automatic. Empty
regions are allowed, so infeasibility also rules out fewer regions.
The CNF and its DRUP proof are precone-region-5.cnf and
precone-region-5.drup. drat-trim independently reports VERIFIED (1006
initial core clauses, 594 proof lemmas). Thus at least six regions
are required and g >= 21-2+6 = 25. Six regions satisfy this relaxation.
