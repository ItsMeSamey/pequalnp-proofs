# Low-degree positive certificates on 2-CNF assignments

Translate a satisfiable exterior 2-CNF by any model x0, writing z=x+x0. Nontrivial clauses become implications, negative pair conflicts, or forbidden singleton bits. Feasible flip sets F are precisely implication-closed conflict-free sets. For every seed S of at most d bits compute its implication closure K and retain it when feasible. On all feasible F the monomial z_S is exactly 1[K subset F], or identically zero when K is infeasible. Distinct feasible seeded closures give a unitriangular containment matrix on those same physical assignments. They therefore span the degree-d moment space and their count is its exact rank. Translate rows back to the original basis.

For each additional clause, pin its literals false and repeat on the pinned exterior 2-CNF. The sum of these physical spans is exactly the degree-d evaluation span of all rejected models. Rank loss versus the full exterior space occurs exactly when some degree-d polynomial is nonzero on the exterior and zero on all rejected models. A retained full-exterior row outside that span gives a physical solution. This costs (#clauses+1)n^{O(d)}poly(input) and does not expand the clause product. Equality of ranks rules out this kind of certificate only.

For positive added clauses in flip coordinates, the minimum certificate degree equals the smallest number of generators of a feasible closed set hitting all clauses. Indeed feasible sets avoiding a clause form an order ideal; vanishing there forces zero coefficients at all its containment indicators, by triangularity. Conversely a monomial on the generators of any feasible closed hitting set certifies success. Without implications, this is conflict-free hitting set; branching on uncovered clauses of width w takes w^d poly(input). With arbitrary implications the degree optimization already contains Set Cover: a set bit implies every covered element bit, all elements are demanded by unit clauses, and an element chosen directly as a seed may be replaced by any containing set. The all-one assignment is nevertheless plainly feasible.

On the unrestricted cube the optimum is the ordinary transversal number. In the complete Latin table of the additive group GF(2^r), r>=2, q=2^r, take y=1 to mean a site is true. The q disjoint forbidden triples (a,alpha a,(1+alpha)a) for alpha distinct from zero and one imply degree at least q, attained by forcing one entire rail true. If one independently pins three FALSE sites a0,b0,c0 on respective rails with nonzero sum, degree on the free cube is 3q/2: any admissible false domains A,B,C are nonempty and their pairwise sizes sum to at most q by translating the other rail's pin. Their total is at most 3q/2. A functional l with l(a0+b0+c0)=1 gives corresponding pin cosets of size q/2 each, attaining equality.

The preceding false-unit interface is enlarged. On the original TRUE-pin signed-star interface, choose a fourth rail site d outside its rail's pin coset, and use the same physical bit with positive sign at d and opposite signs at a0,b0,c0, leaving all other sites independently free. Pin d TRUE. All three false sites are then forced, d is already a true transversal site, and the optimal cosets exclude d. The exact degree on the remaining original free coordinates is 3q/2 minus one. These connected examples are feasible; only the degree of globally sound nonzero acceptance polynomials is lower-bounded.
