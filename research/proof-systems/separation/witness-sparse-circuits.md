# Witness lower bound for circuits with bounded sharing and AND counts

Assume the October witness W in E^NP and quantitative-circuits.tex. For each fixed positive A,B,C, infinitely many lengths L admit no XOR-AND circuit for W with both size at most B L log^C L and total AND plus shared affine gate count at most A L. Fanout counts operand occurrences. Which cap is exceeded may depend on the presentation.

Otherwise guess a well-formed circuit at witness length L=Dn for the source's fixed D and all sufficiently large n. Its polynomial-size description, budget and acyclicity are checked directly. The source verifies the candidate via one clause-violation query on n+O(log n) bits, three independent witness copies, and O(n) decoder gates. The decoder's newly shared outputs number at most O(n) regardless of their fanout. Materialize all decoder gates, the named bits of the three copies, and final constant-size violation logic. Flatten single-use affine subtrees and name each AND operand; tree interiors are disjoint. This gives O(n) logical variables/equations with O(n log^C n) total monomial occurrences, with chronological unique extension. Chunk the long parities as in october-side-equations.md. The sparse polynomial lemma applies to O(n) declared variables and O(n) fixed-degree equations with polylogarithmic monomial lists per row. The deterministic violation test costs 2^o(n) time. On a hierarchy NO input every candidate violates a decoded clause; on YES the true witness's presumed representation passes. Hard-code finitely many small lengths in the contradiction. This contradicts the same unary hierarchy clock, producing the stated restricted lower bound. The proof does not establish hardness for arbitrary polynomial circuits or P versus NP.
