# Chart polynomials by paired minor interpolation

Let the original columns span a binary vector space of dimension r and write the label of a cycle as ℓ(C), the weight of a cocycle with dual functional λ as λ(u). For an (r−1)-subset X of elements other than s,t, define binary determinants

    a = det(s,X),   b = det(s+t,X),   d = det(ŝ,t̂,X̂),   e = det(u,X),   g = d+e.

The lift is v̂=(v,ℓ_v), and ordering signs disappear. The subset is a compatible chart iff a=b=1. On this locus the unique expansion of t in the basis (s,X) has zero s coordinate. Column reduction then gives d=ℓ(C_t), and Cramer's rule gives e=λ_s(u). Thus, under individual-fiber stationarity, the attainable objective bits are precisely the third bits of the attained chart cells (1,1,g).

The formal polynomials P_σ over GF(2) sum distinct squarefree monomials z_X for which (a,b,g)=σ. For any two different cells σ,τ, the polynomial P_σ+P_τ has a polynomial-size arithmetic circuit. The sum of the two Boolean point indicators has multilinear degree at most two (both cubic coefficients are one). Expand its terms using g=d+e. For any of a,b,d,e, its values are maximal minors of a fixed (r−1)-by-n matrix: quotient out its fixed terminal vector(s), with zero determinant throughout if those are dependent. Over GF(2) no orientation scale is needed. For any two such signatures i,j, the coefficientwise product sum is det(M_i diag(z) M_jᵀ) by Cauchy–Binet. Single signatures use i=j since each coefficient is binary. The constant function gives the elementary symmetric polynomial of degree r−1 and is computed by its standard subset recurrence. In particular on instances lacking the cell (0,0,1), P_111 comes from g²+ag+bg pointwise.

Given any target cell τ, choose an alternative σ, and recover terms of P_σ+P_τ using multilinear coefficient restriction and polynomial identity testing. Check each resulting monomial by Gaussian elimination and subtract it if spurious. Batch by round robin over the seven alternatives. In time polynomial in the input and 1+min_(σ≠τ) |supp P_σ|, the procedure produces a verified target term or finds a pair polynomial exhausted after verified spurious terms. Zero-testing by random evaluations in a sufficiently large extension field gives one-sided error for the existence decision, an arbitrarily small error probability per finite execution by using summably decreasing per-test error budgets. Restriction for a variable's coefficient is the sum of its evaluations at zero and one, specialized others unchanged. To avoid exponential circuit growth under iterated restrictions use homogeneous multilinearity: on disallowing variables set them to zero; to force accumulated set I scale its variables by an auxiliary indeterminate y, then interpolate the coefficient of y^|I|. This costs a polynomial factor, also for already subtracted explicit monomials. At most a polynomial number of evaluations per recovered term suffices. This is an output-sensitive conditional algorithm, not a general polynomial bound.

All eight cells can occur even under stationarity. Rank five column masks (19,26,20,17,7,5,13,6,21,4), roots s=0,t=1, ℓ=205, u=28 give counts 11,6,8,24,8,8,1,4 in binary lexicographic order. Enumerating cycles and the 32 dual functionals yields precisely three realized cycles and three realized cocycles; each fixed fiber is constant in the objective. Thus even for stationary instances the cell-111 indicator cannot always be represented by a quadratic in the three signatures a,b,g. This obstruction concerns those signatures rather than arbitrary additional minors.
