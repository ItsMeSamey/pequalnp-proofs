# Selective chart PIT using incidence moments

Use the eight chart cells and the pair-union circuits of chart-pit.md. Let n count ordinary columns, k≥0, and let φ(X) list the squarefree incidence monomials of degree at most k in 1_X, including the constant 1. Write m=Σ_{j≤k} binom(n,j), and let V_i be the binary span of these moment vectors in cell i. Define

    U_ij = V_i + V_j,        B_i = intersection over j≠i of U_ij.

All 28 spaces U are accessible from polynomial identity tests of circuits of size polynomial in n and m. For F=P_i+P_j and a binary linear form h on moments, the coefficient of z_X in h(Θ)F is h(φ(X)) times its original coefficient. Here Θ_l=z_l ∂/∂z_l. On multilinear polynomials, a product of distinct Θ indexed by I can equivalently be computed by summing evaluations obtained by setting coordinates of subsets of I to zero with signs from inclusion-exclusion. This costs at most 2^k evaluations per term and is polynomial for fixed k. Start the U basis empty. For a basis of its annihilator, test these filtered pair polynomials. If one is nonzero, recover a monomial by the interpolation method in chart-pit.md, check it directly and enlarge the basis. If all vanish, the full span was found. At most m successful enlargements per pair occur.

**Positive test.** If U_ij is not contained in B_j, extract a term in that union outside B_j by its annihilator filters. This is necessarily a cell-i term since V_j⊂B_j. Always verify its actual cell, so an affirmative answer has a deterministic witness.

**Negative test.** Every possible cell-i term has its lift in B_i and its constant coordinate equal to 1. If B_i has no such vector, cell i is empty. With exactly computed spans this implication holds unconditionally. With a randomized PIT engine, a zero decision based on provisional spans has bounded error: failed random nonidentity tests can underestimate a span, and the witnesses for its lower bound alone do not certify completeness. Use a union bound over all tests; no unconditional deterministic negative certificate follows from that transcript. If neither positive nor negative test succeeds, the algorithm returns inconclusive. The tests run in polynomial time for fixed k, without hypotheses concerning cell cardinalities.

**Additional positive search guarantee.** For each competitor j separately learn a space W starting from zero. Filter the i,j union by W's annihilator. If a recovered term belongs to i, finish with its witness. Otherwise its nuisance lift increases W. If there is no further term outside W, then V_i⊂W⊂V_j. Consequently the algorithm finds a witness whenever any target lift lies outside V_j for at least one j. This guarantee can hold even when the simultaneous-space positive test alone is inconclusive. It uses at most 7m verified nuisance lifts before exhaustion. Exhaustion alone supplies a containment conclusion, and does not imply absence. Under an explicit separation promise for every nonempty target, exhaustion does imply absence, subject to the error of PIT.

**Exact small instances.** The independent moment checker checks the linear criteria against exhaustive subset enumeration. For the stationary rank-five instance in chart-pit.md, all eight cells are occupied and all eight are identified positively at k=1. In particular column 9 belongs to every subset in cell 001 and to none in either compatible cell 110 or 111. Thus 1+x_9 separates either target from that competitor exactly. For the rank-four example in chart-nonadditive.md, seven cells occur. At k=1 the algorithm identifies every occupied cell and reports the empty 001 cell by the negative test. At k=0 both examples are wholly inconclusive. These tests give no universal bound on the necessary moment degree.
