# Constructing affine witness charts for acyclic guards

Let p observations precede h hidden bits. Each hidden bit can always be zero and may be one when at least one of its affine guard literals is true. Guards depend only on the observations, earlier hidden bits and the current bit. There are k terminal disjunctions of affine literals over all coordinates. Put D(n,k) = Σ_{j=0}^{min(n,k)} binomial(n,j).

**Compiler theorem.** A deterministic algorithm produces at most D(p,k) charts covering precisely the accepted observations. Each chart has at most k affine equations and an explicit affine map to all h hidden bits that witnesses acceptance everywhere on its domain. The running time is polynomial in the input length and D(p+h,k).

A chart on the remaining coordinates describes a flat and carries affine forms giving the hidden bits already eliminated. For a flat defined by independent equations e_i=0, compute its Boolean indicator as the multilinear expansion of the product of 1+e_i. Discard empty flats. Gaussian elimination of the indicator coefficient vectors selects a basis **from the candidate charts** while keeping each selected chart and its own lift. Evaluation at a point is linear. Consequently some original chart covers the point exactly when some retained chart does. The discarded witnesses are unnecessary because every surviving lift already works everywhere on its own domain.

Start with the whole space. For each terminal clause intersect each retained flat with each true-literal condition from that clause, discarding inconsistent intersections. Reduce to a basis after each clause. This computes their conjunction because intersection distributes over union. After i clauses the equations have rank at most i.

Now eliminate hidden bits in reverse order. If a chart's equations ignore the current bit z, substitute zero into its witness map and prepend the zero witness for z. Otherwise an independent row solves z=h(u) for the remaining coordinates u. Substitute this into the other equations, obtaining a base flat of codimension at most k−1. Take its intersection with h=0 and, separately, its intersection with each g(u,1)=1 for the guard literals g. On each such flat use z=h(u), substituting this form into every previously computed witness coordinate. The zero slice is always admissible. On a guard slice the lift is admissible when h=1, and zero is admissible throughout h=0 as well. Conversely any admissible lift falls in one of these flats. Thus eliminating a bit takes the exact feasible projection, with rank at most k on every output piece. Basis selection preserves this projection before the next elimination.

All intermediate indicators use at most D(p+h,k) coefficients. Each stage generates at most the basis bound times one plus its clause length in candidates. Computing products factor by factor uses ordinary Boolean multilinear multiplication by affine forms. All affine equation operations, lift substitutions and row reductions then take polynomial time in the claimed quantities. The terminal count k is the deficiency of the representation in which each guarded bit supplies a private pivot and terminal clauses supply no additional independent hidden dimension.

## An obstruction to generic uncovered-point searches

Already for degree two, deciding whether a supplied list of charts covers all observations is coNP-complete. Given a loopless simple graph, encode each vertex color with two bits. Use a codimension-two flat for color 11 at each vertex and a codimension-two flat for equality of the two colors across each edge. The uncovered assignments are exactly the proper three-colorings with colors 00, 01 and 10. Thus noncoverage is NP-hard, and a point outside every chart certifies noncoverage in polynomial time.

Every chart can be generated by a verifier with no hidden variables and two tautological terminal disjunctions. Write each chart as a pair of affine true-literal requirements. Put all first literals in the first disjunction and all second literals in the second, including a constant true literal in each disjunction. The verifier has deficiency two when clauses are counted as supplied. Selecting the corresponding pair gives each claimed chart. Basis selection preserves the union in polynomial time because degree is two. Hence even an independent list of genuine charts for an everywhere-accepting verifier can have an NP-hard uncovered-point search. This is a limitation of completing an arbitrary given list. The constructive theorem builds its own list by following the verifier's syntax.

Run `python -B check.py` in this directory. It checks every pair of guard and terminal literal sets on one observed and one hidden bit, 800 seeded instances up to four observed and four hidden bits, and every labeled simple graph on one through four vertices. For each formula it tests every observed input, compares against enumeration of all hidden assignments and validates every retained lift on its full domain. The graph test checks every color assignment after indicator-basis compression.
