# A binary boundary trace in three dimensions

Let ordinary binary columns lie in V, and let B≤V have dimension at most three. For p∈B and λ∈B*, seek a subset of ordinary columns summing to p and a functional extending λ that vanishes on every selected column. This trace has a deterministic polynomial-time witness algorithm. If λ(p)≠0 reject. If p=0 use the empty selection. If λ=0 test ordinary span membership. The [triangle representative theorem](../triangle-baselines/README.md) handles boundary dimension at most two.

In dimension three choose a basis a,b,c of B summing to the nonzero target p. Insert these as three named columns, unavailable for the ordinary selection. If a circuit contains all three, take its remaining columns. Their sum is p, and their span meets B only in the line through p: a proper subset landing in B would give a proper dependence by adjoining an appropriate subset of the terminal basis. Thus λ extends while annihilating the selection. The binary three-elements algorithm and deletion self-reduction find such a circuit if it exists. [Seymour](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0195-6698(86)80044-0) and [Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, Section 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20960).

Otherwise the triple is coupled. Add the three terminal pair sums as unavailable columns, giving a rooted K₄ restriction. This padding preserves coupling as in the cited algorithm. Use a root-preserving vertical separation (X,Y) with interface W=span X ∩ span Y of dimension k≤2 and both side ranks greater than k. In every separation one side must span B: two proper subspaces of a binary three-space cover at most five distinct nonzero points, whereas the padding contains six. Orient that side as X and move all six named positions there. This leaves its span fixed, decreases the interface dimension or keeps it fixed, and Y still has vectors outside span X. Thus absence of such a root-preserving separation implies vertical four-connectivity. To find such a separation, try k=0,1,2 in increasing order, enumerate k+1 independent ordinary elements forced into Y, and minimize the submodular function r(Y)+r(E minus Y)−r(E) with these and the root-side assignments fixed. The named rank-three side already has rank greater than k.

Replace Y by an exact unweighted trace surrogate in W. The rank-two theorem gives ordinary copies of a subset of the at most three nonzero W-vectors: the parallel cases already have this form, and the K₄ spoke case realizes every orthogonal pair, precisely the relation of all three boundary directions. The surrogate need not be a minor in the spoke case. The witness and dual functional lift using the recorded original realization on Y for the chosen interface state. Distinguish ordinary copies of vectors equal to unavailable padding.

This replacement also preserves the circuit-through-all-three predicate. For a crossing circuit the X and Y parts are independent, sum to the same nonzero w∈W, and their spans meet in the line through w. If the X part's span meets W in a line, any surrogate realization of sum w suffices. If it contains W of dimension two, prescribe the functional on W whose kernel is the line through w; it already extends while annihilating the original Y part. By trace equality it also extends while annihilating a surrogate realization of w. Thin the chosen realization to an independent subset summing to w. Its span meets the span of the X part in precisely the required line, yielding a circuit using every column of the unchanged X part. The reverse direction follows by the same argument. Circuits confined to X are unchanged. Thus the current triple stays coupled under exact trace replacement, and the ambient rank drops strictly since replacement columns are in span X.

At termination the vertically four-connected coupled case has a polynomially obtainable graphic representation with the three terminal edges incident to one vertex v. The K₄ restriction forces the terminal star in the graphic representation, as in the cited three-elements algorithm. The other ends are distinct because the three columns are independent. Remove all unavailable edges from the ordinary graph. The target sum p requires exactly v and the three leaves to have odd degree. Since λ is nonzero and annihilates p, two leaves have color opposite v, and one has its color. A vanishing functional means that every selected edge is monochromatic. The required trace exists exactly if there are two vertex-disjoint ordinary paths: one between the opposite-color leaves and one from v to its same-color leaf. The handshake lemma gives both paths inside an actual selection. Conversely such paths can be colored as required. The fixed two-pair undirected disjoint-paths algorithm is polynomial. Lifting proceeds in reverse order.

For a weighted rank-two attachment, append a fresh boundary coordinate h and replace every ordinary v_e by (v_e,ℓ_e). Test (p,c) with functional (λ,0) for the requested primal parity c. Vanishing on h ensures that the selected dual functional acts on the original coordinates. This gives the full weighted table in polynomial time.

## Selective-carrier implementation avoiding artificial interface circuits

A direct implementation does not add all boundary points after an elimination. Keep exactly the currently available ordinary occurrences plus the original three designated U and their three pair-sum padding occurrences. Search for a circuit through U on that carrier. The three pair-sum occurrences cannot appear in such a circuit because either one forms a proper triangle with its two designated U neighbors. No other unavailable ordinary occurrences participate.

At a low vertical separation, move the six root columns to the side spanning their rank-three space: two proper planes cover only five nonzero binary points, so one of the sides already spans them. Insert on that side only the ordinary directions S of the exact rank≤2 trace representative of the opposite side. In particular, when only two directions are realizable in their respective mixed states, the third direction is NOT inserted into the carrier. Re-run the triple-circuit test after the substitution. Even if it becomes positive, exact trace equivalence already proves the result for the original unweighted query; no structural coupledness preservation assumption is necessary. If still negative, the coupled-star theorem can again be invoked on this true carrier. Every elimination reduces its rank. This alternative is sufficient throughout and does not require any claim that full completion of an arbitrary limb preserves a coupled triple.
