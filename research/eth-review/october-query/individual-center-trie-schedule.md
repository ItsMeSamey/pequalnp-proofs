# Optimal separable acquisition prices on the native D.1 center trie

Fix the full grid [-L,L), L=2^a, scale 2L, requested origin precision p≥1, and allowed nonzero nodes, setting all forbidden orders to zero. Each node t has its own cap U_t and priced acquisition as a function of the required precision and order. Minimize the sum of these prices subject to recovering at least degree+1 jet coordinates. All purchased centers represent the same physical polynomial. The admissible original nodes with v₂(t)<u suffice as the allowed set. Include setup and actual continuation expenses per shell-profile-recursion-integration.md; a center generally needs degree+1 true scalar sample calls.

For shell i let N_h be the total orders at level h, A=Σ_{h>i}N_h, B=Σ_{h>i}hN_h and k_i=a+1−i. Encode each odd t/2^i modulo 2^{a+1−i} as the a−i bits after its low one, reading bits low to high in the binary prefix tree. Every integer node remains in this full tree, including forbidden leaves. For peers v≠t the shared internal ancestors, including the root, number v₂(t−v)−i. The within-shell loss in v₂(E_t(0)) is consequently Σ_H(mass(H)−q_t), over a−i internal ancestors of t; higher shells contribute B−iA and lower shells zero. Substitution loses k_i per power and reciprocal Taylor series has integral coefficients. The last jet attains this loss, so the uniform precision for independently perturbable jet entries at active t is

P_t=max(0,p−B+iA−k_i+q_t+Σ_H mass(H)).

This is sufficient for source jets and exact for the independent-coordinate error test. Degree correlations can support cheaper schemes. For q_t=0 impose no guard and buy nothing; an active center with P_t=0 needs no numerical acquisition but its order still enters other guards.

For fixed A,B and shell mass y, D(H,j,s) is minimum subtree price with assigned mass j and sum s of its strict ancestor masses. At an internal node minimize D(left,z,s+j)+D(right,j−z,s+j) over 0≤z≤j. At leaves j=q_t, compute the guard from s; require permission and P_t≤U_t and price the actual acquisition, or pay zero when j=0. Start at (root,y,0). In descending shell order global states (A,B) choose a shell total y and move to (A+y,B+iy), adding the local optimum. Retain least total subject to final A exceeding the degree. Keep minimizing split indices to reconstruct a witness schedule. Any shared setup not represented by the states must be separately charged by a uniform upper bound or explicitly incorporated. With arbitrary nonmonotone precision prices minimize over allowable purchased precision ≥P_t if beneficial.

Let T=1+a+max U_t. Every active leaf sees at least its shell's mass, from its root ancestor or its own singleton order. Its finite cap forces N_i≤T+Σ_{h>i}(h−i)N_h. Induction gives N_{a−d}≤2T4^d since Σ_{j≥1}j/4^j=4/9. Thus total jet mass is O(T L²), B≤aA and path masses s≤aN_i. All finite table dimensions and split ranges are polynomial at the actual native numeric scale, and abstract binary-encoded caps instead give pseudopolynomial work. Charge integer price bit lengths and the polynomial DP execution before contraction. See the original-source recursion integration for sampling, birth precision, dense preprocessing, immutable-entry costs, caps and arbitrary-density limitations. This extends the full-shell optimization: individual omissions genuinely change same-shell guards, so full-shell dominance alone cannot implement it. No general exponent improvement is proved.
