# How often an actual greedy step exposes a missing score character

Take the A-leaf of guarded-circulant-positive-handoff.md, and write m=e+1 for its number of clauses. With all base spins zero and the private gate and guard bits chosen independently, every word in GF(2)^m is a physical violation pattern. At a selected prefix C of rank r, the physical support of the accepted distribution is consequently the full subspace K=ker C, with strictly positive integer multiplicities on K. The character group on K is naturally Q=GF(2)^m/row C. Write B⊆Q for the classes having a representative of weight at most the leaf's prescribed source budget P; the zero class belongs to B. This is exactly the set of effective characters probed by its full trial-list enumeration at this prefix.

As proved in the handoff note, while r<m every winner raises rank. Let η∈B be its nonzero class. In the new quotient Q/〈η〉 the radius set is precisely the image of B, since the allowed original trial lists have not changed. It never increases in size. Call this step periodic if B+η=B. In that case B is partitioned into pairs differing by η, so its image has exactly |B|/2 elements. Hence over the whole selected trajectory there can be at most floor(log₂ |B_initial|) periodic rank increases. Zero-padded sequences of P symbols from {0,1,…,m} represent all radius vectors, giving |B_initial|≤(m+1)^P. There are m−r₀ rank increases, where r₀ is the initial Tanner rank. For at least m−r₀−P log₂(m+1) of them, some b∈B has b+η outside B. On this physical family r₀=o(m), while P and log m are polylogarithmic in n and m=Θ(n). Thus the bound covers m−o(m) actual rank-increasing steps, independently of tie-breaking.

For each such step take a physical trial representative for b. On the old K write χ_c(v)=(−1)^{c·v}. When passing to the η-even subpopulation, the unnormalized character sum for b now has integrand (χ_b+χ_{b+η})/2. Its actual even-trial score has integrand

    (1+χ_η+χ_b+χ_{b+η})/4.

All previous-radius trial scores at this same prefix, with their total mass included, have integrands in the span of {χ_c:c∈B}. The missing character χ_{b+η} is orthogonal to this span under uniform counting measure on K. Its coefficient in the new score is 1/4. Thus no pointwise linear identity from even the complete old-radius score table supplies this new score on K. Full physical support makes this a statement on attainable patterns, including when the selected step lies near the end. At the final selected matrix K={0}, exactly the original leaf models pass.

For the corresponding standalone measurement-oracle statement, let H(v)≥1 denote the original integer multiplicity on K. Two strictly positive integer histograms on this same physical support are 2H(v)+χ_{b+η}(v) and 2H(v)−χ_{b+η}(v). They have identical old scores by orthogonality, but distinct new b scores (difference |K|/2). The two measures may be realized by reweighting physical fibers. They are not asserted to be the uniform-assignment histogram of this same fixed leaf. Earlier-prefix scores, other side information and direct use of the CNF are not ruled out; no lower bound for numerical recovery on the original unweighted input follows.
