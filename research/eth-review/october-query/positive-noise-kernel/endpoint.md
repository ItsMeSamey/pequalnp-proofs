# Complete endpoint from tied population counts

Let l_p≤L be the real load of packet p and set S=∏_p(l_p+1). Let m_ep count source slots of logical bit e in packet p (multiplicity two is possible). The joint population multiplicity C_s is the coefficient of z^s in ∏_e(1+∏_p z_p^m_ep). Choosing a monomial is choosing the value of one tied logical bit. Initialize C_0=1. For each e add the previous array to a copy shifted by m_e. All partial totals stay in the same size-S box. This computes all coefficients in O(n S) integer additions plus polynomial indexing overhead, even for arbitrary cross-packet COPY ties. This is amortized over population states.

For each s compute the product of gate probabilities at q_i=s_p/L. For OR on d ports its numerator over L^d is L^d minus the product of false-literal numerators. For COPY it is the sum of the products of one-numerators and zero-numerators. Sum these products times C_s and divide once by L^M. The joint array uses n+1 bits per entry and the final numerator has at most 1+n+M ceil(log2 L) bits for positive-arity gates. Empty clauses give zero; isolated variables double the result. The total is poly(M,L) S bit operations and space, including all gate readouts. For bounded-occurrence inputs M=O(N) for N original variables and n counts subdivision bits. With the balanced layout P≤3M/L, log2 S≤3M log2(L+1)/L.

For t=1-h and fixed X, port q_i=q_p+h(X_e-q_p), where q_p=s_p/L. The coefficient of h^j is a sum over j marked ports R of the mixed derivative of the multilinear gate product at q(s) times the product of j selected deviations. Pin the at most j distinct marked bits to all compatible words. Their populations give a fixed shift; unpinned sources use the same joint table recurrence. For each s the mixed derivative factors across disjoint gates: replace each marked unary in each gate-product term by its slope, and drop the constant OR term if that gate is marked. Multiply the pinned deviations separately. This gives S binom(M,j) 2^j poly(M,L) bit time. Common denominator L^M suffices since each marked deviation displaces a unary with the same denominator. All numerator lengths are O(n+M log(2L)+M), by counting assignments, subsets, and gate terms. For fixed j the endpoint parameter survives up to polynomial overhead. For arbitrary interior t, the conditional product can differ between words having the same populations: in a signed pair of primary units with signs +,-, the words 10 and 01 give different products for 0≤t<1. Thus the same readout on populations alone fails. A fast way of summing the conditional readout for all j is open.

## Exact degree and a positive-node reduction

The all-positive same-position twin-packet graph of overlap-child.md realizes degree exactly M, using any legal matched block with L≥2 and N genuine original variables. There are two independent occurrence bits per variable, M=4N. At t=1-h write d_e=X_e-s_p/L for any occurrence bit in vertex packet p. Its twin packet contains exactly the same word. Its positive unit contributes leading h d_e. Its COPY2 with occurrence bit f contributes 1-q_e-q_f+2q_e q_f, with h² coefficient 2d_e d_f. Hence the h^M coefficient of the entire scalar is 2^N times the sum over words of ∏_e d_e². It is strictly positive: choose each primary packet word to have between one and L-1 ones. Every primary and twin deviation is then nonzero. Thus the Taylor expansion about the rank-one endpoint has a nonvanishing final term even with bounded-occurrence CNF and genuine COPY coupling. This rules out exact truncation at degree M-1 on this example, not using a separate reconstruction or demonstrating a rounding lower bound.

A single exact strictly interior value arbitrarily close to 1 can encode the original count. Write P(T)=L^M F(T) in Z[T] of degree at most M (each port contributes at most one denominator L). Let A be the smallest power of two larger than 2^n L^M and choose t=A/(A+1). Then R=(A+1)^M P(t) is integral and R mod A=P(0)=L^M F(0), since A+1=1 mod A, all positive-degree terms vanish modulo A, and 0≤P(0)<A. Recover the count by exact division. Here n counts remaining subdivision bits; unused variables can be handled separately. Both the query and the answer have polynomially many bits. For example a sufficiently large power of two serves as A. Absolute error less than 1/[2(L(A+1))^M] also suffices by rounding R, using O(M(log L+log A)) bits. This is a sufficient precision; it does not claim matching necessity or fixed-parameter hardness at a universal constant t.

For arbitrary relaxed grouping one packet with L=M gives S=M+1 for this endpoint alone. For any legal layout ∑l_p=M and l_p≤L. Concavity gives log S≥(M/L)log(L+1), irrespective of orientation. Therefore minimizing the number of orientation types cannot make the full population box polynomial when (M/L)log(L+1) grows faster than log M; each actual packet owns a population coordinate. In active balanced-layout schedules L^1000≤s and bounded-occurrence fixed-density inputs have s=O(N) at root with M linear in incidence size, precluding one packet on growing incidence-linear families. This box lower bound is not a lower bound on feasible support or algorithmic time.
