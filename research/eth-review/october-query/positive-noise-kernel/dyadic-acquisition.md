# Dyadic acquisition and precision charged jointly

Assume L is a power of two and put l=log2 L, h=2^j with j≥1, and G(h)=L^M F(1-h). These probes are outside the stochastic interval. At a population vector s each scaled one-numerator is U_i+hL X_i for U_i=(1-h)s_p. Zero-numerators are L-U_i-hL X_i. Let H_L be the product of scaled gates (OR is L^d minus the product of false numerators and COPY sums products of one and zero numerators). This is multilinear in real one-numerators. Its exact expansion at U sums over marked port sets R the term (hL)^|R| (∏_{i∈R} X_i) ∂_R H_L(U). Every marked bit is forced to one. Initialize the joint population DP at the full source-slot contribution of these pinned bits, counting each distinct bit once, then shift/add over unpinned bits. At each final s evaluate ∂_R by replacing marked unary factors by their slopes ±1, dropping the OR constant when marked. Thus arbitrary COPY ties are enforced.

Modulo 2^P retain |R|≤K=min(M,floor((P-1)/(j+l))). A retained order k needs only P-k(j+l) bits before multiplication. The full scalar costs S poly(M,P,l,log(j+1)) times ∑_{k≤K}binom(M,k) bit operations, with rolling space S poly(M,P,l), S=∏_p(1+l_p). Evaluate h mod 2^P directly, including j≥P. This expansion saves l bits per mark and needs one pin pattern. As usual handle empty clauses and isolated variables first.

For any degree-M integer polynomial, the Lagrange coefficient at 1 for distinct nodes 2^a_i has odd numerator and denominator valuation d_i=∑_{k≠i}min(a_i,a_k). Precision P_i=B+d_i suffices for target G(1) mod 2^B. Multiply all weights by 2^D, D=max d_i, invert odd denominators modulo 2^(B+D), sum, then divide by 2^D. For consecutive exponents 1,...,M+1 the sharp degree-only uniform loss is M(M+1)/2: the polynomial 2^(B-1)∏_{i=1}^M(h-2^i) vanishes mod 2^(B+D-1) at every allowed power yet contributes 2^(B-1) mod 2^B at 1. This excludes adaptive power queries for the unrestricted polynomial class at that precision. Analogously all even integer nodes have sharp loss M+v2(M!), via ∏_{i=1}^M(h-2i), divisible by 2^M M! there, and interpolation at 2,...,2(M+1) in steps of two. These adversaries need not have physical coefficient heights or physical realizations.

For N original variables, B=Ml+N+1 suffices for dividing by L^M and recovering the integer count. At the smallest exponent a in ANY such M+1-node termwise schedule, d=Ma, hence K=M. Thus the marked evaluator makes 2^M subset visits there independent of spacing. At other nodes the same generic upper bound gives total 2^M S poly(M,L,Pmax,max a_i) for reasonable explicit nodes (consecutive exponents suffice). Direct subdivision-word enumeration costs only 2^n poly(M,L,Pmax) for the consecutive schedule, n=M/2 subdivision bits. The October fixed-width all-input clock reported in eth-october-primary/group-budget-convolution.md is poly(input) 2^O_k(N loglog N/sqrt(log N)) under its stated source assumptions. Neither schedule proves an improvement. At bounded width and degree, linked-cluster.md instead gives exact low-order acquisition in S² exp(O(J)) poly(M,L) for ordinary endpoint degree J; with J=Θ(M) this bound no longer supplies a subexponential guarantee. The full-order claim is about the marked evaluator with the stated precision schedule, not all physical algorithms.

A height-aware escape from universal quadratic precision is to obtain exact G(2^i) for i=1,...,k. The monic product ∏(h-2^i) divides the difference of any compatible integer polynomials, so their values at 1 agree modulo Q=∏(2^i-1). Repeated exact divided differences construct the integer Newton remainder. Shared prime divisors pose no problem. Since Q is odd and L is a power of two, invert L^M modulo Q: Q>2^N suffices, hence k=O(sqrt(N+1)) (or ordinary degree interpolation if fewer nodes suffice). Each signed G(2^i) can be lifted exactly with P_i=n+M(l+i+3)+2 bits. In fact every one/zero numerator has magnitude ≤L(1+2^i) and expanding gate terms introduces at most 2^M summands per word. This known-safe precision gives K=M at every node. Lower acquisition costs for a physical-class-specific schedule remain open.

To use the improved K in the connected-set method, fix s, take h0 as the least nonnegative residue of h mod 2^P, and replace each scaled one numerator by (1-h0)s_p+y X_i (zero numerator is its complement to L). Compute exact coefficients through degree K in y by linked-cluster.md, then substitute y=hL modulo 2^P. Constant gate values can now be negative. Choose the auxiliary u grid at C+1,...,C+#gates+1, with C=2(2L(1+h0))^d+L^d an upper bound on absolute gate constants for maximum arity d. All normalized constants u+b_g are positive. Interpolating at zero still works. Coefficient and denominator bit lengths stay polynomial in M,P,log L: individual scaled unary coefficient norms are ≤2L(1+h0)+1 and the u offset has O(d(P+log(2L))) bits. The minimum y-degree of a connected support equals or exceeds its gate count, and marker degrees are unchanged. This yields S² exp(O(K)) poly(M,L,P,log(j+1)) bit time on bounded-arity/occurrence CNF, avoiding the binomial factor at low required order. This upper bound at the full-order smallest node still does not establish an improved general clock.
