# Why COPY gluing does not implement the stochastic matrix semigroup

For 0<t<1 and a full packet let K_t(x,y) be the probability of output word y under independent Bernoulli draws with means (W_t x)_i. Complete row COPY2 gates between corresponding positions of two identical-layout packets, one from each of two twin pairs, produce the conditional matrix K_t K_t^transpose: choose an intermediate word and require the two draws at each corresponding position to agree. The corresponding primary packet at each side carries L arbitrary signed unit clauses. Choosing signs accepting output word a yields weight K_t(x,a). Thus all complete physical scalar values indexed by signs form the matrix K_t^transpose (K_t K_t^transpose) K_t. Fusing the two row-mean matrices into one W_{2t-t²} with an identity leg would instead put K_{2t-t²} in the middle, or its transpose for the opposite direction.

The two middle matrices differ for every L≥2. Starting at the all-one word, K_{2t-t²} cannot produce a mixed word y. But the overlap of the all-one and y rows of K_t equals K_t(y,all-one)>0. Transposition likewise cannot restore the symmetric COPY middle matrix. This distinction is visible after the *complete original gate contraction* for some signs. Indeed K_t is invertible. Acting on the squarefree output monomial indexed by S gives the product over i in S of (W_t x)_i. After Boolean reduction this preserves the degree filtration. The degree-k diagonal block is the matrix of permanents of k-by-k submatrices W_t[S,T], since repeated source indices drop degree. This is the Gram compression of W_t tensor-power k to the orthonormal symmetrizations of tensor basis words with distinct indices, one per k-element set. W_t has eigenvalues 1 and 1-t>0; its tensor power and the indicated compression are positive definite. Every diagonal block is invertible, including degree zero. Consequently left and right multiplication by K_t^transpose and K_t preserve inequality. Repeating a distinguishing signed block throughout the admissible identical-template full packing gives different products of positive (or nonnegative unequal) block values. Only corresponding slots across identical template copies are paired.

For the local L=2 illustration at t=1/2 order words 00,10,01,11. The K rows at 00 and 11 are pure. The two mixed rows are (3,9,1,3)/16 and (3,1,9,3)/16. All-positive primary weights are (0,3/16,3/16,1). The physical scalar is 4825/4096; the fused scalar is 4609/4096. The general sign-tomography proof above is independent of whether L=2 is an active balanced source choice.

Even an ideal directed clean intermediate wire yields K_t K_t, which can disagree with K_{2t-t²}. For fixed first input x and two distinct final port indices i,j, conditional independence in the second layer gives their unconditional product expectation as the product of composed means plus the covariance sum ∑_k W_ik W_jk q_k(1-q_k), with q=W_t x. This sum is positive for mixed x. Thus independent row draws cannot be composed by multiplying the row-mean matrices when the next layer can reuse an intermediate bit. These arguments exclude the proposed local fusion identity, not every conceivable simulation of rational nodes by a different gadget.

A fixed rational t=a/b in lowest terms reveals certain modular counts without a gadget. If p divides a and is coprime to L, then the integer (bL)^M F(a/b) reduces to (bL)^M F(0) modulo p. For prime p the prefactor is invertible (and likewise for composite divisors coprime to bL). For example t=3/4 on power-of-two L gives the original count modulo 3 in one query. One fixed residue need not determine the full count.
