# Stochastic packet contraction

Write e(i) for the subdivision bit at input slot i, and set X_i=0 at dummy slots. Sum freely over the n subdivision bits, once per tied logical bit. For each real row i of packet p put q_i=(1-t)X_i+t s_p/L, where s_p is the sum over its L padded slots, counting repeated occurrences. For disjoint port sets I_g of original row gates define H on real port words as the product of their Boolean indicators. The complete scalar is F(t)=sum_X H_ext(q), where the multilinear extension H_ext factors into the individual gate extensions because their row variables are disjoint. This includes both signed literals and COPY (probabilities of all zero plus all one, for positive arity). It follows by summing each output port independently conditional on X. Padding only the input slots preserves exactly the real-submatrix kernel.

Given X, independently mark each real port with probability t, and at a marked port read a uniformly chosen slot in its packet (including dummies); unmarked ports read their own slot. For M real ports, the degree-M Bernstein coefficient b_k is the sum over X of the expected acceptance conditional on exactly k marks. The marked subset is uniform; hence 0≤b_k≤2^n, with denominator dividing binom(M,k)L^k. Conditional on the marks and source indices, draw X only once per bit. This dependence distinguishes the channel from independent resampling of fresh bits. Exact ordinary rational samples t=u/v have a common denominator (vL)^M and numerator at most 2^n(vL)^M on [0,1]. The M+1 samples at j/(M+1), 1≤j≤M+1, therefore admit exact polynomial-bit interpolation; direct acquisition takes 2^n poly(M,L) bit operations when n≤M. At the origin COPY forces incident bits of each used original variable equal, giving the CNF count (with the usual independent factor two per isolated original variable).

In the admissible full-slot primary/free twin packing from native-four-port-gl/circle-sample-logical-table.md, flip the sign of one unary clause in one primary packet. This preserves every incidence and the original violation-count and nearest-solution Hamming-distance histograms, by flipping that original variable. A free COPY1 contributes one for every q. With all signs positive a primary packet contributes sum_k binom(L,k)(k/L)^L at t=1. With one negative sign it contributes sum_k binom(L,k)(k/L)^(L-1)(1-k/L). Their difference pairs k with L-k, yielding for k>L/2 the nonnegative contribution binom(L,k)(2k/L-1)((k/L)^(L-1)-(1-k/L)^(L-1)), strictly positive for L≥2 at k=L. Unchanged packets multiply by positive factors. Thus even the last Bernstein coefficient is not determined by either histogram. For L=2 the values are 3/2 and 1/2.

For this entire mixed-sign twin family, with p positive, h negative, aggregate all words by their a positive and b negative one bits, weighted by binom(p,a)binom(h,b). Put d=t(a+b)/L. Their gate factor is (1-t+d)^a d^(p-a) (t-d)^b (1-d)^(h-b). Multiply these packet sums across independent twin pairs. With N=M/2 original variables, at most N/L packets and (L+1)^2 terms per packet per sample suffice. Exponentiation, exact common-denominator summation, and interpolation take polynomial bit time in N,L and sample precision in bits (L≤M here). For general CNFs the q_i depend on overlapping packet statistics and multiport gates couple them.

In the standard Walsh model independent unbiased bit replacement has conditional character multiplier (1-t)^|S| and preserves the uniform mean. See Ryan O'Donnell, Analysis of Boolean Functions, chapter 2 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10386). Here conditional port means in sign coordinates equal the same convex mixture of slot signs. Expanding a Walsh character selects one source per port in S, with independent row selections. Averaging over uniform logical bits kills every term with an odd multiplicity at a real logical bit, leaving even multiplicities and the fixed dummy signs. Distinct ports can choose the same bit, so nonconstant characters can survive; ordinary noise diagonalization does not evaluate this scalar. Applying true independent bit noise to the whole Boolean predicate on logical bits preserves the uniform sum at every t, and that sum is already the unknown count. The multilinear extension expectation and Bernstein expansion themselves follow by independent sampling and conditioning and carry no novelty claim.
