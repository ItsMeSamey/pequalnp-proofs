# Physical packet overlap at the rank-one endpoint

Start with the admissible full-slot twin packing in native-four-port-gl/circle-sample-logical-table.md, increasing the uniform multiplicities to multiples of four. Each vertex v represents one twin pair, with L positive unit-clause ports in its primary packet and L variable ports in its twin. Pair the variable ports into binary COPY gates subject to equal complete row labels at the two endpoints. In particular, corresponding slots in identical packet templates may be paired. Each matched pair defines an original variable occurring in the two corresponding unary clauses. All subdivision ties remain between corresponding primary and twin ports. This specifies a CNF and keeps the packet slot labels and their loads. Each new gate inherits its common complete row label. There are M/4 original variables and M/2 clauses, so for power-of-two M the ceiling of log₂ of their total remains log₂ M. The resulting multigraph has a vertex per twin pair and an edge per original variable, with the two ports of the COPY gate as its endpoints.

At t=1 let a_v be the number of ones among vertex v's L subdivision bits and x_v=a_v/L. Both packets of the pair see mean x_v. Summing all words of each population gives the exact scalar
$$
  \sum_{a\in\{0,\ldots,L\}^{V}}
  \prod_v {L\choose a_v} x_v^L
  \prod_{uv\in E} [x_u x_v+(1-x_u)(1-x_v)] .
$$
The bits indexed by different vertices are distinct; the only connection between vertices is the summed binary COPY row gate. In particular each effective vertex has L possible contributing states. A tree decomposition of this multigraph of width w gives an exact contraction in poly(|V|,L) times L^(w+1) bit operations for positive L, by ordinary variable elimination on these finite tables (use an elimination order from the supplied decomposition). Clearing a denominator L for each unary port and L² per edge leaves integer factors of polynomial bit length throughout.

Divide each identical-template bin into groups of four packets. In each group, connect the two twin pairs by matching corresponding variable slots, obtaining L legal parallel edges. For K such disjoint two-vertex blocks, separate the left and right vertex of every block. The exact conditional table on the populations 1,...,L on both sides has real rank L^K. Indeed the single-block edge matrix has entries g(x,y)^L for g(x,y)=xy+(1-x)(1-y). Its binomial expansion is a Gram matrix with features sqrt(binomial(L,j)) x^j(1-x)^(L-j), 0<=j<=L. These are a basis through degree L (divide by (1-x)^L on the open interval), and the L distinct nodes have evaluation rank L by polynomial interpolation. Positive unary and multiplicity factors are invertible diagonal scalings. Independent blocks take Kronecker products. The same rank holds if the table indices specify one fixed word per nonzero population and multiplicity is omitted. This rules out exact low separated rank across this prescribed cut, even after the COPY row sums and population compression, while its scalar still costs only O(K L²) summands.
