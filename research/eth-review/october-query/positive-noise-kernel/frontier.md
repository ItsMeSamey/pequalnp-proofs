# Exact acquisition along a supplied physical port order

A subdivision bit e per literal occurrence supplies two tied source slots, one at its clause and one at its original variable COPY. A port record identifies its packet, source bit, output gate, and (for OR) sign. Missing packet slots are zero. This description starts after a legal packing is supplied. It also contracts more general incidence lists without assuming that the preprocessing can realize them. For each packet p guess its final number s_p of ones and tally its ones as its ports pass. For each e guess its Boolean value at its first port and reuse it until its last. Expand each OR gate as 1 minus the product of its false-literal probabilities, and each COPY as the sum of the all-zero and all-one products. Guess one term per gate at first occurrence. Multiply its local unary factor at each port, charging an OR minus sign once. Check the guessed packet total on closure. Forget closed entries and add weights having identical remaining entries. The sole terminal weight is F(t): every choice of all bits determines exactly one accepted set of packet guesses, and summing gate choices expands exactly the complete Boolean scalar.

Including current-port introductions, let E,G,P be the numbers of live bits, gates and packets at a step. Time is polynomial in M,L and sample bit length times the maximum of 2^(E+G)(L+1)^(2P); memory has the same bound. All partial terms at rational t=u/v in [0,1] after i ports have common denominator (vL)^i. Their absolute numerator is bounded by (vL)^i 2^(n+#gates)(L+1)^(#packets), counting all histories and bounding every local absolute factor by one. State keys and integer widths are polynomial. Using M+1 strictly positive nodes j/(M+1), j=1..M+1, recovers the value at zero or the Bernstein coefficients at polynomial bit overhead. Thus E+G+P log(L+1)=O(log M) along a supplied order suffices. For bounded-occurrence inputs M=O(N) after removing unused variables. The bound is on the actual packed instance; bounded occurrence alone does not bound it. This is ordinary variable elimination with packet totals carried explicitly.

A cycle of mixed-sign binary clauses (v_i OR !v_{i+1}) has two occurrence bits per clause and one COPY2 per variable. In the abstract physical port list, group clause i and COPY i together in packet i with four real slots. In cyclic order, at most two crossing occurrence bits and one active packet suffice between blocks. The control below exercises shared original variables, a wraparound tie, and full COPY semantics. It tests a supplied list rather than asserting that an externally fixed packing algorithm realizes this grouping.
