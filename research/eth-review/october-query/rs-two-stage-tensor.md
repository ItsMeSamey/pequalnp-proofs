# Linear-wire Boolean tensor realization of the physical Reed–Solomon control

In the signed all-bases-distance construction (frozen reference 1a61c7e9), h is even, Q=2^h, K=Q/2, r=hK. The observed code comprises all evaluations of polynomials of degree <K, one common injective binary h-to-ℓ inner encoder per symbol (ℓ≤4h), and O(r) signed width-three residual constraints on observed bits. Its raw XOR chains have unique extensions of the r seeds. The following realization counts exactly the same solutions.

Take B=2^(h/2) and a binary subspace U of the field of size B, and compute s(X)=∏_{u∈U}(X−u). Its value is constant on each of the B cosets because translating by u∈U permutes the defining factors. The polynomials X^b s(X)^j with 0≤b<B, 0≤j<B/2 are monic with distinct leading degrees b+Bj=0,…,K−1. Hence they form a basis; their coefficients can be chosen freely and uniquely describe each old message polynomial.

For each b install one tensor of field-symbol inputs c_bj and outputs f_b(t)=Σ_j c_bj s(t)^j for one t in each coset. For each coset install a second tensor taking the B different f_b(t) and emitting Σ_b x^b f_b(t) for its B individual x. These are exactly the required outer evaluations, by constancy of s. Each tensor is the Boolean 0/1 indicator of this input-output relation, with h Boolean ports per field symbol. Its degree is at most 2Bh. Each full-interface stage costs Qh edges at most. Free message bits terminate in unary all-one tensors. Every output is determined by its inputs. Attach the original site-specific coordinate ordering and inner-map indicators of degree at most 5h; fan out observed bits through equality trees, summing unused outputs with an all-one unary tensor. Attach the physical residual clauses with their original signs. All residual occurrence counts sum to O(r). Subdivide every edge by equality if bipartiteness is needed. Every old polynomial has exactly one assignment to all new internal bits and exactly one old seed-and-auxiliary representation. A residual constraint directly observing a raw monomial coefficient would need a separate interface; none occurs on this control.

Set contraction size s₀=Θ(hQ), precision P₀=r+1 and ring truncation length 1. Maximum degree 2h√Q=o(s₀^0.9) holds eventually; finitely many small cases may be enumerated. The number of vertices is polynomial, edges O(s₀), and descriptions polynomial. Construct the field and the original inner encoder by the stated polynomial-in-Q algorithms. Choose U as the first half of a binary field basis, enumerate its cosets, and compute constants by direct field multiplication. An entry evaluator reads the supplied bits, runs the required dense polynomial evaluations, compares the results, and returns one bit in polynomial time and polynomial space. No explicit exponentially long truth table occurs. The October theorem therefore yields deterministic polynomial space and 2^{O(r/√log r)} times polynomial input size. The residue gives the full count as it is ≤2^r. For labeled controls use their representation; for extracted observations with a proposed field/site labeling, verify offsets and equality of binary image spaces by Gaussian elimination, charging discovery separately if no labeling is available.

## Exact interface, wire charge and witness

For the frozen control take the given field polynomial, binary field basis, site order i↦α_i, inner linear encoder and observed labels c(i,t). These can accompany a labeled schema, or be recomputed when the deterministic generator and its label conventions are given. Verify the field, list of distinct field elements, encoder rank h and dimensions, and the correspondence between physical labels and coordinates. Build the binary generator E by expanding basis monomials over this site order; its r independent columns span the physical output image. Independently extract signed parity bundles (four forbidden triples for each XOR, two forbidden pairs for a binary equality); never discard unaccounted clauses. Gaussian reduction on the raw variables must be consistent, including all declared variables; require affine dimension exactly r, the output projection rank r, and the projected coset equal to im E in the claimed labeling. Equality is verified by rank and membership tests, including the offset. This proves one original extension per output and covers equalities even when the direct chain contains only a single seed. For the exact cyclic fixture require one ordered-output scope {c(i,t),c(i+1 mod Q,t),c(i+2 mod Q,t)} per i,t and no other remaining clauses; verify the signs against the input. The proof also covers arbitrary signed residuals using only labeled outputs with fixed total occurrence bound T≤D r and polynomially many clauses (empty clauses handled directly). No bounded occurrence per individual output is necessary. If semantic labels are unavailable, a found candidate labeling is verified as above; polynomial recognition from adversarial relabelings is not established.

Before subdivision charge r unary message edges, Qh=2r interstage edges and Qh=2r outer-to-inner edges. Connecting one output to d≥1 residual occurrences uses at most 2d edges by a trivalent equality tree including the edge from the emitting tensor (d=1 takes one direct edge). An unused output costs one terminal edge. All observation interfaces therefore cost ≤2T+m. For the fixture T=3m≤24r, m≤8r, and the subdivided total is at most 2(5r+2T+m)≤122r. Choose s₀=128r. For arbitrary fixed D the same choice allows a fixed theorem constant C depending on D. Clause degree also has to meet the theorem cap; in the extension require fixed residual width k, or replace larger disjunctions by binary OR trees charging O(T) additional wires and adjusting C. Degree is at most max(2h√Q,5h,3,k), bounded by ceil(s₀^.9) eventually. The indexed broad extension is a mathematical assertion; the specific cyclic example and its endpoint ordering are what the preceding comparison targets.

For a witness with positive count, pin the new message coefficient bits successively, retaining a nonzero branch; replace each free unary tensor by a unary pin without adding wires. At most r+1 contractions suffice when reusing the parent count. Expand Σ_bj c_bj X^b s(X)^j in the monomial basis in poly(Q,h) bit operations to obtain the original monomial coefficient field symbols and their h-bit encodings, then evaluate the original XOR chains on the labeled construction. For a general verified equal-image schema, lift the resulting labeled output word through the original affine system by Gaussian elimination. Either procedure gives a satisfying original assignment; direct evaluation of every input clause verifies it.
