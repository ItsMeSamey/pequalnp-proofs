# Parsimonious signed cores with full physical replay

Use the parameters and fixed primary source of guarded-affine-no-padding.md. For sufficiently large even v, take a simple d-regular graph on v bases, d=2 floor((log₂ v)(log₂ log₂ v)²/64), m=vd/2 indexed edges, n=1+v+m, λ=ceil(log₂ n), ν=ceil(log₂ λ), D=12ν. Let F be any signed exact-width-three CNF on the bases having distinct clauses on distinct triples of variables within each clause and at most c occurrences per base for constant c. Signs and satisfiability are unrestricted. No witness is supplied.

For each edge i=ab insert four clauses (z_i ∨ ±x_a ∨ ±x_b) with independent signs and D fillers (g ∨ x_{(iD+j) mod v} ∨ ¬z_i), 0≤j<D. For two fixed distinct bases p,q insert all four (g ∨ ±x_p ∨ ±x_q). Add the marker (¬g ∨ ¬x_p ∨ z_0) and the core F. Every original clause has three distinct variables, and no two clauses coincide. Every variable has both polarities; the input has neither units nor pure variables. The two kinds of quartet force every gate and the guard true. Conversely g=z_i=1 satisfies every noncore clause for every choice of x. Projection gives a bijection onto models of F, including the empty model set when F is unsatisfiable. Recognizing these forced values and directly solving F computes the scalar, even by enumerating v bits. This does not produce any physical intermediate scores.

For large v the guard has uniquely maximum occurrence, gates have D+4 or D+5 occurrences, and bases have at least 4d. Roles may thus be reconstructed and checked by inspecting the quartets, marker, graph simplicity and regularity, c-core-occurrence bound and D distinct positive filler bases per gate. The checked class may allow arbitrary filler placements satisfying these properties; the displayed ordering gives a specific member. No original variable is unused.

There are at least mD>nν clauses, so the actual source takes the dense branch. Its pair threshold M and singleton threshold M² satisfy D+5<M and 4d+M+c+6<M² eventually, since M≥(sqrt λ log₂ λ)/2 and d≤(log₂ v)(log₂ log₂ v)²/32 with λ=log₂ v+O(log log v). Every descendant triple is original. A pair containing a gate has degree at most D+5. A base-base pair occurs in at most four real clauses, four guard clauses and c core clauses. The negative guard occurs only once. A positive guard paired with a negative base occurs only in the guard quartet. Only pairs {g,+x_b} can reach M and each can be chosen at most once per path, because both branches remove all triples containing the pair.

At a ternary singleton step no ternary pair is eligible. Each positive base has fewer than M surviving filler occurrences, and no negative base occurs in a filler. Either sign has at most 4d+c+5 nonfiller occurrences. Thus no gate, base or negative guard reaches M². Only +g can be chosen, at most once. Fewer than vM surviving filler triples plus at most four quartet triples then contain it. Initially there are no binaries. At most v binaries are inserted by all pair-A choices, and at most vM+4 by the possible singleton-B choice. Other ternary choices insert only units. Each binary branching consumes at least M distinct binaries, inserting only units. Depth is O(v), with at most 2^{O(v)} nodes, independently of tie-breaking. Antichain normalization can only delete additional clauses.

Every original clause contains at most one gate. Inserted hearts and petals are subsets of earlier clauses, and negated heart literals form units. For each leaf and trial, fix the v bases and g. Every violation is now affine in at most one gate, including violations of surviving units. Solve all composed checks in the m declared gates. A consistent system of rank r contributes 2^{m−r}. Summing over the 2^{v+1} conditioning assignments gives the genuine score. Do not fix the semantically forced gates or guard when evaluating intermediate scores. The Tanner setup and exhaustive ordered lists over all 3n rounds cost 2^{polylog(n)} times polynomial factors per leaf before scoring. Depth-first streaming retains polynomial space. Full native replay including both children, emitted scores, every discarded candidate and each prescribed winner costs 2^{O(v)}=2^{O(n/[λ(log λ)²])} bit operations.

To obtain explicit hard raw cores, start with arbitrary exact-three SAT on distinct variables within each clause, discarding duplicate clauses and accounting separately for any unused declarations. Replace each used variable with k occurrences by max(k,2) clones on a directed cycle, using one clone per original occurrence. Replace each link implication ¬c_i∨c_next by two triples (¬c_i∨c_next∨±p_i) with one fresh private bit p_i. The clones must agree and the private bits then remain free. With t clones this yields v=2t, every variable has both signs, maximum occurrence is five, and there are 2^t extensions per model on the used original variables. This preliminary reduction carries a known multiplicity, while the dense embedding preserves the resulting core count exactly. The dense input itself has high occurrence and a recognizable affine variable backdoor of size v+1. The replay bound gives no general speedup for hard cores measured in their own size.
