# A minimum total scope can force a wide clause

Fix r≥3 independent seeds y_1,...,y_r and b. Represent L, the parity of all seeds, by a chain of binary XOR gates encoded by four signed ternary clauses each. Add (b OR y_i) and (NOT b OR y_i) for every i, and (b OR L). Extract the chain equations and use the specified seed basis together with b. Every residual clause separately has a simultaneous falsifying assignment on this affine space. Resolving the two clauses at i yields the unit y_i, so a stronger preprocessor easily solves this control. Other affine bases can also change the outcome.

In the scope-sum graph an old vertex is the dummy or a seed coordinate. A dummy–seed pair differs on the two seed clauses and the long clause, giving weight 3. Two different seeds differ on four seed clauses and agree on the long clause, giving weight 4. Vertex b differs from every old vertex in the first literal coordinate of every clause, giving weight 2r+1. Every minimum tree contains the unique old star centered at the dummy and one arbitrary leaf edge for b. The long clause depends on all r old edges and the leaf edge, even if the latter also changes L, since it changes b. Each seed clause depends on exactly its own old edge and the leaf. Every retained bit is essential because the clause has a falsifying word and the bit changes its literal vector. The truth tables visit 2^(r+1)+8r words. The long clause forbids exactly 2^(r−1) words by independence of b and L; each seed clause forbids one. These are direct enumeration costs before any output minimization.

Instead take the old path dummy,y_1,...,y_r and attach b to the dummy. The long clause uses the first path edge and the leaf. A seed clause uses the leaf and all incident path edges of its seed. Only the last seed has degree one. The table visits 16(r−1)+8+4 = 16r−4 words, at most 16r+4. It emits 4(r−1)+2+1 = 4r−1 falsifying-word clauses. Both trees give invertible substitutions. The exact minimum-sum objective can fail a width cap even when a different tree in the same basis passes it.
