# Model parity in one inactive endpoint coefficient

Consider the raw Boolean incidence network with one original COPY per used variable and one signed OR per clause; subdivide each old literal edge by a column COPY2, both old vertex types on rows. This is an explicit admissible D.1 input at suitable source scale and degree. It is not a claim about the layout of every reduction to D.1. For bounded-width arbitrary-occurrence CNF, replace repeated occurrences of each variable by fresh individual bits, tying them by a directed implication cycle (no cycle for a single occurrence). Each clone has at most three occurrences and every original assignment has a unique consistent extension; preserve unused variables externally and apply the theorem to this new raw network, at its actual larger edge count.

Let e be the number of old literal edges and f(z)=F(−L+Lz). Work modulo two with original Boolean lifts. In every packet only the matched original port has nonzero kernel, of weight z. For row bits a,b ending each old edge the unnormalized sum over its single free subdivision bit X gives Σ_X l_a(zX)l_b(zX)=zK_ab(z), where K has diagonal z and offdiagonal 1+z. Indeed the diagonal entries before factoring are 1+(1+z)^2 and z², and the offdiagonal entry is z(1+z). Therefore f=z^e H where H contracts the old graph using K. At z=1 it glues equal bits, so H(1)=F(0)=ordinary model parity. At z=0 it glues opposite bits. Flipping every variable COPY word swaps 0 and 1 while leaving the clause shore unchanged, interchanging K(z) and K(z+1) on every edge. Hence H(z)=H(z+1), and [z^e]f=H(0)=H(1) in every packing. For variable weights w_i in a characteristic-two extension field, H_w(z+1)=H_swapped(z), where swapping exchanges w_i(0) and w_i(1). Thus [z^e]f_w is the count with swapped weights. Unused bits supply their ordinary or weighted multiplicities separately.

If the input instead splits a variable into an old COPY tree, a sufficient condition for invariance is a flip bit per old COPY, zero per clause, with opposite bits at both endpoints of every old edge; verify this by graph coloring. Without this condition the offdiagonal endpoint propagates complements along paths, so interpret each literal with the read toggled by the parity of its root-to-clause path. Uniform parity per variable is sufficient for the unweighted leading count to agree under a variable renaming. Arbitrary mixed paths do not confer the raw formula identity. None of these claims concern arbitrary inherited tensors.

At precision four no universal direct coefficient equality holds. The positive-unit and negative-unit formulas on one common variable in the same packing have unique models. Their deformations sum identically to two by complementary clause responses and the free old COPY1. Their degree-one coefficients sum to zero; their counts sum to two modulo four. Moreover the negative-unit constant at −L is two, so z^e need not divide f in this ring. In the local L=2 full single-packet fixture with the positive clause first its polynomial is 3z+2z² modulo four, giving the explicit failure. This local check does not assert a large active call.

This half-scale z=(T+L)/L at the inactive node differs from the source active full-scale z=(T−t)/(2L). At the inactive endpoint the characteristic-zero order is r, the number of all-positive clauses (original-root-factor.md). The signed implication cycle plus one positive clause has r=1 while e=2(n+1); the coefficient at e lies an unbounded distance above r. Fixed excess-order r+s procedures therefore do not supply its parity for free. At e squarefree monomials depending on every subdivision bit have no residual factor of two from the sum over outside bits. Computing this selected coefficient for arbitrary cloned 3CNFs is precisely as hard as computing their ordinary satisfiability parity under this parsimonious reduction. No general fast evaluator or inference for active centers follows.
