# Full-polynomial acquisition by assigning each marked physical row exactly once

Consider an ordinary October signed-CNF root, with a supplied legal packet layout and a bipartition into packet modules. Each old gate belongs entirely to one module. Assume each subdivision COPY2 bit has one port on A and one on B. The B bit scopes therefore partition the bits. Let w be the largest B-module physical description size in row and column slots. Let r be the total arity of the A-side old gates remaining after unary variable COPY gates have been removed. The entire rational polynomial F(T) is computable in 2^{O(r+w)}poly(input,M,L) bit time and polynomial space. Unary COPY ports still count in M, packing, and denominators. This theorem uses an explicitly supplied layout.

Write D=C'(0) and N_i(X)=sum_j C(T)/(T+i-j) X_j for the cleared native read, using only real columns and the source offsets in the packet. Equivalently its coefficients are the source cleared kernel entries with the corresponding offset. Clearing D once per real row gives the integer numerator D^M F. A unary COPY sums to D, an OR of arity s gives D^s minus its false-literal product, and the remaining COPY gives the sum of its all-on and all-off products. Each factor in a product is N_i or D-N_i. Enumerate one of at most two summands per genuine A gate (at most 2^r choices), keeping signs and scalars. Each branch has m≤r marked physical factors a_i+sum_b l_ib(X_b), where a_i is 0 or D and l_ib is the appropriate signed part of the row's actual read owned by B component b. Leave the entire integer-polynomial B-side factor f_b(X_b) unchanged, including all its original gates. For the branch use the identity

    sum_X product_b f_b(X_b) product_i (a_i+sum_b l_ib(X_b))
    = [z_1...z_m] product_i (1+a_i z_i)
        product_b ( sum_{x_b} f_b(x_b) product_i (1+z_i l_ib(x_b)) ).

A term contributing to the coefficient assigns each marked row exactly once, either to its constant or to one B component. For each fixed assignment the remaining sums factor across the disjoint B scopes. This proves equality with all physical coefficients and cancellation signs. To extract the coefficient, for every I⊆[m] put z_i=z on I and zero elsewhere, multiply truncated to z-degree m, select [z^m], and sum with sign (−1)^(m−|I|). Only monomials using every row survive, and total degree m forces each exponent to equal one. This works with m=0 and with repeated or overlapping row neighborhoods. No division by a factorial or Fourier normalizing power occurs.

For each branch and I, stream the at most 2^w local words in one B component at a time. Keep only the truncated bivariate product and the next local sum. Each z-degree-q coefficient uses at most q additional marked kernel factors, so the overall T-degree is at most (M+r)(2L−1). The coefficient 1-norm per kernel or constant has O(L log(2L)) bits up to polynomial factors from integer shifts. Each marked factor has at most M possible bit terms. Products of unmarked gates contribute at most M real-port factors, word summations at most M extra bits and the z-degree-m choices at most O(r log(2+Mr)) more bits. Outer sign sums add O(r) bits. Exact integer polynomial arithmetic thus has polynomial bit price and storage. Return D^M F and its denominator D^M; native reads follow by exact substitution at the prescribed integral unramified-ring lifts (or by shifting rational coefficients), cancelling the known two-adic denominator before reducing to the requested precision. The source uses only polynomially many positions and polynomially many precision bits; handle declared unused raw multiplicity separately. No sample is inferred from the support of a signless network.

## Connected packet graph with more surviving Fourier terms than the acquisition clock

Use E distinct private negative unit clauses on distinct variables, E a sufficiently large power of two, M=2E. Use the actual native scale and the fully occupied construction of low-degree-walsh-packet-acquisition.md (4bb4c82b), with R=64^a, L=2^a, and n=E/(R²L) copies of each of R² ordered packet types per shore. These are native growing scales, n and E are powers of two, L grows faster than log E, and L=o(M/sqrt(u log(2+u))). Each type is indexed by its ordered full-label pair (α,β) at A slot zero. B has its high-bit orientation reversed wherever unequal, so a native bit may pair A position j to B position j xor L/2 in its own type. As before the bijections on the n copies for different slots use identity and all single-bit flips in a balanced cycle. Each type therefore remains internally connected even if all matched edges in slots j=0,L−1 are removed: every copy-mask bijection still occurs in at least one of the other slots.

Connect all R² types while preserving physical labels. A slot zero has column label β; A slot L−1 has column label α. Along any spanning tree of the types adjacent by a common first or second label, swap the two B partners of two fresh same-slot A incidences across the adjacent types, at L−1 or zero respectively. There are enough copies to use fresh incidences per tree edge. The two column labels in each swap coincide, and their B slot indices in the two types agree. All physical row and column class loads and all legal orientation strings are preserved. Every underlying original type is still internally connected via nonswapped slots and a new edge links each tree adjacency. The whole packet interaction graph is connected.

Choose the same d=floor(L/sqrt(u log(2+u))) A slots in every A packet as actual negative clause rows; all others are unary variable COPYs. On each B slot put the opposite kind to its partner A slot. These are exactly E separate true old incidences each joining one negative unit to its own COPY. Row labels of the two endpoints need not coincide; the shared subdivision column label does coincide. All source label loads and half-separation are preserved. The raw formula itself is disconnected; the connected graph here is the physically deformed packet graph. Here r=dM/(2L) and w≤2L or another absolute constant times L. The full acquisition above therefore takes 2^{O(L+M/sqrt(u log(2+u)))}poly(input) time in polynomial space.

At the odd source center t=1 and integral lift ζ=1, T=t+2L, every native W entry is negative. An unscaled packet with j negative clauses yields a product of j affine factors 1+sum positive_weight times X. On the Boolean cube its multilinear expansion has nonnegative coefficients, including a strictly positive coefficient on every support of size at most j. Its Walsh coefficient on every such support is nonzero with sign (−1)^|S|, since each contributing monomial has that same sign. This argument uses the actual deformed values; it also proves the corresponding Fourier polynomials are nonzero over the rationals. The scalar D factors for the cleared representation change only local overall signs.

Take any common set J of L−d slots. In each A packet choose independently exactly d elements of J as the Walsh support. At each B packet the induced support is contained in the common J xor L/2, hence has size at most L−d, its number of negative clauses. Every selected global term therefore survives. There are at least binom(L−d,d)^{M/(2L)}=2^{Ω(M sqrt(log(2+u))/sqrt(u))} distinct terms, with agreeing global sign at that sample. Individual survival after reduction modulo the working 2-power is unnecessary here. The comparison is with explicit traversal of the surviving full-polynomial Fourier terms.

The literal native weighted-mode traversal on the same layouts also takes 2^{Ω(M/sqrt(u))} steps at one odd center by full-order-mode-enumerator-clock.md (294e2110). A constant fraction of the B packets have the specified coordinate-zero g,h pattern. Each has at least L/2−d true target negative rows, its selected tile has L/2 true sources, and the B bits are all physically distinct. Choose independently any subset of k=floor(cL/sqrt(u)) modes in each such packet; the weighted budget and total-degree/target bounds are those of that proof for a sufficiently small constant c. Cross-type partner swaps change none of these tests. This last comparison counts feasible loop tuples and does not assert postcontraction survival. The full-polynomial clock includes all native coefficients; the special raw count equals one. Arbitrary recursive child tensors may lack the required marked affine product or disjoint ownership. Checking and pricing an extension to them is a separate obligation.
