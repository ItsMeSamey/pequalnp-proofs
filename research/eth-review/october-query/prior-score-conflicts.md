# Recovering missing next-round correlations from signed clauses

At a current leaf prefix A, write N_A for its number of accepted physical assignments, H_A(S) for the signed sum of the parity of the violations of clauses in S, and N_A(S) for the score of appending that row. Thus H_A(S)=2N_A(S)−N_A. All previous true scores from A give these integers for every |S|≤P: any such mask has a physical length-P realization with zero padding. After appending its actual winner h, the next integer score of c is

    [N_A + H_A(h) + H_A(c) + H_A(c XOR h)] / 4.

Only the final correlation can be new. Every earlier row in A may also be XORed into its argument without changing its value on A. Finding an advantageous representative must be charged; a supplied representative is easy to verify by binary elimination.

Make a graph on clauses, joining two whenever they have opposite literals of the same variable. They cannot be violated simultaneously. Their violation signs obey χ_iχ_j=χ_i+χ_j−1 over the integers for every physical assignment. For S containing such a pair this rewrites H_A(S) as H_A(S without i)+H_A(S without j)−H_A(S without both). Stop if |S|≤P. If a matching of size t=|S|−P exists, using its disjoint edges ensures termination in ≤3^t old lookups. This certificate is computable in polynomial time by maximum matching. More generally α(S)≤P suffices, by recursing until the word is short or its support is independent. A memo table has at most 2^|S|≤4^P entries for the original S=c XOR h. Without the size certificate, collect coefficients of long independent leaves and cancel identical terms symbolically; if unresolved terms remain one can query the physical new candidate as usual. The algebra is valid on every actual Tanner and greedy prefix because it holds pointwise on original assignments. Additional constraint propagation may certify incompatible violation events conditional on A, but is not needed.

Each certified row saves a new tensor query when the old scores are available, including all physical variations of that row. Enumerating, retaining, or retrieving those old scores still costs time/space; explicitly checking every candidate mask also costs its number. The certificate does not assume few supported words, recover unknown old scores, or prove a fixed-k worst-case exponent gain. As a syntactic example a single contradictory pair in a size-(P+1) support suffices regardless of how many unrelated clauses and variables can vary. Neither the existence of such a subset nor its usefulness forces an actual tie winner. The earlier greedy-walsh-batches recovers whole support or uses conditional trial queries, neither of which is required for this reuse test.
