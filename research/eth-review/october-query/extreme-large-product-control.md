# Two original-CNF endpoint controls of orders 55 and 57

Each input has 64 distinct unit-clause variables and one occurrence per variable. Ports 2i and 2i+1 are the clause and unary-COPY endpoints of occurrence i, and its two column reads are tied. The eight disjoint components each consist of eight clauses and one full packet of length 16. The supplied physical center is t=15 and ζ=0. In seven packets the first literal is negative and the remaining seven positive, ordered [OR₁,COPY₁,…,OR₈,COPY₈]. Only slot 0 has a real baseline successor, so exactly seven positive clauses are dormant. The last column bit is the live bit. At the first possible order the seven dormant wrap reads hit each of the seven outside bits once. For the strict determinant case the eighth packet has the same order with the first two literals negative, so it has six dormant clauses and seven outside bits. For the strict free-rank case the eighth packet has eight positive literals in order [COPY₁,OR₁,…,COPY₈,OR₈]. Its eight wrap reads visit the eight distinct column bits, while the only real baseline successor feeds a unary COPY. Thus both whole controls have f=56 outside and b=8 live bits; their dormant orders are 55 and 57 respectively.

For each of 256 local physical column words, sum the read numerators at every OR; negated literals use one minus the row value. A unary COPY's two row values sum to one. Each dormant positive factor vanishes at zero for every word. Consequently its first derivative and all remaining factors' constants give the exact coefficient at the number of dormant clauses, including when any of those factors themselves vanish. Sum over all local words with the common source denominator. Lower orders vanish. Since the eight physical packets and their variables are disjoint, the whole first possible coefficient equals the product of those exact rational coefficients. No determinant, affine-rank computation, large-word enumeration, or invocation of the tested code supplies the reference.
