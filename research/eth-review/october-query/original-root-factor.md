# Exact endpoint order and transferable precision on Boolean CNF roots

Use the ordinary native signed-OR/COPY root with old vertices on the row shore, subdivision COPY2 on columns, and the October Cauchy packet kernel. After removal of tautologies, repeated literals and empty-clause cases, let r be the number of nonempty clauses whose literals are all positive. For every physical packing, the deformed contraction F has EXACT root multiplicity r at T=−L, for satisfiable and unsatisfiable original inputs alike. This extends the positive-units example to arbitrary mixtures of signed clauses. It concerns the physical scalar over characteristic zero before reduction.

Every W_xy on a real packet pair has a simple zero at −L since y−x≥1−L. Its first derivative is C'(−L)/(C'(0)(−L−(y−x))), nonzero with common real sign. For each accepted row tuple Y, every all-positive clause has at least one one-port; all other clauses can take all zeros, and all COPY vertices can take all zeros. Hence its Hamming weight is at least r and one legal Y attains r. In F every column assignment X has 0/1 bits. A nonzero row sum W(T)X has first-order coefficient of the common sign because it sums nonnegative choices of entries; if no such entry is selected it vanishes identically. Factors 1−W(T)X have constant one. Thus all nonzero terms of weight r have leading coefficients of the same sign, and at least one is nonzero using all-one X (each row has its own matched real column). Terms from heavier Y vanish to higher order. This also works for r=0 and yields a positive constant there. The endpoint therefore cannot realize arbitrary high vanishing orders independently of formula signs. It supplies a linear lower bound on exact scalar sparsity when r is linear via the nonzero-root Vandermonde argument in contracted-jet-sparsity.md; it does not lower-bound valuation-truncated support or distinguish SAT. For example adjacent triples on a cycle with alternate all-positive and all-negative clauses give r=floor(n/2) or ceil(n/2) and constant literal occurrence degree.

Define G(T)=L^r F(T)/(T+L)^r. It is a polynomial with G(0)=F(0), and is integral on the source grid jets. To prove integrality termwise, pick r of the one-row factors from Y (one per positive clause). Each L W_xy/(T+L) is integral on shifted jets: at nodes t other than −L the ratio L/(t+L+alpha z) is integral via its power series since 1≤t+L<2L. At −L, after canceling T+L from C, the ordinary product for W omits the two distinct indices −L and y−x. Among the remaining differences at −L the 2-content cancels C'(0) except for the missing factor of valuation v2(−L−(y−x))≤a. L=2^a supplies it. Summing normalized row terms and multiplying with the unmodified factors preserves integrality, including when other clauses are signed.

Use the original tapered Hermite rows and multiplicities on G. At active shell j the change from F to G is multiplication by a power series with valuation r(a−j). Thus scalar values F(t+alpha zeta) supplied to the ordinary interpolation route need only P'_t=max(0,P_t−r(a−j)) bits to obtain G there at precision P_t. When a sample is needed, strip 2^j from t+L+alpha zeta, invert its unit part and multiply by 2^(r(a−j)). The requested root F calls use their actual working precision P'_t, all existing inherited birth precisions preserved. Compute the ordinary field interpolation in z and tapered origin recovery at the original precisions after this rescaling. All polynomial degree and bit operations are charged as before; this is a root improvement only. On the published root schedule D=L/2^j≥2^(a−u+1) for all active j; r≤M and r(a−j)≤Ma, whereas the source P_t≥24MD/u. Consequently even the largest new saving is a vanishing relative fraction of the working precision uniformly as u grows and a=2u. This certified input-specific improvement alone leaves the published time exponent order unchanged.
