## Source-score streaming and exact sparse-residual handoff

Consider any actual accepted-prefix matrix A of the October dense-leaf routine, with m indexed clause violation bits V(x), P its ordered-list length, and N its unpinned integer count. The literal source enumerates all ([m]×{0,1})^P, not just a polynomial shortlist. Its all-zero trial has N(A). The zero-target trial on support S supplies the unnormalized Walsh coefficient W(S)=2N(A;S)−N. For every |S|≤P at least one list represents S. Keep exactly the canonical list with increasing active indices first followed by (1,0) padding, still scoring all lists in original order for greedy minimization. N may be obtained by a separate charged query before the stream, or accumulate the contributions from its common offset separately.

Encode a physical violation profile as z=Σ_{i=0}^{m−1}2^i V_i, an injective integer with at most m bits. Choose s≥1 with q=2s+1≤P. Accumulate the integer power sums M_k=Σ_{x:A V(x)=0} z(x)^k for 1≤k≤q. Only canonical trial rows |S|≤q contribute. Their rational Walsh coefficient for z^k is k! times the Taylor coefficient of degree k in the product of (1−exp(2^i u))/2 on i∈S and (1+exp(2^i u))/2 elsewhere. Truncate through degree q. All supports larger than k have zero coefficient. Thus streaming W(S) times these coefficients computes every required moment without storing the candidate table. Since z^k is a degree-k polynomial in the violation bits, Boolean Walsh coefficients have denominator dividing 2^k (alternatively use the integer recurrence for exponential generating coefficients). A common denominator 2^q q!^m also has polynomial bit length; direct Taylor truncation has polynomial-bit rational entries since a degree-k coefficient uses numerators of at most km bits plus polynomial overhead. Final power sums have O(n+qm) bits. Process each candidate using polynomial time and storage in original dimensions and P; verify exact divisibility or use rationals throughout.

Let μ_i=M_{i+1}, 0≤i≤2s. These are moments of positive weights h(z)z on the positive distinct profiles, where h is raw assignment multiplicity. The zero profile disappears. The Hankel matrix (μ_{i+j}) indexed 0..s is a Gram matrix of monomial evaluations. Its rank is min(s+1,#distinct positive profiles). A nonsingular matrix declines the handoff. Otherwise set ℓ to its rank; ℓ=0 certifies every accepted assignment is a genuine model. For positive ℓ the leading ℓ×ℓ minor is positive definite, and the standard monic degree-ℓ recurrence from moments through μ_{2ℓ−1} yields the squarefree polynomial whose roots are precisely those ℓ positive integers. Recover roots by deterministic polynomial-time univariate rational factoring or real-root isolation. Solve the Vandermonde system for h(z)z and divide exactly by z. Check positive integer weights, the annihilator and moments. Roots have ≤m bits; determinants and linear algebra use polynomial-bit arithmetic in n,m,P by the minor bound. N−Σh(z) is now the exact model count on this leaf, with no planar-count oracle or AP estimate. Preserve the source winner of the training round. To reproduce any further candidate score, add to that model count the residual weights on profiles annihilated by the survivor rows and trial mask. Apply the same source tie policy. All remaining accepted and discarded scores, including targets before exactness, cost polynomial per source list; use streaming and polynomial space. For count alone output the recovered model count immediately.

Each training round costs its previous native queries plus polynomial per list; no lookup table or point-count oracle was assumed. This technique uses the copied literal source list. A shortened list lacks the coefficient coverage without a new proof. To guarantee a handoff at fixed width, accepted residual mass after j chosen rows is at most 2^n α^j, α=(1+exp(−1))/2, by the actual source independent sampling of P indices then P coefficient bits. Thus after j=ceil(log(2^n/s)/log(1/α)) chosen rows there are ≤s positive profiles; the next training round suffices. Clamp j below by zero, and for bounded exceptional sizes use direct counting. Handoff might occur earlier by the exact Gram-rank test. The promise does not guarantee any particular late intermediate retains nonmodels at handoff. This saves a suffix of native queries, or yields exact high-rank intermediate scores when the observed weighted support is small. It does not improve the known asymptotic worst-case SAT exponent, because training and earlier rounds remain fully charged.
