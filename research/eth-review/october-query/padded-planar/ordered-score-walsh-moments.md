# Residual moments from the complete ordered candidate stream

Fix one branch with m≥1 clauses and one matrix A held fixed throughout a trial stage. Let v(x) be its clause-violation word and ν(v)=Σ_i 2^i v_i (indices 0 through m−1). The original ordered lists ([m]×{0,1})^P in main.tex lines 3454–3466 may represent the same binary row. For each support S of size ≤P designate its increasing list of (i,1) for i in S followed by P−|S| copies of (0,0). At precisely this representative the score gives H_A(S)=2N(A;S)−N(A), the signed sum of the character χ_S over all assignments accepted by A. H_A(∅)=N(A) is also available directly. All other list scores retain their original role in greedy selection and ties. No trial is added to A during the stage. A shortened or different list stream must acquire missing scores and charge them.

The normalized Walsh coefficient at S of ν^k is k! [z^k] of the product over i of (1+ε_i exp(2^i z))/2, with ε_i=−1 for i in S and +1 otherwise. Independent uniform Boolean bits prove the identity by multiplying their exponential generating functions. Each selected factor has zero constant, hence |S|>k contributes zero. Compute all k≤P for a given S by storing D_t=2^j t![z^t] of the j-factor product. Initially D_0=1 and all other entries vanish. At factor i replace D_t by (1+ε_i)D_t + ε_i Σ_{a=1}^t binom(t,a)2^{ia}D_{t−a}, using the old array. After m factors coefficients are D_t/2^m. Intermediate D_t is a signed sum of at most 2^j powers bounded by 2^{mt}, so has O(m(P+1)+1) bits; recurrence temporaries also have polynomial bit length. Use O(m(P+1)^2) integer operations per representative.

Accumulate D_k H_A(S) for each k≤P and divide exactly by 2^m at the end. Walsh inversion returns Σ_{x:Av(x)=0}ν(v(x))^k, including multiplicities of coincident violation words. At k=0 the polynomial is constant one. For k≥1 genuine models contribute zero. Partial sums have O(n+m(P+2)+1) bits since there are at most 2^m distinct supports and |H_A|≤2^n. Storage is polynomial with P+1 accumulators. Exact integer scores or adequate reconstruction precision are necessary. The overhead is polynomial per ordered list and polynomial per chosen representative, with no exponential lookup table. This postprocessing recovers moments at that stage without assuming that these moments determine arbitrary residual populations.
