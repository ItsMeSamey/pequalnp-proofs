## Reusing the nonnegative matching evaluator for a signed moment

In padded-planar-arbitrary-pins.md let d=|J|. Replace the original external weights corresponding to J by t times their positive values. The matching sum P(t) is a polynomial of degree at most d because each matching uses each physical edge at most once. Its signed value P(−1) equals Σ_{j=0}^d (−1)^j binom(d+1,j+1) P(j) by degree-d interpolation at 0,…,d. Every P(j) is nonnegative, including at zero. Keep zero-weight edges in the embedding. Restore the external sign (−1)^d and original prefactor twice the product of all unequal-spin edge weights. The d+1 calls run sequentially. Each binomial needs at most d+2 bits and each matching weight acquires at most O(log(d+1)+1) numerator bits. Denominators do not increase. This supplies the signed moment using only the original nonnegative determinant-square-root evaluator in 4^s poly(n+input bits) bit time and polynomial space.
