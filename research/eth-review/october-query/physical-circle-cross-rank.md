# Real-precision aggregation at a required complex circle point

Take the Boolean CNF OR/COPY root and the conditional circle oracle in physical-circle-rounded-count.md. Choose the sample count a multiple of four, so it includes T=1+2i. Use the actual native t=1 grouping of physical rows/columns at coordinate bit zero. Assume a constant fraction of slots have unequal row and column group labels at the matching position; this holds for the balanced native root by the collision/load proof in packet-sparse-newton.md (equal group labels occupy at most M/64+sqrt(2 A M Delta/64), with A=r^(2a)). This concerns compressing crossing row effects for that grouping, not every algorithm for the scalar samples.

On all real row slots of all packets let K be the W(T) matrix with within-group entries deleted. Let I send each original subdivision COPY2 word coordinate into its two equal physical column slots. Then D=KI maps ACTUAL independently variable column-COPY bits to nonlocal row effects. There are absolute constants c,C>0 with ||D||_2≤C and ||D||_F²≥cM. For the upper bound apply the physical-hilbert-height packet estimate at fixed T. The collection of equal-group blocks is a direct sum of compressions of W and hence bounded by the same norm, giving twice this bound after subtraction; ||I||_2=sqrt(2).

For the lower bound take a real slot x with unequal matched labels. Its own source bit contributes W_0(T)T/T to the entry D[x,source(x)]. The other COPY2 slot contributes zero or W_0(T)T/(T−d) for a real integer d in the identical packet. All terms 1/(T−d) have strictly negative imaginary part, so the magnitude of the sum is at least |Im(1/T)|. The factor |W_0(T)T| is bounded below by a positive constant uniformly in L: its finite products converge to a nonzero limit since T is nonreal and the sum of absolute tail errors is finite. Each chosen row therefore has a matrix entry of at least a fixed absolute size. Sum its squared magnitude over the constant fraction of rows.

Since there are at most M singular values, these two norm bounds imply Ω(M) singular values larger than one sufficiently small fixed absolute threshold. Any rank-o(M) approximation in spectral norm consequently has error bounded below by a positive constant. More directly, any linear rank-o(M) surrogate on the actual COPY word cube has a worst-case Euclidean output-vector error Ω(sqrt(M)): for uniform independent Boolean columns, the centered covariance is I/4; the expected squared error of an affine surrogate is at least one quarter of the squared Frobenius error of its linear part. The rank tail gives Ω(M). This obstructs one candidate uniform low-rank approximation of all complex-sample crossing forms, including after physical column identifications. It does not lower-bound the cost of a single scalar weighted sum, approximations tuned to the particular row predicate, or methods not using this grouping. Original fixed-density bounded-degree 3-CNFs with arbitrarily many vertices supply these source layouts.
