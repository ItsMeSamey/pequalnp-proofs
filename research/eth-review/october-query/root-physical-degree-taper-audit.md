# Exact degree and valuation taper at an ordinary signed CNF root

Assume a nonconstant, unweighted Boolean root of the October tensor construction. Write M for real row slots, L for packet length, a=log2 L, D=C'(0), and o for the number of odd positive-arity old-row COPY gates. The subdivision COPY2 constraints identify paired column bits. Empty and directly resolved source inputs are handled first. The argument also works at a padded placement provided each real row slot is paired with a real column slot at the same index. Dummy slots do not increase M.

In the integer numerator D^M F(T), the cleared read N_i(T) on row i has leading coefficient B_i equal to the number of selected real column occurrences in that packet, counted with multiplicity. Its degree is at most k=2L−1. At all-one column assignment every B_i>0. A signed OR factor has candidate leading coefficient (−1)^(#positive+1) times the product of its B_i. An even COPY has coefficient twice that product. In an odd COPY of arity d, the two full monomials cancel as an identity in the reads; the terms of degree d−1 in the reads are D times the sum of the d products omitting one read. For a unary COPY the factor is D. Consequently the candidate leading term in each summand has the same weak sign over all column assignments, and the all-one assignment contributes strictly. Thus the degree in characteristic zero is exactly n_D=k(M−o). For the normalized reflection kernel replace k by 2L−2 and B_i by L B_i. A multiplier 2^unused preserves degree. An identically zero external multiplier, signed external characters or arbitrary incoming recursive tensors are outside this argument. In particular an unsatisfiable signed instance can have this positive degree and a zero constant term.

At native grid parameter 1≤u≤a set A=2L(u−1+2^(−u)), an integer equal to Σ_{i=0}^{u−1}(L/2^i)(u−i). The least uniform linear taper slope sufficient for the degree test is h=ceil((n_D+1)/A); q_t=h(u−i) for i=v2(t)<u. For ordinary sampling use n_D+1 distinct integral lifts at every active center; their differences are units. The original field large enough for M(2L−1)+1 points still suffices. A safe coefficient guard for this actual h is
p'_t=p+6h D_t+(q_t−1)(a+1−i), with D_t=L/2^i.
Indeed the primary Hermite proof gives v2(E_t(0))≥−6h D_t for any such slope, the truncated reciprocal is integral, and substituting −t/(2L) costs at most (q_t−1)(a+1−i). One may instead use the exact larger-of-zero loss from −v2(E_t(0))+(q_t−1)(a+1−i). The primary final exact rational addition and cancellation cost remains polynomial in M,L, precision and field dimension. Its integrality is part of the actual source Boolean root, including for UNSAT. The normalized reflection kernel requires its own previously verified integral shifted samples. Child tensor entry creation, number of modes/packets, actual recursive entry work and inherited canonical lifts continue to be charged. Retain the original subcall certification for arbitrary child tensors. Source layout can allocate up to 3M padded slots, all still paid in setup and mode costs; replacing M by M−o in those physical budgets would be invalid.

For the ordinary unsplit or canonical trivalent raw reduction the count o is at most the number of literal occurrences: a variable of occurrence d contributes one odd COPY for d=1, zero for d=2, and d−2 ternary COPYs for d≥3 after splitting. The total literal occurrences are at most the number M/2 of old graph edges. Therefore M/2≤M−o≤M, yielding only a constant-factor degree reduction by this device, even in the best such nonconstant case. Signed bounded-density cycles with each variable occurring twice give o=0. The retuned full-grid slope is Θ(M/u) at active source scales, odd maximum order remains Θ(M), and the same O(M/√u) root work exponent and polynomial-space sampling recursion bound continue to apply. The arithmetic above is a root schedule, not a new general recursive solver. The published h=ceil(3M/u) included slack even when o=0; credit the previous full-grid comparison separately.
