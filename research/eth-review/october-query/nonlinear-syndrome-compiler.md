# Nonlinear producer interfaces for the original sparse parity queries

Start before the V-lift with P_i(x)=b_i over F₂, n Boolean variables. Normalize squarefree monomials, cancel duplicates, move constants to RHS. Partition equations into test groups g and assign every distinct nonconstant monomial to exactly one producer h. Factor the producer's check columns on group g as C_gh=E_gh D_gh using a basis E of their span. Send exactly rank(C_gh) binary coordinates y_gh. The group accepts when Σ_h E_gh y_gh=b_g. Introduce a separate COPY for every physical variable read by multiple producers, with one port per producer reading it. Private variables belong to exactly one producer, and unused variables supply a factor two each.

At given shared bits and outgoing word, producer h returns the number of private assignments with D_gh m_h(x)=y_gh simultaneously for every g. For every original assignment the syndromes and COPY wires are unique. For any accepted wire settings, private assignments complete independently across producers, giving exactly the product of their JOINT integer fiber sizes. The total contraction is therefore the original count. This bipartite graph has producers on one shore and test/COPY vertices on the other. Its wire count is Σ rank(C_gh)+Σ_shared (#reading producers); charge the full joint arity at each producer, every received rank at a test, and all ports at each COPY. A joint rank below the sum of projected ranks improves producer entry cost only. An inconsistent concatenated outgoing tuple gives zero. Isolated scalar factors multiply once.

Use precision n+1, nilpotent length one. The count is a nonnegative integer ≤2^n. Substitute shared bits and recombine terms before row reduction. Affine dependencies among outputs impose RHS consistency. Optionally eliminate all affine equations obtained by row combinations (kernel on degree≥2 coefficients), then bijectively reparameterize the affine solution and repeat if dimension falls. Degrees remain bounded by fixed k with at most O(n^k) monomials per polynomial. Contradictions return zero. If N free private coordinates and r independent output equations remain, a fiber count is 2^(−r) Σ_z (−1)^(z·b) Σ_x (−1)^(z·P(x)). Accumulate at most N+r+2 signed bits; divide exactly before reduction, or retain p+r bits before division for p output bits. Direct 2^N enumeration remains available.

For quadratic phases, eliminate a coupled pair in xy+xA+yB+C using its signed sum 2(−1)^(AB+C), with affine A,B. A free bit doubles the answer; a nonconstant affine phase sums to zero. This computes even a degenerate quadratic exactly, in polynomial bit time and space. For degree three, let c be the rank of the cubic coefficient vectors of the r equations after substitution. Enumerate cubic parts by linear algebra; each has 2^(r−c) character preimages. Discover a quadratic restriction once per cubic part A. A codimension-t subspace on which its cubic trilinear form vanishes leaves quadratic polynomials on all 2^t translates, independently of the lower-order coefficients. A coordinate cover of the cubic monomials can be found with budget t in 3^t poly time, or a supplied affine basis can be verified on basis triples. For uniform full subspace search quotient by the radical of the trilinear form, then enumerate subspaces by RREF. An optimum subspace can be enlarged by the radical. With quotient dimension j the search costs poly(input)·2^(j²/4+O(log(j+2))). Price failed budget attempts, or use capped searches with direct fallback. Stream classes and their preimages without exponential tables. The per-entry bound is poly(input) times Σ_A [discovery(A)+2^(r−c+t_A)]; direct enumeration costs poly(input)·2^N. For higher k this cubic procedure applies only after a priced reduction to degree three or less.

If on EVERY paid trial and branch total wires O(s), maximum degree ≤ceil(s^.9), n+1=O(s), and the full entry clock is ≤exp(O(s^.9))poly(input) in polynomial space, the native contraction theorem applies. All grouping discovery and failed probes must also be paid. Uniform s=O(n) gives the same fixed-density-scale counting bound under the amended density routing. This is for original sparse binary parity tests, not arbitrary signed recursive child tensors.
