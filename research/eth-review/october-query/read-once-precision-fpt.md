# A complete deformed read-once scalar at precision p

At an integral numerical root sample of the October packet construction, the complete scalar modulo 2^p of an ORIGINAL read-once CNF takes deterministic 2^{O(p²)} poly(M,L,e,p) bit time and polynomial working space. Here e is the degree of the explicitly represented unramified coefficient ring, L the packet length, M the number of physical ports, and p≥1. This covers the numerical lift (descendant jet length one). The origin count already factors. Details of the original-variable scope, direct determinant formulas for p=1,2 and occurrence-two boundary accompany this note in read-once-precision-frontier.md.

Discard empty clauses by returning zero, and carry the ordinary factor for independent unused variables. There are n occurring original variables, exactly one literal per variable, n OR row ports, and n free unary COPY row ports. Each logical edge has one subdivision COPY2 tying its two slot bits. Summing selector values of each free unary predicate gives one. With actual summed slot columns D, the desired sum is F=Σ_X ∏_C g_C((DX)_C). Each g_C=1−∏ f_i, with f_i the affine false-literal factor. Distinct clauses partition the OR row ports. Each logical column merges two true slot columns, possibly in different packets.

For native T=t+2Lζ, L=2^a, the exact shifted column y=x+t, if present, has unit coefficient reducing to 1. For a nonzero binary lift ζ, every offshift entry has valuation a+1−v₂(t+x−y): the simple factor T−t has valuation a+1 and the other C(T) factors retain the valuations of the differences at the integer node, whose derivative ratio C′(t)/C′(0) is odd. All nonzero differences here have valuation ≤a. For ζ=0 only exact shifts remain. Thus the matrix modulo two is a partial function from rows to columns. At p≥2 a row or physical column has at most min(L,2^{p−2}) surviving entries: all offsets lie in one residue class modulo 2^{max(0,a+2−p)}. A logical column has at most twice this support. Create entries by integral cardinal polynomials or by canceling known powers before inverting odd factors. Direct products of O(L) integral factors need at most polynomial guard precision (the integer derivative denominator has O(L log L) bits). Arithmetic in the explicit monic degree-e quotient modulo 2^p costs poly(e,p) bit operations.

**Width reduction.** For a width-s clause with s≥p replace its OR by minus its product of false-literal factors. In the telescoping difference this deletes that whole clause, leaving total degree at most n−s in the raw Boolean variables even after D substitution. Boolean summation of a monomial involving at most n−p distinct bits is divisible by 2^p. This proves congruence of complete sums, not pointwise predicates. Implement its replacement by s unary clauses of opposite polarity and one external minus sign. The resulting clauses partition the same rows and have maximum width w=max(1,p−1).

Let b count the logical columns all of whose entries are even. In a coefficient expansion any term using r distinct such bits has a factor 2^r and summing the unused b−r bits contributes 2^{b−r}. Return zero for b≥p. Otherwise choose one matched unit row for every visible column and complete to a permutation π of n rows and logical columns by pairing the b remaining rows and columns arbitrarily. All but at most b rows agree with π modulo two. Put B_C=g_C((πX)_C) and δ_C=g_C((DX)_C)−B_C. At most b events have possibly odd δ_C. Baseline clauses operate on disjoint variable blocks and their counts Z_C=2^{|C|}−1 are odd. Set Z=∏Z_C. For S a set of clauses put
 w(S)= Z^{-1} Σ_X ∏_{C∈S}δ_C ∏_{C∉S}B_C.
Then w(empty)=1. Only |S|≤k=p+b−1≤2p−2 can contribute. For p=1,k=0 this formula already terminates.

For clause C let T_C consist of its own baseline block plus every block holding a variable on which its actual row forms depend modulo 2^p. Join pairs with intersecting T sets. Every T set has size O(1+w2^p), and each block occurs in O(1+w2^p) sets by column sparsity. The degree Δ is O((1+w2^p)^2). Weights multiply across disconnected components of any selected S: outside their scope unions all baseline block sums factor; inside each scope union one includes all of its clean baseline clauses, and divides by exactly that union's block counts. No division by a pointwise predicate is used.

**Connected-set aggregation in the actual residue ring.** For U⊆V put H_U=Σ_{S⊆U} w(S)∏_{i∈S}z_i, a series with constant one. Define J_V as the product over U⊆V of H_U raised to (−1)^{|V|−|U|}. Unit series inverses always exist. Multiplicative Boolean-lattice inversion gives H_all=∏_V J_V. If V is disconnected, H_U factors on its two components and exponents cancel, giving J_V=1. With any z_i=0 for i∈V, factors pair to give J_V=1. Hence all nonconstant monomials of J_V contain every variable of V. Set all markers to z, truncate at degree k, and multiply only J_V for connected |V|≤k. Summing the resulting coefficients and restoring Z and the external sign gives exactly F modulo 2^p. Higher activity degrees of H_all vanish by divisibility; no such claim is required of its individual factors.

**Local computation without exponential workspace.** Expand each δ as the difference of two products of false-literal forms. For |S|≤k there are ≤2^k resulting terms with h≤wk affine forms. Write their forms c_i+Σ_B L_{iB}(X_B). The raw moment with baseline weights W_B=B_B on clean blocks and W_B=1 otherwise is the coefficient of t_1⋯t_h in
 ∏_i(1+c_i t_i) ∏_B [Σ_{x_B} W_B(x_B) ∏_i(1+t_i L_{iB}(x_B))].
Indeed each form's single chosen occurrence contributes its constant or a linear contribution from exactly one block. Extract this coefficient by summing over A⊆[h] with signs (−1)^{h−|A|}, specializing t_i=u for i∈A and zero otherwise, and extracting degree h. Surviving monomials have full support and degree h, hence are squarefree. This uses O(h) stored ring coefficients per univariate array, no division by factorials and ≤2^w local assignments per block. Divide by odd Z to obtain w(S).

Enumerate connected subsets via rooted walks of length 2(j−1) for size j, accepting only the uniquely ordered depth-first traversal of the lexicographically canonical spanning tree rooted at the least vertex. This costs at most n max(1,Δ)^{2k} poly(n,k) visits. Stream U and S and the local polarizations. Total bit time is bounded by n max(1,Δ)^{2k} 2^{O(k+wk+w)} poly(M,L,e,p), hence 2^{O(p²)}poly(M,L,e,p). Store the input graph or regenerate it, a single traversal, and polynomial-length truncated series over the residue ring. All inversions are of odd integers or unit-constant series. No precision guard for logarithmic denominators and no exponential-sized DP array are needed. Native sample precisions for full Hermite recovery grow with the source size; this bound provides no unrestricted SAT decision or original counting speedup.
