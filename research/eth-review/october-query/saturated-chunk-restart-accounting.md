# A fresh scalar contraction per conditional entry of the saturated chunk cycle

Use connected-expander-local-absorption.md with c=v and w=6v. Each original chunk has v variable sites, v ternary clause sites and 3v original incidence edges, counting the unique outgoing edge by its home port after the cyclic splice. Globally these counts are cv, cv, 3cv. Subdivision introduces exactly 3cv column COPY2 gates and 6cv physical incidences. At each splice condition the common Boolean value of its COPY3; replace its two home ports and one foreign port by pins of that same value. The tensor of conditional chunk values is a 2 by 2 matrix on its left and right boundary values. Multiplying the c matrices and taking the trace sums the shared values once, including for c=2 when both boundaries meet the same neighbor. At a first child a retained rotated gate is still such a COPY; use a singleton boundary alphabet when this gate is absent, lest a spurious factor two enter. Each retained gate belongs whole to g. Raw counting has exactly the same conditional topology and costs O(c 2^{v+O(1)}poly(s)) directly on the original binary variables. The signed satisfiability problem has the matching shortcut.

**Packet count.** Each of the two packet collections in a chunk is fully occupied, with 3v real slots. Thus the actual number of parent packet vertices is w/L, independent of the signs. Replacing each old wire by both of its end legs in a full deformed packet produces exactly 2w Boolean edges per conditional parent chunk (pins can replace cut ends in place). Each exceptional factor consumes 2L legs. A particular (g,β) child uses at most w/L packet descriptors for this chunk, ≤2w retained legs and maximum packet arity 2L. Empty descriptors, if included by an implementation, are degree-zero scalars removable once; even counting them before removal there is at most one per source packet per g. This estimate depends on the exact saturated layout. Counting real edges divided by L on a general partly occupied child would be invalid. Maximum old degree is three, including after the boundary replacement, and max(3,2L)≤w^.9 for large native instances.

**Entries.** At the parent, clear D=C'(0) per real row. At a fixed integer evaluation point the packet entry is an integer product of L explicitly computable row factors, conditional on its row and column legs. Reach every coefficient of the whole numerator by integer-point interpolation at degree ≤cw(2L−1); charge polynomially many samples and polynomial bit heights by the kernel 1-norm estimate. Alternatively one can interpolate each chunk independently and multiply its polynomial matrix entries exactly, costing polynomial overhead. At a fixed first-child birth (t,ζ,g,β), the packet entry on a word is computed at its own creation quotient by expanding its ≤L rows and selecting target degrees equal to its actual β vector. The number of selections is at most (1+poly(s))^L; do not loop over ordered copies of a repeated exponent. Each outgoing source power uses repeated squaring at cost O(log(2+β)) ring operations. Thus per-entry bit time is 2^{O(L log s)}poly(s) with polynomial space. Use the actual immutable coordinate representatives before changing coefficient domains.

**Conditional restart.** For a first child with ring dimensions e,ell, lift each packet word to its coordinate polynomial in U,V. Interpolate the unreduced integer contraction and reduce by its original field polynomial, V^ell and 2^P after exact recovery. Its degree and integer coefficient heights are polynomial in s by counting ≤2^{2w} words and multiplying a polynomial number of polynomial-bit factors of polynomial degree. For either parent or child perform enough evaluations and CRT primes to recover the integer conditional result; prime sizes and all schedules are polynomial in s (integer interpolation may also follow independent exact scalar reconstruction). In a scalar modular query use canonical representatives of local packet values modulo its polynomial-size odd prime q, always AFTER the required original entry has been acquired. The remaining original factors are Boolean gates and pins. Thus the nonnegative integer scalar is ≤2^{2w} q^{w/L}, even when the source coefficients or their lifts were signed. Because log s=O(log w) and L grows faster than log s, this takes O(w) bits. Applying the manuscript's fresh Boolean tensor contraction at size Θ(w), including its entry and degree hypotheses above, gives time exp(O(w/√log w))poly(s) and polynomial workspace for each conditional entry, hence for the root polynomial and separately for each required first-child scalar. In the bound choose a sufficiently large fixed fresh-size constant to cover 2w wires and the desired O(w) precision. Since L log s=o(w^.9), the exceptional oracle fits the fresh entry allowance. Enumerate q and interpolation coordinates sequentially, retaining polynomial-bit accumulators. Unused isolated factors and outer multiplicities are handled in their original rings. Multiplying c constant-size matrices costs a polynomial factor, as does outputting all the requested coefficients or native root samples. Visiting all possible first-generation β separately retains its own enumeration cost. These bounds use the manuscript contraction theorem; direct owner contraction remains available independently. Applying fresh contraction to plain conditional raw chunks also gives the same generic fresh-size exponent for their counts, without any exceptional packet entries.
