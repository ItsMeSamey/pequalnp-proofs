# Entropy and precision of the two-sided tile basis

Fix an active D.1 shell i, D_i=L/2^i, and a physical tile y=y₀+2^(i+1)j. Write q=y₀−x, A=2^(i+1)/(t−q+2Lζ), K=W_(x,y₀). The separated two-sided Cauchy expansion uses one k per tile for 0≤k<D_i/2, with the same physical target and source groups as that tile. Its target numerator contains K, the product of A−2r over 0≤r<k, and the scalar 2^v₂(k!). Its denominators 1−rA are units; the source coefficients have odd denominators and a binomial integer numerator. The factor 1−2k² in the target is also odd. These weights refer to this spatial Cauchy expansion, independently of any separate Newton interpolation of outgoing mode values.

Writing B=A/2 for the already-integral rational expression 2^i/(t−q+2Lζ), one has B congruent to the scalar 2-adic integer 2^i/(t−q) modulo 2^(a+1+i−2v₂(t−q)), hence at least modulo 2^(a+1−i). This statement uses the exact even scalar divided by its known valuation and unit inversion, and does not divide an ambiguous residue by two. For s≤floor(log₂k), B is congruent modulo 2^s to an ordinary integer; among r=0,...,k−1 at least floor(k/2^s) terms B−r vanish modulo 2^s. Counting all levels gives v₂(product(A−2r))≥k+v₂(k!). The argument holds in the native unramified/nilpotent ring, even for nonscalar ζ. As K is even, the total guaranteed target valuation is at least w_k=1+k+2v₂(k!). Use this weight in the actual tuple generator. A conservative routine cutting off at the weaker 1+k+v₂(k!) is algebraically correct, but a generator that enumerates under only that weaker weight does not receive the following clock.

Set f(x)=−ln(1−exp(−x)), θ>0, K₀=ceil(θ^−1/2). The first K₀ summands f(θw_k) contribute at most K₀ f(θ)=o(1/θ). For k≥K₀ the binary digit-sum identity gives w_k≥3k−2log₂(k+1)≥(3−η)k, with η=2log₂(K₀+1)/K₀ tending to zero. Since f decreases and its integral on the positive real line equals Σ_{j≥1}1/j²=π²/6, the remaining sum is at most π²/[6(3−η)θ]. Restricting the sum to actual tiles' allowed k only reduces it. This gives π²(1+o(1))/(18θ) uniformly for any collection with sup θ→0.

At a restarted generation assume the proved precision bound P_i≤P*=(6+o(1))mD_i/u and color-count bound C*=6m/D_i for each k. Then the log of the complete admissible tuple count is bounded by θP*+C*π²(1+o(1))/(18θ). Choose θ=π sqrt(C*/(18P*)). This is uniformly O(sqrt(u)/D_i)=o(1) when D_i≥log²m and u=O(log m). The bound becomes (2π√2+o(1))m/√u. The corresponding coefficient for the ordinary geometric basis is π√24; for one spatial binomial factorial gain, w≥2k−O(log k), it is π√12. These are upper constants on a common scale rather than tight entropy assertions.
