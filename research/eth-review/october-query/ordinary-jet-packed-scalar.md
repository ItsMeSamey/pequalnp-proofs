# A single lifted scalar for the low ordinary jets

Use the audited physical-one-node-analytic.md or physical-hilbert-height.md for J, with circle bound B. Put D=lcm(1,…,2L), H=(L−1)^M (L≥2), A(X)=H F(1+D X). As each ordinary denominator divides (L−1)^M D^j at order j, A is an integer polynomial. Choose an integer b with 2^(b−1)>H max(1,D^(J−1)) B; J=O(M) under the Hilbert bound and b=O(M log L+J log D+log B). At every degree j<J the coefficient c_j of A has absolute value less than 2^(b−1). One scalar W=A(2^b) mod 2^(bJ) determines these c_j: extract the centered signed residue modulo 2^b, subtract it, divide exactly by 2^b, and reduce the remaining modulus by b bits at each step. Terms of higher degree cannot affect these digits. Rational sum of (−1)^j c_j/(H D^j) to J terms and rounding recovers #F. This construction does not divide by an even denominator inside a power-of-two ring.

Implement W with genuine physical old truth tables on one shore and one factor on the other per packet, consuming the Y and X bits of each real edge: 2M Boolean wires in total, maximum degree max(2L, original degree). The packet multiplies (L−1)l_Y((W(1+D2^b)X)_x) over its own real rows x, so it contributes its share of H exactly. The kernel formulas about node 1 consist of the partial products of 1+D 2^b/(1−q), multiplying the signed derivative numerator −(L+1); for δ≠1 omit q=δ and use D 2^b/(1−δ). Each displayed division is exact on D before the power-of-two ring calculation. Empty packets act as identity. Each physical entry thus costs poly(M,L,bJ) bit operations and workspace, including construction of D. The resulting weighted instance calls the high-precision-block-crt.md reduction on its canonical representatives modulo 2^(bJ), so the manuscript counter takes poly(input) 2^O(M/sqrt(log M)) time with a fresh scalar invocation of size Θ(2M) and polynomial space; its threshold and local degree hold for the native L≤s^.001 and fixed-density source M=Θ(s). Direct enumeration is also possible. This packs all desired Taylor coefficients into one scalar with fully charged high precision bJ. The new instance has twice as many wires and signed modular weights, so it is not a recurrence shrinking Boolean SAT. Modulo reduction of the Cauchy kernel must follow its indicated factorization; treating the arithmetic as an odd-field homomorphism would be incorrect.

A separate expansion test explains why a degree ceiling J≥M need not remove hard configurations. Track occurrence tags temporarily at the formal monomial-expansion level. From row x select either its bare intercept 1−Y_x or the FIRST ordinary coefficient of its native diagonal kernel (2Y_x−1)κ z X_x, with κ=W′_xx(1)=−(L+1)/(L−1). The diagonal is always a real slot and W_xx(1)=0. Sum just this tagged sector through degree M, evaluate at z=1/κ, then multiply all old Boolean predicates and sum their assignments. Each combined row factor is exactly l_Y(X_x); thus the tagged sector returns the original SAT count with the original assignment multiplicities. The whole untagged coefficient includes all other offset selections too. The containment is not a hardness proof for obtaining a single untagged coefficient. It explains why reasoning that discarding all higher-than-linear jet orders automatically strips out original assignment correlations would fail.
