# Compiling collective sparse checks on signed affine observations

Take the exact signed parity extraction and Gaussian chart of signed-graphic-observed-rank.md. Its d free bits y biject to all assignments satisfying the extracted equations. Retain m distinct physical observations z=a+Ay on which the remaining signed k-CNF F depends, preserving occurrence signs. Equivalently use distinct nonzero linear normals as the observations with zero offsets and absorb the offsets into each literal. Fold constants and tautologies. A is m by d, r=rank A, and the attainable z form a+im A. Every attainable z has exactly 2^(d−r) lifts. All ranks below are binary.

Fix an integer b≥1 independent of input size. Enumerate every nonempty subset of at most b observation indices. Form its indicator h, test hᵀA=0 and insert each independent passing h into a Gaussian basis. This takes m^O(b) times polynomial input size and polynomial space, including storing at most m independent rows. The final rank is exactly the dimension of the span of all dual words of weight at most b. The direct recognition criterion is rank m−r. If it passes, every attainable point satisfies hᵀz=hᵀa, and conversely any z satisfying the checks has z−a in (ker Aᵀ)^⊥=im A. This full-rank check is essential; necessity alone gives spurious solutions.

Keep all m observed variables declared. For every retained nonzero row h, add one clause forbidding each parity-wrong pattern on its support with respect to its own signed right-hand side. There are exactly 2^(wt(h)−1) such clauses. Append the signed residual clauses unchanged. Width is K=max(2,k,b), and the number M of compiled clauses is bounded by |F|+(m−r)2^(b−1); price the actual M after any simplifications. The original count is exactly 2^(d−r) times the new count. Fixing a satisfying z, Gaussian solving Ay=z−a yields an original witness; witness search by prefix pinning adds at most m unit clauses per call. For m<20 or zero, brute force on the m declared variables. Otherwise the cited quantitative fixed-width October theorem gives (input+M)^O_{k,b}(1) 2^O_{k,b}(m loglog m/sqrt(log m)) bit time and polynomial space, or exponent O_{k,b,D}(m/sqrt(log m)) when M≤Dm for fixed D. Include the explicit enumeration cost and all reached leaves if this is used below branching. An improvement in the source rank-r scale requires suitable m versus r and density. A positive rate and O(m) residual clauses suffice for m=O(r) and fixed density; rank and density are independently measured.

If the test fails, hyperplane cuts or graph-coordinate normalizations may still work. A separate exact extended alternative accepts any supplied bounded-row-weight affine system Jz+Lu=c with t additional bits u. By Gaussian elimination check nonemptiness, project a particular solution and a nullspace basis onto z, then compare the projected affine coset to a+im A (both inclusions or rank and offset). Each attainable z has 2^s extensions with s=t−rank L. Append signed F and compile the bounded checks by the same local pattern exclusions; a zero-row check is either redundant or contradictory. With N=m+t declared bits and measured M_ext clauses apply the same source bounds at N,K_ext and density M_ext/N. Divide the compiled count by 2^s exactly, then multiply by 2^(d−r); a compiled satisfying assignment gives z and an original Gaussian lift. A fallback always exists: choose coefficient coordinates for r basis observations, express all observed linear forms on those r independent bits, and use fresh XOR gates with bounded-arity equations for each form and its offset, retaining the z outputs. All internal gate values and coefficient coordinates are unique given valid z. This can cost O(mr+r+m) variables/clauses before F, not a near-linear encoder guarantee. Supplying or constructing, verifying and running any proposed better extension is chargeable. This does not solve minimum sparse-extension synthesis.

The code used in affine-observation-all-bases-distance.md (the 1a61c7e9 control) exhibits a specific failure of the direct test. There Q=2^h evaluation sites, a degree<K=Q/2 outer Reed–Solomon evaluation, an injective h-to-ℓ linear inner encoder, r=hK and m=Qℓ. Any dual word of weight ≤K touches at most K sites. Interpolation makes the polynomial evaluations at those sites independently arbitrary field symbols. Holding all but one at zero shows that each local restriction of the dual word must annihilate the entire inner binary image. All such dual words lie in the direct sum of the Q inner duals, of dimension Q(ℓ−h)=m−2r. They miss at least r of the m−r required dimensions, for every fixed b≤K. This concerns the physical observation coordinates of that construction (if duplicate coordinates are merged, each inner block retains full dimension and the same codimension calculation holds with the shorter inner length). It does not exclude a sparse extension on more variables. For contrast there are asymptotically good codes defined by bounded-weight checks; for a family with check bound b the direct criterion succeeds. Linear primal distance alone distinguishes neither outcome. See Sipser and Spielman, Expander Codes, https://cs-www.cs.yale.edu/homes/spielman/PAPERS/expandersIT.pdf .
