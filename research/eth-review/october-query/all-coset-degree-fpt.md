# All-coset quadratic restriction in time parameterized by codimension

Let f_1,...,f_m be explicit Boolean squarefree polynomials of degree at most fixed k on an N-dimensional binary space. There is a deterministic 2^{O(t²+t)}poly(m,N^k,input) algorithm to find a subspace H of codimension at most t such that every f_i is quadratic on every translate of H, or certify none exists. It uses 2^{O(t)}poly(m,N^k,input) space if all cosets are stored. Alternatively polynomial space suffices by streaming offsets and regenerating their restricted ANFs from the root. This extends the archived single-cubic search in research/affine-phase-rank/cubic-width/README.md. For k>3, an individual origin restriction is insufficient.

Maintain an ambient W of codimension b≤t and remaining h=t−b. In a fixed basis of W, inspect all m·2^b ancestor translates (or enumerate a complementary basis's offsets directly) for a restriction of degree ≥3. If none exists return W. If h=0 reject this branch. For an offending restriction of maximum degree d within that polynomial choose a degree-d monomial and take coordinate finite differences along d−3 of its variables. This leaves a degree-three polynomial g with a nonzero cubic coefficient on the remaining triple. In every successful H contained in W, every translate of that offending restriction has degree ≤2 along H. Differences of translates preserve this property even when the directions used are outside H. Thus the nonzero alternating trilinear τ=D³g over W vanishes on H³. Only this necessary constraint is used to branch; recursively check the entire original family.

Compute R=rad τ. If dim(W/R)≤h, projection of any such H onto W/R must be proper: otherwise τ descends to a nonzero form and surjectivity gives three preimages in H with nonzero evaluation. Hence some nonzero functional in R's annihilator kills H. Branch on all ≤2^h−1 such functionals. If dim(W/R)>h, take S of dimension h+1 independent modulo R. For any H of codimension ≤h in W, S intersects H nontrivially. For each nonzero x in S take the nonzero alternating form B=τ(x,.,.). For a correct x, B vanishes on H×H, forcing rank B≤2h by a basis adapted to H. Its image meets H's annihilator nontrivially: if B(w,.) is nonzero for some w∈H, that functional annihilates H; otherwise H is in the radical and every member of im B annihilates H. Skip rank greater than 2h, and branch on each nonzero a in im B for all remaining x. Both cases yield at most 2^{3h+1} branches, each replacing W by its true hyperplane ker(a) in W. The intended solution is contained in at least one branch. Returning W only after the full coset-and-polynomial test gives soundness. The empty initial family and t≥N have the immediate full-space test or zero-space solution as appropriate.

Each node costs at most 2^t poly(m,N^k,input) by direct affine substitution of the bounded-degree ANFs, finite differences, and binary linear algebra. Depth t and a product of the displayed branching bounds give the stated clock. Depth-first traversal stores only linear charts, witnesses, indices and one current restricted polynomial; offsets and original-polynomial indices can be repeatedly scanned per node. Each chart has polynomially many bits, depth ≤N. This bounds the full workspace polynomially without storing a list per coset. For one native degree≥3 character profile, capped discovery followed by exact quadratic sums over at most 2^t cosets is now FPT in codimension for any fixed input width. Include all profiles, checks, and rejected attempts as in nonlinear-syndrome-compiler.md. Nothing ensures a small t on arbitrary CNF input.
