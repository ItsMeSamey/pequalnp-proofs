# A long shifted trial with a physical matching certificate

Fix sufficiently large $v$ and declare $n=2v+2$ Boolean variables: $x_i,z_i$ for $v$ gadgets, and $g,z_0$. Put $\lambda=\lceil\log_2n\rceil$, $\nu=\lceil\log_2\lambda\rceil$, and let $H$ be the simple circulant with distances $1,\dots,D/2$ on the $v$ base indices, $D=12\nu<v-1$. Include the real clauses $(x_i)$ and $(\neg x_i\lor z_i)$, the guard mate $(\neg g\lor z_0)$, and a filler $(g\lor\neg x_i\lor\neg x_j)$ for each edge of $H$. The original count of clauses is $2v+1+vD/2>n\nu$. The October source therefore uses its arbitrary-density route. No clause is tautological, repeated, or a superset of another: in particular the positive singleton does not subsume a filler containing its negative literal.

In the width-three sparsifier the proper pair hearts have threshold $M$ and singleton hearts $M^2$. Here $M\ge\Gamma\lceil\sqrt\lambda\rceil$ and $M=O(\sqrt\lambda\log\lambda)$. The only eligible heart is the singleton $g$ at width three. Its degree is $vD/2\ge M^2$; any base literal has width-three degree at most $D$, the pair $(g,\neg x_i)$ has degree $D<M$, and a base-base pair has degree at most one. Each literal has width-two degree at most one, and width one has no nonempty proper heart. In the A-child the source replaces the fillers by $(g)$ and keeps all declared variables and all real clauses, including the negatively signed guard mate. It does not propagate units. This is a leaf with $q=2(v+1)=n$ clauses whose conflict graph contains a specified perfect matching: the two clauses within each gadget, also including the guard gadget.

Use the native $B=2(1+M+M^2)$ and $P=20000B^2(\lfloor\log_2q\rfloor+1)^2$. Then $P=\Theta(\lambda^4(\log\lambda)^4)$ and $q/2\ge P$ eventually. Construct the source's actual Tanner checks; the certificate below is independent of their entries. It applies at every computed prefix whose actual effective winning mask $h$ is nonzero. In particular there is such a first winner for any allowed Tanner construction. The initial rank is $o(q)$ by the source's row bound $q/B+2$. Select one designated clause per gadget. Every word supported on the selected clauses can be realized as the exact violation pattern by independent assignments within the gadgets. Since this gives a coordinate cube of dimension $q/2$, the initial checks accept a nonzero physical pattern. A singleton trial catching it improves the score of the zero row.

Write $k=|h|\in[1,P]$. Suppose $h$ uses both coordinates of $a$ gadget pairs and a single coordinate of $b$ pairs, so $2a+b=k$. Build a candidate $c$ disjoint from $h$. First choose the unused partners of all $b$ singletons. Then take both endpoints of $a$ fresh pairs; at least $a$ are available because $q/2\ge k$. These choices use $k$ labels in $c$ and give exactly $a+b+a=k$ complete conflicting pairs in $h\cup c$. Fill the remaining $P-k$ positions in $c$ with distinct unused labels, possible since $q\ge P+k$. The resulting native trial of length $P$ takes every chosen index once with coefficient one. Its effective support has size $P$. The shifted support $S=c\mathbin{\triangle}h$ has size $P+k$, and $k$ disjoint conflict pairs certify the exact excess over the native prior-score radius $P$.

For clarity, $N_A$ denotes the prefix count and $H_A(S)$ its unnormalized signed parity sum. On any matched pair $(i,j)$ the violations are mutually exclusive, giving the integer identity $\chi_i\chi_j=\chi_i+\chi_j-1$. Expanding the $k$ disjoint pairs reduces $H_A(S)$ to at most $3^k$ signed old correlations of supports of size at most $P$. The next score equals $(N_A+H_A(h)+H_A(c)+H_A(S))/4$. The integer numerator is exactly divisible by four. This is a certificate for a shifted query longer than $P$ and does not depend on generic rank or a guessed check matrix.

For the specified formula, building/recognizing the leaf and indexing its clause pairs costs polynomial time in the explicit formula length; selecting $c$ from the known winner costs polynomial time in $q,P$. Reaching that winner still charges the source construction and all required prefix scores. Expanding and labeling the leaves costs $3^k\operatorname{poly}(q,P,n)$ bit operations in addition to acquiring the prior scores. A streamed old-score sweep need not retain the required entries. Storing an entire old table can cost exponential space; rescans or fresh oracle calls must be paid in a polynomial-space comparison. With random access to those old values, each signed term has $O(n)$ bits and a running integer sum uses $O(n+k)$ bits. All $P!$ orderings of the chosen active labels represent the same effective $c$ in a literal ordered list. This duplication does not establish that all effective queries are saved, that the overall source clock improves, or that no other algebraic certificate exists. In particular the formula's A-child is physically a product of independent gadgets and easy to count directly.
