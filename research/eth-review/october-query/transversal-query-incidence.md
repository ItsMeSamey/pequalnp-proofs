# An original 3-CNF that forces density in same-variable polynomial systems

For any q=2^r, r≥1, take three labelled parts A,B,C of q Boolean variables apiece. The q² clauses forbid selecting A_i,B_j,C_{i+j} together, with indices in GF(q). Thus n=3q, m=q². Every point belongs to q triples and two triples share at most one point. This is a standard Latin-square transversal design.

Consider any finite system of Boolean GF(2) polynomial equalities on these same indexed variables, with the same full solution set. Use multilinear representatives and discard zero equations. Let I be total variable–equation incidences (a variable counts once per equation in whose polynomial it actually occurs). For degree at most three, I≥3q², attained by the original q² clause monomials. In particular no number of rows or changes of row basis can reduce total incidences.

Every support of size below three and every three-element support other than a forbidden triple is a model. Taking the Boolean Möbius transform shows that all coefficients of degree ≤3 vanish except possibly the forbidden monomials. Write each equation as a row of coefficients on triples. Fix a variable v, put all other variables of its part to zero and put v=1. The other two parts split into q disjoint pairs indexed by triples through v. Give both vertices of each pair the same bit t_e. The violation vectors on this subcube run through ALL q-bit patterns on the star of v and vanish elsewhere. The columns for these q triples are therefore linearly independent in the system, or a nonzero kernel word would yield an erroneous physical model. Only equations containing v can carry these columns. Thus v occurs in at least q equations; sum over all 3q vertices.

For arbitrary degree D≥2 there is a weaker quantitative bound I≥3q·ceil(q/(D−1)). On that same chart before fixing v, the whole cube with v=0 consists of models, so the polynomial for each restricted row is v times a polynomial of degree at most D−1 in the t_e (identification only reduces degree). Rows omitting v are identically zero there. At v=1 the common zero set on the t_e is exactly {0}. The Boolean polynomial product of the indicators 1+row equals the point indicator ∏_e(1+t_e); its unique multilinear representative has degree q. Subadditivity of degrees gives the claimed lower bound, without a counting theorem assumption.

This applies to any final compression retaining original coordinates and bounded degree and preserving the set of original assignments. The monotone instance has an immediate satisfying assignment. The calculation does not bound SAT decision, circuits with shared auxiliaries, affine changes mixing the physical coordinates, a network representation of each equation, or a counting algorithm that only preserves a scalar count. In particular it gives a barrier for reducing the incidence statistic in the source wire bound and no lower bound for arbitrary contraction algorithms.
