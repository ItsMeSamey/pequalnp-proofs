# Exact early prism trials with a missing previous character

On the guard-true prism leaf of guarded-circulant-positive-handoff.md put λ=ceil(log₂ n), R=floor(n/λ), and r₀=rank of the genuine constructed Tanner checks. By guarded-rank-windows.md, r₀=O(n/[λ(log λ)²]+1)=o(R). Use the first R−r₀ rank increases, with old ranks r₀,…,R−1. Their new ranks are at most R, so the score streams both before and after every transition are in the early window. This costs at most R−r₀+1 full streams.

In guarded-circulant-radius-periods.md each periodic rank increase halves the number of distinct radius-P probe classes modulo the current row span, whereas this number never increases. At most P log₂(q+1) rank increases in the entire trajectory are periodic. Consequently at least R−r₀−P log₂(q+1)=(1−o(1))n/log₂ n of these early transitions have a physical next trial whose even-score integrand is outside the pointwise linear span of every old radius integrand on the actually attainable kernel. The offending trial can depend on the step. Existence requires no recognition algorithm: the all-trial stream already includes it. This is linear nonidentifiability from current-prefix old probes, with no numerical nonrecoverability claim for the true fixed unweighted CNF.

Every trial in the indicated actual streams is evaluated in total 2^{O(n/log n)} bit time and polynomial space. One proof uses the general binary character expansion of the parity tests (at most 2^{R+1} characters per trial) and evaluates each selected-edge spin partition function with a constant-width prism transfer. Each physical ordered list is charged. The local private-bit calculation gives the same result as summing over the guard-zero affine slices. The archived padded-grid-intermediate-window.md already gives the low-rank streamed character expansion plus local block elimination and separator evaluation for an earlier genuine trajectory. Its printed theorem is for a padded grid and a different prefix length and does not itself state this prism overlap. Specializing that algorithm to prism structure gives the present early clock without a new algorithmic primitive. The separate archived guarded-prism-kernel-suffix.md enumerates primal profiles near full rank. The overlap adds the missing-character witness on the same early physical transitions.
