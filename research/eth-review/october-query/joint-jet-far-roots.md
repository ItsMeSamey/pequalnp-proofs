# A bounded-height, globally consistent jet adversary

At the native root a=2u, L=2^a, let h=floor(M/(4u)). For each positive integer s in [L/2,L) of valuation j<u give it multiplicity h(u−j). Let B(T) be the product of (1−T/s) with these multiplicities. Set A(T)=B(T)W_{xx}(T)^d, d=floor(M/2), using the native diagonal cardinal polynomial.

There are L/2^(j+2) roots at shell j; their odd parts give all odd classes modulo 2^b equally often, b=ceil(log2(4u)), since a−j−1≥b asymptotically. The shell multiplicity is E(u−j)/2^j, E=hL/4. The valuation proof of joint-jet-adversary.md therefore applies to B: every coefficient at an active shell j has valuation at least ceil(3E/(2·2^j)). At inactive nodes all roots have lower valuation. The native integrality lemma for W_{xx} preserves the uniform bound for A, and the cardinal factor makes all jets of order below d exactly zero at every nonzero node. A(0)=1. The total degree is at most (L/2)(M/4)+(M/2)(2L−1)≤M(2L−1).

Each positive integer root s≤L−1 occurs at most floor(M/4) times, so B's denominator divides ((L−1)!)^floor(M/4). Since |C'(0)|=L!(L−1)!, the denominator of A divides the actual integer |C'(0)|^M in ordinary arithmetic. No odd multiplier is needed. On the complex disk |T|≤3/2, |B|≤(1+3/L)^deg(B)≤exp(O(M)). Also W_{xx}(T)=(1+T/L) times the product over k=1,...,L−1 of (1−T²/k²). Its absolute value there is bounded by a universal constant using summability of 1/k². Thus |A|≤exp(O(M)). The pair A,0 satisfies the native degree/denominator, complex disk height exp(O(M loglog L)), reflection-node integer value at −1, and even identical exact low jets at all nonzero nodes. Nevertheless origin parity differs. This is an abstract scalar obstruction and neither claims physical realizability nor rules out stronger physical constraints or new oracles.
