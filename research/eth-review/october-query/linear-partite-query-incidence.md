# Exact incidence optimum for linear partite inputs

Let H be any k-partite k-uniform hypergraph, k≥2, with distinct edges sharing at most one vertex. Each edge forbids its all-ones pattern as a monotone k-clause. For every exact degree-at-most-k GF(2) equation system on the same Boolean coordinates, each vertex v belongs to at least deg_H(v) equations. Total variable–equation incidence is at least k|E|, attained by the original monomials. Arbitrary rows and arbitrary numbers of equations are allowed.

All supports of size below k, and all k-sets other than edges, are models, so Boolean Möbius coefficients through degree k occur only on edge monomials. Fix v to one and all other vertices of its part to zero. The sets e minus v for edges through v are pairwise disjoint. Assign all vertices of each petal the bit t_e, and all unspecified coordinates zero. Only the star clauses can fire and all their violation patterns occur. A kernel vector among the star columns gives a false physical model. Those columns are confined to rows incident to v. For any maximum degree D≥k the same chart with v free yields at least ceil(deg_H(v)/(D−1)) incident equations, by the singleton-indicator argument of transversal-query-incidence.md.

For connected families at varying densities, take l copies of the 3-partite Latin-square design of order q and identify one fixed part-A vertex across all copies. The result is 3-partite, linear and connected, with n=l(3q−1)+1 and m=lq². Varying l independently of q permits diverging densities below every power of n. This obstructs reducing the total incidence statistic. It gives no lower bound on actual wires or time, or on schemes using different coordinates, existential auxiliaries or only scalar count preservation.
