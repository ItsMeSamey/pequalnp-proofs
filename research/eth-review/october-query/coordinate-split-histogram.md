# Coordinate splitting for exact sparse violation histograms

Let h be a nonnegative integer histogram on Boolean m-vectors of violations. The score of a row S counts mass with even parity on S. The empty score is total mass N. Thus its character sum is twice its score minus N. For T⊆[m] the positive moment M(T)=Σ h(v)∏_{i∈T}v_i is the alternating sum of the character sums on subsets of T, divided by 2^{|T|}. This also holds for the zero measure.

For t distinct known words in a prefix, degree at most floor(log₂ t) squarefree monomials span every rational-valued function on those words. Indeed all polynomials span by point indicators. Take the standard monomials of the vanishing ideal under a graded monomial order. Boolean identities force squarefreeness, and the standard set is downward closed and has size t. All 2^d divisors of a degree-d standard monomial belong to that set. Construct a basis without computing a Gröbner basis: begin with the constant, then in degree order test multiplications of retained basis monomials by each coordinate, skipping previously tested terms, and retain each new linearly independent evaluation column. By induction each degree filtration is spanned, since multiplying a lower-degree expansion by a coordinate preserves its evaluation identity. Use ordinary rational elimination on at most t retained columns.

Start with the empty prefix of mass N, or return an empty histogram if N=0. For known prefixes u with positive mass, choose the basis above and interpolate their point indicators e_u on this support. For the next bit i, the child ending in one has mass E_h[v_i e_u]; its sibling has the parent mass minus this number. Each new positive moment involves i and one basis monomial. Retain positive children and report overflow as soon as more than s nonzero prefixes occur. Distinct nonzero prefixes always have distinct nonzero extensions, so overflow is conclusive. Otherwise t≤s+1 throughout, including the possible all-zero prefix. After all m steps the leaves give the full physical histogram, including zero mass h(0) by lookup with default zero. All claims include s=0. The empty CNF has a single empty word if N>0.

The maximum parity order is D=1+floor(log₂(s+1)), or m if smaller. The query count is polynomial in m and s+1 because each moment uses at most 2^D parities and at most s+1 moments per stage. Intermediate determinants of 0/1 evaluation matrices have O((s+1)log(s+2)) bits by Hadamard's inequality; multiplying by the input masses adds O(log(N+1)) bits. Exact elimination has polynomial bit complexity in these quantities. For October's full list of P slots, zero coefficients allow every required row if D≤P. An arbitrary adaptive lookup is charged as a score evaluation or must be served by explicitly retained data; it is not a free lookup into a previous greedy pass. The scores must use the same fixed prefix. Retaining all candidate scores may cost much more than the learner itself.

For unrestricted histograms with m≥P+1 the consequent universal cap 2^P−2 is sharp for recovery or overflow. Compare unit mass on the even words of length P+1 with unit mass on the odd words (append zero coordinates if needed). A permitted character touching the first P+1 coordinates has zero sum on both. A character supported entirely on padding is constant one, and the totals coincide. At cap 2^P−1 the even population is within cap including one all-zero word, whereas the odd population exceeds cap and has no all-zero word. This oracle example makes no assertion about indistinguishable CNFs or a fixed Tanner matrix.

At an empty hash prefix of an explicit CNF, positive moments need no parity oracle. To violate every clause in T, pin every literal in their union false. A conflict gives moment zero; otherwise it is 2^{n−number of distinct pinned variables}. This includes tautologies, repeated literals, empty clauses and unused variables. At an arbitrary hash prefix, those pins still compute its moment if one separately counts assignments satisfying the prefix subject to the pins. The power-of-two formula alone applies at the empty prefix. The empty-prefix algorithm therefore gives an output-sensitive capped CNF solver in polynomial bit time and space in the explicit bit size, n and s+1. It returns exact #SAT on successful recovery or certified overflow. Overflow does not decide satisfiability. Charge the binary length of any printed count if n is supplied by a short binary header.

With unrestricted positive moment queries, maximal-union peeling is a simpler baseline. Subtract previously recovered masses from each queried moment. Starting at the empty set, add any bit producing a positive residual moment until no such addition exists. The residual moment there equals the mass of that exact maximal word. Remove it and repeat; the empty word is recovered last if present. At an empty prefix the same union-of-pins backend implements this procedure. The coordinate-splitting bound concerns the maximum required query order.
