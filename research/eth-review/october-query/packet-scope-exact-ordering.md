# Exact order and binary-tree planning for a physical packet contraction

Fix one verified physical packing and its p nonempty blocks of whole packets. Block i uses the set A_i of actual old OR/COPY branch spins and true tied COPY2 bits incident to its ports. Include unary branch weights exactly once and independently charge any globally unused bits. For any S of blocks let U(S) be the union of scopes and B(S)=U(S)∩U(complement S). Process indexed physical factors, retaining multiplicities. Signs and polynomial or ring values do not affect these purely structural charges. The model prices dense enumeration; zero pruning or compression may change the best order for another model.

For a prefix S, a table counts its product over the assignments of B(S), summing internal coordinates. Start at S empty with scalar one. Appending i enumerates the assignments to J=B(S)∪A_i, multiplies the prefix value and the block entry, and accumulates by restriction to B(S+i). Indeed B(S+i)⊆J and the old summed coordinates cannot occur in i. Each eliminated assignment is used once. With pretabulation charge the 2^|A_i| block entries once, independently of the order. With on-demand preparation charge the block evaluation with every visited joint word. Also charge the 2^|B(S+i)| destination initialization. Let c(S,i) be the resulting weighted sum of enumerated words, including the chosen per-word work weights for index operations, arithmetic or the entry oracle. Setting d(empty)=0 and d(S+i)=min(d(S)+c(S,i)) over last blocks gives the exact minimum of that specified additive charge. A cap on joint width deletes transitions with |J| above the cap. If the cap concerns actual dense stored entries instead, test 2^|B(S)|+2^|B(S+i)| plus any retained block tables and scratch; enumeration of J can stream. Store predecessor witnesses. There are O(p 2^p) transitions.

For a binary tree split S into nonempty L and R. Its interface J=B(L)∪B(R) contains B(S), and J minus B(S) consists precisely of the shared child bits internal to S. With dense caching at all nodes, leaf i visits 2^|A_i| words and sums into its B(i) table; the internal node initializes 2^|B(S)| entries and visits 2^|J| words to multiply and add child entries. Minimize the sum of these charges and both child optima, over all splits of S (one representative per unordered split). Caps on individual interfaces or join enumeration can be imposed locally. A bound on the maximum table size gives, for depth at most p, O(p times that size) simultaneously stored entries via depth-first scheduling; an exact total-memory cap may require a Pareto table tracking child evaluation order and simultaneous storage.

With polynomial-space contraction keep a single fixed assignment to B(S) at entry and enumerate the 2^|J minus B(S)| fresh assignments. Recompute each child at its resulting boundary word. In this specified DFS the scalar-operation charge at this node is its setup plus 2^|J minus B(S)| times (both child costs plus the local join cost); the corresponding leaf makes 2^|A_i minus B(i)| entry evaluations. These counts are exact for unconditional dense loops with fixed costs per evaluation. A separate minimax measure is the maximum number of accumulated fresh bits on a root-to-leaf path, including the final leaf exponent. It obeys a maximum of the two child optima plus the current fresh exponent; it bounds enumeration work up to node-count and polynomial entry factors. Minimizing this exponent and minimizing the full work are two distinct objectives.

All-subsets binary planning examines O(3^p) splits using O(2^p) stored costs and witnesses, times polynomial factors for scope bitsets, caps and integer weights. Every enumerated path has at most p nodes and adds at most p times the total number of distinct spins/bits to its exponent, so the work scores and comparisons have polynomial bit length for polynomial-bit weights. This is fixed-parameter in p; no width-only FPT claim follows. For a supplied leaf order restrict S to intervals and split only between adjacent blocks: O(p³) split comparisons and O(p²) stored states plus polynomial bit factors. Outside scopes include the entire complement, even when disconnected. The exponential all-subsets planner can exceed a polynomial-space contraction's workspace; account for both or use fixed-order interval planning. To search several legal packings pay every plan evaluated. Selecting an order by searching it and then omitting search time can invalidate a purported crossover.

For the original Boolean root clearing C′(0) once per row permits exact full-polynomial arithmetic. At M ports, packet length L, the degree is at most M(2L−1), while coefficients of any partial conditional sum have O(ML log(2L)+M+number of spins/bits) bits by product 1-norm bounds and counting Boolean words. All coefficient arithmetic, kernel preparation, indexing, and a local entry evaluation on a fixed block word take polynomial bit work and polynomial scratch in the explicit parameters. A dense table with t entries costs t times the polynomial representation size, plus indexing overhead. The DFS evaluation stack has at most p frames with polynomial space per frame. Native center jets and origin readouts follow from the exact polynomial at polynomial-bit cost. The same charges with the actual ring-operation weights apply to a single reduced-precision run.

A matched raw comparator uses the original unprocessed signed formula, with its plain undeformed incidence/COPY representation and the same label-to-port mapping. At T=0 the row reads only its own physical column bit. Replace each dense packet product by the product of these incidence-local factors and contract on the same block tree. This computes the original count including isolated multiplicities and never enlarges a block scope. One may also split the incidence factors further and run an independently verified raw formula decomposition, choosing the cheaper route. Thus a small post-packing certificate itself does not establish a speedup for counting that same raw formula. A full deformed polynomial and its off-origin jets are additional outputs. In particular the signed binary cycle benchmark admits a two-state conditioned-cycle raw counter in linear arithmetic operations and polynomial bit time for arbitrary signs. Its full deformed-polynomial bound serves the latter output comparison. A concrete original-model improvement requires separate measured costs for setup, search, evaluation and equivalent outputs.
