# Automatic signed private-block SAT decision and witness

automatic_private_decision.py accepts only the original signed clauses and integer search caps p,b. It removes tautologies and duplicate literal occurrences, then prioritizes the verified four-sign physical pairs described in private-projection-decision.md. It subsequently tries all subsets of at most p appearing variables. For a candidate B take ALL its incident current clauses. Require it to be connected in the current primal graph, to touch a clause of width at least three, and the distinct other variables in those clauses to number at most b. Enumerate boundary words and store one private extension per realizable word. Compile all its valid unary and binary clauses by finding missing singleton and pair patterns. Check all unrealizable boundary words against the resulting binary clauses. A proposed elimination succeeds exactly when this compiled relation equals the projection. Empty projection yields UNSAT. Otherwise replace those incident clauses with the binary relation, recording the extension table. Every successful reduction is equisatisfiable in both directions. It can modify later decompositions, so a failed search returns UNKNOWN rather than claiming no decomposition exists.

Once all clauses are binary, implication-graph SCCs decide SAT. On YES the implementation obtains one joint core assignment with at most one additional binary SAT check per surviving variable. In reverse elimination order look up the stored extension for the current boundary. A previously unconstrained boundary bit can be chosen arbitrarily: if absent from the later formula then all its values satisfy the compiled boundary relation at that step. Check the returned assignment on every original row. Appearing variables lost with tautologies get arbitrary values. Extra declared unused coordinates, if any, may also be assigned arbitrarily outside this list-based decision interface.

For N appearing variables and original bit length L there are at most N successful eliminations. Every current row is either an original row or an explicit unary/binary constraint, giving polynomial working formula size even with literal order distinguished. With C_p=Σ_{j≤min(p,N)}binom(N,j), at most O((N+1)(C_p+poly(L))) candidates are tested including stalled/futile searches. Time is bounded by this quantity times 2^{p+b}poly(L,N,p,b), plus (N+1) polynomial SCC passes and O(L) validation. Stack storage is at most O(N 2^b poly(L,N,p,b)) bits including extension tables. Enumeration is lazy on private words and on candidate subsets. For fixed caps both time and space are polynomial. This is standard bounded existential variable elimination into 2-CNF with automatic search, not a complete tractability characterization.

Coverage is guaranteed on the lifted implication chains in private-projection-decision.md: the four-occurrence private pairs have their own identical incidence sets and all sign pairs. The two endpoint core variables have degree six; internal variables that have degree four each occur in two distinct gadgets and cannot imitate a complete four-clause private pair. No two internal variables have identical four-clause incidence sets on chains of length ≥3. Verified pairs occupy disjoint clause sets, and replacing one removes no variable of another. Every such gadget projects to its original unit or implication. Deleting one clause of any minimally unsatisfiable lifted chain allows its corresponding private gadget and all other gadgets to be satisfied. The raw primal graph has a matching containing all fresh private pairs, so its minimum vertex cover is linear even when no SAT witness exists. Thus this recognized family has a polynomial deterministic decision-and-witness bound rather than the generic sparse fixed-width exponential upper envelope. This compares proved upper bounds; it is not a lower bound for a competing SAT solver, or a win over all resolution or bounded-width elimination methods.

There is also coverage without twin pairs. For p≥1,b≥2, suppose every connected primal component containing a long clause has size at most p+2 (unbounded binary-only components are permitted). Choose all but two variables in any such component, then take one connected piece of that chosen set that touches a long clause. Such a piece exists since a long clause cannot lie in the two omitted variables. Its entire external boundary lies within the two omitted variables, so its projection admits an exact binary description. Every tested successful candidate is connected, so each replacement lies within one existing component; later components can only stay the same size or split. The algorithm therefore cannot stall while a long clause remains on this class. The tests also cover a three-clause private obstruction lacking a four-sign pair.

Pure-literal and matching-autarky reductions cannot discharge any nonempty part of the minimally unsatisfiable controls, and their raw strong backdoor to 2-CNF or renamable Horn is linear by the vertex-disjoint ternary gadget argument. Supplied-separator algorithms and Davis–Putnam variable elimination are relevant baselines. The possibility of encoding an arbitrary bounded-occurrence 3-CNF by the same four-occurrence private pairs persists. When a global binary compilation fails, a caller may resume its existing fallback. The public method alone returns UNKNOWN on a stall.
