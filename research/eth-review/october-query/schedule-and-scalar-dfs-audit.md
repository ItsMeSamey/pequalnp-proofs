For one parent interpolation evaluation at precision P, fix ancestor split-mode exponents with sum of weights z. At a binary group-tree split sum over its own split-mode descriptors with additional weight b<P−z. For each descriptor recursively evaluate both subtrees at z+b and multiply their scalar answers. A leaf evaluates only its group child with the fixed incident descriptors. No histograms or budget convolutions are performed.

Expanding the resulting arithmetic expression amounts to taking precisely those global assignments for which every root-to-leaf path has total weight <P. Each assignment occurs once, since each mode belongs to its unique group-tree split. This superset includes all assignments of global total weight <P. For any assignment in the difference, let t_g sum its w_Z β_Z over modes targeted to g. Every term of the target exponent coefficient of each packet carries the corresponding power of two by the original mode expansion. Source substitution factors L_Z(X)^β_Z and all retained tensors are integral. Multiplication over packets and summing over all child bit assignments preserve divisibility by 2^{t_g}. The complete product over g is therefore divisible by 2^{sum_g t_g}, which is zero at precision P on every extra assignment. Thus the scalar result equals the source result exactly. No division or carry adjustment is made. Per-history histogram storage and O(P²) scalar convolutions disappear; the previously proved path-inventory exponential envelope is unchanged.
