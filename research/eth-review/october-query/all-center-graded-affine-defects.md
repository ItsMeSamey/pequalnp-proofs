# Graded affine defects at all native grid centers

Let L=2^a≥2, grid S=[−L,L−1], α=2L, C(T)=Π_{q∈S}(T−q), and W_d(T)=C(T)/((T−d)C′(0)) as a polynomial. At arbitrary grid center t put T=t+α(ζ+Z), with ζ any integral source coefficient and Z a NEW formal indeterminate, truncated independently of old V modulo Z^J. Coefficient j means the ordinary power coefficient, equivalently the Hasse coefficient along this scaled direction; do not divide by j! or by α^j. At a published active center t≠0 with i=v₂(t)<u, M real rows, h=ceil(3M/u), the local full-precision parameters in variable_packet_machine.py are P_t=p+8h(L/2^i)+8M(a+1), J=h(u−i) for the required jet at ζ=0. Other ζ give translated local jets; inactive grid centers may still be evaluated but are not additional required calls.

Write κ_t=C′(t)/C′(0), a 2-adic unit: valuations of all t-factorials are the same because all binomial coefficients in row 2L−1 of Pascal's triangle are odd. The matched offset has W_t(t+αU)=κ_t Π_{q≠t}(1+αU/(t−q)). The polynomial for offset d≠t has the same formula except the factor at q=d is replaced by αU/(t−d). This identity includes t=−L even though there is no matching real packet offset at that center. The factors have positive linear-term valuation a+1−v₂(t−q). As q ranges over S minus t, the nonzero residues mod 2L occur exactly once; there are 1,2,4,…,L factors of costs 1,2,…,a+1. Let H(j) be the sum of the j smallest costs, H(0)=0, and +∞ beyond 2L−1. Every U^j coefficient (j≥1) is divisible by 2^H(j); translating U=ζ+Z preserves this lower bound at order j by the binomial theorem. Modulo 2, W_d equals the indicator of d=t. Subtracting this Boolean baseline yields a row defect whose Z^j coefficient has valuation ≥g(j)=max(1,H(j)), also at order zero. This argument is coordinatewise in the source's integral extension with old truncated V.

Consider N independent original Boolean row/column coordinates before imposing a signed affine-indicator term of rank R over GF(2); reject inconsistent terms. Allow a global sign character. Each of M real Bernoulli rows has its original affine tie at baseline or its defect linear combination of source reads; a missing match is a zero input. Expand k marked rows choosing ≤L reads each. The affine summation retains M−k ties and adds ≤k pins, so any consistent word fiber has dimension ≥B=max(0,N−R−M). Its signed sum vanishes or equals ±2^dimension, with a polynomial-time GF(2) solve. For uniform packets, coefficient j from k≥1 defects has valuation at least

    B+(k−r)g(b)+r g(b+1),       b=floor(j/k), r=j mod k,

with value +∞ for j>k(2L−1) and the r=0 term taken alone. Indeed the minimal total valuation over degree splits is attained by equalizing their degrees, since g is discretely convex (first increment zero, then increments ≥2 and nondecreasing). This sharpens the simpler B+max(k,j) bound. For k=0 only the constant affine contraction contributes. A coefficient in an external scalar multiplying the whole term, independent of Z, contributes its own 2-adic valuation; if that scalar has Z dependence convolve its actual coefficient bounds instead.

At precision P a target order j only needs row sets k whose bound is <P. For all orders 0,…,J−1 a safe inspected-term count is Σ_{0≤k≤min(M,P−B−1)} binom(M,k)L^k (empty sum if P≤B). For an isolated order use the stricter graded filter, and for each retained k ignore the tail of orders whose bounds already reach P. This is an upper bound on full term inspections, not the number of nonzero terms. Include kernel formation, polynomial-time binary solves and O(J²) source-ring arithmetic per truncated-polynomial multiplication; stream all choices in polynomial space. The all-center executable all_center_packet_jets.py implements the uniform k cutoff, not this optional finer per-order filter. Its native entry charges the true P_t,J, checks the projected work cap and declines excessive jobs. The bound can be exponential there, and a lower-precision answer cannot replace a native jet. Arbitrary inherited tables require an actual signed affine expansion and their immutable birth semantics, with every expansion term, coefficient and verification charged.

A degree-d signed physical OR at one row vertex of a cubic parity/COPY graph equals 1 minus the indicator of its unique falsifying local row word. This gives two genuine signed affine terms: other parity vertices alone (rank v−1), or those vertices plus d pins (rank v−1+d). For cubic graphs N=3e, M=2e; the latter stripes start at B=max(0,e−v−2). This applies on connected cubic expanders and includes interacting, non-affine OR predicates. No universal nonzero assertion over constrained subsets or legal layouts is needed. As a separate implementation route, signed_or_packet_jets.py sums the two parity sectors at that vertex and subtracts the false word. In its prescribed-parity sector the first d−1 bits carry a full character basis, so isolating the false word uses exactly 2^{d−1} characters. Accumulate all 2+2^{d−1} terms with common multiplier 2^{d−1} at P+d−1, canonically lifting ζ coefficients to that precision. The numerator equals 2^{d−1} times the desired integral jet for that lift. Coordinatewise integer division thus recovers the reduction at P irrespective of lift. This lifted calculation only applies to the fixed raw gate signatures and integral parameter ζ. The wrapper reports the sum of the underlying preflight bounds; its lift costs need not match the alternative two-affine-term solver.

Physical controls (not certifications of canonical label balancing): complete K4 edge list in increasing lexicographic order, ports 2j+b, packets (7,0,11,6),(3,10,1,8),(9,4,5,2), vertex 0 replaced by a signed OR false at local binary word 5. At P=4, ζ=1 and J=3 the ordinary coefficients are (0,8,0) at t=1, (6,8,0) at t=−1, and (4,8,0) at t=2. In particular genuine signed non-affine samples and nonconstant jets survive. No matchgate/Pfaffian realization is assumed.
