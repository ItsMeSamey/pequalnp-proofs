# The literal outer-center mode loop already meets the source exponent

At an actual fully occupied, half-separated root, the original weighted-tuple enumerator visits exp(Ω(M/sqrt(u))) feasible tuples at a single odd outer center when charged at the published uniform P_t. The same lower bound holds for the outer-half uniform taper and for the direct whole-jet enumerator that also prunes total mode degree at its requested q_t. This is a running-time bound for those enumeration rules.

Use the genuine private-negative-unit, high-bit-swapped-twin packets of [the crossing minor control](outer-jet-crossing-factor-rank.md), SHA256 f8aa22b92f23cdad178bd5921972ec92c6924126c9a4bb9318053d8bc8df2b82. In its B=M/(64² L) chosen first packets the coordinate-zero row symbols g and column symbols h correspond to identical slot parity. Fix that one physical h tile in each selected packet. There are L/2 real column positions, at least one with tile index j=1. Its moments sum j^b X_y over real columns and none is the zero linear form, including b=0. All selected logical column bits are physically distinct after COPY identification. There are L/2 actual negative-unit rows in g targeted by that tile. The unselected groups, modes and packets below receive exponent zero.

Put k=floor(L/sqrt(u)) for large roots. In each chosen packet independently set the exponent of every one of the k modes of weights w=1,…,k to zero or one. There are 2^(Bk) different tuples. All have weighted sum at most Bk(k+1)/2=O(ML/(64²u)), absolute mode degree at most Bk, and in each chosen packet target at most k≤L/2 rows. Every selected mode has an actual source and target. These sums lie strictly inside the source's P_t=p+8ceil(3M/u)L+8M(a+1) and q_t=ceil(3M/u)u. They lie inside the outer-half uniform bounds p+6(L/2)h+(hu−1)(a+1) and q_t=hu as well, where h≈2M/u. They persist at any uniform precision bounded below by cML/u and length at least c'M for fixed c,c'>0, after decreasing k by a fixed factor if necessary. Including the kernel's extra base divisibility a+1 instead of 1 and an additional O(a) digit factor per marked mode under reflection normalization adds at most O(a Bk)=o(ML/u). At ζ=1 the Cauchy mode coefficients and moments are present, and the formal direct jet has the same mode indices. Locally there are enough distinct participating rows.

The quantitative-contraction.tex rule enumerates all nonnegative tuples within its weighted budget before contracting their children. No assertion of nonzero postcontraction summands is required to count these loop iterations. Adding the total-degree cutoff, removing unused tiles, or checking this row capacity does not remove the exhibited tuples. Thus the root acquisition on this family already incurs 2^Ω(M/sqrt(u)) loop steps before multiplication of child costs. At a true root M=Θ(s), u=Θ(log s), giving the source exp(Θ(s/sqrt(log s))) exponential scale for the exhaustive enumerator together with its published upper bound. The argument leaves open alternate scalar algorithms, genuine pruned contractions and adaptive per-order precision. In particular the private-unit origin count is elementary.
