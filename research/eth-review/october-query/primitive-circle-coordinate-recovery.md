# Bit-costed reconstruction from one approximate primitive embedding

Fix T*=1+2 zeta as in primitive-circle-collision-transfer.md, with the count-recovering prime q>max(3,2L,M/2+3), so B=(2^q+1)/3>2^(M/2). Write D=q−1, A=C0^M and beta=A F(T*) in Z[zeta]. Its conjugates have absolute values ≤U, an effective integer ≥2 with log U=O(M L log(2L)+M), by the Boolean-root Hilbert bound on |T|≤3. In the unique integral representation beta=sum_{i=0}^{D−1}a_i zeta^i all |a_i|≤2U: pad with coefficient a_(q−1)=0, use discrete Fourier inversion, and subtract the formula for a_(q−1) to cancel the unknown value at 1. Monic cyclotomic division ensures integrality.

Choose an integer S>2^(D+2)·20(D+2)U and a power of two K>100(D+2)S [S(D+U)]^D, taking the least integers/powers meeting these inequalities. Approximate beta and the first D powers of zeta to absolute error ≤1/K in the distinguished embedding. The required F accuracy is 1/(AK); roots of unity can be evaluated by certified trigonometric series in polynomial bit time. Form D+1 integral lattice basis vectors whose first D+1 entries are the unit vectors, followed by two rounded entries K times the real and imaginary parts of these approximations to zeta^0,...,zeta^(D−1),beta. The coefficient tuple (a_i,−1) gives a nonzero lattice vector of norm ≤10(D+2)U since the exact relation vanishes and rounding plus approximation error is ≤2 per real entry. Deterministic LLL therefore finds a nonzero lattice vector of norm <S in polynomial bit time by its usual first-vector approximation guarantee.

Write the unit coordinates of this short vector as c_i,t and gamma=sum c_i zeta^i+t beta. The two final coordinates bound |gamma|≤10(D+2)S/K. Every other conjugate has magnitude ≤S(D+U). If gamma were nonzero its nonzero integer norm would give |gamma|≥[S(D+U)]^(−(D−1)), a contradiction. Thus gamma=0 and t≠0 by rational independence of the first D powers. Recover a_i=−c_i/t exactly and use the modulo-B specialization to recover the integer count. All representation and LLL work is polynomial in M,L,q, with log(AK)=O(D(D+log U+log(D+2))+log A) sufficient precision. This is not a necessary-precision claim.

Approximating that one uniformly designated scalar to the specified polynomial number of bits can therefore recover the exact original 3CNF count under a polynomial-time reduction, for any efficiently constructed legal layout. Charge construction, the full scalar oracle and the recovery; direct scalar acquisition by COPY-word enumeration remains exponential. Unused original variables retain their external multiplicity. No hardness claim for constant error is made. Nor is a whole algebraic field element treated as one constant-time word.
