# Squared-node recovery of the normalized origin

Let α=2L, L=2^a. Use the normalized row kernel L W_d(T)/(T+L) with its canceled polynomial interpretation. The physical old layout yields a polynomial G with degree ≤2M(L−1), integral shifted jets, and the original origin G(0). For an exact even layout set P(T²)=G(T), target precision p. Otherwise form P(T²)=G(T)+G(−T) and target p+1, dividing only the final residue by two. The latter uses both physical signs at each retained pair. The local data for P at positive t are the sum of the supplied t coefficient and (−1)^j times the supplied −t coefficient of order j. Both formulas are coordinatewise in the unramified basis and the inherited nilpotent variable.

For i=v₂(t) and β_t=2tα, use X=(T²−t²)/β_t. At T=t+αz this is z+c z² with c=α/(2t) integral (its valuation is a−i). If g_j are the collected physical jets, recover the coefficients b_j in X by triangular substitution, subtracting from g_j the sum of b_l binom(l,j−l)c^(j−l) for ceil(j/2)≤l<j. There is no division by the small derivative 2t in this inversion. Instead β_t enters the origin weight, where it is priced.

Put h=ceil(3M/u). For a retained taper 1≤v≤u take n_t=h(v−i) if i<v and zero otherwise. Exactly N(v)=hL(v−1+2^(−v)) positive-square scalar jet coefficients are available. Select the least v with N(v)>M(L−1); v=u always qualifies by the source degree slack. This drops entire innermost node pairs when v<u. Analogous degree slack exists in ordinary T-grid interpolation. An evenness certificate additionally permits dropping the negative member of every remaining pair. A reflected layout on its own does not certify evenness.

Let E_t(A)=∏_{s>0,s≠t}((A−s²)/(t²−s²))^{n_s}, omitting zero multiplicities. The ordinary Hermite formula at A=0 uses b(X) divided as a truncated series by E_t(t²+β_t X), then evaluated at −t²/β_t and multiplied by E_t(0). Each local inverse factor is integral since v₂(β_t)≥v₂(t−s)+v₂(t+s). For unequal valuations the right side is twice the smaller one. For equal valuations one difference has valuation i+1 and the other ≤a. The valuation of E_t(0) is B_t=Σ n_s(2v₂(s)−v₂(t²−s²)). It equals the ordinary symmetric T-grid basis origin valuation plus n_t: its omitted −t partner had origin ratio of valuation −1 per copy. For order j the exact weight grade is B_t−j(a+2−i), improving the ordinary-grid grade by n_t−j≥1. Hence even the doubled-origin method fits the original precision. In detail, the source ordinary-grid valuation estimate ≥−6hL/2^i applies to the reduced taper as its slope is still h; n_t≤4M bounds ordinary jet evaluation loss by 4M(a+1). Exact enough input bits for every coefficient at node t are max(0,p+double−B_t+(n_t−1)(a+2−i)); omit the node if this is zero. Accumulate all terms with a common power-of-two denominator, then cancel it after summation.

The implementation uses polynomial-size scalar tables and modular operations in the numeric dimensions. It reports node, order, required precision and number of signs. This lowers specified data requests, not necessarily the number of field-point evaluations: the existing local interpolation algorithm may still use its complete degree-sized sample list to obtain a shorter jet. Deriving G from arbitrary old F jets is generally impossible without separate homogeneous-sector or row-kernel information. No improvement to the full native asymptotic clock is claimed.
