# A minimax clock for valuation-only tapering with the source mode enumerator

On the outer-half representative grid, consider *any* finite nonnegative integer multiplicities q_i depending only on valuation i, supported at 0≤i<u, with enough total multiplicity to interpolate D=M(2L−1) or M(2L−2). The multiplicities need not decrease with i. Suppose the evaluator uses the ordinary designated Hermite CRT functional on these data, reserving the necessary precision for each independent coefficient error (or a uniform center precision covering it), followed by the published exhaustive weighted mode enumeration. Some one retained center requires 2^Ω(M/sqrt(u)) enumeration iterations on the fully occupied half-separated private-unit root in [the crossing construction](outer-jet-crossing-factor-rank.md). The same holds for the grouped whole-jet version with its additional total mode-degree cutoff. This compares concrete acquisition schedules; it does not assume that arbitrary CNF scalar evaluators must perform these iterations.

Set q_i=0 also for i≥u. Since the outer grid has L points and D/L=Θ(M), Q=max_i q_i≥D/L=Ω(M). Define suffix maxima R_i=max_(j≥i) q_j with R_u=0. Consider the first segment of the decreasing R sequence that reaches a value below Q/2. Its drops sum to at least Q/2 over at most u steps. Hence some i<u in this segment has

    q_i=R_i≥Q/2,       q_i−R_(i+1)≥Q/(2u).

The equality q_i=R_i follows from the positive drop of the suffix maximum. This step works even for a nonmonotone taper.

At any outer t of this valuation, write D_t=L/2^i. In the customary CRT basis the coefficient of the highest ordinary input at t is E_t(0)(−t/α)^(q_i−1). Nonzero differences between distinct representatives have valuation below a. Pairing higher levels of v₂(b) with those of v₂(t−b) at equal cardinality gives the exact formula

    v₂(E_t(0)) = (D_t/2) Σ_(j=1)^(a−1−i) (j/2^j)(q_(i+j)−q_i).

The inactive residue representing zero has zero weight. Lower valuation nodes contribute nothing. At the chosen i every summand is nonpositive, and the first alone is at most −D_t Q/(8u). There is such a first term since a=2u at the root. The extra origin-evaluation exponent subtracts (q_i−1)(a+1−i) and cannot decrease the precision requirement. Therefore independent error accounting for that highest coordinate requires at least c M D_t/u bits, for an absolute c>0. A uniform center precision must also cover it. This is a statement about the designated functional; oversampling may enable algorithms using other relations.

Use the legal fully occupied high-bit-swapped twin construction, choosing first-copy packets in which the unordered symbols in coordinate i are {g,h} for two different fixed symbols. There are M/(64²L) such packets. Each offers 2^i source tiles of column symbol h, each of length D_t/2, and a disjoint corresponding row sub-tile in symbol g. For a tile of residue y_0 mod 2^(i+1), use corresponding row indices x≡y_0 mod 2^(i+1). Then v₂(t−y_0+x)=i. These rows are actual private negative-unit ports, and the tile columns are actual distinct Boolean logical bits with their mates in excluded twins. Thus there are M/(64² D_t) such tiles. Independently choose any subset of the first k=floor(εD_t/sqrt(u)) mode weights at each, where ε>0 is a sufficiently small absolute constant. All k modes have nonzero source forms and actual targets. Their degree per tile≤k leaves enough separate potential target rows, since k≤D_t/2. Total degree ≤ε M/(64²sqrt(u))<q_i; total weighted sum ≤O(ε² M D_t/(64²u)). It fits strictly below the preceding required center precision. Even charging the stronger per-mode base valuation a+1−i, or a further O(a) for reflection normalization, adds o(M D_t/u) because D_t≥2^u. These budgets apply to the ordinary source jet or sampled ring; at the integral lift ζ=1 the crossing mode coefficients on the designated row sub-tiles have their minimal per-mode valuation. The exhibited exponent tuples are therefore counted by the stated exhaustive rule, including the direct-jet degree and separate row capacity tests.

There are 2^Ω(M/sqrt(u)) tuples. At the active root M=Θ(s) and u=Θ(log s). This obstructs improving the published exponential scale using a different valuation-only taper, even choosing a nonmonotone one, while retaining this backend and its CRT-based precision accounting. Different scalar algorithms, nonuniform node choices, cancellations, nonlinear coefficient encodings and more economical acquisition of isolated high orders require other arguments. The earlier polynomial ambiguity construction gives an independent-error-free statement for the specific minimally rounded outer linear taper.
