# The final-query bound survives affine input recoding

For any test group, the preceding proof gives global period codimension |S|, independently of how its restricted costs are computed. After any common invertible affine change x=Ly+b, its period space becomes L^(-1)W. In particular its global codimension is still |S|. For any direct sum of input blocks F2^n=⊕U_h, the images of U_h span the full quotient F2^n/W; hence the sum of their dimensions is at least dim(F2^n/W). Thus the test-degree lower bound ≥|S| and the entire final-query packing obstruction c27557b0 persist under arbitrary global affine changes of original input coordinates, arbitrary block bases, and linear mixing of equation rows as specified there. The affine shift b cancels in the translation-invariance test. This does not cover adding nonlinear auxiliary variables or replacing grouped tuples by their zero indicator's stabilizer, which can be larger.
