# Exact one-node recovery for the physical Boolean root

At an initial Boolean tensor root with M real ports on each shore, packet length L=2^a, and external unused CNF variables factored out, set K(T)=C(T)/C′(0)=T(1+T/L) product_{j=1}^{L−1}(1−T²/j²). A real pair has W_xy(T)=K(T)/(T−(y−x)). On the circle |T−1|=3/2, |T|≤5/2 and |K(T)|≤(5/2)(1+5/(2L)) exp(25π²/24). For each integer offset δ, |T−δ|≥||δ−1|−3/2|. Within a packet each row sees distinct offsets, and the reciprocal bounds sum to O(log(2L)), also with dummy slots. Thus each row factor has absolute value ≤1+C log(2L) for an effective absolute C. There are ≤2^(2M) port assignments; all original truth predicates are 0/1. Hence |F(T)|≤B=2^(2M)(1+C log(2L))^M on that circle.

Writing f_j=[z^j]F(1+z), Cauchy's formula gives |f_j|≤B(2/3)^j. At T=0 the genuine root scalar is an integer. Its exact value is the nearest integer to sum_(j<J)(−1)^j f_j with J=ceil(log_(3/2)(12B)). The missing tail has magnitude ≤3B(2/3)^J≤1/4. Certified real absolute errors ≤1/(8J) per supplied coefficient still permit exact rounding. Taking the minimum with the full degree plus one is safe. Thus O(M log(2+log(2L))) ordinary coefficients at ONE node suffice, against original degree ≤M(2L−1). For differences use the sum of the two circle bounds. An odd origin difference implies not all its first J coefficients at 1 vanish. This rules out proposed scalar 7d68189a and its grid-zero multiple 3e3a6609 on comparable-wire Boolean roots, since each has an exact root at 1 of order Ω(ML/u) when L=2^(2u). It does not rule out other polynomials with high 2-adic valuation of nonzero low coefficients; far-root variants remain separate.

Real absolute error is not low-bit modular precision. For exact arithmetic put D=lcm(1,…,2L). The derivative ratio at 1 is −(L+1)/(L−1) for L≥2. In the ordinary expansion about 1 each matching W is this ratio times a product of terms 1+z/(1−q). Each nonmatching W omits also its offset δ and includes z/(1−δ). Each selected order-d term incurs at most d denominators dividing D. Consequently (L−1)^M D^j f_j is an integer, and the joint coefficient heights after scaling are O(M log L + J log D + log B) bits for j<J, log D=O(L log L). Odd primes coprime to these denominators with enough total bits permit CRT recovery. The source's shifted 2-adic coefficient equals (2L)^j f_j before reduction; absolute error does not follow from O(log J) low modular bits.

An exact direct evaluator enumerates physical column bits, enforces their original predicates, computes the deformed affine row forms as truncated rational polynomials, and substitutes them into the multilinear extensions of the old row predicates. Each original CNF/COPY vertex after bounded-width splitting has bounded degree. Accumulate in Q[z]/(z^J), using polynomial-bit coefficient arithmetic (alternatively CRT). This costs ≤2^M poly(input,M,L,J) bit time and polynomial space in the original bounded-width Boolean layout. Binary subdivision COPY2 on all column vertices reduces the enumeration to 2^(M/2). This establishes constructibility, not an improvement over the manuscript's general exp(O(s/sqrt(log s))) clock. The fast evaluation or accurate partial recovery of these low ordinary coefficients remains open. The Boolean root restriction excludes general inherited signed tensors and arbitrary-density sparse adapter tables with large weights. Fixed-width fixed-density original CNFs have M=O(n). No SAT decision conclusion follows from an exact vanishing at 1 alone.
