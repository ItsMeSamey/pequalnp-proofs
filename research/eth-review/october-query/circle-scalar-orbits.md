# Sample-independent certification of scalar orbit sums

At the physical Boolean root let h=M/2 be the logical word length after subdivision COPY2. For each logical word x define g_x(T) as the product of actual old multilinear 0/1 row-vertex predicates at W(T)X. This already sums all row bits Y. Thus F(T)=sum_x g_x(T), and g_x(0) is the Boolean acceptance on original incidence wires. This computes scalars, not the vector of all intermediate row arguments.

Swapping two logical coordinates u,v can affect only their ≤4 packets and hence at most 4L old row vertices. Mark every old row vertex incident with any row in those packets. Each of its ≤k'=max(k,3) ports reads at most L logical bits. Consequently the PRODUCT of all marked predicates depends on at most 4k'L² logical bits, plus u,v if necessary. Enumerate that scope and test whether that product agrees identically as a polynomial in T before and after swapping. Degree is ≤4k'L(2L−1), rational coefficient bit lengths polynomial since the unreduced common denominator C′(0)^(4k'L) suffices. Use exact rational polynomial arithmetic, not floating-point zero tests. Unmarked factors are unchanged. Thus the test certifies a joint scalar symmetry at every sample even when individual row effects differ. Remote forced zeros could yield further symmetries not found by this local test. Total pair-test time 2^O(k'L²) poly(input,M,L) and polynomial space is subleading in the native fixed-density regime L≤s^.001.

Connected components of the certified swaps generate full symmetric groups. For each such class C choose a fixed representative at Hamming weight j=0,…,|C|, contributing multiplier binom(|C|,j); take products across classes. For Q=product_C(1+|C|), computing F as an exact univariate rational polynomial by accumulating these Q weighted products requires poly(input,M,L)(Q+2^O(k'L²)) bit operations and polynomial space: total degree O(ML), all denominators divide C′(0)^M, and explicit physical kernel coefficients, products and partial sums have polynomial bit heights. This yields all O(M) circle samples (or F(0) directly) from the same scalar recurrence. One may also compute numeric samples with an extra h+O(1) guard bits on each word's absolute error to charge total multiplicity. If the logical-variable factor graph further decomposes, its component polynomials multiply and their costs add. This promises a genuine savings when Q and the test cost fit below the previous clock; it does not establish arbitrary SAT coverage or conflict with the vector-rank obstruction at 1+2i.

For an all-packing counterfamily take the connected cyclic clauses (¬x_i OR x_(i+1) OR x_(i+2)), n≥4. At origin the three logical wire occurrences of each original variable must agree at its trivalent old COPY. Every all-ones-except-i source word satisfies the formula. Swapping one physical occurrence of i with one of j≠i makes its valid word invalid. Thus even the full group of polynomial-in-T plain coordinate transpositions cannot merge different original variables. Even if symmetry is tested only jointly across the specified O(M) circle points, per-word quadrature rounding preserves g_x(0) because the global analytic bounds are uniform per x. Consequently Q≥4^n for every packing for this global profile recurrence, as subdividing a class only increases the product. This is not a lower bound on general scalar aggregation or on algorithms using component elimination.

For a uniform empirical scalar estimator, the independent shared-uniform-word quadrature obstruction b4db3bfb is complementary. Increasing the number of sample nodes by O(h) and precision by O(h) bits bounds each recovered q_x−g_x(0) in absolute value by 2^(−h−4). In the distinct-positive-units CNF exactly one of 2^h logical words is valid (h=n). Unless that word is sampled, the estimator 2^h mean(q_x) rounds to zero, so success on T independent words is ≤T/2^h. This bound permits cancellations among all the sampled complex values for each x and does not limit a formula-aware importance proposal.
