# Exact local projection covers on physical observation coordinates

Let z=a+Ax be the observed binary word on m physical coordinates, parametrized by d independent raw affine variables; put C=im A and r=rank A. The remaining signed CNF F depends only on z. All observations have exactly 2^(d−r) preimages; any uniquely defined upstream auxiliary bits are restored from a preimage. Test an inconsistent original affine subsystem before this parametrization.

For a supplied family of subsets B of the coordinates, compute a basis of the annihilator of each projected direction π_B C by elimination. Extend its rows by zeros to length m and stack all rows into H. Each lifted row lies in C⊥. Therefore the intersection of all local constraints z_B∈a_B+π_B C equals a+C exactly iff rank H=m−r. The offsets are essential. Empty families, unconstrained singletons and full local images need no special treatment. The test is polynomial in the input and explicit list lengths. For several candidate covers charge their generation and tests; this does not synthesize an optimal cover.

Realize each retained block by one Boolean membership tensor with |B| ports and polynomial entry time via the computed local checks. Realize each residual clause by its original Boolean tensor; tie all occurrences of the same observed bit through a tree of bounded-degree copy tensors. Supply a free unary end if an observed bit occurs just once; a wholly unused bit contributes two. Each consistent z has exactly one extension through these copies. Subdividing edges with equality makes the graph bipartite when required. The total wires after these changes are O(m+Σ_B |B|+occ(F)); degree is O(max(3,max |B|,max clause width)). Isolated scalars and empty clauses can be handled directly. Pick source scale s at least a suitable constant times the wire count, (maximum degree)^(1/0.9), and r+1, including any vertex/description/entry bounds of the contraction theorem. Use modulus 2^(r+1) and truncation length one: the observed model count is at most 2^r, so recover it as an ordinary integer and multiply by 2^(d−r). The October theorem gives polynomial space and poly(input)·2^O(s/sqrt(log s)) time for the explicitly constructed networks at admissible s. Recover a witness by pinning one observed bit per step, reusing the same cover and adding at most m unary pins, and solve Ax=z−a for an original preimage. Empty inputs and zero counts are returned directly. An improvement at rank scale requires measuring total wires and degree, not just block cardinality or rank.

For a rectangular product take binary codes C1,C2 of respective lengths n1,n2, positive dimensions k1,k2 and full-row-rank generators G1,G2. Its arrays are G1ᵀ M G2 for unrestricted k1×k2 binary M. Column membership in C1 implies a representation G1ᵀ Y; a left inverse expresses the rows of Y as linear combinations of array rows, so row membership in C2 gives Y=M G2. The converse is immediate and M is unique. The row and column blocks therefore pass the rank test. Their total incidence is 2n1 n2. A nonzero array has a nonzero column with at least d1 nonzero entries, hence at least d1 nonzero rows of weight ≥d2; a simple tensor of minimum words attains d1 d2. For any <min(d1⊥,d2⊥) chosen cells, the involved columns of G1 and separately of G2 are independent (dual distance criterion). Their pairwise tensor products are independent by left inverses, hence so are the chosen observation columns. Thus global dual distance is at least min(d1⊥,d2⊥); placing a component dual word in one array column or row attains the minimum when finite.

Take balanced length L binary component codes with dimensions about L/2, both primal and dual distances Ω(L). Existence follows for uniform half-dimensional subspaces and their uniform duals by the union bound over nonzero words of weight ≤δL for H₂(δ)<1/2. Supplied matrices are sufficient; no efficient deterministic generator or distance certification is claimed. Then m=L², r=Θ(m), global primal distance Ω(m), and no observed fixed-arity check alone supplies even one nonzero dual equation for large L. Nevertheless the row/column tensors have L ports, two incidences per observed bit, and accept genuine signed residuals of bounded width and O(m) total occurrences in linear wire scale s=Θ(m). The row/column layout is verified on the raw affine image, and all local entries use ordinary binary elimination.

For the archived concatenated Reed–Solomon family (rs-two-stage-tensor.md), when every proposed observed block touches at most K distinct outer sites, interpolation makes their outer symbols freely independent. Hence all local constraints are consequences of the single-site inner images; their intersection contains the entire product of those images, strictly larger than the code since K<Q. This holds even with arbitrarily many overlapping such blocks. It limits observed-only projection covers under that site bound; the two-stage tensor network with hidden field intermediates has no such restriction. The product construction above is likewise only an observed-coordinate example and gives no general impossibility for extensions.
