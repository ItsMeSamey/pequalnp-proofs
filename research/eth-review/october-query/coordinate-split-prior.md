# Prior work on exact clause-signature histograms

The number t of realized violation words equals the global ps-value of the CNF, because complementing every bit gives its exact satisfied-clause set. Sæther, Telle, and Vatshelle's Theorem 3 takes a branch decomposition and uses the maximum ps-value of its induced crossing formulas. It yields O(k³s(m+n)) time for #SAT at decomposition width k and input size s. That theorem's input and parameter differ from the full-formula signature histogram. [Sæther–Telle–Vatshelle](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6485)

An earlier general recovery theorem already gives the unrestricted-order total-time result. Take A_j to be the set of assignments satisfying clause j. The weighted coverage function f(J)=|⋃_{j∈J}A_j| is 2^n minus the number of assignments violating all clauses in J. The latter is zero when the falsifying pins conflict and otherwise a power of two determined by the number of distinct pinned variables. Collapse assignments into positive-weight atoms by identical satisfied signatures, giving at most t atoms. Chakrabarty and Huang reconstruct those atoms and their weights with O(mt) coverage-value queries and polynomial total work. The invisible empty atom has mass 2^n−f([m]); the full atom gives #SAT (with the empty clause list handled directly). [Chakrabarty–Huang, section 3](https://doi.org/10.1137/140964072)

For the CNF-specific incremental distinction, an elementary exact peeling algorithm suffices. After outputting a set of violation masks with their multiplicities, subtract their contributions from every conjunction query. A positive residual means some unseen mask contains the query set. Starting from the empty mask, add a bit whenever this preserves positive residual. After one pass the resulting mask is maximal among the remaining masks, and its residual is exactly its multiplicity. The next output costs polynomial time in the input size and the number of previous outputs. This allows arbitrary clause width and may make high-order queries. Bérczi et al. gave incremental-polynomial listing at fixed clause width, and polynomial delay on classes closed under their tractability assumption. Creignou et al. explicitly called general-width incremental listing open in 2025. Recovery of the unrestricted histogram already follows from the coverage theorem; priority for the simple CNF observation or its incremental refinement is unverified. [Bérczi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.06727) · [Creignou et al., conclusions](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WADS.2025.19)

The interpolation degree on t known points is at most their VC dimension, hence at most ⌊log₂t⌋; this is Theorem 7 of Moran and Rashtchian. Exact sparse Boolean Möbius transforms also have an independent learning literature. Wigderson and Yehudayoff study small windows under noisy sampling; Ban et al. study deletion-channel traces and aggregate subsequence decks. Neither observation model supplies the exact count on a queried, indexed conjunction of violated clauses. [Moran–Rashtchian](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2016.70) · [Wendler et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.00439) · [Erginbas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06246) · [Wigderson–Yehudayoff](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/PUBLICATIONS/WigdersonYe_ML2016.pdf) · [Ban et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05532)

Within this workspace, [the prefix trace histogram](../direct-sat-algebra/trace-histogram.md) already uses adaptive support bases and exact child multiplicities, and [learned inclusion–exclusion](../pnp-general-separation/learned-inclusion-exclusion.md) builds independent intersection bases using exact moments. [Indexed clause violations](/workspace/pnp-research/research/violation-moments/indexed.md) treats identifiability under bounded clause width. The [coordinate-splitting result](coordinate-split-histogram.md) can be presented as an explicit low-order exact-query construction and composition of these ingredients. The literature check does not establish priority for that refined query bound.
