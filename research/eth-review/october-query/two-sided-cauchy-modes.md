# Two-sided separated native crossing modes

At a true active node let K=W_(x,y0)(T), A=2^(i+1)/(T−y0+x), with T=t+2Lζ, and j the nonnegative physical tile index. The crossing weight is K/(1−Aj). Both K and A are even in the integral ring, including at ζ=0 when K=0. Define R_k=K/(1−Aj) times the product over 0≤r<k of (A−2r)(j−r)/((1−Ar)(1−2rj)). Then R_0 is the true weight and R_(j+1)=0. Subtract consecutive remainders using (1−Ak)(1−2kj)−(A−2k)(j−k)=(1−Aj)(1−2k²). The resulting term factors into a function of the target row times a function of the column. All displayed denominators are units.

Write ν=k−popcount(k). Multiply the target coefficient K(1−2k²)∏_{r<k}(A−2r)/∏_{r≤k}(1−Ar) by 2^ν. On the column use binom(j,k)·(k!/2^ν)/∏_{r≤k}(1−2rj) for j≥k, zero otherwise. The cancellation is an ordinary integer identity before modular reduction. The resulting native target coefficient has valuation ≥1+k+2ν. To see the extra ν, put d=t−y0+x, v=v₂(d)≤i. Then A/2=2^i/(d+2Lζ) agrees with the ordinary 2-adic integer 2^i/d modulo 2^(a+1−i), since their difference has valuation at least i+a+1−2v≥a+1−i. For every power 2^b≤k≤L/2^(i+1), at least floor(k/2^b) among the k consecutive indices r=0,…,k−1 have A/2−r divisible by 2^b. Summing these counts gives v₂(∏(A−2r))≥k+ν even for a non-prime-field ζ. The separately transferred source factorial supplies another ν. This bound needs the native near-integer property and tile length; arbitrary even ring elements do not suffice. The source moment sums the corresponding column coefficients times their true Boolean reads, retaining physical COPY constraints. Use the new weights when bounding omitted target monomials; retain the old local weights W for within-group ports. Each crossing tile needs no index k above its last real j, regardless of precision. Old geometric multiplicity and partition upper bounds remain valid with the stronger weights. Exact child substitution uses the same product-of-affine-factors coefficient extraction and the source moments raised to the ordinary tuple powers. The old birth entries stay at their caps. A different basis produces different individual child tensors/scalars, not necessarily the same individual child outputs; their contracted full sum at the requested precision agrees.

The adapter constructs the true native cardinal and ratio using their factored shifted products. It computes C′(t)/C′(0) as an odd-denominator rational before embedding it, multiplies shifted unit factors 1+(2L/(t−s))ζ, and handles the diagonal separately. It inverts only units using the supplied birth-ring arithmetic, including the unramified and V lanes. It does not divide by ζ or reconstruct missing higher bits. Integer factorials have O(L log(L+1)) bits; the loop never needs k≥L, scalar precision is p, and all native coefficient setup and per-packet mode work are polynomial in L,p and the actual ring arithmetic cost. Generic ring callbacks must be exact at the supplied precision. No additional field interpolation, universal full-clock improvement, or per-child count reduction on every layout is assumed.
