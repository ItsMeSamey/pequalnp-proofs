# Normalized high-order bands for every grid center on the signed Boolean root

The digit, hafnian and fixed-excess arguments of extreme-center-fixed-leading-digits.md and extreme-center-fixed-order-band.md work at every t in the actual grid [−L,L−1] at the full-scale T=t+2LZ, without an assumption on the number of negative occurrences. Let B consist of all distinct logical COPY2 bits read by a real physical successor at slot displacement t, b=|B|, f=M/2−b, and r be the number of positive clauses with no real successor row. In every legal packing b≤min(M/2,3M(L−|t|)/L). The proof uses the actual smaller b if available, including b=0. The number r may be zero or grow with M.

At every center c_t=C′(t)/C′(0) is a unit in the 2-adic integers; as a rational number it need not be an ordinary integer. After fixing B, all degree-zero row inputs become constants. Every degree-j≥1 native row coefficient at the 2L scale is divisible by 2^j, since the cardinal polynomial is a product of constant-plus-even-slope factors or has the corresponding mandatory even-slope factor. Thus the entire scaled gate series after dividing its order j by 2^j is integral. Every dormant positive clause has zero constant and first divided coefficient an affine form in outside bits. For a row of such a clause its derivative divided by two has odd coefficient precisely at the physical offset d≠t with |t−d|=L, if such a real read exists. This is d=t−L for t>0, d=t+L for t<0, and no physical offset at t=0. The range of the physical offsets and the grid excludes a further multiple of L. Repeated source reads within a clause add modulo two as before.

For every j, v₂([Z^j]F)≥max(j,f), and the coefficients at j<r vanish exactly. Compute [Z^j]F/2^max(j,f) modulo 2^k for all r≤j≤r+s by the cited fixed-order band algorithm, with unchanged Gaussian and hafnian subcalls, exact physical gate constants, and odd-denominator arithmetic. Its uniform clock is 2^b N^{O(1+s+k²)} with N as in extreme-center-growing-band-clock.md, and the streamed variant has polynomial working space. No real sign or positivity property is used. The separate noncancellation theorem for all-positive and odd-negative clauses at t=±(L−1) does require those extreme real displacements. To report native digits clip to j<h(u−v₂(t)) and P_t>max(j,f), taking at most P_t−max(j,f) digits. Only nonzero t with v₂(t)<u are mandatory centers. At t=0 all identical physical slots match, so b=M/2 in a nonempty root, and this bound permits ordinary exponential cost. At t=−L there are no successors and the scale remains 2L, distinct from the alternative endpoint coordinate of scale L.

For instance simultaneously consider all active t with L−|t|≤L/log M. There are O(L) in this range and all have b≤3M/log M. For s=floor(M/log² M) and k≤sqrt(M)/log M this gives 2^{O(M/log M)} time on the customary polynomial-size root descriptions. When native root L grows as a positive power of M this includes a growing set of distinct active odd centers. The computation obtains only the specified band and normalized digits at each center; full native interpolation still requires its other coefficients, digits and centers.
