# Independent packet XOR masks average whole assignment kernels

Fix one legal original Boolean layout with B packets of length L=2^a and the call's actual birth-precision lifted entries. A mask g in each packet permutes BOTH slot endpoints by i→i XOR g, reversing its coordinate orientation conventions and moving dummies with slots. This preserves the source packing conditions. Describe each real port by its original physical name. For its packet's real slots R the assignment kernel at mask g is

    K_g(Y_R,X_R;T) = product over i in R of l_{Y_i}(sum over j in R of W_{(j XOR g)−(i XOR g)}(T) X_j).

Here W_d is the source cardinal polynomial and l_0(u)=1−u, l_1(u)=u. Sum over all L^B independent tuples of masks. The resulting single polynomial S(T) is the original A(Y)B(X) assignment contraction with the factor Σ_g K_g in each packet; finite distributivity proves the identity even when A,B globally couple packets. Mask sums are taken on the whole product of a packet's rows, using the same mask on every one of its rows. Replacing individual W entries or row factors by independent averages changes the polynomial. Each K_g(0) is the Boolean equality kernel. Therefore S(0)=2^(aB) times the original lifted contraction. For target precision p, obtain the unnormalized S(0) modulo 2^(p+aB) before coordinatewise exact shift by aB bits. Ordinary source sample/derivative precision losses, if Hermite recovery is used, are additional to p+aB. Keep the same specified lifts for all layouts; high-precision arithmetic on them does not mean recanonicalizing a sum of entries. Source definitions and precision: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex .

On a full packet, slot translations act by permutations P_g of the 2^L FULL bit assignments. The mask kernels are conjugate under this representation, and their sum commutes with every P_g. Over the characteristic-zero coefficient field its off-diagonal blocks between different G-characters vanish. Eχ=(1/L)Σ_g χ(g)P_g gives S_packet=L Σχ Eχ K_0 Eχ. Projector denominators must be accounted for in any dyadic finite-precision calculation; the direct unnormalized sum avoids these extra inversions. The invariant sector alone has dimension at least 2^L/L, one orbit sum per orbit. The representation is on configurations, not merely on individual slot indices. With dummies, recover the partial packet kernel from the full one by zeroing dummy inputs and summing dummy output bits, since l_0+l_1=1. Do this in the original physical coordinates; the fixed real-slot subspace need not itself be translation invariant.

For one concrete physical assignment per packet a summed kernel is computable by L calls to the local formula, each polynomial in L, working precision, V-length, algebraic field degree and requested ordinary jet index. Full local tabulation costs up to 4^L times this. Without structural contraction of the resulting packet factors, a full assignment sweep still has 4^M terms for M wires. Alternatively evaluating all original whole-layout contractions costs L^B calls per requested sample. Every sample used for reconstruction evaluates the same S (and its actual coefficients). Summing at a common center commutes with coefficient extraction, but interleaving selected jets from different layout polynomials does not evaluate S. No inheritance of a low-arity packet-factor contraction bound, gain in total clock, or reduction in required interpolation order follows solely from the character decomposition.

For a physical pair with XOR difference 2^i, every mask yields ordinary displacement +2^i or −2^i. Thus averaging the pair never moves that displacement to the outer half when 2^i<L/2. More strongly, append m disjoint positive unit clauses to the core (x),(not y),(x or y), or instead to (x),(not y),(not x or y). These have identical physical topology and respectively one and zero satisfying assignments. This is an original fully used signed-CNF root with M=2(m+4) wires after subdivision. At a nonzero ordinary center t each mask has at most B(L−|t|) real rows with a predecessor. Each of the m positive unit rows multiplies the CGF integrand by its own read, with zero constant whenever that row has no predecessor. Hence BOTH sums have a zero at t of multiplicity at least max(0,m−B(L−|t|)), before dyadic reduction. With the near-full source root template and |t|≥(1/2+ε)L this is Ω_ε(M). Fixed or o(M) order jets restricted to these outermost centers alone agree while the normalized origins differ. This is a sample-only obstruction, not a lower bound for methods inspecting the original clauses or using inner centers.

On m disjoint positive units alone the COPY1 row factors sum identically to one, leaving m OR1 reads multiplied together. Each W_d has degree 2L−1 and exactly the same leading coefficient 1/C'(0). The top coefficient of the scalar for ANY mask tuple is therefore C'(0)^(−m) times the sum over Boolean COPY choices of the product, over OR1 rows, of the sums of real COPY bits in their respective packets. The all-ones COPY assignment gives a positive term and all such products are nonnegative. The expression is independent of masks. Thus the normalized average retains the exact characteristic-zero degree m(2L−1) of every layout on this family. Modular survival of the top coefficient is not needed or asserted.
