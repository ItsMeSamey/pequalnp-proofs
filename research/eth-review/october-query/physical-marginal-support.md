# Generic support of actual Boolean-root word marginals

Fix the original old-row/COPY2-column orientation and any legal packing. For each all-positive clause C let S_C contain every COPY2 coordinate with a column slot in a packet containing a row port of C. After summing row states the physical per-word polynomial f_x(T) is identically nonzero exactly when x satisfies every disjunction OR(S_C). For any fixed partial COPY word the complete marginal sum over other bits is identically nonzero exactly when filling the other bits by 1 satisfies those same disjunctions. Whenever nonzero its exact order at −L is r, the total number of all-positive clauses. This is a statement about full partial marginals; arbitrary intermediate factors in a bucket execution need not be full marginals.

Every native real-packet entry W_xy has a simple zero at −L and all their first derivatives have one strict sign. Sufficiently near this endpoint on the side making the entries positive, every row sum lies in [0,1) and is positive exactly when its packet has an active COPY coordinate. Each all-positive clause factor 1−∏(1−row sum) is positive iff at least one incident row is active, with first derivative then the common-sign sum of the incident derivatives. If no incident row is active the clause factor vanishes identically. Every other nonempty clause and each old COPY factor is one at the endpoint and positive nearby. Thus the first coefficient of order r has the same strict sign for every passing word, so no cancellation can remove it in a marginal. The all-ones-completion criterion follows by monotonicity. Scanning actual packet incidences and COPY identifications constructs the required rows in polynomial time.

The physical generic support can be very simple while the exact origin problem still represents arbitrary SAT. From any normalized 3CNF add a fresh g and the sole positive unit (g). Replace each ternary (a OR b OR c) with a private z and (not z OR g), (not z OR not a), (not g OR a OR z), (not z OR b OR c). These force z=g AND not a; for g true the last row restores the original ternary. Guard shorter clauses by not g, including the empty clause. All clauses except (g) have a negative literal. Each original model extends uniquely, and unused original variables keep their free factor. Thus every physical packing has just one packet-hit disjunction as its full generic support, while exact origin SAT remains NP-complete and exact counts are parsimonious. The first endpoint coefficient is also cheap in this family: for fixed x it is the unit row’s first derivative, linear in x, and each appearing bit is 1 in exactly half the COPY words. Computing any one partial-support query or this leading scalar therefore does not solve origin SAT. This does not apply to a decoder supplied with complete scalar samples.
