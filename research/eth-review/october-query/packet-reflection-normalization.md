# Reflection and same-grid contraction for the normalized packet kernel

Let S={−L,…,L−1}, L=2^a, α=2L, and q=y−x be a displacement between real slots. In the quantitative-contraction.tex D.1 call keep the source's actual integral entrywise canonical lifts, coefficient field and V truncation. Reversing both slot orders x→L−1−x, y→L−1−y, including holes, sends q to −q while preserving each physical port's incidence. This defines a comparison layout P*; a prescribed constructive packing need not select it. Since C(−T)=(T−L)C(T)/(T+L), W_{−q}(−T)=(L−T)W_q(T)/(L+T). Upon marking all linear row forms by s, the s^k sector of the mirrored scalar at −T equals the corresponding old sector at T multiplied by ((L−T)/(L+T))^k. Each such sector contains k kernel factors, all divisible by T+L over the rational coefficient field. Mixing different k in l_0=1−u generally destroys a single scalar transformation. In a legal L=2 full single packet with one positive-unit clause in slot zero and a free old COPY1, the unnormalized F=W_0+W_1=−(T+2)(T+1)(2T−1)/2 has derivative −1/2 at zero.

Set W̃_q(T)=L W_q(T)/(T+L). It is a polynomial of degree 2L−2 because q≠−L, with W̃(0)=I. It has the exact symmetry W̃_{−q}(−T)=W̃_q(T). Replace W by W̃ throughout the row l-factors to define G. For arbitrary signed integral tensors G_P(0)=F_P(0) and G_{P*}(−T)=G_P(T); no homogeneous-sector list is needed. A factor per row kernel does not mean an unconditional factor multiplying the scalar.

**Original grid.** At t≠−L, H_t(z)=L/(L+t+αz) is integral as a power series because v₂(L+t)≤a and α/(L+t) is even. Multiplying W(t+αz) by this series gives the shifted normalized polynomial with all coefficients integral. For t=−L use the exact endpoint product η(−L/k)∏_{b≠k}(1−Lw/b) from inactive-endpoint-single-jet.md, k=L+q, η=(−1)^L binom(2L−1,L), w=(T+L)/L; this is W̃ after the mandatory w cancels. Substituting w=2z proves integrality at the formerly singular center, where W̃ need not vanish. Consequently G satisfies all hypotheses of the existing tapered Hermite lemma: degree ≤M(2L−2)≤M(2L−1), integrality at the origin, and all shifted jets integral. The same original T-grid, q_t, p_t, lifts of residue-field sample points, and ordinary Hermite assembly recover G(0) at p. Computing G needs no mirrored evaluation, squared coordinate or division by two. Its off-origin values differ from the source F in general.

At each active t of valuation i<a and binary-coordinate residue lift ζ, H_t(ζ) has valuation a−i, independent of ζ; compute by stripping 2^i then inverting a unit. Every local coefficient and crossing-mode coefficient in a row form is the corresponding source coefficient times H_t(ζ). No division of a lifted tensor entry is involved. Tile moments, mode labels, geometry, truncated expansions and multiplicities stay the same. Source divisibility 2^(b+1) for the mode of order b still holds, even with the extra a−i powers. The remainder is scaled by an integral multiplier. Thus at this call the published mode-weight enumeration, tensor-entry construction, working precision, child descriptions and bounds on their evaluation costs still apply with polynomial modular multiplication overhead. Existing child contraction can be called unmodified on the resulting tensors, using their actual birth descriptions at their prescribed caps. Equivalently replace the kernel call by call with the same bounds. This preserves the published asymptotic upper clock under its hypotheses; it does not literally reproduce its off-origin scalars or justify discarding their published precision. Nonzero signed entries, arbitrary canonical lifts and the inactive −L require no special symmetry of the tensors.

A separate evenization E(T)=(G_P(T)+G_{P*}(T))/2 has E(0)=F(0) and degree ≤M(L−1) in U=T². In general both layout evaluations are needed for such an average and division by two requires an extra bit before reducing; integrality on the old grid at the desired precision for reconstructed terms must also be justified, not inferred from origin integrality. If reflection is a certified weight-preserving port automorphism of the original layout, G itself is even.
