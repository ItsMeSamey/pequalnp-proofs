The quotient V^ell=0 therefore bounds the degree of the full product by K=k(a+2(ell−1)), for a remaining tests. Apply the Boolean-ring map sending incidence (i,v) to x_(g,v), where g contains i, and imposing x_(g,v)^2=x_(g,v). A monomial maps to its union of indices within each group, with its coefficient unchanged until like terms add. Total degree can only decrease. This reasoning uses the bound on the full truncated product; individual group factors may have contributions whose products vanish by V-order.

There is no second enumeration of pre-projection incidence sizes. Write S_g for the disjoint union across h. Its test tensor entry is

    sum_{T⊆S_g} (−1)^(|S_g|−|T|) product_{i in g} B_i(1_{T∩I_i},V).

This is the Boolean Möbius coefficient of the group product. In particular it already sums all contributing incidence preimages with their coefficients. The variable-group entry is 2^(|X_h|−|union_g S_gh|) modulo 2^p. This retains unused free variables. The result is the original branch residue.
