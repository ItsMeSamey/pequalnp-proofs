# Identity observations on signed 3-CNF

For any ordinary signed 3-CNF F with n explicitly declared variables, an identity representation can retain all n physical coordinates, including unused ones, and pass F directly to a decision backend. Its observation image is the full cube and has zero dual. A graphic representation can use a star rooted at its center, with free leaf potentials equal to the n coordinates. One baseline decision call suffices; on a positive instance at most n further pinned calls recover a witness. This benchmark bypasses parity extraction.

The automatic collective_sparse_dual.py compiler first normalizes clauses and extracts every full signed parity bundle it recognizes, including units. For its identity case assume the normalized formula has no empty clause and no such bundle. Let m be its number of distinct normalized clauses and u the number of variables occurring in them. With nonnegative parameters and clause_cap≥m, the compiler returns the same normalized clauses up to variable renumbering and literal/row order, rank u, zero parity probes and fiber multiplier 2^(n−u). The normalized count on all n declared coordinates is therefore recovered, and a satisfying observed assignment has a unique raw lift exactly when u=n. Raw duplicate rows, repeated literals, and tautologies can change the physical clause count under normalization. The compiler can give different results on formulas with recognized bundles or insufficient caps. The identity benchmarks give no all-input decrease in dimension or signed 3-CNF exponent. Quantitative asymptotic source bounds apply in their stated large-size range; bounded sizes can be enumerated directly.

## Fixed-order optimum for the boundary engine charge

For the iterative engine boundary_scalar_iter.py, fix the physical clause order and the actual substituted normals. Write U(I) for the span of normals in an interval and B(I)=U(I)∩U(complement I) for its boundary. The complement includes every physical leaf outside I. Put b(I)=dim B(I). The leaf score for clause i is 2^dim U(i) times (physical arity(i)+b(i)+2). For any contiguous split I=L followed by R, the internal score is
```
2^dim(B(L)+B(R)) * (b(L)+b(R)+b(I)+4).
```
The minimum total charged work over order-preserving full binary trees is obtained by assigning the leaf scores and taking the minimum over split positions of the two child optima plus this internal score. Induction applies because boundaries depend only on physical leaf sets and the internal score is determined by the split. Save minimizing positions to reconstruct a tree. This matches the engine's pay accumulator on a completed nonempty run after its equation consistency test; with an empty gate list the special empty leaf has score 2. This score counts its configured loop allowances. It excludes parsing, elimination, planning, Gaussian basis preparation, Python dictionary overhead, integer bit costs and witness reconstruction. Budget abort on a partial run has a different reported charge. Computing all interval and exterior spans and all split scores uses O(m³) splits and polynomial linear algebra per split; integer scores need O(n+log(m+n+2)) bits. For identity observations b(I) counts variables occurring both inside and outside I. The DP optimizes this particular work score within the given order. It supplies no minimum over arbitrary leaf orders or peak-space objective.
