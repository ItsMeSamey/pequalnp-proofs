# Whole ordered score stream with no padding variables

For every sufficiently large even v write l=log₂v and choose even d=2 floor(l(log₂ l)²/64). Take a simple d-regular graph on v labeled vertices, for example the circular distances 1,…,d/2. Number its m=vd/2 edges from 0 to m−1. Use v base bits x, one private bit z_i per edge, and one guard g, so n=1+v+m. Write λ=ceil(log₂ n) and ν=ceil(log₂ λ). Put D=12ν. For edge i=ab include the real clause (x_a∨x_b∨z_i) and the D filler clauses (g∨x_b'∨z_i), where b'=(iD+j) mod v for 0≤j<D. These are distinct for large v. All variables occur; there are no input units or isolated variables. Each gate occurs D+1 times, each base appears in either floor(mD/v) or ceil(mD/v) fillers, and m(D+1)>nν. The source takes the dense driver. All clauses are positive distinct triples. If desired take the circular vertex order interleaving the columns of a prism; its edges are a subgraph of the base graph, not its entire real edge set.

For width three the manuscript chooses σ=1/ceil(√λ) and threshold M satisfying Γ ceil(log₂(4CM))≤σ M. Since Γ≥1 and C≥1 this yields M≥√λ and then M≥(√λ log₂ λ)/2. Thus D+1<M and d+M<M² eventually. Every width-three clause during sparsification is an original triple: insertions always have lower width. At width three the only possible eligible pair is {g,x_b}. Each pair containing z_i has at most D+1 occurrences, and each pair of base bits belongs to at most one edge triple. A choice of {g,x_b} removes every remaining ternary clause containing it, so there are at most v such choices on a path. At a width-three singleton step all ternary pairs are ineligible, by the source priority for largest heart at the chosen width. Consequently each base bit occurs in at most d+M−1 surviving triples, and no z_i or base singleton meets the threshold M². Only g can be a width-three singleton heart; once chosen it removes all remaining guard triples. At that moment fewer than vM guard triples remain, since each contains exactly one pair {g,x_b}.

Initially no binary clauses exist. Total binary insertions along a path are at most v from all ternary pair-A steps and fewer than vM from the one possible ternary singleton-g B step. Ternary pair-B inserts only units, and branching on binary clauses creates only units. Antichain normalization can delete additional clauses. Each binary branching step consumes ≥M distinct binary clauses, so their number is at most v+v/M. The actual tree has depth O(v), at most 2^{O(v)} nodes, and polynomially many stored literals per node. These bounds allow all tie choices, ancillary negative units, and the prescribed absence of unit propagation.

Every descendant clause contains at most one gate variable: an inserted positive heart or petal is a subset of a preceding clause and a negative unit is unary. For a genuine leaf and candidate matrix C on its ordered violation coordinates, fix all v base spins and g. Each violation then becomes a constant or affine form in one gate, even for signed units. Solve the composed affine system in all m gates; inconsistency contributes zero and rank r consistency contributes 2^(m−r). Summing over 2^(v+1) assignments yields the exact score, preserving all unused gates and all intermediate unit violations. Emit every ordered physical trial, retain the prescribed tie winner, run all 3n rounds and sum the final scores of the disjoint leaves. Trivial terminal branches use their source shortcuts.

The source has B,P=polylog(n); even its original exhaustive Tanner setup and all (2m_leaf)^P lists per round cost 2^polylog(n) times a polynomial, per leaf before scoring. Explicit polynomials in n bound the active description, Gaussian elimination, candidate generation, tree traversal storage and output integer lengths. Depth-first streaming uses polynomial working space. Since λ=Θ(log v), v=Θ(n/[λ(log λ)²]), the total deterministic bit time is 2^{O(n/[λ(log λ)²])}, including every emitted physical score, both children wherever they occur, actual setup and restoration. This family has a small affine variable backdoor exposed directly by its construction; a recognizer from unlabelled variables may use the unique high-occurrence guard, gate degree D+1, and remaining base variables, then verify all roles, simple d-regularity, participation and coverage bounds. Balance and the displayed modular assignment suffice for construction; the proof needs only the verified degree and clause properties. This time guarantee does not extend to arbitrary unstructured CNFs; a cubic true prism with one gate per edge has v=Θ(n).
