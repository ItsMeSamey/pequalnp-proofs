# First possible 2-adic digit at the dormant-clause order

At either active extreme center t=±(L−1) of the original unweighted signed-CNF/COPY root use the notation of extreme-center-exact-positive-order.md. Let r count geometrically dormant positive clauses. This note permits arbitrary numbers of negative literals in every other clause. Let B be the set of distinct COPY2 bits read by any real baseline successor. Write b=|B|≤3M/L, e=M/2 for the total number of COPY2 bits, and f=e−b. Let Q be the coefficient of Z^r in the true physical F(t+2LZ). Then

    Q is divisible by 2^max(r,f).

The residue of Q divided by that power, modulo two, can be computed in time poly(input,M,L)·2^b and polynomial space. This works even when r grows linearly. At integer precision P greater than max(r,f) this is an actual bit of the required coefficient. If the calculated bit is zero, one more power of two divides Q. Separately restored unused-variable factors retain their own multiplicity.

At a fixed physical COPY word X the r dormant linear coefficients contribute 2^r times a product of r 2-adically integral linear forms H_C(X). Each coefficient of H_C is a sum over real reads of L c_t/(t−d). The signs of those rational coefficients do not matter modulo two. Because 1≤|t−d|≤2L−2 for a dormant row, this is odd exactly at d=−1 for t=L−1 and d=1 for t=1−L. Thus the binary form for a clause is the XOR of its real reads one slot in the indicated direction over all its rows. Missing reads contribute nothing, and multiple reads of the same physical bit cancel in pairs. All remaining constant factors multiply to a value g(X_B). Its reduction modulo two is just the Boolean evaluation of the remaining original gates under the partial successor inputs, because c_t is odd and each input is zero or c_t X_s. This includes every COPY gate and every other signed clause. The exact leading candidate Q is obtained by summing 2^r g times the product of the r forms over all X. Even if Q vanishes, this formula holds.

First take f≥r and fix X_B. The product of the r forms is a multilinear polynomial on the f remaining Boolean bits after using X²=X, of degree at most r. Summing the monomial on a set of size d over the outside cube gives 2^(f−d). Consequently dividing Q by 2^f is integral, and modulo two only degree-r monomials survive. Their coefficients do not use any constant from the pinned B bits. Let A be the r-by-f binary matrix of the outside coefficients of the r wrap-read forms. The coefficient of a squarefree degree-r monomial on S is the permanent of its r-column submatrix, equal in characteristic two to det(A_S). The sum of these coefficients over S equals det(A A^T) in GF(2) by Cauchy–Binet and a²=a. The desired bit is therefore det(A A^T) times the parity of the number of b-bit baseline words with g=1 modulo two. The empty determinant is one.

If f<r, dividing by 2^r already leaves the affine sums of binary products. For each b-bit word with g odd, impose all r equations H_C=1 on the f outside bits, using that word's inhomogeneous offsets. The contribution to parity is one exactly for a unique consistent completion; Gaussian elimination also handles f=0. XOR these contributions. This proves the stated runtime and bit criterion at all r, on any source-permitted layout. It does not determine higher digits, later coefficients or the complete scalar time.
