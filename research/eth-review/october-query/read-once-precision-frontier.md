# Native original read-once precision one and two

Read-once means each occurring ORIGINAL input bit appears in exactly one literal. With nonempty clauses on distinct variables the origin count already factors as ∏_C(2^{|C|}−1), with 2 per unused variable. Each clause can be satisfied independently to obtain an original witness. The following concerns the deformed materialized root, not an advance on counting at that origin. Its n clause ports have companion free variable COPY1 row endpoints; each original edge is subdivided by a column COPY2 tying its two slots. Thus after all selectors and ties the sample is Σ_X P(DX), with the actual merged n×n D and multilinear clause polynomial P of boolean-occupancy-low-precision.md.

Write c for its top monomial coefficient and c_i for the coefficient omitting only row i. Both are computable clausewise from the actual OR signs. At p=1, only full bijections contribute, c is odd, hence the scalar is det D over the characteristic-two residue field. At p=2 the complete answer modulo four is
 c per(D) + 2 Σ_{i,j} c_i det D_{−i;−j}
 + 2 c Σ_{j≠l} Σ_{i<k} D_{il}D_{kl} det D_{−i,−k;−j,−l}.
The determinants multiplied by two require only residue-field precision. The second sum counts partial bijections. For the last sum, j is the missing column, l the doubled one, and the unordered pair i,k its two preimages. No factorial division occurs. Empty minors equal one; impossible index choices give empty sums. For n=0 the constant coefficient is the answer. For n=1 this gives cD11+2c_empty. Replace the leading per(D) by the following formula modulo four:
 det D + 2 Σ_{i<k, l>j} D_{il}D_{kj} det D_{−i,−k;−j,−l}.
For each permutation the sign discrepancy is twice its inversion parity, giving precisely the correction through all marked inversions. These identities hold over any commutative characteristic-four coefficient ring. At a numerical root with e=O(log s), division-free polynomial-time determinant algorithms suffice for the one large determinant; O(n^4) remaining small determinants over the residue field cost O(n^7) field operations by ordinary elimination. Generate minors serially in polynomial workspace. Source entry creation is polynomial in M,L,e,p, and add/mul costs poly(e,p) bits. This does not treat nilpotent entries as automatically invertible.

# Explicit native sector count at growing precision

For sufficiently large power-of-two M=2n, balance all ordered full label pairs with H=M/r^{2a} divisible by 2L. Every packet template appears an even number of times. Pair identical templates A,B, reversing the coordinate-zero orientation convention in B (on equal-symbol coordinates the adjacent index is simply a copy). Use even slots as negative-unit OR1 endpoints and odd slots as free COPY1 endpoints. Tie A's even slot 2j to B's odd slot 2j+1 through a single logical column COPY2. Similarly tie B even to A odd. Their complete column labels match after reversal, while every row vertex is unary. This consumes every slot once and preserves the balanced legal loads. The original CNF has exactly one solution, all zeros.

Modulo two at t=1, for ANY selected lift ζ, W(t+2Lζ) is the unit shift from row x to y=x+1 when present. All even rows have a successor; all odd slots belong to distinct logical variables. Therefore D mod 2 is a permutation matrix π. Now P=∏_i(1−q_i). For each H⊆[n] with h=|H|<p, use monomial support I=[n]−H and the occupancy with columns π(H) omitted and no repeated occupied column. The minor permanent is a unit because its reduction is a permutation. This individual term has exact valuation h including the Boolean factor 2^h. Consequently at least Σ_{h=0}^{min(p−1,n)} binom(n,h) different sectors survive native precision. For p=Θ(log n) this is 2^{Θ((log n)^2)} for the displayed subset sum, or n^{Ω(p)} in this regime. At the source's odd-node p_t>n it includes 2^n sectors. This obstructs explicitly visiting all nonzero occupancy sectors in 2^{poly(p)}poly(n) UNIFORMLY: at any fixed p, the degree p−1 in n can exceed the exponent of any proposed f(p)poly(n). At p=Θ(log n) specifically, n^{Ω(p)} is also 2^{Ω(p²)}; the example does not exclude an unspecified 2^{poly(p)} bound in that scaling alone. Sector cancellations and collective summation are unrestricted. No FPT lower bound on the scalar follows. For unrestricted matrices Curticapean–Xia prove parity-W[1]-hardness of permanent modulo 2^p parameterized by p; transferring that theorem to a native tied Cauchy family needs a separate reduction. https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.02321

# Original-count boundary

At original occurrence two, exact counting at the origin is already #P-hard: monotone edge variables with one vertex clause per endpoint give the number of edge covers, even on the 2–3-regular bipartite class for exact counting. Original variables now have COPY2 row vertices in addition to the subdivision COPY2s; their row selectors no longer sum to one. The degree-n multilinear model in the first section is therefore inapplicable. https://pierre.senellart.com/publications/amarilli2017conjunctive.pdf

Even the FIRST bit of the origin count is parity-P-complete on positive 2-occurrence formulas (the parity statement uses the graph class of its own reduction, not the bounded-degree class above). This is established by the parity edge-cover construction in Appendix A of https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol377-sat2026/html/LIPIcs.SAT.2026.20/LIPIcs.SAT.2026.20.html . One query at precision one does not recover an arbitrary integer count. Precision n+1 always recovers the count of a CNF on n variables. These reductions do not make monotone feasibility hard; choosing every variable true satisfies every nonempty positive clause. They do not transfer hardness to a specific nonzero deformed sample.
