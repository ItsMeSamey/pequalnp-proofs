# Three-bit mismatch synthesis for fixed projected planes

Given indexed binary K,t, take D=ker K. Require the checkable hypothesis L=D∩ker t, where L is the aggregate span of actually matched words computed by the prior scalar first-space solver. Assume Ω has rank four on L. If t vanishes on D then one chart activates all columns. Otherwise choose d* in D with odd tag. Put R=rad(Ω|L); choose x0,y0 lifting an ordered basis of a specified nondegenerate plane in L/R and complete these by z1,z2 to a basis modulo R. All lifts are x0+r,y0+s for r,s∈R. There are constantly many projected planes to try for the original decision.

A mismatch mask m equals t+Kᵀg precisely when m and t agree on a basis of D. Thus m must annihilate R,x0,y0,z1,z2 and give one on d*. Sitewise compatibility requires m_i(x0_i+r_i)=m_i(y0_i+s_i)=0 at EVERY site. With m⊥R that already gives m⊥x0,y0, so only three global output bits remain: (m·z1,m·z2,m·d*)=(0,0,1). Conversely solve Kᵀg=m+t for one true common chart. All lifted pairs preserve Ω.

Compute the physical direct-sum components of R by taking the matroid connected components of the coordinate evaluation columns q_i in a basis of R (fundamental bipartite graph, including loops/coloops). Then R=⊕R_j and R⊥=⊕R_j⊥ on the same indexed partition. Let S_j be the set of three-bit signatures of those m_j∈R_j⊥ for which local r_j,s_j∈R_j satisfy the two sitewise equations. Zero belongs to each set. Global exact signatures are their set sum by concatenating actual local assignments. One elementary exact local algorithm enumerates all local radical pairs then solves for the mismatch for each signature, or enumerates m_j then solves linearly for r_j,s_j. Time is poly(n) times 2 to the maximum over j of min(2dimR_j, |j|−dimR_j), followed by eight-state sum DP. This elementary bounded-rank mechanism falls within the prior bounded-width five-name quotient; q is exactly its private representation.

An actual spanning basis of S_j is deterministically constructible in polynomial time on each arbitrary-width block. For a binary linear test on the three bits, let ℓ_i be its pullback. Form ordinary columns (q_i,x0_i,y0_i,ℓ_i) with named space N of the last three coordinates. Some physical scalar acceptance is equivalent to an ordinary subset X whose span intersects N in exactly the pure ℓ line. Forward use X=supp(m_j): sitewise disjointness supplies two functionals on q_i matching x0_i,y0_i there; every supported q-relation has zero tag and m_j gives pure gain one. Reverse, the exact line ensures both tags consistently extend on q_X since every q-relation has zero tag. Extend these two functions to obtain original r_j,s_j. Solve the representation of the pure gain using X to obtain an actual m_j∈R_j⊥ with scalar one. Apply the deterministic three-dimensional named trace solver; if N lies partially outside the ordinary span intersect it first, rejecting a missing required line. Retain the full three-bit signature and original witness. Iterating on a basis of annihilator tests yields an actual basis of span S_j in at most three successful rounds.

Let k≤3 be the dimension of the sum of the block spans. Reject targets outside. If k distinct blocks supply independent actual vectors, switch some of them from zero to obtain every target in that sum, with original physical witnesses. Enumerating ≤3 tuples from the retained actual bases suffices: independence is detected by a nonzero alternating coordinate determinant and expands multilinearly. At k=1 this always works; at k=2 it works whenever at least two blocks have nonzero span (otherwise failure for all pairs forces one common line on all blocks). At k=3 with at least three nonzero blocks, either it works or all but ONE nonzero block have the SAME LINE span. The independent transversal rank formula is min_J(dim(sum of spans on J) + #excluded blocks). Dropping zero blocks, a minimum ≤2 cannot use dim 0 unless ≤2 blocks exist; dim 1 leaves at most one outside, and dim 2 leaves none, contrary to total dimension three. This classification can also be checked on the finitely many three-bit subspace types. In the common-line case its copies realize the whole line, reducing the remaining exact target to TWO quotient bits on a single block. If the target in that quotient is zero, use defaults plus the free line. Otherwise exact membership needs one q-relation m of a prescribed TWO-bit gain on a SINGLE support where x0,y0 both have matching charts. A scalar sample with first bit one followed by an unrestricted second-bit query does not prove joint membership. If supplied, an exact two-output local algorithm must reconstruct one such common witness. One or two arbitrary-width active blocks can still evade the unconditioned scalar tests; the result does not claim otherwise.
