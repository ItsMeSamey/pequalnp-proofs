# Free components and transfer saturation

In silent-saturation-full.py the low three bits of the ten listed columns give a connected binary projection N. The high two give their lifts, and the two target columns downstairs are e1,e2. All 16 transfers occur on the 54 physical bases. For the 15 listed seeds, one per transfer other than 13, exhaustive equal-transfer component traversal checks that no reached basis has an exchange to 13. Nevertheless the bases with masks 385,131,7 yield 1,2,13. The earlier 67-basis fixture in silent-saturation-obstruction.py also has this barrier when one value is genuinely absent. These examples concern starting with exactly the supplied seeds and crossing at most one nonzero transfer change; they do not rule out a more informative signature or excursions through other known states.

There is no polynomial upper bound on the number of physical components in a single transfer fiber of a connected hard projection. Take two copies of each coordinate direction e_i in binary dimension n≥2, with vertical lifts 0,w respectively. Add one physical column on the sum of all e_i, with lift u independent of w. The projection is connected: the full family of coordinate directions and the connector forms a circuit, and each extra copy is parallel to its coordinate. Give each basis slot a nonzero input row h_i, spanning the input plane altogether. Each coordinate-only basis chooses one copy in every class. Changing one copy gives nonzero difference w h_i. Exchanging with the connector gives (u+αw)h_i, where α is the parity of chosen w copies; this too is nonzero. These are all adjacent bases. All 2^n coordinate-only bases are therefore isolated under transfer-preserving exchanges. Their transfers range over the four matrices w times a binary covector, with exactly 2^(n-2) isolated bases at each since the h_i span. The two residual directions are independent, so the component is hard.

Despite this multiplicity the entire family has an efficient exact table. Coordinate-only choices contribute an arbitrary w times a covector. A basis with the connector omits one coordinate direction j and keeps one copy of each other. In the new basis the coefficient row of the connector is h_j, and the coefficient row at i≠j is h_i+h_j. Thus for each j the reachable transfers form u h_j plus the span of w(h_i+h_j) for i≠j. Gaussian elimination returns actual coordinate-copy selections. This gives a polynomial algebraic compression separate from any bound on the number of silent fibers. It does not bound the nonzero exchange depth for general projections.
