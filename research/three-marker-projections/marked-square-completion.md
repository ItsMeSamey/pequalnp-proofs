# Arbitrary physical marks in a network of four-grant squares

There are at most two square modules containing interior marks. Enumerate all original native settings of these modules, at most 16² possibilities for the four-switch square. In a branch the wiring of these modules is fixed. Retain the two marked positions as passive observed sites with local identity, by subdividing their fixed wires if needed. Suppress the remaining fixed wire paths to produce the fixed permutation of the unmarked modules' sockets and the retained marked sites. Mark-only cycles are retained, and fixed circuits with neither marks nor exposed sockets may be discarded. Then the arbitrary-permutation matching-module solver applies, taking empty local graphs on the retained passive sites. A positive matching lifts through untouched squares and combines with the enumerated settings. If every branch fails there is no native mask joining the marks.

The running time is polynomial in the network size with a factor of at most 256 for the two marked squares. The argument works for other matching modules if all marked responses can be enumerated constructively with a stated cost. A decomposition into such modules or a supplied construction is assumed.
