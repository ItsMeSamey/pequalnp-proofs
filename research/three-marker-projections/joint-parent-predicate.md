# Simultaneous abelian totals and an auxiliary polynomial on one partition

Suppose an original connected spanning p-part partition must have prescribed sums in a fixed finite abelian group Gamma and must make an explicitly represented degree-D polynomial P over a single field nonzero. P's variables are original indexed site-to-color indicators. There is no compatibility requirement between the characteristic and the order of Gamma. The group-parent-skeleton algorithms on circular-arc, chordal, bounded chordal-deletion, or the stated bounded-path-sink plus ancestor-forest classes extend to this simultaneous task.

In either the first arbitrary-tree or the second monotone leaf compression, use independent unprotected leaves as in abelian-parent-skeleton.md. Let m=|Gamma|^{p−1}. If at least (D+1)m independent leaves are available, greedily select D+1 disjoint nonempty zero-sum packets, each of at most m leaves, using partial sums in Gamma^{p−1}. Each packet may be toggled as a single Boolean variable deleting its members. All choices preserve the joint group sums. Every site-color indicator is constant or depends affinely on just the one toggle associated with its original basin, since all selected leaves have undeleted next retained contacts. The restriction of P to these bits has multilinear degree at most D over its own field and is nonzero at zero. It cannot vanish at every nonzero Boolean tuple of D+1 variables. Therefore a nonempty union can be removed without losing either predicate at once. In a minimal retained forest at most 2((D+1)m−1) unprotected leaves suffice. Preserve all earlier roots and physical link endpoint pins as before.

In the constructive bounded-head sweep, optionally after restricting to reference-ancestor source closure, substitute every pre-folded physical site's variable by that of its first remaining contact. This retains the original P, including repeated or overlapping original monomials; it cannot increase degree. Keep separate linear spans of ordinary field coefficient vectors of residual root-demand polynomials within each **exact discrete state** of the group skeleton DP. The discrete state includes all active source walkers, precise demanded roots, physical group inflows at default heads and partial score tuple. At a retained site the (eventually verified) declared terminal root determines all its color indicators immediately, and all pending path walkers must agree with it. At an omitted site the indicators equal those of its later default parent. These substitutions are linear on the entire polynomial coefficient vector, with coalescence and conflict rules as usual. The next discrete state and available physical choices depend only on the current discrete state, site and action; they do not depend on the coefficient vector. For each state retain linearly independent original images, with actual prefix and discrete-state backpointers. At the last site accept a nonzero scalar vector only in a correctly pinned, correctly scored terminal state. This is a single physical choice history fulfilling both conditions. Fixed parameters give polynomially many states and polynomial-dimensional coefficient spaces on all the listed classes.

This does not multiply polynomial indicators over incompatible fields. There is one auxiliary polynomial field; arithmetic for the unrelated group is carried in explicit discrete states.
