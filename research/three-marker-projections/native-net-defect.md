# Exact rational routing by a total fourth exterior form

Use the original cyclic menus, one independent choice at each physical three-socket site, and distinct fixed marks r,s. The signed cofactor deleting row s and column r from 2I−P is nonnegative for every actual setting and positive exactly when its marks join. Replacing the last row of each complete site triple by its common row sum and quotienting the n−1 common independent rows gives two ordered vectors per option/site in a rational vector space V of dimension 2n. The marked site already has two rows and contributes no common row. All resulting determinants have the same weak sign since the common row operations, reordering and quotient volume change are fixed. The preceding paired-row-independent-audit.md proves independence and contains the minimal two-site rank-four example. For an option pair let w denote its decomposable exterior two-form. Every site's options lie in a fixed subspace of dimension at most four: relative to its base rows both row changes are differences of the three destination units, whose span has dimension at most two.

Form C_i=Σ_a w_{i,a}, A=Σ_i C_i, and Ω=Σ_i C_i∧C_i. The original-menu sum of the oriented projected determinants is the top coefficient of ∧_i C_i. It is zero precisely when all original choices fail. Its absolute value is the sum of their absolute determinants, differing from the absolute sum of the original marked cofactors by one fixed positive rational scale. This is a weighted sum, not the count of successful masks.

All forms used here have even degree and commute. Since each C_i³=0, the finite exterior-algebra exponential identity is
∏_i(1+C_i)=exp(A−Ω/2).
Only the term using all n colors contributes in degree 2n on the left. Let S be the support of Ω, the span of its contractions by all triples of dual unit vectors, of dimension t. This is the smallest support subspace: in a basis adapted to the contraction span, contracting successively by three other coordinate forms detects any nonzero coefficient involving a basis direction outside S. Its coordinates and a basis follow by rational elimination in polynomial size. In an adapted ambient basis, compute exp(−Ω/2) on S, storing at most 2^t coordinates. For each resulting coordinate e_U multiply by the signed complementary principal Pfaffian of A. This gives the top coefficient of exp(A−Ω/2), because exp(A) has precisely those Pfaffian coordinates. Polynomially many operations per stored coordinate and polynomial input bit lengths give 2^{O(t)}poly(N) exact bit complexity; all base tensors are rational with a common denominator of polynomial bit length, and exterior power products involve O(N) factors. The basis transformation in native_net_defect.py uses independent pivot coordinates with a unit triangular volume change up to sign.

For recovering a witness fix some physical pairs. If they are dependent their branch fails. Otherwise factor their wedges outside and pass to the quotient by their span. At each fixed site the image of its local at-most-four-space now has dimension at most two, so its C_i² becomes zero. Hence the sum of squares of the unfixed projected sites is exactly the image of the ORIGINAL Ω. Conditioning cannot increase its support dimension, even when the original sum enjoyed large cancellations. Replace the linear part A by the sum of the unfixed site aggregates in the quotient. The same nonzero test on each candidate choice recovers a single original successful mask with polynomially many calls. The implementation does this quotient before recomputing the remaining terms.

A separate solver branches only on sites with nonsimple C_i and decomposes every remaining nonzero simple form into a concrete rational pair. An optional decision-only preprocessing can freely expand a full cyclic menu to S3, whose six-option sum is simple, followed by local restoration to cyclic choices; this changes the numerical sum, and is NOT used by the two reference programs or the exact-sum checks. The supplied binaries with feedback (3,4,5,0,2,1), roots 0 and 3, give two opposite nonzero local fourth forms summing to zero. General rank-four sites can grow linearly even on connected instances. No unrestricted polynomial bound on t is inferred.

The implementations native_paired_rows.py and native_net_defect.py use rational arithmetic and return original shifts. check_native_defect.py compares all projected determinant contributions on 42 instances up to five sites and replays both physical witness algorithms. The checker imports the orbit function in check_native_cut_dp.py.
