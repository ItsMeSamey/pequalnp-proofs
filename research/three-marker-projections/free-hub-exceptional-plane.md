# Four-direction plane complement excludes arbitrary balanced free hubs

Four ternary data sites each rotate three sockets by their own input. A network may contain arbitrarily many independent equal-in/out-degree free routers and fixed directed wires. The two marks are fixed wires. Rooted acceptance means some simultaneous auxiliary setting joins them. After fixing a word, this tests weak connectivity: Euler circuits of the balanced directed components supply simultaneous router pairings. Insert degree-one marked hubs on the marked wires; contract fixed arcs between hubs. Contraction preserves balance and weak connectivity for every word and the merged hub is realizable. Discard components with no data; if one contains a root, only a constant verdict is possible. Thus only twelve departures and twelve arrivals of the data sites need be assigned to balanced hubs, including the two marked ones. Independent optional two-track swaps are free routers of degree two.

Write Z for the nine words with a+c+d=b+c−d=0 over F3. At zero choose the hub pairings to traverse one Euler circuit per weak component. This yields a permutation p on the twelve sockets: p[j] is the next data departure after leaving data socket j. The permutation at w is p composed with its four cyclic shifts. Each marked hub can be represented by one fixed data departure there. Each of the two marked default cycles at zero contains a departure of every site. Otherwise changing a missing site leaves its entire marked component at zero undisturbed, although either unit change must be accepted.

For each site distribute its three sockets between the first and second rooted cycles and the rest. The count triples (1,1,1), (2,1,0), (1,2,0) have 6,3,3 placements. For total sizes a,b,c the two rooted cyclic words and arbitrary residual permutation have a! b! c! realizations. Summing yields 1,647,143,424. Independent cyclic renumberings give 81 symmetries. The monomial GL2(3) action on the four projective coordinate directions realizes all site permutations with local signs and has order 48. It preserves Z. Represent local signs by reversing socket numbering. Fixing the two rooted cycles fixes both words position by position, hence fixes two distinct sockets at each site, so the combined action is free. Canonical words name colors in first appearance order on the first word and give the first socket of each color index zero. The residual simultaneous negation sets the first remaining nonzero socket on the two words to one. There are 423,648 representatives.

For each p compute the permutation cycles at every z in Z. Discard p if the marks share a cycle. Otherwise take all cuts containing the first marked cycle, excluding the second, and freely including other cycles. At zero use exactly the first cycle, since the default Euler routing traversed its entire component. Refine the one-block partition of the twelve sockets by one cut per z. For each resulting partition group its sockets into free hubs, with p[j] defining the hub destination of outgoing socket j. This construction balances every hub and separates all nine codewords. Any exact prospective design appears up to enlargement: use its true first-root weak component at every codeword as the respective cut. Sockets at each original hub have identical cut bits, so merging equal signatures preserves separation and preserves every other successful connection. For any word the exact new connectivity is the equivalence closure of the permutation cycles and partition blocks.
