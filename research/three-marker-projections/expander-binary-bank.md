# Exact binary charges and quadratic tests on four-connected expanders

Fix maximum degree Δ, positive vertex expansion ε up to n/2, a fixed number p of ordered nonempty colors, a fixed binary tag dimension r and a bounded number of precolored sites. On promised 4-connected graphs with these bounds, a deterministic polynomial algorithm decides and constructs a connected spanning coloring with prescribed ordered F₂^r sums and a nonzero degree-at-most-two polynomial P over GF(2) in site-color indicators. This includes arbitrary two-bit tags and Ω on the two physical cuts with original indexed edge coordinates. No abundance, girth or supplied decomposition is assumed. Use the physical routing, giant and clique lemmas in expander-pinned-quadratics.md and expander-distant-moments.md. The algorithm does not recognize expansion.

Suppose x is an accepting partition. Check the total tag identity. Freeze small color classes B in their entirety, forbid their colors elsewhere, and pin all sites of scarce nonzero tag types to their x colors. Also retain the given pins and one representative per remaining color. The scales are specified below. Let H be the span of plentiful tag types; all unfixed sites have tag in H. Pick a basis from those types. Residual targets after fixed sites belong to H by x. With one surviving color just test the unique extension.

At each stage build a bank of at most b=r(p−1) sites. For each basis tag h choose one witness color c_h and a site of that type in x-color c_h for EACH remaining color j≠c_h. Bank sites are pairwise distant and far from B and the current pinned set A. Collect p(p−1) mutually distant sites of the type outside all forbidden balls and take enough of one common actual color. Sufficient multiplicity guarantees this. Restrict site (h,j) to colors c_h and j. For arbitrary ordinary colors respecting fixed pins and the surviving palette the bank has a UNIQUE assignment giving the prescribed totals: its flip adds h to colors c_h and j, giving the star basis of zero-total color-tuples separately in each h coordinate. All residual totals are in the appropriate coset since ordinary-site tags lie in H and the totals sum correctly. Each binary flip, hence every bank color indicator, is affine over GF₂ in the ordinary-site indicators. Substitution into P gives Q of degree ≤2, nonzero since x is in this chart.

Before selecting a bank bound m_* and L_* by the pinned-short-path construction of expander-pinned-quadratics.md with L(m)=2+ceil((|B|+|A|+3b+12+m)/ε). Large colors must exceed m_*+L_*. Set gap g=10(m_*+L_*+|B|+12). Choose bank sites at distances >g from A, B and each other. Restrict Q temporarily by fixing every ordinary site within g of A, B or a current bank site to its x color. The bank has already been eliminated. If degree stays unchanged, a maximum-degree monomial after this restriction has support U of size ≤2 far from all pins and bank sites. Its coefficient is unchanged in the original Q as no strict extension has nonzero coefficient. This remains true for arbitrary outside ordinary colors. Use the canonical one-site basis with one omitted color.

If degree drops, permanently freeze the whole tested neighborhood AND current bank to x, add the newly fixed H-side sites to A, and repeat with a fresh bank. The next Q cannot have higher degree than the preceding outer restriction: every new physical assignment solving the scores belongs to the old chart with the frozen old bank (its assignment is forced by uniqueness and satisfies the old binary restrictions). The remaining old ordinary color indicators become constants, new ordinary indicators, or affine indicators of the new bank. Thus substitution only decreases or preserves degree; canonical one-site reduction does likewise. The value on x stays nonzero. At most two strict drops occur.

At a stable stage omit U and the bank, jointly S, from the graph after removing B. Demand two contacts at each u for its monomial and baseline colors and two at each bank site for its admissible colors. Apply the witness-dependent short-pin-path contractions and private sets of expander-pinned-quadratics.md to the currently fixed pins A. At most m_* contraction losses occur; all affected sites and pin private sets are far from S. For U, at most two sites, obtain up to four disjoint outward arms of length L(m) by the original 4-connectivity, even for adjacent roots. For each far bank site obtain two more. Arms are disjoint from other clusters and all pin private sets; the two U sites can be treated as one group whether near or far. Large n at bounded Δ supplies distant sinks. G−B is connected because all remaining actual colors are large. G−(B∪S) is connected: any nongiant component is small and must neighbor S; it cannot touch B or two distinct groups of S by the distances. Its neighbors would be at most two original sites, contradicting 4-connectivity.

In the contracted graph minus S the named roots are surviving pin classes and all physical contacts. Every root has its disjoint private connected L(m)-site set. A separator of order smaller than their number (at most |A|+2b+4) can be lifted to original deletions including B, S and at most m extra sites. As in the companion, the untouched private set and all the untouched branches of a sufficiently large clique minor force the same giant side. The rooted partition theorem supplies disjoint connected sets joining the correct color groups. Expand contractions and flood once; the algorithm directly tests actual pins and contacts in the original G−(B∪S) for this rooted model, avoiding the unknown contractions. One exterior now covers EVERY ordinary vertex off U and respects the actual pins. Independently toggle the sites U, solving the unique bank correction for each toggle pattern. All bank values are physically possible by their two contacts. Every resulting partition is connected, satisfies scores, and has identical ordinary colors outside U, although bank colors may change. The mixed difference of the SUBSTITUTED Q is its nonzero top coefficient, giving an accepting original P.

For the scale order use p color sizes and at most 2^r−1 nonzero tag-type counts. For a trial small cutoff t, |B|≤pt and all scarce tags occupy at most (2^r−1)t sites. Bound |A| initially using those and the pins. Bound all subsequent merge budgets, radii, ball sizes and the number of frozen sites after each unsuccessful stage, using bounded Δ and at most three stages. Choose M(t)>t exceeding every required large-color size and plentiful multiplicity, including the number excluded when choosing separated bank sites across all types and stages. Iterate T₀=0,T_{j+1}=M(T_j) for p+2^r intervals. At least one interval contains none of the listed counts, so small counts ≤T_j and large counts >M(T_j) meet all thresholds. For sufficiently large n there is a surviving color; bounded n can be exhaustively handled.

Enumerate the bounded small classes, pin assignments, bank sites and their colors, neighborhood assignments after unsuccessful stages, up to two monomial sites and the neighbors providing all contact groups. All lists and neighborhoods have bounded cardinality at fixed parameters. Rooted minor containment on an edgeless pattern with the fixed prescribed color groups returns disjoint connected sets. Expand/flood, compute bank values, and verify original colors, score and P. This process needs no x and yields a polynomial constructive algorithm under the stated promise. Outside it, displayed witnesses are sound and exhausted search may abstain.
