# Six positive hyperplane lifts onto the same score plane

Let π send a physical word to its Ω-polar covector on the aggregate L (rank four). Every nondegenerate score plane P has three nonzero annihilator covectors in the score dual. Adding a private row h to K forces h=0 on both dependencies but allows a chart coefficient ε on h: the original keys then match t+εh. The alternative row h+t gives the same parity constraint on t-even words, and its extra sector matches h. An original witness in the desired plane survives both augmentations. Conversely if two charts match t+h and h on the same physical support, their sum matches t there. Equality of projected planes does not enforce this last condition.
