# A chordal modulator with many free attachments

Let X be k vertices of G such that H=G−X is chordal. Fix a perfect elimination order of H and place X last. For every nonempty subset A of X let N_A be the sites of H adjacent to at least one vertex of A. Require the bipartite cross-edge graph in H from N_A to its complement in H to have induced matching number at most c. For fixed k,c enumerate X, recognize H, and check each cut by enumerating c+1 edges.

In a true connected p-color partition make all k vertices of X stopping roots of their true colors. Each color with no X sites is connected in H, and routes increasingly to its unique maximum. For a color with X sites A, every component J of its H vertices contains some site in N_A, since the full color connects to A. Within J route increasingly toward its maximum. If this maximum is in N_A, continue from it to any physically adjacent root in A. Such components create no new roots. Each remaining component has a site in N_A and its maximum outside N_A, hence an edge crossing this cut. One edge from each gives an induced matching because distinct components of the same color have no edges between them. There are at most c such extra roots per color. Thus the true solution admits at most k+p(c+1) roots overall. The connected original classes supply physical edges spanning their rooted trees. Enumerate roots, colors, and connecting edge endpoint-root pins as in ordered-charged-partitions.md. The deterministic witness time is n^{O(dp+k+pc+c)}, with all p colors and all original sites retained.

Separately, if a chordal graph H on the same sites differs from G by at most k physical edges, use its PEO. Connected colors in G have at most p+|E(G) minus E(H)| H-components overall. In each H-component every nonmaximum vertex has a later H-neighbor. If it has no later same-color G-neighbor, some missing H edge has exactly that site as lower endpoint. Thus at most p+k stopping roots suffice jointly, and the same bridge-enumeration and choice-span algorithm applies. Recognize the promise at fixed k by trying the edited edges. More generally the first difference term can be replaced by the graphic rank of E(G) minus E(H): select intercomponent G edges spanning the H-components in each color; their union on physical sites is acyclic. The second term can be replaced by the number of distinct lower endpoints of E(H) minus E(G) under the chosen PEO.
