# Basis-transfer affine span on a connected binary matroid

Fix a full row rank binary projected matrix N, column offsets Z in W and a target matrix H with columns in the row-coordinate ambient space of N. An ordinary basis B produces the matrix T_B = Z_B N_B^{-1} H. Dimensions of W and the source of H are arbitrary. Coordinates of a basis always refer to basis slots. In the standard chart [I | D] write h_b for row b of N_B^{-1}H, and g_e = Z_e + Z_B D_e for a nonbasic element e. The fundamental graph joins b,e when D_be=1. Its connected components are the ordinary matroid components, including isolated loops and coloops.

On a connected component, the span of all differences of basis transfers equals the tensor product of the span of its g_e and the span of its h_b, inside the space of transfer matrices. A basis swap b/e changes the transfer by g_e h_b (outer product). Under this pivot the h-row at a slot i distinct from b becomes h_i + D_ie h_b while the exchanged slot keeps h_b. For a remaining nonbasic f the residual becomes g_f + D_bf g_e while the outgoing basic column now has residual g_e. Thus the two spans do not change; basis exchanges connect all bases and prove one inclusion.

For the converse take a linear functional K nonzero on the tensor product. Select a shortest fundamental graph path from a basic slot b0 to a nonbasic ek among pairs with K(g_k h_0)=1. Write its vertices b0,e1,b1,e2,...,b(k-1),ek. For k=1 the endpoints give an actual exchange changing K. For k≥2 pivot on b1/e1. There is now an edge b0/e2, and the suffix from e2 to ek persists (pivot toggles the rectangle of neighbors and the chosen path was chordless). The new h_0 equals h_0+h_1. When k>2 the last g_k stays fixed since b1 and ek were nonadjacent; K(g_k h_1)=0 by minimality. When k=2 the new g_2 equals g_2+g_1; all three extra pairings g_1 h_0, g_2 h_1 and g_1 h_1 come from old neighbors and vanish by minimality. In either case the new pair still has value 1 along a path shorter by two. At each iteration select a shortest violating pair in the current chart. This strictly decreases its distance, so the process terminates with an actual exchange. Ordinary GF(2) elimination and breadth-first search give a deterministic polynomial running time and both actual bases. This proves the other inclusion by annihilators.

For disconnected N add the transfers from its direct-sum components, with H projected onto each summand. The attainable image is the set sum of the actual local tables. For the four-name plane write the original ambient coordinates as projected space plus W (dimension two), and choose two target columns with projected matrix H; require the transfer of an ordinary N-basis to equal their actual target offset. This equivalence follows by extending the projected-independent physical witness to a projected basis; conversely the original lifted basis is disjoint from W and spans both target columns. First intersect the named space with the ordinary span and handle lower dimensions separately.

Call a component hard here if both local spans have dimension two. Among four distinct hard components choose one actual basis exchange in each with difference outside the span of earlier differences: choose a functional annihilating previous differences and nonzero on its full tensor, and apply the constructive proof. Fix each component at the first endpoint of its own exchange, remaining components at arbitrary bases. Toggling a subset of the four exchanges gives every one of the sixteen transfers with actual physical witnesses. No inference of local membership from its affine span is used.

Each nonhard component admits an exact local table by three-name queries. If H has local rank one, for its generator h test target offset z via an exact line in span((h,z),(0,w1),(0,w2)). If residual Z modulo the row space of N has rank one, subtract the affine-linear baseline Z_B N_B^{-1} N. The reduced lifts lie in a line, and the graph of a requested map on the projected target has codimension one in at most three named dimensions. In either formulation shorten to an independent witness with projection injective and extend to a projected basis. Rank zero gives a constant. Track the removed linear baseline in the sum. The cases of one, two or three hard components can require further tests.
