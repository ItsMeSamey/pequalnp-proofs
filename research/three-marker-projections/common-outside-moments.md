# Graph-only physical moment representatives from common outside colorings

For each set S of at most D physical sites require an outside coloring of V minus S into p nonempty connected parts such that every s in S has a neighbor in EVERY outside part. Retain one such outside coloring and enumerate all p^{|S|} recolorings on S, leaving every other site unchanged across this cube. All are genuine connected spanning partitions. For p≥2 this is also necessary for a full cube of connected nonempty partitions with common exterior: color all S away from one color to test its outside connectivity, then color a single s in that color to test its contact.

The union of these physically generated cubes spans the same degree-D evaluation rows, over any field, as ALL unrestricted site colorings. Use one distinguished baseline color and the indicators for the other p−1 colors at each site. Products having at most one nonbaseline indicator per site form a basis of all functions on the p-ary cube by tensoring the corresponding one-site bases. For a nonzero test choose a nonzero coefficient of maximum degree t≤D with support S and prescribed nonbaseline colors there. In the cube for S, take the alternating finite difference between those prescribed colors and baseline at all t sites. Every term missing a site of S vanishes, terms using a distinct color at an S site vanish, and no strictly larger support is present. The result is the chosen nonzero coefficient for ANY fixed exterior. A physical cube member therefore witnesses nonvanishing. The constant test uses S empty.
