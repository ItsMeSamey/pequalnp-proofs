# Deterministic exact membership with three hard components

Use connected-basis-transfer.md and its standard pivot chart. At any physically attained transfer T and basis for T, let W0 be a proper output subspace missing a residual g. If some target row h is nonzero, choose a shortest fundamental path among pairs b0,ek with h0 nonzero and gk outside W0. Along b0,e1,b1,...,ek every interior row has h=0 and every interior column has g in W0 by minimality. If k>1 pivot b1/e1. This free move preserves T and h0. The final gk stays fixed unless k=2, in which case gk+g1 is still outside W0. Chordlessness gives b0/e2 and preserves the suffix. Re-minimize to shorten strictly. The final adjacent pair gives a physical exchange with output factor outside W0. Transpose the chart and swap labels for the dual input-factor conclusion. Thus at every T the neighboring transfer differences accessible after free moves have full span separately in each factor.

For the 576 remaining simplex cases with a leaf, run the free search there with W0 the output line of its unique allowed difference. A sixth point is constructed. For every remaining 96 case find a distinguished T (check each of five) with only two allowable nonzero neighboring differences A,B, whose inputs are independent, whose outputs are independent, and T+A+B is absent. For example S={0,1,8,11,13} has T=0 and A=1,B=8. The companion enumerates the property on all 96 directly. At the witnessed T set V0 to the input line of B and W0 to the output line of A. Choose a shortest fundamental path between a row h outside V0 and a column g outside W0. Both endpoint sets are nonempty in every chart by the invariant factor spans. At distance one the exchange cannot be A (wrong output) or B (wrong input). Otherwise all interior h belong to V0 and all interior g to W0. Pivot on b1/e1. A nonzero exchange there has the output of A and input of B, giving a third neighbor and hence a sixth physical point. Otherwise the pivot is free. The first row changes to h0+h1, still outside V0; the final g stays fixed or becomes gk+g1, still outside W0. Chordlessness shortens the path, and re-minimization repeats at T until an extra neighbor is exposed. Intermediate silent charts are allowed. The checked double-sum property is unnecessary.
