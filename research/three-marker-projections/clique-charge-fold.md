# Folding an arbitrary charged region onto clique contacts

Let G be connected, with zero total binary charge. Prescribe three connected spanning parts with distinct nonzero sums u,v,u+v, writing H for their span. Take a connected proper vertex set C with external neighborhood W a clique. Assume that the span of charges inside C intersects the sum of H and the span of charges outside C trivially.

In every solution each part has zero total inside C, since this total lies in both subspaces. Every part must therefore meet the exterior. Each exterior restriction remains connected by shortcutting its excursions through C across W. The total of C itself is zero by global zero sum and the intersection hypothesis. Conversely attach C to any one adjacent boundary vertex and give it that vertex's color. This realizes the exterior solution with exactly the desired sums. The reduced graph is a physical minor: contract C to this vertex and simplify. All newly created edges were already clique edges.

The same conclusion holds for W not originally a clique if a physical rooted clique model on C union W is supplied. Its disjoint connected bags must cover all sites, have precisely one W vertex each, have every two bags adjacent, and the interior of each bag must have zero total. Contract the bags. Every original solution restricts to the exterior, shortcutting through the resulting clique; conversely color each whole bag by its retained vertex. Thus projection need not be constant on the particular contracted bags. Exterior edges away from W are unaffected.

The first condition is recognizable for bounded boundary size b by enumerating at most b vertices and the components of their complement, then using linear algebra and clique testing. Repeat until no recognized fold remains and apply folded-support domination if its parameter is bounded. To see strict enlargement, attach a path of even length in vertices between two adjacent vertices of K4, assigning the latter graph charges u,v,u+v,0 and each new path vertex an independent charge z outside H. This graph is two-connected, with unbounded domination number of its nonzero support; the whole path folds. Replacing each path vertex by an arbitrarily large odd clique of z-charged vertices with complete joins between successive cliques retains these properties and allows unbounded treewidth.
