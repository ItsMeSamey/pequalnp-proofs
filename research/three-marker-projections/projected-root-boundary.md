# One projected rooted-support list and the interface to its unknown mate

In projected-rooted-circuit-sums.md take a four-dimensional U, a target plane H, a complementary plane W, and π:V→V/W. Fix h nonzero in H. Enumerate the minimal projected type supports C of πh. Each C is independent in the simple projected matroid. Let S=span C and let T be the span of all projected types outside C together with πU. Put I=S∩T and s=dim I. Preselecting one physical lift per v in C gives a section σ:S→V with σ(v) that chosen lift. Only σ restricted to I can affect any outside completion.

Indeed, for outside physical set Y and named u in U, a representation u=σ(d)+y with d in S, y in span Y forces d=πu−πy in I. Hence replacing σ by another attainable section agreeing on I preserves the predicate u in σ(S)+span Y, for every u and every Y. It also preserves the trace of σ(S) itself. All physical alternatives above C other than the chosen ones can be deleted from consideration: a desired independent maximal witness is a projected basis and never chooses two columns with the same projected type.

Choose a basis d_1,…,d_s of I. Expand d_i in independent C as coefficients α_iv. Taking one baseline section, compute attainable changes of its evaluations on these basis vectors by a finite table. At type v, its offset g contributes the entire vector (α_iv g)_{i=1}^s in W^s. Store one lift traceback per attainable entry, retaining only those for which σ(πh)=h; πh belongs to I. This needs at most 2^(s dim W) states. For each surviving representative, let P=σ(S). If P∩U contains a direction outside H, discard it. If P∩U=H, accept. Otherwise P∩U=span(h). Quotient the actual columns and names by P and ask for the image line of H in the three-dimensional image of U using the deterministic three-name exact-trace decision/search algorithm. Outside columns suffice by the basis argument. Every success lifts using the stored indexed choices. Conversely some minimal projected h-support of every desired basis occurs and its actual lift section is represented by an equivalent table entry. This proves exact decision in time polynomial in input and one projected list size times 2^(s_max dim W), where s_max is the largest interface dimension on that list. Dovetail the three directions for the best joint bound. The statement uses neither a polynomial bound on list sizes nor a universal small interface assumption. In the separating free-projection example in the companion, s=1 despite supports of size m.
