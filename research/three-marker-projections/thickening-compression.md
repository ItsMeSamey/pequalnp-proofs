# Exact compression of the supplied five-edge gadgets for arbitrary indexed forms

Use the displayed construction in rank-drop-thickening.md, with b copies per original nonloop site. Here the input alternating form Ψ on the expanded indexed ground set may be arbitrary. The gadget memberships and original columns are supplied. A fixed map J sends old site i to the path through u in its first copy, extended linearly. In each copy make two local modes, the triangle on 0,u,v and the triangle on k_i,u,v. For each further copy add a mode given by the sum of its through-u path and the through-u path in the first copy of i. Call these last modes transfer modes.

Fix an old chart. If i is active, extending by zero on its fresh axes activates all its edges. This choice contains every possible previously active support there. If i is inactive, the endpoints have unequal colors; an internally balanced physical word at a copy has even degree at both endpoints, hence is a union of monochromatic cycles. With the two inner vertices, the only possibilities are zero, the first triangle, or the second triangle. Either triangle individually is available by putting both inner vertices in the corresponding endpoint color, but the two are incompatible at this one copy. Choices across inactive copies are independent.

For active i, subtract the through-u path from any gadget word having odd endpoint degree. The remainder is in the two-dimensional cycle space of K4 minus an edge and thus generated by its two triangles. Differences of layer paths reduce to the transfer modes. Consequently after enlarging active gadgets to all edges, each actual matched relation is a sum of J applied to its old projected word, local triangles, and transfers at active old sites. Inactive copies use just the triangle enabled by that copy's two inner colors. If Ψ is positive on two actual words, some pair of terms in these decompositions is positive and jointly realizable.

There are three tests.

1. Two J terms use the single old one-tag query on J*Ψ. A positive pair lifts through J in a chart activating all active-site edges.
2. For a pair of listed modes, first check its indexed pairing. Require each transfer's site active, and require i active if the modes are opposite triangles of the same copy. Solve the at most two resulting old chart equations by elimination. These equations suffice: enable the chosen triangle in each involved inactive copy and activate all edges at active sites.
3. For one mode m test the scalar functional x ↦ Ψ(Jx,m) on the matched old first-space. For a triangle no old chart constraint is needed. For a transfer require its old site i active. To compute this conditioned first-space fix g0(k_i)=t_i, quotient the private space by k_i, delete i, and use tags t_j+g0(k_j). For every matched quotient word append its unique i-bit so that the old private sum vanishes. This gives exactly the matched words in charts with site i active, since charts there are precisely g0 plus pullbacks of quotient charts. Pull the scalar functional back by this linear lifting on the quotient kernel, and obtain an original positive word by the certifying scalar first-space algorithm.

Each successful test yields two expanded indexed words in a single explicit chart. The overhead is polynomial, with one base alternating query, polynomially many scalar queries, and quadratically many explicitly tested mode pairs. The statement applies to recognized or supplied gadget coordinates and addresses no arbitrary two-sum tree.
