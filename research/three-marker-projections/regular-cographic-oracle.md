# Relative sufficiency of cographic supported-image queries

Assume deterministic polynomial decision for exact supported-cycle gain images on cographic private matrices, for each fixed binary gain dimension. The private graphic solver is a separate established input. Then the same family on regular private matrices is polynomial-time Turing reducible to the cographic one. Decision queries can recover supports by forbidding a real site whenever the same image is still possible; once no site can be forbidden the remaining set itself realizes the image. Deletion retains the cographic class. Conversely cographic matroids are regular.

Use the indexed 1/2 decomposition, and for each 3-connected regular component other than R10 use the proper triangle decomposition of Aprile and Fiorini, Theorem 6 and its good-cut refinement, Proposition 27, in https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.08539 . Its assumptions and constructive use for represented regular inputs are detailed in the previously reviewed regular-marker decomposition. The refinement places each triangle interface in a nongraphic cographic piece at the star of a degree-three vertex of a representing graph. Finite R10 can be enumerated. In the primal-triangle-images.md DP, for the cographic pieces substitute its assumed oracle for the local stacked-graphic oracle. Each optional parallel template is a subset of the old triangle sites. Each K4 template amounts to a delta-to-star exchange; on the dual graph it changes the corresponding degree-three star to a physical triangle, so the resulting private matrix remains cographic on the indexed replacement sites. This dual identity also follows from the binary Y/Delta identities of Oxley–Semple–Vertigan as summarized in Mayhew–Royle–Whittle https://arxiv.org/pdf/0902.0837#page=20 . Distinct participating stars share no edge, hence their centers are nonadjacent. They can be replaced in any order; other common neighboring vertices cause no problem. Carry out these star-to-triangle exchanges before deleting any remaining parallel-template ports or imposing forced slots. Deletion/contraction for forced/forbidden slots preserves cographicness. All graphical torso substitutions remain graphic. The lifted gain correction, zero-trace and map exceptions, and physical-witness reassembly are exactly those of the primal DP. When 1/2 and triangle trees are joined, preserve identified singleton ports from the old decompositions, or compute the unary table for each 3-connected component with inherited score and at most its single outgoing trace. The older 1/2 DP permits many incoming child relations and sparse exceptions.

This statement concerns the whole family over fixed parameter values. The local score must include the outgoing triangle syndrome and any bounded number of unary boundary constraints from a nested use of the 1/2 DP. One may instead assemble a single proper mixed 1/2/3 tree retaining singleton ports at their unique original torso; primal-triangle-images.md then uses at most two outgoing bits on top of the real gain dimension. In either presentation an oracle strictly limited to rank-two target planes in ambient dimension four has not been shown sufficient for all intermediate queries.
