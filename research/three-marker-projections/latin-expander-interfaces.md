# Order-independent projected interfaces after Latin row closure

Let G be a simple graph. Use one source bit x_v per vertex and a private p_e per edge uv, and the clauses (p_e or x_u or x_v). Apply the literal signed-star Latin SAT embedding with p_e on A and the endpoint literals on B,C. Each surviving binary row D_e is exactly x_u or x_v. With empty 2CNF background every edge set is closed: setting just the endpoints of a missing edge false and all other x true falsifies that edge alone. An optional row (p_e or x_u or not x_u) creates an actual opposite-sign identification but has identically true D. Hence the number of closure outputs is 2^m after absorption of such tautologies. Whether a decision implementation returns early is a separate matter.

Order all distinct source coordinates arbitrarily. Cut immediately after floor(n/2) endpoint variables x have occurred. Let M be a matching of crossing edges. Restrict p_e to zero on M and one elsewhere. Fix any unmatched x, and keep the 2|M| matched coordinates free. The residual relation is the product of |M| disjoint crossing disjunctions. Its satisfying pairs (a,1-a) form a fooling set: for distinct binary a,b at a differing coordinate at least one of (a,1-b),(b,1-a) violates its disjunction. Thus any exact union of rectangles across this cut requires 2^|M| rectangles, even allowing a shared nondeterministic tag. Deterministic prefix residual functions likewise number 2^|M|. Restricting coordinates cannot increase rectangle cover number. These conclusions concern exact projected source assignments after signed-star identification. They do not automatically cover orders of unconstrained affine combinations, decisions which discard assignments, or symbolic tensor factors permitted to straddle a cut.

If every balanced vertex cut has cn crossing edges and maximum degree is d, a greedily maximal matching contains at least cn/(2d-1) edges. Bounded-degree expanding graphs therefore give an exponential bound in the source length measured in variables and clauses, before Latin polynomial padding. A simple unconditional explicit example avoiding any expander construction is G=K_n: every balanced cut has a matching floor(n/2), and the source has O(n²) variables and clauses. Every member is satisfiable. The argument gives no decision-time lower bound, even when closure order is unrestricted.
