# Global polynomial sections for indexed wedge queries

For fixed b≥1, solve a binary linear system in ANF coefficients on 2r chart bits for all v with Kσ=v identically, each indexed coordinate of σ of degree ≤b, and (ell_j(K_i)+T_ij)σ_i=0 for both tag bits. Compare all squarefree monomials through b+1. In N^O(b) bit time obtain the subspace U of such v and sections on a basis. Write d=r−dim U. Any success extends to a full-rank tag agreement cell by physical private basis extension.

At that cell complete the private basis of U by d actual compatible physical IDs. The indexed word sections, supplemented by their fixed unit words, give a linear right inverse s of K. The indexed residues e_i+s(K_i) over agreeing i span its entire active private kernel: summing over any dependency cancels the section part. Thus two residues have nonzero alternating pairing. Pin the tags on these two IDs and the d complement IDs. Across that affine flat A (codimension k≤2d+4), both residual words are chart-dependent degree-≤b indexed words supported on agreeing IDs. Their Ω pairing is a degree-≤2b polynomial nonzero somewhere on A. If Ω is given only on the private kernel, extend it arbitrarily for this proof. Overlapping sections are allowed.

Take a deterministic family F hitting every consistent k+1-equation affine system on 2r bits, size 2^O(k)poly(r) by polynomial evaluation. It contains a∈A and F∩dir(A) spans dir(A), by appending a separating linear equation if a direction were missing. Substituting these basis directions around a into the nonzero score polynomial gives a nonzero assignment on at most 2b directions by subset inversion. Test every sum of one member of F and at most 2b more. Each trial directly tests the actual indexed agreement kernel. Recover two fundamental circuits with nonzero pairing and extend their private basis to original full rank. Time 2^O(b(d+1))N^O(b), FPT in d for fixed b. No promised small d for arbitrary inputs. Existing full-tag-deficit and universal-private-block deficit results for height determinants use local lookup sections and the same hitting principle. Coefficient matching also appears in controlled-polynomial-policies.md. Here the sections are global original-index words and Ω is arbitrary.
