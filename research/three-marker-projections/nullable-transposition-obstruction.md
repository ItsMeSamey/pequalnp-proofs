# A literal transposition cannot condition two different nullable output transpositions

Suppose one literal binary data site x acts as a transposition of its two sockets when enabled, with arbitrary fixed wiring and any number of other independent optional three-cycle sites. The observed interface consists of three named input and output tracks G,P,N; private feedback and detached circuits are allowed. Ask that every auxiliary setting at x=0 return either identity or L=(GP), and at least one return L. At x=1 the only allowed returns are identity or R=(GN), and at least one must return R. This interface is impossible, even though harmless omission is permitted in either mode.

Choose auxiliary masks A,B realizing L at x=0 and R at x=1 respectively, at minimum Hamming distance among such pairs. At A the data flip cannot give R because L and R differ on all three input images: a literal transposition can touch at most two distinct old input paths and hence change at most two images. The data flip therefore gives identity. It changes the images of G and P, requiring each of the two altered departures to lie on one of their disjoint old input paths. Both departures avoid the old input path of N.

At A with x=0, toggling any one auxiliary difference toward B must give identity; preserving L would contradict minimum distance. This changes the two old input paths of G and P. The third possible altered departure cannot lie on the old N path. If it did, the gate would cut three distinct open paths, once each. Its rotation would cyclically send all three prefixes to different output suffixes, contradicting the unchanged image of N. Therefore none of the auxiliary changes toward B alters a single departure along the old N path.

Simultaneously toggling all those auxiliaries with x still zero leaves the N path unchanged from start to end, since independent native switches act on disjoint physical departures. It still avoids both departures of the data transposition. Flipping x at B therefore preserves the output N of that path. This contradicts the target R, which sends N to G.

The restriction is on the two flat tables for all auxiliary settings, not just on favored or certified settings. It applies to a literal two-port switch implemented with a private closed third channel if no other site or observation accesses this channel. A public three-socket data control, a root-only acceptance predicate, or an extra marking condition on the auxiliaries is a different interface. The accompanying search_nullable_transmission.cpp also tests all 6,652,800 normalized two-auxiliary wirings and finds none.
