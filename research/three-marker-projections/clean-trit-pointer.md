# Two binary auxiliaries do not license three distinct output tracks of one full trit

Observe three named open tracks and one interior mark. For every physical setting require either that the mark be detached from all open paths or that all three returns be identity and the mark lie on the track named by the input trit. Every trit must have some licensed setting. Under arbitrary fixed wiring and at most two independent binary native auxiliaries, one full ternary rotation cannot meet these requirements.

Close each output to its correspondingly named input with an observed passive cut and retain the mark. A licensed setting means the three anchor cuts are on distinct cycles and the mark shares precisely the required one. Detached settings have no anchoring restrictions. Suppress all deterministic segments other than the four observations and active sockets. Each network is represented by a baseline permutation on the retained sites followed or preceded at the seam by local rotations. Marks on an active edge can always be moved onto its own passive subdivision. Any unused detached circuits do not affect observations.

Take a licensed auxiliary mask for input zero and absorb its settings into the baseline. Rebase each selected binary rotation to identity at this mask; its other setting is now an inverse rotation, normalized by renaming its sockets. At the new baseline tracing to the four passive sites gives (anchor0,mark), (anchor1), (anchor2), exactly. The checker inserts the nine distinguishable active sites sequentially, each either as a new singleton cycle or immediately after an existing site. This generates each baseline extending the specified four-site permutation exactly once because removal of the last site uniquely reverses insertion. There are 13!/4! = 259459200 possibilities.

For each candidate the checker traces the mark orbit for the twelve input/auxiliary states, permits every detached state, checks identity boundary permutation and the correct anchor when attached, and checks coverage of all three inputs. No candidate passes. The separate one-auxiliary checker enumerates all 10! unconstrained baselines and also returns zero. Adding a disjoint unused auxiliary embeds smaller budgets into the two-auxiliary model.

Fixing one input of a hypothetical two-trit nine-track device and closing the six never-licensed output tracks induces a three-lane unary device. At a detached state the closed mark circuit stays detached. At a licensed state the output action was identity and the mark still follows the retained correct track. Therefore the same lower bound applies. This excludes budgets through two binary auxiliary sites, not constructions using more sites or alternative syndrome tests.

Reproducers are check_one_trit_pointer.cpp and search_trit_two_aux.cpp in the same directory.
