# Unbounded unary substitutions for one-tag paired dependence

Consider the private one-tag problem in one-tag-joint-seam.md with one central private space and arbitrarily many disjoint indexed gadgets. Each gadget meets the center in a line B_i=<k_i>; the gadget private spaces modulo the center are in direct sum. Original central sites may also be present. The two actual words are required to have zero total private sum, share a single chart consistent with all tagged sites they use, and have indexed alternating value Ψ=1. The original form may have arbitrary rank and inter-gadget coefficients. No assumption that different k_i are independent or distinct is made.

For each child assume access to an actual row basis (1,a,b,Ψ_i(a,b)) for every feasible tuple (α,u,v) of the chart restriction to B_i and the two partial sums there. These tables can be built by the reviewed bounded-branchwidth DP on augmented layouts retaining the boundary (or brute force for constant-size gadgets). Explicitly enumerate its at most eight tuples. The three-name interval oracle in one-tag-joint-seam.md separately supplies through and default witnesses, including for a child with no supplied layout; it need not supply weighted row bases on its own. Our algorithm uses one unmarked center paired query, whose private representation consists only of true central sites and a subset of tagged parallel virtual copies at the k_i, and polynomially many deterministic three-name trace queries. It returns two original words and their common chart whenever the center paired query is certifying.

For each chart value α on B_i that enables a physical through word choose one through word r_iα and its chart. Put one virtual site k_i tagged α(k_i) at the center. No copy is needed for a value without through. Map a virtual site linearly to r_iα by J, and a retained central site to its original site. Only one copy of any one gadget is enabled in any one chart. For fixed α and tuple u,v, default to the multiples of r_iα with those partial sums, or zero if through is unavailable. A zero default always has a chart extending α even when no sites are active. The checked center paired query with form J*Ψ detects actual all-default positives. Any such word pair lifts in the single combined chart using those physical defaults.

Assume now a positive physical pair exists. Hold its center chart, actual central words, and each child's (α,u,v) fixed. Vary independently between actual and default at each child. Each substitution preserves both total sums and chart compatibility. In its Boolean switch expansion around defaults, Ψ is multilinear of degree at most two. If the baseline is zero, a positive assignment implies a nonzero singleton or mixed coefficient. Conversely any nonzero coefficient yields a positive assignment among its at most four Boolean support subsets. It suffices to test these coefficients. For each exception state use actual basis rows, not arbitrary vectors from a span: at fixed exterior/default and tuples the test is linear in its homogeneous row (including the local Ψ coordinate), or bilinear in the two such rows for a mixed coefficient. Thus a positive coefficient is detected for some enumerated actual row(s). These guesses and the number of exception choices are polynomial.

For two exceptions i,j let Δa_i,Δb_i be a local row minus its fixed default, and similarly for j. The mixed coefficient is Ψ(Δa_i,Δb_j)+Ψ(Δa_j,Δb_i); outside cancels. If one, check that the default macro exterior (all actual central sites and enabled virtual copies of the other gadgets) has two partially balanced macro words with required sums u_i+u_j, v_i+v_j in B=B_i+B_j, in one chart with both specified restrictions. Reject incompatible restrictions on shared lines. This is exactly the lifted interval test with named space B⊕F₂_tag of dimension at most three. Recover both outside words and their chart, lift J, and evaluate the four combinations to return an original positive pair.

For a single exception i relabel the two partial words by one invertible binary 2-by-2 transformation chosen from its tuple so that the second outgoing sum is zero. The first outgoing sum u is zero or k_i. Apply the transformation to its physical row and default and conceptually to all other words. Every alternating pairing is unchanged (determinant one) and the other defaults, each a common linear lift, transform compatibly. Thus there is no further constraint imposed on the transformed outside words beyond sums u,0. For that exceptional row and default, the singleton coefficient as a function of outside macro words x,y equals
c + L(x) + M(y),
where c is the difference of the two internal alternating values, L(x)=Ψ(Jx,Δb_i) and M(y)=Ψ(Δa_i,Jy). The chart α on B_i is prescribed. The zero outside second word is always allowed whenever a first word exists. Consequently this coefficient can be one iff at least one of these tests succeeds:

1. A matched first word of sum u has L(x)=1+c, in a chart extending α. Take y=0.
2. One chart extending α has a matched first word x of sum u and a matched second word y of sum zero with M(y)=1. Keep x and compare y with zero to force the coefficient to one.

For test 1 lift each outside macro column to (k,t,L_site). In N=B_i⊕F₂_tag⊕F₂_score require the trace to contain (u,α(u),1+c), and to lie in {(h,α(h),s):h∈B_i, s∈F₂}. For test 2 instead append M and try the two possibilities d=M(x). Require the same single trace to contain (u,α(u),d) and (0,0,1) and obey the same upper containment. Both traces have named dimension at most three. The upper condition excludes all tag conflicts, including on private-zero words of nonzero score, so the partial chart extends along B_i. Solve the requested sums physically on its chosen columns. Evaluate the actual/default pair of all expanded words to select a positive result. The inverse GL transformation is available if the original ordered tuple is desired. This treats arbitrary affine constants and zeros without appending two scalar rows at once.

The rank-three trace test works for arbitrary central matrices, including after removing the exceptional virtual groups. For every success use the one common exterior chart and the stored local charts, extending consistently by the direct-sum-modulo-center property; verify the original sums, tag equations and Ψ value. The total overhead after tables uses polynomially many rank-three trace calls and linear algebra with polynomial bit complexity. If the center belongs to a tractable class closed under the deletion/parallel substitutions above, this yields a tractable decorated star even with unbounded degree and bands whose total width is unbounded. This is a reduction for a star, not a proof of polynomial computation of weighted tables on arbitrary deep 2-sum trees. Width-two seam menus have an additional genuine incompatibility, as exhibited in one-tag-joint-seam.md. The reduction varies the central queries and uses actual witnesses; it is outside the scope of unchanged-host Boolean rank-probe obstructions.
