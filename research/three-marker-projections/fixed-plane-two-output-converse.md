# Embedding an arbitrary two-tag two-output local relation into a physical fixed plane

Input columns q_i spanning a binary q-dimensional space, tags A_i,B_i and outputs C_i,D_i. Seek a dependency m on the q_i with two physical matching functionals for A,B on supp(m), and output (C·m,D·m)=(0,1). Any other nonzero prescribed two-bit target reduces to this by a binary output-basis change. This is one relation, not an exact full two-dimensional image.

Build a generator matrix for a new physical dependency code Dnew. Its row coefficients consist of the q private coefficients a, two extra independent private bits b,b', and five distinguished coefficients X,Y,Z1,Z2,H. The first three groups (a,b,b') are called radical coordinates. Encode each original physical column by its q part q_i, zeros in b,b', and distinguished tuple (A_i,B_i,C_i,0,D_i). For each of b,b' add a separate pair of columns with private projection that unit and distinguished tuples 0 and (0,0,0,1,0). Finally add all 24 columns with private projection zero and distinguished tuple s∈F₂⁵ having (s_X,s_Y) nonzero. These last columns span the entire distinguished space (differences yield last-three units), and the two auxiliary pairs and spanning q_i give full row rank. An actual new private matrix K is any parity check for Dnew. Solve for an indexed new physical tag t whose dot product with each coefficient word equals its H coefficient. Both computations are Gaussian elimination.

Every H-even dependency u is individually in a matching chart. For any H-odd w, choose one dummy tuple so u evaluates to zero and w evaluates to one: choose (s_X,s_Y) nonzero in the kernel of the single linear predicate u_X s_X+u_Y s_Y, and put s_Z1=s_Z2=0; finally choose s_H to make w evaluate to one. Thus no odd dependency is supported inside supp(u), and consistency of tag t there follows from linear duality on its full supported dependency space. Conversely any matched word is even, so the actual aggregate L equals the full H=0 code. Define Ω on L by the standard alternating form on coordinate pairs X,Y and Z1,Z2, zero on all private coefficients. Extend to indexed words by a linear left inverse of the encoding. Its rank on L is four. Fix the nondegenerate XY-plane in the quotient modulo its radical. By a GL2 basis change on a positive ordered pair in that plane, normalized lifts of the pure X and Y coefficient vectors suffice. This operation preserves union of supports and the common chart.

A matching new chart is witnessed by mismatch M=t+Kᵀg. On the fixed plane the allowed pair of lifts must vanish on supp(M). The two radical corrections are independently chosen coefficient triples (a,b,b') for the two words. On each dummy the X,Y bits cannot both vanish, so M is zero there. On an auxiliary pair, orthogonality to its private coordinate forces the two mismatch bits equal; it can supply either zero or one unit of Z2 signature, with no contribution on other distinguished coordinates. On original sites M needs q parity zero, and disjointness means A_i=a_X·q_i and B_i=a_Y·q_i wherever M_i=1; the other terms Z1,Z2,H do not appear in the normalized lifts. For M to be a chart mismatch it must also give the prescribed signatures 0 on X,Y,Z1,Z2 and one on H. The first two vanish automatically by the matching equations and q parity; Z1,H prescribe exactly (C·m,D·m)=(0,1). Z2 is zero on original sites, so auxiliary default zero suffices. Hence any original local solution extends by zeros on additional mismatches with auxiliary radical corrections zero and yields a new chart by solving Kᵀg=M+t. Conversely restricting M to the original sites and reading the two q-coefficient corrections recovers an original q relation and both matching maps. This is a polynomial reduction with original indexed traceback in both directions. It remains valid with repeated columns or q=0.

For a connected q representation its physical radical components are that single original block, two line blocks, and inert dummies. The original block may have arbitrary width. Thus the exceptional two-output residual in radical-block-signatures.md genuinely contains the complete two-tag/two-output local problem. This construction addresses only the designated plane; a different projected plane might be jointly reachable regardless of the original decision. The separate executable radical-four-independent-obstruction.py (97c1e85e) gives opposite fixed-plane instances where all scalar block probes coincide, including after checking independence of all four residual local rows.
