# Constructing a bounded backwards frontier on a fixed order

Fix a graph and vertex order. Among all digraphs obtained by assigning each physical edge either or both directions, require a unique sink strongly connected component on each connected induced subgraph. Let w* be the minimum backwards frontier (count of distinct processed vertices admitting an arc into them from a future vertex, maximized over cuts). A polynomial algorithm constructs a qualifying digraph of width at most 2w*, without a supplied orientation.

We may include every increasing arc at no cost. Adding arcs preserves admissibility: on any connected induced subset every site previously reached a common site, and still does. The original unique-sink criterion is equivalent to this reachability condition. For each increasing physical edge e=a→u introduce a variable x_e that selects also the reverse u→a. Build an undirected conflict graph on these variables. Join e=a→u and f=b→v when an induced undirected path of length at least two has extremities u,v, starts u,a and ends b,v. Endpoints u,v must be nonadjacent, and a=b is allowed. Test by deleting all other neighbors of u and all other neighbors of v, checking survival of a,b, and checking connectivity between them without u,v. A shortest surviving middle path yields an induced witness. Edges in a conflict pair are distinct. The obstruction criterion in cyclic-parent-charges.md shows the selected variables must form, and suffice as, a vertex cover of this conflict graph: a terminal arc stays one-way into the endpoint exactly when its reverse was not selected. Interior arc choices do not affect induction of the physical path.

For each cut j and earlier site a put a variable y_{a,j}. Minimize t subject to 0≤x,y≤1, x_e+x_f≥1 for each conflict, y_{a,j}≥x_e for each e=a→u with a processed and u unprocessed, and sum_a y_{a,j}≤t for each j. All constraints are polynomial in n; the rational LP has polynomial bit complexity. Integral x,y model the objective exactly, so t_LP≤w*. Select all x_e≥1/2 from an optimal rational solution. Each conflict is covered. Every active head at a cut after rounding has its own y_{a,j} at least one half, hence at most 2t_LP distinct active heads occur on any cut. A resulting physical width/witness can be checked directly. This proves the approximation only for this supplied order; it can be combined with any polynomial list of generated orders.

Together with cyclic-parent-charges.md this gives a deterministic n^{O(dp+w*+1)} procedure on the given order for fixed number of colors and binary score coordinates. No direction choices or frontier layout beyond the vertex order need be supplied. If w* grows the procedure remains exact with a larger bound. The LP does not certify optimum integrality.
