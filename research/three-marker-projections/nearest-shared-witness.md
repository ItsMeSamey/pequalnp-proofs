# Recovering shared Boolean witnesses without guard-pair exclusivity

For each Boolean selector i sum its actual occurrence bivectors inside the guard summands: X_{ia}=Σ_g w_{g,a}. A fixed option contributes exp(X_{ia}), since simple w satisfy exp(w)=1+w and even forms commute. Put B_i=X_{i1}−X_{i0} and require B_i³=0, equivalently skew rank at most four over Q. This is checkable with no bound on occurrence count or shared selectors per pair of guards. In particular two guards can share arbitrarily many selectors when each is used once per guard with local variation rank at most two.

Give option 1 probability p_i. Its factor is exp(X_{i0})[(1−p_i)+p_i exp(B_i)], with logarithm X_{i0}+p_i B_i+p_i(1−p_i)B_i²/2. If each p_i is p or 1−p, the nonlinear correction is p(1−p)Σ B_i²/2 regardless of which values are preferred. Let t be its support dimension at p=1/2. Changing preferences leaves t fixed. Each original-menu weighted expectation is evaluated in 2^{O(t)}poly(N) by direct quadratic and quartic accumulation and complementary principal Pfaffians.

Let W(σ) be the product of the nonnegative integer marked cofactors for assignment σ, excluding constant coincident-mark guards. It is positive exactly when σ succeeds. For L Boolean labels and N_w physical wires, Hadamard gives W(σ)≤3^{N_w}. Set K=1+2^L 3^{N_w} and p=1/(1+K). For a preference word y give the nonpreferred value probability p. The expectation divided by (1−p)^L is Σ W(σ) K^{−dist(σ,y)}. At minimum distance d a nearest witness contributes at least K^{-d}; if all witnesses have distance at least d+1 the total is less than K^{-d}. Computed and physical cofactor scales differ by the same fixed positive factor at all preferences. The integer K has polynomial bit length. Fixed singleton menus retain probability one.

If the initial score is zero there is no common witness. Otherwise test the current word on all original physical guards. When it fails, move to a one-bit neighbor of maximum score. A neighbor reducing distance exists, and the strict score separation ensures every maximizing neighbor reduces it. At most L moves and O(L²) evaluations recover an original common satisfying assignment without conditionally deleting any local term. native_preference_witness.py reports an error if the cubic-rank test fails. Its checker includes two equality guards sharing both bits.
