# Three alternating markers via all two-marker projections

For a symmetric zero-diagonal guard matrix, collect the attainable three-bit labels Lx. In deterministic polynomial time one can recover the status of at least seven of the eight labels, constructing witnesses for positives. If at least two labels are unattainable, the procedure recovers the entire image. This leaves a possible singleton hole even when every two-marker projection is onto.

For each nonzero three-bit vector h take the quotient by its span. This is a two-bit map, so the alternating two-marker solver can test every quotient value and provide one witness when feasible. Its four fibers are the unordered pairs {z,z+h}. The seven directions visit every pair of distinct labels exactly once, giving 28 tests.

A failed test certifies both its endpoints absent. If any pair fails, all absent labels occur in failed pairs, and each present label is witnessed by the test pairing it with a missing label. If every pair passes, the returned witness labels cover every edge of the complete graph on eight labels. Hence at most one label lacks a witness. The possibility of a singleton hole occurs already on the connected four-vertex path with edges 03,01,12 and columns (1,1,2,4): among its independent sets all labels except 7 occur, and every feasible support on a forest is independent. The full label span in general gives at least five attainable labels even before projection: three independent singleton columns and zero give four. Among those three vertices, a nonedge supplies a new pair sum, or their triangle supplies the triple sum.

Local complementation with affine marker transport translates the entire attainable set by the column at the complemented vertex. Consequently a sequence that restores the original graph and columns (possibly by a common vertex relabeling) gives a period equal to the sum of the successive columns at the chosen centers. An explicit nonzero period resolves any singleton candidate: translate a known attainable label by this period or observe that absences come in pairs. The assertion does not require every period to come from such a sequence.

## Disconnected instances

Components with only zero columns can be discarded. If there are at least two remaining components, the entire three-bit image and witnesses can be computed in deterministic polynomial time. Compute the witnessed sets separately. A component whose table is incomplete has at least seven witnessed labels. Any other component contributes at least two distinct witnessed labels, either by the complete-table argument or by its own seven witnesses. The sum of two subsets of the eight-element group having combined size greater than eight is the whole group, since any translate of one meets the other. Thus the XOR sums of witnessed labels already exhaust the attainable answers in this case. If all tables are complete, ordinary XOR convolution of the sets is exact. Taking disjoint unions of component witnesses provides actual solutions.

The implementation uses the sibling two-markers solver. Run python -B check.py to check every graph and three-bit labeling through dimension three and 500 larger seeded cases.
