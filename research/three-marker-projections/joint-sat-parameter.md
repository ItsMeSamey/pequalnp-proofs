# SAT compilation and measured support on actual formulas

Normalize a signed 3CNF by deleting tautological clauses and duplicate literals within each remaining clause. An empty clause is immediately unsatisfiable. For each literal occurrence use a distinct Boolean clone. For a variable with d clones put d−1 separate equality guards on a path through its clones. Use a single native equality guard per link (two binary sites, feedback (0,3,4,2,1,5), marks 1,2). The clone assignment satisfying these guards projects directly to a single assignment of the original variables. Conversely every source assignment extends by copying its value. Variables that disappear under tautology removal may be assigned arbitrarily.

For a clause of length k use path rails 0 through k and one optional swap between each consecutive pair, enabled when its original literal is false. Each two-port swap uses an original binary rotation on three sockets with private feedback on the third. Close the path rails in their serial order and mark the endpoints. A product of the transpositions corresponding to edges of any forest has a single orbit on each nontrivial component: adding any forest edge joins different components and therefore merges the corresponding cycles. Thus the endpoints join precisely when every clause literal is false. A fixed interchange of the two marked incoming destinations reverses this predicate because composing a permutation with a transposition merges two marked cycles or splits their common cycle. The resulting native guard tests the original OR. The program expresses the full circuit on the original 3k entrances.

Each clone occurs once in a clause and in at most two equality guards. Distinct equality guards share at most one clone, and a clause and equality guard share at most one because its two endpoints belong to different normalized clauses. Thus the net-t common-witness algorithm of joint-native-cofactors.md gives deterministic 2^{O(t)}poly(N) on this actual SAT image, with direct recovery. This gives a usable parameterized solver for standard SAT. It neither turns those separate controlled occurrences into a single native physical site nor bounds t logarithmically.

The image has a strong lower bound on t. Every nonconstant guard genuinely depending on an occurrence has nonzero difference bivector there: otherwise all its physical projected determinants would be identical for both options at fixed other sites, contrary to two physical settings giving different join verdicts. Any participating guard sharing a live occurrence with another live guard has a nonzero exposed bivector in its own direct summand of the total support, contributing at least two dimensions. Every equality guard participates. In a normalized connected clause intersection graph with at least two vertices, every clause participates. Consequently t≥2 times the number of compiled guards on this connected image. This rules out a growing connected bounded-arity SAT family with sublinear t relative to the number of clauses under this direct compilation.

For n≥2 let the formula consist of x₁, the n−1 implications x_i⇒x_{i+1}, and a final unit x_n or ¬x_n. Both variants have n+1 clauses, occurrence bound two and incidence treewidth one. They are already Horn and bijunctive. The positive-final formula is satisfiable and the negative-final one is unsatisfiable. They give 2n+1 native guards and ambient dimension 8n. At a unary guard its variation bivector spans its two-dimensional space. At each binary OR or equality guard both sites vary, each difference has rank two, and the two supports together span its four-dimensional quotient. These are finite local checks on the displayed fixed cells; reversing a literal only negates its difference. All variations are exposed by unique shared selectors. Thus t=8n on both families. Direct measurement at n=2,3,4,6,8 gives respectively 16,24,32,48,64.

The disjoint binary-clause family ∧_{i≤m}(x_i∨y_i) gives t=0 on the original compilation for every m: the fixed binary OR cell has zero nonlinear correction when its two selectors occur only there. These clauses have 3^m satisfying assignments and depend on all 2m variables. A strong affine backdoor requires m variables: if one clause is untouched, choose a restriction of the proposed backdoor that satisfies all touched clauses; the untouched two-variable projection still has three points and hence is not affine over F2. Conversely one variable per clause leaves at most units and unrestricted variables. This separation uses disconnected clause components and does not evade the connected-image lower bound. The path examples exhibit the opposite behavior against Horn and bijunctive backdoors.
