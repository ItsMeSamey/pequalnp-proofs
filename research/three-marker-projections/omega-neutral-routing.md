# Two-tag routing for an arbitrary original-index alternating form

Let C=ker K. A supplied set of physical router IDs has tag zero after subtracting a verified baseline linear private prediction. Let N be their coordinate subspace. Require C∩N to be in the radical of the given alternating form on C. This is a polynomial basis-pair check. Equivalently the indexed form on C extends to an ambient alternating form having zero rows at all these router IDs: descend the form to C/(C∩N) inside the quotient by N, extend a basis there, and give complementary basis vectors zero pairings.

In a supplied private subspace U of codimension d, require that for every two-bit map ℓ the router columns in U∩ker ℓ span U∩ker ℓ. An explicit polynomial certificate gives a basis of U and a router ID for every nonempty sum of up to three distinct basis vectors. Under each map the minimal binary dependencies among the two-bit images have length at most three; these dependencies generate its kernel. The certificate does not require every nonzero vector of U.

Fix a feasible pair x,y on an agreement set for a common tag predictor, and use an ambient zero-router-row extension of the form. After baseline subtraction project private columns modulo U. The labels consisting of the private quotient, two tag bits and the scalar Ω(e_i,y) have sum (0,0,1) over x. Select at most d+3 original indices from x giving the same sum, obtaining x0. Now Ω(x0,y)=1; perform the same selection inside y with score Ω(x0,e_i) to obtain y0. Both have zero total quotient and tags, and Ω(x0,y0)=1. The original predictor agrees on the union of at most 2d+6 selected columns. Every predictor agreeing on that union satisfies Kx0,Ky0∈U∩ker ℓ. Repair the two private sums independently by real, agreeing router columns. All routers annihilate the auxiliary extension, so Ω stays one; the corrected words are private cycles, where this is exactly the original form. Their supports lie in a common physical cell. Shared router IDs or overlap do not cause a problem.

Run the actual predictor hitting algorithm at p=min(r,2d+6); consider its agreement kernels directly. The proof forces some sampled predictor to agree on the short selectors and supply the repair columns, without bounding the rank of the repaired pair. This now decides the full existence problem on the certified class, with original physical basis and both words. Complexity is 8^(2d+6)poly(input) and is polynomial for d=O(log input). One can replace 2d+6 by 2δ+2 where δ is the joint rank of the quotient-private and residual tag rows. No bound on the rank of the original alternating form is needed. The implementation uses the former parameter.

Source implementation: omega_neutral_routing.py and omega_lift_hitting.py. This extends the one-tag neutral kernel sampling in ../span-separation/neutral-routing.md to two tags and a common physical alternating witness by two dependent score selections. It makes no generic SAT hardness or unrestricted polynomial claim.
