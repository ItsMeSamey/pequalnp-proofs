# Exponentially rare compatible anchors, including after conditioning on a known good polar type

Use the exact nine- and eleven-site seeds in check-fixed-circuit-anchors.py and check-fixed-circuit-rigid.py. Replace a selected nonzero physical column k_f by a chain of L diamonds: use terminal positions 0,k_f, distinct intermediate private vertex directions z_i between consecutive stages, and two new private direction vertices per stage. Include the four edge columns on the two length-two paths across each stage, computed as the binary difference of endpoint positions. All fresh coordinates are independent of the old ambient space. Assign local edge tags as differences of fixed vertex bits, with bit 0 at position 0, bit t_f at position k_f and e.g. 0 internally. Every through path sums to k_f and has tag sum t_f. No old parallel copy of f is retained. On any physical dependency define π as identity outside and at f use the parity of the two edges incident to the terminal at k_f. Extend this linearly to all indexed words and use Ψ'=π*Ψ.

Every physical dependency has balanced internal vertex parities, so the two terminal parities agree; hence π sends dependencies to dependencies. A closed internal subgraph has even degree everywhere. If a circuit uses any outside edge, its gadget restriction cannot contain a nonempty internal cycle, by minimality. A balanced-internal forest is empty or a single terminal-to-terminal path: any nonempty component has at least two leaves, and leaves must be among the two terminals; branch degree above two would require additional leaves. For a through path the projected outside edges together with f form a circuit of the old matrix: any smaller dependency would lift by the same path if it uses f, otherwise unchanged. Conversely replacing f in an old circuit by any of the 2^L paths gives a new circuit by the same parity/minimality reasoning. Entirely gadget circuits project zero (a nonzero terminal parity would require the nonzero old vector k_f to cancel outside).

An active through path forces the old chart to match t_f at k_f; t_f telescopes on that path. More generally an active subgraph with odd terminal parities connects the terminals, so one such path exists. Thus every physical common pair projects to an old common pair. When f is active in an old chart, assign every fresh vertex its fixed tag bit (the endpoints already agree with their vertex bits), making all gadget edges active; if f is inactive, old matched words avoid f and require no gadget changes. This proves surjectivity πL'=L on aggregate spans. If an old anchor circuit avoids f and is completable, it retains a common mate, lifting at f if necessary. If it uses f and is completable, every path lift has a common mate by the same all-edge extension. In particular completion status of old circuit projections is exact, and equality of their polar functionals on L is equivalent to equality of lifted functionals on L'. Fresh vertex axes and distinct two-edge routes ensure simplicity. Connectedness of the matroid is retained: old circuits through f and all corresponding lifted paths link every new edge to an outside edge and reconnect all old circuit chains that formerly used f.

For the nine-site seed choose f=4 (zero-based). The complete eligible balanced circuit list is [75,196,263,332,392,451]; none contains f. Mask 61 does and is balanced. Since Ψ|L is nondegenerate and 61 is nonzero, all 2^L of its matched circuit lifts have nonzero polar and no mate. Exactly the six old completable balanced circuit anchors remain. The number of sites is 4L+8. Thus even uniform sampling among balanced circuits of nonzero polar finds a positive anchor with probability at most 6/(6+2^L).

For the eleven-site seed choose f=5. Enumerating its 32 balanced circuits shows precisely three have the polar of mask 30: 30,682,1667. Of those only 30 is completable; 30 avoids f and 682 contains it. Hence for the announced polar type, known to contain a positive anchor, the whole family has exactly one completable balanced circuit and at least 2^L incompatible balanced circuits of that SAME nonzero type. Uniform sampling within the correct type succeeds with probability at most 1/(1+2^L). The enumeration uses comparison of the polar on the complete supplied basis of L and actual common charts. Counts concern individually indexed circuit supports. Although the second seed was internally 4-connected, the expanded graph has genuine unary seams and is not claimed internally 4-connected. These tests limit uniform sampling strategies; they give neither decision hardness nor an information lower bound for arbitrary host-aware representative selection.
