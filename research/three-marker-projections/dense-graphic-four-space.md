# Exact planes with arbitrary dense names and extra columns over a highly connected spanning graph

Let all ordinary and named columns be binary even vertex vectors. Suppose the ordinary columns include the edges of a supplied spanning 600-vertex-connected simple graph G. Other ordinary columns are arbitrary, in any number. For any four-dimensional named space U and plane H⊆U, there is a deterministic polynomial-time test with physical witness recovery. If attainable, H can be realized with graph edges and at most ONE extra column. There is no sparsity assumption on U or the other columns.

Test G alone by research/span-separation/high-connectivity.md; its strong connectivity lets any abstract ordered vertex partition prescribing sums of r-bit labels in h+1 parts be realized by connected parts if connectivity ≥h+2+21hr. In particular graph-only feasibility of H is equivalent to an unrestricted partition of the vertices into three parts with sums q1,q2,q1+q2, where q1,q2 are any basis of the annihilator H^perp in the dual of U. Give each vertex its U-coordinate evaluation as label. Component sums of a spanning forest annihilate exactly its trace. If the component sums span H^perp choose two independent ones and merge the other parts, obtaining all three distinct nonzero points of the plane since total is zero. Never select an ordinary column in U outside H, which would contradict the trace requirement. We may discard all such columns except when computing graph-only feasibility using the original G; every output chosen by the graph test automatically avoids them.

If H contains a vertex pair, apply dense-graphic-root-pair.md, which covers the entire case. Assume now that graph-only feasibility failed and that every nonzero element of H has vertex weight at least four. The following structural reduction then applies.

For an abstract rank-r vertex-label multiset spanning its dual-coordinate space, discard zero labels. If some nonzero type x occurs at least twice, project the labels and prescribed sums by x. Feasibility of the three-part assignment is unchanged: reserve two copies of x, which disappear in projection, and use one for each of the first two parts as needed to adjust its sum, dumping unused copies into the third. Total zero fixes the third sum. If the two projected target sums have rank ≤1 a feasible assignment always exists: use one subset with the required nonzero sum and its complement of the same sum, or the empty subset for zero parts. If no type repeats, after a change of coordinates prescribe the first two unit sums and exhaust the at most 15 distinct nonzero types. The finite table in graphic-plane-small-labels.py checks exactly all spanning zero-total simple lists. In ranks 2,3,4 it tests 1,8,1892 lists respectively, of which 0,1,48 fail. Every simple rank-four failing list has a nonzero functional annihilating both targets and supported on two occurrences. In rank three the only simple failing list is {4,5,6,7} for target sums 1,2; the annihilating functional 4 is one on all four sites. A rank-two nonsimple list likewise cannot fail, by another projection. The explicit check is exhaustive since the ordinary bipartition of each type between the first two bins or the background is exactly the finite two-sum recurrence in the script.

The original pair-free failure thus has a repeated type x. Its rank-three projection must still have independent target sums, so x does not belong to their original plane. It cannot have another repeated nonzero projected type, as a second projection gives rank at most two. Therefore exactly four original vertices P have nonzero labels modulo x; all remaining labels are 0 or x. The three-dimensional subspace of U annihilating x has zero evaluations outside P and even total, so by dimension it is the full even space on P. Its intersection with H is the one line on the full four-vector p=P by the rank-three table. Write H=span(p,q), picking either other vector q of H. Then U=Even(P)+span(q), each originally repeated x has q-evaluation one, and q has at least two charged outside vertices. Both q and q+p have weight ≥4.

We now solve this obstruction. If all allowable extra columns are graphic (weight zero or two), their presence does not change feasibility. Any successful graphic forest on the larger graph would furnish a feasible abstract vertex partition, already ruled out by the G-only test. Conversely consider any allowable even column z of weight ≥4. There is an abstract partition into four parts, each with a different P vertex, on which the q-sums are constant (all even or all odd) and the z-sums are all odd. Here are the full finite details. Outside P ignore sites zero on both vectors, and count types (1,0),(0,1),(1,1) with multiplicities a,b,c. A type repeated n times can contribute exactly the four-bin parity masks of weight ≤n and parity n. Thus replace counts ≥5 by 3 or 4 of the same parity, retaining exactly the same masks. This preserves parity, the independent conditions a+c≥2, the support-membership tests below, and all even weight lower bounds ≥4: a truncated odd count contributes at least three and any even total including it is at least four. For the four marked sites write Q,Z as four-bit masks. All even vectors supported on P belong to U, and outside P a member of U has evaluations either zero or q. Hence z belongs to U precisely if b=c=0 or a=b=0. In those cases the corresponding test for membership in H requires Z or Z XOR Q respectively in {0,15}. For all remaining allowable patterns graphic-plane-four-bin-check.py checks the claimed partition using exact four-bit mask addition for these three types. All 6,222 admissible truncated patterns pass. The enumeration checks z≥4, nonzero H weights≥4, and excludes only z in U outside H.

The abstract partition can be made connected by the high-connectivity theorem with six labels (q,z and four individual terminal indicators) and h=3: the required bound is 383. On the connected graph-edge parts the map from Even(P) to component sums is the identity on even four-bit masks, while q has image zero or 1111 and z has image 1111. The preimage of span(1111) in U is exactly H. Thus adding the single physical z makes the trace H. One can find the partition by the finite profile computation with traceback, distributing all actual type multiplicities, and then the connected partition solver. Alternatively enumerate all candidates on U+span(z), as in dense-graphic-root-pair.md, which avoids explicit pin bookkeeping. The number of labels stays fixed.

This theorem uses an actual supplied spanning graphic incidence restriction of high vertex connectivity. The seven-terminal dual obstruction does not supply such a spanning graphic chart. It concerns exact existence and search, and gives no formula for the parity of the number of realizing forests.
