# Prescribed charged spanning partitions with a bounded dominating set

Let graph G be connected with vertex charges q(v) in F2^d totaling zero, and prescribe three nonzero distinct sums totaling zero. For an ordinary dominating set D of size k, guess its three colors. Every outsider chooses a fixed neighboring owner in D; its baseline color is its owner's. Each D member has its own prescribed baseline. Protect all D and guess a distinct additional protected root for every color absent from D. Let f_i(v) be v's contribution to the pair of first and second shore sums in color i. Write b(v) for its baseline color and delta_i(v)=f_i(v)+f_b(v)(v) in F2^(2d).

Seek three disjoint colored trees retaining all protected vertices in their assigned colors. Give every omitted vertex its physical owner's color; it attaches directly to the retained owner so all parts are connected. The score is the known baseline plus the sum of deltas on retained vertices. A true partition gives initial trees spanning its parts. Minimize their total retained size subject to that score. If more than 2d unprotected leaves remain, remove a nonempty subset whose deltas sum to zero. All three trees stay nonempty and connected since each has a protected vertex; the reassignment to owners preserves the score. This contradicts minimality. Thus at most 2d unprotected leaves remain across the three trees. Singleton trees contain protected vertices.

Suppress all unprotected degree-two vertices. The three tree shapes each have only leaves, branching vertices and protected sites left, together O(k+d+1) by tree degree sums. Enumerate the shapes, distinct physical images of their remaining vertices respecting all prescribed sites, and the binary delta sum of the interior of every branch. Add contributions of each pattern vertex once and of each branch interior once, checking against the desired baseline correction. For each branch guess length one, length two, or longer. Verify direct edges and reserve distinct single interior vertices for length two, checking their charges. For longer branches guess distinct first and last interior vertices adjacent to the ends; these avoid all pattern vertices and other guessed interior sites. Delete all pattern vertices and reserved length-two interiors, then jointly link the first/last pairs of the long branches by vertex-disjoint paths. A path may be a single edge.

For each possible color i, label each available edge xy by the coordinatewise sum in (Z4)^(2d) of the binary 0/1 lifts of delta_i(x) and delta_i(y); concatenate all three blocks. A demand with middle endpoints x,y (which are the first and last internal vertices of the original branch) and required interior XOR s is constrained in its own color block to 2s−delta_i(x)−delta_i(y) mod 4. All middle-path internal vertices are counted twice and endpoints once, giving the equivalence. Forbid the three unwanted residues per relevant coordinate with Liu and Yoo Theorem 1.3 https://people.tamu.edu/~yyoo/stoc25.pdf . Other color blocks are unconstrained. If no long branches exist, simply check the guessed finite edges. A successful linkage yields the spanning partition by the owner completion. Enumerate D in n^O(k) if it is not supplied. Running time is polynomial for fixed k,d, with an exponent O(k+d+1); no common-color assumption on D and no width or genus bound is needed.
