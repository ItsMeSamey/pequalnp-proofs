# Normalizing cyclic seam with two exterior marks

Each explicit block B_i has a permutation generator g_i. The allowed setting acts on it by h_i in ⟨g_i⟩, followed by a fixed wiring bijection F_i to the next block. The block feedback is a permutation. Require F_i⟨g_i⟩F_i⁻¹=⟨g_next⟩. The allowed simultaneous tuples h form a subgroup L supplied by generator tuples of actual permutations; alternatively the local choices are explicit independent finite lists. Marks are on exterior wires, identified with input sites. Physical recovery uses a supplied constructive lift as detailed below.

Different block cycles cannot join. Otherwise number the relevant block cycle B_0,…,B_(l−1) with marks p in B_0 and q in B_j. Put T_i=F_(i−1)…F_0 with T_0 identity. By normalization E_i=T_i⁻¹ h_i T_i is a power of g_0. The actual prefix to B_j is T_j times the product of E_i for i<j; the full lap is T_l times the product for i<l. This follows by multiplying F_i h_i in physical order. All E_i commute and the two products define homomorphisms on L.

Let d be the length of the g_0 orbit of p. For each generator of L evaluate both products on p and compare to the d powers g_0^k(p), obtaining a pair (s,t) modulo d for prefix and lap. Compute the generated subgroup of (Z/dZ)² by breadth-first additions with generator backpointers. At most d² pairs occur. Powers agreeing modulo d act identically on EVERY g_0 orbit of length d. T_l normalizes the full cyclic group, hence permutes its orbits of length d. For each attainable (s,t), follow p under T_l g_0^t until return. Accept iff some visited y obeys T_j g_0^s(y)=q. These are exactly the visits to B_j in the original permutation; j=0 includes the initial site. This works with nontransitive generators and even if the order of g_0 on the entire block is large.

Recover a realizing generator word from breadth-first backpointers. A physical mask requires a constructive lift of that word to a setting; an abstract promise about images alone furnishes no such lift. For independent explicit local modes use a layered DP adding the contribution pair of exactly one actual listed mode per block rather than taking the group generated by a list. Recover chosen modes via backpointers. Given native marginal witness masks under the all-settings block-preservation hypothesis, combine them by the path-preserving pruning algorithm of native-block-factorization.md. Separate disjoint local modules permit direct concatenation. Trace the physical setting when returned.

Time is polynomial in the explicit permutation and mode or generator lists, plus the stated physical lifting cost. Normalization is recognizable: to check membership in a cyclic subgroup, verify that a permutation preserves each generator orbit and acts there by a constant shift. Test compatibility of the orbit congruences for one exponent by the generalized Chinese remainder theorem. Check both conjugate-generator inclusions. A supplied offset comprising local generator powers can be absorbed into fixed maps. Arbitrary interior marks or wiring that splits a block among multiple successors are outside this theorem.

With independent modes and whole-block feedback of bounded block size b, one can instead retain both prefix and lap permutations, with at most (b!)² states per layer, without normalization. With correlated repetition bits and merely whole-block feedback of width five, consecutive stages can encode Barrington permutation programs. The normalizing hypothesis is substantive.
