# Recognizing the unary stationary-menu class in original coordinates

Given the physical represented binary matrix alone, compute its connected components and a 3-connected tree decomposition into 2-sums (canonical Cunningham--Edmonds convention, amalgamate adjacent circuit nodes or adjacent cocircuit nodes). Bixby--Cunningham's separation algorithm, and its polynomial assembly and actual side identification, are described in Oxley--Welsh, Chromatic, Flow, and Reliability Polynomials: the Complexity of their Coefficients, https://www.math.lsu.edu/~oxley/jodw.pdf (section containing the construction of a 3-connected tree decomposition). Matroid independence calls are binary rank tests. Keep original indices. Distinct connected component subtrees can be attached to a single empty root with contact zero.

At any oriented node use the physical region E0 of its subtree and the disjoint strict-child input sets Ei. Its true local sites are E0 minus their union; here strict-child input means all originals owned below the child, with no virtual shared site counted. For any region D its contact is determined directly by original Gaussian elimination as B(D)=span(K_D)∩span(K_{E\D}). Define V as the sum of B(E0), all B(Ei) and the span of the node's true local keys. Then B(Ei)⊆span(K_Ei)∩V. Every generator of V is in the outside span of child i: its own boundary has this property by definition, other child boundaries lie in those other child spans, parent boundary lies in the external parent complement, and true sites are outside the child. Therefore V∩span(K_Ei)=B(Ei). A dependence among the child subspaces modulo V expresses each involved child vector using the others and V, all in its own outside span. Thus each such vector belongs to its own contact and lies already in V. This proves the exact represented gluing used by stationary-menu-tree.md. No change of the indexed tags or alternating form is involved.

For any supplied bad-rank cap d, run the bottom-up algorithm on this decomposition and report outside its promise immediately when a local bad-span exceeds d. Because an unsuccessful attempt might otherwise compute unnecessarily large implicit state tables, check each node's d after its children have been computed within cap but before enumerating the node's restrictions. One may try every root per connected component, retaining the best successful one, and combine component tables by a zero-boundary node. The decision is 2^{O(d)}poly(n) on inputs whose obtained canonical layouts have suitable rootings, with explicit physical traceback. Abstention outside the cap does not prove no alternative noncanonical layout works. This observation concerns recognition; it does not solve large bad-rank 3-connected leaves.

In stationary-vs-global-policy.md take the center graphic matroid of K_h for h≥4. Each five-edge diamond plus its virtual edge is the cycle matroid of K4, and the center with its virtual edges is the cycle matroid of K_h. Thus the displayed diamond construction is genuinely a 2-sum star of 3-connected summands; the canonical tree retains that star. Trying its central root meets cap d=3 even though the global polynomial-policy deficit is at least twice the number of edges of K_h. The source 2-sum theorem, not a new decomposition theorem, supplies the unseeded layout.
