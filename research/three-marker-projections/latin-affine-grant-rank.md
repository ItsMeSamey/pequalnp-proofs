# Affine grants for the signed-star Latin block

Let x be source data and s optional physical grant sites over F2. Site j has exactly the guard s_j(1+L_j(x))=0, where the enable L_j may be any affine function on all source bits. All grant couplings are k independent affine equations Hs=b(x). Independent source coordinates, signed occurrence copies, and true pins may be substituted through any affine embedding. This theorem allows exact signed equalities for free as a boundary promise, so it also covers implementations of the stars which add no further constraints on grants and whose witness can always be supplied on that boundary.

At each accepted x choose B a basis among the enabled columns of H, with r its size. The affine conditions L_j(x')=1 for j in B and b(x') in span B have total codimension at most r+k-r=k. They hold at x. At every point in this flat, Gaussian solve using only B and set all other grants zero. This proves that the accepted set is covered by affine flats of codimension at most k, even after arbitrary affine restriction of the source or an invertible change of grant coordinates provided the displayed form is restored. No bound on the number of optional sites is required. If H has dependent rows, first separate any induced affine equations on x or count all original independent equations in [H|linear b].

Consider m disjoint original clauses (a_i or b_i or c_i) in the exact rank-one Latin encoding. On the affine section b_i=c_i=0 their joint relation accepts only the all-one assignment to the a_i. Restricting a codimension-k flat containing that point to the section leaves dimension at least m-k. Consequently every assignment-faithful affine-grant compiler needs at least m marker equations on these inputs. The same example can have nontrivial signed stars by appending a duplicate of each clause and tautological clauses (not a_i or a_i or a_i). These additions preserve the original relation on every assignment. This bound is achieved for the disjoint-clause family by three optional grants per original clause, each enabled by its own literal, and one required parity-one equation per clause. The marker lower bound is independent of the number of extra grants and of the supports of their affine enables.

Explicit star costs, if all occurrence coordinates are requested, are linear: complement negative occurrence bits by an affine coordinate change and enforce equal normalized occurrences with a cycle of implications y_j(1+y_next)=0 for every nonsingleton star, with no constraint needed on singleton stars. True pins can be constants in the affine embedding, or zero discrepancy bits forced by one row each. Together with grant rows and m equations this is an exact many-marker construction. Dropping the extra m-1 marker equations has no justification. The lower bound is not for general one-readout native feedback, where grant-to-grant or grant-to-master feedback can destroy the affine-flat covering theorem. Decision-only reductions also need not preserve this section.
