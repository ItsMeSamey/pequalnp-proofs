For ordinary signed NAE-3 each ternary row uses at most three variables. If the coefficient matrix has row rank r, every nonzero column is supported on at least one of any r independent rows, since those rows span all rows. The union of their variable supports has size at most 3r. Thus the two-direction coefficient promise for this particular sparse source permits arbitrarily many input rows or syntactically present variables, but at most six effective variables. The two-score native compiler for general dense linear score tests has no such bound on effective variables.
