# Universally extensible sites for additive scores and one polynomial

Let p nonempty color classes be sought on a graph, with a site weight in a finite abelian group G, prescribed totals by color, and a degree-D polynomial P over one field F required to be nonzero. The following algebraic statement initially allows every coloring, including empty classes. Check the sum of target totals first and omit the redundant last color.

In characteristic l>0 split G into its l-primary cyclic factors of orders q and its coprime part B. In characteristic zero put B=G. On each primary coordinate and color, multiply P by the indicator that the sum agrees with the requested residue. This costs degree at most q−1: in F[z]/(z^q), use the coefficient of z^(q−1) in (1+z)^(q−1−target) times the product of one-site factors (1+z)^weight for sites of this color. Negative exponents use the inverse unit. The coefficient is one exactly at the target by the base-l binomial formula; each nonconstant-in-color factor term consumes positive z degree. Denote the resulting degree bound E=D+(p−1)Σ(q−1).

Set R=|B|^(p−1), K=E+(R−1)(2E+1). For every unrestricted satisfying coloring there exists a set S of at most (|G|+1)K sites such that EVERY assignment outside S extends to a satisfying coloring by choices on S.

Indeed put all sites of types (whole weights) of multiplicity ≤K in S and fix them to the original witness. The remaining polynomial Q is nonzero. In its canonical one-site indicator basis (constant and p−1 color indicators), choose a maximum-degree nonzero monomial, with support U, and include U in S. Its coefficient survives every outside fixing because there is no nonzero strict extension. Let H≤B^(p−1) be generated by the one-site color contributions of common types. The residual target lies in H even after arbitrary outside choices. In a splitting extension of F, allocate to each nontrivial character χ of H a separate block of 2E+1 common sites, disjoint from U, on which its one-site factor χ(g_v(color)) is nonconstant. Such a type exists since the baseline contributes zero and the contributions generate H. Multiplicity >K guarantees disjoint choices. Include all blocks in S.

For each site of the block assigned to χ take a function-space basis beginning with the constant 1 and this character factor. Elsewhere begin with 1. Fourier-expand the remaining target indicator and multiply by the restricted Q. Its trivial-character summand has nonzero projection onto tensors having at most E nonconstant sites: it is Q/|H| itself. For each other character, each monomial of Q leaves at least E+1 of its allocated sites untouched. At those sites the tensor factor is exactly the second basis vector, hence its entire low-support projection vanishes. The product is a nonzero function and gives an extension. This proves the statement including empty common type sets and trivial H.

Under the graph property of common-outside-moments.md for every S up to the displayed bound, construct one exterior partition for each S and list all recolorings on S. All entries are physical nonempty connected spanning partitions. The list hits each satisfiable joint test independently of the weights, polynomial, or witness when G,p,D,F characteristic are fixed. For prescribed weights one can finally Gaussian-reduce degree-D moments separately in actual score buckets. Runtime is polynomial for fixed parameters using the cited rooted-minor algorithm; the executable bounded-core search may abstain. This does not require simultaneous preservation of polynomial values in unrelated characteristics.
