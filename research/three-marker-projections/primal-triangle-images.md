# Primal ternary interfaces for supported cycle images

Hypotheses. A supplied tree glues binary PRIVATE column representations along disjoint physical or virtual triangles. Each proper ternary separator glues along the entire two-dimensional space W of the triangle, deleting its three named ports. The two ambient summands have intersection exactly W in their vector-space amalgam. Assume these triangles remain genuine and coindependent on the two complete sides. Proper unary separators similarly glue along a single point. Real sites have fixed named binary gains. At every torso a supplied graphic reference on the same indexed sites contains its private cycle space with codimension at most k. Equivalently its private rows are graphic incidence rows plus at most k extra private rows, up to row equivalence. Bounded-size torsos are also allowed. For fixed named width and fixed k the full supported-cycle gain image table and a physical realization of every attainable table entry are computable in polynomial time.

For a child support X with real columns A_X and gain Z_X, its outgoing relation consists of pairs (s,Z_X x) for coefficients x with A_X x=s in the port space W. At the root set W=0. This is a linear subspace. Joining child messages uses the same s in the common W. Their real coordinates are disjoint, and the linear balance at the parent is the exact sum of child port vectors and parent real vectors. These are column syndromes, so there is no choice of raw 3-bit trace representative. Include outgoing s in the score width p (at most two extra coordinates).

Fix a realizing support minimal under inclusion for a requested output image. On the space of true global real words giving some outgoing syndrome, the score-plus-outgoing map is injective: if a nonzero word maps to zero, choose one of its real bits and set it forbidden; subtracting that word preserves every output in the image. Thus this word space has dimension at most p. Child private dependencies have zero port syndrome and are independently extendible, so at most p children can contain any such dependency. Keep them exceptional. In other children the selected columns are independent. For Q=span(A_X) intersection W each s in Q has exactly one representing word. Conversely the real word determines s.

Use the physical rooted-minor construction of research/triangle-baselines/README.md on each complete child, ignoring gains in its construction but retaining them afterwards. It contracts an independent outside set C whose span misses W and keeps at most three real representatives. Its outside columns are either copies of distinct nonzero types in W, or the three independent spokes (0,1),(a,1),(b,1) over W with independent a,b. A selected minor support always gives an exact physical lift: permit all of C and exactly the selected retained sites. For any coefficients summing into W after contraction there is a unique correcting coefficient tuple on C bringing the original sum to that very W vector. These corrections and thus real gain are linear in selected coordinates, and there are no extra solutions supported solely in C.

For a nonexceptional child, the spoke template realizes Q=0 by empty support, any line by the two corresponding spokes, and full W by all three; all these selected columns are independent. For a parallel template, use any independent basis of Q in its available types. This is possible: if three types were kept every line is available. For at most one type, the syndrome span of the full child lies within it. If only two types were retained from distinct monochrome components in the rooted-minor construction, each component's private quotient coordinates are independent of other components' coordinates. Thus any word from a chosen support with syndrome a+b splits into two words on the same support with syndromes a,b respectively, excluding an unattainable singleton third-line domain. A unary regular port uses the analogous zero or one representative, a circuit through the port with a zero-dependency outside lift. These are compatible linear response maps on each chosen domain.

Replacing all these child responses keeps the space of compatible parent tuples isomorphic since s determines a unique child word before and after. For every nonexceptional child the old-minus-new score is a linear map of the original real word space of dimension at most p into a score space of dimension at most p. A subcollection of at most p² of the discrepancies sums to their total by elementary binary elimination. Keep those children exceptional as well. For all other children the genus-free physical templates above may be used with optional supports. Conversely every such optional support lifts exactly, including supports that contain a dependency among three parallel-template sites.

An exceptional child's known relation has domain Q, pure score subspace K (zero syndrome), and a score section on Q. Summand K can always be added independently. Represent Q by an independent basis of actual available port directions (all three nonzero directions exist in a proper triangle), always allowing these slots, deleting the others, and placing the section gains on the allowed slots. This gives exactly the required response with no artificial private dependency. Outgoing port state can be obtained by adjoining an always-allowed independent basis of its port space with score bits equal to its coordinate units and deleting the other outgoing port slots. Required allowed slots in the oracle are eliminated by contracting a maximal independent set among them, transferring gains by the unique coefficients on that basis and adding all pure gain dependencies from the remaining forced sites. Target images must contain this latter subspace and are queried in its quotient. These contractions/puncturings and shortenings preserve containment in the graphic cycle cover and do not increase its codimension. Previously accumulated fixed pure gain summands are handled by enumerating compatible remaining output subspaces. No hidden input/output compatibility conditions survive the linear column balance.

For simultaneous spoke substitutions, write the private triangle as t1,t2,t1+t2. Amalgamating and removing the ports leaves replacement columns (0,1),(t1,1),(t2,1) in one fresh coordinate. In the graphic reference the projected triangle sums to zero and so is the three pairwise boundaries of vertices a,b,c, which may coincide (parallel pair plus loop or three loops). A new star to a,b,c represents these three columns after changing the fresh row-coordinate origin by the incidence vector of its first spoke. The original extra private rows extend with entries 0,t1_private,t2_private respectively in the three spokes; each new dimension contributes a graphic coordinate, and the count of extra private rows stays at most k across all substitutions. Parallel templates simply retain the chosen subset of original port types and no other ports. Hence the resulting local code has the exact same bounded graphic cover defect, independent of node degree. Use the fixed-width graphic+stacked-private solver in resolution-bridge/graphic-exact-plane.md for the local supported-image questions. Enumerate at most p+p² exceptional child indices and their finite tables at each node, and store backtracking data. All local constraints involve a fixed dimension independent of depth. This theorem assumes a supplied proper tree with the stated local oracle structure; no claim that every regular or forbidden-minor input has such a tree.
