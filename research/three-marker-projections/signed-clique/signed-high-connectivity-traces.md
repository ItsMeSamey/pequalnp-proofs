# Exact fixed-name trace on highly connected hosts of arbitrary diameter

For fixed k,r there is a computable c(k,r) such that exact named trace with original witness is polynomial on every supplied c-connected simple graph G with ker q=cut(G)⊕Z, dim Z=k and r arbitrary named rows. Use the template, tensor reserves and final trace calculation of signed-clique-mixed-defects.md. The following replaces its clique localization and all routing steps. For two-sided perturbations, add the fixed private checks as named rows and filter the augmented target.

Primary sources: Geelen–Gerards, Theorems 1.3 and 1.4 (https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~jfgeelen/Publications/pims.pdf); Huynh preprint, Lemma 8.1, proof of Lemma 8.2 and Theorem 1.1 (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34307v1#S8.SS1). The former give enlargement/localization of group-labelled clique models; the latter explicitly gives the constructive neutral starting model, preservation of nonsampled trees, and search for prescribed disjoint paths. The initial ordinary clique minor is forced by sufficiently large minimum degree (e.g. https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/minorshort3.pdf) and found by fixed-minor testing.

Let g=2^k, J=(r+1)2^k, n(b)=J+bJ2^k+(2r+1)r bound outside special vertices when |P|≤b. At most Q(b)=2n(b)+k requests and m(b)=2Q(b) stubs are needed. Put H(b)=b+n(b)+3m(b)+10. Choose N_k=H(0)+10; backwards t_j=8g²N_{j+1}, N_j=max(4t_j,H(t_j)+10,N_{j+1}). Take c larger than all these H(t_j)+N_0+10 and H(0)+N_0+10 and the minimum-degree bound forcing the clique minor needed for a neutral model of order N_0. These constants precede the graph.

Apply the procedure in Huynh Lemma 8.2's proof to 4t_j trees of the retained model of order N_j, beginning with the trivial subgroup. In polynomial time this either produces a model of order N_{j+1} over a strictly larger subgroup, or finds |P|<t_j so that the block carrying the majority of representatives shifts into the current subgroup L. At most k subgroup enlargements are possible. At the full group take P empty. In the deletion case G−P remains 2-connected, so the carrier block is the entire remaining graph. At least N_j−|P| trees of the full retained model survive. Integrate labels modulo L on a spanning tree, switch all labels outside P into L and recompute A(z_j). The model stays a complete L-model after this switch: its normalizing potentials on its connected realization, whose current and target edge labels are both in L, lie in a single coset of L and can be translated into L.

Choose the mixed template's actual P-to-bin portals, roots, charge exceptions and correction bases. Test every guessed edge for existence. Delete every non-P special vertex F (≤n(|P|)) for linkage interiors. First choose distinct neighbors outside P∪F for each incidence of each requested path or cycle at a special endpoint; minimum degree guarantees these stubs even at repeated roots. Reserve one actual edge per incidence and adjust each requested gain by its two stub gains. G−(P∪F) is still (m+1)-connected for the actual m stubs and has a surviving complete L-model of order ≥3m+3. Under Huynh Lemma 8.1 the separator of order ≤m separating W=stubs from model trees must contain W (connectivity makes its near strict side empty), hence equals W. All requested gains can be realized simultaneously by disjoint paths; Huynh Theorem 1.1 finds them. Restore stubs to get precisely the desired shared endpoints with disjoint interiors, including simple cycles based at roots. Arbitrary route length is permissible.

Give the fixed core paths and cycles potentials by their projected gains. Delete only P and outside nonroot special endpoints (portals, exceptional-charge sites, correction bases). The remaining graph stays connected, every core interior stays connected to a root, and each path whose reserve endpoint was deleted still retains its rooted prefix. Treat all roots and interiors as fixed seeds; grow a multi-source forest without overwriting any seed. Give each newly reached ordinary vertex its predecessor's bin and the potential determined by the forest edge. Outside-P edges have gain in L, preserving the bin coset. Different seeds may give different bins. Correct part and switch moments using the independent tensor reserves; these permit arbitrary initial menu assignments to ordinary sites. Realize all prescribed portals and cycle gains and recover the exact trace as in the mixed proof. Verify the trace by elimination on the original indexed support.

The algorithm covers graphs of unbounded diameter, for example arbitrarily many consecutive clique layers of fixed size ≥c with complete joins between consecutive layers. It uses external constructive linkage and minor algorithms and does not decide arbitrary low-connectivity inputs. For original physical search, apply this five/six-name primitive only to the actual computed quotient and recover physical words by the established same-support lift.
