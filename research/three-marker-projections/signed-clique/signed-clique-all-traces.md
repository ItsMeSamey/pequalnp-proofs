# All fixed named traces on rank-one perturbations of a complete cut code

Input: a binary private matrix q with edges indexed by a supplied simple K_n, whose kernel D equals C ⊕ <z> for the clique cut code C. Verify this equality by elimination. Let A consist of r≤6 arbitrary indexed named rows; let U be the exact target of dimension h. No surjectivity or marker promise is needed. Return original support or NO. This suffices for every one-/two-tag rank-four dual trace query. For h=0 take empty support.

**Minimal supports.** Any realization S may be shrunk until A is injective on D supported in S. If a supported nonzero zero-gain word has coordinate e=1, delete e and correct every old word having that coordinate by the zero-gain word. This preserves trace and strictly decreases nullity. The limited supported kernel has dimension h. Distinguish whether this space contains a translate of C.

**Cuts alone.** Shrink further to the union of the supported words. These words are cuts, hence S is all edges crossing between the components of K_n minus S. The internal graph is a union of cliques. Some internal cycle must be odd under z: otherwise z is a cut there, producing a translated word supported in S. Some triangle within a component therefore has odd sum. Enumerate such triangles and contract each candidate to one vertex with the sum of its incident vertex charges c_v=A δ{v}. More precisely its new charge is the sum of the three c_v, not a sum over quotient adjacencies. The ordinary complete-graph charge-partition solver (span-separation/dense-cographic.md) tests for pure trace U on this quotient. Lift a partition to the original clique and retain all original crossing edges. The internal triangle rules out all supported translates. This covers every all-cut minimal witness and returns only exact witnesses.

**Translate case: necessary data.** Contracting no edges, the complements of S have exactly h connected components, since cuts supported in S form a space of dimension h−1, one less than the full supported kernel. Their component charges p_1,...,p_h span a hyperplane U_0 of U, and any supported translated word w=z+δx has gain u∈U outside U_0. Necessarily u+A(z) lies in A(C). Enumerate U_0,u,p_i and test these conditions, including sum p_i=0. Test existence of an abstract ordered nonempty partition with these charges by a fixed-state vertex DP ignoring connectivity. Retain an actual assignment.

**Reserves for parts and switches.** Put t=h−1. Choose a subspace W of the r-bit name space minimizing |T|+t dim W, where T are the vertex occurrences charged outside W. Then |T|+t dim W≤tr. There are t disjoint bases B_i of W among the charges in W: comparison against each L⊆W gives at least t(dim W−dim L) occurrences in W outside L, exactly the base-packing condition. Equivalently enumerate bounded-size candidate bases, since r,h are fixed. This is the charge-reserve lemma of code-modular-score/connected-domatic-charge-packing.md. Its t=0 case uses W equal to the total charge span, with T empty and no bases. Separately choose vertex occurrences R forming a basis of all charges A(C). R may intersect T or the B_i.

Greedily find h disjoint sets of five vertices, each containing an odd-z triangle, avoiding T, all B_i, and R. Work on the remaining clique each time. If fewer than five vertices remain before completion, n is bounded in r,h, so exact exhaustive search is constant for these parameters. If no odd triangle remains, z is a cut on the remaining clique: use the star potential. Switch it to zero there, so the defect graph has a vertex cover of bounded size, consisting of the reserves and previously chosen cores. The normalized z generates the same D. Run the complete bounded relational clique-width solver of cographic-code-perturbation.md, with types given by neighborhoods into the cover (each cover vertex gets its own label). Vertex charges do not require edge-color joins. These fallbacks decide the exact original query.

**Sufficient construction.** Place one core in each specified part. Place each vertex of T as in the abstract assignment, and initially put all others in the last part. Every core charge lies in W, hence each part has the right charge modulo W. For each i<h transfer a subset of its disjoint B_i from the last part to part i to correct its W-valued discrepancy. Total charge fixes the last part. The cores ensure nonemptiness.

Independently on each core choose normalized switch bits x giving a connected zero-edge graph on its vertices and offering a zero incident edge to EACH r∈R for EITHER choice of x_r. The odd triangle begins with three connected switches. On adjoining one vertex, every old connected switch extends at least once; at most one normalized switch has homogeneous signed adjacency at the new vertex, and all others extend twice. Thus at five vertices at least nine are connected. Each r forbids at most one normalized switch by homogeneity, and |R|≤6. Enumerate the 16 switches to select one. Fix x for every vertex outside cores and R to attach it to some core vertex of its assigned part. Finally choose the independent bits on R to solve Aδx=u+A(z). This is always consistent by the span of the vertex charges of R, regardless of assigned part and previously fixed switch bits. Every r remains attached to its part.

Take S to be the union of all between-part edges and supp(z+δx). Its complement has exactly the h prescribed parts as connected components. Thus the cut words supported in S have gain span exactly U_0; the translated word of gain u belongs to the same support and all other supported translated words differ from it by a supported cut. The trace is exactly U. Verify directly against the original columns before returning S. Exhaustion of the cuts-only and translate alternatives gives NO.

Complexity is polynomial for fixed r. In particular arbitrary defect edge colors, including unbounded colored clique-width, are valid. The promised graph correspondence must be supplied and checked; this is not a recognition result for all rank-one perturbations. For nongraphic 3-connected examples see marked-clique-irreducibility.md, and for a literal original physical encoding see switching-prime-physical-line.md (the reverse construction extends with two independent odd rows). The independent one-tag controls and earlier bounded-width solver do not by themselves validate this larger theorem.
