# Fixed named trace for signed biconnected bounded-degree vertex expanders

Fix k,r,Δ,α>0. On a supplied simple biconnected Δ-bounded α-vertex expander G with ker q=cut(G)⊕Z and k defects, every r-name target has polynomial witness search. This includes 3-connected cubic expanders. Use the finite template and tensor proof of signed-clique-mixed-defects.md and the unsigned pin-separator charging lemma of cographic-auxiliary/expander-arbitrary-labels.md. The changes are below.

**Subgroup.** Alon–Seymour–Thomas Theorem 1.5 gives a fixed clique-minor model or a balanced separator of size ≤a^{3/2}√n for K_a. Expansion forbids the latter for n>36a³/α² by taking a union of components of size n/6 to n/2. Apply Geelen–Gerards subgroup enlargement as in signed-high-connectivity-traces.md. In the localization alternative a block C of G−P0 shifts into L, with |P0|<t and N−t untouched model trees. Choose N−t−1>(t+1)/α. Every component away from C meets at most one vertex of C and must touch P0 by biconnectivity. There are ≤Δt of them, each of size ≤(t+1)/α: if its size exceeds n/2, expand the complement excluding P0 and its possible single contact, containing all but one vertex of C but with boundary ≤t+1. Otherwise expand the component. Absorb the components into P, now of size ≤t+Δt(t+1)/α. The entire exterior of P shifts into L. At full subgroup take P empty. Recalculate every A(z_s).

**State.** Guess T, projection π, p=h−dim T+1, the target part charges and translated gains u_j as in the mixed proof. Store all physical holes H (initially P) with full states (part,x). Pin every surviving neighbor of H and every surviving vertex charged outside a frequent subspace W; enumerate their states. At each recursion guess the inhabited bulk bins (part,x mod πL). Within each part the skeleton contains its actual hole vertices and these bins; include every zero hole-hole edge and every actual zero hole-pin edge to the pin's bin, labelled modulo L. Require connectivity, nonemptiness and that collective cycle gains contain the image of T-perp. This condition is necessary via the original inactive graph; an inhabited bin need not already be connected. Parts wholly in H are permitted.

Use the full menu M=(e_part,x) in the inhabited bins, and check feasibility of the tensor moment (prescribed part charges and u_j+A(z_sj)) by finite DP respecting holes and pins. For m0 in M and each independent actual difference m_i+m0 spanning its difference space, take a disjoint basis of W as movable vertices (toggles), with two options m0,m_i each. All unpinned remaining charges belong to W, so arbitrary ordinary initial menu choices with toggles at m0 are correctable as in the tensor lemma. W=0 needs no toggles. No surviving vertices is a bounded direct case.

**Routing.** Make a disjoint connected anchor bag per bin, assign a root potential and propagate along a chosen tree. Demand one path per pin to its anchor, two per toggle to its two possible anchors (possibly identical), and a closed request at one anchor per basis vector of L∩T-perp. For open requests prescribe any full gain in L giving the requested endpoint potential. The m demands have 2m distinct incidence clones. Delete all holes and branches (bags, pins and toggles); each clone joins all ordinary neighbors via actual attachment edges with gain adjusted by the tree path from its branch root. Retain all parallel gain/attachment choices. The linkage graph is L-labelled with a surviving complete L-model of order >4m. The routing claim in the proof of Huynh Lemma 8.1 (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34307v1#S8.SS1) implies failure on 2m distinct terminals gives a small-side separator of order STRICTLY less than 2m containing all clones on the near side with a missed whole model tree strictly opposite. Theorem 1.1 recovers all paths on success. Enumerate bounded cuts on failure. For zero requests skip linkage.

Restore the actual bag paths; when a closed request shares edges inside its anchor it still gives a closed walk of precisely the required gain. Every rooted path prefix stays attached after temporarily removing its flexible endpoint. Every pin remains attached in its assigned state. G minus holes and toggles is connected as proved below. Grow a multi-source forest there without overwriting pins, anchors or routed interiors, assigning each new vertex the predecessor's bin and potential by its edge gain. Correct the tensor moment by toggles, each attached to its anchor for either choice. Every outside bin connects to its anchor. The actual hole contacts realize the finite skeleton, and rooted cycles give L∩T-perp. Projected potentials imply every inactive cycle has gain in T-perp. Its cycle gain span is therefore exactly T-perp. Return the actual crossing edges plus all translated supports and verify on original q,A.

**Strict pin decrease.** Use the physical unsigned separation bounds from expander-arbitrary-labels.md, counting both incidences of every rooted cycle. For B total branch size take Q≥ceil((|H|+B+2m)/α). Each anchor of incidence degree d_i has max(1,ceil(d_i/α)) vertices. Nonpin branches are mutually >2Q+4 apart and equally far from H and pins. After H and all branches retain >Q+2m+2 model trees. After ≤2m further deletions the model component has >Q vertices and hence exceeds n/2 by expansion; all other components total ≤Q. After deleting only H and toggles all anchors are in the giant (small component would have H or toggle within Q+1 of the anchor). On success a small component adjacent to H contains a pin routed to an anchor. Otherwise its boundary consists solely of toggles; biconnectivity requires two, inconsistent with their separation. Thus G minus H and toggles is connected.

On failed clone linkage let Y be ordinary separator vertices. Delete H, all branches and Y in the physical graph. A branch with a surviving clone has no neighbor in the giant. To each such nonpin branch add the adjacent off-giant components. All exterior neighbors are in Y by separation of branches, and size ≤Q+bag size<n/2. Each anchor thus charges at least d_i distinct separator vertices by expansion, each toggle two by biconnectivity. Charges from distinct nonpin branches are disjoint and far from the pins. Fully deleted nonpin clone groups pay their incidences directly, as do deleted pin clones. Since total separator order <2m, the uncharged Y budget is strictly less than the number of pins R' with surviving clones. The set U'=R' plus all off-giant components adjacent to R' has size ≤#pins+Q. Its neighbors outside H are old pins or uncharged Y close to R'. Enumerate full states on U' and its external boundary respecting prior pins. Add U' to H, pin its surviving boundary and discard temporary anchors, bins and toggles. Strictly fewer pins remain. The actual feasible assignment extends an enumerated branch. Rebuild the skeleton from ALL actual hole edges next time, retaining cycle gains that unsigned virtual edges would lose.

**Uniformity.** Given b bounding initial holes let K=2^r, T0=0. At each j<K define P_j=Δb+KT_j, J=(r+1)2^k, V=(2r+1)r, m*=P_j+2V+k, A*=max(1,ceil(2m*/α)), B*=P_j+V+JA*. From h_0=b iterate Q_i=ceil((h_i+B*+2m*)/α), h_(i+1)=h_i+P_j+Q_i for P_j+1 steps. Call the final bound H*, set Q=ceil((H*+B*+2m*)/α), and choose T_(j+1)>T_j+(H*+P_j+V+J+1)(Δ+1)^(2Q+2A*+12). Since ≤K−1 nonzero charge types exist, some (T_j,T_(j+1)] misses their frequencies. Let W span higher-frequency types, so ≤KT_j vertices have charge outside W. There are enough copies of a basis of frequent types to choose all distant toggle sites, avoiding balls near every hole/pin. Then select sufficiently distant anchor centers and grow bags within radius A*. Insufficient graph size for this or the size tests is bounded in the parameters and permits direct enumeration. For model order take F(b) exceeding H*+B*+4m*+Q+10 for every frequency level. At full subgroup choose N_k≥F(0)+10; backwards t_j=8|Γ|²N_(j+1), b_j=ceil(t_j+Δt_j(t_j+1)/α), N_j≥max(4t_j,t_j+2+(t_j+1)/α,t_j+F(b_j)+10,N_(j+1)). The nonsampled retained model pays for all bounded holes/branch deletions. All constants are fixed before reading n.

This uses the external constructive group linkage theorem, not a native implementation. The claim is for biconnected bounded-degree expanders; arbitrary pendant gadgets require additional signed states. Two-sided code perturbations use the pre-existing augmented-names slice. For original one/two-tag physical search, first verify the derived indexed representation and finally lift and recheck the witness.
