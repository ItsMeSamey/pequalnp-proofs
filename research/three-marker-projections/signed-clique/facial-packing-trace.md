# Exceptional bounded-degree surface hosts with deficient signed packing

Let G be a supplied finite simple connected cellularly embedded graph of Euler genus γ, with maximum degree Δ, and let D=ker q=cut(G)⊕<z_1,...,z_k>. The face syndrome of a representative is its summed edge bits along the facial walks, traversals counted with multiplicity. Switching by a primal cut preserves face syndromes and D. No assumption of orientability is needed over F2.

For every subset of p faces its intersection graph (two faces share any primal vertex) has O_γ,Δ(p) edges. For subcubic G any two distinct faces at a vertex share an incident primal edge, so the assertion follows directly from edge bounds for the simple geometric dual. For general bounded degree, take the embedded bipartite radial incidence graph restricted to the selected faces, delete duplicate incidences and centers touching at most one selected face. Degree-two centers suppress to edges between two face nodes; the number of distinct such edges is at most 3p+O(γ+1) by the simple embedded graph bound. Let b centers be incident to at least 3 selected faces. Their simple bipartite incidence subgraph satisfies 3b≤2(p+b)+O(γ+1), hence b≤2p+O(γ+1). Each such center contributes at most binom(Δ,2) face pairs. This applies to all face subsets, proving a computable degeneracy/color bound c(γ,Δ), including small p by enlargement of constants. Construct an actual proper coloring by minimum-degree ordering. It is immaterial if a facial walk repeats vertices or edges.

For a scalar z, if >c(m−1) faces have nonzero syndrome, some color contains m such faces with disjoint vertex sets. Split each odd closed walk at repeated vertices to extract a simple cycle of odd z sum. This produces m indexed vertex-disjoint odd cycles, without treating parallel uses of a bridge as a cycle. Therefore if each of k chosen basis translations has packing number <m, the union B of defective faces has cardinality ≤kc(m−1). The algorithm checks |B| directly; a dual certificate for small |B| does not require distinguishing a globally maximum packing. Large |B| yields an explicit packing for some coordinate. For the stronger basis-independent promise bounding every nonzero direction, the conclusion holds for every basis.

Identify the vertices B of the geometric dual H into one, preserving every indexed edge and loop. Faces outside B have zero dual divergence for every z_j; on the single merged vertex total divergence is zero. Primal cuts lie in the ordinary dual cycle code before and after merging. Hence the new dual cycle code contains D. Euler's formula gives surplus dimension γ+max(0,|B|−1)−k. This is nonnegative when the supplied code verification succeeds. Encode a basis of these surplus private linear checks as additional names and enumerate augmented exact traces with the required kernel slice. Invoke the constructive fixed-name graphic trace solver on this supplied dual. Its returned support is checked on the original q,A. This is a genuine algorithm for the low-packing exceptional branch at fixed γ,Δ,k,m and named dimension, even when no bounded primal transversal exists. It is an application of the established auxiliary-graphic solver, with a facial certificate obtained through bounded-degree packing. No solution is asserted here for unbounded face syndromes or unrestricted high-genus cubic expanders.
