# Mixed global and localized defects on complete hosts

Fix k,r. The input includes an indexed K_n representation D=ker q=C⊕<z_1,...,z_k>, where C is its cut space. Compute or check a basis modulo C. Edge labels d_e=(z_j(e)) live in G=F2^k; A consists of r arbitrary named rows, c_v=Aδ{v}. All claims are for the original indexed exact trace U; no named rank assumption. This extends signed-clique-many-defects.md to all mixtures. Small n bounded in k,r can always be handled exhaustively.

**Prepare the labels.** Maintain a bounded set P and a subspace L≤G with every d_e on edges outside P in L, initially empty P, L=G. For the present size of P, seek sufficiently many vertex-disjoint packs of dim L disjoint triangles whose gains span L, lying entirely outside P. A pack implements a prescribed L-gain simple path between any two distinct outside endpoints not in that pack, or a cycle at one outside root, as in signed-clique-many-defects.md. Its joining edges lie outside P and therefore have gains in L. Obtain these packs greedily. If the required supply fails, add all vertices used in complete or partial packs to P. Every remaining triangle has gain in the proper subspace L' spanned by the last partial pack, possibly zero. Since the outside is a clique, modulo L' its edge labels are a vertex coboundary (take a star). Switch the representatives by vector-valued vertex potentials taking values in L so that all outside edges now have labels in L'. Extend potentials over P arbitrarily, and recompute all A(z_j) afterwards. This preserves D and original named rows. Restart with L'. Each failure strictly lowers dim L, hence |P| remains bounded by a function of k,r. For L=0 no packs are needed: any two outside vertices are directly joined with zero full gain. Quantitative supply bounds appear below.

**Necessary template.** For nonzero U, put h=dim U. Shrink any positive support until A is injective on its supported kernel by deleting nonzero coordinates of zero-gain words. Guess the supported coefficient subspace T≤G, dim t≤h, its basis s_j, and π:G→F2^t with coordinates s_j·g. The inactive graph has p=h−t+1 components. Guess their charges spanning U_0≤U of dimension p−1 and the named gains u_j of w_j=z_{s_j}+δx_j inducing a basis of U/U_0. Put x_v=(x_j(v)). Guess part assignments and x_v for P. Each outside vertex lies in a bin (part, x_v mod πL); guess exactly which bins are inhabited. If there are no outside vertices then n is bounded. For each zero edge from P vertex a to an outside bin, record its defect d. Here zero means same part and πd=x_a+x_v. Guess one actual outside representative v per occurring triple (a,bin,d), with its part and exact potential; representatives may overlap consistently. Validate these properties against original labels. This guessing costs n to a bounded exponent. Extra zero adjacencies need not be forbidden.

Make a finite graph within each part with nodes its P vertices and inhabited bins. Include actual zero P-P edges and one arc per guessed portal from a to its bin, full label d modulo L. Require each part graph nonempty and connected and the cycle labels of all these finite graphs collectively to span the image of T-perp in G/L (require containment of that image). Multiedges retain their distinct gains. Necessity: collapsing all outside vertices into the appropriate bins sends each original inactive edge outside P to a loop in the same bin of gain zero modulo L. Each actual zero P-edge maps to its recorded type. This preserves connectivity and sends every cycle to a closed walk with the same label modulo L. The actual inactive cycle gain span is T-perp, since s defines a supported translate exactly when its scalar labels are a cut on the inactive graph. This proves the required condition even if original vertices of a bin were disconnected outside P.

**Abstract assignment.** For each permitted bin and each x in its specified coset use a menu element m=(e_i,x), where e_i records its part. Every nonportal outside vertex has this same menu M. Part charges and all switch equations are the components of one tensor moment sum of c_v⊗m_v. For switches the targets are u_j+A(z_{s_j}); P vertices and pinned portals have their prescribed contributions. Test abstract feasibility of the complete list of moment equalities by finite-state DP; retain one actual assignment. Bins will be forced inhabited by roots so this DP need not impose individual bin counts. Every actual witness passes.

**Moment reserve.** Remove the pinned portals and P from the pool of outside charges. Fix m_0∈M and choose a basis m_i+m_0 of span(M+m_0) from menu elements, i≤a≤p+t. Apply the same charge-reserve lemma with a disjoint bases: there are exceptional sites E and a subspace W with charge bases B_i⊆W, all from the pool, and |E|+sum |B_i|≤ar. All other pool charges lie in W. Freeze E to their assignments in the abstract solution. The B_i are held initially at m_0. Any assignment respecting these frozen/pinned choices has discrepancy from the target in W⊗span(M+m_0), since each free vertex contributes c_v⊗(m_v+m_v^abstract). Therefore write the discrepancy as sum w_i⊗(m_i+m_0) and move a subset of B_i of charge sum w_i to m_i. This preserves all fixed choices, independently corrects every component, and allows the other vertices to start from arbitrary individual menu entries. For a=0 there are no correction moves. No rank bound on the graph or q is used here.

**Realize the skeleton.** Pick a fresh outside root for each inhabited bin, avoiding all portals, E and B_i. Insufficient vertices implies bounded n. Discard all packs meeting any root, E, B_i or pinned portal. In bin (i,ξ) give its root an arbitrary potential x_0∈ξ. For each pinned outside portal and each E vertex build a separate path from its assigned bin root to that vertex with full gain g∈L satisfying πg=x_0+x_v. For each charge reserve site build a separate path from the corresponding root for each of its two possible entries m_0,m_i to that site (one path if identical). Set internal path vertices to that bin and give them potentials from cumulative π-gains starting at x_0. All internal vertices are from disjoint packs avoiding the designated endpoints. Only the final endpoint's part/potential is left dependent on its ultimate menu choice. It attaches from the chosen path while all other paths' interior vertices remain attached to their roots. For L=0 use the direct root edge for these paths, with no interior. If L∩T-perp is nonzero, at any inhabited bin root build additional fresh cycles, one per basis element of this space, of respective full gains; their projected gains vanish, so switch potentials are consistent. This forces their cycle gains internally. When L=0 this list is empty.

Assign all remaining pool vertices outside the reserves and cores to any fixed reference bin, choosing potential by the edge to its root. This is an allowed menu value since the edge label lies in L. Correct moments using the B_i. Form S from all between-part edges and all supports of the w_j. In its complement each vertex of each outside bin is connected to its root. Each guessed P-to-bin arc is realized by its P edge and an internal path from its outside endpoint to that root, with the same gain modulo L; each P-P arc exists. Therefore the inactive graph has precisely the required components, and the span H of its full cycle labels projects onto at least the required image of T-perp in G/L. (Concatenating realizing paths gives closed walks; repeated edges cancel over F2.) It contains L∩T-perp by the additional rooted cycles. Conversely H⊆T-perp since each inactive edge satisfies the prescribed projected potential equation. By elementary dimension H=T-perp. Hence the supported translation space is exactly T, the supported cut gains span U_0 by the part charges, and the full trace is exactly U. Replay on original q,A. The guesses were necessary for every YES, and an accepted template yields a witness.

**Uniform bound.** For P of size b, at most J=(r+1)2^k bins and bJ2^k portal vertices are ever needed, and a≤2r+1. The total number N of special outside vertices to avoid is at most J + bJ2^k +(2r+1)r. There are at most N discarded packs and at most 2N+k subsequent path/cycle requests (each E, portal or basis vertex counts at most twice). Greedily request 3N+k+1 packs per stage. On failure |P| increases by at most 3k(3N+k+1). At most k failures occur, all parameter bounded. Hence runtime polynomial in input size for fixed k,r. The proof uses the clique on original indexed sites and supplied representation; it does not assert arbitrary cographic solvability.
