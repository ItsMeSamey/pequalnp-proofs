# Three fragile pins with pin-to-free adjacency rank at most two

Let A be an indexed loopless binary symmetric adjacency matrix, P its three specified pins and F the remaining sites. If C=A_FP has rank at most two, there is a randomized polynomial-time algorithm with one-sided error and original indexed traceback for an induced-even support containing P. It uses the diagonal-completion equivalence of three-fragile-diagonal-completion.md. This is a sufficient recognizable structural class; no width bound on A_FF is imposed.

Find s≠0 on P satisfying C s=0. For each free diagonal e, write D=A_FF+diag(e). If a nonzero u satisfies Du=Cᵀu=0, every pin completion is singular; its determinant cube is identically zero. Otherwise [D C] has full row rank, so the free-compatible pairs (u,p) with Du+Cp=0 form a vector space of dimension three. Their images (p,y) with y=Cᵀu+A_PP p have zero kernel by the same condition. Symmetry shows their image L is isotropic for (p,y)·(p',y')=pᵀy'+p'ᵀy, hence is a three-dimensional Lagrangian. Taking u=0,p=s shows that L contains the fixed, nonzero w=(s,A_PP s), independent of e. For a pin diagonal z, let f_z(e)=det(A+diag(e,z)). In this nonzero-response case it equals one iff L has zero intersection with the cap {(p,diag(z)p)}. Thus every possible determinant cube on F lies among zero and fifteen Lagrangian cubes containing w.

The finite binary lemma is executable in check-fragile-fixed-relation.py SHA 4c97f9f13ebd638db69c7f88231c82e1c9c18e134e66921e13c574805463902a. It generates all 135 three-dimensional isotropic subspaces by independent triples in binary six-space, identifying the seven nonzero elements of each span. For each of the 63 nonzero w it computes the eight principal cap transversality bits f_z of each of the 15 containing subspaces. A cube is accepting if it has a one with all three Hamming neighbors zero. For every accepting cube the vector (1, f_z, f_z f_v for z<v) lies outside the binary span of those vectors from all rejecting cubes including zero. Row elimination therefore constructs a quadratic scalar certificate for each accepting cube, nonzero there and zero on *every* rejecting cube of that same w-stratum. There are six accepting cubes in 35 strata and none in the remaining 28. No mixed row/column boundary minors occur. The same exhaustive lemma shows no nonempty intersection of two Lagrangian-cap transversality predicates in any such stratum contains only accepting cubes. Consequently a simple OR of two-cap feasible intersections is insufficient, despite quadratic separation by XORs.

For a fixed quadratic certificate Q, the weighted polynomial
H(Y)=sum over e of Q(f(e)) times product_{i:e_i=1}Y_i²
is nonzero precisely when some actual free e gives a detecting cube. Distinct e have distinct monomials. The certificate constant is zero because the zero cube is in the negative span. Linear bits can be treated as their own square at each binary e. Each quadratic monomial therefore requires a product f_z(e) f_v(e) on the SAME e. Here is the two-principal-copy evaluator. In characteristic two a symmetric determinant expands as a sum of matchings with diagonal singleton weights and transposition weights given by squares of off-diagonal entries; permutations with longer cycles cancel. Use two copies of the graph with their fixed pin diagonals z,v. Put a_i=Y_i²/(1+Y_i²) at each copy of a free vertex i, and put one new intercopy edge at their matching vertices with matrix entry b_i=Y_i/(1+Y_i²). Multiply the resulting symmetric determinant by product_i(1+Y_i²). For fixed internal matching edges in both copies, if neither site needs a free singleton the local multiplier before normalization is one; for exactly one it is a_i; for both it is a_i²+b_i²=a_i. After normalization these are respectively 1+Y_i²,Y_i²,Y_i², exactly the weighted sum over the common Boolean diagonal bit. This proves the evaluation term by term, without any asymmetric minors. Sum over the certificate terms evaluated at the same random point.

Degree is at most 2|F|. Choose an explicitly constructed binary extension field of polynomial size (e.g. enumerate irreducible degree-O(log n) binary polynomials and test them) and sample independently away from one. Schwartz--Zippel makes a nonzero certificate visible with probability at least 2/3 by taking the admissible field size greater than 6|F|. For a YES input at least one certificate is nonzero, so a common evaluation point suffices. For traceback keep the first detected certificate. In H=H0+Y_i² H1, each specialization at fixed bit i is evaluated by fixing that free diagonal in both physical copies, omitting its bridge and normalization. At a nonzero evaluation at least one branch remains nonzero using the SAME remaining field values. Iterating recovers an actual e, with no error accumulation after the first success. Find its accepting z by testing eight binary determinants. Compute diag(M⁻¹) for M=A+diag(e,z) and verify the resulting support against A and P. No random false positives occur. If F is empty one can inspect all eight pin choices directly.
