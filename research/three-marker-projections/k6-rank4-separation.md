# Same-index separation for Boolean probes of alternating rank two

Use the fifteen indexed edges of K6, with vertices 0 through 5. In the first host take the ten rows of K to be the fundamental triangles ij5 for 0≤i<j<5, and the single tag t to be the matching 01,23,45. Thus ker K consists of cuts. Each chart's mismatch vector is precisely an edge set with odd degree at every vertex, since the row space of K is the cycle space. An active cut is constant as a vertex potential on every mismatch component. Every component has even cardinality, hence there are at most three. Conversely every perfect matching is a mismatch set. Every even vertex subset and its even complement can be matched internally. Consequently the aggregate relation space L is the four-dimensional space of even potentials modulo constants. A perfect matching attains chart dimension two, exactly two below dim L.

The terminal dot product descends to a nondegenerate alternating form on L, and vanishes on each chart, since the mismatch components have even size. In the basis f_i=e_i+e_5, 0≤i<4, disjoint pairs f_i and f_j+f_k with distinct i,j,k are jointly available in a perfect matching. An annihilating alternating form therefore has equal off-diagonal entries in each row. Symmetry forces a common value, so the annihilator of the jointly attainable exterior space has dimension one, generated by the rank-four dot form.

Represent the dot form on physical cut words by the sum of all wedges of distinct edge coordinates i5,j5 with 0≤i<j<4. Indeed, in the gauge with potential at 5 zero, the potential at 4 is the sum of the first four. For the second host append row t to K and set all tags to zero, with the identical edge indices. Its zero chart attains all of L. Every nonzero decomposable form on L thus detects both hosts, and forms restricting to zero detect neither. The displayed indexed dot form detects only the second host. Pulling physical linear probes back to L permits all probes, with any change of basis acting by congruence and preserving rank.

This separation covers adaptive Boolean queries of decomposable forms to the unchanged host and common abstract L. A physical restriction, contraction, returned witness, or use of the supplied private matrix gives additional information outside this comparison.
