# Exact removal of an aligned subgroup patch from a Latin block

Use the explicit additive Latin block with signed occurrence identifications and pins, plus an explicit 2CNF background. Fix a dimension bound h. In the three rails choose patches a+W, b+W, a+b+W for the same binary subspace W of dimension at most h. Enumerate bases and the two shifts in |G|^{h+2} times polynomial work. A zero triple meeting two patches necessarily meets all three. In coordinates translated by a,b,a+b a triple meeting just one patch has its two other coordinates in the same nonzero quotient coset.

Take all representatives used by the patch sites, retaining as boundary names those used outside or explicitly protected. Enumerate assignments to these at most 3·2^h representatives respecting pins and signs. For each assignment discard it if some internal triple is false. For mixed triples substitute the patch bit; the residual is a binary clause or true. Substitute all enumerated representatives wherever they occur in these clauses and the old 2CNF background; reimpose their assigned boundary values as units. Resulting 2CNF F_i is on exterior names only. The exact interface is U=union_i Mod(F_i). Remaining Latin triples use exclusively exterior sites and are conjoined unchanged. This also covers shared stars: a boundary literal has exactly its actual exterior value. Given a successful assignment, select a mode whose F_i holds to reconstruct its private assignments. If per-input original named values must be preserved, mark them as protected, even when they have no exterior occurrence.

Test exactly whether U has a 2CNF representation. Compute its binary-clause hull Q by listing every unit and binary clause entailed by every F_i, using 2SAT; include the empty clause if U is empty. If there are no modes this handles the empty relation directly. Test whether Q implies U by enumerating one clause from each F_i: a counterexample exists iff some such tuple can be falsified simultaneously with Q, checked by 2SAT and unit pins. An empty clause can be falsified with no pins, an empty conjunction F_i already covers the whole space. U implies Q and any binary representation of U is entailed by Q, so the test is necessary and sufficient. At most 2^{3·2^h} modes give a polynomial input exponent for each fixed h, potentially very large. If accepted, replace old background by Q and unlink all patch sites from their representatives, fixing these sites true. Keep all exterior equalities, rerooting stars as needed. This decreases the count of unpinned physical sites whenever an unpinned site was removed. Repeat at most 3|G| times; a background never needs more than quadratically many distinct clauses. Keeping mode witnesses per step permits reverse reconstruction. Failure to find a patch does not imply unsatisfiability.

On the forced-subgroup mass example with H of dimension 10, all H sites are constants once the shared bit z is forced by the unit source clause after occurrence identification. Its false set S is sum-free. The three hard rails outside H use distinct nonzero tag cosets; a mixed clause has a true outside site. Hence the entire patch contributes no new requirements except preserving any boundary value not otherwise forced. Even if z is left symbolic and the designated S sites are all controlled by this same literal, either their false set is empty or it is S, and both are safe. One may shrink the ambient space further. Let A,B,C be potentially false exterior coordinates. Choose a0 in A,b0 in B, translate the three rails by a0,b0,a0+b0, and restrict to the linear span of the three translated sets; outside sites are true, and all zero triples are preserved. Empty rails make the Latin constraints vacuous. In the mass example the translated span lies in the original hard K. This leaves arbitrary encoded SAT within K.

There are also reductions on literal hard-image cores themselves. For any two different clause labels i,j in the isolated-clause embedding choose W spanned by (i+j,0),(0,i+j), and a=(i,0), b=(0,i). The three chosen patches contain exactly those two clauses' live occurrences on their respective rails: their intersections with the two coordinate axes and diagonal allow precisely labels i,j. Any mixed Latin triple is already true after pins. For example a private z appearing only in (z or x or y),(not z or x or y) disappears, leaving x or y even if both x and y have other occurrences and both clauses contain three distinct literals. For feasibility z can be projected; for assignment preservation even a protected z is harmless here since the conjunction equals x or y on all three coordinates.
