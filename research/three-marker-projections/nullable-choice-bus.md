# A nullable equality bus and guarded endpoint reads

Use rails v0,...,v(k+1) and path transpositions e_j=(v_j,v_(j+1)) for 0≤j≤k. In increasing order site i=1,...,k contributes e_(i−1) for L, e_i for R, or the identity for *. A nonnullable L/R site is implemented by one optional native rotation and a fixed transposition. The nullable result is an interface theorem for sites whose actual returns have the stated three actions.

An internal e_j is used by at most two consecutive sites, so duplicate copies cancel. Surviving edges occur in path order and form a forest. For k≥2 the seam marks v1,vk join precisely if every internal edge survives: [c_j=R] XOR [c_(j+1)=L]=1. With no star this forces all letters equal. A star forces every preceding letter to be R and every later one L, by induction through these same equations. Conversely uniform words and all R^a * L^b with a+b+1=k satisfy the equations. Thus every accepting word has at most one erased site and a uniquely directed change of sign there. The k=1 language is tautological, agreeing with the word characterization if two marks are placed on one rail.

With binary nonnullable bus sites there is also an arbitrary-length guarded read. Add one optional verification transposition before the bus on e0 and another after it on ek; mark v0,v(k+1). A private verifier block for a predicate A may either return identity or return the transposition when A holds. For deterministic two-port predicates this can be obtained by conjoining the predicate with a fresh existential bit through the two-edge path with endpoint marks, then cutting at the two marks. The two private verifier blocks and the bus share no internal controls. Any duplicate endpoint transpositions cancel. A nonuniform binary word still leaves a missing internal cut. On the uniform L word e0 is already present; choose its tester off, and supply ek through its verifier. On the uniform R word supply e0 and choose the ek tester off. Thus original marks join under some tester setting precisely for uniform inputs passing the verifier for their common value. All mismatches reject regardless of both tester settings.

The bus is not an unguarded wire that distributes its common value into other subcircuits. Local reading or interactions that alter the prescribed return actions require their own composition proof.
