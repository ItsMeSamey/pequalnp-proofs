# Four ternary syndrome tests require seven auxiliary switches

For the four distinct projective directions in F₃², a fixed equal-syndrome fiber in F₃⁴ is a nine-word code with every pair at distance three. A representation with one unrestricted physical three-rotation per trit and independent binary auxiliary rotations must use at least seven auxiliaries.

By ternary-radius-two.md each productive fixed auxiliary mask accepts a singleton. Retain the twelve input-trit sockets and two passive marked cuts, suppressing all other paths under that auxiliary mask; make the cuts distinct from trit sites by deterministic subdivision. This gives one fixed cap permutation K before testing the trit choices. At the licensed word x, its actual physical return is K R(x). The uniqueness proof fixes its form up to labeling: one circle (r,u₁,u₂,u₃,u₄,s,v₄,v₃,v₂,v₁), with a singleton w_i on each of four distinct outside circles. A trit triple is labeled u_i,v_i,w_i in some order and the four triples may occur in any nesting order. These are all possibilities, not merely selected example topologies.

Normalize one licensed word to zero by a constant right composition at both caps; changing labels at individual trits can also reverse their orientations. Hence for the distance test fix the first cap to the above canonical permutation K. For another licensed word y enumerate its 4! nesting orders, 6^4 socket roles and all eighty nonzero trit words. If P is its actual permutation at y then its raw cap is P R(−y). The number of changed return tails equals the support size of (P R(−y))K⁻¹. Direct enumeration in singleton_cap_metric.c gives minimum support 3,4,7,8 at input Hamming distances 1,2,3,4. The corresponding minimum transposition distances are 2,2,4,4. The program checks all cap pairs modulo the stated normalizations and does not branch on hypothetical witnesses.

One binary native switch changes at most three first-return tails on this fixed fourteen-site boundary, since every changed tail requires a changed departure on its own old return segment. In consequence any two auxiliary settings licensing different fiber words are at Hamming distance at least three. Choose one auxiliary witness for each of the nine words. They cannot fit in six bits: after padding a shorter code to length six, append one parity check to obtain nine length-seven words of minimum distance four. Their total pair distance is at least 36×4=144. Each coordinate splits nine words into two groups and contributes at most 4×5=20; seven coordinates contribute at most 140. This contradiction proves the bound. It excludes six or fewer auxiliaries in this trit interface, not arbitrary polynomial-size controllers.
