# Mixing conditional broadcasts and matching modules

Retain the independent public-nullable-broadcast modules and their arbitrary fixed exterior permutation. Permit additional independent modules on disjoint socket sets which realize exactly the matchings of given graphs, with constructive local lifts. Mark placements are exterior initially.

For fixed broadcaster data choices and chosen clause matchings M use the available graph on baseline cycles: put in every available broadcast edge and all selected edges of M. If a physical setting joins the marks then the two baseline vertices lie in one graph component. Conversely take a simple connecting path, retain only the edges of M on it, and enable only its required broadcast taps. Submatchings are permitted. These transpositions form a forest on the original baseline cycles, hence their physical product joins the marks. This argument is existential over allowed M; it does not require a prescribed unmodifiable M to make the forest effect.

Contract fixed-availability ordinary edges and every nonempty broadcast leaf set as in public-nullable-broadcast.md. Keep every candidate clause-matching edge between its quotient vertices. Give both incidence ends of each variable-choice edge its own variable color, shared by its two alternatives. At each end of a clause edge use an incidence color equal to its original terminal socket, with a separate color namespace. Discard loops; parallel edges remain distinct. A simple path using distinct incidence colors at every internal vertex uses at most one alternative for any variable (the two share the contracted leaf endpoint), and its selected clause edges cannot share an original socket (that socket belongs to one quotient vertex). Conversely any connected choice of variable modes and clause matchings gives a simple path obeying these restrictions. The incidence and color-pair perfect-matching construction uses only the color at that end of an edge, so it works without any requirement that both ends have the same color.

Extract the data decisions and allowed clause matching M from the path, fill every unspecified variable choice, and reconstruct a simple connecting path in the original available graph. Rebuilding before choosing the final activated subset avoids treating a contracted leaf connection as physical wiring. When both root vertices coincide after unconditional contractions, arbitrary data values and empty clause matchings suffice for the connectivity phase; then rebuild to select the witness as before. Internally marked broadcast modules are treated by pinning their data switches and converting their taps into ordinary independent transpositions. Enumerate the original settings of any internally marked matching module if its size is bounded or its marked behavior otherwise has a supplied constructive table. No new auxiliary coupling is assumed.
