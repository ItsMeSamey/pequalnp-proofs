The bounded-apex result already proves binary score-only closure and its bounded-apex, bounded-genus corollary. A separate orientable-surface result supplies the group-only base case. The charged-modulator extension handles a fixed finite abelian group and a fixed-degree polynomial on original vertex-to-part indicators. Its assembly combines representatives across components and recovers an original connected partition. The polynomial case assumes a base oracle that supports the corresponding local polynomial tests.
