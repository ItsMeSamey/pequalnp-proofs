# A two-score native cap preserves all shared-row tests at once

Every relation on two elements of F₃ is represented by a native constant-size rooted cap, specifying which of the nine pairs accept. The two inputs here are three-channel cyclic rotations, not binary truth values. Number eight tracks from zero. First rotate tracks 0,1,2 by a and 3,4,5 by b, leaving tracks 6,7 fixed. Apply one fixed permutation, then at most two independently optional three-cycles on the tracks in the stated order. Close each track to its input and mark cuts 6,7. Acceptance means that some auxiliary setting joins these marks.

Each line of two_trit_catalog.txt lists the hexadecimal truth mask with bit a+3b, auxiliary count, eight permutation images and the ordered lists of optional rotation triples. The 36 representatives, under independent input relabelings by S₃, cover all 512 relations. Of these rows 21 use no auxiliary, 12 use one and 3 use two. The checker evaluates every word and every auxiliary setting, and independently verifies the disjoint coverage. Every permutation of F₃ has form t↦±t+c, implemented by reversing the direction convention for that accumulator and absorbing a fixed rotation into the wiring.

Consequently a single physical instance with n designated logical switches can evaluate every list of tests depending only on a pair of mod-three linear scores on disjoint groups of binary variables. Each source bit applies its optional rotation on its own triple of sockets in the three-track serial accumulator of its group. Coefficient −1 reverses the orientation and constants are fixed rotations. Evaluate all tests together on the nine possible score pairs to form one table; no input is copied across diagnostic caps. Choose the corresponding representative and relabel the scores. From a successful physical setting extract the logical bit values of the original switches. Conversely a logical witness fixes those switches and one can test at most four auxiliary words to extend it. Unused variables may be assigned isolated switches. The size and uniform construction time are polynomial in the explicit input.

For signed NAE-3 rows, the sum of the three literal bits modulo three is nonzero exactly on satisfaction. Substitution of literal signs gives a constant vector c and a ternary coefficient matrix A for the list of all clauses, even with repeated literals. If every nonzero column of A is proportional to either U or V, the joint list is c+aU+bV with disjoint variable groups; rank zero and one need only the corresponding number of independent groups. The nine-entry test accepts when every coordinate is nonzero. This includes arbitrarily many interacting rows and repeated variable occurrences under the stated coefficient promise. A rank-two matrix can have four projective column directions and the construction alone does not cover that larger class.

The special four-bit weight-zero-or-two predicate uses the representative 18a with score transforms 1−a, 2−b. After expansion and suppression of deterministic wire segments the six physical gates have wiring (3,4,5,15,8,12,9,10,11,0,14,13,6,16,17,7,1,2) and roots 1,14, with the first four gates the inputs. Across all 16 inputs the auxiliary success counts are 1,0,0,2,0,2,2,0,0,2,2,0,4,0,0,0. This replaces the earlier eight-switch witness for the one remaining signed orbit in the complete four-bit catalog, sharpening the uniform upper bound to six.
