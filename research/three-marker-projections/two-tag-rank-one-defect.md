# One private outlier direction in a linear combination of two tags

Given binary private columns q_i, two tags and two outputs, seek one q-dependency m with prescribed nonzero gain b and both tags simultaneously matched on its support. Suppose a nonzero combination of the tags differs from a private linear prediction only on columns at a single private point k, possibly many indexed copies. Recognize this by enumerating the three combinations and candidate column k and solving linear equations outside that point. Replace that tag by its residual B and choose a complementary tag A; this invertible change and private shift preserve matching and transport charts. Remove zero columns with any nonzero tag as permanently inactive. If B vanishes after this, solve the one-tag two-output case directly. Otherwise k is nonzero. There is no width or rank bound on the connected private host.

Write E for the physical indices with B=1. For words avoiding E take the B chart zero. In the named three-space (A,gain), enumerate the exact traces of ordinary columns (q_i,A_i,gain_i) outside E which lie in the pure gain plane and contain b. Such a trace supplies one common A chart and the actual dependency m by elimination; conversely supp(m) gives such a trace. This is the prior three-name solver.

Every solution using E has B chart one at k. Guess the A chart value α at k. The eligible E sites are exactly its copies with A=α. The selected subset of E must have even cardinality: the sum of B on every matched dependency is zero and B vanishes outside. Thus the exceptional private sum vanishes, and possible exceptional gain corrections are a linear subspace V of the output plane obtained from even subsets of the eligible class. Keep actual subset preimages by elimination. If b belongs to V take that exceptional subset alone, extending the two chart values from k. If V=0 any positive with E would already yield a solution to the first branch by discarding E. Otherwise V is a line avoiding b. Choose the nonzero functional ℓ annihilating V, so ℓ(b)=1.

For sites outside E lift to (q_i,A_i,ℓ(gain_i)) and use the named space N=span(k)⊕F₂_A⊕F₂_score. Query its EXACT pure score line. Necessity: on selected outside sites, B chart vanishes but is one on k, so k is outside their q-span. The A chart gives zero A on their private dependencies, and the outside part has ℓ=1 because exceptional gains lie in V. Sufficiency: the exact line gives an ordinary dependency with ℓ=1, forces k outside the selected private span (any representation of k lifts to a forbidden trace), and makes A consistent there. Therefore both chart values freely extend on k, for B by one and for A by α. The difference between b and the true outside gain lies in V. Take its stored even exceptional subset, expand to original indices and undo the tag change. This deterministic polynomial algorithm preserves one support throughout.

More generally if a supplied matched dependency a has gain c≠0,b, a chart matching a and attaining b exists iff there is a compatible dependency with ℓ(gain)=1 where ℓ annihilates c. Toggle a as needed. The scalar query with a forced active can be reduced by contracting its support span as in fixed-circuit-completion.md: if ℓ is nonzero on an internal dependency take it directly, otherwise translate both tags and ℓ to vanish there, solve the two-tag one-gain pure-line query on the quotient, and correct the lifted private sum by true support sites. This does not discover arbitrary a.
