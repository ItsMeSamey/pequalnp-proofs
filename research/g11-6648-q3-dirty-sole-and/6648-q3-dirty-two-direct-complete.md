# Two direct dirty-AND-receiving OR gates at q=3, dirty sole x-AND

Under the original size-21 least TopForm hypotheses, x=3's sole reader is dirty g=x∧S and its unique reader is the raw-empty M=g∨D; j≥3, q=3. Besides M exactly one other exceptional OR Q has a direct dirty AND pin. The third exceptional OR W is either pure or inherits dirty information through ORs without having a direct dirty AND pin. We prove U≤j+2c+3. Charge gates, not pins. Treat the original two x edges separately whenever using the least size-19 x=false card; it removes exactly g and M and aliases M to the singleton-negative D. M and D both have empty raw support, although the supplier of a surviving card pin can change. Original star labels are retained. A positive signal on both singleton 6 and singleton 7 cannot have an OR-only path to an ordinary top inlet: its opposite raw vertex would have to be x and x only reads into dirty g.

## Pure W, unrestricted location of Q

D cannot be a pure raw-positive wire; if D is an OR with empty raw support it can only be Q with empty support, since W is pure and M later. After the x restriction the one possible pretop dirty injection is Q, with W the sole pure exception. No pretop OR other than Q can read M: it would have an empty-raw input and would therefore be charged and impure, excluding W. Any direct pin change at Q itself due to deleting M just forwards D. If Q is raw-positive, that alias cannot occur at Q because the other input is a direct dirty AND and the two raw-empty pins would not give positive support. If Q is raw-empty its two card pins are dirty ANDs: an earlier raw-empty OR besides the removed M does not exist in that case. This covers Q before M in S or D, Q strictly downstream of M, and Q incomparable to M. The pre-Q ancestry after removing M consists of pure ORs and AND products. The two children of W are pure ordinary anchored supports or literals in the original and unchanged.

For empty Q, all outgoing pretop paths use only ANDs after Q. Least-19 factor extraction localizes Q to a single centreless root; expanding its two arms there adds at most one batch to the c batches of pure-clause products. Each clean batch has only the one binary exception W with anchored/literal children, thus accepts ≤2 residuals by singleton_6648_q2_sole_or_all_c_cases.md. For positive Q=K∨R, a centreless root expands into at most two such products by projection of the dirty pin false, or true with actual K retained as a guard. Mixed Lambda ORs project to their own original raw support, while Q gives its pure arm R. Ordinary corrections aggregate into one K times the union of opposite guards, anchored by any original raw member of R. If R=W this positive arm is the exceptional W, not an assumed anchored clause. In particular only original physical W can be exceptional wherever it occurs.

In the positive case these ≤2c+1 batches give U≤4c+4. If there are ≤4 original stars there is at most one exclusively assigned matching double among the batches and at most one touching double: an induced double requires at least three anchored corners because only W can reject a fourth orientation. Distinct anchors would cost at least five stars; with a common anchor only edges 14 and 59 can be the double. A touching double forces W to equal its two mates, with unique pivot. Hence U≤2c+5 in this sparse-star case. At ≥5 stars U≤11, enough except c≤1 (use 4c+4) or c=2,j=3. There, two distinct direct empty operands at the centreless tops exhaust the j−1 surviving dirty ANDs including K. Distinctness follows by grouping two top terms with a shared direct operand into one top. The root reading K needs only its K-true branch; hence at most four batches and U≤10. Details of both geometric arguments are in bounds_6648_q3_two_entrances_one_pure.md, hash 4a12eef7de52d9e4db4f4e5f6d059f7f8bc6bfd723417398daa09e3faec64c27. The minimum-card reasoning here supplies the previously unstated extension to non-upstream Q.

## Inherited W: dispatch by original OR-only paths

With no pure charge, at least one direct entrance must occur in S. Otherwise its pure AND frontier requires a pure clause containing both 6 and 7, with only possible anchor x. Thus Q precedes S and M, and Q is the first dirty OR entrance. If W is itself upstream of S, that still requires Q upstream of W. The first pretop OR reached along an OR-only path from empty M is charged, so if W inherits M, it reads M directly. Four exclusive cases follow.

1. **No M OR-only path to W; Q positive.** Here W inherits Q. After x=false Q is still the only direct dirty-AND entrance: no new M→D pin points into a pretop OR (such a reader would have to be W). Write Q=K∨R. The raw branch at Q projects W to its exact original raw support, common at every fanout. Each of W's two projected child supports is ordinary: a Lambda child gives its own original anchor; a direct Q child gives its physically pure arm R; a raw input is a singleton. R is pure and ordinary since Q is first and W is strictly later. In K-true mode any W that inherits Q is true, so it either vanishes as a conjunction factor or its downstream OR cone is true. W is thus the only possible exceptional projected primitive and its two projected children are each ordinary. A correction at an ordinary root still has an original anchor from R (also contained in every W-inheriting arm). The entire positive-Q pure-W batch, touching/matching, and c=2,j=3 reasoning above applies, with the single global projected support of W playing the role of its support. Successor AND/OR reconvergence does not alter that projection.

2. **No M OR-only path to W; Q empty.** Q=K1∨K2 has two distinct dirty AND arms, each a conjunction of pure anchored clauses. W=Q∨R is the sole raw-positive restoration and R is pure ordinary; otherwise an earlier charged gate, additional dirty AND inlet or duplicate Q would be required. After x=false no pretop OR reads the M→D alias; independent direct D factors can remain and are handled in the same global Q projection. For actual Q=false set Q false throughout; W becomes R and there is at most one clean anchored batch per centreless top, no ordinary correction. For actual Q=true set Q true throughout, including at all AND and OR fanouts, and guard each resulting clean top term separately by K1 or K2. Each guarded term is below the original top since Ki forces Q. Positive OR descendants of W become true; all other pure clauses remain anchored. There are at most two more batches per centreless top, and two ordinary corrections Ki times a union of pure opposite guards anchored by R. Thus ≤3c+2 batches, implying U≤3c+5 when U≥8 by the no-x-star exclusive-double footprint. Distinct raw-empty operands selected at the centreless original boundary in the least card come from the j−1 surviving dirty ANDs plus Q, so c≤j. At c=j−1 or c=j at least two or three of K1,K2,Q respectively are selected at distinct roots. Each such root is false on actual Q=false and omits its low-mode batch. This pays any deficit; when c≤j−2 no rebate is needed. At U≤7 the target holds if c≥1, and at c=0 the two x edges plus two ordinary batches of size ≤2 give U≤6. No disjointness of input donor cones is assumed.

3. **W directly reads M and its other pin inherits Q by an independent OR-only path avoiding M.** For positive Q this is bounds_6648_q3_joining_secondary.md (dc92437b2948d3099ec1c23528a8a2bdd18d8f9ec17a4c3aa71161a18802f703, 07/08 source GO). Its two flags distinguish K=true and K=false, projecting the latter W to A∨L with a separate original guard anchor; c=j−1 forces a useful direct K root. For empty Q no fourth charge exists for a raw-positive restoration on either pre-W route; W is empty, all three charges are raw-empty, and 6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md applies. The independence here means two different routes to the two W pins; their ancestors and dirty AND factors may be shared.

4. **W directly reads M, other pin has no such Q route.** That pin is pure and nonempty, since a dirty AND pin would make W direct and an earlier dirty OR ancestor would provide precisely the excluded Q bypass (or a redundant M path). Therefore W=M∨R with R pure raw-positive. With Q empty, S=Q=K1∨K2, j≥4, the D-positive edges use at most four protected anchored batches and the other edges at most 2c; see bounds_6648_q3_empty_then_m_only.md (a78fcaff9d385979e6492643f962b22d13e61f415a41598a4ae98bdac0016a56, 07/08 source GO). With Q positive S likewise equals Q by the actual paired-singleton frontier test, and Q cannot OR-reach any ordinary top. With M and D suppressed on D-negative pairs there is no ordinary correction. The j=3 protected two-/three-clause bounds pay the remaining tight case; see 6648-q3-inherited-pure-arm-positive-Q.md. These statements allow Q→S→g→M→W or Q→D→M→W paths, which do not meet case 3's separate-pin hypothesis.

The cases exhaust all two-direct configurations. The numerical dependencies are the previously established graph/card and anchored matching lemmas and the original no-x-star footprint, not minimality of any intermediate donor-pin projection.
