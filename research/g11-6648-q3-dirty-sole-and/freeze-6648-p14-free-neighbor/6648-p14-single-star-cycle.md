# Single star edge and the true-endpoint cofactor

Let a least AO2 TopForm realization have exactly one star edge ab and t ordinary tops. The number of gates saved at a=true is at least 3t−2 for t≥1. The symmetric bound holds at b=true.

Each ordinary top has raw supports {a},{b}. Its a-side pins are pairwise physically distinct. In fact two tops sharing an operand can be replaced by one AND with the OR of their opposite pins, then their outputs combined as one term. The old clean output region is an OR aggregation of the clean top values: a clean OR has two clean predecessors, no raw input is clean, and no top can feed an AND by TopForm. Flatten this region first, at no cost (k distinct top terms require k−1 ORs, and the old connected binary region contains at least that many). Regroup the two terms as siblings, saving one gate. The same reasoning applies to b-side pins. Use a tree of k−1 aggregation gates from now on; in a least circuit the regrouping preserves size and all original top input signals.

Set raw a true. At least t−1 nonraw distinct a-side pins are true because each is OR-reachable from a. Delete them, along with the t ordinary ANDs, replacing their outputs by their b-side wires. Preserve the unchanged OR paths from b to all these b-side wires, and the aggregation tree above these wires, before any further constant folding. The three sets of named gates (a-side ORs, ordinary ANDs, retained OR paths/tree) are disjoint by raw support or clean status. Gates outside this structure may temporarily read constants. Each retained OR gate has two pin occurrences even if they now coincide. No retained output-cone signal is constant true, since the restricted function is nonconstant.

Let O be the maximal output OR region in this partly folded circuit, including the output OR and recursively all OR predecessors. Include each distinct non-OR boundary wire as a vertex and each pin incidence of an O gate as a separate edge. The graph is a connected undirected multigraph with |O|+h vertices and 2|O| edges, where h counts distinct boundary wires. Its cycle rank is |O|−h+1. The old aggregation tree and OR paths from b represent a subgraph of rank at least t−1: take the subtree joining the t ordinary leaf *positions* in the aggregation tree, retaining their t inlet edges, and attach the t positions to their actual b-side pins. The latter lie in one connected lower subgraph by OR reachability from b. Before attaching, the upper aggregation subtree and lower connected graph are disjoint; t attachment edges therefore supply at least t−1 independent undirected cycles. This count includes raw b as a possible pin and applies to ancestor-related pins. For t=1 only the trivial rank bound is needed.

Flatten O to the OR of its h boundary wires with at most h−1 gates. An interior O wire which also feeds an external gate can be replaced by false on every such external use, so those interior gates need not be retained. To see correctness, monotonicity gives the decreasing direction; on an original true input choose the earliest true interior O gate. It has a true boundary predecessor, as all earlier interior O gates were false. Substituting false for earlier interior uses leaves this boundary predecessor true. The new output is therefore true. The flattening saves at least |O|−h+1≥t−1 gates, in addition to the earlier t+(t−1) deletions. Fold any remaining constants/aliases. The nonconstant output admits an AO2 circuit, giving the asserted cofactor savings.
