# Two dirty-AND-receiving OR gates and one pure exceptional OR

In the size-21 q=3 dirty sole x-AND case let the charged gates be M=g∨D, one secondary dirty-AND-receiving Q upstream of M, and a pure OR W. Set x=false; the least size-19 cofactor removes g and M, aliasing M to D. Original-star accounting is on the surviving edges plus the two x spokes. The hypothesis of this note is that after the alias Q is the only pretop OR receiving an AND and all other pretop OR wires project to their original raw supports after erasing the Q donor pin(s). In particular this holds for upstream Q and the original charges precisely M,Q,W: no mixed raw-positive extension of raw-empty M can cost a fourth charge. Original x never stars an edge.

If Q is raw-empty, its two pins are dirty ANDs; there is no charged pretop OR to restore positive raw reach after Q. The sole downstream centreless top follows by factoring Q once, then doing the same for its AND descendants in the least card. Splitting its Q arms gives two clean pure-clause terms, and every other centreless root has one. Only W is an exceptional primitive, with anchored/singleton children. Hence the one-exception bound of singleton_6648_q2_sole_or_all_c_cases.md gives U≤2+2(c+1).

If Q=K∨R is raw-positive, K is a dirty AND whose upstream clauses are pure. Every mixed boundary OR in the cofactor is K∨L where L is its original raw support and contains R. The raw terms and K-positive terms at an original centreless root are two clean conjunctions: the former sets the donor pin false, the latter retains K and the pure guard clauses, dropping all the mixed clauses. In addition, all residuals of ordinary roots can be assigned to one term K times the disjunction of their opposite guards. Such guards are pure since mixed Q on both sides would activate a singleton of R. Every member of R stars every guard vertex, providing an anchor for the entire union. In both kinds of term the only unanchored possible primitive is W: Q projects to its other pure arm, an original Lambda boundary projects to its anchored support, and W's two child wires are pure anchored/singleton. Every batch is clean and accepts at most two residuals. Assign surviving residuals exclusively; there are at most 2c+1 batches, giving U≤4c+4 after adding the x spokes.

When the original stars cover at most four graph edges a sharper bound holds. A disjoint assigned double is an induced matching by the one-exception lemma. Its four independent orientations require four distinct rejecting clauses and at least three are anchored. The three distinct complementary corners thereby have anchors outside all marked vertices (otherwise their incident mark is starred). Three distinct anchors create six distinct star edges. A repeated anchor on adjacent corners covers three endpoints, and any other anchor supplies at least two different star edges; the anchors cannot be marked endpoints. A repeated anchor on opposite corners covers all four endpoints and is the unique possible anchor for every corner by C4-freeness. Hence with at most four stars all three anchors coincide, and the four endpoints lie in one neighborhood. Only N(8) contains two disjoint graph edges, namely 14 and 59, so this can occur in at most one batch. A touching double at p forces the exceptional support W to be exactly its two mates on the card (the one-exception lemma with anchored/singleton children). Their common neighbor p is unique, so at most one such double occurs across the exclusive allocation. Therefore U≤2+(2c+1)+2≤2c+j+3 in this case.

With ≥5 stars U≤11. This suffices except possibly c≤1 or c=2,j=3. For c≤1 use 4c+4. In the last case the two original centreless roots have raw-empty direct operands that in the 19-card are AND gates: the sole mixed OR Q in this card is raw-positive, D is singleton-negative and therefore an AND here, and an ordinary G11 star cannot become newly centreless. The two chosen operand wires are distinct, since two roots sharing the same direct pin admit an immediate factoring and smaller aggregated circuit. They therefore exhaust the two dirty ANDs remaining from the original j=3 after removing g (the raw-empty operands cannot be original clean tops, by TopForm). In particular K itself is such a direct operand. At this top the K branch includes its entire top; the raw branch is redundant when K is present as a separate conjunct. At most four batches remain, so U≤2+8=10 as required.

This gives U≤2c+j+3 under the stated upstream configuration without new enumeration. It relies on the pre-established 19-card, masking, and one-exception geometry lemmas. No combined Lean check was performed.
