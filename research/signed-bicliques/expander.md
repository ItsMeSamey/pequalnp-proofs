# Sparse signed 3-CNF on expanding incidence graphs

## Exact interface size of a graphic parity core

Let G be a connected simple cubic graph on N vertices and M=3N/2 edges. Use one Boolean variable per edge. Each vertex parity constraint is encoded by four signed three-literal clauses on distinct variables, forbidding its four wrong-parity words. Each variable occurs eight times. There are zero solutions if the total charge is odd and 2^(M-N+1) otherwise: choose nontree bits freely, solve tree edges from the leaves inwards, and use the charge condition at the root. Each edge in a bridgeless graph belongs to a cycle; flipping this cycle pairs solutions with opposite values on that edge. Every variable is unbiased despite the exponentially small solution fraction 2^(1-N).

For a feasible charge the satisfaction matrix on any split S|T of edge variables has ordinary rank over any field exactly 2^lambda, where lambda=N+1-c_S-c_T and c_S,c_T count spanning-subgraph components including isolated vertices. The binary incidence matrix H has rank N-1 and H_S,H_T have ranks N-c_S,N-c_T. Group rows and columns by partial syndrome: nonzero blocks are all-ones rectangles matched by the charge. The matching syndromes form a translate of the intersection of im(H_S) and im(H_T), hence there are 2^lambda disjoint blocks.

Suppose G is 3-vertex-connected with edge expansion h. For EVERY partition with both sides having between M/3 and 2M/3 edges,

    lambda >= 1 + hN/[18(3+h)].

Let B be the number of vertices touching both colors and c the total number of nonisolated monochromatic components. Each interior vertex is isolated in exactly one color, hence lambda=B+1-c. Each nonsingle-edge component has at least three boundary vertices. Otherwise its nonempty interior is separated from any outside vertex by at most two vertices. If the component spans G, its at most two boundary vertices could support at most one edge of the opposite color, contradicting balance. Each single-edge component has two boundary endpoints. At any boundary vertex just one color has degree one, so the number a of single-edge components satisfies 2a<=B. Counting boundary incidences yields 2B>=3c-a and c<=5B/6. The smaller pure color class X has size at most N/2. Its union with the boundary hosts all edges of that color. Cubicity gives |X|+B>=2(M/3)/3=N/3. Every edge leaving X reaches the boundary, so h|X|<=3B and B>=hN/[3(3+h)].

An exact pointwise tree tensor representation on the original variables needs exponentially many bond states at a balanced tree edge when leaves have o(M) variables. Descend via heavier children to the first node of size at most 2M/3. A sum of separable products across the cut needs at least the ordinary matrix rank many terms even with signed coefficients. Scalar counting and auxiliary-variable rewrites are outside this model. The cut identity has precedents in code trellises and Schmidt decompositions; exponential DNNF lower bounds for satisfiable Tseitin formulas are also established.

## Actual CNF incidence expansion

For a base graph of maximum degree Delta and edge expansion h, replace each vertex by a nonempty connected cluster of at most K vertices and preserve a distinct intercluster edge for each base edge. Extra edges are allowed. For a cut U of at most half the new vertices, let C count its boundary edges and p its split clusters. Then C>=p. Each side contains at least |U|/K-p pure clusters. The smaller pure class sends at least h(|U|/K-p) base edges out; at most Delta p reach split clusters. Combining yields C>=h|U|/[K(1+h+Delta)].

In the parity CNF, orient the cubic base so all indegrees are 1 or 2 (add a matching on its odd vertices, orient an Euler circuit in the resulting multigraph, discard added edges). Group each edge variable with its head's four clauses. Each group induces a connected K_(4,1) or K_(4,2), and distinct original edges are represented between corresponding groups. Thus the variable-clause incidence graph has edge expansion h0>=h/[6(4+h)].

Morgenstern's cubic Ramanujan Cayley graphs have h>=(3-2sqrt(2))/2 by applying the spectral bound to a centered indicator of a set of at most N/2 vertices. They have growing girth. For sufficiently large members, deleting at most two vertices cannot disconnect them: the smaller resulting component has at most 6/h vertices since its boundary has at most six edges. Large girth makes this component a tree and prevents any removed vertex from having two neighbors in it. Its cubic edge boundary would have |component|+2>2 edges. This gives 3-connectivity on the tail.

Theorem 5.13 and Corollary 5.14 of Morgenstern use any irreducible binary polynomial of even degree t>=2, giving N_t=2^(3t)-2^t vertices and N_(t+2)/N_t<=68. Thus the least admissible size above a target is O(target), after any fixed cutoff. Deterministically enumerate irreducible polynomials by testing monic candidates and trial divisors up to degree t/2 in O(2^(1.5t)poly(t)) bit operations, find a root of i^2+i+1 by scanning the field, enumerate normalized nonsingular 2x2 projective matrices (O(2^(3t)) candidates), and multiply by each of the three given generators. Sorting indices costs O(N polylog N) bit operations overall.

## Nonaffine defects on a graphic parity core

Let D be the d distinct edge coordinates touched by any additional fixed-width clauses. Form all connected components of the spanning graph with D deleted, including isolated vertices; write c for their number. For a given assignment to D, feasibility requires that in each component the XOR of charges agrees with the XOR of marked edges leaving it. Internal marked edges cancel. These conditions suffice: choose untouched nontree bits in each component and use its spanning tree to complete. Every feasible assignment has exactly 2^(M-d-N+c) extensions.

The component tests have at most 2d literal incidences altogether. Check empty supports directly; serialize every other parity check into O(arity) uniquely determined XOR gates and their fixed-width CNF constraints. This leaves O(d) variables and O(d) parity clauses, independently of original graph size. If the number of additional clauses is O(d), the October 25 paper gives poly(L) 2^O(d/sqrt(log d)) time; arbitrary density gives poly(L) 2^O(d log log d/sqrt(log d)). L is original explicit representation length; d=0 and small cases are elementary. No expansion hypothesis is required for the algorithm.

## Parsimonious reduction with every clause genuinely ternary

Normalize duplicate literals and discard tautologies in any width-three CNF, retaining declared free variables. For each variable with r occurrences create r copies, one per occurrence, linked by a directed cycle of implications if r>=2. For r=0 keep one free coordinate. All copies agree, giving an assignment bijection. Each resulting source variable occurs at most three times. Allocate one cotree edge per such variable and one fresh reserved cotree edge for each missing literal position in a clause shorter than three. Pad each short clause with its own reserved positive literals. An empty clause uses three reserved zeros. Choose a cubic expander supplying these positions and at least three cotree coordinates. Set all vertex charges to zero.

All cotree coordinates except the designated source variables must be zero. Index the full cotree cyclically. For each such unused coordinate x_i insert four clauses (not x_i OR +/-x_(i+1) OR +/-x_(i+2)), one per choice of signs. Together they impose precisely x_i=0. Every clause has three distinct variables. A coordinate appears in at most twelve pin clauses (four as target, eight as guard), eight parity clauses and at most three source clauses, including at most one use if reserved for padding. The maximum occurrence is 23. Every source assignment determines all cotree coordinates, hence has a unique tree completion. Every output model yields a source model.

Anchor each added clause to one neighboring variable. Each connected cluster around a core incidence vertex has size at most 16, since a variable has at most 15 new occurrences. The core graph has maximum degree eight. The cluster lemma gives full incidence edge expansion at least h0/[16(9+h0)]. Duplicate overlay clauses, if removed, merely reduce the number of anchored additions. The core cannot duplicate an overlay because each 3-variable star includes at least one spanning-tree edge. Source size n+m requires O(n+m) cotree capacity. Morgenstern's geometrically spaced sizes give O(n+m) output variables and clauses and O((n+m)polylog(n+m)) deterministic construction time. Indexed text encodings may introduce a logarithmic bit factor relative to combinatorial size.

Consequently bounded-occurrence signed three-distinct-literal SAT on this expander promise is NP-hard and exact counting is #P-hard parsimoniously. The controlled test has a visible affine cancellation; the source difficulty survives entirely on free cotree coordinates. Positive monotone clauses alone admit the all-ones assignment. A uniquely satisfiable input remains uniquely satisfiable; nothing here requires a deterministic reduction from general SAT to Unique-SAT.

## A polynomial preprocessor and exact width test for affine elimination

Group clauses by their exact variable scope after deleting tautologies and duplicate literals. For each group of fixed arity k, form its truth table R. Empty R immediately gives zero models. For nonempty R choose r in R and compute A=r+span{x+r:x in R}. Emit equations for A. Keep only the originally forbidden tuples that lie inside A, using one clause each. Tuples outside A are already excluded. This step is exact for every scope and extracts all affine consequences of its local truth table. Additional affine consequences across scopes may exist. It is not exact to discard residual holes inside A. In particular a full local parity coset is recognized with no residual clauses, without a supplied graph. If only a nonempty subset of that coset remains, its affine consequence is still implied by the extracted equations.

Let P be variables private to the resulting affine system, D those used by remaining clauses. Optionally retain additional variables in D. Solve the affine system or return zero on inconsistency. Express its projection on D as Q=q+U over F_2, and its full direction space as W. Every feasible D-word has 2^(dim W-dim U) extensions. Thus exact residual counting is counting the original nonlinear residual clauses restricted to Q, times this factor.

For fixed w>=1, Q has an exact width-at-most-w CNF on D without auxiliary variables iff U-perp is generated by vectors of Hamming weight at most w. Let H_w be the span of all such dual vectors. Enumerate supports of size at most w, testing membership in U-perp by inner products, and keep a basis of H_w. Its at most |D| vectors with the right-hand sides fixed by q give at most |D|2^(w-1) clauses. Conversely the single forbidden word of any valid width-w clause on S is absent from the affine projection onto S. Linear separation on S gives a dual check supported inside S which the word violates. Therefore ALL valid width-w clauses together describe q+H_w-perp, and width w suffices exactly when H_w=U-perp. If this condition fails, elimination gives a word in (q+H_w-perp) outside Q as a certificate. Full cube has an empty conjunction; an empty feasible space has an empty clause. Running time is polynomial for each fixed w. This criterion concerns the affine projection alone, without auxiliaries; the nonlinear residual can further simplify the final formula.

Consequences: if the projected dual has such a bounded-weight generating basis, turn it into O_w(|D|) bounded-width clauses. With O(|D|) nonlinear defects the manuscript's bounded-density bound runs in poly(L)2^O(|D|/sqrt(log |D|)); with arbitrary density use its general bound. Large projected checks can be genuinely necessary without private wires: a chain of ternary XOR gates imposing one global even parity on d retained bits projects to an affine space whose only nonzero dual vector has weight d. Retaining or reintroducing auxiliaries describes this same relation sparsely.
