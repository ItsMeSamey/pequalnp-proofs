# Fixed finite abelian vertex labels

For ordered connected parts, the expander, isoperimetric and finite cut-profile arguments only require an additive finite abelian label group Γ. Take the subgroup S generated by sufficiently abundant labels. In an order g_1,…,g_k of contributing abundant labels, discard redundant generators. Let S_i be the subgroup generated through g_i and m_i=[S_i:S_(i−1)]. The coset of g_i generates the cyclic factor of order m_i. Descending through the factors expresses every s in S as a sum of c_i g_i with 0≤c_i<m_i. Reserve m_i−1 copies of g_i as private toggles per nonlast part. Their total is at most |S|−1 because sum(m_i−1)≤product(m_i)−1. The target minus current sum in a part is corrected by adding its digit representation from the last part. Subtraction and conservation are in Γ; congruences lie in Γ/S. S=0 is settled by the sparse rooted-minor case. Replace the uniform correction cap r by |Γ|−1 and use |Γ| frequency intervals for the at most |Γ|−1 nonidentity types. Boundary joins, pendant folding and fixed-region deletion use the same additive gluing rules. This extends the graph partition algorithm without implying a nonbinary matroid trace reduction.
