# Ordered binary vertex sums on highly connected graphs

Suppose vertices of G carry labels in a binary vector space of dimension r. Fix b≥2. If the underlying simple graph has vertex connectivity at least b+25(b−1)r+2, then every ordered tuple of b part sums achievable by any vertex assignment is achievable by a partition into b connected nonempty parts. For cographic traces the total label is zero, and b=h+1 suffices for a target of dimension h>0.

Write l=b−1. Select S minimizing the number R of labels outside S plus l dim S and put d=dim S. Comparing with the whole space proves R≤l(r−d). Comparing with each T≤S shows there are at least l(d−dim T) labels in S outside T. By linear matroid union, l disjoint vertex sets in S give bases of S. In detail, the union rank on all in-S vertices E is the minimum of |E−A|+l rank(A) over vertex subsets A. The preceding inequality for the span of A bounds every term below by ld. The opposite inequality holds by rank. Use one such basis as private toggles for each of the first l parts.

Mark all R exceptional vertices outside S and prescribe their parts as in the desired assignment. Mark the ld toggles and choose b further distinct vertices as anchors, one per part. The bounds guarantee enough fresh vertices. Form a template edge from each exception to its assigned anchor and edges from each toggle to its owner's anchor and to the last anchor. There are B=b+R+ld≤b+lr branch vertices and m=R+2ld≤2lr edges.

For each end of each template edge greedily select a fresh distinct neighbor of its branch vertex outside all branch vertices. The minimum degree exceeds B+2m−1. After deleting the B branch vertices, vertex connectivity is at least 12m when m>0. This guarantees m-linkedness, so join the chosen pairs by disjoint paths and attach the end edges to obtain the rooted subdivision. For m=0 skip routing. The required 12m bound follows from [Kawarabayashi, Kostochka and Yu](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/combinatorics-probability-and-computing/article/abs/on-sufficient-degree-conditions-for-a-graph-to-be-klinked/98892C879FEF311999491323E9A15DB3).

For each anchor its paths to prescribed exceptions belong entirely to its core, and its paths to toggles belong there with only the toggle vertex omitted. These cores are connected and disjoint. Every toggle neighbors both its own core and the last one. Deleting all toggles leaves a connected graph because there are at most lr toggles. Grow the cores to cover this remaining graph by repeatedly attaching an adjacent unassigned vertex. Provisionally put all toggles into the last part. Every part-sum deficit belongs to S because every exception is in its prescribed part. For each of the first l parts correct its deficit by moving a subset of its private basis from the last part. Connectivity survives because all toggles adjoin their receiving core, and the last part was connected before toggles were assigned. Its sum is correct by conservation.

Unrestricted assignments and witnesses are found by DP on ordered sums. For cographic traces check that the tuple spans H, and take the edges crossing the resulting connected partition. Subspace enumeration, matroid union and fixed-size disjoint paths make this deterministic FPT in b+r on the stated class. A decision-only disjoint-paths oracle can recover paths by greedily deleting edges that preserve feasibility. [Korhonen, Pilipczuk and Stamoulis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03958)
