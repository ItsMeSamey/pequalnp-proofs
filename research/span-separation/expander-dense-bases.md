# Dense labels in biconnected vertex expanders

Let a simple 2-connected graph on n vertices have vertex expansion α∈(0,1] for sets of size at most n/2. Vertex labels span a binary space of dimension d>0. For b≥2 parts put t=(b−1)d, m=2t. Use b anchor bags with demands d_i=d for the first b−1, d_b=t. Put a_i=ceil(d_i/α), a=max a_i, B=t+sum a_i, c=B+2m, q=ceil(c/α). Assume n≥2(q+a+1). Require a clique minor of order M=B+max(3m+1,q+2m+1). For n>36M³/α² this last condition follows from the Alon–Seymour–Thomas excluded-minor balanced separator theorem: its separator of size ≤M^(3/2)√n would leave a side between n/6 and n/2 and violate expansion.

Choose one singleton toggle per basis label per part except the last, plus connected anchor bags of sizes a_i. Require pairwise distance >2q+4 among the bags and toggle vertices. A sufficient condition is that every member of a full-span label basis occurs more than (t+b) times the largest radius-(2q+2a+6) ball size. Select all t+b centers greedily among the plentiful types and grow the anchor bags within distance a of their centers. This is a constant occurrence threshold for fixed dimension, part count, expansion and degree bound. All other labels are unrestricted.

Demand one path from each toggle to its own anchor and another to the last anchor, for m demands. Remove all B original branch vertices, throw out the clique minor bags touching them, and create one terminal clone per demand end, adjacent to the ordinary outside neighbors of its original branch. The residual model order is ≥3m+1. By Robertson–Seymour Graph Minors XIII Theorem 5.4, the desired disjoint paths exist unless a separation of order <2m places all clones on one side and a whole model bag exclusively opposite. See unbreakable-minor.md for the theorem statement and sources.

Suppose such a separator exists, with ordinary vertices S. Delete S and the B original branch vertices in the original graph. The intact model bags have over q vertices collectively, are pairwise adjacent and thus lie in a component of size >n/2 by expansion. The union outside this giant and the deleted vertices has size ≤q by expansion. The giant contains the exclusively opposite model bag. No branch with a surviving clone has an ordinary neighbor in this giant.

For each branch with a surviving clone, reinsert it separately and take U consisting of it and all adjacent off-giant components. All exterior neighbors of U belong to S because the other branches are farther than 2q+4. For an anchor i we have a_i≤|U|≤a_i+q≤n/2; expansion charges at least αa_i≥d_i distinct separator vertices. For a toggle, biconnectivity charges at least two. Every charged vertex lies within q+1 of its branch, so charges are disjoint. Branches with no surviving clone instead charge all their deleted clones. The total charged equals or exceeds 2m, a contradiction.

The resulting paths share no internal vertices, and no endpoint is used internally. Restore each anchor bag as a connected core and include its paths up to but excluding the neighboring toggles. Deleting all toggles leaves a connected graph: otherwise some component of size ≤n/2 has size ≤t/α≤q, has two different toggle neighbors by biconnectivity, and contradicts their separation. Grow all cores to cover this residual graph. Initially assign all toggles to the last part and then move privately chosen basis subsets to their owners. Every ordered tuple of part sums in the label span adding to the total is attainable. A fixed-number disjoint paths search constructs the linkage without a supplied clique minor once the graph satisfies the numeric sufficient bound. Witness extraction thereafter uses only graph growth and binary linear algebra. For d=0 use a spanning tree.
