# Deterministic separator sampling through neutral exterior columns

Write the representation as Q⊕W with W=K⊕⟨w⟩, r=dim W, m=dim Q, u∈K. Reject w∈K; when w∉K and u=0 use the empty set. Let a kill K and take value one on w. Write each ordinary column as (z_e,h_e). Suppose D={z_e:h_e=0} spans ker x by D∩ker x for every x∈Q*, including zero.

For each x the eligible columns satisfy x(z_e)=a(h_e). Any eligible selection spanning u solves the problem because (x,a) separates w from K and that selection. Conversely any solution admits a separator (x₀,a). Its W labels span u. Use at most r independent labels from it with total u. Their t eligibility equations are consistent. For EVERY x satisfying those equations the sum z of the selected exterior projections is killed by x since a(u)=0. Represent z by eligible neutral columns and add them to obtain a selection summing to u. Hence the successful separators contain a nonempty affine flat of codimension at most r.

A direct n^{O(r)} algorithm enumerates short labelled sets. For deterministic single-exponential dependence on r, choose a binary extension field F with 2^d>2^r m and d minimal (for m=0 just test the only x). For all α,β∈F take x_j=Tr(αβ^j), 0≤j<m. For any nonzero binary z its character expectation equals the fraction of β at which Σ_j z_j β^j=0, by orthogonality of trace characters over α. That fraction is at most (m−1)/2^d. The indicator of any consistent rank-t affine system has expectation at least 2^{-t}(1−(2^t−1)(m−1)/2^d)>0 for t≤r. Enumerate these x and test whether u is spanned by the eligible columns, reconstructing a witness by elimination. A binary irreducible of degree d can be found by exhaustive search with divisibility tests in 2^{O(d)} time. Total deterministic running time is 2^{O(r)}poly(input size). Every returned witness is valid even outside the structural class.

A polynomial-size sufficient certificate is an exterior basis b_i whose members and all pairwise sums b_i+b_j appear as neutral columns. For nonzero x the zero-evaluating basis vectors and the sums of pairs of one-evaluating basis vectors span its kernel. For x=0 use the basis. This gives a spanning clique matroid M(K_{m+1}). It is round (the edge complement of a disconnected spanning subgraph of a complete graph is connected), so adjoining arbitrary exterior directions preserves roundness. For m≥4 adding a direction b_1+b_2+b_3 creates a Fano restriction alongside the six clique directions on the first three coordinates. Thus the class includes arbitrarily high-rank simple nonregular round quotients. The hypothesis concerns the given splitting or an exhibited section of the port basis.

For comparison, when all 2^m−1 nonzero exterior directions appear, exhaustive testing of the 2^m functionals is polynomial regardless of their W labels.

## A routing subspace of bounded codimension

It suffices to have a specified U⊆Q of codimension d whose neutral directions span each hyperplane of U using members within that hyperplane and span U itself. Project the labels of each eligible column to (h_e,z_e mod U). A successful witness spans (u,0) in these labels; choose at most r+d independent projected labels summing to it. Include the corresponding eligibility equations, even when a selected column has h_e=0. For any solution x of these equations the selected exterior sum belongs to U∩ker x. Correct it using eligible neutrals inside U, and reconstruct the original target. Thus the same deterministic hitting family with parameter r+d completes the search in 2^{O(k+d)}poly(input). This permits arbitrary additional columns and a bounded number of exterior directions outside the supplied neutral clique.
