# Dense graph case of cographic terminal traces

Suppose the ordinary quotient matroid modulo a fixed-dimensional interface W is supplied as the cographic matroid of a connected graph G. Keep each edge's W label σ_e in this presentation. Put γ_v equal to the binary sum of σ_e over edges incident to v, counting loops twice. For a selected edge set X, the trace consists of the labels of quotient cycles supported on X. In a cographic matroid those cycles are graph cuts. A cut is supported on X exactly when its vertex shore is a union of connected components of G−X. Its label is the sum of γ_v over that shore. Consequently exact traces are precisely the spans of the part-label sums of connected partitions of V(G). To realize a partition, take X to be the edges between its parts.

If a trace H of dimension h is attainable, it has a realizing connected partition into exactly h+1 parts. Take a realizing partition with the fewest parts, say t, and put its part labels as columns of a matrix L. Their sum is zero, so rank L=h≤t−1. For every adjacent pair i,j, merging the two parts must decrease label rank. Hence some functional on labels vanishes on all the other part labels and their combined sum, yet is nonzero on these two labels. Thus the indicator over parts of the pair i,j lies in the row space of L. Since the part adjacency graph is connected, these pair indicators span the t−1 dimensional even-weight space of the parts. Therefore h≥t−1, as claimed.

Assume the simple underlying graph complement has maximum degree d. A disconnected induced subgraph cannot contain more than 2d vertices: in any partition of it into two nonempty unions of distinct components, every vertex misses every vertex on the other side. To test target H, guess which of h+1 distinguishable parts have size at most 2d and enumerate their disjoint vertex sets. Check that each is nonempty and connected. Assign the remaining vertices by dynamic programming to the remaining parts, recording each part's W-valued sum and its size capped at 2d+1. Require every such part to attain the cap, every part label to be in H, and the combined span to equal H. Backtracking constructs X. For r=dim W the runtime is n^{O(d(r+1))} 2^{O(r(r+1))}(2d+2)^{O(r+1)}poly(input). At d=0 connectedness only requires nonempty parts. The algorithm does not assume that a cographic presentation of the lifted columns exists.
