# A three-bit aggregate selector using Gaussian elimination

For any binary column matrix D and linear tags T_e:F₂²→F₂³, a constant number of Gaussian basis choices and eight-state score computations decide whether two words f,g in ker D have prescribed checksum t and independent common zero columns. The algorithm returns the words. They directly specify the contraction witness for the [four-name aggregate selector](../aggregate-flow-selector/README.md). No regularity assumption is required.

On a column basis B, require that every nonbasis chord has a nonzero two-bit color. The fundamental circuits c_j form a basis of C=ker D. Assigning the nonzero colors on those circuits gives all such word pairs. Eligible words occur on some B since their common zero set extends to a basis. Write M_j=Σ_{e∈c_j}T_e. Rank zero contributes {0} to the checksum, rank one contributes a full binary line, and rank two contributes the image plane with zero removed. A finite set-sum computation on eight states returns actual words.

Two punctured copies of a plane sum to that full plane. Two distinct punctured planes P,Q sum to F₂³ except for the unique nonzero point w in P∩Q. Every point has two decompositions in P+Q, both involving a zero summand exactly at w. A third nonzero factor fills this single hole. Once two copies of the same plane give a full plane, every subsequent factor either lies inside it or meets both cosets. Hence the whole linear image is then attained.

Reject t outside the linear image. If its reference basis already attains t, return the words. Otherwise exactly one of the following occurs.

**One punctured plane.** The exceptional matrix U has image P; all others are rank ≤1, spanning a space L with L∩P=0. Necessarily t∈L, as (P outside zero)+L contains everything else in P+L. The linear matrix gain on C, modulo L, is ℓ times the image of U modulo L, which is injective. Here ℓ is the coefficient of the exceptional reference circuit.

Any other basis with only one nonzero ℓ-value still misses t, since a nonzero chord color then projects nontrivially modulo L. With at least two such circuits, their lifted planes avoid L except at zero. Two *different* lifted planes can miss only a point outside L, while any third nonzero factor fills the hole. Two equal lifted planes give the entire linear image after including the other factors. A basis with at least two nonzero ℓ-values therefore attains t.

Let d_e∈C* be evaluation at coordinate e. Bases of these evaluations are precisely cotrees. The expansion of ℓ in a cotree basis records its values on the corresponding fundamental circuits. There is an expansion using at least two members if and only if the evaluations unequal to ℓ span it. A minimal such representation is independent, has at least two members and extends to a basis. Conversely, an expansion of weight at least two in a basis cannot use ℓ itself. Gaussian elimination produces the desired cotree, or proves none exists.

**Two different punctured planes.** There are exactly two nonzero reference matrices U,V. Their images P,Q are distinct, and the failed t is the nonzero w∈P∩Q. The gain map on C is ℓU+ηV where ℓ,η are independent. Set J=span(ℓ,η)⊂C*. In any basis, gain matrices can only be 0,U,V,W=U+V. Any basis with three or more nonzero gains attains w. If at least two have rank two, either two equal planes or two different planes and a third nonzero factor fill the linear image. Otherwise W has rank one, the sole rank-two gain is U or V, and that gain alone contributes w while the other factors contribute zero.

Greedily take a maximal independent set of physical evaluations outside J and extend it to a cotree. If that cotree contains fewer than two members of J, the union of supports of ℓ and η uses at least three cotree members, since two members spanning J would themselves lie inside J. Return the word witness on this cotree. Otherwise J and the span of evaluations outside J are direct summands of C*. Every cotree splits into exactly two independent members of J and an outside basis. At most three nonzero directions of J occur. Enumerate the available independent pairs, choosing one physical representative of each direction and a fixed outside basis. These exhaust the possible nonzero gain lists and hence all attainable scores.

Run `python check.py` with the sibling `unseeded-root-states/solve.py` routines. The checker compared all eight targets on 2,025 matrices with direct enumeration of word pairs and validated each returned witness. It exercised all seven decision branches, including Fano matrices. This direct small-checksum algorithm is independent of the more general pinned-spanning-set recursion.
