# Unweighted three-port parity representatives

Assume no direct port edges. Record three bits for nonzero parity on the corresponding selected pair, and three bits for that same nonzero parity when all ports are selected. Mask order is 01,02,12 for both groups. The all-zero parity is always available.

A pair bit means that its ends remain connected after deletion of the third port: necessity uses an induced edge set with exactly two odd vertices, sufficiency a shortest path. If the three ports are connected in the graph, at least two pair bits are present: consider the minimal subtree connecting them in any spanning tree. Otherwise each component with two ports supplies the sole possible pair, both in pair and full selection states, or there is no pair at all. If exactly two pair bits are present, the common port separates the other two upon deletion. Choose an induced path to either terminal in its component plus the common port. Their union has no cross-chords and supports both paths independently. This realizes all three pair parities at full selection. For three available pair bits, there are at most eight full masks. These arguments give fifteen possibilities in all.

templates.py exhaustively checks seven representatives and all port permutations, covering exactly fifteen traces. Each is a proper subdivision of a simple subcubic graph, using new pendant edges beyond some ports to ensure sufficient arm length. Any such pendant leaf cannot be selected with the incident port and therefore has no effect on attainable traces. A four-edge path in one template ensures its cycle has length six. Port-edge contributions may be added separately as fixed parity translations for each selection mask.

## Composability obstruction

If all ports can eventually have degree greater than two, every internal neighbor of any port must have degree at most two to keep every edge incident to a low-degree vertex. Under this restriction every available pair parity also occurs with all three terminals selected: choose a shortest terminal path after deleting the third terminal. No interior vertex can be adjacent to the third port because that neighbor already uses its two path edges and there are no direct terminal edges. Thus the third terminal can be added in isolation. This implication is violated by the three-star, whose full state has no nonzero parity but which realizes all three pairs. These representations are standalone, and the general three-port gluing of such pieces need not preserve the structural hypothesis.

## Exact ports requiring low final degree

When all three pair bits are present, use one center with three arms. The arm to port i has length one precisely when the pair complementary to i is missing at full selection. All other arms have length two. In a pair-only selection choose the path through the center. At full selection a nonzero trace consists of the center and exactly two activated arms. For any selected center, a length-one arm is compulsory and a two-edge arm is optional. The desired parity pair therefore occurs exactly when the omitted port has a long arm. This proves directly that all eight full-state masks occur and puts a high-degree neighbor at exactly the ports where it is necessary. For fewer than three pair bits use the corresponding independent pair paths; their middle vertices have degree two, so no port needs a high-degree neighbor. These minimal representatives have degree-one ports in the star case. In an eligible global skeleton each such port with a short arm may receive at most one additional incident edge overall.
