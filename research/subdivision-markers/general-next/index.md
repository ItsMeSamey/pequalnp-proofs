# Native reductions and matrix-fiber audit

- **General native to ternary sums and back.** ../surplus/README.md covers arbitrary guarded disjunctions, cycles and self-references; the resulting independent full transversal has rank surplus exactly the number of terminals. Independent witness-set comparisons cover 1,163 projected native instances and 350 transformed ternary instances. The fixed-terminal problem corresponds to rank surplus and not the total-rank matching solver parameter.
- **One polynomial-size alternating lift.** ../slice-normalize/README.md and ../alternating-lift/audit.md prove exact slice normalization and a polynomial-size graph lift with b+c markers, where c is the exact product count of the scalar conflict-parity quadratic on the marker slice. It is half polar rank r if zero is favored, r+1 otherwise, with zero constant taking zero products. Every feasible original state lifts by repairing parity among marked auxiliary vertices. The implementation passed 7,899 projected witness cases, 14,259 recovered witnesses, 120 planted large instances, and a 3,297-instance product-minimum audit. The independent repair proof shows exactly c extra unmarked vertices suffice and are necessary for a universal gadget when c≥3. ../quad-cover/README.md gives the independent disjoint/covering affine chart approach without extra graph vertices.
- **Binary cycle/cocycle lift.** ../bipartite-lift/README.md and lift.py there give the exact minimum residual factor ranks for a prescribed real-vertex bipartition, one bipartite graph and b+k markers. The fundamental matrix gives a represented binary matroid. All 2,101 small matrix/partition cases and 300 larger cases passed. For alternating real guards, ../bipartite-lift/partition/README.md recognizes and constructs a partition of residual rank ≤k without a supplied coloring. Its 9,776 budget checks on 1,222 graphs agreed with brute force. No polynomial algorithm is asserted for arbitrary marker count or unrestricted represented binary matroids.
- **Polynomial direct native 3SAT compiler and its barrier.** ../bridgehard/next/peeling.md gives at most 2n choices and one affine literal of width at most two per guard, with m−n+σ terminals optimal within the clause-pivot compiler. A simple 3-regular triple-system family forces linearly many terminals independent of any complexity assumption.
- **Cyclic structural algorithms.** ../cyclic-next/README.md includes the order-specific minimum-rank correction to an acyclic presentation, bidirectional guard-response tree solver and their audits. ../prg/next/README.md gives exact counting at shared row-span dimension w on a supplied clause tree, O*(4^w), with 500 exhaustive comparisons. This folder's README.md gives the separate low-flat-count conditional counting algorithm with 1,803 random marker slices and explicit long-cycle families. ../four/cyclic/README.md checks an unconditional terminal-cost nonadditivity example with r indispensable cycles of length L.
- **Heterogeneous quadratic composition.** ../compression/fiber/README.md gives exact disjoint AND gate counts for the supplied fiber types and largest flats through a witness with k shared observed bits in 2^{O(k²)}poly(n). All 1,024 four-variable quadratic cases and 400 joint systems passed.
- **Shared matrix products.** ../matrix-fibers/README.md proves the exact largest-flat dimension s(n−r)+g(r) of XᵀY=B at every solution of full-row-rank B, and exact covers for r≤4. ../compression/fiber/matrix_audit.md proves g(3)=2 mathematically. ../matrix-fibers/g4-census/README.md and its independent C++ and Python checks establish g(4)=4, classify all 35 normalized extremizers, and count 44,100 affine maximizers before normalizing. This gives ≥27 Boolean AND gates for 4×4 inverse verification, without claiming the gate bound is attainable.
