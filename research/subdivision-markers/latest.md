# Latest reviewed results

1. **Three-port boundary traces.** Exactly 15 normalized types occur; realizable with at most 3 interior vertices, sometimes requiring 3. Proof and explicit minimal representatives: four/three/README.md. Independent derivation and C++ census: threeports/README.md and threeports/census.cpp. All 4,233 labelled internal graphs through three interiors checked. A separate construction identifies exactly which ports necessarily abut a high-degree interior vertex in any representative: three-subdivision/README.md, three-subdivision/templates.py.
2. **FPT gluing for three ports.** Use those representatives in gluing.md, replacing each child by its canonical unweighted gadget including the physical port edges it owns. Its boundary state gives a fixed canonical witnessed label and a child correction set containing zero. The lookup is a Boolean function of at most six local selected-vertex bits. If the local map has degree a, each bucket selector raises the degree to at most 6a+1. A nonroot table can require zero or two odd ports; in the latter case add a length-two path joining those ports, force it fully active, and give its middle no label. The degree and rank costs depend on the augmented pieces. For an exceptional three-port arm star a single center covers all new internal high–high edges. gluing.md proves the sparsification and bucket steps. The weighted checker four/three/weighted.py additionally compares 1,671 full subtree tables across 496 three-port trees against direct induced-subset enumeration, covering all boundary states and labels at dimension up to three.
3. **Unbounded native depth with tree structure.** pathkernel/README.md proves deterministic O*(2^b) decision for arbitrary fanin but at most one later guard per state bit and fixed terminal rank b as parameter. The simple direct implementation passed 4,800 marker checks. depthfree/README.md gives the unary-ancestor forest algorithm and ordered future-response rank DP; tested on 4,000 targets and depth-1,000 examples.
4. **Fixed-layer sparse option elimination.** macro/README.md gives the elimination theorem, implicit availability oracle, and direct parameterized reduction from disjoint DAG paths. Conditioning a different option on a different guard in one atomic block is permitted by this new model; arbitrary such blocks are not being asserted to be native. At unbounded height the option model is unconditionally W[1]-hard even for two positive guard literals. The fixed-macro-height algorithm includes concrete native guarded chains and binary choice-tree blocks of unbounded elementary depth. macro/solver.py and macro/test_solver.py passed 1,320 targets across 361 instances. macro/routing_check.py independently compared the path reduction with enumerated disjoint paths on 280 demand/DAG instances; source is Chitnis (primary link in proof).
5. **Sharp native representative lower bound.** branchbarrier/representatives.md proves exact terminal-independent list size and degree-k evaluation rank on h disjoint unary paths of length q. Finite differences give zero/unit symbols and lead to rank Σ_{j≤k} binom(h,j)q^j for k≤h. Width-two terminal tests can isolate each of the (q+1)^h tuples. This lower bound concerns the specified method.

6. **Exact one-marker interfaces on one-hot domains.** onehot-interface.md proves that the rejected bipartite graph must be a disjoint union of complete bipartite components, and the converse holds. Isolating k independent choice pins requires exactly k terminals on their product domain.
