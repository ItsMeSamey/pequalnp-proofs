# One uniform clean column signature

Suppose the four-bit scores q,r of (A,B),(B,A) are unique among disjoint raw cycle/cocycle supports on a partition A,B, and p is absent. For a maximal-A basis I put X=A∩I and Y=B outside I. If all Y columns have the same fundamental primal gain and the same incidence on exceptional B basis rows, then |Y|≤1.

For a common freely selectable score subspace H, first project scores to the quotient and inject it linearly into four bits. For each target this preserves exactly its possible raw supports, irrespective of the number of hidden assignments realizing an offset. Use the three projected targets and projected gains; the exceptional incidence part of each signature is unchanged. Thus uniformity of projected gains suffices. The statement requires offsets freely available for every pair.

Assume |Y|>1. The exception counts e=|A outside I|=null(A), k=|B∩I|=conull(B) sum to at most three. The supported A cycles and B cocycles have jointly injective gains by uniqueness at q, and a four-dimensional image would attain p. B is a nonzero cocycle. A cannot be empty since otherwise two equal-gain fundamental cycles can be toggled at (B,A); hence A is a nonzero cycle. Thus e,k≥1. B itself is a circuit: all B-supported cycles have gain given by the parity of their Y subset times the common gain, and the reverse witness forces injectivity. In particular b=L(B) equals the nonzero common gain and |Y| is odd. Summing these fundamental cycles gives ones on every B basis exception, so their common exceptional pattern is all ones. B is also a cocycle and hence equals the sum of its k exceptional dual rows. On Y this yields ones, forcing k odd, so k=1. The clean matrix N has all row/column sums even by the B cycle and A cocycle, and kernel spanned by 1 since an even kernel vector would give another B-supported cycle.

Write Q for the dual gain space and g=D(A), the sum of the clean dual gains. We have g nonzero, and r=b+g lies outside Q by uniqueness versus pure dual witnesses. Each row has a zero somewhere by even weight and odd |Y|. Thus no clean row gain equals g, since a proper Y fundamental cycle could combine with this row to attain r. This rules out rank zero or one, and rank four would realize p purely. For rank three apply rank-three-exclusion.md if e=1, and split-gains-proof.md with split-gains-final.js if e=2. In the latter case the two A fundamental circuits partition A, giving exactly the two sector incidences 10,01. Each sector's size is odd by the A cocycle at its corresponding exceptional column.

For rank two with e=1 see ../fields/rank-two-one-exception.md. A rainbow induced-even triangle forces the clean Gram graph to have only three vertices; the full chart is J−I of order four. Four families of witnesses fill the score span, and if the span has dimension below four a second witness attains r. For rank two with e=2 normalize Q={0,1,2,3}, b=4, g=1 and r=5. Order the two A circuit gains α<d. Write β=D(B), q=α+d+β, P=span(α,d,b), W=span(α,d,β). W has dimension three, p,r are outside W, and q,r,p are all outside the pure spaces P,Q. Also q lies outside span(b,g) attained by subpairs of (B,A). There are 264 normalized score tuples obeying these tests. For each, use the column/parity/Gram tests in split-gains-proof.md on both sectors. With F={p,q,r}, each proper primal A_i avoids F on its coset α_i+span(β,gains opposite); A_i+c_j avoids F on b+α_i+span(zero-row gains opposite, pair differences of one-row gains own). A same-sector difference v yielding q+v or α_i+β+v in F forces a zero Gram block. All type/parity profiles are rejected, as checked by rank-two-split.js and independently by ../degree/one-column-rank-two-split-independent.js using counts of ones.

The result applies when the chosen maximal-A chart has one signature on all its clean columns. Restricting a multicolored chart to one signature need not preserve the opposite witnesses.
