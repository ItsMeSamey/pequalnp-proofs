# Projected difference criterion

Let S be the raw selector vectors, a,b the opposite witnesses, and δ=a+b. Let π be quotient by span(δ), and let F consist of the nonzero points of π(S+a). A linear t-bit score makes a,b uniquely attainable at distinct targets if and only if some (t−1)-bit linear map on the quotient is nonzero at every point of F. Indeed Lδ must be nonzero. The induced map modulo Lδ is nonzero on F: both original representatives of each such direction are differences from one of the two designated models to a different raw model. Conversely lift a nonvanishing map on F and add one coordinate detecting δ. A missing target remains a separate condition. In particular nine points with every pair difference in F defeat four-bit uniqueness by the pigeonhole principle.

For twelve elements the dimension and parity inequalities of two-signature-core.md (before using signature counts) leave 5×5 rank-four clean charts with one exception on each side, or 4×5 rank-three clean charts with a 1+3 split and one B basis exception, up to duality. There are 6 square and 10 split row/column orbits. Fourteen orbits have a five-dimensional subspace of raw forbidden differences as certified in ../degree/twelve-packets.txt. The other two square charts do not have such a subspace (exhaustive search ../degree/twelve-exact-packets.cpp), but each has a quotient nine-clique. The checker twelve-residual-cliques.js verifies all 36 differences for each, with 261 forbidden quotient points on each chart. It packs z=(dual x<<6)|primal y and uses min(z,z xor 4095) as quotient representative. Both lifts were independently checked. Thus all twelve-element cores are excluded, and the two residual charts disprove universal five-space extraction without supporting a successful four-bit labelling.

For fourteen elements the same dimension and parity conditions give 6×5 rank-four clean charts up to duality with 1+5 or 3+3 exceptional row sectors. Canonical chart counts are 94 and 128 respectively. All 222 have an explicit quotient nine-clique. See ../degree/fourteen-quotient.cpp and ../degree/fourteen-quotient.txt. The separate ../degree/verify-fourteen-quotient.py checks both raw lifts on all 36 edges per chart (15,984 checks). These finite exclusions do not imply that every larger chart has a nine-clique or has a ten-element parity-preserving quotient.
