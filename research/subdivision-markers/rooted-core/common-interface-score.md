# Exact response across a common represented interface

Fix a subspace W of dimension b. Partition actual columns by connected component E_i of the column matroid projected modulo W, counting each quotient loop separately. Write V_i=span(E_i), W_i=V_i intersect W, b_i=dim W_i. Let U_i be the sum of the primal scores of cycles of E_i and dual scores of evaluations on E_i of functionals on V_i zero on W_i, with v_i=dim U_i. These are ordinary linear images.

If b_i+v_i≤3 for each component, construct the full global four-bit score image and physical disjoint witnesses in polynomial time for fixed b. Pieces with a separate exact table algorithm (including bounded-size ones by enumeration) can also be included. Width inside a piece satisfying the inequality is unrestricted.

Tabulate local disjoint pairs with prescribed flux w=A_i c in W_i and restriction delta of their dual functional on W_i. Necessarily delta(w)=0. Choose a basis of W_i containing w first when nonzero; the coordinate vector of w is disjoint from the vector of evaluations of delta on this basis. Append these basis columns as virtual sites. The desired local vectors lift exactly to disjoint cycles and cocycles on this matrix with the associated virtual states. Each virtual state needs one affine marker: c=1 for 10, d=1 for 01, c+d=0 for 00, using disjointness. Solve the primal and dual linear equations independently to get an affine score offset before imposing disjointness. Every candidate disjoint score is in that offset plus U_i, so each desired value requires v_i scalar score equations chosen injective on U_i. The constructive three-marker solver gives the complete table in b_i+v_i rows per call. Orthogonality alone does not give attainability.

Enumerate one common dual functional on W. Only local states extending its restriction to W_i are allowed. Such functionals extend globally: the V_i spans form a direct sum modulo W, so compatible extensions on V_i+W agree on every relation. Local cycles concatenate to a global cycle precisely when their fluxes sum to zero. Convolve the running flux and score by XOR, with backpointers. This gives exact ordinary-coordinate witnesses upon discarding virtual coordinates. Every physical column, including chosen generators of W, is counted once.

For a fixed budget b, enumerate independent tuples of up to b ordinary columns and use their spans as candidates for W. Elimination computes the quotient components, intersections and score ranks. This recognizes all qualifying physically spanned interfaces; a supplied W can also be used directly. A retained rank-two physical line with arbitrarily many high-width attached pieces and at most one internal score dimension per piece is one example. Rank four globally can arise from different blocks. Virtual interfaces not spanned by a bounded tuple of actual columns are outside this recognition guarantee.

## Recognition tests and safe reductions

For the octahedron with missing score 12 in mixed/safe-minor, check-octa-bus.py enumerates every physically spanned interface. Among dimensions 0,1,2,3 it checks respectively 1,12,50,71 distinct spaces, none satisfying the three-row condition on every block. Dimension four has 28 possibilities and exactly one wins: taking the rank-four flat on all vertices except vertex 2 leaves one four-edge component with local budget three and eight singleton components with budget one. Thus direct response tables decide this genuinely stalled input without assuming bounded size for its blocks. check-common-interface.py independently compares convolution with global exhaustive witnesses on 80 small represented inputs, recovering and validating 1,090 physical score witnesses.

Each success removes a physical site, so a fixed deterministic order and fixed interface bound give polynomial termination with a positive witness, a certified negative on a solved remainder, or an unresolved remainder. In the series/parallel controls the proven sentinel switches reduce unbounded chains; standard rank-one class compression can also keep just a basis of the augmented class gains. The original eight-element control is solvable by bounded enumeration. For a full spectrum after target-specific reductions run this protocol separately at each target.

## Supplied tree of large blocks

The same local criterion works without a single common low-rank ambient when a binary tree of blocks with bounded inter-block connectivity is supplied. For any node X write V_X for the span of its columns and L_X=V_X intersect span(all outside columns). Store the exact table of (w in L_X, delta on L_X, score), with witnesses. At a leaf use the local three-marker test with boundary L_X. Other exact methods can replace that leaf test.

For siblings A,B with union X, only pairs with w_A+w_B=w in L_X are compatible. The two child restrictions delta_A,delta_B and proposed delta_X must be restrictions of one functional on L_A+L_B+L_X, a linear consistency test. This condition suffices for any corresponding physical child models. In fact V_A intersect V_B lies in both child boundaries, so their interior functionals glue. For every v in L_X, choose v=a+b with a in V_A and b in V_B. As v is in the outside span, a and b must already lie in their respective child boundaries. Thus the glued functional has the required value on v. Conversely any physical witness yields these states because each child's flux belongs to the span of its complement once the parent flux lies in L_X. Add scores and store traceback. At the root take zero flux and the empty restriction. If the largest boundary dimension is d, joins use 2^O(d) times polynomial work for fixed score length. This is standard boundary dynamic programming with the three-marker algorithm computing the leaves, allowing unbounded width internal to a leaf. Finding an unrestricted optimal block tree is a separate recognition question.

## Limits of fixed-rank physical interface recognition

For any fixed b take a large complete graphic host. Put primal label 1 on one edge of each of 2b+1 vertex-disjoint triangles, and label 2 likewise on separate triangles. Put dual labels 4 and 8 on two matchings of size 2b+1 using otherwise fresh vertices. All other labels are zero. After contracting an arbitrary basis of at most b physical edges, at most 2b vertices are touched. For each primal type one entire labelled triangle survives untouched; for each dual type a labelled edge with both ends untouched survives. All nonloop edges lie in one quotient matroid component, since their simplification is complete on at least three vertices. In this component each untouched triangle yields a zero-flux cycle of its one specified type. The singleton vertex potential on an endpoint of an untouched labelled matching edge vanishes on W and its local score is its specified dual type. Thus the central component has v_i=4 regardless of W. Its size grows with the host, so bounded enumeration also cannot certify the recognition condition. All four scores are feasible together here (take the desired labelled triangles, then prescribe cut values on the separate matching endpoints). This is an explicit failure of this fixed-rank recognition criterion on yes-instances, not a general decision lower bound.
