# Opposite unique scores and bounded branch-width

Let columns v_e span V over GF(2). A raw witness consists of a dependence c of the columns and a functional alpha on V evaluating to a vector d on the columns, with disjoint supports. Its score is Lc + (alpha(w_i))_i modulo a fixed free offset space H, with t total score bits. Let two distinct target cosets have unique raw witnesses (A,B) and (B,A), where A and B partition all ordinary elements.

**Theorem.** For fixed t and ordinary branch-width b there is a bound on the total size of every such instance. A missing third score is unnecessary.

In V plus t extra dimensions, replace each ordinary column by (v_e,L_e). For each score bit add separate elements h_i=(0,unit_i) and z_i=(w_i,0), each individually distinguished by a color. The ambient rank increases by exactly t. Restrict raw witnesses in the augmented matroid by requiring cycle bits zero at z_i and cocycle bits zero at h_i. A cycle then has ordinary bits c satisfying the original dependency equations, with bits Lc on h. A functional is exactly (alpha,0) on the ambient span. Its z bits are the dual score, and its ordinary evaluations are d. Thus the ordinary witness extends uniquely, and its full score is the xor of the h cycle bits and the z cocycle bits. Root coincidences and root loops still have separate ground elements and colors.

A rooted minor preserving all terminals can only delete or contract ordinary elements. Its witnesses lift to witnesses in the larger augmented matroid preserving both terminal channels. Specifically, cycles lift with zero coordinates on every deleted element, and cocycles lift with zero coordinates on every contracted element; these choices preserve disjointness throughout the removed region. Lift the two target witnesses independently. The unique ordinary cycle supports cover all ordinary elements, so none could have been deleted. The unique ordinary cocycle supports likewise exclude every contraction. Two encoded instances for equal t,H and target data are therefore incomparable under proper rooted minor. There are only finitely many choices of the score data for fixed t.

For branch-width use the boundary dimension lambda(X)=rank(X)+rank(complement X)−rank(E). Attach one terminal subtree to an ordinary decomposition. An ordinary cut with roots all on one side increases boundary dimension by at most 2t: the root-free rank increases by at most t, the other rank by at most 2t, and total rank increases by t. Cuts with only roots on one side have boundary dimension at most 2t. Under the convention that branch-width includes a plus one, shift both width bounds consistently.

The finite equality order on colors (one ordinary color and individual root colors) is a well-quasi-order. Apply Oum's labeled Theorem 6.1 to the cycle/cocycle product chain group; the matroid identification and width relation are Lemmas 8.4 and 8.5. Distinct root colors enforce retention and exact terminal roles. Consequently there is no infinite proper-rooted-minor antichain of these encodings at bounded width. This WQO argument supplies no numerical bound. [Oum](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1007.3807)

In particular, a genuine four-score core whose clean chart is bichain has bounded size. The forbidden doubled-triangle packet graph is itself bichain. Excluding it makes a proper hereditary subclass of the bichain class, which has bounded clique-width. Adding at most three exceptional vertices increases cut rank by at most three, and fundamental graph cut rank equals ordinary matroid connectivity in the above convention. [Bichain minimality](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01628)
