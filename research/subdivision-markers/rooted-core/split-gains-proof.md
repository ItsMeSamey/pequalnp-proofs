# Four-score rank-three exclusion with two exceptional A columns

Use the notation and assumptions of rank-three-exclusion.md, allowing both exceptional row patterns. Assume the ordinary maximal-A chart has two A exceptions and one B basis exception. Since A is a cycle, every clean row has exceptional incidence 10 or 01. The A-supported cycle space is generated by the two disjoint fundamental circuits A1,A2. Their clean basis sectors U,V both have odd size: A is a cocycle too, with all clean basis coordinates selected, and it has entry one at each of the two exceptional columns. B is a circuit, its clean fundamental cycle gains are all b=L(B), and its basis restriction as a cocycle is the sole exceptional B basis coordinate. Set β=D(B).

Use the three forbidden translates F={f=q+b,g=r+b,h=p+b} in Q from one-column-gains.md. All three are distinct and nonzero, and b is outside Q. Every clean row has a zero entry, so its gain avoids F. All scores from a pure primal or a pure dual are attainable. In particular q is outside Q, hence precisely one of the two primal A-circuit gains is outside Q. Write α1=b+a outside Q, α2=d inside Q after ordering the circuits. At (A,B) any switches among the two primal circuits and the dual B cocycle stay disjoint, so uniqueness makes α1,d,β independent. In particular d,β are nonzero and distinct. We have a=f+d+β and Q is spanned by the row gains together with β. Write the basis of Q so that f=1,g=2, and h=4 if independent or 3 if dependent.

Each augmented clean row type is determined by its sector and an allowed gain in Q outside F. There are at most ten possible types. Enumerate every set of actually present types, and their multiplicity parity mask m. Both sectors are odd, and the XOR of gains with odd multiplicity is D(A)=g. The following necessary tests use the actual graph pair semantics.

* Primal A1 can be paired with B and any sum of fundamental duals from sector V. Hence a+span(β,gains of V) avoids F.
* For one column j let z,o be the masks of types actually exhibiting a zero, respectively a one. Their union is the set of present types. Primal c_j permits every dual clean row with zero at j; span(gains of z) avoids F. Primal A2+c_j permits arbitrary clean duals from U with zero at j and any even number of clean duals from V with one at j: their pivots are available and the latter rows cancel on both A2's exceptional column and j. Thus d+span(gains of U∩z, pair differences of gains of V∩o) avoids F. Each primal used here differs from both A and B, by its exceptional A coordinates or proper B-column support.
* Let k be the vector of parities of zero counts by row type at this column. Outside z it is zero, and outside o it equals m. Its parity is even because all N column sums are even and the two odd sectors give an even number of rows. Each included type occurs in some z. Since there are oddly many columns and every row has even weight, the XOR of all k is m.
* Certain Gram blocks between distinct types must vanish. Incomparability of two row supports gives columns j,l with opposite patterns 10,01. In the same sector the dual B plus the two fundamental row cocycles is disjoint from A+c_j+c_l. This pair reaches q,r or p if the row gains differ by 0,f+g or f+h. The same dual is disjoint from A1+c_j+c_l when both rows are in U; gain difference d then reaches q. Both switched primal supports differ from A and B. Therefore those rows are comparable and their Gram entry is zero, since both have even weight.
* For each forbidden Gram pair of types i,t, the XOR over columns of k_i k_t equals m_i m_t. Indeed G=J+WWᵀ where W=J+N is the zero incidence matrix. Summing a zero Gram block gives this identity. Concatenate all pair products to (k,1); the corresponding target built from (m,1) must belong to the binary span of the allowed column vectors. Different coordinates use the same collection of columns.

The executable split-gains-final.js checks these necessary conditions. For type count 1 through 10 the independent case leaves 0,0,2,5,5,2,0,0,0,0 profiles immediately before imposing the Gram equations. The dependent case leaves none. Adding the Gram equations leaves zero for each count. Independent implementation using minimal possible one masks and BigInt Gaussian elimination reproduced all counts. Thus the original two-sector chart cannot occur at any size. Along with rank-three-exclusion.md and the exception count bound this excludes all nontrivial one-column-signature cores of dual gain rank three under these assumptions.

Separate raw support searches for four augmented types at three and five clean columns are in ../degree/three_column_actual.cpp and ../degree/five_column_actual.js. Their scope includes dual ranks two and three; the universal certificate above concerns rank three.
