# Target-safe switching without a supplied witness

Let C,D be the cycle and cocycle spaces, p a four-bit target, and Lc(c)+Ld(d) the linear score. To certify deletion (primal zero) at e, first try at most two score characters together with c_e=1; an infeasible projected target certifies deletion. Otherwise form Z_e of all f for which some score character lambda makes c_e=1, d_f=1 and lambda(score)=lambda(p) infeasible. The three-marker solver handles these tests; the character can vary with f. Solve for h in C supported on Z_e with h_e=1 and Lc(h)=0. For any p witness with c_e=1, the cut vanishes on Z_e. Replacing c by c+h preserves disjointness and p and clears e. This proves exact target preservation. The dual test certifies contraction. Project away common free score offsets first. Successful steps use exact scored-minor backlifts and the updated representation. Failure is inconclusive.

The conditional test is stronger than a single flip that works irrespective of the target. In the graphic theta with paths uv, uav and ubv, let e=uv, set the primal gain to zero and impose d_ua=0. A witness with c_e=1 has equal potentials on u,v and equal potentials on u,a, so triangle uav is safe to flip. Unconditionally, choosing cycle ubv and cut delta(a) blocks that flip; the other triangle similarly admits an unconditional blocker.

In the secret series/parallel control (adaptive-prefix-obstruction.md), an added series atom and its sentinel have equal primal occupancy, their pair is a cocycle and their dual score coefficients vanish. The dual neutral switch certifies contraction of the added atom. For a parallel atom the dual occupancies coincide and its pair with the sentinel is a zero-score cycle; deletion follows. The sentinels can be retained. In the three-connected triangular-grid control the three marked dual bits are score characters fixed to one at 1111. Each contradicts a primal pin on that edge, certifying all three deletions. There the NO instance loses its only primal score terms; the YES control still has its independent fourth facial triangle.

## Stalling on every site

Replace every ordinary element of the one-hole NO base, or its full-image YES control, by a 2-sum with a fresh cycle matroid of K_{a,a}, a≥5, using a distinguished edge x as virtual port. Only internal gadget edges remain. Cycles and cocycles glue with equal respective port bits; each internal restriction recovers its bits by cycle/cocycle orthogonality with a port-containing cut or circuit. The recovered bits cannot both be one for a disjoint internal model, again by orthogonality within that gadget. Hence transferring the old score using these recovered bits preserves it exactly.

In one gadget any prescribed port state 00,10,01 permits c_i=1 and d_j=1 simultaneously at any distinct internal edges i,j. For port cycle bit one, place i and x each on its own square using two new vertices per square (one on each shore), avoiding all endpoints of i,j,x and the other square's new vertices. The two squares are edge-disjoint, and on distinguished vertices their components impose exactly the endpoint identifications of i,x. Edge j remains a nonloop after these identifications because a simple triangle-free graph has no third edge in the span of two others. A potential constant on each square can make d_j=1, and d_x=0 automatically. For zero port cycle bit, take just the square on i. Now x and j remain distinct nonloop nonparallel edges on the contracted components, since the only distinguished endpoint identification is across i and the graph has no triangles. Their two incidence forms are independent, permitting either prescribed d_x and d_j=1. In both constructions the cycle is precisely the union of the chosen squares. For pins in different gadgets use just the desired unary consequences. Without pins every port state lifts as well.

Consequently every old attainable score remains attainable with c_i=1 and d_j=1 for any distinct resulting edges. Both base images project surjectively onto every proper quotient of four-score space. All one-pin queries with up to two score characters succeed on target 1111; every two-pin query for different sites also succeeds with any single score character. On both YES and NO instances Z_e is exactly {e}, and the analogous dual set is {e}. Unary liftability implies every ordinary element is a nonloop and a noncoloop (for loops, dual occupancy is always zero, and for coloops primal occupancy is always zero). Thus neither neutral space contains the required singleton. The rule halts before removing any site even though these instances can have arbitrarily many elements. This only refutes completeness of this certificate scheme; the 2-sum layout can be used by other algorithms.
