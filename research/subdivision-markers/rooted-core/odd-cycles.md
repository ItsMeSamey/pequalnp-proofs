# Nonbipartite correction codes

On C7, take the cyclic binary Hamming code with column labels all successive powers of a root of X³+X+1 in GF(8). The 16 words are the empty set, full cycle, translates of {0,1,3} and their complements. Every odd word has an odd-edge component. Every even nonzero word is a translate of {2,4,5,6}, with runs of one and three. Thus three checks suffice and m−tau=3 gives optimality, whereas m−nu=4.

In one C5 the only unrealizable nonempty even masks are its five two-edge matchings. They form a five-element binary circuit: e_i+e_(i+2) are the edge incidence vectors of a five-cycle on the coordinate indices. Thus no two-dimensional subspace of the even masks can be safe, since it would give three distinct dependent members of this circuit. The maximum safe dimension is two, attained by adding any odd direction to a matching mask.

There is a seven-dimensional safe code on three disjoint copies of C5. Let alpha generate the multiplicative group of GF(16) and H be its subgroup of order five. This subgroup is a binary five-circuit since 2 has order four modulo 5. Choose a linear isomorphism f to the four-dimensional even mask space of C5 sending H to the five two-edge matchings. Use the four-dimensional joint code z → (f(z), f(alpha z), f(alpha² z)). Every nonzero z has exactly one of these field arguments in H. Add arbitrary independent odd directions in the three respective blocks; any nonzero choice of these directions makes at least one block odd and hence unrealizable. The combined kernel therefore admits 8 correction rows versus 9 for separate optimal corrections.

The bound for three C5 components is exact. In a safe kernel K let L be the subcode with even parity in all three blocks. For each nonzero vector choose a block where its mask is one of the five two-edge matchings. Each color class is sum-free because the five matching masks form a circuit and no three of them sum to zero. Color the edge joining x and y in the complete graph on L by the color of x+y. A monochromatic triangle would violate sum-freeness. Any three-coloring of the complete graph on 17 vertices has a monochromatic triangle: at a fixed vertex some color has six neighbors, within which the two remaining colors force a triangle by the elementary K6 two-color Ramsey theorem. Thus dim L≤4 and dim K≤7. The same two-color argument gives a maximum kernel dimension of four on two disjoint copies.

For s independent C5 components, grouping triples and using separate checks for the remainder gives safe dimension at least s+floor(4s/3). A spectral bound gives dimension at most s+floor(s log₂(8/3)). On the sixteen-element even-mask alphabet let T be the five two-edge matchings, and let B(x,y) equal one on the diagonal, zero when x+y lies in T, and one half elsewhere. Its constant eigenvector has eigenvalue six. For a nontrivial binary character, the number of negative character evaluations on T is either two or four since T is a spanning circuit with total zero. Its eigenvalue is (1−sum of character values on T)/2, equal to zero or two. Hence B is positive semidefinite. On the even subcode L the tensor power of B has zero entries at all distinct pairs and unit diagonal. The constant eigenspace gives |L| ≥ (6/16)^s |L|², so |L|≤(8/3)^s. The parity map costs at most s more dimensions. This also bounds any nonlinear even codebook whose every pair differs by a two-edge matching in at least one component.
