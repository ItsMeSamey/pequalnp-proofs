Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, §8 Lemma 8.1, already prove preservation of labelled minors across a separation of order at most two using the torso formed from realizable lines. The companion shore note presents anchored submodular minimization to locate a protected shore and gives a constructive procedure to recover witnesses. The separate rank-three catalog classifies joint spectra and their smallest replacements.
