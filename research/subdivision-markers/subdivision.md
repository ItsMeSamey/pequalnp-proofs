# Parity markers on proper subdivisions

Let G be a simple graph of maximum degree D with t-bit vertex labels. Seek an induced even-degree vertex set with prescribed label sum h. Suppose each edge has an endpoint of degree at most two. Any even edge vector induces exactly its support graph: any edge between active vertices has a low-degree endpoint, which must use both its incident edges. Every original witness consists of such a support plus isolated vertices. Retain at most t of its isolates realizing their same label sum, by choosing a basis among them. Enumerate this independent set I, delete its closed neighborhood, and adjust the target by its label sum.

Use a binary cycle-space basis for the residual edge vectors. Each incident edge bit is a linear form. Since their sum at a vertex is zero, vertex activity is the OR of all but one incident edge bit. Its degree as a multilinear Boolean polynomial is at most max(0,D−1), with isolated vertices inactive. Each of the t label bits is consequently a polynomial of that degree. Their joint target indicator has degree at most t max(0,D−1). A nonzero multilinear polynomial of degree d is nonzero on a vector of weight at most d by Boolean Möbius inversion. Testing these vectors deterministically recovers a witness in polynomial time for fixed D,t. See cubicmarkers/solver.py and test_solver.py.

## Subcubic graphs

For D≤3 the vector of label bits Q(z) is quadratic in the cycle coordinates z. Count target assignments as

    2^(−t) Σ_a (−1)^(a·h) Σ_z (−1)^(a·Q(z)).

Each inner character sum is polynomial time. If the polynomial has a cross term ij, write it as ij+iL+jM+R, with L,M affine and R quadratic on the other variables. Summing over i,j yields twice the sign (−1)^(LM+R), removing them while retaining degree two. Finally, an affine character has sum zero if its linear part is nonzero and otherwise its constant sign times the number of assignments. All integers have polynomial bit length. Recover an actual witness from a positive count by restricting each coordinate successively. The entire time is O(2^t poly(n) Σ_{j≤t} binom(n,j)) with polynomial space. For pure cycle supports it is O(2^t poly(n)). The count is for a fixed isolate guess, so it need not count distinct original witnesses across guesses.

## Covering edges with two high-degree endpoints

Let X meet every edge whose two endpoints have degree at least three, and branch on the incident edge bits of each surviving x in X after removing the guessed isolate neighborhoods. There are at most 2^(D−1) even patterns for each positive-degree vertex. For an active x and each absent incident edge xy, set all incident edge bits at y to zero. These and the selected edge patterns impose linear restrictions on the cycle space. In a consistent branch, any edge between active endpoints is present by either its low-degree endpoint or its specified pattern at the covering vertex. A true induced witness satisfies the branch provided by its own edge vector. Parametrize the resulting affine space; all polynomial degree and quadratic-sum bounds persist. For D≥1 the time overhead is at most 2^((D−1)|X|). A greedy maximal matching on the high-degree edges supplies a cover within twice minimum size, so logarithmic minimum cover still yields polynomial time for fixed subcubic marker count.

## Fixed-parameter deterministic algorithm in the marker count

To avoid guessing isolates, introduce independent seed bits y_v in addition to the cycle parameters. Denote cycle activity by A_v. Set I_v equal to y_v times the product of (1+y_w)(1+A_w) over all neighbors w of v. When v has positive degree, inactivity of all neighbors implies v is inactive, since every active vertex has an active incident edge. Degree-zero vertices are always inactive. Thus the I bits are independent isolates anticomplete to the cycle support. Conversely choosing y to be the indicator of the desired isolates realizes any given original witness. The generated vertex bits are A_v+I_v over the binary field.

On an eligible subdivision, their maximum polynomial degree d is at most 2D+1 for D≥1: a high-degree vertex has only low-degree neighbors, each contributing at most one from its activity polynomial, while a low-degree vertex has at most two neighbors, each contributing degree at most D−1. Without the subdivision restriction the same formulas have degree at most D²+1; apply them on each affine branch of the high-degree-edge cover construction.

For a uniform parameter vector, a feasible t-bit target has probability at least 2^(−dt). Indeed the target indicator is a nonzero Boolean multilinear polynomial of degree ≤dt, and a degree-s monomial of maximal degree guarantees at least one success for every setting of its complementary coordinates (its coefficient remains one). By Fourier expansion, approximating the expectation of all degree-d signs to additive error ε approximates each target probability to additive error ε: all 2^t character coefficients have absolute value 2^(−t). Set ε=2^(−dt−1). Viola's generator has seed length O(d log N+d·2^d log(1/ε)) on N parameters and deterministically enumerating the seeds must hit every nonempty target. Evaluate the original graph map on every output to recover actual witnesses. This gives 2^{O_D(t)} n^{O_D(1)} time for fixed maximum degree D, times the branching factor for a supplied exceptional cover. The generator theorem is external. The explicit finite-field generator, target search and its five tests are in prg/. The separate map_check.py compares the entire generated relation with direct enumeration on 1,239 graphs.

A different parameter avoids branching. For each high-degree–high-degree edge e=uv, test the defect (1+e)A_u A_v. On the slice e=0, the remaining incident edge bits at each endpoint sum to zero, so evaluate activity from all but one of those remaining bits. The resulting defect degree is at most 2D−3, and for cubic endpoints it is (1+e)p_up_v, with p_u,p_v any one of the two remaining incidence bits at its endpoint. Compute the rank r of these explicit multilinear polynomials in the cycle coordinates by Gaussian elimination on coefficient arrays. Requiring a basis of r of them to vanish makes the active support induced. Use the same isolate map and apply the generator to t+r output coordinates, prescribing zero for the defect bits. This gives 2^{O_D(t+r)} n^{O_D(1)} time, allowing logarithmic r and fixed t. The graph-map result itself needs no bound on graph size, girth or cycle-space dimension.

For a rank-sensitive degree bound, orient G according to a degeneracy order of maximum indegree h. Replace the independence factor in I_v by y_v times the product of (1+y_w) over incoming neighbors alone. If adjacent vertices both have seed one the later vertex is suppressed, and any independent set is obtained from its own seed vector. The requirement that no neighbor be active is the common zero set of the edge coordinates incident to all neighbors. In a linear cycle-space parameterization its indicator has degree at most their binary rank ρ_v. This gives overall local degree at most 1+h+max_v ρ_v even for unbounded maximum degree. A defect for edge uv depends only on both endpoints' incident forms, all in the set defining ρ_u, hence its degree is at most ρ_u. This permits bounded degeneracy and bounded local ranks in the defect-rank result.
