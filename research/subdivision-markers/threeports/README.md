# Three-port trace representatives

Remove all edges between the three ports and keep them for restoration at the end. Let P be the set of pairs uv that may be made odd when exactly u,v are selected. Let T be the set of pairs that may be made odd with all three ports selected. Every state includes the option of no odd ports, by using only the selected ports. The handshake identity says the remaining states are determined by P and T.

A pair belongs to P precisely when an interior component neighbors both ports: a witness connects the two odd vertices, and conversely a shortest path with its interior in that component is induced. Thus if P is proper, no component touches three different ports. With all ports on, each component contributes a parity vector either zero or its unique pair, if it touches two ports. Shortest paths in distinct components can be chosen independently. Therefore T consists of the nonzero elements of the binary span of P. The representative uses one fresh degree-two connector for each pair in P.

When P contains all pairs, all eight labeled sets T are possible. For empty T use one fresh vertex joined to all three ports. For a singleton T add an independent connector at the desired pair to that star. For two pairs T use one connector per desired pair and join the two connectors by an edge. When all ports are selected, selecting both connectors is forbidden because each would have odd degree. The missing pair is attainable with the third port off using the path through both connectors. For full T use three pair connectors, all independent. Physical edges between ports may now be restored, since they add exactly their deterministic parity offsets and leave interior degrees unchanged.

The normalized census therefore has 15 types and uses at most three fresh vertices, all with degree at most three. The included census.cpp independently enumerates simple labeled graphs with three fixed ports through seven total vertices and outputs a smallest witness and normalized signature. Enumeration through five vertices misses the full/full type, showing the worst-case size bound is sharp.

Only a child with full P and proper T needs a representative containing a degree-three interior vertex. All high-high edges incident with fresh interiors can be covered by at most two vertices per such child; all other representative interiors have degree two. Direct port edges and parent edges must be evaluated at their final degrees. For an omitted pair uv with opposite port w, every shortest eligible u-v path avoiding w must meet a neighbor x of w, since otherwise one could add isolated w. Such x has degree at least three in the original child, counting the two path edges and its edge to w. This detects an obstruction in the original child when w also has high degree.

The normalized representer describes unlabelled feasibility. For the labelled problem, the full child table supplies a canonical attainable label for each boundary state and exceptional corrections as in the two-port proof. The canonical label and correction eligibility are Boolean functions of at most six skeleton bits (three ports and three fresh interiors), of degree at most six times the degree of a skeleton bit. Parent parity requests have either zero or two odd ports; a mandatory selected length-two path between the two requested odd ports offsets the requested parity, with no augmentation needed for zero requests.
