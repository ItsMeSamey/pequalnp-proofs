# Integral weighted-translation proof

Let P(s) be the product of the b clause symbols. Encode each clause's binary literal truth vector injectively and linearly in a common extension field E of characteristic two, so the product is nonzero exactly when every clause succeeds. The space V of multilinear polynomials in b syndrome coordinates has dimension 2^b and is closed under translations T_s. Affine constants are absorbed at the evaluation point h. For block i let D,A,B,C be its sets of contributions at coordinate selections 00,10,01,11. Zero belongs to D after placing all constant terms in h, since the all-zero hidden choice is legal.

For each c in C the exact producing hidden assignment lies on an affine section valid for both observations. Freeze its inactive hidden bits to zero and choose one true literal g_i per active bit. Impose g_i+e_i z_i=1+e_i for Boolean choices of diagonal toggles e_i. Either toggle imposes a sufficient constraint for that guard and contains the given assignment. The active coefficient matrix has determinant whose unique full multilinear monomial in the toggles has coefficient one, so there is a nonsingular setting. Solving yields an affine assignment on the observations. Its four syndrome vectors sum to zero, proving C is contained in A+B+D.

Introduce independent indeterminates for each member of each A,B and each nonzero member of each D, block by block. Let A_i and B_i denote the sums of their translations with those indeterminates as coefficients. Let D_i be the identity plus the similarly weighted nonzero D translations. Then D_i squared is scalar lambda_i times the identity, with lambda_i the square of one plus the sum of its indeterminates. This uses commutativity, characteristic two and T_s squared equal to the identity. The scalar is nonzero. Work over the rational function field solely to define ordinary matrix rank.

Set M_uv = ev_h (prod_j D_j)(A_u D_u)(B_v D_v)P, where composition is indicated by adjacency. The row functional ev_h (prod_j D_j)(A_u D_u) and the column vector (B_v D_v)P give rank at most dim V. For u unequal to v the value equals lambda_u lambda_v times the weighted sum over exactly the possible local contributions on that promised input, so rejection makes the entry zero. On the diagonal i the entry is lambda_i times ev_h (prod_{j unequal i} D_j)A_i B_i D_i P. For a true witness write its c as a+b+d and retain its remote D contributions. The chosen indeterminates for a,b, all nonzero remote contributions and nonzero d identify a unique term with coefficient equal to the nonzero P value of that witness. Thus each accepting diagonal has a nonzero diagonal entry. The identity relation has at most 2^b values. Testing equality of b-bit signatures coordinatewise attains equality. One-sided triangular rejection on the ordered diagonal indices also gives this bound.

With k shared native hidden bits, suppose fixing them removes all cross-block guard dependencies, and all guards of local hidden bits then read only their own observed coordinate pair. Branching on their value gamma creates at most wt(gamma) additional global terminal clauses, one per shared bit set to one. Every off-diagonal is rejected in every branch. Apply the bound to each branch's accepted diagonal subset, then sum over branches. This gives m at most 2^b 3^k; if shared bits are unconditionally free the sum is at most 2^(b+k). This bound concerns the stated block interaction model.
