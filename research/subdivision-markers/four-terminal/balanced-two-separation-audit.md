# Balanced two-separation via a five-dimensional lifted boundary

**Reviewer verdict: GO for the balanced witness promise.** Let the ordinary parts span V_A and V_B with intersection the line <z>. Assume the four terminals are independent and their span W lies in V_A+V_B. Write t for their nonzero sum. Replace the ordinary ambient by the external direct sum V_A⊕V_B, using phi(a,b)=a+b. Its kernel is <(z,z)>. The inverse image W+ of W has dimension five. There are two lifts u of t, both nonzero. Enumerate them, with four-dimensional G+=W+/<u>.

A selected subspace S upstairs with S intersect W+=<u> must be disjoint from ker phi, since any kernel vector belongs to W+ and u has nonzero image. Its projection therefore has intersection exactly <t> with W. Conversely, for any downstairs witness with intersection <t>, choose a minimal expression of t from the selected columns, such as the support of its expression in a basis of those columns. This expression is independent; consequently the projection from the span of its upstairs copies is injective. Its upstairs trace is the line of its unique sum u. This is an existence equivalence. A redundant downstairs selection can contain the two independent side lifts of z and need not itself have line trace upstairs.

For each component projection pi_i and selection S_i require pi_i(u) in S_i. Set K_i=(pi_i^(-1)(S_i) intersect W+)/<u>. The upstairs line-trace condition holds exactly when K_A intersects K_B trivially. The balanced promise requires some witness and lift with both dimensions at most two. Given those traces, extend K_B into a two-plane H disjoint from K_A. Such extension exists by extending bases: a complement of K_A containing K_B has dimension at least two. Enumerate the 35 candidate H.

For H query whether side A admits a trace avoiding H; its inverse image before quotienting by <u> has dimension three. The actual projection kernel must first be contained in <u>. Then recover local columns whose span contains pi_A(u) and meets the projected query space in exactly <pi_A(u)>. If pi_A(u)=0 this becomes a zero-trace test. Compute the full K'_A of this recovered selection in G+. Avoidance of H gives dim K'_A<=2. On side B repeat the disjointness query for K'_A; its inverse image has dimension at most three. The initial witness on side B still works because K_B is contained in H. No canonical or minimum-size answer is needed from the side-A oracle. Conversely any two successful queries produce disjoint traces, hence a real witness downstairs, regardless of whether the returned witness happens to be balanced. Reduce recovered ordinary columns to a minimal expression of t and adjoin the original four roots.

The test rejects only when no branch succeeds; its completeness statement covers balanced YES instances. Other YES instances require separate handling. In particular, splitting an arbitrary instance on a rank-one overlap does not establish a polynomial algorithm without the balance promise. Exhaustive finite linear algebra checked plane extension for all 1,711 ordered disjoint pairs of subspaces of dimension at most two in the four-dimensional binary space.
