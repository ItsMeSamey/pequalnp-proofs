# Fixed-target exponential covers on both sides

Use a chain of k>=2 diamonds from s to t, closed by e=st. Add m>=8 mutually adjacent apex vertices, adjacent to every diamond vertex, and k free vertices adjacent to every apex. Root graphic incidence coordinates at apex 0. For three other apex a,b,c add z=e_a+e_b+e_c. The K4 edges on 0,a,b,c together with z restrict to F7. Deleting any two graph vertices leaves a universal apex, so the graphic matroid is three-connected. The simple single-element extension remains three-connected: any two-separation with at least two old elements on both sides survives deletion of z; a side consisting of z and one old element has rank two and the opposite side spans. Arbitrarily large clique restrictions remain.

The score is the primal bit on e and dual evaluations on edges sa,ab,bc. Target (1,1,0,0) is outside both pure images. The three test edges form a forest and the primal bit is nonzero on a cycle.

Choosing one path in each diamond gives an induced cycle C with e. Set potentials constant on C and the other value everywhere else, normalizing the root to zero. C and this functional attain the target. In its zero-edge graph, s belongs to the isolated component C. Column z has no coordinate on C. Projecting any dependence through e onto the C vertices forces all edges of C, so the unique circuit through e in the kernel is C. Covering every feasible dual witness therefore takes at least 2^k primal circuits.

Split the apex into A,B each of size at least four with 0,a,b,c in A. Put all diamond vertices in B and assign free vertices independently. The cut gives a cocircuit: same-shore edges span its hyperplane and the two connected shores force potentials constant on each. The complementary restriction has no coloop, since every same-shore edge belongs to a cycle and z lies in the within-A span, so this cocircuit is chordless. Indeed a dual chord would admit a functional with just that chord as support on the complement.

Inside each shore choose two apex poles p,q and one p-v-q path for each other vertex. On B replace the paths for s,t by the path p-s-t-q using e. Add pq if the number of paths is odd. These two even edge sets form a dependency containing e. Their connectedness on each shore forces every disjoint functional to be zero or the designated cut. The latter attains the target. Thus serving every feasible primal witness takes at least 2^k dual cocircuits on this same fixed input. Constant m gives O(k) elements. The result concerns explicit lists covering all opposite-side witnesses; a decision procedure can seek any single pair.
