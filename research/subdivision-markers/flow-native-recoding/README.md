# Input-dependent edgewise relabeling of three native states

Use the cubic source and native representation from the [literal flow analysis](../flow-native-bridge/README.md). Allow each edge e its own input-dependent bijection from the three native disjoint states 00,10,01 to the three flow colors. The bijection is fixed across witnesses on the same input. At a given accepting witness let U be the visible edges physically in state 00. Let n be the vertex count and g the girth. If every jointly accepted pair decodes to a proper three-edge-coloring, the conditional marker ranks obey

    b_P+b_Q ≥ |U|−2 floor(n/g).

To prove it, fix the dual witness. A variable primal bit permits at most two possible decoded colors on its edge. At each cubic vertex there are at most two proper local permutations consistent with three such lists. If two permutations differ at all positions, their union is one six-cycle in the bipartite graph of positions and colors and exhausts its row lists. Otherwise three distinct permutations would differ pairwise by transpositions, impossible since two different transpositions compose to a three-cycle. The local raw tangent image is therefore a linear space of dimension at most one. Every nonzero local change flips at least two incident edges, since two permutations cannot differ in just one position.

The visible coordinate functionals of the tangent space at each vertex are consequently either zero or a single common nonzero functional. The graph of edges carrying any one such functional has minimum degree at least two on its incident vertices, hence contains a cycle. Distinct functionals occupy disjoint vertex sets. There are at most floor(n/g) of them, bounding the full visible tangent rank. Fixing the primal witness gives the same bound for the dual rank. Apply the conditional native-minor argument on the physically free positions U. The two allowed projected direction dimensions are at most floor(n/g) each, giving the inequality.

Suppose the compiler realizes all six global recolorings of one proper source coloring. In exactly two of these colorings any fixed edge has the color assigned to its 00 symbol. Averaging over the six lifted witnesses yields |U|≥|E|/3=n/2 at one of them. Thus even edge-dependent local relabeling with this completeness property cannot give a constant-rank encoding on the growing girth-six family. A compiler that promises just one decoded coloring per yes input need not satisfy this averaging hypothesis.

An attempted escape using always-saturated raw encodings has an executable boundary case. If some accepting witness has at most h positions equal to 00 on its entire guarded ground (including guarded hidden positions), enumerate those positions Z. Require p=q=0 on Z and p+q=1 elsewhere on that ground. All constraints then are binary linear equations on native primal and dual coordinates, including any named pins. This yields deterministic N^O(h) decision with witness recovery under the promise. The emptiness parameter covers all guarded positions, and small emptiness only on visible edges does not suffice.

The checker enumerates the possible three local lists, verifies the maximum number of proper assignments and the local change condition, and checks the six-recoloring average with nonconstant edge-specific permutations. The bound does not cover arbitrary nonlocal decoding or mere decision equivalence.
