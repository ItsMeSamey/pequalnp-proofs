# Identifying the global literals creates false rooted witnesses

The unbounded-terminal span-and-avoidance reduction from graph three-coloring uses global bit directions Z_v,T_v for each vertex and a constant direction w. Block b has a private demand u_b and two private directions a_b1,a_b2. Its ordinary columns are a_bj,a_bj+u_b for j=1,2. The terminal subspace includes the demand u=Σ_b u_b and the equality vectors a_bj+L_j for global literals L_j. These literals are Z_v,T_v at a vertex and Z_i+Z_j+w,T_i+T_j+w at an edge ij. Span u and exclude w modulo the supplied terminal subspace. Independent private block spaces force each local demand to be attainable; the separating functional has the specified global literal values. [Original reduction](../../span-separation/unbounded-terminals.md)

A candidate fixed-root compiler quotients the ambient space by the equality vectors a_bj+L_j only. This identifies private representatives with their global literals and retains u as the required target. The projected problem asks for u in a selected ordinary span and w outside that span. It accepts **every graph with an even number of edges**, including K₄ and arbitrarily large non-three-colorable graphs.

Set φ(w)=1 and φ(Z_v)=φ(T_v)=0. Give u_b functional value zero at each vertex block and one at each edge block. In every block select the shifted first literal L₁+u_b. The functional kills all these columns. Their sum equals u plus (|E| mod 2)w plus the sum of Z_v for vertices of even degree. At each even-degree vertex also select its available unshifted Z_v, which φ kills. Thus for even |E| the selected sum is u and φ separates w. On K₄ all degrees are odd, so the shifted columns alone suffice. Their private u_b coordinates require keeping all of them, but upstairs their independent a_b1 directions prevent their sum from equaling u. No subset of this witness lifts. The four mutually adjacent vertices admit no three-coloring.

These selected ordinary positions realize exactly the line generated by the first of two named columns (u,w) in their coefficient kernel. The original avoidance condition uses a much larger terminal subspace. The proposed quotient preserves the functional's literal equations but loses the blockwise span conditions on physical contractions. This rules out the indicated quotient compiler only.

Run `python check.py` for every labeled simple graph through five vertices having even edge count. The checker constructs the selected columns and separating vector, tests their linear equalities, and computes three-colorability independently.
