# Unseeded boundary states with large binary or graphic blocks

Let D be a represented binary matroid, C=ker D and C⊥=row D. Seek disjoint c∈C, d∈C⊥ with score Lc(c)+Ld(d). Use ordinary columns O=(D;Lc) and protected columns R=[(0;I),(D Ldᵀ;0)]. A score split (a,b) asks for a sum R(a,0) of ordinary columns killed by one ambient covector whose root evaluations are (0,b). This preserves both literal gains. [Protected matrix and binary-tree merge](../unseeded-root-states/README.md)

## Cut dimension and outer composition

For ordinary positions S write C[S] and C⊥[S] for the words supported there. The exact augmented port dimension is

    dim(span O_S ∩ span(O_complement,R))
      = λ_D(S) + dim Lc(C[S]) + dim Ld(C⊥[S]).

Indeed rank O_S = rank D_S + dim Lc(C[S]). The rank of (O_complement,R) is t+rank(D_complement,D Ldᵀ), and the rank including all ordinary columns and roots is t+rank D. The increase over rank D_complement equals the rank of β↦β D Ldᵀ on row functionals annihilating D_complement. This is the rank of the indicated dual gain.

Supply a tree whose leaves partition the ordinary positions into blocks. Only its outer cuts are evaluated. For each port U store the pairs (u,ψ)∈U×U* for which ordinary columns summing to u are killed by some extension of ψ. Each positive state stores physical positions. Merge by matching the child functionals on their common span, summing the two vectors and requiring that sum to belong to the parent port. The union of the stored supports is valid because the child spans intersect inside both ports. At the root solve the zero conditions for the chosen support together with the prescribed root values. For maximum outer port width w, gluing takes 2^O(w+t) polynomial time in addition to producing the leaf tables.

## Binary leaves up to dimension three

For local columns A and ordinary positions X, put W_X=span A_X∩U. A state holds exactly if 〈u〉⊆W_X⊆ker ψ for some X. Reject ψ(u)=1. The empty support works for u=0; for ψ=0 use any representation of u.

At dimension two a nontrivial compatible state forces W_X=〈u〉. Choose independent a,b in U with a+b=u. The condition is equivalent to a circuit through these two extra columns in [A|a,b]. Thin a representation of u to an independent support. If its span has the prescribed trace, appending a,b creates a single circuit. Conversely a circuit through a,b has independent ordinary remainder summing to u and its span contains neither a nor b. A fundamental-circuit graph determines whether the named elements lie in one component. Repeatedly discard ordinary elements while that test stays true to extract one common circuit.

At dimension three a nontrivial state has W_X either the line through u or the plane ker ψ. For the line case choose three independent terminal columns B summing to u. A common circuit through all three is equivalent to that exact line by the same minimality argument. For the plane case choose B with ψ-value one on all three columns. In the dual of [A|B], contract the complementary ordinary set and delete its complement. The resulting named kernel is the orthogonal complement of the primal named kernel in B-coordinates. The three dual terminals are independent and belong to the span of the ordinary dual columns because the primal terminals are spanned by the ordinary columns and independent, respectively. Thus the second case is another common-triple-circuit test. On success, solve for u using primal ordinary columns outside the returned dual support. The binary three-elements algorithm is deterministic polynomial time, and deletion self-reduction supplies the witness. [Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S2) [Three-dimensional trace](../../three-dimensional-trace/README.md)

## A regular quotient for wider leaves

For a nontrivial compatible (u,ψ), let H=A modulo 〈u〉 and take g(u)=1. A cycle c of H with g(Ac)=1 has Ac=u. Let v_i complement u to a basis of U and find λ_i with Aλ_i=v_i. Require a cocycle d of H to satisfy d·λ_i=ψ(v_i). Such a cocycle is φA for some φ killing u. The equations give φ|U=ψ. Requiring physical disjointness c∩d=∅ finishes the equivalence. All constraints use ordinary cycle/cocycle lanes with dim U prescribed binary score bits, including the required primal one. A regular H admits the fixed-marker deterministic algorithm. [Regular marker algorithm](../../regular-markers/README.md)

For the other orientation dualize [A|B] for a basis B of U and exchange vector and covector in their B-coordinates. Complementing the ordinary contracting set exchanges the terminal kernel with its orthogonal complement. In a basis with ψ(B)=(1,0,…,0) the second projected host contracts the first dual terminal and deletes the others. Its dual is represented by A/ker ψ. Thus regularity of either A/〈u〉 or A/ker ψ suffices. Recover u on the ordinary complement of the dual support. A negative answer from a supported orientation decides the state. If both projections lack a supported oracle, this test leaves it unresolved.

## Graphic blocks and arbitrary named kernels

Suppose A has a supplied graphic realization on the same edge occurrences. Checking equality of the binary dependency spaces verifies the realization. Transfer each port vector to a vertex parity vector using any ordinary lift. A covector on edges is the cut of a vertex coloring. Asking that an edge support be killed by several covectors restricts it to edges whose two ends have identical color vectors. A demand T is realizable on these edges exactly when every monochromatic connected component contains an even number of vertices of T. A spanning forest or an incidence solve reconstructs a physical edge support.

Suppose the underlying simple graph has k supplied neighborhood classes. Each class is a clique or independent set, and pairs of classes are completely joined or completely separated. For r demand vectors and s prescribed port functionals on a d-dimensional port, use q=2^s colors. Guess the nonempty color set occurring in each class. For each color, form the induced graph on the occupied classes. A connected component containing two or more classes is one connected component of ordinary vertices of that color. A single clique also forms one component. In a single independent class without occupied neighbors every vertex of this color is isolated, so prohibit this color on any vertex demanded in any lane. For all other components require even parity of every demand. Process vertices with a table of sd port-evaluation bits, at most rkq component parities and kq occupancy bits. Check the predicted color sets exactly. This uses 2^O(sd+(r+1)k·2^s) polynomial time including guesses. For one paired state it is 2^O(k+d) polynomial. Loops leave every demand unchanged and parallel edge occurrences preserve connectivity. [Neighborhood classes](https://www.lamsade.dauphine.fr/~mlampis/papers/metatheorems_journal.pdf)

For an arbitrary full named kernel K on additional columns B, take demands Bz for basis z of K. Reject a demand outside span A. For each basis vector h of K⊥ require the root values h on B to be consistent. Restrict any such root functional to U=span A∩span B. These restrictions are well defined. The color algorithm simultaneously finds ordinary supports for all demands killed by all the restrictions. Compatible functions on the ordinary and named spans glue. The union X of the recovered supports makes the kernel of B modulo span A_X contain K, while the dual lifts of K⊥ force the reverse inclusion. This handles dependent named columns and roots outside the ordinary span. The parameter is the number of neighborhood classes plus the number of named columns. The reference implementation returns X and the physical supports.

For supplied bounded clique-width expressions, the same condition is expressible in counting monadic second-order logic on vertices. Quantify the color sets. Require the port evaluations by parity against unary input labels. For each color and each lane require every subset of that color closed under same-color adjacency to contain an even number of its demand vertices. These subsets are the unions of monochromatic components. This gives a parameterized decision and witness algorithm through the existing model-checking theorem. The direct quantitative bound above uses neighborhood diversity. [CMSO₁ formulation, Theorem 2.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41974v1#S2)

## Infinite controls

Take the reduced vertex-edge incidence A of K_m rooted at vertex 0, with even m≥8. Let u be the indicator of the first m−4 nonroot vertices. Let e,f,g be the last three nonroot unit directions. In U=span(u,e,f,g) request u with ψ(e)=1 and ψ(u)=ψ(f)=ψ(g)=0. Both quotient-regularity tests fail. Contract the root spokes of all but four vertices in supp(u). In the resulting quotient the K₅ on the root and remaining four vertices has four individual unit classes and three distinct complementary-pair classes, all seven nonzero points in rank three. Killing f and g additionally does not affect this restriction. Thus A/〈u〉 contains F₇ as a minor, and the actual dual orientation has an F₇* minor.

This state is forced by a native binary guard. Append a single extra ordinary column u to A. Give that column primal gain 1, and only the three spokes in directions e,f,g dual gains 2,4,8 respectively. Target 3 forces the extra column into the primal support and out of the dual, forces the chunk inside K_m to sum to u, and fixes its cut functional exactly as above on U. The augmented ordinary port of the clique is U. A matching on supp(u), the extra column, and the cut isolating the vertex of e supply a witness. The clique solver finds it even as the number of odd demand vertices grows. Indeed for a connected clique every compatible paired state succeeds: a compatible two-coloring extension puts even demand in each color, each nonempty color induces a clique, and matching within colors suffices.

K_m has unbounded native branchwidth. Every subcubic branch tree admits an edge partition with both sides containing at least one third of the graph edges. A simple graph of graphic rank r has at most binom(r+1,2) edges, so both sides have rank Ω(m). At least one of two complementary spanning subgraphs of a complete graph is connected; thus this cut has connectivity equal to the smaller side rank. This rules out constant-width full decomposition as an explanation for the clique family. Another control takes two clique summands and two separate gain coordinates on each. A triangle through its primal pin and a disjoint external star through its dual pin give all four local scores. Coincident opposite pins in one summand forbid exactly its local score 11. The whole four-bit spectrum sizes are 16 and 12, while the two macro leaves each have port dimension two.

## Implementation and reproducibility

Run `python check.py`, `python check_three.py`, `python check_regular.py` and `python check_graphic.py` from this directory with the sibling unseeded-root-states module available. The two-terminal and neighborhood-class algorithms are standalone polynomial or fixed-parameter implementations. The dimension-three and regular-host interfaces take callable witness solvers. Their tests use exhaustive small-instance solvers; no production implementation of the general three-element or regular decomposition algorithm is included here.

The checks compare 100 two-port local tables, 300 native/augmented cut identities and 150 decomposed spectra. The three-dimensional controls compare 63 local tables and 80 outer spectra, exercising 183 dual successes. The regular projection controls compare 351 states in each orientation, check 200 dual rank identities for K₈, and recover the forced target. Graphic checks compare 1,392 state decisions and 768 full named kernel decisions with raw enumeration and exercise dense forced guards at m=8,16,24. An independent audit checked a further 1,005 four-root kernels including loops, parallel edges, dependent and out-of-span roots.

The hypotheses apply to the supplied outer cuts and local representations. Fixed score bit width alone does not imply bounded outer width, three-dimensional local ports, regular projected hosts, or a bounded-neighborhood graphic realization. This gives no algorithm for unrestricted uniform SAT.
