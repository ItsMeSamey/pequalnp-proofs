# Directional probes for unrestricted quotient components

Let U have dimension four and H≤U dimension two. Compute the components C of the ordinary matroid projected by U. For each C, let S_C be the span of the union of all realizable local traces contained in H. The following test computes S_C even when the entire U lies in the span of C and it is unknown whether C realizes H.

For each of the three lines h≤H, quotient everything by h and on C ask the exact three-name trace question with target H/h. For a selected X⊆C its trace there equals ((span(X) intersect U)+h)/h, since h lies in U. Therefore success is equivalent to some realizable K⊆H with K not contained in h. A negative answer means S_C⊆h. Intersect all negatively answered lines, interpreting the empty intersection as H; the result equals S_C in every dimension zero, one and two. Each positive call supplies physical columns, so its actual K can be recomputed without assuming it is a line.

If the sum of all S_C has dimension below two, the answer is NO. If it has dimension two and at least two components are nonzero, the answer is YES. For the latter choose distinct components and independent vectors in their available traces: if all nonzero spans are lines use distinct lines; otherwise choose a vector first from a different component and probe the full-span component avoiding that line. Real witnesses realizing those vectors have local traces in H. Their union realizes H because component spans modulo U are jointly independent. Otherwise exactly one nonzero component has span H. Delete all other components: every solution uses zero traces there, and every solution in the retained component is a global solution. Consequently a general target reduces in polynomial time to a single contributing component with connected private quotient. Three three-name queries per component suffice; line witnesses from different components are chosen compatibly. This procedure includes components of arbitrary local named rank and does not require a catalogue of their exact lines.

For a concrete mixed control take two private dimensions a=16 and b=32 and use columns a+x and b+x for each x in {0,1,2,4,8}. Each private quotient component is a five-element parallel class. Its full named span has rank four, hence it is outside local spectrum enumeration restricted to rank at most three. For H=span(1,2) the rule may choose {a,a+1} and {b,b+2}. Single-component uncertainty is genuine: for a single private point a take all a+x with x in a support Q. Every nonempty selected set's trace is its affine difference span. If in each coset of H there are at most two members of Q, H cannot be attained, yet two distinct H directions from different pairs can give S_C=H. In particular Q={0,1,4,6,8,12} and H=span(1,2) have this property, with total named rank four.
