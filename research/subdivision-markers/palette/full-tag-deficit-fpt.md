## Deterministic single-exponential parameterization by full-tag rank deficit

Write d for the rank of observed K directions, t=d−rank(fully tagged directions) and m=2d. There is a deterministic 2^{O(t)}poly(input) physical witness algorithm. Thus t=O(log(input length)) with a fixed implied constant gives polynomial time. By the preceding argument a YES supplies t physical complement anchors and two residual columns. Their joint tag equations cut out some nonempty affine slice A in the m chart bits of codimension at most c=2(t+2). On that slice the two relevant residuals have a determinant P of degree ≤4 which is not identically zero. Any g in A with P(g)=1 suffices. None of these records or constraints must be known to the algorithm.

Construct a binary family S that hits every nonempty affine slice of codimension ≤c+1. For m=0 evaluate the unique chart. Otherwise choose a power of two q strictly above m·2^(c+1) and at most 2m·2^(c+1). Use F_q and, for every x in it and every binary linear functional τ on it, include the m-bit vector with coordinates τ(x^j) indexed j=0,…,m−1, using x⁰=1. At most q² seed points occur. Construct the field deterministically in poly(q,m) by testing monic polynomials of degree log₂q for irreducibility. For k≤c+1 independent binary constraint rows, read each row as coefficients of a polynomial of degree <m. Every one of their 2^k−1 nonzero binary sums has at most m−1 roots. The union cannot exhaust F_q. At a surviving x the k evaluations are independent over F₂, so a binary functional exists giving any prescribed right sides.

S∩A affinely spans A. Otherwise some opposite affine hyperplane inside A would be disjoint from S, contrary to the hitting property at ambient codimension ≤c+1. Pick s₀∈S∩A. Differences s+s₀ contain a basis for its translation space. In these parameters a nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree ≤4 has a nonzero evaluation at Hamming weight ≤4 by Boolean subset inversion. That chart is an XOR of one, three, or five seed points, after cancelling pairs. Enumerate all such charts independently of A and check each actual active physical span for inclusion of H. It cannot intersect U outside H, and Gaussian elimination extracts a physical subset. For zero-dimensional A the one-point check suffices. This also handles repeated buckets and pure H sites. No returns false positives; completeness on YES follows from the successful unknown slice.

O(q^10) direct chart checks suffice, giving 2^{O(t)}(d+1)^10 poly(input) time with the remaining polynomial exponent independent of t. The q² seed table costs 2^{O(t)}poly(input) space; XORs may be streamed. Full 4^d chart enumeration can be used when cheaper. No bound on t for arbitrary instances is claimed.
