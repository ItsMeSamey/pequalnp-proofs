# Ordered exterior synchronization

For a rank-r spanning binary matrix, the squarefree basis polynomial has coefficient one precisely on bases. Across a partition X,Y of physical variables, index its coefficient flattening by subsets on each side. Its rank in characteristic two equals 2^k, k=dim(span X intersect span Y). Each entry is the top exterior coefficient of the wedge of the two sets, zero on total sizes other than r. Physical subset wedges span the full exterior algebra on each side since the columns contain bases. Decompose V into private complements on each side and their common k-space W. Only the top wedge on each private complement contributes. The remaining pairing is the perfect complementary exterior pairing on ΛW, of dimension 2^k. This computes coefficient rank, not rank after assigning values to variables.

An explicit ordered algebraic branching program attains these widths. For every prefix span L choose a complementary basis P to W in L. Retain signature coordinates indexed by J subsets of a W-basis, the coefficients on P wedge J in ΛL. The signature unit J lifts to the actual decomposable wedge P wedge J. Compute its skip and take transitions by applying the next cut projection to this lift, or its wedge with the next physical column. Each output coefficient of the right size is one minor in the next prefix basis. Compute by elimination, without constructing the entire exterior algebra of V. The discarded kernel pairs to zero with every suffix wedge, hence remains in the kernel after optionally appending the next physical column. Thus the transition does not depend on the lift. Initial and terminal dimensions are one and terminal readout gives the basis indicator.

Apply this to the four quotients in four-plane-common-basis.md with the same physical order. Use the tensor product of four skip maps and the tensor product of four take maps separately, multiplying the latter by the physical variable just once. It computes the coefficientwise product. If the largest k on the original order is k0, each two-space quotient has overlap at most k0+2 by the rank formula. Combined width is at most 2^{4(k0+2)} and construction/evaluation cost is n poly(r) 2^{O(k0)}. For deterministic search retain a linear basis of actual prefix tensors after each cut, trying the two original physical bit extensions from each retained representative. A nonzero terminal signature certifies an actual witness, recovered by backpointers. This avoids cancellation in a parity count. Computing the cut intersections and verifying a supplied order use only linear algebra.

The coefficient flattening lower-bounds the corresponding width of every read-once oblivious program with that order by factoring the program at the cut. Add four independent ordinary axes, also used as names, to any disjoint binary matroid. Request the plane on the last two axes. The normalized witness polynomial is the old basis polynomial times the two mandatory axes' variables. Specializing all four auxiliary variables to one preserves an upper bound on ordered width. In the cycle matroid of K_m, halfway through the edges in any order both sides have rank at least m/sqrt(2)−O(1), since a simple graphic set of rank t has at most binomial(t+1,2) edges by summing over its components. Hence k≥(sqrt(2)−1)m−O(1). Every order requires exponential width in m, superpolynomial in input size. This excludes general polynomial-width ordered synchronization, not ordinary determinant circuits or general polynomial algorithms.
