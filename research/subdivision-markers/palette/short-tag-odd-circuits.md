### Short tag-odd circuits control low-deficit leaves

For a nonzero binary character λ on B let P_λ be the private span of sites belonging to private circuits of length ≤k+2 with total λ-tag one. At a still-four-name branch the contraction set C projects independently to K. Any full private direction in the descendant has two sites i,j with the same nonzero private image modulo C and differing λ-tags there. The unique relation k_i+k_j=Σ_{a∈C'} k_a for C'⊆C defines an original private circuit, since neither i nor j belongs to span C. Its original tag sum equals the tag difference after contracting C; changing the original splitting adds a linear correction summing to zero. Thus the full-tag space is contained in the image of P_λ. A live branch has deficit ≥d−k−rank(P_λ), and deletions add no potential pairs. If d−rank(P_λ)>τ+k for any λ, only regular/rank leaves can occur in a promised depth-k tree, giving the same strong-backdoor transfer. Efficient computation of P_λ on arbitrary binary matrices is not assumed.

With a supplied graphic private representation, compute a safe upper bound in polynomial time. For each indexed edge e=(u,v) delete just e. In the two-sheeted parity graph an edge of label s=λ(b) moves (w,p) to (w',p+s). Mark e if BFS finds a walk from (u,0) to (v,1+λ(b_e)) of length ≤k+1. Each short odd circuit containing e supplies such a walk; false positives from nonsimple walks are harmless. Odd loops use a zero-length walk, parallel edges remain indexed. The binary rank of the marked private columns upper-bounds rank(P_λ). Test this for all three λ. The shortest odd closed walk computed by parity BFS also gives the true shortest odd cycle, since a closed walk with odd total label contains an odd cycle.

In the Cartesian product C_L square K_s sign only wrap edges connecting layers L−1 and 0. Odd cycles have length at least L by winding parity on the C_L projection. There are triangles in every clique layer for s≥3. Give three separate internal edges in different clique layers the other three independent named charge coordinates. Their individual triangles and a wrap cycle show physical spanning of all four named dimensions. For L>k+2 the signed-girth condition succeeds although ordinary girth is three. This example establishes the precondition, without claiming a small terminal tree for this graph.
