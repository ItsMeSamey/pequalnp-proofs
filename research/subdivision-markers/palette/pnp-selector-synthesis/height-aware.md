# Height-specific selector degree

Fix the indexed keys, tags and two-bit heights. For each attainable private constant u, seek supported Boolean selector coefficients of degree at most s with constant private sum u and both coordinates of the selected height sum of Boolean degree at most j, where 0≤j≤s. Use a distinct unknown family for every indexed site. Canonicalizing equal key/tag pairs blindly would discard choices with different heights. Impose vanishing of every squarefree height coefficient above degree j in the global chart coordinates. These are linear restrictions on the unknown selector coefficients. Row elimination computes the exact private image under these restrictions in input size to the power O(s), for fixed s≥1.

Take lifts for a basis of this image. Each lift is a genuine active physical sum at every chart and its height has degree at most j. Let t be the complementary private rank. In any successful witness chart, keep t actual complementary sites and two active residual sites by the same chart extension used in the fixed-coordinate exact-plane proof. On the affine slice fixing those t+2 tags, the complementary heights and both residual-site heights are constant. Their height residuals after elimination have degree at most j, so the two-dimensional determinant has degree at most 2j. A deterministic chart family consisting of odd sums of at most 2j+1 points of the codimension-2(t+2) seed suffices. End-to-end time is n^{O(s+1)} 2^{O((j+1)(t+1))} n^{O(j+1)}. This claim fixes the heights before constructing the private image. It supplies no universal alternating-form cover.

For a strict example use the reviewed 24-site four-dimensional palette with private degree-two image of rank two and degree-three image of rank four. Give its original sites zero height. Choose any remaining direction k. Both of its two allowed tags have physical representatives. Copy one site of each tag and give the copies respective heights 1 and 2. No new key/tag pair has been introduced. The all-zero-height originals supply degree-three selectors with constant zero height lifting every private direction, so j=0 suffices. An exact plane is impossible since both gained sites would need to be active and they require conflicting tags on k. For a YES counterpart copy sites on two distinct remaining directions instead. These two directions are independent over F₂, their tags admit a common chart, and the equal-key/tag baseline and copy pairs produce both pure height directions. There are no physical pure height sites in either construction. The examples distinguish original selector degree from reconstructed height degree and do not imply a runtime lower bound.
