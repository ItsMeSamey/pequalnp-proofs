# A five-object parity obstruction to one terminal identification

Take five objects A={0}, B={1,5}, C={2}, D={3,6}, E={4}. The 15 partitions into two object-pairs and one singleton expand to seven-terminal partitions. Their sum as a coordinate functional evaluates to one on the desired {0,1,5}|{2,3,6}|{4} indicator. All occur as distinct indexed forest monomials in K7.

Identify any two terminals and query any fully specified two-component terminal partition of the quotient. If the identified terminals belonged to the same original forest component the quotient contains a cycle (or loop). Otherwise their distinct components merge and the third is unchanged. If they occupy a single object the query is identically zero on our 15 coordinates. For distinct objects a,b the resulting component away from the merged point prescribes one intact object-block. Inconsistent partial objects give zero. An unchanged singleton leaves four objects to pair with a,b separated, exactly two ways. An unchanged pair leaves three objects, with either a or b necessarily the singleton for those two to lie apart, again two ways. Hence every quotient test vanishes under the parity functional. A query specifying fewer terminal labels is a sum of full patterns.

A three-root mixed Laplacian minor also vanishes. If either transversal selects one object twice it gives zero. Otherwise row and column triples I,J determine 3-subsets of five objects. The singleton must be some s in their intersection. If I=J, each s allows two pairings. If the intersection has two objects, each s allows one pairing. If it has one object, that s permits two pairings. All totals are even. Thus no scalar linear combination of ALL such minors and ALL exact two-forest evaluations after ANY one terminal identification returns the target characteristic-two polynomial, even with the latter evaluations freely available. This concerns linear identities, not arbitrary nonlinear or interior-aware computations.

The argument applies to any three-block target with two parts of size at least two: split each of those two parts into two nonempty objects, leaving the third intact. For the seven-mark fixture contraction_probe_combined.py also computes combined row rank 230 on 301 terminal partitions, the target outside. A disk restriction eliminates crossing patterns, so the obstruction does not apply to the unitriangular disk recovery.
