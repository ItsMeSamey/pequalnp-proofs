# First and second indexed relation spaces on a private branch decomposition

Supply any binary tree whose leaves are the indexed physical sites. For a subtree S write K_S for the span of its private keys, J_S=K_S∩K_out for the boundary, and w for the largest boundary dimension. For each linear chart restriction γ:J_S→F₂² and each pair p,q in J_S, use a separate state. A representative consists of two indexed bit vectors u,v supported in S, and one local chart on K_S extending γ, matching all sites used by either vector. Their private sums must equal p and q respectively. The state stores the linear span over the binary field of the feature (1,u,v,u∧v) over such representatives, in a fixed global indexed ambient. The empty representative family has zero span. A basis can retain actual original features, separately from the echelon vectors for membership tests.

At a leaf try both membership bits of its site, and all local charts on its private key span. At a join the child chart restrictions must agree on K_left∩K_right, which lies in both child boundaries. The actual charts then glue. Their restriction to the parent boundary is determined by the two child boundary restrictions: for x in J_parent write x=a+b in the child private spans. Because x also lies in the parent's exterior span, a lies in the left boundary and b in the right; the sum of the two chart values is independent of the decomposition. Add the partial sums and check that both lie in the parent boundary. Conversely, every parent representative restricts to admitted child states: a child partial sum equals the parent's corresponding sum plus the other child's sum, hence belongs to that child's exterior span.

Write the four feature slots as (s,u,v,z). For a compatible pair the glued feature is the value of the bilinear map
(s,u,v,z)*(t,x,y,w)=(st,tu+sx,tv+sy,tz+sw+u∧y+x∧v).
This identity holds on actual representatives by disjoint concatenation. Bilinearity means products of any bases span all glued products; after summing contributions over compatible state pairs the result is the exact parent state. Retaining actual independent features preserves witness extraction for later linear tests. At the root J=0 and both private sums vanish. Project its sole state's last coordinate to obtain W=sum_g exterior-square(L_g), **not** exterior-square(sum_g L_g). An arbitrary alternating form is in the sought annihilator exactly when it vanishes on this computed basis. A nonzero test has an actual representative pair; interpolate its joint chart on the support union for a physical witness.

There are 2^(4w) states per node, at most 2^(8w) pairs at a join, and the feature space has dimension 1+2N+binomial(N,2). All remaining work is polynomial in explicit input length, including Gaussian elimination, rational-free binary basis conversion and actual traceback. Consequently arbitrary alternating tests can be solved in deterministic 2^O(w) poly(input) on a supplied tree, even for growing r=dim sum_g L_g. With an arbitrary tree this is an exact potentially exponential linear-algebra characterization. No polynomial all-input width bound or optimal tree finder is assumed.

For a private direct sum the formula specializes to W being the direct sum of the component W spaces and every cross-component L_i tensor L_j. Component charts extend independently. On a private rank-one axis, nonempty tags are mutually exclusive: only wedges of two same-tag local relation vectors occur. On different private axes chart compatibility is independent. These two cases distinguish the absent-plane controls that global first-space rank cannot decide.
