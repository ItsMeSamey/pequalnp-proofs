# Degree and codimension tradeoff

Fix a supplied physical section with remaining private rank t. Form all squarefree determinant ANFs of the actual height lifts plus the two constant generators. Let D be the maximum degree. For j descending from D to zero, compute V_j, the common space of shifts for which every difference has global degree at most j−1 (negative degree means zero). The descending coefficient kernels in common-period.md compute all intermediate spaces in one traversal. Write c_j for their codimensions.

Each determinant has degree at most j on every V_j coset. Its affine-coordinate derivative in a standard basis direction is the restriction of a global difference, hence has degree at most j−1. If a squarefree monomial had degree above j, differentiating in any variable it contains would preserve a distinct monomial of degree at least j. The zero case follows from vanishing differences. The unknown successful coset and the usual t+2 active sites thus yield a nonempty affine slice of codimension at most c_j+2(t+2) on which the residual determinant has degree at most j.

For each supplied selector budget and every j, hybrid.py compares the full cube with Q² + sum of binomial(Q²,l) over odd l at most 2 floor(j/2)+1, where Q is the seed field size for the corresponding cap. It selects the least candidate-work bound. The j=D and j=0 endpoints recover global degree and exact periods. Charge all trial section constructions and filtrations, including 2^D possible translation expansion per monomial. Candidate and field-pair work have polynomial factors. The method asserts no global optimum among all physical sections or subspaces.
