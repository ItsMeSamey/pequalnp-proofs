# Claw-free duals with many neutral vertices

Suppose the connected supplied plane dual has claw-free simple shadow, with all nonzero vertex charges equal. Let h be the size of its largest neutral-induced component.

Every connected induced monochromatic component with even charge has a partition into single neutral vertices and paths with two charged endpoints and only neutral internal vertices. Replace each neutral vertex by two adjacent true twins, retaining all appropriate external adjacencies, and leave each charged vertex single. Clique substitution preserves claw-freeness since the three independent leaves must be in distinct cliques different from the center's. This connected expansion has even order and has a perfect matching. In the subgraph of that matching plus each neutral's twin edge, counted twice if also matched, neutral vertices have degree two and charged vertices degree one. Its components are cycles with no charges or paths with two charged endpoints. Contract each twin edge; each original neutral occurs only at one position in its path because the two twins are consecutive. Refine all neutral-only pieces into singletons.

List all neutral singletons, all adjacent charged pairs, and the unions of any two distinct charged vertices with a nonempty subset of one neutral-induced component. All charged paths above appear on this list, since their neutral interiors are connected. Its size is O((1+n)^3 2^h). Individually feasible listed sets span L; jointly feasible DISJOINT listed pairs span W. Indeed partition all monochromatic components as above for a known pair. Each resulting piece is connected with charge zero. Merging all but one or two pieces into the background leaves only unions of zero-charge pieces as background components. Thus every required pair in the wedge expansion is feasible, possibly via a different chart after merging. Conversely verify every candidate pair directly by the dual component test and output its original physical cuts. This gives deterministic 4^h poly(n+|E|) computation and actual witnesses, with no bound on total neutrals. When every vertex is neutral all pairs of arbitrary dual cuts are feasible directly.
