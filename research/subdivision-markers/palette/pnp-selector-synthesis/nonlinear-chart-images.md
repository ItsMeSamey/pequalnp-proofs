# Exact planes in a polynomially parametrized chart image

Let F map n Boolean assignment bits to the fixed physical chart coordinates, with coordinate degree at most a and evaluation cost T. Fix a supported section of private rank d−t. Suppose all augmented generator minors have degree at most j on each coset of a supplied chart subspace of codimension c; j=D,c=0 recovers the usual determinant profile.

For a successful b0 let g0=F(b0). Its active private keys span K', containing all global section directions. Use t'≤t active physical anchors to complete their basis to K'. At g0 two active physical sites have independent height residuals in that basis. The coefficients in the eliminations are constants. Fix compatibility of these t'+2 sites and fix the coset through g0. This costs at most 2(t'+2)+c scalar affine constraints. On the common slice the residual determinant is a constant linear combination of the generator minors and has degree at most j. No change to g0 or extra active rank is needed.

Extend the restricted determinant to the ambient chart via an affine retraction onto this nonempty slice. Multiply by its indicator, a product of indicators of independent affine equations. The result equals one at g0 and can be one only where the chosen physical sites together with supported section lifts certify an actual plane. Composing with F gives a nonzero assignment polynomial supported on successful inputs, of degree at most B=min(n,a(j+c+2(t+2))). No hidden anchor, successful coset, or residual polynomial needs to be computed.

Evaluate F on all assignments of Hamming weight at most B and check the original physical plane by active ranks. Boolean subset inversion guarantees a witness whenever one exists. A nonzero degree-B Boolean polynomial has at least 2^(n−B) ones by induction using a nonzero derivative. Thus O(2^B log(1/error)) uniform samples also suffice with verified successes and one-sided error. Each chart costs T plus a physical rank check; charge the section and its profile separately. An isolated successful assignment forces n≤a(j+c+2(t'+2)), since its indicator has degree n. This solves the specified image problem; unrestricted physical charts may include additional solutions.

For arbitrary Boolean circuit encoders the image problem is NP-complete even on a fixed host. Use one private key with four zero-height copies covering all tags, another copy at tag 1 with height 1, and one pure height-2 column. A chart succeeds exactly when that key has tag 1. The zero-height section gives t=D=0. Encode a circuit C by mapping the key to (C(b),0). The physical success predicate becomes C(b). Verification is polynomial, and circuit SAT gives hardness. For n-fold AND this isolates one assignment with degree n. To make the encoder injective, append n independent zero-height fully tagged directions displaying the bits b_i. Extra keys create no height relations. Unrestricted feasibility of the fixed host itself is immediate.
