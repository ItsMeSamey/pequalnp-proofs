# Arbitrary cographic alternating queries with one independent vertex-charge bit

On a connected indexed cographic host assume the boundary charges of the two-bit edge tags have binary rank at most one. Identify their one possible nonzero value with 1, writing σ_v. Edge tags themselves need not lie on a common line. Expand the given physical alternating form Ω on cut vectors as a vertex alternating form H_uv by substituting endpoint differences. Fix any middle vertex b. For any spanning three-forest let p,q indicate the two trees not containing b, in either order. Put D_uv=p_u q_v+q_u p_v. The uncharged wedge bit is Σ_{u<v}H_uv D_uv. Multiplying by (1+Σσ_xp_x)(1+Σσ_xq_x) enforces full neutrality because the total charge is zero.

The quadratic term of the neutrality factor is Σ_{x<y}σ_xσ_y D_xy since p_x q_x=0. On D_uv=1, the endpoint colors are the two nonmiddle colors and p_x+q_x=D_xu+D_xv. Consequently each summand with H_uv=1 is exactly

    D_uv D_uv + Σ_x σ_x (D_uv D_xu + D_uv D_xv)
               + Σ_{x<y} σ_x σ_y D_uv D_xy.

A weighted mixed Laplacian minor deleting rows {b,u,v}, columns {b,x,y}, has forest coefficient D_uv D_xy. The reduced incidence minor for either triple has determinant 1 precisely on a spanning three-forest with one marked root per tree; Cauchy–Binet gives their product. A repeated index WITHIN a triple means zero. Repeated indices across triples are permitted and signs vanish over characteristic two. Thus O(n^4) explicit determinant evaluations suffice on arbitrary hosts, using the prior mixed-transversal technique. Distinct original edge variables distinguish all indexed forest monomials, including parallel choices; loops never occur.

By connected-three-part generation the polynomial is nonzero iff Ω tests positively on an actual compatible pair. At degree ≤n−3, independent uniform weights in a binary extension of order >2n give failure probability at most one half for such a query. A zero evaluation leaves the answer unknown. Starting with a nonzero evaluation, delete an edge when its zero-weight specialization stays nonzero; otherwise its weight is nonzero and the contraction coefficient is nonzero. Preserve vertex charge on deletion, XOR endpoint charges and push forward H on contraction (merge coefficients and discard diagonal). The fixed middle vertex follows its image. Track physical forest indices to recover original connected component cuts and verify neutrality and Ω. For n≤2 W is zero. Any returned pairs give an exact certified lower subspace. Full basis recovery with prescribed failure ε uses fresh amplified tests on each of at most binom(M,2) adaptive separating forms and a conditional union bound. It gives a high-probability W basis with genuine physical generators, not an independently certified upper subspace. Rank-two charges still have only the conditional seven-mark reduction on unrestricted hosts.
