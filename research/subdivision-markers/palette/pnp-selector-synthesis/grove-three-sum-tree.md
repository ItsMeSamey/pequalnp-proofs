# Exact fixed-marked grove evaluation on three-clique-sum trees

For every fixed number of marked vertices, positive integer weighted grove sums for any specified partition are exactly computable in polynomial time on a supplied polynomial-size tree of completed torsos with adhesion at most three, provided each completed torso has fixed orientable genus or bounded treewidth. A grove is a spanning forest with exactly one component per prescribed block, containing precisely that block of marked sites. Original indexed edges must have unique ownership in incident bags; virtual clique edges carry no original weight. Original loops make no forest contribution. Treat each connected component separately and reject an unmarked component or a requested block crossing components. Empty adhesions split the component problem.

For the base surface primitive extend the reviewed (3,3,1) median reduction to any fixed terminal partition. In an overfull block of k>2 sites choose three determined by physical order and split at their unique tree median as in surface-grove-spine.md: cut two chosen indexed departure edges and allocate every remaining named site among the three resulting blocks. Each block has at least one old anchor and at most one new helper endpoint (including the median). Consequently the excess sum of max(0,block size−2) decreases from k−2 to at most k−3 within this block. Discard inconsistent repeated sites and reassemble using the same fixed rule as in the original three-terminal case. Every original forest contributes exactly once to its actual cut-edge choices and assigned old-site groups. A bounded number of reductions gives the pair/singleton evaluator. Fixed small exceptional topologies for one or two named sites can alternatively be evaluated by ordinary Laplacian forest minors. Bounded treewidth uses standard connectivity-partition forest DP with marked membership and a cycle check when adding an edge or joining states.

Root the tree. Each subtree table is indexed by partitions of its original marked vertices together with its ≤3 upper boundary sites, identifying matching physical sites. Count exactly those physical forests in the subtree (including isolated vertices) for which every component hits the named set and the named connectivity equals that partition. A marked site on a child boundary is already represented there and need not mark that strict child as active.

At a bag collapse every child containing NO original mark in its strict interior by the Schur clique of grove-three-port-satellites.md (prior source in pnp-selector-synthesis) and its common scalar det(L_II). Positive weights and physical reachability of each interior component to a boundary give invertibility. In a connected input this reachability follows from tree separation, even with actual edges on interface vertices owned at an ancestor. An exceptional unmarked detached graph component makes the global grove impossible and is prehandled. For ≤3 boundary vertices all local partitions are determined by terminal Laplacian minors, so the replacement is simultaneous for all exterior forests. A leafless empty strict side and owned physical boundary edges are also covered by the same normalized response; each physical edge occurs in only one region.

At most k children carry original marks in their strict interiors. Enumerate their individual table states and multiply their actual counts. A child component with no boundary is final and must supply exactly one requested global block at this DP node. Every other component is represented by a forced abstract tree on its boundary vertices and its original terminal identities carried to one of those vertices. All its boundary vertices are identified for connectivity only after testing forced cycles. The union of the forced boundary trees must be acyclic (use indexed copies even when two children supply the same pair), else the true forests already make a cycle. Acyclicity with any parent forest is equivalent to acyclicity with the abstract boundary trees: contract the connected pieces of each child forest, or use component ranks; the interiors are disjoint and each tree component spanning b boundary vertices contributes exactly b−1 independent joins. Contract the forced trees, transfer every attached original mark, bag mark and parent-boundary mark to its image, check prescribed component labels agree at merged sites, and call the exact fixed-mark grove evaluator for the remaining prescribed blocks. If no blocks remain, only the empty residual vertex set has forest weight one. Multiply by the selected child counts and all unmarked normalization factors and sum over child states. The forced edges are in the completed torso by adhesion cliques; indexed copies can be drawn parallel to available clique edges, or simply contract their endpoints and preserve physical optional edges as distinct, dropping loops. This gives a minor of the completed torso up to parallel copies, so the base assumptions persist. No child virtual edge is returned as an original witness.

Induction gives the exact weighted polynomial evaluated over positive integers. The number of marked child choices per bag is constant, with constant state count per child. Exact intermediate child forest totals are bounded by the product of (1+w_e) over their original edges; Schur entries are ratios of positive rooted-forest sums of comparable bit length (zero off-diagonal effective weights may be omitted). A bag's rational weights can be cleared with one common denominator using the known component count and thus known homogeneous forest degree. The resulting bit growth is polynomial. Calculate each unmarked physical child response directly on its original subtree if desired to avoid nested denominators. Thus the algorithm is polynomial in original graph size and bit length at fixed parameters. Lift finite characteristic-two extension field input weights to positive univariate integer evaluations as in surface-grove-spine.md, then interpolate and reduce. To recover original edges under a nonzero evaluated forest polynomial, use physical edge zeroing (or forced subdivisions/forcing as in the existing original-ID trace note), not an abstract Schur edge. Edge deletion preserves the supplied torso support, while weight zero can be handled at the interpolated positive lifts. For each fixed single-crossing graph H, the constructive Demaine–Hajiaghayi–Nishimura–Ragde–Thilikos decomposition gives exactly such an at-most-three sum of planar and bounded-treewidth pieces on H-minor-free input. Their primary paper is https://erikdemaine.org/papers/CliqueSum_JCSS/ . The construction partitions indexed original edges among bag positions and retains common virtual triangles in the torso supports. Parallel physical edges may be placed alongside the underlying edge; the decomposition can be computed on the simple shadow. Consequently the original indexed cographic wedge problem has one-sided randomized polynomial decision and physical witness recovery on this class, via its seven-mark grove reduction.
