# Chart-compatible physical dictionaries

For a fixed indexed binary private representation and two-bit tag vector on every column, let L_g be the private kernel on the sites matched by g. Suppose a finite list D of physical kernel words satisfies span(D intersect L_g)=L_g for every chart. Then W=sum_g exterior² L_g is generated by pairs from D whose combined physical supports admit a single interpolating chart. Bilinearity proves completeness for each g, and interpolation gives soundness, including exact support witnesses. Construction after receipt of the list takes |D|² polynomial-time interpolation and exterior Gaussian operations. The semantic coverage premise itself needs a proof or a recognized construction.

For an indexed private graphic representation, choose vertex potentials in F₂². A chart activates uv of tag b when the endpoint potentials sum to b. Every potential assignment defines a chart on incidence columns, and conversely. The gain lift has four copies of each vertex, and edges (u,c)(v,c+b) for every indexed edge and c. Every potential assignment selects an induced transversal, whose graph is exactly the physical active graph. Handle physical active loops and paired parallel edges directly as circuits of lengths one and two. Discard impossible nonzero-tag loops. The simple shadow of the remaining lift is enough to bound the lengths of chordless cycles of length at least four.

For fixed b≥3 test this shadow for induced cycles of length >b. Enumerate induced paths on b vertices. For each path keep its endpoints, delete its internal vertices and all their neighbors outside the path, and check endpoint connectivity. Endpoints are nonadjacent. A shortest alternate path gives an induced cycle of length at least b+1. Conversely any induced cycle of that length contains a successful path. This is polynomial for fixed b. Passing the test implies that each active cycle decomposes by physical active chords into cycles of length ≤b. Enumerating indexed cycles through b, filtering by tag interpolation, gives D with size O((1+N)^b). This covers globally balanced chordal hosts for b=3 and globally balanced weakly chordal hosts for b=4, since switching splits the lift into four copies. The condition only needs absence of long holes in the lift. Bare chordality of the private graph does not suffice under arbitrary tags.

## Filling with alternative physical routes

Allow additional indexed edges on the same vertices. For each virtual edge uv with tag b supply a finite list of physical edge subsets. Each subset must have incidence sum e_u+e_v. Require that at every potential assignment with c_u+c_v=b some listed subset is entirely active. This can be checked by enumerating 4^s assignments on the union of the involved vertices, including u,v, with s charged separately for each edge. Thus logarithmic local size suffices for polynomial verification. For example, a two-hop route with all four physical tags on each of its two links supplies four alternate paths indexed by the common intermediate potential relative to u. No fixed two-edge representative works for all such potentials.

Check the bounded-hole condition on the augmented lift. Enumerate its underlying augmented physical-index cycles of length ≤b, including parallel pairs and loops. In each substitute every choice from the virtual-edge lists, taking the indexed XOR, then keep words with a common physical chart. If M is the augmented number of edges and P≥1 bounds list sizes, enumeration costs O(((1+M)P)^b poly(input)), in addition to the local coverage and lift checks. Every retained word is a genuine physical kernel word. For completeness fix g, choose one active physical representative of each active virtual edge once, and extend by linearity, identically on physical edges. This map preserves incidence. The augmented active short-cycle generators map into the enumerated list and span the original active cycle space because every original active cycle maps to itself. Subsequent pair tests use actual physical supports, including cancellations. No virtual edge is returned as a witness. This disjunctive-path result complements the fixed-path PEO construction of binary_modular_bases.

## Actual-dictionary obstruction on a complete host

Take the complete graph on m≥4 cyclic layers of two vertices. All edges joining consecutive layers have tag zero and all others have tag 2. Assign potentials 0 and 1 to the two vertices of each layer in independently chosen order. Exactly two disjoint m-cycles are active. Up to interchanging the colors this yields 2^(m−1) different cycle pairs. A nonzero word in any of these two-dimensional chart kernels is one entire cycle or the union of both. Each word determines the unordered pair, hence no nonzero word is shared between these kernels. Every chartwise spanning dictionary therefore has at least 2^m entries on this family of complete hosts with O(m²) sites. This bound concerns spanning each L_g by actual listed words. W itself has polynomial dimension.
