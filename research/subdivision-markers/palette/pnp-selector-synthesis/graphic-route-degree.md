# Linear selector degree even with two fully tagged physical paths

Use graphic-universal-join-hardness.md (dab2468d) on the bounded-degree non-four-colorable graphs of Lauria–Nordström, CCC 2017 Theorem 3.6, or arXiv:2306.00125 Theorem 3.7. Suppose an always-active physical chain of degree s joins one pole in each path, allowing selectors to depend on every chart bit. Restrict both copies of each input vertex to equal colors (x_i,y_i), an affine substitution. All three-tag diagonal links become inactive. Taking the cut parity of the chain gives 1=sum_ij α_ij over the tag-zero graph-edge crossing sites. Each α_ij vanishes outside δ_ij=(1+x_i+x_j)(1+y_i+y_j)=1, so 1=sum_ij α_ij δ_ij in the Boolean quotient ring. The verified unary translation in binary-modular-bases/selector-degree-colouring.md now applies literally: this yields a canonical unary polynomial-calculus refutation of 4-colorability of degree ≤max(4,s+2). The cited graphs require linear degree over F₂. Therefore s is Ω(number of input vertices), also Ω(number of indexed physical sites) for the bounded-degree source family.

Every chart has an active route, and exhaustive lookup gives an upper bound linear in physical vertex count. The physical full-tag private deficit is exactly one: the full-link forest has two components and the entire graph is connected. Expressing the incidence chart via vertex potentials is linear, so the same lower bound applies in chart coordinates. This gives the former arbitrary-binary-column selector obstruction on graphic private incidence columns with two supplied fully tagged physical paths. It has no implication of existential exact-plane hardness.
