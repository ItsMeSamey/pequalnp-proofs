# Composing decision-only charged quadratic partition solvers over true cliques

Fix p, charge dimension r over GF(2), and adhesion a. A supplied tree decomposition of a connected graph has nonempty intersections of size ≤a, each a clique in the ORIGINAL simple shadow. Bags use their induced original edges, including physical clique edges on both sides when testing connectivity; no edge-disjoint paths or artificial edges are needed. Each component of every strict child side meets its adhesion by an actual edge (automatic by connectedness). Assume a uniform oracle on every bag graph and every subpalette of ≤p ordered colors that produces an actual connected spanning partition satisfying supplied per-vertex binary charges, target color totals, boundedly many color pins and nonvanishing of an arbitrary quadratic polynomial of the one-hot indicators. The oracle needs only compare connected solutions with connected solutions. For the expander bank theorem the intrinsic bag must satisfy its own promised four-connectivity, bounded degree, expansion and size regimes. The assembled graph need not expand; decomposition discovery is separate.

For every connected original color class its intersection with a bag, or with a whole side of a tree edge, is connected or empty. Any excursion outside it along a monochromatic path rejoins two same-colored vertices of a common incident adhesion, connected by their physical clique edge; shortcut it. Maintain for each region its exact palette, boundary colors and all strict-interior charge totals, plus a basis of degree-≤2 indicator moment rows from actual colorings whose nonempty color classes are connected. Charges count each vertex once. Closed colors (used but absent from the adhesion) cannot reappear anywhere outside the region in a connected extension. An open color reaches its own boundary representatives. Conversely child and bag certificates satisfying these requirements glue into genuinely connected colors, because each bag color has a connected physical core and every matching child color reaches its interface. Permit a closed color in at most one place.

Fix an actual region solution giving a nonzero separating quadratic P and a desired record. Keep at most p children carrying a color genuinely closed inside the child. Freeze all bag colors. For each other nondefault child consider resetting every strict-interior component to the color of a fixed neighboring adhesion vertex (owner chosen physically, independent of all coloring); this connects the component to the unchanged bag. Multiply P by the ≤pr affine score-bit tests =0/1 encoding the desired strict totals. Its degree in independent reset switches is ≤m=2+pr. The original mask is successful; choose a successful mask minimizing nondefault children. If more than m eligible exceptions remain, m+1 switches give a Boolean delta at the all-unreset vector, impossible in degree m. Palette and connectivity remain valid: all nonclosed child colors attach, the original projected bag partition is physically connected, and reserved closed colors are kept. Some ≤F=p+m exceptional child choices therefore suffice.

Enumerate such exceptions, child boundary/score records and actual representative rows, the full bag palette and interface pins; pin one representative of each used bag color as needed. Replace each exceptional witness within its record by a retained actual degree-two representative preserving P, one at a time via linear separation. Fold default strict-interior charges onto their chosen owners; fix exceptional contributions and account for outgoing boundary charges exactly once. All original strict-interior indicators in defaults copy one physical bag label. With exceptions fixed the original quadratic pulls back to a quadratic; pins are explicit and never multiplied into it. To enumerate a spanning family of degree-two bag moments in the correct exact-score/pin bucket, adaptively query a basis of linear forms vanishing on the previously obtained row space, increasing dimension upon a positive, marking a separating form zero otherwise. A candidate expanded original row for each returned actual coloring is witnessed and checkable. The unchanged connected bag colors guarantee at least one nonzero for any original separating test, so the resulting row list spans the whole region record. At the root derive all original indexed cut wedges by linear evaluation on these quadratic rows. Distinct edge indices can share endpoints without affecting the actual cut map.

Fixed parameters give polynomial enumeration (at most constant F exceptional children at a time, each record has polynomially many actual rows). In particular clique sums of locally promised expander-bank bags and other exact joint-oracle bags admit deterministic full W; unlike a general three-separator whose fill edges are missing, a true clique preserves connectivity even before any repair. No transfer to arbitrary separators or nonexpanding irreducible bags follows.
