# One seed chart for low-dimensional height quotients

Fix a physical section synthesizing r independent constant private directions on every chart. Let P be the common translation-period subspace of the resulting height ANFs on m=2d fixed chart bits, with p=m−dim P. This is exactly the common period of the generator minors in determinant-profile.md: two constant height generators recover every scalar coordinate by a minor, and periods of all coordinates preserve all products.

Write Δ_vF(x)=F(x+v)+F(x). Begin with the full shift space V and descend degrees from s−1 through zero, where s bounds all scalar height degrees. At level k all Δ_vF for v in V have degree at most k. The joint map sending v to their degree-k coefficient vectors is linear on V. Indeed Δ_(v+w)=Δ_w+translate_w(Δ_v), and a translation leaves the degree-k homogeneous part unchanged when the polynomial has degree at most k. Evaluate this map on a basis of V and use a Gaussian kernel update. After degree zero precisely the periods remain; for constants or no input functions keep the full shift space. Sparse monomial translation may expand each degree-s monomial into 2^s terms. The s rounds and elimination are polynomial in this expanded size and m, with fixed-s polynomial cost for supplied sparse ANFs.

For a successful chart g0 choose t=d−r physical complement sites and two residual sites as in the supplied-section proof. Intersect the slice fixing those tags with g0+P. Each height residual on the intersection is a fixed linear combination of now-constant heights, so the successful determinant stays nonzero. Its codimension is at most 2(t+2)+p. A global universal seed hitting every flat of this codimension supplies a successful chart as a single seed point. The algorithm need not know g0, its coset, or the sites. Test indexed physical witnesses by Gaussian elimination. Seed construction and checks cost 2^{O(t+p)} poly(N+d), in addition to section synthesis and common-period computation. This statement fixes the heights before computing P.
