# Exact indexed paired space from perfect matchings in a claw-free plane dual

Suppose a connected supplied plane dual D has the same nonzero tag charge at every vertex and its simple shadow is claw-free. There are an even number of vertices. By the dual compatibility rule, jointly active face labelings x,y are exactly those whose four color classes have all components even.

Every connected claw-free graph of even order has a perfect matching. For completeness take a longest simple path ending in u,v. For order two use the single edge. Otherwise the path has at least three vertices. If deleting u,v disconnects the graph, there is a component not containing the remaining path prefix: v has no outside neighbor by path maximality and u must have a neighbor w in that component. The predecessor t of u has no edge to w; v also has no edge to w. Claw-freeness at u forces t adjacent to v. Replacing t,u,v at the end by t,v,u,w contradicts maximal length. Thus u,v can be deleted preserving connectivity, and induction supplies the result. This is the classical connected claw-free perfect-matching theorem; the proof is included to pin the dependency.

For a feasible x,y choose a perfect matching in every monochromatic component. Their union M is a perfect matching of D. Conversely any perfect matching M supports every labeling that is constant separately on the endpoints of every matched edge: each monochromatic component is a disjoint union of matched pairs. Hence the chartwise compatible spaces of face vectors are covered by the spaces of vectors constant on each edge of M, as M ranges over perfect matchings. This claim concerns simultaneous existence of a chart for a pair of vectors; it does not require one primal chart matching all primal edges outside M.

For a dual edge e=uv write a_e for the physical cut indexed by the two face indicators e_u+e_v. Then L is generated by the a_e for which D−{u,v} has a perfect matching. The paired space W is generated by a_e wedge a_f for every disjoint pair e=uv, f=xy for which D−{u,v,x,y} has a perfect matching. One direction follows by expanding the face vectors in the pair-indicator basis of a shared M. The other follows by extending the listed edges to a perfect matching. All witnesses are original indexed cuts; any successful alternating evaluation is witnessed directly by a listed pair. Use the ordinary polynomial matching algorithm, one test per edge or disjoint edge pair. No chart search and no exponential list of matchings is required. Parallel dual links need one representative for matching; preserve their distinct original indices in the physical cut extraction. Loops make no matching contributions.

This recognition class is different from routing-complete duals. On the cycle of length eight every vertex has the same charge, and the two alternating perfect matchings yield two four-vertex-pair partitions. Each of their pair-indicator spaces modulo constants has dimension three, so W has dimension at most six, whereas L has dimension six and the exterior dimension is fifteen. The single charge moment alone cannot capture its annihilator. The perfect-matching algorithm nevertheless computes W. The result concerns private plane graphic hosts, not general or cographic charge partition.
