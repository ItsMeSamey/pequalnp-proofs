# Alternative verification of universal physical routes

For a virtual edge with endpoint equation c_u+c_v=b and P alternative physical edge subsets, uncovered endpoint-compatible colors exist precisely when each alternative contains at least one inactive physical edge. Enumerate one physical edge and one of its two tag bits from every alternative. Solve the endpoint equations together with the P equations specifying the selected bits disagree. Any feasible system supplies an explicit bad coloring. Every bad coloring is found, since an inactive edge disagrees on at least one bit. The number of systems is the product over alternatives R of 2|R|, including product 1 for no alternatives. An empty alternative has no way to fail. The incidence-boundary test for every proposed alternative remains necessary. This deterministic test is polynomial for a fixed number of alternatives even when the union uses an unbounded number of internal vertices. Compare its charged cost to the 4^s direct check and take the smaller. Indexed paths need not be enumerated anew.
