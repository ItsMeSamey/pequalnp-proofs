# Determinant degree for a fixed physical section

Fix global chart coordinates, indexed heights and a supported Boolean selector for each of r independent constant private directions. Let f_i be the resulting two-coordinate height ANFs. Form generators f_i together with the two constant height vectors. Let D be the largest degree of any nonzero Boolean determinant of two generators. The two constant vectors have determinant one. For sparse ANF length at most L per coordinate, direct Boolean multiplication and parity cancellation take O((r+2)^2 L² poly(s,d)) operations including mask manipulation, in addition to construction of the sections.

If t private dimensions are missing, the exact-plane proof fixes t complementary physical sites and two residual sites on a hidden affine slice of codimension at most 2(t+2). On this slice each height residual is a linear combination of the f_i and a constant vector. Its scalar coefficients are the original private-key coordinates in the selected private basis, independent of the chart. Bilinearity bounds the residual determinant by D, and affine restriction cannot increase degree. The fixed seed family therefore needs only odd sums through 2 floor(D/2)+1. For odd support size i the affine base cancels; for even i it contributes one further summand. Witnesses are recovered on chart-active indexed physical columns as usual.

This computation is invariant under fixed invertible height-coordinate changes and includes cancellations between the two product terms. Separate coordinate bounds j1,j2 on the same lifts imply D≤j1+j2. No simultaneous optimization over different selector representations is claimed, and the height values are part of the input.
