# Simple, cosimple obstruction to fixed conflict depth

For any depth q join four graphs at two common poles a,b only. Each gadget is a clique on its poles and 2q+12 private vertices with edge ab omitted. Use graphic incidence private columns. All tag and height values in a gadget are coboundaries of vertex potentials zero except at b. The four values at b are (0,0),(0,h1),(t,0),(t,h2), where t is nonzero and heights independent.

An internal simple cycle is compatible and neutral. A crossing simple cycle uses an a–b path in two gadgets and is compatible precisely if their tags agree. In one chart all active a–b paths give the same chart value on a+b, so all active simple cycles, and thus all active relations, have heights in one line. Both lines are separately attainable. The target determinant vanishes on W but not on the exterior square of L. There are no loops, parallel edges, bridges or two-edge cuts: every nontrivial cut splits some gadget, and each gadget's smallest cut has at least 2q+12 edges.

After ≤q deletions every unbalanced circuit C still has ≥4 edges, since each crossing path has length at least two. For each of its edges xy choose a private vertex w of that gadget avoiding endpoints, neighbors of x and y on C, and endpoints of any deleted edge incident to x or y. Triangle xyw is a neutral realizable relation whose restriction to C is exactly xy. The site signatures of C on the exact surviving first space are independent.

To delete an edge e permanently choose a fixed surviving two-edge detour in its gadget. Substitute that detour for e in all indexed words linearly. Any compatible simple cycle containing e can be realized by a chart on all edges of its one or two gadgets: assign gadget potential values agreeing at a, and at b when two gadgets are used. Hence its rerouted word is compatible even if some detour edges cancel. Actual compatible simple cycles generate L, so the substitution maps the old L into the new L while fixing every e-zero vector. This proves exact semantic closure is just the induced coordinate hyperplane.

The hierarchy-barrier.md induction therefore forces the adaptive depth-q upper bound to equal the full exterior square, even though the true W is smaller. This family survives literal series/parallel simplification and remains two-terminal decomposable. It asserts no obstruction to general higher-connectivity preprocessing or unrestricted algorithms.
