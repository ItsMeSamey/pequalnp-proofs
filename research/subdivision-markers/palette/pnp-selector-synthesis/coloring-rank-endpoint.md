# Exact optimized parameter on the two-path coloring obstruction

In the two-path reduction the dense-disabled space identifies both copies of each vertex and leaves all its two-bit colors free. The sparse link for graph edge ij therefore contributes the restricted first read λ_ij(c_i+c_j), for a selectable nonzero binary functional λ_ij. Any spanning forest of the input graph gives independent read forms for every orientation choice: a leaf potential appears in exactly one forest read and has nonzero coefficient, and peeling leaves settles all coefficients. The lower bound is |V|−number of components. A common functional on every edge attains it by the rank of the ordinary incidence matrix. Thus q* equals that number exactly on these controls, including isolated vertices and disconnected graphs.

On the non-four-colorable linear-degree families, the explicit upper bound on constructive route degree from the general optimized algorithm is consistent with the independent polynomial-calculus lower bound: with internal fully tagged paths the initial route degree is at most two and q* grows linearly. This calculation does not imply exponential running-time lower bounds.
