# Enumerating one small neutral part on an unrestricted cographic host

Every connected neutral spanning three-partition A,B,C has the same indexed wedge for any two distinct part cuts. Choose its smallest part by vertex count. Enumerate all nonempty subsets A of size at most floor(n/3), keeping only connected A with zero charge such that every component of D−A has zero charge. For such A use its indicator as the first vertex potential and take the second potential y identically zero on A. On each component of D−A it may be any individually feasible one-bit potential: every component of its monochrome subgraph must have zero charge. Absolute single-potential spaces in distinct components combine by direct sum, with root-gauge copies and component constants. Compute actual basis generators as in five-terminal-plane.md by its polynomial scalar first-space subroutine; extend each by zero to the other components. Then δA∧δy is an actual joint witness. Conversely in the chosen spanning three-partition, the indicator of B is among the jointly realized y, and its indexed wedge lies in this generated span. Thus the procedure returns exactly W with actual original pairs. It requires at most Σ_{j≤n/3}binom(n,j)poly(n,M) time, below 1.89^n times polynomial. Counts are in graph vertices; the independent parameter 2^dim L remains advantageous on other inputs.

Alternatively minimize the number of nonzero-charge vertices in A. It has at most floor(t/3) of the graph's t charged vertices. For any actual partition where A contains charges, shrink A to an inclusion-minimal connected vertex subset A' containing its charged vertices, keeping C unchanged. The difference δA∧δC+δA'∧δC equals a sum of δ{z}∧δC for neutral z removed from A. These pairs are jointly feasible because the disjoint old B, old C and A' are three connected neutral anchors covering all charges, leaving z free. The same anchors ensure δA'∧δC jointly feasible. Original complement components of A' are neutral since each contains whole B or C or neither. If A has no charges, all its terms already belong to W0 by the same argument using just B,C. Consequently W modulo the neutral-singleton W0 is generated by the one-small-part routine restricted to inclusion-minimal connected neutral sets with at most floor(t/3) terminals. When t≤8 these are only induced paths joining two equal-charge vertices (discard paths containing further charges). With polynomial-delay path enumeration the running time is polynomial in the number of explored paths plus input size; no absolute polynomial bound on their number. The result does not identify induced paths only from their endpoint names.
