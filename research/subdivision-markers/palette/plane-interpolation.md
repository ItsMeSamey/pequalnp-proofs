# Thirty-five physical binary quotients recover the plane polynomial

Let A have physical columns spanning a binary space V of dimension r and four independent names spanning U. Fix a target plane H in U. Let F_H be the multilinear polynomial with integer coefficient one on every (r−2)-set S of independent physical columns with span(S)∩U=H. Existence is equivalent to this full-sized test even for initial partial witnesses: extend an initial witness span greedily by physical columns independent modulo its sum with U until the projection spans V/U. This preserves its intersection with U. Take a basis of the resulting selected span.

For each of the 35 planes Q in U use the ordinary binary basis polynomial B_Q for the original column indices projected into V/Q. A monomial S in this homogeneous degree r−2 family can be nonzero only when S is independent in V and span(S)+U=V. Otherwise S either already has smaller rank or its image together with U still fails to span V. On the remaining sets let X=span(S)∩U, a plane. Then B_Q has coefficient 1 exactly when X∩Q=0.

Define K on the planes by K_XQ=1 if X∩Q=0 and zero otherwise. The row sum is 16 since each complement is the graph of a binary two by two matrix. For distinct transverse X,Y their common complements correspond to invertible two by two matrices, six in number. If they meet in a line, choose coordinates X=span(a,b), Y=span(a,c) and complement span(c,d) to X. Of its sixteen linear maps to X, precisely eight yield a graph disjoint from Y: the b coordinate of the image of c must be one, while the other three entries are free. Thus K²=8I−2K+8J and KJ=16J. Writing J for the all-ones matrix, K(2K+4I−J)=16I gives the coefficientwise integer identity

    16 F_H = 2 sum_{Q transverse to H} B_Q + 4 B_H − sum_Q B_Q.

Every quotient is an actual binary linear representation; intermediate nonsimple bivectors play no role. The denominator 16 is optimal when expressing F_H as a rational linear combination of these basis polynomials universally: all 35 plane rows occur in the physical instance V=U with all nonzero points as physical columns; K is invertible and its inverse above has odd numerators in every position. More generally, a homogeneous linear combination universally computing 2^k times the target modulo 2^{k+1} requires k≥4: multiply the desired congruence by the integer inverse numerator 2K+4I−J; its H-column is odd everywhere while 16 times the coefficient vector is divisible by 2^{k+1} if k<4.

If evaluating binary basis polynomials over (Z/32)[z]/f is available, with f monic of degree m lifting the defining binary field polynomial, evaluate these 35 at any weights lifted from F_(2^m). Divide each resulting coefficient of their combined value by 16 and reduce modulo two to obtain the field value of F_H. Arithmetic and query descriptions have polynomial bit length in n,r,m. An evaluator only for scalar integer weights modulo 32 gives scalar parity at binary weights; it does not establish the extension-weighted oracle. Given the extension oracle, sample independent weights in a field of size at least 2(r+1) for success probability at least one half on YES. From a nonzero evaluation scan sites and set a weight to zero whenever the value remains nonzero. In the other case every surviving witness giving a nonzero contribution requires the physical site in the following sense: one may instead formally take its coefficient (contract the site in the ordinary polynomial after conditioning). To avoid cancellation invalidating earlier indispensable sites after later deletions, maintain the restricted polynomial with all earlier compulsory variables extracted. Each required coefficient is the evaluated difference of weight one and weight zero, but naive repeated differences would cost exponentially many calls. Implement all restrictions within each quotient by ordinary contractions of compulsory elements and by setting rejected elements to weight zero while retaining their columns; for a term whose compulsory set is dependent return zero. If one physically deletes rejected columns instead, also return zero whenever the residual rank falls below r−2 minus the number of compulsory elements. Multiaffinity and the integrality of the formula preserve the same 35-term identity on conditioned polynomials. At the end the recorded subset is a physical solution. The field trials give one-sided error with polynomially many oracle calls. A deterministic universal algorithm or a fixed-precision basis evaluator is not supplied.
