# When a blend of integer Gram determinants computes binary basis weights modulo eight

Let A have full binary row rank. Allow any integer lifts A_j of its fixed entries modulo two, with arbitrary fixed integer scalars c_j. Their Gram determinants, with one commuting weight per indexed physical column, sum to the binary basis polynomial mod eight coefficientwise if and only if the represented binary matroid is regular.

On every basis, every squared integer minor is one mod eight, so the coefficients c_j sum to one mod eight. Write A_j=A_0+2B_j for the zero-one lift A_0. On a corank-one nonbasis S each squared determinant divided by four equals its unsquared determinant divided by two mod two. Multilinearity makes the latter equal to det(A_0[S])/2 plus the cofactor pairing with B_j[S], modulo two. The weighted sum is therefore the same as the single effective lift A_0+2 sum_j c_j B_j. Higher-corank determinants are multiples of four in every lift. Thus the desired equality holds precisely if some lift modulo four has unit maximal minors on binary bases and zero maximal minors otherwise.

This lifting property persists under integer row transformations invertible mod four and unit column scalings. Normalize at a binary basis to obtain [I|D] over Z/4. Deleting a noncoloop and contracting a nonloop preserve the property, by choosing a basis avoiding or containing the element and using complementary maximal minors after pivot. Coloop deletion equals contraction, and loop contraction equals deletion as matroid operations. The representation [-D^T|I] on the same sites gives the dual via complementary minors up to sign.

F7 cannot so lift. Normalize three unit columns. Coordinate-entry vanishing modulo four whenever the binary entry is zero follows directly by completing by two unit columns. By scaling unit rows and renormalizing the corresponding unit columns, and scaling nonbasis columns, put the three pair columns into forms (1,1,0), (1,0,1), (0,1,t), all displayed nonzero entries units. Their dependent triple imposes t=-1. The all-ones column lifts to (x,y,z), all units. Its dependent triples with a pair column and the complementary unit column impose x=y, x=z, and z=t y. Thus x=-x, impossible for an odd residue modulo four. Duality excludes F7* as well. Tutte's excluded-minor characterization now gives regularity. Conversely take a totally unimodular representation of a regular matroid. Its reduction modulo two differs from A by an invertible row change, because after normalization at a common basis each binary entry is uniquely prescribed by basis exchange. Lift that binary row change to a unimodular integer transformation by elementary row additions and swaps. The resulting congruent lift already has squared maximal minors zero and one over the integers.

This obstruction permits arbitrarily many lifts and arbitrary fixed scalar coefficients, including cancellations; it concerns the full symbolic binary basis polynomial on the same chart. It imposes no limitation on general determinant circuits, transformations introducing auxiliary rows, or a numerical evaluation at prescribed weights.
