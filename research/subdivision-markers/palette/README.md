# A sharp six-column replacement for binary interfaces of dimension three

Let W be binary three-space contained in the span of ordinary columns A. The spectrum consists of W intersect span(F) for every subset F of A. Exactly 148 labelled spectra occur. Each admits one replacement with at most six ordinary columns, allowing private coordinates independent of the exterior. Six columns are necessary for 35 spectra.

Every attainable subspace J lies in a full attainable coordinate apartment. Take a basis I of a realizing subset, choose a projected basis I0 of I modulo W, and extend I0 to a projected basis B of all ordinary columns. Subtracting the unique projected B expansion of each column leaves a residue in W. The residues of I outside I0 form a basis of J and are unchanged by the extension to B. All residues span W, so extend these to a basis of W using residues. B plus the columns for any subset of those three basis residues realizes exactly that coordinate subspace. Each spectrum is therefore a union of apartments of the 28 unordered binary bases.

If a line h and a plane H not containing h are both absent, there are at most ten states. Append as named T the three points outside H other than h. They form a basis and sum to h. A circuit containing T is equivalent to an exact line h: the surviving terminal triangle has precisely the all-ones dependence after contracting a suitable subset and deleting the rest. A cocircuit containing T similarly means terminal minor U(1,3), whose dependency plane is H in this basis. Since neither exists, T meets at least two matroid components. Indeed a connected component containing T reduces to a connected minor on T by choosing either deletion or contraction at each other element preserving connectivity. Its terminal triangle or triad lifts to a circuit or cocircuit through T. All attainable traces split by these components in the T basis. There are at most two subspaces on one coordinate and five on two coordinates, giving the bound ten. See Oxley, Matroid Theory, Proposition 4.3.6, https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~benoist/refs/Oxley.pdf .

Run python classify.py. finite.py enumerates the sixteen subspaces of W and every family which is a nonempty union of apartments (344 families). The missing nonincident pair rule excludes 196. Independently the code tests all 32768 subsets of nonzero points in binary four-space with W as the low three bits. It computes all subset spans by incremental updates and obtains 141 labelled spectra containing W. Append the named basis of W before dualizing; represent the dual by a kernel basis and normalize its named basis to the same three unit columns. Recomputing the spectra of these configurations and their duals yields all 148 remaining families, verified by equality of the literal labelled sets. single-gadgets.json stores one ordinary configuration per spectrum; the mask bits index subspaces in order of cardinality and then lexicographic sorted coordinates.

For n≤6 ordinary columns the matroid with the three additional named basis columns has rank plus dual rank n+3≤9, so either it or its dual has rank at most four. Loops and ordinary parallel duplicates do not change the subset-span spectrum. The enumeration thus covers any improvement to the returned size. The numbers with minimum size 3,4,5,6 are respectively 28,42,43,35.

For a represented binary input with named columns on the exterior, enumerate seven ordinary anchors and minimize the rank connectivity function over shores containing them and no names. This finds any terminal-free shore of connectivity at most three with at least seven sites, as in ../rooted-core/root-free-shores.md. For rank three compute its entire spectrum on the intersection W of the two ambient spans by seven primal and seven dual applications of the binary three-element circuit test, plus polynomial self-reduction for witnesses; zero and full are immediate. This uses the existing three-dimensional trace result in /workspace/pnp-research/research/span-separation/three-dimensional-traces.md. See Seymour https://doi.org/10.1016/S0195-6698(86)80044-0 and Geelen, Gerards and Whittle https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S2 .

Embed the catalogue representative's private directions freshly, sharing exactly the labelled W with the exterior span V. For each choice Q of exterior contractions, the intersection of span(Q∪F) with V equals span(Q)+(span(F) intersect W), in both representations. Thus all named coefficient kernels are preserved without any promise on the dependence of names. Store an original shore witness for each attainable trace and substitute the matching witness as a unit during reverse traceback. Each pass removes an ordinary element. At most O(n^8) polynomial submodular-minimization calls are made overall; lower-dimensional shores use their smaller representatives. Row rank may be recompressed. The final input has no such large terminal-free shore; there is no general bound on its size. This is a virtual replacement and need not be a labelled matroid minor.
