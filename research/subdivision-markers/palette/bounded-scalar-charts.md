# Exact binary-plane search by scalar hyperplane enumeration

Use the equality-spanning coordinates of quaternary-cocircuit.md: V=Q plus H, dim H=2, with physical v_e and two-bit offsets o_e. For an ordered basis λ,μ of the two binary output characters, call φ:V→GF(2) admissible if φ(H)=0 and its active indices Eφ={e:φ(v_e)=λo_e} span V on physical v_e. Every full solution induces such a φ. For each admissible φ, run the identical one-bit search on Eφ with character μ. The second search succeeds exactly when φ extends to a full witness: the jointly active v_e span V, so the two bits combine into the required equality map. Reverse extraction uses only active original indices. Try any of three first characters.

To obtain a scalar witness on any physical restriction first test full physical v-rank. Form the binary lift (v_e,λo_e) and the three additional indexed probes z=(0,1), (h1,1), (h2,1) with h1,h2 the H basis. The ambient rank is dim(V)+1. Every cocircuit through all three has a physical hyperplane complement spanning the graph of the uniquely determined φ, and conversely. The cocircuit support is the three probes together with all inactive physical indices. Thus each feasible scalar chart corresponds to one actual cocircuit, with no multiplicities from choices of basis inside its hyperplane.

To find a new chart after j charts have been seen, choose one physical index from the inactive set of each previous chart. Enumerate the at most n^j unions D of such choices and seek a circuit through the three probes after deleting D in the dual binary matroid. Every new cocircuit misses at least one physical index from each old cocircuit by incomparability of distinct circuits, and is thus returned on some branch. Every successful branch gives a genuinely new chart. If one of the inactive sets is empty the sole cocircuit consists of the probes and no second candidate exists. Deletion takes place on the dual representation; it filters existing cocircuits rather than introducing virtual primal spanning columns.

For fixed k this yields deterministic n^{k+O(1)} time and physical traceback on the recognized class with at most k admissible charts for some first character. Search up to k+1 per first character; positive second tests are valid on unrestricted inputs and exhausting a first-character family certifies the global answer. If every list passes k without a witness, the procedure may abstain. This solves a full four-name target on the promised class, independently of private rank or component count. The physical matrices are binary throughout. This enumeration bound is XP in k.
