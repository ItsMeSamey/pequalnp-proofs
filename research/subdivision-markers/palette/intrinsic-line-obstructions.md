# An intrinsic obstruction to a coupled basis

For ordinary binary columns E and a named space U, let P consist of each nonzero h for which some physical F has span(F) intersect U exactly in the line through h. For any basis B of U, a circuit of E together with B meets at least three basis names if and only if some vector of P has B-weight at least three.

For the backward implication take a minimal ordinary representation of h inside its line witness. Its columns together with the B-support of h form a circuit by the line condition and ordinary independence. Conversely take a circuit with named part D of size at least three and ordinary part F. The images of D form a minimal dependent set modulo K = span(F) intersect U. Choose P0 inside F projecting to a basis modulo U. Each remaining f gives a residual in U using f and columns of P0. Each nonzero residual has a physical exact-line witness P0 union {f}. These residuals span K. If all have B-weight at most two, the quotient by K identifies unit coordinates and kills some equivalence classes. Every minimal dependence among its unit columns has size one or two, a contradiction. The circuit sum itself need not be an exact line.

Consequently some B is coupled precisely when the abstract binary matroid on P is graphic. Weight-at-most-two vectors give graph incidence columns. Conversely, for each component of a graphic realization remove one vertex row in its incidence matrix. The resulting representation has full row rank. After normalization to the same column basis, binary columns of both representations agree since their supports are the fundamental circuits. The resulting coordinate isomorphism on span(P) extends to U.

For four names enumerate the 840 unordered bases. At a failed basis, obtain a circuit through three names using the binary triple-circuit decision algorithm and deletion self-reduction, then extract a high-weight actual line as above. If all bases fail, the collected vectors already have a nongraphic matroid. Tutte's graphic excluded-minor characterization gives a nongraphic restriction on at most nine collected vectors: over the binary field only Fano, dual Fano and the bond matroid of K3,3 fit rank at most four. An Fano minor needs at most one independent contracted vector, the other two need none. Enumerate subsets up to nine and test representability as weight-at-most-two vectors over the 840 bases. Return the nongraphic list with original physical span witnesses for every member. This procedure certifies failure to enter the coupled class; no target-plane infeasibility follows. The bound nine is sharp for this certificate: take the nine simple rank-four columns of the bond matroid of K3,3 as all ordinary columns in U. Its exact lines are precisely its nine physical types. All proper restrictions are graphic by excluded-minor minimality.

Tutte's characterization is Theorem 10.3.1 in Oxley's Matroid Theory; GGW Theorem 15.1 and the separate coupled-normalization proof apply to the positive outcome.
