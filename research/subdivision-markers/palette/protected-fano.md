# An eleven-site obstruction protecting four names

The indexed ordinary columns span a binary space V, four independent names span U, and H is the required plane. A state fails our terminal test if its ordinary columns still span the ambient space, the four names remain independent, and some ordinary quotient by a two-plane Q of U is nonregular. This is a syntactic test, independent of target feasibility.

Every failing state has a constructive minor on at most eleven actual sites preserving U injectively, ordinary spanning, and nonregularity at the same Q. In the offending quotient obtain a Fano or dual Fano minor by greedily deleting/contracting single elements preserving nonregularity until minor-minimal. Let F be the seven survivors and C the contracted indices; choose an independent subset of C with the same span modulo Q, deleting redundant sites instead. Choose a basis C0 of C modulo U and contract it in the original space. The named space injects. The remaining E=C minus C0 now lies in the named space and is independent even modulo Q by the original independence of C modulo Q.

Let L be the sum of U and the span of F and E in this representation. Choose physical sites forming a basis modulo L and contract them; their joint span meets L trivially. The remaining columns span the resulting ambient copy of L. Keep F and E, and complete their span to L with further available columns. At most 4−|E| additional columns suffice since E is independent inside U. Delete everything else. There are at most 7+|E|+(4−|E|)=11 survivors. In their quotient by Q, contracting E and restricting to F recovers the original excluded minor, because the additional contractions preserved all relations within L. All contractions outside U together were independent modulo U.

For at most k sites X, assign each to inclusion or rejection. For mandatory C and rejected D, reject a dependent C; otherwise contract C, call the resulting named spaces U', H', and let Z span the remaining physical sites. Reject if span(C) meets U outside H, if H' is not contained in Z, or if Z+U' does not span the contracted ambient. In all other branches solve the exact intersection H' with named space U'∩Z within Z. Any partial witness can be completed inside Z with independent columns modulo U': the projection of Z onto the ambient quotient by U' is surjective. Its rank together with C becomes the required r−2 since dim H'=2−dim(span(C)∩U). Use the existing three-name solver when dim(U'∩Z)≤3. Otherwise U' has dimension four, C is independent modulo U, and Z spans the ambient. At such branches use the 35 individual regular determinants if all quotients are regular, otherwise the branch fails the terminal test. A semantic NO beyond the listed criteria is not a terminal in this parameter definition.

Enumerate the at most 2^|X| assignments. If all pass the terminal test, return X. For a failing assignment extract an eleven-site protected minor. Any completing successful set must hit those sites: otherwise assign its extra sites according to the protected minor's operations. These contractions remain independent modulo U, and eventual survivors together with any pending contraction sites span the current quotient, so ordinary spanning persists. The same nonregular named quotient still contains the excluded minor. Thus the new assignment fails. Branch on each extracted survivor to depth k; total discovery time is O(22^k poly(n)). Successful leaves give physical witnesses by retaining the compulsory sites and greedy completions. Seven-way branching for plain strong regularity, without the named terminal rules, already appears in scratch/satbridge/regular-backdoor/README.md.

The direct sum of Fano and four physical axes also serving as the independent names attains eleven. Any Fano site deletion or contraction regularizes that component, and the quotient of the four axes has rank two and is regular. Deletion of a named physical axis loses spanning; contraction loses an independent name. These losses cannot be reversed in a smaller minor which keeps U injective and physically spanned (the missing axis has no other physical support). With arbitrarily many disjoint Fano summands the target-aware site set still has size one, while any set making all thirty-five quotients regular under every assignment must hit every Fano summand. These examples are also decidable by ordinary component decomposition.
