# Fourth diagonal representatives on certified physical orbits

Let V be the r-dimensional binary span of indexed ordinary columns and p=r−2. For each independent p-set S, let v_S be its exterior vector. The fourth tensor power of v_S lies in an ambient binary space of dimension binom(r,2)^4. The four appended-pair determinant tests on that same S evaluate to one linear functional on the tensor, held fixed at the original named coordinates throughout.

Given actual independent seeds and invertible linear maps permuting the indexed physical columns, start with independent seed tensors. Repeatedly apply each generator to each retained physical set, retaining its physical image set exactly when its tensor increases the span. At most binom(r,2)^4 insertions occur. Recompute images' minors directly from the indicated original columns. The stable span contains every seed orbit by linearity and invariance; conversely every stored vector is the actual diagonal tensor of a physical orbit member. Therefore any target accepted on an orbit is accepted by a retained physical set. The maps may move the names; they act only on candidate material, never on the target functional. Physical permutations must respect duplicate indices.

For global coverage a verifier checks, for every independent single-exchange neighbor of every seed, a supplied physical linear automorphism carrying some seed to that neighbor. Include those maps as generators. The union of the resulting orbits is closed under single exchanges, by pulling each neighbor back to the corresponding seed through an inverse automorphism. The graph on independent p-sets is connected by basis exchange in the rank-p truncation. Hence every independent p-set is covered. Verification and construction take polynomial time in the input and certificate size. This alone does not discover a small cover on general inputs.

The four coordinate units, with the two additional physical vectors 3 and 12 (units numbered 1,2,4,8), illustrate failure of single-wedge representatives. Complements of two coordinates in the fixed unit basis span the complete second exterior power. None of their six coordinate planes equals H=span(3,12), which the two added vectors attain. The four-test functional vanishes on all six corresponding diagonal tensors.

A universal polynomial constructor preserving arbitrary quadruples of two-column extensions would also decide fixed-four binary common cocircuits. Remove the four prescribed elements from selectable material, append one independent coloop z, and, for each of the four named elements u_i, append the test pair z,u_i to an (r−1)-set of ordinary indices in the enlarged (r+1)-space. All tests pass exactly for an old physical hyperplane basis avoiding the four names. Handle clean rank r−1 by testing its sole hyperplane and reject lower rank. This uses a possible five-dimensional span of probe pairs; it does not claim the same consequence for representatives tailored just to the four-root plane template.
