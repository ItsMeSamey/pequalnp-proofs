### Cographic signed recognizer

For private M*(G), circuits are bonds. Work one connected component at a time with unit capacities on indexed edges. Let T be the vertices of odd degree in the subgraph of edges with λ-tag one. Then λ(δ(S))=|S∩T| mod 2. In a Gomory–Hu tree with edge-induced minimum-cut shores mark each tree edge whose fundamental shore contains an odd number of vertices of T. These marked edges have odd-degree set T, by summing subtree parities upward. Every T-odd cut crosses a marked tree edge, so its capacity is at least the minimum marked weight. A marked edge’s fundamental cut attains its weight and is odd. A cut decomposes into disjoint bonds, at least one odd if the cut is odd. Thus the minimum marked weight equals the shortest odd bond length; no marked edge means infinity. This global step uses the classical odd-cut algorithm. [Gomory and Hu](https://doi.org/10.1137/0109047) · [Padberg and Rao](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.7.1.67)

For a safe local support estimate, an odd short bond containing physical e=uv must separate u,v and the endpoints x,y of some marked tree edge. For each such pair minimize cut capacity over the two orientations with u fixed. Identify same-side endpoints, skip inconsistent identifications, and run ordinary s–t mincut. Mark e if any such minimum is ≤k+2. This includes every eligible site but need not ensure oddness for the selected minimum cut. The rank of marked sites in the original private representation safely bounds rank(P_λ). Polynomially many max flows suffice. Graph loops never enter bonds, and indexed parallel edges retain their capacities. Apply the mixed-tree transfer only after testing the earlier rank inequality.

### Regular signed augmentations and general parity relaxations

For the same λ form the binary matrix K_λ whose physical columns are (k_e,λ(b_e)) and append the marked column p=(0,1). A λ-odd private circuit C gives a circuit C∪{p}, since the only dependence on the original minimal support is the full circuit relation. Conversely removing p from a circuit containing p gives a λ-odd circuit of K; any proper projected dependence, even or odd, would contradict circuit minimality in the augmentation.

If K_λ with p is regular, obtain a site-aligned TU representation A. Minimize Σ|y_j| subject to Ay=0 and y_p=1. Eliminate the fixed coordinate, keep its cost one, and split remaining signed variables into nonnegative parts; [A',−A'] is TU with integral right-hand side. A binary circuit through p lifts to a signed TU circuit with coefficients ±1. Conversely an integral optimum reduces modulo two to a cycle containing p; one of its disjoint constituent circuits contains p. Hence optimum minus one equals the minimum λ-odd circuit size (infeasible means infinity). To test possible membership of a physical e also fix y_e to ±1, taking the better of the two programs. This objective minus one lower-bounds the shortest actual λ-odd circuit containing e; e may lie in another constituent, so only the exclusion direction is valid. Use unit positive costs. This is the one-marked-column specialization of existing Walsh regular decoding, with the two-pin relaxation used for safe support marking and the earlier rank test. [Walsh regular decoding](eth-layout-obstruction/walsh-regular-decoding.md)

There is a polynomial sufficient test for every explicitly represented binary K. Relax the binary syndrome equations Kx=0 and Σλ(b_e)x_e=1 by the standard intersection of single-check parity polytopes with shared site marginals. One explicit formulation sends one unit of nonnegative flow through each check’s indexed variable list on two parity states, starting at zero and ending at the required parity. Enforce at each position that the flow using the bit-one transition equals its shared coordinate x_e, with 0≤x_e≤1. Any integral codeword induces such flows. This gives O(total check lengths plus number of sites) variables and constraints. Additional valid parity checks may tighten it. [Feldman, Wainwright and Karger](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/~andrew/ee512/projects/LinearProgrammingDecoding.pdf)

For each i impose x_i=1 and minimize Σx_e. Mark i if the optimum is at most k+2; infeasibility excludes i. The indicator of every eligible short odd circuit containing i is feasible, so this relaxation safely overmarks P_λ even when the LP solutions are fractional or decompose into different circuits. Rank the actual private columns of the marked set and apply the earlier mixed-tree transfer. The optimum without a site pin may already exclude every short odd circuit. Neither global exact LP decoding nor guaranteed recognition on arbitrary inputs is claimed.

For a strict nonregular example, take the direct sum of a long L-site circuit [I|1] with an F₇ block. Put λ-tag one only on the last long-circuit site. Its syndrome check forces x_L=1; each private long-circuit check forces x_j=x_L even in the relaxation. Every feasible point thus costs at least L, so the global screen certifies the necessary bound when L>k+2. The Fano restriction ensures both the private and signed augmented representations are nonregular. Other three named coordinates can be supplied by linearly independent charges on the Fano cycle space, of dimension four, giving full physical named spanning. This illustrates the scope of the LP screen and has an evident direct-sum decomposition.
