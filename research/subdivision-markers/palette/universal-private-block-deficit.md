## Universally spanning private subspaces

For fixed p≥1 enumerate subspaces J generated by at most p independent observed private directions. Call J good if for each a:J→B the projections of actual columns with k∈J and b=a(k) span J. Test every one of the ≤4^p local charts by Gaussian elimination. Every good J is generated by physical directions, so this enumeration is complete at the stated dimension. Let D_p be the sum of all good subspaces and t_p=d−dim D_p. For p=1 this is the fully tagged direction span.

Choose an independent global basis of D_p made from directions assigned to good subspaces. For each chosen u_j in an owner J, every local chart admits a deterministic physical combination summing privately to u_j. Precompute one indexed combination by elimination per local chart. Its H-sum h_j is a lookup on ≤2p bits, hence has Boolean degree ≤2p, preserved under linear substitution of ambient chart coordinates. The lift (u_j,g(u_j),h_j(g|J)) is physically attainable within every global chart. Owners can overlap: one selected lift per independent u_j suffices. Differences between lifts on other points are allowed.

On YES extend its partial chart to the sum of its private witness domain and D_p, include the selected physical lifts, and extend using t_p actual independent complement anchors as in the original bounded-deficit proof. At the resulting chart the virtual and real anchors define a linear section σ of the private projection inside the active physical span. For every active column e, the difference e−σ(k_e) is a physically attainable vertical H residue. All such residues span the full vertical intersection, since linear σ cancels in any combination with private sum zero. Thus two indexed active columns give independent residues. On the slice pinning those two columns and the complement anchor tags, determinant degree is ≤4p and codimension ≤2(t_p+2). Apply the finite-field affine seed family and odd sums of at most 4p+1 seeds. Gaussian-test the actual active span and return a physical basis of size ≤d+2; virtual witnesses need not be returned as individual physical sites.

The algorithm costs 2^{O(p(t_p+1))}n^{O(p)} times polynomial input-length overhead. This is FPT in t_p for fixed p; no simultaneous FPT in p is claimed. Full-rank named spanning, indexed duplicates and pure H sites retain the same semantics as for the original FPT proof.

For strictness set K=F₂⁴, q(k)=k₀k₁+k₂k₃. At each nonzero k supply every tag except (q(k),0). There are no fully tagged directions. If some chart did not span K, all points of a hyperplane coset ℓ(k)=1 would be inactive, forcing its linear first tag g₁ to equal q there. Its three-dimensional tangent space cannot be totally isotropic for the nondegenerate polar form of q (maximal isotropic dimension two). Thus q is not affine on the coset. All 256 charts span K; active-direction counts 6,10,12,14 occur 10,96,120,30 times. Direct sums give unbounded original deficit and t₄=0. Two physical pure-H directions can be appended to ensure physical named spanning.

Rank four is minimal for a nonzero universal block with no fully tagged directions. For dimension three enlarge any unfilled tags to three choices per nonzero point, leaving a missing function f. The sum of f on ℓ(k)=1 is a linear map from ℓ∈K* to two bits, so vanishes for some nonzero ℓ. On that four-point affine plane sum zero implies f is affine and it extends to a linear global chart, inactive on the whole coset. In dimensions one and two each opposite-hyperplane coset has at most two independent points and arbitrary missing tags interpolate. This lower-dimension argument and an explicit smaller inclusion-minimal restriction are from resolution-map positive-controls. No global minimum size outside the stated family is claimed.
