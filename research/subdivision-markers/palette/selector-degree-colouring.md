# Universal active-span recognition and selector degree

A chart g:K→F₂² has 2 dim K unconstrained Boolean coordinates. A selector for w∈K is a tuple α_i(g) supported on its respective site activity conditions g(k_i)=b_i, satisfying Σ_i α_i(g)k_i=w identically. In degree ≤s represent each α_i by squarefree Boolean monomials of degree ≤s. Set every coefficient of α_i(g)(g(k_i)_a+b_ia) equal to zero for each of the two bits a after reducing x²=x. Set all coefficients of Σ_i α_i(g)k_i−w to zero as well. These are linear equations jointly in the coefficients and w; projecting their solution space gives exactly the degree-s selector space D_s by uniqueness of Boolean algebraic normal forms. This recognizes every selector in the specified representation. Pointwise lifts for an independent basis of D_s yield a linear right inverse to the active private map on that space, regardless of support overlaps. After complement pivots and two sites are activated on an affine slice of codimension ≤2(c+2), c=dim K−dim D_s, their indexed residual vectors have degree ≤s. An arbitrary alternating form applied to the pair has degree ≤2s.

Let a finite simple graph G have one private basis vector v_i per vertex and one extra private direction u. At each v_i offer all four tags, at u the three nonzero tags, and at u+v_i+v_j just tag zero for each edge ij. There are 4|V|+3+|E| sites each of private weight at most three. The active private span always contains D=span(v_i). When g(u) is nonzero, an actual u site is active. When g(u)=0, a site outside D is active precisely for a monochromatic edge under the four-colouring i↦g(v_i). Thus all charts span K iff the graph has no proper four-colouring. This gives coNP-completeness of universal spanning even with full-tag deficit one. A deficient chart and a Gaussian rank computation certify a negative answer.

Suppose w outside D has a degree-s selector. Set g(u)=0 and write x_i,y_i for g(v_i). Taking the u-coordinate forces Σ_e α_e=1, and α_e is supported on the event δ_ij=(1+x_i+x_j)(1+y_i+y_j)=1. Thus 1+Σ α_e δ_e multilinearizes to zero. Ordinary Boolean axioms derive that identity in degree ≤s+2: replace powers by squarefree monomials, with each reduction a monomial multiple of a Boolean axiom.

Use the standard unary four-colour polynomial equations over F₂: Boolean indicators X_ic sum to one at each vertex, distinct indicators at one vertex have zero product, and X_ic X_jc=0 for edges ij. Replace each coordinate bit by the linear sum of the indicators of colours having that bit. The substituted Boolean axioms are sums of unary Boolean axioms by characteristic two. For each colour c, the product φ_c of the two agreement indicators at one vertex equals X_ic by a degree-two derivation from vertex axioms. Each agreement factor is the sum of corresponding indicators, distinct-colour products vanish, and squares reduce. The formal equality δ_ij=Σ_c φ_c(i)φ_c(j) follows from (1+x)(1+x')+xx'=1+x+x'. Derive δ_ij=0 in degree ≤4 from these local equalities and edge axioms. Multiplying this derived degree-two equation by monomials of α_e costs degree ≤s+2, yielding a unary polynomial calculus refutation of degree ≤max(4,s+2).

Lauria and Nordström construct, for every fixed k≥3, bounded-degree graphs on O(k⁴n) vertices with no k-colouring and canonical unary polynomial-calculus degree Ω(n), over arbitrary fields. See [CCC 2017, Theorem 3.6](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol079-ccc2017/LIPIcs.CCC.2017.2/LIPIcs.CCC.2017.2.pdf) or [arXiv version, Theorem 3.7 and equations 2.2a–c](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.00125). With k=4 these graphs therefore have full semantic active span, but any selector for any vector outside D requires degree Ω(|V|). Conversely any lookup on all charts has degree ≤2 dim K. Thus the worst minimum degree for reaching full semantic span on these sparse examples is Θ(dim K).

For fixed s≥2 and a sufficiently large graph in this family, D_s=D, since every v_i has the usual degree-two matching-tag lookup. Disjoint direct sums of t such blocks share one tag alphabet and have independent private spaces. A selector projected to one block, with all outside chart variables fixed, is a local selector of no larger degree. Conversely combine local selectors. Consequently the direct sum has semantic deficit zero and both algebraic degree-s deficit and full-tag deficit t. Neither the universal-span reduction nor the degree lower bound establishes hardness of existential exact-plane decision.
