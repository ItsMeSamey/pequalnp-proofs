# Three-name exact-line audit

The input is an explicit indexed binary matrix of ordinary columns and an embedded rank-three binary subspace T with a nonzero prescribed direction u. Zero, parallel, and repeated ordinary columns are allowed. Extend u to a basis u,a,b and append fresh labels for a,b,u+a+b. A circuit containing all three exists precisely when some ordinary span meets T in the line of u. Given such a circuit, its ordinary part is the witness. Conversely, in a witnessing ordinary span choose a basis, express u in it, and use exactly the nonzero coefficients together with the three terminals. Every proper terminal subset is excluded by the trace or by independence. Deletion self-reduction needs at most N additional decisions and preserves original labels.

The primary algorithm is Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, section 2, paragraphs following Theorem 2.1, https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S2. Its binary matrix representation, independent roots, minimum-order vertical separations, bounded interface, and graphic recognition are material. Rank uses elimination. For k=0,1,2 enumerate independent k+1 column anchors on the other side, fix all six padded root positions on the root side, and minimize rank connectivity over the free positions by deterministic submodular minimization. Any qualifying minimum-order separation can be oriented this way since the six distinct root points cannot lie in two proper subspaces of their rank-three span. Replacing the opposite side as in the cited algorithm decreases rank. Deterministic graphic recognition suffices at termination. No seed or advice enters.

For L the physical matrix is (k_i,b_i,s_i) with private binary keys, exactly two binary tag coordinates, and one scalar s_i. Columns are indexed even when equal. For any selected X, the trace on the last three coordinates consists of the tag/scalar images of private kernel vectors supported on X. Exact last-axis trace gives a common linear tag chart by factorization through the keys, and Gaussian solving gives a scalar-odd original kernel vector. Conversely any active odd relation gives this trace. An indexed support restriction is literal column deletion, with zeros restored in returned masks. Annihilator-basis queries recover L in O(N²) decisions plus at most O(N²) deletion decisions for successful updates.

For a forced support C first test simultaneous tag interpolation and reject any inconsistency. Its trace can now only be zero or the requested line. A line is immediate success. When the trace is zero quotient by the full augmented span of C, carrying all three names by the quotient map; that map is injective on the named space. Query remaining ordinary columns and lift by adjoining C. Pullback of the trace gives the required trace on C union the returned sites. Recover the original second indexed relation by elimination on this union. For the determinant scalar this proves independent physical heights. For W use any linear extension of the alternating functional in its second slot to the indexed ambient, and evaluate physical feasibility on the union. Both witnesses share one chart. The recursion's first-space oracle on a restricted support has exactly the unpinned contract. Enumeration and canonical state counts remain separately charged.
