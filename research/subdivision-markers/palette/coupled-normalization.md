# Constructive graphic reduction for a coupled binary named basis

A named independent set X is coupled if no circuit of the ordinary matrix augmented by X contains at least three members of X. Use Seymour's triple circuit test on every triple, leaving other names in the tested matrix. At fixed named rank one may enumerate the bases of the named span and try them all, translating the target coefficient kernel under each basis change. The following algorithm needs a coupled basis but does not need a supplied graphic representation or patchwork.

**Normalize.** Apply the full-spectrum replacements of README.md whenever a smaller representative exists on a terminal-free shore of connectivity at most three. Enumerate seven anchors with constrained submodular minimization for shores of size at least seven, and explicitly enumerate all subsets with at most six elements for the remaining strict improvements. For a small candidate compute its actual boundary spectrum and use its *minimum* size from the catalogue. In ranks zero and one the minima are zero and one; at rank two the minimum equals the number of attainable lines, either two or three. Every step reduces the number of ordinary columns. The process preserves the full labelled named spectrum and traceback. It also preserves coupling: contracting the ordinary elements of any circuit produces its named intersection as a circuit on the labelled terminal minor after deleting the other ordinary elements. Conversely such a circuit in a labelled terminal minor lifts to an original circuit through those named elements. The kernel gives all circuits of that terminal minor. Testing only lines in the full named space would not suffice for this implication.

**What strict minimality implies.** Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, Sections 8–9 and Theorem 15.1, provide an independent V containing X in the binary ambient for which each component A of the ordinary representation modulo span V is a patch with at most three attachment coordinates Z in V. Put L=span A intersect span V, contained in span Z. Every attainable exact line on this interface is a point in the local torso: a minimal ordinary representation of its point inside a witnessing subset is a strand. Since the rooted torso of a patch is a framework, this point uses at most two attachment coordinates. In particular when there are three attachments the line 111 is absent. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S9 and https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S15 .

The actual separator W_A=span A intersect span(other ordinary columns and X) lies in L by joint independence of the component spans modulo V. Hence each patch is size-minimal also with respect to L: any smaller material configuration realizing its full L-spectrum gives the same W_A-spectrum by intersection with W_A, and is a competitor for the actual normalization. In particular all material bridges have at most six elements.

**Every minimum patch is itself a set of graph edges in compatible coordinates.** For dim L three, Z is a basis of L. The finite verifier minimal-graphic.py tests *every* minimum material representation with the exact line 111 absent, rather than only one chosen member of each spectrum. Enumerate all simple ordinary subsets in binary rank at most four in the canonical terminal basis, testing both primal and labelled dual. The rank sum including the three terminals is n+3≤9; min size is invariant under duality, and loops/ordinary parallel duplicates could be deleted in a minimizer. This covers every possibility. For each actual configuration rebase the ordinary span fixing the first three unit vectors and extend by independent ordinary columns. Enumerate possible injective images for the additional basis vectors among the binary vectors of weight at most two, rejecting whenever any material column fails the same weight test. All 806 oriented configurations checked admit such a map. Thus these are literal edge representations of the actual material, fixing the common four-pin coordinates.

At rank one there is just a single attainable boundary edge; rank zero is empty. A two-column minimum at rank two consists of independent boundary edges. A three-column minimum at rank two has all three lines attainable. If the ordinary rank is two these are three boundary edges. If the rank is three, all three independent columns must be outside L and in the same nonzero coset modulo L: fewer outside columns give at most two attainable lines. Their pair sums are all three boundary edges, forming a triangle since their binary sum is zero. Map the three actual columns to a star from a fresh vertex to the three triangle corners, fixing their differences and hence L. This handles L a proper subspace of span Z as well.

Identify V with unit graph-vertex vectors relative to a single hub. Each local embedding extends by the identity on V, using disjoint fresh vertices. Their union is an injective linear representation: each map is injective on the patch private space modulo V and the private spaces on both sides form direct sums modulo V. Consequently the *actual reduced matroid with named X included* is graphic. This conclusion does not depend on finding V or on locating its bridges.

Reconstruct any graph for the reduced binary graphic matrix using polynomial graphic recognition; it need not exhibit the common star. The physical named edges touch at most 2|X| vertices. Enumerate their component partitions and use fixed-terminal disjoint connecting trees as in ../rooted-core/graphic-supported-trace.md to obtain all labelled named kernels, then backtrack to physical choices in the original input. At fixed named count, time is polynomial: O(n^8) polynomial submodular minimizations, polynomially many binary triple-circuit and rank computations, and fixed-terminal graph linkage. The structural graphic reduction itself allows arbitrary independent name count. A failed coupled-basis search is inconclusive for unrestricted target feasibility.
