### Cographic signed recognizer

For private M*(G), circuits are bonds. Work one connected component at a time with unit capacities on indexed edges. Let T be the vertices of odd degree in the subgraph of edges with λ-tag one. Then λ(δ(S))=|S∩T| mod 2. In a Gomory–Hu tree with edge-induced minimum-cut shores mark each tree edge whose fundamental shore contains an odd number of vertices of T. These marked edges have odd-degree set T, by summing subtree parities upward. Every T-odd cut crosses a marked tree edge, so its capacity is at least the minimum marked weight. A marked edge’s fundamental cut attains its weight and is odd. A cut decomposes into disjoint bonds, at least one odd if the cut is odd. Thus the minimum marked weight equals the shortest odd bond length; no marked edge means infinity. This global step uses the classical odd-cut algorithm. [Gomory and Hu](https://doi.org/10.1137/0109047) · [Padberg and Rao](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.7.1.67)

For a safe local support estimate, an odd short bond containing physical e=uv must separate u,v and the endpoints x,y of some marked tree edge. For each such pair minimize cut capacity over the two orientations with u fixed. Identify same-side endpoints, skip inconsistent identifications, and run ordinary s–t mincut. Mark e if any such minimum is ≤k+2. This includes every eligible site but need not ensure oddness for the selected minimum cut. The rank of marked sites in the original private representation safely bounds rank(P_λ). Polynomially many max flows suffice. Graph loops never enter bonds, and indexed parallel edges retain their capacities. Apply the mixed-tree transfer only after testing the earlier rank inequality.
