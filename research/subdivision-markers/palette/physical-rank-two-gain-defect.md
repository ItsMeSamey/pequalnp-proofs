## Physical rank-two seams and the gain defect

For local gain-labelled columns let L be their joint lifted span in J⊕G (G=F₂²), J their physical span, W=J∩X. Gaussian elimination computes δ=dim L−dim J, the dimension of the vertical subspace of L. The projection of B₀=L∩(W⊕G) onto W is onto: every w∈W⊆J has some lift. The kernel is precisely the full vertical subspace of L. Hence dim B₀=dim W+δ. Every attainable trace on W⊕G lies in B₀ and can equivalently be computed there. In particular every rank-two seam with δ≤1, or rank-three seam with δ=0, has a polynomially computable exact exterior profile, using the known at-most-three-name trace solver. This condition on gains is recognizable without a coordinate guess.

For dim B₀=3 use the known rank-three six-column representative in B₀⊕P, with P its fresh private space. For a column (b,p) take physical vector (pr_W b,p) in W⊕P and label pr_G b. The combined lifted embedding is injective and meets W⊕G in exactly B₀ on its ambient space, so the represented trace catalogue agrees. Thus the representative with at most six indexed physical columns has precisely the same extension predicate in every exterior; recover original map witnesses from the old trace and independent physical complement. Keep indexed labels on coincident physical projections, including zero projections. This application does not assume that the replacement is a physical minor.

For unrestricted gains on a physical rank-two seam, the complete local problem already includes the arbitrary four-name exact-plane decision problem. Given V spanned by physical columns with four-name space U⊂V and target plane H⊂U, choose D complementary to H in U. Identify V with J⊕G for J=V/D and G=D by any linear section, using the H-section on W=U/D. Encode each original column by its physical projection and its two gain coordinates. X=W is spanned by two pin vectors with fixed map zero and has no exterior physical matches. A successful extension requires an attained trace T exactly equal to H in the original coordinates: T⊆H and pr T=W force equality. Conversely a witness to H extends by the previous lifted criterion. To prescribe independent nonzero values on the two pins, shear all gains by a reference linear map restricting to the desired pin values; this relabels graphs bijectively. Since the original columns span V, the lifted matrix has full vertical defect two. The construction uses polynomially many binary Gaussian operations and exactly one indexed physical/gain pair per original column. This is equivalence relative to exact-plane decision, with no SAT-hardness or derandomization inference.
