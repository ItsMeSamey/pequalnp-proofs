## Sparse exact-plane charts with a recognized additive skeleton

Choose a linear splitting V=K⊕B⊕H with U=B⊕H and dim B=dim H=2. A physical column is (k,b,h). First assume a unique indexed physical column for each nonzero bucket (k,b); check by sorting. At k=0 discard columns with b≠0 because no exact witness can include them. Take the span H₀ of all physical H columns (k,b)=(0,0). For each other k record available b and h.

A chart is a linear g:K→B. Its available directions A_g are the nonzero k with tag g(k). Any relation on their physical columns projects either outside U or into H, since the corresponding quotient points lie in the graph of g. Conversely every exact witness lies in a chart: modulo H its span has zero intersection with B, so is the graph of a partial linear map on K; extend linearly. If the relations of A_g are generated by its triangles of directions (x,y,x+y), all H residues available from that chart are spanned by H₀ and the triple defects h(x)+h(y)+h(x+y).

For each pair of physical columns on distinct nonzero directions look up their sum modulo H and save the resulting actual triple, its tag equations and its defect. This yields at most O(n²) triples. For H₀=0 enumerate candidate pairs of triples with independent defects; for H₀ a line consider one defect outside it; if H₀=H return its physical basis. In all cases test that the tag equations admit a single common g by Gaussian elimination. On success return the participating physical columns and a basis for H₀; their span meets U in exactly H. Under universal triangle generation this is deterministic and complete in O(n⁴ poly(input)). Binary-modular-bases supplied the complete-bucket paired search; following is a sufficient recognizer for incomplete supports.

Let D consist of all nonzero directions carrying all four tags, or use a supplied subset of fully populated directions. Compute the rank of the binary incidence vectors of all present internal triples in F₂^D and test equality to |D|−rank(D). This means they generate the relation space on D. Also check every possibly available nonzero k belongs to D or is a sum of two distinct directions of D. Both conditions are polynomially checkable by enumerating pairs and Gaussian elimination. For every chart A_g contains D. An arbitrary scalar function on A_g vanishing on triangle relations restricts to a linear functional on D, hence extends linearly to span(D). Each additional k=x+y agrees with that extension by its existing chart triangle x,y,k. Thus the triangle annihilator on A_g is exactly the linear evaluation space. Dualizing over F₂, triangles generate all its relations. No chart enumeration is needed.

Example: fully populate an independent coordinate basis and allow arbitrary partial tags and physical h labels on e_i+e_j for edges of any graph. The recognized support has O(d²) directions in a space of size 2^d. The graph may have unbounded width. No uniform recognition guarantee for arbitrary inputs follows.

For a separate sufficient certificate, if A⊆K includes formal 0 and |A|>3|K|/4, any scalar f with f(0)=0 satisfying all triangle equations extends linearly. Define Δ(t)=f(a)+f(a+t), for a,a+t∈A. For two such pairs a,b find z with z,z+t,z+a,z+b∈A by the strict four-translate union bound. Triangle equations imply Δ(t)=f(z)+f(z+t) independently of the pair, with repeated points covered by f(0)=0. Choosing x,x+t,x+t+u∈A gives Δ(t)+Δ(u)=Δ(t+u). For t∈A use a=0. This proves triangle generation under the stated density bound. A count of fully populated directions plus formal zero provides a uniform lower bound on every chart size.

An explicit control outside the recognition promise is YES but has no usable physical triples. Take K=F₂⁶ with basis e₁,…,e₆. Use the directions e₁,e₂,e₃,e₁+e₂+e₃ in one summand and e₄,e₅,e₆,e₄+e₅+e₆ in the other. Supply just tag zero on each; give its last point gain h₁ or h₂ respectively and all six basis points gain zero. Include two additional physical points in U with their B components equal to the two independent B axes, to span V physically. They cannot be in an exact witness. The eight usable physical columns together meet U exactly in H (two four-term residues), yet there is no additive triple on their eight K directions. Here D is empty, so the algorithm correctly reports that its recognition promise fails.

Repeated nonzero (k,b) buckets also work. Choose one canonical indexed physical representative per bucket. Add a defect record for each other indexed column and its representative, storing their difference h+h_rep and the single condition g(k)=b. Compute ordinary triangle records only on canonical representatives. At a fixed chart the map from the free indicator space on physical choices to the indicators of A_g is onto. Its kernel is generated by these two-site records; the canonical triangle records lift generators of the direction-relation kernel. Thus their combined residues with H₀ generate precisely the physical vertical space. There are at most n additional records. The same compatible-event solver and O(n⁴ poly(input)) complexity apply. No bucket-simplicity hypothesis is needed for this generalized solver.
