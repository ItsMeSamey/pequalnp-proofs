# A sparse seven-dimensional control for plane capacity tests

In seven binary coordinates take the four lowest bits as the named space U and the two lowest as target H. Ordinary indexed sites are 17,23,24,28,35,38,40,47,62,64,74,83,90,92,100,104,109. All are outside U and are distinct modulo H. The subset checker enumerates 131072 choices and their binary spans. There are 2066 distinct spans, and intersecting with U produces all 66 named subspaces other than H. Every proper named quotient is positive, using possibly different physical subsets; the 136 pair and 24 primal/dual projection filters are positive by the 34 attained competing planes.

A full target witness would have rank five and project modulo H onto a three-space transverse to U/H. Enumerate all 64 such three-spaces as graphs of binary maps from the three private bits into the two bits U/H. For each gather the eligible physical sites. Twenty-six charts have at least five sites and span the three private bits. Their maximum physical rank is four; thus rank, as opposed to bucket occupancy, excludes every witness. For every individual site, a choice of five eligible sites spanning all private bits can include it, and another such choice can exclude it. There are 6–12 charts accepting its inclusion and 17–22 accepting its exclusion. The elementary capacity, pair-pin and single-site capacity propagation tests therefore leave the decision unresolved.

No deletion of at most three columns loses H from the ordinary span, so the pair-of-sites implication tests comparing selected spans and complementary spans have no positive clauses. For physical shores of at least seven columns the histogram of intersection dimension with the outside plus the names is 53,2932,30059,76250 at dimensions 4,5,6,7. Thus the earlier rank-three protected-shore shrink is unavailable. The ordinary matroid has connectivity two on some nontrivial partitions; this is not a claim of absence of all separations. The direct 64-chart rank test decides the example, independently of the previously verified six-element rooted-minor equivalences, and exhaustive rank enumeration is polynomial at fixed ambient rank. No lower bound on arbitrary algorithms is implied.
