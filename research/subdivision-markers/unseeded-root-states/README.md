# Exact unseeded score queries by protected boundary states

Let D be an explicit binary matrix on n columns. Seek a binary cycle c∈ker D and cocycle d∈row D with disjoint supports and prescribed t-bit score Lc c + Ld d. This algorithm computes the entire score set with representatives, using a supplied binary decomposition of the ordinary columns. It does not assume any score witness or missing score. A common freely selectable offset space may first be quotiented; each recovered raw pair then realizes its whole offset coset.

## Protected encoding and the terminal functional

Use the augmented ordinary columns Oₑ=(Dₑ,(Lc)ₑ) and protected columns R=[(0,I),(D Ldᵀ,0)]. Retain all protected occurrences, including loops or coincident columns. For any subset X of ordinary elements put K_X=ker(R modulo span O_X) in the space of named root coordinates. A primal gain a and dual gain b are attainable together iff some X satisfies (a,0)∈K_X and (0,b)∈K_X⊥.

Indeed, the first condition expresses y=R(a,0) as a sum of O columns from X. Its top coordinates give Dc=0, and its bottom coordinates give Lc c=a. The second says that the prescribed root values (0,b) extend to an ambient functional annihilating O_X. Because its values on (0,I) vanish, its evaluations on ordinary columns are a native cocycle d, zero on all of X. Its value on D Ldᵀ is Ld d=b. Conversely take X=supp c and use the functional inducing d on the top coordinates. This directly checks both sides of the [protected-root representation](../rooted-core/README.md). Ordinary regularity alone need not survive the augmentation. For example take D the reduced incidence of K₄ in rank three (the six nonzero columns apart from 111), Lc zero and one dual gain row chosen so its D-weighted sum is 111. The ordinary columns plus the one dual root now restrict to F₇.

Equivalently, (0,b) must descend to a consistent functional β on span R. Among extensions φ of β, find one for which the ordinary columns killed by φ span y. The unrestricted binary matrix remains visible in this formulation. No theorem for unrooted minors is invoked.

## Exact boundary recurrences

For a node with ordinary leaves A set U_A=span(O_A)∩span(O_outside A,R). The reference recurrence stores the distinct subspaces span(O_X)∩U_A for all X⊆A, retaining one realizing X per subspace. Two child states K₁,K₂ merge to (K₁+K₂)∩U_A. Indeed, any parent boundary vector splits into two selected child-span vectors, each of which lies in that child's outside span. This computes all root traces using at most 2^O(w²) states per cut of dimension at most w. [Reference implementation](solve.py)

A smaller exact state stores a pair (u,ψ) with u∈U_A and ψ a functional on U_A. The state is present exactly when some extension φ_A to span O_A annihilates a set of ordinary columns in A whose sum is u. Store one such subset. At a leaf the empty subset gives u=0 for every ψ. A nonzero included column gives its own vector only if that vector lies in the port and ψ kills it.

For children 1,2 let Vᵢ=span Oᵢ and T the outside span of the parent, including roots. Then Uᵢ=Vᵢ∩(Vⱼ+T) and U₁∩U₂=V₁∩V₂. Compatible child functionals glue on U₁+U₂. Every vector in U_A=(V₁+V₂)∩T splits into child port vectors, hence U_A⊆U₁+U₂. Merge two child pairs when the functionals agree on their intersection and u₁+u₂ belongs to U_A. Record that sum, the restriction of the glued functional to U_A, and the union of the stored subsets. Any extensions witnessing the two stored states agree on all of V₁∩V₂, hence extend jointly; this validates arbitrary representative choice. Conversely a certifying parent subset and functional split across the children and produce exactly such a merge.

At the root, check that y∈U_root and the stored ψ agrees with β on that boundary. The two functionals then extend jointly on the ordinary and protected spans. Solve for the global functional from the recorded subset's zero requirements and the prescribed protected values; its ordinary evaluations give a directly verifiable cocycle. This boundary table serves all score splits. The implementation accepts a nested binary tree of ordinary indices or constructs a balanced tree on their supplied order. [Paired-state implementation](pairs.py)

There are at most 4^w possible pairs at any node. Enumerating functionals on U₁+U₂ and vector representations on both sides takes at most 2^(4w) polynomial work per merge. Testing all score splits adds a 2^O(t) factor. In terms of the native cut width λ=dim(span D_A∩span D_outside), the projected augmented port has dimension at most λ+t because the top coordinates of the dual roots add at most t directions. The vertical projection kernel has dimension at most t. Therefore w≤max λ+2t. In particular a supplied logarithmic-width decomposition at fixed t gives a uniform deterministic polynomial-time decision and witness algorithm on that class. Width is a property of the supplied representation and decomposition. Even when t=0, a cut between two copies of a w-element independent basis admits exactly 3^w pairs: on each coordinate a chosen vector bit one requires functional bit zero. Thus small t alone does not bound this explicit table.

## Comparison and an independent partial search

The eight-element four-bit example misses precisely score 14. A new coloop of dual gain 1 fills that hole; the exact state retains it and recovers a witness, whereas shrinking only the fifteen previously supplied scores can lose it. The paired-state peaks were 129 and 387 respectively. On the simple n-edge circuit, give its first edge primal gain one and the next three dual gains 2,4,8. Then raw cycles are the empty and whole ground. The exact spectrum is all even four-bit values together with 1, for n≥5. At n=10,32,64 the observed port width was four and the paired table peaked at 81 states despite native rank n−1. The [checker](check.py) also compares both algorithms with raw enumeration on 150 matrices and the paired solver on 60 additional permuted decompositions. Independently, a separate local-state comparison matched 6,645 states at 1,382 nodes on 150 instances. These are proof reviews and executable checks, without a Lean certificate.

For fixed split, a different deterministic search applies if a realizing X has h=rank(span(O_X) modulo span R) small. Parameterize all extensions of a consistent β by k free bits. Requiring φ(O_X)=0 is a consistent affine system of rank h on them when such a witness exists. A distribution with parity bias below 2^(−h) hits every such flat by its character expansion. One elementary construction takes a binary field of size Q>(k+1)2^h, two field parameters a,b, and coordinate i the first binary coordinate of a b^i for i=0,…,k−1. Every nonempty parity has bias ≤(k−1)/Q by polynomial root counting and uniform multiplication by a. Construct the field in poly(Q) time by exhaustive irreducible-polynomial search at degree log₂Q. For each of Q² parameter pairs, test y in the span of ordinary columns killed by that functional. This gives 2^O(h)poly(input) time for the split under the promised rank cap. Without the cap a failed search excludes only those witnesses. [Small-bias constructions](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.3240030308)

Fixed t does not imply small h even after trying the dual orientation. Take a direct sum of an m-edge circuit A and an m-edge parallel class B. One primal-score bit reads one A edge, the other reads one B edge on the dual side. Target 11 forces all A into the primal support, all B into the dual support and nothing else. The primal-support columns project outside the dual-root span with rank at least m−1. On exchanging primal and dual the same construction reverses. This is a regular host of bounded branchwidth, so the two parameters measure different restrictions.
