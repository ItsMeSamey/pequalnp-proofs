# Polynomial bulk Taylor evaluation on a connected unweighted grid CNF

Place a Boolean variable on every edge of the 2s by 2s grid. At every vertex add a positive clause requiring at least one incident edge and negative binary clauses on all incident pairs requiring at most one. With identity rows these clauses enforce perfect matching at every node. Partition the vertices into m=s² disjoint 2 by 2 squares and mark the top edge x_i of each. Every marking pattern a extends to a matching: tile each square horizontally if a_i=1 and vertically otherwise. Let N_a≥1 count the extensions.

For each mark add fresh guard units ¬g_i and ¬h_i and two clauses (literal_a(x_i) OR g_i) and (literal_S(x_i) OR h_i). The first literal pins x_i=a_i. The second is positive precisely on S. The guards occur positively in the binary clauses so the negative units do not subsume them. There are no repeated clauses, subsumed clauses or tautologies. Scope sets are fixed over all signings. The unweighted CNF has n=10s²−4s variables, width at most four, occurrence at most ten, and connected incidence graph. Its at-least-one vertex clauses and edge variables alone contain a subdivision of the grid, proving unbounded treewidth.

Only x_i in the second binary clause receives a nonconstant row, namely (1−T)e_i; all others are identity. A vertical bisection with r=2 yields a legal static layout. Assign each edge variable to one endpoint's side and guard variables to their mark's side; assign vertex clauses to their vertex's side. Measure every crossing edge coordinate by a unit row. O(s) measurements suffice, with D=1. Q=3 nodes and degree bound R=1 are legal. The direct Taylor cutoff floor(dR/Q) exceeds m (the guards already account for 6m separate literal incidences).

At every T the exact diagonal sum is N_a times the explicit polynomial product of the second literals evaluated at (1−T)a_i. Thus one pinned planar perfect-matching count and elementary polynomial arithmetic give every Taylor jet at every node. Compute N_a by deleting marked edges forced absent and enforcing the pairwise disjoint marked dimers forced present (delete their endpoints and all incident edges, retaining the matching on the remaining vertices). Alternatively use a signed Kasteleyn matrix for the whole grid, zero forced-absent edges and competing edges at the endpoints of forced-present edges; the absolute determinant is N_a. Its orientation remains valid because restriction only removes terms of the original sign-coherent determinant expansion. These operations have polynomial bit complexity. See [Kuperberg, Kasteleyn cokernels](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v9i1r29/pdf/).

At t=1 independent correction substitution Y makes a positive second literal equal to −zY_i and a negative one equal to 1+zY_i. Guards remain forced false even off the diagonal. In a weighted query with Y fixed and arbitrary fixed scalar mask on the actual x, all a-dependence is a single scalar (the weighted sum of matchings of marking pattern a); all S-dependence lies in the second-literal product. Thus any common linear scalar readout of a query's entire z jet gives a rank-at-most-one matrix in a,S. Contrast the diagonal order-j coefficient when |a|=|S|=j: it equals (−1)^j N_a on the diagonal a=S and zero elsewhere. Its rank is binom(m,j), exponential in n for j=floor(m/2). This is a separation between one explicit all-node polynomial-time diagonal computation and fixed separated reconstruction on the same legal layout. It does not show runtime hardness for these formulas; reading the pinned signs is part of the positive algorithm.

bulk_dimer_check.py enumerates the 36 matchings of the 4 by 4 grid, independently reconstructs all 16 pin counts by a signed determinant, and compares the complete degree-four Taylor polynomial for all 256 sign pairs at five points. The diagonal coefficient ranks by order are 1,4,6,4,1. The rank argument and layout were independently reviewed.
