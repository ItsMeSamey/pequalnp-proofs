# Three bounds inside the quantitative tensor-contraction scheme

The notation follows the OpenAI manuscript's quantitative contraction appendix. These estimates concern three operations in that algorithm. None supplies a complexity lower bound for general tensor contraction or SAT. [Appendix source](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex)

## Explicit exponent enumeration

Consider the root with M edges, u the number of active valuation levels, a=2u, packet length L=2^a and r=64 groups. At odd interpolation nodes the active coordinate is zero and D=L. Every ordered label class is padded to H_pad slots. Consequently exactly a fraction 1−1/r of the padded slots lie in packets with unequal symbols in coordinate zero. Each such packet contributes two tile variables per weight under the stated enumeration. Writing D_arr for the number of ordered classes, their number per weight is

    K = 2(1−1/r) H_pad D_arr / L.

The published packing bound gives 2(1−1/r)M/L ≤ K ≤ 6M/L. Put J=floor(L/√u). Each subset of the KJ variables of weights one through J is an admissible exponent tuple. Its total weight is at most KJ(J+1)/2 ≤ 6ML/u, strictly less than p_t ≥ 8 ceil(3M/u)L. Therefore the literal enumeration contains at least 2^{Ω(M/√u)} tuples. The published upper bound matches this exponent up to constants. In an active root with M proportional to s and u proportional to log s this is 2^{Ω(s/√log s)}. This count includes formal terms that may vanish. In particular, a zero tensor can kill the entire contraction.

## A lower bound for Hermite basis coefficient height, including nonmonotone tapers

Allow arbitrary nonnegative integer multiplicities q_i supported on levels 0≤i<u. Use the same complete residue grid and the paper's Hermite cardinal factors E_t. Set all later q_i to zero. Assume a≥2u, u sufficiently large, and total multiplicity exceeds M(2L−1), the degree threshold. Then some retained level i satisfies

    −v₂(E_t(0))/D_t ≥ Ω(M/u),   v₂(t)=i.

Indeed the maximum Q of the q_i is at least M(2L−1)/(2L). Put b at an index attaining Q, and let c_j=j/2^j. By the exact grid valuation formula, the quantity on the left is the finite sum of c_j(q_i−q_{i+j}) for 1≤j≤a−i. Extend the sum to all positive j temporarily. Summing the extended expressions from i=b to u−1 telescopes, for each j, to c_j times a sum of the first min(j,u−b) multiplicities in this interval. These sums are nonnegative and the j=1 term alone is Q/2. The extra tails have total magnitude at most

    Q ∑_{i=b}^{u−1} (a−i+2) 2^{−(a−i)} = o(Q),

since a−u≥u. Hence one finite expression is at least Q/(4u). It occurs at positive multiplicity because a zero q_i makes every summand nonpositive. For decreasing tapers the single largest adjacent drop also proves the bound immediately. This bounds the normalized valuation of an individual intermediate Hermite coefficient. Dependencies among available jets and a change of recovery basis could alter the precision needed for a different implementation.

## Smith exponents for a fully occupied crossing subtile

Work over the appendix's unramified 2-adic discrete valuation ring before reduction modulo a power of two. Fix t with v₂(t)=i, an interpolation parameter ζ that is a unit, and T=t+2^{a+1}ζ. Take rows x=x₀+2^{i+1}r and columns y=x₀+2^{i+1}j with 0≤r,j<m≤2^{a−i−1}, assuming the slots exist and are occupied in a packet with unequal symbols at coordinate i. Their equal bits there make them crossing ports. The Cauchy submatrix has Smith exponents

    a+1−i+2b+2v₂(b!),   0≤b<m.

To prove this, write N=C(T)/C'(0) for the common numerator. Its valuation is a+1 since all node differences other than T−t retain their valuation and the grid derivative valuations are equal. Each denominator T+x−y has valuation i. In a k-minor the Cauchy determinant contributes N^k, two Vandermonde products, and k² denominator factors. Strides contribute (i+1)k(k−1) to the valuation. Each unscaled Vandermonde on k distinct integer indices has valuation at least the sum of v₂(b!) for 0≤b<k, attained on consecutive indices. This follows by taking the determinant in the integer-valued binomial polynomial basis. Thus the minimum valuation among k-minors equals

    k(a+1−i)+k(k−1)+2 ∑_{b=0}^{k−1} v₂(b!).

Successive differences give the claimed invariant factors. Modulo 2^p the number of exponents below p equals the minimal number of generators of the image and the minimal inner dimension of a linear factorization. For the latter statement any submodule of a module generated by d elements over this principal ideal ring also has at most d generators. This is a local statement conditioned on occupancy.

The geometric expansion also admits a Newton basis on each tile. With d=T−y₀+x and stride s'=2^{i+1}, the kth forward difference at zero of N/(d−s'j) is N k! (s')^k divided by the product of d−s'j for 0≤j≤k. The identity follows inductively by taking one forward difference. Multiply these differences by binom(j,k) and sum through k=j to recover the exact value. If v₂(d)=i, the kth coefficient has valuation a+1−i+k+v₂(k!). This increases precision divisibility over the geometric basis while preserving a comparable linear growth rate in k.
