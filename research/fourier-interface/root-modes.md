# Surviving root summands and a direct aggregation criterion

Consider an active root of the [quantitative-contraction algorithm](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-contraction.tex) with M proportional to s. Choose the allowed residue-distinct interpolation set to include ζ=1, and use t=1. Then i=0, D=L, a=2u, L=2^a and T=2L+1. Write N=C(T)/C′(0), with v₂(N)=a+1 by the grid derivative identity. The following examples are about this single evaluation. They give no hardness result for general contraction.

## Boolean survival

Use a matching as the original graph. Its unary tensor entries can be chosen after running the topology-only packing. At most 3M/r padded slots belong to equal-symbol packets at coordinate zero, because they comprise a fraction 1/r of all padded slots. At most 3M/L packets exist altogether. Thus unequal-symbol packets with at least L/8 real slots hold at least (1−3/r−3/8)M real slots. There are K=Θ(M/L) such packets. In each, choose a parity class containing at least L/16 real slots. Pin one column y=y₀+2j of that parity to one, with j>0. Pin all other columns to zero. Pin J=floor(cL/u) distinct rows of that parity to zero, for a sufficiently small absolute c>0. Leave all other rows unrestricted, so their affine factors sum to one. Outside these packets pin every column to zero and leave every row unrestricted.

For each selected packet choose any subset B of the mode indices 0,…,J−1. Give each of these modes exponent one and the others exponent zero. With d=|B| and e the sum of its indices, the packet contribution is

    (−N)^d 2^e j^e ∑_f ∏_{b∈B} d_{f(b)}^{−b−1},

where f ranges over injections to the selected rows and d_x=T−y₀+x is positive and odd. Indeed, the single active column is across groups, and each row factor is one minus the sum of its targeted modes. The sum in the display is positive. An odd common denominator is the product of d_x^J over these rows. After clearing it, the positive numerator is at most J!(3L+1)^{J²}. Together with the removed factors this gives valuation at most J(a+1)+J²(1+a)+log₂(J!)+J² log₂(3L+1). Over at most 3M/L packets this is O(c²ML/u)+o(ML/u), less than p_t≥24ML/u for sufficiently small c. The total formal budget also fits because each summand has valuation at least its budget weight. The 2^{KJ}=2^{Ω(M/u)} distinct tuples have nonzero scalar contributions. Some values can coincide.

## Distinct weighted values

Choose one real row x and its matching column in each of m=Θ(M/L) occupied unequal-symbol packets. Pin only that column to one in its packet. Set the two selected row weights to [1,1+P_i], with distinct polynomially bounded primes P_i>4L. A polynomial-sized sieve provides enough primes, and input precision P₀ proportional to s preserves the weights. Pin unused columns to zero and leave other rows unrestricted. Selecting the b=0 mode on subset U and no modes elsewhere gives the scalar

    ∏_{i∈U} P_i N/d_i.

The primes P_i divide neither the numerator nor denominator of N and do not divide any d_i, since every grid factor is at most 3L+1 in absolute value. Thus U is recoverable from the rational product. These products remain distinct at precision p_t. In lowest terms N=A/B has odd denominator and O(L log L)-bit numerator and denominator from the grid products. After clearing the common odd denominator B^m ∏d_i, the numerators and their differences have O(mL log L + m log M) bits, which is o(ML/u). There are therefore at least 2^{Ω(M/L)} pairwise distinct nonzero summands. Both unary examples permit efficient batching by factorization.

## Frontier aggregation

For fixed T the original deformed sum has one packet factor K_p(X,Y), the product over its real rows of l_{Y_x}((W(T)X)_x). These factors can be evaluated in polynomially many ring operations for a complete local assignment. Pin unary indicator ports. A freely summed unary row weighted [1,1] can be removed using l₀+l₁=1. A pinned row's factor is retained when it depends on unfixed columns. Count b as the largest number of unfixed remaining row and column bits in a packet after these eliminations.

Given a packet order, define its frontier after a prefix as the already processed unfixed bits on original tensor vertices incident to an unprocessed unfixed bit. Pinned coordinates are known in advance. Let w be the maximum frontier size. A table on frontier assignments stores the sum of products over completed packet factors and original factors fully assigned by that prefix. At the next packet enumerate its at most b bits, evaluate its packet factor and newly completed original factors, multiply, and add to the induced assignment on the new frontier. Evaluate constant original factors separately. Induction proves the final empty-frontier entry is precisely the deformed contraction modulo the prescribed precision. Time is O*(2^{w+b}) and space O*(2^w), assuming polynomial-time, polynomial-space original entry evaluation. Both parameters can be read from the supplied order and the local unary tensors. This computes the sum directly and includes all surviving modes without forming them.

A linked example uses an even number of copies of the same fully occupied unequal-symbol packet type. Leave one distinguished column bit X_i and the matching row bit Y_i live in each. Pin other columns to zero, leave other rows unrestricted, and impose equality on Y pairs (1,2),(3,4),… and on X pairs (2,3),(4,5),…. Identical types and slot positions give identical full labels for joined row or column endpoints. The layout can be constructed by filling all ordered label classes equally to a multiple of L as in the packing lemma, producing arbitrarily many repeated copies. Joining the indicated matching vertices creates degree-two equality tensors and preserves the supplied label layout and every port load. This exhibits a valid layout; a fresh run of the greedy conditional-expectation algorithm on the joined graph need not return this particular layout. Packets outside the chain can be neutralized by unary pins. The residual graph is a path. For the distinguished slot its matrix in row Y and column X coordinates is

    K = [[1, 1−w₀], [0, w₀]],   w₀ = N/T.

The nonzero rational determinant w₀ has valuation a+1<p_t, hence each packet matrix has a nonzero 2-by-2 minor at the evaluation precision. Equality edges are identity matrices. Thus the chain is genuinely linked at the factor level while its contraction takes O(m) ring operations after eliminating the unconstrained rows, alternating orientations as required. The polynomial frontier criterion evaluates a broader family. No claim here rules out efficient algorithms on other networks.
