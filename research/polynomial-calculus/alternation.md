# Serial affine propagation with full discovery

This is a closure theorem for a satisfiable instance. Let each block F_i consist of a balanced simple bipartite core with a planted perfect matching. Identify matched pairs and regard a nonmatching edge as an arc p→h. Take the resulting directed graph strongly connected, with minimum outdegree at least two; the complete three-by-three core suffices. Add one unmatched pigeon with a single edge e_i to h. Order that edge immediately after the planted first edge at h and before all nonmatching core arcs. Compile as usual, omitting the marker. Shift the affine factor of e_i by input u_i, with u_1=0 and u_i=a_(i−1) for i>1.

Follow F_i by a connected incidence switch on bounded-degree graph H with root bit a_i. Its vertex rows are A_v(sum_{f incident v} z_f + [v=r](1+e_i)); its edge rows are z_uv(A_u+A_v). The sum of switch rows is a_i(1+e_i). Take H=K4 for the full-bag relaxation in this note. All guarded rows have distinct pivots. For deficiency exactly one, append an independent bit b, its tautological guard b(b+1)=0, and the sole marker b=0. Distinct pivot normals give deficiency at most one, and there is one more row than ambient coordinates. Eliminate this independent pin throughout the analysis.

A PC pass is complete ordinary degree-two polynomial calculus, given all originally present Boolean and guarded rows and the previously exported affine GF(2) equations. It exports every derivable affine equation. An LP pass uses distributions on each original factor bag, consistent on all intersections, and previously exported Boolean parity equations as additional factors. To discover any further affine equation l=0 one may refute the extra constraint l=1 using another full bag containing its entire support. All members of a simultaneous pass are tested against exactly the same prior equations. The argument also bounds repeated LP-only attempts within one pass, as explained below. We allow the decoder to treat known pins by substitution.

## The two block facts

With u_i=0, the prefix telescope forces e_i=0 even for the LP. On a hole, let C be the running parity starting with the substituted planted bit. Active later pivots change the parity from zero to one. The shared parity of every intermediate prefix is measurable on the two successive bags. The initial parity has expectation at least one minus the sum of the nonmatching bits of its pigeon. Summing these progressions over all holes gives the sum of final parity expectations at least the number of holes plus E[e_i], since the other nonmatching bits cancel. Each final parity is bounded by one. Actual solutions with u_i=0 therefore have e_i=0 and are exactly the perfect matchings of the balanced core.

At u_i=1, across the two values of e_i, the core coordinates span the entire space. The planted matching (all core bits zero) plus e_i satisfies the block. Every directed simple path from p to h is another valid core vector: reassign each pigeon on this path to its successor. Hole p becomes empty; h retains its planted bit and receives the last arc. The shifted e_i, placed between these selected edges, resets the prefix to zero. For a directed simple cycle avoiding h, its incidence vector is also feasible, as a disjoint cycle update of the planted matching. For the spanning assertion combine the paths with the perfect matchings available at u_i=1,e_i=0: a linear functional constant on both fibers vanishes on every directed cycle by this latter fiber and on every path to h by the former. Vanishing on all directed cycles identifies the weights as a gradient on a strongly connected digraph: append a fixed directed return path to compare any two directed paths with identical endpoints; decompose closed walks into simple directed cycles. Vanishing on all paths to h makes that gradient zero. Only this two-fiber spanning assertion is used below. The fiber e_i=1 alone need not span: the outgoing arcs of h are zero in every assignment with u_i=e_i=1, since e_i needs the planted first edge active. Thus the full span claimed here is the joint conditional span at u_i=1, across both values of e_i.

At e_i=1 the switch admits arbitrary vertex bits when z=0, in particular either root value. At e_i=0 its root is zero in every genuine solution. For an LP witness it can instead take all vertex bits one, leaving odd-charge edge parity constraints. On H=K4 the local vertex-star distributions are uniform among the four assignments of the prescribed parity on three edges. Distinct stars meet on one edge with a fair marginal. Edge-pivot bags contain their two deterministic endpoint bits and the single fair edge. Consequently the whole switch has consistent local distributions. This also works using linear levels of local distributions on a sufficiently large expander.

## Bounding all possible exports

All actual solutions have e_i=a_i=0, by induction. They may vary each z coordinate alone with all vertex bits zero, and each nonroot vertex bit alone with z=0. Core assignments may independently range over all perfect matchings. Hence a valid affine export has zero constant, contains no z or nonroot vertex bit, and has core weights orthogonal to all directed cycles in each block. In particular the only uncertain coordinates of the odd-switch local witness do not occur in any candidate valid affine constraint. Adding parity bags of arbitrary support for these equations, including a violating test assignment, therefore introduces only deterministic coordinates in that witness.

Say an affine export is confined through switch j if its support is in blocks F_1,...,F_j and root bits a_1,...,a_j. Begin with no exports and j=0. Suppose all prior equations are valid and confined through switch j. An LP pass can add support in F_(j+1), but cannot add any coefficient on a_(j+1) or beyond. Fix all earlier blocks at the zero assignment, as well as F_(j+1) at zero. Break the relay using the odd local witness at switch j+1, with root one. All future blocks now admit genuine configurations. Keep the active relay at one all the way down to any chosen test block; isolate its core path vectors by setting the other blocks identically in the comparison. A candidate affine export that contains nonzero weights on such paths fails on one of the witnesses. All cycle weights vanish already on actual models, so the gradient argument forces every core weight after F_(j+1) to vanish. With all those core bits zero, downstream interfaces range over all initial strings of ones followed by zeros, in the order a_(j+1),e_(j+2),a_(j+2),...,e_L,a_L. Each such assignment has a local witness (turn off the first unwanted output, then leave the rest zero), as does the all-zero genuine assignment. Those vectors span the entire interface space, forcing every coefficient there to vanish. Earlier exports remain true on the fixed zero prefix. The same witnesses satisfy any additional valid exports confined to that prefix and F_(j+1), since those coordinates are zero; thus repeated LP-only discovery cannot extend this frontier.

After that pass, even if all valid affine equations through F_(j+1) and earlier switches are granted, PC2 cannot add any support past a_(j+1). Substitute zero for every coordinate through switch j+1. All granted equations vanish. The residual starts at F_(j+2) with input zero. In this residual the squarefree quadratic projections of all guards are linearly independent. For each core arc p→k, its guard has a private core-core monomial with an arc k→g, g distinct from p; only the first arc sees the second through its planted-bit substitution. This kills all core row coefficients in a dependency. The e guard at each block has the monomial e times any outgoing core arc of its planted h, now unique after removing core rows. Thus all e guard coefficients vanish. In each switch, e_i a_i next kills its root coefficient; each incidence monomial z_f A_v equates vertex and edge coefficients throughout the connected switch. Distinct block variables prevent any other cross-block cancellation. So the residual has no nonzero affine PC2 consequence by the rank criterion in affine-export.md. The affine tail of any original derivation would survive substitution and would give just such a consequence. The full PC closure is idempotent, so repeating it alone cannot enlarge this frontier.

Conversely LP derives e_(j+1)=0 when a_j=0, and PC derives a_(j+1)=0 by summing the switch and multiplying the exported e_(j+1)=0 by a_(j+1). Induction gives exactly L successive LP–PC pairs to expose a_L=0, including when every sound affine equation detectable by each pass is sought. An initial PC pass gives no head start by the same quadratic-rank argument on the untouched chain. This theorem measures discovery of a semantic pin and gives no lower bound for refuting a chain that additionally pins a_L to one. Such a terminal pin introduces a second propagation frontier.

On an unsatisfiable instance, testing both values of the parity of *all* coordinates with this full-query-bag interface immediately detects infeasibility. Its hypothetical bag contains the original scope of every constraint, so full overlap consistency would concentrate that bag on globally satisfying assignments. Consequently a refutation analogue requires a different query primitive or a scope bound.
