# Variable-tree rank for mixed-field satisfiability

Let g_i be explicit sparse Walsh polynomials in n binary variables over fixed odd finite fields F_i, all required nonzero. Supply a full binary tree partitioning variable positions, with individual variables as leaves. For a node t, flatten the sparse coefficient tensor of g_i into a matrix M_it with row indexed by frequencies restricted to t and column by exterior restrictions. Use only occurring rows and columns. Let r_it be its rank over F_i. Choose a column basis B_it. For any subtree assignment store its vector of Walsh evaluations of these basis columns. A zero polynomial rejects immediately. The root basis consists of g_i itself.

For u,v children of t and each selected column with exterior index z, reshape the column into a matrix N_z on the children's respective projected frequencies. Every column belongs to the column space of B_iu and every transposed row to that of B_iv, because each is a slice of the original coefficients. Therefore N_z=B_iu K_z B_iv^T. Compute K_z with left inverses of the two bases. The parent coordinate equals e_u^T K_z e_v on the child evaluation vectors. All construction matrices have dimensions polynomial in the original explicit sparsity (zero-fill projected pairs as necessary). This is the hierarchical tensor representation of the sparse coefficient arrays.

Enumerate all attainable tuples of signatures bottom up using these maps. If Q=max_t product_i |F_i|^r_it, this takes Q² times polynomial in the input and recovers witnesses. Simultaneous supports may have arbitrarily large common span. A bounded-degree global Boolean guard can be included by keeping representative binary features for each signature as in dihedral-overlap.md. For degree d their dimension is sum_{j≤d} binomial(n,j); the time is polynomial in that dimension, not necessarily FPT in d.

## One unbounded-rank predicate

For pure mixed-field tests choose one distinguished predicate g_* whose ranks need not be small. Put Q'=max_t product_{i other than *} |F_i|^r_it. At each signature tuple of the other factors keep actual witnesses whose distinguished evaluation vectors form a basis of their span over F_*. In particular a state in which only zero vectors are attainable may be discarded: zero propagates to the distinguished root test along every future bilinear join. At a leaf the two concrete assignments suffice as candidates. At a parent, enumerate pairs of retained bases for each child signature pair and select pivot witnesses after computing their parent evaluation vectors. Every pair of witnesses from the two entries is admissible since they concern disjoint variables, and the other parent signatures depend only on the two signatures. Bilinearity implies all attainable distinguished vectors are spanned by retained candidates. At the root, an actual basis witness exists exactly when g_* can be nonzero together with the other required tests. Complexity is (Q')² times polynomial input size, since the distinguished ranks never exceed explicit sparsity. Multiple tests in the same distinguished field may first be multiplied when the number of tests is fixed.

For the explicit-word D15 equivalence in dihedral-overlap.md, compile the residue conditions to separate indicators over F3 and F5. The algorithm is polynomial on inputs having a supplied variable tree of logarithmic rank for either indicator, irrespective of the other's ranks. As an example both residue indicators of ordinary Hamming weight can have full-dimensional frequency differences, while any fixed-modulus weight indicator has cut matrix rank bounded by the modulus: in the assignment basis each entry depends only on the pair of partial weight residues. Invertible local Walsh transforms preserve rank. The rank test is instance-specific and computable directly from the sparse representation. An arbitrary binary linear change of parameters preserves Walsh sparsity and is also allowed when supplied before taking these cuts.

## Graphic comparison

For a cut on vertex bits x each edge uv of group weight w contributes w(x_u+x_v−2x_ux_v). Under a vertex partition S|T all bilinear interactions are captured by the exterior profile (−2 sum_{u∈S} w_uv x_u) indexed by v in T. A supplied variable tree with at most H distinct profiles per node permits exact histogram computation in H² poly(input,|A|) time for fixed A. Store representative assignments and counts per internal score and profile. At a join the cross bilinear term is determined by both profiles: changing either assignment while fixing its profile leaves that term unchanged for every opposite assignment. Parent profiles are restricted sums; scores add with the cross correction. Place all linear vertex terms at their own leaf. Exact integer counts have at most n+1 bits. For a uniformly weighted clique all exterior coordinates coincide, so each cut has at most the order of the weight many profiles. For distinct edge cut vectors divide vertex counts by 2 to the number of connected components. The more general Walsh rank theorem applies to arbitrary non-graphic parity masks after any supplied binary change of variables.

The nested matricization/transfer construction is standard tensor algebra. See [Grasedyck, Hierarchical Singular Value Decomposition of Tensors](https://doi.org/10.1137/090764189). The proof here uses exact field elimination.

For a mixed example give every edge of K_n weight 1+3ξ_uv modulo 15, with arbitrary binary ξ_uv. Modulo three the cut score is k(n−k) in the size k of the chosen vertex side. Its target indicator has rank at most three on every vertex split, because its assignment matrix has at most three different rows, determined by the partial size modulo three. The modulo-five graph interactions vary with every ξ_uv. The distinguished-field theorem decides simultaneous membership on any supplied tree. In the other direction the chosen tree matters even on a matching: separate the two endpoints of every edge across one cut. Singleton rows and singleton columns of the Hamming-distance-zero-modulo-p indicator form an identity matrix for odd p, so the spectral cut rank is at least the number of matched pairs.

In the same example take K_(2m) and set ξ to the indicator of a perfect matching. For a split separating all matched endpoints, the two-vertex shore formed by singleton left i and singleton right j has cut score 4m+2−6[i=j]. At target 4m−4 the mod-five indicator contains the m×m identity, while the mod-three tree ranks remain uniformly at most three. The independence of the distinguished rank is therefore essential to this bound.

The independent spectral_tree_check.py compared every subtree's distinguished signature span within each ordinary signature to exhaustive enumeration on 240 random trees (1–7 bits, 1–4 factors over F3,F5,F7). Parent signatures in this check are evaluated directly from the retained assignments.
