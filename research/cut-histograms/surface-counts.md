# Exact finite-group histograms near surface graphic codes

Suppose each edge of a supplied graph embedded in an orientable surface of fixed genus g has a pair of algebraic weights a_e(0), a_e(1) from the fixed cyclotomic field relevant to a fixed finite abelian score group. For its even-subgraph code U, the multilinear sum Σ_{x∈U} Π_e a_e(x_e) is polynomial-time evaluable. With all a_e(0) nonzero factor out their product and use the multivariate high-temperature Ising polynomial on ratios. Its surface Pfaffian expression uses 4^g signed Pfaffians of polynomial size. If some a_e(0)=0, simultaneously add an indeterminate z to those zero weights, evaluate at nonzero integer z=1,…,|E|+1 and interpolate at zero. This keeps bit sizes polynomial at bounded genus and fixed coefficient field for roots of unity and their constant-size linear combinations. Use the combinatorial Pfaffian implementation, with signs and field-valued entries. [Cimasoni, a generalized Kac–Ward formula, including its equivalent Pfaffian expression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.3158)

For the cut code U^⊥, character orthogonality expresses its weighted sum as 1/|U| times the sum over x∈U of products of a_e(0)+(−1)^{x_e}a_e(1). Hence one surface oracle covers cuts and cycles, even for assignments where transformed local factors vanish. An arbitrary affine translate swaps local weights at the shifted coordinates. Taking a_e(b)=χ(b w_e) over all characters χ of any fixed finite abelian group and Fourier-inverting returns exact histograms. A fixed number of arbitrary dense binary linear conditions is handled by adjoining mod-two marker factors to the same group.

This statement survives a supplied weak minor with k deleted or contracted elements. Let U' be the cycle code of that minor on real coordinates R. Guess the 2^k removed primal coordinates in the original matroid. If feasible by elimination, choose one lift x₀. Its allowed real continuations are x₀|R+U₀. Here U₀ consists of the restrictions of cycles vanishing on every removed element. Meanwhile U' consists of the restrictions of cycles vanishing on the specified deleted elements; the contracted elements are unrestricted before projection. Thus U₀⊆U', with codimension at most the number of contracted elements, and at most k binary linear conditions isolate that coset inside x₀|R+U'. Compute the conditions by elimination, add them to the score group and take the prescribed subgroup coordinates of the enlarged histogram. Summing the branches, including their fixed removed-edge scores, counts every original vector once: its removed bits name one branch and its surviving real coordinates name one member of that branch. The dual construction interchanges deletion and contraction. Affine shifts of the original code can equivalently be applied by swapping local weights.

Therefore for fixed g,k and fixed score group, every exact bin is in FP on supplied mixed deletion/contraction extensions of graph cycle and cut codes with supplied genus-g embeddings. The original matroid need not be regular, and none of the induced real-coordinate shifts need be sparse. This is a single-code counting result; requiring a simultaneous disjoint vector from the orthogonal code changes the partition function.

The same exact-count result permits a supplied direct- and two-sum tree of such bounded-genus basic matroids, mixing graphic and cographic bags. At one Fourier character, a child sends its two partial partition functions indexed by the singleton trace, or just one scalar for an empty interface. These are precisely local weights on the parent's virtual element. Process arbitrary-degree bags with the weighted surface oracle above. To calculate an outgoing trace, fix its two local weights to (1,0) or (0,1). This computes the sum over all simultaneous agreeing bag vectors. If projection onto the physical code has a kernel, divide by its size, a power of two computed once by binary elimination; every physical vector has the same number of linear lifts. The coefficient bit lengths stay polynomial since the exact messages sum products of roots of unity over at most exponentially many binary assignments to the explicit tree. This allows unbounded total genus and applies before the same outer weak-backdoor reduction.
