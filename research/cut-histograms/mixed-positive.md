# Mixed odd primes with small shared frequency dimension

Fix any finite abelian marker group, with a fixed number of primary factors. Form the sparse Walsh predicate g_i over the appropriate odd field for each odd-primary factor. A predicate with empty support rejects. Multiply each g_i by one Walsh character already occurring in it, so that zero frequency occurs; this does not change its nonzero set. Write V_i for the binary span of its remaining Walsh frequencies (equivalently, the original frequency differences from the chosen anchor). Compute

    I = Σ_i ( V_i ∩ Σ_{j≠i}V_j ).

The dimension r of I is computable by elimination, independent of which support frequency was chosen. For fixed modulus and r≤C log(input length), with C fixed, membership of a given target is decidable in deterministic polynomial time. More generally the runtime is 2^r times a polynomial whose exponent depends on the fixed marker group. The individual V_i may all have unbounded dimension. Arbitrary 2-primary constraints on the same input are allowed.

Enumerate the 2^r possible values of the parity forms in I. Restrictions of the V_i in the quotient by I are in direct sum. Indeed every element of I decomposes as a sum of elements of I∩V_i. If a sum of vectors from the V_i belongs to I, subtract such a decomposition. Each term of the resulting zero sum belongs to V_i∩Σ_{j≠i}V_j, hence to I. Complete a basis of I by these separate quotient bases and then to an ambient basis. After fixing the shared parities, each g_i, up to a sign irrelevant to support, depends only on its own private block. Restricting the dictionary cannot increase its sparsity. The joint odd support is a Cartesian product, including a free factor of unused coordinates.

Given also the Boolean polynomial test for the 2-primary component, use the connected support graph of support-differences.md independently in each private block and ordinary unit shifts on the free block. The union of these sets of localized shifts connects the Cartesian product. Its initial point is found by taking one support point per factor. To recurse on a derivative edge in block i, replace g_i by g_i(x)g_i(x+v) in its own field and retain every other nonzero-set predicate unchanged. For a free-coordinate shift all g_i are retained. The translated Boolean difference loses one degree at each level, including mixed monomials across different factor blocks. This gives polynomial time per assignment of the shared parities, with constructive recovery.

A separate checkable sufficient condition requires no frequency separation. If all but one of the odd predicates have GF₂ algebraic degree at most a fixed d, compute their unique truncated ANF candidates by evaluations on inputs of weight at most d and Boolean Möbius inversion. Certify each candidate P_i by checking absence of both g_i≠0,P_i=0 and (1−g_i)≠0,P_i=1 using support-differences.md on the original zero/one indicators. If certified, multiply the Boolean candidates with the original 2-primary indicator and solve against the remaining sparse odd-field predicate. Failure of either sufficient condition does not reject the original instance.
