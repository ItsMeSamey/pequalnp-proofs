# Original Oo, two direct entrances

Review scope: least size-21 TopForm G11, q=3, j≥3, sole reader M=3∨E, with E a raw-empty OR. Exactly two physical OR gates E,Q read dirty ANDs directly. M is positive and nondirect. Only Q can be a positive direct entrance. The conclusion for original residuals is U≤2c+j+3.

* Q is a pin of E. It must be raw-empty. Use the serial proof.
* Q strictly feeds an AND pin of E without directly feeding E. The external E escape reaches a centreless top. For empty Q use the earlier-empty split in the relative-placement proof. For positive Q use its final joint protected-product argument. This supersedes the exploratory thresholds earlier in the same file.
* Q is incomparable with E or downstream from E. Use the first two escape cases of the relative-placement proof. They cover either sign of Q, allow shared earlier dirty ANDs, and distinguish an AND-only escape to Q from an escape to a clean AND. No chronological ordering assumption is needed for incomparable gates.

The cases exhaust ancestry: M can neither precede E nor supply a pin of E. Positivity of M rules out a circuit with all three paid ORs raw-empty.
