# Preliminary local computation: one dirty-reading OR for a visible clean S at q=3

Take x=3, y=6, z=7, original residual edges U, H induced after deleting x,y,z. Assume original size 21, leastness and TopForm; A=x AND S clean is sole x reader, j≥3 and q=3. Assume S has positive raw support and the sole gate M having a physical dirty AND-to-OR pin lies in S's OR-only closure. A pure S not containing M, and empty S, are outside this theorem.

All direct dirty donor pins of M are ≤S and clean on H, since a positive independent H input could otherwise be extended by isolated x. Their AND-only expansions before M use no earlier dirty-reading OR. Simultaneously zero the dirty pin(s) of M, and allocate donor-positive H edges first. Every remaining original H edge has its evaluation unchanged. Projected ordinary tops accepting that edge star it originally (projected raw support consists only of original raw endpoints after deletion of dirty contributions). Projected nonzero centreless tops give one clean CNF apiece; their primitive raw disjunctions now have the original OR-reachable raw supports, restricted to H. Every nonsingleton such support has an original star anchor, except possibly the root support of one of the two remaining paid physical ORs. At M the surviving raw pin is an existing wire or zero; it gives no additional unanchored root. All roots, including the upstream clean-on-H donor terms, must use the SAME two physical exceptional supports when present. Incomparable exceptional supports are contained in unions of at most two guarded terminal supports apiece. In the nested case the higher unanchored root is either another two-terminal union (if intervening anchored roots are used), or exactly the first exceptional support union a guarded terminal. This representation concerns the permanently assigned original U and does not invoke minimality of any specialized circuit.

For U_H fixed, the prototype allows every subset of the H-neighborhood of any original G11 anchor after deleting that anchor's edges in U_H. It also allows every singleton, and gives no penalty for stars outside H. These are relaxations of genuine circuit constraints. For each prospective batch r, use all guarded clauses hitting all r edges and any available global exceptional clauses that hit all r. If some maximal independent set of H meets them all, the resulting conjunction is dirty and no permitted subset could clean it. Otherwise the batch is combinatorially feasible. The partition DP intersects allowed ordered pairs of exceptional supports across batches, combining partitions by union. A zero count is an obstruction; a surviving feasibility mask need not be realizable by gates.

At most three near residuals are present. Two visible neighbors star xy,xz. With one visible neighbor, a common AND adds yz, and four individual OR readers give at least one lambda reader because q=3. Its anchor cannot be x because its second raw-reaching pin must bring a distinct raw leaf, which would be another visible neighbor. Otherwise it gives a near star distinct from the x-edge. In the common OR case the two-spoke loss proof forces paid B,Ry,Rz; with one visible neighbor the physical h=y OR z is itself paid, since its only possible lambda witness would require both visible at A, contradicting q=3.

If M has one dirty donor and a raw-positive other arm, at most c+1 H batches remain. Exact local prototype zeroes and the local one-batch bound give H residual counts ≤3,5,6,8,9 for c=0,1,2,3,4 respectively. Add at most three near residuals. For c≥5 the graph bound U≤14 from the two near stars suffices. Thus U≤2c+j+3 throughout this scoped branch, subject to independent replay of the computation.

If M is raw-empty and OR-reaches raw-positive S, at least one other paid OR has an empty inlet in M's OR-only region and its projected output collapses to its remaining arm on H (or vanishes). The number of distinct donor batches below M can be two. Under the direct-two-dirty-pin assumption, every raw-empty wire in that region projects to zero; this gives at most one remaining effective exceptional support of binary guarded cost on H. The local computation rules out six H edges at c+2=3 batches, eight at 4 batches, ten at 5 batches; for larger c the bare |H| bound or global U≤14 suffices. At c=0 there can still be four H edges in two relaxed groups (one-exception local bound is four); this leaves a gap only for j=3 if all three near residuals persist. A raw-empty pin of M not directly a dirty AND would itself be an earlier raw-empty OR requiring a prior distinct dirty-reading OR, impossible under the sole-reader assumption.
