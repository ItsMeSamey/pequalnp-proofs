# Raw-empty OR sibling with a raw-empty OR pin

In the original least size-21 TopForm G11 circuit suppose q=3, j≥3, and x=3 has sole reader M=x OR E. E is a raw-empty OR that directly reads a dirty AND C and a second raw-empty OR Q. Q is the other physical OR that directly reads a dirty AND. We prove U≤2c+j+3. This is the serial topology, distinct from two disjoint dirty AND branches entering E directly.

The three dirty charged ORs are Q,E,M. An earlier raw-empty OR ancestral to Q would itself be charged: it is upstream of an AND since Q is dirty and live (and passes through E,M toward a clean output). Hence it cannot have a clean AND ancestor by TopForm. Expanding its OR-only boundary with empty raw support gives a dirty AND witness, so it is dirty and neither inlet reaches raw input. Such a fourth gate is impossible. Consequently Q=A OR B has two dirty AND pins. Their primitive OR boundaries are pure and originally Lambda-anchored whenever nonsingleton. C cannot be downstream of Q: any OR reached directly from raw-empty dirty Q or from an intervening dirty AND is charged and cannot precede E. The intervening ANDs cannot be clean by TopForm with destination C. Thus any Q-to-C path would consist entirely of ANDs after Q, implying C≤Q and making E redundant in a least circuit. C consequently has the same pure anchored-conjunction expansion. All three arms may share antecedents or have multiplicative dependencies on each other.

The private-donor exclusion (6648-size21-private-x3-donor.md) gives E another direct reader besides M. It cannot be an OR, which would be charged because E is dirty and raw-empty. Follow ANDs up to the first clean AND. Any attempted OR exit from a dirty AND requires a direct dirty-reading OR, and both Q and E precede the path. Since no dirty wire is the graph output, a clean AND T is reached. It is centreless and implies E. In particular c≥1.

Each of A,B,C implies E. The standard sole-OR shielding lemma makes E clean on either deletion 9 or 10. Every original E-positive residual edge can thus be allocated once to an accepting arm and one deletion on which it survives, since 9 and 10 are nonadjacent. This gives six pure anchored-conjunction batches. On E-negative pairs Q is also false. Replace Q and E by false throughout, and forward M to x. This preserves these pairs and globally decreases the circuit. Surviving pre-top ORs are raw disjunctions with their original raw supports, since Q and E originally had none, and M had exactly x. No dirty-to-OR injection survives; each surviving ordinary top accepts only original starred pairs, and every remaining centreless top gives at most one clean anchored-conjunction batch. T is inactive on all allocated E-negative pairs. There are at most 6+c−1 batches in total, all evaluated on the original graph or one deletion, with original star anchors.

For completeness invoke the exclusive-double footprint of 6648-q3-sole-or-downstream-positive.md. Each batch accepts at most two residuals, and two must form an induced matching on their endpoints. Four independent orientations force anchored original spokes at all four opposite corners. A homogeneous double is uniquely the 14,59 pair in G11. A nonhomogeneous double forces at least seven stars, and if there are exactly seven its connected bipartite seven-edge footprint has a uniquely determined four-mark side, admitting only that induced matching. Thus with at most seven starred edges at most two exclusive double batches exist globally, even if some anchors are deleted on a particular slice.

A failure of the desired bound has U≥2c+j+4≥9, leaving at most seven of the sixteen graph edges starred. Hence U≤(6+c−1)+2=c+7≤2c+j+3. This argument does not assume E has two dirty AND pins or that different protected arms have disjoint dirty ancestry. It is a source-level proof using the cited original-graph lemmas.
