# Empty exceptional OR upstream of a positive mixer, clean sole x-AND with visible raw S

Use the genuine size-21 least TopForm G11 setting, original residuals, x=3, y=6, z=7, A=x AND S clean, j≥3,q=2 and nonempty raw support of S. Assume one exceptional OR E is raw-empty and upstream of the other exceptional OR Q which is raw-positive with an empty input. The first exceptional OR E=J1∨J2 has two direct dirty AND pins: any prior raw-empty OR would itself be paid, and clean AND ancestors of an AND contradict TopForm. Ordinarily a clean OR cannot provide a dirty OR below the first clean AND; its clean AND ancestor would violate the same rule. Each Ji is an anchored-clause conjunction, since there is no other earlier charged exit.

Write Q=D∨R, with R raw-positive. No path from E can escape from an AND signal into a raw-positive OR cone before Q: this would require a third paid OR. Similarly E cannot directly enter an OR with raw-empty support before Q. Hence R is a pure raw disjunction, guarded if nonsingleton by its original lambda witness, and either D=E or D=E∧K, where K is a conjunction of anchored pure clauses. This follows by unfolding the AND-only region and using idempotence for any repeated E occurrences; K may itself share multiplicative ancestors with Ji. In particular D is the union of the two pure products Ci=Ji∧K, taking K=true in the direct case.

Q OR-reaches S, since otherwise S's OR closure reduces to raw/OR only (no other raw-positive exit from a dirty signal), and positivity on both y,z would give the physical y∨z wire excluded by the common-OR theorem. Thus D≤S. On H, the graph induced by deleting x,y,z, S is clean since x may be added to any independent H assignment without creating an edge. Both Ci are therefore clean on H. Allocate exterior residuals positive at D first to the two corresponding Ci batches. For all remaining H residuals project Q to R and leave E unchanged. Their evaluations persist; the projection decreases the circuit on arbitrary inputs. On a path from E to a first clean top in this projected circuit no dirty OR can intervene, since E has raw-empty support and no later exceptional exit remains. Every affected top requires E as an AND factor and is centreless. Unaffected ordinary terms contribute only original stars, unaffected centreless tops give one anchored pure product, and each E-affected centreless top splits into two anchored clean products (its pure multiplicative guard times either J1 or J2). Thus there are at most 2c exterior top batches besides the two Ci batches.

The original near stars include at least two distinct edges: with a single visible raw neighbor, there is its x-edge and another near star. A common-reader AND stars yz, while a common-reader OR was excluded; with four individual OR readers at least two are in lambda. Such a lambda OR uses a second raw leaf different from its raw input by irredundancy. Its singleton opposite anchor therefore cannot be x when raw(S) is just one vertex, so it stars another near edge. With both neighbors visible xy,xz are already starred. Thus at most three of the five original near edges remain residual. Apply the G11 anchored batch footprint bound on H. Each batch has at most two original residuals; all doubles cannot be separately allocated except the unique homogeneous pair 14,59 when U>7. Indeed an H nonhomogeneous double requires at least seven internal stars (no near vertex can anchor two H vertices); with two near stars this forces U≤7. Thus U≤2c+6, sufficient for j≥4. Here and below the region c=0 can already be discharged by the established c=0,q=2 result; for c≥1 the escape U≤7 always suffices.

If both neighbors are visible, the original S remains live on the least 19-card x=false and hence has an external reader other than A. S cannot have a direct OR reader: such a reader is dirty on singletons, must be paid (the only common raw anchor x is available solely at A, upstream of that reader), and both E and Q are upstream of S. Therefore a direct AND use exists. If clean, its top is centreless because an ordinary top's nonempty opposite side would require the unavailable x. If dirty, follow its live AND path to a clean top; no additional dirty-AND-to-OR exit is available after S, so this top is centreless as well. It multiplicatively requires S. After Q is projected to R, raw S is supported on y,z only, so on H this entire top gives zero even with E intact. Remove at least one exterior top batch. This improves the bound to 2c+5, sufficient also for j=3.
