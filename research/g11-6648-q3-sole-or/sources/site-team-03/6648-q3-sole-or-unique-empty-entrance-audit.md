# Sole OR port and a distinct, unique raw-empty dirty entrance

Assume q=3, j≥3, least TopForm, and a sole raw reader M=x∨W for x=3. Assume the unique OR directly reading a dirty AND is E, it has empty raw support, and E differs from M. The remaining paid OR is F. Then U≤2c+j+3.

The sole-port argument says W is singleton-negative and has an independent positive witness. Its raw support is empty. It cannot be an AND (then M would be another direct entrance). Trace the OR-only cone of W backward. No raw wire can occur. No clean AND can occur, since dirty M must have a later AND to prevent singleton x from reaching the output. Every last dirty AND therefore enters its first OR at E. All OR-only paths from those ANDs pass via E, so W equals E as a function. Leastness excludes a distinct duplicate wire. Thus M=x∨E physically. Both pins of E are earlier dirty ANDs; an earlier raw-empty OR would require an earlier direct entrance. No assumption on their sharing is necessary.

Every independent positive assignment of E must contain both 9 and 10. Remove x, which E ignores. A neighbor y=6 or z=7 in the assignment would distinguish toggling x although M was already true. Both neighbors are thus absent, and each needs a neighbor in the remaining assignment: otherwise adjoin that one neighbor, still independently, and toggle x for the same contradiction. The external choices are precisely 10 and 9. Both direct arms D1,D2 imply E, so they are clean separately after deleting 9 and after deleting 10. The converse direction of the wire implication would be insufficient.

Before E the AND expansion of each arm has only pure raw clauses. Every nonsingleton clause is anchored at an original ordinary star, with at most one exception, the physically pure F if it occurs earlier. The children of that exception are raw or anchored. On each deletion slice, the one-exception clean-product lemma of 6648-clean-unique-direct-entrance.md gives ≤2 original residuals. Without an exceptional primitive these are an induced matching; the earlier ≤3 over two slices argument for D itself occurs in the opening of 6648-q3-sole-or-downstream-positive.md and 6648-q3-dirty-both-downstream.md.

With one exceptional primitive, union size four would mean two exclusive edges on each deletion, hence two incident to 9 on the minus-10 slice and two incident to 10 on the minus-9 slice. The one-exception spoke proof makes the exceptional support on each slice exactly the two mates and makes it omit the pivot. Consequently F's original support omits both 9 and 10. Its support is identical on the two slices; its same two vertices are common neighbors of 9 and 10, contradicting C4-freeness. Thus each arm accepts ≤3 original residuals in total. Every graph edge is represented by these slices since 9–10 is a nonedge.

Finally set E=false globally and M=x. The projection preserves all donor-negative edge inputs and only decreases gate outputs. Ordinary tops supply only original stars. Each projected centreless root has pure primitive clauses, at most one exceptional support F°, whose children are anchored/raw when they survive. Earlier E collapses to false, M to raw x, and every other uncharged raw projection retains its original anchored raw support. The one-exception lemma gives ≤2 residuals per centreless root. Adding ≤6 from D1,D2 proves the assertion. This is a source-only audit.
