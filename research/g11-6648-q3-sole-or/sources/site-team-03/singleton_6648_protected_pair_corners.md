# One protected mode counts as a single batch at |U|≥9

Take any family of pure conjunctions of original anchored clauses and singletons, each clean on both G−9 and G−10 (in particular it may be globally clean). Allocate original residuals exclusively. If |U|≥9 then at most seven original stars exist. On either safe deletion each batch is an induced matching of size ≤2. We show the full union has at most one edge per batch plus one global surplus.

Suppose a batch has 9u and 10v. If u=v=0 then any clause omitting 0 contains 9,10, whose unique anchor 0 stars both edges; otherwise the singleton 0 activates it. If u=0,v≠0 the cross 0–10 rules out primitive traces containing both 9,10 or both 0,v by original anchor obstruction. Each trace meeting both marked edges thus equals {0,10} or {9,v}. The independent 0v hits both, contradicting safety. Similarly v=0. For nonzero u,v the cross uv would force traces {u,v} or {9,10}; then independent 9v activates the batch, impossible.

Thus u=U_i and v=V_j for distinct indices in (U_i,V_i)=(5,2),(7,6),(8,4). The independent pairs 9v,10u,uv each survive a safe deletion and must be rejected. Clauses missed by those pairs must hit both original marks and hence contain respectively {u,10},{9,v},{9,10}. Their unique original anchors V_i,U_j,0 yield the six-spoke path
    U_i – V_i – 10 – 0 – 9 – U_j – V_j.
Distinct ordered pairs need ≥8 stars jointly: in addition to the common base 09,0–10, at most two of the four nonbase spokes are common (two bridges on reversal, or one bridge and one lateral spoke on sharing an index in position). The homogeneous double 14,59 requires both 18 and 58 outside this path, impossible.

A full safe nonhomogeneous double needs ≥7 stars. When exactly seven its four corner labels reuse just one anchor on two adjacent corners, yielding a six-cycle with one pendant edge. Its bipartition classes have sizes 3 (anchors) and 4 (marks). If this graph contains the displayed six-edge path the seventh edge must join two path vertices at distance five. Its four marks are precisely the even positions U_i,10,9,V_j. Only the already allocated 9U_i and 10V_j form a matching there, so such a double cannot be exclusive of the protected pair. A homogeneous and nonhomogeneous safe double cannot coexist either, as this seven-star graph has maximum degree three while homogeneity needs four original spokes at 8. Two distinct nonhomogeneous doubles would have the same bipartition mark class and two perfect matchings, forbidden by C4.

A triple in one protected product necessarily has one 9-edge, one 10-edge and a third edge surviving both deletions; otherwise one safe deletion contains three or two incident marks. Both slices would then have a double with that third edge. Under the forced six-star path the homogeneous case is impossible and the only possible nonhomogeneous marks give the original protected pair itself. Thus a triple is impossible. Any two-edge batch either forms such a protected pair, or has a double on a safe deletion. The preceding uniqueness and coexistence tests show at most one exclusively assigned double over all products. No computation was used.
