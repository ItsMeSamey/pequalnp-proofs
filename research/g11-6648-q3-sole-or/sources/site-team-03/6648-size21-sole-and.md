# Size 21 with vertex 3 read by one AND

Write x=3, y=6, z=7. In a 21-gate least TopForm graph realization suppose x has sole reader A=x∧S. The false-x lower bound 19 forces A's unique reader to be B=A∨V. Neither A nor B directly reads y,z: S is true on singleton y,z; V is false on both because every x path must distinguish xy from y and xz from z. Thus setting x false removes exactly A,B and yields a least 19-gate graph card without changes to physical direct readers of y,z; S remains live. Each coordinate y,z has at least two readers in the original by the independent-dominating-set obstruction to a sole OR, and because a sole AND sibling must be true on singleton x and on the other triangle vertex, whereas raw x alone is true on singleton x.

Let U be the union of the direct readers. Swap asymmetry rules out two shared-only readers. The 15-gate bound on H=G−{x,y,z} gives |U|≤4 by setting y=z=false on the 19-gate card. A forced-false gate in U must eventually have a reader outside U on a forward path, as each further U gate combines that zero with a false raw input and the output is nonconstant.

* If |U|=4 there is no forced-false U gate: one more removal would violate 15. Thus there is no common reader and every U gate is OR. Each of y,z has two distinct OR readers.
* If |U|=3 there is a common h=y⋆z. By leastness on the actual 19-card, h has no U reader since y,z are comparable to h. Every h reader is therefore outside U, but only one additional removal is possible. Hence h has one reader E, an OR with just one forced-false inlet; if E were itself false, continuation along U would force a second outside removal. There cannot be two distinct common readers, since each would have to feed E. The remaining individual readers Ry,Rz must be ORs: an individual forced-false AND would propagate via U until E, requiring entry through h, which reads raw y,z only. Hence each of y,z has two readers with only h shared. These gate types also hold in the original. The unique h→E use refers to the reduced circuit.
* For a common OR h in the original, if h belongs to Lambda its witnessing ordinary top has raw center x since only x neighbors both y,z. The only AND reachable from raw x by an OR-only route to one of its inputs is A. Thus A is clean, h reaches S by OR gates, and xy,xz are starred. Otherwise h is an explicit charged OR. A common AND h is clean and produces the minterm yz.

A further equal-size trial replaces B by B'=S∧(x∨V). It globally decreases and preserves all graph minterms unless B is essential on a core edge e with V(e)=1, S(e)=0. Indeed any edge meeting y,z has S positive. This gives a concrete necessary obstruction to the trial circuit computing G; if it succeeds, the resulting circuit's TopForm and signature still need independent justification. No complete exclusion for size 21 follows here.
