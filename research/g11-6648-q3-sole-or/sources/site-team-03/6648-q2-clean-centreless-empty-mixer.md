# Clean x3 sole AND with raw-empty sibling and a raw-empty exceptional OR

Assume the G11 least TopForm hypotheses, size 21, j≥3 and q=2. Put x=3, y=6, z=7. Let A=x∧S be clean and the sole reader of x, with raw(S) empty. Both charged ORs read dirty inputs and at least one has raw-empty output. We show U≤2c+j+2 for the original residual count and every c; A is among the centreless tops, so c≥1. The argument treats both the common-AND and four-distinct-OR reader alternatives without postulating a common reader.

1. No original star is incident to x since its sole raw path reaches A but A has empty opposite raw support. No Lambda support can contain both y and z, whose unique common neighbor is x. S is independent of x, accepts singleton y and singleton z, and is clean on H8=G−{x,y,z}: an independent positive there could be extended by x, activating A on an independent set. By the exact 19-card and 15-core reader argument in 6648-size21-sole-and.md, y and z each have two readers, either four different ORs or one common y⋆z and two separate ORs. A common OR cannot be Lambda by the unique common neighbor and cannot be either of the genuinely mixed paid ORs. There are at least two original stars touching y or z. If common AND, yz is starred, and at least one of the other two OR readers is Lambda (only one paid OR can be raw-positive). With four ORs both vertices have a Lambda reader. If only yz were available as a near star to witness any such Lambda, the raw v reader's opposite pin has a different raw leaf s: a pin OR-reaching v would absorb the separate raw v. The witnessing center t would be its triangle mate and would star ts also. In the four-reader case a single common near star must be yz to cover both vertices.

2. We exclusively allocate edges to clean anchored-conjunction batches as in 6648-q2-dirty-sole-and-complete.md §§6–7. No batch gets three and in the homogeneous regime at most one batch gets two (only 14 and 59). A nonhomogeneous double forces U≤7 by the same footprint lookup: x has no stars, each feasible seven/eight-corner footprint is seven stars internal to H8, and two more near stars are known. This suffices immediately for c≥1,j≥3. All batch counts below may thus use one extra edge globally.

3. A raw-empty dirty OR E with no charged OR descendant below the clean tops has only AND consumers up to its first clean tops. Any intermediate OR would be charged by its raw-empty pin (E itself or a dirty AND). Its only possible terminal tops are centreless. It reaches exactly one such top. For completeness, for E or an AND-only dirty descendant W, each such top implies W and has form W times its W=true cofactor. Project W=true only on the union of downstream dirty ANDs and their terminal tops (all consumers there are multiplicative). All direct AND readers of W disappear. Keep all preexisting siblings even if reused. Regroup the final pure-OR clean aggregation, factoring W once around all affected tops, using at most one new AND and no more aggregation ORs. True top cofactors absorb any other factors; constants can be simplified without extra gates. Two distinct direct readers would save one gate, contrary to leastness. Repeat the same argument at each dirty node on the path, giving just one terminal top. Regrouping costs at most the old aggregation size since k live top functions require ≥k−1 binary ORs to aggregate. No claim of private pure factors is used.

4. When the two paid gates are raw-empty, take their topologically later E as in (3). Every other centreless top expands into at most two pure anchored conjunctions by choosing an arm of the earlier raw-empty charge; E's sole affected top expands into at most four by choosing an arm at each exceptional OR. Pointwise these unions are exact because substituting each actual OR by whichever arm equals its value attains the original, while each fixed projection is smaller. No dirty AND can otherwise enter a pure raw OR without a further charged exit. Since both original exceptions are raw-empty, no ordinary top correction occurs. The homogeneous total is at most 2(c−1)+4+1=2c+3.

5. With one raw-empty E and one raw-positive P, suppose E is not upstream of P, so E has the unique top of (3). Write P=H∨R with R pure raw and H a pure anchored AND product (E is not upstream of P). After choosing an arm at E, a centreless top has at most two clean anchored terms under P. Expand its conjunctive frontier: the clauses receiving H through P are H∨R_i, where the raw union R_i is original and anchored (at P use R; at any Lambda extension use that gate's own raw support). Pure clauses are unchanged. The product is its fully raw term together with H times its unaffected clauses. Direct multiplicative occurrences of H may be retained as their pure factors. This works also if both inputs to a Lambda gate carry P, since repeated arrivals of H leave the same disjunction H∨R_i. Thus other centreless tops yield at most two terms and E's terminal top at most four. Ordinary tops cannot receive E except before a centreless AND; they contribute at most one extra clean term H times the union of opposite raw guards, whose union is anchored by any raw literal of R. A single ordinary top cannot have P on both sides due to P's independent dirty witness. The homogeneous total is ≤2(c−1)+4+1+1=2c+4.

6. Now E is upstream of P. Write E=E1∨E2 for pure dirty AND products, and P=H∨R with R raw-positive. E can only reach P via a direct pin or an AND-only sequence (the first OR after E or a dirty AND would be charged). Therefore H is E or E times a pure anchored product K. R is pure because any AND exit into its OR region would be a third charged OR. In particular H≤E; K may have no clauses. No other dirty-to-OR exits exist.

Expand S down through ANDs to input/OR primitive clauses. Each primitive accepts both singleton y and singleton z, and a pure anchored primitive cannot do so. Each impure primitive is either exactly E, or P extended by OR gates with form H∨R_i as in (5). An OR that first reads E (or an E-dependent dirty AND) must be P itself. In particular all impure alternatives outside P's raw support arise through its same donor H, irrespective of reconvergent Lambda paths. If there are no P-bearing primitives then leastness makes S=E. Otherwise H implies every primitive and so H≤S. Each P-bearing R_i is anchored and cannot contain both y,z. Consequently H accepts at least one singleton y or z. If it accepts both then every pure clause of K must contain both, impossible; H=E. Thus whenever S=E or H accepts both, E≤S and E is positive on both. The two pure products E1,E2 each accept at most one of the two singletons since no anchored primitive contains both, so one accepts y and the other z. Each is clean on H8 and accepts at most one H8 edge by (7). With two near stars at most three near residuals remain. Hence all E-positive residuals number at most five. On E-negative edges set E=false: H vanishes, P returns R, other Lambda gates return raw disjunctions accounting also for reconvergence. Ordinary tops only yield original stars, and each remaining centreless top an anchored conjunction. A's own two positive minterms are E-positive. Thus U≤5+2(c−1).

7. The one-singleton donor fact used here is as follows. A pure anchored product T positive at v∈{y,z}, clean on H8, independent of x, and upstream of A or H, has every primitive containing v. A singleton primitive forces T=v, with no accepted H8 edge. Otherwise the nonsingleton clauses have star anchor at the triangle mate or the private neighbor b of v, since x has no stars. For v=7 the mate-anchored clause must be {7,10}; the private anchor gives raw support within {7,0,5,8}. After y=z=false a mate clause requires 10, so only 0–10 can activate T on a core pair; if there is no mate clause, only 5–8 is an internal edge. For v=6 the total remaining support lies within {9,0,2,4}, with only the internal edge 0–9. In all cases singleton-rejection on H8 ensures pairs with an endpoint outside the remaining support cannot activate T. This establishes at most one H8 residual. For a product all of whose factors come from a single mate-anchored clause, its H8 cofactor is not clean; the implication above remains valid.

8. Finally suppose H accepts precisely one of y,z, called v, and T0 is the other. Then H≤S, and the two pure anchored products E1K,E2K are clean on H8. At least one is positive at v, so by (7) it contributes ≤1 H8 residual; the other contributes ≤2 by the ordinary anchored clean-batch bound on H8. There are five edges touching y,z, at least two starred, and H misses xT0 since it is x-independent. This edge is definitely residual (no star touches x), leaving at most two near residuals for H. Hence H accounts for ≤5 residuals globally. Pay these and the single edge xT0 separately, covering all edges of A since xv activates H. Set P to R on H-negative pairs: only the raw-empty E survives. It and its AND continuations can reach ordinary operand ORs only through the deleted exceptional exit P, so ordinary tops yield original stars. Each other centreless top yields ≤2 anchored terms by choosing an E arm. For Lambda gates which had received P multiple ways, P→R restores the exact original raw support, while any E path through an AND could enter their OR-only region only through P. Thus the total in the homogeneous regime is ≤5+1+2(c−1)+1=2c+5≤2c+j+2. Together with (2) this proves all promised placements.

This is a mathematical deduction, relying on the graph-specific card and footprint lemmas. The separate premises_6648_raw_empty_two_positive_mixers.md and its four-distinct-OR supplement concern two raw-positive dirty-reading mixers. Neither is a hypothesis of the proof above. A paid pure OR is outside the stated premise.
