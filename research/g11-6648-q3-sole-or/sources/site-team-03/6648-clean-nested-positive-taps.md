# Two positive mixers with nested donors under a raw-visible clean sole AND

Assume the issued size-21 least TopForm hypotheses, j≥3, q=2, and that x=3 has sole reader A=xS, clean, with nonempty raw support of S. Put y=6,z=7 and H=G−{x,y,z}. The two charged gates are M1=D1∨P1 and M2=D2∨P2, distinct positive-raw mixers in chronological order, with dirty AND inputs. Allow D2 to depend on M1. A tap means an OR-only path to S. The result is U≤2c+j+2, with no restriction on dirty-cone sharing. The previously proved c=0 case can be used; below c≥1.

Use the source facts in 6648-size21-sole-and.md, singleton_6648_clean_two_taps.md and 6648-q2-dirty-sole-and-complete.md. S is independent of x, accepts both singleton y,z, and ≤ y∨z on independent inputs; its raw support is contained in {y,z}. Since x=false must leave 19 gates after A and its sole OR reader are removed, S has another live reader. At most three of the five edges outside H are residual. With both raw neighbors present, both x-edges are starred. With one present, the y,z-reader classification gives a second outside star: in the four-reader case an uncharged individual OR is Lambda, and its other raw input differs from its direct vertex (else the OR is redundant). An anchor x would force both raw leaves into S. In the three-reader case a common AND stars yz; a common OR cannot be either direct dirty-input charge and requires the forbidden x anchor.

In H, exclusively assigned residuals of a clean conjunction of anchored clauses form an induced matching of size ≤2, with original star anchors. At most one batch may own a homogeneous double, since its two edges must be 14 and 59. Any nonhomogeneous double forces at least seven original starred H edges. Together with the two outside stars this yields U≤7, sufficient since c≥1 and j≥3. Hence it suffices in all cases to bound the number of H batches by 2c+1; then at most 2c+2 H residuals plus at most three outside suffice.

Shrinking a positive mixer to its raw partner is sound on assignments with its donor false. After all dirty-to-OR pins are erased, each ordinary positive pair is starred, and each centreless top gives one anchored raw batch. With one such mixer left, each centreless top gives at most two clean conjunctions by positive expansion. All ordinary dirty contributions form at most one conjunction D·Hguard, with a common raw vertex of the mixer anchoring the union Hguard of opposite raw leaves by actual star edges. The same mixer cannot be an OR disjunct on both sides of an original clean top due to its independent dirty witness. After projection, use ORIGINAL ordinary containment, so the common anchor relations persist. Any false clauses or terms are discarded. These statements count logical batches, not allocated physical gates.

If both mixers tap S, D1 and D2° (project M1 to its raw partner throughout D2) are individually bounded by S, hence clean on H by adjoining x to an independent assignment. D1 and D2° are anchored conjunctions (or zero). Any H edge on which either original donor is true belongs to one of these two batches: take the earliest true donor. Remaining H edges are raw-projection edges, contributing at most c batches. Here c+2≤2c+1.

For one tap, S on H equals the tapped donor, since its raw inputs lie in y,z and all other OR-only terminals below it supply no other donor. An OR strictly above S on an external path cannot be Lambda: any ordinary top witnessing it would have on the opposite side a raw vertex adjacent to *both semantic singleton positives* y,z; only x is possible, and x's sole raw reader is A whose S pin is earlier. An OR-only path to the output would accept a singleton. Until a later charge is reached, therefore, the external path uses AND gates only. Its clean endpoint is centreless: an intermediate AND gives a raw-empty pin, while an immediate ordinary AND would again require opposite x. This argument works with just one visible raw neighbor.

If only M1 taps, D1 itself is clean on H, giving one batch. Shrink M1 on the remaining D1-negative H residuals. M2 now has a purely projected anchored donor D2° and projected positive raw partner. There are at most 1+2c further batches before savings. Follow an external S path. If it reaches a centreless top via only ANDs, that top requires S and cannot own any D1-negative H residual. If the next exit is M2's dirty input then D2≤S by the AND path; D2° can accept no D1-negative H residual, eliminating the dirty-sided aggregate. If the path enters M2's positive raw pin, it can have no intervening AND (such a pin with positive raw reach would then require another charged exit from the AND); no intervening OR is Lambda. The mixer inherits both singleton positives, so no ordinary top OR-reaches it, eliminating the ordinary aggregate. The raw-only centreless terms remain in all cases. Thus at most 2c+1 H batches suffice, including D1.

If only M2 taps, its positive raw pin cannot OR-reach M1. In the AND region of D2, pure clauses give K and each M1-containing factor is D1∨R_i with anchored R_i. If D1 occurs directly as an AND factor, then D2=D1K where K contains the pure factors, and mixed factors are absorbed. Otherwise D2 is a union of at most two terms, its raw projection D2° and D1K; omit the latter if there is no M1-containing factor. Both terms are clean on H since bounded by D2≤S there. Thus D2-positive H residuals cost at most two batches. Shrink M2 to its raw pin for D2-negative edges; M1 remains the only mixer and contributes at most one ordinary and two per centreless top. No later charge is available on the external S path, so a centreless top requires S and accepts no D2-negative H residual. At most 2+1+2(c−1)=2c+1 batches remain.

With zero taps, the raw-visible S is pure: no clean AND can lie below A, and every dirty-to-OR entry would be a tap. Both raw neighbors are present and any such least pure OR cone contains their literal common OR, excluded at q=2 by premises_6648_common_or_all_c.md. This leaves no unhandled positive nested tap case under the stated hypotheses. The note is a source-level argument, not a kernel verification.
