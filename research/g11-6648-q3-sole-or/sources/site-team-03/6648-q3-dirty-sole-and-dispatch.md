# Physical dispatch for dirty sole g=3∧S, q=3

The mandatory charged gate is M=g∨D, raw-empty and the only reader of g. Classify the two other charged ORs in the original circuit. A direct gate reads a dirty AND pin. An inherited gate has no such pin but inherits a dirty AND on an OR-only path. A pure gate has neither. Signs 0,+ mean empty and positive original raw support. Two dirty inputs into one OR count as one direct gate.

Without a pure exception at least one direct secondary must be an ancestor of S. Otherwise S has a pure conjunctive frontier. Since it is positive on 6 and 7 singletons, each primitive contains both; only possible anchor 3 never stars, so a pure charge would be necessary. With one direct Q this forces Q≺S≺g≺M, whether or not W feeds S too. An empty M costs a charge at its first pretop OR exit; if W inherits M it therefore reads M directly. W's other pin with no independent Q or M OR path can only be pure; a dirty AND input would instead make W direct. Duplicate M pins are redundant. With two direct Q,T, name Q in S, taking the earlier if both feed S. T has three exclusive positions relative to M: strict predecessor (feeds S only, D only, or both), strict descendant, or incomparable. Within each flag ancestry between Q,T and shared donor identities separately; no red-row result assumes independence. Both strictly downstream with no pure exception is impossible by the pure-frontier argument (also subject to the separate conditional source 6648-q3-dirty-both-downstream.md). The table describes necessary partitions, not realization of every flag.

For the Q0 inherited-no-M row the minimum 19-card has Q=K1∨K2 with distinct dirty ANDs whose products have anchored primitive clauses. W is the only positive-raw restoration Q∨R. Set Q=false everywhere for one raw batch per centreless root (ordinary terms are stars). Set Q=true and independently multiply its pure projected top guard by K1 or K2; Ki implies Q, so each product is clean. The ordinary correction yields two more Ki times the union of opposite guards anchored by R. Thus there are ≤3c+2 anchored batches, plus two x edges; the no-x-star footprint adds at most one double for U≥8. Distinct raw-empty direct operands of centreless tops in the least card are chosen from j−1 surviving dirty ANDs plus Q, giving c≤j. At c=j−1 or c=j at least two or three of K1,K2,Q respectively occur directly at distinct tops. Each such top is false on actual Q=false, saving its low batch; this pays the deficit. For U≤7 the target is immediate if c≥1; if c=0 two ordinary batches and two x edges give ≤6. This extension has no independent review or new computation.
