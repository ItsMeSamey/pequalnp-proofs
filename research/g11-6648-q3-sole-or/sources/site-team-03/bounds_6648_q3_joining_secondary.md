# q=3: the last OR joins the upstream mixer and the original sole x-AND channel

Under the issued least size-21 TopForm and j≥3 hypotheses, let x=3 be read solely by the dirty g=x∧S, with unique reader M=g∨D. Consider exactly the three exceptional OR gates Q,M,W. Here Q is a direct dirty-AND entrance upstream of M (in S or D or both), and W is downstream of M and receives M on one pin and Q on an OR-only path to its other pin that avoids M. In particular mere Q→M→W reach is not the joining hypothesis. There are no other charged gates; the clean output aggregation is outside this definition. We prove U≤2c+j+3. We use the false-x floor 19, the singleton-negative donor lemma, and the original no-x-star anchored-double footprint from 6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md paragraph 1 and its cited proofs; the footprint itself is a graph lemma for exclusive batches of clean anchored terms and is independent of the all-charges-empty hypothesis of the encompassing theorem.

M has empty raw reach. Any first pretop OR leaving M along an OR-only route to W is charged, so W reads M directly. The second pin P receives Q by the specified separate OR-only route. For positive-raw Q, write Q=K∨R, K a dirty AND and R a pure positive-raw wire. No earlier mixed OR exists, as Q precedes M,W, so K is a pure-clause conjunction and R is a literal or an anchored pure disjunction. A positive-raw Q cannot OR-reach D as D rejects all singletons; and it cannot OR-reach g, an AND. Thus the Q OR-only signal indeed enters W through P. P is either Q or an original Lambda OR; its raw support L contains raw(R), and L itself is a literal or originally anchored (when P=Q use R). Every originally nonempty OR after Q and before W with multiplier K has raw-positive inputs and keeps a raw superset of R. Every OR after W inheriting W similarly keeps a raw superset of L. When Q is raw-empty, if the two OR-only inputs M and Q meet without any earlier charge, W is also raw-empty: an earlier join restoring a raw input would cost a fourth gate. This all-raw-empty case is covered by 6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md. Equivalently the new argument below requires positive-raw Q and an OR-only Q path to the non-M pin of W.

Set x=false. The actual minimum 19-gate cofactor deletes only g,M and aliases M to D. This is the sole cardinality use of that projection; count the two original x-edges separately and keep the original starred edges as labels. D is a physical dirty AND in the positive-Q case: a raw input, a pure OR, or an OR inheriting Q's positive raw support cannot be singleton-negative, and no earlier raw-empty charged OR exists. Outside the aggregation the only non-pure OR entrances in the card are Q and W, the latter now reading D as one pin. Direct uses of D, K, and ANDs containing them elsewhere are retained.

Use actual K as a positive guard, cofactoring just its specified pin at Q. On the guard K=true, Q and its OR-only descendants are true, including P and W. Every retained AND-only factor prior to W whose ancestors include Q reduces to a pure conjunction of its remaining anchored clauses. In particular D becomes a pure conjunction B or a constant: OR descendants of Q are true, and unaffected primitive wires are original pure Lambda supports or literals. This handles D whether upstream of K, downstream of Q, or incomparable to Q. Direct K factors may remain and are themselves pure anchored conjunctions. For each centreless top keep one K-guarded pure conjunction. It implies that top: when K is true this evaluates exactly, and when false it vanishes. For ordinary tops receiving Q or W, one affected side is true, and the other is pure. Both sides cannot inherit these mixers since both then raw-reach the same nonempty R and would accept its singleton. Aggregate their pure opposite guards into H_K. The K correction is one clean batch K∧H_K; each vertex of R has original star edges to every vertex in H_K, so this union is anchored. All other ordinary terms in this mode contribute only stars.

For assignments with actual K=false, globally substitute false only at Q's donor pin. The resulting circuit decreases pointwise and agrees with the actual one on K=false. Q projects to R; each pre-W mixed OR projects to its ORIGINAL raw disjunction, so P→L and D→a pure anchored conjunction A (possibly constant). Direct K uses remain their original pure terms. W is now A∨L. For every subsequent OR-only cone carrying W, its boundary is A∨L' with L' the original raw support containing L. Such L' is an anchored original Lambda support or L itself. Any other downstream dirty AND-to-OR return is forbidden by the charge inventory. For each centreless top use at most two pure conjunctions: set A's W-pin false to obtain its raw branch, or conjoin A to the expression obtained by setting that W-pin true and dropping the W-affected OR clauses. Both are subterms of the reduced clean top, which is below the original top even on K=true. Direct appearances of D elsewhere have already become A, so no assumption of a separate private copy occurs. Ordinary tops can contribute at most the correction A∧H_A, where H_A is the union of pure projected opposite guards over W-affected tops. Both sides cannot contain W because of their common raw L. An opposite guard may earlier have inherited Q; it has now become its original raw support. Every vertex of L anchors H_A through original ordinary stars. This proves cleanliness and original anchoring of all three modal batches per centreless top and both ordinary batches. If a projected operand is constant, drop a true clause or omit a false product. If a correction is empty, omit it.

Allocate the surviving original residuals exclusively once to these at most 3c+2 pure anchored batches, using the K-positive batches on actual K=true and the remaining batches otherwise. For U≥8 the no-x-star footprint implies at most one global double, so U≤2+(3c+2)+1=3c+5. It suffices when c≤j−2. Every original centreless top on the least card has at least one raw-empty direct operand, which must be an AND because Q and W are raw-positive and M aliases the raw-empty AND D. Such a wire is one of the j−1 surviving original dirty ANDs, not a clean original top by TopForm. Two different top roots cannot share a direct operand, since (F·G)∨(F·H) can be replaced by F·(G∨H) after regrouping the clean top aggregation, strictly saving a gate. Choose one such operand per root: c≤j−1. If c=j−1, every surviving dirty AND is chosen, including K; the particular root directly reading K contributes no edge on actual K=false. For that root both second-stage batches may be omitted, reducing the preceding upper bound by two. Thus c=j−1 is also paid. When U≤7 and c≥1 the target is immediate. At c=0 only two ordinary batches exist, each contains at most two edges by the basic anchored-matching lemma even without the stronger U≥8 footprint; including the x spokes gives U≤6. The conclusions concern original unprojected residuals, since every non-x minterm was retained on its assigned actual mode and all anchors are original.
