# Raw-positive S and a raw-empty charge E having no other charge downstream

Let x=3 have sole clean reader A=x AND S and let raw(S) be nonempty, with y=6,z=7. Assume q=2 and a genuinely mixed empty OR E. If both charges are raw-empty, neither can inject a nonraw signal into the OR cone of S: the first OR on the route at which raw support becomes nonempty must have a raw-empty dirty inlet and therefore be a raw-positive charge. A clean AND below A is forbidden. Thus S is pure, contains the common physical OR of y,z by leastness, and contradicts the common-OR theorem. This argument also covers an empty charge with a pure charge when S has no mixed positive injection.

Suppose the other charge P is positive and E is not its ancestor. E cannot OR-reach S, as its first OR reader would have to be a further charge. If P does not supply a mixed signal to S, apply the same pure/common-OR argument. Otherwise P OR-reaches S and directly reads a dirty AND D: any earlier dirty-AND-to-OR frontier would be a charge and E is not upstream. P's other input is pure with nonempty raw support. D is a product of anchored pure clauses, since no earlier charge is an ancestor of P. D implies S, so every independent D-positive set must contain y or z (otherwise adjoining x contradicts cleanliness of A). On H=G minus x,y,z, D is clean.

At least two of the five edges outside H are original stars: if both neighbors are visible this follows at A. With one visible neighbor, the exact 19-card reader classification gives either a common AND starring yz, or four individual OR readers. A common OR is already excluded. At least one individual OR is uncharged. Its raw other pin has a leaf distinct from its direct coordinate by irredundancy. An x anchor would put two distinct leaves in raw(S); hence its original star at the directly read vertex is a second outside star.

As in 6648-q2-clean-centreless-empty-mixer.md §3, E reaches exactly one clean top, necessarily centreless, so c≥1. This uses physical leastness on the original circuit: every consumer down to a clean top is an AND, and any dirty node with two distinct such direct consumers can be factored once across all affected clean tops, saving at least one AND after rebuilding their final OR aggregation.

On D-negative H inputs shrink P to its raw partner, preserving all evaluations there and decreasing them elsewhere. Both E arms are now pure products of anchored clauses (raw supports at pure ORs are their original ones), since the only mixed positive exit was P. E's sole top expands into two clean anchored batches by distributing E, while the other c−1 centreless tops contribute one batch each and ordinary tops only stars. With the D batch this gives c+2 H batches in total. Allocate exclusively. In the homogeneous regime at most one double survives, and U≤3+(c+2)+1=c+6≤2c+5. A nonhomogeneous H double yields seven internal original stars, as no vertex x,y,z has two H neighbors, giving U≤7 with the two outside stars. Both bounds suffice for j≥3 and c≥1.
