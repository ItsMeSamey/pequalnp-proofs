# One charged OR, vertex 3 a sole OR port: all centreless counts on G11

Assume the official G11 hypotheses, q=1, and the only direct reader of x=3 is M=x OR D. This branch imposes no bound on dirty sharing, number of affected ordinary tops, or j,c.

By the sole-port argument in singleton_6648_size21_c0q2_sole_or.md, D is independent of x and singleton-negative: every independent positive set of D, with x stripped, avoids 6,7 and dominates both, and therefore contains both 9 and 10. M is dirty on {3}. Liveness forces some AND descendant of M (an OR-only path could not end at the clean output). No ancestor AND below M can be clean, by TopForm. D has empty raw support by singleton negativity, so M is a charged OR. If D were an OR, its own OR-only boundary would be raw-empty, would have dirty AND ancestors immediately behind that boundary by TopForm and exact binary finite wiring, and would therefore also be dirty and non-Lambda, contradicting q=1. Thus D is a dirty AND. No other OR can directly read a dirty AND, since such a gate is dirty on its AND operand's independent witness and has a raw-empty operand.

The conjunction cone of D has only pure raw primitives, anchored in ordinary stars whenever nonsingleton (M lies later and all earlier positive raw ORs outside BelowF are Lambda). With d distinct dirty AND ancestors including D there are at most d+1 primitive clauses by binary connectivity. D is clean on each graph restriction deleting 9 or 10, because its every independent positive contains them both. On each restriction the induced-matching and endpoint-orientation proof of singleton_6648_size21_c0q2_sole_or.md bounds the accepted original residuals by floor(log2(d+1)). Original star anchors suffice even if a deleted vertex belonged to their raw supports. The vertices 9,10 form a nonedge, so every graph edge survives at least one restriction. There are therefore at most 2 floor(log2(d+1))<=d+1<=j+1 D-positive residuals.

For the remaining residual pairs replace M by its primary input x. On those pairs the entire evaluation is unchanged as D=false. On every other input the replacement only decreases signals. A clean top remains bounded by the graph function, even when original dirty cones had shared gates. Since no OR other than M directly read a dirty AND and TopForm excluded all clean AND ancestry of an AND, every resulting top expands as a conjunction down to pure raw-disjunction boundary clauses (including raw singletons). The support of each such raw clause is its original OR-reachable set, since M had raw support {3} even before replacement. Each nonsingleton boundary root was an OR outside BelowF and distinct from M, hence Lambda. Its original witness gives an ordinary star with a common neighbor joined by star edges to all support vertices. For an ordinary top in particular, its two original raw-reaching sides now evaluate to their plain disjunctions: a side with nonempty raw reach cannot contain any direct dirty AND except behind the charged OR M. Consequently every positive pair at an ordinary top is an original star edge. Only centreless original tops may accept any remaining residual on the raw projection.

For each such projected clean conjunction, the residuals it accepts form an induced matching by the anchored-clause argument. If three marked edges existed, every one of their eight endpoint transversals would be independent and rejected. Select for each transversal a missed clause. That clause contains the three unselected mates, one from each edge, and consequently has a physical Lambda star anchor. Transversals differing in one choice have two unselected mates in common, so their anchors agree by C4-freeness. Flipping coordinates one at a time shows all eight anchors coincide. Every one of the six endpoints appears among the mates of at least one transversal and would therefore neighbor that same anchor. This contradicts maximum degree four in G11. Thus each centreless top covers at most two residuals of the remaining batch, with no requirement that distinct batches have disjoint gates or vertices.

Every positive output has a clean top witness, by expanding its clean OR aggregation; the preceding count gives U<=j+1+2c. This is precisely the balanced conclusion at q=1. The new part here is the degree-four raw-projection bound combined with G11-specific D safety; a general q=1 theorem for arbitrary graphs is not assumed. The size-21 private-donor exclusion is unnecessary in this proof.
