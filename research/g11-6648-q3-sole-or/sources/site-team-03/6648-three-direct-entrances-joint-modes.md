# Joint projection at three physical dirty-AND-to-OR entrances

Assume q=3 and that THREE distinct ORs below the clean top aggregation each directly read at least one dirty AND. Count physical gates, not inlet incidences. These gates are exactly all exceptional ORs; every other pre-top OR is Lambda. TopForm excludes clean AND ancestry at a dirty supplier or below a clean top. This original condition is essential for the assertion that every surviving nonsingleton raw clause has an original star anchor.

Treat each raw-empty exceptional OR by a two-way choice of its actual input wires. In each mode replace its output by the chosen wire everywhere. No additional obligation is imposed. A raw-positive exceptional OR has precisely one dirty AND pin d and a raw-positive other pin. Group all such exits by identical physical d. Choose one bit per group. With bit zero each exit forwards its own raw-facing pin (which may depend on earlier exits); with bit one each exit is symbolically true. Impose once per one-bit group that the projected value of its supplier d is true. Compute these values in the same topological projection as the top, preserving multiplicative uses of d. Raw-empty exits may forward another earlier raw-empty OR rather than a direct AND; the rule still applies.

For any original clean top T its function equals the union over modes of its projected value times all the required supplier values. Under the obligations each projected signal is at most its original value, inductively: forwarding an OR input never increases a gate; forcing an exit true is sound because its projected supplier is true and lies earlier. Conversely for each assignment select a maximizing actual input at each raw-empty exit and set each group bit to its true original supplier value. Then all wires retain their original values and all obligations hold. Apply this argument to the ancestral cone of the top, omitting unused switches. A direct supplier group can have different reachable exits and paths without any additional copy of the obligation.

In a fixed mode each raw-positive pre-top OR is true or a disjunction of its original raw support. For an uncharged gate the support is originally Lambda-anchored. For a raw-positive exceptional gate forwarded to its other pin, inherit the support or anchor of that pin, following earlier forwarded positive exceptional gates if needed. A raw-empty OR can never directly enter an uncharged OR, whose two pins both have nonempty raw supports. Thus expansion of all ANDs yields pure products of anchored disjunctions, raw singletons and foldable constants. Products of these with all obligation products are again single products, irrespective of shared ancestry and reconvergence. They are clean when the starting top is clean. In particular each centreless top gives at most 2^(b+s) such products, where b counts causal raw-empty exceptional gates and s counts different causal physical dirty suppliers to positive exits. This is at most eight in the specified setting, even if the three ORs have a total of six direct dirty input pins. It is an upper bound on certificates, not a gate charge.

For ordinary tops, expand their two operand OR cones directly in the original circuit. Each side is its raw disjunction plus the dirty suppliers of its raw-positive exits (including earlier exits reached through the raw-facing pins). A raw-empty exit could not appear directly on such an OR path: the first OR acquiring positive raw support would then lack a direct dirty AND pin, contrary to exhaustion of q. If it reenters through a dirty AND, its contribution is already encapsulated in that later direct supplier.

The union of all ordinary tops is the original stars together with d_i times G_i for each raw-positive exit i and d_i times d_j for each realized opposite-side pair. Here G_i is the union of all original opposite raw guards at ordinary tops reached by i, and is anchored by any fixed raw leaf of i's other pin. An opposite-side pair must be realized at the same original top, never guessed from independent occurrences. An exit cannot be on both sides, and two distinct exits with the same or comparable dirty donors cannot make such a pair, because a dirty independent positive for the smaller gate would activate the ordinary top. Expand each of these global correction terms once using only the earlier switches it actually needs. For a joint term already requiring d_i, any positive exit reading d_i can be forced true with that same obligation. Do not assume G_i∪G_j has a shared anchor just because i and j have the same supplier.

The mode invariant holds without applying leastness to any adjusted circuit, and it also generalizes to any number q when all paid gates are direct entrances. It alone does not bound the total allocated batches by j+2c+q: unrelated roots may need different raw guards or may attain different terms of the same modes. Gates outside the chosen cones cannot be called private merely because their witnesses were not assigned in one mode. If a paid OR need not have a dirty AND pin, surviving raw disjunctions can instead require extra unanchored support accounting.
