# Dirty sole AND with both further mixer gates downstream, q=3

Assume the G11 size-21 least TopForm hypotheses, x=3 has sole reader g=x∧S dirty, and q=3. Its unique reader is M=g∨D, a dirty raw-empty OR. Assume the two remaining exceptional ORs Q1,Q2 both descend from M and each directly reads a dirty AND or a dirty raw-empty OR; no paid pure disjunction or unsupported positive-raw OR is assumed. Write them in topological order. Downstream includes paths using arbitrary AND gates; neither disjointness of cones nor domination of every M-to-output path is required.

Use 6648-q2-dirty-sole-and-complete.md for the source interface, original-star H footprints and unique possible homogeneous double. D is x-independent, singleton-negative, and every independent positive contains both 9 and 10. Hence D is clean after deleting either. D lies before M and is a product of pure anchored clauses: no prior exceptional OR exists. It cannot itself be a raw-empty OR, which would require an earlier exception; it is an AND. Every graph edge survives at least one of these deletions because 9 and 10 are nonadjacent. Allocate original residuals accepted by g and D first. The g slots are xy,xz (y=6,z=7). There are at most two D batches, one per deletion; their union contains at most three residuals, since any leftover from the first deletion contains the same vertex and the second batch is an induced matching.

Replace M by false on its outgoing pins. This decreases the circuit everywhere and preserves all M-negative graph pairs. Until Q1 a path from M to its first OR uses only ANDs and the original M, so the receiving pin is zero. A clean AND on such a path could not reach another AND, nor could it lead through a dirty OR to a clean output without a further AND. The other pin after projection is either zero, a raw disjunction, or a product of pure anchored clauses when raw-empty. A raw-positive pin cannot receive dirty information prior to Q1. Any surviving raw disjunction is singleton or retains its star anchor via its original pure Lambda gate. If Q1's surviving value is a nontrivial conjunction, Q1 originally had empty raw reach. In that case an OR directly reading Q1 would be charged, and the only possible further such exit before a top is Q2. These observations remain valid on reconverging paths.

Consequently pre-Q2 ordinary OR signals project to raw clauses and AND-only products to conjunctions of anchored clauses (or zero). The single surviving Q2 selector either has nonempty raw reach, and is now an anchored conjunction OR its pure anchored raw partner, or originally has empty raw reach, and is now an OR of at most two anchored conjunctions. If Q2 is raw-positive, ordinary corrections aggregate into at most one clean anchored batch: the common raw partner anchors the union of all opposite raw guards by ordinary star edges. The same Q2 cannot occur on both sides of an ordinary top, since the original Q2 has a dirty independent witness. If Q2 is raw-empty it cannot feed an uncharged OR below any AND and cannot contribute to ordinary tops. The centreless tops each provide at most two anchored batches in either case: expand the single positive selector into raw and donor sides, or expand its two conjunctive alternatives just once in a multiplicative region. Original clean tops and their projected subterms remain clean by monotone shrinking. Singleton/raw supports at projected positive ORs are taken from the original graph, and a false clause gives no edges.

Assign remaining M-negative residuals exclusively to these batches, never ordinary raw stars. Each batch is an induced residual matching of size ≤2, checked on the whole graph or on a protective deletion with original star anchors. No x edge remains in them. In the absence of a nonhomogeneous double, all batches together have at most one extra edge beyond their first (the unique homogeneous double 14,59). Including 2 individual g edges, 2 D batches, 1 ordinary batch and at most 2c centreless batches gives U≤2c+6.

A nonhomogeneous double forces at least seven original stars; if fewer than nine stars are forced by its corners alone, the enumerated small footprints have at least seven stars internal to G−{3,6,7}. This is the existing G11 corner lemma under no x star, before the q=2 near-star improvement. At least one star incident to y or z is present: in the four-reader case four raw OR readers of y,z include at least two Lambda gates because M is raw-empty and at most the two further mixers can use a raw vertex directly. A common AND reader stars yz; a common pure OR reader cannot be any of the three charged gates and its only possible Lambda anchor would be x, which cannot star any edge. This gives U≤8, sufficient for c≥1, j≥3.

For c=0 use the direct count, independent of all footprint cases: U≤2+3+2=7, sufficient for j≥4. When j=3, g is outside the upstream AND cone of D, so that cone contains at most two ANDs, yielding at most three primitive clauses by connected binary DAG counting. A deletion batch having two induced residual edges requires four distinct clauses (four independent orientations); hence at most one edge per deletion, and U≤2+2+2=6. In every case U≤2c+j+3, as required by the issued balance.

This source-level class does not treat cases with an additional exceptional OR that fails to descend from M, nor arbitrary paid pure disjunctions. No Lean or new graph sweep was used.
