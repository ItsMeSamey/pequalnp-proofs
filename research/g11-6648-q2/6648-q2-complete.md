# The two-exceptional-OR case of the issued G11 inequality

Take the original circuit and graph from L-meta-6648, and impose q=2. Write j≥3 for the number of junk ANDs, c for centreless tops, and U for the cardinality of Ustar. The component arguments below give U≤2c+j+2 for all c. For the selected original sets X and J, the balance |X|+j=U+|J| yields |X|≤2c+|J|+2. No transfer of X, J, or their fibers to a cofactor is needed.

The published G11 lower bound and the issued size cap imply size 21. By the false-3 floor 19 and the two-OR exclusion, input 3 has exactly one physical reader. The sole-OR case is in 6648-q2-sole-or-index.md, and the dirty sole-AND case is in 6648-q2-dirty-sole-and-complete.md. The following dispatch treats the remaining clean sole AND A=3 AND S. The already-reviewed c=0 theorem applies when invoked within individual cases.

Count distinct OR gates in the original circuit which directly read at least one dirty AND. Every such OR is paid: it inherits that AND's independent positive witness and its directly read AND operand has empty raw support, precluding Lambda. Therefore this physical count is 0, 1, or 2.
 
* At count zero both paid ORs are pure. An impure OR with a dirty AND ancestor has a first OR reading an AND. A clean AND ancestor of a paid OR would reach a subsequent AND, by liveness and rejection of the independent positive witness at the output, contradicting TopForm. Apply twin-6648-two-pure-residual-count.md.
* At count one use 6648-clean-unique-direct-entrance.md. The other paid OR may be physically pure or inherit an impure signal without a direct dirty AND pin. Both cases are included.
* At count two both paid ORs directly read dirty ANDs. Classify their physical raw supports as empty or nonempty. When both are nonempty and S is raw-empty, use premises_6648_raw_empty_two_positive_mixers.md. If S is raw-positive, use 6648-clean-nested-positive-taps.md. Order the two positive mixers by gate index. Its proof permits the earlier mixer to reach the later dirty donor and also applies when it does not. In particular it covers equal or comparable dirty donors, shared pure factors, and all four choices of which paid ORs reach S through OR gates alone. The earlier independent-case derivations in singleton_6648_clean_one_tap.md and singleton_6648_clean_two_taps.md can alternatively be used under their respective hypotheses.
* At count two with at least one raw-empty paid OR, if S is raw-empty use 6648-q2-clean-centreless-empty-mixer.md. For raw-positive S, the placement where a raw-empty OR is upstream of the other, raw-positive mixer is treated in premises_6648_empty_upstream_visible_S.md. Both raw-empty, and the other placements of one raw-empty mixer, are treated in singleton_6648_clean_positive_s_last_empty.md.
