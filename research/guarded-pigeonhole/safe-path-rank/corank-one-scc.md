# Private affine basis and component composition

Use the setting and path formula in corank-one-diagonal.md. Choose one multi-source breadth-first search forest on R, rooting it at every marked vertex. Let p_j be the path indicator to j and set each d_j to the formula for its forest path. At this common candidate every p_j violates exactly the complementarity equation at j: rows outside its support vanish and the previous prefix rows have zero residual as in the path proof. The p_j are triangular with ones on the diagonal in a breadth-first vertex order, hence linearly independent. They lie in beta.x=1 and form an affine basis of this hyperplane on the R coordinates. Thus if the flats of violations cover that hyperplane, the cover is irredundant with explicit private points. A cover verdict at the candidate is not implied by those private points.

Let U be a source strongly connected component and D the remaining vertices. Thus there are no arcs from D to U. Conditional on a downstream feasible y, the equations on U have their fixed off-diagonal part and diagonal d_U+Ly, where L=A[U,D]. Zero on U is always feasible. For a parity beta=(b,c), first test whether some downstream feasible y has c.y=1; any such y succeeds by zero extension. Otherwise the downstream contribution is identically zero. If b=0 reject. If b is nonzero, every vertex of U is reachable from its marks in the local graph. Compute its unique candidate d*. If even at d* an odd b support exists, then every downstream word allows an odd b support, in particular zero. If d* is truly NO, success occurs exactly when d_U+Ly differs from d* for some feasible y. Test y=0 and then each row of L on a basis of the downstream feasible span. A row test may use actual independent feasible representatives for that span. Path prefixes give the upstream witness when the diagonals differ; at d* use the candidate oracle's witness.

Process components in reverse topological order, maintaining a basis consisting of actual feasible vectors of their union. Given the preceding witness test for an arbitrary beta using the downstream basis and a local candidate oracle, construct the enlarged span by querying a basis of the annihilator of the currently collected vectors. A positive answer supplies a new independent feasible vector. If every query in that annihilator basis is negative, all feasible vectors lie in the collected span. At most quadratically many queries per component suffice. Thus general single-parity feasibility and its feasible span reduce with polynomial overhead to marked candidate decisions/witnesses on individual strongly connected blocks. When each block has at most k vertices, exhaustive local candidate testing gives time 2^k poly(n), including arbitrary dependencies across blocks. No decomposition width bound on the condensation DAG is needed.
