# Fixed-dimensional graphic trace by gain-constrained subdivisions

In a supplied graphic representation of private binary columns, give each edge its full named label in Γ, a fixed binary vector space. Let U be a prescribed subspace of dimension h. The trace of an edge set is the image of its binary cycle space in Γ. For h=0 choose the empty set. Otherwise take a witness of trace U and a spanning forest. Choose h fundamental cycles of independent gains. Their union F has cycle rank exactly h: all except their h distinct chords are forest edges, and they have h independent cycles. Every edge of F lies on a cycle.

Suppress degree-two chains in every component, retaining one designated vertex on each cycle-only component. In a noncycle component of cycle rank s the sum of degree excesses above two is 2(s−1). Its suppressed template has at most 2(s−1) vertices and at most 3(s−1) edges. A pure cycle gives one vertex and one loop. Hence there are at most 2h template vertices and 3h template edges in total. Enumerate all these fixed multigraph templates and their edge gains. Retain only templates of cycle rank h whose gain homomorphism on the cycle space has image U. Every subdivision with the specified gains has exactly trace U by the cycle-space isomorphism under suppression. Conversely, taking the gains of the chains in F yields such a subdivision. Extra ambient edges remain unselected.

Use the author-hosted [Liu–Yoo STOC 2025 paper](https://people.tamu.edu/~yyoo/stoc25.pdf), Theorem 1.3 or Corollary 1.4, with d=dim Γ copies of GF(2). For each path and each coordinate forbid its one undesired value. The graph labels are unoriented, so the path sums are exactly the required XORs. Fixed d, pattern and forbidden-set bound f=1 satisfy the hypotheses, with constant-time finite group operations. In a possibly loopy original host replace every edge by three segments with two private vertices and put its gain only on the central segment. Even a loop becomes a triangle in this loopless host. Use Corollary 1.4 for each retained fixed pattern, allowing template loops. The host has size O(N) for N=|V|+|E| and the stated running time is O(N^{|V(template)|+8}) for each such call. Every intended traversal between original anchors has length at least three, so the length-two prohibition in the corollary's proof does not discard intended witnesses. Recovered branchpoints of degree at least three are original vertices; an anchor of a cycle-only component is immaterial. Suppress the introduced degree-two vertices of the union to recover edge IDs. Any used segment of a physical edge compels the entire chain, since the returned union has minimum degree two.

Equivalently use the socket construction in graphic-exact-plane.md with Theorem 1.3. Every template loop is itself a nonzero cycle vector and must have nonzero gain because the template cycle space maps injectively onto U. Thus a two-step zero-gain socket bounce cannot realize a returning template edge. The incidence-private construction prevents paths from sharing any physical edge. Huynh's [Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34307v1) also supplies the requisite constructive deterministic linkage algorithm with an unspecified exponent depending on the group and pattern.

Thus testing and extracting a witness take deterministic polynomial time for each fixed total named dimension, for arbitrary graphic private rank. This statement does not establish a uniform polynomial bound for input-varying named dimension. It uses external algorithm theorems rather than an implementation of the general linkage routine.
