# Guarded pigeonhole masks and local distributions

Use a bounded-degree compiled FPHP base from the [base construction](README.md). Write L for the parity of its unmatched edges and fix one such edge e. The point x consisting only of e violates exactly its own unmarked guard: the row mask there does not contain e and the offset is one. Flipping that offset makes the point satisfy every unmarked equation. Assume the unmatched pigeon has degree at least two for the two-block construction. Let B be the published-mask relaxation in the [base construction](README.md).

## Single-row shift with a cycle

XOR a fresh t into the mask of e alone. Add four cycle rows y_i(y_prev+y_next+1)=0 and the fifth row t(Y+1)=0 for Y equal to the sum of all four y variables over GF(2). Keep the old marker L=1. The sum of the four cycle polynomial axioms is Y, since each unordered edge occurs twice. Multiplying by t and adding the fifth row gives t=0 in PC degree two. Thus PC preprocessing that discovers this linear consequence, followed by substituting t=0 and rebuilding B on the restricted old system, refutes the extension. Restricting all five new variables to zero yields the original hard PC instance, preserving its degree and size lower bounds. The new system is unsatisfiable and its maximum hidden deficiency is one by its independent pivots and single marker. Just one old matrix cell is changed and both matrix degree bounds stay constant.

For B take t=1, x=e, with honest parities on every mask supported there. Give cycle variables marginal 1/2, each opposite-neighbor pair parity one, each cycle-row triple parity 1/2, and give Y parity one and Y+t parity zero. The masks from the old and new pieces do not identify distinct assigned values. Each row equation and support inequality holds. Each individual factor also admits the necessary distribution: on a cycle row the opposite neighbors have opposite values and its pivot is a fair bit; on the t-row take an odd assignment to the y's with fair singleton marginals. Full overlap consistency is stronger. The t-row bag contains all four cycle pivots and their neighbors, so its y distribution must actually satisfy all cycle guards. It has Y=0 always and forces t=0; the remaining locally consistent distributions yield the original telescoping contradiction.

## Two blocks with cross-shifts

Take disjoint blocks and XOR the opposite unmatched parity L_other into the mask of each distinguished edge e_j only. The sole marker is L1+L2=1. Every hypothetical model has one parity zero and the other one; the block with parity one is unshifted and would solve its impossible original marker. Fix all coordinates in block two to zero to recover the original first-block refutation target. The same PC lower bounds transfer. Row and column degrees remain bounded, and again the maximum hidden deficiency is exactly one. Branching on the two legal marker parity choices exposes the original short telescoping LP proof.

The assignments where both blocks are zero or both contain only their distinguished edge satisfy all guards. Mix them with probability one half, giving each row parity its true marginal on the mixture. Give the final marker parity marginal one instead. This does not alter a row mask or row-plus-pivot mask: in any row of one block its own unshifted prefix, with or without its pivot, contains at most one unmatched edge, whereas its own marker contains at least two. The marker support inequality holds with equality. Its own marginal assignment can sample the two distinguished bits anticorrelated, preserving individual bit marginals. The row and marker distributions thus have their own honest witnesses, but they disagree on a larger intersection.

In fact the stronger LP with distributions on each full guard and marker bag, required to agree on every intersection, rejects this family. For each block take the unshifted running prefixes in each hole. Consecutive row bags contain the shared prefix, including at the exceptional row, and therefore give its parity the same marginal. Ordinary rows change the expected prefix parity by −E[x_i]. At the one shifted row the change is −E[x_e]+2 Pr[x_e=1,L_other=1]. The initial prefix parity is bounded above by the sum of its bit marginals and the end prefix parity is nonnegative. Summing over holes as in the original certificate bounds E[K] by 2 Pr[x_e=1,L_other=1], where K is the number of active unmatched edges of this block. The intersection of its shifted-row and marker bags includes x_e and every bit of L_other. Thus the probability on the right is also its value in the marker distribution, which likewise supplies E[K]. On every marker witness,

    K ≥ 1_{L_own=1} + 2·1_{x_e=1, L_other=1}.

The second event forces K even and positive, and the first event is disjoint from it. The two inequalities force Pr[L_own=1]=0 for each block. This contradicts the odd marker. The full-bag LP is polynomial-size for these bounded-width factors. These separations concern the specified mask relaxation and PC separately.

The [cycle checker](combined.py) verifies the forced-zero deduction and fractional assignments on all 448 planted 4-by-3 graphs with positive unmatched degree. The [two-gadget checker](check_separations.py) tests both assignments against every constraint of the mask LP on all 256 such graphs with unmatched degree at least two.
