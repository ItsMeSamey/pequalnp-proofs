# A constructive all-syndrome criterion

For a quadratic map Q:F2^N→F2^r let B be its vector-valued polar form. Suppose 2r independent supplied directions a_i,b_i satisfy B(a_i,a_j)=0, B(a_i,b_j)=0 for j<i, and s_i=B(a_i,b_i) form an output basis. On x=x0+Σu_i a_i+Σv_j b_j the map is affine in u, and its ith derivative column is D_i(v)=d_i+Σ_{j≥i}B(a_i,b_j)v_j. Choose v_r through v_1 in that order. The previously chosen columns complement span(s_1,...,s_i). The hyperplane generated by these columns and s_1,...,s_{i−1} omits s_i. The two options for D_i differ by s_i, so one lies outside the hyperplane. This preserves the invariant and yields a basis of derivative columns. Solve u by Gaussian elimination for any target, subtracting Q(x0+Σv_j b_j). All checks and witness operations are polynomial for a supplied flag.

For proposed mutually polar-orthogonal independent coordinate blocks, list each block's polar signatures on its internal basis pairs. A rainbow independent output basis supplies the required directions, and standard matroid intersection recognizes this sufficient condition. One can take blocks from the connected components of the nonzero-polar input graph on a chosen coordinate basis. Failure is inconclusive. No inference about a general target's multiplicity is implied. The unconstrained commuting-Pauli positivity theorem has different input hypotheses.

## Direct regular-optimizer obstruction

For the direct 3-color integer energy (sum of native violation bits), simple graph edge e contributes nonzero edge-unique Fourier directions (g_e,0),(0,g_e),(g_e,g_e), g_e its two-endpoint incidence vector. Deletion removes all three; contraction contracts the first two then deletes the remaining loop, quotienting by g_e independently in both coordinate blocks. Thus every graph K4 minor supplies the following vector-matroid minor. In K4 encode reduced incidence using three bits, ordering edges 01,02,03,12,13,23, with triples a,8a,a XOR 8a. Contract indices 0,4,11, respectively 3,40,54. Reduced images are 0,5,5,5,0,5,1,8,9,5,5,0,1,13,12,4,8,12, all seven nonzero vectors of a rank-three quotient. Simplifying gives Fano. Consequently the full direction matroid and its dual parity check H are nonregular on every graph containing a K4 minor; no extension [H|U] can restore regularity. Changing the energy representation is outside this obstruction.

## Partial output elimination

More generally put Z=span(a_i,b_i) and U=span(s_i) of dimension k for a flag of k pairs. Check in a Z-adapted input basis that all linear and quadratic coefficients involving Z belong to U, including coefficients mixing Z with its complement. Thus projection modulo U of Q(w+z) depends only on the complementary variables w. For every fixed w the shifted triangular argument applies on Z with derivative offsets altered by B(w,a_i) and preserved polar signatures. Its image is exactly Q(w)+U. One may existentially eliminate Z and U, solve the quotient system, and reconstruct z deterministically in reverse. Each step removes 2k variables and k target dimensions. Exact witness recovery survives composition with another decision solver; exact model counts require additional, potentially nonconstant fiber weights.

This partial reduction cannot start in any input basis on the direct 3-color map of a graph without isolated vertices. Given Z of dimension 2k>0 let S be the set of vertices on which Z has nonzero projection. Then 2k≤2|S|. Project the output span of all B(Z,whole input) to the vertex-equation coordinates alone. For each v in S choose z with a nonzero local bit and a vector supported only on v with local alternating product one; their projected polar output is exactly the vth unit. Thus the span has dimension at least |S|. If |S|=k, Z fills the entire coordinate subspace on S. It now produces pure vertex units B(x_v,y_v) for all v in S, as well as a nonzero pure edge-output vector B(x_v,x_u) for any neighbor of such a v. Its dimension exceeds k in this case too. Therefore no required k-dimensional U contains this polar image. This argument is invariant under output basis changes and does not rely on a guessed coordinate flag.
