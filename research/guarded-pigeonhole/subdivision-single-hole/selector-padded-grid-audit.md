# Independent review of the selector-padded grid

The proof applies to both satisfiable and unsatisfiable source formulas. Its critical point is that the giant leaf needs a linearly reachable flux whose affine-closure calculation survives. It does not need a satisfying assignment to that leaf.

* A selector-one clause pair existentially enforces precisely its source clause: if a is false the first clause needs w=1 and the second then needs b or c. If a is true, w=0 works. At selector zero, w=0 always works. The equality clones for the OR controller may be set consistently. The optional parity gadgets extend every fixed variable value.
* The grid centroid counts only grid occurrences. The cut of its heavy leaf splits at most h incidence vertices in the **whole** formula. An internal vertex of a gated copy belongs to just one copy. With more than h copies one can therefore choose a copy without any split internal variable or clause. Full selector clones still require explicit pinning on the analysis subspace; the proof freezes these control truth values.
* In that active copy each first gate has its selector literal false. Set the selector witness to zero. The remaining two odd-summing clause witnesses are a free bit and its complement, multiplying different free truth variables. Their quadratic parts lie in distinct variable–clause tensor coordinates. Every ordinary three-literal active clause similarly contributes one nonzero quadratic part per occurrence, with a different truth variable for each occurrence. Witness parameters of different full clauses are independent. This argument remains valid even if the original active CNF is unsatisfiable.
* Frozen inactive and control clauses admit fixed honest witnesses, including the padding of all binary clauses. In intact private gadgets on unfrozen variables the four strict clauses use independent witness parameters, so they do not produce affine dependencies. On the grid the restricted active rows have the same independence, while a singleton witness of a split grid clause is fixed zero by the outside-witness choice. The attachment variable and its private gadget are exceptional. These details ensure that every zero-polar combination on the restriction vanishes identically, not merely that the intended grid rows are present.
* A stable affine restriction therefore exists with quadratically many independent grid quadratics and no affine contradiction. Monotonicity transfers its rank lower bound to the ambient leaf fiber. Independently, Hall's lower bound uses the touched grid clauses alone and their grid neighbors. Both bad costs occur in the same giant leaf.

The reduction uses n+1 selector copies with fixed h and n sufficiently large. Finitely many smaller source instances do not affect NP-hardness. For a connected source promise one may connect the components of any strict bounded-occurrence starting formula in a chain with fresh extendible ternary clauses, using at most two additional occurrences at a representative variable per component. The obstruction is for the specified native representation and applies to the leaf cost over its linearly possible, affinely surviving states. A criterion evaluating only globally satisfying boundary states would need a different argument on NO instances.
