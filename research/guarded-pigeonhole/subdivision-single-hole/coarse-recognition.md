# Two coarse layout recognition controls

For a fixed pair-respecting partition tree, let h be its largest flux dimension and r be the maximum raw squarefree quadratic coefficient rank on a leaf's affine fiber, before absorbing additional affine consequences.

On the direct strict ternary SAT encoding, any such tree can be refined to the canonical single-occurrence leaves with width at most h+2r+1. For a leaf B, mark at most h incidence vertices (variables or clauses) having occurrences on both sides of B. A variable with all occurrences in B retains its independent truth degree of freedom on the zero-deviation flux fiber; a full clause retains two private witness directions. Thus the squarefree rows from pairs between full variables and full clauses inside B are independent: within a strict clause their three truth variables are distinct, and across clauses the witness parameters are distinct. There are at most r internal-to-internal edges. Add both endpoints of those edges to the marked set P, giving |P|≤h+2r. Every remaining vertex incident to B has all its edges there and neighbors only in P. Cluster those edges by their unmarked endpoint, and treat edges between marked vertices individually. On any atomic tree obtained by replacing B by a tree of these clusters, with each cluster further refined as a star's occurrence set, each new cut splits only vertices of P and at most one unmarked vertex. Hence the claimed bound. All old cuts keep their widths. The one-leaf full-input case has no outside cut and follows with empty original boundary. Consequently fixed h and fixed raw leaf rank on this direct strict-SAT family are already covered by recognizable bounded atomic width. This argument does not bound post-absorption joint width.

At h=0, arbitrary binary systems permit exact peak post-absorption leaf-rank recognition after treating affine contradictions as immediate rejection. Compute the finest pair-respecting direct-sum components from the fundamental matrix graph augmented by pair edges. Every zero-flux block is a union of components. Their joint affine solution space is a product, with disjoint variables and equations per component. The polar maps split as direct sums, since degree-two monomials on disjoint sets are independent. Any affine dependence is generated by the affine consequences from within each block. Restrict and repeat until stable; absent an affine contradiction, stabilized residual ranks add on unions. The finest partition therefore minimizes peak rank exactly and is polynomial to obtain. An affine contradiction in any component already certifies zero global count.

## Discovering conditioned bags from incidence separators

Enumerate a set X of at most k incidence vertices of the original SAT instance. The components of its incidence multigraph minus X define bags: keep each occurrence in the component containing its non-X endpoint(s), and place edges wholly in X in singleton bags. Empty components can be omitted. Cuts of any tree on these bags split only vertices of X, so flux is at most k. Test all feasible leaf fluxes, computing their stabilized ranks, and retain the layout when their maximum is at most r. This finds any layout of this specified form in n^(k+O(1)) 2^O(k) preprocessing, and then counts physical compatible assignments in additional 2^O(k+r) poly(n) time. This is exact for recognizing this family of separator choices, not for arbitrary coarse trees.

There are strict ternary formulas with unbounded atomic width and full global residual rank 3m that this search recognizes with k=2 and r≤1. Choose distinct control variables s,t. Take any connected strict F on variables x_i, with large incidence branchwidth if desired. For each x_i add four clauses (l_s OR l_t OR x_i), for all four polarity choices of l_s and l_t. Add a private w and clause (s OR t OR w). For X={s,t}, the bags are the giant F-side and the last clause. Both s,t split, so on each leaf flux their truth values are fixed. On the F-side, for each x_i precisely one of its control clauses has both control literals false. Its guards force the first two witness bits to zero, then the third is one, forcing x_i=1. Repeated affine absorption implements this substitution. Afterward all truth variables are fixed, so all guards are affine. If an affine contradiction arises the state is immediately rejected. On the last clause only w is unfixed, giving at most one nonlinear guard. Thus maximum residual leaf rank ≤1 even though global residual rank equals three times the enlarged clause count. The source contains F's incidence graph, so its atomic width can be arbitrarily large. For an all-true satisfying F (such as positive grid-edge clauses with private third variables), the giant leaf has genuinely feasible fibers. This example distinguishes post-absorption rank from the raw-rank bound above and gives no bound for general SAT.
