# Two rooted bipartite copies give an exact quartic refutation

Use the 19-vertex graph and weight in `rooted-bipartite-gap.py`. Its vertices are indexed from zero. Pinning the adjacent vertices 0 and 1 selects 340 models. All satisfy the affine equation of parity one on mask 286. In ordinary polynomial calculus over the binary field with explicit Boolean axioms, deriving that equation requires degree four. Adding its opposite yields a cubic refutation. The script checks both statements directly.

Take two copies, removing edges (0,1) and (19,20) and adding (0,20) and (1,19). Pin vertices 0 and 20 and demand that the sum of the two weight parities be one. Summing all guard equations on vertices 0 through 18 cancels each internal edge twice, leaving the two crossing edge products. Their sum entails that the product on (1,19) equals one. Multiplication by each endpoint and Boolean reduction now yields both private pins at degree three. All four endpoints are pinned, so degree-two consequences restore the original guards on both copies. One can derive both local affine weight equations at degree four and sum them with the marker to refute.

For the lower bound, run `python series-raw.py` in this directory. It uses the raw polynomial ring up to degree three, including repeated variable powers. With either the two specified pins or all four pins the closed row space has rank 7918 and omits the constant one. Degree order makes the rows of degree at most two a basis for the intersection of the current space with the quadratics. Multiplying every such newly discovered basis row by each variable therefore computes ordinary cubic closure. The independent 24-vertex strong-source verification is in `double-rooted.py`.

The search in `lc-chart-pure.cpp` checks all 59049 nonroot local chart choices of the original 12-vertex seed, with root coordinates fixed. Among 22176 invertible charts, no root-edge-preserving cut can carry any of its four transported missing affine representatives on a single shore while meeting the necessary two-defect condition for independent edge subdivision. This rules out that particular method of obtaining a pure-shore weight.
