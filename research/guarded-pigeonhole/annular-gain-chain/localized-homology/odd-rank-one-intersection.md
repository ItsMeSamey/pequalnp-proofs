# Odd nonsingular principal sets after a rank-one update

Let A be alternating over F₂ and M=A+uvᵀ. An odd X with det M[X]=1 can be found, or its absence decided, in deterministic polynomial time. Odd cardinality refers to all selected indices. The inputs have no additional coupling conditions.

For odd X put C=A[X] and d_i=Pf(C minus i), including the empty Pfaffian one. At nullity at least three, all codimension-one minors vanish. At nullity one adj(C) is symmetric of rank one, hence wwᵀ over F₂. Its diagonal is d since squaring fixes binary elements. Thus adj(C)=ddᵀ in either case. The rank-one determinant identity gives det M[X]=(u_Xᵀd)(v_Xᵀd). Each factor by expansion equals the Pfaffian of C bordered with the corresponding vector and zero diagonal. Even X cannot make either bordered matrix nonsingular because it has odd order.

For each a=u,v border A by a at new index b to obtain B. Reject if a=0. Pick j with a_j=1, pivot on the invertible pair {b,j}, delete b and twist the remaining represented family by {j}. The principal pivot identity gives exactly those X for which B[X plus b] is nonsingular. Intersect the two represented families by unweighted linear delta-matroid parity. In the direct sum pair every ordinary index with its copy. Zero split pairs means that the two selected sets coincide, supplying the original X. Binary represented twisted alternating matrices meet the algorithm's hypotheses. Original witnesses may also be directly checked by elimination.
