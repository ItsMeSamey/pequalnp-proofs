# Polynomial two-step certification for large acyclic routing patches

Let T be the implicit external permutation table of a p-track native patch. The condition studied here is that for every return cap k and distinct input proxies a,b, some physical setting h joins them within at most two iterations of kh in at least one direction. This gives a complete-junction substitute by the balanced exterior proof of universal-cap-reachability.md. The length bound refers to patch traversals in the capped network, not to the number of gates inside.

Precompute which individual turns and which pairs of turns are simultaneously realizable, saving mask witnesses for positives. For a directed acyclic native patch, do this in polynomial time: for every ordered pair of distinct input tokens sweep the gates in topological order, keeping their ordered positions among the current frontier wires. There are at most quadratically many states; try the two physical settings of the current gate and store a predecessor for each reached state. A state uses one common setting on both tokens at a gate. Untracked tokens move according to the same permutation and place no constraints on the suffix. This computes the entire one- and two-token transfer relation without listing the full table. One-token queries or p≤1 are immediate. An arbitrary patch may instead supply verified positive witnesses; using a partial table then gives just a sufficient test.

Enumerate distinct a,b and distinct u,v where k(u)=b, k(v)=a. If either a to u or b to v is realizable, this header has a one-step certificate for every cap completion. Otherwise use the remaining inputs c and outputs t as the shores of a bipartite graph. Declare (c,t) good if a common mask realizes a to t and c to u, or a common mask realizes b to t and c to v. A completion of the cap is a perfect matching of these shores, pairing t to c when k(t)=c. For that completion a two-step join in one direction exists exactly if its matching uses a good edge. An intermediate position equal to a or b could add nothing since a direct join was ruled out. Thus there is a violating cap for this header precisely when the complement of the good-edge graph has a perfect matching. Test these O(p^4) headers by bipartite matching. With complete two-token tables this recognizes the stated two-step condition exactly. On success and a concrete cap, read a direct witness or the witness for one good matched edge; on failure the negative result concerns the length guarantee. Apply the standard sequential patch fixing to assemble an actual physical mask in a network of certified, mutually independent patches. For up to five ports a common cycle always gives distance at most two in one direction, although the complete-junction requirement can still exceed ordinary cap passing on intransitive tables.

**Strict improvement at unbounded arity.** Partition the tracks into disjoint U,V of size two each and nonempty Z, so p≥5 is arbitrary. Cascade an acyclic mixer on V union Z and then one on U union Z, with other tracks bypassed at each stage. Each mixer need only realize every one-token transfer in its own pool. A native construction for a pool of size at least three is to schedule, for each ordered pair x,y of distinct tracks, one optional rotation sending x to y using any third pool track. Enable just the nominated rotation for that transfer, and choose no rotation for identity. Thus the full individual relation has missing rectangle U times V and no other holes: a V to U transfer can go by any member of Z. The one-step matching test fails.

When both direct transfers for a header are missing, a,b are in U and u,v in V. Every c outside U and t outside V form a good edge: first mixer sends c to u, second sends a to t, with the other chosen track bypassing that mixer's gates. For these particular sizes all remaining inputs and outputs have those properties. Thus every matching uses a good edge and the two-step condition holds for every p≥5. No assumption of joint controllability of the mixer is needed. This yields deterministic polynomial recognition and reconstruction on an implicit family with unbounded arity; no unrestricted solver for cyclic patches is inferred.
