# Polynomials on bounded cycle rank with a supplied parallel-band reference

Apply an opposite-parity reference B. At gate i the allowed modes transpose successors on (a_i,b_i) or (a_i,c_i). Suppose b_i,c_i belong to the same B cycle for every i. This is recognizable for the supplied reference. The two alternatives then represent the same unordered endpoints in a fixed abstract multigraph G on baseline cycles. Let β be the cycle rank of the connected component containing both marked baseline cycles. Marks in different components always give NO.

Represent each baseline cycle by an oriented vertex disk with sockets encountered in successor order, and each chosen transposition by an untwisted band at the two chosen sockets. Following the boundary at a cut jumps to just after the paired cut, exactly computing the updated successor permutation. Locate a marked input wire at a small corner immediately before its socket, including when that socket is used. A root whose circle has no band lies on the corresponding standalone disk boundary.

Keep the two marked disks. Repeatedly remove each other degree-one disk and its band. A leaf disk and band give a boundary excursion from and back to a single interval of its parent disk, with no marked corner in that excursion. Its removal preserves the equivalence of all remaining corners. Repeat, keeping isolated marked disks. On each remaining unmarked degree-two disk the cyclic order of its two remaining socket occurrences is unique. Consequently switching an attachment there without changing the other abstract incidence or a marked corner cannot change any rooted boundary orbit. This also covers loops, counted twice. If the same gate has an endpoint at a marked or degree-at-least-three disk, retain its bit for enumeration; fix all other bits arbitrarily and evaluate original native trajectories, giving a witness for each YES. Bits belonging to pruned branches may be fixed as well.

In the trimmed connected multigraph with at least one edge, let l≤2 be the number of leaves. The excess sum over high-degree vertices equals 2β−2+l. Hence the sum of their degrees is at most 3(2β−2+l)≤6β, and the marked disks not yet counted have at most four more incident edges. At most 6β+4 bits are enumerated. The running time is 2^(6β+4) times a polynomial with original physical witnesses. This bound does not restrict other components. The n-cycle from forest-deficiency-core.md has unbounded canonical deficient core but β=1. No efficient discovery of such a B for arbitrary inputs is claimed.

parallel_forest_core.py implements the supplied-reference solver. check_parallel_forest_core.py compared it with exhaustive masks on 3,420 rooted samples with up to seven gates, including 849 negative cases.
