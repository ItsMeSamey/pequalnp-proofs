# Native arm codes and a polynomial local-rank-three solver

Use the cellular edge space modulo all triangle boundaries, before arbitrary further quotients. At each gate take the two-dimensional binary even-arm spaces B and W for the two colors. The formal domain A is their direct sum over gates; Φ projects to the cellular quotient. For connected input with m gates, L=ker Φ is maximal totally singular of dimension 2m.

The local q_i(b,w) counts mod two whether chords joining the selected black and white tips cross in the alternating six-tip disk, with an absent color contributing zero. This pairs B and W perfectly: each nonzero black pair separates one white tip from the other two, so the three black pairs give the three different nonzero white functionals. Its polarization on B⊕W is symplectic and each color plane is totally singular. For x in L the separate black and white chains are closed because their disk vertices are disjoint and every switch degree is even. Their homology classes agree. Their intersection number, computed by these crossings with the remainder drawn in disjoint color disks, is Σq_i(x_i)=[black]·[white]=0. The image rank of Φ is (number of black disks minus one)+(number of white disks minus one)+2g: each color incidence graph is a connected cellular skeleton with the opposite disks as faces, so it realizes every even disk boundary of its color and generates all surface homology by closed chains. Euler's formula gives image rank 2m, proving maximality. Components separate. A target with nonzero disk boundary lives in the full cellular quotient. Reject t outside the image; otherwise choose one lift x₀. All lifts form x₀+L.

## Local reductions

Set A_i=B_i⊕W_i and K_i=L∩A_i. At image rank d_i we have dim K_i=4−d_i, hence 2≤d_i≤4. Each local word belongs to x₀,i+K_i^⊥. With x₀ as origin take the symplectic quotient K_i^⊥/K_i. The induced direction L/(⊕K_i) is Lagrangian in their direct sum by isotropy and dimension. In each affine local quotient keep the cosets containing a word of either physical color. Independent local lifts are legitimate because all K_i lie in L.

When d_i=2 inspect the only local coset for feasibility. When d_i=3 the quotient is a plane and K_i has generator e=(b,w). For a pure generator, say w=0, the slice of pairing zero with e permits the entire black plane and a white line; modulo e these give three points. The slice of pairing one permits only an affine white line, giving two points. Exchanging colors changes nothing. For a mixed generator the crossing of b and w is zero by singularity. The pairing-zero slice restricts the colors to the lines generated respectively by b and w; their quotient lines coincide since their sum is e. This gives two points. The pairing-one slice gives one affine line of each color. Their quotient directions coincide, but no two points from opposite colors differ by e: that would force their components to be exactly b and w, which both have pairing zero with e. Thus these two parallel affine lines give all four points. One may enumerate the 16 local words directly.

## Polynomial solver

Each two-point restriction is an affine line. Enforce these by linear equations, reject inconsistency, and delete the enforced sites. The new direction is still Lagrangian. For one site with imposed direction D, project the direction L'∩(D⊕A_rest) to the remaining sites. The kernel is L'∩D; the imposed linear functional has rank zero precisely when the embedded generator of D lies in L'=(L')^⊥. Thus exactly one dimension is lost, and isotropy is preserved since D is isotropic. Record lifts when doing elimination.

Each remaining site excludes one point or is free. If r planes remain and the affine direction is M, select one local functional per plane whose restrictions to M form a basis. For any subset J of sites the span of the available restricted functionals has dimension at least |J|: its annihilator inside M is supported in the complement and is isotropic, of dimension at most r−|J|. The independent transversal exists by the linear Hall–Rado theorem and is computable by linear/partition matroid intersection on three functionals per site. Prescribe the value opposite to each site's excluded point, with arbitrary values at free sites. Surjectivity gives a point in the given affine solution space. Lift it to permitted raw local words and reconstruct the witness. This is deterministic polynomial time for all targets whenever every d_i≤3, with no further genus or layout assumption.

For q full-rank sites branch on their physical colors and impose the corresponding Lagrangian coordinate planes before solving the plane system. Projection after a feasible such restriction again yields a Lagrangian affine direction. Indeed in a site of dimension 2a with imposed Lagrangian D, the imposed rank is a−dim(L'∩D), and projection loses the remaining dim(L'∩D). The half-dimension therefore falls by a. This gives 2^q times polynomial time. One can recalculate local reductions after conditioning. Arbitrary prior quotients on the range can invalidate this proof if their preimages cease to be totally singular.

There need not be F₄-linearity preserving both physical color planes. For one gate (012) with feedback (01), the black baseline has disks {0,1},{2} and the white baseline has {0},{1,2}. This chart has genus zero. The black arm pair 0,1 is the sole nonzero black kernel word, so L meets the black plane in a binary line, incompatible with its being an F₄-subspace of that field line.
