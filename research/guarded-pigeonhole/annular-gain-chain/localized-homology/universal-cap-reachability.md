# Fixed return caps characterize composable relaxed reachability at every arity

A patch uses each choice only inside its own gate set; its nonempty table T lists the realizable permutations from p incoming wires to p outgoing wires, with reconstruction witnesses. Neither mark lies inside a patch. Relax a patch by allowing every individually realizable input-to-output turn. The resulting directed wire graph is the union of the successor permutations of the possible joint settings: every individual turn can be realized while all other patches are set arbitrarily. In a finite union of permutations, weak components are strongly connected: every directed arc is on a directed cycle.

Define the cap test. For every permutation k wiring the p outputs of the patch to its p inputs, and every two input positions, check whether reachability under the union of the permutations kT agrees with membership in one cycle of at least one member of kT. This takes p! poly(p,|T|) time. The same T need not realize all pairwise joins at once. Each failure immediately exhibits a fixed native return wiring and marks giving a false positive for relaxed reachability.

**Composition theorem.** In a closed network of disjoint patches passing their individual tests, relaxed reachability of two marked exterior wires is equivalent to physical joinability and a mask can be found. This holds across different arities; it covers ordinary deterministic wiring, including fixed gates. Consequently the fixed cap test is also necessary for a table to be universally admissible even against other passing tables.

To see sufficiency, start with two weakly connected marks in the full relaxation. Remove all transition arcs of one undecided patch P, leaving the exterior wires (and both marks) as vertices. Its weak component blocks each contain equal numbers of the p incoming and p outgoing positions, with positions counted individually even when a wire loops back to P. To check balance, fix any settings at all other patches. These chosen exterior arcs decompose into closed circuits and open paths from outgoing positions of P to incoming ones, with zero-length paths allowed. Their endpoints are within the same exterior weak component. The paths bijectively account for all positions, proving equality block by block.

If both marks are in one exterior block, any choice at P preserves their weak connectivity. Otherwise write A,B for their distinct blocks. The directed quotient with these blocks as vertices and individually possible patch turns as arcs is weakly connected between A and B. It is a union of balanced directed multigraphs (for each h in T use its turns once; balance at each vertex follows from the preceding equal counts), so weak connectivity implies directed reachability. Choose a directed simple block path from A to B. For each internal block, pair the arrival output of P with the departure input of P along that path. Since no block repeats, these prescribed pairings extend to a bijection from outputs to inputs separately inside each block. Together these bijections form a fixed return cap k. Choose as first proxy the departure input at A, and as second proxy the image under k of the arrival output at B. In the relaxed capped patch the turns of the path connect these proxies. The cap test gives h in T with the proxies on one circuit of kh. The refinement k only connects positions in the same exterior block. Consequently the exterior plus h at P weakly connects A to B, and hence the original marks.

Fix any such h, found directly by checking the available choices against weak component membership. Repeat on the remaining patches. Previously fixed choices are deterministic exterior transitions so the proof applies at every step. At termination the mark pair is connected in an actual physical permutation. Lift by the saved internal witnesses. If the marks were initially disconnected, every setting is impossible. The online reconstruction uses only a polynomial number of ordinary graph searches in the explicit tables, after their local tests. A bounded number of exceptional patches may instead be branched first.

For p=3, six-wire-reachability.md already gives exactly the 39 passing tables out of 63 by a direct shortcut proof and fixed cap counterexamples. This theorem gives necessity and sufficiency at arbitrary p rather than just a computable sufficient shortcut test. A single ordinary optional three-cycle fails; this local equivalence criterion alone does not decide an arbitrary network of such gates.
