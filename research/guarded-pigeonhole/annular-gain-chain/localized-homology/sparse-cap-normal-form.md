# Explicit cap distinction for rows moving at most three names

Normalize the candidate new entry to identity by output relabeling. Its opponents are swaps and 3-cycles; an opposing identity rules out distinction. For distinct prescribed marks r,s, assign each label 0,1,out, with r=0,s=1. Swap endpoints must receive colors 0 and 1. Each triple must contain both colors. If all three are colored, impose one comparison between the two equal-color labels: starting immediately after the singleton-color label, they must occur in the order prescribed by the inverse triple. Require the two precedence graphs to be acyclic, placing r first in color zero and s first in color one.

Necessity uses the two half-open mark arcs [r,s) and [s,r) on the root circle of the return k. Both arcs must be cut by each separating row. With exactly two triple positions on the root circle the trace of k on its three cuts swaps the two root labels and fixes the other. Composing with either 3-cycle separates the two root labels, hence the intervening segments containing the two marks. With three on the root circle, matching its first-return orientation preserves one orbit, while reversing it splits the three segments into separate orbits. Thus a split occurs precisely with the specified inverse order. Conversely topologically order each color class, concatenate the orders as one cycle, and fix every out label individually. This explicitly separates every opponent while identity joins. All other non-root cycles can be taken as singleton fixed points.

For swaps alone test bipartiteness after adding an edge r,s. In general reject a nonbipartite swap graph. Let c count its nontrivial connected components meeting triple supports, and f count swap-isolated symbols in triples. Enumerate the 2^c component orientations and 3^f isolated label choices, enforcing marks. Orient the remaining nontrivial components consistently with any prescribed marks. Check all triple colors, both order DAGs, and that the marks can be first on their sides. Thus time is 2^c 3^f poly(input), at most 3^u poly for u distinct triple-supported symbols. An unspecified pair adds a quadratic loop. Combined with explicit-cap-distinguishing.md, this gives the support-two versus support-three boundary.
