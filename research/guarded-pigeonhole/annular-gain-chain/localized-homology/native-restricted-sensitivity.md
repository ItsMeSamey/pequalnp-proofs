# NP-completeness of maximizing native rooted sensitivity on a specified face

The input is a closed network of independent optional three-cycle switches, two marked wires, a specified set of coordinates fixed to zero for the baseline, and an integer K. Seek a baseline satisfying those pins for which at least K single-coordinate flips change the answer to the marked-circuit join query. Flips may leave the baseline face. In the construction all baselines on the face are NO, and all sensitive coordinates are pinned coordinates. The same result holds when only those designated coordinates are counted. A guessed baseline and direct traversal of its one-flip neighbors give membership in NP.

Reduce from positive Not-All-Equal 3-SAT with three distinct variables per clause. The objective is preserved exactly, assignment by assignment. [Darmann and Döcker, monotone NAE hardness](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04198)

Make two directed reference rings, with distinct marks r and s. Ring 0 contains r followed by a block for each variable i in order. Its block begins at u_i, then contains two consecutive private sockets per occurrence of i, and ends at v_i. Ring 1 contains s followed by the landing sockets w_i. The variable switch is the optional cycle (u_i,v_i,w_i), with the convention that the new successor at u_i is the former successor at v_i. Switching it on moves the interval from immediately after u_i through v_i from ring 0 to immediately after w_i on ring 1. The disjoint blocks and landing sites give exactly these moves for every subset. Each occurrence therefore lies on its variable's chosen ring. Both reference marks remain on distinct rings; separate mark labels may be suppressed into the corresponding wires.

For a clause in a fixed order, let x1,x2,x3 be its three first occurrence sockets and y1,y2,y3 their immediate successors. Add independent optional diagnostic gates (x1,x2,x3) and (y1,y3,y2). Pin every diagnostic off for the baseline. All positions of all variable and diagnostic gates are disjoint.

If the three variables of one clause have the same value, either single diagnostic flip touches only one ring. The marks still lie apart. Otherwise one ring has two of the three sockets and the other ring has a singleton. The two sockets on the former ring divide it into two segments; the latter ring opens to one segment. A three-cycle on the cut successors closes one of the former segments onto itself, splicing the other with the entire singleton ring. Reversing the three-cycle exchanges the two former segments' roles. Using the immediately consecutive y sockets instead of x preserves the cyclic order and the positions of both marks relative to these segments, since no mark lies between paired sockets. Exactly one diagnostic joins the roots. A flip of a variable alone remains on the baseline face and never joins them. Thus the sensitivity equals the number of NAE clauses for that assignment, and threshold K equal to the number of clauses decides the source problem.

The off-face query asks about individual flips from the same baseline. Allowing all diagnostic settings in an ordinary existential join question does not test simultaneous NAE satisfaction. On the seven lines of the Fano plane the best sensitivity is six, but 952,938 of the 2,097,152 completely unpinned masks join the marks. Setting every diagnostic on at once also fails as a test: its two gates per clause form consecutive opposite rotations on that clause's three private rails, so their composite is identity outside those private positions, for any variable setting. There are no marks between the two rotations. Already a single satisfiable clause gives zero joining masks with both diagnostics on.

Run check_native_sensitive_clauses.py to verify every baseline assignment and all physical simultaneous masks in three fixtures. For one clause the maximum sensitivity is one and six assignments attain the satisfaction threshold. For the Fano plane minus one line it is six and ten assignments attain threshold; 233,452 unrestricted masks join. For the full Fano plane no assignment attains threshold seven. The checker follows the actual native successors throughout.
