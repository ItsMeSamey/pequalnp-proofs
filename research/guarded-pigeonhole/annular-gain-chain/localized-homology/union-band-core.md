# Arbitrary paired native bands in a supplied reference

Let B be a supplied opposite-parity artificial reference. For each gate list its possible transpositions as (a,b),(a,c). Their union multigraph has baseline circles as vertices. Insert two separately indexed edges a–b and a–c when their endpoints lie on different unordered pairs of circles, or one compulsory edge when those pairs coincide. Options from distinct gates always retain distinct edge identities. Let β be the cycle rank of the component containing the two marked corners. Distinct components give NO. We give a deterministic 2^{O(β+1)} polynomial algorithm that returns an original gate mask.

Use the oriented disk-and-band model and corner convention of parallel-band-core.md. Delete every pendant unmarked vertex iteratively, retaining the marked vertices even if isolated. For any setting the selected part of a pruned branch is a forest with at most one attachment to the retained graph. Such a forest preserves equality of the boundary components containing the two marked corners. Declare every surviving vertex of degree other than two, and each marked vertex, a terminal. The edge set partitions into unmarked-interior corridors between terminals, with terminal-to-itself corridors including single-edge loops. The component contains a terminal even if every degree is two. An isolated marked disk has no corridors.

Enumerate bits of all gates with an edge incident to a terminal in the trimmed graph. This fixes the precise socket or absence of every such edge, including the compulsory abstract edges. Enumerate also one status for every corridor. A through corridor contains all its edges. After suppressing its unmarked degree-two disks, it contributes one orientable band with its prescribed endpoint incidences. A broken corridor contributes only dangling unmarked paths at its ends, and possible wholly detached segments. Those paths do not change which marked corners share a boundary cycle. This description handles a broken return corridor with both ends at the same disk. Therefore all settings with any one enumerated profile give the same rooted verdict.

Through status forces individual edge-presence literals, excluding inconsistent pins. Broken status yields a single disjunction that an optional edge be absent; a corridor containing only compulsory edges cannot break. The resulting constraint system has unit pins and at most one additional Boolean clause per corridor. Across unpinned variables, keep all subsets of clauses satisfied so far, which one bit value extends by taking a union. A full-clause subset at the end yields a physical setting by backtracking. The procedure accounts for both occurrences of a gate variable, even within one clause. Simulating one recovered mask for each attainable profile suffices for the original query.

There are at most two degree-one vertices. Consequently the sum of degrees at vertices of degree at least three is at most 6β, and the terminal degrees sum to at most 6β+4. There are at most 6β+4 enumerated gate bits and at most 3β+2 corridors, counting a return corridor twice in the terminal degree sum. Thus one explicit time bound is 2^{12β+8}poly(input), including subset satisfaction; the algorithm does not need a planar embedding. All bounds refer to the given B. No guarantee on optimizing B is required.

union_band_core.py is the witness-producing implementation. check_union_band_core.py compares against direct enumeration for 3,498 rooted queries with at most five gates, including 456 negative cases.
