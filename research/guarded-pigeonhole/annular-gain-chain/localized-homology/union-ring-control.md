# Linear bridge-block rank for every reference on a directed three-lane ring

Take n≥2 gates in cyclic order, each with three wires to the next, and allow independent native rotations at every gate. Each reference trajectory visits every layer. Thus at most three baseline disks occur, and every gate's three sockets meet all of those disks. The gate's two options have a common center and reach its remaining sockets, so its indexed edges connect the full disk set, including after collapsing two parallel options of the same gate. Removing any one indexed edge leaves the edges of another gate untouched. Those edges already connect all disks; hence the connected union multigraph has no bridges. Its single block has at least n indexed edges and at most three disks, giving cycle rank ≥n−2 for every B and every marked pair. This strengthens the whole-component control in union-bridge-blocks.md.

The family is tractable by following possible permutation products on three lanes. Given marks, split the ring at their two layers; each of the two segments realizes at most six permutations. Enumerate each attainable pair of segment products and evaluate whether the two marked lanes lie on the same closed trajectory. For marks in one layer simply use all products around the ring. Storing one path of choices for each attainable product reconstructs the physical mask.
