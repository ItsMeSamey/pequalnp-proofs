# Shifted circle guards as group-labelled perfect matching

Given a chord word representing the graph after removing d designated vertices, t arbitrary binary vertex markers can be decided with one-sided randomized error in 2^{O(d+t)}poly(n) time. Witnesses can be verified directly. The full input need not be a circle graph. For each fixed selector assignment on the designated vertices, define D_i as the parity of selected designated neighbors of chord i. The inner guards are x_i((Ax)_i+D_i)=0 for the interlacement adjacency A. Each selected designated vertex gives one linear parity constraint on its selected chord neighbors, with the constant contributed by other selected designated vertices. Together with the original targets, conditioning leaves at most t+d linear marker rows.

Assign binary statuses to the tour arcs between successive chord word entries. The status toggles at both occurrences of i precisely when x_i=1. Starting with either bit, these statuses close consistently after traversing the cyclic word. For an active i, the two incoming bits are opposite precisely when the number of selected occurrences strictly between its appearances is even, i.e. when (Ax)_i=0. For D_i=0 impose ordinary integer balance between the two incoming and two outgoing arc bits at i. For D_i=1 swap the roles of the incoming and outgoing ports at the second occurrence before imposing balance. A balance equation forces the two visits to have equal toggle status; if active it forces the correct crossing parity, and if inactive the equation holds automatically. Thus the projected feasible arc statuses are exactly the shifted guard solutions.

Make a graph with four port vertices at each chord and add a K₂,₂ between its two positive and two negative ports. For each tour arc, add a wire joining its endpoint ports. Interpret wires used by a perfect matching as status one. The remaining ports in each K₂,₂ can be matched exactly when the selected wire ends were balanced. If a wire coincides with an internal edge, keep distinct parallel edges or replace the wire by a length-three path, whose two outer edges used together represent its selected state. The chord selector is the XOR of the statuses on the two arcs adjacent to its first occurrence. Hence its marker column can be added to those two wire labels (to one outer edge only on a subdivided wire), giving matching label equal to the native marker vector.

For r retained parity coordinates use an odd field of size larger than four times the number of matching vertices. Attach a separate indeterminate to each matching-graph edge. Evaluate its skew adjacency Pfaffian after changing the sign of each variable according to each of the 2^r binary group characters on its label. The signed average evaluated at the required character target extracts precisely the monomials for perfect matchings of that label. Distinct matchings give distinct monomials with coefficients ±1, including parallel edges, so the filtered polynomial is nonzero exactly in positive instances. With uniform independent field evaluations, polynomial identity testing has bounded false-negative probability by the total-degree bound. A fixed positive apex branch suffices for constant overall success even when testing many branches; false positives are impossible. Use the SAME random edge values in every character. Once a filtered evaluation is nonzero, recovery is deterministic at those values. Test the filtered Pfaffian of G−e at the current target. If nonzero delete e. Otherwise the part containing e=uv is a nonzero signed edge weight times the filtered Pfaffian on G−u−v with target shifted by its label. Retain e and remove both endpoints. Repeat and verify the final witness. Summing 2^(t+|J|) over active apex subsets J costs at most 2^t·3^d character terms up to polynomial overhead. An empty core is handled by directly testing the designated bits.

The ordinary circle circulation construction appears in /tmp/guard-publish/circle-flow/README.md; the signed second-visit balance handles arbitrary shifts and makes the matching graph potentially nonbipartite. The 2026 apex-vertex-minor theorem of [Barabash et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06776) explicitly forgets adjacencies among apices (Section 2). It cannot directly test the target bits on pairs of apex ports. The matching argument does not use that theorem.

## Deterministic rooted three-marker case

For two fixed vertex selectors and one arbitrary binary parity, the algorithm is deterministic when d≤1. If a prescribed vertex is an apex enforce that in the apex branch. For each prescribed chord selector equal to one, enumerate its two possible patterns of four adjacent wire bits: its two visits toggle with the same/opposite direction according to D_i. At most four cases suffice for two chord pins. Fix an included wire by adding its label to the residual target and deleting both its endpoints after reserving that edge; remove an excluded wire. Reject conflicting prescriptions on any shared arc. A pinned zero chord similarly has four locally balanced patterns or can be deleted from the circle.

If the exceptional vertex is absent, no extra equation is needed. If selected, summing the shifted inner guards gives D·x=0, because the alternating inner edge products cancel. This is exactly its guard: D is its core neighborhood and it has no other active exceptional neighbor. Only the one genuine marker row remains. Apply [El Maalouly, Steiner and Wulf, Correct Parity Matching](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2023.28) on the resulting general graph. When parallel wires arise, first subdivide every wire twice and mark only one outer edge; forcing its state can then use that distinguished edge. Standard edge deletion self-reduction retrieves a perfect matching. This proves deterministic weighted rooted decision and search on any circle graph plus one arbitrarily attached designated vertex, without requiring a bipartite interlacement. More generally drop one active apex constraint in any nonempty branch since their sum is exactly the automatically enforced D·x=0. The residual matching is deterministic whenever at most one independent parity row remains after local pins and these redundancies.

The implementation solve.py supplies the general randomized Pfaffian branch. check_solver.py compared its actual reconstructed witnesses to direct guard enumeration on 85 seeded instances (63 positive, 22 negative). check.py independently compares all arc subsets to direct shifted selectors. The two-root deterministic specialization uses the cited matching algorithm as a subroutine.
