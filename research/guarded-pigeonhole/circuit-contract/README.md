# Four mixed markers from three via signed circuit contraction

Let binary columns v_e span V, let c be a cycle (its selected columns sum to zero), and let d be a covector with c_e d(v_e)=0 at each element. The score is ℓ(c)+D(d), with four-bit primal edge labels and four dual moments d(u_i). The standard representation [I | B] encodes a bipartite graph: right selectors are free cycle coordinates, left selectors specify the covector. For an arbitrary representation choose a column basis. Basis-element labels transfer to nonbasis weights by expressing the basis cycle coordinates in the nonbasis coordinates; express dual moments in the same basis.

## Single-circuit contraction

For a circuit T with λ=Σ_{e∈T}ℓ_e nonzero, consider the branch where d kills W=span(T). Choose a linear section σ:W→F₂^T supported on any basis T minus one element, and a projection π:V→W fixing W. Replace outside columns and dual moments by their images in V/W, and outside labels by ℓ_e+ℓ(σπv_e). Project scores and target modulo the line generated by λ. This leaves three marker rows.

A quotient cycle has original outside sum w∈W. Its two on-T fillings are σ(w) and σ(w)+1_T, with scores differing by λ. The corrected outside weights give the score of the first extension. One extension realizes the original target precisely when the projected scores agree. Both respect all guards because the quotient covector pulls back to zero on W. Every original witness in this branch descends. This proves decision and witness recovery, including nonzero-score loops and unequal-label parallel pairs.

## Bounded-length solver

The pure-dual case is linear with empty primal support. Otherwise every witness has a nonzero-score circuit in the kernel of its covector, by decomposing its primal cycle. When a circuit has a proper chord splitting it into two strictly shorter circuits, the chord is in its span, so both children are killed by that same covector. Their scores add to the original score. Continuing with a nonzero-score child gives a signed circuit with no proper shortening chord. For simple binary matroids this is the usual chordless condition. With parallel elements, a nonshortening two-element peel is excluded. Alternatively check loops and unequal-score pairs first, then enumerate on a simplification: in a killed parallel class all representatives have the same label unless the nonzero signed pair already works.

If all such signed chordless circuits have length at most k, enumerate circuits through size k and apply the contraction to each. This gives deterministic n^{O(k)} decision and recovery. An efficiently enumerable covering family with polynomial total size also suffices. A truncated search does not certify NO without coverage. In simple chordal binary matroids, triangles suffice. More marker rows can be handled with the same one-row reduction, conditionally on a solver for the smaller budget.

## Symmetric structural promise

Duality exchanges cycles and cocycles, and their score maps can be presented in the dual representation by linear algebra. Let P,Q be the pure primal and pure dual score images. If target s lies in either, set the other support empty and solve linearly. Otherwise the primal contribution of any witness lies outside Q and its dual contribution outside P. Decompose both supports, choosing circuit C₀ with primal score outside Q and cocircuit T₀ with dual score outside P. Shorten by proper chords, preserving nonmembership in the respective subspace.

First shorten C₀ within the kernel of the original d. The resulting C is still disjoint from T₀. Both the original cycle c and the indicator of C are covectors of the dual representation, zero on T₀. Hence both vanish on its dual span. Shortening T₀ yields a cocircuit T which misses both C and c. These two chordless objects are disjoint; each contraction is individually admissible at the SAME original witness. Their scores lie in P outside Q and Q outside P, respectively, so are independent.

It therefore suffices that in every disjoint pair of such oppositely weighted properly chordless circuits/cocircuits, at least one member has length at most k. Try all primal and dual circuits through size k. Failure of both pure tests and all short contraction branches, on an instance that nevertheless has a witness, certifies the existence of a longer disjoint pair. Existence of some such pair by itself does not prove the target attainable.

verify.py compares the fixed-circuit transfer and one-sided covering identity on 547 small represented instances and 3,368 non-pure-dual target occurrences, with loops and parallel elements.


If both members of the pair are supplied, contract C first and then dually contract T in the new matroid. A cocircuit disjoint from C survives as a cocircuit of M/C: its binary covector vanishes on C, and a smaller nonempty supporting covector after contraction would lift to one before contraction. In the quotient representation the original primal still vanishes on T. The second score direction is the image of the old dual score of T modulo λ (the first correction attaches only to outside primal labels; dual covectors are pulled back). Independence implies this second direction is nonzero. Thus the simultaneous branch imposes the dual vanishing on C and primal vanishing on T at a cost of two fewer marker rows and has an exact two-row oracle. When both sides of a covering pair can be enumerated this yields a reduction to the elementary two-marker routine without the three-marker matroid theorem.

## The minimum eleven-vertex affine-peeling residual

The example in ../mixed/split-peeling-obstruction.md has three-connected nonregular represented matroid. residual.py checks binary adjacency cut rank at least two for every partition with both sides of size at least two. On a fundamental matrix [I | B] that cut rank is the matroid connectivity: the two off-diagonal blocks have ranks equal to the respective rank excesses over the included basis columns. It is invariant under basis exchange. A pendant vertex, same-shore twin pair, or a separated nontrivial lobe at a bridge or cut vertex would give cut rank at most one. Thus no sequence of edge pivots can expose any of these reductions. Forced-affine-form closure is likewise invariant under the invertible linear selector map of an edge pivot. For nonregularity, using the shore containing vertex 0 as the basis, contract element 0 and delete 1,5,7. The remaining rank-four matroid has seven elements and rank four after deleting any pair, so its dual is simple of rank three on seven elements, namely the Fano plane.

The pure images omit target 6. There are exactly eight disjoint signed chordless pairs outside opposing pure images, with sizes (4,4) twice, (5,3) four times, (5,4) twice. Both-sided circuit testing through size four therefore certifies the NO. The example is irreducible for the specified pivot, twin, bridge, connected-component and affine-inference procedures, but is decided by the circuit method. Ground-set minimality refers to the canonical bridgeless split class and the full selector/parity inference tests, as documented in the census, not to every nonregular matroid.
