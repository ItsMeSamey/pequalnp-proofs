# Affine exports from ordinary degree-two polynomial calculus

Let a Boolean system over GF(2) have quadratic guards and a single nonconstant affine equation m=0. Eliminate one variable using m, expand, and squarefree-reduce each guard to obtain rows g_i. Let A contain their full coefficient vectors and Q their squarefree degree-two coefficients. The only affine polynomials derivable in ordinary degree-two PC are 0 and m exactly when rank(A)=rank(Q).

Indeed, failure of equality gives a linear combination of guards whose reduced image is a nonzero affine polynomial. Each guard can be rewritten to its reduced image at degree two by the marker, its degree-one multiples, and the Boolean axioms. Thus the image itself is a new derivable affine polynomial. In the other direction, rank equality says the span of reduced guards contains no nonzero affine polynomial. The quotient map sends the marker and Boolean axioms to zero. By induction the reduced image of every line in an ordinary degree-two derivation stays in that span: addition is immediate, and every multiplication step has an affine input whose image hence vanishes. The kernel on affine inputs is exactly the span of m.

For the two independent charged switches, take a simple, balanced bipartite core of minimum degree at least three, a planted perfect matching, and two unmatched edges placed last at distinct holes h1,h2. Identify each matched pigeon with its planted hole. View a nonmatching core edge as an arc p→h. Eliminate the two-bit marker by writing the first unmatched coordinate as t and the second as 1+t. In Q, the columns for t times core variables have entries only from the two shifted unmatched guards. Their projected rows are the indicators of the undirected incident arc sets at h1 and h2, respectively, retaining both orientations as separate coordinates. These are independent: both are nonempty and h1 has an outgoing arc to a vertex other than h2. Thus in every dependency both trigger coefficients vanish.

For any core guard with pivot p→h, choose an outgoing arc h→g with g distinct from p. Such an arc exists since h has at least two nonmatching outgoing arcs. The monomial (p→h)(h→g) occurs only in the first pivot's guard: its affine mask contains all outgoing arcs of h. The second pivot's guard sees outgoing arcs of g and previous incoming arcs of g; the first arc is neither. No third core pivot can contribute this monomial, and all trigger coefficients are already zero. Thus every core coefficient vanishes.

In each switch, t times its root vertex bit occurs in the root guard, and its only other possible occurrence is in its shifted trigger guard. The root coefficient therefore vanishes. For each end v of each switch edge f, the monomial a_v z_f equates the coefficients on its vertex and edge guards. Connectivity propagates the zero root coefficient to the entire switch. Hence Q has full row rank and the only affine export is the original marker. This concerns ordinary PC, where multiplying a quadratic line by a variable charges degree three before Boolean reduction. It does not assert the same conclusion for an inference rule allowing multilinearization before measuring the degree of a multiplication.

## Bidirectional affine exchange refutes the two-switch family

Write h for a core hole, p(h) for its reference pigeon, O_h for the nonreference core edges leaving p(h), and I_h for the nonreference core edges incident to h. The affine selected value of its reference edge is 1+parity(O_h). The terminal core prefix has value 1+Q_h, where Q_h=parity(O_h)+parity(I_h) over GF(2). The unmatched edges are last and excluded from these prefixes.

For sufficiently large constant local marginal level the real engine can obtain Q_h=0 for every h. In each hole start with the expected parity of the reconstructed reference edge, bounded below by 1 minus the sum of the O_h bit marginals by the union bound. Crossing a core row, the old prefix cannot be one at the same time as its pivot. The expected new prefix therefore increases by the pivot marginal. All scopes involved have bounded width; local consistency identifies the common marginals. Summing these lower bounds gives a sum of terminal prefix expectations of at least the number of holes because each residual core edge occurs once in the negative and once in the positive term. Every terminal expectation is at most one. Hence all terminal parities equal one almost surely. In particular imposing any one opposite parity on its bounded-width scope destroys feasibility. This proves the affine communication step in the convention where the real engine may refute an opposite pin.

The Boolean engine at degree two already derives each ordinary unmatched guard x_j(1+Q_hj)=0 by summing the corresponding incidence gadget into its modified row. Multiply the imported Q_hj by x_j and add to get x_j=0. Derive both unmatched units and add the marker to refute. Thus the two-switch unit-exchange barrier does not extend to bidirectional affine exchanges, even when messages have bounded width.
