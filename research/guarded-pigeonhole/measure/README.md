# Safe-line projection with affine markers

Let A be an alternating binary adjacency matrix. At vertex i the guard is x_i (Ax)_i=0. On a set P of k vertices, impose independently one affine line x_i=0, y_i=0, or x_i+y_i=1, where y=Ax. Every line excludes the local violation (1,1); each valid point belongs to two lines.

Each such branch projects exactly to an unshifted alternating graph on E=V minus P, after coordinatewise affine bijections of the three valid pair symbols. At most the original marker rank r is needed. Every resulting witness lifts by linear equations, even when the measured coordinates contain a kernel. Consequently, given P, one can recognize a sufficient class for a same-marker bipartite solver by enumerating all 3^k branches and retaining those recognized by the marker-aware chart algorithm. Accept this reduction if their lines jointly cover every potentially feasible valid local pattern. Potential feasibility is tested by solving the original graph and marker linear equations with the prescribed local pair bits, ignoring exterior guards. The coverage test is sufficient. For fixed k and r<=3 it and the subsequent decision and witness algorithm are polynomial.

## Proof

In the binary symplectic pair space the graph W of A is Lagrangian. Put K=0 on E and on P take the product of measured line directions, so dim K=k. The measurement affine space translates the orthogonal complement of K. The pairing between W and K has rank k-dim(W intersect K). Thus the measured direction in W has dimension n-k+dim(W intersect K). Its exterior projection has kernel W intersect K and is isotropic because all measured directions are mutually orthogonal. The exterior direction is Lagrangian. Every unmarked branch even has a base point with all exterior selectors zero. Set x=0 also at x-measured vertices. On remaining measured vertices J solve Mx=diag M with M equal to A[J] plus a unit diagonal exactly on x+y=1 sites. This is solvable since any kernel vector z satisfies z dot diag M = z^T M z = 0.

The summed quadratic form q=sum x_i y_i vanishes throughout the exterior coset, since it vanishes on W and on the measured coordinates individually. Swap pair coordinates as needed to form a symmetric graph chart and then clear its diagonal with local shears. Use the affine lift of each local linear change T that fixes (1,1), namely v maps to T(v+11)+11. This preserves each local defect and takes the exterior direction to an alternating graph W_B. Its resulting q-zero coset equals W_B by polarization and W_B being its own symplectic orthogonal. Intersecting with r independent affine marker constraints before projection either yields inconsistency or costs at most r dimensions in the affine image. The marker equations in the new selectors and a witness lift both follow by elimination.

The retained branches are sound since every eliminated pair was safe. Every true witness occurs in one of them when the coverage test passes. The criterion permits several branches with different charts and permits the measured guards to have arbitrary degree.
