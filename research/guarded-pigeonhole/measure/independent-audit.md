# Partial safe-line measurements

Let W={(x,Ax)} over GF(2), where A is symmetric with zero diagonal. Partition the coordinate pairs into exterior E and measured I. Write the measured affine product of safe lines x_i=0, y_i=0, or x_i+y_i=1 as a+L in the measured symplectic space. Its direction L is Lagrangian, since it is the direct sum of one line in each pair. Set K=0⊕L in the full space. The measurement conditions define a translate of K⊥.

Without markers these conditions are always consistent. Set all exterior selectors and all selectors measured as x_i=0 to zero. On the remaining measured indices J solve Mx=diag(M), with M=A[J] plus ones on the diagonal at sites measured as x_i+y_i=1. For every z in the kernel of this symmetric matrix, z·diag(M)=zᵀMz=0, so the right side is in its column space. Such a basepoint is also native-feasible, as every measured local product vanishes and exterior selectors are zero.

The measurement fiber has direction J₀=W∩K⊥. The rank of the symplectic pairing of W with K is dim K−dim(W∩K), since W=W⊥. The exterior projection of J₀ has kernel W∩K and hence dimension |E|. Its exterior symplectic pairing vanishes: the full directions belong to W, and their measured coordinates belong to L. Thus the exterior image is a translate of a Lagrangian. Every exterior point in the image has q_E=0 because q vanishes on W and every measured local product vanishes.

Let H be the original linear map of selector markers. Imposing Hx=t can make the branch empty. In a nonempty branch the additional exterior codimension equals rank(H restricted to J₀) minus rank(H restricted to W∩K), in particular at most rank H. This follows by fixing an exterior point: the marker values on its lifts comprise one coset of H(W∩K). Gaussian elimination computes the resulting affine exterior equations and lifts any valid exterior witness.

Choose local linear swaps to put the exterior direction into symmetric graph form and local shears to clear its diagonal. Denote the combined linear transformation by T and the resulting alternating graph by W_B. Apply the canonical affine lift θ(z)=Tz+1+T1, where 1 is the tuple of local (1,1). This transformation fixes each unique defective local pair and hence preserves q. It maps the full exterior image to a q-zero coset b+W_B. Taking w=0 and then polarizing q(b+w)=0 for w in W_B proves q(b)=0 and b∈W_B⊥=W_B. Thus the full coset maps to W_B itself and the marked affine subfiber maps to ordinary affine marker rows on its selectors. At that point the existing completion recognizer applies with the residual rank.
