# A checkable class for all single-marker queries

For a diagonal-free matrix M, write F for the Boolean solutions of all unmarked guards x_i(b_i+Σ_j M_ij x_j)=0 over GF(2). Fix a constant t. Enumerate all members of F supported on at most t coordinates, retaining actual models forming a basis for their span W_t. Compute all linear consequences E derivable by ordinary degree-two polynomial calculus (PC) from the guards and Boolean axioms. Let U be the orthogonal complement of E. Always W_t ⊆ span F ⊆ U. The recognizer accepts exactly when dim W_t + dim E = n. On acceptance it handles every single marker: if it is nonzero on a stored basis model, that model is a witness; otherwise its coefficient vector belongs to E and adding its degree-two derivation to the marker refutes the instance. This equality of dimensions is computable from the input, without testing the semantic condition span F = U. Runtime is polynomial for fixed t. The zero model makes every affine consequence homogeneous.

For concreteness, normal-form PC2 saturation uses coordinates for linear and squarefree quadratic monomials. Begin with the guard rows. Extract the entire linear subspace of the current span by prioritizing quadratic columns in elimination. Adjoin the multilinearized products of a basis of that linear subspace by each variable. Repeat until the row space stabilizes. This computes ordinary PC degree two: ordinary degree-two multiplication by a variable requires an input of degree at most one; each reduction of x_i² costs just its Boolean axiom. Conversely all represented operations lift without exceeding degree two. The space has O(n²) dimensions. This refers to ordinary PC; a calculus that permits multiplying a quadratic if its quotient reduction stays quadratic can have additional rules.

**Concrete asymmetric families.** Take any connected graph of reciprocal arcs spanning all the vertices, put b_i=1 at every vertex, and allow further directed arcs. The two-point models are exactly the reciprocal pairs. Their incidence vectors span the even-weight subspace. If three vertices induce a subgraph in which every vertex has exactly one outgoing arc among the other two, the triple is feasible and has odd weight. Thus W_3 is the full ambient space and every nonzero marker has a witness on at most three vertices. The hypotheses can be tested with graph searches and triple enumeration. In particular, use a bidirected cycle of any length n≥6, and add a directed triangle on three pairwise nonconsecutive vertices. Row and column degrees are ≤3, the entire graph is one asymmetric strongly connected component, and the all-ones marker requires exactly three vertices. Its weight-two witness span is strictly smaller for every n. Arbitrary disjoint unions with balanced symmetric components can also have nonzero exported equations; for b_i=1 on connected symmetric components, their reciprocal edges already span the even-weight hyperplane, whose normal is exported by summing the component guards. These instances with both types of components have nontrivial positive and negative marker queries.

**Limit of exporting from an asymmetric strongly connected component.** In any single strongly connected asymmetric graph with n>1, ordinary degree-two PC exports no nonzero linear consequences from the unmarked axioms, for any b. For a guard sum with row coefficients c_i to be affine, every distinct pair must satisfy c_i M_ij = c_j M_ji. Thus coefficients coincide along reciprocal edges and vanish at a one-way arc's tail. If c_i=1 then every outgoing arc from i is reciprocal and its head likewise has coefficient one. Strong connectivity makes any nonempty such support the entire graph, contradicting the asymmetric arc. Hence the zero guard combination is the only affine one. Boolean axioms are absent in the squarefree presentation and cannot help cancel mixed terms in the ordinary presentation. The saturation therefore terminates at initialization. Strong connectivity and asymmetry alone say nothing about semantic completeness of these exports. For example on a simple directed cycle with every b_i=1, the only models are all-zero and all-one. The positive class above succeeds because its feasible short supports prove semantic completeness individually.

The symmetric case and the earlier algorithm for symmetric or simple-cycle strongly connected components appear in the [component solver](../scc-guards/README.md) for reciprocal components. The bounded-support recognizer can be combined with existing algorithms. It can reject easy instances when their model spans need larger supports or their true affine equations have higher PC degree.

**A strict hierarchy even on bounded-degree asymmetric components.** For any t≥2 and any k≥3, form a directed cycle of kt vertices, in k successive blocks of length t. In each block set the first t−1 offsets to one and the last offset to zero. Add a directed arc from the zero-offset terminal of the first block to the zero-offset terminal of the second. The graph is strongly connected, is not a simple cycle or symmetric, and every row and column has at most two entries. In each block every suffix ending at the terminal is a feasible support: each selected nonterminal has exactly its selected successor, and both possible outneighbors of a selected terminal are outside that suffix. These suffixes have size at most t and form an invertible triangular basis block by block. Consequently W_t is the full space. Conversely, selecting the first vertex of a block forces by its guard every later vertex of that block, so no support of size less than t contains the first coordinate. Thus W_(t−1) is proper and the corresponding criterion rejects, since the PC2 export space is zero. The marker consisting of this one coordinate has minimum witness size precisely t. The parameters t and k vary independently.

**Logarithmic supports at bounded degree.** Let D≥2 bound the degree of the undirected dependency graph formed by forgetting arc directions. Every weak component of the support induced by a feasible vector is itself feasible: all selected vertices in it retain the values of their selected neighbors. Summing the disjoint component indicators recovers the original vector. Therefore the span W_t needs only connected supports. Enumerate from each starting vertex all walks of length at most 2(t−1). For each visited set of size at most t, test feasibility and add an independent solution to the actual-model list. The depth-first traversal of a spanning tree of any connected s-set has length 2(s−1) and visits precisely that set. The cost is n D^{2t} times a polynomial in the input size, including elimination, even without deduplication. For bounded D this is deterministic polynomial time for t=O(log n). For example, in the preceding hierarchy choose k≥2^t so t≤log₂ n. This single polynomial-time recognizer then accepts asymmetric examples requiring unbounded witnesses, with no witness algorithm for arbitrary unrestricted matrices assumed.
