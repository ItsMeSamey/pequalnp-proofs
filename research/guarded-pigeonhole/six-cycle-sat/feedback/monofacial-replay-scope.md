The theorem assumes the fixed hub is loopless and deleting it leaves a connected graph. The independent audit checked 1,841 queries satisfying these restrictions. An unrestricted test also finds a counterexample. Take a triangle on the hub and vertices 1 and 2, add a loop at the hub, interleave its two ends with the triangle incidences, and insert a second edge from 1 to 2. There are no bridges, yet under every free rotation the two darts of the inserted edge occupy different faces. [Theorem](one-hub-monofacial.md) · [Exploratory checker](check_monofacial_bridge.py)
