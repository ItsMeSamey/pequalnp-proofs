# A proportional reduction from counting SAT assignments to counting open transfers

For any signed circuit over AND and NOT, build a signed exact-one formula with a unique extension for every input assignment that makes the output true. Handle a NOT gate by changing the sign of its output name. For each AND on signed names x,y, introduce fresh q00,q01,q10,z and use these four exact-one triples, with z naming the output:

    (¬x,q10,z), (x,q00,q01), (¬y,q01,z), (y,q00,q10).

The first two constraints place exactly one unit in the row indexed by the truth of x in the two-by-two table (q00,q01;q10,z), and zero units in the other row. The last two do the same for the column indexed by y. Their intersection forces the unique table with its unit at (x,y). This proves functionality also when the signed inputs repeat or are complements. A signed output name v can be forced true by the exact-one triple (¬v,¬v,v). Represent the original SAT formula by a linear-size AND/NOT circuit via De Morgan, retaining every original input variable even if unused. Thus the number of solutions of the resulting m exact-one clauses equals the original #SAT answer. Trivial constant formulas can be handled directly.

For each exact-one triple (a,b,c), insert an ordinary ternary clause (a∨b∨c) and the three binary clauses (¬a∨¬b), (¬a∨¬c), (¬b∨¬c). Use the [independent native open compiler](README.md) with one gate per occurrence, including repeated occurrences. Build its ternary choice device as a full binary depth-two tree of three forks and three merges. Three leaves carry the literal occurrence tests. At the fourth leaf the fork's output goes to a fresh external sink and the merge's corresponding input has its own fresh external source. No wire joins these terminals. Every successful route therefore visits two forks and two merges, irrespective of which literal it chooses. Use the usual binary fork and merge on the three pair clauses.

At a satisfying variable assignment there is one possible path through the variable stages, and both control bits of each variable fork/merge pair are fixed. In the ternary clause, precisely one occurrence is true. Both false occurrences' blockers have been traversed; the true occurrence's test must be traversed. All three occurrence bits are fixed. The two visited forks and two visited merges are fixed by the selected path; the unvisited fork and merge have two choices each. This gives four ternary-clause settings. In two of the binary clauses only one literal is true, so both occurrence bits and the two routing bits are fixed. In the remaining binary clause both literals are true. Either test can be chosen and the other occurrence gate is free, giving four settings. The product for one triple is exactly 16. This argument counts literal positions and applies to repeated and opposite signed names without modification. Every unsuccessful assignment has zero completions, since a successful path through all four ordinary clauses would certify exact-one.

All multiplicities refer to full native switch masks and multiply across gadgets only after fixing the variable assignment. Each full mask has just one route from the named source, so complete settings contributing to different paths are distinct. Consequently the number of accepted masks is exactly 16^m times #SAT. The compiler uses 2n+21m independent gates for n variables of the exact-one formula. Exact division, or a binary right shift by 4m, recovers the original answer. Decision instances with no satisfying assignment consequently map to zero. The unbounded-arity open input/output model permits the fresh sources and sinks; no closed routing assertion is used.

The [physical checker](check_proportional.py) applies the padded fork and merge to the actual open compiler wiring. It branches on the bits encountered by the marked token, and for each successful partial mask counts every unused gate bit. All 216 ordered signed triples over three variables, including repeats and complements, matched the expected per-assignment histogram. Four functional AND variants and a forced-output circuit also passed. This checks complete-mask multiplicities by aggregating identical partial traces; it does not enumerate the exponentially many full masks of the larger gadgets individually.
