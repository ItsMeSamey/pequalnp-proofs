# An exponential two-cut minimum-orbit value oracle lower bound

There are m labeled optional rotations of three distinct tracks, fixed arbitrary feedback, and two marks at one seam. An oracle query specifies arbitrary penalties by original gate setting and by straight/crossed marked seam, and returns the minimum of penalty plus orbit count. Infinite penalties allow pinning. This includes any separate reversible auxiliary circuit plugged into the two cuts: each of its settings has one through permutation and an additive number of internal cycles. A deterministic procedure operating only through these scalar queries needs 2^m queries in the worst case.

Use 2m+2 tracks a,x0,x1,y1,...,xm,ym. For the positive feedback F use cycles (a,x0) and (x1,y1,...,xm,ym). For the negative feedback use (a) and (x0,x1,y1,...,xm,ym). At time i left multiply optionally by gi=(xi,yi,x(i-1)). Observe the seam immediately before feedback, so the full return map is gm^sm ... g1^s1 F. Mark a and xm.

Before gate i the image of xi is yi, since earlier gates never affect that output. Left multiplication by gi deletes xi immediately before yi and reinserts it immediately before x(i-1). The previous arrows p→xi, xi→yi, q→x(i-1) become p→yi, xi→x(i-1), q→xi, with distinct sources. Thus both permanent cycles remain intact on all coordinates other than the moved point. The active gate copies the cycle membership of x(i-1) to xi, whereas an inactive gate leaves xi on the false backbone. The marked pair belongs to one cycle exactly when all gates are active and x0 started with a. Every setting in both instances has exactly two cycles.

To move the unique positive mask to any theta, replace controls s by s xor b where b is the bitwise complement of theta. Rewrite each complemented gate factor g^(1-s) as g^(-s)g. Push fixed factors rightward using C h^s=(C h C^-1)^s C. Absorb the final fixed permutation into F. All conjugated optional gates remain single oriented 3-cycles, and the return map agrees pointwise at the same seam.

The uncrossed count is always two. Crossing the marked wires gives one on every mask of the negative case and on every mask but theta of its positive counterpart, where it gives three. A query differs only if EVERY minimizer on the negative input has crossed seam and mask theta. Thus each query on the negative transcript excludes at most one hidden positive, even adaptively. With an equal mixture of negative and uniformly random singleton positive, a q-query randomized algorithm has success at most 1/2+q/(2·2^m), by conditioning on its coins. This is a black-box interface lower bound. Explicit circuit descriptions may expose the structure directly.
