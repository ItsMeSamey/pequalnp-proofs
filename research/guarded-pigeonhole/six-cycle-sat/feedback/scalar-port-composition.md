# Rank-two composition of paired Pfaffian scalar representations

Work over the binary field. A scalar representation of an alternating matrix on indexed pairs is a square matrix M on the pair names such that every principal minor equals the Pfaffian on the corresponding union of pairs. All subsets are required, including those containing any already-present virtual pair.

Suppose the cross block at a whole-pair split factors as UVᵀ with two columns on each side. Append U to the first side and V to the second as the incidences of a new virtual pair, with zero internal edge. For a restricted side write f for its ordinary Pfaffian and g for its Pfaffian after adding the virtual pair. Group perfect matchings of the full restriction by their cross endpoints I and J. Each contribution is the product of the two remaining internal Pfaffians and the determinant of the cross minor on I,J. In characteristic two the determinant sums precisely the cross matchings. The number of cross edges must be even, and all minors larger than two vanish. For two edges the minor factors as det U[I,:] det V[J,:]. Summing over I and J independently gives g on each side because the two virtual vertices must match distinct ordinary vertices. Thus the full Pfaffian equals f₁f₂+g₁g₂. For rank zero or one only f₁f₂ remains.

Let the supplied scalar side matrices, with virtual pair name last, be [Mᵢ,uᵢ; vᵢᵀ,0]. The corner is necessarily zero by testing the singleton port. Remove the port names, use diagonal blocks M₁,M₂, and put u₁v₂ᵀ and u₂v₁ᵀ in the off-diagonal blocks. In the determinant expansion fix the outgoing rows and incoming columns on each side. The numbers of outgoing and incoming indices on each side agree. The rank-one cross minors vanish for sizes above one. Size zero contributes the product of the ordinary determinants. Size one contributes the product of the two bordered determinants, by independent sums over a local row and a local column used by each port. This proves the same product-sum formula, simultaneously on every principal restriction. A direct sum suffices for rank at most one.

In a recursive decomposition, any prior virtual pair is kept intact on its chosen side and is treated as an ordinary label until its own parent consumes it. Because the invariant quantifies over every subset, it applies also when prior ports are present or absent. A fresh pair name is consumed at each rank-two join. No terminal pair is required at the root. With n₁,n₂≥2 the child measures (n₁+1)−2 and (n₂+1)−2 are positive, sum to n−2, and are strictly smaller than the parent measure. These closure statements assume suitable scalar representations at the leaves. They do not establish either their general existence or a recognition algorithm for arbitrary unsupplied trees.

Run python check_scalar_ports.py. Four random asymmetric scalar bags per trial are converted to alternating bipartite doubles, then independently transformed by determinant-one basis changes within physical pairs. The checker performs three binary joins, including two intermediate reconstructions in which a different port remains live, and compares recursively enumerated Pfaffians with independently expanded scalar determinants. On 60 seeded trees all 5,760 principal restrictions agree. The alternating joins use the swap matrix between the boundary columns, an invertible two-by-two change of basis of determinant one; this preserves each bordered Pfaffian.
