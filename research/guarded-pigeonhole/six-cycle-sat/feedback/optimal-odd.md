# Only the minimum-cycle routings need to have odd masses

Let k be the minimum number of orbits among physical switch settings. The paired delta-matroid algorithm computes this number; apply the minimum componentwise. A cyclic cap at k points can produce one orbit only if it started from exactly k orbits, each containing one mark. In particular all canonical cap polynomials used here have coefficient zero on every nonoptimal switch word. The coarsening theorem says a rooted-positive switch word exists exactly when a rooted-positive optimal word exists.

If k supplied landmarks hit every optimal routing once per orbit, then use P_landmarks in place of the universal mask envelope in the protected-landmark theorem. The O(k²) omitted-pair sum is the separated-optimum indicator and P_landmarks is the indicator of every optimum. This hypothesis is weaker than universal validity on all switch settings. The earlier universal landmark recognizer tests a different hypothesis.

For fixed k at least two, suppose there exists an unknown binary weighting on n candidate points giving odd mass to every orbit of every optimal routing. Let F(w) denote the sum of cyclic caps on k-sets, weighted by the product of the chosen bits, and R(w) the analogous sum on k−2 sets capped jointly with the two roots. Duplicate positions by passive subdivision before capping. On an optimum the coefficient of F is the product of all cycle masses. For a joined optimum the coefficient of R is zero. For a split optimum it is the product of masses of the k−2 nonroot cycles. All coefficients at nonoptima vanish.

Maintain linear equations on w, initially empty. Sample w uniformly from their affine solution set and look for a nonzero evaluation of D=F(w)+R(w) over a field of size at least 4m for m positive gates. If successful reconstruct a physical mask using the fixed field evaluation. Return its witness if joined. Otherwise the mask is split, all nonroot cycles have odd mass, and at least one root cycle C has even mass. Add the necessary linear condition w(C)=1. This increases the rank or certifies inconsistency. Repeat with fresh randomness after each update.

If there is a joined optimum, fix one for the analysis. Under the existence promise, adding the k equations of that optimum to the current affine space is consistent. At least 2^-k of trial vectors therefore give D a nonzero coefficient at this optimum. A random evaluation detects a nonzero D with probability at least 3/4. Thus after O(2^k log((n+1)/epsilon)) trials at any stage with neither a discovered witness nor a new equation, reporting no witness has error at most epsilon/(n+1) on a positive promised instance. There are at most n rank increases. Every reported positive can be traced directly, including outside the promise. Inconsistency disproves the odd-weight promise and calls for abstention. The runtime is n^O(k) times a polynomial in the explicit input length and log(1/epsilon). The k=1 case is direct from a single-tour witness. The method neither recognizes arbitrary valid land­marks among all optima nor requires the total orbit count to be constant across masks.
