# Physical cuts of an untied circuit

Take a connected 4-regular multigraph with a reference Euler circuit, and partition its vertices into I and J. Let δ count crossing edges with multiplicity, let c_I,c_J count induced components (including isolated vertices), and let Q give the parity of the crossing multiplicities between these components. For the chord interlacement matrix A of the Euler circuit,

    rank A[I,J] = δ/2 − c_I − c_J + 1 + rank Q.

All ranks are binary. Label each edge with a bit. At I impose even sum over incident ends; at J impose identical labels on all four ends. Traverse the reference tour. The two changes at the two visits to each I vertex coincide by conservation. Starting with any one initial edge bit and one change per I vertex recovers the labeling along the entire circuit. At a J vertex the common bits at its two visits coincide exactly when the sum of changes at interlacing I chords vanishes. This gives dimension 1+|I|−rank A[I,J].

Alternatively assign one bit per J component, thereby fixing every cut edge. The remaining incidence system within I is solvable exactly when its prescribed parities sum to zero in each component, i.e. Q times the assigned bits vanishes. There are |E(I)|−|I|+c_I free internal edge bits. Loops are zero columns in the incidence system and count twice in the degrees. Equating dimensions and using |E(I)|=2|I|−δ/2 proves the identity including empty shores.

For unbounded width use the L by L torus (L≥4) with three directions horizontal, vertical and diagonal, six incidences per site. Every proper nonempty subset has at least six crossing edges: if it is non-invariant under all three unit translations, each direction contributes at least two. If invariant under one, it consists of parallel full lines; translations in the other directions cross in multiples of L, and each contributes at least 2L. For a balanced cut with sides ≥L²/3, if at least L/3 rows are partial then horizontal edges give ≥2L/3 crossings. Otherwise both a full and an empty row exist and every column contributes at least two vertical crossings.

Orient one forward arc in every direction from each site. Realize each site by one whole three-track gate (paired consecutive transpositions sharing a central track), with its own three tracks. In one feedback permutation join its outgoing track ends to the destination's incoming track ends according to the oriented arcs, using each port once. Disjoint gates may be scheduled in any order. Suppressing passive segments leaves two 4-valent locations per site, joined by the shared track segment. Contracting that internal edge recovers the torus, including exactly its six external incidences. Every connected component of either induced shore of a nontrivial whole-gate cut corresponds to a proper torus vertex set, and its boundary lies entirely in the cut. Thus c_I+c_J≤2δ/6, and the rank is at least δ/6+1. Every subcubic leaf layout has an edge with both shores of size at least one third (orient toward any shore of size greater than two thirds and use a sink). Consequently maximum cut rank is Ω(L), for every reference tour and whole-gate layout in this family.

The checker generated 450 double-occurrence Euler words including loops and parallel edges and tested every one of 51,100 cuts against the direct interlacement matrix.
