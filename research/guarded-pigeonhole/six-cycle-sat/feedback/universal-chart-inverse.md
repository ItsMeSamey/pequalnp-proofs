# Inverse transfers of a universal paired chart

Let the binary alternating pencil have disjoint variable edges, one per gate. Translate each edge variable by one so the universal envelope is E=∏h_i. Let U be its untagged mandatory coordinates. The top coefficient is Pf(A[U])=1, so eliminate U by Schur complementation. Write C for the residual constant alternating matrix, and M=C+⊕h_i J for the residual pencil, with J the two-by-two alternating unit. Universality means precisely that every nonempty principal union of whole pairs has Pfaffian zero in C. Work in the rational function field and set t_i=1/h_i.

## A lineable bag

A bag is lineable if there is one chosen nonzero binary vector per pair with mutually vanishing pairings between these vectors. A supplied choice can be checked directly. Use each as the first vector of a local determinant-one basis. Grouping first coordinates together puts the bag matrix in the form [0,D;Dᵀ,Q], with D=diag(h)+N. Every principal minor of N on a nonempty subset is zero, as shown by the paired Pfaffian on the corresponding set. Thus the directed support of N is acyclic. A loop would be a nonzero singleton minor. On the vertices of a shortest directed cycle any extra arc would induce a shorter cycle, so that principal minor would be the nonzero cycle product. Topologically order the gates.

In this order each entry of D⁻¹ is the sum over directed paths from its row to its column of the products of their arc entries and one t_i for every visited vertex, including endpoints. The length-zero path has value t_i. This follows from the finite geometric series for (I+diag(t)N)⁻¹diag(t). The full inverse is [D⁻ᵀQD⁻¹,D⁻ᵀ;D⁻¹,0], as direct multiplication shows. Its entries and arbitrary constant bilinear probes have read-once oblivious algebraic branching programs in the same topological variable order. For each path sum scan vertices in order, retaining the mass whose last visited vertex was j and the empty initial path. At vertex i add its incoming weighted sum, multiplied by t_i, to state i, permitting a start at the designated source. Prior masses persist. Read the target at the end. Width is O(n+1). Products of two such programs use layerwise tensor products, sums use direct sums, and the Q contraction has at most n² terms per entry. The resulting individual layer degree can reach two before cancellations. This is an ordinary polynomial construction over F₂[t], without Boolean reduction.

## Partition with global cut rank one

Suppose the pairs are partitioned into supplied lineable bags and, for each bag, its incidence with the union of every other bag has rank at most one. Verify these conditions by Gaussian elimination and check the supplied lines. No maximality condition is needed. Let f_i span this exterior column space in bag i, using zero for an isolated bag. With F having one column f_i supported on each respective bag, the off-bag blocks form FHFᵀ for a symmetric zero-diagonal constant matrix H. Indeed each cross block has its columns in the left bag's line and its rows in the right bag's line. Write K for the direct sum of the bag pencils. Each K_i is invertible over the function field, and its inverse is alternating by congruence. Hence FᵀK⁻¹F=0, including its diagonal. Direct multiplication now gives

    M⁻¹ = K⁻¹ + K⁻¹ F H Fᵀ K⁻¹.

Consequently any constant bilinear probe is a sum of at most b local transfers plus b(b−1) products of two local transfers on distinct bags. Concatenate the b topological orders arbitrarily. Every term has polynomial width in this same order by padding unused variables with identity layers. Summing all terms preserves polynomial width. This certificate uses each bag's cut in the full matrix. Small cuts in successively restricted matrices do not verify it.

## Mandatory coordinates and bordered ratios

For pencil [A,B;Bᵀ,G] in mandatory-then-paired order, Schur elimination gives M=G+BᵀA⁻¹B. For probes a=(a₀,a₁), b=(b₀,b₁), the inverse transfer is a₀ᵀA⁻¹b₀ plus the M⁻¹ transfer with probes a₁+BᵀA⁻¹a₀ and b₁+BᵀA⁻¹b₀. Thus mandatory coordinates require only constant linear operations. For a border with incidence V and alternating constant corner W, the bordered Pfaffian divided by E equals Pf(W+VᵀP⁻¹V), where P is the original translated full pencil. For a fixed even border size this Pfaffian is a constant-size sum of products of transfers. The same-order width stays polynomial by tensor products. These identities hold in the function field and the ratio is represented as a polynomial in t. The construction does not infer lineability or an appropriate partition from universality alone.

## Arbitrary recursive cuts and deterministic scalar identity tests

A stronger test needs only a supplied recursive binary decomposition. At every internal node the two children have off-diagonal block uvᵀ of rank at most one **within this node**. Every leaf is lineable with a supplied basis. Each induced subtree is universal, since its nonempty paired principal restrictions are also restrictions of C. At each node retain the polynomial vector x consisting of 1 and all inverse entries of its induced pencil in fixed original coordinates. A leaf changes back to original coordinates by constant congruence. For children L,R the parent inverse has diagonal blocks L⁻¹ and R⁻¹ and upper-right block (L⁻¹u)(vᵀR⁻¹). This follows by multiplication using uᵀL⁻¹u=vᵀR⁻¹v=0. The lower-left is the transpose. For a zero cross block take u=0. The parent vector thus equals a fixed bilinear map B(x_L,x_R): each unchanged entry is multiplied by the other child's constant coordinate. The parent dimension is at most 1+4n². Parent maps may be built densely in polynomial time.

For fixed d compute the coefficient span of x raised to its d-fold tensor power. This one vector represents every degree-at-most-d polynomial expression in inverse entries by a constant linear functional, using the constant coordinate for padding. At a leaf, combine its same-order entry programs into a vector-output program using direct sums and a constant output matrix. Tensor the variable layers d times and keep ordered tensor outputs. For a row-state program, start with the initial row span; at every layer replace the current basis by a basis of its images under all coefficient matrices of that layer, and finally multiply by the output matrix. Distinct variables per layer justify this span recursion; tensor products at one layer can contain powers up to 2d, whose coefficient matrices must first be summed by like degree.

At an internal node tensor B d times, permuting tensor factors to group the left and right inputs. Variables in the two subtrees are disjoint, so every monomial coefficient is the image of a left coefficient and a right coefficient, and each such pair occurs for its concatenated monomial. Therefore the parent span is exactly the span of the images of all pairs of child basis vectors. Row reduction bounds all dimensions by (1+4n²)^d. For fixed d, dense arithmetic and elimination at every node take polynomial time with no depth or common-layout restriction. A scalar linear functional of the root tensor is identically zero precisely when it annihilates the computed span. The rank-one tests in this construction apply to induced cuts, with no requirement that earlier border directions remain rank one in descendant cuts. All target probes use the same universal chart and decomposition. The method by itself provides no common tree for unrelated charts.
