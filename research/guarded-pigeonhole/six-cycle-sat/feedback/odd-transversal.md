# Learning an odd transversal

Assume every mask produces exactly k cycles, with k fixed. Let n count candidate mark positions. For each k-set T form the canonical one-tour mask polynomial P_T after cyclically reconnecting T. Its coefficient tests hitting every cycle. For binary trial weights w, the coefficient of F(w)=Σ_T (∏_{t∈T}w_t)P_T equals the product of all k cycle masses. Test F(w) against the universal mask polynomial. Any discrepancy extracts a mask and an actual cycle C of even mass. Append the linear equation Σ_{i on C}w_i=1 to a Gaussian system and choose any new trial solution. Each violation increases rank or proves inconsistency. At most n+1 rounds suffice. Fresh field samples and a union bound bound premature termination. Under the exactly-k promise, an inconsistent system proves that no odd transversal exists. Passing the randomized tests only supplies probabilistic recognition. The odd-transversal rooted algorithm of two-cycle-promise.md then uses n^O(k) polynomial-size Pfaffian computations including weight discovery. Every returned rooted witness is traced and checked. Absence of an odd transversal calls for abstention.
