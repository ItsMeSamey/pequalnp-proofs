# Three protected orbits, deterministically

Suppose three landmarks z1,z2,z3 meet each orbit of every routing exactly once. This supplied promise has a deterministic check by the universal single-cap theorem. Roots in different untied components cannot share an orbit. In the component of both roots keep its own landmarks; one or two use the corresponding earlier algorithms. Assume all three lie in the retained component.

Choose one untied reference tour with matrix A and twist T. For a physical routing let X be the changed transpositions. Binary arc labels constant on each of the three routed cycles modulo global constants identify with ker A[X] by their jumps at the transpositions. For marked cuts p,q the fixed column c(p,q), recording reference interlacements with their chord, restricts on X to the functional giving the difference of the two labels. This follows by summing jumps along the reference between the cuts. Passive subdivisions separate coincident physical positions if needed.

On that kernel e1=c(z1,z3) and e2=c(z2,z3) restrict to a basis of the dual. Put d=e1+e2. The restriction of b_p=c(p,z3)+d has coordinates (0,1),(1,0),(1,1) in this basis according to the class of p. Two such columns restrict independently exactly when the roots lie on different orbits.

Append to A the two fixed border columns b_a,b_b, their transposes and a zero 2 by 2 corner. Keep these indices mandatory. For each legal X its bordered section is nonsingular exactly when the columns span the dual kernel of A[X]. Indeed any kernel vector (u,v) satisfies A[X]u+Bv=0 and B^t u=0. Pairing the first equation with the original kernel forces v=0 when the restrictions span, then the second forces u=0. Otherwise there is a nonzero original kernel vector annihilated by both columns.

Construct the raw tagged-edge pencil using the enlarged matrix: for an even-twist gate add a tagged bypass, for an odd-twist gate add an auxiliary vertex with one tagged and one untagged edge. No tagged edge touches a border vertex, so its matching expansion takes the desired mandatory bordered section for every physical mask. Its Pfaffian coefficient is one precisely for separated roots. Raw tag complements do not affect comparison against the all-mask product. Apply deterministic single-pencil universality and gate pinning to recover a failing physical setting. The auxiliary border need not be realizable by physical switches.

For more than three protected orbits build the graph on landmark classes whose edges ij mean the roots can occupy these two distinct classes. Each edge is tested deterministically by a one-tour cyclic cap on the two roots and the remaining landmarks, using zero-broken-pair existence. Given a proper coloring of this graph in at most three colors, merge classes of each color by fixed swaps at their anchor arcs. Every mask then has one orbit per color and two formerly distinct root orbits remain distinct. Keep a representative anchor per color and apply the preceding result. This gives e.g. a recognizable class when the attainable graph is 2-degenerate. A polynomial algorithm for coloring arbitrary graphs is not assumed.
