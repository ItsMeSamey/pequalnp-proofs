# A single binary totally singular cap cannot recognize all abstract splits

Fix k≥4 distinct class labels and two root labels a,b, for N=k+2 labels in any fixed linear order. Consider every ordered split permutation (a i)(b j) with distinct class labels i,j, and both oriented joined permutations on a,b,i for every class i. Other labels are fixed. In the binary boundary model below, any one maximal totally singular cap transverse to every split is transverse to every join. Exactly 2^((k−1)(k−2)/2) caps are transverse to all splits.

This statement quantifies over the full abstract family of permutations. It does not assert that a single physical routing instance realizes that family, or that a cap cannot distinguish the smaller family realized by a particular instance. It treats one binary totally singular cap with acceptance by transversality; protocols combining several cap outcomes are outside its scope.

## Boundary spaces and the residual form

Use E, the even vectors in F₂^N, and its dual E*, represented by vectors modulo the all-ones vector. In E⊕E* put q(y,z)=y·z. Let T be the matrix with ones strictly above the diagonal and zero elsewhere. For a permutation σ use the subspace Lσ of all (y,[x+Ty]) with y=x+σx, over all x in F₂^N. For these y, exactly half the differing coordinates have x equal to one because x and σx have equal weight. Thus x·y equals wt(y)/2 modulo two. Also y·Ty equals binom(wt(y),2), the same parity. This gives q=0. The parametrization has kernel the span of the all-ones vector, so Lσ has dimension N−1, maximal possible. Its projection Y onto E is two-dimensional for each listed permutation: for a split it is generated by e_a+e_i,e_b+e_j, and for a join by e_a+e_i,e_b+e_i.

For a maximal totally singular cap K let P be its projection onto E, of codimension d. Its vertical subspace is P⊥, the annihilator in E*, by polarization and dimension. Similarly the vertical subspace of Lσ is Y⊥. Transversality means zero intersection and requires P+Y=E since otherwise the vertical subspaces intersect. Assuming P+Y=E, compare the two lift covectors of y in D=P∩Y by adding them and restricting the result to D. Their sum on D is independent of the chosen lifts. This map from D to its dual defines an alternating bilinear form, because both lifted spaces are totally singular. A nonzero y lifts to a common vector iff it is in the radical: vanishing of the difference on D means it lies in D⊥=P⊥+Y⊥ and can be removed by vertical adjustments. There is already no common vertical vector. Hence transversality is equivalent to nonsingularity of this residual form.

## The possible projection codimensions

If d>2, then P+Y cannot be E. If d=1 and P+Y=E, D is one-dimensional and its alternating form is singular.

If d=2, transversality is equivalent to the projection of Y onto E/P being an isomorphism. Choose a reference coordinate r and represent every coordinate i by the quotient label of e_i+e_r, so pairwise sums have their intended images. Denote the root labels by α,β and the class labels by l_i. Universal split transversality requires α+l_i and β+l_j to be independent whenever i≠j. In particular each l_i differs from both roots. If α=β, all class labels must be distinct among three remaining values, impossible for k≥4. If α≠β, two values remain; their sum is α+β. Assigning different values at i,j would give α+l_i=β+l_j. Hence all class labels coincide. Both generators of every joined plane project to distinct nonzero vectors, so all joins pass. Conversely this pattern makes every split pass. The kernel P is unique: after translating labels to make the common class zero, the two root offsets form a basis of the quotient, unique up to change of basis. For fixed P, lifts defining K modulo P⊥ are precisely alternating forms on P. Its dimension is k−1, giving the count above.

If d=0, K is the graph of an alternating form B on E. For an ordered split take u=e_a+e_i and v=e_b+e_j. The lift of u in Lσ can use x=e_a, giving covector [e_a+Tu]. Its value on v is v·Tu since the four endpoints are distinct. This equals χ(ai,bj), the crossing indicator of the two chords: the number modulo two of endpoints of the second chord strictly between the endpoints of the first. The residual form on this plane is nonsingular exactly when
     
    χ(ai,bj) + B(u,v) = 1.

Exchange i,j and add the two required equations. The two B terms add to B(e_a+e_b,e_i+e_j), by bilinearity and alternation (one may first express all coordinate pairs relative to a common reference r). Among the three endpoint pairings exactly one crosses, so the two crossing terms sum to 1+χ(ab,ij)=1+t_i+t_j, where t_i records whether i is strictly between a and b. Write b_i=B(e_a+e_b,e_i+e_r). The equation becomes (b_i+t_i)+(b_j+t_j)=1 for each pair of distinct classes. Adding the three equations on any triangle gives 0=1. Thus no graph cap is transverse to every ordered split.

## Exhaustive check

Run python check_pure_cap_four.py. It enumerates every subspace P in reduced echelon form, every alternating lift on P, and tests transversality by direct rank against permutations constructed from the coordinate definition of Lσ. For four classes with interleaved roots it checks all 4,590 caps. Exactly eight are transverse to every split; each is transverse to all eight oriented joined permutations.
