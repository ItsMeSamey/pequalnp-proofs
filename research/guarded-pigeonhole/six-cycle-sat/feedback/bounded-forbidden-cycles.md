# A normal form with all forbidden cycles of length two or three

Number wires by their incoming sockets, grouped into triples by switch. Write F for the forbidden-turn permutation and B for the product of the independently chosen forward or backward rotations of the switch triples. A physical step is F after B, since the two available outputs differ from the missing output by one socket in either direction.

For any cycle (a,b,c,…,d) of F longer than three, introduce a fresh switch triple x,y,z. Replace that cycle by (a,b,x), (y,c,…,d) and (z). This splits a white rotation into two trivalent-tree pieces joined by the fresh black switch. The two choices of black rotation both send a visit arriving at x onwards through y, and a visit arriving at y onwards through x, suppressing any intermediate visit at z. Indeed, one transition is direct and its reverse-direction counterpart goes via z, whose white successor is itself. Thus the original white successor at b is still c after suppressing fresh wires, and at d it is still a. All original wire identities and all switch choices are retained. Repeated splitting peels successive pairs until all white cycles have size at most three.

Eliminate each singleton i by cutting its wire and inserting a fresh switch whose external input and output are 0 and 2, with output 0 linked to input 2 and output 1 to input 1. Each physical mode transmits and creates one detached physical circuit. Under forbidden turns, the external path is direct and the two internal positions form a 2-cycle. The former singleton therefore becomes a 2-cycle, accompanied by a second 2-cycle. Keep original marks at the original incoming sockets.

With s original singletons and D the sum of max(length−3,0) over original forbidden cycles, the construction adds D splitting switches and s+D caps. Every original setting has exactly 2^(s+2D) extensions and each gives the identical ordered trajectories on original wires. Hence rooted setting counts acquire exactly this factor. The linear-size result can be built in linear time from a cycle list using direct successor updates. The construction allows raw self-loops. It proves equivalence with unrestricted rooted routing under a bound on every forbidden-cycle length. The earlier one-long-cycle construction controls the number of exceptional cycles instead.

The executable bounded_forbidden_cycles.py uses one_long_forbidden_cycle.py for cycle traversal. All 726 native wirings with one or two switches were checked over every original setting and every extension, totaling 60,780 mask lifts. Checks compare the ordered original-wire cycles and verify all resulting forbidden lengths.
