# Sole x OR with raw-empty OR sibling and exactly one dirty-AND-reading OR, q=3

Let M=x OR w be x=3's sole reader, where w is an OR with empty raw support. Assume exactly one physical OR gate has a dirty AND input. The earliest raw-empty OR in w's ancestral cone must read two dirty ANDs: a raw input or a raw-positive OR gives nonempty support, and a clean AND before M's downstream AND violates TopForm. Call this gate E=A OR B. Every later raw-empty OR in that cone can only take two earlier raw-empty ORs (an AND input would be a second dirty-AND-reading OR, or a forbidden clean AND). Inductively its function would equal E and leastness forbids that duplication. Thus w=E, the direct entrance is w itself, and no strictly earlier entrance can serve a different raw-empty w. Repeated A=B is redundant. Ancestors of A,B are pure raw/OR factors or dirty AND products of such factors, since no previous dirty-AND-to-OR entrance exists.

M and E each count in q: both are dirty, and each has an empty raw inlet precluding lambda. Sole-reader safety from singleton_6648_q1_sole_or_all_c.md applies to w and hence both A,B: on either restriction deleting 9 or deleting 10 there is no positive independent input. The two vertices are nonadjacent, so their separate deletions cover all graph edges. Project E to false throughout to handle E-negative edges. This projection decreases the whole circuit, transforms M to x, and keeps original raw supports at all surviving ORs (any OR whose value becomes false has empty support). Ordinary projections give only original stars: any active AND continuation of E outside OR-only paths would give an empty ordinary inlet unless a different dirty-AND-to-OR entrance existed. Projected centreless tops can be killed or become clean pure conjunctive products.

If E has a direct OR reader other than M it is the third paid gate Q, as E has empty support. Its other pin has positive raw support: no earlier distinct raw-empty OR can survive irredundancy, and an AND input would be another dirty-AND-reading OR (a clean AND could not precede this dirty OR's downstream AND). All primitive supports upstream of A,B are anchored or singleton because there are no other charges. Each arm therefore covers at most three residuals: after deleting 9 it supplies an induced matching of ≤2, and the remaining 9-incident edges belong to the matching after deleting 10 with at most one incident edge. After E=false, Q reduces to its other pin, which is raw, M (now x), or originally anchored. Its own raw support equals that other pin's. Thus all projected centreless products are anchored and contribute at most two each. The result is U≤6+2c.

If E has no other direct OR reader, the size-21 private-donor exclusion in 6648-size21-private-x3-donor.md supplies another direct AND reader. Following ANDs to a clean top encounters no dirty-to-OR exit, since the unique such OR E is earlier. The reached clean top is centreless and requires E, hence has no E-negative residuals. At most one other paid gate Q exists. Each pre-E arm is a product of clauses anchored except possibly one pure Q whose children are originally anchored or singleton. If Q is downstream then both arms are fully anchored. The one-exception two-child lemma in singleton_6648_q2_sole_or_all_c_cases.md bounds each arm by two edges per safe deletion, hence four in total. On the global E=false projection all other centreless tops have at most one exceptional Q; its children are singleton or anchored, as M reduced to x and all other surviving ORs have their original anchored raw support. Thus the E-positive edges contribute at most eight and the E-negative ones at most 2(c−1). Again U≤6+2c, sufficient at q=3,j≥3. Both placements permit arbitrary sharing of the pre-E AND products and all pure ORs.
