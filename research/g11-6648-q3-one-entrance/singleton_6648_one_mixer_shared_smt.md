A dirty OR sibling at M would need an earlier dirty-AND-to-OR transition; it and M would not have the stated direct-donor shape. The construction below is restricted to the displayed shape.

The private donor exclusion (6648-size21-private-x3-donor.md) gives another reader of D. Follow ANDs until a clean top: a dirty-AND-to-OR escape distinct from M is impossible, and a path back into M must use its designated D or raw x inlet. The reached clean top is centreless since its inlet is an AND, and implies D. Hence c≥1. Project M to raw x. This preserves all inputs with D=false and decreases others. Ordinary tops now yield only original stars because all nonclean inlet ORs project to the unchanged raw support and every uninterrupted AND continuation has empty inlet. Thus a violation gives a set F of j+q+2c+1 original residuals contained in the donor-positive edges and the union of c−1 clean projected centreless top products. For c=1 there are no additional top products.

An unmatched nonempty OR in a projected inlet is a pure raw disjunction. If its original gate lay in Lambda, its raw support lies inside N(a) and every spoke av is an original star and avoids F. A clean aggregation OR cannot be upstream of these tops.
