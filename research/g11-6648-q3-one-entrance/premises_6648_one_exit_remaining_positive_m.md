# Two further q=3 one-exit slices

Continue with a size-21 least TopForm graph circuit, A=x∧S clean and sole x reader (x=3, neighbors y=6,z=7), j≥3 and q=3. Exactly one physical OR M reads a dirty AND. Write H for the eight-vertex deletion, and count original residuals.

## Entirely pure S

A pure S positive on both y,z with raw support contained in {y,z} is the physical common OR h=y∨z: each proper singleton OR would be redundant and the first such common OR already computes S. The common-OR proof forces exactly three paid gates B=h∨W, Ry=y∨V and Rz=z∨V', and both spokes y10,z9 are original residuals. The 19-card losses force W false on both spoke inputs. Also Ry(z)=0 and Rz(y)=0 by yz toggling through h. On H all three gates specialize to their respective other pins, regardless of whether those pins read a dirty gate. These three gates in particular cannot conceal another dirty-reading gate.

If M=B it cannot have an OR reader below an AND. Such an uncharged OR would carry raw y,z and require its sole possible anchor x in the earlier A. Either other paid reader contains raw y or z and would absorb the direct B pin. If M=Ry, a first uncharged OR directly reading Ry would have to carry raw y, so its anchor is x,z or 10. The first two possibilities involving A or the spoke y10 exclude x and 10. The remaining z anchor forces all raw leaves to be y because x cannot enter any OR and z9 is residual. Its other positive-raw pin could receive y only through Ry (h also contains z). This makes the direct OR redundant, and an empty pin would make it a further paid gate. The other two paid wires cannot read Ry directly: placing it in W contradicts the loss at y10 and placing it in V' contradicts Rz(y)=0. The Rz case is symmetric. Thus M has no OR exit below the first clean tops. Every route after its first AND remains multiplicative until a clean top because there is no subsequent dirty-reading OR.

On H every projected centreless output is now one globally clean anchored pure conjunction: the mixed M is either reduced to its dirty AND operand (raw direct arm gone) or irrelevant, and the other two paid ORs collapse on H. A mixed ordinary correction is impossible for M=B (opposite raw singleton x would lead only to A); for M=Ry an opposite raw guard must lie in {z} since x's only raw star is A and y10 is residual. The correction vanishes on H. The argument for M=Rz uses its mate y. Hence U_H≤2c by the ordinary anchored G11 bound, and U≤2c+3 from the two original x stars. This slice uses no numerical DP.

## Empty raw S, M raw-positive with one dirty donor

There are no raw-empty OR wires below a top in this case: an earliest such OR would directly read an AND and be a second dirty-reading OR. Thus a centreless top always has a direct dirty AND pin and two different clean tops cannot share a direct dirty pin by fusion. In particular c≤j, and S is a dirty AND because it is a positive nonraw empty-support operand of A.

On H the x-dependent top A contributes nothing. Each other centreless root is the union of at most two clean guarded products, evaluated by cutting the unique dirty donor D at its OR exit and distributing the two possibilities. D is a pure conjunction and each mixed pin is D plus its raw disjunction, so the two terms are the raw product and D times the nonmixed factors. Direct appearances of D can be kept as conjuncts. Ordinary dirty corrections aggregate into D times one guard disjunction: a leaf of M's positive raw side anchors the opposite guard support in every affected ordinary star, including OR-only enlargements of that mixed side. All batches use at most the same two exceptional projected supports from the two paid gates other than M, as in premises_6648_shared_projection_scope.md. There is at least one original near star: common AND stars yz, the four individual OR case has at least one lambda reader, and the common OR case cannot occur since h adds a fourth paid gate when x has no raw star. Thus at most four near residuals.

For c=1,2,3 the H batch count is ≤2(c−1)+1. The prototype upper bounds on H size at 1,3,5 batches are 3,6,9. Adding the four near residuals suffices except when c=j=3. In this latter case choose a distinct direct dirty pin per top with S chosen for A. If D≠S some non-A root directly has D as a factor and needs only one mode (the AND-only multiplicative factor D remains). This reduces to at most four H batches, covering at most eight by the prototype. If D=S, it is globally clean on H and can be charged once before projection; on D-negative H pairs the ordinary stars suffice and the other centreless roots need only their one projected batch each. Thus at most c H batches suffice. For c≥4, j≥c and the bare bound U≤15 suffices. This part is conditional on the separately frozen H batch computation and its independent verification status.
