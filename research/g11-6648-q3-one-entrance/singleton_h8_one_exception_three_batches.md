# H8, one shared exceptional binary clause, at most three clean products

Let H=G11 minus 3,6,7; its 11 edges are 09,0–10,14,18,25,2–10,48,4–10,58,59,89. Assume each product is clean on the full H-cube and all nontrivial primitive clauses are subsets of original star-anchored supports except at most the same exceptional binary OR F. Its two child traces are each singleton or subsets of originally anchored supports. Count original residual edges; the statement allows factors to differ across products. The single-product bound is two by the one-exception lemma (singleton_6648_q2_sole_or_all_c_cases.md). Its matching bound uses three induced edges' eight independent orientations; at least seven must have an original anchor and force one vertex of degree six, impossible. Its incident-pair case forces both children to be singleton on H and yields only two residuals. These arguments concern original anchors, irrespective of restrictions.

Suppose three products jointly covered six residual H edges. Assign them exclusively to three two-edge batches. At most five original stars can then lie in H. An incident-edge double hu,hv requires F to occur and omit h since all ordinary clauses contain h (otherwise their original anchor stars the two spokes). Every surviving F leaf is a neighbor of h by cleanliness. A child containing two such leaves is originally anchored at h, and cannot supply either residual mate. Both children are consequently singleton on H and F has the exact trace {u,v}. Since this trace is the same across all products and h is its unique common neighbor, at most one exclusive double can have incident edges. Thus at least two doubles consist of disjoint edges.

For a disjoint double ab,cd, ambient crosses are impossible by the one-exception lemma. Explicitly, a cross ac forces all ordinary clauses to have trace ac or bd: co-occurrence of b,c would force original anchor a and star ab, while a,d would force c and star cd. At least one such ordinary clause occurs, since an OR alone cannot be clean and accept both edges. If ad or bc is present neither allowed anchored trace is possible by the same uniqueness; bd would make a four-cycle. The nonedges ad,bc both meet every ordinary clause, and a single F cannot miss both while accepting ab and cd. Thus all four orientations choosing one endpoint per edge are independent. Each missed clause must contain both complementary mates, and distinct orientations require distinct clauses. At least three of the four corners therefore have original anchors. The anchor lies outside all four marks (a loop or original marked star otherwise) and inside H (no removed vertex has two H neighbors).

If a common anchor supplies opposite corners, it sees all four endpoints. Every anchored corner then uses that same anchor by C4 uniqueness. The only H neighborhood containing two disjoint H edges is N(8), with marked edges 14 and 59. This homogeneous case forces four original internal spokes all at one vertex. If the anchored corners are nonhomogeneous, three distinct anchors would require six distinct spokes since their centers are outside the marks. With at most five spokes, two adjacent corners must share a centre h covering three marks, and the third uses a distinct k adjacent to the fourth mark d and one shared mark b. Exactly five internal spokes are forced. Their graph consists of h joined to b and two leaves a,c, with a further path b–k–d. This has maximum degree three. Thus a homogeneous and a nonhomogeneous double cannot coexist within the same five internal stars. Nor can two homogeneous doubles be exclusive.

Finally, any second nonhomogeneous double must force exactly the same five-star graph. Its h is the unique degree-three vertex, k the unique degree-two vertex at distance two from h; its marks are the remaining four vertices. Two distinct perfect matchings on the same four vertices form a four-cycle, so these marks admit at most one matching in H. This precludes two exclusively assigned disjoint-edge doubles. Consequently F(1,3)≤5 with the stated common-support and original-star premises. No scan was used.
