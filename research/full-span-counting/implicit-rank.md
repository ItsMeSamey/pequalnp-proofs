# Rank-sensitive discovery of implicit violation monomials

For a feature family of nonempty positive violation monomials, let r be the rank of their evaluation matrix on all positive profiles realized by core H. Let s be the minimum number of exceptional positive profiles for a rational linear predictor equal to 1. For the family of *all* positive monomials of degree at most d, the exact model count requires poly(n,m,d,r,1+s) original-scale fixed-width core calls and polynomial overhead in these parameters. No global evaluation matrix, rank, exceptional set or witness predictor is supplied.

Maintain the finite list of profile equations in the conflict algorithm of pattern-repair.md. On these rows represent a monomial by its actual clause-index support and its vector of zero-one evaluations. Initialize with an independent subset of singleton columns spanning their restrictions; degree zero yields an empty list. For each further degree layer, pool the preceding basis with all its products by singleton variables, and retain a basis of actual columns by exact elimination. The vector space after the layer is exactly the restriction of the full degree-bounded dictionary, since multiplication by a singleton is linear on evaluations. Multiplying uses the basis frozen at the preceding layer to avoid skipping the degree bound. Repeated factors use Boolean idempotence. A stable span remains stable under every further multiplication. The constant monomial is excluded so all selected columns vanish at the origin.

Solve for a sparse predictor supported on the witness-row basis. It fits the full finite equations iff those equations can be fit by the implicit dictionary. A returned violating pattern is certified by the positive squared residual; its full multiplicity is recovered by the maximal-profile procedure, independent of how many features were generated implicitly. If the enlarged equation set stays consistent, its coefficient rank in the full dictionary must strictly increase (the missed RHS cannot be consistent in the span of the existing rows). If it becomes inconsistent, there are at most r+1 actual rows in the conflict. Remove every row in the conflict and record its mass. The conflicts are disjoint and each contains an error of any predictor; at most s rounds delete at most (r+1)s known patterns. Correct the final count as in pattern-repair.md. The fitted predictor has at most r nonzero explicitly recorded monomials even though all features are considered. The positive-measure expansion involves only those monomials. No coefficient matrix with all implicit columns is needed. When d is unknown, dovetail the m+1 complete procedures for d=0 through m to exploit the fastest one with a polynomial slowdown.

## General implicit families

A finite union/product circuit can specify a family of positive monomials using variable leaves, union gates combining feature lists, and product gates forming every pairwise monomial product. This is the support of a monotone arithmetic circuit, with duplicate positive coefficients disregarded. On the finite witness rows, retain at each gate a column basis consisting of actual monomials. At a union take a basis of the pooled lists; at a product take a basis of their pairwise coordinatewise products. Bilinearity proves that all full gate columns lie in the computed span. Every computed column has explicit support of length at most m; overlapping variables reduce by idempotence. Polynomial cost per gate follows from the bound r+1 on the current number of rows even if the full-data rank of an internal gate is enormous. If an internal constant is needed, track separately the empty monomial and nonempty family to exclude the empty monomial at the output. The implicit result therefore also applies with a factor polynomial in circuit description size, parameterized by the rank of the output family only. This includes layered decision diagrams describing allowed index subsets.

## Quantitative separation

Take q column bits b_j and t=floor(q/log₂ q) row bits a_i. A short core constrains the column Hamming weight to 1 or k=floor(q/2), with O(q) uniquely extending gates via balanced binary addition. For every pair add the clause (Y or not a_i or not b_j), where Y is a common disjunction on q further guard bits, leaving n=Θ(q). The number of wide clauses is m=tq. Fixing the guards false and choosing singleton row and column supports gives an isolated violation of every clause, so each is essential. Denote by z_i the sum of its row's violation bits. On any core model z_i lies in {0,1,k}. The union indicator is exactly 1 minus the product over all t rows of (1−z_i)(1−z_i/k). It therefore has profile degree at most d=2t.

A profile monomial of degree at most d pulls back to the common guard-off indicator times an ordinary monomial of degree at most 2d in q+t independent row/column bits. Thus r is at most the sum of binom(q+t,j) for j≤2d, which is 2^{O(q loglog q/log q)}, smaller on the exponential scale than the cited native exponent. The explicit dictionary contains binom(m,d)=2^{Θ(q)} columns and there are at least binom(q,k)=2^{Ω(q)} distinct positive profiles. Moreover for each fixed weight-k b and false guards, any degree-d0 predictor becomes degree at most d0 in the independent row bits. The desired function is their OR, and for d0<t the Boolean support bound in robust-gram.md forces at least 2^{t−d0}−1 nonzero row inputs with errors. Nonempty rectangle patterns determine both their row and column supports, so different fixed b give different erroneous profiles. Every fixed-degree robust fit therefore has exponentially many errors. The implicit growing-degree certificate is exact. The family has other syntactically visible simplifications; the separation concerns these three algorithmic guarantees. Arbitrary additional short clauses preserve certificate and rank upper bounds, though they may remove individual witnesses.

The independent check_implicit.py passed 90 tests through degree three, including 147 conflict rounds. Every computed finite-row column span matched explicit dictionary enumeration, all recovered masses and counts were exact, and every conflict obeyed the actual-rank bound.
