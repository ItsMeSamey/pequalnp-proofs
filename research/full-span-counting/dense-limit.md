# A common dense obstruction to several structural tests

Take q bits x_i, q bits z_j and q guard bits g_l. For each of q² pairs choose a set A_ij of exactly q/2 guard indices and add the positive clause x_i or z_j or the disjunction of its selected guards, for even q. The witness setting all guards false, and setting just x_i,z_j false on their respective sides, falsifies only this pair's clause. Every clause is essential and every pair is compatible.

Even granting separate exact treatment to at most K n chosen wide clauses (n=3q) cannot universally shrink the degree needed in the violation-profile program. Some column j contains at most 3K chosen pairs. Set all guards false, z_j false and all other z true, and set x_i true for the chosen rows in column j. Then all handled clauses hold. The remaining q−O(K) variables x_i independently toggle precisely their own residual column faults; every other residual fault is zero. Thus a residual predictor of degree d misses at least 2^{q−O(K)−d}−1 distinct valid profiles by the Boolean cube argument in robust-gram.md. This applies to the semantic profile criteria, without establishing any running-time lower bound for the instance.

The guard sets can also force every literal order to have almost all row supports poorly approximated by any fixed number of intervals. Select them independently and uniformly among q/2-subsets. For a fixed guard order and fixed p, at most (q+1)^{2p+O(1)} subsets have at most p runs, by listing interval endpoints. The probability for any one random row to be such a subset is at most 2^{−q+O_p(log q)}, by the central binomial coefficient bound. The probability that there is an order and k good rows is at most q! binom(q²,k) 2^{−kq+O_p(k log q)}, which tends to zero for k=ceil(3 log₂ q) and large q. Likewise all guard subsets are distinct with probability tending to one, by a pairwise union bound. If a row had at most p runs in a full signed-literal order, retaining only positive guard columns cannot increase its runs. Thus Θ(q²) rows must be exceptional for that run criterion in every order. All unate clauses are mutually compatible, so a partition into hitting groups has at most one clause per group. This family combines elementary limitations without ruling out other factorizations or implying computational hardness.
