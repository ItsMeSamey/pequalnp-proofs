For the zero-star case use the paragraph beginning “There are 16 graph edges” in research/g11-6648-pure-or-empty/6648-regime-index.md (SHA-256 ccbcc078c6863704a0eec4a2ac6f94c77a13e159e9aea3d4267d5d3e4384949b). With no ordinary top, lambda is empty and rebuilding the output OR tree gives j+2c+q≥22.
