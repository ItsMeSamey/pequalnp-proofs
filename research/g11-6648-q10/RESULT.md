# Two starred edges with three junk gates and no centreless tops

For the graph and original circuit hypotheses of L-meta-6648 let U be the number of residual edges, s=16−U, j the junk count, c the centreless count and q the number of paid ORs. If s=2, j=3 and c=0 then U≤j+2c+q for every q. In particular the issued statement holds on the entire q=10 stratum.

For the first assertion U=14, so q≥11 suffices by arithmetic. When q≤10 the two distinct original starred edges either have a common endpoint or have four distinct endpoints. The first case is ruled out by the exterior separation and paid OR count in 6648-q10-adjacent-two-stars.md (e90a3a33964c1865f8a240796d14deb3d27dcdf2170f8dd83af6a23681a514c4). Its original graph has eight exterior vertices and at most six raw junk-pin supports. The six-label extremal incidence pattern is incompatible with the graph, which forces at least nine paid ORs for exterior unions. A positive exterior edge needs two distinct OR gates reading a junk AND, separate from those unions. The second case is ruled out by 6648-two-disjoint-stars-three-junk.md (eafb68336fb0fbfabc0b28cac0be175580adb2e6440f9335c5c76e013afd3b51). Six of the paid ORs must read exterior variables. At q=10 saturation leaves six pin supports containing at most two exterior labels apiece. Seven exterior vertices require seven distinct incomparable codes, impossible for six such supports by singleton and pair incidence counting. This proof uses the previously established exact size 21.

For the second assertion suppose U>j+2c+q at q=10. The zero-star and one-star exclusions give s≥2. Thus 14≥U≥j+2c+11. The issued j≥3 forces equality, c=0, j=3 and s=2. This contradicts the first assertion. For any issued selected sets X,J, the balance |X|+j=U+|J| transfers this bound to |X|≤2c+|J|+q. The proof concerns the original least TopForm circuit and requires no feasibility claim about an unrestricted 21-gate circuit.
