# Two adjacent stars are impossible at q=10, c=0, j=3

Use the original size-21 TopForm and the original maximal-independent-set separators of 6648-star-exterior-separation.md and twin_6648_exterior_merge_cost.md. The two adjacent stars have three endpoints, leaving e=8 exterior labels; the six dirty AND pins supply k≤6 exterior target supports. The only OR gates building incomparable nonempty exterior raw sets are paid gates without a direct dirty AND inlet. Write m for their count and b for the physical direct dirty-AND-reading OR count; m+b≤10.

Strip singleton target sets and their labels, empty and duplicate targets, as in the exterior merge argument. The resulting r labels have d≤r−2 distinct nonsingleton targets; their membership words are nonempty and pairwise incomparable. At least six labels remain by Sperner. For r=6, d=4 the words must be all six pairs of four coordinates. Some retained label v has original degree ≥3 (there are only four degree-two vertices); name its word 12. For any pair of labels with respective words in {13,14} and {23,24}, their union covers 12. These labels must be adjacent: if independent, a maximal independent set containing both omits v, or can be chosen to omit v by adjoining a neighbor of v compatible with both. Indeed when neither is adjacent to v, each excludes at most one of v’s neighbors by four-cycle-freeness. The original exterior separator for that maximal independent set would then contain v while omitting both labels. Singleton stripping preserves this condition since the removed singleton/empty targets cannot distinguish v from the chosen two on the remaining labels. Thus all four cross pairs would form a four-cycle, impossible. It follows that r≥7.

Prune the projected union construction to the d required targets. Identify equal results and bypass comparable unions; its m' effective gates each have two operand slots. Every remaining label appears in at least two distinct initial singleton slots, else all targets containing it contain a fixed other label from its unique first union. No remaining target is a singleton. Every one of the m'−d non-target gates also consumes at least one downstream operand slot because the construction was pruned. These slots are disjoint from singleton slots. Therefore 2m'≥2r+(m'−d), and m≥m'≥2r−d≥r+2≥9.

Finally b≥2. There exists a graph edge wholly on the eight exterior vertices, since the independence number is four. At its input some ordinary top is positive, and neither raw pin support contains an exterior input. Both pins must therefore have a physical OR-only path from a dirty AND. Each such path has at least one OR, because ordinary pins have nonempty raw support. If there were just one direct dirty-AND-reading OR P, both paths pass P. This would make the top positive whenever P is positive, including the independent positive witness for a dirty AND feeding P. This contradicts top cleanliness. If no such OR exists there is no positive top on the edge. Therefore m+b≥11, excluding the signature for every reader form.
