# Exterior separation forces paid raw unions

Let O be the complement of the original star endpoints, e=|O|, k=2j+c. Use the physical supports and rectangle necessity of 6648-star-exterior-separation.md. Let m count paid ORs whose two raw supports restricted to O are nonempty and incomparable under containment. Write b for all physical direct dirty-AND-reading ORs. Then m≤q−b. Every raw set seen on a physical pin restricts to an O-set built from singletons by at most m genuine binary unions over the whole circuit, with unlimited sharing. Raw traversal stops at AND. An OR with exterior support cannot be Lambda, since its original anchor makes every raw leaf a star endpoint; it cannot be below the graph, since a raw singleton already activates it. An OR with comparable exterior operands simply forwards the larger exterior set.

The selected ≤k pin supports restricted to O separate every ordered pair of exterior vertices: for distinct u,v there is a maximal independent set containing u but omitting v. Adjacent u,v are immediate. For nonadjacent u,v, choose a neighbor w of v that is not a neighbor of u, possible by minimum degree two and no four-cycle. Extend {u,w}. The separator support containing v and avoiding that maximal set omits u. Thus the row words of exterior-vertex membership over the selected sets form an antichain.

Suppose Δ=e−k is positive. Repeatedly remove empty/duplicate selected sets; when a selected singleton occurs, delete its label from the universe and discard that singleton. This preserves the membership separation of every remaining pair, decreases the available gate count, and never decreases the deficit of labels over sets. Deleting labels from every intermediate set preserves a raw-union construction with at most m effective gates. In the remaining core there are r labels and d≤r−Δ nontrivial sets; r≥2 since an uncovered last singleton would violate the original per-label separation from a maximal independent set omitting it, and the singleton stripping does not destroy coverage of a remaining label. A nonpositive empty core is excluded by the positive deficit. The r nonempty words are an antichain on d bits.

Discard all unused intermediate union gates, identify equal results, and bypass unions with nested operands. Each core label must appear as a singleton operand of at least two distinct effective union gates. If its only initial use were to add a nonempty partner, every target set containing that label would also contain a fixed partner label, against the antichain. Thus at least 2r operand slots are consumed. In fact m≥r+1: equality would make every operand slot an initial singleton use, hence all d target sets would be pairs. Each label belongs to at least two targets (a label present in only one nonsingleton target is dominated by a partner), so 2d≥2r, impossible.

* If Δ≥1 then r≥5 by Sperner (the width on at most three bits is at most three), hence m≥6.
* If Δ≥2 then r≥6. If m≤7 then r=6 and d≤4; the six words must be the entire middle layer on four bits. The four target sets are triples and any two intersect in one label. Each triple requires a prior intermediate pair contained in it. Those four pairs are distinct and none is a target. This requires at least eight unions.
* If Δ≥3 then r≥8, since d≤r−3 and four bits have width six. Hence m≥9. If Δ≥4 then r≥9 and m≥10.

There is also a graph-sensitive strengthening independent of m. For any exterior v of degree ≥3, its word cannot be contained in the union of the words of two distinct nonadjacent exterior vertices a,b different from v. There is a maximal independent set containing a,b omitting v: if either neighbors v, extend {a,b}; otherwise each shares at most one neighbor with v, so adjoin a third neighbor of v adjacent to neither and extend. An original support containing v must avoid this set. This persists through stripping. In particular the six-word, four-bit middle layer is itself impossible on G11: choose a label v of degree ≥3 (only four vertices have degree two). Rename its word {1,2}. Each pair of words chosen one from {13,14} and one from {23,24} covers it. All four corresponding graph pairs must be adjacent by the preceding test, creating a four-cycle. The analogous five-word/four-bit representation omits one pair and its label opposite the omitted pair must have degree two.

For q=10,j=3,c=0, two intersecting stars leave e=8 requiring m≥8 and b≤2; disjoint stars leave e=7 requiring m≥6 and b≤4. These are necessary source-level exclusions, with original maximal independent sets and no projected minimum-circuit assumption.
