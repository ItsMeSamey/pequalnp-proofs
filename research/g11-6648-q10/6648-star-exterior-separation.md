# Separating exterior vertices from the original maximal independent sets

Take a binary monotone circuit computing the original graph function, with TopForm and all gates live. Assume the graph is nonempty and has no loops. Let W be the endpoints of the ordinary top star edges and O its vertex complement. Write j for the number of junk ANDs and c for the number of centreless clean ANDs. Let M be the zero-one matrix with rows indexed by O and columns indexed by all maximal independent sets I in the original graph. Entry (v,I) is one exactly when v is absent from I. Its Boolean rectangle cover number is at most 2j+c.

For every AND pin trace its raw input labels through ORs only, stopping at an AND. Keep the raw supports for all junk AND pins and for the nonempty raw pin, if any, of each centreless top. There are at most 2j+c such supports, counted with multiplicity. At an ordinary top both raw pin supports are nonempty and disjoint by singleton rejection. Every raw member on one side makes an original graph edge with every member on the other, so all these labels belong to W.

Fix an entry (v,I) equal to one. By maximality, I is rejected and adding v makes an accepted input. Every ordinary raw pin has the same raw disjunction on these two inputs. If the raw disjunction of every kept pin were also unchanged, a topological induction would give identical values at every AND. Indeed each pin is the disjunction of its raw contribution and earlier AND signals with OR-only paths to that pin. The output is the disjunction of its boundary ANDs and possibly raw inputs, the latter excluded by singleton rejection. Hence some kept support P contains v and is disjoint from I. Each P covers the all-one rectangle (P intersection O) times {I disjoint from P}. This proves the bound, allowing arbitrary mixed ORs, repeated pins and shared cones.

The same result with a smaller gate count applies on a restricted graph if one separately proves that every exterior raw influence still enters through the retained physical pin list. The required maximal independent sets here are for the original graph, even when some endpoints of W are present. A fooling set of 2j+c+1 entries, with an opposite zero in every off-diagonal pair, proves a mask impossible. This lemma alone excludes no scalar signature without a matching graph-side lower bound.
