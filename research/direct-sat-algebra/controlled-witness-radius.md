# Witness extraction from a nonzero normalized residue

Use the aligned independent control pencil and source assumptions of controlled-character-precision.md. Choose any independent subfamily I of control columns, of size B. The other columns have rank T. Put t=B+T-R, the dimension of intersection of the two spans. Selection requires no assumptions on the activator forms. One valid deterministic choice is greedy independent columns in input order.

For fixed data y the controls form a possibly empty affine fiber of size 2^(k-r_y), where r_y is the rank of columns whose activators equal one. If j of the activators in I are zero, r_y<=B-j+T, hence any nonempty fiber contributes a multiple of 2^(max(0,j-t)) after division by 2^(k-R). Consequently, if the normalized count is nonzero modulo 2^p for p>=1, some witness has j<t+p. This is a certificate on the original source count with its unique extension semantics.

Search all missing index sets J of size at most min(B,t+p-1). On each impose exactly the corresponding affine activator pattern on I. Let H be the column span of the active indices in I. Project the output equations modulo H and discard all controls in I. The remaining independent controls span a subspace of dimension at most T in the quotient. Every projected solution lifts: the active selected columns are a basis of H, giving a unique completion on those controls, and inactive selected controls can all be zero. Use the exact character count on the projected affine data space, retaining the affine restriction that the projected right hand side lies in its remaining column span. A nonempty instance admits deterministic y-pinning using its exact projected count; neither the output quotient nor control span changes during pinning. Finally solve for controls by Gaussian elimination. Boolean circuit gates have unique extensions.

Thus the unconditional search costs poly(input) 2^T times the sum of binom(B,j) over the indicated missing sizes, including all witness calls. No prior residue calculation is necessary. A miss certifies normalized divisibility by 2^p; it does not certify infeasibility in general. If T and p are constant the entire search is polynomial. A dense affine right hand side is allowed. The parameter t can be replaced patternwise by the exact rank deficit of the union of H and the remaining output span for pruning. Feasible simultaneous affine patterns can be filtered during search. Pinned coordinates may create trivial constant activators, which the character formula already handles.
