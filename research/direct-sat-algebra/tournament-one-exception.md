# One extra unmarked odd-loop vertex

Use outgoing rows. A feasible marked support has odd population in A, with each selected vertex having outgoing degree of its prescribed loop parity. A marked vertex with loop zero wins as singleton. Assume all marked loops are one. Let Z be the unmarked loop-zero vertices. A directed edge from A to Z gives a feasible pair; a directed triangle in A is feasible. Otherwise Z dominates A and A is transitive.

Suppose at most one other unmarked vertex w has loop one. Without a selected w the last selected marked vertex has no outgoing edge in the support. Thus w must exist and be selected. Scan A backwards in transitive order retaining two bits: p, selected A parity, and g, parity of selected a with w→a. An a may be added exactly when p+[a→w]=1. Enforce p=1 at termination.

For each possible g, the internal target on z is d'(z)=[w→z], because it already sees odd A and sees w exactly when z→w. The w equation asks the parity of selected d'=1 vertices to equal 1+g. For target zero use empty Z. For target one partition Z into marked d'=1 and unmarked d'=0. Any directed marked-to-unmarked edge supplies a pair. Otherwise no marked vertex sees an unmarked vertex; its selected marked set must itself have odd outgoing degrees. This is possible exactly if the marked tournament contains a directed triangle, since a transitive selected set has a zero-degree last vertex. Return the corresponding original witness. Quadratic-time transitivity-or-triangle discovery follows by growing a transitive list until insertion fails: the new vertex and a consecutive violating pair form the triangle.

For multiple fixed selected extras W the residual loop is 1+|W|+sum_w [w→z], with one output readout per w. For two extras this algebraic relation alone still encodes every single-mark mixed problem on Z: given loops d and mark beta, put lambda1=beta and lambda2=ones+d+beta. Then d=ones+lambda1+lambda2 and feasibility is the disjunction of the two target pairs (1,0),(1,1). This observation concerns the residual interface; it does not force the two extras in a tournament having only two unmarked loop-one vertices.
