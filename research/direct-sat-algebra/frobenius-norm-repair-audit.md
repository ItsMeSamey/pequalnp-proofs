# Rank perturbations of an arbitrary Frobenius term

Let σ=F^j on K=GF(2^d), let H=GF(2^g) be its fixed field, and m=d/g. Subtract the scalar center and divide by the nonzero Frobenius coefficient, obtaining σ+UV. The columns of U are field elements u_i and the rows of V are absolute trace forms Tr(v_i x). Check the zero slope first. The algorithms below work with the actual residual rank at most k and lift slopes back by the original scaling and translation.

For y of relative norm n≠1, the inverse of σ+M_y is Σ_i a_i σ^i with

    a_i = w ∏_{l=i+1}^{m−1} σ^l(y),       w=(1+n)⁻¹,    0≤i<m.

Indeed y a_i=σ(a_(i−1)) for i>0 and y a_0+σ(a_(m−1))=1. The last coefficient is w. In rank one, varying the slope over yσ(z)/z leaves its relative norm fixed. Conjugation by input z and output 1/σ(z) converts the baseline to σ+M_y. The safety indicator is 1+Tr(vz (σ+M_y)⁻¹(u/σ(z))). It is a Laurent polynomial with at most 1+dm monomials. Only i=m−1 has a zero exponent 1−2^((i+1)j mod d), even after Frobenius rotations modulo 2^d−1. Hence its constant coefficient is 1+Tr(vwσ⁻¹(u)). If the zero slope was unsafe, the determinant lemma gives Tr(vσ⁻¹(u))=1. When g>1 choose any nonzero w∈H killing the functional Tr(vwσ⁻¹(u)); w cannot be one. This gives n=1+1/w≠0,1 and a nonzero safety polynomial. Relative norm lifting is polynomial by CRT into the component coprime to m and the primary components at primes dividing m. Only primes ≤d require discrete-log digit searches. When g=1 the earlier reciprocal-trace scattered repair applies.

**Outer parity for arbitrary g>k.** Write q=2^d, Q=2^g, N=(q−1)/(Q−1). For regular norm n the determinant lemma gives det(I+V(σ+M_y)⁻¹U). Expand the k-dimensional determinant and all traces before restricting the norm. Each term has shape c w^b y^E. The exponent b is a sum of at most k binary marks modulo Q−1; E is a sum of at most k(m−1) binary marks modulo q−1. Summing over the fiber of n kills E unless N divides its reduced exponent. On E=N e, with 0≤e<Q−1, the sum gives n^e; the kernel has odd size N. The binary expansion of N e repeats the g-bit expansion of e exactly m times without overlap. Cyclic carrying does not increase Hamming weight, including when a multiple of q−1 reduces to zero. Consequently m wt(e)≤k(m−1). Substituting n=1+1/w expands n^e into at most 2^k inverse powers of w; their exponents are submasks of e. The outer Laurent polynomial therefore has at most (g+1)^(2k) distinct exponents before multiplying by 1+w. Reduce modulo w^(Q−1)−1 to work on H*. The extra factor forces zero at the invalid parameter w=1 and preserves nonzeroness elsewhere.

When g>k the norm-one baseline has kernel dimension g, so no rank-k perturbation there can be invertible. With zero slope already unsafe, the globally odd number of safe slopes guarantees at least one regular norm fiber with odd parity. Thus the outer polynomial is a nonzero function on H*. The sparse polynomial can be assembled explicitly in d^O(k) bit time. A nonzero function with s distinct exponent residues is hit by one of s consecutive powers of a primitive element, by invertibility of the Vandermonde matrix. Enumerate Shoup's polynomial-size candidate list and verify individual evaluations without testing primitivity. Apply this in H to obtain w, then lift n to y and apply it again in K to the inner Laurent determinant. Conjugation gives the inner k-by-k trace matrix directly; its entries have O(dm) terms. Powers σ(z)/z cover the norm-one kernel, since the map has kernel H*. Every returned slope is checked by binary rank.

For g≤k there are at most 2^k−1 nonzero relative norms. Try each. In the norm-one case, complete the fixed corank-g baseline by a rank-g matrix, and include this same correction among the updated columns and rows. The fixed core is then invertible. Mixed conjugation factors have Laurent expansions of polynomial support because its binary inverse has at most d linearized coefficients. The determinant lemma has size at most k+g≤2k and admits the same sparse hitting argument. These bounds are polynomial for fixed k, with degree depending on k; a fixed-parameter time bound f(k)poly(d) is not established here. If an unknown center must be recovered separately, a rank-decoding argument with its own hypotheses is needed.

Independent checks symbolically constructed 48 outer parity polynomials and directly verified every regular norm fiber, including 24 fixtures whose zero slope failed and g>k. A separate 32-case rank-one check compared every inner Laurent value against the direct ratio set and validated the deterministic norm preimages. It also tested the inverse on 222 original binary basis vectors.
