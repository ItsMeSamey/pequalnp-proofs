# One genuine prefix per verified frontier syndrome

On the original proxy chart, compute the verified full suffix translation group at each cut, with past selectors fixed and future selectors available for the lift. Project the kernel to the crossing names and take its orthogonal parity-check matrix H. Rejecting the codimension budget at this stage returns skip. Store one concrete prefix word per H value. For each predecessor pin every old boundary coordinate, including pass-through bits. Test each next selector setting and new syndrome until representatives have been found or candidates exhausted. Keep a physical extension by using the oracle result on newly introduced names only.

Equal old syndromes mean the difference of the two boundary words has a full physical suffix lift. Apply it to the whole continuation, preserving every tail factor, to replace a discarded history by its stored representative. Later selector settings and syndrome values may change, but all their choices are available; this preserves decision, not counts or each individual future syndrome. Every actual retained prefix is feasible and the final word is replayed on the recoded and original CNFs.

The transition oracle substitutes old pins, Gaussian-reduces the parity rows and checks 2SAT. Complementing bits of a binary model yields zero baseline with implications and negative constraints. Distinct principal implication closures represent individual SCCs. In a minimal feasible upset satisfying r remaining parities there are at most r minimal SCCs. Otherwise a nonempty collection of minimal SCCs has zero total parity contribution, and deleting it preserves implications and negative constraints. Thus trying unions of at most r principal closures suffices. Free coordinates occurring only in parity rows are included. The empty union is checked first.

If D is maximum frontier codimension, there are at most 2 sum_j 2^{d_prev+d_j} oracle calls, and each call costs (N+1)^{O(D)} polynomial input work for N physical variables. One physical word per attained syndrome is stored, with polynomial preliminary linear algebra. No polynomial-space or fixed-parameter claim for growing D follows. Caps count codimension, actual oracle invocations and nonempty seed subsets; they exclude polynomial setup and the unit cost per enumerated candidate. A cap miss is skip. With fixed D and sufficient budgets the method is polynomial regardless of how many boundary bits remain nonfixed.
