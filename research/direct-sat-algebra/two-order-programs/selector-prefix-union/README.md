# Exact reachable frontier as a union of affine cosets

Group original clauses on equal physical variable scopes in first-occurrence order. For a processed prefix let S be its variables, B those also used later, and L the joint translation space preserving each processed grouped table. Enumerate the stabilizing translations on each local table (at most eight tests of eight entries), take orthogonal equations and lift them to original coordinates. Intersect by binary elimination. The projection P of L to B preserves the set of reachable frontier words. For each populated P coset store one actual full-prefix assignment. For any physical syndrome map H the exact catalog is the union of affine spaces H(b)+H(P). Cosets with different offsets can only be identified modulo H(P).

For a new scope T and allowed tuple a, I=T∩S is contained in B. For each stored representative h solve for a translation l in L with l on I equal to a+h on I. The code carries full physical lifts alongside the Gaussian basis of the restricted translations. If consistent, apply l also on retired sites and glue a on T. Every projected output of this old orbit and tuple belongs to one new orbit. For any actual old history its boundary is h+u on B for some u in L; the difference between u and l vanishes on I, so extended by zero on fresh coordinates that difference preserves both earlier factors and the new tuple. Discrepancies on retired sites outside L cannot affect the new frontier. Conversely every point of the retained new orbit has a valid complete history by applying a lift in the new joint stabilizer. The orbit can include contributions from other pairs. No implication about multiplicities follows.

With m groups, n original variables and C_j populated prefix cosets, there are at most 8 times sum C_j candidate extensions. One conservative bit clock is O(m(n+m)^5 + 8 n^3 sum C_j), including elimination and raw witness construction. Without optional inspection, at most two tables of representatives are live plus polynomial Gaussian data. The state cap gives an inconclusive skip. An isolated variable absent from every group may be set arbitrarily in the final witness. For named query coordinates it must be kept live through the query, e.g. as a final table.

## Designated cut with retired-bit lifts

For each i use a prefix table on x_i,z_i,p_i given by (x_i XOR z_i) OR p_i. Its raw CNF has clauses (x_i OR z_i OR p_i) and (¬x_i OR ¬z_i OR p_i). The joint shift of x_i,z_i preserves this nonlinear table. The private z_i,p_i retire while x_i remains live. All 2^k frontier words form one orbit. As suffix use disjoint positive OR triples on the x_i. Each suffix table has trivial translation stabilizer, so the full suffix syndrome is injective on the designated k-bit frontier. Append individual x pins for either verdict; pinning the three sites of a suffix clause low gives NO. Process the tail clause groups triple by triple with pins adjacent. Between triples at most three bits in the current triple lose their shift freedoms before retirement; at most eight orbits occur at any cut. Both prefix and suffix constraints include nonaffine relations; the suffix triple is non-bijunctive. The Gaussian affine-hull relaxation of OR3 misses the unsatisfiable all-zero target. This family is independently easy by eliminating private prefix variables and testing disjoint tail triples. It demonstrates an exponential full-syndrome-catalog separation and original witness lifting, without establishing a distinct general tractability class.
