# Decision-only merging of selector prefixes from physical 3CNF

Run `prefix.solve(n, rows, orbit=residual.ResidualTranslation(n))` after importing those two modules from this directory. It receives signed physical clauses. The candidate selectors are the union of the least-indexed sites of normalized ternary clauses. Verify that each physical row contains at most one selector and schedule selected sites in increasing index order. Failure of this particular recognition heuristic returns skip. Numeric order matters. This is a recognized class, not a general selector recovery algorithm.

After fixing a prefix, the active constraints are binary on data. Keep only data sites mentioned both in the processed and future banks. Nonemptiness and all feasible unary and binary patterns on these sites are computed by 2SAT calls. These projections specify the exact existentially projected prefix relation: projection of a 2CNF remains bijunctive. Use a separate UNSAT state, including when no sites remain. Extend with the next active bank and repeat. The implementation does not enumerate full boundary assignments.

For two reachable prefixes R and S, a sufficient reason to discard R is a translation of its boundary models into S that extends to a symmetry of all remaining raw clauses taken together. The future may use a different assignment of unprocessed selectors. Every completion of R then gives a completion of the actually retained S history. This preserves decision. At completion, rerun the binary constraints for the surviving original selector word and replay the resulting raw word. Counts, a prescribed selector word and the entire source model set need not be preserved.

The prefix-only unshifted containment rule is the earlier compression approach to pruning projected bijunctive disjuncts (fc58de298860a714). Equality modulo tail translations uses the sibling selector-coset-native implementation. It groups the future signed clauses by identical raw scope, checks local periods and solves their linear intersection. These are verified sufficient tail symmetries.

directed.py performs strict translated containment when projected tail shifts are free on disjoint equality blocks. residual.py implements the stronger d=0 case of selector-translation-inclusion-algorithm.md (e1355763): for every unary/binary source and target projection, enumerate the at most four shift patterns which translate the source inside the target. Absorb all affine admissible sets by Gaussian elimination. Only three-point admissible sets need residual clauses. Project the resulting affine shift space to those remaining coordinates, group coordinates that differ by a constant or are always equal, and verify independence of the distinct nonzero column signatures. If independent, a binary SAT test on those block bits is exact. A successful test reconstructs an actual full tail translation and checks all local inclusions. The program can decline an individual comparison outside this class; keeping both states is always safe. It does not implement the broader fixed-deficit XP routine.

Let M bound the number of retained histories at every layer, and w the largest crossing support. At most 2M candidate diagrams are generated per layer, each with O(1+w²) binary model tests on a formula of polynomial size. At most O(M²) directed comparisons per layer perform polynomial work. This gives deterministic polynomial time on inputs with polynomially many retained histories. The configured cap returns skip if a layer exceeds it; raw validation, projection, comparison, and traceback all remain charged. No unconditional polynomial bound on M is available.

The raw narrow(m) family in check_translation.py includes a branch forcing x_i=y_i=0 and a second branch forcing x_i=1 while leaving y_i free. Redundant ternary guards make the selector IDs automatically discoverable. Future clauses impose u_i=x_i XOR t and v_i=y_i XOR t. At the late cut all 2^m selector histories give pairwise incomparable ordinary relations. Translations preserve which y_i are fixed, so equality modulo any tail shifts cannot merge distinct histories either. Yet the suffix permits translating x_i together with u_i and y_i together with v_i. The narrower branch fits in the wider branch after shifting x_i. The directed routine therefore retains one history per layer. The optional UNSAT variant adds the two opposite terminal units; its contradiction is deliberately trivial to global unit propagation. This is a separation of state retention under the specified unpreprocessed schedule, not a SAT lower bound or a claimed improvement over every raw algorithm.

Run python check.py, python check_translation.py and python check_residual.py. In the m=4 SAT fixture ordinary containment and orbit equality each retained 16 histories and made 2831 probes. Directed containment retained one and used 491. The residual check also verifies recovered full shifts against exhaustive small group enumeration. No recovery of a general SAT model from a discarded prefix is promised.
