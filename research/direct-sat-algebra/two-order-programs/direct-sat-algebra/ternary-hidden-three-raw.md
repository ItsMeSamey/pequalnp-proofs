# Non-coordinate three-clause cover after raw XOR extraction

Use three bits p,q,r and m>=3 disjoint pairs a_i,b_i. The common linear form h=p+q+r is never a physical wire. Each i has private raw helpers with four individually recognizable ternary XOR definitions E_i=a_i+p, F_i=b_i+q, G_i=E_i+r, C_i=G_i+F_i. Express each by its four signed parity clauses. Add one arbitrary-sign main clause on a_i,b_i,C_i. Allow arbitrary signed binary/unary clauses on all a,b and p,q, including cross-group clauses. Extract the ternary affine groups and retain exterior rows unchanged.

In the free dual the main direction spaces are U_i=span(a_i,b_i,h), intersecting pairwise in span(h). No single physical coordinate equals h and no two span it. Modulo core forms p,q,r the only repeated nonzero private signatures are a_i,E_i,G_i (differences p,r,p+r) and b_i,F_i (difference q). C_i is unique with private signature a_i+b_i. Distinct groups have distinct nonzero signatures; two core coordinates cannot span h either. Any two original wires leave some U_j untouched in both its private coordinates, so their span could intersect U_j only in h, which it does not. Thus at least three physical coordinates must be fixed even AFTER XOR extraction to lower the direction ranks of all main clauses to two; the three p,q,r suffice. The unique one-dimensional semantic cover is span(h). This is a difference between rank-drop certificates, not a lower bound on other SAT search methods.

In each h coset the U_i images form disjoint two-dimensional summands. The exterior raw sites are among independent a_i,b_i,p,q. Seed a dual basis with the distinct nonzero normals of variables in original unary/binary rows. Append independent literal normals from each remaining main group, then complete to a basis. Thus all main expressions use at most two coordinates and each exterior site uses at most one, independent of raw name/literal/clause order. Verify these support properties before solving by 2SAT and lifting all raw sites. The seven-way search finds the common direction without named roles or supplied forms.

For b disjoint copies allow arbitrary exterior binary rows across their permitted exterior bits. Each U_i lies in just one summand. Every cover must meet each summand nontrivially, hence has dimension at least b, and the b local h forms attain it. Raw-coordinate pins split by blocks, needing at least 3b. The same chart argument solves every coset. At dimension b any cover has one direction per summand, necessarily its local h. Total original-input deterministic clock O(7^b poly(input)+2^b poly(input)), and actual original witness replay. The large-b counterfamily and zero-transversal NP-hard residuals remain independent limits.
