# Recover hidden selector blocks polynomially; unrestricted selection is NP-complete

In the disjoint copies of ternary-hidden-three-raw.md, compute each remaining ternary normal space U_i after purely local ternary hull extraction. Connect two scopes when their U_i intersect nontrivially, via Gaussian algebra. Within a block every pair intersects precisely in its hidden h line, and cross-block scopes have trivial intersections. Three or more scopes per block guarantee that the connected components and one line from any pair recover all hidden selectors, with no supplied group, ordering, roles or 7^b search. On general inputs verify line independence, coverage and actual chart support on each coset. On this family the deterministic clock is O(2^b poly(explicit input)), polynomial space, including discovery, any signed binary exterior on the allowed sites and raw backlift.

If b is unrestricted the family is NP-complete even with only signed equality stars and unary pins as exterior. For each original clause (L1,L2,L3) create one private three-group block of the cited construction. For group one tie a,b to L1,L2 by signed equalities on occurrence sites, and choose the main clause forbidding (a,b,C)=(0,0,0). Since C=a+b+h this rejects exactly h=0 and L1=L2=0. In group two tie distinct a,b to L3 and forbid (0,0,1), rejecting exactly h=1 and L3=0. In group three pin a true and use a positive a literal, neutralizing its main clause. Each used old variable is recovered from a chosen occurrence representative with its actual sign, with other literal occurrences wired directly to that physical representative. Different stars are disjoint, and group-three pin is separate. All remaining dummy bits can be arbitrary. For any original assignment some h passes both tests iff the source clause is satisfied. Independent physical p,q,r per block allow simultaneous choices. Conversely every physical witness yields an original model. The construction is linear in occurrences and is not parsimonious. Repeat literal positions for shorter clauses. For source with an empty clause return the image of any fixed unsatisfiable 3CNF, and handle a formula with no clauses directly.

Leave exterior rows out of the local affine extraction as specified in the family, preserving its block geometry. This positive O(2^b poly) algorithm does not by itself give polynomial SAT for unbounded b. No general lower bound against alternative decision procedures follows.
