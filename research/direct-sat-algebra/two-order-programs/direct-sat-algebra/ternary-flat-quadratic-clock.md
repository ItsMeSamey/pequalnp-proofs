# Mixed raw 3CNF via a transversal of clause directions

Group original clauses by equal active raw scope and enumerate their local tables, at most eight words each. Collect all affine equations implied by these tables. Reject an empty table or inconsistent system. Parametrize its solution set on d free bits with a bijection to the raw affine chart; remove groups equal to their hull, retaining the original clauses of all others. This preserves counts and assignments exactly.

Each remaining clause forbids the product of at most three affine literal-falsity forms being one. Seek a space S of at most b normal forms whose kernel makes every indicator at most quadratic on every coset. If the three normals of a clause are independent modulo S, branch by adjoining each of the seven nonzero vectors of their original span U. Indeed an eventual S' containing S reduces the rank of U exactly if it contains a nonzero vector of U. Stop after coverage or b steps. This is complete for the rank-drop criterion and costs O(7^b poly(input)). It is the ternary-source counterpart of the earlier plane-cover search for gate operands, not an arbitrary equivalent-formula search.

Enumerate the disjoint 2^dim(S) parameter cosets. Expand all surviving indicators into Boolean ANFs on d−dim(S) variables; a product depending on at most two independent affine forms is quadratic. Row-reduce the equations and, whenever combinations give affine equations, incorporate them via Gaussian substitution; repeat until stable or contradictory. For the resulting q independent quadratic equations on r bits, the exact count is 2^(-q) times the sum over 2^q equation combinations of their signed quadratic Gauss sums. Each is polynomial time via binary quadratic elimination. Alternatively enumerate 2^r assignments. Numerator bit length at most q+r+1. Total deterministic count clock poly(input) times [7^b + sum over attained cosets 2^min(r,q)], polynomial space. Obtain a raw witness by pinning residual chart bits, which cannot increase q or r, and reversing affine changes; replay on original. For decision only, if every indicator depends on at most two coordinate bits of the actual computed chart, emit its binary forbidden patterns and use 2SAT in that coset. The latter does not count. Exhausted cover search merely declines. Report actual total preprocessing, branches and residual systems.

Polynomial original-input decision follows when b=O(log(input)) and each coset of the returned transversal either passes the binary chart check or has min(r,q)=O(log(input)). An existential claim about the best transversal is insufficient without searching/charging all such transversals. All affine-Gauss and bounded branching ingredients are established. This procedure can handle sources that have no uniform affine, meet or majority class among their original groups.

Quantitative obstruction: partition d raw variables into equal blocks of size h>=3 and give a single positive clause on EVERY coordinate triple of each block. Every local seven-word table affinely spans its whole scope. After quotient by S each block has rank at most two under complete coverage, or three independent images give a displayed uncovered triple. Hence b>=d−2d/h; h near log d gives O(d log^2 d) clauses yet near-full required transversal dimension. The raw all-positive formula is easy by other methods. This contradicts only a uniform small transversal for the stated original factorization, not arbitrary SAT complexity.
