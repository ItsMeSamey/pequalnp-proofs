# Point histories modulo verified suffix translations

Use the raw guarded 3CNF chart in selector-prefix-antichain/prefix.py (sibling directory), including bank zero of unguarded binary clauses. Every selector occurs only in its own bank. At cut j let B_j be the data names incident to both sides; compute the full joint stabilizer of each remaining grouped local truth table via Gaussian intersection, then project to B_j as in the reviewed fd_clock.py. Let H_j be an independent parity-check matrix for that projection, with d_j rows. No finite assignment table on the boundary is required.

Store one genuine partial assignment per realized H_j boundary value. For each stored predecessor, pin its entire physical old boundary in the next bank. Try each setting of its sole new selector; bank zero needs none. For each candidate new syndrome solve the resulting binary CNF together with H_j x_B=t, including old boundary values even for bits passing straight through. Each successful check gives an actual partial extension consistent with the retained predecessor; keep any one per new syndrome. Finally replay the actual assembled physical assignment, filling unused variables arbitrarily and decoding any bijective input chart. Exact UNSAT follows from exhaustion. All boundary names and all unused polarities of the current group must be accounted for when configuring original clauses.

To prove completeness, take any global model compatible with a reachable predecessor syndrome. Replace its entire prefix by the representative history. The XOR of old boundary words is the projection of one simultaneous stabilizer of the entire unprocessed suffix, by equality of syndromes. Translate that suffix by a physical lift, gluing exactly on the old boundary. The resulting model may choose a different next selector, but both settings are tested. Replacement at subsequent cuts repeats the same argument. The invariant is existence of an accepting continuation, not preservation of every prefix history or its full output syndrome set.

Solve 2SAT plus d_j parity rows by the degree-d_j representative theorem for 2CNF on the product of their affine GF2 indicators, retaining an actual source word. With D=max d_j the total cost is polynomial source and Gaussian preprocessing plus at most 2·sum_j 2^(d_prev+d_j)·(n+1)^{O(D)}·poly(source bits), and space bounded by that time. Timings and polyspace are not inferred. This eliminates the nonfixed-coordinate parameter c at fixed D; for growing D, the earlier c,d orbit-key bound may give the better algorithm. It also handles arbitrary previously enumerated proxy charts with their actual size and recognition costs charged.

A matching parameterized obstruction uses Multicolored Clique with k colors, at least three vertices per class after padding with isolated vertices, k≥2. Put an original data variable x_v on each vertex. Bank zero forbids any two selected vertices of the same class and every incompatible cross-color pair, by binary clauses. For each color in order, then each vertex v of that color in order, introduce a fresh selector g_v equal to x_v; its own bank also enforces r_next=r_prev XOR g_v by the four forbidden ternaries. Every color has its own chain with the initial r pinned to zero and final r pinned to one. These pins are bank zero clauses, so the endpoint names may cross cuts but their presence in the boundary does not by itself create additional *tail* constraints. Number all selectors first. Every ternary contains exactly its single g_v, preserving direct raw recognition. One selected per color is forced by odd parity and the clique pair conflicts; the original selector assignments and carries then reconstruct uniquely.

At bank zero all x_v and the two endpoints of every chain occur in the crossing boundary. Projection of the remaining joint stabilizer consists exactly of shifts satisfying one equation per class: the sum of the x shifts equals the XOR of the two endpoint shifts. Equalities transfer shifts to g_v, intermediate carries backsolve. Hence d_0=k (all x_v occur in same-color conflicts). At later cuts a remaining nonempty chain has one start carry (the last processed carry or original endpoint) and one terminal endpoint, plus its remaining x_v. There is again precisely one equation per such chain and all are independent; finished chains leave no crossing sites. Thus D=k. This is a parameter-preserving W[1]-hardness reduction for the actual whole original SAT problem on this recognized chart, not just for the auxiliary merge test. It limits an FPT improvement depending on D alone under FPT≠W[1], without claiming an unconditional exponent bound. The incidence-expander fixture separately remains subsumed by Gaussian elimination followed by a constant-state prefix computation.

For a self-contained transition oracle, use the elementary principal-closure argument from ../compression/two-sat-generator-interaction.md. Find any binary model and complement its one bits, obtaining a zero baseline. The translated binary constraints consist of implications and downward-closed negative constraints. Contract the implication SCCs. Choose an inclusion-minimal feasible set satisfying the d parity rows. It is an upward-closed set of SCCs. Deleting any subset of its minimal SCCs preserves implications and negative constraints; if more than d are minimal, their d-bit parity labels have a nonempty zero-sum, contradicting minimality. Enumerate at most d minimal SCCs, take the union of their implied closures, and test both the signed constraints and all parities. This gives an actual assignment in (n+1)^d poly(input) without a moment oracle. Accounting uses the number of parity rows even if old pins lower their rank.
