# Hardness even for an existential semantic transport between singleton prefixes

Use the guaranteed satisfiable strict 3CNF tail H in README.md whose semantic invariance under flipping designated b is equivalent to UNSAT of arbitrary strict 3CNF F. Set the boundary to ALL tail variables. With a fresh prefix selector s, force every boundary variable other than b to zero and enforce b=s. As a strict 3CNF, encode each unit L by the four clauses L or (+/-u) or (+/-v) on fresh distinct private u,v; each binary D by D or w and D or not w on a fresh private w. Prefix projections are the singletons 0 and e_b. There is an existential translation matching these projections AND preserving the full semantic tail relation exactly when flipping b is a semantic symmetry: the entire translation on the tail is forced. On this supplied specific prefix template the recognition problem is coNP-complete, by the direct reduction and the original polynomial assignment countercertificate. Therefore the general existential semantic transport question is coNP-hard; no coNP upper is claimed on unrestricted prefix representations. This does not assert hardness of evaluating the tail at the two singleton words or of testing one concrete suffix decision. The comparator requires preservation on ALL words.
