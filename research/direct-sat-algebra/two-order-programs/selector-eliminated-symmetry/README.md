# Frontier translations after bounded physical elimination

Keep frontier sites named. For a private variable collect its incident residual tables. If their joint scope has at most w names, replace the bucket by its exact projection and store the two-bit mask of allowed private values at every remaining tuple. Repeat, retaining blocked residual tables. Reversing the masks supplies a physical witness; branching over their allowed values reconstructs every original word. A canonical choice can change fiber multiplicities.

Try every single transposition of remaining private names with arbitrary literal flips on the exact residual tables. Include pure translations, including flips of a boundary variable now absent. These directions P preserve the reachable frontier set: compose the residual signed maps and then apply reverse sections. The clause x OR v with v private has one extension at x=0 and two at x=1. Its projected x-flip therefore cannot be lifted to a full-word bijection, but has a total witness section in both directions.

## Moving between independently computed groups

Given old and new projected groups P,Q, an occupied old offset h and an allowed incoming tuple a, solve for old generator coefficients matching a+h on shared sites I. If consistent, Gaussian elimination gives one solution and a basis of the coefficients vanishing on I. Project each kernel direction to the new frontier (zero on fresh sites), reduce it modulo Q, and keep source coefficients for the independent remainders. If t independent remainders survive, enumerate precisely their 2^t subsets. Each gives a distinct genuinely occupied Q-coset. Hence 2^t ≤ C_new for every consistent pair. This charges quotient enumeration before materializing arbitrary affine words and permits early abstention on the state cap.

For a subset, recover one original coefficient vector, compose its residual signed generators and apply the old reverse sections once, then glue a. Each computed offset has a full physical history. Any old history with this frontier can be exchanged with that history because retired differences cannot meet future factors. Every word in the new orbit has a section via the independently computed Q. The recurrence requires neither commuting physical lifts nor any inclusion between P and Q. It enumerates sets rather than counts.

For m distinct scopes and explicit source size N including n variables, preprocessing costs 2^{O(w)}poly(N) per layer, including table bit sizes and translation testing. There are at most 8 choices per old orbit, at most C_new quotient representatives per consistent pair, and polynomially many original residual generators followed by one section pass per representative. Thus the full bit clock is 2^{O(w)}poly(N) times the sum of (1+C_old)(1+C_new) over the cuts. No general small bound on occupancy or width follows. This uses ordinary bucket elimination and signed SAT symmetries. Its purpose is recovery of complete physical frontier support and original witnesses after separately recognizing group actions at each cut.

Independent audit on 250 raw CNFs (n≤6, widths 0–4) checked exact reconstruction of each complete assignment, projected frontier images at 885 layers, and disjointness of orbit keys. Unequal fibers, SAT pins and incompatible pins passed on x OR v.
