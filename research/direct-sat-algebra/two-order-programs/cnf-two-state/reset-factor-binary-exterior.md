# A complete two-state factor with arbitrary original binary clauses

Given one certified complete width-two read-once factor and arbitrary 2CNF H on the same raw variables, the last-reset partition gives O(n) disjoint branches of unary pins plus at most one parity. Apply pins to H, discard contradictory 2CNFs, and choose a satisfying assignment u of the current H. If parity matches return u; otherwise translate by u. Every nontrivial clause now is an implication, a negative unit, or a negative conflict. Feasible flip sets are exactly implication-closed and conflict-free sets respecting negative units.

Compute each singleton's implication closure; discard those violating negative constraints. If one retained closure has odd weight against the parity coefficient mask, its flip from u is a shared original witness. Completeness: for an odd feasible flip set F, condense the implication graph into strongly connected components. Every component in F has its entire principal closure in F, hence feasible. If all such closures had even weight, sink-to-source induction would make each component even because its strict downstream components are disjoint and even. That contradicts odd weight of F. No instance of H needs weighted compilation or a bit order. Total polynomial time and space, genuine witnesses replayed. This uses only the previously established degree-one moment case for 2CNF, not a generic algorithm for arbitrary parity systems on H.

A source-level dispatcher may retain all binary clauses of an explicit 3CNF as H (also units), and seek a width-two certificate for the conjunction of the remaining ternaries. Redundant binary rows may be copied into the factor; a sound certificate or syntactic extractor suffices. O(3^t poly(input)) automatic source recognition with t ternary clauses is given in cnf-two-state-probes.md, while the unrestricted gate-to-certificate step is coNP-complete. An unknown factor is inconclusive for the whole formula. The outside 2CNF may have arbitrary size, signs and sharing.
