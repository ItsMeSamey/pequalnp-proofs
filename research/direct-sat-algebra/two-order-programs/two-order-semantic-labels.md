# Recognizing the complete two-state side from arbitrary weighted input

Let an explicit layered read-once oblivious program over GF(2) be given on an original variable order. Its literal transition matrices may be rectangular and nondeterministic. At layer i compute, backwards, a column basis B_i for the linear span of all suffix evaluation columns. The last basis spans the final output column. To build its predecessor span both bit matrices times B_i and keep independent columns. A prefix row u and v have identical Boolean functions on the entire remaining suffix precisely when u B_i=v B_i. Future transitions on these signatures are well-defined by the backwards recurrence.

Test a cap K forward, starting at the actual initial row. At each layer keep one actual prefix row for each distinct signature, with its incoming bit and pointer. Advance the two transitions from each kept representative; stop immediately on K+1 inequivalent signatures in one layer. In this case those reached prefixes give a witness against width K in this supplied order: any complete deterministic layered read-once program must separate the K+1 distinct suffix functions. Otherwise the retained signature labels and transitions give a complete deterministic program of width at most K computing the entire original scalar. Induction over the order proves surjectivity onto all reachable signatures until the first overflow; K+1 suffices for this certificate even if later signatures could merge. Completion includes the identically zero suffix whenever actually reachable. Time is polynomial in the explicit input and K with ordinary dense binary elimination; retained prefixes/pointers and the bases have polynomial size. Distinct suffix signatures can be certified by actual suffixes if the backwards basis stores its generating suffix pointers.

In particular apply K=2 independently to two differently ordered arbitrary weighted programs. If either succeeds the last-reset algorithm of two-order-width-two.md decides their intersection in polynomial time with an original common witness. This uses semantically minimized complete-deterministic width in the side's supplied order, independent of its displayed weighted width. K=3 on both sides is already parsimonious SAT-hard by two-order-program-sat.md, including instances with displayed weighted width 2: the ever-zero residual consumes a complete deterministic state. This characterization of deterministic width by reachable residuals is standard; the combination here is an effective front end to the last-reset decision procedure. No automatic order optimization is assumed.
