# Paired Pfaffian contraction with square-zero pin labels

Use the kernel-selected nonzero line λH* in an even binary extension of degree 2a, where H is its degree-a subfield and the centered alternating Dickson matrix D is singular. Let l<a be the number of coordinate pins. Work over the coefficient field with l square-zero commuting label variables ε_j. The multiplicative encoding of the pin values has a constant affine-pin factor and a product ∏(1+B_i t^(2^i)), i=0,...,a−1, on t in H*. The top-label coefficient tests all pins. The cyclic merger from label_safe_slope applies also after substituting arbitrary zero-constant elements for the labels. Indeed (1+Bz)(1+Cz)=(1+(B+C)z)(1+BCz²) because B²=C²=0. The carry BC gains vanishing order; a predetermined polynomial number of steps, at most l+1 per insertion, suffices. This gives a polynomial-size ring evaluation circuit for every B_i and the affine factor. The formal linear part of B_i is Σ_j α_j^(2^i) ε_j.

**One cyclic carry word.** Let e,f be the two slots of a paired Dickson digit, and let α,β be the nonzero multiplicative weights for deleting these slots respectively. Let g count deletions together with the one selected label factor B. The retained exterior signatures for g=0,1,2,3 are respectively

    ef,   u+B ef,   αβ+B u,   αβ B,       u=βe+αf.

Here juxtaposed vectors denote their wedge. Replace D by its entrywise square: over the original field det(D[J])=pf((D entrywise squared)[J]). This substitution takes place before any R-valued coefficients are introduced; it avoids square roots in the ring. For g=1 write u+B ef=u(1+Bv), where v=f/β. For g=2 use αβ(1+B u/(αβ)). Thus the product signature is a scalar times mandatory wedge vectors and optional factors (1+w). On the list of all slots form the alternating pullback of D entrywise squared; add a unit entry on each unordered pair of optional slots. If the list length is odd first append an optional zero vector. Its Pfaffian sums precisely over optional subsets: every remaining even optional complement has a complete-graph Pfaffian of one. This gives one ring Pfaffian per word, with every g=3 digit contributing an explicit factor B_i.

Ring Pfaffians can be evaluated in polynomially many ring operations. One elementary construction adds a scalar X times the standard consecutive-pair alternating matrix J; interpolate the polynomial Pf(M+XJ) of degree at most half the size and read its constant coefficient. Over a field extension of polynomial cardinality increase if necessary, skip X where any even leading principal Pfaffian modulo the maximal ideal vanishes. Those polynomials are monic of consecutive degrees, hence only quadratically many field elements are forbidden. For accepted X, ordinary paired elimination has only unit pivots. Field extensions of the supplied coefficient field can alternatively be avoided by using polynomial rational arithmetic; in this application the ambient field already has 2^(2a) elements.

**Carry count.** Use target digits all ones. For a carry step c→c', g=1+2c'−c. For cyclic words on 0,1 the four steps 00,01,10,11 give g=1,3,0,2. The constant state 2 gives all g=3; exponent zero is handled separately. Those endpoints vanish here by l<a and singular D respectively. Every other surviving exponent multiple occurs once: its start carry is (total exponent−(2^a−1))/(2^a−1). For i upward steps there are 2 binom(a,2i) binary cyclic carry words, because a switching edge set of even size and a starting state determine the word. Each word has exactly i explicit B factors.

**Eliminate exposed labels before evaluating a word.** Let r be the rank of the linear terms of these i exposed factors. By adding one factor to another, the product is unchanged since every factor squares to zero. Thus one can make i−r factors have zero linear part; if 2i−r>l the product vanishes. Otherwise choose r independent exposed factors F_1,...,F_r and r pivot variables in their linear coefficient matrix. For any remaining polynomial expression V the top coefficient of (∏ F_j)V equals the top coefficient in the l−r other variables of V evaluated at F=0, multiplied by the determinant of the pivot-variable Jacobian evaluated at that solution. For one variable x, expand F=F0+xF1, V=V0+xV1. Its coefficient is F1 V(x=F0/F1), since F1 is a unit. Multiple eliminations follow successively (pivot the constant Jacobian by field row operations or reorder pivot rows/columns); if performing row scaling, retain its determinant factor. The successive derivative pivots multiply to the full Jacobian determinant by Schur complements and the chain rule. These formal derivatives are well-defined in characteristic two even after quotienting by x²=0. Equivalently, simply permute rows and columns in a field pivoting order to avoid row scaling.

Compute the simultaneous substitution directly in the smaller ring. Fix its free variables, start all pivot variables at zero, and iteratively add the constant Jacobian inverse times the evaluated residual. Starting with vanishing order one, the residual order increases by at least one at each step, so l+1 passes suffice. All substituted variables have zero constant, hence square to zero. Evaluate the remaining exposed factors, affine multiplier, Jacobian and contracted Pfaffian at this substitution. First derivatives are obtainable by forward differentiation of the fixed-length carry circuit, or using one additional square-zero variable and extracting its coefficient. The latter requires allowing one extra carry step. This costs poly(a,l) ring operations in dimension 2^(l−r), without ever materializing full-ring B_i. The rank test uses only the original linear terms. With ranked subset convolution this is O*(2^min(l,2(l−i))) bit time per word. Scalar Gaussian inverses cost polynomial bit time.

For l≤7a/50 sum these costs over i≤l. At x=i/a the exponent is bounded by H₂(2x)+2(7/50−x). It increases on 0≤x≤7/50 since H₂'(2x)>1 for 2x<1/3. Therefore the sum is O*(2^(H₂(.28)a)), with H₂(.28)<.856. Use l=floor(7a/50) prefix pins and scan the remaining at most 2^(a−l) suffix values from the odd subcube. This gives O*(2^(.86a))=O*(2^(.43d)) deterministic bit time at d=2a, improving the .44d bound. Working space is O*(2^(.14a)) from the label arithmetic. This derivation is for an alternating centered matrix on a certified quadratic-subfield line.

**Implemented Vandermonde pins and polynomial-space evaluation.** Compute a full-degree element γ of H by the existing deterministic prime-power Frobenius decomposition. Choose trace normals 1,γ,…,γ^(l−1). They are independent. For i exposed distinct digit positions p, the linear coefficients in the first i labels form a square Vandermonde matrix with distinct nodes γ^(2^p). Thus eliminate exactly i pivot labels and evaluate the contracted arithmetic circuit on k=l−i free labels. The suffix enumeration uses the trace-dual basis of the full power basis.

The ordinary implementation keeps 2^k squarefree coefficients. Alternatively, lift the same arithmetic circuit gate by gate to ordinary polynomials, evaluate the k free labels at T for any selected subset and at zero otherwise, and retain degree at most k. XOR the degree-k results over all 2^k subsets. A monomial with missing labels occurs an even number of times; every degree-k monomial using every label is squarefree. Thus this extracts precisely the square-zero top coefficient in polynomial working space. The implicit Jacobian correction is differentiated using a separate dual variable of square zero. Unit inversion in determinant/Pfaffian elimination lifts the original multiplication gate x/x₀², even though that expression need not invert x in the univariate evaluation. All pivot and interpolation choices depend only on constant terms; all carry and implicit updates may be padded to their polynomial degree bounds (zero residual updates change nothing). Each evaluation costs polynomial bit time and space. Enumerating k-label subsets stays within the same word cost O*(2^k). Consequently the optional streaming mode attains the O*(2^(.43d)) bound with polynomial bit space.

Both modes are available in labeled_paired_line.py with original-column input and direct original-rank verification, using stream=True for polynomial space. The original-column control checks directly enumerate each tested half-field line. The generic Pfaffian uses at most n/2+1 regular interpolation points. At most (n/2)(n/2+1)/2 points can be excluded by the monic even leading principal Pfaffians. Here n≤2a (before optional padding, also after since 2a is even), and the containing field has 4^a points, enough for every a≥1.
