# A safe multiplier for weighted nearest Frobenius neighbours

Over a supplied polynomial-basis binary field K of degree d, consider the trace-self-adjoint binary operator A(x)=b x²+sqrt(bx)+u x. The first two summands are adjoints by trace invariance under squaring. For nonzero b, squaring the kernel equation for A+(u+c)I (the notation means scalar multiplication by u+c) gives x times the cubic x³+(c/b)²x+1/b, after division by b². Thus every scalar shift has binary rank at least max(0,d−2). For b=1 and d=1,2 the two Frobenius maps coincide, giving zero drift before u. Over the prime field directly choose the scalar opposite the drift (u+1).

For b=1 let e=3^v with v the 3-adic valuation of d. Start a=1, then v times choose a root of Z³+Z+a in K and replace a by that root. Indeed a starts as z+1/z for z of order 3. The identity (w+1/w)³+(w+1/w)=w³+1/w³ shows that each of the three preimages corresponds to a primitive root of unity of the next 3-power order, modulo inversion. A trace z+1/z at order 3^(j+1) has binary degree exactly 3^j: its conjugates repeat when 2^k=±1 modulo that order. The order of 2 is 2·3^j, by the binomial induction v3(2^(3^j)+1)=j+1. Consequently at stage j≤v all candidate traces lie in K; the next three all lie outside K. Therefore Z³+Z+a after the final stage is irreducible over K. Since a lies in the subfield of odd degree e, cubing permutes its units. Set c=a^(-1/3) there. Scaling roots of Z³+Z+a by c gives X³+c²X+1. This supplies a verified safe scalar.

For general b≠0, solve h³=1/b over K. With no root choose c=0; the kernel cubic has no root. Otherwise c=b h c0 where c0 is the b=1 answer: substituting x=h z gives h³(z³+c0²z+1). Return u+c for the original affine drift. For b=0 return u+1. This includes arbitrary b subject only to the displayed form. Given a claimed representation, compare images of every binary basis vector to certify it; unknown b,u can also be fitted using a binary linear system since each basis evaluation depends binary-linearly on both coefficients.

The implementation uses deterministic trace splitting of cubic polynomials over the supplied field. O(log d) root calls on degree three each require O(d²) field arithmetic operations using the existing routine; inverses and square roots may cost O(d) multiplications each. A conservative schoolbook bit bound is O(d^5 log(d+1)), including the binary rank replay. It never enumerates field elements in its solving routine. Testing checked degrees 2–18 and 27 and all 508 weights in degrees 2–8 by enumeration of every nonzero possible kernel vector. No property of an unrestricted self-adjoint operator is assumed.
