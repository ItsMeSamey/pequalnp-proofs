# Symmetric field pencils with independent diagonal forms

Let K have q=2^m elements. The homogeneous pencil is a symmetric r-by-r matrix of linear forms in r field controls. Require its diagonal forms to be independent. An invertible control change gives diagonal t₁,…,tᵣ. Affine constants may be included in the additive leaf drift. The map (t,y) ↦ M(t)y + A(y) + g(t), with any binary-linear leaf drift A and any center-only map g, hits every target. With extra controls, fix a complementary set before normalizing.

**Parity without drift.** In characteristic two, permutation terms for the symmetric determinant cancel in inverse pairs, except for involutions. A transposition contributes the square of an off-diagonal linear form, hence every degree-r term using it has a repeated variable. The coefficient of the squarefree full monomial in D=det M is one, from the identity alone. Expand D^(q−1) as a product of m Frobenius powers. To obtain ∏ tᵢ^(q−1), select monomials represented in factor k by at most r marks of weight 2^k. The desired exponent vector has aggregate binary popcount mr. Thus each factor uses all r marks, with no carries, and must select its full squarefree monomial. The coefficient is one. On summing over K^r, a variable power sums to zero unless its exponent is a positive multiple of q−1; exponent zero also sums to zero. The total degree bound r(q−1) leaves exactly the monomial above. Hence the number of invertible choices is odd.

**Binary drift.** The determinant of the mr-by-mr binary leaf matrix is its invertibility indicator. Adding A changes it by a polynomial of degree at most mr−1 in the mr binary control bits: every term of the difference uses at least one constant column. Each such monomial sums to zero over the cube, including after Boolean reduction. Thus invertible choices still have odd cardinality. General deterministic witness search takes at most qʳ rank tests, followed by evaluating g(t) and solving a binary linear system.

**Constructive endpoint-local case.** Suppose the full leaf operator including constant drift is symmetric over K, with each off-diagonal entry affine in its two endpoint controls only. Given an invertible leading block P, the next control t leaves P fixed. The border vector is u+vt. Its Schur complement is t+c+(u+vt)ᵀP⁻¹(u+vt)=t+β+αt². Symmetry cancels the two mixed terms. The polynomial has at most two roots since its linear coefficient is one. Three distinct trials suffice over q>2.

**Constructive nonlocal case.** For the same field-symmetry assumption, permit every off-diagonal to depend on all controls, and suppose q>r. Set future controls to zero. Assume the leading i−1 principal determinant is nonzero before choosing tᵢ. In the enlarged i-by-i determinant, all factors for transpositions in the involution expansion are squares, with no linear coefficient in tᵢ. Only the ith diagonal depends on tᵢ among the diagonal factors. The coefficient of tᵢ is exactly the previous principal determinant at tᵢ=0: it comes from involutions fixing i. The enlarged determinant polynomial therefore has degree at most i and is nonzero. At most i+1 distinct trials find a nonzero value, which supplies the invariant for the next step. Earlier principal minors may change and need not remain invertible. The implementation tests the enlarged minor directly.

Over GF(2), the pencil [[s₁,s₂],[s₂,s₂]] has only invertible choice (0,1). Securing the first prefix with s₁=1 leaves no extension. For the separate binary-drift hardness construction on k disjoint P₃ paths, there are 3k field inputs and 2k field outputs. A symmetric section with a full leaf space and independently controllable diagonals would need at least 4k field inputs. Thus that hardness family does not meet this theorem's dimension hypothesis for k>0.

The executable accepts normalized coordinates and explicit affine off-diagonals, detects symmetric field-linear constant drift and endpoint dependence, chooses the applicable algorithm and replays the resulting target. It does not search for hidden symmetric coordinates.

**Nonsymmetric drift over a subfield.** Write the full leaf matrix as the homogeneous symmetric pencil plus constant binary operator A, absorbing all affine constants. First find an invertible homogeneous direction M(t₀) by the nonlocal construction. If A is K-linear and q>r then det(A+λM(t₀)) has degree r and nonzero leading coefficient det M(t₀); r+1 trials suffice. More generally take K₀⊆K with A linear over K₀ and |K₀|>r[K:K₀]. This inequality also ensures |K|>r. The K₀-determinant has degree r[K:K₀] with nonzero leading coefficient because M(t₀) is invertible over K and hence over K₀. Trying that many plus one distinct λ∈K₀ produces an invertible choice at controls λt₀. Test subfield linearity by binary commutation with every multiplication operator in a basis of the Frobenius-fixed subfield. Constant field-linear offsets in the original off-diagonal entries commute automatically.

## Binary trace pairing and an alternate parity proof

Identify each field output with a binary dual leaf coordinate using the bilinear pairing (u,v) ↦ Σᵢ Tr(uᵢvᵢ). For a binary field basis e₁,…,eₘ, its trace Gram matrix G is invertible by nondegeneracy of the field trace pairing. Multiplying the binary leaf matrix on the left by the block Gram matrix produces a symmetric matrix whenever the field pencil is symmetric. The diagonal for leaf axis (i,a) is Tr(eₐ² tᵢ). Squaring sends any binary basis to a binary basis, so these mr diagonal forms are independent. More generally, arbitrary *varying* binary blocks may be used when opposite blocks are adjoints with respect to the supplied pairing, each diagonal block is self-adjoint, and the full binary diagonal map is surjective.

In a symmetric N-by-N binary pencil the determinant again retains only involutions. On the Boolean cube every squared off-diagonal entry equals itself. An involution containing k≥1 transpositions therefore contributes degree at most N−k. The identity contributes the product of independent diagonal forms, which has odd sum over an N-dimensional complementary control section: exactly one control vector sets every diagonal bit to one. Constant diagonal offsets can be absorbed in drift. Any fixed binary leaf drift, symmetric or otherwise, changes only lower-degree terms by multilinearity of the determinant. Thus every complementary control section has an odd number of invertible choices. If more than N controls are available, the total cardinality across all sections may be even.

**Recognition with a supplied leaf space.** Verify leaf isotropy and choose any complement as controls. Polarizing each control basis vector with each leaf basis vector gives the varying binary matrices Mᵢ. Solve the homogeneous binary linear equations X Mᵢ=(X Mᵢ)ᵀ in the entries of a map from outputs to the leaf dual. For each candidate in the solution space test the rank of the matrix with columns diag(X Mᵢ). Full row rank also implies that X is invertible: if wᵀX=0 then wᵀ(X Mᵢ)w=w·diag(X Mᵢ)=0 for every i. Full span gives w=0. The successful pairing is therefore a polynomially checkable certificate. The companion program tries basis elements, then enumerates their remaining sums, optionally with a trial budget. Its worst-case search cost is exponential in the solution-space dimension. A general deterministic polynomial-time construction of such a binary pairing is not established here.
