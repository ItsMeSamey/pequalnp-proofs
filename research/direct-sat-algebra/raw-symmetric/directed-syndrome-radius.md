# A directed-cycle bound for prescribed principal-minor syndromes

Let W be any binary square matrix. Each vertex has a binary vector label, and a feasible support S has det W[S]=1 and prescribed label sum u. The empty principal determinant is one. In the commutative algebra with z_i²=0 define P as the sum of z^J over nonsingular principal supports J. Then P is the product of (1+z^C) over simple directed cycles, including loops and directed two-cycles. Cycles are taken up to rotation. Expanding gives collections of vertex-disjoint cycles, exactly the permutation terms of each principal minor. Repeated factors with the same vertex set cancel in pairs. P²=1.

Take any inclusion-minimal feasible support S for the target and restrict the matrix and labels to S. Choose independent syndrome equations h with right sides b, retaining an equivalent system on subsets. Write E_h(z^J)=(h·J)z^J. These are derivations and the product π of the operators (1+b+E_h) keeps exactly the target syndrome. By minimality π(P)=z^S, and Pπ(P)=z^S since only the constant term of P can multiply z^S. Conjugate the factors of π by multiplication by P. Each resulting operator is (1+b)+E_h plus multiplication by P E_h(P). The latter polynomial is the sum of z^C over surviving cycle supports C with h·C=1: use the product rule, the square-one factors and z^{2C}=0. Applying the conjugated factors to P would give Pπ(1), while applying them to P times P, namely 1, gives Pπ(P) as required. Thus |S| is at most the sum over equations of the largest size of an odd-labeled surviving cycle support, taking zero for an empty maximum. Cycles on a fixed support and their parity survive induced restriction unchanged. Global cycle bounds therefore apply to every minimal solution. For a symmetric matrix all length-three-or-greater cycles cancel by reversal, recovering 2 rank(H) without matrix inversion.

The executable computes upper bounds without listing cycles. Long cycles lie in strongly connected components and a surviving long support must use some asymmetric edge, since cycles using only reciprocal edges pair by reversal. For an asymmetric component use its size whenever it intersects the row label; if its underlying simple undirected graph is a cycle, every long cycle has the full component as support, so its exact row parity can discard the whole long-cycle contribution. Loops and reciprocal pairs are checked separately. Partitioning further into undirected biconnected blocks would give another valid refinement. Enumeration through the resulting radius B takes n^{O(B+1)} bit operations (also polynomial in label encoding size).

The bound can be attained even after changing a symmetric matrix by a rank-one directed chord. Take an odd-length path on n≥3 vertices, put reciprocal arcs on its edges and add the single arc from the last vertex to the first. Give all vertices label one and require syndrome one. Every proper principal permutation uses only two-cycles, hence has even support. The full directed cycle is the unique full-support cover since n is odd. Thus the minimum is n for syndrome rank one. Disjoint copies with distinct unit labels force additive distances. The directed-cycle-plus-loop control in the executable additionally has I+W invertible, enabling a diagonal-toggle realization.
