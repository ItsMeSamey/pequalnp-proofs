# An infinite raw-recognizable family with one symmetrizer

For r≥7 with subscripts modulo r, use r controls c, one control z, and r leaves y. The crossing matrix is diag(c)+zA for the cycle adjacency matrix A. Add the pure control term cᵢcᵢ₊₂ to output i+1 for each i. Arbitrary binary affine drift and invertible binary affine changes of input and output are allowed.

**Unique symmetrizer.** Write a candidate embedding on output covector eᵢ as (Pᵢ,pᵢ,Qᵢ) in the three input groups. Symmetry tested on arbitrary leaf inputs says, for each i,j,

    eⱼ Pⱼᵢ + pᵢ A eⱼ = eᵢ Pᵢⱼ + pⱼ A eᵢ.

Fix i, choose k outside {i} and its neighbors, then take j neighboring k. In coordinate k only the term pᵢ survives. Thus p=0 and P is diagonal. Test symmetry for u=eᵢ, v=eᵢ₊₁ and input x=cᵢ₊₂. The diag(c)y contribution is supported at i+2. The pure-control polar contributes Pᵢᵢ in output i+1 on the left. Its right-hand output i differentiates cᵢ₋₁cᵢ₊₁ against cᵢ₊₂, giving zero. Thus P=0. The remaining conditions require each EⱼⱼQ symmetric, forcing Q diagonal, and AQ symmetric, forcing equality along every cycle edge. The solution consists of the zero map and the standard leaf inclusion. Invertible binary input/output changes biject the linear solution spaces, and affine effects disappear on polarization.

**Recover the diagonal hyperplane.** In the unique pairing coordinates, crossing matrices are binary congruences of the original family. In a computed basis of their dimension-r+1 span, form all two-by-two minors and Boolean-reduce products of linear entries. Solve for their linear combinations whose quadratic coefficients vanish. Any resulting linear form vanishes on all r independent diagonal units, which have rank one. Conversely choose rows 0,3 and columns 1,4: the corresponding physical minor is z²=z. These edges induce a two-by-two diagonal submatrix for r≥7. Left/right basis changes preserve the minor span by Cauchy–Binet in both directions. The linear slice is therefore exactly the one-dimensional span of z, and its kernel is the diagonal hyperplane.

**Recover the axes.** In the diagonal space all coefficients are binary. For each diagonal basis matrix H, split each current axis group U at K=U∩ker H. The other group is the simultaneous orthogonal to K within U under all diagonal forms; the presence of every diagonal matrix unit proves this is exactly the complementary axis group. Every pair of axes differs in at least one diagonal basis matrix, so the final groups are individual lines. Their dual evaluations give the output coordinates. All operations so far are polynomial in r.

**Raw witness.** Lift each diagonal matrix unit to a raw control by binary elimination on the crossing tensor. Fix z=0 in the varying pencil. The remaining leaf matrix is diag(t)+D for an arbitrary constant binary matrix D. In each leading principal block the next free bit contributes only to its new diagonal entry. If the previous principal determinant is one, exactly one choice makes the enlarged determinant one. Choose those bits sequentially, evaluate the center offset including all control-only terms, solve for the leaves, and replay in the raw coordinates. A control lift may include leaf directions, which have zero crossing matrix and do not affect this argument. This is a deterministic polynomial-time raw target algorithm for the entire recoded family.

The standalone regression at r=7,8,9,11 checks the dimension of the full symmetrizer space, equality of recovered raw leaf axes to the planted set, and raw target witnesses with dense input/output recodings and drift.
