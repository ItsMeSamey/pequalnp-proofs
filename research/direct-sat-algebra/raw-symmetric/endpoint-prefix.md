# Endpoint-local prefix parity and residual-syndrome witnesses

For a fully symmetric binary matrix with diagonal t_i+d_i, write f_ij(t_i,t_j) for the edge function. Each determinant involution is a matching with monomers at its uncovered vertices. On Boolean assignments a dimer contributes f_ij itself. Within a fixed matching, summing over coordinate pins and free controls factors into independent sums: the loop at i contributes Σ_(allowed x)(x+d_i), and a dimer ij contributes Σ_(allowed x,y) f_ij(x,y). Form a numeric binary symmetric matrix from these marginals. Its determinant is exactly the conditional parity. Thus the previously problematic binary endpoint-local case admits polynomial-time odd-prefix extension even when a greedy leading-minor pivot fails. The formula works for unrestricted two-bit functions, although a nonlinear pencil need not have odd total parity. For an affine dimer with both endpoints free the sum is zero. A zero pinned loop forces a dimer and requires no separate branching.

More generally let f_ij(t_i,t_j,u) be any supplied Boolean oracle with shared syndrome u=Ht. For each frozen u use the same matching marginals with signs on control values to filter for consistency. A direct integer determinant of marginals is incorrect here: transpositions would square their integer weights. Instead use a skew-symmetric integer matrix on 2n vertices. Original-pair edges carry signed dimer marginals, vertex i joins private dummy i with its signed monomer marginal, and all pairs of dummies are joined with weight one. A matching of original dimers has 2j dummies left to pair, yielding (2j−1)!! completions including the empty completion. Its Pfaffian signs may differ but their sum is odd. For a fixed assignment, reduction modulo two thus recovers exactly its original matching weight.

Fix additional affine pins, eliminate coordinate-pinned bits first, and choose a stacked independent check basis with pin rows first. Write p for the affine pin rank on the free controls and r for its rank together with H there. For each of 2^(r−p) consistent stacked syndromes and each of 2^r sign characters, compute the integer marginals by assigning every possible value x at vertex i the sign (−1)^(x c·H'_i), where H' denotes the stacked basis. Fixed bits have unit weight after their contributions have been removed. The Pfaffian expansion sums over original control assignments with their character weights: choices at each matching block are disjoint, so each original bit is signed exactly once. Orthogonality extracts the desired coefficient by signed summation and exact division by 2^r *before* parity reduction. Stream the signed Pfaffians in polynomial space. The implementation uses exact rational skew elimination; intermediate Schur entries are ratios of Pfaffian minors, all of polynomial bit length by the matching expansion and bounded entry weights. Total time is O*(2^(2r−p)); with only coordinate pins this is O*(4^rank(H)). Enumerating the actual remote coordinate assignments directly gives the alternative O*(2^b) when all other controls are endpoint-local after b coordinates are frozen.

The shared space need not be supplied for affine off-diagonals. A row mask on ij has four permissible residuals, obtained by toggling i and j. Seek one representative of each row in a common space of dimension w. Starting with zero, find a row with no representative in the current span, branch on its four representatives, and add the chosen independent vector. Rows already representable stay so. In a solution space at least one branch for the first failing row stays in that space. Iterative depth bounds up to w find the minimum shared rank in O*(4^w) time and polynomial space. The recovered decomposition gives the edge oracle. For even n≥4 put global parity plus t_i on successive cycle edges. These n rows have full binary rank because I+11ᵀ is invertible for even n, whereas removing each designated endpoint gives the same shared parity row. Every raw control occurs remotely, but residual rank one suffices.

Purely biasing the search toward zero or one normalized diagonals gives no universal guarantee. On a two-by-two block det=x_i x_j+f_ij, an affine f_ij can make the determinant the indicator of any prescribed bit pair. Disjoint blocks can therefore have any unique good word in even dimension. Nor is a universal improvement asserted for congruence-based reordering or arbitrary dense pencils. The new bound improves Fibonacci time on verified structural families.
