# Discovering a leaf space and field pencil from raw binary quadratic coefficients

For a map on 2b input bits with b output bits, try coordinate scalar selectors, the all-ones selector and selectors of weight two. For a nonsingular scalar polar B₀, form T_j=B₀⁻¹B_j. A common isotropic b-space L is B₀-Lagrangian. Thus for x in L every T_j x lies in L, since B₀(T_j x,L)=B_j(x,L)=0. If the induced operators commute on the quotient by L, images of their commutators lie inside L. Close their images under the T_j. If its dimension reaches b it equals L. The code checks the recovered space directly against every polar and invokes the binary symmetry-pairing verifier, independently of this sufficient condition.

A second candidate uses the center Z of the commutant of the T_j. Compute its nilpotent subspace as the kernel of z↦z^(2^k) on Z for 2^k≥2b. This map is binary linear because Z is commutative. Test the invariant images of individual basis nilpotents and their summed image. An image is accepted only after the same dimension, isotropy, symmetry and diagonal-rank checks. These tests can expose an invariant leaf even when the commutator images extend outside it. They do not guarantee an identifiable leaf. For a plain field product ty with no nonlinear center drift the ratios commute and these two tests gave no leaf on the controls. Independent arbitrary binary-linear terms and a raw affine translation do not change the input polars.

Once a complement C and a leaf L have been found, compute (C,L)↦B(C,L) and discard center-center polar values. This crossing does not depend on a center-only nonlinear g or binary-linear leaf drift. Its scalar triples P,Q,R obey B(Pt,y)=B(t,Qy)=RB(t,y). The implementation obtains them by computing the polar centroid of the zero-extended crossing. Because this centroid requires full output span and zero common radical, its domain actions preserve C and L: a component mapping C to L would have zero pairing with all of C, by the zero C×C polar, and similarly for L to C.

For a tested extension degree k dividing b, look for a scalar action generating a field of degree k. The full crossing centroid is commutative on the required nondegenerate full bimap, so Frob^k−identity in this algebra is binary linear. Search its fixed space for an element with irreducible minimal polynomial of degree k and retain its simultaneous power actions on all three modules. No completeness claim is made for the bounded candidate list. Construct module bases for C,L,output using these powers; the final field row and output transformations must pass a raw replay.

With r=b/k, seek an invertible K-linear output change X for which every crossing matrix XM_i is symmetric in the recovered leaf frame. This is exactly the symmetry constraint for a pairing compatible with the chosen scalar actions; imposing K-linearity from the start avoids assuming that the first accepted binary pairing intertwines the chosen field. Each candidate X is checked with the independent raw_symmetric_overlap.fit, including the diagonal rank and all raw coefficients. This returns an ordinary field-pencil chart; its witness method evaluates and verifies the raw target. The scalar search and pairing search use an explicit cap. The witness runtime depends on the recovered pencil and drift as in symmetric-overlap.md; arbitrary drift can require exhaustive center search.

The three-by-three GF4 example uses diagonal entries (ωt₀,t₁,t₂) and off-diagonal entries ω times the remaining center variable. Every off-diagonal couples two leaves to a third center. Dense binary changes hide the coordinates; a separate nonlinear center-only map is present. The first nonsingular scalar selector produces raw commutator images of rank 10 in 12 input bits. A central nilpotent instead exposes the true six-bit leaf. The crossing scalar centroid has binary dimension exactly two, ruling out both a single GF64 multiplication chart and a direct sum of three independent GF4 products, whose crossing centroids would have dimension six. The recovered r=3 chart gives verified witnesses for all 64 raw targets. Eight dense r=2 GF4 cases also give verified witnesses for all 16 targets each.
