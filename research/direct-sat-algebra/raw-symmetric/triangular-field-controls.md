# Recovering triangular multiplication controls

Supply ordered binary bases of leaf summands Y₁,…,Yₖ and output summands E₁,…,Eₖ, with equal corresponding dimensions mᵢ and total input dimension twice the output dimension. The polar vanishes on the entire leaf space. Binary-affine drift is unrestricted, and pure control quadratics are unrestricted.

The promised private control summands Cᵢ, which are not supplied, satisfy three conditions. On Cᵢ × Yⱼ for j<i the full polar is zero. On Cᵢ × Yᵢ there are no output components earlier than Eᵢ, and its Eᵢ component is field multiplication in unknown binary coordinates. Its later output components are arbitrary. On Cᵢ × Yⱼ for j>i there are no restrictions on any output component. Thus leaves can participate in quadratic interactions outside their own multiplication block.

Start in any binary complement to the leaves. At step i compute the common kernel of all polar evaluations on Yᵢ inside the surviving control space. Earlier steps have already killed all earlier leaf columns. Injectivity of the diagonal multiplication, and vanishing of every later control on Yᵢ, identify this new kernel with precisely the later controls modulo the leaves. The complement chosen for the ith block may contain arbitrary later-control and leaf additions, neither of which affects its diagonal pairing. Check all earlier output projections, align the diagonal axes by evaluating at a pair with nonzero polar, certify the normalized field multiplication table and compare every diagonal basis pair. Check overall leaf isotropy first. These polynomial tests certify the triangular structure in the returned frames.

At step i, all previous leaf columns are unchanged by the new control. Restrict them to earlier output rows to form an invertible square matrix P. Let the current leaf column block in these rows be V and the previous leaf columns in the current output be W. The effective diagonal is D+WP⁻¹V. Selecting the ith control changes neither P nor V nor W, while D receives precisely the chosen scalar multiplication. A safe slope therefore makes the new leading matrix invertible. Later controls kill all already processed leaf columns on every output. After all choices the full leaf map is invertible. Its affine offset, including the unrestricted pure control quadratic, is evaluated at the chosen controls. Solve once for all leaves and verify the raw target.

The executable control includes an earlier-control/later-leaf Frobenius coupling, a diagonal-to-later-output coupling, a control-control product and a later-leaf-to-earlier-output drift. Dense binary recoding and translation preserve all 16 verified targets. A nonisotropic leaf space and an incorrect block order are rejected. The ordered leaf and output decompositions are side information. Finding these decompositions in an unannotated raw map remains open here.
