For G=D+UV^T binary with D upper triangular off diagonal, guess z=V^T x. Sweep backward. At cut prefix L and chosen suffix R retain D[L,R]x_R, partial V^T x_R and mark parity. The next selected bit is legal iff its diagonal, known residual suffix contribution and U z offset sum to zero. Zero is always legal. Update the three summaries; at the end accept signal z and mark one. Each physical solution has just one guess and trajectory. If the maximum cut rank of this actual D is w, exact count and witness take 2^(w+2r+1)poly(n) time, each table <=2^(w+r+1) states. Smaller correction rank need not preserve w.

On a tournament inverting all arcs inside S changes adjacency by 1_S 1_S^T off-diagonal. A list of k inversions to transitivity gives r<=k and w<=1 (diagonal absorbed in D). Without a supplied list the constructive tournament inversion recognition algorithm of Alon, Powierski, Savery, Scott and Wilmer, Section 5, runs in O_k(n²) and returns the order and sets: https://doi.org/10.1137/23M1547135 . Overall exact count/witness time O_k(n²)+4^k poly(n). The executable tests supplied inversion certificates; it does not implement that literature recognizer. On incomplete oriented graphs an inversion leaves missing edges missing, so the same outer-product identity can fail.
