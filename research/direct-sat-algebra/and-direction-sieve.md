# Cancellation along gate-output directions

Use the original acyclic binary AND equations z_i=a_i b_i on n raw bits and m gate outputs, with affine a_i,b_i and affine output o. Each raw input extends uniquely. For a nonzero binary direction v on z define A_v=supp(v) and N_v the gate indices whose a or b changes under translation by v. Require A_v disjoint from N_v and pairwise disjoint footprints A_v union N_v among chosen v. These conditions are checkable on the normalized circuit. Bounded weight candidates can be enumerated in polynomial time for fixed weight and any disjoint packing is sound.

Choose i_v in A_v. On the physical graph replace the signed output phase o by the quadratic phase Q=o+sum_v k_v(z_i+a_i b_i), i=i_v, with k_v=o(v)-o(0)+1+|A_v| in GF(2), evaluating the direction with raw bits zero. Each chosen product is invariant along every chosen direction, since no chosen pivot lies in any N. Thus Q changes by 1+|A_v| under translation by v.

Expand the product of the integer indicators [z_i=a_i b_i]=(1-z_i)+(2z_i-1)a_i b_i. Index U selects the second summand. Its affine support imposes a_i=b_i=1 on U and z_i=0 outside U; its sign is Q+|U|+sum_(i in U) z_i. Whenever A_v is contained in U and N_v misses U, translation by v preserves all constraints and reverses the sign. Each disjoint footprint rules out exactly one local membership pattern. Enumerating the survivors and evaluating the quadratic Gauss sums on the affine solution spaces computes the signed output sum in deterministic polynomial space and time poly(size) times 2^m product_v(1-2^(-|A_v union N_v|)). Inconsistent terms contribute zero. Ordinary acceptance count is (2^n-signed sum)/2. With raw bits pinned, use the number of free raw bits in this normalization; the involutions preserve all pins. Successive original-bit restrictions recover a verified witness with polynomial overhead.

An explicit family separates this bound from both the original coordinate-star enumeration bound and the affine quotient rank bound on the supplied circuit. Set n=m=3k. Process consecutive triples of gate indices i,i+1,i+2. Let B be the XOR of first and third outputs of each completed triple, and A the XOR of the middle outputs of completed triples. For j in this triple a_j=x_j+A. The three b forms are B, B+z_i, B+z_(i+1); only for the very first gate use x_2 instead of B. Output o=z_m. Running accumulators give a linear-size original circuit.

For each triple use v=e_i+e_(i+2). Its N is exactly {i+1}. Each triple removes one of eight patterns, giving 7^k terms. The single-coordinate stars of all indices outside the last triple contain every gate in the last triple, so a packing has at most four centers. In the matrix of affine forms for the two inputs per gate and the final readout, the raw matrix has rank n by the a rows. All b rows except the first and the final output row have zero raw part (the final output also has zero raw part). The m-1 b rows numbered 2 through m have unit subdiagonal and no later entries, and the final output adds e_m. These m zero-raw rows witness full quotient rank tau=m, while rho=n. Other algebraic rewrites or source counters can exploit further structure on this family. No all-circuit savings follows.
