# Odd safe-slope parity on a recognized affine fixed locus

The graph of a binary matrix A on K=GF(2^d) has vertical direction missing, and its other missing directions are the ordinary safe slopes G. Each bad slope has a nonzero binary kernel, and the nonzero vectors in those kernels partition K*. Every part has odd size. Thus |G| is odd.

For j from 0 through d−1 form all 2-by-2 K-matrices T with T12=0 satisfying T Frob^j(graph A) contained in graph A. This is a homogeneous binary linear system tested on the given basis of the graph. For a basis of its solution space the restriction of det(T) is a binary-coordinate polynomial with K values of degree at most two. If any invertible T exists, one supported on at most two basis elements works, by uniqueness of Boolean ANF. The invertible T gives equality of the graphs and acts on finite slopes as φ(s)=(T22 s^(2^j)+T21)/T11. The original projective mover shortcut handles T12 nonzero separately.

Raise φ by affine-semilinear composition to the odd integer (2^d−1)·oddpart(d), stripping its odd-order part, since the affine semilinear group has order 2^d(2^d−1)d. The resulting 2-element ψ preserves G. Its non-singleton orbits have even size, so Fix(ψ) contains an odd number of original safe scalars. If 2^r is the full 2-part of d/gcd(j,d), the Frobenius component of ψ has order 2^r. Its affine fixed equation has linear part x+a x^(2^l). If this linear part has a nonzero kernel, it is a one-dimensional space over GF(2^gcd(l,d)); this follows by dividing by one nonzero kernel element. Consequently at most 2^(d/2^r) roots must be scanned. Solve the binary affine system, try these roots, and verify the original binary rank. Search the highest r first. When r≥2 this is a strict improvement over square-root time on this recognizable class. If no applicable map exists the solver abstains.

Passing to a full 2-subgroup does not give independent fixed equations beyond cyclic generators: orbit counting gives a common fixed safe slope for the subgroup. After centering there, its Galois projection has kernel in the odd-order group K*, hence is injective; its image is cyclic. Conversely, for odd d a Frobenius symmetry alone does not guarantee an odd good fixed orbit. At d=3, x²+sqrt(x) is Frobenius-equivariant yet neither 0 nor 1 is a safe scalar.

The native implementation and checker are affine_semilinear_safe.py and check_affine_semilinear_safe.py. The bound applies only to the recognized stabilizer types, and never assumes that an extension-field answer directly decodes.
