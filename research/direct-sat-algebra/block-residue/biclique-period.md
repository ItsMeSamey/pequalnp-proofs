# Collective syndrome directions for signed comparisons

Represent failure of each distinct signed request by a bit. Keep different signs distinct. On the multigraph of requests, let H have a basis of the binary cycle space as rows and d be the request parities. Exactly the failure words f with Hf=Hd are physical: integrate d+f along a spanning forest and check each nontree edge. Signed raw copy pins are undone at the end. Every physical paired shell forbids choosing both of two failure flags. A self-conflict forces its flag zero. For a verified disjoint complete-bipartite conflict component, an independent support lies entirely in one of the two shores. The resulting local syndrome set is the union of the spans of the two shores' columns of H. Different components choose independently. A cover with overlapping flag memberships requires a different encoding.

Maintain fixed viable shore choices and a subspace G contained in the sum of available ORIGINAL columns for every tuple of remaining choices. Initially use freely selectable flags. In the quotient modulo G, let U,V be the spans of a component's shores. If U is contained in V, fix the V shore. For a complete tuple choosing U, replacing it by V loses no achievable total syndrome, since the new complete sum contains both G and representatives for V. The symmetric case is identical. Add the larger span of each fixed pair and the intersection of each still-incomparable pair to the quotient directions pulled into G. Every surviving tuple still spans the new G. Simultaneous fixations preserve the invariant because the replaced tuple was possible under every earlier restriction. Repeat to stability. Finally enumerate the remaining orientations and solve once on ORIGINAL allowed columns per orientation, with provenance returning the actual failure bits. In particular if the target lies in G the first orientation works.

With N physical input bits plus vertices and listed requests, all syndrome masks and provenance have polynomial bit length. Each nonterminal pass eliminates a component or increases rank, and there are at most the number of components plus rank many such passes. Ordinary binary Gaussian elimination, intersections, verification of the complete conflict graph and all copy lifts have polynomial bit complexity. With k remaining incomparable components the full cost is O(2^k poly(N)) in polynomial space. No independent-shore eligibility assumption can be inferred from overlapping covers. Shore choices can duplicate a physical solution, so this algorithm addresses decision and witness recovery.

The obstruction in marked-cycle-span-barrier.md concerns the intersection of all pre-dominance orientation sums. That space can be smaller than the translation stabilizer of their union. On connected triangle SAT images, the syndrome coordinate of a flag is its clause triangle, so either shore supplies the clauses admitting its polarity. A tautological clause lies in both shores' span for its variable; its coordinate is eliminated for all other components as well. Clauses on ordinary variables remain arbitrary source SAT. The registered implementation does not call a generic SAT solver.
