# High-width ordered four-state constraints on a single NAND output

The original source highwidth_order_fixture.py exposes ordered_source(n, strict_final) for n≥5. Use n sites per shore, each with private high and low primary bits encoding 0,1,2,3. Require every Lᵢ≤Rⱼ, plus L₁<L₂<L₃<L₄ and either L₄≤L₅ (YES) or L₄<L₅ (NO). A YES witness has left states 0,1,2,3,3, then zeros, and every right state 3. Five strictly increasing states are impossible on NO. Neither circuit accepts the all-zero raw word.

Use the four one-hot indicators per site from promoted-bare-nand-path.md. Each forbidden pair contributes a NAND on the two corresponding site indicators. Add a fresh raw guard g, a NAND(g,I) for each of the 8n rails I, and the final unary leaf NOT(g). Left-fold AND the verifier leaves followed by that unary leaf, implementing AND and NOT by ordinary NANDs. The sole designated output is positive. This yields connected physical wiring. The source forces g=0, so all guard checks are vacuous on true words but expose every state rail, including extremal states never forbidden along some cross edges. A satisfying raw word decodes directly to the original states, and the YES word sets g=0.

A weak order forbids six state pairs and a strict order ten. Including guards, the number Q of verifier NANDs is 6n²+8n+36 (YES) or 6n²+8n+40 (NO). The builder uses 4n+1 primaries and 20n+3Q+1 NANDs: ten per site, Q verifiers, one guard inverter, and Q two-gate ANDs. Signed normalization retains 8n indicator-producing gates plus 2Q verifier and conjunction gates. The two raw bits of a site generate exactly its four indicators. No verifier can be generated from those local raws alone; each test needs another site or g. Each pair of verifier inputs is distinct and generates just one verifier. Each conjunction needs two produced leaves or a preceding conjunction plus its next leaf, and its two inputs generate nothing else. Thus the maximal cones are the 2n sites and 2Q singleton verifier and conjunction cones. All four indicator ports per site, both verifier inputs, both conjunction inputs, and each individual gate output at those singleton cones are visible. Their initial number of bad tables is 2n+2Q.

The binary hull of each logical AND includes output-implies-each-input. The positive root consequently forces all leaf values true and g=0. Every verifier then has fixed output and is a relation on two inputs. Every conjunction has a single row. They all promote, putting the true negative incompatibilities into the common binary base. Each site still has all four one-hot rows, and three distinct rows have absent all-zero majority. To see local support, select a real state at that site and assign logical zero to every indicator at the other sites. Such ghost rows satisfy the sites' binary at-most-one projections. All verifiers and conjunctions can then take their required true values. The same construction extends two real rows precisely when the direct pair is allowed, or unconditionally on a nonedge. Exactly 2Q tables promote and 2n remain, with four rows each before arc consistency. The recovered graph is exactly Kₙ,ₙ plus four left-path edges, having n²+4 edges, and has treewidth ≥n. Indeed, contracting n−1 disjoint cross edges creates n−1 pairwise adjacent branch sets; both unmatched opposite vertices are adjacent to every such set and to each other, yielding a Kₙ₊₁ minor.

The physical entrance enumeration is in numeric state order since the high raw bit has lower wire ID. Complemented indicator ports do not reorder the real rows. Both < and ≤ are closed under coordinatewise minimum and median in that order. For strict order use x+1≤y and monotonicity of these operations. Enforce arc consistency to a fixed point. Empty domains certify infeasibility. Otherwise choose the least remaining state at every vertex. On any edge, take a support for the left minimum and a support for the right minimum. Both are legal rows and their coordinatewise minimum is the pair of simultaneous minima. Hence the assignment satisfies the entire cyclic CSP and lifts to the original raw bits. Checking closure, pruning, graph construction, and raw replay all take polynomial bit time and space. A direct product enumeration fallback on this cyclic component is budgeted for 4^(2n) tuples. This is a budget comparison, not a lower bound on SAT runtime. The explicit inequalities also give a direct source-level decision.

Minimum and median here operate on four-state rows. On the original endpoint bits the permitted pairs (0,0),(1,1),(1,2) for ≤ have coordinatewise bit majority (1,0), which is forbidden. The bitwise meet of the latter two gives the same forbidden pair. Its ten satisfying assignments exclude an affine flat over the four Boolean bits. These facts distinguish the local relation in its specified raw encoding and imply no lower bound for different global encodings.
