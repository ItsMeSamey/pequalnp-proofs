# Signed bit copies on physical matching edges

For each K2 shell its two comparison bits d0,d1 must not both be zero. First solve the raw signed copy equations by graph propagation, remembering every bit's offset to its component representative; reject inconsistent pins. Contract to a multigraph whose edges are physical comparisons on representative components. Adjust each comparison by its two endpoint offsets. A proposed comparison vector lifts iff its adjusted XOR is zero around every cycle, including loops and digons. Root each forest component, integrate its comparisons, apply the saved offsets, and check the original constraints.

When a spanning fundamental-cycle basis has disjoint edge supports, start with all comparison bits one. Make a bipartite graph between cycles of incorrect adjusted parity and the K2 shells that own one or more of their comparisons. A covering matching is necessary: every bad cycle needs a zero, while each shell tolerates at most one, and the cycles are edge-disjoint. It is sufficient: zero one owned comparison on each matched cycle, changing no other cycle, then lift the entire solution. Outside the disjoint-cycle promise consistent pins cause NonCactusError, rather than an UNSAT claim.

If each raw bit occurs in at most one copy pin, the copy and comparison edges form alternating paths and cycles, meeting the condition. At two occurrences even unsigned copies suffice for NP-hardness, as shown in equality-pinned-shell-hardness.md. The polynomial matching method does not require bounded pin rank. It decides and reconstructs a physical witness; it does not compute weighted multiplicities.
