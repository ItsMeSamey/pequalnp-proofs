# Exact boundaries for shared-control NAND corridors

A corridor has entrance x=x₀, a fixed pilot h, and x_{i+1}=NAND(x_i,h XOR l_i), where the l_i are fixed polarity bits. A tap is an explicitly observed physical wire. Separate corridors have disjoint internal wires unless a permitted interface explicitly identifies their values.

## Exit formulas and costs

On any pilot value a control zero resets the following state to 1, while a control one toggles it. If no reset precedes exit t then x_t=x XOR (t mod 2). Otherwise, if j is the last reset step before t, then x_t=1 XOR ((t−1−j) mod 2). These statements also cover arbitrary early exits before the first switch. The entrance x₀ itself is a separate input, not a disabled exit.

Let g=h XOR l₀ be the first run's control. Before the first polarity switch, all positive even-time exits share a bit E and all odd-time exits share O. When g=0 both equal 1; when g=1 they equal x and its complement respectively. An unused parity may be introduced as a private auxiliary. Starting with the first exit produced by a changed control, every exit depends only on h. For trailing constant-polarity run length L and last control b, the exit equals 1 if L is even and 1 XOR b otherwise: when b=0 a reset just occurred; when b=1 the preceding run ended in a reset. This costs one scan over the physical gates, without a global table of pilot assignments.

With arbitrary shared source-literal entrances and pilots, 2CNF source constraints and **unary exit pins**, a pre-switch tap pinned to one emits the binary clause ¬g OR (x XOR parity); pinned to zero it emits the units g and ¬(x XOR parity). Later taps give units or constants. Solve the resulting linear-size 2CNF once across all pilots, recover original bits and recompute the full NAND trace. Counting is already #IS-hard: pin NAND(x_u,x_v) to one for every graph edge uv. Allowed raw source assignments are exactly independent sets, with a unique trace each. Arbitrary coupled suffixes must still be charged to the residual solver. If branching on pilots is preferable, branch once per distinct pilot or on the actual rank of their affine forms, rather than separately per corridor.

## Private pilots with arbitrary 2CNF exterior

When entrance and pilot are both private to each corridor, project its exact four input rows onto its distinct observed ports. Any relation on at most two Boolean ports is bijunctive: forbid each absent row by the corresponding clause. This includes post-switch pilot literals and constants. Distinct here counts effective bit values after deduplicating copies, constants and complemented copies, retaining their signs for the exterior. An arbitrary number of actual side exits of the same two types is allowed. The conjunction with any supplied 2CNF exterior is again 2CNF. A satisfying port assignment lifts per corridor by taking any compatible one of the four input rows. Recompute every gate and replay exterior constraints. Runtime is linear in total corridor gates, explicit tap references and exterior literal occurrences in the indexed model.

If only pre-switch taps and constant later taps are exposed, the projected relation is E OR O. One may even retain a shared or externally observed entrance x while eliminating independent private pilots: the exact relation is (E OR O) AND (E OR ¬x) AND (O OR x). For E=O=1 take g=0 with the supplied x, otherwise take g=1 and the two clauses force x=E. If x is private choose x=E on unequal ports and any x on 11. Thus sharing an entrance does not itself spoil this projection. Sharing a supposedly private pilot does invalidate the independent lifting argument unless its agreement is enforced.

## Three exposed bits

Expose both pre-switch parities and one nonconstant post-switch tap; after complementing the third tap if necessary it is g. With private entrance the three rows on (E,O,g) are exactly 110,101,011. Their coordinatewise majority is 111, which is absent. Every 2CNF relation is closed under majority, including after existential introduction of auxiliary bits, so there is no such 2CNF description. The length-three physical control list g,g,1−g realizes O,E,g at the three successive gates; no abstract extra port is needed. With only one pre-switch parity f together with g the exact projected relation is f OR g.

The resulting three-port family with even an equality-only exterior is NP-hard. Start with positive exact-one-in-three SAT. Give each clause a fresh independent three-gate corridor and identify, using equality constraints, all ports belonging to occurrences of the same variable. Interpret a zero port as selecting that variable. The attainable triples impose exactly one selected variable per clause. Conversely any such selection lifts by the four-row rule. For ordinary 3SAT a direct alternative uses arbitrary 2CNF exterior clauses forbidding simultaneous zero selectors on complementary literal occurrences. Every source model can choose one true occurrence per clause, and compatible selectors give a consistent source assignment. These are decision reductions on existentially private pilots and entrances, with all additional coupling explicitly in the exterior.

For shared raw pilots even binary exits can expose ordinary SAT directly: encode a∨b as NAND(¬a,¬b), then impose the external binary clause between this tap and c. Thus closed-form corridor substitution alone gives no uniform Boolean decision bound for unrestricted exterior relations.
