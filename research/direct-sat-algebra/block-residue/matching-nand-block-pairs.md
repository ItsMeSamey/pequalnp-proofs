# Noncontiguous original NAND block coarsening

Let c(U) be the zero-or-one majority failure of the exposed table of an admissible block. Exposure is computed from original wire consumers and exterior observations. Consequently c(U) is independent of how the remaining gates are partitioned. For two seed blocks recompute the true table of their union U∪V on at most four entering words if it has at most two distinct entering wires. Give that pair the positive gain c(U)+c(V)−c(U∪V), omitting nonpositive edges. A set of disjoint pair merges is a matching, and its total weight is exactly the reduction in bad blocks. Cycles between noncontiguous groups cause no problem for witness lifting: induct along original gates.

The dependency-free solver selects disjoint weight-two edges greedily, then weight-one edges. Charge each edge of an optimum matching to the first chosen edge that met it. Its weight is at most that of the chosen edge, which receives charge from at most two optimum edges. The greedy algorithm captures at least half the best saving available from a single round of pair merges. This guarantees a saving in the exponent, not a factor approximation of search time. The maximum-weight-matching formulation gives the exact one-round optimum if a deterministic weighted blossom implementation is available.

The solver independently coarsens the singleton and previously optimized contiguous partitions, chooses the lower residual b, verifies the physical projection costs and passes the original netlist and output pin to the witness solver. It does not optimize over all partitions or orders. For k seed blocks containing g gates, the sum of the two group sizes across all tested pairs is (k−1)g. Thus scoring, sorting and graph construction take O(kg log(g+2)+k²) indexed operations, and candidate storage is O(k²). The preceding contiguous optimizer requires O(g³) indexed operations. If N counts declared inputs, L counts exterior occurrences and W=N+g+L+1, the subsequent solver requires O(2^b W) indexed work and polynomial space, including N bits for an explicit original assignment. Index arithmetic, sorting, allocation and dictionary access admit conservative polynomial bit overhead in W.

For the supplied z_i,w_i family the sequence z_1,w_1,z_2,w_2,…,z_k,w_k followed by the original aggregator and suffix is itself a legal topological reordering. Thus the degree of improvement demonstrated there is also attainable by legal reordering followed by the previous interval solver. On general inputs the implementation need not materialize any such order: some disjoint groups induce cyclic group dependencies while the original gate DAG remains acyclic.
