# Original signed-shell witnesses from the syndrome quotient

Contract consistent signed copy pins in the four raw positions of each matching shell. Keep both comparison occurrences even when they have equal signed endpoints. Inconsistent pins reject. Let H be the indexed fundamental-cycle matrix, d the vector of requested differences, and t=Hd. A failure vector f integrates to vertex potentials precisely when Hf=t. A satisfying assignment has at most one failed occurrence per pair.

For each orientation O designate one occurrence in each pair as eligible to fail. The guarantee G is contained in the span of H restricted to O for every O. The incident provider obtains the exact intersection for the charts it handles. The default uses zero as a guaranteed subspace on nonincident inputs. The blockwise option obtains the exact intersection there as well, with the separate cost of branching on nonincident pairs inside provider blocks. For the provider's ordered chord set Q, the matrix M=H[:,Q] is invertible and the provider's fundamental matrix is M inverse times H. Multiply the returned primal directions by M before quotienting.

Pass all columns and t modulo G to the syndrome solver. Any original feasible failure vector projects to a feasible quotient vector. Conversely its returned quotient flags choose at most one occurrence per pair. Select the flagged occurrence as eligible, or either occurrence for a zero pair. The residual between t and these original columns' flagged sum lies in G and hence in the chosen oriented span. Thus t itself lies in that span. To realize it, impose the requests on all complementary occurrences and propagate signed differences. Consistency follows from this span condition. Restore the signed copy offsets and replay every original shell and pin. The returned original failure bits may differ from the quotient flags.

After removing free directions and splitting independent coordinate pieces, consider a piece of rank r with g pairs. Packed dynamic programming costs polynomial factors times 2^r and orientation enumeration costs polynomial factors times 2^g. Let B=2^max(0,r-3) bytes. For sum S of local shore ranks the packed solver uses O((g+S)B) indexed byte operations and at most (g+r+4)B bytes of bitset payload, including the stored predecessor layers needed to recover a witness. Runtime on a bit machine additionally charges index arithmetic. Auto selects DP if r≤g and its estimated payload fits max_dp_bytes, when specified. Otherwise it enumerates orientations in polynomial space. Forced DP reports MemoryError if its payload would exceed the supplied cap. A cap on packed bitsets excludes Python object overhead, the fixed byte permutation table, graph and elimination storage, and the separate guarantee computation. Sequential pieces add time and take their maximum payload. This selection compares exponential factors and does not measure wall-clock speed.
