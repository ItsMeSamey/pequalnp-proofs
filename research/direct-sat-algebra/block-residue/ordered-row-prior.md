# Ordered row relations and prior results

Jeavons and Cooper give a polynomial algorithm for constraints preserved by coordinatewise maximum on ordered domains. Reversing each domain order gives the minimum version. For a binary instance, enforce arc consistency until no row loses a support. On any remaining edge take a supporting pair for each endpoint's least surviving value. Their coordinatewise minimum is precisely the pair of least values, so those choices satisfy every edge at once. This works on cyclic graphs. Arc consistency alone can accept infeasible arbitrary binary instances, as the disequality triangle on two values demonstrates. [Jeavons and Cooper, *Tractable constraints on ordered domains*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370295001077)

For a proposed order on a site's rows, encode value a by its threshold bits [a ≥ j]. The Boolean codewords are exactly the monotone bit strings. For any three rows, the coordinatewise majority of their threshold bits encodes their median. Thus a median-preserving edge relation becomes a majority-closed Boolean relation on the two groups of threshold bits. Its unary and binary projections suffice by majority decomposability. Forbid each absent unary or binary tuple by a clause, impose threshold monotonicity, and solve the resulting 2-CNF. This is a direct specialization of the closure theorem. [Jeavons, Cohen and Gyssens, Proposition 5.4](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/~richoux/teaching/Jeavons-Cohen-Gyssens-97%20-%20Closure%20Properties%20of%20Contraints.pdf) · [Aspvall, Plass and Tarjan, 2-SAT](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(79)90002-4)

Verifying a supplied order tests each relation for closure. Permuting domains independently to uncover a tractable language is a separate recognition problem studied explicitly in the literature. A min ordering of a single digraph similarly orders the domain of one relation. Here each physical site has its own domain, and every incident table constrains the same local choice of order. Searching two candidate orders per site certifies that restricted search space. An unsuccessful search can leave other orders or other tractable representations. [*Domain permutation reduction for constraint satisfaction problems*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370207002093) · [Hell et al., *Min-Orderable Digraphs*](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1241763)

The circuit-specific step constructs exact physical rows after signed normalization and promotion, queries the common binary relaxation for row-pair compatibility, and recovers a jointly consistent assignment for the original wires. The order and closure tests apply to those measured row domains and tables. Projection and lifting are what connect the ordered CSP solution back to the designated NAND output.
