# Compressing matching-flat NAND sources without expansion

Use the sparse original-bit program in shell-stream.md. At a cut each live proper-color biclique has two disjoint color masks, giving at most 3^4=81 possibilities. Its mask is discarded when the opposite shore is exhausted. General uniform difference masks require at most 256 combinations. Ordinary forward edges impose named future bans; each crossing edge contributes at most four original-color cases. Partial syndromes lie in an affine cut interface of dimension lambda. Thus one coarse width bound W is 2·81^a·4^g·2^lambda for a live proper bicliques and g crossing ordinary edges (replace 81 by 256 for arbitrary relation masks). The leading two handles a partly read color. Shared vertices update all of their incident factors on the same original bits.

For an observed physical bit retain its two transition matrices; for a summed bit apply their sum. The modular-support adapter represents the latter as that sum on dummy input 0, zero on 1. Distributivity proves this represents the full observed residue function. Weights are added when histories merge, regardless of differing past multiplicities. A separate Boolean path restores an accepting original coloring even when a signed residue vanishes. Sparse construction and a fixed-word scan cost polynomial in explicit source size N, ring precision p and original incidence sizes times the sum of layer occupancies. Densifying for the earlier modular-support algorithm can cost O(NW²) entries and its arithmetic bit clock is O(N p^5 W^4). No assumption bounds W on arbitrary inputs.

On the matching-flat obstruction process one K_{d,d} block at a time, with at most two adjacent ordinary bridges crossing outside the attached core. The shell has polynomial cost despite its super-truth-table number of surviving affine supports. For arbitrary H, attaching one bridge to the unpinned bipartite shell never prevents extension of a proper coloring of H. Enumerating H can cost 4^|V(H)| times polynomial; alternatively charge its own cut widths. Conditional exact counts must keep the actual attachment color. In the all-z-equal NAND variant the all-proper and all-equal cases exhaust the possibilities; they coincide for edgeless graphs. Free t and its unique suffix give two original assignments per coloring, or one per t if t is observed. A zero modular count cannot decide feasibility in general.

matching_nand.py regenerates the canonical complete literal-indexed NAND netlist from the supplied shell description and compares every gate and output; the cost includes the specified pair comparators. An unrecognized netlist is skipped. On accepted inputs the wrapper constructs the physical guard and suffix bits and replays the original NAND output. This is a verifier for the declared parse. A universal semantic parse recognizer is not assumed. The archived vertex_union.py already gives unweighted colorability in suitable layouts; support-based coloring DP on given clique-width expressions is classical. These results concern mixed observed/summed signed functions and original-NAND verification; no generic NAND saving follows.
