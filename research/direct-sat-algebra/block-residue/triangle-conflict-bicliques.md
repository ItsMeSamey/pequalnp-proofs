# Conflict bicliques and signed-cycle feasibility at a singleton fixed point

Work after consistent copy contraction. Retain the original physical lookup for every indexed request, but share its failure flag f_i=x_u XOR x_v XOR a_i when endpoints and offset agree. Each paired shell forbids f_i=f_j=1. Build the conflict graph on these signed flag types. Duplicate conflict edges have the same Boolean constraint. A loop forces its flag to zero and is processed as an unconditional signed equation. Distinct offsets on the same endpoint pair remain distinct flag types and are related automatically through the common potentials.

Supply bicliques (L_k,R_k) covering the remaining conflict edges exactly in union. Check both containment directions, or add singleton bicliques for uncovered original edges. Never complete a missing cross edge by assumption. Every feasible selected flag set avoids at least one entire shore per biclique: otherwise its two selected flags form a conflict. Introduce one orientation bit per biclique choosing a shore to force to zero. Orientations are simultaneous, and a flag forced by any incident chosen shore must be zero. Conversely any potentials satisfying these chosen signed equations avoid every conflict. Orientations may be nonunique; counting orientation assignments would not count physical models.

For fixed orientations, forcing f_i=0 imposes x_u XOR x_v=a_i. The necessary and sufficient condition is zero target sum on every cycle of the activated edge multigraph. This includes a failed self-loop and conflicting parallel differences. Signed forest propagation either produces potentials (one arbitrary root bit per component), or exhibits a contradictory cycle. Apply saved offsets and replay every original indexed shell. With k verified bicliques this gives a deterministic O*(2^k) decision and witness method; constructing and verifying a cover is separate. A polynomial-size joint Boolean encoding also follows: retain the physical vertex bits and for each shore membership use the orientation literal to imply its signed equality, which takes at most two ternary clauses; include all unconditional equations. Its size is linear in total verified memberships and vertices plus physical reconstruction records. The signed implications share the vertex bits. Testing each biclique separately does not test their joint cycles.

If only orientation variables are desired, each contradictory signed cycle forbids every consistent combination of activating literals that covers its edges. For a cycle witnessed in one branch, choose one true activating literal per nonfixed edge; the disjunction of their negations is a sound clause blocking that branch. Fixed edges need no literal. An inconsistent all-fixed cycle alone refutes the instance. The full such clause family is exact but can be exponentially large; forest checks avoid explicitly enumerating it.

In the cited triangle fixed point the distinct failure flags on an original triangle have prescribed odd XOR. No cycle uses different triangles, since they meet only at an articulation. With zero flags forced by oriented shores, exactly one local case fails: all three flags have been forced to zero. Otherwise choose one unforced flag to be one and set the other two to zero; integrate around the triangle and glue by the common root. If an occurrence is incident to several bicliques, its being unforced is the conjunction that every one of the corresponding orientations avoids its shore. Each triangle therefore gives a disjunction of three conjunctions on the *same* orientation bits. This reasoning counts no slack flags twice and uses the existence of actual local potentials.

In the constructed 3SAT image every literal occurrence belongs to exactly one conflict biclique, with the positive occurrences on one side and the negative occurrences on the other. These components can be recognized directly by a bipartite traversal and a complete cross-edge check; the added tautologies keep both shores populated. Choose the false polarity as the forced shore. Every unforced condition is then a single literal of that variable's orientation, each triangle is exactly its original three-literal clause, and the added triangles are tautologies. An orientation satisfying these clauses gives an actual physical witness by the triangle lift; a physical witness selects compatible true literals and hence gives an orientation. Thus the compression can replace quadratically many indexed complementary-pair shells by linear occurrence data after inspecting the raw input. It recovers general 3SAT on the hard image. A smaller presentation, or a polynomial per-orientation cycle test, does not imply polynomial unrestricted decision.
