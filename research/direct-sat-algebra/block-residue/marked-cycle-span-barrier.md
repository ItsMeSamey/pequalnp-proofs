# Universal span membership for paired cographic columns

Let G be an undirected simple graph. Take a matrix H whose rows form a binary cycle-space basis. Write h_i for its column at edge i. Partition all indexed edges into disjoint pairs. An orientation O selects exactly one member per pair. A marked column h_e lies outside the intersection of span{h_i : i in O} over all O precisely when a simple cycle containing e takes at most one edge from each pair.

Indeed h_e outside one chosen span has a separating binary linear functional. Its values on all columns form an Eulerian subgraph through e avoiding O. Decomposing it into edge-disjoint simple cycles supplies a cycle through e. Conversely the incidence vector of any such cycle is a row combination separating h_e from the span obtained by choosing one off-cycle edge per pair. This argument permits zero columns, including dummy bridges.

## Reduction

From 3SAT make a serial chain of two-branch variable stages followed by three-branch clause stages and add a closing edge e between the chain's ends. Use internally disjoint paths of length at least two in every stage and distinct stage terminals except for consecutive identifications. A simple cycle through e traverses exactly one whole branch of each stage. For each literal position put a fresh distinguished edge in its clause branch and a distinct shadow edge on its variable's branch that makes the literal false. Lengthen each branch as needed by subdivision. Pair just these two indexed edges. Give each remaining original edge, including e, a private mate, a new bridge to a fresh leaf at one fixed old vertex. These bridges can never occur in a cycle. Repeated literals and repeated variables use distinct occurrence pairs.

A feasible cycle specifies a truth value per variable and an occurrence per clause. If a chosen occurrence were false, its shadow and its distinguished edge would both lie on the cycle. Conversely a satisfying assignment and its choice of true occurrence in each clause give a feasible cycle. Construction has linear size in the occurrence list; a binary fundamental cycle matrix can be computed in polynomial time when explicit columns are requested. A proposed cycle is a polynomial certificate. Thus outside-intersection is NP-complete and universal membership is coNP-complete. The reduction concerns a target required to belong to every oriented span. It gives no corresponding hardness result for membership in at least one span.

## Physical layout and degrees

Use a fresh physical matching shell per indexed pair. For compared edges uv and wz put colors (X_u,X_w) and (X_v,X_z) at its two endpoints, with four fresh raw bit slots even when vertex names coincide. Connect all occurrences of each graphic vertex by a separate path of unsigned equality pins. Quotienting these pins returns exactly the two indexed graphic comparisons. No physical comparison belongs to two shells and each slot has at most two incident pins. Unsigned pins suffice for the structural columns. If a separately supplied signed target must be encoded in request offsets, those offsets can be realized by signed copy pins and undoing the offsets in witness recovery.

For minimum degree three, after building the entire paired graph attach one fresh K4 at each existing vertex of degree below three, identifying precisely that vertex and pairing the six new edges internally. Every new vertex has degree three and each attachment vertex gains three distinct neighbors. A simple cycle containing e cannot also traverse such a singly attached block. The original dummy bridges remain bridges and every original feasible cycle can avoid all new edges. This has linear overhead and establishes the same barrier even at minimum degree three. The graph can still have articulation vertices.
