Even n>=6: on the odd-total-parity slice, each selected i requires the sum outside {i,i-1} to be one, with predecessor cyclic. The native graph is strongly connected with n-2 off-diagonal entries per row. Its skew adjacency is the undirected cycle of rank n-2: kernel words are period-two. On the marked slice its identical gated conditions are x_i implies x_{i-1}, so only empty and full supports could work and both have even cardinality.

Eliminate x_n. Rows 2 through n-1 give binary clauses forcing the first n-1 bits to be a prefix of ones. On every prefix the two other falsifiers f=x_1(1+x_n) and g=x_n(1+x_{n-1}) sum to one: for interior prefixes first=1 and last=0, while odd parity forces x_n=1 at the empty prefix and zero at the full prefix. Therefore 1+f+g is a certified quadratic vanishing of the learned 2-CNF and the learner refutes. Initial generator span does not contain 1: each singleton forces its own coefficient to equal one, but {1,2,4} has two runs and hence two falsified implications.
