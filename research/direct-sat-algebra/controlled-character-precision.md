# Exact precision for an aligned control pencil

In the notation of rank-one-isolation.md let E be the span of the k columns u_i, of dimension R. Restrict data y to the affine flat D defined by Ay+b in E. If D is empty the count vanishes. Choose a basis of E. For each linear functional h on E intersect D with the equations q_i(y)=0 for those i with h(u_i)=1, where q_i(y)=v_i^T y+c_i. Call this flat D_h. Discard an empty flat or one on which h(Ay+b) varies. Otherwise let d_h be its dimension and e_h the constant sign exponent. The exact source count (including the controls) is
                  
    Z = 2^(k-R) sum_h (-1)^e_h 2^d_h.

Indeed the orthogonality test for the equation sum x_i u_i q_i(y)=Ay+b uses 2^R characters. The independent control sums contribute 2 each precisely on D_h. Summing the sign over affine data yields the stated power of two or zero. All computations are in original Boolean fibers, before any 2-adic reduction. Gate encodings must have unique extensions. This is unaffected by a singular pencil, including instances with a rank gap of one. The formula uses O(2^R poly(input)) deterministic bit operations and polynomial space. For normalized precision p only d_h<p need contribute; this saves arithmetic but alone does not skip enumerating h. If a nonzero normalized residue is found, pinning y coordinates one by one preserves at least one nonzero child residue at the same precision, because child counts add and k,R stay fixed. The surviving y has a solvable linear system for x. Exact computation also supports complete witness recovery; exact outputs fit n+k+1 bits.

A data-sensitive improvement in the dimension bound follows by conditioning on R independent equations of the dual parameter or simply enumerating flats in a trie: for a partial h, impose on D only forced q_i=0 (upper bound on D_h); also impose every q_i=0 except those whose h(u_i) is already forced zero (lower flat D_low). If D_low is nonempty of dimension at least p, every completion yields either a zero signed sum or an integer divisible by 2^p. Prune the whole node. Empty upper flat also allows pruning. Visits times polynomial per node is an actual certified clock. For example, on disjoint private y_i and u_i=e_i with A=b=0, q_i=y_i, every h contributes 2^(k-|h|); exploring h coordinates in order prunes once p zeros occur. With p fixed only O(k^p) nodes are visited, even with R=k. Exact integer count is 3^k; the normalized parity is one. This control illustrates non-enumeration of exponentially many dual characters; feasibility on this separable example is easy. No claim on the universal branching clock follows.

## Recognizable uniform bound

Suppose D contains a point at which every q_i vanishes. Write s for dim D minus the rank of the linear parts of all q_i on D. Find all indices whose linear part is a coloop (removing it drops this rank), then greedily select a maximal independent set of their u_i. Its size is B. Choose the corresponding h(u_i) as the first B coordinates of the dual recursion and extend to a basis of E*. Every prefix with j zero bits has a nonempty common lower flat of dimension at least s+j: simultaneous removal of j coloops drops rank by exactly j. If p<=s the normalized residue vanishes. Otherwise only assignments with fewer than p-s zeros among these B bits need attention. Deterministic precision-p time is bounded by a polynomial in input and p times

    2^(R-B) sum_{j=0}^{min(B,p-s-1)} binom(B,j).

The prefactor includes the at most B internal nodes per surviving prefix or its first pruned sibling; alternatively generate the surviving full dual strings directly. The linear part Ay+b on D and its signs may be entirely dense. When U=I and the q_i give an affine coordinate system on D of dimension k, B=k and s=0, so every dense A and b on this space admits polynomial fixed-precision counting; at precision one only the all-ones dual word contributes, giving odd parity. Setting all q_i=1 immediately supplies a primal witness in this square independent case. General pinning may change the structural parameter B, so the stated one-call bound applies to witness extraction only when it holds on its pinning path; the general 2^R bound remains valid along that path.
