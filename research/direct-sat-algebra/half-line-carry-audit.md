# Centered alternating half-line moments

Let d=2a, L=GF(2^a), H=2^a−1. On a selected odd punctured line λL*, let Δ denote the centered safe-shift indicator, represented by its Dickson determinant polynomial over the large field. Its moment at r is Σ_{t∈L*} Δ(λt)t^r. The exponent e of a retained monomial satisfies e≡−r mod H. Write e=eₗ+2^a eᵤ and let each digit uᵢ be the sum of the two corresponding half bits. For 0≤r<H use b=H−r. All retained pairs come from cyclic binary carries satisfying uᵢ=bᵢ+2cᵢ₊₁−cᵢ, except e=0 when r=0, which is added separately. Indeed eₗ+eᵤ ranges from 0 to 2H and the starting carry is (eₗ+eᵤ−b)/H. It is 0 or 1 except for that exceptional term. The digit recurrence then gives the unique carries, including for the all-two pattern at r=0.

For a fixed carry path, uᵢ=0 selects both coordinates for the complementary minor. For uᵢ=2 select neither and multiply by λ^(2^i+2^(i+a)). For uᵢ=1 select the single column sqrt(λ^(2^i))e_(i+a)+sqrt(λ^(2^(i+a)))e_i. Compute the determinant of the centered alternating Dickson form restricted to these columns. Odd orders contribute zero. In even order the Pfaffian is alternating multilinear and its square is the determinant. Its expansion sums the required complementary principal minors with their field weights, by squaring in characteristic two. The implementation independently compares the resulting field-valued sums to direct rank tests.

If r has k>0 marks, b has k zeros. At each zero a carry may rise but cannot drop; at each one it may drop but cannot rise. Specifying each zero's outgoing carry and one drop (or none) in each ensuing one-run gives at most 2^k ∏(ℓᵢ+1)≤(2a/k)^k cyclic paths; k=0 has two paths.

For the aggregate across all exponents obtained while pinning h bits, weight a zero of b by z. The transition matrix for unweighted *integer counts of paths* is T(z)=[[1+z,z],[1,1+z]]. Therefore the number at mark weight k is [z^k]tr T(z)^a=2[x^(2k)](1+x+x²)^a, obtained from the two eigenvalues 1+z±√z. For h<a the r=H word is excluded automatically. All prefixes/candidate exponents live in this support: products of up to h trace-affine coordinate indicators have exponents that are sums of at most h powers of two modulo H. Binary cyclic carrying cannot increase weight, and such a nonempty sum cannot be zero modulo H without at least a marks. Computing the successive indicator polynomials uses polynomial work per retained exponent by multiplying each prior sparse polynomial by one trace factor. Field traces here are those of L; coordinate duals must be formed with its own trace.

Take h=floor(3a/25). For x=1/5 the total number of paths across weights ≤h is O((1+x+x²)^a x^(−2h)), whose base-two exponent per a is ≤log₂(1.24)+0.24log₂5<0.88. The same bound with a constant factor applies to *open* paths: at positive z=x² the nonnegative matrix has a positive eigenvector with both entries bounded away from zero, so the sum of the entries of T(z)^a is O((1+x+x²)^a). Every recursive partial path can be extended by staying in its current state, so the number of visited prefixes of paths is at most a+1 times the number of complete open paths for those words. This accounts for branches that fail cyclic closure. Prefix storage, determinant evaluation and all h parity queries have only polynomial overhead beyond this bound. An odd prefix admits an odd child, so choose the zero child on nonzero parity and otherwise choose the one child. The suffix costs 2^(a−h) rank tests. Thus the overall deterministic time is O*(2^(11d/25)) for all even-degree **self-adjoint** maps, using the kernel-selected odd line or immediately returning the safe center. The degree-asymptotic prefactor hides floors and polynomial factors. The Pfaffian contraction here does not apply to arbitrary non-self-adjoint maps.
