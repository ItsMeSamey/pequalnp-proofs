# Coordinate recoding of individual forbidden flats

After eliminating the mark, each forbidden gated event is an affine flat with at most two normals. Discard inconsistent events, reject an all-space event, and retain every affine offset. For the layout only, merge equal normal planes. Any common coordinate basis must include every rank-one normal line and each nontrivial intersection of distinct planes. Check that these directions are independent, and complete each plane from its contained mandatory vectors to two independent generators. Extra directions cannot be shared between distinct planes, or they would be mandatory. Thus the union has fixed cardinality and spans the full joint space. Independence of this union is necessary and sufficient for a common coordinate basis; extend it to an ambient basis. Equivalently use a maximum-weight independent set on all normal directions, weighting a direction by the number of spaces containing it; equality with sum of dimensions is exact. The classical common-coordinate characterization is Polishchuk and Positselski, Quadratic Algebras Ch.1 Prop.7.1; weighted basis greedy is Edmonds, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01584082 .

For arbitrary chosen generators B containing one line of each rank-one space and two per plane, bits Bv form a code with h=|B|-rank B independent parity checks. Each original exclusion becomes one unary/binary 2-CNF clause with its own offset in its chosen generators. On any satisfiable 2-CNF, translate models by an anchor and find least flip-supports containing each feasible set of up to h indices. The syndrome indicator is a product of h affine parity indicators and has squarefree degree <=h. The closure-zeta evaluation argument in marked-bijunctive-closure.md means it is nonzero anywhere iff nonzero on one of these samples. Lift a positive sample by Gaussian elimination, or reject after exhaustive samples. Complexity poly times sum_{j<=min(h,|B|)} binom(|B|,j), with polynomial working space if streaming. Under a sample/plan cap return UNKNOWN. This uses bounded-degree majority vanishing, cf. Mastrolilli https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611975482.29 .

For the general 2-CNF and parity interface, Multicolored Clique reduces by excluding every same-part pair and nonedge with a negative binary clause, then imposing one odd parity per part. Exactly one vertex of each part is selected. Eliminating the h parity rows gives rank-two affine exclusions with supplied normal generators of defect at most h. Consequently fixed-parameter tractability in h would imply FPT=W[1] for this interface. This hardness does not extend by this proof to marked complementarity.
