# Affine binomial feasibility over a finite field

Suppose the original equations in n variables equate two coefficient-weighted monomials with nonnegative binary-encoded exponents. Zero coefficients are allowed. The field has known cardinality q, with arithmetic polynomial-time in log q. A polynomial-size supplied change of coordinates can be used if independently verified to preserve the equations and domain.

For each nonzero monomial its nonzero condition is the conjunction of the nonzero statuses of its variables. An equality of two nonzero-coefficient terms gives two Horn implications between their support sets. Equality with a zero coefficient forbids the support of the remaining term. Forward chain all implications starting at the empty set, taking an empty premise as true. Let S be the resulting least closed support. If a forbidden premise is included, no solution exists. For every solution, its nonzero support contains S. Setting all coordinates outside S to zero preserves both terms of every equation active on S, and forces both sides of every other equality to zero. Thus existence is equivalent to existence with support exactly S.

On this torus write N=q−1, m for the number of active equations and A for their exponent-difference rows restricted to S. The constants ratio vector c is evaluated by field arithmetic. Although a hypothetical generator represents c by discrete logarithms y in (Z/N)^m, the algorithm never computes them. Form the column lattice of [A | NI_m], compute its square Hermite basis H, and form the integral T=NH^{-1}. Integrality follows since NI_m belongs to the lattice. The hypothetical y belongs to this lattice iff Ty=0 mod N, tested by raising the actual constants to the corresponding entries in each row of T and multiplying. For a feasible instance the number on this support is N^|S| det(H)/N^m by kernel-image counting. Empty matrix cases have their natural unit products. Exponents of powers in the field are reduced mod N before evaluation. Using a polynomial-time HNF algorithm gives polynomial bit cost, including log(N^m). Thus decision takes deterministic polynomial time in binary input size. The concrete implementation uses SymPy's modular HNF with the known determinant multiple N^m. No generator, field factorization, or root extraction is needed for the decision.

Example boundary: over GF(2) equations x_i x_j=0 have a solution for every graph; the total count over all supports is its number of independent sets. The solver returns the least-support count, which decides existence. A general root on the least support is a separate modular root extraction task. The chart must genuinely have binomial equations. Allowing unrestricted sums includes standard SAT encodings.

Prior work on monomial systems over finite fields separates zero patterns from exponent systems over Z/(q−1); see [Colón-Reyes et al., Monomial Dynamical Systems over Finite Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605439). The Horn closure and lattice membership argument above is explicit for simultaneous affine binomial feasibility with arbitrary nonzero coefficients. No priority claim is made.
