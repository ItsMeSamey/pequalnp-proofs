# Low-precision Taylor repair on aligned XOR-CNF banks

Take a bounded-occurrence bounded-width XOR system, compile each constraint separately into the full CNF excluding its wrong parity assignments, and form the source clause/equality network. Assume a supplied bank of aligned identical copies as in native-residue-blocks.md. At a translated interpolation node each row measurement r_i(X) reduces modulo two to a shifted Boolean column bit or zero. Write z_i for the actual 0/1 baseline and δ_i=r_i−z_i. Its coefficients are even. The multilinear Taylor expansion of the row-factor product R on its distinct ports sums (∂_D R)(z)∏_{i∈D}δ_i over all D. Modulo 2^k retain |D|<k only. The mixed derivative equals a signed sum over 2^{|D|} Boolean substitutions on those row ports. Each δ_i uses at most L+1 column-bit terms; after expansion every extra bit product is an indicator of 1-pinnings. Column equality tables remain intact throughout.

In each low-order summand all unreplaced inputs follow the baseline matching. Alignment means each live replica reads its corresponding shifted column replica uniformly; rows beyond the boundary read constants unless explicitly replaced by constants. A damaged clause can spoil its containing complete XOR block. A damaged copy-tree gate can disconnect the occurrences of its original variable; mark all blocks incident to each such variable. By bounded occurrence only O(|D|) bounded-width blocks are marked. Enumerate actual occurrence bits of these blocks; their remaining nonlinear clause factors have fixed values, while all pin and copy equations, including touched copy gates with replaced constants, are affine. Untouched parity blocks can be merged into affine equations using their intact copy trees. Evaluate boundary rows on their fixed inputs. Count the resulting Boolean linear systems by Gaussian elimination, including multiplicities of unconstrained column bits. Add all surviving coefficients in the source ring.

This yields (ML)^{O(k)} polynomial-bit-overhead exact computation modulo 2^k for the stated supplied layouts. It need not be polynomial at the large root reconstruction precision, even when Gaussian elimination solves the original target formula efficiently.
