# Exact sparse inequation decisions at bounded character-image index

On (F*)^n of order N per coordinate, impose the feasible equations x^A=c. Block i is a single sparse inequation. Drop zero coefficients, combining equal exponents mod N, and divide its monomials by one anchor monomial. Its truth value then depends only on the difference-character tuple x^B_i. Let J be the image of all tuples on the homogeneous kernel x^A=1 and let P be the product of their marginal images. The computable index D=|P|/|J| is the budget parameter. For D exceeding the supplied limit the implementation returns UNKNOWN; it does not need a discrete logarithm or a representative of the base coset.

Put R=[A;B_1;…;B_s], with h rows. The column HNF H of [R|NI] spans the integral exponent-image lattice. Membership of a tuple, in temporary logarithmic coordinates y, is equivalent to Ty=0 mod N, where T=NH⁻¹ is integral. Translating back to field elements makes each of the h checks a monomial equality. The block i signature consists of these h monomials restricted to its B_i columns, evaluated at a local x satisfying the base. The product of all block signatures must equal the inverse contribution of c in the A columns. Keeping c fixed on the base makes this criterion both necessary and sufficient, because H spans exactly the image lattice.

On P the signature product homomorphism has kernel J. Indeed the lattice test on the tuple (1,p) is precisely membership in J. Therefore its image has order D. Independent legal base-coset block choices are translates of their homogeneous marginals, so the list of all signatures for one block and the product of any prefix are each contained in a single translate of this same order-D subgroup. This proves the cap for both local lists and intermediate DP states without presupposing a common point for any arbitrary partial selection.

To enumerate a local signature, compute its row L as the linear combination of B_i rows specified by each row of T in turn. Condition on previous local signature pins. Append L to the already fixed rows [A;previous pins], repeat the HNF test, and collect the resulting conditions z^s=b_s on its unknown value. Put g=gcd(N, all s). Every occupied prefix has at least one extension, so the solutions form a coset of the g-th roots of unity in F*. A Bezout combination of s/g equal to one mod N/g gives d=product b_s^u_s and all solutions are exactly z^g=d. Its g roots are found by deterministic polynomial root splitting over the binary field. There are at most D possible z at any occupied prefix. This consistency argument permits reducing the Bezout coefficients modulo N/g even on nonidentity cosets.

At a completed signature, test the single local inequation by grouping terms modulo equality of their restricted characters on that pinned torus; use the forced ratio, including its scalar coset value, when combining coefficients. Distinct kernel characters are linearly independent. The block is allowed precisely when some combined coefficient survives. A set-valued DP multiplies one allowed signature per block; finding the prescribed product proves simultaneous feasibility, since the single common point has the selected difference tuples, and each inequation depends only on its tuple. More explicitly, for each allowed signature choose a passing local difference tuple; compatibility of their product depends solely on the signatures, so the lattice criterion gives a common point for those passing tuples. Work is polynomial in input length, log |F| and numerical D, including at most D² candidate products per block. This gives decisions, without reconstructing a common point or counting its multiplicity.
