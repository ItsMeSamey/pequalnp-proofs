# K-linear drift makes star networks NP-complete

Fix K=GF(2^m) with q≥4 fixed. Every output is a distinct edge product x_i x_j plus an arbitrary affine K-linear form on all vertices. Even if the supplied edge graph is a disjoint union of stars of maximum degree q without isolates, zero-target feasibility is NP-complete.

Forbid x=a by giving x a fresh leaf c and the equation xc+ac+1=0. A leaf value exists exactly when x≠a. Thus q−2 leaves impose x∈{0,1}. To enforce L=0 introduce a fresh b with one leaf forbidding each nonzero a; b must vanish. A final fresh leaf d and equation bd+L=0 enforce the desired affine condition. Boolean AND w=xy uses two fresh operand copies x',y', tied to the master wires by these affine gadgets, and an isolated edge with equation x'y'+w=0. NOT gates and output requirements are affine. Intermediate master wires may receive one extra leaf forbidding 2 to ensure they occur in the polar graph. Induction in the Boolean circuit recovers a satisfying input from every field solution. Any satisfying assignment extends by evaluation and leaf reciprocals. Output size is O(q) per gate and witnesses check in polynomial time.

Every edge has an independent K-output and the full nondegenerate multiplication pairing, so the polar output span is full and its common input radical zero. The graph has treewidth one, even with bounded connected-component sizes for fixed K. Arbitrary K-linear drift therefore already includes general SAT; checking it arises from a common translation is essential. This is a decision reduction, irrespective of free leaf multiplicities. star_drift_sat.py verifies 80 constructive Boolean lifts over GF4/GF8, both SAT and UNSAT, all zero-forcing center values and graph restrictions.

For growing m, allowing arbitrary binary-linear drift gives a uniformly polynomial smaller gadget: pick δ of absolute trace one. The single row b²+bc+δc²=0 forces b=c=0 by anisotropy. A further edge bx+x²+x=0 forces a master wire x to be Boolean and incident. Each remaining affine L=0 uses its own norm pair and edge bd+L. The graph comprises only three-vertex paths and isolated edges. Squaring is additive over GF2; this stronger size bound does not treat squaring as K-linear when m>1.

Uniform K-affine alternative with only four-vertex paths and isolated edges: enforce L=0 by a private P4 with rows uw=1, ub=0, bd+L=0. The first forces u≠0 and the second b=0. Conversely choose u=w=1 and b=d=0. Booleanize a master x with a new mate y and edge xy+x=0, imposing y=x by a separate P4 affine gadget. Then x²+x=0, without any squaring term in the K-linear drift. Give every master its own such edge, use fresh copied operands at each AND edge, and use P4s for the copy ties, NOT and final pins. Every polar edge is distinct and all vertices are incident. The maximum graph degree is two with at most four vertices per component, independent of q. This variant is uniformly polynomial for all supplied binary fields, including GF2. It uses paths of length three whereas the fixed-field version above uses literal stars.
