# Finding an invertible scalar shift of a binary-linear field map

Write A(y)=Σ a_j y^(2^j) on K=GF(2^m), N=|K|−1. A slope s is bad precisely when A(y)/y=s for some nonzero y. The corresponding fiber size is 2^dimension−1, an odd integer, since it is the nonzero kernel of A+M_s. The total number of nonzero y is odd, so the number of good slopes g is odd and positive. For each positive t their field-valued power sum is the sum over all field elements plus Σ_(y≠0)(A(y)/y)^t. At t=0 it is one, counting zero as an element.

For positive t reduce first to 1+((t−1) mod N). Mark each set bit k. Assigning k to coefficient j contributes a_j^(2^k), and exponent destination d=(j+k) mod m. The sum over y≠0 retains exactly assignments with Σ n_d 2^d≡t mod N, where n_d counts the assigned bits. Let r=t mod N. Process destinations in order, choosing a subset of the remaining marks and advancing c_next=(c+n_d−bit_d(r))/2 when this is a nonnegative integer. Require final carry equal initial carry and all marks assigned. Each valid assignment has exactly one starting carry, (Σ n_d 2^d−r)/N, between zero and the number of marks. Its intermediate carries are nonnegative integers by the same congruence on the processed prefix. Accumulating products by subset and carry computes the moment with O(poly(m) 3^popcount(t)) field operations. For numerical t≤T, this is polynomial in m and T. The full-field term is one exactly for positive multiples of N.

Double a candidate bound b and compute moments 0 through 2b−1. At b≥g, the leading g-by-g Hankel matrix is V Vᵀ for the square Vandermonde V on all distinct good slopes, hence invertible in characteristic two; all larger leading matrices have rank ≤g. The largest nonsingular leading matrix therefore gives g. Solve its moment recurrence with monic leading coefficient to reconstruct the locator polynomial whose roots are the good slopes. Factor its field-root part by deterministic trace splitting. At an undersized bound the recurrence may be spurious, so every candidate is verified by binary elimination on A+M_s. If doubling brings 2b−1 to N before success, enumerate slopes directly; then q=O(g) since the previous unsuccessful bound was below g (or q=2). This finds one safe slope in time polynomial in m and g. It neither needs to know g nor claims a complete good-slope list on early success.
