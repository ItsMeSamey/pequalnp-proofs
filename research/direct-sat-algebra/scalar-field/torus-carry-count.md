# Exact binary-field point counts by finite-ring traces

Let f have b variables over K=GF(q), q=2^m, with each variable degree ≤D. Set M=bm+1. In the ring R=(Z/2^M)[z]/g(z), g is the monic integer lift of the field modulus. R is free of rank m, and an element reducing to a nonzero field element is a unit by lifting its inverse modulo the nilpotent ideal 2R. Each member of K* has a unique lift solving x^(q−1)=1: the derivative is a unit so each successive binary precision step has a unique correction. Their group H is cyclic and maps bijectively to K*. The sum of x^j on H is q−1 if q−1 divides j and zero otherwise: in the latter case multiply by a member t for which t^j−1 is a unit. No lifts or generators need be enumerated.

For i=0,...,m−1 form f_i by taking the 2^i-power of the coefficients in K and lifting them coefficientwise to R. For x∈H^b, T=product_i f_i(x^(2^i)) reduces to f(x)^(q−1), hence zero or one. Define P(T)=sum_{j=M}^{2M−1} binom(2M−1,j) T^j(1−T)^(2M−1−j). If T reduces to zero all terms vanish; if it reduces to one all omitted terms of the complete binomial expansion vanish, giving one. P is the exact unit indicator modulo 2^M. Actual lifted Frobenius need not be computed.

Expand P in ordinary powers. For the kth moment let g_i=f_i^k. Torus summation retains exactly the terms with sum_i 2^i e_i coordinatewise divisible by q−1, where e_i is an exponent in g_i. Their weight is multiplied by (q−1)^b. Compute the retained coefficient sum as a matrix trace: transition r→s at stage i has weight coeff(g_i,2s−r), with all coordinates of r,s between zero and kD. A closed walk gives sum_i 2^i e_i=(q−1)r₀ by telescoping. Conversely a retained sequence forces this r₀, the weighted average of the e_i. Each successive r_j=(r₀+sum_{i<j}2^i e_i)/2^j is an integer because its numerator is congruent to q r₀ modulo 2^j. All carries stay in range by averaging recurrence. Thus each term appears once. Moment zero is the torus size. Subtract the sum of P from the size to count zeros.

Partition affine space by the exact coordinates set to zero. Use a torus for the others; the empty torus has one point. The disjoint sum as an embedded integer modulo 2^M uniquely determines the answer (≤q^b<2^M). The algorithm uses 2^b strata, O(M) moments and (kD+1)^b states per matrix. Even dense matrix multiplication and schoolbook binary quotient-ring operations have polynomial bit cost in m,D when b is fixed. This is an asymptotic bound, not a practical speed comparison.

For the one-variable Laurent sign sum, replace T by S=sum_i f_i(x^(2^i)), which reduces to Tr(f(x)). The sign equals 1−2P(S) modulo 2^M. For each S^j also track prefix allocation k; choosing local power l adds binom(k+l,l), yielding the correct multinomial exactly. Signed exponent carries range from j min(0,minExp) to j max(0,maxExp); only these carries are cyclic, and the allocation runs from zero to j. At M=m+2 exact signed recovery follows since the magnitude is ≤q−1.

Finite ring trace formulas and the same coefficient indexing have prior art. Harvey's trace formula and the explicit torus form use an indicator on lifted points and matrices indexed by p times the output state minus the input state. The elementary proof here specializes to binary characteristic and one extension-field point count. [Harvey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.3439).
