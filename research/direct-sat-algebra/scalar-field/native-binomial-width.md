# Two tests on native contraction controls

In the legal unit-clause packing at t=ζ=1 from native-laurent-support.md, the nonzero first-bit assignment support is a binary linear subspace of size S=2^b with b=M/(2L) dividing M. A binary-linear change of coordinates sends it to the unique subfield GF(2^b)⊂K=GF(2^M). On K* its exact membership indicator has (q−1)/(S−1) multiplicative characters: it is the geometric sum of x^(j(S−1)) for 0≤j<(q−1)/(S−1). The index is odd, so the sum is one on the subgroup and zero off it. These distinct characters are independent. More generally any function with precisely this nonzero support needs at least that many characters by the translate-support argument. The same support is defined on the full affine line by the single binomial equation x^S=x. Thus moving the constraint into the binomial base removes an exponential root-width cost for this promised control. This describes which assignment weights are odd, not their sum at full precision. native_binomial_width.py exhausts small field/subfield instances of this coordinate chart.

For the actual modular scalar construction in modular-trace-scalar-hardness.md, each nonconstant trace output is y_i=a_i+Tr(α_i s) on one field input, α_i≠0. On s≠0, either choice of required bit makes its indicator a sparse polynomial y_i or 1+y_i. For M≥2 its characters include exponents 1 and 2 with nonzero coefficients, so relative exponent differences generate the full cyclic group of order N=2^M−1. For h nonconstant factors the individual normalized character tuples each have N possible values and all h together still have exactly N, giving D=N^(h−1). The full-torus bounded-overlap algorithm therefore charges exponentially many states already for two nonconstant trace masks. Simultaneous pinned trace bits nevertheless reduce to binary Gaussian elimination. The source's unsigned modular weighted sum of all bit patterns can decide ExactCover and is not identical to existence under prescribed trace masks. A combined method must account for aggregation rather than infer its complexity from either support count or trace-mask consistency.
