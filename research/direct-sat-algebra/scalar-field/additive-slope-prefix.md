# Uniform safe-slope search via Dickson minors and prefix parity

Write F=GF(2^m), N=2^m−1 and A(x)=Σ_j a_j x^(2^j). The Dickson matrix D has entry D_ij=a_((j−i) mod m)^(2^i). It takes the Frobenius column v(x)=(x,x²,…,x^(2^(m−1))) to v(A(x)). For a binary basis of F, these columns form an invertible Moore matrix M: a nonzero row in its left kernel would be a nonzero linearized polynomial of degree at most 2^(m−1) vanishing on the entire field. Thus D M=M[A], where [A] is the binary coordinate matrix. Multiplication by s similarly corresponds to diag(s,s²,…). Consequently det(D+diag(v(s))) equals the binary determinant of A+M_s, the zero/one indicator of a safe slope. This uses the classical Dickson matrix correspondence; see Baofeng Wu and Zhuojun Liu, “Linearized polynomials over finite fields revisited,” Finite Fields and Their Applications (2013).

Expanding the diagonal gives the indicator polynomial Σ_J det(D[J,J]) s^(N−Σ_(j∈J)2^j), including the empty minor one. For t>0, summing s^t times this expression over the field retains precisely powers divisible by N. For 0<r=t mod N<N its value is the principal minor on the set bits of r. For a positive multiple of N it is det(D)+1. For t=0 the constant term sums to zero and the leading term contributes one, so the entire safe set has odd size. One moment requires O(m³) field operations after constructing D. The implementation cross-checks these moments against the separate cyclic-carry implementation.

The first h polynomial-basis coordinates of s can be fixed with h binary trace indicators. Each is an affine constant plus a sum of at most m terms with exponents powers of two. A product of at most h such exponents' monomials gives an exponent that is a sum of at most h powers of two. For a positive exponent reduce by 1+((e−1) mod N). Each cyclic binary carry merges two equal marks into one on the next position modulo m and therefore terminates. The final nonempty distinct marks encode exactly this reduced positive exponent (the complete set encodes N). Consequently at most Σ_(j=0)^h binom(m,j) monomials occur in each prefix indicator.

Compute the parity of the child with next bit zero by summing its coefficients times the Dickson moments. If even, take the other child, whose parity is odd since its parent was odd. Finally exhaustively test the 2^(m−h) completions of the odd prefix by binary elimination. With h=floor(11m/50), the prefix support is at most 2^(H₂(0.22)m), where H₂(0.22)<0.761. Thus the total worst-case time is O*(2^(0.78m)) in the binary input dimension; prefix count and storage bounds do not require any promise on the number of safe slopes. This algorithm finds one verified slope.
