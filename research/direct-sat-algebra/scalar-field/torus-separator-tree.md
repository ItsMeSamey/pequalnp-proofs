# Character separators on a supplied factor tree

For each subtree S with complement R let J_S,J_R be the groups generated by their B rows on ker A. The cut index D_S=|J_S∩J_R|=|I_S||I_R|/|I| follows by counting the group sum. Compute it from HNF indices. The two-block construction in torus-overlap.md computes signature exponent rows L_S involving B_S alone. On globally feasible x the signature x^(L_S) has exactly D_S possible values and is determined by B_R(x) and the fixed A constants. For any two feasible points their halves can be pasted precisely when their S signatures agree, by applying the joint HNF membership test to the pasted tuple.

Enumerate all cut states as in torus-overlap.md. At leaves test the local inequation on each state. For parent S with children U,V retain state s if two accepting child states u,v coexist with s on the full source torus. Coexistence is a binomial feasibility query with three lists of pins. Start at such a point and paste into it an accepted U tuple realizing u, keeping every B coordinate outside U. Then paste the accepted V tuple with state v. The first paste preserved the entire V tuple, and the second preserves the accepted U tuple. Both preserve the outside of S, which determines s. Necessity follows by restriction of a satisfying point. The root has a single state.

Deterministic bit cost is polynomial in sparse input length, supplied tree size and max_S D_S. At most the cube of that state bound is tested per join. The torus support is fixed. For arbitrarily many triangles joined at articulation vertices, the torus 3-color encoding gives exponential global D. Arranging each triangle as a subtree gives all cut widths at most three: the cycle relations have disjoint edge supports and each displayed cut splits at most one relation.

A single global T improves the join. Set L_S=sum_{i in S} L_i using its original factor columns. The signature map on independently feasible inside and outside tuples still tests precisely the two-block image. Its restriction to inside directions has image order D_S, since for every inside tuple some compatible outside tuple exists. Equivalently the quotient of I_S directions by the kernel of signature equals the quotient by inside changes attainable with unchanged outside. Enumerate P_S, the possible signatures on S. For accepted child signatures a,b accept exactly when a·b belongs to P_S. Choose x0 realizing this parent signature; concatenate its outside tuples with the accepted child tuples. The global T condition is unchanged and ensures this tuple lifts. This needs only D_U D_V pair products with membership lookup per join.

The group state-space interpretation and exact tree inference are established graph-realization techniques. See [Forney and Gluesing-Luerssen, Codes on Graphs: Fundamentals](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6264) and [Kashyap, On Minimal Tree Realizations of Linear Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1383). Here augmented Hermite matrices construct those states without discrete logarithms; polynomial root splitting and sparse nonvanishing test generate and filter them without scanning the ambient field.
