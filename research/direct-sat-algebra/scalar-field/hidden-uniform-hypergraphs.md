# Untwisted hidden uniform hyperedge products

Fix d≥2. Coordinates and output mixing are arbitrary binary affine maps. In hidden coordinates each vertex carries one element of K=GF(2^m), q>d, and each distinct d-edge outputs the product of its d incident vertices. Assume the hypergraph connected with no isolated vertices. The structural ANF is given explicitly through degree d. The following takes n^O(d) binary operations times polynomial field-arithmetic overhead, with n charging the input dimensions. One extra raw Boolean predicate of fixed bounded degree has a separately charged polynomial-sized expansion.

Take the dth binary polarization. On one input from each of d distinct hidden vertices forming an edge, the corresponding field output is their product. All input patterns missing an edge are zero. In a centroid relation isolate an input block from i to k by evaluating an edge containing k with its k slot replaced by i. If i is another vertex of that edge, the initial tuple has a repeated vertex and the centroid acts on zero. If i is outside the edge, transfer the action to any other slot: the unchanged i precludes that edge's contribution. Thus off-diagonal maps vanish. Each edge's diagonal actions identify through field multiplication, so connectedness gives one scalar field K. Compute the scalar action and common field presentation as for graphs.

Extract the homogeneous ordinary K-form of degree d by interpolation on λ↦F(λx) at d+1 field scalars, valid also for translated inputs. In field coordinates interpolate its individual coefficients with polynomial work for fixed d. Compute the K-linear maps S admitting T with DQ_x(Sx)=TQ(x) as a formal polynomial identity. For i≠k, an entry S_ki is allowed iff every edge containing k remains an edge on replacing k by i, with no repeated variable. A forbidden term from one edge has a unique missing index k and added index i. Thus distinct forbidden entries cannot cancel. The allowed replacement relation is transitive: first replace by the intermediate vertex and then by the final vertex. Diagonal entries are unrestricted.

The resulting input algebra is the preorder incidence algebra. Its trace radical, quotient-center idempotents and orthogonal lifts recover twin-class frames as in untwisted-graph-refinement.md. Applying simultaneous allowed replacements to distinct vertices in an edge always yields another edge, as can be seen by making the replacements successively. No intermediate replacement can overwrite an unprocessed vertex because it would create a repeated index. All units preserve the homogeneous edge-monomial span. After recovering K frames and independent edge output images, inspect each translated edge polynomial in its own output coordinate. The coefficient of the monomial on E minus v equals the vertex shift at v. Reconcile across incidences and verify all raw coefficients, including the output constant. Every accepted chart can now be used for deterministic decision by affine binomials plus one sparse inequation. This argument does not give deterministic general witness recovery on hyperedges; see uniform-hyperedge-roots.md.

The derivation condition on squarefree monomial ideals specializes [Brumatti and Simis](https://doi.org/10.2307/2161115). Independent symbolic checks tested the replacement and trace-radical arguments on 997 connected labeled three- and four-uniform hypergraphs through five vertices, including 330 with strict comparabilities and 91 with nontrivial twin classes.
