# Hidden product with affine slack and a quartic section

For Q on 3m binary inputs, solve for its common polar radical R and require dimension m and a bijective centered map R→Y. On a complementary 2m-space, use the verified field-valued alternating polar and its field Gram rank two. Normalize coordinates so the cross term is xy. Then Q minus xy is binary affine, and its restriction to R is bijective. This makes it the last coordinate z of an invertible affine chart, with output field coordinates Q=xy+z. The implementation verifies the original ANF by interpolation at inputs of weight at most two.

Given raw affine pins, select in supplied order m rows independent on the slack. This determines z as a binary affine function of x,y. Convert each additive coordinate to its Frobenius polynomial. For the degree-four and higher terms, inverse Frobenius on the coefficients identifies a candidate common null direction. In coordinates t,s on the resulting K-plane test the full identity Q-target=(g t+b)s+L(t)+c, where g is nonzero and L is additive. A failed fit or absent pivot chart yields UNKNOWN. Thus the method applies to the verified restriction; different pivot choices can expose different restrictions.

Translate t to T=t+b/g. On T=0 the equation either excludes the whole line or admits it; all raw side constraints and the negation of any one component of a raw affine equality can be tested there by Gaussian elimination. On T≠0 there is one value of s, namely (L(T)+c')/(gT). Every residual raw binary pin becomes a trace Laurent polynomial in T, with at most O(m²) terms obtained by explicit Frobenius expansion. Normalize exponents modulo 2^m−1. Multiply the satisfaction indicators of k independent residual pins and, in turn, each component asserting failure of the designated raw affine equality. A nonzero reduced polynomial is a nonzero function on K*, because distinct multiplicative characters are linearly independent. This gives deterministic decision with m^O(k) times polynomial bit complexity and no field scan. A term-width cap can return UNKNOWN. No general witness search is asserted.

In the dense binary affine controls for GF(8) and GF(32), m raw pins secretly impose z=x⁴. In natural coordinates the zero target has the exceptional line x=0 and the graph y=x³ for x≠0.
