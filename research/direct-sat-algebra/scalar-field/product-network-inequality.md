# One raw quadratic bit on a recovered field-product fiber

For a recovered chart, let n be its binary input dimension and m the common field degree. Expand the target minus the output translation in the independent packed edge bases. A residual outside their span gives immediate infeasibility. Otherwise every edge imposes an affine binomial z_i z_j^(2^t)=b_e, including b_e=0. These are full-field equations; keeping zero coordinates is essential.

Transport the designated raw predicate into binary frame coordinates by evaluating at the translated origin, unit vectors and pairs of unit vectors. The interpolated squarefree ANF is 1+q+required, hence nonzero exactly when the requested bit holds. For basis bit j of a field symbol z, solve the binary trace-pairing system for dual element d_j and expand Tr(d_j z)=Σ_h d_j^(2^h) z^(2^h). Replacing each frame bit by its trace emits at most 1+nm+binom(n,2)m² candidate monomials before merging, with exponent bit lengths O(m). A positive exponent k is reduced to 1+((k−1) mod (2^m−1)); ordinary residue reduction to zero would corrupt the zero input. One sparse affine-binomial inequation decision then handles all edge target values, without computing discrete logarithms or enumerating field supports. Exactly one separately supplied raw Boolean quadratic bit is compiled; an unrestricted conjunction of such bits would require an additional argument.

The tests compare translated dense frames pointwise over F4 and F8, including repeated-vertex and twisted edges, zero target values and an ambient target outside the span. Ten full decisions are also replayed starting from a raw ANF for a densely recoded F4 five-cycle. The recognizer receives no chart; each recovered result with the extra scalar requirement agrees with direct Boolean enumeration.
