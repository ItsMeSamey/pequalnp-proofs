# Shared external signals in the native component graph

Use native-pseudoforest-count.md with only the original mark eliminated. Let D be that affine slice. In component C let B_C be its normal bits and I_C its retained unary/binary original row indices. For homogeneous parts set F_C=span(B_C), X_C=span(x_i:i in I_C). Every retained row with a nonconstant original coordinate includes that coordinate in its normal span. Thus X_C subset F_C and rank X_C=|I_C|-epsilon_C, with epsilon_C one iff I_C contains all the mark support. At most one component is exceptional. Connectivity gives |B_C|<=|I_C|+1. Consequently g_C=dim F_C-rank X_C<=1+epsilon_C, and |B_C|-rank X_C<=2.

Extend a local basis of X_C to F_C with g_C extra normal forms. Lift each extra to an affine function of all physical x bits; store its offset. Coefficients on its own coordinates I_C can be arbitrarily changed as long as the difference is retained as a local affine correction. Erase just these entries from the matrix of extra forms. Each physical column has at most two erased entries. If a completion has rank s, take a common row basis G and represent every extra as a combination of shared signals Gx plus its known own-coordinate correction.

Enumerate b=Gx. For each local normal word y_C impose intrinsic membership in the affine image B_C(D), then its g_C extra values as functions of b and the reconstructed own x_I. Every x_i for i in I_C is a known affine function of y_C. The combined number of local affine tests is |B_C|-dim F_C+g_C<=2. Impose global beta*x=1 and Gx=b by s+1 Walsh bits. Their character signs become unary weights and constant signs on local normal bits; physical bits not in any I_C are independent summands with unary signs. Perform local parity filtering (at most four characters) and native bounded-cycle exact tree DP on each component; multiply local totals and uncovered-bit terms. Normalize every local Walsh sum by its number of characters, normalize the global sum, and add over the guesses b.

This counts each physical solution once. The global checks put reconstructed x in D. Intrinsic local membership ensures all local identities hold. The local words agree with B_C(x) on X_C by decoding and on complementary extra forms by their constraints, hence on F_C. Conversely each actual x fixes b and every normal word uniquely. No free-lift multiplier belongs in this representation, which explicitly retains any uncovered physical bits. Recover a witness by restricting b, normal bits and uncovered bits with unary pins and positive exact counts, then decoding own x_I. All parameters stay fixed. Supplied certificate clock 4^s poly(k), polynomial space, polynomial bit lengths.

For threshold s enumerate at most s pivot physical columns and at most two erased coefficients in each pivot column. Once these columns are filled, test other columns independently for membership in their span on observed rows and fill them with linear solutions. Every rank-s completion has such basis columns, so this test costs poly times sum_{j<=s}binom(k,j)4^j and certifies a completion if one exists for the fixed lifts. Other extensions off D differ by multiples of beta (rank at most one over the full matrix), so keeping one lift per row loses at most one rank against allowing those further choices.
