# Exact physical orbit solutions with decomposable Boolean and affine operations

For a family of four-color orbit/subcube inputs on D physical coordinates, any decomposable negation-normal form (DNNF) describing exactly all attainable pinned orbit words requires 2^Ω(√D) nodes, even with arbitrary affine F2-relation leaves and existential extra coordinates. The bound covers all original coordinate orders, adaptive read-once branching and arbitrary decomposable AND/OR circuit topology. Count nodes plus wires and leaf descriptions for explicit storage.

**Projection and compilation lemma.** For any affine-leaf DNNF C whose existential projection on n physical bits is a 2CNF relation F, there is an ordinary DNNF for F of size O((n+1)|C|). Push forgetting down through OR and variable-disjoint AND, Gaussian-project each leaf. For an inconsistent affine leaf keep false. Otherwise write its bits as a_i+v_i·z with unconstrained z. Enlarge its relation by retaining all fixed bits and grouping equal nonzero coefficient vectors. Each group may choose either consistent orientation, independently of other groups. This gives exactly the closure under unary and binary projections since two distinct nonzero forms over F2 have full joint image. Each group has a linear-size OR of two literal conjunctions, and groups combine by decomposable AND. Gaussian elimination and grouping take polynomial time in the original explicit representation. For any assignment accepted after enlargement, unfold an accepting trace, choosing one satisfied input at each OR and both at AND. Nonconstant leaf supports in this trace are disjoint. If the assignment violates a source clause, restore its at most two offending bits simultaneously inside each touched leaf, preserving the prescribed values, and independently select any old model for every other leaf. Bits absent from the trace remain free. This yields an assignment accepted before enlargement violating the same clause. The reverse implication follows by enlargement. Empty clauses and constant leaves cause no exception.

**Graph and actual coordinates.** Use the bounded-degree expander graph vertex-cover CNFs of Bova, Capelli, Mengel and Slivovsky; literal complementation gives independent-set indicators with the same DNNF size. For an n-vertex such G construct a graph J. Use a palette clique on four vertices named 0,1,2,3. Enforce each list by adjacency to the palette vertices outside the list. For every v add L_v with list {0,1}, R_v with list {0,2} and two witnesses with lists {0,2} and {0,1}, each adjacent to L_v and R_v. A witness is feasible exactly when the two neighbor colors do not exhaust its two-element list. Consequently the allowed pairs are exactly (0,0) and (1,2), both with joint witnesses. For each arbitrarily oriented edge u→v of G add L_u R_v. This forbids precisely the state in which both endpoints select 0. A 4-coloring of J thus projects to an independent set and every independent set extends (choose any palette naming and fill the separate witnesses). No uniqueness of auxiliary bits is necessary.

Feed J to the two-explicit-permutation orbit construction of four-color-orbit-boundary.md. Pad J to odd order m with a single isolated vertex when required. D=4 binom(m,2)=Θ(n²). Each vertex pair has exactly one marked color-difference site. Pin its zero-difference coordinate to zero on every J edge. Observe for each v precisely the physical site (palette0,L_v,zero difference). These n selected sites give EXACTLY the independent-set projections above, for any actual global palette permutation. To lift any independent set choose physical colors with palette0=00, realize both states and the local witnesses, and add colors via the at most 2m available conjugate generators. The resulting two-permutation group word and all zero pins replay by construction. Expander CNFs need ordinary DNNFs of size 2^Ω(n); applying the lemma to these n selected sites proves the claim. Even if existential extra variable names outnumber n, their projected leaf outputs have at most n variables. Normalizing literal complements does not affect size.

**Scope.** The complete four-color orbit feasibility problem is NP-complete. These constructed instances have the empty independent-set witness, and feasibility on this particular family is immediate. The lower bound is on compiling all exact pinned physical solutions, or an obligatory interface retaining their entire indicated projection. It does not apply to a decision algorithm that discards feasible alternatives, a temporary cut without that obligation, an arbitrary nonlinear decoding, or decomposability defined after arbitrary dense affine coordinate changes. In particular, after a dense change of variables the forbidden two-site pattern need not be local; the pair-restoration lemma no longer applies. No all-input decision lower bound follows.
