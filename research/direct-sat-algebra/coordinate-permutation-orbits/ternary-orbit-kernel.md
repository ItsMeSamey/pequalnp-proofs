# Three-position blocks with noncommuting conjugates

Let R,F act on D coordinate positions, F involutive and R fixing p. Recognize the conjugate subgroup's blocks by the graph of R-orbits of F's transposed pairs as in the size-two theorem. For blocks of at most three the problem is NP-complete. For a simple graph pad to m≥2 vertices with 3 not dividing m. On each unordered pair use the three indicators of the ternary color difference x_u−x_v, oriented by the numerical order u<v. Use all-zero colors at the start. R rotates the vertices, negating a difference label if the rotation reverses the pair order. F is the color transformation x↦−x+e_0; it induces on each pair the involution a↦−a+[u=0]−[v=0]. Its conjugates act on the colors by −x+e_i. Their products translate by e_i−e_j, spanning the sum-zero vectors of F3^m. Every vector of color differences has a sum-zero representative: adding a constant to all m colors changes the sum by m times that constant, and m is invertible modulo 3. Thus the orbit is precisely the 3^(m−1) color-difference states. The coordinate blocks under conjugate flips have size three (a translation changing either endpoint changes the difference). Pin difference zero off on every edge and reconstruct colors from pairs with vertex zero. This reduces graph three-colorability with two fixed permutations per graph order; SLP membership is as before. It does not contradict the commuting case, in which all translation orbit sizes are powers of two.

There is a different, exact parameter algorithm for the entire recognized size-at-most-three class. Embed each block in three points (add fixed dummy points for smaller blocks). Identify those labels with F3, so S3 acts by affine maps z↦εz+b with ε=±1. The conjugate subgroup H≤S3^m has binary sign image V and ternary translation kernel K≤F3^m, of dimension r. Enumerate only K, at cost 3^r poly(D), even if |V| or the total vector orbit is exponentially large.

Use the first 3m+1 successive conjugates of F by R to generate H. Each new irredundant generator at least doubles subgroup size, |H|≤6^m. Once a step adds no generator the previous subgroup is stable under conjugating by R (inclusion implies equality), so all later and earlier conjugates are contained. Compute an independent sign basis and corresponding generator lifts h_j. For each generator remove a sign-matching product of basis lifts to obtain a translation. Include these translations, the squares h_j² and commutators [h_j,h_k], then take their ternary span closed under sign multiplication by the h_j. This computes K: the span is normal, and modulo it the group is abelian of exponent two generated by lifts whose signs are independent. All operations are explicit on affine maps and linear spaces.

There exists c∈F3^m such that (1−ε_g)c−b_g∈K for every generator g. In the quotient space by K, the sign quotient acts affinely. Average the orbit of any point under that group to get a fixed point, since |V| is a power of two and invertible in F3. Compute a lift c by Gaussian elimination of these simultaneous constraints, without averaging explicitly. Now every action takes the form z↦c+ε(z−c)+k: for each attainable sign ε all k in K occur, since translations can be composed on the left.

For each of 3^r vectors k calculate, block by block, which sign values send the marked starting subset away from the pinned-zero positions. Empty/both/singleton binary sign lists require rejection, no condition, or one sign equation respectively. Solve those equations jointly on V over F2. If feasible, form the corresponding product of sign basis lifts and correct its translation by an element of K using retained Gaussian witness expressions. Products, inverses, conjugations and linear combinations from the construction yield polynomial-size SLP witnesses, not necessarily short positive generator words. Verification applies the final permutation to the original bit vector. On the coloring hard images r=m−1 (all ternary translations by differences of sum-zero vectors; only a uniform global color shift could be invisible). Thus this algorithm supplies no uniform small parameter on the NP-complete class.
