# Modal projection and raw words

Within each bag every internal list must allow zero. Between bags use the intersection of their difference lists. For a difference absent from this intersection, form the bipartite graph of pairs still permitting that difference. Modal color classes of the two bags occupy at least s=ceil(|A|/4) and t=ceil(|B|/4) sites. If the exceptional graph had to support those modal colors, it would contain a complete s-by-t rectangle. Maximum left degree below t, maximum right degree below s, or fewer than st edges rules this out. After all tests pass any original solution projects by arbitrary choices among tied modes, and each base solution lifts by coloring each bag constantly.

The quotient search fixes one global translated color to zero. It assigns a remaining vertex of minimum available domain, prioritizing vertices of high constraint degree on ties, and removes all incompatible neighbor colors. Empty domains prune a branch. The explicit stack is exhaustive and each popped partial assignment is charged to the shared cap, including completed assignments. Exhaustion of the stack after successful certification proves UNSAT. Exhaustion of the cap with work left gives UNKNOWN. A failed modal certificate alone also gives UNKNOWN for that partition. solve_auto tries common-neighbor proposals of the graph of forbidden zero differences, deferring the singleton fallback until last. Proposal generation, per-proposal certification and raw validation incur their own polynomial overhead outside the node cap. Preprocessing all proposals can take polynomially many full pair scans. Search is exponential on unrestricted quotients.

For n odd there are 2n rotation steps. At elapsed step i, flipping then finishing all rotations toggles vertex −i mod n in bit i mod 2. This enumerates every desired vertex/bit pair once, so a constant lift needs at most 4n letters. Replay follows the checked start's active positions through the two supplied permutation arrays, then compares their final set with the physical assignment and checks all original zero pins. It costs O(nD) indexed operations for D raw sites, apart from polynomial input and representation handling.

End-to-end controls use KG(6,2) and KG(7,2) tensored with K5 via the direct graph product, yielding 75 and 105 physical vertices. Automated proposals and certification yield SAT with a replayed 215-letter word for the former and UNSAT for the latter under a 50,000-node cap. Both give UNKNOWN at cap one.
