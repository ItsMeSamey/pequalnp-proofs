# Deciding dense connected four-color orbit cores

For a verified canonical input let L_xy be the allowed difference list on each actual vertex pair. Partition the vertices into bags; require zero in every within-bag list. For bags I,J intersect all cross lists to obtain L. For a difference delta outside L, its exception graph has an edge xy when L_xy still allows delta. Set s=ceil(|I|/4), t=ceil(|J|/4). Require the exception graph to contain no K_s,t; polynomial sufficient certificates are maximum left degree <t, maximum right degree <s, or fewer than st edges. No general biclique oracle is used.

Every quotient solution gives an original solution constant on bags. Conversely choose any most frequent color per bag of a real solution. If two choices have excluded difference, their occurrence sets yield the forbidden biclique. Thus this decision kernel works for arbitrary, nonuniform difference lists and returns a real witness by lifting the quotient and replaying the word in the original permutations. Counts need not be preserved. A verified b-bag partition permits polynomial verification and at most 4^b times polynomial search work. Discovery is charged separately.

## A pair of growing full cores

Take H=KG(a,2) with a=6 for YES and a=7 for NO; base vertices are pairs of [a], adjacent when disjoint. With h≥|H| form G=H times K_h: (u,i) and (v,j) are adjacent iff uv is a base edge and i differs from j. Forbid zero on precisely the edges. An unconstrained isolated padding vertex may be omitted in connectivity statements. The original family can use odd h to avoid padding. In the natural bags of h copies per vertex, each required exception graph is a matching. Modal contraction already succeeds for h≥5. At h=4, K5 times K4 is 4-colorable by layer although K5 is not. For h≥|H| the exact product also has the simpler section u↦(u,i_u) with distinct i_u and projection to H.

Every pairwise-intersecting family of 2-sets fits in a star or triangle. Three stars and a remaining triangle color KG(6,2). For KG(7,2), if s of four colors use stars, deleting their centers leaves binom(7-s,2)>3(4-s) pairs that the triangle colors cannot cover. The verdicts are thus opposite. Common-neighbor counts recover the bags without layer labels: same-base distinct copies have d(h-2) with d=binom(a-2,2), other pairs at most binom(a-3,2)(h-1). This gap is strict for h≥5. Trying the polynomially many thresholds and checking the resulting partitions against the raw lists avoids assuming a supplied isomorphism. Every open neighborhood is distinct.

Any two distinct sites have at least h-2 common neighbors. Hence G is biconnected and at least four-edge-connected for the stated large h. If proper edge subset S has c spanning components (including isolates), its plane rank is 2(|V|-c). For c≥2, summing the at least four edges leaving each component shows at least 2c edges were deleted. For c=1 at least one edge was deleted. Therefore |E|-r(E) strictly exceeds |S|-r(S) by |E minus S|-2(c-1)>0. The entire constrained graph is the unique maximum-deficiency Rado core, of rank 2(|V|-1). The graphic matroid of a biconnected graph is connected; the two generators of each normal plane are coupled, so the plane decomposition also has one component.

A base edge induces a K_t,t on disjoint sets of t=floor(h/2) layers at its two ends, giving a K_t minor. Graphic-matroid cut connectivity is minor-monotone on restricted edge orders and invariant under duality. In a balanced edge cut of K_t, each side has about t²/4 edges, implying graphic rank at least t/sqrt(2)-O(1) since rank r allows at most binom(r+1,2) simple edges. Every cycle-syndrome edge order therefore has cut parameter at least (sqrt(2)-1)t-O(1). This is about the earlier parameter, not a runtime lower bound.

The independent vertex-order solver vertex_union.py retains four exact sets of forbidden future sites, one per color. For concatenated forbidden-difference row blocks of cut rank k, each of the four color classes gives a subspace of a k-space whose support equals its union of rows; there are at most 2^{4k²} keys. Representatives recover actual original color assignments. Directly in layer-major order the product cut adjacency is repeated base adjacency plus a correction only on the split layer; its rank is at most 2|H|. This is an instance of classical neighborhood-union DP; see [Bui-Xuan, Telle and Vatshelle](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4796). The modal certificate has the advantage of a small explicit quotient found even after reordering product sites. Neither algorithm implies a small parameter on arbitrary canonical orbits.

The five-layer controls also have the full core. Each base edge induces a crown K_h,h minus a matching. A cut of a crown with smaller side of s=p+q≤h vertices has at least (h-1)s-2pq ≥ (h-1)s-s²/2 crossing edges. Concavity and integrality at the two endpoints show at least h-1 for h≥4. Any nontrivial cut in the connected product splits a crown, because crowns on adjacent base edges share all copies of their common base site. Thus edge connectivity is ≥4 at h=5. Deleting a vertex leaves every crown connected and every adjacent overlap nonempty. The automatic solver checked the 75-site YES and 105-site NO products with five layers, returning a 215-letter physical word for YES. Both gave UNKNOWN under a one-node cap.
