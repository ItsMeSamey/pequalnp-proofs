# Proposing modal fibers on original graph labels

For each pair of vertices count their common neighbors. In descending order of the distinct counts t, union all pairs with count t and emit the resulting component partition if it changes. Include the initial singleton partition. The algorithm emits at most n partitions on n≥1 vertices because every change reduces the number of components. With packed adjacency it uses O(n³) elementary bit work for the counts and polynomial time for grouping, disjoint sets and materialization. On an arbitrary graph it proposes partitions only. Each candidate must pass the independent modal certificate and its original-input checks.

For the categorical product KG(k,2)×K_h at k=6,7 and h≥5, distinct vertices in the same base fiber share d(h−2) neighbors, d=binom(k−2,2). Distinct fibers share at most binom(k−3,2)(h−1), because their underlying pairs cover at least three base elements. The latter bound is strictly smaller for these k,h. The largest count therefore recovers precisely the nontrivial base fibers, with any extra isolated raw vertices in singleton bags. The supplied enumeration preserves every vertex label and includes isolated vertices even without an incident edge.

Four-colorability of this product agrees with its base. A repeated color in each h-fiber gives a proper coloring of the base, since adjacent fibers cannot share a repeated color; projection lifts a base coloring. For k=6 take the first three stars and the remaining triangle as four colors. For k=7 every intersecting family of pairs is a star or lies in a triangle. After deleting the centers of s star color classes at least binom(7−s,2)>3(4−s) pairs remain for all s from 0 to 4.

For the standard four-color pair-moment SDP, let e be the base KG(7,2) pair equality-probability matrix (diagonal 1, disjoint pairs 0, otherwise 3/7). The explicit singleton-color binary law in binary-modular-bases/global-palette-counterexample.py has bits X_u with E X_u=1/4 and E X_u X_v=e_uv/4. Thus C=(4e−J)/3 is a covariance matrix scaled by 16/3 and is PSD. The uniform four-color block matrix is (J⊗J_4+C⊗(4I_4−J_4))/16, PSD with nonnegative entries, correct normalizations, and zero equal-color edge entries. Pullback duplicates the appropriate Gram vectors in each fiber and preserves PSD, including congruence with these exact pair laws. This calculation concerns pair moments.
