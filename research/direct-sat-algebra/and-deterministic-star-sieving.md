# Deterministic counting by erasing output coefficients at independent AND sites

Normalize a binary XOR–AND circuit to n raw bits, m named AND outputs z_i=a_i b_i on earlier affine inputs, and final affine output o. Draw i→j whenever z_i occurs in either normalized affine input of gate j. Write N(i) for successors. Choose centers I with pairwise disjoint sets {i} union N(i), so no two centers are adjacent. Original raw dependencies are unrestricted.

The count with o=1 is (2^n minus the signed physical sum with phase o)/2, by triangular uniqueness. Under raw constraints replace 2^n by the number of their raw solutions. Before expanding the physical gate indicators, replace o by the quadratic Boolean phase
Q=o+sum_{i in I with coefficient of z_i in o equal to 1}(z_i+a_i b_i).
On all physical tuples this has the same phase. Q is independent of each center z_i: its linear term is erased and no added product reads any center. This remains valid even when the original final parity mentions all outputs.

Expand each [z_i=a_i b_i]=(1−z_i)+(2z_i−1)a_i b_i. For U the sites choosing the second summand, the constraints are z_i=0 outside U, a_i=b_i=1 inside U. Its signed term is a global sign times the quadratic Gauss sum of Q+sum_{i in U} z_i over this affine space. Such sums cost polynomial time: eliminate linear constraints, then for any cross term uv express the phase as uv+uA+vB+C. Summing both bits gives 2 times the sign with residual quadratic phase C+AB. At affine termination use character orthogonality.

If a selected center i belongs to U while no successor belongs to U, none of the imposed linear rows reads z_i, Q is independent of it, and the signed phase contains it once. This term vanishes. Generate only the permissible masks by choosing on each disjoint neighborhood all patterns except the one selecting the center and no successor. The other sites are unrestricted. The exact number of generated terms is 2^m times the product over I of (1−2^{−|N(i)|−1}). Each term and the full running integer sum use polynomial space. Additional raw pins never read a center variable, so repeated exact counts recover an original witness in the same exponential clock.

For maximum undirected degree D in the normalized nonlinear graph, greedy distance-three packing gives at least m/(1+D²) centers (a radius-two ball has at most 1+D² vertices). For fixed D and m≤n this gives a deterministic poly(input) times 2^{(1−epsilon_D)n} exact count and witness bound, epsilon_D=−log2(1−2^{−D−1})/(1+D²)>0. The product bound also applies without a uniform degree promise when a sufficient packing is exhibited. This argument is independent of the external October theorem and does not establish a uniform clock for arbitrary dense nonlinear dependencies or arbitrary n log n AND counts. For m=0 use the affine solver.
