# Finding the optimal split for quadratic Boolean systems

Let Q be an explicitly expanded vector of quadratic Boolean ANFs on V over GF(2). Write P for the linear span of its alternating polar forms. For a subspace T put w(T)=codim(T)+dim(P restricted to T), and let w be the minimum. This quantity ignores constants and affine terms for discovery. Each evaluation and reconstruction uses the actual ANFs including these terms.

At budget b first compute the dimension r of the restricted polar span. If r≤b stop. Otherwise choose b+1 independent forms. Any subspace W certifying budget b makes these restrictions linearly dependent, hence is isotropic for a nonzero combination B. Enumerate the combinations. If rank B=2s, an isotropic subspace has codimension at least s. Skip s>b. Modulo the radical, the image of W extends to a Lagrangian by adjoining vectors in its orthogonal complement until dimension s. Enumerate all dimension-s subspaces of the 2s-dimensional quotient using reduced row-echelon bases, filter for isotropy, and recurse in each inverse image with budget b−s. This contains W on at least one branch and codimension is additive along inclusions. Every reported split has the stated cost.

There are fewer than 4 times 2^{s²} candidate subspaces in the quotient, by the Gaussian binomial formula. A node with budget b considers fewer than 2^{b+1} combinations, each with its own s. Bounding their total by their number times the largest per-combination work yields discovery time 2^{w²+O(w)} times a polynomial in explicit input size, including iterative deepening. Indeed 2s(b−s)+s≥b for 1≤s≤b, so the decrease from b²+C b to (b−s)²+C(b−s) pays for b+s² plus a fixed additive constant for sufficiently large C. Depth-first enumeration takes polynomial space.

For a returned split of codimension a and residual polar dimension r, enumerate its 2^a cosets. Output row operations leave at most r nonlinear coordinates per coset. The remaining affine coordinates impose a Gaussian system, after which Walsh inversion on r outputs uses 2^r scalar quadratic Gauss sums. Counts are exact and nonnegative after summation. Source coordinate pins for bitwise witness extraction restrict a previously certified split without increasing either a or r. Check the recovered witness in the supplied equations. Counting and witness extraction therefore cost 2^{a+r} times a polynomial.

For m≥2 consider m equations with homogeneous parts H(z)+h x_i, where H is the sum of m pair products on disjoint private z coordinates. Their independent polars initially have dimension m. Conditioning on h leaves only H and attains two. Cost one cannot suffice since a hyperplane cannot isotropize a rank≥2m polar. A heuristic conditioning on the radical of any displayed output incurs codimension at least 2m on its first nonempty prefix, leaving initial raw span m as its cheapest choice. The separation is for this coordinate-prefix heuristic.

The executable and independent exhaustive comparator are optimal_joint_split.py and check_optimal_joint_split.py in scratch/direct-sat-algebra, with Gauss backend isotropic_hybrid.py. The checker compares 42 optimal costs through dimension five and all 336 target counts with source witness checks.
