# Induced-subtournament replacement at a transitive cut

Row i sees j if i points to j. The constraints are x_i(Mx)_i=0 with h affine binary readouts Lx=t and any named pins outside U. U is a transitive shore in source-first order with arbitrary loops. Put V=outside, B=M[U,V], k=rank B and write each row b_i=v_i R with R a row basis. For fixed outside assignment y only Ry enters the U rows. The exterior update from U equals ones times p plus R transpose times z, where p is selected U parity and z the sum of selected v_i. The readout update w is the sum of corresponding columns of L.

For every candidate Ry scan U backwards. Track (p,z,w) from zero. Skipping preserves the state. Taking i is permitted when d_i+p+v_i(Ry)=0 and adds (1,v_i,l_i). Store a minimum-cardinality representative for each state. These transitions compute the exact boundary relation. Any minimum representative selects at most 2(k+h)+1 vertices: pair consecutive selected vertices, leaving at most one singleton. Removing whole pairs leaves the count of selected successors of each surviving selected vertex unchanged modulo two, since each removed pair either precedes or follows that vertex. Each pair contributes zero to p. Its increment in (z,w) lies in a space of dimension k+h, so dependence among these increments permits removing a nonempty zero-sum subcollection, contradicting minimality.

Keep the union of one shortest representative per reached final state and external syndrome; delete other vertices of U. At most (2(k+h)+1)2^(2k+h+1) shore vertices remain; runtime 2^(2k+h+1) times polynomial original input length. For every outside assignment the attainable exterior and readout updates are unchanged. Each kernel witness lifts just by zero-extension. All surviving orientations and loop bits are unchanged. This is a decision replacement, not a multiplicity-preserving replacement. A candidate shore can be checked by sorting and Gaussian elimination; exhaustive discovery of suitable shores is not assumed.
