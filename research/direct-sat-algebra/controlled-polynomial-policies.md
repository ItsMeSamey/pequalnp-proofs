# Polynomial control policies

For an aligned control system on affine data D impose affine RHS b(y) in span U and reparameterize D injectively. Compute W_d of all constants expressible as sum u_i q_i(y)p_i(y) identically with squarefree Boolean polynomials p_i of degree <=d. Match coefficients through d+1 by linear algebra and project the solution space. Its cost is polynomial in input size and sum_{j<=min(d+1,dim D)}binom(dim D,j).

Quotient output by W_d of dimension w and choose k' independent original projected maps spanning all projected affine maps. At any y the old active column span contains W_d, hence has rank w+r'_y for residual active rank r'_y. Consistency at y is equivalent before and after projection. Feasible fiber sizes are respectively 2^(k-w-r'_y) and 2^(k'-r'_y). Restore count by factor 2^(k-k'-w), even when exact division is necessary. For p bits with shift e<0, compute p-e residual bits; for exact arithmetic original count fits k+dim D+1 bits. Recover original controls by a linear solve at residual data. Iterate only when dimensions or control count actually shrink. A data-brute baseline is 2^rank(V restricted to D) poly: for each activator pattern solve the joint affine equations on its data fiber and controls and count solutions by rank-nullity.

For strictness take independent a,b and c=a+b. Columns e2 b, e1(1+b), (e1+e2)c and e1(1+c) with controls (c,1,b,b) constantly produce e1. A fixed combination that is constant has all coefficients zero: differentiate in independent c,b to see this. Include its swapped e1/e2 copy on two fresh bits. No nonzero constant fixed combination exists, but degree-one policies reach the entire output. Direct sums on fresh coordinates with arbitrary affine RHS yield unbounded ranks and native count 2^(dim D+k-output rank).

Let W* be the intersection of all active column spans over y. A functional h separates w from one such span exactly when h(w)=1 and the affine system q_i(y)=0 for i with h(u_i)=1 is consistent. Enumerating all 2^R functionals on the full output span computes W* by taking the annihilator of consistent functionals. General membership is coNP-complete. From signed NAE3 take w=e0 so a separator has h0=1. For every variable include products h_j(1+y_j)=0 and (h_j+h0)y_j=0. Each has the required form (h dot u_i)q_i(y)=0. These force h_j=y_j. NAE(l1,l2,l3) means (1+l1+l2)(1+l2+l3)=0; use first affine form on h, with h0 supplying the constant, and second on y. Existence of separator with data is thus equivalent to satisfiability. Nonmembership is in NP.
