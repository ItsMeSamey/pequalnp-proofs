# Arbitrary binary-linear drift in deterministic square-root time

Given K=GF(2^d) in polynomial coordinates and binary columns of A:K→K, find s such that A+M_s is invertible. All d are covered; for GF(2) simply return 1+A(1), since the bundled BinaryField constructor starts at degree two. For d≥2 the native solver uses O*(2^(d/2)) bit time and space. It does not assume self-adjointness, a balanced integer divisor, positive density, or access to any enlarged field.

Let Δ(s) be the 0/1 Dickson determinant. For the safe-slope moment E(F)=Σ_s Δ(s)F(s) over K, let z_i=s^(2^i). Then E(1)=1; for a proper nonempty set I, E(∏_{i∈I}z_i)=det D[I]. These follow from the unique squarefree digit choices in det(D+diag(s,s²,...)) and the finite-field power sums. At the full set the moment is 1+det D, since the endpoints of that polynomial both contribute. All equalities use characteristic two, so a binary indicator sum gives integer count parity.

For h specified binary affine factors f_j=c_j+Tr(α_j s), take R=K[y_1,...,y_h]/(y_j²). The top label coefficient of ∏(1+y_j f_j) is the product of the factors. Each term splits over the constant and d Frobenius digits because y_j²=0. To merge two factors at digit i, use
(1+b z_i)(1+e z_i)=(1+(b+e)z_i)(1+be z_(i+1)).
Every augmentation element squares to zero and z_i²=z_(i+1), with cyclic wrap on K, including s=0. Store one label value per digit and carry the factor be to the next digit. Its minimum label degree strictly increases unless it vanishes. Consequently each input factor requires at most h+1 digit visits, even on wrap. After all insertions we have the separate constant multiplier and a product ∏(1+B_i z_i).

The ring-valued expectation of this last product equals det(I+diag(B_i)D)+∏B_i by the principal-minor expansion and the full-index exception. The exception vanishes whenever h<d. All Gaussian pivots have constant coefficient one, hence are units equal to their own inverses. Ranked subset convolution multiplies two ring elements in O((h+1)^2 2^h) field operations, with O((h+1)2^h) field entries. Each prefix expectation costs 2^h poly(d) bit operations. Start with odd total, choose the odd child at each of floor(d/2) trace-coordinate splits, then check at most 2^ceil(d/2) original slopes. End with original binary rank replay.

The production original-column regression included every binary endomorphism at degrees two and three, as well as degree four, five and seven cases and unique-safe slopes. A separate original-matrix audit checked all 7,792 coordinate-prefix parities for all maps in degrees two and three. Independent sparse-dictionary ring tests included arbitrary affine coefficients and prefix depth beyond d, exercising the endpoint correction.

The label dimension cannot be reduced by identifying generators inside another commutative algebra while keeping all square-zero identities and the top product nonzero: a nontrivial linear relation among squarefree products, multiplied by the complement of an inclusion-minimal supported monomial, would kill every other term and force the full product to vanish. This is a limitation of that particular algebra representation, not a runtime lower bound for the search problem.
