Take a connected simple graph, one data bit per vertex and one independent control x_e per edge uv. Row e is x_e (y_u+y_v)=y_u over the binary field. Native XOR/AND gate encodings have unique extensions. Data 11 are forbidden, 00 gives a free control, and unequal bits determine one control. Thus data solutions are exactly independent sets. With U=I, normalized Z sums 2^(number of 00 edges) over independent sets.

Use m edge-disjoint triangles linked by consecutive vertex identifications at distinct contact vertices on each interior triangle. The graph is connected of maximum degree four. Every cut misses at least one edge of every triangle. Thus every assignment misses at least m activators. Each activator takes both values on the empty set and singleton endpoint solutions. Each row is necessary: removing edge uv makes the two-endpoint assignment feasible. Therefore normalized Z is divisible by 2^m with T=t=0 even after component and constant-activator checks. Feasibility here is easy. Separately, the NAE reduction also has T=t=0 with all row-unit columns selected, so constant T alone includes NP-hard feasibility.
