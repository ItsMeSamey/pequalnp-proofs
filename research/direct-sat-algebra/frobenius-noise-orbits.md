# Original-coordinate orbit solver

For a supplied binary field K of degree d and a rank radius k, frobenius_noise_orbits.py finds a safe original scalar for A=bI+cσ+E with σ any field Frobenius and binary rank E≤k in d^O(k+1) bit time. This is polynomial for each fixed k. The proof of outer support, nonzero parity, singular norm handling and the inner trace character sums is in frobenius-norm-repair-audit.md, independently checked by audit_frobenius_outer.py.

Recognition tries verified centers using shifted_frobenius_decode.py. Rotating both coefficient positions into width at most d/2 permits monic rank-k linearized key-equation decoding and exact compositional division when k<d/4. For larger k brute force costs at most 2^(4k). A scalar-only fit is handled separately by the degree-k determinant update on a (k+1)-dimensional space of inverse parameters. A failed fit returns inconclusive.

Once a nonscalar center is known, test it, then normalize by c. Put g=gcd(j,d), Q=2^g, m=d/g. On slopes t=yσ(z)/z, scale input by z and output by 1/σ(z). A bordered determinant with constant base σ+M_y uses at most k update columns M_(1/σz)U and k update rows V M_z. Thus the inner indicator on K* is a Laurent polynomial supported on at most (2d+1)^(2k) exponents, whether or not the base is singular.

For g≤k enumerate all nonzero norm targets. Otherwise the norm-one coset is entirely singular after rank-k noise. There is an odd regular norm fiber if the center test failed. Writing n for the norm and w=1/(1+n), the inverse formula in the audit gives at most k conjugates of w and k(m−1) conjugates of y per monomial. Norm-fiber summation retains only y-exponents equivalent to e(2^d−1)/(Q−1), whose binary form repeats the g-bit e exactly m times. Carrying can only reduce Hamming weight. Consequently wt(e)≤k, and substituting n=1+1/w yields at most (2g+1)^(2k) signed-power exponents. Multiplication by 1+w kills the invalid w=1. The resulting sparse function is nonzero at some valid w.

A Laurent function with T nonzero frequencies is hit by the first T powers of a primitive element, by the Vandermonde determinant. Use the unconditional first theorem in Shoup, https://www.shoup.net/papers/primroots.pdf : over characteristic two and ANY given irreducible modulus a primitive residue has polynomial degree O(log d). Enumerating power-basis encodings in increasing order includes one by index poly(d). No primitivity test or unknown implied constant is required. The production search doubles a common candidate index cap for both fields and retries the outer and inner lists together. Thus no bad norm fiber can consume an entire field search prematurely. Absolute and embedded-subfield power charts are explicitly constructed. Norm lifting factors only the extension degree, uses smooth primary logarithms for primes bounded by d, and replays the norm. The final original scalar is rank-verified.
