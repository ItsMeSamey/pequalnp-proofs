# Three-package test for parity on anchored 2-SAT models

Use marked-coordinate-defect.md or a supplied 2-CNF on N bits with h parity checks. Find a model a or reject, and translate by a. In flip coordinates every clause is a unary exclusion, implication, or pairwise conflict. Include the anchor shift in target b. Each feasible singleton has a least completion by implication reachability; impossible starts are discarded. Collapse duplicate closures and let G<=N count remaining generators. The least model for feasible generators is the union of their singleton closures; this union is feasible iff all comprised conflicts are avoided. Joint feasibility of package unions is therefore pairwise. The degree-h sample theorem guarantees an attainable b has a witness generated by at most h singletons.

For physical coordinate j let A_j be all generator indices whose closures contain it. Aggregate its h-bit parity column over equal A_j; discard empty signatures and zero labels. The syndrome of generator set X is sum_A lambda_A [A intersects X]. Let t count surviving signatures of size >=3. List internally feasible packages of at most K=ceil(h/3) indices, including empty, giving M<=sum_{j<=K}binom(G,j) packages. Use three copies; two packages must have disjoint generator sets and feasible joint union. Any feasible h-generator support splits this way; allowing up to 3K generators causes no false witness.

For each Walsh character theta and small signature |A|<=2, expand the hit-bit as sum of its generator bits plus their product for size two (in F2). For disjoint packages these contribute unary and pair package signs; place within-package terms in the unary. For each large signature with theta.lambda_A=1 expand its phase as -1+2[all packages avoid A]. Across at most t such factors this yields 2^t terms with separable package filters. The signed triangle sum for each term is trace(M12 M23 M31) with compatibility, pair signs and unary signs placed once per package. Compute one matrix product then an entrywise dot with the reverse edge matrix. Multiply by (-1)^(theta.b), sum and divide by 2^h. This is a nonnegative count of ordered package triples, NOT of original assignments. A positive count enables bisection of the three lists with O(log(M+1)) further exact queries per list. Undo the anchor, reconstruct the data by Gaussian solution and verify the original physical predicates.

The deterministic clock is poly(input) 2^(h+t) M^omega, e.g. omega=log2(7) by exact Strassen. Inputs to each product are 0,1,-1, matrix entries and Strassen additions grow only polynomially in M, and all expanded scalar accumulators have O(h+t+log(M+1)) bits using the sum of absolute weights <=3^t. This algorithm stores O(M²) words up to matrix multiplication workspace, in contrast with direct streaming samples. For a k-row one-mark source N<=2k when each flat supplies two generators. Include layout construction and choose the cheaper certified clock versus direct sampling, other solvers or brute force. No generic exponent improvement follows.
