# Recognizing physical paired-column layouts

After projecting as in controlled-code-blocks.md, keep each indexed V_i=span(a_i,d_i) as ONE subspace. Invertibly recoding the data does not change the projected affine relation. A new row basis maps the entire arrangement through one isomorphism. Use Jeong, Kim and Oum, Theorem 1.1 (https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1285895), to construct a branch decomposition of width <=k or certify none. Their cost is O(r m²+(k+1) r m N+k³ N³+f(2,k) N²) for N binary blocks represented by m<=2N vectors in dimension r. Subdivide an edge to root the tree, treating at most one block separately. Then exact counting/witness costs 2^(2k)poly(input). The unspecified f does not give polynomial time for growing k. Width NO does not exclude cheap character terminals. A heuristic cap miss does not prove width NO.

Parameterize the nonempty affine code C of pairs by Gaussian free coordinates v. Express the endpoints w_i and w_i+q_i as affine functionals of v. Merge their identical nonconstant linear parts into spin classes, preserving occurrence offsets; record constants directly. The distinct spin bits z lie in a linear code of dimension dim C, since reconstructing every endpoint reconstructs all pairs. Let h=#classes−dim C be its number of independent parity checks. The interaction graph has one factor per pair on its possibly complemented endpoints, with value 2 on 00, zero on 11 and one otherwise. Loops/constants become unary or scalar factors. Equality up to complementation is intrinsic to C.

Filter the h parity checks by 2^h binary characters, absorb each sign into unary weights, then eliminate spin bits along any constructed graph elimination order of fill width t. Divide the exact signed sum by 2^h and restore the common y-lift factor 2^s. All integers have polynomial bit length: each intermediate entry sums signed products of a subset of original factors, each partial product bounded by 2^N and at most 2^(#classes) summands. Time poly(input) 2^(h+t). A fixed min-degree heuristic supplies a verified order and t. Unary spins can be pinned for deterministic witness reconstruction without changing h or t; lift to original coordinates afterward. Over-budget evaluation returns UNKNOWN. On triangle chains h=0 and the recovered graph is a cactus with treewidth at most two. Each graph of treewidth at most two has minimum degree at most two; eliminating a vertex of degree at most two and completing its neighborhood is a minor operation, hence arbitrary greedy tie choices maintain the width-two bound. No pinning/exactness relies on the value of the normalized residue.
