# Physical address comparison with overlapping missing cylinders

The separate source stage5_comparator.py (df515a7c) reads two d-bit unsigned addresses X and Y in little-endian order, then reads the 2d physical complements of those bits in reverse order. Distinct signed aliases tied to the original address slots enforce this relationship. Next come p≥1 unconstrained independent payload bits and an enable slot pinned to one. The deterministic total raw-byte machine skips the fixed prefix, obtains a marker's value from its first signed decimal character, and scans the remainder through LF. All stack operations stay. The carrier itself contains the standard eight contradictory clauses.

At the final address bit, associate a flag C=[X≤Y] with the stored address. Retain both through the next round, XORing its reread signs into a running bit. On the final reread emit C XOR the running bit into a one-bit register, drop the addresses, and XOR every payload bit and enable into it. Each output has exactly 2^(2d+p−1) valid original words: for each address and first p−1 payload bits, the last payload bit selects either outcome. At d=1,p=3, original keys 140 and 156 yield zero and one. The output genuinely observes a comparison and a payload; signed copies and codebook coordinates are checked against original words by the physical solver.

## Canonical intersections

Use the supplied codebook, which includes both the before- and after-register and the physical slot bit in each record. The immutable-position tags and bottom stack top can be recovered from position. This is injective for both byte and marker views. We give the compression proof on marker records. Write N=4^d. Temporarily replace the one assignment C=[X≤Y] by an unrestricted bit C. Then every coordinate of the resulting encoded run is affine in the 2d address bits, C and the p payload bits. Prefix, sign complements, register carry, score accumulation, enable pin and terminal output are all affine.

The affine base compiled from local factor hulls is exactly this pseudo-run space. For containment in the base, the real graph of C=[X≤Y] affinely spans the address times flag space. Indeed the binary comparison is nonaffine already on the slice where the higher bits of both words vanish: on two single bits it takes value one on three corners. Therefore the encoded full pseudo-run lies in the affine span of full genuine runs, and satisfies each local hull. For the reverse containment, all register semantics except the comparison itself, the fixed control phases, the terminal parity, adjacent register consistency, enabled pin and signed bit equalities are linear identities on their respective actual unary/binary tables. Their hulls enforce those identities. Following them in chronological order determines the whole pseudo-run from precisely the address, C and payload.

Every omitted cylinder with nonempty intersection with that affine base fixes an entire address and its wrong flag. To see this directly, ordinary seeding before the flag operation and ordinary parity after the rereads have affine local domains and path relations. A constraint depending on the comparator graph involves a record containing the entire address and flag (the last seeding record includes them in its exit, the last rereading record in its entrance). The signed links back to earlier markers also touch one of these full records. Fixing both local encoded records of any such constraint fixes the whole address and C. The correct C cannot be forbidden on a pseudo-run at that address. Only the adjacency between the final reread and first payload marker can further restrict a payload bit, since the remaining markers all use the one-bit affine parity machine and have independent IDs.

Consequently, on the free coordinates, all nonempty intersections of omitted cylinders have one of at most three forms for each address: the wrong-flag fibre with all payloads, or that same fibre with the first payload fixed to either value. Subsets requiring two different addresses or two inconsistent first payload bits give empty intersections. A signed dictionary merging equal intersections at each update has at most 1+3N entries, including temporary update entries, regardless of the order of processing. Work per intersection, local complement materialization, codebook widths, integer coefficients, and witness recovery still count. These bounds apply to the supplied codes; no optimization over encodings is assumed.

In contrast, even the final address-seeding record alone contains at least N distinct omissions in its affine hull (one wrong flag per address), because its exit includes the entire address and the flag. The distinct raw cylinders therefore force at least 2^N terms for the ordinary unmerged subset sum. With p growing independently, direct enumeration of all true keys also costs N·2^p. The register count during comparison and rereading is O(Nd), and during the independent parity tail is O(p); the literal table uses 256 transitions per allocated control. This is a separation of the stated work measures, not a lower bound on modular counting generally.
