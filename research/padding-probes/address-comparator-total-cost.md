For the supplied comparison chart, each semantic before/after register uses at most 2d+2 bits after phase tags are removed. A record encoding concatenates the two registers and one marker sign, so its width is at most 4d+5 and each full binary factor has width at most 8d+10. With N=4^d this bounds even a full ambient local factor by 1024 N^4 words. Thus listing local hull omissions is polynomial in N and the payload length p, even before deciding which exclusions intersect the global base. There are O(d+p) markers and factor edges in the macro representation. Dependency-free payload also charges all its finite-state table entries, present records, compiler scans and decoded witness bytes.

The comparator uses an explicit transition table with at least N represented distinct address states. The global coordinate width U is at most O((d+p)(d+1)). The number k of distinct raw omissions is polynomial in N,p, though k≥N and the unmerged inclusion–exclusion sweeps require at least 2^N terms. The RREF dictionary has at most 1+3N entries at every stage, so its cumulative updates, polynomial-size linear algebra, and signed integers of at most k+O(U) bits yield a polynomial deterministic full clock in N,p. This distinguishes two executable strategies on an explicit finite-state predictor and a supplied record encoding. It does not assert efficient compression of an address comparator supplied as a succinct transition circuit.
