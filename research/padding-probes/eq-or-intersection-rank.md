# Exponentially many signed intersections on the fixed EQ/OR byte chart

For a cubic simple graph with m edges, replace each edge uv by the two strict clauses (z,−u,−v) and (−z,−u,−v), with the guard first in both. Apply the actual paired-occurrence reduction. On accepting words pin the guard to zero, all private dummy signs to one and enable to one. Choose an induced matching of k edges. Set every vertex outside the matching to zero. Greedy selection gives k ≥ m/13, since excluding an edge and all edges at distance at most two in the line graph removes at most 1+4+8 edges per choice. Write X and Y for opposite endpoints of the matching edges. The resulting source acceptance matrix, indexed by X and Y, is the k-fold tensor power of [[1,1],[1,0]].

Consider either event_csp byte records or event_macro marker records, with any injective per-event encoding into binary words. There is an affine map from the 2k formal endpoint coordinates into the ambient coded chart. On the product of legitimate pair corners {00,10,01} it sends endpoints to their actual uniquely determined good execution, copying source values into all occurrence and link IDs. To construct it, inspect each local record there. Prefix, enable, and dummy records are constant. Equality group records depend on at most their one variable. An edge outside the induced matching has at most one free endpoint. Within a matching-edge OR group the guard is fixed and the second slot depends only on X. Each third-slot record carries its physical sign determined by Y, including every raw byte in its marker line. Successful completion immediately resets the finite control's triple buffer. Thus its three records are either functions of Y only or all distinct. The conclusion holds for the vacuous guard polarity too.

A constant or one-coordinate code extends affinely with that dependency. Three distinct binary words on the three allowed corners extend uniquely to an affine injection on both coordinates, since distinct nonzero differences are independent in characteristic two. Combine these maps for all records. The legitimate corners span the full parameter space affinely, so every affine equation valid on all positive records still holds on the entire section, including affine-hull and output constraints. A singleton record code on the section pulls back to a constant condition, one axis constraint, or a fixed pair. These are rectangles for the X-versus-Y partition. Each omitted local tuple and every intersection of such omissions therefore pulls back to a rectangle.

A coded assignment on the section with a forbidden endpoint pair 11 cannot be a feasible accepting record assignment. In each corresponding variable's equality group its occurrence bits remain exactly those of that variable throughout the affine section. Alias consistency and acceptance would then force a false OR test to pass. The full feasibility indicator on the section is consequently the claimed tensor-power matrix.

Each nonempty intersection in the ordinary signed inclusion-exclusion dictionary is an affine base intersected with a set of omitted local tuples. Its restriction to the section has rational matrix rank at most one. The acceptance matrix has rank 2^k, so even after identifying equal affine intersections and removing zero coefficients, at least 2^k nonzero terms remain. The bound concerns this actual chart, these intersection terms and arbitrary injective local codebooks. An alternative compiler or a representation allowing nonrectangular summands requires its own argument.
