# Projected algorithms for physical predictor outputs

Keep the exact explicit physical record constraints in common injective bit coordinates. Compile their affine hulls and missing local words. Include original table construction, omission materialization, record encodings and physical replays in the cost. A missing local word excludes an affine cylinder. After pinning the output, project each cylinder onto the Gaussian base. Discard impossible cylinders; an exclusion of the entire base proves infeasibility.

The opposite of a rank-one exclusion is a forced affine equation. Group rank-two exclusions with identical normalized normal spaces. Two distinct missing syndrome words leave a line, three leave a point and four preclude all assignments. Reproject after a rank increase. This calculation is exact for each group and does not claim every implied equation.

The decision backend compresses to the active normal span of dimension d and calls the archived deterministic affine-flat solver. It caps d before that call; the recursion is exponential in d. Every witness lifts back to original coordinates and bytes. The three unchanged legacy modules can be placed next to the wrapper.

The counting backend creates two PRIVATE flags per rank-two exclusion. Each flag is one when its equation takes the forbidden value, so only 11 is excluded on that pair. For the flag map f=As+c, global feasibility is Hf=Hc with H a basis of the left nullspace. Its rank is r=2m−rank A for m pairs. On each syndrome character the pair factor sums the signs of its three legal assignments. The signed character sum divided by 2^r counts attainable legal flag vectors. Each lifts to the same number of original assignments by Gaussian kernel dimension. Sequential pair pinning takes at most 2m further character sweeps, followed by inversion to an original seed and physical replay. The exact counting/witness bit clock per output is O((2m+1)2^r poly(explicit input)), with polynomial space. Accumulated numerators need O(m+r+1) bits. Equal or shared forms have distinct private flags and are tied by the image syndrome.

For arbitrary ranks, maintain an exact signed indicator sum of affine flats. On exclusion C replace each c[F] by c[F]−c[F∩C], skipping empty intersections. Merge equal canonical RREF keys and drop zeros. After i exclusions the coefficient l1 norm is at most 2^i. Charge every layer's dictionary and every intersection, canonicalization and key/weight update. The final dictionary suffices for every subsequent prefix or output count by intersection with its pinned equations. Counts and originals are only conclusive when computations finish. Independent exclusions can produce 2^k distinct nonzero terms. A small final dictionary alone cannot certify a cheap construction.

These algorithms count or witness canonical strings classified by the supplied predictor, not satisfying assignments of the represented CNF.
