# Propagating the positive physical equality checks

stage6_projected_solver.solve preserves the source and budget conventions of the pinned-dummy successor. Its ordinary physical stages receive an altered alias chart: every occurrence ID and equality link for source variable v is replaced by v's first occurrence ID, retaining each marker offset. Dummy IDs and enable are preserved. A derived ID can now occur more than twice. The original chart remains available for validation and the original byte machine is unchanged.

On an enabled accepted original word all equality triples pass. Therefore each chain (or ring) uses just one underlying value, making that exact physical byte word legal on the coalesced chart. Conversely any derived word is an original word by copying each representative value back across its merged group. Normalized dummies remove multiplicity, positive words agree between charts, and setting the fresh enable to zero provides a negative anchor even after source pins. The two charts need not have equal negative counts.

The projected_gaussian, projected_compressed and projected_rank2 stages exploit the actual predictor's fixed local transition semantics. On positive words every physical equality test now succeeds, all normalized dummy ORs succeed, and enable equals one. For each remaining OR triple build a local record carrying its three actual printed signs. Admit the seven nonzero sign words, filtering by repetitions and original pins; for each bit add a binary relation tying its sign to the appropriate signed source value. The original controls at completed pairs have a uniquely specified successful state; the omitted prefix, internal byte operations and equality/dummy checks are deterministic. This is a specialized exact projection of positive runs, not the raw byte-event compilation. A satisfying assignment to these records exists exactly when the complete physical word is accepted. For one strict clause with one satisfying literal pinned true, the other two bits remain free and the local record is an affine plane. The corresponding projected Gaussian test has a nonempty positive instance without falling back to source enumeration.

Each projected stage calls its actual generic backend and returns that backend's counters. A positive-only complete nonempty answer typically has generic status constant because its designated enabled output is one. The public result is source SAT only after canonical physical reconstruction, rechecking both original and merged aliases, direct fixed-DFA replay, checking normalized dummies, and original-CNF/pin evaluation. Absence of any enabled completion proves UNSAT; a budget refusal proves neither outcome. In addition to backend costs, constructing the projected input examines at most seven words per prepared source/dummy OR and builds the binary tie records explicitly. No unconditional polynomial-time SAT bound follows.
