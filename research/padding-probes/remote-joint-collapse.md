# Joint pivot assignments collapse to single-site certificates per ternary

Fix a satisfiable binary background B and one ternary C. Suppose each of its three singleton sign words extends B, as checked by the three full 2SAT tests. Let K be any set of original variable names. Say K resolves C if every assignment on K makes B ∧ C representable as a 2CNF on the full original variables within its cofactor.

For singleton word i let R_i be its extendible projections on K. Let R be the projection of all models of B on K. Each R_i is exactly R restricted by its forced individual coordinate values. Indeed any word of R can be combined with the singleton's pins unless some pair in their union conflicts, by the pinned-unit pair property for 2SAT. Each side is individually feasible, so this conflict must lie across the two sets of pins. The singleton therefore forces the opposite of a coordinate in that word. Projection R itself is bijunctive by existential closure.

The three R_i have a common word if and only if some K-cofactor still admits all three singleton witnesses, which by majority is precisely failure of resolution. If their intersection is empty, their collected coordinate pins are inconsistent over R. By the same pair property there is a conflicting pair of pins u,v originating in different singleton families. Then R entails u implies not-v, so one family forces not-v and another forces v. Either setting of the original coordinate underlying v excludes an entire singleton family. This coordinate alone resolves C. The converse follows by retaining a resolving singleton pivot in K. Under the per-clause criterion the minimum Set Cover theorem is thus exact even if joint assignments are permitted: each initially unresolved clause needs some selected member in its computed candidate list. Interactions across distinct ternaries can yield further reductions outside this criterion.

## Discover all pivots together

Build the implication graph for B once. For each original ternary make at most three singleton 2SAT feasibility tests. If a singleton is missing then use its immediate unconditional binary replacement. Otherwise, for each of the three words propagate the three literals through the shared implication graph. A singleton can force a literal l either because B already entails l or because one pinned literal reaches l: if B together with the singleton and not-l conflicts, pin pair sufficiency gives the implication. Literals already forced by B are constant across all singleton families and cannot distinguish them. Thus a candidate coordinate resolves C exactly when opposite literals of that coordinate occur in the reachability closures of two singleton words. Record, for each pivot value, a conflicting singleton index to drop. This requires at most three linear-time traversals and three linear-time 2SAT tests per clause plus scanning the candidate records. Indexed work O((m+1)(v+|B|)) plus parsing, greedy cover, and branching; working memory O(v+|B|+mv) with explicit incidence storage. Deterministic adjacency sorting and sparse-ID bit costs are charged separately. This constructor differs from issuing new SCC tests for every candidate coordinate.
