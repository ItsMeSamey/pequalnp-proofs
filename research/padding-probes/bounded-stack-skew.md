# Bounded height difference for two byte-driven stacks

Use the canonical acyclic byte graph and a supplied properly nested signed-alias schedule. On the first byte committing a marker sign, the alias validator pushes a normalized bit at an open or compares and pops it at its paired close. A failed comparison rejects the branch. The second machine is an actual deterministic byte table with independent stay, push and pop actions. Both machines may inspect their own top. A finite-state filter or regular schedule may be included in the product control. Heights exclude the protected bottoms. Fix a bound k on their absolute difference after every consumed byte, including the final byte.

Pair the two stacks by depth up to their shorter height. The product stack holds these pairs bottom to top. Finite control holds both logical controls, the identity of the longer stack, and its remaining suffix bottom to top. This encoding is invertible. The two logical tops come from the suffix or the top pair, using the specified bottom symbols when empty. To perform a byte move, temporarily extract the top pair if present, place its two symbols before the respective suffixes, and perform both logical actions. No logical pop can reach below these temporary tails. Pair their common initial positions and reinstall those rows; leave the excess in finite control. At most two new paired rows result because the previously shorter temporary tail had length at most one and gained at most one symbol. At physical bottom keep the protected bottom. Thus each valid byte induces exactly the intended pair of logical transitions, also when one logical stack pops and the other pushes. Induction in both directions gives exact correspondence up to the first rejected or overflowing byte.

This local rule is written in stack_zip_transition.py. It yields a general normalized pushdown replacement. To target a stay/push/pop-only engine, a pop followed by zero to two pushes can be lowered to bounded forced microsteps keeping the replacement in private control. Internal steps use no further physical bytes, permit no alternative, and contribute no source edge to traceback. Equivalently use the normalized pushdown reachability procedure directly. The local file is not an end-to-end recognizer or a tested integration.

For stack alphabet sizes g1,g2 without bottom, the suffix has at most B=1+sum_{j=1}^k(g1^j+g2^j) states. Paired alphabet size is 1+g1*g2. With V graph vertices and input control counts q1,q2, normalized product control count is at most V q1 q2 B, with graph edges, table enumeration, and O(k+1) work per local rule charged. Pushdown saturation is polynomial in these expanded counts and rule table size. Equivalently its total deterministic clock is poly(input,k+1) times (1+g1+g2)^{O(k)}. This is FPT in k for fixed alphabets and in k log(2+g1+g2) for variable alphabets. A statically paired canonical schedule needs only one bit per alias stack cell; different alias names are already checked by schedule position. An additional explicitly tracked b Boolean live values multiplies the expanded graph size by at most 2^b.

Check the source-side close bit before testing overflow. On the first otherwise admissible transition exceeding k, emit a separate reachability target with the original offending edge as provenance. Search globally for this target, including intermediate prefixes; testing only the two inferred output words cannot establish the bound. On the unrestricted canonical paired fiber each consistent prefix extends to a legal complete word: finish its fixed marker spelling, fill the mates of pending names and freely choose future names. This reconstructs an actual physical overflow word with the first violating prefix. For a filtered graph that lacks the extension property, a prefix overflow supports conservative abstention only. Under finite computation caps distinguish an unfinished search from an exhausted one. A checked output pair on legal complete words remains valid in the presence of overflow elsewhere; an unrestricted global constancy verdict requires excluding overflow or a separately established semantic promise. Deterministic source replay must check both physical byte strings, full alias consistency and the actual predictor bits.

## A separating family with two changing outputs

For depth n>=1 the physical marker order comprises an opening block x1,y1,...,xn,yn followed by closing markers x'n,y'n,...,x'1,y'1. Give each x'i the same signed alias as xi, normalized offsets zero. Every yi and y'i is its own singleton. The final source enable slot can be a singleton ignored by this table. The predictor pushes the bit yi at its first sign-committing byte, pops and compares at y'i, keeps a sticky mismatch flag, stays at x markers and all other bytes, and emits one iff no comparison failed. A clocked explicit total deterministic byte table needs O(L) controls, for fixed byte alphabet and three stack symbols including bottom, using the canonical known marker lengths in the L-byte fiber. Unreachable malformed rows may enter a rejecting sink without popping bottom. These are native table-format controls described by construction, not a claim of an executed test.

Every legal prefix has skew at most one. At depth i the operations are alias-push, predictor-push, alias-pop, predictor-pop, enclosing all deeper intervals. The first three events already conflict with the supplied tag-at-open convention; moreover the two actual intervals at each depth cross rather than nest, so an ordinary chronological tagged stack cannot process these events either. Both logical stacks reach height n. On the legal signed fiber the response accepts exactly when y'=y componentwise, hence its positive density is 2^{-n}. Choose all normalized signs zero and compare that formula to the one changing only y'1. Both satisfy the alias chart, the actual table outputs opposite bits, and the original contradiction carrier makes both formulas UNSAT. These are instructions for producing physical witnesses at every n. They need no native run to define.

This separates recognition by the stated schedules, not minimal recognition by every alternative predictor. Recognizers may be unioned. Conversely a synchronized alias schedule plus an arbitrarily deep neutral predictor excursion need not obey any fixed skew bound. Finally two unrestricted independent visible stacks can enforce x#y#reverse(x)#reverse(y). Restricting the four blocks to separate unary letter types gives a^i b^j c^i d^j, which is not context-free by the pumping lemma. This obstructs a uniform compilation of all such schedules to one pushdown; it is not a hardness assertion about individual finite fibers.

Han and Ko use the closely related bounded top buffer and synchronized lower stack in their alignment PDA for edit distance of visibly pushdown languages [§4](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3006316/1/VPL.pdf). Their edit-distance setting differs from the pinned physical SAT-prediction fiber considered here. No priority claim for the paired-stack mechanism is intended.
If the compared blocks share an alphabet, the familiar non-context-free four-block restriction uses finite-state tagging by block followed by regular intersection and erasure of separators, rather than a literal restriction to four disjoint alphabets.
