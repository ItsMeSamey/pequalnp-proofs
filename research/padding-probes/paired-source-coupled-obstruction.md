# Coupled source under the fixed alternating equality/OR byte compiler

Use stage6_source_solver.compile_source with exact_twice=True and that module's fixed_dfa. For each edge uv of a simple graph G on n nonisolated vertices include the strict clauses (z,−x_u,−x_v) and (−z,−x_u,−x_v), in that literal order. Here z is a fresh source variable. Fixed source masks satisfy this formula exactly when the x-support is independent in G; either z is allowed. The compiler emits its original equality/OR sequence, signed aliases, explicit enable and immutable contradiction. Its positive physical traces project exactly onto IS(G). One can optionally pin z and all dummy IDs to the compiler's canonical witness choices without changing this projection.

For the growing control replace each vertex of a simple cubic base by a triangle, assigning one distinct external edge to each port. This is cubic, has n/3 disjoint triangles and contains the base as a minor. If the chosen base has a uniformly positive edge-expansion constant, the replacement also does. For a balanced or smaller cut with few split triangles, round its split triangles into whole base vertices; the base expansion costs a linear number of crossings minus at most three per split triangle. If many triangles split, their own crossing edges already suffice. Expansion is optional for the rank bounds below.

The fixture stage6_coupled_source.py (4543cb31) compiles these strict source clauses without changing the native predictor. The default base K4 gives n=12, 18 edges, 36 strict clauses and 648 assignment markers plus the enable marker. The triangle replacement has 163 independent sets: each triangle chooses none or one of its three ports; an external edge violating independence forces both endpoint triangle choices. Distinct simultaneous violations form a matching in K4. Inclusion-exclusion therefore gives 4^4−6·4²+3. With free pad and dummies there are 163·2^109 positive physical keys. The fixture supplies an original positive word and the same assignment with enable cleared as a negative word.

## Active rank and private-flag nullity

Fix output one. Use any injective coordinate code on each actual event record. Each occurring graph variable has an affine readout P_v on the *positive* records. Choose a marker in a successful equality triple of that variable. The triple starts with the reset finite control, its three physical bits are identical, and previous successful checks leave no additional control data. Its first-byte record (or complete marker record) therefore takes exactly two distinct values, between which an affine bit functional can distinguish. Assemble the readouts into P. Every independent set is attained, including zero and the unit vectors. These are also the only projected positive assignments. These assertions hold in both macro and byte views, with either free or canonically pinned dummies.

After any sound affine closure, let a seed and the remaining forbidden flats still represent those positives exactly. A direction in the common normal kernel preserves the entire feasible set. Its projected direction under P would be a period of IS(G). This set has zero translation-period space: a period T must itself be independent by translating the empty set. For nonempty T take a neighbor j of its support, necessarily outside T. Translating the valid singleton {j} by T fails. Thus the common kernel lies in the kernel of P. Since the image directions of P span n dimensions, the active normal span has dimension at least n.

Suppose the residual exclusions are all pairs of affine bits forbidding 11, as required by the private-flag Fourier method; include rank-one equations in the seed first. Write m for the number of pairs, and H for the image in the 2m private coordinates, of codimension r. The preceding kernel argument implies P factors affinely through H and has surjective linear part onto F2^n. Choose a feasible flag tuple mapping to the empty independent set. Within each pair there are two linearly independent directions from that point to the other two legal values. Across all pairs take one direction per pair to span E and the other to span F. Both spaces have dimension m and are complementary. All translates by E alone, or F alone, have legal blocks. Intersect each with dir(H); these yield disjoint spaces of dimension at least m−r. Their sum consequently has codimension at most r in dir(H). Its image in source coordinates has dimension at least n−r.

Each individual projected space is a linear subspace of IS(G) through zero. Its coordinate support is independent: if two endpoint forms were both nonzero, a vector with both evaluations one exists over F2, whether the forms are distinct or equal. The sum of the two projected spaces is supported on a union of two independent sets, containing at most two vertices per disjoint triangle. Thus n−r≤2n/3 and r≥n/3. Residual higher ranks can instead make this particular backend ineligible. This is a lower bound on the verified representation parameter, including for arbitrary sound affine consequences, not on all decision algorithms.

## Signed dictionary for the actual stored tuples

Choose an induced matching of k edges; a cubic graph permits k≥|E(G)|/18 by greedy deletion of all edges touching either endpoint or a neighbor of an endpoint. Pin all other x to zero, pad z to zero, the dummies to their canonical values, and enable to one, as an analytical restriction. Each matched edge has coordinates (a_i,b_i), with allowed local corners 00,10,01. On valid assignments, any chosen byte or macro record on the fixed positive run is constant, depends on one matched bit, or depends on one matched pair. This follows from control resetting between triples; an equality triple sees only its one source variable, and a real OR sees only its edge with its pad first. Three allowed corners are affinely independent. Extend each coded record affinely from those corners, producing a section Φ on all 2k bits. The affine base contains the whole section because the 3^k legal assignments affinely span the ambient 2k-bit space. Record readouts on equality triples fix all source bits, so a completed section assignment is globally legal exactly when no pair is 11.

On this section the inverse image of every singleton local record code is a rectangle for the a-versus-b partition. For a matched real OR, the first marker sees just the pad, the second sees one endpoint, and the third includes the physical bit of the other endpoint in its record. At that third marker the three distinct legal corners either all give distinct codewords (injective affine extension, all fibres points), or two with identical observed final bit coincide (the extension depends only on that bit). The same reasoning covers all bytes of a marker, including the tail where the slot bit is retained. A cylinder fixing one or two entire record codes, and any intersection of such cylinders, therefore restricts to a rectangle. Intersections with the global base add nothing on the section.

Accordingly a final exact signed dictionary of these canonical intersection indicators restricts to a sum of rank-one rectangle matrices over the rationals. The required matrix is the k-fold tensor power of [[1,1],[1,0]], of rank 2^k. At least 2^k nonzero entries are required in the final dictionary. This concerns the specified whole-tuple inclusion-exclusion dictionary. It does not constrain unrelated signed decompositions, other encodings that jointly mix multiple record locations, ordinary witness shortcuts, source preprocessing or a generic runtime bound. The source has an immediate positive all-zero graph assignment.
