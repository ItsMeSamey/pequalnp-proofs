# Inclusion–exclusion cost for distinct local omissions

Let F be the number of explicit unary and binary factors, U the total width of the supplied injective codebooks, and k the number of distinct forbidden cylinders after localization in global coordinates. For each factor with rank d and s distinct allowed records, its omission count 2^d−s is first computed symbolically. The configured omission_cap tests the sum of these local counts BEFORE deduplication and before materializing the omitted words. Once materialization starts each local complement has at most k entries: distinct missing tuples of one factor map to distinct global words on its scope. Consequently the sum over factors of their local omissions is at most Fk, even if the same cylinder arises on several factors. This distinction matters for cap semantics without invalidating FPT(k) after explicitly charging all input and codeword widths.

For each subset of the k cylinders append its coordinate equalities to the global affine equations and to the specified output/prefix restrictions. Gaussian rank gives zero for an inconsistent system and exactly 2^(U−r) otherwise. Pointwise inclusion–exclusion counts the allowed encoded records. A signed accumulator needs at most U+k+2 bits. Counting both outputs and tracing at most one physical assignment per positive output needs at most 2(U+1)2^k rank terms. Include O(Fk) omission materialization, complete physical compiler input and tables, codebook construction, full signed-integer arithmetic and all raw byte replays. This yields deterministic 2^k poly(explicit input,U,k) work for the counting and tracing phase. The rank terms cap must permit a whole alternating sum; an arbitrary partial sum cannot certify impossibility or existence.

The original byte result counts only distinct canonical padding words in the specified chart under its predictor. Every appearing identifier is fixed by its first recorded sign, and the machine execution on a word has one true stack restoration at every pop. Those facts eliminate auxiliary multiplicity. This count is not a #SAT count of satisfying assignments of the represented CNF.
