# Executable padding searches

padding_cnf.py constructs and parses DIMACS bytes. On n challenge variables key bit n enables a test and the lower bits encode the proposed assignment. Each immutable contradiction and every pair of writable signed markers is physically checked. All emitted source formulas are UNSAT and keys for a given challenge produce equal byte lengths. The reference predictor accepts exactly enabled satisfying challenge assignments.

walsh_probe.probe compares physical formulas produced by deterministic callbacks. It records the actual constructed bytes and call counts. The finite binary field is used to make queries; Walsh coefficients live in odd characteristic or characteristic zero. If m>0, a constant verdict on probe exhaustion requires a valid sparsity promise. A replayable disagreement needs no promise. For q the least power of two at least two and greater than s(m−1), the implementation enumerates up to q² (alpha,beta) pairs and evaluates only distinct keys. Charge field construction, O(q²m) field operations, and every constructor and predictor execution. No error need manifest as disagreement on any one chosen fiber.

sentinel_probe.double_until_error submits DIMACS bytes for 1,2,4,... positive clauses on disjoint triples. Rejection returns a direct satisfiable false negative and all-ones word. The finite clause cap gives unknown if no rejection occurred. Without a cap termination requires eventual rejection. sentinel_flat_predictor(R) validates this specific source form and computes b≤R; no general affine-hull recognizer is assumed.

support_probe.py compares the origin to the Hamming ball of radius d. A disagreement region containing the support of any nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree at most d must intersect this ball (triangular monomial evaluation). No bound on the degree of the full response is needed. A miss yields unknown except after full cube exhaustion. monotone_affine_probe.py takes a supplied monotone gate DAG on affine observations. It uses Gaussian elimination to realize the formal low and high endpoints, where possible, and replays the physical predictions. A conditional constancy verdict requires a correct supplied representation. For instance AND after an invertible affine transformation yields a one-point response with full Boolean Walsh support. No procedure for extracting either structural promise from an arbitrary predictor is asserted.
