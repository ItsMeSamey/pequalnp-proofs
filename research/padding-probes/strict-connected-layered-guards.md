# A connected strict-width control for unsigned pair selection

Use sufficiently large D, k=lcm(1,…,D), q=k H_D. On left labels [k] and right labels for contiguous j-blocks partitioning [k], for every j=1,…,D, use the containment edges. Define G+ by adding an edge between the two layer-1 right endpoints 1,k. Define G− on the identical vertices by cyclically shifting only the left names in the containment edges by k/2, and adding a right edge between layer-1 positions 2,k−1. At each fixed right label the two sets of left neighbours are disjoint because j≤D<k/2. The added edges are distinct. Each graph is connected: for any i<k some j≤D does not divide i, so the two left positions straddling i share a block. Each graph is nonbipartite. Every odd cycle uses its one additional edge; walking two containment edges moves the left position by at most D−1 in the graph's own pre-relabelled coordinates, so its odd girth is Ω(k/D).

For xy in G+ introduce (u,x,y),(u,−x,−y). For xy in G− use the same two clauses with −u. A value of u activates all two-colour disequalities of one of the nonbipartite graphs. Hence the formula is UNSAT. For every layered raw variable, the hub, and 40kD additional raw variables w, include (s,t,w) and (−s,−t,−w) on two new common watch variables. Setting s≠t satisfies all watch clauses simultaneously. Every original clause is strict ternary, all variables occur in both signs, and the incidence graph is connected. Even after removing the three common variables, the co-occurrence graph on the genuine layered vertices remains connected. Any UNSAT subset needs source clauses from a growing odd cycle in each activated graph: if its represented edge subgraph is bipartite, pick the enabling hub value, a proper colouring and s≠t.

Assign positive IDs with u below all right IDs and all right IDs below all left IDs. The two watch pairs have uniquely maximal frequency until selected (tied only with one another); both expose every raw w through exterior binary clauses. In each positive-endpoint sign channel the containment spokes are independent of all other channels for frequency and tie purposes. The two chord endpoints have degree 2 until the last layers, and all rim pairs have frequency one. From j=D down to j=3, the maximal-degree tie picks a still-intact right group of size j, choosing its hub pair. Unaffected right groups retain degree j, left degrees only fall. This gives k(H_D−3/2) distinct positive right spokes in G+ alone. Every legal interval containing two of these gates would need three independently originating inputs. The exposed output and the observed or externally entering versions of both inputs realize a nonbijunctive signed OR graph. Thus the optimized old circuit has at least k(H_D−3/2) bad blocks. It has at most 4(kD+1)+2 by singleton partition. With v≥40kD, the actual cover ceiling exceeds 3^(v/4−o(v)); the old uncapped mixed solver dispatches and on UNSAT exhausts all branches.
