# Shared signed guards in the mixed source compiler

The generator in mixed_guard_source.py uses 12h+2 variables and 16h clauses. Each of four signed disjunctions on public bits a,b occurs h times, with a new positive third literal y each time. Its private binary rows are (¬y∨z),(¬z∨w),(¬w∨¬y). They force y=0. In the negative family the four guards then exclude every public assignment. The positive family replaces the last row with (¬w∨y) in the second guard class. It has public assignment (0,1), forces all three private bits high in that class, and admits three private settings independently in every other class. Its model count is 3^(3h).

The source_mixed_nand compiler keeps the binary clauses native. The repeated public pair wins the greedy frequency test separately for every sign pattern. At h=1 its signed pair wins the tie against the positive, larger private ID. The six gates comprise two raw inverters and four signed pair gates. A single two-entry interval contains all six. Its four output bits attain exactly the one-zero words. The majority of any three distinct such words is all ones, so the projected relation is nonbijunctive. A partition made exclusively of bijunctive factors could only produce a bijunctive existential projection. Thus the exact bad-block optimum is one and its branch clock is two.

All variables occur in both signs, no unit occurs, and the four public polarities forbid every Horn renaming. Each binary or ternary scope has one forbidden corner and occurs only once, preventing a codimension-at-most-two affine cylinder from covering the point on any ternary scope. In the default normalized physical chart every padded binary contributes a three-point row and every genuine ternary a seven-point row, each missing a point of its affine hull. The at least 16h omissions exceed the old default caps. The remaining block stage must cover 288h+2 vertices by bags of size at most four. Even one row per bag exceeds the 2048 state cap for h≥29. The zero-enable trace gives a globally consistent output-zero assignment, so no subtree can return empty first.

In the literal compiler all 16h distinct clause outputs precede the left-fold conjunction joins. An interval covering two joins has at least three exterior input wires. An interval mixing final clause work with the first join also needs a third wire beyond the first two clause outputs, unless it contains the whole final clause calculation, which itself needs three independent raw origins. Consequently each of the 16h−1 joins needs its own bad interval: on its two incoming ports and its exported result the relation is a signed NAND graph, including when adjacent accumulator inverters are absorbed. At cap two this compiler skips.

For h≥29, default physical prepasses, NAND branch cap two and zero cover nodes give UNKNOWN in the literal-only route. The mixed route decides both families in at most two branches. With unlimited cover nodes on the negative family a completed literal branch search enumerates all its branches. If skipped, the first covering ball alone visits at least 2^(R+1)−1 nodes: every violated clause branches on at least two literals, with no deduplication and no satisfying leaf. For v=12h+2, block size floor(log2(v+1))≥8 gives R≥v/4−ceil(v/block)≥v/8−1. These are costs of the specified implementations. Direct branching on a,b or resolution of the private implications also decides both families in polynomial time. All implementation comparisons here are source audits.
