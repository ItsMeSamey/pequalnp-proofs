# Literal NAND intervals on strict source clauses

For the source_nand_compile.py literal compiler (83196a88), let m≥4 distinct normalized residual clauses all have three distinct variables. Its final conjunction is a left fold, emitted after all clause outputs C1,…,Cm. Every successive join NAND is followed by its fresh inverter. Clause disjunction gates are fresh too; only inverters are shared.

Every interval with at most two entering wires contains at most one join. Inside the conjunction suffix, two successive joins require the entering aggregate (or its pre-inverter), and two new distinct clause outputs. For an interval crossing the boundary, it cannot start before both OR stages of the final strict clause: their combined inputs require representatives of three distinct original variables. Any shared precomputed wire used by these clause stages is just a signed copy of one original variable. Thus C1,C2,C3 already exist outside the interval. A straddling interval cannot reach the second join, and cannot include any clause-stage gate together with the first join, because the first join's two incoming C1,C2 already exhaust its entries and clause-stage gates do not take these outputs as operands.

Each join interval exposes its two inputs up to negation, and exports its output or complement to the next gate or to the observed target. All incoming gate-output wires count as ports. Its projection therefore contains the full graph of a signed NAND, which violates majority closure. This gives b≥m−1. It does not bound actual early termination. On an already pure/unit-irreducible strict instance v≤3m/2; the existing cover clock is at most (3/2)^(3m/2) times subexponential slack, less than the 2^b term of the NAND bound.

For a comparison on the same source language, start with all eight clauses on a fixed triple, then append k positive strict clauses on 3k fresh variables, all disjoint. The formula is UNSAT. Each fresh clause has a zero-cost two-interval construction: combine the first two private inputs' inverters with their NAND, and then the partial disjunction's inverter and final private input's inverter with the final NAND. Each interval exposes at most two ports. The fixed core costs O(1), and each conjunction join plus its inverter costs at most one, proving b≤k+O(1). Source NAND runs in polynomial(k)·2^k on this family. The frozen source_cover_sat.solve_dense keeps all v=3k+3 variables. It pushes three children for every visited non-leaf of the first covering ball, since every assignment violates a strict row and this routine does not memoize states. Its depth is v/4−O(v/log v), requiring at least 3^(3k/4−o(k)) visits even in its first ball. This is a separation from that particular uncapped routine. Other portfolio routes, including preprocessing capable of recognizing the fixed core, may finish earlier.

For arbitrary width, a single all-positive clause admits the same private-input grouping extended for each fresh literal. It has b=0 however long the clause. The earlier physical source 3CNF API rejects width above three.
