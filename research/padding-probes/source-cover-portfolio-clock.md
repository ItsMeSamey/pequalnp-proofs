# Complete sparse-source search

The original signed clauses may have size at most three. Only appearing or pinned names are renumbered. The default finite-budget stages apply source simplification (unit/pure literals, binary SCC and renamable Horn), then the physical or projected engines, and finally covering-code search. Each returned SAT witness is checked against the original clauses, pins, and fixed byte DFA. A preliminary UNKNOWN advances to the next stage. An uncapped cover search is complete; a user node budget can leave UNKNOWN. Cover preparation and physical compilation are separately charged.

Let v be the number of unpinned appearing variables after restriction. Partition into blocks of sizes at most b=max(1,floor(log2(v+1))). Use radius floor(s/4) on a block of size s. Greedy set cover selects a center containing the most uncovered words. Averaging over centers yields at most ceil((2^s/V)(1+s ln 2)) selected centers, where V is the ball volume. All block words fit in polynomial space since 2^b≤max(2,v+1). Stream the Cartesian product of centers. Around each center, search to depth R equal to the sum of block radii, branching on flips in one false clause. If a satisfying assignment lies within the remaining radius, some such flip reduces its distance. Every assignment lies in at least one searched ball.

With default bounded physical settings and original encoded input length L, the complete clock is poly(L)·(3/2)^v·2^O(v loglog(v+2)/log(v+2)), with polynomial space. Indeed V≥2^(sH(1/4))/poly(s+1), each radius search costs at most poly(L)·3^R, and 1−H(1/4)+(log2 3)/4=log2(3/2). The correction term pays polynomial slack once per block. Custom callbacks or unbounded nondefault physical budgets must be charged separately. Overlapping balls support decision and witness recovery, not model counting.

This improves the existing implementation's 2^v complete fallback only. Deterministic covering-code local search is classical. See Dantsin et al., Theoretical Computer Science 289 (2002) 69–83, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(01)00174-8.
