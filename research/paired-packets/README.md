# Exact Cauchy packet batching on connected bounded-degree expanders

Keep one live column bit X and one live row bit Y at the same distinguished slot y of a packet. Pin other columns to zero. Sum other row bits against [1,1], except for a set J pinned to zero. Appendix D's affine factors l₀(s)=1−s and l₁(s)=s give the exact transfer

    K_e = [[1, P_e(1−w_e)], [0, P_e w_e]],
    w_e=W_yy(T),   P_e=∏_{x∈J}(1−W_xy(T)).

Use the source's denominator-cancelled W polynomial at all nodes. Put g_e=P_e w_e. Two packets with the same g_e and endpoint labelings (Y,X) and (1−Y,1−X) contract to g_e·1[Y=X]. For a mismatch one packet has K(1,0)=0; otherwise one copy contributes g_e. This uses only Boolean indicator tensors and requires no inverses. Different logical edges can have different genuine Cauchy weights. Matching preparations within a pair ensure agreement at every interpolation evaluation. Unequal preparations give diag(g_2,g_1), whose weights cannot in general be discarded.

Take a connected simple bipartite graph G with v vertices and m edges. Each edge carries one primary z bit and b syndrome h bits through complementary pairs, using 2(b+1) packets per edge. At each vertex impose affine constraints on incident primary bits and, for layer j, the Boolean predicate that the incidence parity of h_j equals a supplied quadratic q_{v,j} of its incident primary bits. Every creation-time input entry is zero or one. Write B for the binary vertex-by-edge incidence. Its rank is v−1 and its image is the even-weight subspace, by a spanning tree and the even weight of each column. Each system B h_j=(q_{v,j}(z))_v contributes 2^{m−v+1} assignments exactly when Q_j(z)=Σ_v q_{v,j}(z)=0. The full scalar is therefore

    (product of g over all pairs) · 2^{b(m−v+1)} · #{z: Az=c and every Q_j(z)=0}.

After affine elimination, substitute free coordinates into the quadratics. Row-reduce their quadratic coefficients, impose the purely affine consequences, substitute and repeat until no more linear consequences remain. If h independent nonlinear equations remain, the integer count is 2^(−h) times the sum over all linear combinations of the h equations of their signed Gauss sums. Every division takes place exactly in the integers before mapping back to the output ring. Write a polynomial containing x_i x_j as x_i x_j+x_i A+x_j D+C on the remaining variables. Summing over x_i,x_j gives twice the signed sum for C+AD, still quadratic. A nonconstant affine remainder sums to zero, and a constant one gives ±2^d on d bits. The resulting time is polynomial times 2^h, with polynomial space. No signed coefficient is needed in the original tensor or in its canonical lifts.

For a fixed-degree example set b=1 on any connected simple cubic bipartite expander, leave z free, and choose q_v=Σ_{e<f incident v}z_e z_f at every vertex. The polar matrix is BᵀB over F₂: each distinct incident pair contributes once, while its diagonal vanishes. Its rank is v−2, since im B is the even subspace and ker Bᵀ is generated by the all-ones vector, which has even weight. The nonlinear phase thus has linear rank and the interaction graph is the line graph of the expander. The local physical tensor degree is twelve. The all-zero primary and syndrome choice is one feasible assignment.

## Source layout, mode survival and recovery

This is a criterion on a supplied valid Appendix D packing. Use sufficiently many repeated, fully occupied copies of an unequal-at-coordinate-zero packet type, with the same distinguished column and row slot in each partner pair. A balanced initial catalogue with all ordered label classes filled to the same multiple of L gives repeated copies, as in the linked-chain construction. Joining the distinguished row ports of the appropriate copies into one left tensor at each logical vertex and doing the same for the column ports on the right preserves their full endpoint labels and group loads. The bounded degree fits the source's Δ for large instances. Neutralize unused packets with pinned-zero columns and freely summed rows. The conditions can be checked from a supplied layout. A fresh run of the source's greedy packing need not find that layout. A single type has its own limited repetition count; the number of selected packets should never be assumed proportional to M/L without an additional construction.

At the distinguished t=1, ζ=1 use y=y₀+2j with j>0 and J=floor(cL/u) distinct extra pinned rows of that same parity per selected packet, as in the source-mode example. Choose a feasible merged assignment (the all-zero one works in the displayed family), setting exactly one packet from each complementary pair active. For every active packet independently choose a nonempty subset of the first J mode indices and give each selected mode exponent one; leave all other packet modes at zero. Positive exponents force that packet's active source bit X=1, as its tile has only one nonzero candidate X_y and j>0. The mate then has X=0. Its zero-mode distinguished row must have Y=0 because its only possible active column was crossing. Complementarity forces Y=1 in the active packet. Thus all logical bits in the surviving assignment are uniquely fixed.

On an active packet, coefficient extraction injects the chosen indices into distinct rows, including the distinguished row with Y=1; every other chosen row is pinned zero. All injections have the same sign after removing the common mode factors. At t=ζ=1, T=2L+1 and d_x=T−y₀+x are positive odd numbers on the selected parity. Each active contribution includes N^{|B|}2^{Σb}j^{Σb} times a strictly positive rational sum up to sign, with N=C(T)/C'(0). Clearing odd denominators using ∏d_x^J bounds its valuation by O(J² log L+J log J+J(a+1)) per packet; the uniform construction has a=log₂ L. If k packets are active then k≤3M/L. Choosing c sufficiently small makes the total valuation <p_t (the source witness uses p_t≥24ML/u). The formal weight budget fits as well. Hence at least (2^J−1)^k distinct exponent tuples have nonzero scalar contributions at this single evaluation (they need not be distinct numerical values). All are batched without following the tuple recursion. No survival after interpolation is claimed.

For full-node reconstruction process the required active t and interpolation ζ values at their prescribed p_t, with the creation-time representatives for all original tensors. At every such point the paired transfer identity still holds, even if g=0 or is a zero divisor. Aggregate counts in the integers, evaluate the Cauchy products in the designated ring and apply the source's ordinary Lagrange and buffered Hermite recovery. The number of exterior evaluation calls and their arithmetic overhead are polynomial in the source size; the preceding gain is conditional on the supplied layout and tensor structure.
