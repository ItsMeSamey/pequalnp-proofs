# Five-AND lower bound for 9822

The finite proof excludes exactly five live ANDs in a monotone binary circuit with at most 17 live gates after constant and copy simplification.

Fix one binary choice per AND and resolve each AND to that operand. The output is a disjunction of the raw leaves reached. The original monotone output accepts a set of inputs exactly when every resolution reaches it (for a rejecting input choose false operands). Thus the direction masks of the endpoints of each graph edge cover all 32 words. Each maximal independent set has a rejecting direction; this direction reaches every vertex outside it because adding any of those vertices creates an edge. There are 16 distinct maximal independent sets. Every ordered pair of vertices is separated by one such signature, so the ten receiver masks are nonempty, proper, and incomparable.

Reverse AND propagation splits an activation into the two coordinate halves. ORs duplicate the reverse signal; forward fanout uses binary reverse union. Counting the output observer, the total number of binary reverse unions is 2g+1−(g+10)=g−9≤8. Each live switch activation is nonempty and is full or a union of previous branches. The catalogue's fiveprofiles.cpp enumerates all such activations up to independent sign flips, dropping cases below twelve trace atoms (we require sixteen). canonicalfive.cpp then quotients by simultaneous permutations of masks and axis indices. These searches relax physical order and use only orbit-invariant constraints.

Call d the number of raw masks equal to a primitive branch. The 10−d distinct nonprimitive masks consume paid unions, hence d≥2. Opposite signs on a single switch cannot be two raws, because the 16 maximal independent sets include one omitting both; its witness reaches both. Identical signs would give equal receivers. On different axes the opposite bit choices simultaneously suppress the two branches, so corresponding direct vertices cannot be adjacent. The graph independence number is four. Thus d=2,3,4 exhaust the possibilities.

rawbank.cpp handles d=2,3. Helpers on OR-only paths to raw masks cannot depend on another raw since raw masks are incomparable. After reserving 10−d unions for the receivers, at most d−2 helpers remain. Every nonbranch raw is a union of two branches or raw-serving helpers. The first such helper is a union of two branches. An activation not equal to full, a primitive, or a raw-serving helper must equal a raw or consume a spare helper; the latter is granted arbitrary construction, including a raw-fed construction. The checker grants all branches at once. It enforces the 13 edge unions and all 16 exact maximal-independent-set witnesses. Partial rooms of possible directions must admit distinct representatives. It rejects any room containing more other required rooms (including itself) than it has available atoms.

loose4.cpp handles d=4. For a subset T of direct vertices, each witness reaching all of T lies on the corresponding distinct signed axes, so at most 32/2^|T| directions qualify. The number of maximal independent sets disjoint from T cannot exceed this. Five independent quadruples remain. The program enumerates direct receiver assignments, then grants other raws arbitrary unions of the ten branches, disregarding all union costs and chronological restrictions. No assignment extends. Geometry independently reviewed all three subcases.

raw2.log, d3-complete.log and loose4.log each cover catalogue IDs 0 through 616. The searches found no solution in 61,497 d2 nodes, 2,575,164 d3 nodes or 68,446 d4 viable initial direct assignments. For d4 the 617 profiles split into 221 with too few trace atoms, 220 with no direct assignment, and 176 with viable initial assignments. upper18.json verifies all 1,024 full inputs at 18 gates; reverse5.py accepts its six-switch, nine-union translation as a positive control. No Lean replay is claimed for these native finite certificates.
