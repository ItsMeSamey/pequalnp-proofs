The earlier ordered-root grammar applies to four through six nonfull selectors out of seven. For p ordinary (full) roots and no exceptional activation, list signed forests of size seven with exactly p roots. For one distinct nonprimitive activation U occurring r times, retain p ordinary-root trees and a forest of r distinguished roots with the common activation U. The first physical use of U can draw only on branches in the ordinary-root forest, or unions thereof. Every distinguished root can independently tap U. Enumerating all ordinary subforest sizes and all forest shapes for the distinguished component therefore covers this layer, even when the shared nonprimitive mask has multiple uses. Once again the primitive activation parent relation is acyclic because a later signed branch has essential dependence on its own selector bit. Each direction profile's edges and all 16 maximal independent sets are checked by the separate raw-bank kernel.
