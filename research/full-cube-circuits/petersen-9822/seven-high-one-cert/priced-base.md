For a fixed choice of granted nonraw activations, let B be the original primitive branches and G the granted mask set. A candidate receiver is either primitive, or one union of a pair from B ∪ G ∪ H for the optional helper bank H. In jointbase.cpp the unions of pairs from B ∪ G form a fixed set, the same in every admissible H; store it once and attach the bitset of all admissible H. All other one-join candidates must have an operand from H, so compute these separately in each bank. This is the same family as the full Cartesian product before the change. Named raw goals may belong to either pool and are tested against their union.
