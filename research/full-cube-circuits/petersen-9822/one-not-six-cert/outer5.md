# One inverter, at most seventeen gates, and an outer cone with at most five ANDs

Treat the inverter output as an independent source z and retain live outer binary gates. For each maximal independent set I there is a resolution rejecting I with the actual z. On activating any v outside I the inverter value cannot rise, so that resolution must see v. It sees exactly the raw complement of I. This gives sixteen distinct ten-bit patterns and pairwise incomparable raw masks. The ten raws and live z are eleven sources, so there are at most 16−11+1=6 reverse joins. Direct labels need not be independent vertices.

Each nondirect raw needs a distinct join, whence d≥4. Two direct raws cannot share an axis (equal or disjoint masks contradict the sixteen signatures). With five axes d=4 or 5. Fewer axes pad to five with unused full switches; old masks ignore fresh bits. Use the 617 orbits in ../calculus/canonfive.out described by ../calculus/five-switch.md. For each choice of signed axes and direct vertex labels, each projected signature bin needs enough distinct trace atoms. outer5direct.cpp finds 190,622 placements on 190 orbits at d4, and 20,804 on 89 at d5.

At d4 all six unions produce distinct raws. A raw operand to a different raw would violate incomparability; hence all six are pair unions of primitives. Every nonprimitive nonfull activation must be one of them. The kernel enumerates these receivers retaining a nonempty room for each full signature. There are zero completions before any z test.

At d5 each switch activation strictly contains its direct raw, so cannot be any raw. There is at most one distinct nonprimitive nonfull activation W, using the sole spare union. No nondirect raw can contain a chosen direct primitive. If W exists, allow either a pair of opposite primitives or W union one opposite primitive. If W does not exist, grant each raw an independent arbitrary union of up to three opposite primitives. The sixteen signature requirements still have zero completions.

Build outer5direct.cpp, outer5d4.cpp and outer5d5.cpp with a C++20 compiler; feed canonfive.out on stdin. The .tsv and .log ledgers cover all 617 IDs. outer5-controls.py generates synthetic all-full one-profile planted receivers for each d; both return extensions 1. These exercise the pre-interpolation assignment stage where the target instance rejects every case. The d4 control stops immediately at its first complete assignment.
