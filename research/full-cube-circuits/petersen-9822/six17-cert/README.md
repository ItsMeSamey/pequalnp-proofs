# Six AND gates at size seventeen for the 9822 graph

All results here concern binary monotone circuits with exactly six live ANDs after simplification. They exclude size at most 17 for this stratum. No seven-AND or signed lower bound is asserted. The graph 9822 has ten inputs, thirteen edges and sixteen maximal independent sets.

## Reverse representation

A direction chooses one operand of each of the six AND gates. OR gates pass the direction to both operands. The output on a set S is true exactly when every direction reaches at least one raw in S: when the original output is false, select a false operand at each false AND. Thus for each edge uv, the masks R_u and R_v of directions reaching these inputs have full union. For each maximal independent I some direction rejects I and therefore reaches precisely its complement: adding any vertex outside I creates an edge. These sixteen exact signatures are distinct and sufficient. They imply that all raw receiver masks are proper, nonempty and pairwise incomparable. Two distinct vertices are adjacent if and only if their masks have full union.

In reverse order an AND splits its activation into opposite coordinate halves, forward ORs merely duplicate their reverse signal, and a forward fanout of m combines m reverse contributions by m−1 binary unions. With the output observer added, the union budget is 2g+1−(10+g)=g−9≤8. Let B denote the twelve primitive branch masks and W the set of distinct switch activations excluding full and any primitive. The catalogue was generated using profile6.cpp at arguments 10 4 and canon6.cpp. The ten-atom minimum is harmless; 16 are needed. The first split must have full activation and the second must use full or one branch of the first, so the cutoff of four paid activation masks cannot exclude a candidate. Simultaneously permuting the six sign coordinates and their switch identities preserves all mask tests.

The number d of receivers semantically equal to primitive branches is at least two, because 10−d distinct other receivers cost a union each. Direct vertices occupy different switch axes: equal masks are forbidden; if they occupied opposite signs their masks would be disjoint even though some maximal independent set omits both and must reach both. Different axes have a direction choosing the opposite of both signs, hence these vertices are independent. The graph has independence number four. After reserving the 10−d distinct raw unions, at most d−2 auxiliary unions remain.

An auxiliary used in another raw's pure union ancestry cannot depend by pure unions on a different raw, by incomparability. Thus a first raw-serving auxiliary can be granted as a union of two primitives and a second as a union of two primitives or of the first and a primitive. A non-raw-serving auxiliary may instead be a named activation or its precursor. Granting a named activation directly relaxes its construction. Duplicate or early primitive values do not increase the available semantic raw union closure. When checking a required raw activation at switch j, its own branches and all branches from switches whose activation depends on bit j can be omitted from its precursor supply: these masks depend on bit j and cannot have existed before j. Other branches are granted regardless of chronology. An accessible auxiliary feeding this raw can be generated from granted precursors and the other raw-serving auxiliary; a raw-fed auxiliary cannot feed this raw by unions. The full-scope kernel otherwise grants branches simultaneously.

## Finite partition

All profile IDs index the 41,719-line decompressed six-all.txt.gz in original order. The 96 profiles with four full activations had pre-existing checks and are supplied separately wherever a global ledger marks them as previous.

- For d=2 and d=3, enumerate respectively no auxiliary and at most one auxiliary. Every remaining named activation in W must occur among the raw masks. The global d=2 ledger and the four-full ledger are complete. The d=3 global ledger covers IDs 0–41717, together with the four-full ledger. ID41718 is all-full and is excluded below.
- For d=4, classify by how many distinct W masks are not raw. At most two are possible. If two, they are the only auxiliaries. If one, grant it and one optional extra. A possible raw-serving extra is a union of two primitives or of the named helper and a primitive. These two strata yielded zero solutions, including the earlier checks on the four-full subgroup.
- If every W mask is a raw, enumerate its injective vertex assignment subject to the required independent-set witness rooms. Of 41,719 profiles, 290 survive, partitioned as 48,78,95,65,4 by the number of required masks 0,1,2,3,4 respectively.
- For these 290, assign the four direct labels and their primitive masks along with the required raw masks. Grant each remaining raw any independent union of at most four primitives: two auxiliaries plus its own union have at most four distinct primitive leaves. The vertex reflection is a graph automorphism applied before choosing the named raw labels. Signed switches used for direct assignment pruning permute the primitive branch family and fix each required activation individually, as well as the previously assigned direct masks. Full independent-set signatures leave only profiles 9616, 21163, 21580, 27723, 40994 and 41718.
- For 9616 and 21163 enumerate all admissible two-helper banks separately: 1,993 and 2,086 banks, no hits. For 21580, 27723 and 40994 index all banks by bits, record which banks produce each raw candidate, and intersect those bank bitsets along the direct/named receiver DFS. A complete assignment must have at least one common bank. The three counts are 1,981, 2,144 and 2,251 banks, with zero hits.
- Profile 41718 has six full activations. Tap the root full register for all six splits, keeping all primitive masks and all receiver unions. Forward transposition then yields a star-product circuit at no greater size. All ANDs are terminal in this representation and their operand cones are OR-only. Each product is a biclique with disjoint vertex shores since singleton inputs reject. The graph is four-cycle-free so these bicliques are stars. Merge equal centers by adding one neighborhood OR while saving one AND and one collector OR. A nontrivial neighborhood OR cannot be shared across distinct centers, since two common neighbors would make a four-cycle. If the star sizes are s_i and their number is t, the gate count is at least Σ(s_i−1)+t+(t−1)=Σs_i+t−1≥13+6−1=18 by the edge count and minimum vertex cover. The root retapping argument avoids any assumption that the original full switch already had a dedicated OR-only path to the output.

The DFS prunes each potential independent-set witness to a room of possible direction atoms and rejects any room containing more required rooms than distinct atoms. Signed coordinate compression keeps one representative per primitive-branch signature; every mask inspected is a union of branches and is constant within each atom. Induced-graph and mandatory-target branching preserves every assignment of the required masks. The d4 positive control is a six-switch physical circuit of size 18; it is reconstructed and checked on all 1,024 inputs by forward.py, and checked against its three-auxiliary library in the joint solver. No 18-gate positive can satisfy the cap17 two-auxiliary requirement at d4.

The native searches returned complete negative receipts on the listed scopes. replay.sh rebuilds their kernels in a scratch directory, verifies outputs byte for byte, and uses the checked catalogue included here. To regenerate that catalogue, build profile6.cpp and canon6.cpp and pipe the output of profile6 10 4 into canon6; compare it with six-all.txt.gz after decompression. No Lean formalization of this finite partition is claimed.
