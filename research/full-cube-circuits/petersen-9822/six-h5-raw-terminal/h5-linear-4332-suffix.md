# Eight pair leakage cases for the raw 4,3,3,2 profile

Fix four distinct raw scopes of widths 4,3,3,2 with pairwise intersections at most one. Their exact prefix cost is eight. By h5-linear-large-prefix-triples.md there is exactly one outside join for U and no residual V signal. Since the widths cannot be paired into two equal-width products on each side, U is mixed. Call its hidden product H=A∧B and write U=H∨Y; Q labels the other raw-only product. Both chronological orders are covered by swapping these labels since neither product depends on the other.

If distinct maximal negatives i,j falsify U, each is the complement of a different cover clause. Assign the order of A,B so that A∪Y is the complement of i and B∪Y is the complement of j. For each outstanding representative the two complements share five vertices. Outside Y their common part must be in A∩B, of size at most one. Thus Y has at least four labels. As a raw signal in an optimum linear-prefix bank, it is a subset of one prefix target and has size at most four. It is exactly the unique four-label operand, a whole operand of Q: were it A or B, one cover clause would have only four labels. There are exactly five candidates for Y obtained by dropping one of the shared five labels. For each enumerate all physical A,B fulfilling the clause equalities and linear intersections, then all remaining original masks D such that Q=Y∧D has the prescribed widths and every pair of the four scopes meets in at most one label. This yields 920 choices each for representatives 2,8,11,44,65 and 4300 each for 18,29,114 before doubling by the chronological order of H and Q.

Grant every subset of each of the four scopes as an operand, together with all twelve primitive singleton labels and the one U signal. This includes all unprojected realizable sink operands in any optimum prefix library. Evaluate the chronological raw products anew on each of the original 29 extremal inputs. A candidate sink must accept a positive edge and at least one leakage row under productive/leaky normalization, and must reject every maximal negative outside the specified pair. The union of three such sink traces must be exactly the thirteen positive rows plus that pair. Retaining only maximal legal traces preserves this necessary condition. None of the 17,500 choices passes, even with this permissive bank. No hypothetical prefix saturation or additional OR gate is used to exclude a circuit.
