# Exact terminal completion of a fixed physical h5 prefix

Fix all five earlier ANDs and the actual OR circuitry feeding them. Charge every physical prefix OR once. The terminal may reuse every original raw input and every computed prefix signal, including OR intermediates. This queries existence of terminal operands. For prescribed literal masks one must enforce those masks. By safe-hidden-normalization.md, every earlier AND may be assumed leaky on a maximal negative. Each raw input leaks as well. Every nonconstant prefix OR is consequently leaky; eliminate formal zero wires and fold constants. A constant-one signal, if available, leaks on every maximal negative and cannot be used by a feasible terminal pair of leaky arms.

Evaluate each available signal i on thirteen minimal positives and sixteen maximal negatives as masks p_i,n_i. For allowed negative set P let C(P) be the minimum size of a family of available signals jointly covering all thirteen positives with every n_i contained in P. Infinity denotes infeasibility. The minimum number of additional ORs is min_P C(P)+C(complement P)−2. Both arms must cover all positives and their observed negative leak masks must be disjoint. Unused negative rows can be allocated arbitrarily to make the allowed sets complementary. Conversely these extremal tests imply agreement on all 1024 original inputs by monotonicity.

Stop the two new terminal OR cones at original prefix signals. Any common new OR node reaches each operand by an OR-only path, possibly through different ancestors. Whenever it is true, both terminal operands and hence F are true: it would be safe. A nonconstant OR of leaky prefix signals is itself leaky, so the two cones share no such node. Each cone joining t distinct available signals needs at least t−1 new binary ORs by connected DAG edge count. The two bounds add. Conversely joining a minimum cover on each side attains this bound. Returned indices reference the original available signals. Old shared ORs are free to reuse only after charging the actual fixed prefix cost.

h5_terminal_prefix.py chooses a missing positive bit, branches on allowed signals hitting it, and memoizes the remaining mask. This costs at most O(2^16 2^13 m) transitions, apart from bitset and witness handling, for m signals. With hidden flags supplied it adds one mandatory bit to each side, ensuring that both physical operand masks contain at least one hidden primitive. A prefix OR flag is the disjunction of its children's flags; an AND output is a new hidden primitive. Zero-positive signals can serve that extra requirement and must be retained. Interchanging the two arms allows one orientation per allowed-negative split. No census of the hidden prefixes is implicit.
