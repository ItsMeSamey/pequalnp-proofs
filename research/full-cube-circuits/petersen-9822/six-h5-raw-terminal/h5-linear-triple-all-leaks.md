# Four linear raw triples in the three-sink layout

Fix chronological raw-only H and Q whose four distinct operands are three-label sets with pairwise intersections of size at most one. No assumption on union size, connectedness, order of the products, or raw-label symmetry is needed. At eleven OR gates a three-sink 0+3 completion fails for all eight outstanding pair representatives and every triple leakage set.

The four operands require eight nonshareable OR gates. Every optimum prefix bank comprises the four triples and one pair subunion for each. A raw triple cannot cover all positive edges since the minimum vertex-cover size is six. Thus neither H nor Q can serve as the entire hidden-bearing sinkless arm U. Its OR gate lies outside the four raw cones, forcing empty residual V, one gate for U, and an optimum eight-gate prefix bank by the exclusive-sink surcharge. U is H∨Q or one hidden signal joined with a raw subunion Y of size at most three.

Write H=A∧B where operands denote the disjunctions of their raw sets. For U=H∨Y both A∪Y and B∪Y cover all thirteen positive edges. Both have size six, so Y is a triple disjoint from A and B. It must be a full operand of the other product. A rejected maximal independent input equals the complement of one of these covers: it is contained in that independent complement and cannot be extended. U thus rejects at most two maximal negatives, both of size four. If both are rejected their intersection has size 1+|A∩B|, at most two.

For U=H∨Q with Q=C∧D, all four cross unions of A,B with C,D must be minimum covers. Enumerate the six-bit masks hitting all thirteen edges, which gives thirteen covers. For each triple A let P(A) consist of all triples C whose union with A is one of these covers. For all unordered A,B sharing at most one vertex there is no pair C,D sharing at most one vertex inside P(A)∩P(B). The partner loop in h5_linear_triple_mincovers.py generates exactly these rectangles. An independent calculation confirmed the zero count. Hence every eligible U has only two cover clauses. This rules out all triple leakage sets. Five of the outstanding pairs contain a size-three row; in the other three (18,29,114) the size-four rows intersect in three vertices. Thus all eight are excluded.

A second check generated every positive-covering placement from the minimum covers and all physical possibilities for the fourth triple. Across 14,104 chronological prefixes there were 14,300 (prefix,U) choices. None rejected both rows of any of the eight representatives.
