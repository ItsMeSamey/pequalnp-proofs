# Six ANDs, one exposed AND, and a raw-only terminal operand

Use the official graph edges in h5_raw_terminal.py. The sole exposed terminal computes F=(OR A) AND G on the ten raw variables. This is only the subcase with a raw-only operand A. Each edge input is true, hence A is a vertex cover. The opposite operand is a monotone superfunction of F. If G=F, dropping the final AND leaves a circuit for F with at most five ANDs and at most eleven original ORs.

If G>F, any extra true input W must be disjoint from A. Let Z be its complement shore V minus A, independent since A is a cover. For each a in A, monotonicity of G gives F(W+a)=1, so W has a neighbor of a. Hence W dominates A, Z is maximal independent, and G equals F except for a nonempty upward family of subsets of Z which dominate A. Conversely every such family defines a monotone G realizing the displayed factorization: any strict extension containing an A-vertex acquires an edge. The exhaustive ten-bit checker verifies 16 choices of Z and 45 distinct strictly larger functions G; every extra input has size at least three. Both G=F and all strict cases reject every singleton and every nonedge pair. Thus the OR expansion of G has no raw leaves. Its nonempty direct hidden support K has between one and five elements.

If all five hidden gates occur in K, clamp their internal uses to zero while retaining all five direct collector contributions. On a G-positive input the earliest previously true hidden gate remains true, and on a negative all values decrease. Each reobtained gate is the AND of two raw ORs, possibly zero. Rejection of all singletons forces its two raw shores disjoint. Rejection of every nonedge pair forces their complete bipartite graph to lie in the target graph. This graph is C4-free, so every nonempty positive contribution on a two-bit input is a star. Five stars cannot cover all thirteen edges: the two vertex-disjoint pentagons need at least six cover vertices. Thus |K|≤4. This argument uses clamping only for semantic impossibility and does not assume any favorable OR rewiring.

Apply the OR support graph necessity to all twelve actual operand masks on ten raw and five hidden labels. A and K are disjoint connected induced sets, using at least |A|−1 and |K|−1 distinct edges. Contract each to a separate representative in the support graph, drop loops and merge duplicate edges. Every projected original scope remains connected, as the image of a connected subgraph. The remaining budget is at most 13−|A|−|K|. One may also contract A alone for bound 12−|A|. This is a necessary graph relaxation; a contraction does not eliminate shared gates in the physical circuit. In strict cases |A| is six or seven; OR sharing between all original operands is allowed. No claim is made for a terminal with hidden sources on both operands.
