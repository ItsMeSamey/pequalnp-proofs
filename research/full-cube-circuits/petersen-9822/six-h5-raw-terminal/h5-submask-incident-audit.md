# Fixed-mask enumeration receipt

The shared h5_submasks.py generator holds its input mask fixed throughout and updates only the current submask. Zero inclusion is explicit. The active h5_linear_4332.py uses it for the common cover, possible shores and relaxed OR bank. The check compares ordered lists against independently generated combinations of bit positions for every ten-bit mask, including all nonconsecutive masks, in both zero conventions. It adds sparse wide, sixteen-bit and negative-input tests.

The earlier corrected mixed-width producer at frozen-4d8ed9a1/h5_linear_4332.py used the same fixed-mask recurrence directly. Its immutable complete eight-row receipt has hash 1c031d64 and contains 17,500 oriented placements with no passing suffix. In a distinct check the auditor swept every raw mask numerically, compared sets of complete placements, and checked the three-sink coverage by union DP. The mutable-mask defects affected preliminary work in three places:
* The historical first-raw-product extension search underestimated maximum matching. The conjecture that matching never exceeds three is false, e.g. shores 2 and 84. The corrected fixed-source computation and independent all-raw-product enumeration gave maximum six at shores 68 and 130. Later t4 and t3 proofs used the corrected list.
* A discarded draft mixed-width producer emitted zero candidate placements. The reviewer independently exhibited actual candidate terminals, and the corrected producer used the fixed original mask.
* The reviewer's preliminary mixed-width submask generator returned one of five possible Y masks, giving one fifth of the true placement counts. Its final independent numerical all-mask enumeration reproduced the corrected producer's full set.

A source scan of resolution-bridge checked decrement-and-mask forms, reversed forms, compound bitwise assignments and named subset helpers in the Python/C/C++/JS sources. The H5 loops in check_h5_parallel.py, h5_four_sink_zero_grants.py, h5_pairing_corrected.py and h5_zero_three_four_corners.py all use a fixed outer mask. h5_zero_three_four_terminals.py imports the latter helper. The frozen corrected mixed-width producer also uses the fixed outer mask. The C loops in probe_aux_patterns.c and two_pointer_search.c use fixed f and ok[0], respectively. Other syntactic hits were shifts or ordinary bit bounds rather than submask traversal. No additional affected claim was identified in this source scan. This audit does not attest to unrelated projects or undocumented temporary code.
