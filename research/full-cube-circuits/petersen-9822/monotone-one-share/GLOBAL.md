For completeness let S be the raws absent from the exterior after cutting H. They are independent for a cold H, since on a pair within S the only changing exterior bit could be H, while H alone cannot trigger the exterior at zero other raws if H accepts an independent input. More directly, the exterior must reject the all-zero raw assignment even with H=1 by monotonicity from that accepted independent input. An unsafe maximal independent set accepted by H must contain S: adding any absent member of S preserves H=1 and exterior inputs. Hence |S|≤4 by the graph's independence number. The exterior needs 10−|S| raw leaves and at least two H leaves, so an inner cost of at least ten forces equality everywhere, |S|=4, cost ten. The accepted maximal independent set is precisely S. With S on and H=1, adding any outside raw triggers the target. Therefore the exterior at H=1 computes the OR of all six outside raws. Each outside raw occurs once. In that formula every maximal subtree with raws must be false at zero raws after pinning H=1, or its raws would cease to be individually pivotal there. No AND can have two raw-bearing children, or its singleton pivots would be blocked. Every AND thus has an H-only operand. Pin H=0: the resulting function is an OR of a subset of raws, necessarily empty by singleton coldness of the target and helper. Thus the entire exterior computes H AND the disjunction of six raws, costing six gates with only one use of H, which contradicts the established formula bound.

This is a finite and mathematical proof for the monotone model. An unrestricted circuit can also contain NOT gates. Independent checker receipts are identified in the component source manifests.
