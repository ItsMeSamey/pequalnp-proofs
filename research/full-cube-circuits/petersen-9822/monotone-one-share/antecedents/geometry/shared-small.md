# A single shared gate computed with one or two binary gates

Write the circuit as a helper formula with h leaves feeding an outer formula with p leaves on the raw alphabet enlarged by H. A seventeen-gate circuit gives p+h≤19. Repeated variables for h≤3 simplify via absorption or idempotence.

For H an AND of two variables, a conjunction of three, or x AND (y OR z), set its occurrences to false. The resulting monotone formula is bounded by the original target. Its only potentially missing minimal positive inputs are graph edges on which H was true. For a pure conjunction this is at most one edge. For the mixed conjunction these edges form a substar of at most two edges centered at x. Append that substar, when nonempty, by disjunction. These repairs preserve the full truth table: all minimal positive assignments are accepted and every new disjunct implies the target. At most two gates are needed for one edge and three for two edges sharing a center. These are paid by deleting the helper and folding at least one outer parent of a helper occurrence. For H=x OR (y AND z), replace every occurrence with x. Only edge yz can be lost, and adding it costs at most the two gates removed from H. Each case gives an ordinary formula with at most seventeen gates, contradicted by the exact formula bound.

The other helpers are ORs of two or three distinct raws. For raw set S the extra protocol label is valid on positive edges meeting S and maximal independent sets disjoint from S. A circuit yields a monotone protocol with at most 19−|S| leaves in this enlarged alphabet. Rational cell weights summing to more than that threshold exclude it. For equality apply the checker described in tight-monotone-formula.md with this extra label. This protocol relaxation allows any number of uses of the extra label.

The standard-library verifier verify-helper-weights.py reconstructs the original graph domain, checks all 165 rational certificates in its enlarged alphabets, and transports one certificate by graph reflection. It finds 161 strict inequalities. The four boundary sets {0,3}, {0,9}, {3,9}, {0,3,9} have no tight protocol, verified by tight_helper.py with the helper vertices supplied as individual command-line integers. The state counts are 5,289, 2,802, 4,136 and 1,660, respectively. The source files and weight ledger must be in the same working directory. Consequently size seventeen is impossible throughout the one-shared-gate class with a helper of one or two gates. No assumption about circuit size outside that class is needed apart from the established exact monotone formula bound.
