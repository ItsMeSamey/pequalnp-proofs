# A singleton-cold eight-gate helper is impossible at total size seventeen

Assume one shared intermediate H, eight binary gates in its inner formula, 17 or fewer gates overall. Write S for the raws private to H, U for those only in the outer formula J, and T for the other raws. The inner has nine leaves, so |U|≥1. The outer has at most ten leaves including two copies of H and all 10−|S| exterior raws, so |S|≥2. Every H-hot maximal independent set contains S, since adding any missing private raw retains H and the exterior inputs but creates an edge. At least one such set M is necessary: otherwise H implies the target, allowing all exterior H occurrences to be bypassed with false and one copy of H ORed onto the output. The result is a formula at no increase in size, contradicting the formula lower bound eighteen. Thus S is independent. No edge joins S and U, since singleton coldness means adding a private singleton to an exterior singleton cannot change H. A maximum independent S of size four dominates all other vertices, which would leave no U. Therefore |S| is two or three.

Two adjacent singly occurring U variables cannot occur in J: after fixing all other raws H is independent of them, whereas the graph exhibits both AND and OR in its two-essential sections on this pair. AND comes from an empty context, OR by setting one further neighbor of each endpoint true; the two chosen neighbors are distinct and nonadjacent by girth five. The operation at the lowest common ancestor of two singly occurring variables in a monotone formula fixes the type of all its two-essential sections, even with variable-dependent values elsewhere fixed. Hence the repeated U raws cover its induced edges. At most |S|−2 exterior raw names can repeat because two leaves are reserved for H. Deleting this cover from U gives an independent set together with S, of size at most four. Thus |U|≤2. The inner repeat allowance is at most |U|−1 distinct labels.

For each permitted (S,T,U,M), force values of H and of J0,J1 obtained from J by fixing its helper argument. The Boolean identity on every full assignment is F=J_H, with J0≤J1. In particular J0≤F≤J1. Require H to be false at zero, on the singletons, and at maximal independent sets missing any member of S. Require H(M)=1. Outer cofactor tables ignore S; H ignores U. Iteratively saturate the positive and negative entries under these independences and monotonicity. Also force H=1 from F=1,J0=0; H=0 from F=0,J1=1; J0=1 from F=1,H=0; J1=0 from F=0,H=1. All four implications are necessary, even without assuming this propagation finds every consequence.

For H and J separately collect pairs of raw labels on which some prescribed essential section is AND while another prescribed section is OR. For J the two sections may use different values of its helper input. By the fixed lowest-common-ancestor argument at least one endpoint of each conflict must repeat. Enumerate whether any |U|−1 raw labels of H or |S|−2 exterior labels of J cover their respective conflicts. All 682 labeled cases are examined. Of these, 122 pass the inner test; none of those pass the outer test.

cold8.py uses 1,024-bit integers for the full input cube; bit position m is ordinary unsigned assignment mask m. Each H/J table is represented by disjoint bitsets of required positives and negatives. cold8-control.py checks forced entries against a real decomposition of the same graph on all 1,024 assignments, plus direct controls for the conflict tests. No external solver is required.
