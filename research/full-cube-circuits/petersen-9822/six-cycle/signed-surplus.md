# Exact placement of six surplus input occurrences

Assume an optimal 15-binary signed DAG for 9822 and the private-chain results in signed-protected.md and signed-private-chains.md. The internal fanout surplus is the sum, over every non-output gate, of its number of downstream input occurrences minus one. Add to this the sum of raw use counts minus one across the ten essential raws. There are 30 gate inputs, of which 10 are needed for the first occurrence of each raw and 14 for the first use of each non-output gate. Hence the combined surplus equals six. All counts refer to physically used live wires, with free signs.

By the two signed positive private chains, Gi=xi OR Pi up to output-edge sign for i in {1,2}. Each Pi is nonraw and has a live reader beyond Gi. They must be different underlying wires. If they were the same wire, that wire would precede both owners and be independent of both raws. On the all-zero outside background the function of the two raws is AND, forcing both guards to be zero. They must consequently have the same sign. Writing P for the common guard, whenever P=1 the original ignores both raws by their unique entries, and gives one because the assignment with both raws true contains edge 12. Bypass G1,G2 to raw wires (preserving downstream signs) and append disjunction with P at the output. On P=0 all computations are unchanged; on P=1 the result is one as required. This saves one binary gate, contradicting the signed lower of 15. Thus P1,P2 account for at least two internal surplus occurrences. The total raw surplus is at most four.

Furthermore each Hi, the sole immediate reader of Gi, has no raw operand j: fixing j at its controlling value would kill the sole path from xi to the output while leaving xi pivotal on some background. For j distinct from i use minimum degree two and absence of C4; j=i contradicts raw uniqueness. Thus H1,H2 are distinct from all four raw entries.

Pinning every raw outside an induced four-vertex path to zero can create a signed alias ending at a fanout gate. The alias can make a singly read original raw feed several remaining gates. Therefore this restriction alone does not establish that each induced P4 originally had a raw with multiple readers.
