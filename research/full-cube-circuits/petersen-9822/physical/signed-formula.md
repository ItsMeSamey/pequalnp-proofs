# Internal sharing at fifteen binary gates

Assume a trimmed fifteen-binary signed circuit has no shared internal node. Push all signs down its formula tree. Each essential increasing variable occurs positively. Every raw except 9 occurs at most twice: its zero-face requires at least thirteen signed gates, and all occurrences have distinct binary parents (equal or opposing sibling literals simplify). Pinning a raw deletes each parent.

If a raw occurs once positively and once negatively, consider their lowest common ancestor L. When L is OR, replace the negative literal by true. Any output change is an increase at raw value one. If it changes the output, the negative branch of L now gives true. In the original formula at zero that branch is equally true regardless of the positive branch. All nodes above L have other inputs independent of this raw. The original output was therefore true at zero, contradicting monotonicity. When L is AND, replace the negative literal by false. A change would decrease the true output at zero, yet the original false negative branch at one would give a false output there. Either replacement folds its parent, contradicting the signed floor fifteen.

Thus the only possible negative leaves are copies of NOT(raw 9). If none exist this is a monotone size-fifteen circuit. Otherwise share a single physical inverter across all negative leaves of 9. On pinning raw 9 zero, at least one positive parent and a distinct negative parent disappear along with that inverter. The result has at most thirteen monotone gates, contradicting the checked minimum fourteen for G minus 9.

Consequently at least one internal binary gate must have fanout two or more; at most five units of total fanout excess six can belong to raw inputs. This excludes the formula sector only. Inputs to this argument are the signed Face true floors in physical/seed_surplus.py and seed_surplus.log (9752 and 9825), physical/binary-fifteen.md, monotone-seventeen.md, and verification/h9.md.
