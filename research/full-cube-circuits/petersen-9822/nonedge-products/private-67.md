# The outer-seven private OR pair 6,7 is impossible

Use private-or-pair.md for a reduced seventeen-site circuit with private H=6 OR 7 and seven outer ANDs. Write z=NOT H. For s=6,7 the only external positive reader is U_s=s AND P_s. Its sole successor V_s is OR. The zero-clamped P_6 is true on the singletons 1,8,9, the neighbors of 6. The physical inverter has exactly one reader r. The U sites differ: a common reader would be 6 AND 7, unable to carry the required edges to other neighbors when z=0.

Zeroing either s removes exactly H, U_s and V_s, since the nonconstant target G−s has a signed lower bound fourteen. The remaining circuit has thirteen binary gates (six ANDs, seven ORs) and the sole NOT on the other raw t. The double-zero graph K consists of the five-cycle on A, the pendant edge 49, and an isolated edge 12. It has ten maximal independent sets: if 4 is absent, 9 is included and the remaining cycle P4 has three maximal independent sets; if 4 is included there are two choices on the remaining adjacent cycle pair. Multiply five by two choices on the isolated edge. Thus K requires four ANDs. It requires six ORs: zero 1 then 9 while preserving the five-cycle, each time losing an OR by essentiality and nonzero residual; the cycle costs four more. Its size is at least ten.

In either single deletion no OR gate can directly read NOT t. Pinning t=0 can lose only one OR by the six-OR double floor. A negative OR reader would be the unique OR folded and would be forced to one. Every positive path from t avoiding its inverter must meet a first OR along its initial AND-only segment (otherwise the zero cofactor vanishes identically), and every such first OR folds on this pin. Thus all positive influence of t passes through this same gate, already one at t=0. This gate and NOT t can only decrease when t is added, contradicting increasing essential dependence. Consequently the physical r originally was AND: otherwise it would have to be removed in each single deletion, forcing r=V_6=V_7. That common OR already reads both distinct U_6,U_7 and cannot also read z.

In the single deletion the positive reader of t is still uniquely U_t: the only removed sites were H (which feeds only the inverter), U_s (forced zero), and V_s which bypasses its other operand. This last operand cannot be raw t or any occurrence of it introduced by the other two removals, since t has no exterior direct use other than U_t. U_t remains AND. On now setting t zero, U_t folds; at least one distinct AND reading the inverter folds; and an OR also folds on a positive path as above. The resulting circuit for K has at most ten gates, hence exactly ten. Combining the two pins removes exactly seven original sites by pin simplification. There is no budget for any further deletion of unused or bypassable sites.

Track P_6 through the double restriction. On singletons 1,8,9 with 6 and 7 zero its value can only increase from z=0 to actual z=1 in the monotone outer circuit. At the all-zero raw input all outer signals evaluate to zero except z: its unique reader r is AND and its other operand precedes r, so it is zero. Therefore the specialized P_6 is neither a constant nor a raw. Ordinary constant folding represents its value by a surviving binary site, possibly via aliases. That site cannot be discarded as unused: the seven removals already paid for by the pins leave the exact ten-gate lower bound. In particular an internal signal of the ten-gate circuit is true at singleton 1.

Finally pin 1=true in K. The result is 2 OR (cycle A with pendant 49). It requires nine binary gates by successively zeroing essential raws 2 and 9 and using the seven-gate lower bound for the cycle. Thus raw 1 has exactly one reader in the ten-gate monotone circuit. This must be AND, since an OR would become one and force an additional successor to fold or give a constant output. Every nonraw gate then rejects singleton 1: the first positive gate would have to combine the lone true raw with another true signal, or be its direct OR. This contradicts the surviving P_6 signal. Only the named private outer-seven pair is excluded here.
