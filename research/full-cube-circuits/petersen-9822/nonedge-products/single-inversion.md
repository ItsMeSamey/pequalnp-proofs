# Positive replacement of a single inversion

Suppose a circuit for the ten-vertex graph edge predicate F contains one NOT and b≤16 binary AND/OR sites. Trim unused gates. Write H for the monotone predicate feeding the NOT and G(x,z) for the output with the NOT output replaced by a freely chosen input z. The same positive wires may occur in H and G. The two cofactors G0 and G1 satisfy G0≤F≤G1 on every assignment.

Let P be the graph edges e where G0(e)=0 and N the maximal independent sets I where G1(I)=1. An arbitrary monotone substitute Z produces F exactly if Z(e)=1 for every e in P and Z(I)=0 for every I in N. Indeed the two tests enforce all the minimal positives and maximal negatives, and the substituted circuit is monotone. Thus the condition is also sufficient on the full cube. Necessarily H(e)=0 on P and H(I)=1 on N. If P or N is empty, pinning the NOT output to the appropriate constant computes F. This folds at least one binary reader of the NOT output; a sole NOT cannot be the output of a nonconstant increasing target. A circuit with b≤16 would yield ≤15 total gates, contradicting the known lower bound 16. In particular H must reject a target edge and accept a maximal independent set.

Let J be any subgraph of the target graph containing P. Its edge predicate is a valid positive substitute. A target-only choice uses the edges e with H(e)=0, which always contain P. Let d be the number of binary ancestors of the NOT that are used only through the NOT; these disappear on replacement. Let f be the number of distinct direct binary readers of the NOT output. Inserting the J predicate gives a monotone circuit of size at most b−d+c(J), where c(J) is any positive construction cost. Alternatively pin the NOT output to zero, removing all f readers as well as the d ancestors, and append the J predicate to G0 by OR. This gives b−d−f+c(J)+1. Thus for nonempty J the effective saving before paying c(J) is at least d+max(0,f−1). Overlap between the added graph and edges already in G0 is harmless. A nonempty J has a star construction of size |E(J)|+τ(J)−1: assign its edges to a vertex cover and collect the resulting disjoint edge stars. For empty J pin zero directly. Any result of size at most fifteen is impossible. Whenever this procedure with the star construction gives size at most seventeen, at least two original retained AND gates must already accept nonedges with z=0, by the reviewed monotone lower for circuits with at most one such AND. The inserted star products reject all nonedges. On a nonedge the substitute is zero too, so both constructions have precisely the z=0 gate values on their retained old sites.

Define S as the vertices accepted singly by H and write s=|S|. One may always use J=G_graph−S, the induced target on the vertices outside S. Edges rejected by H avoid S. For a private prefix, meaning every binary gate of the H cone dies when the NOT is removed, d≥s−1 when s≥1: at least s distinct raws are essential for H, and a connected binary DAG reaching one root from s essential inputs needs at least s−1 gates. If H differs from the raw disjunction of S, at least two further raws are essential. Indeed its restriction outside S rejects all singletons but has some nonempty minterm. Then d≥s+1. No assumption is made about fanout of the NOT output.

* At s≥6 the residual induced graph has at most four vertices. Since the original graph has girth five it is a forest, with at most three edges and vertex cover at most two. Its star cost is at most four, whereas d≥5. This excludes every such private prefix.
* At s=5 the same calculation excludes 93 of the 252 possible sets S for every private prefix (residual star cost at most three). For a private H differing from OR(S), d≥6; all 247 complements except the five induced five-cycles are excluded. In a remaining five-cycle, if H accepts even one of its edges, the H-rejected edge graph is contained in a five-vertex path, whose cost is at most five. That excludes this case too.
* At s=4 exactly 37 of the 210 sets yield a private-prefix replacement of size at most seventeen from d≥3 and f≥1 alone. This is a conversion, not a contradiction by the sixteen-gate lower bound.

The companion not-prefix.py computes the finite counts from the thirteen listed edges by direct minimum-vertex-cover enumeration. These are statements about the one-inversion stratum; they leave the unrestricted minimum in [16,18].
