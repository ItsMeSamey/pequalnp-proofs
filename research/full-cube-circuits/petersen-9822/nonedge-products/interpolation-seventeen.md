# Composition-sensitive replacement of a sole NOT with sixteen binary gates

Suppose all monotone realizations of the target of size at most seventeen have exactly seven ANDs and that size sixteen is impossible. Let J be a nonempty target subgraph whose edges include those rejected by F0. Write c,t for the total and AND counts of a chosen monotone realization of J. Let d,u be the total and AND counts of private inverter ancestors, and f,v the total and AND counts among the direct inversion readers. Write a for the original binary AND count.

Direct substitution of J for the inverter gives total 16−d+c with a−u+t ANDs. Alternately take F0 OR J; this has total at most 17−d−f+c, with a−u−v+t ANDs before further simplification. To count exactly, remove the private sites, bypass the f direct gates at z=0 (including any resulting constants), leave every other old site in place, and append the indicated repair and output OR. If counting unused or constant-bearing gates overestimates the mathematical circuit size at budget seventeen, dropping even one would contradict the size-sixteen lower bound. Thus at a total bound seventeen each displayed AND count must equal seven.

In particular if c≤d+f−1 the clamp gives a contradiction. If c=d+1, direct substitution fits seventeen. The alternative already fits sixteen if f≥2. When f=1 exactly, both formulas fit seventeen and their AND counts differ by v. Hence the region c=d+1 is impossible if the sole reader is AND. The surviving equality f=1 with OR reader instead forces a−u+t=7. At c=d+f and f>1 the clamp forces a−u−v+t=7. No raw-reader hypotheses are used.

For a second composition test let n0,n1 count the graph's sixteen maximal independent sets according to the value of H there. Each H=0 set is a maximal negative input of F1: F1 equals the actual target there and dominates it on all strict supersets. Each H=1 set is a maximal negative of F0 since H stays one upon enlargement. If n0>0, F0 has at least one additional maximal negative: it rejects every graph-independent input, and a target maximal independent set with H=0 cannot be contained in any of the distinct n1 maximal independent sets with H=1. Both clamped circuits retain at most a−u−v ANDs; every monotone circuit with k ANDs has at most 2^k maximal negative inputs by the direction argument. Thus n0≤2^(a−u−v) and n1+1≤2^(a−u−v) if n0>0. Since n0+n1=16, at least four ANDs survive each formal removal count a−u−v. This bound does not assume H has an accepted singleton or any special physical reader pattern.
