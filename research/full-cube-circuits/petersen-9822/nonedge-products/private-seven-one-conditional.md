# Seven outer ANDs with exactly one conditional switch

Assume the private inverter input is the OR of two raws, the whole circuit costs at most seventeen, and the outer cone has seven ANDs with exactly one conditional activation. Let K0 be the outer circuit clamped at z=0. At least one outer OR folds on this clamp (a path carrying zero to the nonzero output must pass an OR). Thus K0 has at most fourteen gates and seven ORs. It is ≤F on every assignment. Every edge K0 misses has two nonhot endpoints, since any hot endpoint makes z=0.

On the direction cube the unconditional rays are the twelve signed coordinate literals for six axes. The conditional rays are C+=U∧b and C−=U∧¬b, where U is a union of earlier unconditional literals. It is nonempty and nonuniversal. Each raw or z is observed on a union of rays. A collection of rays covers the cube if and only if it contains a complementary unconditional literal pair, or contains both C signs and an unconditional literal opposite one of the literals of U. Indeed in the absence of an unconditional complementary pair the assignments falsifying all unconditional literals form a nonempty face. Both C signs are needed to cover its two b-fibers, and U must be identically true on the face. For a non-tautological disjunction U this happens precisely when a literal is forced true there.

Apply this to the combined rays of Z and a missed edge. A covering literal pair must use Z, else K0 accepts the edge. But Z plus either nonhot endpoint is not a cover, since that endpoint's singleton is rejected with z=1. Thus only a covering triple is possible. Name its roles +, −, L, where any literal opposite any term of U fills role L. Z must supply exactly one role: none would place the full cover in the raw endpoints, and any two plus the remaining raw endpoint would falsely accept a singleton. The role supplied is uniform over all missed edges, since any representative of L works equally well. No nonhot raw can itself supply both remaining roles. All nonhot raws supplying one remaining role therefore form a class disjoint from those supplying the other. Every cross pair is a positive edge, because z=1 and all three roles are present. This biclique is a star by the absence of four-cycles. Hence the set of missed edges is contained in one star.

If there are at most two missing edges, OR the missing star into K0, using at most two further ORs (combine the leaves if needed, and append the star). This computes F by monotonicity, since K0≤F and all minimal positive inputs are edges. At most nine ORs contradict the ten-paid-site floor in duality/unrestricted-ten-paid.md. Otherwise, since max degree is three, all three edges incident at some center v are missed and every other edge is covered. Thus K0 at v=0 computes G−v. Using the previously established minimum of at least fourteen monotone gates for each vertex-deleted graph, no live reader of v can occur in K0: pinning it would leave at most thirteen. Fix either hot owner s, necessarily distinct from v and its three neighbors by the missed-edge condition. On the assignment {s,v,w} for each neighbor w of v, F=1 and z=0, so K0(s,w)=1 by independence of v. Thus s must neighbor all three w, contradicting absence of four-cycles. This excludes the one-conditional sector for every number of direct raw masks, conditional on the stated cofactor lower bounds.
