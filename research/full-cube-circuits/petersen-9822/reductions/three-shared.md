# Exactly three shared binary gates are insufficient

Assume a signed 15-binary-gate solution for graph 9822 and the protected raw readers, positive private chains, and shared guards in comb/signed-protected.md, signed-private-chains.md and signed-surplus.md. Write Gi=xi OR Pi for i=1,2, with Pi a fixed signed polarity. By third-shared.md, if only P1,P2,Q are shared then one owner first reaches Q, the other first reaches the output. The graph automorphism (0 3)(1 2)(5 8)(6 7) lets us take raw 1 first Q, raw 2 first output. P1 precedes Q through the private entry of 1. The three candidate chronological orders are 012,102,021, using indices 0=P1,1=P2,2=Q. With six fanout-excess units at most three distinct raws R can repeat, all outside 0,1,2,3. Assign every remaining raw to its first shared site or output. Each bin is independent. A singly-used raw 6 cannot first reach P1 since 16 is an edge; raw 7 cannot first reach P2 since 27 is an edge (same pivot argument as third-shared.md).

Fix R and all earlier bin raws to arbitrary t. Write S for the current bin, U for the later bins including output. Every path from S to the output meets the current gate, whose signal is independent of U: a U raw leading to that gate would first reach an earlier shared site or the current one. Thus the target table with rows s and columns u, at fixed t, has at most two distinct rows. Likewise with only R fixed, the raws of the first k bins communicate to the outside by at most k bits determined by them and R. Their table therefore has at most 2^k rows.

At a guard Pi, each row depending on owner i forces Pi=0: for some completion u the owner must toggle the target, and its only reader is xi OR Pi. The guard is already determined by s,t. Two distinct rows both depending on i contradict the one-bit property, since both must send the same bit. Complemented uses of Pi downstream are allowed.

The labeled counts by number of repeated raws 0,1,2,3 are 832,3632,5796,4292 after the initial structural tests. All 14552 placements and 43656 chronological cases were examined. Pure causal row counts retain 102 placements, all with three repeats, in 249 orders. Each of the 249 fails a guard test. three_bins.py and guard_bins.py use adjacency to compute row signatures; truth_replay.py instead enumerates all 1024 truth values and compares literal cofactor rows. audit_guard.py emits one explicit pair of owner-pivotal rows and distinguishing column for each order, in audit_guard.log. The independent verification/audit-three.py reproduced all counts and checked every emitted obstruction against the original function.

The optional smaller replay only458.py assumes the repeated raws are exactly {4,5,8}. Of 128 static placements, four in nine chronological orders pass pure causal cuts; each fails guard visibility. The general computation does not use this extra premise.

Consequently at least four distinct shared internal gates and at most two raw fanout-excess units are necessary. This does not by itself exclude four or more shared gates.
