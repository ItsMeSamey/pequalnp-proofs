# Six-axis terminal checker and padding audit for graph 9822

The checker takes the six chronological nonempty activation masks, independently selectable selector bits, ten observed raw masks and a budget of eight paid binary unions in the reverse scan. An edge must have full union and each of the sixteen maximal independent sets must have a direction reaching exactly its complement. These witnesses force ten distinct incomparable proper raw masks. If exactly d are primitive, the others consume 10−d joins, leaving d−2. Direct raws occupy distinct switch axes and form an independent set; consequently 2≤d≤4. The two-direct full-axis lemma is a separate hypothesis used by the prefix test (boolean/direct-switches.md). The physical common-bank extraction is And9822CommonScan.common_from_scan in the separate Lean development; use the actual namespace/signature in that file. The native exhaustive terminal cover below has its own evidence status.

The catalogue has 41,719 canonical rows. Grouped prefix and suffix tests are in JOINT-PREFIX.md; counts below refer to its remaining catalogue IDs. Let W be the distinct activations after removing full and primitive values; let G be the subset of W which are not observed raw values. The disjunction is over the actual G for a candidate. Testing q=0,1,2 in the prefix is a necessary filter for |G|≤q, hence row lists overlap even when the terminal choices of (d,|G|) do not.

Every bank grants the primitive rays simultaneously. It may grant a nonraw named activation without pricing its producer; the slot for that activation is still reserved. Other distinct named masks must be witnessed as raw masks. An auxiliary serving a raw cannot depend by pure union ancestry on another distinct raw. Only this observation and the counted spare auxiliary slots justify bounding auxiliary operands or leaf counts. For a fixed partial assignment, subdivide direction traces by assigned raw membership, and compare each cell's available traces with its number of demanded MIS witnesses. Two-variable refinements must pass on the same conditional cells; a common-bank test must preserve one bank for all assigned values. Pair consistency by itself need not suffice. The archived programs either exhibit empty domains, perform complete branching where needed, or verify a terminal proof instruction. No hypothesis requires every primitive to be selected. ## Five-to-six padding

Lift each five-bit mask T by T×{0,1}, append a full activation on the fresh bit, and retain exactly the old joins and observations. The dummy arms are the two half-cubes, neither equal to a lifted nonempty raw or a lifted old activation. Every old ray, every old receiver and every named raw identity is preserved. Both new rays may have zero users. Exactly the same union recipes work with the new rays unused. Direction traces double before compression by observations; finer trace classes increase allowable capacities. The 16 originally distinct witness traces still exist, and every old full-union edge equation lifts. Padding also works when fewer than five axes are active by repeated independent full splits. Starting from a physical circuit with dead gates requires the scan/hypotheses that justify its eight-union bound; the padding itself does not spend a join and does not add a forward physical gate to the size budget.

The generator admits full at every step, including the first; its paid-activation cap of four is unchanged by a full dummy. Its trace cutoff (ten) is weaker than the sixteen distinct required MIS witnesses. It requires nonempty activation, which the dummy satisfies. There is no raw-use test for either new branch in the generator, the prefix tests, helper enumeration or the hard-row tape. Canonical signed-coordinate permutations preserve these properties; the output representative is an actually generated chronological row. If a different representative places the dummy earlier, the prefix tests still use only necessary conditions on the currently last axis. The prefix strict-containment argument applies when that last axis is direct; it never forces it to be direct. A new unconditional dummy can make a conditional-bound test weaker, which preserves exclusion. Neither global positivity of a ray on the abstract cube nor its presence in the available dictionary establishes consumption by an observed raw.

For all-full activations each proper raw is a union of a consistent set of signed literals on independent coordinates. An adjacent pair must include a complementary literal pair, since otherwise both raw unions can be falsified simultaneously. For each coordinate, the vertices using opposite signs therefore form a complete bipartite subgraph, and these subgraphs cover every edge. Empty shores yield no edges. Since the graph has no four-cycle each nonempty edge-bearing biclique is a star, and covering the edges takes at least six stars (minimum vertex cover six). Thus every coordinate in an all-full six-axis candidate would have to contribute an edge. In a padded all-full candidate every observed raw ignores the dummy bit; its consistent literal union cannot contain a dummy literal without becoming dependent on that bit. This gives at most five stars and rules out such padding without retapping or charging a dummy physical gate. For the genuinely six-active all-full case, use the retapped star counting proof in ../../six17-cert/README.md: 13 edges and at least six centers cost ≥18 gates. Do not invoke a presumed six physically used gates on the padded case.

## Checks and scope

padding-probes.py exhausts all directions for full, conditional and nonprimitive five-axis examples; the respective primitive trace counts go 32→64, 24→48 and 28→56. It checks that the added rays do not change directness of any original tested pair-union and that the named masks lift, then computes the four-cycle and vertex-cover facts from the thirteen graph edges. The general padding proof above is independent of those three probes. Chamber controls include both signs of the 1,024-input physical size-18 example, the successful three-helper/failing two-helper four-direct test, the successful two-helper/failing one-helper three-direct test and the synthetic prefix injection control. No successful planted relaxed control establishes a size-17 circuit. SIX-ASSEMBLY.sha256 pins the constituent manifests. This package maps the complete native checks; no Lean compilation or terminal kernel replay was performed for this map.
