When all three conditional switches have different nonprimitive activations, there are just four ordinary roots. Move their full activations first. Construct the activation of each remaining gate as an arbitrary union of prior branches, discarding full, empty, primitive and previously chosen masks. The first activation is a union of two, three or four ordinary signed literals up to hypercube symmetry; as a fraction of 128 directions its size is 96,112 or120. The signed permutations retained at each prefix preserve every old activation equation with its switch relabeling and preserve the named previous activation masks, hence every orbit represented has equivalent raw feasibility. three_paid.py generates 4,736,6,486,3,979 profiles respectively. The full list can be regenerated from source and its SHA256 is in three-paid-census.txt.
