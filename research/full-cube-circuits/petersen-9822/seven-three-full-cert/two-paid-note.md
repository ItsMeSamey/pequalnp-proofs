With exactly three conditional switches and exactly two distinct nonprimitive masks, call those masks U,V in the order of their first available feeding occurrence. Treat every switch consuming U or V as a distinguished root. The other conditional switches, if any, have a previously available signed branch as their activation, so have a literal parent. This gives a forest with four ordinary roots, a nonempty distinguished U-root forest, and a nonempty distinguished V-root forest; five size/shape triples fit seven switches. The first occurrence of U uses only branches of the ordinary forest. The first occurrence of V can also use branches of the U-forest. All U-root switches can tap the first U, so placing every U-root tree before V only enlarges the physical scheduling choice. This order is available for the branch equations regardless of other shared union gates. The chronological union costs and raw constraints are imposed by the solver. The generator removes zero, full and primitive options at each step; for V it also removes U. No later-created primitive branch can equal a prior mask, since it depends on its bit. The signed permutations used in options preserve every prior activation equation up to the same permutation of switch positions, and the second orbit pass fixes U. This produces 2082 signed profiles. Duplicate orbits beyond the subgroup used are harmless.
