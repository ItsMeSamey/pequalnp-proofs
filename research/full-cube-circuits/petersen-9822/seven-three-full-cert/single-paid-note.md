For four unconditional switches and three conditional switches, suppose precisely one conditional activation is not a signed branch. Designate that switch as a new root. Every other conditional switch reads a branch from an earlier switch. Choose that branch as its parent. Ignoring the activation of the designated root gives two sets of signed trees: a forest with four ordinary roots, and the single tree at the distinguished root. There are eight unordered pairs of shapes. Every node in the ordinary forest can be processed before the distinguished root since its activation depends only on its own ancestors there. Its branch orientations are independent of inputs in the distinguished tree. The distinguished root's own activation must be a union of branches available earlier, all belonging to the ordinary forest; the full output as a source would make its activation full. All unions are enumerated and empty, full or primitive activations are excluded. A descendant branch depends on its own later bit and cannot equal the activation.

single_paid.py enumerates swaps or flips of ordinary switch axes preserving all ordinary activation equations with the same permutation on switch positions. These transformations preserve the entire ordinary signed primitive library, fix the distinguished-tree coordinates, and transport the whole completed activation pattern once its root activation is transported. Computing the action via any selected union recipe is legitimate because it comes from a permutation of directions. The overlap of ordinary and distinguished activations imposes no further restriction in the generator. The resulting 177 rows form an exhaustive representative list; additional equivalences might remain.

repeat_paid.py uses a distinguished forest of two or three roots with one common nonprimitive activation mask. The first physical occurrence of this mask must be reachable without any branch downstream of any occurrence. Thus ordinary-root components provide all operands needed to build that first copy. All later copies can tap it. This adds 18 profiles for two repeated roots and three profiles for three roots. The results cover at most one distinct nonprimitive mask. The sibling seven-two-cert package contains the physical controls and search kernel derivations.
