# The six-AND case at 16 total gates for 9822

The graph, maximal independent sets, reverse representation and five-switch proof are described in five-switch.md. With six live ANDs, size at most sixteen allows seven reverse unions. At least 10−d distinct unions serve nonbranch raws; since direct receivers occupy distinct switch axes and are graph-independent, d is either 3 or 4. Thus the number of other unions is zero or one.

profile6.cpp with arguments 10 4 enumerates chronological activations on all 64 directions, quotienting by signs and skipping profiles with fewer than ten different branch signatures. Sixteen signatures are necessary. The first activation is full; the second can only be full or one of the two first branches, as the union of the first branches is full. Thus at most four chronological activations can require a union. The generator's cutoff of four is universal here. canon6.cpp quotients by signed axis permutations, simultaneously moving each activation index and its truth table; the checked corpus has 41,719 representatives. Orbit tests below do not rely on the representative's displayed order.

Write B for all primitive branch masks; W contains distinct activations other than full or any member of B. For d=3 no further union is affordable and every member of W must be a raw, built from at most two primitive branches. For d=4 there is at most one nonreceiver auxiliary h. If it feeds a raw by a union-only path, it cannot depend on another raw on such a path by incomparability; absent an earlier auxiliary, its value equals a union of two primitive masks. If it serves only a split activation, grant an arbitrary member of W. If a value equals a later branch but is needed earlier, granting the branch globally relaxes the count; the extra union may be used for it. The bank enumeration grants B and the optional h, and each raw must be primitive or the union of two granted values. Every W other than h must appear among the ten raw values. Full, duplicate, or unused semantic joins need no further case.

Whenever an activation U of j is also a required raw value, it must be constructed before the split at j. It cannot use j's own branch, or a branch of a switch whose activation depends essentially on selector bit j. Prior to j all available masks are independent of that coordinate. The program grants every other branch and any auxiliary that can be computed as a union of those available masks, ignoring all other chronology. Proper OR predecessors of U must be subsets of U. A raw-serving auxiliary on a union path into this mandatory U cannot be fed by a different raw. When equal activation masks occur at multiple switches, imposing the necessary condition at just one is sufficient for a relaxation. The test allows repeated operands. All filters respect change of signed coordinates. Oracle states are represented by one bit per distinct branch signature, including the full 64-bit case.

six16.cpp applies arc consistency and exact search over the d=3 bank family. For each pair of raws and each of its four membership patterns, its region must have at least as many trace atoms as there are distinct maximal independent sets requiring that pattern. Room intersection and a necessary Hall test implement the entire 16-signature requirement in complete assignments. six16-fast.cpp uses the induced graph condition for efficient d=4 search: for two vertices, the union of their receiver masks is full if and only if those vertices are adjacent, since any nonedge lies in a maximal independent set. Both solvers require all mandatory targets, exactly d primitive receivers, all 13 edge conditions, and distinct witnesses for the sixteen independent sets. The DFS need not require an additional exact matching test at complete assignments: the 16 resulting exact signatures are distinct, hence their nonempty rooms are already disjoint.

The d=3 ledger has 41,719 rows, 2,140 candidate libraries, 144,699 search nodes and no hits. The d=4 ledger has 41,719 rows, 149,091 libraries, 33,085,167 nodes and no hits. Both native kernels independently accept the ten known receiver masks from an 18-gate six-AND circuit. forward.py also round trips that physical witness and checks all 1,024 assignments. This is a native, finite six-AND exclusion for total size 16. Combining other separately established AND/OR-count exclusions requires those results as additional premises. General size 17 remains open.
