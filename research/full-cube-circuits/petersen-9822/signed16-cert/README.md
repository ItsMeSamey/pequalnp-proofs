# Signed binary lower bound 16 and physical size interval [17,18] for L-meta-9822

The target is true on a ten-bit input exactly when its active vertices contain one of the thirteen edges listed in replay.py. A signed binary gate may be AND or OR, with free complements on any input, intermediate signal, or output. An ordinary physical NOT costs one gate.

## Deduction

1. Every signed circuit costs at least 15 binary gates. At x3=false the graph equals face 1015 of the archived L-meta-9825. An essential occurrence of raw 3 costs a binary reader that folds under this pin. The archived face needs at least fourteen signed binary gates. See physical/binary-fifteen.md.
2. Suppose an optimum has exactly fifteen. Remove unused gates and signed aliases. Its combined internal and raw fanout excess is six. comb/signed-protected.md, signed-private-chains.md and signed-surplus.md give distinct shared guards P1,P2 for the positive entries of raw 1,2. reductions/third-shared.md gives at least three shared gates. The signed C5 floor and comb/pentagon-arbitrary-cut.md ensure exactly one owner reaches an internal fork; the other first reaches the output. The guards and this owner fork each have two consumers. All raw excess lies on {4,5,8}. This deduction uses the numerical signed C5 lower bound seven, with no structural classification of C5 optimizers.
3. There are at most four shared gates. In the earliest binary gate let distinct inputs be i,j. For j=false, i remains pivotal by choosing a different neighbor of i. For j=true, either i is adjacent to j, so its singleton witnesses the pivot, or some neighbor k of i is nonadjacent to j, so {j,k} witnesses it. The latter k exists by minimum degree two and absence of four-cycles. Fixing j to its controlling value would therefore be impossible if i had no other reader. The same applies to j. Repeated or opposite copies of one operand would make a removable signed alias or constant. Thus two raw excess units are mandatory, leaving at most four for internal gates.
4. Exactly three shared gates are impossible by reductions/three-shared.md and its replay. All 43,656 chronological placements in the broader three-gate test leave 249 causal candidates and zero after the guard-pivotality condition. Exactly four shared gates are impossible by reductions/four-shared.md. All 84 order/repetition cases leave zero complete assignments. Thus signed binary size is at least 16.
5. Every physical circuit with any NOT has total size at least 17 because erasing its paid NOTs into free signs leaves at least sixteen AND/OR gates. A circuit without NOT is already monotone, where the independently published lower bound is seventeen. calculus/upper18.json gives a physical monotone circuit of size eighteen. The resulting unrestricted optimum is seventeen or eighteen. This does not settle the official theorem excluding size seventeen.

The wrapper checks the manifest, compares the principal complete census logs, verifies all concrete three-site contradictions and runs the independent three- and four-site programs, then checks the physical upper on all 1,024 inputs.
