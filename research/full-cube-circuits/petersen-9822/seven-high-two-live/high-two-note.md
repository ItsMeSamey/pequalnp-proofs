With one to three full roots, two distinct nonprimitive masks U and V partition the switch vertices into signed trees. The ordinary forest has p roots, and both the U- and V-rooted forests are nonempty. Other switches are literal descendants. If a branch mask has multiple presentations choose a feasible earlier parent. The first U uses a union of ordinary branches. The first V may use ordinary and U-component branches. Every later U copy can take the same U directly. This enumerates repeats and literal children without enforcing an order between incomparable branch equations. The signed transformations preserve the whole assigned switch prefix and fix U at the second step.
