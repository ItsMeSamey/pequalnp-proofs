Consider a required raw W during two-helper generation. Only operands contained in W can contribute to its OR. Let E be all eligible earlier primitive or granted masks contained in W. If W belongs to the pairwise-union closure in one step from E, no extra helper is needed. Otherwise let x,y be the optional helpers contained in W. Each may be immediately reachable in one step from E. If neither is immediately reachable, neither can be the first computed, so the case fails. The remaining non-immediate helper, if any, must equal the union of the reachable helper and one member of E. Finally test W as a union of two reachable helpers or one reachable helper and one member of E. This expands exactly the original two closure passes and final union check. 0 is only a sentinel, never an actual chosen primitive or helper in the computation.
