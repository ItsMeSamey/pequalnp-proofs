At each fixed allocation of named helper activations, pool all raw masks available from any admissible helper bank and all primitive receivers. A candidate x for a vertex v needs at least as many accepting and rejecting atoms as the corresponding membership counts of v in the sixteen maximal independent sets. It also needs distinct incomparable masks having full union for each neighbor. Arc consistency removes x whenever some other vertex has no support with the prescribed adjacency and incomparability. Every realization gives supports drawn from its actual bank, hence it also satisfies this relaxation. The checker uses it only on pools of at most 128 masks, before the existing exact joint-bank search.
