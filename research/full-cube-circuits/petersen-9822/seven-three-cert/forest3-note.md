Consider exactly three conditional selectors and four full selectors. Suppose each conditional activation is a signed branch. Order switches by physical chronology. A branch equal to a conditional activation cannot come from a later switch: its direction mask depends essentially on that later switch bit while an earlier activation is independent of the bit. Thus choose an earlier branch as parent for each of the three conditional switches. This gives a forest on seven switch vertices with four roots. Children hang from one of the two local signs; independently flipping each switch swaps its two unordered child lists. Permuting independent switches and siblings preserves all direction conditions. forest3.py enumerates this finite class as unordered multisets of four nonplane rooted trees on seven total vertices with two unordered groups of children per vertex. It emits eleven forests. Some switch-mask identities may admit several parent choices; duplicates are harmless.
