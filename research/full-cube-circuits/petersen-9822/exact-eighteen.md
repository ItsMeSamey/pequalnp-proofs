# Physical circuit complexity of the thirteen-edge 9822 graph

The graph's edges are 12,34,04,05,16,27,38,49,57,79,69,68,58 on ten zero-based vertices. On every one of the 1,024 input vectors the desired bit says whether two active vertices share an edge. The gate basis is AND, OR and NOT with unrestricted fanout. All physical gates, including NOT, count toward size.

The upper circuit in calculus/upper18.json has eighteen gates and has been evaluated on all 1,024 inputs. For the lower bound take a minimum circuit of at most seventeen gates, trim unused paths and bypass constants and aliases. Replacing physical NOT by free complements of signed wires leaves a signed binary circuit computing the same function. By the previously archived signed-binary lower bound of sixteen (official9822-signed16-cert), this allows at most one live NOT.

If there is one live NOT, one-not-coverage.md and its source manifest exhaust the case. This deduction uses the lower bound on the number of outer ANDs and proves that all seven ANDs occur in the monotone outer circuit; it accounts for positive uses of the guard shared with that circuit. mixed-seven-four.md closes the last two reflected cases with a nine-variable full-cube cofactor.

If there is no live NOT, theta-assembly/monotone-eleven.md requires at least eleven ORs. At most six ANDs are left. The exactly-five and exactly-six cases are excluded by calculus/five-switch.md and six17-cert/README.md, both conditional on total size at most seventeen. For completeness, at most four ANDs cannot suffice even without that total-size cap. There are sixteen distinct maximal independent sets, each of which must be the exact set of omitted variables of some resolved choice of AND operands. Four switches therefore saturate all sixteen directions. Every AND is reached in every resolution; flipping an unreached switch would otherwise duplicate a resolution signature. An omitted raw is then described by avoiding a union of coordinate literals, hence occurs on a subcube with cardinality zero or a power of two. Vertex 1 belongs to seven of the sixteen maximal independent sets, a contradiction. Fewer switches cannot supply sixteen different signatures.

Together the lower bound and the evaluated upper give physical minimum eighteen. The proof uses mathematical arguments and finite computational certificates. End-to-end Lean verification of the official negated size-seventeen statement remains a separate obligation.
