# Missed edges with one conditionally reached outer AND

Cut one physical inverter and write the edge predicate as F(X)=K(X,¬H(X)), with H monotone and K monotone in its raw inputs and the free inverter input z. Write K0,K1 for the clamps. A missed edge uv has K0(uv)=0. Since F(uv)=1, H(uv)=0, and H is false on every subset of {u,v}. On those four subsets K1 agrees with F, while K0 is constantly false. Consequently K restricted to raw u,v,z computes their three-way conjunction. These conclusions require no bounds on the circuits for K or H.

A reverse direction selects one operand at each binary outer AND. Each input label has a receiver set of directions. K is true precisely when the union of its active labels' receiver sets covers the direction cube. With an always-reached AND, its two branches give opposite full coordinate literals. With no conditional ANDs, every label is a union of ordinary literals, possibly also containing an unconditional path to the output. A union of literals covers the cube exactly when it contains opposite signs on a coordinate; an unconditional path covers it on its own. The three labels of a missed edge would thus already have a one- or two-label subcover, contradicting the three-way conjunction. Hence K0 computes the target. A binary outer circuit of at most 16 gates with a live z has at most 15 gates after the zero clamp; this contradicts the known 16-gate lower bound on the original graph.

Now suppose just one live AND is conditionally reached. Its activation A is a nonempty proper union of ordinary literals: the incoming reach is accumulated from earlier reverse steps, all from always-reached ANDs, OR propagation, and binary unions. Any unconditional contribution would make A full. Its two branch sets are A intersected with the new coordinate and its opposite. Each raw or z receiver consists of ordinary literals and some subset of those two branch sets, unless unconditional. Give a label role P or N when its representation contains the corresponding branch, and role C if its ordinary literals include the opposite of any literal in A.

For a set of active labels without an unconditional source or ordinary complementary pair, their ordinary union L is proper. Their full receiver union covers the cube exactly if they jointly have P,N,C. Both conditional branches are needed since the new coordinate is freely variable above any point outside L. Once both are present the cover is L union A. Both L and A are consistent literal unions, so L union A is full exactly when they contain an opposing pair between them. This is exactly role C. Thus K is the disjunction of the unconditional contributions, ordinary complementary-pair test, and the three-role product.

Consider any missed edge uv. Its three labels u,v,z cover the cube, with no proper subcover. No ordinary conflict can occur in the triple as two labels would suffice. Each of the three labels must own precisely one different role: three labels covering three roles irredundantly have this property. Globally, z has one fixed role. The set R of endpoints of missed edges uses only the two other roles, and each missed edge joins different roles. K0 on R is consequently a union of ordinary pair conflicts; it has no singleton term since K0 rejects all singletons. Each such pair must be an edge of the graph because K0 is everywhere bounded above by the actual output.

Fix a missed edge uv and w in R with the same role as v. If w differs from v and uw is a nonedge, the three-role rule yields K1(uw)=1, so H(uw)=1 to reject that pair. Monotonicity gives H(uvw)=1; thus K0(uvw)=F(uvw)=1. Its ordinary pair witness is neither uv (missed) nor uw (nonedge), so vw is an edge. Exchanging u,v shows that every missed edge dominates all of R within the graph induced by R.

For a triangle-free and four-cycle-free graph, that induced graph consists of uv and stars about its endpoints. There are no chords within either arm by triangle-freeness, and an edge between the two arms would complete a four-cycle. If both arms are nonempty, only the central edge dominates the entire induced graph. It would be the sole missed edge, in conflict with the definition of R because there are arm vertices. Hence the missed edges share a common endpoint. The empty missed-edge set and a single missed edge are allowed. The argument does not bound their number beyond the maximum degree and makes no claim for two or more conditional ANDs.
