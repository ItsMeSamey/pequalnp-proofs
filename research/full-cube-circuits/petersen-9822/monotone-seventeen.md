# Monotone size at least seventeen
Suppose a fan-in-two monotone circuit for the ten-input graph of L-meta-9822 had at most sixteen gates. It needs at least ten OR gates by unrestricted-ten-paid.md specialized to a circuit without NOT. Consequently it has at most six AND gates. Four or fewer ANDs fail by the sixteen-maximal-independent-set selector saturation argument in boolean/directions.md. The five-AND census rules out this AND count at total size at most seventeen using the reverse-union budget (calculus/five-SHA256SUMS). The remaining six-AND case at total size at most sixteen is ruled out by calculus/six16-SHA256SUMS, including both possible direct receiver counts. The argument also applies to smaller circuits: all the budgets used in these exclusions are upper bounds.
The explicit eighteen-gate monotone witness was evaluated on the entire 1,024-input cube. Thus the monotone minimum is seventeen or eighteen. This corollary does not exclude sixteen-gate circuits containing a NOT.
