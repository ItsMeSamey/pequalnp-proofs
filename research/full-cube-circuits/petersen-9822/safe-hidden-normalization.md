# Promoting every safe hidden AND at no extra OR cost

Let F be the monotone target on the full Boolean cube. A wire is safe if its truth implies F (equivalently it vanishes on every maximal negative). Hidden AND gates have no OR-only path to the output. Work with a live acyclic positive AND/OR circuit, unrestricted fanout. Normalize constants and aliases before the argument as needed.

If any AND operand W is safe, replace that AND output Y everywhere by W. This preserves positives since Y≤W and the rest of the circuit is monotone. It preserves negatives since both Y and W are zero there. This removes one AND without introducing an OR. Thus a circuit irreducible under this rule has an unsafe operand on each side of every AND.

Now let J comprise all currently safe hidden ANDs. Each j in J has an OR-only path to a downstream AND operand, allowing a length-zero path. That operand is unsafe, so its formal primitive support cannot be contained in J. Trace the entire physical OR bank, stopping at raw and AND sources. A pure gate has nonempty support in J. Each j reaches a mixed OR through pure wires before the downstream operand. In any weak component of pure OR gates and J sources with t such sources and n ORs, connectedness gives 2n≥n+t−1, and at least one mixed OR has a pure input from the component. Different components cannot have the same mixed exit: both inputs would be pure. Clamping J's outgoing uses removes at least n pure gates plus the distinct mixed exit in every component, refunding at least |J| physical ORs in total, with sharing and overlapping operand masks allowed.

Retain the recomputed value of each selected AND for a fresh collector. Return the clamped old output OR all recomputed selected gates. On positives either there was no true selected gate and nothing changes, or the earliest originally true selected gate is unchanged. On negatives the clamped old output is false and every recomputed selected gate is bounded above by its originally false value. At most |J| new ORs are needed. Fold internal zeroes and aliases afterwards. If any AND folds, the AND count drops with no OR increase. Otherwise every old exposed AND retains an OR-only output path (the old OR-only output cone had no selected hidden primitive), and all selected gates are now exposed.

If desired iterate, since recomputation can turn formerly leaky gates safe. Lexicographically minimize the pair (total AND count, hidden AND count); every bypass or nonempty promotion round strictly decreases it. No step increases the OR count. Hence every circuit admits a representation using no more ANDs and ORs in which every hidden AND leaks on some maximal negative and every AND operand is unsafe. The exposure inference refers to the reconstructed circuit, not the original masks. Targets or circuits with constants use the usual constant-folding boundary cases.

For the six-AND h5 case, J of size at least three reduces to at least four exposed ANDs or at most five total ANDs. For J empty both original terminal operands may still have hidden sources; they share no primitive in a normalized circuit for the graph target, because any shared primitive implies F and hence is safe. This does not settle that case.
