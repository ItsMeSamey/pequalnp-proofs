# Pinning vertex 9 gives a graph of monotone gate complexity 14

Use the 9 low input bits of L-meta-9822. The ten positive pair masks are 6,24,17,33,66,132,264,160,320,288. Its twelve maximal independent sets are 42,50,77,108,116,139,201,208,261,276,387,402. Enumerating subsets shows independence number four. For every ordered pair of distinct vertices there is a maximal independent set containing just the first, and for each pair one omits both.

The induced six-cycle on 1,2,5,6,7,8 requires five OR gates (../comb/c6.py). Restoring 0,3,4 costs at least one OR each since each newly added vertex has an active neighbor at the point of restoration and pinning it leaves a nonzero function. Equivalently pin 4,3,0 successively in this order; each vertex still has a neighbor. This gives eight ORs and hence at most five ANDs at size thirteen.

Resolve the ANDs and let a raw receiver mask contain precisely the directions reaching that raw. As in ../calculus/five-switch.md, each maximal independent set needs a direction reaching exactly its complement. Thus the nine masks are incomparable. Direct primitive branch masks on different axes cannot cover the full word space together, and two opposite signs on one switch cannot jointly occur at a direction reaching both. Thus the direct receivers form an independent set. With at most thirteen gates the number of reverse joins is at most 13−9+1=5. There must be at least four direct receivers, so exactly four. Each remaining receiver consumes exactly one join; no union-only path to it can pass through any other receiver by incomparability. Each is therefore a union of two primitive branches. The possible direct sets are the seven maximum independent sets in the list above. Append dummy full-activation switches if needed to reach five axes; existing masks ignore the new bits. A nonconstant old receiver independent of a fresh bit cannot be a new primitive. At least twelve distinct branch traces are necessary. The archived canonfive.out enumerates all 617 five-axis profiles with this cutoff up to signed permutation.

h9.cpp enumerates candidates for each receiver, requiring exact union-to-full on an edge, nonfull union on every nonedge, incomparability, and simultaneous nonempty witness rooms for all twelve maximal independent sets. It does not constrain precursor timing, which enlarges the search. Contiguous traversal of the archived list found zero hits in 4,319 cases. As a positive control, removing the edge with mask 288 and adjusting the MIS list in h9-control.cpp produced a packing for the first tested all-full profile using direct set 77. This control exercises the same assignment procedure. A cover of five vertices (the complement of 77) yields the 14-gate upper bound by partitioning the ten edges into five nonempty stars, forming the five products and ORing them. For example assign stars with centers 1,4,5,7,8 the edge groups (6,66), (17,24), (33,160), (132), (264,320,288).

Rebuild with a C++20 compiler and feed ../calculus/canonfive.out on stdin. Full search output is h9.log; positive control should use its final (all-full) row. The graph bound applies to the monotone minor. For an essential unique raw-9 inverter, both the raw and its complement must have binary readers (otherwise the inverter can be discarded by monotonicity in bit 9). At pin 0 both a positive and a negative reader fold. They are distinct since a common reader of complementary literals would compute a constant and can be removed. Thus any such minimal size-16 circuit would imply a size-13 monotone minor.
