# Strong regularity backdoor reduction

For a represented binary matroid M call X strong if every choice of deleting or contracting each coordinate of X produces a regular matroid. This is equivalent to hitting all seven surviving labels of every F₇ or F₇* minor. A nonregular choice has such a further minor; conversely if a forbidden-minor witness misses X, perform its prescribed operations on X before the remaining operations. Minors on distinct labels commute. [Tutte's binary excluded-minor characterization in Truemper](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/EMIS/monographs/md/matroiddecompositionbook.pdf) · [Regularity recognition](https://discopt.github.io/cmr/regular.html)

A decision-only regularity recognizer can extract seven surviving labels. Repeatedly scan single-element deletions and contractions and retain a nonregular one, recording its label and operation. When all single-element minors are regular, minor-minimality means the current binary matroid is F₇ or F₇*. At most quadratically many recognition calls suffice. To find X, test every one of the 2^i prescriptions for a candidate of size i. From a failing prescription take a forbidden minor; any successful extension must hit one of its seven remaining labels. Branch on these seven choices to depth k. This takes O*(14^k) time and polynomial space, and iterative deepening finds the minimum size.

Write a_f,b_f for cycle and cocycle labels. On a nonloop noncoloop e, delete it with c_e=0 or contract it with d_e=0. The branches overlap when both bits vanish, which is harmless for decision. For deletion choose a cycle z with z_e=1. Each remaining cocycle extends uniquely by d_e=∑_{f≠e} z_f d_f, by orthogonality and restriction of the dual space. Set b_f←b_f+z_f b_e. The remaining cycle lifts with c_e zero and a unchanged. For contraction reverse roles, choose a cocycle z through e and set a_f←a_f+z_f a_e. At a coloop c_e is always zero, while its independent free d_e contributes either zero or b_e. Delete and project all remaining scores and target modulo the span of b_e. A witness lifts by choosing d_e to restore the target. At a loop contract and use the analogous quotient by a_e. All transformations are elementary binary linear algebra; marker dimension does not increase. A strong X thus reduces a disjoint cycle/cocycle decision to at most 2^k regular instances with at most t markers. This reduction does not assume or prove an unconditional marker solver on regular matroids.
