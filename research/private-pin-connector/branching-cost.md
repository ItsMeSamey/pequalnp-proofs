# Exact cost of branching under a shared lower pin

Fix three literal pins A,T0,B. The upper cycle is A,T0,Z1,T1,...,Zm,Tm,A, with optional leaves at Zi. Otherwise disjoint lower modules share B and each attaches to the upper graph only at its own Zi. Each has independent raw inputs, a positive tuple forcing closed output one, a zero tuple forbidding closed output one, and honest witnesses. The sharp number of extra arbitrary affine rows for an asserted conjunction of exact such predicates is m−1.

B must be adjacent to every Zi, since otherwise selecting only the two would realize output one at zero input. In any full witness for the positive raw tuples, the currents (parities of selected local degrees) at B,Zi agree within each child by handshaking. If Zi=0 both vanish and the child would be closed at the wrong positive-input output. Thus every Zi=1 and every leaf is off. Denote the local current of child i by ei.

For a binary vector δ, replace each child with δi=1 by the state selecting only B,Zi. Its raw tuple is now zero and its local current changes by ηi=(1+ei)δi. Add the prefix sum of η to each upper Ti, holding T0 fixed. Impose only sum(η)=0, so Tm and the total B current are preserved. Each upper Zi experiences current change ηi, canceling its lower change. Both neighbors of each selected upper T are selected. The full assignment depends affinely on δ, and each nonzero variation falsifies at least one independent positive input tuple. The variation space through every accepting model has dimension at least m−1. Additional affine scores retaining that model must be injective on the space.

Conversely m−1 rows setting individual child B-neighborhood parities to zero imply the last by the B guard. Local handshaking also zeroes the currents at Zi. Each child is now closed, and the upper rooted cycle forces every Zi=1. Honest witnesses glue for all positive inputs. Thus the arbitrary-affine lower bound is attained. Raw inputs shared among children or cross-edges outside these interfaces are outside the theorem.
