# A strict SAT decision in the exact majority of original assignments

Given an r-input Boolean circuit φ, set m=r+4 and use m source bits in MSB-first order (t,u,v,x,b), with b the least significant bit. Write A for the number of satisfying x. Define

    F = u AND v AND ((NOT t AND φ(x)) OR (t AND [x=1^r] AND b)).

The left branch has 2A models because b is free. The right branch has exactly one, the all-ones word. Thus F has M=2A+1 models and M<2^(m−1), including at r=0. The gates, wires and input pins cost O(|φ|+r+1) in a bounded-fanin circuit. Compile F into the [four-site masked NAND circuit](odd_masked_circuit.py) G on h=4m raw coordinates. The upper 3m bits are the masks, in consecutive triples (a_i,c_i,e_i). Within a fixed mask prefix, the low m-bit tail is translated by XOR with the vector of a_i+c_i e_i. Each prefix block contains exactly M raw models. At prefix zero, decoding is the identity. The public raw word w=2^m−1 has prefix zero and tail all ones and satisfies G.

Use exact majority over the original raw assignments consistent with the mistaken rows, with ties predicting zero. First ask G. Its true population is 2^(3m)M out of 2^h; the strict forecast is zero. Supply one. Then, for k=h−1,h−2,…,m, ask the ordinary raw threshold T_(2^k−1)=[z≤2^k−1] and supply one. Before this query the survivors are exactly the raw models of G in [0,2^(k+1)−1]. Each of the two halves comprises 2^(k−m) complete prefix blocks. Both bins therefore contain exactly 2^(k−m)M words. Each zero forecast is a genuine tie mistake. The same w realizes the source label and every one of the 3m threshold labels.

At this state all surviving words have prefix zero. Ask T_j with j=2^(m−1). This threshold includes all the left source models. The only right word in the threshold's raw range has tail (1,0,…,0) and fails the guard u AND v. The negative and positive histogram bins are exactly **(1,2A)**. Their strict majority is one precisely when φ is satisfiable. The exact counts recover A by dividing the positive bin by two. The planted w realizes all preceding mistakes on both YES and NO source circuits. Its label on the final probe is zero; a final mistake occurs exactly on YES sources.

The [earlier blind predictor](masked-positive-threshold-support.md) produced strict forecasts using virtual pairs. Here the actual raw majority ties at each prescribed trim. The sequence is supplied directly; no assertion is needed that bisection by the exact learner would autonomously choose those thresholds. The [range counter](tail_permutation_range.py) can compute this majority in O(2^m poly(h+|φ|)) time: the complete decoder wires give a valid m-bit tail cut on each live source coordinate, and unused tail inputs may be retained as unused coordinates. Any later eligible dominating cut chosen by the recognizer for that same coordinate still intercepts every live path from its decoded wire to the output. The additional negation in the source-negative bin preserves this property. The final threshold solely changes the raw interval. Thus a budget of 2^m=2^(h/4) latent assignments suffices throughout the displayed history. The uniform polynomial reduction proves hardness for this exact prediction rule, without a time lower bound for other learners.
