# Explicit large affine cover at the same gate budget
Fix B>0. For all sufficiently large n an explicit circuit on N=n+O_B(log^2 n) physical bits has <=B N log2 N bounded-fanin-two gates and 2^n accepting assignments, with no contained affine flat of dimension exceeding O_B(n/log n). Thus covering its exact accepting set needs at least 2^{N-O_B(N/log N)} affine pieces, even allowing overlaps and alternate circuit representations.

Let L=max(1,floor(c_B log2 n)) for a small fixed positive rational c_B<=1. Choose prime n<p<=2n by enumeration, and let P be the smallest power of two exceeding 2Lp. For index i=1,...,n choose weight w_i with L base-P digits i^h mod p, h=0,...,L-1. No signed sum of t<=L distinct weights is zero: every signed digit sum has absolute value <P, so reduction modulo P and successive division force all digit sums zero. The first t powers give an invertible Vandermonde matrix on those indices over GF(p), contradicting nonzero signs. Each weight has O(L log n) bits.

Accept precisely when the R=ceil(log2(1+sum_i w_i)) bits of y encode the integer sum of w_i x_i, including all leading zeros. The N=n+R bit support has exactly 2^n points. To implement the sum, partition coefficients into chunks of floor(log2(n)/2) bits. Within a chunk group raw bits by equal coefficient and compute all group population counts. Carry-save trees followed by binary addition take O(n) gates in total across groups. Each full adder reduces live weighted bit tokens by one; the final two rows cost O(group size). At most sqrt(n) counts need multiplication by their O(log n)-bit constants and summation, costing O(sqrt(n) polylog n)=O(n) more gates. There are O(L) chunks with absolute constants, and final shifted adders and equality cost polylog n. The bound O(nL+n) has an absolute coefficient for c_B<=1, so choosing c_B sufficiently small fits the claimed budget. All wiring and constants have polynomial construction time.

Parametrize a d-dimensional contained flat injectively by t in GF(2)^d. Each coordinate is an affine bit with integer-valued expansion (1-sigma chi_ell(t))/2 for its sign sigma and linear character ell. Comparing the coefficients of nonconstant characters in sum_i w_i x_i(t)=sum_j 2^j y_j(t), a nonzero character occurring on input coordinates but absent among y must have a zero signed weight sum. Each such character consumes >=L+1 distinct input positions. There are at most R other occurring nonzero characters. Their total linear rank must be d by injectivity, giving d<=n/(L+1)+R and the announced cover bound.

Any exact branch decomposition that returns an affine set of accepting physical inputs at each leaf (including projection from an auxiliary affine system) obeys this bound regardless of gate syntax. It bounds printed/enumerated leaves, not symbolic aggregation. The support is the graph of a total explicit function: for existence one can choose x freely and compute y. A SAT procedure performing that elimination or stopping at one witness is outside the assertion.
