# A common-source obstruction for two counting routes

Let F be an explicit NAND circuit on r≥1 inputs x. Add two bits t,b and an even number m≥4 of fresh bits u. On the original coordinates form

    G(t,b,x) = (¬t AND F(x)) OR (t AND b AND [x=0^r]),
    H(t,b,x,u) = G(t,b,x) AND AMO(u),

where AMO accepts words of Hamming weight zero or one. The exact count is (2·#F+1)(m+1). The term with t=0 leaves b free; the term with t=1 has one model. The blocks are independent. The count is odd and recovers #F by exact division followed by subtraction and division by two. H always has a model, so this implication concerns counting. The direct decision control F(x) AND AMO(u) is satisfiable exactly when F is satisfiable, and its count is odd precisely when #F is odd. It does not force an odd count for an arbitrary positive input.

The arity is h=r+2+m. Use the following specific ordered gate list. First build G without any u inputs. Let its output be P. For each unordered pair i<j, in a fixed order, append three fresh NANDs:

    E = NAND(u_i,u_j),    J = NAND(P,E),    P_new = NAND(J,J).

Replace P by P_new and continue. This costs s+O(r+1)+3 binom(m,2) gates for an s-gate F. Taking r=m and s polynomial in m gives encoding length L=m^O(1), including the wire addresses.

## Equal fibers and the October envelope

A verified full-cube quotient of rank q with 2^(h−q) preimages per word has original count 2^(h−q) times its quotient count. Oddness forces q=h. A composition of such reductions, including bijective pivots and deletion of genuinely inactive coordinates, cannot decrease the number of primary bits on H. This applies to all accepted chronological section certificates, regardless of their discovery or supplied wire lists. The syndrome and permutation range counters on the full cube also have uniform fibers, of size 2^(h−q). A refusal under a width cap supplies no count. [Section certificate](section-syndrome-october.md) · [Syndrome range counter](affine-probe-learner/syndrome-tail-range.md)

For width three and fixed clause density at most five, the pinned October theorem gives a deterministic counting clock polynomial in the input length times 2^(A V/√log₂(max(2,V))) for a fixed A>0 and declared variable count V. Tseitin gate variables extend each assignment uniquely. The theorem returns the count; it gives no map producing a new formula on V/√log V variables. A polynomial number of explicitly constructed leaves with V=O(log L·√loglog L) and polynomial discovery cost would suffice for a polynomial clock. This is the scale obtained by inverting V/√log V=O(log L). [October main theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/42907c9e999cc00d6abe7a50bfc1023250edd1b9/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/main_full_version.tex)

In the [published leaf encoder](section_syndrome_cnf.py), exhaustively guessing b quotient inputs or gate outputs entails 2^b cases. It declares every unguessed primary, even when constant propagation leaves the primary unused. Thus V≥h−b on each emitted leaf. A polynomial enumeration requires b=O(log L). In the family above this leaves V=Ω(m), and the stated exponential factor is superpolynomial in L. In particular at least one accepting branch exists, so not all branches can be discarded by discovering an empty clause. If b=ω(log L), enumeration alone is superpolynomial for the specified full-schedule procedure. The argument is about the published bound and fixed enumeration scheme; it does not assert a running-time lower bound for the October counter or for adaptive pruning.

## Every merge tree for the original tap factors has linear width

The [tap solver](linear_tap_separator.py) records fresh nonlinear NAND-output coordinates and retains just the factors required by the output. Its affine recognizers include a specified four-NAND XOR pattern and a self-inverter. Every displayed E has distinct primary inputs, so it introduces a nonlinear factor whose operand span contains the raw unit rows for both u_i and u_j. Every J joins two nonconstant distinct, noncomplementary forms: E is its own fresh coordinate and P was defined before E. Initially P is nonconstant because G takes both values. Later P is the complement of the preceding fresh J. A join cannot be recognized as XOR because neither parent of E is an internal shared gate. At the self-inverter a spurious XOR match would require E to be NAND(P,P), contrary to its distinct primary inputs, or P to be NAND(E,E), contrary to the ordering. Consequently each P_new is represented by the complement of J's fresh coordinate. Backward liveness from the final output keeps every J and E. This argument uses the actual encoding, including the gates introduced by the conjunction.

Mark the binom(m,2) pair factors with weight one and all other leaves with weight zero. Any binary merge tree has a node containing between one third and two thirds of the total weight. Starting at the root, descend into a child of weight greater than two thirds while one exists; at the first stop a heavier child has weight greater than one third and at most two thirds. Each side of the resulting cut therefore has at least binom(m,2)/3 distinct pairs. If v distinct raw sites occur on one side, binom(v,2)≥binom(m,2)/3, so v≥m/√3−O(1). The two sides share at least (2/√3−1)m−O(1) distinct raw sites by inclusion-exclusion. In fact one side meets every site: if two sites were touched exclusively by opposite sides, the pair between them could belong to neither side. This strengthens the overlap bound to m/√3−O(1).

For every shared site, the same raw unit row occurs in the span of each side. These unit rows are independent in the augmented space, even with all gate coordinates present. Their span lies in the interface of the chosen node. The parent enumerates a space containing this interface, so the solver's k is Ω(m) for every binary merge schedule. The implemented solver explicitly iterates over all 2^k states at that join or declines under an insufficient cap. Schedule search cannot evade this lower bound for these extracted factors. [Tap interface definition and clock](linear-tap-separator.md)

The two obstructions apply simultaneously to the full-cube equal-fiber quotient dimension and the literal tap-factor parameter of this same ordered source. They do not apply to arbitrary recircuitizations or automatically to the tap width after a different preprocessing step. AMO has only m+1 models and a compact alternative circuit; a solver that recognizes the disjoint conjunct can count it directly. Oddness and the tap-width bound say nothing about unrestricted SAT running time or a uniform separation of P and NP.
