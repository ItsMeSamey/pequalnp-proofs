# Resolution on recovered ordered-image inputs

For the fixed [selector-free physical language](read-two-ring-image.md), define one proof system for NO answers. An empty certificate suffices if polynomial recovery rejects the input. On a recognized word, a certificate is a resolution refutation with weakening of its exact recovered signed CNF. The verifier checks each axiom, inference and final empty clause. This defines one sound, complete proof system with verification time polynomial in the word and proof lengths. The physical positive CNF remains satisfiable on both YES and NO compiler outputs.

Use a canonical explicit bit encoding B listing each residue individually in unsigned binary, without sharing or compression. Binary length delimiters and explicit names cost O(log(E+2)) per record besides its content. Let a be source arity, e and v the encoder's original graph edges and vertices, and E=13e+6v its expanded edge count. Precision k=E+a+1. Each expanded edge contributes two individually listed physical point entries congruent to 1−4, hence both equal 3^k−3. Since e,v≥3a, E≥57a. When E>0 these 2E entries occupy Ω(Ek)=Ω(E²) bits. The other direction uses O(E+1) entries of O(k) bits each and O(E+1) width-three clauses with logarithmic names. Thus B=Θ((1+E)²) for canonical words, including the empty source up to a constant.

**Upper bound.** Recover the source and enumerate all 2^a assignments. This yields a total deterministic decision algorithm in poly(B)·2^{O(√B)} bit time, returning a checked source witness on accepted words. For an unsatisfiable source use the assignment tree: at each leaf weaken an axiom it falsifies to the clause excluding that leaf, then resolve siblings to exclude their common prefix. The result has O(2^a) lines and 2^{O(√B)} bits including explicit labels, clause literals and pointers. This does not rely on a counting algorithm.

**Projection of proofs.** Translate an unsatisfiable width-three G into the [even-cycle instance](ordered-ring-planar-phase-boundary.md), producing recovered signed source H. Map each cycle's successive edge variables alternately to x_i and ¬x_i, and map every bridge to false. Stars and negative conflicts become tautologies or true constants. Each extra demand becomes its corresponding clause of G.

For each nontautological projected proof line retain a derived subclause, removing false constants and duplicate literals. Suppose a resolution step from A∨y and D∨¬y projects to a nontautological conclusion R=A′∨D′. If y maps to a constant, retain the subclause of the parent containing the false pivot. If y maps to a literal l and one projected parent is tautological, A′ contains ¬l or D′ contains l respectively, so the other parent is already contained in R. Both parents cannot be tautological in this case. Otherwise, if either stored parent subclause lacks its designated pivot, retain it. If both contain their designated pivots, resolve them on l to obtain a subclause of R. This treats identification of variables and repeated literals. Projected weakening reuses its parent's stored subclause. At the empty conclusion the retained subclause must be empty. Consequently a DAG refutation of H, even with weakening, yields an ordinary resolution refutation of G with at most as many lines.

**Lower bound.** Use odd-charge Tseitin contradictions on connected cubic edge expanders on m vertices at arbitrarily large m. There are 3m/2 edge variables and four width-three clauses per vertex. They require 2^{Ω(m)} general resolution lines. The phase construction has Θ(m) recovered variables, rows and occurrences. The physical encoder has E=Θ(m), hence B=Θ(m²). The preceding projection forces every resolution certificate for these physical words to have at least 2^{Ω(√B)} lines, and therefore at least that many bits. This matches the universal upper bound in its exponential scale along this infinite sequence of input lengths. [Ben-Sasson and Wigderson, Corollary 4.5, crediting Urquhart](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/PUBLICATIONS/MYPAPERS/B-SW99/bw00journal.pdf)

The lower bound applies to this proof format. The hard source contradictions admit an efficient different check: XOR their parity equations, obtaining an inconsistent right-hand side. On the constructed cactus instances these equations can be read after cycle-phase contraction. Transferring the resolution bound to an unrestricted time lower bound would require an argument covering uniform procedures that use this check and other types of reasoning.
