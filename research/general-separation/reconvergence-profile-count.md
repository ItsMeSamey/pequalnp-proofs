# Exact outside messages for reconvergent CircuitSAT

Fix k raw inputs U of an n-input Boolean DAG with s fanin-two gates. Mark signals live if reachable from U; constants are not live. Once the other n-k raw inputs are assigned, every structurally dead signal is fixed. A gate with two live predecessors is a merge using its original fixed truth table, even if its effective function becomes simpler. A gate with one live predecessor is a unary map on it (possibly constant). Each chain of one-live gates has a unique latest merge or free raw input as its source, including when the chain fans out. For each distinct unary endpoint that feeds a merge or the final output, record the two values it takes on source=0 and source=1. Direct wires from a merge or a raw U input use the identity and need no profile bits. If the final output is structurally dead, its single value is the required message.

Construct a polynomial-size circuit on the n-k outside bits producing this profile. Evaluate the dead subgraph once. In topological order propagate each unary gate's pair by applying its truth table to the live predecessor pair and its dead predecessor's scalar, respecting pin order. Where a new chain starts, initialize its source pair to (0,1) rather than simulating the source's actual variable value. Thus reconstruction works despite arbitrary fanout and reconvergence. For any U input and endpoint table list, the original merges can be evaluated in order and the final output obtained.

Further compress the message by any vertex cut of size r in the profile circuit separating the outside input bits from the profile outputs, allowing input/output vertices themselves. A minimum such cut follows by standard node-split max flow. Remove outside-independent constants from this separation problem; their known values remain available in reconstruction. Given values on the cut one can compute all profile outputs without access to the outside input, since every unfixed raw influence meets the cut. A recognized linear dependency among cut values permits sending a basis and reconstructing other values, if this dependency is verified.

Histogram cut messages over 2^{n-k} outside assignments. For each occurring message count by enumerating 2^k free inputs with the reconstructed circuit. Return the sum of outside frequencies times their corresponding inner counts. A representative outside assignment and accepting inner assignment give one common physical witness, also verifiable by evaluating the original DAG. Time is O*(2^{n-k}+2^{r+k}) and worst-case space is exponential (s polynomial). Deterministic external sort/merge gives the same clock on a multitape machine without assuming unit random accesses to exponential memory; alternatively enumerate all 2^r messages for the inner table and merge the sorted tables. A superpolynomial savings factor requires k=omega(log n) and n-r-k=omega(log n). A polynomial list of trial choices U can be tested by the explicit construction; no guarantee of a successful U is known for all n log n-gate circuits.

For a binary formula with s leaves one can choose the k least frequent raw variables, giving p<=ks/n live leaf occurrences. The fixed restriction skeleton has at most p-1 merges and O(p) unary endpoints, so r=O(p) suffices. For s=O(n log n), k a sufficiently small fixed multiple of n/log n gives time O*(2^{n-Omega(n/log n)}). For a forest obtained after guessing b internal signals, include each guessed producer's defining root condition, discard inconsistent dead roots, and charge one desired truth bit per live root (at most p). Enumeration costs 2^{n-k+b}; number of common live profiles is still 2^{O(p)}, provided the unfolded forest is polynomial-size and p counts its actual occurrences. The formula bound is previously known from Lokshtanov–Mikhailin–Paturi–Pudlak, https://users.math.cas.cz/~pudlak/BoundedTreewidthCircuitSAT.pdf . Seto–Tamaki also treat full-binary-basis formulas, https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2012/071/ . Gate elimination bounds of Golovnev et al. for general circuits below fixed linear thresholds do not assert the corresponding n log n premise, https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2016.45 . No formula unfolding bound follows from a DAG's raw occurrence count alone.
