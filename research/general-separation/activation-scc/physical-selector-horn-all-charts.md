# Balanced occurrences force source coordinates in every affine Horn chart

Use the physical odd-selector SAT reduction with independent private coordinates for distinct clause copies. Assume three distinct source variables per clause and at least two occurrences of each polarity per variable. Under arbitrary invertible affine change of ALL free coordinates, a simultaneous polarity making every individual conflict Horn exists exactly when a source assignment makes at least two literals true in every clause.

Absorb polarities into the origin. Let W denote the dual span of all source normals. Selectors from distinct clauses (one per clause) are independent modulo W because their original private parts are nonzero and supported on disjoint clause blocks. This independence is invariant under arbitrary affine chart changes.

Fix an original source variable with normal f. Every physical conflict is a rank-two forbidden flat and therefore has at most three active positions in a Horn chart. If f has weight three, all selectors paired with this variable, of either sign, must lie in its three-position support. The span on that support has dimension at most two modulo W, since W contains f. This contradicts independence for three distinct occurrences.

Suppose f has weight two. Take a polarity of the variable whose falsity readout equals one at the shifted origin. At each of its at least two occurrences the selector p equals zero there, because forbidding the origin violates the active-zero test. Thus the forbidden linear syndrome in order (f,p) relative to the origin is (0,1). If this conflict has three active positions, its two forbidden patterns must each have at most one zero. The two words differ on two axes (otherwise an active column would vanish, or the two complements could not both have weight at least two). The two columns on which they differ equal a common v, while the remaining column w is independent of v. The two forbidden tuples have their zeros on opposite copies of v, so their syndrome equals v+w. Distinct nonzero v,w summing to (0,1) both have first entry one. All three axes would belong to the support of f, contradiction. Therefore each such false-literal occurrence has only two active axes, exactly the support of f. Its selector lies in the same two-dimensional plane containing f. This plane has dimension at most one modulo W, contradicting independence for two distinct occurrences.

All source normals thus have weight one, and are distinct by source independence. Relabeling coordinates reduces the rest to the audited global-private case, physical-selector-horn-global-private.md. Conversely that private construction works whenever there is a source assignment with two true literals per clause. The exact test is the 2SAT conjunction of all pairwise disjunctions within clauses.

The four ternary clauses forbidding the even inputs on three bits meet the occurrence hypothesis with distinct clauses. Their four odd solutions each violate the majority requirement on some clause. Thus NONE of the affine charts produces the stated factorwise Horn property on this actual satisfiable instance. On pure-free three-distinct-variable inputs, duplicating each clause with fresh selectors ensures the hypothesis. Removing duplicate constraints before compilation changes the physical input. The result concerns individual physical conflicts; it does not rule out semantic rewrites of their conjunction or other solving methods.
