# Repairing a nearly Horn system while retaining all affine checks

Use the supplied chart on independent free bits after Gaussian preprocessing. Each constraint prohibits one rank-two flat. Inconsistent forbidden flats are vacuous; rank-one prohibitions may be absorbed as linear equations before choosing this chart. Let d be the number of factors touching more than three coordinates. Among remaining factors, define k as the minimum over common shifts of the number whose complement fails meet closure.

Apply the exact polarity clauses of affine-forbidden-flat-horn.md to each small factor separately, keeping separate indexed clause occurrences even when two factors generate identical clauses. At most 12 binary conditions come from each three-site factor. A shift with ≤k bad factors violates ≤12k of these conditions. Almost 2SAT therefore returns a shift violating at most 12k conditions, which leaves at most 12k non-Horn small factors. Run it with increasing budget if k is unspecified. A shift need not satisfy the original constraints. Its purpose is to find a small exceptional list. Deterministic FPT for clause deletion is due to Razgon and O'Sullivan, https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.1300.

Emit ordinary Horn clauses for every other small factor. Enumerate the physical values on all variables of exceptional small factors, at most 36k bits, retaining only settings satisfying those factors and passing the pins to the Horn formula. For each dense factor choose one of its two independent defining equations to violate, imposing that one linear equation at its opposite value. A union of 2^d such branches exactly enforces the dense factors.

In any one branch the remaining task is Horn SAT with at most d affine equations. Let P be the product over GF(2) of their affine success indicators, degree ≤d. For every set T of ≤d bits, forward-chain the Horn implications from T and their positive unit facts, and discard closures violating a negative Horn clause. The remaining closures are actual models. On any model, the monomial on T is the indicator that it contains the closure of T, or zero if infeasible. Ordered by size, the distinct feasible closures have a unitriangular containment evaluation matrix on themselves. Consequently if P is nonzero on any Horn model it is nonzero on one of the retained closures. Evaluate P there by simply testing the d original equations, without expanding its polynomial. Return that actual model, undo the common shift and Gaussian chart, and check the physical constraints.

The deterministic time is 2^{O(k+d)} times input-size^{O(d+1)}, with polynomial space using streamed seed enumeration and a polynomial-space Almost 2SAT implementation; alternatively omit the space claim if using another decision subroutine. If d=0 this is fixed-parameter tractable in the minimum number of small factor violations. All-input coverage charges d and k explicitly.

On the actual three-selector reduction the usual private chart has d=0 for every input. More directly on a source 3CNF, apply Almost 2SAT to the three pairwise literal disjunctions per clause. If any assignment has at most k clauses with fewer than two true literals, the minimum number of failed pair clauses is ≤3k. A returned near-optimal shift leaves ≤3k deficient source clauses, even if it itself falsifies some of them. The remaining source clauses are Horn under that polarity. Guess one true literal in each deficient clause, pin it and solve the Horn remainder. This is a decision and witness algorithm for original 3SAT. The renamable-Horn/deletion parameter can grow linearly, as witnessed by disjoint parity-clause gadgets. This source application uses established SAT backdoor techniques and does not yield an unrestricted polynomial clock.
