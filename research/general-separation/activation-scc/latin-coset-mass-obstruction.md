# A constant obstruction to arbitrary additive mass padding

Take H=F₂^{10} of order Q=1024 and S the 31 points (x,x³) for nonzero x in F₃₂, represented by X⁵+X²+1. No two S points sum to one in S: u³+v³=(u+v)³ forces uv(u+v)=0. The checker gives nontrivial character sums −9 (186), −1 (527), or 7 (310).

Keep exactly S false inside H on EACH rail of a larger binary group G. Within any outside coset the three false sets, translated into H, must form an independent set of a graph on three copies of H. Connect u,v on different rails when u+v∈S: a false old site on the remaining rail would otherwise complete a zero triple. Its adjacency is the tensor product of K3 adjacency and the Cayley matrix of H with steps S. It is 62-regular on 3Q vertices. Characters diagonalize the Cayley matrix with eigenvalues 31 and the listed smaller ones. K3 eigenvalues are 2,−1,−1. The minimum product eigenvalue is −31, attained only on vectors constant on each rail and summing to zero across rails.

In a D-regular N-vertex graph of minimum eigenvalue λ<0, any independent-set indicator of weight m satisfies m≤N(−λ)/(D−λ): subtract m/N from the indicator and bound its adjacency quadratic form below by λ times its squared norm. Here m≤Q. Equality forces each rail indicator constant, so exactly one entire rail is false on that coset. Summing gives M≤93+(|G|/Q−1)Q=|G|−931. This holds under EVERY additive enlargement with ANY new bits or constraints, provided the physical H and its old false support on each rail stay fixed.

To attach it to arbitrary 3SAT use another subgroup K large enough for its isolated-clause reduction with one extra unit forcing fresh representative z=0. Work in H×K×F₂². Put the old S rails in the H copy with K and tag zero, controlled by z, and pin the other H sites true. Put the hard rails in separate cosets with tags 01,10,11 and H coordinate zero. Pin all remaining sites true and reuse z through its same physical star. The hard tags sum to zero and no proper nonempty subset of them does, so no mixed false zero triple appears. Models extend uniquely on used source bits. This obstructs any effort to raise these images above critical mass by merely padding ambient binary dimensions while retaining the guard. It does not forbid preprocessing which removes the guard or other decision strategies.
