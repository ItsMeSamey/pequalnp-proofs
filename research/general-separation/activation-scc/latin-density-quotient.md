# Dense models of the full Latin block with physical signed stars

For G=F₂^d of size q, let A,B,C be the false positions on each rail under one assignment of all signed-star representatives. The block accepts iff (A+B)∩C=∅. Put M=|A|+|B|+|C|. For given t≥1, decide existence of an actual model with M≥q+t in q^{O(1+log(max(1,q/t)))}4^{max(1,q/t)} poly(input) time and polynomial space. Fixed positive t/q gives polynomial time. For t>q the target is impossible: an empty rail permits M≤2q, and otherwise each pair of masses sums to at most q.

Test empty rails by forcing their bits true one rail at a time and maximizing M on each remaining independent sign representative, subject to all pins. Otherwise Kneser's theorem with H=Stab(A+B) gives |A+B|≥|A+H|+|B+H|−|H|. Since |A+B|≤q−|C|, any required witness has |H|≥t. Enlarging A and B to their H-coset saturations leaves their sum fixed; C lies in its complement. [Primary proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3539)

Enumerate subspaces H of index s≤q/t via independent tuples of at most floor(log₂(q/t)) dual vectors. For each, enumerate subsets U,V of G/H; W is the quotient complement of U+V. Require every position outside the respective allowed false domains U,V,W to be true, imposing unit conditions on the SAME original signed-star representatives. Intersect with all pins. Any consistent branch permits direct M-maximization by independent choices according to star multiplicities, and any resulting model satisfies the block. Check its M and original constraints. All witnesses survive some branch by the stabilizer argument. Duplicate subspaces only increase time within the stated bound.

A checkable class for ordinary decision is when the minimum boundary-compatible M already reaches q+t, found by the same per-star minimization. In particular when all 3q positions are partitioned into opposite-sign pairs with no pins, M=3q/2 identically. There is a polynomial exact decision and witness on this class. At this mass with all rails nonempty, each pair of sizes sums to at most q, hence all three equal q/2. The stabilizer has index two and all three rails are full cosets. The sparse-live NP-hard construction is outside this density condition.
