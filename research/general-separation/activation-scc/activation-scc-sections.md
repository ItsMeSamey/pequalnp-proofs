# Affine covers of cyclic activation modules

For binary A and t, a physical assignment obeys x_i((Ax)_i+t_i)=0. Draw i→j when an off-diagonal variable is read. Process SCCs from sinks to sources. Let u be the new SCC variables, y the previously processed variables, M its diagonal block and b(y)=t_C+A_CD y its affine right side. Previously processed rows do not read u. The block may have arbitrary incoming rank.

For each subset T of the block choose once a binary diagonal D_T making M[T,T]+D_T invertible. Starting with determinant one in dimension zero, add indices in their physical order. The two choices at the new diagonal differ in determinant by the preceding nonsingular leading minor, so exactly one works. Use binary Gaussian elimination. Retain the physical indices on all restrictions, and include T empty.

Define the affine map phi_T(y) to be zero outside T and (M[T,T]+D_T)^{-1}(b_T(y)+diag D_T) inside T. It satisfies the local activation equations for every y: on a nonzero output coordinate the two diagonal corrections agree and cancel. Conversely any feasible u at y is phi_T(y) for T=supp(u), because its restriction to T is all ones and solves those invertible equations. Thus these maps cover the local feasible fiber as a set. Overlaps are permitted; summing counts over the maps is unsound.

Maintain a basis of degree-at-most-d squarefree feature vectors, including 1, for the feasible partial assignments; choose actual retained assignments by independence and store them. For each retained y output every (y,phi_T(y)), then thin again. For a fixed T, every feature restricted along this map is a Boolean polynomial in y of degree at most d, since affine substitution and squarefree reduction preserve that upper bound. Therefore evaluation rows of the retained inputs, lifted by this single map, span its entire image rows. Taking all T spans the union. The empty initialization and the SCC order prove exactness by induction. Nonvanishing of an explicit degree-d test is decided by checking the retained genuine assignments, one of which is a physical witness when the answer is positive. This includes conjunctions of d affine readout equalities via multiplication of their binary indicators.

With n sites, largest SCC k, and F=sum_{j=0}^{min(d,n)} binomial(n,j), straightforward streaming Gaussian elimination costs 2^k poly(n,F,input size) time and poly(n,F,input size) working space. Generate one affine map at a time and stream its lifted rows against the current retained basis; old basis remains frozen for this block. No product of local section counts across blocks and no assumption on global directed feedback size. For fixed d and logarithmic k this gives a polynomial-time algorithm. The known weighted DAG method covers acyclic activation guards and other weighted degree cases. The affine module proof also allows arbitrary known blocks with one-way interblock dependencies. A single unbounded SCC still has exponentially many section candidates, so there is no all-input polynomial conclusion.
