# Optimal linear cut for fixed bounded readout banks

Let W_j be the binary dual spans of the actual affine observations in fixed banks on n+m original signals. Set V=sum W_j, r=dim V, R=sum dim W_j, h0=R−r. A linear coset cut that guesses the values of a subspace S of normals restricts the live direction to S-perp. It suffices to consider S≤V: replacing any S by its intersection with V preserves all bank and joint rank losses and costs no more guesses. For S≤V the guess charge is g=dim S, the local ranks lose dim(W_j∩S), and the joint rank loses exactly g. Therefore the total guess-plus-Fourier exponent is
g+h(S)=h0+2 dim S−sum_j dim(W_j∩S).

There is a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm finding the exact minimizer on ORIGINAL binary coordinates. Take E=direct sum of private copies of all W_j, dimension R. Form a rectangular matrix space A from E to V⊕V. For each j put its inclusion into the first copy in one matrix, zero on all other summands; put its inclusion into the second copy in another. For any subspace T≤E let L be the span in V of all its block projections. Then the joint image A(T), the sum of all matrix images, is exactly L⊕L. Also dim T≤sum dim(W_j∩L). Conversely, starting from S≤V, take T=direct sum (W_j∩S). Its projected span lies in S, so its shrinkage dim T−dim A(T) is at least sum dim(W_j∩S)−2 dim S. These inequalities prove equality of the two maxima and show that from a maximum-shrinkage T its projected span L attains the desired maximum.

Pad the matrices with zero rows and columns to common order N=max(R,2r). The added N−R dummy input directions increase the maximum shrinkage by exactly N−R, since projecting any padded input subspace leaves the same images and loses at most that many dimensions. From a maximizing padded subspace take the projection onto E. Obtain a maximum-shrinkage subspace over GF(2) deterministically using Corollary 8(2) of Ivanyos, Qiao and Subrahmanyam, https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol067-itcs2017/LIPIcs.ITCS.2017.55/LIPIcs.ITCS.2017.55.pdf . Return L and a binary basis, usable for actual coset guesses. Empty banks and r=0 are trivial. Equivalently the minimum charge is ncRank(A)−r with rectangular noncommutative rank defined via padding. No unproved small-field descent or extraction of a favorable ordinary scalar matrix is required.

For m=O(n) producers of uniformly bounded arity on affine readouts, take the observation list of each producer (including its actual output comparison) as one bank; final output pin is another. Even after an arbitrary chosen cut, each bank can reconstruct all of its own observations in O(1) XOR gates because its dimension and number of observations are bounded. When a producer takes observations from several supplied bounded-size banks the same argument holds if its own arity is bounded. Thus the optimal cut removes any need for supplied cut directions in this class. The conditional exact circuit count/search clock of fourier-graphic-cone-banks.md becomes poly(input) times 2^{min(g+h)+O(n/sqrt(log n))}, using the cited October fixed-density theorem. For larger banks, restricted joint readout complexity must be checked after picking S: this optimizer does not constrain graphicality or local truth-table costs. It also does not find an adequate cone partition of an arbitrary superlinear circuit or establish a uniform strict saving.
