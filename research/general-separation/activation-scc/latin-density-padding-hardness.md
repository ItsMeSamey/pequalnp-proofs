# Hard images immediately below critical false mass

Start with the exact Latin hard image on H of order Q, with at least one original signed representative s. At most 3 sqrt(Q) rail positions can be false. Enlarge the binary group by successive index-two extensions, eventually reaching q≥8Q positions per rail. Retain all old rail bits, pins and star identifications on the subgroup at each step. In the outside coset pin one whole rail true, label a second whole rail by s and the third by its complement. Append these latter positions as leaves of the single existing representative. In the final three doublings choose the ordered nonpinned rail pairs cyclically as (A,B),(B,C),(C,A).

For each original assignment exactly one new outside rail is entirely false and the other two are true. A zero sum in the index-two quotient requires either zero or two outside points; at most one rail can contribute a false outside point. Thus no new violation appears and every old violation persists. This gives a unique extension preserving all original assignments. True pins and signed equalities in disjoint stars suffice. The expanded ternary block remains exactly one full positive global rank-one XOR block and emits q² clauses and O(q) additional boundary constraints.

Each outside coset adds its order to false mass M regardless of s. Hence q−Q≤M≤q−Q+3 sqrt(Q)<q uniformly for Q>9. Every individual rail receives an all-false coset in the last three doublings of size at least q/8 for either value of s. For any fixed 0<α<1 fix an integer K≥1/α and choose q the least power of two at least max(8Q,Q^K), avoiding real-number computations. Size remains polynomial in the original 3SAT instance for fixed α. Decision on these exact Latin instances is thus NP-complete even when all boundary assignments satisfy q−q^α≤M<q and every rail has at least q/8 false positions. Small source sizes can be handled directly or padded.

If the old false sumset A+B+C fills H minus zero for every model (use latin-no-proper-quotient.md), it fills the expanded group minus zero as well. Keep an old false point in each of the two other rails (every base rail is nonempty by the coverage property); summing them with all false points of the new outside rail produces the whole outside coset. Induct. Thus no proper common linear quotient preserves these satisfying witnesses even with constant individual false density and near-critical total mass. This constrains the unchanged padded coordinates, not preprocessing that identifies and removes the guard or the new coset wiring.
