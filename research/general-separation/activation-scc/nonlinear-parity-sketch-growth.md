# All-block-orders growth for exact linear-sketch tables

On a connected bounded-degree edge-expander G with N vertices, give each edge a variable x_e∈F₈ in a fixed three-bit basis. At each vertex require Σ_{e incident}π(x_e)=0 where π(x)=x³. These are constant-size Boolean relations; zero is feasible. Eliminate whole edge variables by ordinary factor substitution and retain the exact single product/projection factor as a truth table on its minimum linear sketch.

For k≥3 the predicate R=[Σ_jπ(x_j)=0] on k boundary variables has trivial binary translation-period space. The permutation π has inverse z^5. If translation by (a_j) preserves R, T_j(z)=(z^5+a_j)^3 preserves zero sums componentwise. With c_j=T_j(0), Σc_j=0. The functions f_j=T_j+c_j agree by substituting equal values in any two coordinates and zeros elsewhere. Substituting z,w,z+w in three coordinates makes their common function additive. But T_j(z)=z+a_j z³+a_j²z⁵+a_j³. Every additive map on F₈ has its unique polynomial of degree <8 supported on exponents 1,2,4: there are 8³ such linearized polynomials, distinct by the root bound, and exactly 2⁹ additive maps. Thus all a_j vanish. A factoring linear sketch has kernel in the period space, and its full indexed truth table requires 2^{3k} entries.

Track components formed by eliminated edges. A merge creates the predicate R on the boundary of the new component: summing vertex equations cancels each internal decoded edge, and the zero-total condition suffices by solving on a spanning tree of eliminated edges. A surviving edge internal to a component has canceled and may be ignored. Some first merge creates a component with size in [N/3,2N/3]. Its edge boundary has Ω(N) members by constant expansion on it or its complement. At that step the minimum linear-sketch dimension is Ω(N), requiring exponential table size for EVERY block order. The factor still has a small nonlinear description, and decoding π makes the entire problem linear.

This stresses the exact projected table under the indicated elimination operations rather than an unconditional decision or circuit bound. Bit-interleavings and nonlinear rewrites of an intermediate or original factor remain outside the test. The local constraints admit constant-size CNF representations and can then be routed to the rank-one Latin target; the same all-orders table bound is not implied for such new presentations.
