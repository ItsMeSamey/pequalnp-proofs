# Low local rank in a cyclic activation system

Use the physical activation equations and genuine degree-d feature bases of activation-scc-sections.md. Inside one module adding diag(a) to M and a to b(y) preserves every guard when its index is active. Suppose the resulting matrix M has rank r, with M=LR. Diagonal entries need not originally vanish.

Fix a downstream y and any feasible local u; freeze z=Ru. Let E be the indices where Lz+b(y) vanishes. The feasible local words with this same z are precisely the solutions Ru'=z supported on E. The fiber is nonempty by construction. Select a basis of columns of R indexed in E and parameterize the fiber by the nonpivot bits on E. Each pivot is an affine function of them and there are at most r pivots. For any Boolean degree-at-most-d test on local and downstream coordinates that is one on the original word, fix y and restrict to this affine parametrization. The restricted Boolean test is nonzero and has degree at most d. A nonzero squarefree function of degree d takes value one on a word of weight at most d by subset inversion. Thus a genuine word in the same fiber with at most r+d original local ones also passes the test.

It follows by duality that the evaluations on local solutions of weight at most r+d, with y fixed, already span all evaluation rows. Retain just the support sections T of size at most r+d in the predecessor theorem. They cover all these sparse solutions at every y, and remain affine and feasible at every y. For any fixed section, lifting an actual downstream feature basis suffices by degree preservation. Trimming genuinely produced assignments therefore yields the exact joint moment space. The clock is n^{O(r+d+1)} polynomial in input size when every module admits the stated bound, with no factor exponential in module depth. The smaller support-section enumeration can be chosen independently per module. High rank and large SCCs simultaneously retain the original gap.

The diagonal rank bound can be sought within this clock. For each 0≤s≤r enumerate s pivot rows I, s pivot columns J, and all diagonal bits on I union J. Once these are assigned the column and row skeletons M[:,J] and M[I,:] are known. For every invertible pivot C=M[I,J], the only possible rank-s completion is M[:,J] C^{-1} M[I,:]. Check it against every fixed off-diagonal entry and assigned diagonal bit. Conversely any rank-s completion has such a nonsingular pivot; handle s=0 by checking all off-diagonals zero. This is an explicit n^{2r}2^{2r}polynomial search, and the supplied/successful completion can be verified directly.

The local rank promise is independent of largest SCC size in one direction: a dense complete directed graph with arbitrary diagonal has off-diagonals all one and a rank-one completion, even with arbitrarily many vertices. Small-rank completion of the intra-module block does not constrain arbitrary inter-module affine reads.
