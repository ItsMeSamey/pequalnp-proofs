# Global private-coordinate mixing still gives the majority test

Retain each distinct original source bit as a signed individual free coordinate in the odd-three-selector SAT reduction. Allow arbitrary invertible changes of all other coordinates, mixing private bits across clauses and adding arbitrary affine functions of all source bits. The existence of one polarity making every individual conflict Horn is still equivalent to making at least two literals true in each source clause on distinct source variables.

Absorb the proposed polarity into the origin. Every pair of selectors in a clause has independent linear parts on the kernel of the source-bit projection, so each selector needs at least one private coordinate. Every conflict f_i=p_i=1 has at least two active coordinates, hence cannot forbid the shifted origin: the all-zero active tuple has at least two zeros. The origin selects only true literals and selects an odd number in each clause.

Suppose a clause has exactly one true literal k at the origin. Then f_i=f_j=1, p_i=p_j=0 and p_k=1. For either false literal the source bit is already at its forbidden value. Changing one outside position on which p_i depends makes p_i=1. If there are at least two such positions, the resulting forbidden tuple has the unchanged source coordinate zero and at least one untouched active coordinate zero, which violates the Horn criterion. Thus each of p_i,p_j depends outside its own source bit on exactly one private coordinate and no other variable. Their private coordinates differ by independence on the source kernel.

The odd equation forces p_k=1+p_i+p_j to depend on both distinct private bits. A Horn rank-two forbidden flat has at most three active coordinates. The k source bit is already the third active coordinate, so no other source bit may occur in p_k. The only possible source dependence of p_i,p_j is on their own two distinct source bits; neither can therefore occur. In particular p_k is independent of the k source bit. Change only that bit at the origin: f_k becomes one and p_k remains one. This forbidden tuple has both private positions zero, a contradiction.

The converse is the simpler clause-private construction in physical-selector-horn-majority.md. Thus 2SAT on pairwise source literal disjunctions exactly recognizes this expanded family. The argument concerns simultaneous local Horn clauses and leaves open changes mixing the actual source bit functions, other encodings, and semantic simplification across the whole conjunction.
