# Cached coordinate-flag joins

Use fixed original clause addresses and signed-star substitution as in latin-coordinate-flag-separators.md. At a cell the boundary consists of names occurring in surviving clauses both inside and outside. Store feasible assignments to the boundary with inside-only names existentially quantified. At a full address leaf, testing a key requires at most three literal branches on the one native clause, with all binary clauses at its address included in 2SAT.

Write P for the parent boundary and B_i for the four child boundaries. Partition children into pairs. For each pair enumerate assignments to the union of its boundaries. Look up both child tables, retaining feasible projected keys on names also present in P or the opposite pair. Store witness pointers. Then jointly enumerate on the union of the two reduced name sets, look up both tables and retain parent keys and pointers. Forgotten names have no occurrence outside the joined group, so actual witnesses merge; traceback and replay at the root. Extra background clauses keep their addresses throughout.

If each cell has boundary size at most b, the two first joins use at most 2b names each. The middle bag contains P plus internal names that cross the pairing. A nonparent boundary name has multiplicity s≥2 in child boundaries; for a uniformly random pairing its crossing probability is 2/3 for s=2 and one for s≥3, hence at most s/3. Total internal multiplicity is at most 4b−|P|. Some pairing therefore has middle bag size at most |P|+(4b−|P|)/3≤2b. Test all three. The deterministic time and conservative space bounds are 4^b poly(q,input), including intermediate pair tables; permanent cell tables have at most 2^b keys each. Choose an optimal maximum cell-boundary flag by min/max DP on the 5^d coordinate cells.

For semantic pruning take H from the actual global binary clauses, retaining each at its address. Enumerate only bag assignments extendible to H by depth-first prefix search with 2SAT tests, with polynomial delay including exhaustion. Every global model survives, and every child witness used at the root satisfies its local addressed constraints. In particular the returned root answer is exact. For a bound K, test each contemplated cell boundary and each intermediate join bag by enumerating up to K+1 H-projections. Over all cells, coordinate splits and three pairings the number of tests is polynomial in q. A bottom-up Boolean planner recognizes flags with at most K projections on each required bag; the search and selected joins take K poly(input,q) time using whole-bag assignment enumeration and deterministic dictionary lookups. This is an explicitly charged test, not a projected counting oracle. Witness pointers recover original bits. The generic operations are relational joins, projections and constraint propagation; the specific layout family admits polynomial search.
