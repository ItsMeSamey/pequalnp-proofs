# Joining incompatible sparse readout charts with one parity per Fourier character

Use n raw bits and m acyclic producer-output bits with all original argument, output-comparison and final-pin observations explicit as affine readouts (see graphic-cone-cut.md). On a supplied joint live subspace U of dimension k, partition restricted readout normals into banks. For bank j let r_j be the dimension of its span. A graphic realization of its labeled columns gives an independent chart on r_j bits where every readout costs at most one XOR of two bits. Zero normals and coset constants are allowed. Alternative explicitly supplied linear circuits are sufficient when their total size is charged. Let R=sum_j r_j and r be the joint rank on U.

The map sending the live vector to all local coordinate vectors y is linear of rank r. Its image is therefore the kernel of an explicitly computed binary matrix D of h=R−r independent rows. Use linear (not affine) local chart coordinates for the live direction, carrying each coset's constants in the producer tables. Then the same D works in every coset. Each y in the kernel has exactly 2^{k−r} live preimages. For incompatible y, producer predicates are still Boolean functions: within each producer use a basis of its observations as LINEAR FUNCTIONS ON THE INDEPENDENT LOCAL y, and tabulate on all those values. Its rank rho_i on this enlarged space can exceed its previous rank on U. Bounded physical arity bounds rho_i unconditionally. Charge sum_i 2^{rho_i}, including all producer checks and the final output condition, even when a producer reads several banks.

Let P(y) be the conjunction of these predicates. For each lambda in F2^h compute an exact integer signed sum of P(y) with sign (-1)^{lambda·D y}. This takes two ordinary fixed-density counts: add one parity computation on y with its required value zero or one, respectively, and subtract. Computing the linear combination lambda D is polynomial offline; its parity circuit uses only O(R) gates, independent of h. Every chart input is one variable used consistently throughout the leaf. Fourier orthogonality gives
count_per_coset = 2^{k-r} ( sum_lambda signed_count_lambda ) / 2^h.
This filters incompatible chart tuples exactly, even if they happen to satisfy all the relational producer checks. Signed intermediate values and running totals have O(R+h+k) bits. There is no randomization or modulus precision requirement. Unique Tseitin extensions and unique forced padding preserve all counts.

For search, find a coset with positive exact count. Pin successive y bits, recomputing the Fourier-filtered subtotal; pinning costs at most R units and preserves density. Solve the resulting joint linear system for an original live vector, undo the coset, and verify the original acyclic circuit. Search needs only polynomially many extra calls once a positive coset is found.

If m,R,sum_i 2^{rho_i}=O(n), with uniform constants, the external October fixed-density bound yields deterministic time poly(input) 2^{n+m-k+h+O(n/sqrt(log n))} and polynomial space. With full U no coset guesses occur. Any certificate guaranteeing n+m−k+h ≤ n−c n/sqrt(log n) for a sufficiently large constant c gives an actual gate-scale speedup. The criterion accepts banks with a nontrivial overlap and needs no single graphic realization of the whole system. The h term is charged and is uncontrolled in general. Fourier filtering of parity constraints is classical; this use retains actual affine producer correlations without adding h dense parity circuits to every leaf.
