# Sparse hard-image guard defeating every proper payload quotient

Take any supplied 3-CNF with m nonempty clauses, short clauses padded by repetitions. Choose h a power of two with h≥8m and h≥a sufficiently large absolute constant. Use all indices of V=F₂^{log₂ h} in the isolated-clause routing with payload G=V×V. Reserve m slots for the original clauses. The remaining slots carry disjoint auxiliary variables and clauses. We construct them so that in EVERY satisfying physical assignment the three false-position sets have A+B+C=G minus {0}. Thus projecting each rail to any common linear quotient of payload with nonzero kernel makes a false source triple collide with zero. A periodic outer-domain template on such a quotient cannot contain any original witness. Original true pins and signed stars are part of the construction.

Independently label each unreserved slot U with probability 2/3, or N_a for a=0,1,2, each probability 1/9. An N_a slot will have exactly its position a true, and its other two positions false. For each (u,v)≠0 in V², partition V into cosets of span(u,v) and pick one k from each. The three positions i=k+u, j=k+v, k are in that coset, and they are not all equal. Discard a coset if any needed slot is reserved; at most m cosets are discarded, leaving at least h/4−m≥h/8. We want a false A occurrence at i, a false B occurrence at j and a false C occurrence at k. For each distinct index among these, at most two of its positions are prescribed false, so some N_a label meets them. Each coset succeeds with probability ≥9^{-3}, independently across the tested cosets. The probability of failing an entire nonzero difference is ≤(1−9^{-3})^{h/8}.

Also require the number K of N slots to be at most half the unreserved slots and at least three. Its binomial distribution with parameter 1/3 violates this with probability exponentially small in h. Sum the two tail probabilities and (h²−1) times the preceding failure bound; this is <1 for all sufficiently large h. The labels are constructible deterministically in polynomial time: condition one slot at a time, minimizing the expected number of failed differences plus the two tail indicators. Each conditional failure probability is a product over disjoint cosets of complements of the required local-label event; the conditioned tails are binomial sums. Their rational numerators/denominators have polynomial bit length. A constant lower cutoff can alternatively be found by doubling h until the explicit initial expectation bound is <1. One can test it with exact rational powers and exact binomial sums.

Number the U slots by fresh variables z_0,…,z_{U−1}, placing the constraint ¬z_j in its unique U slot. They force all guard bits zero. Number the K≤U normal slots by j<K. In slot N_a use the three distinct variables z_j,z_{j+1},z_{j+2} (cyclic modulo U), negating just the occurrence in position a. This yields exactly the promised false signs with all z=0. The guard has a unique satisfying assignment, and any source model extends. Upon padding each U unit with repeated copies, all three rails at that slot are true. Other rail positions are pinned true as in the standard construction. The signed equality stars and all physical pins are unchanged in type, and size remains polynomial; no new type of equation is required.

For the coset witness of (u,v), the sum of the three false payloads (i,0),(0,j),(k,k) equals (u,v). All nonzero payloads occur regardless of original source choices, and zero never occurs under a model because exactly the intended diagonal clauses survive. Hence no nontrivial quotient of these same payload coordinates maintains even existence on ANY satisfiable input in this guarded reduction. The obstruction also applies after applying a common invertible linear change of payload basis. This does not preclude preprocessing the guards away, splitting the rails into separately handled domains, or representing extra non-periodic constraints across a quotient. These inputs have only O(h) unpinned slots on rails of size h².
