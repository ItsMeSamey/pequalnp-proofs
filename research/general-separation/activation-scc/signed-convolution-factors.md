# A recognizable shared-witness representation for signed 3-CNF

Label n≥2 original variables by distinct elements of G=F₂^r with q=2^r between n and 2n. Fix a variable order on each ternary clause. Copy shorter clauses directly. Remove duplicates. For each sign pattern σ and δ∈G, form the zero–one matrix M indexed by i,j∈G. Its entry is one if the signed ternary clause on (i,j,i+j+δ) occurs with σ. These matrices partition the actual clauses. Find a prime q<p≤2q by trial division, and let R be the sum over matrices of their ranks over F_p. This parameter is polynomial-time computable on the supplied labels.

Write M=UV^T with s=rank(M) columns. Let a_i,b_j,c_k be original bits or complements indicating that the corresponding literal is false in positions 1,2,3; zero-pad unused labels. All positions may reference the same original variable. For each column z compute group convolution of j↦V_{jz}b_j with k↦c_k via Walsh transforms over F_p. Inversion divides by q, invertible as p is odd. Set T_i=Σ_z U_{iz} conv_z(i+δ) mod p. This equals Σ_j M_{ij}b_j c_{i+j+δ} mod p. The integer on the right lies in [0,q], so T_i=0 iff no clause in this row has both latter literals false. Require T_i=0 whenever a_i=1. Keep signed/offset blocks separate; no cross-block modular cancellation is allowed.

Each column costs O(q log q) modular additions/subtractions and O(q) modular multiplications including the transforms and row accumulation. Ripple arithmetic and schoolbook multiplication plus reduction by a fixed modulus cost O(log q) and O(log² q) gates respectively. Thus O(R q log²(2q)+n+number of short clauses) bounded-fanin gates/clauses and auxiliary bits suffice. Standard acyclic gate equivalences give a unique extension; recover physical inputs by reading their bits. Recognition and factorization are polynomial (e.g. O(q⁴) field operations). Joining on original variables preserves projection and original multiplicities if auxiliary weights are neutral.

A residual set of clauses may be left untouched. Separate supplied positional label maps also work if each is injective on its active variables, padding to the largest part and reusing original bits across positions. With its natural positional labels the full Latin-square example has R=1. In general R can grow to order q². The representation adds real wire variables to any subsequent variable-sensitive contraction. It yields no unrestricted polynomial-time SAT clock.
