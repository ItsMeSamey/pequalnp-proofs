# Exact fibers and arithmetic recovery from short Boolean traces

In binary-boundary-grid-projection.md put d=n² and K=O(n^6) variable boundary slots. Each legal labeling determines one tuple per vertex, and those tuples uniquely recover all edge labels. Every legal register word has exactly one nonzero extension. Thus each boundary monomial coefficient is one if its tuples match and zero otherwise. Boolean inputs can contain several monomials. Minimal true sets of the monotone support predicate are exactly those d-element monomials. No variable divides them all for n≥2, since every vertex is incident to an edge whose label can be changed.

Let C be a monotone binary AND/OR gate DAG for the boundary predicate, with variable leaves (false constants may be pruned). Arithmetize AND by multiplication and OR by addition over GF(2), giving P. Prior to cancellations the terms are proof trees. The support of every term is accepted and thus has at least d variables. On any minimal true set S the surviving traces have support exactly S. Suppose the number of traces on each such S with at most d+t leaves is odd. There are no disjointness or uniqueness requirements on individual gates. We recover the hard formal polynomial with K^O(t+1) poly(d+t,|C|) gates.

For each j from zero to t extract the degree d+j component P_j. Enumerate sets J of at most j indices and exponents e_i≥2 with sum over i of (e_i−1) equal to j. Extract the exact coefficient in P_j of the indicated powers on J (a polynomial in the remaining variables), then remultiply by the product of the variables in J. For j=0 take P_0 unchanged. At a gate, truncated coefficient arrays with convolution at a product suffice; product(e_i+1)≤4^j and homogeneous extraction costs polynomial in d+t per gate. Each resulting monomial has degree d and exactly the same support as before. Hence its support size is d and all coordinates outside J were already squarefree. Every contributing input monomial is counted exactly once at its own repeated indices and powers. The coefficient of each minimal S is now the promised parity. No other monomials survive.

A polynomial-size certificate can establish that parity condition for certain circuits. The independent permutations of labels on every edge act transitively on legal global labelings. For every adjacent transposition on each edge, provide a permutation of C's gates implementing its induced renaming of boundary tuple variables, preserving output, gate types and incidence multiplicities. Each permutation and its action can be verified in polynomial time. Counts of traces of each length are then the same on every minimal S. On the all-zero labeling's minimal set substitute z for selected input variables and zero elsewhere. Truncated univariate circuit evaluation through degree d+t and one sum of parity coefficients tests the condition on this representative. The certificate checks the trace condition assuming C computes the target, as is appropriate for a lower bound against correct deciders. Without symmetry it still has a polynomial check at each supplied S.

Conditional on the characteristic-two source arithmetic lower, all polynomial-size correct families meeting the trace condition are excluded at all sufficiently large n for fixed t, even for t=o((log n)^(1/12)). The latter bound follows since log K=O(log n). DAG sharing, odd ambiguity and repeated variables in short traces are allowed, and long traces are unrestricted. This does not cover arbitrary circuits: doubling a subcomputation at OR can destroy parity, and duplicating it at AND can shift all its contributions beyond the short window while leaving its Boolean value intact.
