# Factoring shared affine tests in a Boolean DAG

A boundary bit can indicate membership in an affine flat on the original inputs, or its complement after changing the corresponding suffix gate. Recognize such bits with forward rules, storing reduced affine equations. An affine XOR of affine signals supplies its one-equation predicate. Conjunction of flat predicates takes their intersection by stacking equations. An OR of nested flats gives the larger. Two distinct cosets of the same direction subspace merge under OR into their affine span over F₂, since the displacement adds just two cosets. The empty/full cases and complements via De Morgan have their usual values. These exhaust affine unions of two nonempty flats: for overlapping nonnested flats the sum of their sizes minus the intersection size cannot be a power of two, while disjoint flats would have to be two disjoint hyperplanes in their affine union and therefore parallel. Track either or both truth-value certificates where available. Each raw wire needs at most two reduced systems. The time and stored description are polynomial in the original DAG and input length. Failure of a rule is inconclusive. For the cycle-edge AND tree, all pair gates and their conjunctions merge into one flat x_i=1 for every i.

Suppose r such boundary bits separate p inputs u from an arbitrary H on those bits and the remaining q−p inputs z. Write g_i=1[A_i u=b_i(z)] for fixed binary matrices A_i and shared RHS circuits. Arbitrary RHS circuits are allowed if their form is verified separately. Absorb boundary signs into H. Stack all A_i into A mapping to the direct sum of equation-coordinate spaces E_i. Compute S=im A and P_i=S∩E_i, using the natural coordinate-block embedding. Decompose S=J⊕(⊕_i P_i), let p_i=dim P_i, and use c=rank A−Σ_i p_i bits w for J. Denote the E_i component of J(w) by L_i(w). Bases and a right inverse of A on S follow from elimination.

For fixed z,w, predicate i compares a freely selectable v_i∈P_i to h_i=b_i(z)+L_i(w). Different i have independent choices. Let t_i=1[h_i∈P_i]. Replace g_i by t_i when p_i=0, and by t_i AND d_i for one fresh bit d_i when p_i>0. This gives a SAT circuit on q−p+c+|{i:p_i>0}| inputs. Its additional affine maps and membership tests have polynomial gate size in the explicit matrices and initial representation; dense transforms carry their actual cost. The savings include dim ker A and p_i−1 for each p_i≥1. Thus r predicates with at least k≥2 private equation-space dimensions each save at least r(k−1) inputs even with arbitrary shared equations.

To map an original assignment forward, decompose Au into J(w) plus the v_i and choose each new bit to reproduce g_i. To lift a satisfying code, choose v_i=0 whenever h_i is incompatible. For compatible i of positive p_i use h_i for a required one or h_i plus any fixed nonzero member of P_i for a required zero. At p_i=0 choose zero. Lift J(w)+Σv_i through the right inverse to original u. This reproduces every boundary output and hence the witness. At fixed z,w the multiplicity of a prescribed mask is 2^{dim ker A} times the product of local multiplicities: if t_i=0 it is 2^{p_i} for false and zero for true, whereas if t_i=1 it is one for true and 2^{p_i}−1 for false. The raw replacement can change ordinary counts.

At a given z, full joint surjectivity onto all r bits holds if and only if b(z) lies in S and every p_i>0. Necessity follows from the all-ones target and each one-zero target by subtracting its realizing u from the all-ones witness. Conversely start with Au=b and for each requested zero add a preimage of any fixed nonzero vector in P_i. This independently disables the required equations. If b(z)∈S for all z and all p_i>0, omit w and use r free bits directly. For affine b in z, uniform consistency is equivalent to checking its constant and every coefficient vector belong to S. For arbitrary supplied RHS functions additional verification may be needed for this shortcut. The first replacement still works.

Neither procedure guarantees a gain on an arbitrary boundary or suffix. On cycle-edge masks feeding an arbitrary H, the repeated endpoint rows give P_i=0 and c=n. Flattening a complete AND suffix changes the boundary and permits compression. The separate forbidden-pattern algorithm for cycle masks accommodates arbitrary H.
