# Preserving the joint image of privately quadratic circuit wires

Let H(z,Q_z(u)) be a verified circuit decomposition, with p private bits u and r frontier wires. The quadratic parts of Q_z in u are fixed; their affine terms may be arbitrary shared functions of z. Let B(s) be the polar matrix of s·Q_z. Let D⊂F₂^r be a linear subspace of dimension d, let T be a basis matrix with row space D, and put t=dim{B(s):s∈D}. Define S(D)=Σ_{s∉D} 2^{−rank(B(s))/2}.

If S(D)<2^{-t}, then for every z the full image of Q_z is precisely {y : Ty ∈ image(TQ_z)}. Every completion of every feasible projected output occurs. The projection may have nonlinear support.

Write M(y) for the number of u with Q_z(u)=y, and M_D(a) for the count with TQ_z(u)=a. Fourier inversion splits M(y) into the contribution 2^{d−r}M_D(Ty) from D and a remainder of magnitude at most 2^{p−r} S(D), by the quadratic Gauss bound. Choose another basis of D with d−t zero-polar equations, hence affine, and t equations of private degree at most two. If Ty is feasible its indicator is a nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree at most d+t. A nonzero degree-e polynomial on p bits has support at least 2^{p−e} for e≤p, by induction using a first derivative; for e>p the real lower bound still holds. Hence the low contribution is at least 2^{p−r−t}. The strict bound makes M(y)>0. Conversely every attainable y has feasible Ty. An individual feasible a suffices whenever 2^d M_D(a)>2^p S(D). The theorem is about support and allows unequal fiber counts.

## Computing and compiling projections

A sufficient condition is rank B(s)≥2(r+t) for every s outside D. It gives the strict bound since there are 2^r−2^d terms. Compute all 2^r ranks once. Starting from D=0, adjoin and span every selector below 2(r+t), recompute t, and repeat to stability. At most r strict enlargements occur. The result is the least subspace satisfying this sufficient condition: by induction every such subspace contains the current one, since its polar dimension is no smaller. The full space qualifies vacuously. The sharper aggregate bound can be tested on any supplied D. All ranks, spans and exact dyadic sums cost 2^r times a polynomial in the description size and dimensions.

If a shared outside DAG supplies the binary affine coefficients of Q_z in u at cost G, construct the predicate M_D(Ty)>0 without u. For each of the 2^d projected characters the fixed polar has a precomputed alternating normal form. Its Gauss value is zero unless the radical coefficients vanish; otherwise its magnitude is 2^{p−rank/2} and its sign is a quadratic function of the private linear coefficients plus the phase constant. This follows by summing over paired coordinates and the radical. Compute these conditions and signs from the shared wires, include the corresponding character of Ty, and sum signed integers of O(p+d+1) bits. Positivity is the membership test. For r,p≥1 one implementation adds O(rd+2^d(p²+rp+p+d+1)) gates besides G and H on the q−p+r outside and replacement input bits. All preprocessing and the coefficient-supplier circuit must be charged. Small d controls the size of the compiled instance. Alternatively one can compute the projected table once per z using 2^d Gauss sums and a Walsh transform, then test candidate y. Without the criterion, tabulating all r-bit output multiplicities for every z already costs only 2^r Gauss sums per z.

For example take r≥3, d=t=2 and low coordinates ab,(a+1)c. They miss 11 and realize 00,01,10. For each of the remaining g=r−2 outputs sum r+2 products on fresh independent pairs disjoint from every other output. Each selector with nonzero high part has polar rank at least 2(r+2). The closure finds exactly the low span and the joint image is the cylinder over those three patterns. The affine hull is full. The native construction takes O(p) gates with p=3+2g(r+2). All binary-affine perturbations remain covered even if the low image changes. Each projected membership uses four Gauss contributions, irrespective of g.

An arbitrary input DAG need not expose a private cut or a small D. The identity frontier can leave the entire original H. Neither unrestricted CircuitSAT nor uniform P versus NP is resolved.
