# Which two privately quadratic outputs survive every affine perturbation?

Let B and C be the homogeneous quadratic coefficient arrays, equivalently alternating 2-forms over F₂ on the private input space V. Different Boolean quadratics with the same polar differ by an affine function. Say the pair is robust if every pair of Boolean quadratics with these respective polars maps V onto F₂².

The pair is robust if and only if B∧C is nonzero in the exterior algebra. The positive test is the mixed Pfaffian on a four-direction bag from the companion private quadratic note. When negative, one can explicitly construct two affine perturbations and an omitted output. Both decisions and certificates take deterministic polynomial time in dim V. In particular one can examine all four-coordinate minors of B∧C. This criterion asks for arbitrary independent affine perturbations, which is stronger than surjectivity of a specified map.

If B∧C≠0, a four-coordinate bag has nonzero mixed Pfaffian. Every one of the four output values occurs with odd multiplicity on that bag under every affine perturbation; fixing the other private bits does not affect the quadratic parts. For the converse, a dependent pair of polars can be perturbed to satisfy a nontrivial linear relation on the two output bits. Assume henceforth that they are independent and their wedge vanishes. An invertible output basis change preserves both robustness and nonvanishing of B∧C, since alternating 2-forms over F₂ have zero exterior square.

We claim the three nonzero members of their pencil include a rank-two form. Suppose first that some member B has rank at least six. In alternating coordinates write B=P₁+…+P_k with each P_j a disjoint coordinate pair and k≥3. For a coordinate pair I which is not one of the P_i choose a P_j disjoint from I. In the four-coordinate union I∪P_j, the only entire pair from B is P_j, so the coefficient of B∧C on this four-set equals C_I. It follows that C contains only P_i terms. On P_i∪P_j their coefficients sum to zero, so C is proportional to B. This contradicts independence.

If the maximum rank is four, take B=P₁+P₂. For any pair I containing an outside coordinate one can again choose a disjoint P_j, leaving only that entire B pair in I∪P_j. Consequently C is supported on the same four coordinates. On that block the quadratic Pfaffian obeys Pf(B+C)=Pf(B)+Pf(C) because the cross coefficient is precisely B∧C=0. Since Pf(B)=1, at least one of C,B+C has Pfaffian zero. It is nonzero and hence has rank two. If the maximum rank is below four, the rank-two conclusion is immediate.

Choose a basis of the pencil with first form B=ℓ∧m of rank two, where ℓ,m are independent linear bits. Let W=ker ℓ∩ker m. Vanishing of ℓ∧m∧C means C restricts to zero on W by extending ℓ,m to a coordinate basis. Set the first quadratic equal to ℓm and demand its value be one. This pins the input to the affine flat ℓ=m=1. The other quadratic restricts to an affine function on that flat; extend this affine function globally and add it to the second quadratic. The output (1,1) is now impossible. Convert the perturbations and target back through the invertible output change. All steps use binary Gaussian elimination and polynomially many coefficients.

## A recursive obstruction and a high-rank construction

For r≥2 outputs, a zero-polar pencil member can similarly be made a constant, defeating robustness. With a rank-two member ℓ∧m, choose output basis starting with this member and restrict all other polars to W. The full exterior product is nonzero exactly when the restricted product on W of the other r−1 forms is nonzero. This follows by keeping only indices outside ℓ,m in the remaining factors. A constructive failure of robustness for these restricted forms extends to a failure for the full pencil: realize it on ℓ=m=1 by extending affine terms globally and demand the first product equal one. Hence repeated rank-two pivots terminating at at most two outputs give a complete positive-or-negative decision through the wedge test. For one remaining output any nonzero polar is robust, since no affine perturbation makes its Boolean function constant. Nothing here establishes necessity of the wedge test on a general r-output pencil.

For a complementary positive test, if every nonzero pencil member has rank ≥4r(r−1)+r, a verifying 2r-direction bag can be constructed in 2^r poly(dim V,r) time even with no chosen coordinates. After j pairs are chosen, take U to be their common orthogonal complement under all r forms. Its codimension is at most 2rj, and restriction lowers any rank by at most twice this codimension. For j<r all nonzero combinations on U therefore have rank at least r. Their radicals occupy a union strictly smaller than U by the sum bound (2^r−1)2^{-r}|U|. Select v outside this union deterministically: fix its U-coordinate bits one at a time and count completions in each radical by linear algebra, retaining strict inequality of the sum to all completions. The r functionals B_i(v,·) on U are independent. Choose w∈U so these functionals have the values of the (j+1)th standard unit vector. Across different pairs every form vanishes, while inside each pair exactly its designated form is one. These 2r directions are independent by testing potential relations against each designated partner. Their mixed Pfaffian equals one. The high-rank hypothesis and all searches use only 2^r times polynomial work. This bound is sufficient, without an optimality claim.
