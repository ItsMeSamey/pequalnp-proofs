# Rewriting overlapping conjunctions before an arbitrary shared circuit

Let a q-input circuit have p private bits reaching its shared suffix H only through r conjunctions of signed literals. Other inputs are free arguments of H. Output complements and outside-dependent guards can be applied in H. Flatten AND DAGs using associativity and idempotence, or complemented OR gates via De Morgan, when gate boundaries certify this presentation. Store a literal set per recognized gate, removing repeats and contradictory terms. Preprocessing costs polynomial time and can retain original gates for evaluation. D counts the boundary terms containing any one private coordinate after simplification. It is distinct from raw gate fanout.

A local reset certificate for v prescribes bits on a set B_v containing v with x_v=1. On this pattern flipping only v to zero must leave every boundary output unchanged for every completion. For pairwise disjoint such B_v one can enforce avoidance of all prescribed patterns by doing just those resets. Disjointness preserves every other tested local pattern. Thus every original input has a polynomial-time equivalent representative. Conversely every representative is an original input. Enumerating the allowed patterns in each block and unrestricted remaining bits costs poly(description size + q) · 2^q Π_v(1−2^{−|B_v|}). This gives deterministic SAT and a raw witness for arbitrary H. Original #SAT multiplicities may differ. The lemma applies to arbitrary locally verified gates admitting such certificates.

For an explicit SAT circuit each block B has radix 2^{|B|}−1, skipping the forbidden word in binary order. One mixed-radix integer for the product of these radices needs the ceiling of its binary logarithm, besides unchanged input coordinates. Reject padding codes. Binary long division by known radices gives a polynomial-size decoder (O(q³) binary gates suffices). Reuse the original boundary gates and H on decoded inputs. To encode an original witness, do the resets then encode the digits. For fixed maximum block size b a linear-size decoder saves a constant fraction of packed blocks: group K=ceil(1/[−log₂(1−2^{−b})]) blocks at a time. Their product of allowed alphabet sizes fits in one fewer bit than their total raw length. Use a constant-size table per group. An incomplete group can be left unrestricted. The linear gate constant depends on b.

## Unate terms of arbitrary arity

If every private variable has one consistent sign, flip coordinates to make the terms positive; undo the flips on witnesses. Fix a cyclic order in every term of size at least two. For a vertex v with no incident singleton term let B_v consist of v and its successor in each incident term. Prescribe v=1 and every successor zero. This blocks all terms incident to v regardless of its value. Isolated vertices also qualify. Each B_v has size at most D+1. Each coordinate belongs to at most D+1 such sets, since it has just one predecessor per term. Greedy disjoint packing from e eligible vertices finds at least e/(D+1)² blocks: each selected block meets at most (D+1)² candidates including itself.

Therefore SAT costs poly·2^{q−Ω(e/((D+1)² 2^D))}, with absolute constant. For e=Θ(p) and D≤(1−ε)log₂p with fixed ε>0 the saving exceeds all polynomial factors in p; the same holds in q if p≥q^δ for fixed δ>0. This includes cycles of edge ANDs feeding arbitrary nonmonotone H. The familiar canonical set in the unate case is the union of enabled terms. Monotone signatures as union closure and polynomial-delay enumeration are established results. The displayed reduction uses forbidden raw patterns and a circuit decoder. [Bérczi et al., Remark 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.06727) [Mary and Strozecki](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/5549)

On disjoint complete graphs of k=D+1 vertices an edge mask identifies every vertex subset of size at least two. The empty set and all singletons give zero. For p divisible by k there are (2^k−k)^{p/k} masks. Even an arbitrary decoder covering every mask for every possible suffix can save at most O(p/2^D) bits for growing k. Along p=k floor(2^k/k) this loss is O(1) with D=log₂p+O(1) and Θ(p log p) edge gates. One star already has 2^{p−1} masks at p−1 gates. Small average occurrence alone does not ensure an entropy saving growing faster than log p.

## Mixed signs

With sign changes across different occurrences of the same bit, use its raw orientation. To reset v it suffices to set one other literal in every incident term false, consistently on all chosen blocker coordinates. In a linear hypergraph (two distinct terms meet in at most one variable), cyclic successors of the same v on different terms are distinct. The same bound thus applies to arbitrary signs at all eligible variables on such hypergraphs.

There is a uniform extension to every signed family of nonsingleton terms at maximum occurrence two, of any arities. First detach each component on a pair of variables occurring together in two size-two terms. At occurrence two these variables have no other incidences. Each such pair of conjunctions supplies at most three output patterns on four inputs, as it consists of two possibly equal minterms. Enumerate one representative per pattern using a constant-size table.

For each remaining v choose cyclic successors in its at most two incident terms. A conflict can occur only if the successors coincide and prescribe opposite blocker bits. At least one of the two terms then has size at least three; switch its choice to the second cyclic successor, which is distinct from both v and the conflicting neighbor. Each repaired block has size at most three. Each coordinate is chosen as first or second successor at most twice per incident term, in addition to its own center occurrence, so belongs to at most five blocks. From the p' remaining vertices pack at least p'/15 disjoint blocks. Together with the detached pairs the enumeration cost is at most poly·2^{q−p}(3^{(p−p')/2})2^{p'}(7/8)^{p'/15}, hence poly·2^{q−Ω(p)} with absolute constant. The reset and table maps produce raw witnesses in both directions. For constant group packing the detached pair radix 3 yields the same linear-size decoder conclusion.

Maximum occurrence three already permits injectivity: on every disjoint pair take three distinct two-literal minterms. The four raw states map to three distinct one-hot vectors and zero. A suffix-independent decoder covering all attainable masks requires at least the original number of bits. This leaves open savings from using a particular H and gives no lower bound for unrestricted SAT.
