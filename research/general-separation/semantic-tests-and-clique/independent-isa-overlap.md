# Arbitrary Boolean decoding with bounded feature overlap

Let S Boolean atoms have maximum simultaneous firing count h. Any Boolean answer determined by their outputs has an exact integer representation on products of at most h atoms. Extend the decoder arbitrarily to all subsets U of [S] of size at most h, assigning bits g(U). For |T|≤h give the product on T coefficient sum_{U⊆T}(−1)^{|T|−|U|}g(U). Inversion on subsets recovers g at every realized pattern. Each coefficient has absolute value ≤2^h, there are at most (S+1)^h products for h≥1, and their total absolute weight is ≤[2(S+1)]^h. The empty product is constant. Conjunction of j binary formulas has at most j times their leaf cap, and concatenation of j acyclic existential programs preserves existence with at most their summed query budget (up to constant terminal conventions). No complement compilation is needed. This is a change of dictionary: it handles arbitrarily large or even absent original linear weights.

## One language with different overlap thresholds

At large N split the input into halves of sizes H=floor(N/2),ceil(N/2). Write k=floor(log₂H). In the first half take t=floor(k/2), and in the second take t=floor(3k/4). As in [the independent-bank construction](independent-isa-all-lengths.md), m=2^t, P=2^floor(log₂(m/t)), and r=floor(H/(log₂P+Pt+m)). Ignore trailing bits. Output the balanced parity sign of all selected payload bits across both halves, converted to a bit. Length computations are uniform and the shared circuit has size O(N). The first half has m,r both Θ(sqrt(N)). The second has m=Θ(N^(3/4)) and r=Θ(N^(1/4)). For small lengths set the output to the first bit where available.

Fix constants d≥1 and b≥0, and suppose S≤N^d, each atom has size W≤N(log N)^b and some arbitrary Boolean decoder computes this language exactly. Then for all large N some input must activate at least

* Ω_d,b(sqrt(N)/(log N)^(b+1)) atoms for binary formulas;
* Ω_d,b(N^(1/4)/(log N)^(b+1)) atoms for unrestricted-successor acyclic existential programs.

For formulas hardwire all banks in the second half to yield fixed sign +1, and for programs do this in the first. The worst-case overlap cannot increase. Apply the expansion above, with new per-atom cap hW. The [correlation bounds](independent-isa-all-lengths.md) give per-bank brackets O((D/m)^(1/3)) for formulas and O(sqrt(D)(r/m)^(1/4)) for programs, with D=1+hW/(rP). In either retained half D≤1+C h(log N)^(b+1). Suppose h≤c r/(log N)^(b+1) for a small positive constant c. In the formula half m=Θ(r); in the program half m=Θ(r³). Hence the respective per-bank bracket can be made at most 1/4 for all sufficiently large N by taking c small. The balanced target has correlation 1/2 with its sign, so the expansion must have absolute weight at least 4^r/2. But its log weight is at most h log(2(S+1))≤C_d c r. Reducing c again gives a contradiction. The exponent b=0 is included.

At near-linear atom budget of at least a sufficiently large constant times N the powers of N are attained up to polylogarithmic factors. For formulas use one atom for each bank and primary row: test that row and its payload lookup. Only one atom can fire per bank, and the decoder takes parity across outputs. There are O(N) such atoms and O(sqrt(N)) can be active. For programs use the same row atoms on the second half, costing overlap O(N^(1/4)). In the first half each entire bank bit or its complement has an unambiguous program of O(m+P sqrt(m)) nodes from the [pooled lookup construction](isa-sparse-range.md). Compute the XOR of a group of Θ(N/(m+P sqrt(m))) complete banks in one O(N)-node program. Carry two parity states across banks using unambiguous checks for either value. The groups partition the first-half banks, giving O(1+r(m+P sqrt(m))/N)=O(N^(1/4)) programs. Decode all outputs by parity, with at most O(N^(1/4)) active features. Counts and budget constants can absorb rounding at large N.

These bounds constrain activation patterns of every small dictionary, including a dictionary for an exact real SUM with unbounded coefficients. An unrestricted polynomial-size dictionary is allowed to have the exhibited high overlap. There is no unrestricted signed sparsity lower bound in this conclusion.
