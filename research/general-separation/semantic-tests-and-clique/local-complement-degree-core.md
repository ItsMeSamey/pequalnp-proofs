# Local complementation strengthens proof-degree core extraction

Fix formal binary polynomial-calculus degree D≥2 and three pinned inputs equal to one. At a free vertex v with neighbor indicator a, local complementation changes adjacency to A′=A+aaᵀ+diag(a). Write y=Ax. Set x′_v=1+x_v+y_v and x′_i=x_i elsewhere. Then y′_v=y_v and y′_i=y_i+a_i(1+x_i) elsewhere. This is an affine bijection with inverse given by the same rule in the changed graph. At v the difference x′_v y′_v+x_v y_v is y_v²+y_v. At every other site it is a_i(x_i²+x_i). Thus every named guard, including its vertex identity, is preserved modulo degree-two Boolean equations. The three pinned inputs are unchanged, and neighbor pin output corrections vanish after setting the pins to one. Substitution of affine forms is degree-preserving in both directions for D≥2, so local complementation at a free site preserves the exact bounded-degree refutation status.

Use the previously known two physical branches at v: delete v, or locally complement there and then delete it. By zeroing the deleted input the first child represents x_v=0. Inverting the second represents x_v+y_v=1. Their union covers all three legal local states. Both retain the three named original pins, and a recorded sequence lifts a final witness in reverse.

This strengthens the [ordinary deletion core](degree-critical-pin-core.md). From an unrefuted input, move to any degree-D unrefuted child among these two at any free site. Stop at three pins or when every such child refutes. If only pins remain, an unrefuted instance has the three-pin word as a witness. Otherwise a terminal node is a genuine NO with no unrefuted child in either orientation at any free site. Its exact minimum formal degree is D+1. To see the upper bound, choose one free site. Lifting the direct deletion proof with the indicator 1+x_v gives 1+x_v=0 at degree D+1. Lifting the second child in its own graph similarly and undoing the affine transformation gives x_v+y_v=0. Together these say x_v=y_v=1. The original guard immediately refutes at degree at most two given these derived linear equations. Each lift multiplies the restricted proof by the indicator and uses the Boolean field equations to cancel the zeroed generator differences. This costs at most D+1 as in the ordinary core proof.

At most n−3 iterations with at most 2n child tests per iteration are necessary. If the starting graph was YES the chosen unrefuted child could still be NO; the reported NO in that case certifies a failure of degree-D completeness on the reported core. On a genuine starting NO this descent must end at a genuine counterexample of exact degree D+1 whenever the input survived degree D. Thus searching for a counterexample to universal cubic completeness may enforce stronger criticality with no change to whether one exists.

The [bounded checker](local_pin_degree_core.py) exhausts both branch truths and checks every pin-respecting input's guard identities for the nine- and thirteen-vertex fixtures. On the thirteen-vertex degree-three NO exactly four of its ten free vertices have both children refuted at degree two. The deterministic descent at degree two finds an eleven-vertex core with all sixteen children refutable at degree two. The subroutine for computing the bounded-degree closure used by the checker limits the affine-slice dimension to sixteen. The mathematical bound uses ordinary polynomial-size fixed-degree linear algebra.
