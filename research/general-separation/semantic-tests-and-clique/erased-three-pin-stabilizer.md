# Recognizing the stabilizer left after fixing the pins

A graph can have only scalar sitewise endomorphisms and still admit polynomial-time three-pin feasibility and witness recovery. Two sufficient conditions here are that the remaining code after fixing the pin pairs is locally CSS, or is locally GF(4)-linear. They also allow more pins if the binary rank of their cut is at most three.

Let A be loopless symmetric binary adjacency and L={(x,Ax)}. Fix each named pair to (1,0) by elimination; reject inconsistency. Denote the free restriction of one linear solution by v. Let S be the free restrictions of words of L whose pinned pairs are zero. The free fiber is v+S. A solution is precisely a word of that fiber avoiding (1,1) on all free sites. The vector v is symplectically orthogonal to S, and q(z)=Σ x_i y_i vanishes on S by alternation of A. For f free sites put s=f−dim₂S. The free first bits in the nullspace defining S satisfy only A[P,F]x_F=0, so s equals the rank of that cut. A returned word in v+S recovers the original witness by reattaching the fixed pairs.

## CSS on the remaining code

Apply Wang and Krastanov to S, which need not be maximal. A successful conversion supplies local invertibles g with gS=D×E and D orthogonal to E. Put e=(1,1) everywhere and h(z)=e+g(z+e)=gz+w. This bijection preserves the forbidden pair locally. Thus q(hz)=q(z), and q(w)=0. For z∈S, q(z)=q(gz)=0, so w is orthogonal to gS. Consequently h(v)=(u₀,v₀) belongs to E^perp×D^perp. The transformed fiber is (u₀+D)×(v₀+E). This requires only w∈(gS)^perp. [Wang and Krastanov, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28737v1)

Let B have independent rows spanning E^perp and columns b_i. Functionals on the ambient column space correspond bijectively to rows of E^perp by evaluation on B. The coefficient functionals yielding D have an annihilator W of dimension dim E^perp−dim D=s. Prescribing on W the functional lambda from u₀ is exactly the condition that the evaluated row lie in u₀+D. A subset of columns with indicator v' sums to p=Bv₀ precisely when v' lies in v₀+E. Moreover p∈W since v₀ is orthogonal to D. Hence a functional extending lambda and killing each selected column, with selected sum p, gives exactly an admissible word. Invoke the established three-dimensional trace theorem when s≤3 and invert h on the output. This handles zero and repeated columns. [Three-dimensional trace](../../three-dimensional-trace/README.md)

## Locally GF(4)-linear remaining code

There are just two binary two-dimensional maps with irreducible polynomial t²+t+1, conjugate by swapping the two site coordinates. A sitewise change making S linear over GF(4) exists exactly when a local preserving map J has trace and determinant one everywhere. Coordinate swaps g then make all blocks equal to one primitive field multiplication. They preserve q and (1,1). For full graph codes, recognition of equivalence to a linear GF(4) code via an equation on the adjacency was given by Danielsen and Parker, crediting Van den Nest. The test below applies the same local-symmetry concept to a possibly nonmaximal binary subspace. [Danielsen and Parker](https://www.codetables.de/larsed/selfdual.pdf)

Compute by linear elimination the space of binary masks b for which diag(b_i I₂) preserves S. It contains one and is closed under componentwise multiplication. Group sites with identical patterns across a mask-space basis. Each class indicator belongs to the mask space by multiplying basis masks or their complements. Hence S splits across the classes. Process each independently. Impose JS⊆S and block traces one, an affine linear system. By Cayley–Hamilton J²+J is a preserving scalar mask of the block determinants, constant on each class (extension by any constant on other classes preserves their subspaces). On a single class impose determinant one at one representative. In affine parameters, a reduced binary quadratic equals zero identically if and only if it is zero at all parameter vectors of Hamming weight at most two. Thus find a parameter attaining one or certify failure. Combine the successful classwise maps. This checks the order-three case; a CSS rank-one projector has determinant zero.

Put C=gS. It has field dimension (f−s)/2 and is Hermitian self-orthogonal: the binary symplectic pairing is the trace of the Hermitian pairing and C is closed under field scalars. Every word has even weight, and q=0 by the swap construction. The parity of the number of sites with exactly one bit equal to one is therefore zero. This says that e is binary orthogonal to C. Every binary orthogonal vector is Hermitian orthogonal to C by field linearity. Adding e turns avoidance of (1,1) into full support. We seek a full word in v'+C with v'=gv+e∈C^perp_h.

The quotient C^perp_h/C has field dimension s and nondegenerate Hermitian form. Compute an isometry phi to the standard Hermitian space GF(4)^s by Gram–Schmidt. In any nonzero nondegenerate such space a vector of norm one occurs among basis vectors or two-vector combinations with one nonzero scalar: if all singletons have norm zero, a nonzero pairing gives a nonzero trace for some scalar multiplier. Restrict to the orthogonal complement and continue. Construct

K = {(u,phi(u+C)) : u∈C^perp_h}.

This is Hermitian self-orthogonal because the two pairings in a dot product agree and cancel. Its dimension (f+s)/2 is half its length. Fix the s auxiliary coordinates to phi(v'+C); only the original f coordinates must all be nonzero. A solution gives a word of the required coset and inverse translation and swaps restore the physical witness.

When s≤3, the number of zeros among these fixed values must be even, because K has even length and every word has even weight. With no zeros, invoke the [three-pin fusion algorithm](f4-three-pin-fusion.md). With fewer than three prescribed symbols, pad by trying nonzero values at enough free coordinates, or enumerate directly if the total length is below three. For exactly two zeros, fuse those two sites along their equality line and puncture as in the fusion proof. In the full-fiber case choose the zero pair by adjusting the lift. Otherwise the unique lift has equal coordinates f(w); impose the one field equation f(w)=0 on a full residual word. That proof solves it by at most two binary affine readouts on an all-even graph. At most one additional prescribed nonzero symbol remains, which global scaling restores without disturbing the zero pair. For an empty residual the zero word has vacuously full support. This procedure prescribes zeros by a constrained lift; directly shortening both zero coordinates need not preserve self-duality.

## A scalar-only family

Subdivide every edge of K_{m,m} once, with m≥3. Add an edge joining distinct original vertices u,v of the same old side, require them as pins, and require a third original vertex of that side. The graph is connected, of minimum degree at least two and maximum degree m+1. Its girth is at least five and the new edge creates a five-cycle. For any loopless connected nonbipartite graph of girth at least five and minimum degree at least two, the entire site endomorphism algebra is scalar. Indeed, writing local blocks in row-major order as a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i, preservation requires diag(c)+diag(d)A=A diag(a)+A diag(b) A. Two distinct neighbors of i are nonadjacent and have no second common neighbor, forcing b_i=0. The diagonal then gives c_i=0, and on every edge ij one has d_i=a_j. Connectivity of the even-length walk relation in a connected nonbipartite graph forces all a_i,d_i equal. The full host therefore has neither the local CSS projector nor an order-three symmetry.

Nevertheless removing the edge uv gives a bipartite graph. Toggling an edge between two pins does not change S: all zero-pair words of the two graph charts agree since their x bits are zero at the endpoints. On a bipartite graph the full graph code is locally CSS by swapping the site pairs on one side. Shortening a CSS space at complete pin pairs keeps it split, so the S for the displayed graph meets the CSS condition. Thus a positive recognition reaches unbounded-degree scalar-only inputs. This example does not show hardness of the other scalar-only instances. Neither reduced-code symmetry is proved necessary for tractability.
