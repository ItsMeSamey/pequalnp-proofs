# Signed joint counting for all successor neighborhoods

One switch per long clause and one shared parity calculation suffice for exact counts at unrestricted incidence density. Bannach, Demaine, Gomez and Hecher give this clause-switch identity in Proposition 2.1 and the even-minus-odd step in Section 3. Their additional symmetry argument achieves two width-two calls. The weighted implication also occurs in the relaxed Tseitin transformation. The application below concerns projected query programs and their hierarchy clock. [Bannach et al.](https://dbai.tuwien.ac.at/staff/hecher/easy_sharp2sat.pdf) · [Meert et al., “Convert to an Implication”](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~guyvdb/papers/MeertStarAI16.pdf)

Let F conjoin any collection of width-three-or-shorter clauses and p other clauses C. For each other C introduce z_C. For each literal l in C impose (NOT z_C OR NOT l). Keep the short clauses and add a fully defined chain computing XOR of all switches. Count twice, pinning parity to zero and one. For p=0 the parity is zero. The equivocal choice of a switch is intentional; the XOR gate outputs themselves are uniquely determined by width-three clauses.

For each assignment meeting the short clauses let f be the number of false other clauses. Only the switches for those f clauses are free. The assignment contributes (1−1)^f to the even count minus the odd count, with value one for f=0. Assignments violating a short clause contribute zero. Thus the difference is precisely #F even with empty or tautological clauses. A CNF with n variables and m arbitrary clauses can be counted with two width-three counts on O(n+m) variables and O(nm+m) clauses after removing repeated literals. This preserves the original acceptance weight assignment by assignment by cancellation.

Apply the identity to the [projected nondeterministic branching programs](nondeterministic-program-sum.md). Each product after heavy-bit pinning has v=O(n+nL) input and node-value variables, O(v²) clauses of width at most three and O(v) other clauses of length O(v). The original node recurrences have one truth assignment for each original input. The signed difference counts exactly the assignments satisfying these recurrences and output pins even for ambiguous programs. Distinct moment factors have private node variables and the switches can feed a single parity chain.

The resulting two formulas have O(v) variables and O(v²) clauses of width at most three. The [October arbitrary-density bound](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/main_full_version.tex#L68-L82) gives time poly(v)·2^{O(v loglog(v+16)/sqrt(log(v+2)))} and polynomial space. On arbitrary-width CNF with m=O(n), the corresponding clock is poly(n)·2^{O(n loglog n/sqrt(log n))}. Anthropic previously applied disjoint width reduction to obtain qualitative subexponential counting with n long clauses in its dominating-set application. [Corollary 7](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/blob/11b6c7e3c927df2658f513b6b68ea7f8929c62c5/eth/paper.pdf)

## NP consequence

There is an absolute c>0 such that for every fixed integer d≥1 some language L_d in NP fails at infinitely many ambient lengths M to have an exact real sum of at most M^d mixed atoms, each of size at most MK(M), for

    K(M) = ceil(c sqrt(loglog M) / logloglog M)

at sufficiently large M, extended by one at small M. Each atom is an arbitrary binary Boolean formula with its leaf-occurrence budget or an acyclic nondeterministic single-bit query program with its node budget, as in the preceding theorem. Fixed constant-factor integer log envelopes can be used for all cutoff computations.

Choose δ>0 with 4δ<1/4 and a heavy threshold multiplier C for the common-slot projection as in the [formula proof](np-sum-formula-projection.md), independently of d. The raw evaluator satisfies n≤M≤2^{O(n)} under s≤2^{εn} with ε≤1. The density function is eventually increasing. Thus L=ceil(CK(M)) is at most O(Cc sqrt(log n)/loglog n)+O(C+1), with absolute constants. If necessary pad leaf instances to n variables, exactly dividing by the corresponding power of two. The fixed-width counting exponent is now O(Cc)n+o(n). Choose c small enough that this part costs at most n/4 asymptotically. For fixed d choose ε small enough for the common-slot union bound and all polynomial-in-M scans, coefficient processing and quartic expansions. This leaves enough time for the nondeterministic 2^{(1−ε)n} gap-UNSAT verification. The hierarchy consequence and contradictory common ordinary-circuit bound under the negation follow as in the [previous proof](nondeterministic-program-sum.md). The language may depend on d. Standard P versus NP remains open. [Finite signed-count checks](signed_long_clause_check.py)
