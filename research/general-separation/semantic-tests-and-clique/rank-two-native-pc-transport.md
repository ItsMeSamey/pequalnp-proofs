# Bounded additive proof-degree transport at a rank-two cut

Take a pin-free shore X of physical cut rank two. Use the [physical replacement](rank-two-cut-replacement.md) with outgoing and incoming two-bit vectors s,t. Summing the shore's guards derives Q=s₁t₁+s₂t₂=0 at degree two. Retain all named pins on the exterior. For a partial boundary table the two-site replacement preserves every iterative polynomial-calculus refutation threshold D≥2. For the full ten-state table use the independent three-site replacement with colors (1,0),(0,1),(1,1). A degree-D refutation on this compressed graph gives one of degree at most D+1 on the original. A degree-D refutation on the original gives one of degree at most D+2 on the compressed graph. These additive overheads apply to one replacement.

## Partial tables

A partial table has exactly two nonzero color classes, independent, written a,b after a basis change. Use the physical replacement with edge flag e. For e=0 no component meets both classes. Sum the native guards on components containing a sites to obtain s₁t₁=0. For e=1 the a,b classes are completely joined and no neutral component touches both. Sum the native guards on the a class and its incident neutral components to obtain s₁t₁+s₁s₂=0. Adding Q gives the second replacement guard in either case. Substituting the two color parities for the replacement inputs therefore simulates every replacement row at degree two. The two-site graph is induced by a nonadjacent actual a,b pair for e=0 and an adjacent pair for e=1. Setting all other shore selections to zero specializes to that graph. Both operations preserve degree.

## One quadratic coordinate for the full table

In the independent replacement use affine input coordinates (s₁,s₂,z) so its selections are s₁+z,s₂+z,z. On the original graph substitute Z=s₁t₁ for z. The handshake gives Z=s₂t₂. In degree three it gives Zt₂=Z by multiplying Q by 1+t₂. Likewise Zt₁=Z. Therefore the three replacement guards (s₁+z)t₁, (s₂+z)t₂ and z(t₁+t₂) follow in degree three. The exterior equations match because the sender is s.

Boolean normalize each compressed proof row as p₀+z p₁. For a row of degree j, substitution gives degree at most j+1. Ordinary affine multipliers cost one degree. When the parent has degree j≤D−1 and the multiplier is z, multiply the mapped parent first by s₁. Reduce s₁Zp₁ to Zp₁ using s₁²=s₁, leaving degree at most j+1. Then multiply by t₁ and reduce to Zp₀+Zp₁. Each temporary degree is at most j+2≤D+1. Affine-coordinate multipliers are simulated term by term. Boolean normalization for affine forms uses the same bounds. This proves the overhead of one.

## Reverse transport on four boundary bits

Let A₀ be the ten points with Q=0 and A₁ the six with Q=1. On A₀ every function has a squarefree representative of degree at most two. Choose a linear interpolation operator E fixing all constants and affine forms, extending their basis to a ten-element basis. On A₁ every function also has a degree-at-most-two representative. These claims can be checked by listing the monomials on four bits. The cumulative binary ranks at degrees 0,1,2 are 1,5,10 on A₀ and 1,5,6 on A₁. A squarefree polynomial of degree at most four vanishing on A₀ agrees throughout the cube with Q times a polynomial of degree at most two, by interpolation on A₁. Thus it is derivably zero from Q at degree four, including Boolean normalization.

For each A₀ state choose a legal original shore word. Let G_i be its bit at original shore site i. Group each squarefree original proof row by its set M of exterior variables. Evaluate the interior coefficient on the chosen words, getting F_M. Replace it by E(F_M) and retain the exterior monomial y_M. The normalized row Σ_M y_M E(F_M) is evaluated at the replacement's s,t. This grouping precedes substitution even though t depends on exterior variables. A degree-j row maps to degree at most j+1, since |M|=j gives a constant coefficient.

An interior guard normalizes to a polynomial of degree at most two in the boundary from its interior terms, plus E(G_i)·(u_i·t) from its exterior terms. It vanishes on A₀ and follows in degree four as above. Each exterior guard maps to its compressed counterpart, as the chosen sender is s on A₀ and E fixes affine forms. Pin equations on the exterior work identically. Q follows from the compressed guards.

For a parent of degree j≤D−1, exterior multiplications commute with the normal form after Boolean reduction. For an interior multiplier multiply the mapped parent by E(G_i), keeping formal degree ≤j+3≤D+2. For |M|=j the constant coefficient times E(G_i) is already normalized. For |M|≤j−1, E(F_M)E(G_i) differs from E(F_M G_i) by a degree-at-most-four polynomial vanishing on A₀. Its correction times y_M follows at degree ≤4+|M|≤D+2. The resulting line is the normalized image of the desired product. Interpolation is linear, so additions transport directly. This bound is independent of the number of removed shore sites. The [six-site affine obstruction](rank-two-full-affine-obstruction.md) rules out one way to strengthen it and does not imply that either degree overhead is necessary.
