# Historical attribution for the exact-maximum printer

Kannan proved that a language in the third level of the exponential hierarchy, in both its existential and universal forms, attains maximum circuit complexity. Miltersen, Vinodchandran and Watanabe gave a deterministic single-exponential-time construction with a second-level polynomial-hierarchy oracle. Their Lemma 5 constructs the truth table by deciding prefix extendibility. Under P = NP the oracle collapses to P, giving the single-exponential-time exact-maximum printer as a classical corollary.
