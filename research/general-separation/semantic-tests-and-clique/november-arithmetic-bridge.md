# The November arithmetic bounds and the Boolean transfer

The November 23 preprint reports exp(Ω((log n)^(5/4))) border size for an explicit VNP witness over every field. Its November 24 successors report VF⊊VBP⊊VP⊊VNP and an eventual exp(c(log N)²/(log log N)²) lower bound for directed Hamiltonian cycle HC on N vertices over every field. The constant c is absolute and the threshold depends only on the characteristic. The latter bound also holds for permanent outside characteristic two; in characteristic two permanent equals determinant. Binary +,−,× gates have free scalars and unrestricted reuse and intermediate degrees. The degree-N coefficient vector may approach the target in Zariski closure over the algebraic closure. All other output components are unrestricted. The identities are formal even over finite fields. Suitable supports retain at most three incoming and outgoing arcs per vertex. Official Lean scope includes the eventual exact superpolynomial HC/permanent bounds and the strict exact arithmetic hierarchy. The quantitative degree-component border HC/permanent bound and the explicit grid exponent are manuscript results. This audit checked source statements and the proof components used below without independently rebuilding Lean or certifying the entire manuscripts. ## Two steps that do not transfer to Boolean decisions

The HC proof splits on a metric expansion property of the characteristic. Without expansion, one placement makes all terms with low rank across individually chosen balanced cuts cheaper than an explicit binary tensor. With expansion, tensor-network compression lets a clocked program simulate a proposed representation of its own signed count and add one. Completeness projections transfer these witnesses to HC and, outside characteristic two, permanent. [HC proof outline](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/Circuit-lower-bounds-for-Hamiltonian-cycle-and-the-permanent-November-24-2026/build/intro.tex)

The parser matches the leaf multiset of an ordered binary parse tree to a target monomial. A degree-n contribution has n variable leaves and 2n−1 tree nodes in the normalized computation. In the Boolean quotient x²−x vanishes but its formal coefficient of x is −1. A sequence of s repeated squarings can produce 2^s variable leaves folded to one input variable on the Boolean cube. A single chain can be simplified independently; the example pinpoints the failure of the leaf-count invariant. [Parse tables](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/Circuit-lower-bounds-for-Hamiltonian-cycle-and-the-permanent-November-24-2026/build/parse.tex)

The clock and batch arguments preserve sums of signed field weights. A linear scalar projection and guessed multiplicities yield the required count modulo p. Adding one accepting branch on the diagonal gives Z=1+Z, impossible in a field. Replacing counting by existential acceptance gives z=1∨z, satisfied at z=1. Opposite signs can cancel despite nonempty support; so can p identical unit paths. The boundary proof recovers the value of a regular sum on a nonzero vector even when individual terms have poles. An existence test cannot preserve this linear identity. A uniform SAT decision algorithm supplies neither the signed count nor a compression theorem for it. ## A bounded-repetition transfer on sparse Boolean graphs

Let B_i be linear interpolation in variable x_i at zero and one, holding other variables free, and let Δ_i=B_i−I. These operators commute. On a monomial B_i folds every positive exponent of x_i to one. Thus Δ_i annihilates exponents zero and one, and never changes the support of surviving terms.

Let a size-s arithmetic circuit C on m=N² arc variables decide directed Hamiltonicity on Boolean inputs of weight at most N. Let h be the maximum number of distinct repeated variables in a formal output monomial of support at most N. Internal degrees, individual powers and higher-support output terms are unrestricted. The degree-N homogeneous component of
[
 T_h C=sum_{|S|le h}left(prod_{iin S}Delta_iight)C
]
equals HC. On terms of support at most N the truncated expansion agrees with ∏B_i, because an omitted summand applies Δ_i at an exponent of at most one. All transformed terms of support above N still have degree above N. The degree-N multilinear coefficients of a fully folded polynomial are fixed by Möbius inversion on inputs of weight at most N. The output is zero below size N, and a size-N input is positive precisely when it is a directed spanning cycle. All nonmultilinear degree-N coefficients vanish.

A Δ_i application copies the given circuit O(1) times and adds O(1) gates. Thus T_h C has size at most (s+1)(m+1)^O(h+1). The November theorem gives
[
 log(s+1)+O((h+1)log N)ge c(log N)^2/(loglog N)^2 .
]
Every polynomial-size arithmetic circuit correct on this sparse promise has a surviving output monomial of support at most N repeating Ω(log N/(log log N)²) distinct variables. The promise predicate itself is in P by simply checking that the selected arcs form one directed spanning cycle. Its efficient pointwise arithmetization must incur these collisions. Uniform polynomial-time decision alone cannot imply this proposed bounded-repetition transfer.

For the total predicate, two nearly equal directed cliques of sizes a,b, one fixed return arc B→A and (a−1)(b−1) free forward arcs avoiding its endpoints restrict Hamiltonicity to an OR of those free arcs. Any one enables a tour via paths within both cliques. Its multilinear degree is (a−1)(b−1) over every field. Folding cannot raise degree, so no degree-o(N²) polynomial represents total Hamiltonicity on the cube.

## Promise transfer for the grid and a depth-four control

On an r×r grid use d=r² slots, with q choices for each edge and at most q⁴ local tuple coordinates. In each slot give a zero or one unit vector. Accept iff every slot is assigned and the two ends of each edge report the same label. Define P_i C=C(0)+Σ_a X_{ia}(C(e_a)−C(0)) within slot i. It fixes local degree at most one, folds a positive power of one coordinate and annihilates products of distinct coordinates within a slot. The fully folded promised checker is the grid state sum, by interpolation on the product of the partial one-hot domains.

If every all-slot output monomial using only one distinct variable per slot repeats at most h slots, truncate the expansion of ∏P_i into products of P_i−I at subsets of size h. Its all-slot coefficient tensor is unchanged: missing slots stay missing, mixed slots remain mixed or vanish, and every omitted term kills a locally linear slot of each remaining monomial. At d≤(log q)^(1/3) the resulting circuit has size (s+1)q^O(h+1) by copying at most O(q⁴) circuits per operator. The grid paper's balanced-slot lemma and price comparison apply to any polynomial with the specified all-slot tensor, giving q^Ω(√r). Thus log_q(s+1)+O(h+1)≥Ω(√r). For r the integer part of (log q)^(1/6), polynomial-size circuits correct on the promise require an all-slot monomial repeating Ω(√r) slots, even if arbitrary elsewhere. [Grid theorem and proof](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/Paths-trees-and-grids-separate-Valiants-hierarchy-November-24-2026/build/grid.tex)

For a concrete control set L(v,e,a) equal to the sum of the local coordinates at vertex v that assign label a to edge e. The polynomial ∏_{e=uv}Σ_a L(u,e,a)L(v,e,a) evaluates the promised checker and has size O(r²q⁴). Replacing sums and products by OR and AND gives a depth-four monotone formula correct on the promise. In the arithmetic formula every formal monomial has the same slot degrees deg(v); local choices consistent or inconsistent at a shared vertex cannot be distinguished by any such degree projection. The Boolean all-slot tensor nevertheless has flattening rank exactly q^|∂U| across each cut U. Distinct boundary labelings give disjoint-support, nonzero rank-one blocks. This shows concretely how using the same variable at different equality tests implements the expensive tensor in the Boolean quotient.

## Prior work and the remaining gap

Hrubeš established that VP≠VNP implies a P Boolean function with a hard canonical multilinear representative over every field, even for monotone 2-CNF. The bounds here locate output monomials in small pointwise circuits. Exact evaluation away from the cube can carry counting information: for the easy permutation-matrix test its multilinear representative M satisfies M(A/2)=2^(−|E(A)|)per(A) for every 0–1 matrix A, by retaining each available edge independently with probability one half. Valiant proved the 0–1 permanent #P-complete. Bürgisser’s classical transfer starts from algebraic equality to derive a nonuniform Boolean collapse; its converse is unavailable here. None of these results derives a formal circuit producing HC merely from P=NP. [Hrubeš](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2015/067/) · [Valiant](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304397579900446) · [Bürgisser](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397599001838)

There is a precise limit even if efficient proof search under P=NP is granted. Work over a fixed finite prime field. Given a polynomial size cap, guess a list of circuits for orders 2 through N. Check the base HC₂=X₁₂X₂₁ and the following formal recurrence at each m≥3. Sum over j<m the product of X_mj and the previous circuit on the graph deleting m and replacing every incoming weight X_ij, i≠j, by X_im. This contracts the forced edge m→j and bijects directed tours with tours of the smaller instance. A failed polynomial identity has a short extension-field evaluation certificate: circuit size S gives degree at most 2^O(S), so an extension of polynomial degree over the fixed field suffices. A supplied irreducible modulus and all arithmetic can be checked in polynomial time. Existence of a correct list with any fixed bit prefix is consequently in Σ₂^P. If P=NP and such polynomial-size lists existed, prefix search could recover one in polynomial time. The source lower bound rules out the list-size premise; efficient certificate search cannot create a nonexistent small circuit.
