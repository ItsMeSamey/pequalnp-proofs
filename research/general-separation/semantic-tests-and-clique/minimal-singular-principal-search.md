# Short minimal singular obstructions in symmetric matrices over a finite field of characteristic two

Let k be a finite field of order q and characteristic two. If a symmetric k-matrix has any singular principal submatrix, it has one of order at most q. This order is sharp. Throughout the empty principal determinant equals one.

Take an inclusion-minimal singular principal matrix B, of order m≥2. Deleting any one coordinate gives a nonsingular matrix. Therefore rank B=m−1 and its kernel generator is nonzero at every site: a zero coordinate would give a kernel vector after deleting that site. By diagonal congruence scale B so its kernel contains the all-ones column. Set C=B+e_m e_mᵀ. Its determinant equals the nonzero cofactor of the last diagonal entry of B. Write W=C⁻¹. Since C1=e_m, one has W_im=1 for all i, including i=m.

For distinct i,j the determinant of B with i and j removed equals det C times (W_ii+W_jj). If j=m, the update disappears on deleting j. Jacobi's identity gives the binary-characteristic two-by-two minor W_ii W_mm+W_im²=W_ii+1. Otherwise deletion retains the update, so the ratio is det W[{i,j}]+det W[{i,j,m}]. Expanding at W_mm=W_im=W_jm=1 gives W_ii+W_jj. Every such deletion determinant is nonzero by minimality. Thus the m diagonal entries of W are distinct field elements, giving m≤q. Order one also meets the bound.

For sharpness, give the q vertices of a path distinct potentials s₁,...,s_q in k. Give edge i conductance c_i=1/(s_i+s_{i+1}) and use the weighted Laplacian. It is singular since the all-ones column is in its kernel. For a nonempty set R of deleted root sites, its complementary principal determinant is

    (product of all c_i) times (product of s_a+s_b over consecutive roots a,b in R).

Indeed the Laplacian is the vertex-edge incidence matrix times the diagonal conductances times the transposed incidence matrix. By Cauchy–Binet the complementary minor sums spanning forests with one root per tree, weighted by their edge products. An incidence minor on each such forest is a unit by leaf elimination; a rootless component or a cycle gives zero. On a path the omitted edges consist of exactly one edge from each gap between successive roots. In each gap the sum of reciprocal conductances telescopes to the sum of its endpoint potentials. Every factor is nonzero. Hence every proper principal submatrix is nonsingular. Earlier work studies the enhanced principal rank sequences that describe vanishing of all, some or none of the minors at a specified order. The binary-field AA Theorem and its failure over general characteristic-two fields appear in Martínez-Rivera's work. ## Enumerating missing principal sets and comparing the two cap channels

For fixed q there is a deterministic polynomial-delay, polynomial-space enumeration of all singular principal sets of a symmetric matrix, including under any initial mandatory and forbidden sites. For a prefix in a fixed order of free sites, collect all mandatory sites in T. If the T-block is singular, setting every remaining bit to zero already supplies a missing principal set. Otherwise Schur-complement the T-block on the still undecided sites, dropping the forbidden sites. There is a singular extension precisely when some principal minor of order at most q of that residual vanishes. Enumerating these minors costs n^{q+O(1)} field operations and polynomial space for n total sites. Running this oracle in an ordinary depth-first binary prefix tree prunes empty subtrees; at most O(n) oracle calls separate consecutive outputs including the initial and final delays.

Apply this to the two [GF(16) cap](even-odd-extension-cap.md) slices, with the original three pins mandatory in both and the dummy fixed differently. Use the same exterior order and merge the two sorted streams of singular exterior sets. The first mismatch gives the physical exterior and hence an original witness by testing its eight binary pin choices. If both streams end together the supports agree. Up to the first mismatch or termination the time is bounded by (k+1)n^{q+O(1)}, where k is the number of common singular exteriors encountered before that point, and the space remains polynomial. In particular a full-support channel can be recognized deterministically without a promise and compared in polynomial time. On unrestricted inputs k may be exponential. An arbitrary distinguishing exterior need not have size at most q: smaller principal sets may be singular in both channels.
