# A single unseen edge changes the original two-pin answer for arbitrary path-layout windows

For every window length w≥1 there are finite simple graphs Y and N on the same 18+2w vertices, maximum degree five, with the same named pins and the same supplied width-seven path layout of the putative selected support. The layout covers every vertex exactly over one interval. Every original adjacency restricted to the union of any w consecutive bags is identical in both hosts. Y has exactly one odd induced support containing the pins, namely its full vertex set. N has no such support. The layout is a decomposition of Y, not a decomposition of N; the whole purpose is to audit a purported support layout against an original host.

The 16-site fixture is single-chord-no.json. Pins are 15 and 12. The base was obtained by six actual paired/singleton inverse cap moves, P,S,P,S,P,S, beginning with two disjoint edges. The two hosts differ solely at edge {10,13}. A direct scan of every 16-bit mask verifies that the base has exactly the full pinned word and that the augmented host has none. No SAT or algebraic library is needed for verification. The augmented seed has just one degree-five vertex sharing three neighbors with another vertex (8 shares three with 10). It is outside the restrictive promise excluding all such pairs.

Amplify from these pins by w+1 paired relays. At each step add the absent edge between the old pins a,b, append two new vertices u,v, add au,bv, and declare u,v mandatory. Both leaves force their distinct old parents; both old parents gain exactly two selected incidences. This is a bijection in both directions on all pinned odd supports, in YES and NO cases alike. The first two parents have degrees ≤3 before expansion; subsequent parents have degree one. Consequently both graphs stay within the bound and all padding sites in Y are compulsory.

Read the five fixture bags in reverse order, calling them A,B,C,D,E. They have A={11,12,13,14,15}, B={8,11,12,13}, C={7,8,9,10,11}, D={5,7,8,9}, E={0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7}. Place the first new pair Z1 in both A and B. Between B and C insert one bag (B∩C)∪Zi∪Zi+1 for each i=1,…,w. Finish with C,D,E. All original intervals remain intervals; at the cut the carried original sites are exactly 8 and 11. Every lift edge is covered in A∪Z1 or one of the two-pair bags. Width is at most seven. Site 13 disappears after the augmented B, while site 10 appears first in C. Hence no w consecutive bags contain both endpoints. The unique difference is undetectable even by inspecting the complete induced graph on every such window. single_window.py verifies the base by exhaustive masks and checks graph degree, edge coverage, interval property and window equality for representative w up to 100.

This refutes inferring whole-host YES from local induced consistency on an arbitrary supplied support layout, even with injective labels, a unique compulsory full word in the YES host, and no unused padding sites. It does not refute a solver that uses the original host outside the windows, validates a decomposition of the whole host, reroutes at a discovered chord, or enforces the canonical cap chronology. The arbitrary-window amplification deliberately interleaves true relay vertices with a different part of the seed layout. In the degree-four twin ladder exact-continuation collision, the first mismatching chord is repaired by the disjoint-edge branch, which extracts a smaller witness. The NO fixture above rules out any such global repair for arbitrary width-seven layout windows.
