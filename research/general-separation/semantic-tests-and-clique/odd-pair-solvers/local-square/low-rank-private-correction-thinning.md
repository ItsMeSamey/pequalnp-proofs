# Constructive thinning with an imperfect private matching

Let H be an induced all-odd support with h compulsory vertices. All variables and ranks below are binary. Choose k distinct optional sites W={w_i} and distinct matched parent sites p_i outside W; a parent may be compulsory. Write U=V(H) minus W, P={p_i}. With U indexing rows and W indexing columns, define J by J[p_i,i]=1 and zeros elsewhere, and E=A[U,W]+J. Let r=rank E. For any supplied binary diagonal D on W put s=rank of the vertical stack of E and A[W,W]+D. Then inclusion-minimality implies

    k ≤ h+r+2s.

No degree bound or disjointness of auxiliary contacts is required, and different diagonals may be tried. The statement does not assume that an optimal diagonal or matching can be found. For supplied data with k>h+r+2s, a proper native witness is reconstructible in 2^(2s+1) poly(|V(H)|) deterministic time and polynomial space.

Use deletion bits x on U. Impose x=0 on all pins, E^T x=0, and (A[U,U]x)_j=0 at sites j outside P. There are at most h+r+|U|−k independent conditions, leaving at least k−h−r free dimensions. Put z=(A[U,U]x)_P, in the matching order, and delete on W the vector y given by y_i=x_(p_i) OR z_i. In binary polynomials this is x_(p_i)+z_i+x_(p_i)z_i. Require Ey=0 and (A[W,W]+D)y=0. A row basis of the stack gives at most s binary quadratic equations in the free linear parameters, each with zero constant.

If k>h+r+2s, take a (2s+1)-dimensional subspace of the allowed x's. The Boolean polynomial product of one plus each equation has degree at most 2s in the subspace coordinates. Its sum over the binary cube is zero modulo two: each monomial omits a coordinate and occurs evenly often. The origin solves the equations, so some nonzero vector does also. Enumerating the subspace recovers x and y. For any retained U site outside P, the deleted-neighbor parity is zero from its linear row and Ey=0. At a retained p_i, x_(p_i)=0 implies y_i=z_i, canceling the deleted ordinary neighbors. At a retained w_i, y_i=0 gives x_(p_i)=0; E^Tx=0 eliminates other cross contributions, and (A[W,W]y)_i=D_ii y_i=0. Consequently every retained vertex remains odd and all original pins remain. Since x≠0 the new support is strictly smaller.

The net graph (a triangle of optional w_i with one pendant parent p_i apiece, two of those parent leaves named) has the full six-site set as its unique witness. The named parents force two triangle sites; each would have even degree without the third site, which then needs its own parent as well. With W equal to the triangle, k=3>h even though E=0; the internal drift must be charged. Here A[W,W]+I has rank one, so the theorem allows this physical control. With five optional clique sites each adjacent externally only to its own distinct parent, the same ranks r=0 and s=1 exclude all five in a minimal two-pin witness. The selected clique sites would each have degree five and no pendant vertex is required on that shore. For independent exact private leaves r=s=0, recovering [the optional-leaf bound](optional-leaf-pin-bound.md).

More generally the proof measures the available dimension after ordinary pin rows and anomalous interface ranks, rather than counting gadgets as if they were independent. This is a sufficient thinning method; failure of its rank inequality is not a pathwidth bound.
