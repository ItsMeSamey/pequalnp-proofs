# Several universally adjustable shores and blocks with articulations

Let H be an inclusion-minimal all-odd support through h pins P. Choose disjoint nonempty pin-free interiors W_i with no edges between them. Their physical boundaries I_i belong to the common remainder K. Call a shore universally adjustable if at every occupancy of I_i it can supply every parity vector on the occupied sites, keeping its selected interior odd. Count all edges within K exactly once there. At a pair interface this is equivalent to positivity in the [physical table](universal-pair-shore-spqr-path.md), since moving the boundary edge to K translates the requested vector.

The union I of these boundaries can be left unchecked simultaneously. For each occupied site of I assign the parity needed to make it odd to one incident shore, and zero to all the others. Independent physical interior selections realize these assignments. Hence |I|≤h. Otherwise the adjacency submatrix of K with rows V(K)∖I and columns V(K)∖P has more columns than rows. The support of a nonzero kernel vector can be deleted while preserving every retained checked row and every pin. Complete the shores as above to obtain a proper witness. This argument allows leaf pins, overlapping boundaries, and arbitrary degrees at unchecked sites.

For h=2, two such shores with boundary size two cannot coexist. Distinct pairs give at least three sites in I. For identical pairs keep all of K selected and empty the first shore; the second can give their original combined contribution. This exclusion uses neither biconnectivity of H nor a degree lower bound on its pins.

Take a biconnected block B with at least three vertices in a connected minimal two-pin all-odd support of maximum degree five. Use the reduced SPQR tree of B, omitting Q leaves. In a nontrivial tree call a leaf clean if its exclusive interior has neither a pin nor an articulation of H. Its exclusive vertices have no edges outside B, so their degrees in H are odd and at least three. An S leaf cannot be clean because of its exclusive degree-two vertices. P leaves would duplicate real edges. A clean R leaf has actual boundary a,b and an opposite shore in B meeting both sites. Adding ab to its induced shore gives the 3-connected skeleton with maximum degree at most five. Full H minimality forces unique full realization of its boundary parities when a,b are both occupied. The [rooted limb classification and table checks](universal-pair-shore-spqr-path.md) make this an adjustable shore. Different clean leaves have disjoint nonadjacent interiors. At most one can occur by the joint argument.

Each other leaf can be charged to a pin in its exclusive interior, if present. Otherwise choose an articulation v of H there and an off-block component attached at v. Such a component misses the remainder of the block: a path joining two distinct block vertices through new interior sites would enlarge the block. Charge a pin in that component if present. Otherwise it is a singleton physical leaf by the [cut-vertex lemma](minimal-odd-pair-separators.md); charge that unnamed leaf. These charges are distinct across different SPQR leaves, so at most four leaves are charged (two pins and at most two unnamed leaves).

If a clean leaf exists, no other leaf can be charged to an unnamed leaf. Such a physical leaf is itself a universal singleton shore: if its parent is selected it can supply either bit. Its parent would be in the exclusive interior of the charged SPQR leaf and therefore outside the clean leaf's contact pair. Together the two shores would expose three distinct adjustable sites, impossible. Thus with one clean leaf there are at most two other leaves. In total the SPQR tree of B has at most four leaves, or at most three if there is a clean leaf. The singleton-tree case is immediate. This controls branching even for physical pendant pins and changed block parities; the stronger path conclusion is asserted separately when H itself is biconnected.
