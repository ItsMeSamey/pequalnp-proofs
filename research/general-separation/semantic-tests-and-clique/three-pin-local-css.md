# Three physical pins on locally CSS graph states

Let A be loopless undirected binary adjacency and L={(x,Ax)}. Three pinned vertices admit deterministic polynomial-time decision and witness construction whenever L is locally Clifford equivalent to a CSS space. No bipartition or local transformations are required as input.

Wang and Krastanov, Theorem 1, recognize and construct LC-to-CSS conversions for arbitrary stabilizer codes in O(mn²+n⁴) binary operations for m generator rows. Their Lemma 2 uses sitewise rank-one projectors preserving the stabilizer space, and Section VI treats graph states. Brijder and Traldi, Corollary 44, earlier characterized local bipartiteness by two disjoint isotropic matroid transversals. The application here transports actual induced-even supports and physical pin values to a three-dimensional trace. [Wang and Krastanov](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28737v1) [Brijder and Traldi](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v23i4p1/pdf/)

A dimension-n CSS space here means K=D×D^perp. The prior conversion constructs invertible local maps g_i with gL=K. A direct graph-case check searches for a block-diagonal P preserving L with rank-one idempotent blocks. Local bases with the images on the first axes and kernels on the second split the space. The coordinate subspaces are orthogonal since local invertible binary two-dimensional maps preserve the symplectic pairing and L is isotropic. Dimension n makes them orthogonal complements. Conversely projection onto the first coordinate preserves any split space.

Solve the affine linear system PL⊆L and tr(P_i)=1. Cayley–Hamilton gives P_i²+P_i=det(P_i)I. The block scalar map therefore preserves L, which requires its diagonal scalars to commute with A and agree along every edge. On each connected component separately impose just one quadratic condition det(P_v)=0. In the affine parameters test inputs of Hamming weight zero, one and two. If the Boolean reduced determinant is one on all those inputs then every nonconstant coefficient vanishes. Otherwise a tested input gives zero. Each site then has determinant zero and trace one and supplies a rank-one projector. This specializes the prior recognition mechanism.

## Transport of the three named values

A word z=(x,y) in L is admissible if every site avoids (1,1); a pin demands (1,0). Put e=(1,1) everywhere and h(z)=e+g(z+e)=gz+w. Each h_i is a permutation fixing (1,1). Thus q(hz)=q(z) for q(z)=Σ_i x_i y_i, the binary sum of forbidden-site indicators. We have q(w)=q(h0)=0. On L the quadratic q vanishes because A is alternating. On K it vanishes because the CSS coordinates belong to orthogonal spaces. Polarizing q(gz+w) for z in L shows that w is symplectically orthogonal to K. The dimension-n isotropic space K is its own symplectic orthogonal, so w∈K and h(L)=K. Admissibility is preserved site by site, and each pin is sent to its actual value h_i(1,0).

Take a generator matrix B for D with columns b_i. First coordinates of K are functional values on the columns; second coordinates specify a zero-sum subset. At each of the three pins let (f_i,s_i)=h_i(1,0). Remove all pins from ordinary selection, and prescribe lambda(b_i)=f_i on their span W, rejecting inconsistency. Set p=Σ_i s_i b_i over the pins. Query the binary trace for an ordinary subset summing to p and a functional extending lambda that vanishes on that subset. The boundary dimension is at most three. Adjoin the prescribed named positions with s_i=1 to get a zero-sum word. The functional values and the indicator of selection are disjoint, including at the pins since f_i s_i=0. Conversely every admissible word meeting the named values yields such a trace. Apply h^{-1} and check the actual pins and all induced degrees. Polynomial time invokes the full project trace theorem. ## Two residual choices at an unpinned vertex

At unpinned u it suffices for decision to try deletion and local complementation followed by deletion. Direct deletion permits x_u=0. Local complementation changes x_u to 1+x_u+(Ax)_u and preserves pinned feasibility by the affine witness calculation of the bipartite note. For the remaining allowed pair (1,0), the changed coordinate is zero and this second branch applies. Conversely each child witness lifts by zero-extension and, in the second branch, the inverse local complementation. Branches may overlap. Their witness counts cannot simply be added.

With a supplied strategy of depth k, visit at most 2^{k+1} nodes and apply the locally CSS algorithm at its covered leaves. A decision follows if every remaining branch is covered by a recognized case or a direct contradiction. A failed recognition is not a negative answer. A search for a successful branching strategy must be charged separately. In the five-cycle, either branch at a free vertex gives a bipartite graph, respectively a four-vertex path and a four-cycle. The original five-cycle fails the projector test. Writing the blocks as a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i in row-major order, preservation gives diag(c)+diag(d)A=A diag(a)+A diag(b)A. Distance-two pairs force every b_i=0 because each has a unique common neighbor. Edges now force d_i=a_j, which together with trace one would properly two-color the odd cycle. This example exhibits a strict extension of the class handled by direct conversion. No general bound on the required branch depth is known here.
