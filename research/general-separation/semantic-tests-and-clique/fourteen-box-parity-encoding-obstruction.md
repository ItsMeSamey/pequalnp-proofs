# Two obstructions to absorbing a third readout by adding boxes

Consider nonloop two-port tables of size at least fourteen on occupancy and defect bits, with affine required defect parity on occupied sites. Every exposed site meets a box. Distinct physical private realizations of table rows can be joined when their interiors are disjoint.

**No readouts.** The feasible occupancy words affinely span the entire exposed binary space, even using only witnesses of weight at most two. Use the [ordinary-component and directed-edge decomposition](all-fourteen-two-readouts.md). Let U be the union of ordinary components containing a directed head, with S its complement. All occupancies on U are freely serviceable on taking S empty. In particular every U unit and zero have witnesses. An S site in a nonsingleton ordinary component can be selected alone: its component has proper occupancy. A singleton S site i has at least one directed release incidence to h∈U. If b releases it, occupations b e_h and e_i+b e_h both have witnesses. Other S sites are vacant and the chosen U occupancy is serviceable regardless of drifts. The differences of the displayed pairs and the individual unit words span all site directions. One can choose an actual affine basis by elimination on this explicit list, constructing all defect rows with the original table solver.

Consequently under any affine linear occupancy decoder the projected words have the entire affine image of the decoder as their affine hull. In particular, freely exposed independent terminals cannot realize a nontrivial homogeneous parity constraint using any number of zero-marked helper sites and no readout. A violating occupancy and its private lift occur among the displayed weight-at-most-two tests. Conditioning on existing readouts can invalidate the affine-hull statement.

**Keeping the two old readouts and augmenting the canonical star.** Start with n≥1 old terminals a_i, private copies of the canonical box from a_i as tail to one common hub h as head, and two readouts on the old occupancies alone. Add arbitrarily many new boxes of size at least fourteen on any pairs of old sites, hub or additional helpers. Change all affine drifts arbitrarily. Provided each original box retains its table and independent choice of private witness, the projection onto the old terminals is exactly the affine solution set of the two readouts.

Indeed take any point of that affine set, put h=1, and leave every fresh helper vacant. Every added box has some row with its now fixed pair of occupancies: a complete occupancy fiber contains four rows whereas at most two rows are missing. Choose one such row per added edge. At every occupied old terminal, the original star tail bit is unrestricted because the hub is occupied. Set it to correct the drift after all added defects. Each original head bit is independently unrestricted even after its tail bit has been chosen, so adjust the hub parity using any one original edge. No vacant site imposes a parity test. Retrieve the physical rows' original private realizations. The reverse inclusion is immediate from the two readouts themselves. The canonical table and its physical realization appear in the [star reduction](fourteen-port-uniform-boundary.md).

Thus augmenting this star cannot enforce a third parity condition cutting its two-readout solution set, or even exclude a single point in that set. This conclusion allows new boxes incident directly to the original terminals. It does not address replacements that remove original slack incidences, change occupancy decoding nonlinearly, or change the two readouts to involve helpers.
