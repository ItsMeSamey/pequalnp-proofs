# A theta branch survives a linear fraction of vertex queries

The parsimonious literal projection in regular-fiber.md admits a variant. Keep its data component and fixed bypass copy. Pad the initial local verifier graph to k=2t+1 classes, with t≥10. Its switched version A has at most C₀k vertices for a fixed constant, because each gate has bounded arity, at most constantly many local tuples, and each variable pin receives constantly many controlled edges. Use as blocker the join of t pentagons with each vertex replaced by p independent twins, where p≥1 is the smallest integer such that D=p(5t−3)≥Δ(A). The integer p stays bounded for the fixed basis. This graph B is D-regular and maps in both directions homomorphically to the join of t pentagons. Its invariants are exactly ω(B)=2t, χ(B)=3t, and θ̄(B)=t√5. The theta identities come from the usual graph join and homomorphism properties. [Lovász](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1979.1055985)

On each final side take the regularized data graph with 2D copies and a disjoint union of k copies of B. Take the complete join of the two sides as before. This produces N≤Ckt vertices for a fixed C. The K=2k clique count equals the accepting witness count. Every graph is regular, connected with diameter at most two and vertex connectivity at least N/2, with fixed chromatic number 6t, complementary theta 2t√5, and 1-WL signature.

Removing a vertex lowers complementary theta by at most one. The original graph is a spanning subgraph of the complete join of that vertex and the remaining graph, so join additivity and edge monotonicity prove the claim. For a=√5−2 choose δ=(a−1/10)/(2C)>0. If at most δN vertices are queried across the final graph, there is a blocker on each side losing at most δN/k≤(a−1/10)t/2 vertices. Each surviving induced blocker has theta strictly greater than k=2t+1 for t≥10. The surviving complete join has theta strictly greater than K. Thus the all-exclude branch survives even after a fixed positive fraction of adaptive vertex queries. This establishes a depth bound for theta pruning.

The same argument concerns edge branching on membership of both endpoints in the sought clique. On the answer excluding that joint membership, delete the queried edge. For each queried edge with endpoints in distinct sides, charge both endpoints, and for a queried edge inside either side charge one endpoint. Deleting all charged vertices removes all deleted edges and leaves an induced join inside the residual graph. Replacing δ by δ/2 in the preceding bound suffices. Queries that read a graph's adjacency bits have different semantics.
