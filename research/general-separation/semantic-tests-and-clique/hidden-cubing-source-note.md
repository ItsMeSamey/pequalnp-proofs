In odd binary extension degree, Li and Wang classified single-block permutations of the form c·z³ + L(z) + b in known field coordinates, where c is nonzero and L is linearized. Every such permutation is a translated and scaled cube. The sparse trace criterion in the hidden-polar note gives an alternative single-block verification; the note separately treats recovery of hidden coordinates and coupling between blocks.
