# Fifteen of sixteen port states give sparse witnesses; fourteen can encode the original graph problem

Each box has two distinct exposed sites. Its four raw data bits are occupancy and internal parity defect at each end, with all private guards enforced. The overlap graph H has one edge per box, may have parallel edges, and has no isolated vertices. At each occupied shared site the XOR of its incident raw defects must equal a given affine drift g_v of exposed occupancies. This models XOR composition of the copies' local parity constants and any additional physical edges. Readouts see exposed occupancies. For explicit tables, recovery of internal physical witnesses uses stored representatives; the two finite boxes referenced here provide them.

## At most one forbidden tuple per box

Suppose each box admits at least fifteen of the sixteen possible raw tuples; the missing tuple and its coordinates may vary across boxes. Call a box critical if it has a missing tuple and both occupancies in that tuple are one. At a critical edge e write its forbidden defect bits as b_{v,e} at the two ends. A connected component of H imposes no restriction except in the following case: it is a tree, every edge is critical, every vertex is occupied, and

    g_v = 1 + Σ_{e incident to v}(1+b_{v,e})    for every vertex of the component.

This case has no extension. To prove the claim, first fix the occupancies. A critical edge with both ends occupied permits all defect pairs except its forbidden pair. At each end transform its defect to the mark 1+b_{v,e}+defect. Its two ends cannot both be marked. Every other edge offers a free bit to each occupied end. Indeed if it actually forbids one tuple under the chosen occupancies but has an unoccupied end, that end may avoid its forbidden defect without imposing any constraint on its occupied neighbor. If both ends are occupied on a noncritical edge all four defect pairs are available. Marks on free incidences can be translated by arbitrary constants. As in the [eight-site network](mixed-projection-and-merged-gates.md), vertices with required odd mark parity can use distinct incident edges, or an available slack incidence. Cover all but a root by parent edges in any spanning tree. A cycle, an unrequired root, or a slack at a root suffices to complete the component. If all vertices of an occupied critical tree require marks and it has no slack, this is a whole component of H and its edge count is too small. Its failed drifts are precisely those displayed.

For r affine readouts a satisfying occupancy of weight at most 2r always exists when any does. In a minimum-weight witness of weight W retain the r affine rows together with one correcting drift equation for each of its K fully occupied all-critical tree components. On its selected columns these equations have a solution of weight at most r+K, which remains valid after dropping selected vertices. Also W≥2K. Enumerating supports of size ≤2r gives a deterministic polynomial-time decision and reconstruction algorithm for each fixed r. The sharper bound 3 at r=2 from the eight-site network proof also applies, since every component can forbid at most its entire occupancy and a single drift vector there.

## A physical fourteen-state box

Take exposed vertices a,b and private vertices k,y,u,v. Use edges ak,bk,ay,bu,bv. Give a and v parity prescription one and all other sites zero. For fixed occupancy a,b the four private guards are

    k(a+b)=0,    ya=0,    ub=0,    v(1+b)=0.

The raw defect at a is 1+k+y. It is forced to one precisely at (a,b)=(1,0), and both defect values are possible at every other occupancy pair. The raw defect at b is k+u+v. At either b value one of u,v is a free bit, so either raw b defect can be obtained independently of the a defect. The full raw relation therefore has fourteen states; both omitted states have (a,b,e_a)=(1,0,0).

Given any native mixed graph on n vertices with symmetric loopless A, drift c and two affine readouts, use one copy for each i. Expose its a as a_i and identify all b endpoints at a single hub. Keep that hub unoccupied using one additional affine readout b=0. Preserve both original readouts on the a_i. Add the original arbitrary physical edges between a_i and apply the additional drift 1+c_i at each a_i. The total external contribution there is (Aa)_i+1+c_i. When a_i=1 its box supplies exactly the raw defect one, enforcing (Aa)_i=c_i. When a_i=0 it imposes no external requirement. The hub has no guard to satisfy at occupancy zero. The projection onto the original a sites is exactly the original mixed solution set; each solution lifts using independently stored box representatives. The physical host has 5n+1 vertices, 5n private-incidence edges plus the original edges, and three affine readouts. It is a tree-shaped overlap network (a star) when additional affine drifts are accounted for separately as stipulated. The three original literal-pin all-even problem has the earlier [witness-preserving reduction to a mixed graph with two affine readouts](three-pin-even-reductions.md). Thus extending the fifteen-state algorithm indiscriminately to these fourteen-state boxes at r=3 would solve the unrestricted three-pin graph language. No NP-completeness assertion for that language follows.

The [raw construction checker](fourteen_port_table_check.py) enumerates the six-site table and tests all 762 original occupancy words across 21 seeded matrices, with each accepted word lifted to an actual private assignment. The extra affine readout and permission for external drifts on exposed sites are part of the reduction.

## Fixed occupancies even for arbitrary tables

For any explicit two-port tables, once the exposed occupancies are fixed, the remaining extension problem is polynomial-time constructive. At each edge restrict to that occupancy and project away defect bits at unoccupied ends. Reject an empty relation. A unary relation pins a bit or supplies a free slack. A two-bit relation of cardinality one pins both bits; one of cardinality two is an affine line. An affine line either pins one end while freeing the other or equates/complements the two bits. Cardinality four gives endpoint slacks. Cardinality three becomes an at-most-one mark edge after endpoint translations. Absorb all translated constants into the required vertex parities. Each equality edge can toggle both ends together; in each connected component of equality edges these toggles realize every even parity vector by spanning-tree elimination. Contract each such component and retain its aggregate required parity. An internal at-most-one edge now acts as a slack, since one of its ends may be marked or neither. The other at-most-one edges and slacks have the preceding distinct-resource matching solution. Reverse the equalities' tree elimination and choose a table representative per edge. This solves extension of fixed occupancies even in networks whose projected decision problem is the unrestricted three-pin problem above.
