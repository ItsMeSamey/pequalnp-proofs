# Same-dictionary coefficient normalization

Let a Boolean target be an exact real linear combination of S fixed Boolean features. If deleting e designated columns makes every input row activate at most two remaining columns, the same dictionary admits exact total absolute weight (S+2)^{O(e+1)} or smaller. At e=0 the bound sharpens to O(S²).

Write z for the e exceptional coefficients. Every row is 0=b(z), w_i=b(z), or w_i+w_j=b(z), with b(z) its target bit minus the sum of its active exceptional weights. Form a graph on ordinary columns with an edge for each pair row. In every component take a spanning tree. Traversal expresses each w_i as ±v plus an integral affine expression in z of coefficient and constant magnitudes at most S. A singleton row or an edge with equal endpoint v-signs fixes v as an affine expression with denominator at most two and coefficients O(S+1). If neither occurs, all remaining constraints cancel v and one may set it to zero. This also handles isolated unused columns.

After substituting and multiplying by two, the remaining constraints are an integral system only on z with coefficients and right-hand sides O(S+1). The original representation ensures consistency. Choose independent columns spanning the column space, set free coordinates to zero and solve their full-rank row minor. Cramer's rule and Hadamard give |z_j|≤(C(S+1)sqrt(e+1))^e, since the nonzero integer denominator has magnitude at least one. Substitution into all weights gives the claimed total. For e=0 the tree formulas immediately give O(S) per weight.

Thus any polynomial-size exact real representation of the [all-length lookup targets](independent-isa-all-lengths.md) at N polylog N budget requires deleting at least N^(1−o(1)) formula features, or N^(1/2−o(1)) existential-program features, to leave overlap at most two. Indeed log total weight of a normalized representation is O((e+1)log(S+2)), whereas the cited lower bounds apply to every exact representation. This is a necessary condition for the actual unchanged feature list.

With three simultaneous firings exponential unchanged-list weight is possible. Use rows and columns indexed by (j,b), 0≤j≤k and b in {1,2}. A row activates its own column and, for j>0, both previous-layer columns. Its target equals one in layer zero and zero elsewhere. Exactness forces w_(j,b)=(−2)^j. Each column accepts at most three row labels and so is a disjunction of at most three O(log(k+2))-bit singleton tests. Unused binary labels activate nothing. Replacing this dictionary can make the target easy. A controlled replacement by product features is described in [the sparse decoder theorem](independent-isa-overlap.md).
