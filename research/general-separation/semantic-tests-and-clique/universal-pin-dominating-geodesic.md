# A universal pin and a bounded-degree complementary remainder

Delete the named universal vertex h and complement the remaining graph, obtaining H with two mandatory vertices p,q. If S is the selected set other than h, then the guard at h says |S| is even. For such S and v∈S, its degree in the original induced support including h is 1+|S|−1−deg_{H[S]}(v), congruent to deg_{H[S]}(v). Thus the task is exactly an even-cardinality induced even subgraph of H containing p,q, and any answer lifts by inserting h. When pq is absent the two vertices alone work.

Suppose pq is an edge. Find a shortest p–q path P in H−pq. If none exists, reject: an edge of a finite graph with every degree even cannot be a bridge, by summing degrees on one shore. Otherwise P together with pq is an induced cycle C, since any further chord shortens P in H−pq. If |C| is even, take its vertices. If |C| is odd and some vertex has no incidence to C and is outside C, append that vertex as an isolated selected site. It suffices henceforth to handle a dominating odd cycle C formed by this shortest path.

In H−pq every vertex is on P or adjacent to P; removing pq only deletes an edge internal to C. Group vertices by their distance from p in H−pq. Each prefix of P is shortest, so P has exactly one vertex for each distance from 0 to its length. A vertex in distance layer i is within the closed neighborhood of the path vertex at distance i−1, i or i+1; absent indices are ignored. Consequently if the maximum degree of H is Δ, each layer has at most 3(Δ+1) vertices. In fact it suffices to measure the actual maximum layer size W after the constructive early exits, or to bound only the degrees on P. Every edge other than pq stays in one layer or joins consecutive layers. Bags consisting of consecutive layers plus p give a path decomposition: in particular pq is covered when q enters.

Run a parity dynamic program on this decomposition. Introduce each vertex with its chosen occupancy, forcing p and q occupied, and add its occupancy to one global parity bit. At each bag vertex store a bit for its occupancy and one for the parity of its selected neighbors processed so far. Process each edge once where both ends are present, adding each endpoint's occupancy to the other's parity bit. Forget a selected vertex only with accumulated parity zero. Retain predecessor choices and accept the empty final bag with global parity zero. This returns an actual even set of even cardinality. Its running time is 2^{O(W)} poly(|V(H)|+|E(H)|), hence 2^{O(Δ)} times polynomial and polynomial for every fixed maximum degree, including all subcubic remainders. The degree bound is on the complementary remainder H. In particular no degree bound is imposed on the original universal pin. The dynamic program permits disconnected supports and vertices of higher even selected degree. The dominating-geodesic reduction alone gives no uniform bound on W for arbitrary H.
