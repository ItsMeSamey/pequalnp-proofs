# An odd-fiber certificate for removing private quadratic gates from SAT

Let a circuit on q bits be presented as H(z,Q(z,u)), with p private bits u reaching the output only through r wires Q_i. H is an arbitrary shared DAG. For every z the private dependence of each wire is quadratic, with fixed homogeneous quadratic parts independent of z. The affine and constant parts can depend arbitrarily on z. These data must be verified, for example from a symbolic description or sound propagation through the original circuit. Assume p≥2r, r≥1.

For an ordered list W of 2r private directions, write B_i[W] for the alternating polar matrix of the ith quadratic form on those directions. Compute K(W)=Σ_{s∈F₂^r} det(Σ_i s_i B_i[W]) in F₂, at a cost of 2^r polynomial-size binary determinant computations. If K(W)=1, every output y occurs an odd number of times on every coset of span(W), for every z. In particular the directions are independent, and each full private fiber has at least 2^{p−2r} points. SAT reduces exactly to H(z,y) on q−p+r inputs. For a supplied verified positive W the total time is poly(description size + q)·(2^r+2^{q−p+r}) by exhaustive evaluation of H, or replace the second term with a faster sound solver. This beats brute force by a superpolynomial factor for polynomial description size when p−r and q−r are both ω(log q). For p=2r it also preserves the parity of the total witness count for each H. For p>2r every full fiber has even size.

Parameterize a slice by u=u₀+Wt with 2r binary t variables. The indicator of Q=y is Π_i(1+y_i+Q_i). Its parity sum over all t annihilates every squarefree monomial except the one containing all 2r variables. Its coefficient can only come from r quadratic monomials on disjoint pairs, using each color i once. This is the coefficient of T₁···T_r in Pf(Σ_i T_i B_i[W]) by the characteristic-two perfect matching expansion. Summing evaluations at all binary T kills each monomial missing a color, while total formal degree r implies only T₁···T_r can survive. A binary alternating determinant equals the square of the Pfaffian, hence its value equals the Pfaffian over F₂. Dependent directions would give every parameterized point even multiplicity and K=0. This proof includes arbitrary outside-dependent affine terms. The nonvanishing principle is the top-coefficient Boolean case of the Combinatorial Nullstellensatz. [Alon, Theorem 1.2](https://www.tjhsst.edu/~rlatimer/papers/null2CombinatorialNullstellensatz.pdf)

## Discovery and actual cost

K(W) is alternating and multilinear in its 2r column directions. In every coefficient matching, one vector from each column enters a single bilinear form. Repeated columns give zero by the parity proof. Thus K is nonzero somewhere if and only if some tuple of distinct coordinate basis vectors gives a nonzero value. Deterministically trying them costs poly·2^r binom(p,2r), which must be charged if used. For p=2r the full coordinate basis suffices, even after an unknown invertible affine change.

There is also a verified one-sided random search. For a nonzero alternating j-form, the linear map taking v to its first contraction has kernel of codimension at least j. Take a tuple where the form is one; the j contractions in those directions are independent by testing each on the complementary tuple. Conditional on a nonzero remaining form, a fresh uniform v keeps it nonzero with probability at least 1−2^{−j}. Independent columns succeed with probability at least Π_{j=1}^{2r}(1−2^{-j}), bounded below by a positive absolute constant. Every positive is checked by the determinant formula. After failures one can revert to a sound general solver. This probability is conditional on the form being nonzero and supplies no deterministic worst-case saving.

## Nonuniform fibers and false converses

For Q_i=u_i v_i on disjoint private pairs, one colored perfect matching uses every coordinate. Thus K=1 even though the minimum rank among nonzero polar combinations is only two. Without perturbations the all-one fiber has one point and the zero fiber has 3^r points. Decision elimination survives all private affine perturbations, including arbitrary mixing with other pairs. The native implementation needs r AND gates before H. Affine changes and perturbation circuits have their actual cost.

The map Q=(ab,(a+1)c) omits (1,1), including after a dummy fourth private variable is added. No bag can have K=1. Conversely Q=(u₁,…,u_r) is surjective but has zero polars. The method supplies no guaranteed eligible private separator in arbitrary O(q log q)-gate circuits. The uniform P versus NP problem remains open.
