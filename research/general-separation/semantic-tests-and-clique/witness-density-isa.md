# Constant witness density can require exponentially many positive witness products

Let m≥2 be an even power of two and r=m/2. Use a balanced indirect-storage-access (ISA) block z consisting of m data bits, m pointers of log₂m bits, and a log₂m-bit address selecting one pointer. Read the selected data bit. ISA is balanced since one free data bit determines its output for each address and pointer assignment. Define the P language F(z,x)=ISA(z) AND [wt(x)≥r] for m-bit x. Reject invalid lengths. A fixed polynomial-time verifier on a uniform log₂m-bit witness i accepts iff F=1 and x_i=1. On every YES input at least half the witnesses accept; no witness accepts on a NO.

Let y_i denote witness i's acceptance. The possible y are zero and all vectors of weight at least r. On these patterns F equals their OR. Each high-weight pattern occurs for x=y and all 2^{|z|−1} accepting ISA blocks. Zero has at least this many realizations. Any single function of y making fewer than 2^{|z|−1} raw-input errors must therefore be exact on all realized patterns. This includes any fixed polynomial error count in the total input length, for all sufficiently large m.

Any input-independent list hitting every YES must use at least r+1 distinct witnesses: otherwise its complement supports a vector of weight r. A list of r+1 distinct witnesses suffices. At x=all ones and ISA(z)=1 all list members accept. Density thus does not ensure bounded simultaneous multiplicity.

The minimum multilinear degree of numerical exact reconstruction on the realized patterns is r+1. Symmetrize a representing real polynomial over permutations: at weight t the average of a degree-j monomial is binom(t,j)/binom(m,j), a polynomial in t of degree j. The resulting p satisfies p(0)=0 and p(t)=1 for all integers r≤t≤m. The nonzero polynomial p−1 has r+1 distinct roots. OR on any r+1 witnesses attains the bound. This degree statement permits complemented literals.

A signed sum of products of *positive* witness indicators needs exactly 2^{r+1}−1 distinct nonzero terms. Multilinearize and combine like supports, writing P(y)=Σ_{S≠∅}a_S y_S since P(0)=0. Define μ(∅)=−1 and μ(S)=a_S for nonempty S. The polynomial P(1−v)−1 vanishes on every v of weight at most r. Triangular Möbius inversion on these inputs makes all coefficients with support T of size at most r zero. Each such coefficient equals (−1)^|T| Σ_{S⊇T}μ(S). Thus the nonzero signed measure μ on characteristic vectors annihilates all multilinear tests of degree at most r.

Any nonzero real measure annihilating these tests has support at least 2^{r+1}. For r=0 the sum is zero and at least two points are needed. Otherwise choose a varying support coordinate. Both nonzero slices annihilate every degree-(r−1) test on remaining coordinates: multiply it by the chosen coordinate or its complement. Induction gives at least 2^r points on each slice. Remove the empty support to obtain the asserted term lower bound. Inclusion-exclusion for the OR of r+1 witnesses attains it.

Arbitrary complemented factors permit 1 minus one conjunction of r+1 negative factors, using two terms of high degree. A threshold of a linear score also recovers OR. Another verifier can compute F directly. The result only limits black-box numerical reconstruction of this verifier's witness bits; it gives no lower bound for direct formulas, unrestricted SUM atoms, or the nonuniform E(k) slope.
