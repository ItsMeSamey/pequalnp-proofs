# Tour recovery and isolation advice for the black-box counter

The [small-palette construction](two-out-small-palette.md) hardwires arc weights and counts rooted two-colour cycle covers of weight w by fixed-density #3SAT. A cover with c cycles contributes 2^{c−1}. The bucket's parity equals tour parity even after pinning arc-selection bits.

Starting with any odd bucket, try pinning the next arc bit to zero with earlier pins retained. Choose zero if its count is odd, otherwise choose one. The two child counts sum to the odd parent count. Fixing all m bits gives a tour of weight w. Verify its degrees, weight and orbit. The m additional sequential exact queries add a polynomial factor in time and use polynomial space. Pins are unit clauses. On a yes input the original schedule finds an odd bucket with probability at least 2/3. A no input has none. The [checker](cut_count_recovery_check.py) extracted tours from 128 odd buckets in 685 pin queries.

For a supplied upper bound H on the tour count, the rooted p-colour count is Σ_X p^{c(X)−1}. Modulo any prime p it equals the tour count. Binary colour variables reject codes outside 0 through p−1. Equality on selected arcs and palette checks need O((N+m)(1+log p)) binary gates with unique Tseitin extensions. The product of primes up to C log(H+2) exceeds H for a suitable constant C by the Chebyshev prime-product bound. CRT reconstructs the exact count. Clip H at 2^m if necessary. For sparse graphs the bit time is poly(N) times 2^{O(N(1+loglog(H+2))/√log(N+2))}. The checker verified 192 CRT reconstructions.

For m≤dN pad canonically ordered arc slots to ceil(dN). With K=Θ(log(N+2)) palette ranks, each random assignment isolates a minimum tour on any one yes instance with probability at least q=(1−1/K)^{ceil(dN)}. There are at most 2^{O_d(N log(N+2))} labelled graphs in this range. A union bound gives a single list of O_d(N log(N+2)/q) rows working for every yes instance at this length. The advice uses 2^{O_d(N/log(N+2))} bits. The proof selects it existentially. Exhaustive row enumeration costs K^{ceil(dN)}. Scanning all labelled simple graphs in this range costs 2^{Θ_d(N log N)} for d>0. A direct Boolean encoding of the graph's arc endpoints alone uses Θ_d(N log N) bits in the worst case. With that variable budget the advertised fixed-density counter guarantee gives an exponent O_d(N√log N). Family search also involves quantifiers over graphs and tours.

The characteristic-two tour polynomial has one squarefree monomial per tour. Its value at all ones gives count parity. In module i take h_i→a_i,b_i, a_i↔b_i, and a_i,b_i→h_{i+1} with cyclically indexed hubs. A tour chooses either order of the two internal vertices in each module independently, giving 2^t monomials on 3t vertices at maximum outdegree two. A hitting set polynomial in monomial count may already have exponential size. A hitting-set bound parameterized by arithmetic circuit size requires a representation of this polynomial with a known size bound. A Boolean decision algorithm supplies no such bound. The [signed rectangle reduction](directed-signed-rectangle.md) bypasses isolation using the internal contraction theorem.
