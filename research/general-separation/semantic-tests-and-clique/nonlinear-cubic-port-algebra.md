# Cubic calculus realizes the entire Boolean algebra on a rank-two port

Write x=(s₁,s₂,t₁,t₂) and Q=s₁t₁+s₂t₂. On the ten states U={Q=0}, every binary function has a squarefree representative of degree at most two. Every polynomial of degree at most three vanishing on U is derivable from Q and Boolean axioms at formal degree three.

Indeed a quadratic vanishing on U has zero constant and linear coefficients by testing zero and the four units. Of the six weight-two states, the four with Q=0 also kill their four quadratic coefficients. At the all-ones state the two remaining coefficients must agree, leaving precisely the multiple of Q. The quadratic evaluation rank is therefore 11−1=10. The evaluation kernel among cubics has dimension 15−10=5. The five members Q,x₁Q,x₂Q,x₃Q,x₄Q after Boolean reduction are independent since the four latter cubic leading terms are the four distinct triples. These are formal-degree-three consequences. Thus after multiplying a quadratic representative by any affine port literal, one can immediately normalize the resulting cubic to a quadratic of identical value on U at the same cap.

Suppose actual native guards and pin equations of a physical graph derive port relations P_j(x)=0 at degree D≥3. Here P_j have degree at most three in boundary coordinates. Let U' consist of their common zeros on U. Every quadratic port relation vanishing on U' is then derivable in the physical graph at cap D. First normalize each P_j. For each u outside U' choose one P_j nonzero at u, multiply it by the four literals specifying u one by one, normalizing after each multiplication. This derives a quadratic representative for the point indicator on U. Add the desired indicators and use Q to adjust representatives. All helper calculations have degree at most three, even if a low-degree input relation was originally derived at a higher cap. Port bits are linear or affine expressions in actual graph variables; multiplication by an affine expression is simulated by a sum of permitted variable multiplications. If substituting the physical port expressions drops degree, the argument applies without needing their independence.

For a full-table pin-free rank-two shore, replace it by the independent three-color cap. Its third bit z must equal s₁t₁ at the six compatible points with t≠0 and is arbitrary on the other four. The augmented affine vectors of those six points have rank five and their only dependence is their sum; their required z values sum to one. Thus an affine interpolation on U' exists exactly when U' omits at least one of those six points. Choose it and set the other cap bits to s₁+z and s₂+z. All three cap guards are now quadratics vanishing on U' and have physical proofs at cap D by the preceding argument. The sender remains s identically, preserving all exterior and pin equations. Affine substitution therefore lifts a cap-D refutation of the replacement to the physical graph at the same cap D. This is a sufficient forward proof transfer; failure of affine interpolation does not exclude other proof simulations.

For example choose a quadratic representative P of one single forced point. Its nine remaining states affinely span all four bits: any proper hyperplane contains only eight points. Thus the new criterion genuinely permits full affine span of the surviving states. It is conditional on the physical proof of P, and does not assert existence of a three-pin NO core producing that particular relation. As a necessary condition, a minimum-order degree-three-unrefuted NO containing a full rank-two shore of size greater than three must have every derived cubic boundary relation vanish on all six forced points. This strengthens the [affine criterion](codimension-one-cubic-port.md). For relations vanishing on all six the method can still recover all their local Boolean implications, but cannot interpolate a single affine z there.
