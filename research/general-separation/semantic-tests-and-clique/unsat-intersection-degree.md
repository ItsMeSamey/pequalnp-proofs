# Unsatisfiable bounded-width instances resisting intersection learning

Some unsatisfiable bounded-width CNFs with linearly many variables and clauses require linear degree in every real identity 1=Σ_T c_T∏_{j∈T}C_j using nonempty products of original violated-clause cubes. The [rank-adaptive learner](learned-inclusion-exclusion.md) also reaches exponential span before certification. This is an application of classical Tseitin degree lower bounds. Buss, Grigoriev, Impagliazzo and Pitassi's Theorem 10 includes characteristic zero. [Primary source](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~sbuss/ResearchWeb/lineargaps/journalfinal.pdf#page=15) · [Bounded-degree expander existence](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0405020)

Take a connected simple graph G on v vertices of fixed maximum degree k and edge expansion h>0: every set of at most v/2 vertices has at least h times its size outgoing edges. Put bits on edges and prescribe vertex parities of odd total charge. This is inconsistent because the sum of all left sides is zero mod 2. Encode each vertex by one clause excluding each wrong full assignment of its incident bits. This gives O_k(v) clauses, width ≤k and Θ_k(v) edge variables in a constant-degree expander family. Gaussian elimination recognizes the contradiction.

For any edge set U of size <hv/2, G−U has a unique component larger than v/2. Otherwise summing expansion over its components gives 2|U|≥hv. Choose r in the giant. The uniform distribution on full edge assignments satisfying all charges except r exists: toggling that charge makes the total even and a spanning tree realizes every even charge vector. Project this distribution onto U. The marginal is independent of r in the giant: toggling a path inside it transports the defect bijectively and leaves U untouched. For U'⊆U the giant at U lies in the giant at U', hence the projected marginals are consistent.

Assign each multilinear monomial on <hv/2 edges its expectation under the corresponding marginal and extend linearly. This functional sends 1 to 1. A polynomial scoped on U can be evaluated using its joint marginal by consistency. Any product of ≤d original bad cubes uses ≤kd edges. If kd<hv/2, the entire star of each involved vertex is in U, so that vertex is isolated in G−U and cannot be the defect for v large. Every nonempty such product receives zero. A pointwise identity on the cube is an identity of multilinearized polynomials. Thus these products cannot generate 1. No positive-semidefinite claim is needed.

More generally let each new test on ≤s fixed edges vanish whenever all original constraints on its designated set of ≤v/2 vertices are satisfied. For a product of d such tests with sd<hv/2 take U as their joint edge scope and choose r in the giant outside the designated vertex set of one factor. The factor vanishes everywhere under that distribution and the marginal on U is unchanged. This statement restricts the original constraints certifying each added test; it cannot exclude global parity reasoning.

At least v/(k+1) pairwise nonadjacent vertices can be selected greedily. Their incident edge sets are disjoint. Choose one bad cube per vertex: all joint Boolean patterns occur, so their squarefree products are independent by the Boolean monomial basis. At degree floor(v·min(h/(4k),1/(4(k+1)))) the positive-product span therefore has dimension 2^{Ω(v)} before it admits a certificate.

## A control for changing the generators

On a t-cycle require equality at the first t−1 edge-variable pairs and inequality at the last. With each bad parity represented by one affine flat the violation vectors range over all odd vectors. A degree-d positive-product identity symmetrizes to a polynomial in syndrome weight, of degree ≤d, zero at weight zero and one at ceil(t/2) distinct odd weights. Hence d≥ceil(t/2), tight by interpolation. Splitting each bad parity into two literal cubes changes the answer. On the equalities the signed local indicators give x_i−x_{i+1}; on the last inequality they give x_t+x_1−1. Their sum is 2x_1−1 and its square is one. Literal degree is ≤2 and equals two when t≥3: averaging any linear identity under global bit complementation would give an impossible degree-one unsplit identity.
