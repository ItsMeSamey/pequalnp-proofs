# A cubic-refutable NO family requires stretched-exponential local branching with quadratic leaves

Start from the [thirteen-vertex NO of exact binary proof degree three](prime-three-pin-proof-degree.md). Leave its three named pins untouched. Recursively replace every free site by five sites inducing a pentagon, joining whole bags when the parent sites were adjacent, and joining all five sites to every old pin neighbor. After h≥1 rounds there are N=10·5^h+3 vertices. These graphs have exact proof degree three. At every free site all three fully fixed choices of its selection and neighbor parity still have exact degree three. Thus the [degree-saving local rewrite](singleton-state-degree-rewrite.md) can decline at every free site on a cubic-refutable NO.

More generally consider a branching proof on this graph. Each branch choice at each node restricts the local pair of one original free site to a nonempty proper subset of its three legal states. The children cover the three local states, or all states still allowed there by the path's earlier direct restrictions. A leaf certifies the restricted native system by formal binary polynomial calculus of degree two. Every such proof has at least (3/2)^{ceil(2^h/5)} leaves. Singleton and two-state predicates have affine or paired affine equations on the legal domain. The statement uses those native affine face equations, with the usual Boolean field equations. Tests may be adaptive and may overlap. No charge or lower bound is asserted for other global inference rules.

## One layer

Write X for the parent selection parity and Y for the external parity arriving at each site in its bag. For a vector w of five selection bits with parity X, site j sees Y plus the parity of its two cycle neighbors. The native child pairs must avoid (1,1). For each legal parent pair (X,Y) there are exactly six encodings:

* For (0,0) use the empty set or any two nonadjacent sites.
* For (1,0) use one site or all five sites.
* For (0,1) use the empty set or any two adjacent sites.

These lists follow by testing the 32 masks. Choosing uniformly among the six gives each of the three states at any specified child with probability one third, for every parent state. The [finite checker](pentagon_state_privacy.py) verifies all local marginals.

In any actual legal word the bag parity always obeys XY=0 by summing the five child guards, since every internal edge occurs twice. As a polynomial identity this represents each original guard by the sum of its five child guards after substituting bag parity. Original pin equations under the same substitution are the new pin equations. This preserves the degree-three upper bound through every layer.

## Privacy under a set of probes

Independently encode each node recursively by its six choices, conditional on the parent ternary state. For any strictly fewer than 2^h leaf locations in a single height-h bag, the joint law of their states is the same for all three possible parent states. At height zero the assertion concerns the empty observation. In the induction, at most one child has at least 2^{h−1} tested leaves. The other four subtrees have parent-independent laws, and their encodings are conditionally independent. For the potentially exceptional child its own state is marginally uniform regardless of the single root state. Integrating it out proves equality of the full joint laws. Therefore any positive-probability transcript on fewer than 2^h tested leaves of one original bag has realizations for every root state, including partially restricted observations.

## Transfer back to the original polynomial system

For each original bag independently and for each of its three legal parent states, choose a full encoding consistent with a given transcript, whenever one exists in all three states. Each individual leaf selection thus becomes a function on three affinely independent binary points. Extend it to an affine function of the two formal parent coordinates X,Y. The whole bag's selected parity equals X identically because these are two affine functions agreeing on all three points. Substitute X=x_i, Y=(Ax)_i on the original graph, keeping its pins fixed. The sums of all outside neighbor bags give exactly Y identically. Consequently each resulting leaf pair is an affine function of X,Y and equals its prescribed encoded pair at each legal parent state. A Boolean-reduced child guard is therefore a quadratic vanishing on (0,0),(1,0),(0,1), hence is zero or XY. The same-degree Boolean normalization is valid before and after substitution. Every lane's pinned output row is preserved and every affine observation equation vanishes identically. This is a linear-substitution reduction of any degree-two proof on that transcript to a degree-two proof of the original NO. The latter has no such refutation.

In particular there is no degree-two refutation at the root or on any single physically fixed allowed state. The inherited degree-three proof still applies there. The probability argument only proves existence of simultaneous ternary sections. It uses no division by six in characteristic two.

## Leaf count and connectivity

For the counting argument generate virtual observations by assigning an arbitrary legal root state to each of the ten original free bags and encoding independently down their trees. These root states need not form a physical solution of the thirteen-site NO. The preceding affine reduction is precisely what prevents positive-probability short transcripts from becoming quadratic refutation leaves. Any such leaf must therefore have restricted at least 2^h distinct physical sites. For any fixed leaf with s restricted sites, there are at least ceil(s/5) distinct bottom pentagons touched. Choose a single nontrivial restriction in each touched pentagon. Conditional on all parent states immediately above these bottom pentagons, the chosen observations are independent and each restriction has probability at most two thirds. Thus this fixed leaf has probability at most (2/3)^{ceil(s/5)}, even for histories chosen adaptively along the path. Every virtual full tuple follows at least one path by the covering rule. A union bound over the leaves gives the stated lower bound.

The thirteen-site free graph is connected and every pin has a free neighbor, directly from the displayed rows. Fewer than 5^h vertex deletions leave every original free bag nonempty. The complete joins across the connected free base join all surviving free vertices, including nonadjacent vertices of the same bag via a neighboring bag. Every surviving pin still sees a nonempty free bag. The raw vertex connectivity is at least 5^h. The large host still has rank-one module cuts. Exact module contraction or unrestricted degree-three closure detects this family in polynomial time. It gives a counterexample to the completeness of the local degree-two-state rule, not to universal degree-three completeness.
