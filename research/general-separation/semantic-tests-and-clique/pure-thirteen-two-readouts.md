# Two affine readouts with overlapping singleton-11 physical boxes

Let each nonloop edge of an arbitrary overlap multigraph have all four defect pairs at the three proper occupancies, and exactly one prescribed pair at occupancy 11. Every vertex has an incident table. Permit arbitrary affine required defect parities with symmetric off-diagonal coefficients and at most two affine occupancy readouts. Decision and witness recovery are deterministic polynomial time.

Choose each incidence's baseline to be its bit in that edge's unique 11 row. After incorporating baselines and diagonal terms into drift constants, the selected vertices whose every neighbor is occupied must obey the usual symmetric affine parity guards. Every other selected vertex can correct its parity independently on one edge to a vacant neighbor. Multiplicities affect baselines, while neighbor tests use distinct neighbors. These conditions suffice to reconstruct original table rows and, with the supplied representatives, physical private witnesses.

Row-reduce the readouts, rejecting inconsistent constant rows. Target zero uses the empty support. At rank one a marked singleton works. At rank two a site with column equal to the nonzero target likewise works. Otherwise there are precisely two nonzero column colors a,b with a+b equal to target; both their occupancy parities must be odd. Test all two-site supports with one of each. If all fail, every a,b pair must be adjacent and at least one of its vertices must have no other neighbor, since otherwise both parities are independently correctable. Both color classes therefore cannot have size at least two. Choose their names so one consists of a single site h and every site of the opposite color is a leaf of h. If both classes are singletons, choose as the opposite site one having only its partner as neighbor. If just one class is a singleton, its site has at least two neighbors, forcing every opposite site to be a leaf.

Now h must be occupied. Let T be all sites whose only neighbor is h, including unmarked leaves. Write Z for all other sites except h. Every site of Z has a neighbor in Z: it has an incidence, cannot meet T, and does not have h as its only neighbor. Thus z=0 and every unit Z word satisfy all Z guards regardless of occupancies on T. On T the remaining readout is β·x=1. With h fixed to one, every selected leaf is guarded. Its degree prescription c_i(z) is affine on the other occupancies after separating out the symmetric zero-diagonal T drift matrix A. Here A records exterior drift couplings and need not equal the leaf adjacency of the overlap graph.

First try to leave one neighbor j of h vacant. If j∈T set x_j=0, delete that variable, and restrict A,c,β accordingly. If j∈Z restrict to the coordinate subspace z_j=0. For fixed z there is a legal T word with β·x=1 unless c_D(z)=β_D on every component D of A carrying a nonzero restriction of β. This follows from the [connected prescribed-degree span lemma](symmetric-single-row-p3.md); it constructs the required marked word when a component differs. If no component is marked, failure is universal. Thus failure is an affine set, possibly empty, in the permitted z-space. Zero and the permitted units affinely span that space. If any z works at all while ignoring the Z guards, one of these test words works, and the test word itself satisfies the Z guards. The vacancy j releases h. This is an actual lifted solution.

If every vacancy trial fails, temporarily ignore the guards at h and Z. Every relaxed solution now has all of T occupied and every Z-neighbor of h occupied: otherwise the trial at that neighbor would have succeeded by the affine-span argument. If β·1≠1 there is no solution. Otherwise the relaxed z projection L is precisely the affine solution set of the T guard equations evaluated at x=1. It is also the complement of the affine failure set of the unrestricted one-mark T test. Reject if L is empty. If both L and its complement are nonempty affine sets then both have size half the ambient cube, since each is proper and their sizes sum to the full size. Thus L costs at most one independent affine equation, also covering the case where L is the entire cube. Gaussian elimination on the all-ones T equations obtains this equation directly.

On L every neighbor of h is occupied, so h contributes exactly one further affine drift equation after fixing T to ones. Delete h and T and retain these at most two readouts in the same model on Z. For an occupied surviving vertex, each removed neighboring incidence went to an occupied vertex and contributes its fixed 11 baseline; shift its drift accordingly and substitute all deleted occupancies. At a vacant surviving vertex there is no guard. Every surviving site still has an incident surviving edge, and the drift symmetry survives restriction. An empty residual is decided by the constant equations. In reverse, restoring the occupied h,T satisfies all deleted guards and permits reconstruction of all incident rows. There is at most one recursive call and it removes at least two vertices. The vacancy trials, row operations and witness calculations at each level use polynomial time. No enumeration of the possible hard residuals is needed.

The [physical thirteen-state box](thirteen-state-isolated-double-pairs.md) realizes the stated profile. This theorem permits its exceptional edges to overlap without restriction; it treats the pure profile rather than mixtures with signed directed or ordinary boxes.
