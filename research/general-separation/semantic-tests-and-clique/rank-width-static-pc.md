# A logarithmic-height static certificate from a rank decomposition

For an unsatisfiable induced all-even instance on n≥2 vertices, with arbitrary compulsory pins and a supplied rank decomposition of width r, the original Boolean GF(2) system has a static Nullstellensatz refutation of formal degree at most 6r(ceil(log₂(2n))+1)+3. Use the two cut signals per edge of the decomposition from the [iterative degree proof](rank-width-native-pc.md). The argument applies to unbalanced decompositions.

Form a finite constraint problem on the decomposition tree. Its edge variables are the pairs of directional signals, with at most 2r bits per edge and a fixed convention for ordering directions. An internal node allows every tuple of incident edge states that occurs for some assignment of the original vertex bits, independently of the guards. A graph-vertex leaf allows a state when some setting of its vertex satisfies its pin and guard with the indicated incoming signal and yields the indicated outgoing signal. These node constraints share only edge variables. A satisfying physical word supplies a satisfying labeling. Conversely, root the tree at a subdivided edge, giving the root its corresponding two-edge compatibility constraint. Glue from the leaves upward. At an internal node take an assignment realizing its entire tuple of states. Replace the assignments in its two child shores by their witnesses. The two outgoing signals reproduce the contributions to the opposite child and to every vertex beyond the parent shore. Accordingly both witnesses remain legal, and the parent outgoing signal has the prescribed value. At the root this gives a physical witness. Thus on a NO graph the tree constraint problem is unsatisfiable.

Search for a failed native axiom on a Boolean input using only parity queries. Keep an unsatisfiable connected component of the tree constraint problem with its exterior edge states already fixed. At a centroid, query all unfixed incident edge states, at most 6r individual linear forms of the original inputs. Treat inconsistent query branches as empty. On a consistent branch a centroid that is an internal node has a legal tuple, because all answers came from one possible assignment. If a centroid that is an original leaf has an unattainable state, query its own bit to identify which of its pin and guard fails, then terminate. Otherwise the centroid's own constraint is satisfied. With its incident states fixed, the remaining components are independent, and at least one is unsatisfiable. Keep any such component. Each surviving component has at most half as many nodes. There are at most 2n−1 nodes initially, so the search takes at most ceil(log₂(2n))+1 centroid rounds. Selecting a component only involves the fixed finite tree constraint instance conditioned on the answers so far. A singleton internal component with a tuple inconsistent with every Boolean assignment can occur only on an empty branch.

The search tree partitions the Boolean cube. For each reached leaf let J be the product of its affine bit-answer literals. The sum of all leaf indicators is 1 by the binary branching identity. Empty leaves have zero squarefree reduction. At every nonempty leaf its designated original generator g equals 1 wherever J=1. Hence Jg=J modulo Boolean axioms. Summing constructs a static certificate. Each unreduced Jg has degree at most the parity query depth plus two, and squarefree reduction of an identically zero Boolean polynomial of that degree uses Boolean axioms within the same bound. No auxiliary polynomial variables are introduced.
