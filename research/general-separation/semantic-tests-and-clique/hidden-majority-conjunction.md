# Deterministic recovery of a conjunction of large affine majorities

Fix k≥1 and η>0. Suppose q independent affine sign coordinates are partitioned into k unknown blocks of odd sizes nᵢ≥ηq. The clean membership bit P is one exactly when the majority sign on every block is positive. There is a uniform deterministic membership algorithm recovering this entire clean function in poly(q)2^{(1−ε)q} time and queries, for some ε=ε(k,η)>0, even with polynomially many adversarially changed inputs. More quantitatively, the proof tolerates E≤cₖ2^{q−d}/√q changed inputs for a sufficiently small cₖ>0 and d=floor(δq), where δ depends on k and η. Claims are asymptotic in q. k is supplied. Evaluation time of a membership circuit is charged per query.

## Product spectrum and block identification

Work with the centered rescaling F=2^kP−1. Its constant Fourier coefficient is zero by independence. At any nonzero frequency, decompose its label in the unknown block bases. The coefficient is the product of the signed majority coefficients in the nonzero components, with factor 1 from every zero component. In each block the singleton labels and the block total have magnitude αᵢ=binom(nᵢ−1,(nᵢ−1)/2)/2^{nᵢ−1}; every other nonzero label has magnitude at most αᵢ/(nᵢ−2). Therefore the frequencies supported in one block at a singleton or its total have magnitude at least a=c/√q for a fixed absolute c>0. Every other coefficient is Oη(1/q), using the product of at least two Oη(1/√q) factors for a mixed label. Take c=1/4 for all sufficiently large q. [Majority spectrum and deletion identity](affine-majority-decoding-radius.md)

The set of heavy columns is a direct sum of k disjoint binary matroid circuits: one per block, with its only dependence consisting of every column. Given the columns, compute any maximal independent subset by elimination. It omits one column per block. Express each omitted column in that basis; its nonzero coordinates together with itself recover precisely its own block. All coefficient signs equal the signs for the corresponding single majority, since all unused blocks contributed 1. On the true list, deleting any one entry per block preserves that majority by the signed no-tie identity. Hence both the partition and a valid presentation are recovered without prior block sizes.

## Aliasing of restricted transforms

In hidden frequency coordinates let C be the 2^k masks that are constant on each block. For a label u write L(u)=dist(u,C), and write Lᵢ for the distance in block i to its nearer endpoint. Choose d=floor(δq), r=ceil(Kd²/q), with fixed K sufficiently large depending on η, followed by sufficiently small fixed δ depending on k,η,K. A d-dimensional frequency kernel W is good if no nonzero vector of W is within 2r of C. In every coset of W, at most one vector is within r of C: a difference of two distinct nearby vectors is forbidden because C is a subspace. All heavy labels are within distance one. We show the absolute sum of the coefficients at distance >r in any coset is o(q^{-1/2}).

For r<L<d/2 and block component of positive odd weight wᵢ put jᵢ=min(wᵢ,nᵢ+1−wᵢ). Then Lᵢ≤jᵢ≤Lᵢ+1. The ordinary single-block recurrence gives the magnitude bound αᵢ(2jᵢ/nᵢ)^{(jᵢ−1)/2}. Even positive levels vanish. A zero component contributes 1. Since jᵢ≤L+1, the complete product has magnitude at most q^{O(k)}(Cη L/q)^{L/2}, where Cη is a fixed constant depending on η. Indeed the sum of exponents (jᵢ−1)/2 on active blocks is at least (L−k)/2, and Cη L/q<1 by the choice of δ. For each corner, an information set of d coordinates for W embeds its coset bijectively into a d-cube. Labels at distance L from that corner have projected distance at most L and number at most (ed/L)^L. Thus the total contribution at distance L is bounded by

    2^k q^{O(k)} (e√Cη d/√(qL))^L.

For K large enough the parenthesized term is at most 1/2. Summing from r, linear in q for fixed δ, gives the claim on these levels.

For L≥d/2 some block has Lᵢ≥d/(2k). Its coefficient is zero or, by monotonicity up to the middle of the reflected odd-level sequence, bounded above by (2j₀/(ηq))^{(j₀−1)/2} for j₀ the largest odd integer at most d/(2k). This comparison is valid by choosing δ small enough that j₀ is below the middle of every block. All other factors have magnitude at most one. At most 2^d coset vectors are involved, giving total o(q^{-1/2}) when δ is sufficiently small, since (j₀−1)/2≥d/(5k) eventually. Every good bin consequently has either a unique heavy with total absolute interference o(a), or total magnitude o(a) on every translate, accounting also for its at most one near-corner nonheavy label of magnitude Oη(1/q).

## Finding the list

At most B=2^k Σ_{j≤2r}binom(q,j) nonzero labels are forbidden. For fixed k,K the binary entropy bound allows δ>0 small enough that 2B+1<2^{d/2} eventually, since H₂(2Kδ²)=o(δ). Use 2B+1 pairwise trivially intersecting d-dimensional kernels from graphs of distinct multiplication maps over GF(2^d) embedded in 2d ambient coordinates. At most B kernels are bad. The field can be built in 2^d poly(q) time.

For each kernel compute the fast Walsh transforms on its orthogonal subspace U and d translates corresponding to a basis of the quotient primal space. Transform coefficients are signed sums over the corresponding frequency coset. The perturbation on each translate is at most 2^k E/2^{q−d}<a/8 by choice of cₖ. Threshold the base transform at a/2; multiplying comparisons by integer normalizers or using a rational Θ(1/√q) cutoff preserves the margin. Comparison of the signs on the d translates with the base sign determines the missing frequency coordinates by linear elimination. Every good kernel returns the same sorted signed list. Take the majority list (reject malformed lists); for implementation each kernel may discard lists with cardinality different from q+k. Total time is poly(q)(2^d+(2B+1)2^{q−d}), yielding ε=δ/2 eventually. The good-kernel argument and restriction-transform procedure build on the [single-majority decoder](hidden-affine-majority-deterministic.md). This recovers the clean conjunction; arbitrary unlisted corruption sites are outside the conclusion.
