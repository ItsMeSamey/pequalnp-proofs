# Cubic and quadratic matchings with linear drift

On q=3m bits take m disjoint cubics covering every bit, quadratic terms forming a matching on the raw bits, and arbitrary affine drift. The integer gap has a polynomial-time algorithm with zero linear drift. With unrestricted drift, computing its residue modulo three is #₃P-complete even when the quadratic matching covers every raw bit. A supplied vertex cover of h mixed-drift links after the identifications below gives a poly(q)·2^h bit-time, polynomial-space algorithm for the residue.

## The latent two-state model

Write e=(1,1), δ=(0,1). Each triple sign tensor is e⊗³−2δ⊗³. Its latent bit s chooses the first term for zero or the second for one. A raw bit with drift l transforms its vectors to e_l=(1,(−1)^l) or (−1)^l δ; absorb the latter scalar in the vertex weight. A quadratic link contracts its two raw ports through H with rows (1,1),(1,−1). An unpaired port contributes a unary factor by summing entries. A constant drift changes just the overall sign.

For undrifted endpoints the link matrix in the basis e,δ is diag(2,−1). Thus with zero drift all linked latent spins agree. Sum two products of scalars per connected component of the triple graph, then multiply. Pairing two raw bits of one triple contributes its matrix diagonal. All intermediate products and sums need polynomially many bits. More generally, with arbitrary quadratic couplings any clean link whose raw endpoints have zero linear drift and no other quadratic incidence can be removed this way. Fuse its endpoint spins. For C components after all such fusions enumerate 2^C spin settings, pin the remaining raw ports of δ triples, and evaluate the remaining quadratic Gauss sum. Multiply by all removed link scalars and intrinsic triple coefficients. The companion cubic_clean_link_count.py implements this poly(input)·2^C rule.

If one endpoint has l=1, call it the source. Ordered source to target, the matrix after absorbing δ signs has rows (2,2),(0,−1). Modulo three it equals −1 on s_source⇒s_target and zero otherwise. If both endpoints have drift one its rows are (−2,2),(2,−1), which modulo three give the sign (−1)^{s+t+st}. Two undrifted ports give −1 on equality. Unpaired ports give unary weights, possibly zero. Contract the undrifted equalities. Every nonzero unary weight modulo three is a sign, so after applying unary pins we have a quadratic sign sum subject only to mixed implications. Fix a vertex cover of these mixed edges, discarding tautological self-implications. Remaining tests are unary pins or constants. Gaussian elimination on the remaining affine space yields each exact residue. In particular all-one drift has a polynomial-time modulo-three algorithm regardless of the interleaving.

## Linear-size modular reduction

Orient each edge of a bipartite graph from left to right. Its independent sets correspond bijectively to assignments satisfying every directed implication: s=1 denotes selected on the left and unselected on the right. At a vertex of degree d and outdegree o take a path of t cubic triples, choosing the least integer t≥max(1,d−2) for which t+2−d+o is even. Use distinct raw ports for the t−1 internal clean links. They fuse all t spins. Of the t+2 available ports reserve one per logical edge. Pair the two reserved endpoints with a raw quadratic and put linear drift only at the source port. Each arc yields its implication matrix and an absorbed sign (−1)^{s_source}.

At each of the U=t+2−d unused ports attach a fresh cubic triple by a clean link to its first port. Pair its second and third ports to each other with drift only on the second. The clean link copies the logical spin. The self-pair is a mixed arc on equal spins with absorbed sign (−1)^s. The two link scalars are each −1 modulo three before this sign, and all intrinsic cubic coefficients −2 are one modulo three. Thus each dummy contributes (−1)^s. The total unary factor per logical spin is one since o+U is even. Consequently

Z ≡ (−1)^{number of base clean links + number of arcs} · #independent sets(G)  (mod 3).

Every raw bit occurs once in each matching. A self-pair in a dummy joins two distinct raw coordinates within a single cubic triple, which the matching conditions allow. Isolated vertices obey the same construction. There are O(|V|+|E|) triples and gates. Faben's Theorem 3.7 gives the hardness premise for bipartite independent sets modulo three; for this prime its fixed cancellation graph is K₂, with three independent sets and two after deleting its distinguished vertex. The construction uses constant-size gadgets per CNF variable and clause and O(1) extra edges per literal. A parsimonious bounded-fanin circuit-to-3CNF conversion is linear in original primary bits and gates. Thus from a binary circuit a single linear-variable, linear-gate paired instance and a known invertible scalar recover its count modulo three. Membership in #₃P holds by branching with multiplicity one for positive signs and two for negative signs. [Faben, Theorem 3.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.1836)

For all-one drift on a cubic graph of triple blocks with no unpaired ports, the integer problem is already #P-hard under the known signature dichotomy. With the signs absorbed each logical vertex has activity two and each edge has J with rows (−2,2),(2,−1). Let t be the real cube root of two and absorb t for each incident spin-one endpoint. After normalizing by 2t per edge the signature is [a,1,b]=[−1/t,1,−t/2]. The invariants X=ab=1/2 and Z=((a³+b³)/2)²=9/64 avoid the tractability conditions including Z=X³=8/64. As the number of cubic graph vertices is even, (2t)^{|E|} is integer. This is a deduction from Kowalczyk and Cai's classification under its exact evaluation model; the explicit mixed-drift modular reduction above does not rely on it. [Kowalczyk and Cai, Theorem 1.1](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2010.2482)

The bounded checker cubic_paired_match_check.py verifies both perfect matchings and the signed #BIS residue on 90 bipartite graphs, with direct raw sign sums on four controls. The paired phased polynomial still has its own Boolean satisfying input decidable by inspection of its nonzero ANF. Its gap modulo three answers the original counting residue, which may vanish on satisfiable circuits with a multiple of three witnesses.
