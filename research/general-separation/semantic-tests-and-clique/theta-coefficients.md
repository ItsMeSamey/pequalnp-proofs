# Which clique coefficients can theta mimic simultaneously?

For N vertices let m=binom(N,2) and fix k. A Boolean function's binary ANF coefficient at E(H) is the parity of its values on all labeled spanning subgraphs of H. A frequently useful calculation cancels all nontrivial orbits of a permutation 2-group acting on those subgraphs.

**A single small circuit passes every such calculation whose fixed graphs are in the clique/colorability promise.** Give the circuit advice specifying a threshold τ in [k−7/8,k−13/16] at distance at least 2^(−m−8) from every one of the at most 2^m values θ̄(G)=θ(complement of G). It exists on a dyadic grid: take 2^(m+1) equally spaced candidates at spacing 2^(−m−5) in that interval. Each value can invalidate at most one candidate. The advice has O(m+log(N+2)) bits. Approximation at additive error 2^(−m−9) takes polynomial time in N and the binary accuracy parameter by the ellipsoid algorithm for theta. Thus the circuit computes precisely the graph-invariant monotone Boolean function T(G)=[θ̄(G)>τ] in P/poly. A separate threshold may be used for each k; at most N such thresholds per graph size still have polynomial total length. This step supplies exact invariance on *all* labeled graphs, including graphs close to the usual rounding boundary. [Grötschel, Lovász and Schrijver](https://www.zib.de/userpage/groetschel/pubnew/paper/groetschellovaszschrijver1984b.pdf) · [Tardos](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~eva/Gap.Between.Monotone.NonMonotone.Circuit.Complexity.is.Exponential.pdf)

Suppose a 2-group preserving H has the property that every fixed spanning subgraph G of H contains a k-clique or admits a (k−1)-coloring. Both ordinary CLIQUE and T are invariant under this group. Both therefore compute the parity coefficient at E(H) by summing their fixed-graph values. On those fixed graphs they agree by the theta sandwich, hence their coefficients coincide. This holds simultaneously for any number of supports, including every one with only perfect fixed graphs. In particular the binary-tree Sylow calculation in the multiplicative-profile note by itself cannot eliminate this polynomial-size shared gate list. [Sylow graph method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08988)

**An explicit separating coefficient five degrees below the top.** For k≥3 take H=K_(k−3) joined to a pentagon on N=k+2 vertices. Its clique number is k−1, while θ̄(H)=k−3+√5>k−13/16≥τ. Every proper spanning subgraph admits a (k−1)-coloring. Removing an edge of the pentagon leaves a bipartite path. Removing a clique edge saves one clique color. If a join edge uv is missing, with u in the clique and v in the pentagon, assign u and v the same color, use two further colors on the remaining path, and distinct colors for the remaining clique vertices. These arguments cover each possible deletion. Hence at E(H) the ANF coefficients of T and CLIQUE are exactly one and zero: T is one only on H itself among its subgraphs, whereas every such subgraph is k-clique-free. The support lacks only the pentagon's five diagonals, so the discrepancy occurs at degree binom(k+2,2)−5.

The mismatch eliminates this specific theta surrogate and shows why supports having off-promise fixed graphs matter. On N=k+2 vertices CLIQUE itself is decidable by enumerating two omitted vertices. No hard unrestricted circuit profile follows from this discrepancy.
