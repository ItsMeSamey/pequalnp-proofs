# Hard certificates for supplied features, and an easy target saturating the diagnostics

For the balanced two-half target of [the activation overlap theorem](independent-isa-overlap.md), let a dictionary contain S≤N^d formula or acyclic existential-query atoms of size W≤N(log N)^b. A feature certificate is a consistent list of answers to specified dictionary predicates that fixes the target bit among all raw inputs. For some c>0 depending on b,d, the uniform fraction of inputs having a certificate of size at most q is at most 2^(−Ω(r)) if q≤c r/(log N)^(b+1), where r=Θ(sqrt(N)) for formulas or r=Θ(N^(1/4)) for existential programs.

Indeed condition on outside variables in the appropriate half exactly as in the overlap proof. For every product of ≤q atoms, concatenation/conjunction gives a predicate of budget at most qW (the constant predicate has zero correlation). The signed proof in that half bounds its absolute correlation with the full balanced sign by 4^(−r) for c small. This bound holds even with arbitrary interleaving by conditioning on the other half; the constant outside sign has absolute value one. A conjunction with complemented predicates expands to at most 2^q such products with unit absolute coefficients. If the conjunction is monochromatic its probability equals its absolute correlation. There are at most (2S+1)^q signed lists; the union of all monochromatic ones has mass at most (2(2S+1))^q 4^(−r), exponentially small in r after decreasing c. This uses genuine certificates across the full cube. It applies to any exact decoder of the feature outputs.

## Joint falsification control

Take a power of two s≥8 and k=s/2. One raw bit b is the target. Encode s leaf bits z in a complete binary tree, with one binary count field at each internal node of height j and j+1 bits in that field. Declare valid when every parent count equals the sum of its two children, and the root count is k+b. This has O(s) raw coordinates: at height j there are s/2^j fields. Validity is one O(s)-leaf binary formula. Addition/equality of j-bit fields has size polynomial in j, for example by the explicit carry expression as an OR of generates followed by propagates. The total leaf count is O(s sum_j poly(j)/2^j)=O(s). If valid, set y_i=z_i. Otherwise supply a fixed canonical vector of weight k+b, for example its first k+b positions one. Thus every individual y_i has an O(s)-leaf formula. The full image is exactly both adjacent middle slices because every such y has a valid count-tree assignment. Raw length N=Θ(s).

For every unordered pair i,j include distinct atoms X_ij=y_i XOR y_j and A_ij=y_i AND y_j. Each has O(N) leaves. There is a polynomial-size signed representation, with every displayed feature having nonzero weight:
[
 b=-k+\frac{1}{s-1}\sum_{i<j}(X_{ij}+2A_{ij}).
]
Indeed each summand equals y_i+y_j. Its total absolute weight including the bias is O(s).

Yet no two distinct realized feature patterns have Hamming distance ≤2. If two slice words differ on a proper nonempty set T, their unordered pair sums change on all |T|(s−|T|) crossing pairs. The two associated features distinguish the three possible sums. If T is all coordinates, every monochromatic pair changes from sum zero to two or conversely, and at least three such pairs exist. Hence the adjacent-difference subspace in [the normalization criterion](adjacent-activation-rank.md) is zero. The pair-AND columns are linearly independent on the two slices: if sum a_ij y_i y_j vanishes, subtract the equations for a k-set T avoiding j and for T plus j. The sum of a_ij over i in T is zero for every such T. Exchanging two members forces all coefficients incident to j equal, and the k-sum then forces zero. Thus the residual rank e is at least binomial(s,2), quadratic in raw input length. There are Θ(s²) active features in every realized pattern.

Finally q pair-feature observations depend on at most 2q positions of y. At a weight-k pattern, as long as 2q<k there is an untouched zero that can be flipped. At a weight-(k+1) pattern there is an untouched one. The opposite pattern is realizable and all the observations agree. Every input therefore has feature-certificate complexity at least k/2, despite the small explicit coefficients and the trivial raw target b. A constant feature included for the bias changes none of these arguments. These criteria discriminate particular dictionaries, and can all be large even for a polynomial list with an easy target.
