# A rank decomposition bounds the native polynomial-calculus degree

Let G be a simple graph with an arbitrary set of required vertices. Work over GF(2) with input variables x_i, Boolean axioms, linear pin equations x_p=1, and the quadratic equations x_i(Ax)_i=0. If the instance is unsatisfiable and a supplied binary rank decomposition has maximum binary cut rank r, it has an iterative polynomial-calculus refutation of formal degree at most max(6r,2r+3). All polynomial lines use the original variables. The conclusion for three named vertices follows immediately.

Root the decomposition by subdividing an edge. At each shore X with cut rank h, store the following two signals, expressed by their coordinates in bases of the respective images:

    A[X,V−X] x_(V−X),       A[V−X,X] x_X.

Each signal uses h bits and its coordinates are linear forms in the original variables. A state σ fixes both. It is attainable on X if there is a choice of x_X satisfying every pin and guard inside X with the prescribed incoming signal, and yielding the prescribed outgoing signal. Let I_X(σ) be the product of the at most 2h affine literals for these bits. The indicators sum to 1. We derive I_X(σ)=0 for every unattainable σ.

For a singleton X={i} also split on x_i=b. Each resulting indicator J either vanishes identically on the Boolean cube or evaluates to one only where a particular original generator g evaluates to one. Indeed σ fixes the incoming parity and b fixes the selected bit, so in a consistent cell each relevant generator has a fixed value. If all these values were zero then b would witness the state. Since Jg=J on the cube, multiply g by J to derive J=0, with formal degree at most 2h+3. Add the two lines for b=0,1. Every Boolean functional identity used here is justified by squarefree reduction with the field equations; multiplying Boolean axioms up to the degree of the unreduced product suffices.

If X has children Y,Z, expand its indicator by the two partitions of unity for their states. Consider a product I_X(σ)I_Y(τ)I_Z(υ) realized somewhere on the cube. If both children were attainable, choose a witness in each and retain the original exterior of X. Each witness reproduces its old outgoing signal at every site outside its own shore. In particular, the other child's incoming signal is unchanged, and the total outgoing signal of X is unchanged. The combined witnesses therefore attain σ. For an unattainable parent, every realized triple has an unattainable child. Multiply that child's previously derived zero indicator by the other two indicators to derive the product equal to zero. Unrealized triples have identically zero squarefree reduction. Summing yields the parent line within degree 6r. Multiplying a line by an affine form is simulated by its constant multiple and the sum of its variable multiples in ordinary polynomial calculus. The root has no signal bits, and its unattainable state gives 1=0.

This proof uses derived child indicators at multiple levels. It gives an iterative degree bound; it makes no corresponding bound on the degree of a static certificate. In particular, unbounded iterative degree for the three-pin graph encoding would require unbounded rank-width. High degree for a different system, even over the same field, needs an encoding and a degree-controlled proof simulation to imply such a family. For example, average-case lower bounds for the ordinary graph three-colouring polynomial system apply over any field. [Graph colouring lower bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17022)
