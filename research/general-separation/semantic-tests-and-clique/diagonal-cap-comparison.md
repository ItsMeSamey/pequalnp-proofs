# Comparing the two cap determinants by diagonal perturbation rank

Let M be a symmetric matrix over GF(16) indexed by exterior sites F and a dummy e, with K=M[F]. Given a nonsingular diagonal D on F put k=rank(K−D). There is a deterministic algorithm taking 2^{O((k+1)log(k+2))} poly(|F|) field operations to find S⊆F for which exactly one of M[S] and M[S+e] is nonsingular, or certify that none exists. An empty principal matrix has determinant one. The test applies in particular to the normalized three-pin common cap. It requires the supplied diagonal and makes no bound on its achievable rank for arbitrary prime cores.

Factor M−diag(D,1)=UVᵀ with h≤k+2 columns, by elimination. For each i, form R_i=d_i^{-1}v_i u_iᵀ using column versions of the two factor rows, with d_e=1. The rectangular determinant identity det(I+XY)=det(I+YX) shows that det M[S]/∏_{i∈S}d_i=det(I_h+A), where A=Σ_{i∈S}R_i. Including e adds R_e with the same normalizer. Choose a binary basis from the R_i for i∈F by flattening field entries into four bits. Its dimension m is at most 4h², and each aggregate is realized by selecting a subset of the basis sites.

For coordinate bits x of this basis put p(x)=det(I+A) and t(x)=det(I+A+R_e). Each is a determinant of a matrix of affine forms. On binary x, raising a determinant to 2^j conjugates the field coefficients while fixing each x, so each conjugate has degree at most h. Thus p^15+t^15 is Boolean-valued with multilinear degree at most 4h, and equals one exactly on a distinguishing support. A multilinear polynomial of degree d vanishing on the Hamming ball of radius d is identically zero by subset inversion. Enumerating the ball in m bits therefore proves the stated bound and returns the actual sites. If h=0 the two determinants always agree.

For a randomized alternative, any nonzero binary multilinear polynomial of degree d has density at least 2^{-d}. Fix a degree-d monomial and vary its d bits after fixing all other bits. Its coefficient stays one, so each such subcube contains a witness. Consequently O(2^{4h} log(1/ε)) uniform aggregate samples suffice for miss probability ε, and each reported support is checked directly.

The argument also works over GF(2^a), with m≤ah² and degree ≤ah. Over GF(16) an additional randomized variant allows a supplied block-diagonal baseline whose blocks have size at most b and every principal restriction nonsingular. Use its direct sum with a singleton baseline for e and factor the perturbation of rank h. For each block J and chosen subset T its contribution is V_Tᵀ D[T]^{-1}U_T, zero when T is empty. The product of nonzero block determinants cancels from both support tests. On the b raw mask bits of each block, every entry of the contribution has Boolean multilinear degree at most b, also after Frobenius conjugation. The discrepancy therefore has degree ≤4hb in the original mask bits. Uniform masks give a witnessed one-sided algorithm in O(2^{4hb} log(1/ε)) poly(|F|) field operations. No binary-basis compression of the block choices is needed here.

A limit of the diagonal parameter already occurs for a loopless path on 2t vertices: the odd-row/even-column submatrix is triangular with unit diagonal, unchanged by any diagonal correction. Hence k≥t even though the underlying graph has small cuts. The comparison algorithm and decomposition methods exploit different supplied structure.
