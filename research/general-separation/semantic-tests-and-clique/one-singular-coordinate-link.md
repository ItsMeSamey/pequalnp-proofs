# One singular coordinate suffices after changing linked clamps

Use one [anisotropic-quotient component](one-missing-rank-three-types.md) with baseline w₀ the unaccepted state. In clamp coordinates z the alternates are a+Hz and port shift is Cz. Only shifts 0 and r are admissible, where r≠0 spans the radical of the polar pairing on im C. We have H+Hᵀ=CᵀJC. Call T regular when H[T] is nonsingular, including the empty set. Using the alternates on T as free coordinates and retaining the other clamp coordinates gives a linked linear chart for the same fixed physical pin symbols. No physical graph edge-pivot sequence is being claimed for each individual chart change.

An escape exists if and only if some regular chart has a successful zero clamp word or a successful clamp word with just one bit set. Every such candidate can be reconstructed and verified by linear algebra. This is a certificate over charts; it does not identify a successful chart in polynomial time.

Take an escaping original choice of minimum support S. It satisfies H[S]1=a_S. If H[S] is invertible, changing the clamps on S realizes this physical word with new input zero. Otherwise each k∈ker H[S], extended by zero, makes 1_S+k legal: alternates on S stay zero and outside inputs stay zero. Its shift is either 0 or r, so C maps the kernel into ⟨r⟩. A nonzero k mapping to zero would give a strictly smaller escape. Hence the kernel has dimension one, generated by k with Ck=r. Radicality gives H[S]ᵀk=0 as well. Its binary adjugate is kkᵀ by the one-dimensional left and right kernels and existence of a nonzero cofactor, including the empty cofactor for a one-by-one zero matrix. Choose j with k_j=1. Then T=S minus j is regular; after changing the clamps on T this physical word has just the j clamp bit set.

There is a directly verifiable singular certificate even without a given escape. If H[S] has corank one and its kernel generator has Ck=r, then k spans the left kernel too. The quadratic identity on k gives a_S·k=Q(r)=0 since kᵀH[S]k=0. Thus H[S]z=a_S has two solutions. Zero extension gives legal physical words with distinct shifts, so one escapes. Gaussian elimination suffices given S.

## Pivot size and search radius

Any regular target T is reachable from the initial chart by successively changing at most four sites at a time while staying in linked linear charts. In nonsingular H[T] the determinant expansion guarantees a directed cycle, with arcs from nonzero entries and loops allowed. A shortest cycle of length ℓ≥3 has no other arc on its vertices since a reverse edge creates a two-cycle and a chord creates a shorter directed cycle. Its principal block is a cyclic permutation and its skew part is undirected cycle adjacency. A binary kernel vector of cycle adjacency is determined by its first two entries. Hence ℓ−2≤rank(H+Hᵀ)=2, so ℓ≤4. A shortest loop or two-cycle also gives a regular block. Change clamps on this block; its Schur complement on the remaining target indices is nonsingular. Rank of the skew form is preserved under a nonsingular coordinate change: the symplectic pairing of two homogeneous real words is expressed using the new coordinates by invertible congruence. Repeat at most |T| times. This proves constructive reachability toward a supplied target. Polynomial diameter and polynomial outdegree alone give no polynomial search algorithm.

No fixed radius of such changes suffices. Take the complement of a cycle of odd order m≥21 with three consecutive pins. Its [unique witness is the whole cycle](complement-cycle-rooted-cut.md). Translate these pins to unit colors and replace each source site by t nonadjacent false twins with that color. Any successful selection restricts on its odd-multiplicity classes to an old successful selection; hence it has at least m sites, and one per class works. Apply the [35-state reduction](one-missing-reservoir-universality.md) with the invertible color relabeling there. Also blow up each of the six inner sites to t independent false twins. Odd-class reduction preserves their exact 35-state table.

The resulting graph has connectivity at least t. After fewer than t deletions every inner and payload class stays occupied. The inner base is connected, all surviving pins have inner neighbors and some payload class has an inner neighbor. Between distinct payload classes the old complement-cycle edges remain except for possible toggles among the three marked classes. Every marked class has an unmarked neighbor, and the complement of the path on the m−3 consecutive unmarked classes is connected. Thus all surviving vertices are connected, regardless of within-class adjacencies.

Start with original payload inputs as clamp coordinates, with missing zero-shift state. After j changes of at most four coordinates, inputs outside their union are still the old inputs. A new word of weight zero or one therefore has original payload support at most 4j+1. Each success requires at least m, so j≥(m−1)/4. This applies also when seeking escape inside a single component. Taking t=m makes both connectivity and required local search radius unbounded. It is not an asymptotic lower bound against algorithms that choose pivots from the full matrix description.
