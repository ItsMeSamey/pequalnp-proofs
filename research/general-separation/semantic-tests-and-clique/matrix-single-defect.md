# One rank-one defect can carry the full cost of matrix saturation

All vector spaces and matrices in this note are over the binary field. A Boolean circuit has binary XOR and ordered products implementing AND. Its gates may share predecessors. Let T be an exact matrix representation and H an additive, unital matrix map on truth functions which agrees with T on all input coordinates. The defect of an ordered product with operands p,q is H(pq)−H(p)H(q). The two constructions distinguish unavoidable saturation in an arbitrary implementation from error propagation along an explicit circuit's live wires.

## Full saturation is unavoidable for every implementation in a simple tester

Let Ω be a finite promise with distinct raw input patterns, D=|Ω|, and T(g)=diag(g(a):a∈Ω). Fix a target f outside the span of 1 and the input coordinates. Choose a linear functional φ on the Boolean functions with φ zero on that span and φ(f)=1. With u the all-ones D-vector and v any nonzero D-vector, define

\[
H(g)=T(g)+\phi(g)uv^\mathsf T.
\]

This preserves XOR and the pins. For every fixed product p,q, expansion shows that its defect image lies in span(u,T(p)u), so every individual product defect has rank at most two. In every circuit for f, the first wire with φ=1 must be an AND whose operands have φ=0. Its defect is exactly uvᵀ. The matrix algebra generated by just the raw pins contains every singleton diagonal projector, by taking the product of the literals matching that input pattern. Consequently the invariant span of this one rank-one defect's image is the full D-dimensional space. This is the actual charge of the gate in the [saturated-space bound](./matrix-bundle-price.md).

For OR_n on the entire cube with n≥2 take φ to be the full-degree coefficient in the squarefree binary normal form. In the sequential OR computation, implemented by p∨q=p+q+pq, no AND except the final one reaches degree n. The final product has degree-n coefficient one. Thus the only nonzero product defect on this linear-size circuit is rank one and its saturation has dimension 2^n. The output error in this tester has rank one. This first example identifies an overestimate due to saturation.

## Exponential rank growth on live downstream paths

The second example has true output error of exponential rank. Its ordinary Boolean target on 2k+2 bits is

\[
f(z_1,z_2,x,y)=z_1z_2\,[x=y],\qquad x,y\in\{0,1\}^k .
\]

For D=2^k index matrix rows and columns by a,b∈{0,1}^k. Define a matrix representation T and an additive error map E by

\[
T(g)_{ab}=[a=b]g(0,0,a,a),\qquad
E(g)_{ab}=\sum_{z\in\{0,1\}^2}g(z_1,z_2,a,b),
\qquad H=T+E .
\]

T is a unital algebra homomorphism. The binary sum vanishes on constants and individual coordinates, so H is unital, XOR-exact and pin-exact. Write P_i=diag(a_i). Pulling an x_i factor out on the row side or a y_i factor out on the column side yields the following identities for every Boolean function h:

\[
H(x_i h)=P_i H(h),\qquad H(h y_i)=H(h) P_i.
\]

They hold with complemented literals and I−P_i as well. They assert exactness in the specified product orientations.

Compute the seed g₀=z₁z₂. Its product defect is the all-ones matrix J: the H-values of the seed inputs vanish, whereas their product has mixed z-difference one everywhere. This is rank one. For each i=1,…,k form

\[
g_i=(x_i g_{i-1})y_i+((1+x_i)g_{i-1})(1+y_i).
\]

This circuit uses 1+4k AND gates, sharing g_{i−1} in its two branches. It computes the stated target because each step multiplies the previous function by [x_i=y_i]. All AND gates after the seed have defect exactly zero by the left and right pin identities. The matrix E(g_i) has entry one precisely when the first i coordinates of a and b agree. It is therefore a block diagonal matrix with 2^i nonempty all-ones blocks, giving rank 2^i. At the output E(f)=I_D. The only faulty product was the seed.

In particular, any charge accounting for the full output error rank as a sum over defective gates must assign at least D to this one rank-one defect in this circuit. Using only live downstream left actions does not repair the gap: their row masks successively separate all 2^k prefixes. The other implementation-independent example and this live-path example use different testers. The latter makes no uniform low-rank claim for products outside its displayed circuit.
