# Exact multiplication cost of a correction on a constant-theta family

Let q=Σ_i x_i y_i over F₂ for l≥1 pairs, and f=zq. For K_d, the minimum number of AND gates with free XOR matching all ANF coefficients of degree ≥d, the exact value is K_3(f)=l+1. This holds after arbitrary quadratic perturbations, where degree alone certifies two gates for every l.

Indeed an r-multiplication circuit's nonlinear part is a function of at most 2r affine forms on the original input: take the original input parts of the two operands of each AND, whose remaining terms depend only on earlier gates. The output also admits one affine term. In an independent basis of these 2r forms, extended to a full linear coordinate system, every higher-degree head therefore lives in the exterior algebra of their span. The leading part of f plus any quadratic is z∧ω where ω=Σ_i x_i∧y_i. Its first contraction map has zero kernel. Contraction by α∂z+w gives αω+z∧ι_wω, whose terms have distinct z-degrees, and the symplectic contraction w↦ι_wω is invertible. Hence the three-form has full supporting dimension 2l+1 and requires 2r≥2l+1. Computing all l products, XOR-ing, and multiplying by z uses l+1. The input-linear-span argument derives from Schnorr's multiplicative-complexity framework. [Schnorr](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-51083-4_47)

## A small literal projection with optimal witness count

Use l mutually distinct step classes and two singleton anchors, giving k=l+2. A step tuple records previous parity and one of three disjoint local input types x_i=0, (x_i,y_i)=(1,0), (x_i,y_i)=(1,1). Next parity toggles only for the last type. Require initial parity zero and final parity one by removing invalid endpoint tuples. Between consecutive steps enforce parity consistency, and between other distinct steps use all edges. Anchor-to-tuple edges respectively test the required x_i and y_i values, the latter constant one when x_i=0. The edge between anchors is z. This uses at most 6l+2 vertices and only literals or constants as edge entries. On a positive input exactly one k-clique exists because both the input type and parity state are determined.

Over all inputs precisely (3^l−1)/2 distinct candidate cliques occur. This is the odd-toggle count of length-l words with two nontoggling symbols and one toggling symbol. It is optimal among all literal/constant projections, even allowing overlapping witness cliques. Each fixed clique defines an axis-aligned input subcube or an empty set. On the slice z=1 consider all words over 01,10,11 with an odd number of 11 coordinates. No 1-subcube of q contains two of them: its projection onto any given input pair must have constant product because the other pairs can be fixed independently, and a subcube containing both 01 and 10 also contains 11. The number of these private inputs is (3^l−1)/2.

If arbitrary affine edge controls are allowed, each witness is an affine flat. The corresponding optimal affine cover size of q=1 is 2^l−1, already proved by Cohen and Shinkar. Fixing each nonzero x-vector and imposing x·y=1 attains it disjointly. Indeed a constant flat of dimension ≥l must be a coset of a maximal totally isotropic space of dimension l for the polar form. Only one coset of that space is constant, and it has value zero since the complete sign sum is +2^l and all nonconstant cosets cancel. Thus each positive flat has at most 2^(l−1) elements. [Cohen and Shinkar, §6](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/099/download/)

## Coupled coefficient obstruction

Apply the regular-fiber construction's switches, blocker, regularization and final join to this parsimonious gadget. Supply a fixed positive reference copy (choose x₁=y₁=z=1 and every other pair zero), and fixed negative copies with z=0. Pad with singleton classes as needed to reach k≥13. At h=k, the result has O(l²) vertices and threshold 2k. The graph predicate pulled back to the literal input cube computes exactly f. Its complementary theta, chromatic number, edge count, whole degree sequence, and unlabeled 1-WL values are all constant on the cube; the vertex-conditioned theta decision also accepts throughout. Therefore any Boolean rule depending only on those summaries restricts to one constant bit. To correct that rule to exact CLIQUE on this family requires K_3=l+1 multiplication gates even allowing arbitrary quadratic adjustments for free. In fact its exact unperturbed cost on the cube is l+1 as well. Ordinary XOR/AND/NOT circuits restrict directly under literals without extra multiplication gates; ordinary OR costs only one AND. The family itself has an elementary polynomial-size decision circuit and the lower bound is linear in its 2l+1 underlying bits, not superpolynomial in the graph encoding. The verifier version shows the same scalar graph summaries can be constant on an NP-hard image.
