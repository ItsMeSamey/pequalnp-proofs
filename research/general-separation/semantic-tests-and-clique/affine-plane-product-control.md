# Many individual gates stay nonlinear on every affine plane

There are circuits on q primary bits with O(q loglog q) binary gates containing a displayed layer of O(q) products of affine forms such that, on every affine subspace of dimension at least two, more than q product occurrences remain genuinely nonlinear. The constants are absolute for a fixed choice of code parameters. The example is nonuniform and applies for arbitrarily large q. It rules out requiring all or nearly all individual product outputs to become affine by ordinary or adaptive affine restrictions. The XOR of all these products is a single quadratic whose exact satisfying count can be computed in polynomial time.

**Encoder.** Use a binary linear code C of output length N=O(q), dimension q and relative distance δ>0 having a depth-three XOR encoder with O(q loglog q) wires. Gál, Hansen, Koucký, Pudlák and Viola, Theorem 1, give nonexplicit families at constant rate and distance with these bounds; splitting an unbounded parity gate along a binary tree costs at most its incoming wires. One can take lengths with the stated input dimension tending to infinity. Write C_i for the resulting coordinate linear forms. [Primary theorem](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/home/viola/papers/ecc-ckt.pdf)

Fix independent directions u,v. Label output position i by (C_i(u),C_i(v)) in GF(2)². At least two distinct nonzero labels each occur on at least δN/2 positions. Indeed if a most frequent nonzero label is t, its annihilating nonzero linear functional evaluates to one on the other two nonzero labels. The codeword for that linear combination of u,v has weight at least δN, so a second label occurs at least δN/2 times and the largest is at least as frequent. For independent uniform positions i,j the chance of two distinct nonzero labels is therefore at least p=δ²/2. Precisely then

    C_i(u) C_j(v) + C_i(v) C_j(u) = 1.

For the gate C_i(x)C_j(x), the displayed expression is the mixed second difference along u,v, independently of the translate x. A mixed difference of one rules out every affine function on that plane.

Select M=ceil(Kq) independent ordered pairs of positions. For a fixed u,v, the probability fewer than pM/2 products have that mixed difference is at most exp(−pM/8). Fewer than 2^{2q} ordered independent directions need protection. Choose the absolute constant K so that pK/8>2 ln 2+1 and pK/2>2. A union bound gives one choice protecting all planes with more than q nonlinear occurrences. Approximating or finding these pairs uniformly is unnecessary for disproving a proposed structural property of every circuit. Add one AND per pair and optional XOR aggregation, still using O(q loglog q) gates. This fits Bq log q for every fixed B>0 eventually on the family.

Consequently any affine decision tree all of whose leaves make at most q of these particular product occurrences nonlinear must leave at most one-dimensional cells. A partition of the q-cube into such cells needs at least 2^{q−1} leaves, even with adaptive parity tests and arbitrary shift choices. This applies to the actual restricted truth function of each gate, not just syntactic operand incidence. Restricted products can coincide and their sum can cancel, so the proof gives no comparable lower bound for the rank of these quadratic functions, for a new representation of the vector, or for deciding the final circuit. Grouping the outputs in one quadratic phase is an explicit escape.
