# Exponential local branching on highly connected cubic-refutable graphs

For all sufficiently large t there are simple graphs on N=10t+3 vertices with three named pins, vertex connectivity Ω(N), and no proper nontrivial module, whose minimum native binary polynomial-calculus refutation degree is three. Every restriction fixing the full allowed local state (selection and induced-neighbor parity) at up to floor(t/20) free sites still has exact degree three. Consequently every branching refutation testing nontrivial subsets of the ternary state at single original sites and using degree-two refutations at leaves has at least (3/2)^{floor(t/20)+1} leaves. The graphs retain rank-one cuts. This is an existence theorem for a restricted proof search on a graph language.

## Simultaneous encoding lemma

Draw a uniform simple graph on t vertices with adjacency A. Given a boundary state (X,Y) in {(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)}, an encoding is a selected bit vector d of parity X such that the state (d_i, Y+(Ad)_i) is legal at each site, meaning it differs from (1,1). With probability 1−2^{−Ω(t)}, every boundary state and every assignment of allowed states to any p≤t/20 designated sites P admit an encoding.

Fix the request, write I⊆P for its selected sites, and set m=t−p. Take a candidate support d=I∪U where U⊆V∖P has size k within two of m/3, with |I|+k congruent to X modulo two. For large t, k>0, so d is nonzero. Use indicator vectors and subsets interchangeably. The candidate imposes exactly the equations (Ad)_i=Y for i in d and (Ad)_i=Y plus the required second coordinate for i in P∖d. Their binary coefficient vectors in the random edge variables are e_i∧d for i in P∪d. The map w↦w∧d has kernel span(d), so their rank is p+k−1. The sole consistency check sums the equations over d, giving |d|Y=XY=0. Thus the success probability of this candidate is 2^{1−p−k}.

For distinct candidates d,e these success indicators are pairwise independent. In the full exterior square, (d∧V)∩(e∧V)=span(d∧e), by extending d,e to a basis. If d∧e were in d∧span{e_i:i∈P∪d}, then e would lie in that coordinate subspace, since d already does. This fails: their distinct equal-size sets U have an element of e outside P∪d. The constraint row spaces have trivial intersection, and their combined consistent affine events have product probability. The [finite checker](dense_module_sections.py) checks joint ranks for 2,544 additional pairs and realizes every pair of ternary requests on a particular fifteen-site graph for all three boundary states.

The expected number of successes is binom(m,k)·2^{1−p−k}, at least 2^{m log₂(3/2)−p−O(log t)} by the binary entropy bound at k=m/3+O(1). Pairwise independence bounds the variance by the mean, hence failure by its reciprocal. Apply a union bound over the three boundary values, the sets P and their 3^p requests. With δ=1/20 the exponent per vertex is at most

    H₂(δ)+δ log₂3+δ−(1−δ)log₂(3/2) < −0.13.

Polynomial factors are absorbed for large t. Conditioning any one designated edge to be present increases failure probability by at most two.

## Physical proof transfer

Use these random bags on the ten free sites of the [thirteen-site NO with exact degree three](prime-three-pin-proof-degree.md), leaving its three actual pins unchanged. Old edges make complete joins; old pin incidences attach to the whole corresponding bag. Every old guard becomes the sum of the child guards when replacing the old selection by bag parity. Thus an old degree-three refutation gives one on the new graph.

For fixed arbitrary ternary requests on at most floor(t/20) free sites, in each bag choose an encoding meeting them separately for each of the three possible parent states. Each leaf's selection, prescribed on three affinely independent binary points, extends to an affine function of the two parent coordinates X,Y. The sum of the leaf selections equals X identically. As a result substituting the original physical X=x_i, Y=(A_old x)_i gives the intended exterior drift in each bag identically on the whole input space. Each child guard becomes a quadratic that vanishes on all three legal parent states, hence equals zero or the original XY modulo the degree-two Boolean equations. Each observed local coordinate is affine, so agreement at all three source states makes its prescribed equation identically zero. A degree-two refutation on the restricted graph would transport to one on the thirteen-site original, which has none. The inherited degree-three proof still applies on every such restriction. This is an algebraic proof transport and makes no use of characteristic-two averaging.

Finally condition a designated edge in each bag to exist and pivot once on it. The labeled principal pivot swaps the two local coordinates at its free endpoints, preserves all other physical pairs, and preserves the degree bound in both directions. Prescribed ternary states and the original pins therefore transport exactly. The [random pivoted bag construction](prime-three-pin-proof-degree.md) for this same distribution proves that with probability tending to one the transformed host is modular-prime and has vertex connectivity Ω(t). Briefly, after pivoting, each interbag join uses the active sites with coefficient 1+A_{sv}+A_{tv} off the endpoints, with both endpoints active. Each random large bag stays internally connected after a sufficiently small linear number of deletions, contains linearly many active sites and has no proper nontrivial internal module. Except with vanishing probability no single site's internal neighborhood is either empty or the activity pattern on all remaining sites. A global module could therefore meet at most one bag partially; a singleton intersection with an outside site is already impossible, and a whole bag forces all neighboring bags and then every pin. Distinct pin neighborhoods exclude a pins-only module. Intersecting the high-probability events yields the asserted existence.

## Branching scope

A branching node covers the remaining local possibilities at one free site by one or more children, each imposing a nonempty proper subset of that site's three states. Full state requests use the two native linear equalities. A two-state line has one affine equality on the ternary domain. A positive-probability path fixing at most floor(t/20) different sites cannot be a valid quadratic refutation leaf: choose any full state in each path intersection. The resulting stronger face remains unrefuted by the transport above. Under independent virtual uniform legal symbols at all original free sites, a particular leaf restricting s distinct sites has probability at most (2/3)^s. These virtual symbols need not satisfy the unsatisfiable graph, but every virtual tuple lies on at least one complete path by the covering rule. The union bound over all positive-probability leaves gives the claimed count. The statement permits adaptive choices and overlapping children. It does not cover branching on arbitrary affine functions across distinct sites, rank-one cut contraction, or unrestricted degree-three closure.
