# Three degree-two named vertices

Complementing the three possible edges among mandatory pins preserves every candidate's degree parity. Each pin sees exactly two toggles to selected neighbors; every other site's row stays fixed. Thus one-edge and induced-path pin patterns interchange on exactly the same feasible supports. Independent and triangle patterns already admit the pins themselves.

Every input also has a parsimoniously equivalent graph with three degree-two pins and exactly one pin edge. Use the [one-pin subdivision move](three-pin-path-edge-interchange.md): replace required u by new t adjacent only to u and another required vertex v, and toggle uv. Since t and v are selected, t forces u. Each of u,v sees one added incidence and the toggled incidence to a selected neighbor, preserving its old parity. Extension by t and deletion of t are inverse witness maps.

Write the original pins p,q,r. Replace p anchored at q and then r anchored at q, obtaining degree-two pins t_p,t_r adjacent to q. The current pin triple is an induced path with its two endpoints of degree two. Next replace q anchored at t_p. The edge q–t_p is removed and the new t_q is adjacent to q and t_p. Now t_p,t_q,t_r all have degree two and their only mutual edge is t_p–t_q. Their incident edges form two forced segments p–t_p–t_q–q and q–t_r–r. Construction adds three sites and only constantly many edge changes. Deleting the three new sites restores exactly the original witness.

If instead three degree-two pins a,b,c form the induced path a–b–c, the question is ordinary connectivity. The outer pins have unique further neighbors u and v, respectively, outside the pins. If u=v the four-cycle through u is a witness. Otherwise ask whether u and v connect after deleting a,b,c. Necessity follows by removing the forced segment from a witness: u and v become the only odd-degree vertices and hence lie in the same remaining component. A shortest remaining path is induced. Adding the forced segment yields an induced cycle since all three pins have degree two. This gives linear-time decision and reconstruction. The one-edge pattern with three degree-two pins still encodes every unrestricted instance by the preceding construction.
