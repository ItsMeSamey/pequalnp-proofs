# Normalization from adjacent activation patterns

Let V span the realized Boolean activation rows a(x) of S fixed predicates, over the reals. Let D span differences of realized rows of Hamming distance one or two. Write e=dim V−dim D. If the dictionary admits a real exact SUM for a Boolean target, one can choose weights on the same predicates of total absolute magnitude at most (S+2)^{O(e+1)}. For e≤1 the bound is O(S³). The actual rows may be arbitrarily dense.

Neighboring patterns give one- or two-term equations on coefficients with signs ±1 and right-hand sides −1,0 or 1. In the graph on coefficient indices with an edge for each two-term equation, traverse a spanning tree in each component. This expresses w_i=σ_i u+c_i with σ_i=±1 and integer |c_i|≤S in terms of a root parameter. A singleton equation or a chord with nonzero parameter coefficient fixes u to an integer or half-integer of magnitude O(S). Without such an equation u is free: all other edge equations cancel u and are consistent by the original representation. This also handles isolated indices. These coordinates describe the entire affine solution space of the difference equations.

Imposing the original row equations on the free root parameters gives entries that are integers of magnitude at most S and half-integral right-hand sides of magnitude O(S²). Their rank is e: the first stage has homogeneous solution space D-perp; the restriction of the row functionals V to D-perp has kernel D inside V. Choose independent columns spanning the image and e equations, put other parameters zero and solve the resulting nonsingular minor. Its integer determinant and Hadamard give bounds (S+2)^{O(e+1)} on all coefficients including after substitution. For e≤1 one divides at most one O(S²) scalar by a nonzero integer, giving total O(S³).

If the graph on realized patterns joining those at Hamming distances ≤2 has h components, their images in V/D contain at most h distinct vectors, hence e≤h. In particular e≤1 whenever a single raw input flip changes at most two atom outputs, because the input cube is connected. The [all-length lookup lower bounds](independent-isa-all-lengths.md) imply any polynomial-size exact signed dictionary for the hard targets at near-linear atom budget has e≥N^(1−o(1)) for formulas or deterministic programs and e≥N^(1/2−o(1)) for existential programs. Thus there must be at least that many components; high overlap alone does not imply this rank obstruction.

Three changes per raw bit flip already permit exponentially ill-conditioned dictionaries. Take the two-column-per-layer [triangular example](sparse-dictionary-weight-normalization.md) and encode each binary row label by duplicating its bits. Invalid codewords have all zero features and labels. Distinct valid codewords cannot be single-flip neighbors, and each valid row activates at most three features. This preserves the forced coefficients of magnitude 2^j at layer j while limiting each bit flip to three feature changes. Each predicate is a disjunction of at most three O(log S)-bit row tests. Different dictionaries can compute the example target easily.
