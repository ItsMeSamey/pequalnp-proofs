# Cyclic linear coupling of cubing blocks

Let the recovered field blocks have local permutations D_j(z)=c_j(z+t_j)³+b_j, c_j≠0, in odd binary degree m_j. Let Q_j(x)=D_j(x_j)+Σ_{i≠j} A_ji x_i for binary linear maps A_ji. An edge i→j means A_ji≠0. In known field coordinates the classification of Li and Wang already implies that every permutation of the form c z³+L(z)+b is a translated cube of this form, including L=0. The sparse trace test in the companion recovery note is an alternative one-block verification. [Li and Wang, On EA-equivalence of certain permutations to power mappings](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-010-9406-8)

## The exact fiber in a directed graph of indegree at most one

Assume every cross-block edge has rank one and every vertex has at most one incoming edge. Write the edge i→j as x_i↦u_ji ℓ_ji(x_i) with u_ji and ℓ_ji nonzero. Fix an output vector y. In any component without a directed cycle, topological inversion gives one input. On a cycle, every vertex j receives the bit b from its unique predecessor and sends an outgoing bit to its cycle successor k. Its unary transition is f_j(b)=ℓ_kj(D_j^{-1}(y_j+u_ji b)). Once the cycle inputs have been chosen all attached outward trees extend uniquely, even when branches use distinct functionals on the same parent.

Let δ_j be 0 for a constant transition, +1 for identity and −1 for complementation. Composing unary functions gives one fixed point if some transition is constant, two if the product of the nonzero signs is +1, and zero if it is −1. Equivalently each cycle C contributes 1+∏_{j∈C} δ_j. Distinct cycles belong to distinct components. Thus
|Q^{-1}(y)| = ∏_C (1+∏_{j∈C}δ_j).
The formula includes empty products. It computes the entire distribution implicitly, including outputs with no preimage.

For each local nonzero output shift u the possible input differences v between preimages of y and y+u are exactly the nonzero v satisfying Tr(u/(c_j v³))=1: substituting (x+t_j)/v gives the Artin–Schreier equation with constant 1+u/(c_j v³). This range has size 2^{m_j−1} and excludes zero because v↦v^{−3} permutes the multiplicative group. It cannot fit in the kernel of any nonzero linear functional, which has this same size and includes zero. At every vertex some local y_j therefore makes its cycle transition bijective. Choose all such local outputs independently on a cycle. That cycle output then has zero or two preimages, so its square local input-output map is not bijective and must omit some output. Other components cannot remedy a missed cycle output. The full map is a permutation if and only if the graph is acyclic.

Given an arbitrary downstream circuit H one can count with a single exact circuit count. Use q output bits y and one extra input z_C per cycle. Compute all δ_j on cycles and reject if any factor is zero. For each cycle with factor one force z_C=0; a factor two leaves z_C free. Append H(y) on its original shared DAG. The number of accepted (y,z) is exactly the desired count. In field coordinates cubing inverse uses exponent (2^{m+1}−1)/3, with O(m) multiplications/squarings of O(m²) binary gates each. Thus added gates are O(Σ_{cycle nodes} m_j³+q) for prepared local coordinates, plus the true cost of translating y to local coordinates if H uses another basis, with no extra copies of H. Exact counting charges these gates and the added inputs, with no guarantee of a saving on every instance. If the indegree constraint is dropped, simply multiplying individual cycle factors can fail. In F₈ with ℓ the low bit, Q=(x³+ℓ(a)+ℓ(b), a³+ℓ(x), b³+ℓ(x)) has one preimage of zero. The two individual cycle factors each predict two. Indeed at zero a=b so x=0 and all three vanish.

## A graph criterion with arbitrary indegree

Write C_i for the one stacked linear map sending an input difference at vertex i to all its outward edge values. Let r_i=rank C_i. Suppose every nonzero local shift u has its possible difference set R_u={v≠0:Tr(u/(c_i v³))=1} meeting every coset of ker C_i. Then arbitrary many incoming edges and any ranks of individual edges are allowed: Q permutes if and only if its graph is acyclic.

For a cyclic graph take a shortest directed cycle. It has no directed chord, since any chord would create a shorter directed cycle. For each cycle vertex prescribe an outgoing signature in im C_i which is nonzero on its edge to the next vertex; for every other vertex prescribe the zero signature. Let Δ_i be the sum of prescribed incident incoming values. Every cycle vertex has Δ_i≠0 by chordlessness, since all off-cycle signatures are zero. When Δ_i≠0 choose v_i with the prescribed outgoing signature and v_i∈R_{Δ_i}. This choice admits an x_i with D_i(x_i+v_i)+D_i(x_i)=Δ_i. When Δ_i=0 the vertex is off the cycle and v_i=0 works. Now x and x+v are distinct and their outputs agree at every vertex. Acyclic graphs always invert topologically.

For m≥5 odd and r_i≤1 the coset hypothesis always holds. The preceding size argument proves that R_u meets the nonzero coset of each hyperplane. If it missed the zero coset, it would equal the nonzero coset, and v↦Tr(u/(c_i v³)), extended by zero at zero, would be a linear functional. In the univariate trace expansion the exponent 2^m−1−3 has binary weight m−2 and distinct cyclic rotations, since its two adjacent zero bits form a single run. No nonzero coefficient can cancel and all occurring exponents have weight m−2>1, incompatible with a linear function.

More generally the coset hypothesis holds if 2^{m−r}>(1−2^{-r})(1+4·2^{m/2}), where m=m_i and r=r_i. In particular r≤(m−5)/2 suffices. Extend f(v)=(−1)^{Tr(a/v³)} by f(0)=1, a=u/c_i≠0. Its constant Fourier coefficient is zero. For w≠0 the additive sum of (−1)^{Tr(a/v³+wv)} on nonzero v has absolute value at most 4·2^{m/2}. Indeed the Artin–Schreier curve z²+z=a/v³+wv has rational poles at zero and infinity of orders 3 and 1, both odd. Its smooth projective model has genus ((3+1)+(1+1)−2)/2=2 and one rational point over each pole. Geometric irreducibility follows already from the odd pole at zero. Over the other field elements the number of z is one plus the additive character. Its total rational point count is therefore 2^m+1 plus precisely this sum. Apply the Hasse–Weil bound. After restoring f(0), each nontrivial unnormalized Fourier coefficient has magnitude at most 1+4·2^{m/2}. The character expansion of an affine-coset indicator averages 2^r coefficients, with zero constant term from f. Thus the absolute sum of f on that coset is at most (1−2^{-r})(1+4·2^{m/2}), strictly below its cardinality under the stated condition. Every coset then contains a point with f(v)=−1, as required. [Artin–Schreier pole and genus formula](https://dms.umontreal.ca/~mlalin/ASordinary-BDFL.pdf) [Weil bound for curves](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-010-0606-3)

The graph criterion establishes nonbijectivity, rather than computing every fiber at arbitrary indegree. The exact fiber product above requires the stated unary feedback structure. Neither assertion grants a worst-case CircuitSAT saving.
