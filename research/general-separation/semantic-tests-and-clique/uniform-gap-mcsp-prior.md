# Prior work on the conditional Gap-MCSP learner

The conditional learner gives an alternative derivation for its stated promise. McKay, Murray and Williams already obtain a stronger exact MCSP result under P = NP using a one-pass streaming algorithm. The learner supplies no separation of complexity classes. [McKay, Murray and Williams, STOC 2019](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/MCSP-MKTP-stoc19.pdf)
