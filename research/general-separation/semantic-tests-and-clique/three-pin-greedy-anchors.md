# Greedy construction of mutually compatible anchors can stop on YES graphs

In the [fixed chart model](three-pin-many-anchor-bound.md), the next candidate after any supplied list x¹,...,xᵏ solves an ordinary binary affine system. Require g_P=1, (Ag)_P=0, and for every prior vector x impose g_i=0 wherever (Ax)_i=1 and (Ag)_i=0 wherever x_i=1. Every solution is compatible with the whole list. If a candidate is an actual physical witness, return it. Otherwise its nonempty self-defect prevents it from satisfying its own next-candidate constraints, so appending it strictly decreases the candidate affine space. At most n+2 iterations of Gaussian elimination suffice, including a final inconsistency check. An empty space certifies there is no further common candidate for this list.

For any k≥1 there is a YES graph admitting a maximal sequence of k bad anchors with no further candidate. Start from the [two-clique obstruction](three-pin-many-anchor-bound.md), with named path u,c,v and k-cliques A and B joined to u and v respectively. Add one free vertex s joined to u, v and all vertices of A. Selecting just the pins and s gives an induced four-cycle. Every witness must select s because fixing s=0 restores the original obstruction. Nevertheless each of the k old anchors xⁱ=P∪{aᵢ,bᵢ} obeys the pinned rows, and they remain pairwise compatible as the new vertex is absent from all their supports. For each i the parity (Axⁱ)_s equals one, from the two endpoint pins and aᵢ. Hence no chart compatible with even the first such anchor can contain s, and no compatible chart can be a true witness. Once all k old anchors are present, the only additional candidates would have s=0 and select odd subsets of A and B disjoint from every one of their vertices. This is impossible.

Even the set of individual seeds blocking every actual witness can have exponential cardinality: every choice of odd subsets of A and B, with the pins selected and s=0, is a distinct legal initial candidate. All 2^(2k−2) of them have parity one at s, so each blocks every true witness in every subsequent common-chart extension. The graph is explicit, undirected, connected and has 2k+4 vertices. This is an obstruction to correctness for arbitrary greedy choices. A method that reads the graph to prioritize s, or that restarts with carefully selected anchors, need not follow these sequences.
