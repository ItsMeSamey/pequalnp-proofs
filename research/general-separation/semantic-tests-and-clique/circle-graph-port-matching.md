# Prescribed induced-degree parities on circle graphs by ordinary matching

Let a circle graph be specified by a cyclic word in which each vertex occurs twice; two letters are adjacent if their endpoints interleave. A word can be recovered by constructive circle-graph recognition and checked against the input. For arbitrary site prescriptions c_i and h named pins there is a deterministic 2^h poly(n) algorithm to find S containing the pins with deg_{G[S]}(i)=c_i modulo two at every selected i. [Gioan, Paul, Tedder and Corneil](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.3284)

Give each chronological arc between consecutive endpoints a binary state. Define Δ as the outgoing state minus the incoming state at an endpoint, as an integer. For the two occurrences of chord i require Δ₁+(−1)^c_i Δ₂=0. Both changes are zero or both are nonzero; select the chord in the latter case. Nonzero changes alternate direction around the circle. At a selected chord its induced degree parity is the parity of the selected endpoints strictly between its occurrences: crossing chords contribute one, and nested chords contribute two. An even number between corresponds to opposite changes and an odd number to equal changes. Conversely toggle a binary state at all selected endpoints. Its even total number of toggles ensures closure and the equations then hold. For all c_i zero the equations are ordinary unit-capacity integral circulation on chord vertices with chronological arcs.

Each general signed equation has two plus and two minus incidences. At a plus port define activity as the arc state, and at a minus port define it as one minus that state. Exactly two of the four ports must be active. Create two slots at each chord adjacent to all four distinct port vertices. A port matched to a slot is active. If both incidences of an arc have the same sign, connect their ports directly: either the two inactive ports match each other or both are active. If the signs differ, put a private vertex adjacent to the two ports; it matches exactly one inactive port. No further edges are needed. Even a chronological loop uses distinct port vertices. Perfect matchings realize exactly the admissible states. For every pinned chord guess the direction of its nonzero first change. This fixes both changes and all four incident arc bits. Reject a guess conflicting on shared arcs. Enforce the resulting activities at both ends of each fixed arc by deleting slot edges at inactive ports or arc-gadget edges at active ports. Ordinary deterministic maximum matching on this linear-size graph tests perfection. Reconstruct the actual supports and replay them in the input graph.

Both [high-connectivity anchor fixtures](high-connectivity-anchor-obstruction.md) contain an induced wheel on one private pentagon and an opposite-shore site. This wheel is not a circle graph. All rim chords would meet the hub and have one endpoint on each of the two complementary arcs. Two such chords cross exactly when their endpoint orders agree. Orient crossings by the first order: any directed two-edge path forces its third edge by transitivity. On a triangle-free rim this would make each vertex a source or sink, impossible around five vertices. Circle graphs are hereditary, so the two fixtures are correctly declined.

## Additional affine readouts

For r binary parity constraints L1_S=t, each chord selection bit is the sum modulo two of the two arc bits at its first occurrence. Thus the readout is a known linear function of the arc states. Pick a representative incidence on each arc. Its slot edges record the activity, equal to the state or its complement. Give those slot edges the coefficient of the arc in the readout and incorporate complement constants in the target. The constraints now prescribe the binary r-vector sum of the labels in a perfect matching.

A randomized one-sided 2^{O(r+h)}poly(n) procedure constructs witnesses. Use a distinct indeterminate per matching edge. Over an odd prime field larger than four times the matching-graph order compute a Pfaffian for each binary character, multiplying its edge variables by the character signs of their labels. Average these Pfaffians against the character of the target. Orthogonality retains just the signed matching monomials of the required label. Different edge sets give distinct monomials. A random evaluation at independent uniform field elements detects nonemptiness with probability at least 7/8 by the degree bound of half the graph order. Edge-deletion self-reduction with amplified tests recovers a matching; replaying the actual physical witness guarantees one-sided error. Prime search of polynomial size suffices. Use union-bound amplification across the tested restrictions and pin branches. No deterministic algorithm for arbitrary prescribed-vector matching is assumed.

Consequently suppose the actual adjacency is circle adjacency plus a supplied alternating form b_iᵀJb_j on s-bit site labels. Guess w=Σ_{i∈S}b_i, set the local circle prescription to c_i+b_iᵀJw and enforce the readout w as above. For fixed h,s this gives one-sided randomized polynomial-time decision and witness recovery on the supplied representation. Its word, labels and adjacency equation are directly checkable.
