# Constructively enumerating a joint reset and activation tail

Let c independent control bits and p other primary bits feed a supplied gate DAG. A local forbidden-pattern predicate Rⱼ on controls certifies a truth-preserving reset: on occurrence there is a polynomial-time move removing a one, changing no zeros to ones, and preserving the decision behavior of the residual DAG for every assignment of the other bits. More generally a supplied polynomially bounded decreasing potential suffices. Repeated moves terminate at a control word with every Rⱼ=0; overlapping resets may create fresh occurrences. The search over these canonical controls is decision-preserving. Original fibers need not have uniform or known sizes.

Let Aᵢ be any supplied local Boolean activation predicates on the same controls, with nonnegative integer weights wᵢ, and h=ΣwᵢAᵢ. There is no independence or preservation assumption between Aᵢ and the resets. Certified residual cost bounds must hold at the canonical words actually being searched. Suppose all predicate supports and tables are explicit or permit exact conditional counts within a fixed polynomial cost. Use a uniform read bound D≥1 for the combined family: each still-free bit belongs to at most D predicates, counting occurrences on both sides. For a threshold k and an integer t≥1 put

    V(σ)=2^u t^(−k) ∏ⱼ(1−Pr[Rⱼ|σ])^(1/D)
                      · ∏ᵢ(1−aᵢ+aᵢ t^(Dwᵢ))^(1/D),   aᵢ=Pr[Aᵢ|σ].

Here σ is any partial control assignment and u is its remaining number of free control bits. Probabilities average uniformly over that cylinder. Already determined factors may be pulled outside the products at their actual values: a determined reset violation forces V=0, and a determined active predicate contributes t^{wᵢ}. They can also be left as displayed. The enumeration bound holds on any externally pinned control cylinder. To use that enumeration as a decision-preserving reduction of the pinned original SAT problem, require that the normalization moves used there preserve every external pin, or certify directly that each satisfying pinned instance has a satisfying canonical representative on the same cylinder. Without that condition, take an always-accepting one-bit circuit with reset 1→0 and pin the bit to one. The pinned original is satisfiable and the pinned canonical search is empty.

**Prefix certificate.** For either next unpinned bit, V(σ0)+V(σ1)≤V(σ). Indeed, conditioned expectations in each changing factor average to its parent expectation. Factors not reading that bit pull out of both children. On the two equally likely bit values apply ordinary generalized Hölder to the at most D remaining nonnegative factors with exponents D, padding with ones to D factors. The power of two accounts for the two child cylinder sizes. At a full control word V equals zero for a reset violation, and otherwise equals t^{h−k}, at least one at every desired leaf. Backward induction bounds the number of desired leaves in each cylinder by V. This proves the joint read-bound estimate and makes it algorithmic.

In a binary prefix search prune whenever V<1. At each depth the sum of the prefix bounds is at most V at the root, even if different nodes choose different next bits. Therefore at most floor(V(root)) prefixes survive at a depth; tested and rejected children contribute a factor of at most two in work. At full depth check the target predicate directly. All satisfying high-tail controls are enumerated in poly(input)·(1+min(2^c,V(root))) bit time under the following arithmetic convention. For the cap by 2^c one may just use ordinary exhaustive search. Compare V^D with one by clearing dyadic denominators and integer powers of t. This has polynomial bit cost for polynomially bounded D, weights, threshold magnitude, table descriptions and log₂(t+1). There is no unit-cost real arithmetic assumption. A polynomial menu of integer tilts can be searched for the best root bound. The same proof gives fractional weights θⱼ with each coordinate sum at most one, using factors (E[fⱼ^{1/θⱼ}|σ])^{θⱼ}, but bit-time claims for those weights require their own exact or certified-precision arithmetic bound.

**Two residual clocks.** For canonical controls with h≥k use any certified decision solver of bit cost T_hi; for the rest use T_lo. Set W=Σwᵢ. Run the same enumeration on 1−Aᵢ with threshold W−k+1 for the low class. For 0≤k≤W+1 this enumerates a disjoint cover. Optimize t separately and denote the root bounds capped by 2^c by V_hi,V_lo. The combined decision clock is bounded by poly(input)·(1+V_hi+V_lo)+O(V_hi T_hi+V_lo T_lo). Thresholds outside the range give a single class directly. A residual solver can return a raw witness by adjoining its assignment to the enumerated canonical control word. This counts original satisfying assignments only if the needed multiplicities are justified; the stated algorithm is for SAT. Even if the low-residual theorem changes after pinning a controller, its actual cost must be charged.

**Where multiplication of marginal savings fails.** On disjoint pairs (xᵢ,yᵢ) let Rᵢ assert xᵢ=1,yᵢ=0 and reset xᵢ to zero. Let Aᵢ assert yᵢ=1; a suffix reading only these activations is invariant under the resets. The all-active event implies reset avoidance, so its joint probability is 2^{−m} on m pairs, while multiplying the two true marginal probabilities would give (3/4)^m2^{−m}. A suffix requiring every activation can put all witnesses in this high-tail region. Pinning all yᵢ to one makes all factors deterministic: for k=m the bound equals the actual 2^m surviving assignments of x. On the other hand, if reset and activity factors use disjoint sets of coordinates, the ordinary read-one case for internally disjoint predicates recovers the product moment exactly. These controls distinguish a valid joint bound from an uncharged assumption of negative dependence.

For a fresh SAT query with additional external pins, disable every reset whose move is not certified to preserve the pinned coordinates. Remove the corresponding forbidden-pattern factors, specialize the remaining factors on the new cylinder and recompute the read bound or fractional certificate and residual clock for this query. Disabling all resets is always allowed. An API that cannot certify either a pin-preserving family or a satisfying same-cylinder representative must return UNKNOWN for this reduction. A raw witness already obtained from the previous unpinned query does not certify an unrelated pinned query. In the pair example the moves change only x coordinates, so pins on y are retained by every normalization step. A pin x_i=1 can invalidate its reset and requires the per-query handling above. Counts of surviving canonical words may be used to budget enumeration; original satisfying multiplicities require additional fiber information.
