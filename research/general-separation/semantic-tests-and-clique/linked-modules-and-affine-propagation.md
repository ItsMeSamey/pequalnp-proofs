# Module compression before affine propagation

Consider linked clamp bits z with legal coordinates z_i(a_i+(Hz)_i)=0 and target Cz=d. A module M may be replaced by one bit when the columns C_i and bits δ_i=a_i+H_ii agree on M, every outside row and column has constant incidence on M, and the off-diagonal H[M] is symmetric. Symmetry is automatic for equal port columns by the polar identity. Give the representative the same port and external incidences, diagonal zero and offset δ_i.

In a legal word let s be the parity of the selected inputs in M. Its selected sites require the same internal degree parity, equal to δ_i plus the outside contribution. When s=1 the handshake lemma forces this requirement to be zero, so the representative at s=1 satisfies its equation. At s=0 its guard is vacuous. Outside equations and port shifts are preserved. Conversely use any fixed single member for a selected representative and none for an unselected one. Repeated contraction and recordkeeping restore original z and then the actual exterior input. This preserves all port states but not multiplicities.

For discovery, start with two compatible sites and adjoin any outside site distinguishing current members by its incoming or outgoing incidence. If a different color or offset enters, discard the start. Every admissible module containing the initial pair would contain every such distinguisher. A closed compatible set may be contracted. Trying all pairs and repeating after a merge takes polynomial time. This is a linked-coordinate version of [physical rank-one side replacement](rank-two-cut-replacement.md), including dense irregular module interiors.

On the quotient impose Cz=d. At each site inspect the affine image of the current solution space on (z_i,a_i+(Hz)_i), remove the value 11, and pull back the affine hull of the remaining values. Empty means inconsistency. A strict update reduces dimension and triggers another pass. A two-dimensional local image retains its full hull; a constant-one coordinate forces the other zero. A singleton global space may be checked and lifted. If closure stalls, enumeration of the residual affine dimension gives an exact fallback, whereas propagation alone need not decide.

## Large-radius and low-sampling controls solved by propagation

Use the [fixed 35-state reduction](one-missing-reservoir-universality.md) on the complement-cycle payloads, in the original payload-input chart. Its cross coefficients differ from source adjacency by fixed functions of the vertex colors, including the directed rank update. On a monochromatic candidate module these functions cannot distinguish members or cancel differences between their respective adjacencies to an outside site. Port colors distinguish the original payload colors. Each clone class is therefore contractible, even with arbitrary simple internal edges before the shear. Moreover the complement of a cycle of size m≥5 has no proper nontrivial module. A proper module in the connected cycle has an outside neighbor adjacent to all its members, hence has at most two members, but the cycle has no distinct twins. Complementation preserves modules. The projection of a monochromatic module to base classes is a module because every class outside that projection sees all its members identically. The projection cannot be the whole cycle, since several colors occur. Thus merges recover the singleton quotient, independently of merge order.

The target forces three consecutive marked quotient sites to one. For a selected site on the complement of the cycle the condition is T+1+z_{i−1}+z_{i+1}=0 with T=Σz_i. The middle pin forces T=1; the two outer marked sites force the next sites, and the deductions propagate all around. These are affine guard deductions because on the target slice, for each selected site, the chart equation equals the old degree equation by the [reduction](one-missing-reservoir-universality.md). Full selection succeeds for odd m and contradicts T=1 for even m. On these supplied charts the procedure thus decides both signs in polynomial time despite [large radius and low regular-chart sampling probability](regular-chart-sampling-and-conflicts.md).

## Linear-size affine lookahead stalls on examples of both signs

Start with either nine-site [physical comparison fixture](single-missing-rank-three-state.md). Replace each of its six unpinned vertices by t sites with complete adjacency on each old interclass edge, choose all internal module edges independently and uniformly, and retain the three pins and their adjacencies to each class. Parity contraction gives the old answer for every realization. Fewer than t deletions leave each free class occupied. Their base graph is connected, and each surviving pin has a free neighbor, so connectivity is at least t. The three pin equations Bz=d are consistent since B has rank three. Its six column colors distinguish the six classes; contracting them yields the old bounded instance.

With probability 1−2^{-Ω(t)} the two local normals e_i,A_i per free guard are jointly independent modulo row B for EVERY set of at most floor(t/16) guards. Fix a combination of s such groups, choosing one of e_i,A_i,e_i+A_i in each, and one of the eight pin-row combinations. If some A_i appears, test this relation at every coordinate j in its class outside the chosen indices. Their random bits come from disjoint sets of internal edges, and each tested bit is fair due to at least one chosen row in the class. Other classes contribute deterministic terms there. The probability is at most 2^{-t+s}. If just unit vectors occur, a nonzero pin-row combination has support on at least one full class of size t, so cannot equal these s units. A zero pin-row combination also cannot yield a nonempty unit relation. Summing over choices gives an exponent at most [6H₂(1/96)+(log₂3+1)/16−1]t+O(log t), which is negative linear. This includes dependence internal to a single displayed pair.

On the permanent pin-equation slice the map to every such tuple of local affine pairs is consequently onto. The three allowed values for one pair affinely span the binary plane, and their Cartesian products span the full product affinely. Every fiber has the full kernel of the tuple map. Hence enforcing all guards in ANY such local family leaves affine hull equal to the original slice. Even adding every affine consequence of every subfamily of up to floor(t/16) guards cannot start propagation. On the NO fixtures no local subfamily of this size is inconsistent. Both signs remain amenable to the direct module quotient. Existence in all sufficiently large orders follows from the probability calculation, without a uniform polynomial-time constructor for the local independence property.
