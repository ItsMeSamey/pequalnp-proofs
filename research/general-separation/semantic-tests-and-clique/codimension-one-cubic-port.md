# One exposed affine boundary relation removes the full-table rank-two degree loss

Let a pin-free shore X have a rank-two cut and the full ten admissible states. Let R replace it by three independent vertices of colors (1,0),(0,1),(1,1). Use outgoing s and incoming t in both graphs. Fix D≥3. Suppose the original graph derives any nontrivial affine relation L(s,t)=0 at degree at most D (nontrivial on the abstract four-bit boundary). A degree-D refutation of R then transports to the original at the same cap D. A relation can for instance be tested by intersecting the linear pin span with the affine boundary coordinates; it may also be an identity after their substitution. If the *same* relation is derivable on R and its hyperplane has four admissible states, refutation transports conversely at the same cap.

The handshake Q=s₁t₁+s₂t₂ is zero in both systems at degree two. In the independent replacement, if its third bit is z then the others are s₁+z,s₂+z. For t≠0, legality forces z=s₁t₁, while t=0 leaves z free. There are six Q-zero points with t≠0. Written (s,t) in binary mask notation, they are (0,1),(2,1),(0,2),(1,2),(0,3),(3,3). Their augmented affine vectors have rank five and sum to zero, so any proper subset is affinely independent. No proper four-bit hyperplane contains all six. Thus on every hyperplane there is an affine z matching its required values. In the absence of the hyperplane the six points have odd required z sum. This is the specific affine obstruction overcome here.

Here is the remaining formal-degree check, independent of either large graph. For each of the thirty nonconstant hyperplanes in four binary coordinates, compute the degree-three closure of the two equations L and Q, with Boolean axioms. Its degree-at-most-two subspace is the entire set of quadratics vanishing on the admissible points in that hyperplane. The [finite calculation](codimension_one_port.py) orders squarefree monomials by total degree, inserts the two generating polynomials, and repeatedly inserts every variable multiple of independent rows of degree at most two. Every resulting quadratic vanishes on the test points. Gaussian elimination on the eleven monomial evaluations through degree two proves equality by the following dimensions.

| Hyperplanes | Admissible points | Rank of augmented affine point vectors | Dimension of vanishing quadratics | Dimension derived |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 15 | 4 | 4 | 7 | 7 |
| 15 | 6 | 4 | 5 | 5 |

Take the affine choice for z and use it to substitute the three replacement bits in the compressed proof. Each compressed shore guard becomes a quadratic vanishing on the restricted admissible set and hence has a proof within degree three from Q and L. Every exterior equation is unchanged because the substituted sender equals s identically. Composing this constant calculation with the original proofs of Q and L, then simulating the compressed proof by affine substitution, gives the claimed forward transport. A high-degree derivation of L does not add to the degree of its subsequent low-degree symbol manipulations.

For the reverse direction, choose one genuine X assignment for each of the four admissible states. Those points are affinely independent. Each original shore bit therefore has an affine representative interpolating its chosen values. After substitution, each original shore guard becomes a quadratic vanishing on the four points, and the same constant calculation derives it. Any mismatch in an original sender coordinate is affine and vanishes on these four points, so it is derivable and its product with an exterior variable can be used at degree two after that derivation. This reconstructs the original exterior equations, including the original pins. Any exterior-only linear relation in the variables s,t and untouched vertices that yields L without use of changed guards is transported directly if derived from the common pin equations; the two stated directions only require the respective derivability hypotheses.

In particular a minimum-order three-pin NO graph surviving degree three cannot have a full-table pin-free rank-two shore of size greater than three with any exposed nontrivial affine boundary relation in its cubic closure. Its smaller replacement would be a true NO of lower order and would refute at degree three, contradicting the forward transport. This narrows the [unconditional additive transfer](rank-two-native-pc-transport.md); it neither proves existence of a counterexample nor requires assuming its boundary table as an oracle, since the rank-two table and witnesses can be found by the physical replacement algorithm.
