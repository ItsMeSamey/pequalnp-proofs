# Clocked search for a succinct Cook witness

Let H be the unary language in NTIME(2^n) outside NTIME(2^{n/2}), with the fixed local Cook reduction of the October witness proof. A formula at length n has q=n+O(log n) clause-index bits and t=dn variable-label bits. A satisfying witness of size s means any fan-in-two Boolean circuit of ≤s gates on t bits whose truth table satisfies all decoded clauses. This definition does not require that it produce the lexicographically first assignment. [Williams, Theorems 2.1 and 6.1](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/improved-algs-lbs2.pdf) · [Local reductions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.3171) · [October quantitative proof](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-circuits.tex)

**Conditional uniform consequence.** If NP=coNP, there is an ε>0 such that at infinitely many YES lengths n every satisfying witness has more than 2^{εn} gates. Define a single language B on padded inputs (1^n,1^s) by asking if a size-s satisfying witness exists. A circuit description needs O((n+s)log(n+s)) bits. Guess it and test validity at every q-bit clause index using the fixed polynomial-time decoder. A violation has a polynomial-size certificate consisting of one index; validity of a supplied well-formed circuit is therefore in coNP. If NP=coNP, B belongs to NP. Let its fixed machine take O((n+s)^D) time. Choose 0<ε<1/(2D) with strict slack, and construct s=2^{floor(ε n)} in unary, using a fixed rational ε. The composed nondeterministic machine takes ≤2^{n/2} time at all sufficiently large lengths. At every NO length it rejects every branch. If it accepted every sufficiently large YES length, finite hardwiring would contradict the choice of H. At the infinitely many hard YES lengths no satisfying witness of this size exists. Reducing ε by a fixed factor absorbs rounding.

Under P=NP, B belongs to P and the same exponent argument uses a deterministic decider. Prefix-constrained B is still in the second polynomial hierarchy level, hence in P under this assumption. Fix a polynomial-length padded circuit description with canonical padding; querying prefix extensions produces a satisfying circuit whenever one exists, in polynomial time in n+s. Choose ε after the aggregate synthesis exponent if synthesis must also fit the clock. The canonical witness language W from October belongs to E under P=NP, since its E^NP oracle queries have exponential length and any fixed NP language has a polynomial-time decider in that case. Therefore the hard YES lengths supply an exponential circuit lower bound for this W in E under P=NP. Its E bit generator has an unspecified exponential constant. None of these implications establishes the existence of subexponential witnesses on every sufficiently large YES length.

**Unconditional quantifier.** If generation and complete checking of a candidate description cost 2^{αn} and 2^{βn}, respectively, with fixed α,β<1/2 and sound rejection of every candidate on NO, their sequential cost has exponent max(α,β)+o(1). Nondeterministic generation needs just one successful branch per YES length. Thus each such pair misses infinitely many YES lengths. The October small-circuit argument instead guesses every circuit up to the threshold and consequently forces infinitely many YES lengths simultaneously hard for every circuit up to that threshold. Neither argument proves eventual hardness. For example, at least one parity restriction of any such H remains outside NTIME(2^{n/2}); retain H on that parity and accept all lengths of the other parity, using a tautological formula there. This retains the separation and supplies infinitely many easy YES lengths.
