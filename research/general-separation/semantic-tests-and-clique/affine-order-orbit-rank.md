# Affine-order rank obstruction for a logarithmic orbit

Take n = 2^j and f(x) = Tr(x^7) on GF(2^n). The field monomial x^7 = x x² x⁴ is a product of three linear maps, so its Boolean ANF has degree at most three. Since 3 does not divide n, 7 is coprime to 2^n−1 and f is balanced. A field tower can use quadratic extensions α²+α=γ with absolute trace of γ equal to one; γα again has absolute trace one. Multiplication in the resulting basis and expansion of the cubic take polynomial time.

For nonzero δ, f(x+δ)+f(x), with an arbitrary added affine form, reduces by Frobenius under trace to Tr(a x⁵+b x³+c x)+constant with a=δ². The radical condition for the quadratic polar form, raised to the fourth power, is a⁴x¹⁶+b⁴x⁸+b²x²+a x=0. It has at most 16 roots. Squaring the complete character sum and summing translations shows its magnitude is at most 4·2^(n/2). Expanding an affine b-flat indicator in characters therefore bounds the normalized derivative bias on that flat by 4·2^(n/2−b).

## Realizing the truth table

A reversible NOT/CNOT/Toffoli circuit toggles y by f in O(log n) depth on arbitrary dirty workspace. Work separately by homogeneous degree k≤3. Allocate disjoint dirty clones to each monomial and role. For every subset S of roles, XOR the corresponding source bit into each clone in S, add the sum of clone products to y, then restore the clones. Summing over S gives exactly the sum of source products by finite differences. To broadcast a source into multiple dirty clones, conjugate a single CNOT by a depth-logarithmic CNOT tree taking its root basis vector to all ones. To add the parity of disjoint products, accumulate the parity of dirty targets before and after toggling them by the products, then restore them. A cubic product can use a dirty intermediate via the four-gate commutator of a Toffoli computing its first pair and a Toffoli multiplying the intermediate by its last input.

Convert every NOT/CNOT to positive Toffoli with fresh private guard controls. Run this circuit into a borrowed target t, flip a fresh observed y controlled on t and every guard, undo the circuit, and repeat the controlled flip. Dirty logarithmic-depth positive multi-control synthesis is detailed in positive-marked-mcx.md. The resulting output equals y XOR ([z=1] f(x)) on every input, with arbitrary initial state on all auxiliary bits. Banking disjoint layers as in logarithmic-quadratic-orbit.md implements this observed output as one coordinate of a logarithmic iterate of a reversible matching-quadratic map.

## Rank in every invertible affine query basis

Drop dependent columns after projecting the basis onto (x,z), fixing their query bits to arbitrary constants. The retained columns form a basis and keep their relative order. The affine y factor in the output sign table only rescales rows and columns. Write U,W for the complementary spans across a cut. Let a,b be their respective intersection dimensions with ker z, and t the dimension of the overlap of their guard images. Then a+b+t=n by rank-nullity. The value of a rises in unit steps from zero to n as columns move to the prefix. Choose a=floor(n/5).

When t≥n/5 the Boolean guarded table decomposes into 2^t compatible diagonal blocks and zero elsewhere. The corresponding x-flats partition all x into equal sizes. Balance implies at least half the blocks are nonzero. The sign rank here means ordinary real matrix rank of the sign-valued table, at least 2^(t−1)−1 by subtracting the all-ones matrix. When t<n/5 restrict to one compatible block. Its R=2^a normalized sign rows have pairwise inner products of magnitude at most 4·2^(n/2−b) by the derivative bound. The trace-squared over squared Frobenius norm bound on Gram rank gives at least R/(1+16·(R−1)·2^(n−2b)) ≥ R/17. Thus all affine query orders require exponentially many residual functions for these efficiently computable examples. This obstructs an ordered branching program representation and supplies no unrestricted P versus NP lower bound.

## Binary tensor trees

Consider a tree of degree at most three whose physical query bits occur at leaves, with arbitrary tensors at inner nodes and real edge indices. Pin the omitted query bits and contract their now-constant branches, merging degree-two vertices without increasing the maximum index dimension. Root the reduced tree at a subdivided edge. At every edge whose guard overlap t is at least n/10 use the preceding compatible-block argument. Otherwise pick an inclusion-minimal rooted subtree with a≥n/10. For large n it has two children, each with a_i<n/10. The intersection of the two child guard images has dimension at most t_child<n/10, so rank-nullity gives a=a_1+a_2+dim(intersection)<3n/10. This also excludes the root, whose a=n. At its cut b=n−a−t>6n/10. The Gram bound is at least min(2^a,2^(2b−n))/17, hence at least 2^(n/10)/17. Cutting one tree edge shows its index dimension bounds the corresponding flattening rank from above. Thus every such tree needs an exponential edge dimension. This assumes a tree, bounded node degree, and one physical variable per leaf.

The single traversal admits an escape since f is balanced. Concatenating two copies of the toggle gives a NO instance after the full traversal, since the toggle restores all borrowed wires and squares to the identity. Every cyclically rotated layer product is then also the identity. The half-traversal still has the same exponential rank. This gives a NO control for methods requiring a compact representation at every intermediate time.
