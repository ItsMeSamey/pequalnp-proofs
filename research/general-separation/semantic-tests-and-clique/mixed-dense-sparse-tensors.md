# Counting a dense CNF together with a sparse tensor on the same input

Let F be a fixed-width CNF on N declared Boolean variables at arbitrary clause density. Let a second Boolean tensor network with S wires including its external ports compute W(x) when the shared coordinates are pinned. Its entries are scalars modulo 2^{N+1}, with uniformly polynomial-time local entry procedures and total description length D. Coordinate repetitions can feed equality trees using O(S) additional wires. Assume polynomially many vertices in S+N and maximum sparse degree O((S+N)^{9/10}). For N≥20 put s=S+N ceil(loglog(N+2)), using binary logarithms. Then

    sum_x [F(x)] W(x) modulo 2^{N+1}

is computable deterministically in poly(D+|F|+s)·2^{O_k(s/sqrt(log(s+2)))} bit operations and polynomial space with constants depending on the fixed source-interface bounds. This adapts the October manuscript's dense counting proof and its internal Boolean tensor contraction theorem. The modular guarantee allows signed W. When the resulting integer lies in [0,2^N] the residue gives the exact integer. [Dense reduction and contraction](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-reduction.tex)

Sparsification partitions satisfying assignments into disjoint leaves. Construct each leaf's greedy sparse parity checks using ordinary unweighted counts. All satisfying assignments pass every linear test on their zero clause-violation vector. The selected final system has exactly the leaf count, so its solutions equal the leaf solutions pointwise. Signed objectives could lose this implication. Keep all declared bits. When substituting a bit also pin it on the external network. With no surviving equations contract the external network alone, summing each free bit exactly once. [Row selection and sparsification](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/main_full_version.tex#L3208-L3229)

Keep the high-incidence branching, degree arrays, test-group tensors and monomial wire indices of the final sparse equation system. Its parity lift lives in B=(Z/2^p)[V]/V^ell with p=ell=N+1. Let b_v count selected incidences of v in the indexed monomial. Give each variable-group tensor one new port per free variable it owns, including variables absent from the equations. Its entry becomes the product of [x_v=1] over v with b_v>0. Invalid indices still give zero. Connect these ports to the external network, summing any unused port with the all-ones unary tensor. Supply already pinned bits directly. Subdivide every wire by equality to restore bipartiteness. All sparse entries are degree-zero elements of B.

At each fixed x the selected monomials evaluate to their usual values on x. Summing arrays reconstructs the truncated product of all parity-test factors. Applying the source's coefficient-sum map then yields the common-zero indicator modulo 2^p by the pointwise total-order identity. W(x) is independent of V and commutes with this linear map. Summing over x gives the claimed scalar, including for external weights coupling different variable groups. [Local lift and signed-weight checks](october_weight_attachment_check.py)

The dense side has O(N loglog N) wires and degree N^{3/4}(log N)^{O_k(1)} before opening variables. The O(N) new ports add O(N^{3/4}) per group. After a fixed size-parameter scaling the combined graph satisfies the source's wire, degree, precision and entry conditions, for sufficiently large N. Bounded N is covered by 2^N separate sparse contractions. The ordinary candidate selection, sparsification, high-incidence branches and degree array enumeration cost at most the original poly(|F|)·2^{O_k(N loglog N/sqrt(log N))} before external contractions, and the number of remaining calls obeys this same bound. The exponent is absorbed by s/sqrt(log(s+2)). Sequential calls use polynomial space. Increasing p above N+1 may change the monomial-index budget.

## One NP lower bound with two atom budgets

For some absolute c>0 and each fixed d≥1, some L_d in NP, at infinitely many ambient lengths M, has no exact real SUM of at most M^d atoms even when one sum freely combines these types. Put K_1=ceil(c sqrt(loglog M)) and K_2=ceil(c sqrt(loglog M)/logloglog M) at sufficiently large M and extend by positive constant cutoffs at small lengths.

- Binary Boolean formulas with at most MK_1 leaves and deterministic single-bit query programs with at most MK_1 nodes, including cycles and rejecting infinite runs.
- Acyclic nondeterministic single-bit query programs with at most MK_2 nodes, arbitrary successor sets and arbitrary ambiguity, accepting if any computation accepts.

Use the common parity-slot projection from the [hierarchy argument](np-sum-formula-projection.md), with thresholds L_i=ceil(CK_i) per atom type and a common constant C. The expected heavy-coordinate bound and the Chernoff union bound depend on K_i/L_i. One slot choice thus leaves at most δn heavy coordinates per atom simultaneously. After pinning at most 4δn heavy coordinates in a moment product, represent its formula and deterministic factors by their [sparse signed tensor](cyclic-deterministic-covers.md), retaining original coordinates as open bits. This gives S=O(n+nL_1) and a Boolean-valued pointwise W. For all ambiguous DAG factors use the joint unique node-value recurrences and two signed-switch parity buckets. The width-three formulas have N=O(n+nL_2) variables and O(N²) clauses. Declare the unbranched input bits even when unused. One may pad to n with free variables and divide by the corresponding power of two. The sparse side depends only on the original unbranched inputs. The switch transform is due to Bannach, Demaine, Gomez and Hecher. [Signed clauses and prior attribution](signed-joint-clauses-attributed.md)

Each mixed bucket has integer value in [0,2^N], hence is recovered exactly. Even minus odd gives the true product count by assignment-wise cancellation. Absent sides are covered by W=1 or F=true. Since n≤M≤2^{O(n)}, s=O(Cc n sqrt(log n)+n loglog n). Take δ with 4δ<1/4, then C, then c small enough that the inner exponent is at most n/4 eventually. For fixed d choose ε to cover the original polynomial-in-M descriptor work, exact rational-weight processing, quartic expansion and common-slot union bound. This yields the same nondeterministic gap-UNSAT contradiction. All atom types admit ordinary polynomial-size circuits by gate evaluation or reachability, with a fixed exponent under the negation. The hard language can depend on d. Both atom types can occur in one moment product and the shared original assignment is summed once.
