# All quadratic identities on induced-even supports and a cubic obstruction

Let A be a loopless symmetric adjacency matrix, and let f_i=x_i Σ_j A_ij x_j in the squarefree Boolean polynomial algebra over GF(2). For every polynomial p of degree at most two, p vanishes on all induced-even supports if and only if p belongs to the binary linear span of the f_i. In polynomial time one can return either coefficients of the identity or an actual even support with p=1.

Evaluation on the empty set and all singletons tests the constant and linear parts. If these vanish, each nonedge pair is an even support isolating its quadratic coefficient. When these coefficients also vanish, the remaining coefficients c form a binary edge weight. Every chordless cycle is an induced-even support. Their edge vectors span the cycle space because a cycle with a chord splits into two shorter cycles adding to it over GF(2). Solve c_ij=y_i+y_j on the edges by propagation on a spanning forest. If it succeeds, p=Σy_i f_i. Otherwise a fundamental cycle has odd c-sum. Split along chords, always keeping a shorter cycle with odd weight, to find a chordless physical witness. This handles an arbitrary quadratic objective, including the product of two affine readout indicators.

For three actual pins the target is a cubic monomial. One valid partial algorithm tries reducing it to a quadratic modulo the f_i and all x_j f_i by a linear system. Upon success use the preceding physical solver. This static cubic reduction is incomplete. Use the triangular prism with edges 01,02,03,14,15,23,25,34,45 and pins 0,2,4. Encoding vertex i by bit i, the eight possible pinned support masks are 21,23,29,31,53,55,61,63. Respectively they have a selected odd-degree vertex 0,2,3,0,0,1,2,0, so the target vanishes on the physical domain.

Here is a binary functional zero on all coefficients of degrees zero through two. Its value on each cubic monomial is one exactly for these triples:
014,015,024,035,045,123,124,125,134,135,145,245,345.
For each i,j the value on x_j f_i is the parity of neighbors k for which i,j,k are distinct and their triple is listed. It is zero on every one of the 36 choices, but the value on x₀x₂x₄ is one. Hence this cubic has no quadratic representative by the stated static rule. Substituting the pins can make a refutation easier.

Even allowing repeated multiplication of derived affine consequences after pin substitution leaves a gap in degree-two refutation. An eleven-site example and exact certificate are included in [the independent checker](three_pin_quadratic_closure.py). Its adjacency rows are 160,1572,10,1796,640,1859,1184,1105,1064,58,490. The last three sites are pinned. The pin equations restrict the other bits to 32 points. All fail, while the residual equations have rank seven both before and after retaining only their homogeneous quadratic parts on that flat. Their span therefore has no nonzero affine row with which to continue and cannot contain one. The [high-connectivity fixtures](high-connectivity-anchor-obstruction.md) are also positive and negative regressions for this refuter. These counterexamples concern the specified algebraic rules.
