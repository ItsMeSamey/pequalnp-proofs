# One source for the planar, centered and fixed-automaton tests

Start from the [connected private-guard construction](private-parity-guard-portfolio-audit.md) with arbitrary strict 3SAT core and cubic triangle-expanded graph of order N. Use a positive marker triple and the specified two opposite-sign redundant attachments per marker variable. The resulting width-three CNF Q has a occurring variables and m rows, both O(N). Every witness gives an original core model. Conversely any core model extends with any independent set of the graph; the numerical truth of the core is never supplied. Positive-record statements below require a satisfiable core. Equivalence of the decision problems holds also on NO instances.

Construct the [occurrence cactus](ordered-ring-planar-phase-boundary.md) and retain the literal order of Q in its marked demands. The resulting signed edge-variable CNF F is literally the common input to the three frontends. Their emitted encodings differ. There are 12m cycle vertices, a−1 bridges, b=12m+a−1 edge variables, and 2(a−1) degree-three vertices. Thus there are 12m+4(a−1) binary negative conflicts, 12m positive stars, m positive demands, and C=25m+4a−4 total rows. F takes O(N log N) bits and its explicit four-field ring encoding takes Θ(N²) bits. The fixed DFA uses a constant number of events per literal plus explicit names and equality checks; its representation has polynomial bit length. The centered comparison matrix likewise has polynomial construction cost.

**Planar and affine calculations.** Every marked demand is a triple of pairwise disjoint edges and marks are disjoint across demands. No singleton restrictions occur and no marked demand contains another, so the direct FKT inclusion–exclusion reserves (1+b)2^m determinant calls. Contraction recovers Q up to signs and permutation. Fix a core solution, the empty graph independent set, zero private edge guards and all other markers to a satisfying setting. A three-bit marker triple has exactly seven feasible corners. All cactus-edge variables are affine functions on this section. Any nonempty cylinder specified by at most two affine equations contains at least two of the eight corners. Consequently neither Q nor F admits one exact conjunction of binary clauses on arbitrary affine forms, even after adding sound affine equations. The restriction is on the original variables; disjunctive branches and existential lifts have different semantics.

**Centered calculation.** The centered compiler repeats the last literal in each binary row. Its two shells per row have original affine dimension b+C and Fourier codimension 3C−b. In the separate grouped orientation procedure the initial conflict graph has 2C copies of K2. Every source variable has A/B occurrences in two different binary conflict rows, one at each vertex of its corresponding edge in a simple minimum-degree-two root. Within each row the unpadded A and B are distinct. These are exactly the conditions used in the [compatible-cycle coverage](centered-cactus-orientation.md) and [cycle-span calculation](centered-cactus-full-cycle-span.md). They do not require a special graph order or the numerical parameters of that example. Thus every pair survives the first closure, and the full 3C−b syndrome rank remains. This gives a linear exponent parameter in each of these two specific centered backends. It does not require a particular time to the first successful orientation.

**Fixed-DFA positive geometry.** From a successful word for F, read one alternating occurrence variable per graph phase at its successful equality marker. Up to complement these are affine bits and the image is exactly the independent sets. Both zero and every singleton occur. There is no nonzero translation period: a nonzero period must itself be independent, and adding a neighboring singleton then breaks independence. The active normal dimension is at least N. If all remaining exclusions have the required rank-two/private-flag format, the disjoint triangles imply private-image codimension at least N/3 by the two-subspace argument of the [DFA control](cactus-phase-fixed-dfa-transfer.md). Otherwise this particular private-pair backend does not apply.

For the explicit signed whole-record intersection dictionary use the separated induced matching and private-variable section in the private-guard proof. Fix the core solution and satisfying markers. Every cycle variable at an equality marker is a constant or one source bit or its complement. A structural star or conflict reads at most one such source phase. Every marked row port carries precisely its corresponding signed source reading, in the same order. On the legal corners of a selected edge, any actual event record is constant, distinguishes just one current axis, or distinguishes all three corners. In the last case its binary affine interpolation has singleton fibers. This follows from the fixed byte control buffering both preceding signs, with the current marker input retained, as checked in the private-guard proof. Thus singleton record fibers and their intersections restrict to rectangles. The matrix of permissible independent choices on a linear-size separated matching has rank 2^{Ω(N)} over the rationals. An explicit equality-of-entire-record intersection dictionary representing this positive set therefore requires that many entries. The canonical-dummy section and the specialized positive projection likewise preserve the independent-set readouts and the rectangularity of fixed signed OR records.

None of these facts is a lower bound on a general decision algorithm. They price named representations or rule out a particular affine description. They do not prove that a finite-budget source solver returns unknown, that all block searches are slow, that a witness cannot be found early, or that optional general preprocessing must preserve the representations. A polynomial-time source model finder for all cores would also find a satisfying word on the YES cases above. The open standard question is unchanged.

**Actual elementary dispatch and width cutoffs.** The same outer F has neither units nor pure variables. Every edge variable has a positive star occurrence and a negative conflict. Because a occurs in the construction with more than one phase, some root vertex has degree three. Its positive degree-three star requires at least two incident variables to be flipped to obtain a Horn clause, while the three negative pairwise conflicts forbid flipping any two. Hence F is neither binary nor renamably Horn under any polarity. No two distinct raw rows with equal support differ in a single pivot: the positive and negative binary rows on a degree-two star differ in both literals. Different long stars and distinct marked triples have different supports. No structural conflict or star is contained in a marked triple of disjoint edges. All-zero and all-one words both fail structural requirements.

For a cubic triangle-expanded expander G, its treewidth is linear in N by the balanced-separator argument. In the incidence graph of Q each original graph edge uv is represented by a path from x_u through a guarded row, its edge-private variable, and the opposite guarded row to x_v. Use two rows sharing the same private variable; all internal vertices are disjoint between edges. In the incidence graph of F, each phase cycle's edge names together with the binary negative conflict at each adjacent pair of that cycle induce a connected subgraph. Contract these disjoint subgraphs, discard bridges and surplus structural clauses, and retain the demands. This exhibits the incidence graph of Q as a minor. In the fixed byte construction, connect the occurrences of a signed source variable through its equality chain and retain the nodes of the real OR triples. The source incidence graph is a minor of this declared physical incidence object, also on padded binary rows. Accordingly a backend requiring a verified decomposition of that object of fixed width cannot accept a large member. This last conclusion only applies to an explicit unreduced incidence object with these chains still present. Optional semantic quotients or other presentations require separate analysis. The source chart's sparse allowed-hull prepass also cannot describe the seven-corner marker section by a single affine-and-binary chart. None of these initialization checks precludes a later constructive fallback from deciding a particular input.
