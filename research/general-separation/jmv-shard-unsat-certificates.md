# What NP = coNP would force inside individual JMV witness buckets

Either NP differs from coNP, or the following holds. **For every fixed 0≤β<1/2 there is a fixed σ>0 such that, at infinitely many YES lengths n of H, every satisfying assignment has some native prefix of length floor(βn) on which its remaining Boolean function needs more than floor(2^(σn)) binary gates.** The YES lengths may depend on β; the offending prefix may depend on the assignment. Use the unary hierarchy language and the explicit right-padded JMV naming of the [native prefix-cover proof](jmv-native-unary-prefix-cover.md). Its cover has at most O(nB) reached ordered triples for B buckets, not counting signs. The underlying source decoder and its satisfiability equivalence are from [JMV, ECCC TR13-099 revision 3, §§3–5](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/099/revision/3/download/).

Assume NP=coNP. Ordinary UNSAT of an explicitly encoded bounded-fanin circuit then has one polynomially clocked nondeterministic multitape machine. Choose a fixed integer e≥1 so every path rejects or accepts within O((L+1)^e) steps on an encoding of length L, increasing e if needed for multitape simulation, syntax checks, counters and resetting the work tapes. On malformed encodings the machine rejects. Equivalently choose a verifier, bound both witness length and checking time by fixed polynomials *in the instance length*, and enlarge e to cover the entire process. On a satisfiable circuit every path rejects, including malformed or truncated certificates. On an unsatisfiable circuit at least one path accepts. The same machine and e work for every length, bucket, and β.

First take rational 0<β<1/2 and choose a positive rational σ so β+eσ<1/2. The public gates-per-shard cap is floor(2^(σn)); for rational σ=a/c one may compute it by the integer cth root of 2^(an), in time polynomial in n. The machine rejects nonunary inputs. On unary length n, construct the actual decoder, choose b=floor(βn), and nondeterministically write one bounded, syntactically valid shard circuit per b-bit prefix, with inputs the remaining address coordinates. Guess these records once in bucket order. Reject an ill-formed record. Thus every guess defines a common complete assignment of bits to all variable names.

Enumerate all realized bucket triples **deterministically** by the prefix-trie procedure of the cover proof. Feasibility of a partial output prefix is a fixed-width CNF on q=n+O(log n) bits with O(n) clauses, by listing the wrong patterns on the constant supports of its specified decoder output bits. The deterministic fixed-density counting theorem gives an exact empty/nonempty answer in 2^o(n) bit time with polynomial workspace per test; constants and very small instances can be handled directly. At most O(1+bK) queries suffice for K realized triples. Both decisions, including pruning an empty prefix, are therefore reliable regardless of the nondeterministic choices elsewhere. [October theorem, fixed-density clock and space](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/42907c9e999cc00d6abe7a50bfc1023250edd1b9/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/main_full_version.tex)

Issue the three requests per realized triple and attach its three already-guessed records by the two tape mergesorts and ordered join of the [base verification argument](jmv-native-bucket-triples.md). For each triple form Bad on the single original clause index. It checks its decoded bucket prefixes and whether all three signed literals fail under the corresponding selected shards. Run the fixed nondeterministic UNSAT machine on Bad. Reject this entire computation branch if that run rejects or exhausts its clock; only an accepting run proceeds to the next triple. Reuse work storage and never replace a guessed shard. Accept after all triples pass.

The individual Bad encoding length is O((n+ℓ)log(n+ℓ+2)) with ℓ=floor(2^(σn)). The total time, on successful and unsuccessful branches alike, including reading or guessing all certificate bits, is bounded by

    (1+bK) 2^o(n) + (B+K)(n+ℓ) poly(n+log(ℓ+2))
                   + K · O(((n+ℓ)log(n+ℓ+2))^e)
      ≤ 2^((β+eσ)n+o(n)).

On a YES length with small shards there exists a correct common guess. Every Bad is unsatisfiable. Choosing an accepting run for each of the finitely many tests gives one accepting path through the whole ordered list. On a NO length every common guess violates an indexed clause. Its actual bucket triple occurs in the deterministic enumeration, so Bad is satisfiable there. No sequence of certificates can pass all tests. If all sufficiently large YES lengths had some all-small assignment, the machine would decide H in nondeterministic time 2^(n/2), after hardwiring finite exceptions. This contradicts the unary hierarchy choice of H and proves the claimed infinitely-often quantifiers. [Hierarchy witness and local test setup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/42907c9e999cc00d6abe7a50bfc1023250edd1b9/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-circuits.tex)

For β=0 or an arbitrary real β<1/2 in the stated range, choose a rational β′ strictly between β and 1/2 and use its σ and hard lengths. A finer β′-prefix slice is obtained from the enclosing β-prefix slice by fixing additional inputs, which cannot increase gate count in the allowed basis with constants. On those same lengths an all-small coarse partition would therefore give an all-small fine partition. The verifier itself only uses the computable rational β′.

The earlier [unsharded witness argument](/workspace/pnp-research/research/general-separation/semantic-tests-and-clique/october-collapse-witness-floor.md) tests one globally small circuit per assignment. The present conclusion excludes representations whose individual shard circuits are all small even when their aggregate gate count includes a factor of 2^(βn). It concerns the specified native address convention and uses no completeness assumption on H. It gives a disjunction, without deciding whether NP=coNP.

**Certificate-only traversal.** The revised local-reduction primary is [JMV, ECCC TR13-099 revision 3](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/099/revision/3/download/), from 2014 following the initial 2013 report. The fixed-density counting theorem cited above is a separate October 2026 source and is optional for this implication. Under NP=coNP, guess the answer to each trie-extension question. Certify a positive answer by a concrete q-bit index and direct decoder evaluation, or a negative answer by the clocked UNSAT machine on the polynomial-size prefix-match circuit. Reject immediately on any invalid or missing certificate, failed computation or timeout. Every continuing path follows exactly the true trie, so even a failing path makes at most O(1+bK) calls including its last failed attempt. Some path supplies correct certificates for both outcomes throughout. Their length and validation time are bounded by a fixed polynomial per node, independent of β, using the same exponent e enlarged once if necessary. The local Bad checks and common shard guesses are unchanged. This gives the same exponential bound using the local reduction and the nondeterministic hierarchy without an exact-counting assumption.
