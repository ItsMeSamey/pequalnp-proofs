# Ordered-image counting with a simultaneous rank bound

[count](ring_image_span_gram_count.py) accepts the original four fields and returns the exact number of assignments of the recovered signed source, the physical residue and, for a positive count, a checked source witness. Off-image is distinct. The two independent nonnegative caps max_terms and max_determinants default to None. No callback, guessed image parameters or hidden source is an input.

After [ordered decoding](read-two-ring-image.md) the wrapper computes the [raw clause-miss basis](cnf_miss_span.py). Its miss indicators span every original clause miss, so an assignment with every basis coordinate zero satisfies exactly the original formula. Write b for its rank and h for the distinct IDs appearing in the retained basis clauses. The generic counter requires at most T=min(2^h,(h+1)2^b) actual terms, including witness reconstruction, and zero terms for an empty basis or one containing the empty clause. The first term bound sums all basis assignments in a single pass. The second sums all subsets for the count plus up to h conditional counts. Thus a cap of 2^min(h,b) need not suffice for raw witness work. The wrapper uses T when determining whether fallback is guaranteed. It computes the rank once for scheduling and the public solver repeats the same polynomial projection only if needed.

If raw fallback is guaranteed, set the effective determinant cap to the lesser of 2^min(h,b) and the explicit determinant cap, treating None as unlimited. Otherwise retain the caller's actual determinant cap, so that a feasible Gram calculation can still succeed despite a small raw budget. Try the [planar Gram backend](ring_image_gram_demands.py) on the decoded source. If it declines, run the raw counter with the caller's term budget, even when that budget is less than T: zero-count and other short instances can finish early. Each path reports its actual charged operations; raw results retain the partial Gram result including its call count. An exhausted raw budget returns unknown rather than a zero count. The caps do not limit polynomial preprocessing.

On raw success the returned mantissa already includes variables occurring originally but absent from the retained rows. Shift by its separately returned unused-variable exponent once. This expansion and the dense original witness cost polynomial space because recovered source arity a is bounded by the explicit physical word. This conclusion does not apply to arbitrary sparse instances where a is supplied independently in binary. Either exact path validates any returned witness on the recovered signed source. Its physical residue is (−15)^(k−a−1) times the full integer count modulo 3^k, with k the supplied physical precision.

**Bit cost with default budgets.** Let B denote the explicit input bit length and g=min(b,h). On ineligible inputs the Gram rejection is polynomial, and raw completion takes at most (h+1)2^g poly(B). On eligible inputs let u count nonlinear demands, ρ be their rational Gram rank, and r the independent affine-check rank. Full Gram discovery uses O(u²) requests and polynomial exact rational algebra. The remainder uses at most (1+e)2^(r+ρ) requests for e surviving edges, including the witness. There is at most one additional determinant for sign calibration. Discovery repeated deletion sets are cached; building the entire discovery matrix still costs poly(B). In each subsequent filtered sum on a certified nonempty matching family, every term with nonempty deletions or nontrivial twist uses a determinant. The independent character masks make only the empty subset have zero twist, and nonlinear demands have already had empty sets removed. Thus at most one term in each such call can reuse an untwisted base determinant without charge. This bounds partial work even if stopped in discovery or mid-sum.

The scheduled Gram attempt takes at most poly(B)(u²+1+2^g), while the raw fallback also fits poly(B)2^g. If the automatic cap stops Gram, its unrestricted workload would exceed 2^g determinants, hence 2^g < O(1+u²+(1+e)2^(r+ρ)). Paying for the raw fallback therefore also fits poly(B)2^(r+ρ). If Gram finishes, its full bound applies directly. Combining yields **poly(B)·2^min(b,h,r+ρ)** on eligible inputs and poly(B)·2^min(b,h) otherwise, with polynomial working space. With restrictive caller caps the wrapper can refuse or intentionally switch away from the faster full route; spent work is polynomial per charged term/determinant, plus polynomial preprocessing. No optimality of these exponents is asserted.
