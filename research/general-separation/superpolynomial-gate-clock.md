# The superpolynomial-factor threshold for the next gate bound
Assume there are a constant B>0, a deterministic multitape algorithm and h(q)=q^{omega(1)} such that, on every q-input fanin-two circuit with at most B q log2 q gates, SAT decision takes <=2^q poly(q)/h(q). Testing UNSAT uses the same clock. Then one language W in E^NP needs Omega(t log t) unrestricted fanin-two gates at infinitely many input lengths t. No assumption is made about the uniformity of the circuits being lower-bounded.

Use the unary nondeterministic hierarchy (Williams, Theorem 2.1, https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/improved-algs-lbs2.pdf) with t1(n)=2^{n-ceil(log2 n)} and t2(n)=2^n. The functions are time-constructible up to harmless constant factors and t1(n+1)=o(t2(n)). Obtain H in NTIME(2^n) outside NTIME(2^n/n). Apply the fixed local decoder construction in the October circuit corollary: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-circuits.tex . For each unary length n, the CNF on exponentially many variables has clause indices of q=n+O(log n) bits, a poly(n)-constructed O(n)-gate decoder producing 3 variable labels and signs, variable labels padded to t=Dn for fixed integer D, and satisfiability iff 1^n in H. On length Dn define W(i) as bit i of the lexicographically first satisfying assignment, zero if none; define zero at other lengths. Explicit exponential CNF expansion and at most 2^{Dn} NP prefix queries yield W in E^NP.

If W eventually has circuits with <=eta n log2 n gates on lengths Dn, guess one on each sufficiently large unary input. Connect three copies plus the O(n) decoder to test for a violated indexed clause. Choose fixed eta>0 with 3eta<B; the resulting circuit has q primary inputs and <=B q log2 q gates eventually. Accept the guess if and only if this circuit is unsatisfiable. On YES an honest circuit gives acceptance, and on NO every completed assignment violates a clause. Guess construction and all simulation overhead are polynomial in n outside the stated deterministic clock. Since 2^q<=2^n poly(n) and h(q) beats every fixed polynomial, this decides H in nondeterministic O(2^n/n) time including finite exceptions, a contradiction. The conclusion concerns infinitely-often gate size for W in E^NP, not NP or uniform polynomial-time decision. A heuristic, structural promise on the violation circuits, or a solver with only a fixed polynomial savings factor does not supply the displayed hypothesis.
