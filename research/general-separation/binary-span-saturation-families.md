# Explicit families for binary-indicator saturation

All ranks are over GF(2). Each test uses a common background B for the entire round. Any candidate whose nonzero failure lies in the residual span vanishes at every original model. Restriction to the strengthened background kills the span of the accepted rows, so rank falls by at least its dimension s. Keeping only an independent subset gives the identical strengthened model set and adds at most the initial rank r clauses overall. The reference implementation batches all accepted rows. On u occurring variables, at most r+1 sweeps each probe at most 1+2u+4 binom(u,2) candidates by a zero test and one augmented rank call plus a per-round base computation. A contradictory binary background certifies UNSAT. Positive rank at stability still requires a decision procedure.

## Connected chains

Module i uses x,p,z1,...,zL, with fresh variables across modules. Seed it with (x or z1 or ±p). Add (x or not zj or z(j+1)) for j=1,...,L-1. Join the m modules by z1 implies h in each. On h=1 the cube is unrestricted. Private cubic tail monomials force every tail coefficient in a dependency to vanish. The private seed cubic forces equal seed coefficients and the surviving private quadratic forces them zero. Initial rank is m(L+1).

Within one module every nontrivial binary consequence contains positive x since x=1 is a free face. On x=0 an affine target forces away every tail carrying a distinct quadratic. The two seed coefficients must agree, so the first sweep learns exactly x or z1. After k rounds the prefix z1,...,zk is fixed on x=0 and the frontier is now the failure of the unit z(k+1) on this face. Every later row has a private quadratic. On the h=1 product slice, any genuine binary probe mixing independent modules has a nonzero mixed rectangular difference, whereas sums of local failures do not. A probe involving h restricts to zero or a single-module target. Thus no off-slice discoveries accelerate the prefix. Conversely each isolated deduction is valid globally. Exactly L productive rounds suffice. Each ordinary substitution-only syntactic weak or strong 2CNF backdoor hits the m disjoint seed supports, and all m variables x suffice. The relation itself admits a 2CNF from the outset. For h=1 the count is (2^(L+1)+2)^m and for h=0 it is 2^(Lm). Complementary-pair splitting followed by suitable resolution also handles this family.

## Cyclic parity

For n=2^k>=8 take T_i={i,i+1,i+3} modulo n. Impose even parity on each triple using four clauses forbidding the odd patterns. The six nonzero directed differences ±1,±2,±3 are distinct, so two triples share at most one variable. The local four rows sum to x_i+x_(i+1)+x_(i+3). These n functions are independent since 1+t+t^3 is coprime to (t+1)^n=t^n+1. Every negative unit is learned in one round; zero is the sole model.

The initial rank is exactly 4n. All quadratic and cubic terms in a dependency cancel locally by private supports. A local affine function vanishing on the even subspace is a multiple of parity. Independence of the n parities forces each multiple zero. The local four disjoint cells are independent. Any syntactic backdoor by substitution hits n triples, requiring at least ceiling(n/3) variables. Unit propagation, binary-only resolution and exact complementary-pair splitting have no first step. Within a triple two clauses differ in two signs, so each resolvent is tautological. Across triples any resolvent has width four. Hence width-three resolution stalls.

## Incomparability and PC

In the opposite direction (a or b or x),(a or not x or y),(a or not y or b) admits two width-three resolutions deriving a or b. Distinct cubic supports block every nonzero binary span target. A small reverse example imposes even parity on (1,2,3),(4,5,6),(2,5,7),(3,6,7),(1,4,8). The 20 rows sum to x8. Exhaustive enumeration gives eight models at x8=0 and none at x8=1. The 116 pair/pivot resolvents give 60 tautologies and 56 proper width-four clauses.

For CNF inputs of clause width at most three, degree-three Boolean polynomial calculus over GF(2) simulates each learning step. Derive the binary implicates of B by binary resolution up to weakening. A resolution of failures xA and (1+x)C obtains AC by summing xAC and (1+x)AC at degree at most three. Switch coordinates about a model. An infeasible monomial contains a prohibited singleton or pair because implication closure preserves unions and all prohibitions are at most binary. Multiply this binary implicate by the remaining factors. For feasible terms, substitute fixed SCC representatives using the sum of mutual implication failures x+y; remove redundant y implied by x via xy+x multiplied by the remaining factors. Equal closure keys now give identical minimal generator antichains. Boolean multilinear reductions and literal complementation preserve the degree bound. Earlier learned binary clauses may be reused at their established degree. Strictness follows from the eight ternaries encoding x+y+z=x+y+w=0. Only z implies w and its converse are nontrivial binary implicates. Both involve the monomial zw absent from all rows, so saturation stalls. PC obtains z+w, then multiplies by z or w.

## Hard control

In exact-three SAT replace each occurrence of x with fresh y and enforce equality by (not x or y or z1),(x or not y or z2) with global pads. Force each z=0 by six clauses (not z or D) over private variables where D runs through (not s or a),(not a or b),(not b or not s),(s or c),(not c or d),(not d or s). The implications contradict either s value; at z=0 five internal variables are free. Every ternary in the entire output has its own triple support. Thus rank equals the number of clauses, and no binary, unit or empty-clause failure lies in span. For nonempty sources the pads connect the incidence graph on occurring variables. There are 7L+12 clauses for L source clauses, and each source model has exactly 1024 extensions.
