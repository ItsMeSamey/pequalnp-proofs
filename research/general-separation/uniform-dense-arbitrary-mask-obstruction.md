# Uniform dense controls for arbitrary fixed parity masks
Let Q(n)>=1 be polynomially bounded and polynomial-time computable, and let f_j(x), 1<=j<=Q(n), be uniformly polynomial-time computable Boolean masks on n-bit words. Fix an integer c>=1. For all sufficiently large n a uniform polynomial-time predicate C_n accepts at least a 1-n^{-c} fraction, its support is indecomposable across every nontrivial variable split, and the parity of C_n intersected with each mask is zero.

Let d be the smallest integer with 2^d>4(n+1)^{c+1}(Q+1), so d=O(log n)<n. Split each word into a d-bit prefix u and a suffix z. For a requested z compute v_z(u)=(f_j(u,z))_j for all 2^d prefixes, a canonical subset B_z of at most Q actual prefixes spanning their columns, and their total column s_z. These operations take polynomial time per slice. Choose a canonical prefix a at distance greater than one from B_0 and outside all B_z with a weight-one suffix z. The union of excluded prefixes has cardinality at most (d+1)Q+(n-d)Q=(n+1)Q, so a exists and can be obtained by examining at most n+1 slices.

At nonzero suffix z reject a subset of B_z whose columns sum to s_z. At the zero suffix reject a and a subset of B_0 with column sum s_0+v_0(a). Use deterministic elimination. Each slice has zero parity syndrome and rejects at most Q+1 out of 2^d words. The missing word (a,0) has every one-bit neighbor accepted. For each nontrivial bipartition both of its projections therefore occur, although the word itself does not. Thus the support cannot factor across that partition. Evaluating C_n on a word needs only n+2 polynomial-sized slices, yielding a uniformly constructible polynomial-size circuit. The polynomial may depend on the mask family and c. No iteration over all suffixes and no unproved representation assumption enter.

The masks are independent of the description of the constructed circuit. There is no near-linear bound on circuit size. This obstruction addresses fixed parity-mask reductions, not arbitrary polynomial-time deciders. For deterministic adaptive black-box mask generation along the all-zero transcript, the same reasoning applies provided each successive pointwise mask is uniformly computable in polynomial time and the transcript length has a known polynomial bound.
