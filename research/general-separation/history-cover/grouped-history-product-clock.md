# Original-input counting after sharing nonlinear roots
Let an explicitly given binary circuit on n raw bits have s gates and m nonlinear gates. Expand affine wiring into forms in raw bits and earlier nonlinear output symbols. Name t actual nonlinear gate outputs y as shared ports. Replace their uses by y but retain every defining computation. The final readout is affine in raw bits, names, and unnamed nonlinear gate outputs. The roots to extract are every named definition and every distinct unnamed term in the final readout. For each root restrict to its backward cone, stopping at *uses* of names, and test the history rank in its inherited order with this one root as readout (as defined in temporal-nonlinear-rank.md). Require rank at most one in each cone independently. Thus shared consumers are charged and the simultaneous full circuit history need not have rank one.

The scalar recurrence on this cone, with coordinates (x,y), is s_i=A_i s_(i-1)+B_i, s_0=0, with A affine and B a product of two affine factors plus one affine term. Unrolling gives sum_i B_i product_{h>i} A_h. This is at most 2l products of affine factors for l local nonlinear gates, plus an affine output term. All coefficients, product lengths, and dense factor descriptions can be explicitly written in polynomial time. Conjoin every equation y_j=its extracted definition and the one final acceptance equation. Each distinct root occurs only once in this list after affine cancellation. Every physical x has unique consistent y by topological gate evaluation, and any solution has those actual gate values by induction. Definitions apply even when one port fans into many roots. A product count P=O(n+t+1+sum of checked cone sizes) bounds occurrences across equations, not just distinct products. The sum of footprints is at most polynomial in s.

An equivalent strictly raw-input algorithm enumerates the t port values and applies the system counter separately on its n physical variables, checking all definitions. Lokshtanov, Paturi, Tamaki, Williams and Yu, Theorem 1.4, give deterministic exact counting of such explicit systems in poly(description) times 2^{n-Omega(n/(1+log2(1+P/n)))} per guess. Pad the bound to P=Theta(n) when smaller, or separately handle zero variables and constant systems. Alternatively keep y formal and Gaussian-eliminate e independent affine-only conditions; for consistency this yields D=n+t-e variables, and the same theorem with D in place of n and no 2^t factor. Affine substitution preserves each product without expansion. The polynomial overhead counts the written input, including all factors and variables per factor. Physical prefix pins and positive-count self-restriction produce one original witness. See https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~daniello/papers/polyEQFF17.pdf .

For s=O(n log n), total footprint O(s) and t=o(n/loglog n) yield an Omega(n/loglog n) exponent saving over raw exhaustive search. A polynomial total footprint and t=o(n/log n) still give an Omega(n/log n) saving. For example use n chains of length floor(beta log2 n), each alternately ANDing/ORing a scalar state with a raw literal. Start the first t=floor(n/(loglog n)^2) independently. Their endpoints are shared named controls: in the other chains insert an earlier named control, optionally XORed with a raw bit, at every third step. Accept the XOR of the chain endpoints. Arbitrary raw sharing is allowed. The sum of truncated root footprints is O(n log n), and beta can be chosen small enough for any fixed B n log2 n gate budget. Definitions and all final outputs refer to the same raw assignment. At t=0 there are no added coordinates even if arbitrarily many nonlinear chains are aggregated. The naive independent last-reset enumeration would list a product of (chain length+1) choices per chain; the compiled algorithm avoids this enumeration. This is a property of the supplied layout, not a lower bound on its minimum circuit size.

No universal small port set has been proved for B n log n circuits. Only an algorithm covering all circuits at this budget with a uniform superpolynomial saving would yield the conditional E^NP gate lower bound in superpolynomial-gate-clock.md; the checked class by itself gives no P versus NP separation.
