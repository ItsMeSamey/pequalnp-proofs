The complete solver enforces output acceptance and closes affine implications of each original c=uv gate. In the current affine chart, constant, equal, or complementary factors give affine constraints; c=1 forces u=v=1. Each successful round adds rank or finds a contradiction. Project the resulting joint affine relaxation onto the raw coordinates, take an independent basis of projected directions, and substitute this affine chart into EVERY original gate. The relaxation contains every satisfying assignment; retaining all original gates makes the restriction exact. Raw witnesses lift through the chart and are checked on the original circuit. The dispatcher tries capped sparse substitution, then triangular Gaussian character counting with charged rank, then exact enumeration in the d-dimensional raw chart. Fixed polynomial budgets give polynomial overhead beyond the 2^rank times polynomial character clock and the 2^d times polynomial fallback. A frontend builds physical gates for 3CNF including empty clauses.

For a connected 2CNF control take f to be independent-set membership on the n-vertex path. An induced matching with k=floor((n+1)/3) edges is obtained by taking the first two vertices of successive triples, and the last pair if present. Setting all other coordinates to zero gives the product of k factors (1+a_i b_i), hence 2^k original-coordinate monomials and degree 2k on that restriction. Zero and all unit vectors are accepted, so the affine hull of the full accepting set is the entire raw space. The monomial lower bound need not survive arbitrary affine coordinate changes; the degree lower bound does.

Consider ANY affine-plus-quadratic system whose raw fibers have odd cardinality on YES and even on NO, including unique-extension formulations. Its affine subsystem has full raw projection because every YES has an extension. After carrying whole rows through Gaussian elimination let r be the rank of quadratic parts modulo affine and include every affine residual row in the affine subsystem. Parametrize all affine solutions over x as y=L(x)+D t, where L is affine and t has h free bits. Over F2 the sum over t of the product of the r indicators 1+q_i(x,L(x)+Dt) equals f. After multilinearization, summing over t kills every monomial missing any free bit; each surviving monomial loses h factors. Therefore deg f≤2r−h and r≥k+ceil(h/2). This holds regardless of auxiliary count, gate order or invertible affine chart. It rules out uniformly polynomial character-list size for the 2^r dispatcher preserving every raw fiber. Path independent set is easy, and its all-zero witness can be returned immediately; no SAT runtime lower bound follows.

In fact the exact degree of the path predicate is n minus one when n is 1 modulo 3, and n otherwise. The top monomial coefficient equals the XOR of all values, the parity of the independent-set count F_(n+2). Deleting the first vertex or choosing it gives the Fibonacci recurrence, whose mod-two period is three. When the top coefficient vanishes, fixing the last coordinate to zero gives the (n−1)-path with nonzero top coefficient. Thus the stronger representation lower bound is 2r−h≥n−1_{n≡1 mod 3}.
