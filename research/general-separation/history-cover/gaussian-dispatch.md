Given the compiled System, backend(system) checks each equation as symbol + RHS = 0 (target output symbol is the constant one). It removes duplicate affine factors by Boolean idempotence and disposes of zero products or complementary pairs, then expands certified factors into squarefree monomials. If the quadratic parts span dimension at most one, subtract the representative equation from equations with the same quadratic part. All remaining equations and new raw coordinate pins are affine. A failed certification returns Unsupported; counts are exact on accepted systems. In particular a declined higher-degree presentation might simplify algebraically by other methods.

Use quadratic_target.affine_domain for a bijection of the linear slice with d free bits. On an inconsistent slice the count is zero. A constant, d first differences and d choose 2 mixed differences recover the unique quadratic form on these bits. If its Gaussian bias is b, exactly (2^d+b)/2 assignments satisfy the representative equation. With no representative the entire slice qualifies. The existing iterative quadratic_image.gauss treats degenerate polar forms, linear radicals and signs. Original auxiliary coordinates are uniquely forced by the grouped compiler. Repeated pinned calls through System.count_witness give a physical assignment, evaluated in the original control tests.

Let N be the total variable count, L the explicit system length. Normalizing at most two affine factors per product takes polynomially many bit operations: O(L N^2) monomial toggles of O(N)-bit keys after reading the lists. A query uses one polynomial Gaussian elimination, O(N²) evaluations on at most O(N²) monomials, and at most N/2 pair-removals on O(N²) pairs. All operands and counts have O(N+log(L+1)) bits. The wrapper invokes at most n+1 queries. This is a polynomial bit clock. Hash tables may be replaced by deterministic ordered sets or dense indexed coefficient arrays with polynomial overhead. Measured timings in check_gaussian compare the compiled count to full direct original circuit enumeration, exclude compilation, and do not apply to general circuits.
