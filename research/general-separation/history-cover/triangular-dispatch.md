The compiled input has explicit triangular defining equations z=p(earlier) over squarefree Boolean ANF. Eliminate gates in reverse dependency order. The frontier contains the output acceptance equation and every retained later definition. Simultaneously substitute p for z in each frontier equation, combining identical monomials modulo two before testing the degree. Commit the entire update only when every result is quadratic or affine. Otherwise retain z and add its defining equation to the frontier. Each successful substitution is a bijective projection on solutions because z has exactly one possible value given its predecessors. In particular a shared producer is substituted identically at every occurrence. At termination retained definitions are still triangular, permitting the original exact character backend with its full rank charge. Algebraically this is evaluation at p of each downstream constraint before expanding the defining delta function in characters. No Fourier independence between branches is assumed.

Each input equation of the certified quadratic fragment has O(N²) monomials. Substitution can create terms of degree at most three because its argument was quadratic and squarefree. The accepted frontier remains degree at most two. Exact expansion, cancellation, testing, and renumbering thus cost polynomial bit time and space in explicit size. The residual rank r costs 2^r polynomial time; pins and witness reconstruction use original variables. The earlier direct-sat-algebra star sieve enumerates characters of star-center equations under its star layout; this implementation reuses Gaussian evaluation but performs exact producer substitution before forming characters. This is an implementation-level structural sufficient condition without a literature-priority claim.

A connected accumulator z_i=z_(i−1)+x_i x_(i+1) has independent quadratic rows of rank k and overlapping raw inputs. Reverse elimination leaves only the single quadratic output, with two rather than 2^k total scalar characters. The original triangular producer gates implement one affine-plus-product update each. A 3CNF compiler shows the fixed-degree pruning rule cannot simply delete every producer: even conjunctions of k disjoint three-literal clauses exceed residual rank one in the supplied presentation. Numerical controls for k=2,4,8 leave ranks 5,11,23 respectively, although these special disjoint formulas have elementary counts by other means.
