# Larger private banks from gate-by-gate verification

Use the model and footprint convention of the [private-bank theorem](np-sum-formula-private-dag-banks.md). Fix integers d,k≥1 and K(N)=ceil((log log(N+16))^(1/3)). There are at most N^d atoms; each has an arbitrary Boolean DAG bank of at most N^k binary gates and an outer binary tree of at most N^k nodes with at most NK(N) direct raw literal occurrences. Bank outputs may be repeated throughout that tree. The union of all raw inputs named in the bank's supplied interfaces and gates, including **all nominated raw output wires even if no internal gate reads them**, has size at most γN per atom. Auxiliary identities and aliases charge their raw names. Banks may overlap. There is a language in NP lacking these exact representations at infinitely many input lengths in each of the following regimes:

* Real linear combinations, for every fixed 0≤γ<1/3.
* Signed integer linear combinations, for every fixed 0≤γ<1/2, without an a priori bound on coefficient magnitudes.

The language may depend on d,k,γ and the coefficient convention. We use the common parity-slot projection and ordinary circuit gap-UNSAT contradiction of the linked proof. Here are the changed verification and description bounds.

## Real coefficients

Write v_i(x) for the normalized signed sum at gate i. Guess the coefficients as integer numerators over a common positive denominator, of O(S log(S+1)+1) bits apiece for S atoms, by the independent-column and invertible-minor argument. Verify input and constant nodes by summing their squared differences from the prescribed value. Process the remaining gates topologically. For a gate with verified parents A,B, let f(A,B) denote the multilinear interpolation of its binary Boolean table, of degree at most two. On every x, A and B equal their actual Boolean values, so f²=f pointwise. Require the exact sum over x of

    v_i² − 2 v_i f(A,B) + f(A,B)

to vanish. This is the sum of (v_i−f(A,B))², even if the guessed v_i is a malformed real gate reading. It is zero exactly when the reading is correct everywhere. Reject a failed check before using the gate as a parent. At a NAND, the equivalent expression is (v_i−1)²+2(v_i−1)AB+AB. After clearing the positive denominator cubed, only integer arithmetic and at most three atom factors are needed. Finally check that the sum of the certified output is zero.

## Compression over the integers

Let V be the Boolean matrix of the S atom columns evaluated on all raw assignments, and suppose Vc=f with c integral and target f Boolean. There exists such an integer vector on the same supplied columns with O(S log(S+1)+1) bits per entry. If the rank is zero, take all coefficients zero. Otherwise choose r independent columns and r rows on which their square minor U is invertible. Put Δ=det U and D=|Δ|. For every nonbasis column v, multiplying its restricted values by adj(U) supplies an integer vector a such that Δv=V_basis a. The identity extends to every row since the restriction is injective on the rational column span.

Reduce each nonbasis coefficient modulo D into [0,D−1], transferring the removed multiple of Dv to the basis with the appropriate sign of Δ. This retains integral coefficients and all target values. The final basis coefficients solve Uc_basis=b on the selected rows, with |b_i|≤1+S(D−1). Hadamard gives D≤r^(r/2). Each Cramer numerator has magnitude at most r^(r/2)(1+S(D−1)), by bounding the norm of the one replaced column separately. These bounds give the claimed bit lengths. No exponential evaluation matrix is constructed by the guessing machine. Nonbasis columns need not disappear.

## Signed integer coefficients

Use NAND, input and constant gates; converting a bounded-fanin binary Boolean gate into this basis uses only a fixed number of gates and does not increase the number of primary inputs. With signed integer coefficients every projected reading v_i is integral, even on a malformed guess. Require the sum of v_i(v_i−1) over all x to vanish. Its summands are nonnegative integers and vanish only at zero and one. Check input and constant nodes by squared differences. For each NAND with certified parents A,B and now Boolean v_i, put H=1−v_i. Require

    sum H(1−A)=0,     sum H(1−B)=0,     sum H=sum AB,

where each sum is over all live assignments. The first two checks put the support of H inside the joint one-set of A,B. The last matches the two cardinalities, hence H=AB pointwise. The output-zero test now uses a Boolean certified output. No expanded term has more than two atom factors.

## Clock

For clarity, p is three for real weights and two for integer weights. The padded universal gate evaluator has raw length M=nR+O((n+s+1)log(n+s+2)), with R=(n+s+2)^2, where s includes inputs and the guessed test circuit uses n primary bits. Thus M/(nR) tends uniformly to one. Fix γ<δ<1/p and C sufficiently large that γ+1/C<δ. Before specializing any header or gate index, mark the full bank footprint in each atom, including all nominated raw outputs and auxiliary identities, together with all names occurring more than L=ceil(CK(M)) times directly in the tree. This gives at most (γ+1/C)M marked names per atom. A uniform independent slot in each block selects at most (γ+1/C)M/R marked positions in expectation. The probability of exceeding δn is exp(−Ω(n)) by bounded independent concentration. When s≤O(2^(εn)) a union bound over M^d atoms works for sufficiently small fixed ε. Guess one good slot map and verify its per-atom bounds. All implemented thresholds may be fixed rationals with slack. No uniform representation of arbitrary evaluation circuits is assumed: the contrary hypothesis for all NP languages supplies an exact representation of the fixed P evaluator on full raw inputs, as in the linked proof.

Each distributed verification term branches on at most pδn selected marked coordinates in the union for its own factors. All banks then become constant after pinning C, the relevant gate indices and the other slots. Each atom has at most nL surviving direct raw leaves. Folding constant and unary subtrees yields O(nL+n) gates for the product. A unique-extension, fixed-density 3CNF encoding retains all unbranched inputs, including those absent from its output cone. Its exact count costs 2^o(n) bit operations and polynomial space in the resulting CNF size, since L=O((log(n+2))^(1/3)). The manuscript gives the underlying fixed-density clock V^O(1)·2^O(V/√log V) at declared variable count V, with polynomial space; small instances can be counted directly. Guesses, coefficient arithmetic, all gate checks and expanded term counts are polynomial in M with exponent fixed by d,k. Thus the overall time is 2^(pδn+O_(d,k,γ)(εn)+o(n)). Since pδ<1, choose ε small enough for O(2^((1−ε)n)) and the projection union bound. Under the contrary hypothesis every sufficiently large unsatisfiable circuit instance has an accepting verifier guess; every accepting guess certifies actual unsatisfiability. Failed guesses output unknown, with a separate branch producing no only on finding a witness of satisfiability. This supplies the required gap-UNSAT protocol for ordinary circuits. Murray and Williams then give for every fixed exponent j an NP language outside size N^j at infinitely many lengths. The contrary hypothesis supplies a single fixed polynomial upper exponent for all NP languages, by evaluating the explicit bounded bank and tree descriptions and summing polynomial-bit coefficients. Taking j larger gives the contradiction in each case. The argument relies on the stated October counting theorem.
