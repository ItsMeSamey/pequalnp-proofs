# Searching origins on a full active saturated scope

In a rank-d essential scope a centered full-tuple Horn representation sends each forbidden tuple to the all-ones point or one of its d neighbors. Thus more than d+1 distinct projected forbidden patterns rule out that representation for all origins. Exactly d+1 distinct patterns force the center itself to belong to the set. These conclusions hold after deduplication in essential coordinates.

The [sparse wrapper](ring_image_saturated_origin.py) computes the sum A of every essential normal space on the surviving affine hull. If a saturated scope W has dimension equal to dim A then W=A. For each of its d+1 listed patterns, solve the equations for a hull point inducing those evaluations on W, and run the [exact fixed-origin solver](ring-image-origin-horn.md). The other groups' evaluation offsets are fixed by the restriction to A. The fixed-origin kernel test is consequently identical for all lifts in the same fiber. The procedure examines every possible full-tuple Horn center up to this equivalence, including all adapted bases. Once it finds a valid chart the ensuing SAT decision is exact. Exhaustion gives unknown for SAT and certifies absence of this particular representation. Without a full-rank saturated scope, the implementation retains the canonical-origin fallback. The number of trials is polynomial in explicit input length, and each trial returns sparse original witnesses.

More generally one could enumerate sets of at most k saturated scopes and choose one of their listed patterns independently per scope. If their span has codimension h in A, each compatible choice extends to at most 2^h evaluations on A. For fixed k and logarithmic h this is a polynomial candidate search. The implementation covers the single full-active-rank case.

The [control](check_ring_saturated_origin.py) translates the five-point shear fixture by 1111. Its full-rank forbidden patterns exclude zero, ruling out the default origin, while the translated original center induces a valid adapted chart.
