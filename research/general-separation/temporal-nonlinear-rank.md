# Nonlinear temporal rank one permits exact polynomial-time counting; rank two contains 3SAT

Fix a topological order of a binary circuit. Express each affine wire as a binary affine form in the raw n-bit input x and formal symbols for earlier nonaffine output bits. Every nonaffine gate has the form z_i=(a_i(x)+u_i z_<i)(b_i(x)+v_i z_<i)+c_i(x)+w_i z_<i. Here u_i,v_i,w_i are fixed binary rows on prior symbols and a_i,b_i,c_i are raw affine forms; a binary truth table supplies these by its multilinear expansion. Absorb any affine output gates into a final affine readout. At cut i let H_i be the span of the restrictions to the first i symbols of all operand rows belonging to gates strictly after i, and of the final readout row. Use all three rows per gate, including that of its linear term. All rows, spaces and their ranks can be computed in polynomial time for the specified order and representation.

If every H_i has dimension at most one, choose its nonzero basis row h_i or use the zero row when empty. Let s_i=h_i z_<=i, with s_0=0. All three previous-history terms in gate i are scalar multiples u,v,w of s_(i-1). Moreover h_i restricted to the first i-1 symbols belongs to H_(i-1), so s_i=t s_(i-1)+d z_i for known scalar bits t,d. On Boolean inputs this gives s_i=A_i(x)s_(i-1)+B_i(x), with
  A_i=t+d(a_i v+b_i u+uv+w),  B_i=d(a_i b_i+c_i).
The first coefficient is affine and the second quadratic. The output is a raw affine bit plus a scalar multiple of s_m.

Partition raw inputs by the last index j with A_j(x)=0, including the separate case of no such index. The j-th case requires A_j=0 and every later A_i=1, all affine constraints. There s_m=sum_{i>=j} B_i. For no zero sum all B_i. These m+1 domains are disjoint. In each, Gaussian elimination parametrizes its affine solution space; the remaining acceptance condition is a single quadratic Boolean polynomial. Its exact solution count is polynomial-time computable: for a nonzero cross-term uv, write the polynomial as uv+uL+vM+Q on that pair and remaining variables, with affine L,M. The sum of signs over u,v is 2 times the sign of Q+LM. Iterate until the residual is affine, whose signed sum is zero or the signed domain size. Recover the number of ones from this sum, and handle inconsistent/zero-dimensional domains directly. Raw prefix pins are further affine conditions and give an actual witness by self-restriction. No randomization, oracle, bounded raw read load or small gate depth is required.

For a 3CNF of c>=1 nonempty clauses of width at most three, sequentially compute its literal ORs, then AND the current clause with the prefix conjunction. Each literal is a raw affine readout. Each clause uses at most two binary OR gates, and all prefix updates together at most c-1 AND gates (use the first clause directly). Signed literals may be absorbed into incident gate truth tables, or implemented with at most n shared affine NOT gates. At each cut all future operand rows on past nonlinear outputs involve only the latest completed prefix and, inside a clause, the current partial clause. Therefore their span has rank at most two. After the AND update only the new prefix is used. This is a parsimonious reduction on the same n raw inputs because all gate values are deterministic. Empty formulas or clauses have trivial constant-circuit handling. Thus decision on circuits given with an order of nonlinear temporal rank at most two is NP-complete, and exact counting is #P-complete. This leaves exponential-time improvements open. Rank-one recognition here tests a supplied order; no optimal-order recognizer is used.
