# Near-isometric Reed–Muller encoding against local exterior-preserving compression

Fix B>0 and unrestricted Boolean fanin-two gates including XOR. A q-input circuit of at most B q log2 q gates has a deterministic polynomial-time SAT-equivalent encoding within B N log2 N gates, N=q+o(q/sqrt(log q)). Every original witness lifts and each encoded witness decodes. On every nonempty encoded instance and every physical-coordinate set S of size <D, all 2^|S| cofactors have nonempty pairwise disjoint exterior supports, where D eventually exceeds every fixed power of log N. Thus even an existential local simplification keeping only actual block settings must keep every setting if it must preserve the exact union for all exteriors. Distinct rows also forbid sharing a nonempty rectangle contained in the accepting support across rows. All statements are on the original physical cube.

Write lambda=floor(log2 q), k=floor(cuberoot(lambda)), b=2^k, r=floor(k/4), R=ceil(3k/4). Work at sufficiently large q, with finite earlier inputs handled separately. Fix an integer C>10+4/B. Let N=b ceil(q(1+Ck/lambda)/b). Every b-bit block is the evaluation table of a unique multilinear polynomial on k bits. Its coefficient transform uses kb/2 XOR gates via one butterfly update per upper entry per bit. Force coefficients of degree >R to zero. Across blocks feed q distinct coefficients of degrees r+1 through R to the original circuit, leaving all other unforced coefficients free. Independent coefficient slots give the claimed witness lift by choosing the remaining coefficients zero. A Bin(k,1/2) tail bound gives missing slot fraction <=2 exp(-k/8)=o(k/lambda), ensuring q slots. Rounding costs <b=o(qk/lambda), so N-q=Theta_B(q/(log q)^(2/3)). Overhead Nk/2+O(N) is covered by B(N-q)lambda.

The ambient code is the direct sum of evaluation codes RM(R,k). Every logical fiber includes a translate of the direct sum K of RM(r,k). RM(j,k) has distance 2^(k-j): induction on the last variable using (f,f+g), with deg g<=j-1, gives the lower bound, attained by a degree-j monomial. Its dual is RM(k-j-1,k) since products have degree <k, hence even parity, and the complementary binomial dimension counts add to b. Direct sums take the minimum block distance. For D=min(2^(k-R),2^(r+1)), every restriction to fewer than D coordinates of K is onto, since a failed projection gives a short nonzero dual word. Thus a single logical witness already permits every setting of S. No two such settings can share an exterior, by the ambient distance. D>=2^floor(k/4).

A uniform algorithm with clock poly(N) 2^{N-Omega(N/sqrt(log N))} on these encoded circuits would yield the same clock measured in q on arbitrary B q log2 q circuits. Apply the compiler and use N-q=o(q/sqrt(log q)); the reverse implication is subclass inclusion. This is an equivalence of algorithmic hypotheses, not an achieved speedup. Decoding and then enumerating q bits only saves o(N/sqrt(log N)) in the exponent. The October bound after ordinary Tseitin encoding still acts on N plus the number of gates.
