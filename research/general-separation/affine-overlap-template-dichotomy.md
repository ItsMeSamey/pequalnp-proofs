# Fixed Boolean templates on affine forms

Let R be a fixed relation on GF(2)^k. An instance gives a conjunction of tests R(A_i x+b_i) on one shared physical assignment; the matrices and offsets are explicit. If R is empty or an affine flat, feasibility and search are deterministic polynomial time by binary elimination (empty conjunction accepted). Otherwise the language is NP-complete, even with at most four physical variables per affine form. To see this, a nonempty relation closed under ternary addition is affine: translate by any member to obtain an addition-closed subset containing zero. Thus choose distinct u,v,w in R with t=u+v+w absent. The affine map (a,b) -> t+a(u+t)+b(v+t) maps 00 to t, 10 to u, 01 to v and 11 to w, simulating the two-input OR exactly, with no new existential choices. On a graph require this OR on each two-bit vertex color and on every bitwise difference across an edge. This is precisely proper three-coloring; no local model is chosen independently of the global bits. Each coordinate of the composite affine map uses up to four original bits. Membership in NP is immediate.

For homogeneous inputs b_i=0, if zero belongs to R, the all-zero witness works on every instance. If R is nonempty and zero is absent, choose r in R, introduce a single common bit z, and pin it to one by the homogeneous test R(zr). Replace each constant vector by its multiple of z, giving the preceding classification with at most five physical variables per row. For R empty the answer just checks whether the conjunction is empty. This is an elementary template classification, with no novelty claim relative to the general Boolean CSP dichotomy.

For succinct input-dependent F_i promised to define affine flats in their local cubes (possibly empty), support extraction has a different access cost. Using a local SAT decision oracle obtain one accepted point by self-restriction or declare the factor empty. Maintain a basis of discovered differences from this base point. For a basis of annihilating linear functionals of that span, ask whether a local witness violates the corresponding equality. If yes obtain it by ordinary self-restriction and increase the rank; if none do then the hull is exact. At most O((m_i+1)^2) local SAT calls suffice since only successful outside queries require witness extraction. A test of one affine equality and explicit prefix pins costs O(m_i+1) extra gates per call. Under the affine promise the discovered hull equals the full local relation, and joint pulls back to a single linear system. Unconditional polynomial compatibility when local flats are explicitly given must be distinguished from support extraction from opaque circuits. For the latter access model the two-point method in affine-overlap-two-versus-three.md uses only parity calls and up to two extra gates.
