# Fixed-origin Horn charts for complete forbidden tuples

The [sparse solver](ring_image_origin_horn.py) applies the previous [allowed-hull and translation-period reduction](ring-image-period-chart.md). Fix an original point p in that hull. By default solve the hull equations by Gaussian elimination with free bits zero; callers can instead give the true original IDs of p. IDs outside the formula do not affect its projected coordinates. A supplied point outside the surviving nonempty hull returns unknown. An empty hull gives zero directly.

On a group's essential normal space W each projected forbidden tuple is a linear functional b giving the prohibited bit on each axis in W. Let d=b+evaluation at p, also a functional on W. For global chart row a use Boolean coordinate y_a=1+a·(x+p), so p has all coordinates true. The clause forbidding this tuple has a positive literal precisely at the chosen axes with d(a)=1. If d is zero the clause is all negative. Otherwise ker(d) is a hyperplane in W. Given an adapted basis of W, at most one positive literal is equivalent to ker(d) being spanned by the chosen axes lying in it. Indeed a nonzero functional has at least one nonzero basis evaluation, and its kernel has dimension dim(W)−1. Thus an ambient simultaneous basis for all W, all nontrivial tuple kernels, and the surviving free-coordinate space exists if and only if this fixed-point convention can make every full projected tuple clause Horn. The [common basis algorithm](arbitrary-rank-common-chart-referee.md) searches this class exactly. The number of added spaces is bounded by the total explicitly projected tuple count. A minus sign on negative literals in the implication form corresponds to putting the associated axes in the rule body.

Solve the resulting Horn implications from the all-false assignment, lift through the affine coordinate shift and saved hull equations, and check every signed source clause. This costs polynomial bit time in the explicit source plus sparse origin encoding. The physical endpoint runs exact ordered recovery first. No search over origins or over logical simplifications of a group's full tuple CNF is asserted. Semantic Horn recognition for a chosen chart remains a distinct backend.

In the [shear control](check_ring_origin_horn.py) the four-variable scope forbids masks 0,1,2,7, low coordinates first, and the last two coordinates forbid 00. The affine hull stays full and all periods vanish. With p=0, one chart uses l1+h1, l2+h1, h1, h2 before complementing all bits. The large tuples map to the all-ones center and three distinct neighbors. Block-diagonal changes acting on the original low and high pairs separately cannot do this: large-scope forbidden differences 1 and 2 force independent singleton low images, whereas 7 then has both low bits nonzero. This case requires the freedom to mix vectors having comparable membership signatures.
