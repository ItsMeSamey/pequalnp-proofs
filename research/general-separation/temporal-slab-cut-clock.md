# Exact temporal cuts with rank-one interiors
Use H_i and the affine expansion of temporal-nonlinear-rank.md. Partition indices into p intervals (a,b]. For each interval take its gate operands and a basis of H_b as terminal readouts. At each i in [a,b] restrict subsequent interval operand rows and all terminal readouts to local coordinates a+1,...,i. Require joint rank <=1, including at i=b. Test all such ranks by Gaussian elimination; every singleton interval is valid.

Extend each boundary basis vector to length m by zeros after its boundary, for all interval ends including the final H_m. Let R be their joint rank. Guess values for a single basis of this joint span at cost 2^R. The past portion on positions at most a of each interval operand or terminal readout belongs to H_a by restriction of the original future rows. Its contribution is therefore fixed by the guessed start summary. Local raw affine terms still use the same n physical coordinates x.

Use the rank-one recurrence on each interval with the fixed start history. The at most b-a+1 disjoint latest-zero cases give affine domains in x on which the effective local terminal bit is quadratic. After subtracting the old-history constants all terminal H_b readouts have joint local rank <=1, so checking the prescribed end summary adds at most one quadratic equation or an immediate contradiction. The final output pin itself is affine in x once the last summary is guessed.

For each combination of reset cases, impose their affine restrictions together. Count the at most p ensuing quadratic checks on this single common flat. For t checks Q_j=0, Fourier inversion gives 2^-t times the sum of the 2^t signed quadratic Gauss sums for all linear combinations of the Q_j. Each raw evaluation has unique boundary and reset choices. Conversely, the consistency equations imply gate-by-gate by interval induction that each guessed summary is genuine. Counting and self-restriction return exact physical multiplicities and one physical witness.

The clock is O*(2^{R+p} product_j(length_j+1)). For a polynomial partition finder first upper-bound R by the sum of the ending cut ranks: test all intervals and minimize the product of integer factors 2^{dim H_b+1}(b-a+1) by dynamic programming on endpoints. Product bit lengths are polynomial. Then rank-compress the selected boundaries. This does not optimize the nonadditive rank globally. Savings require this actual cost to beat 2^n; there is no universal cost estimate at B n log n gates.
