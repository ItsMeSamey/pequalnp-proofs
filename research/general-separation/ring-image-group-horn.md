# Common Horn renaming after equal-scope semantic reduction

The [backend](ring_image_group_horn.py) offers three entry points. solve_tuples consumes (scope, forbidden_masks) records, where scope[j] is the positive integer label for bit j of a nonnegative integer mask and the least significant bit is bit zero. Equal scopes merge even if coordinates arrive in different orders. solve_sparse consumes arbitrary signed CNF clauses over sparse positive labels and takes an optional labels list for unused variables. It omits tautologies when constructing forbidden tuples and validates a positive result against the full original clauses. decide consumes only the four ordered physical fields, recognizes the selector-free image, and returns off_image or the source status; its witness uses original variable order. The reusable prime_kernel consumes tuple records in any supplied Boolean coordinate system; it discovers no coordinate change.

For each distinct scope U with explicit forbidden set S, a non-tautological clause on U is an implicate of the local allowed relation precisely when its falsifying subcube lies in S. Deleting a literal strictly enlarges that subcube. Every (nonempty) subcube has unique least and greatest masks l,h in the coordinatewise order and is their entire Boolean interval. Its cardinality is two to the number of bits in h XOR l. Enumerate comparable pairs of elements of S including equal pairs. Count listed masks in their interval and compare with this cardinality. The retained intervals are exactly all forbidden subcubes. Keep those maximal under inclusion. Their complementary clauses are precisely all non-tautological prime implicates on U; together they define the relation since even a singleton forbidden tuple belongs to some maximal cube. An empty scope with forbidden mask zero yields the empty clause. An empty forbidden set contributes no clauses.

A Horn-definable relation has only Horn non-tautological prime implicates: if a prime clause had distinct positive literals x and y, minimality supplies an allowed assignment falsifying the clause with x deleted and another falsifying the clause with y deleted. Their coordinatewise AND falsifies the entire clause, contradicting closure of Horn models under AND. Conversely, if the computed prime clauses are Horn, they already give a Horn definition. This argument also works after any coordinate complementation. Let f_i mean flip i. A positive original literal i becomes negative exactly when f_i holds; a negative original literal −i becomes negative exactly when ¬f_i holds. For every two literals a,b of a prime clause add the binary disjunction (a OR b) interpreted on flip bits. It forbids exactly their simultaneous positivity in the transformed clause. Strongly connected components give a satisfying flip assignment or prove none exists. Thus recognition is exact for a single common renaming making **each equal-scope relation** Horn in the supplied coordinates.

On recognition, forward-chain from the empty true set in renamed coordinates. A rule fires if all its negative-body variables are true. If its head is missing, the source is unsatisfiable; otherwise set its head true. Every forced bit belongs to every model, and the final fixed point satisfies all clauses if no headless rule fires. Invert the flips, check all original tuple records and, on the signed source path, all original clause rows. Return zero or nonzero with witness. A failed common renaming yields unknown, irrespective of satisfiability.

As a strict distinction, list the four forbidden tuples with x=0 and arbitrary y,z on the same scope. The four corresponding raw clauses cannot be simultaneously renamed Horn because all four y,z polarity pairs appear. The maximal forbidden cube recovers the single positive unit x. Conversely impose even parity on three bits as one group and pin all three to zero on singleton scopes. The whole relation is a Horn singleton. The parity group admits no Horn renaming: translations of parity are its two parity classes; in the even class 110 and 101 meet at excluded 100, while in the odd class 100 and 010 meet at excluded 000.

With q distinct forbidden tuples on one scope of width w there are at most q² endpoint pairs, O(q³) interval membership tests and O(q⁴) interval comparisons. Masks need O(w) bits including interval cardinalities. At most q² local prime clauses are emitted, each of width at most w, giving O(q²w²) candidate binary renaming constraints. SCC and repeated Horn-rule scans take polynomial bit time and space in the explicit data. No pass enumerates all assignments to a large scope or indexes an array by the largest sparse label. Ordered-image recovery has its separate polynomial cost. This is a source decision on the recognized image; it computes no model counts or physical residues.
