# What can be computed from a positive read-twice CNF?

The same small Boolean formula can have a hard formal multilinear extension and easy evaluation at every point of a finite field. Here ML denotes the unique multilinear representative of a Boolean function.

| Problem | Result |
|---|---|
| Construct full ML for a signed CNF of width at most two and read at most two | Linear arithmetic size over every field, using paths and cycles. |
| Construct full ML for a uniform family of positive width-three CNFs with exactly two occurrences per variable | A variable-or-constant projection recovers the Hamiltonian-cycle polynomial. The projection is over the base field if it contains more than three elements. At the two exceptional base fields a quadratic extension suffices. |
| Extract the lowest nonzero ML component of an explicit cubic bipartite family | A perfect-matching polynomial at degree M projects to the dense permanent. Its degree-component border size is superpolynomial outside characteristic two. The same component is a determinant in characteristic two. |
| Evaluate full ML on a fixed field of size two or three | Deterministic polynomial bit time. Ternary points reduce to quadratic sign sums, even with unrestricted positive clause width. |
| Decide if a full ML evaluation is nonzero over any larger fixed finite field of characteristic p | Mod_p P-complete. One calibrated weighted point yields the source accepting count modulo p. Every source formula emitted by the reduction has an immediate all-ones Boolean witness. |

The projection uses O(N³) raw variables and clauses for Hamiltonian-cycle order N, with O(N³ log N) bits sufficient for an indexed clause list. The topology is independent of the field. A small exact circuit for the full extension over a smaller field extends by scalar inclusion to the field used for the projection. Consequently the paper's eventual exact circuit lower bound exp(Ω((log N/log log N)²)) applies to this explicit family over every fixed field. The quantitative border statement for Hamiltonian cycle is a paper theorem. The named Lean endpoint establishes eventual superpolynomial exact size. [November paper](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e2abf66c3db7aa31438d3010bc7956f/preprints/Circuit-lower-bounds-for-Hamiltonian-cycle-and-the-permanent-November-24-2026/build/intro.tex) · [Lean endpoint](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e2abf66c3db7aa31438d3010bc7956f/lean/OAI/Computability/VPVNP/Separation.lean)

Hrubeš previously proved all-field multilinearization hardness for polynomial-time predicates and monotone 2-CNFs with unrestricted read counts, assuming VP differs from VNP. His characteristic-two results include edge-cover generating polynomials. Such generating polynomials attach a monomial to each accepted support. Canonical multilinear interpolation additionally weights the zero bits of an assignment. Arvind, Joglekar and Srinivasan previously identified the permanent as a coefficientwise product of two small monotone multilinear formulas. [Hrubeš](https://users.math.cas.cz/~hrubes/PDFs/Mult.pdf) · [Arvind, Joglekar and Srinivasan](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2009.2304) · [Monotone decisions and products](monotone-decisions-and-folded-products.md)

The numerical evaluator takes zero-based raw variables and ternary point coordinates. The signed-CNF frontend takes one-based source literals and emits positive clauses with weights. Its executable supports fixed primes at least five and rational numbers. Nonprime finite fields are covered by the theorem. A supplied width-two formula also permits direct arithmetic construction, while access only to its pinned ternary value oracle may require exponentially many probes for an extension-field evaluation.
