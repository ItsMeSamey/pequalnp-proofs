# How many distinct clause failures can have rank k?

Normalize clauses by removing repeated literals and ignoring tautologies. The nonzero failure indicator of a clause is the characteristic vector of its forbidden coordinate face on the full Boolean cube, over F₂. In each coordinate its tensor factor is (1,0), (0,1) or (1,1), according as it requires zero, requires one or omits that coordinate. This includes the empty clause and its constant-one failure indicator. Repeated identical indicators count once.

Let W be any k-dimensional subspace of the full truth-vector space on n bits. It contains at most k^p such vectors, where p=log₂3. Thus it suffices to take k as the binary rank of the distinct failure vectors of the given formula. The coordinate description also gives a coding interpretation: the matrix with all 3^n faces as columns is the tensor power of a generator of the binary [3,2,2] code. It has full row rank 2^n. The maximum number of columns lying in a k-dimensional subspace is 3^n minus the minimum support size of a (2^n−k)-dimensional subcode, by taking the annihilator of the subspace (zero-dimensional support is zero). Generalized Hamming weights and the product-code formulation predate this argument. [Wei, Generalized Hamming weights for linear codes](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/133259/) · [Wei and Yang, On the generalized Hamming weights of product codes](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tit/WeiY93.html).

**Induction.** Rank zero contains no nonzero faces. For n=0 the sole face is the single entry one. For n>0 and each of the three nonzero binary vectors e, let U_e consist of v for which e⊗v belongs to W. Write r_e for its dimension. Any two different e are independent, giving r_e+r_f≤k for e≠f. The occupied first-factor strata are disjoint, and induction bounds their total by the sum of r_e^p. If their largest dimension a≤k/2 the sum is at most 3(k/2)^p=k^p. Otherwise it is at most a^p+2(k−a)^p. This is strictly convex on [k/2,k] and takes value k^p at both ends, proving the bound.

For k>0, equality requires a dimension pattern (k,0,0) or (k/2,k/2,k/2), in either order, and equality in every nonempty recursive stratum. In the former case W=e⊗U_e. In the latter case two independent first factors span W as their direct sum with the respective U spaces. The third U is their intersection, hence all three coincide by dimension. Thus W is the whole first factor tensored with one common U. Induction characterizes equality precisely: W is a tensor product with each coordinate factor either the full binary plane or one of its lines. For d full factors it has k=2^d and exactly 3^d faces. For a listed collection attaining k^p, it must also contain every face of that W. These assertions concern the original coordinate order, including coordinates globally omitted by all clauses.

If every listed clause has width at most w, select k actual failure vectors as a basis. They depend collectively on at most wk variables. Every linear combination ignores the remaining variables, whereas every fixed coordinate of a nonempty face is essential to its indicator. Thus the support union of the original normalized clauses also has size at most wk. Vanishing of the basis indicators at an assignment is equivalent to vanishing of them all. This is a size comparison; the proof does not establish a fast way to compute rank from a succinct representation.

**Satisfiable family surviving subsumption.** Set n=3ℓ and w=2ℓ≥6. On each w-subset forbid every local pattern of even weight between 2 and w−2 by its signed width-w clause. All rows are distinct and equally wide. Subsumption removes none, and two rows on a common scope never differ in exactly one polarity, so complementary one-bit pair fusion removes none. Both signs occur at every coordinate. The clause count is binom(n,w)(2^{w−1}−2)=Θ(3^n/√n) by Stirling's formula. The rank k≤2^n, hence m≥Ω(k^p/√n); conversely the bound implies log k=Θ(n), giving m≥Ω(k^p/√log k). The ratio of listed clauses to basis rows grows exponentially in n and is at most k^(p−1).

The feasible assignments are exactly the two radius-one balls about zero and one. For a global weight s between 2 and n−2, the achievable local weights range from max(0,w−n+s) to min(w,s). After intersecting with [2,w−2] the interval is nonempty and contains an even weight: its endpoints either include 2 or w−2, both even, or they differ by n−w>0. Every such assignment violates a clause. The four remaining global weight levels avoid all forbidden patterns. On any fixed scope a Horn renaming allows at most w+1 distinct clauses using its whole scope, whereas 2^{w−1}−2>w+1 here. Semantic 2-CNF on the original variables also fails: e₁,e₂ and the all-ones assignment satisfy, and their coordinatewise majority e₁+e₂ does not. Every binary clause is majority-closed since falsity of both literals in a majority forces some voter to falsify both. The 2n+2 models are explicit; no decision-time lower bound is asserted.
