# Same-budget control for syntax-aware low-degree parity certificates

Fix 0<alpha<1 and B>0. A q-input bounded-fanin-two circuit F of size s<=B q log2 q can be compiled uniformly to an equivalent SAT instance D with N=mq primary bits and at most B N log2 N gates for all large q, for a constant m depending on alpha. Every accepted word decodes to an actual witness. Every nonempty encoded support is aperiodic under translations. Yet for every Boolean mask H of multilinear GF(2) degree <=alpha N the parity of D AND H is zero, including masks depending on syntax or previous answers.

Choose m=4+2r>5/(1-alpha), r>=1. On four bits use U0={0,1,2,4}, U1={3,5,6,8} with binary indices. Each four-set has nonzero total XOR (7 and 8), whereas a nonzero period pairs four points into two pairs of the same difference, giving total XOR zero. The union has seven points below 8 and only the single point 8 above: a high-bit shift swaps unequal levels and a nonzero low-bit shift moves that unique point. On 2r other bits let Q=sum z_(2i-1) z_(2i) over GF(2). Each nonzero shift gives a nonconstant derivative by its nondegenerate polar form, hence the support of Q admits no nonzero period. Let S_b=U_b times the support of Q. Both fibers and their union have trivial period by projection. Each indicator of U_b has degree <=3 since its degree-four coefficient equals its even truth-table parity. Thus each indicator of S_b has degree <=5.

Require every block to lie in S0 union S1 and feed each decoded symbol to F. This costs s+O_r(q) gates, within the claimed budget because m>1 and B is fixed. Any original witness has an encoding. When the support is nonempty, every block projection equals S0, S1 or their union, so every period of the full support vanishes in each block. D equals the GF(2) sum over accepted x of the products of the block fiber indicators (the corresponding supports are disjoint). Its degree is <=5q. The algorithm need not expand this polynomial. Since 5q+alpha N<N, the full-degree squarefree monomial in D H has coefficient zero, equal to parity of the truth table. Invertible affine reparametrizations preserve degree. Single-output bounded-fanin-two masks of fewer than N-5q-1 gates have degree below N-5q since their gate cone depends on at most one more input than its number of gates.

The encoded SAT problem remains NP-hard even with O(N) gates: compile a 3CNF using linearly many gates in the written size and pad the physical input count to that scale before encoding. Padding bits introduce no period in the nonempty encoded support. This rules out positivity certified by an odd degree-bounded full-cube mask on the encoded instance. It does not rule out syntax-based decisions, higher-degree masks, decoding the circuit first, or moving to a different smaller-dimensional witness domain.
