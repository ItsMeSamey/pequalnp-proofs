# Recovering binary affine coordinates from the original signed CNF

The [solver](ring_image_coordinate_2sat.py) takes either an explicit signed source via solve_source or the four raw selector-free fields via solve. The latter first invokes [exact ordered recovery](read-two-ring-image.md) and reports off_image on rejection. On its recognized domain it decides satisfiability and lifts a satisfying assignment in original variable order. An unsupported representation returns unknown.

Discard tautologies and repeated literals within clauses. Group the remaining clauses by identical absolute variable scope. Each clause forbids one explicit bit tuple on that scope. Deduplicate these tuples. Subtract one listed anchor and compute the rank r of the differences. Their affine hull has exactly 2^r points, so equality with the number of distinct listed tuples proves that the list is exactly this affine flat. A full flat prohibits all assignments and yields zero. For all other groups require flatness and codimension one or two. Compute an orthogonal basis to the differences by taking each free bit in turn and solving the homogeneous row system; its scalar products with the anchor give the prohibited values. Embed these normal vectors by zeros into the original arity. The method need not enumerate missing tuples or ambient bases.

A common chart means an independent family of global linear forms such that every normal line or plane is spanned by a subset. Duplicated normal spaces count once, including planes with different affine offsets. Any singleton space forces its nonzero axis. The shared line of any two different planes is forced as well, since intersections of coordinate spans are spanned by their common axes. A binary plane has exactly three nonzero vectors. Three forced vectors in a plane preclude a chart. Otherwise complete its forced vectors to a pair by taking arbitrary remaining vectors in that plane. Every extra vector so chosen belongs to exactly one of the distinct planes and is no forced singleton. Thus all choices give exactly K=number of forced lines plus the sum of (2−number forced in each plane) distinct candidate axes. Every choice spans the same aggregate normal space, namely the sum of all the input spaces. The aggregate rank is fixed, so independence is equivalent to rank K for any one choice. Conversely a feasible chart can discard every unused axis and then attains exactly K. This proves completeness of coordinate_axes on the specified collection. Independent axes need not span the whole assignment space.

Each forbidden affine flat specifies one joint setting on the one or two chosen axes in its space. For the third nonzero vector of a plane its prohibited bit is the xor of the other two prescribed bits. Exclude the joint setting by a unary or binary clause. Solve by the two implication graphs' strongly connected components and lift the chosen axis values by binary Gaussian elimination. Independence guarantees a lift. An explicit check on every original clause protects the witness interface.

If the direct common-chart test fails, a further sound domain extension quotients out compelled unary constraints. A forbidden setting a·x=b forces a·x=1−b. Eliminate equations from each remaining forbidden conjunction; if inconsistent it is redundant, if empty after reduction it forbids every remaining assignment, and if of rank one it gives another compelled equation. Repeat until no equation is added. At most the original arity of independent equations can be added. Run the chart test again on residual normals in the coefficient space of the remaining free coordinates, and lift by solving chart and saved equations together. This extension can decide instances without an original chart. A nonflat or higher-codimension original group is outside this recognizer unless another group by itself already forbids all assignments.

For arity a and explicit total input length B, there are at most B groups and listed prohibited tuples. Every bit-vector operation, rank test, and implication-graph traversal has cost polynomial in a+B, as do the at most a+1 quotient passes. Polynomial cost includes writing the a-bit source witness even for unused variables. An empty conjunction returns any assignment, and an empty clause has the full flat on the empty scope. Uniform ordered recovery bounds a by the physical input length. This algorithm has no supplied chart, branching guard, truth-table scan of the ambient space or state cap.
