# Counting ordered-image models by the rational rank of missed demands

The [matching backend](ring_image_gram_demands.py) counts the source models and returns a witness when the count is positive. It first recovers the signed source from the four ordered physical fields. Its exact uncapped domain is the [recognized planar bipartite matching class](ordered-image-affine-demand-count.md). Failed recovery gives `off_image`, and a recovered source outside the recognized class can give `unknown`. On this class, the earlier solver already performs star restrictions, drops duplicate and subsumed demands, discovers the affine hits on actual perfect matchings, and retains r independent parity equations. The remaining u demands were previously handled by inclusion–exclusion over all u. [Matching and signed-count precedents](planar-affine-demand-prior.md)

Let Ω contain every perfect matching after the initial star restrictions, before imposing the r equations. For each remaining demand Dᵢ, put F[M,i]=1 if M misses Dᵢ and zero otherwise. The entry Gᵢⱼ of G=FᵀF is the exact integer count obtained by deleting Dᵢ∪Dⱼ alongside the initially blocked edges. These queries carry no character signs. The [signed determinant oracle](planar_character_oracle.py) calibrates the common term sign against the unrestricted planar determinant once. The resulting Gram entries count actual matchings with positive unit weight. Over the rationals, vᵀGv is a sum of squares and ker G=ker F. [Demand-function rank proof](pm-demand-function-ranks.md)

Rational symmetric Gaussian elimination keeps an original demand whenever its diagonal Schur pivot is positive. After pivots on a selected index set I, a candidate pivot equals det G[I∪{i}]/det G[I], using principal submatrices and initial denominator one. Positive semidefiniteness makes a zero pivot a zero residual vector. Thus the selected ρ demands form a column basis of F. If their miss indicators vanish on a matching, every miss indicator vanishes there by homogeneous linear dependence. The same pointwise implication holds on every restricted family used later for witness extraction.

For each subset of the ρ selected demands, delete its union and weight the result by its inclusion–exclusion sign. Impose the r binary equations by averaging the 2^r signed matching character sums with their target signs, retaining the common calibration. The resulting integer C counts exactly the original source models. When C>0, scan the m surviving edge names, deleting an edge whenever the filtered count remains positive. Every retained edge is necessary, so the final retained set is itself a feasible perfect matching. The code checks its assignment against the original clauses. On the ordered image it returns C and (−15)^(k−a−1) C modulo 3^k at precision k and recovered arity a.

At most u(u+1)/2 Gram queries, one shared calibration, and (1+m)2^(r+ρ) further queries bound the actual number of determinant evaluations by **u(u+1)/2 + 1 + (1+m)2^(r+ρ)**. Repeated Gram deletion sets share a cached value. The optional cap is cumulative across discovery of the Gram basis, counting and witness extraction. Exhaustion reports unknown, calls spent and the next required call index. Binary affine discovery uses polynomially many matching-hull computations outside this determinant cap. Gram entries are at most 2^m. Every reduced rational Schur entry is a ratio of bordered and principal integer minors with O(u(m+log(u+2))) bits by Hadamard. The O(u³) rational updates therefore take polynomial bit time. Filtered sums add at most r+ρ bits to the individual term bound. For explicit physical input length B the total bit time is 2^(r+ρ) poly(B), with polynomial space. At the signed-source entry the polynomial factor includes the numerical declared arity.

On the [connected subcubic balanced-demand family](rational-gram-connected-balanced-demands.md), for even h≥2 the actual solver parameters are r=0, u=binom(h,h/2), ρ=h, and C=h. Every individual hit is nonaffine, yet the rational Gram algorithm takes polynomial time in the explicit physical input length. The implemented compression uses rational rank throughout.
