# Exact affine demand recognition on perfect matchings

The [kernel](pm_demand_affine.py) accepts a simple bipartite graph with explicit ordered edges and a set of demanded edge indices. It handles arbitrary topology, disconnected graphs, unmatched vertices, empty demand and the empty matching. It returns empty if no perfect matching exists. Otherwise it returns an actual base matching M and independent edge masks spanning all matching differences. Every returned direction individually toggles M to an actual matching. Combinations of these directions span the affine hull and need not be matchings. If the demand-hit predicate admits an affine representation on the actual matchings, the return also includes mask a and bit c with hit_D(M')=c+⟨a,M'⟩ over GF(2) for every perfect matching M'. A nonaffine verdict means no such pair exists.

Obtain M by integral unit-capacity bipartite flow. Contract each pair matched by M, identified by its left vertex. A nonmatching edge from left u to the right mate of v gives directed arc uv. Every simple directed cycle lifts to an M-alternating cycle; toggling it gives a matching. Conversely the difference to any other perfect matching decomposes into alternating cycles. The arc-to-edge-mask map sends uv to its original nonmatching edge plus the matching edge at u. On directed cycles this gives exactly the toggle mask.

Generate a spanning list of actual simple cycles within each strongly connected component. Fix root r and directed paths P_v from r to v and Q_v from v to r, choosing shortest paths. Emit closed walks P_v Q_v for all vertices and P_u (uv) Q_v for all internal arcs. For a directed cycle C, sum its arc walks and its vertex walks over GF(2). Each path occurs twice and only the arcs of C survive. Split each closed walk into simple cycles by keeping its current simple path on a stack and popping the closed segment upon revisiting a vertex. Thus the emitted actual cycle masks span and Gaussian elimination can keep independent original masks. All paths and walks have linear length, with at most one walk per arc and one per vertex. This argument does not presume every undirected cycle is alternating.

Compute the hull of matchings avoiding the demand by deleting its edges. For each demanded edge separately, fix that edge and remove its endpoints, compute the residual hull, then add the fixed edge to its base only. The union of those forced fibers is exactly the hitting matchings, allowing overlaps. Impose on a,c one equation per nonempty fiber base with RHS zero for avoid and one for hit, and homogeneous equations on its directions. These are equivalent to the required identity on all actual matchings, regardless of whether the hitting union itself is affine. Gaussian elimination yields coefficients or certifies inconsistency. For coordinates relative to the returned base and direction list, the intercept is c+⟨a,M⟩ and the coefficient array is ⟨a,d_i⟩. Coefficients off the actual matching hull need not be unique.

At most |D|+2 matching hulls are computed. Each uses polynomially bounded integral flow, directed reachability/path generation, and binary elimination on at most the input edge count plus one bits per mask. All bit time and space are polynomial in the explicit input. A quick shore-size versus edge-count exit avoids allocating an enormous binary-coded shore when the edges cannot cover it. No counting oracle or planarity assumption enters classification.
