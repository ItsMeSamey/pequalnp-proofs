# Compatible simple cycles span all 200n syndrome characters

Keep F and the centered comparison graph of the [coverage proof](centered-cactus-orientation.md). This result strengthens its residual rank bound to equality. Work in the binary edge-chain space of the original signed multigraph, treating parallel edges as distinct indices. Let S be the span of incidence vectors of compatible simple cycles. Write r_j for the private-to-rail edge of row j. For every occurrence of source variable x in an A/B slot of j write P_{x,j} for the path of two edges from x through the private potential to the rail.

For each fixed x, all these paths have one class P_x modulo S. Indeed two distinct occurrences must have distinct private potentials, since the two A/B entries in a row are distinct. The sum of the paths is exactly the compatible four-cycle through their private potentials. Thus an A/B edge from the private potential of j to y has class r_j+P_y.

For the C edge e of row j ending at x, choose y distinct from x among its A/B ports. By the coverage proof choose distinct other binary conflict rows h,l containing x,y respectively in their A/B ports. The six-cycle consisting of e, P_{x,h}, P_{y,l}, and the A/B edge from y to the private potential of j is compatible. Hence modulo S the class of e is P_x+P_y+(r_j+P_y)=r_j+P_x. This argument includes a repeated last variable, whose C edge remains separate from its parallel A/B edge.

Select every r_j and one A/B occurrence per source variable. These edges form a spanning tree, since they attach every private potential to the rail and each source variable to one private potential. For every edge between row j and variable x its tree path has class r_j+P_x, by choosing the selected occurrence to represent P_x. Thus every fundamental cycle vanishes modulo S. Since S consists of cycles, it equals the entire cycle space. Its dimension is 3C−k=200n.

At initial zero free space, compatible cycles satisfy all dual shore-product equations, so every linear consequence returned by squarefree quadratic elimination evaluates to zero against their full independent span. Thus the solver gains no linear direction. The coverage proof distinguishes both columns of every candidate K2, precluding containment and common intersections. The first closure pass therefore leaves all 158n components and exactly 200n residual syndrome coordinates.
