# Exact ordered-image counts after binary span saturation

[count](ring_image_binary_saturation.py) accepts the four physical fields and an optional nonnegative max_round. It returns off_image when decoding fails. A live non-tautological clause wider than three dispatches directly to the [unsaturated complete counter](ring_image_binary_background.py). For width at most three the wrapper computes the [saturation](cnf_binary_span_saturation.py) once, then retains both the original source and the source augmented with the derived clauses.

For each batch, the miss indicator of a newly derived clause is in the binary span of residual miss indicators on assignments satisfying the current binary background. Every current source model has zero in every residual miss coordinate and therefore satisfies every derived clause. Adding the batch preserves exactly the complete set of assignments; induction applies to later batches. No auxiliary discovery bit enters the augmented source. Count the augmented source with all variables declared at their original arity, convert the factored count once, verify a positive witness against the original source and return the physical value (−15)^(k−a−1) times the full count modulo 3^k. An exact result from the competing original path already supplies these fields. Neither branch infers model count from decision alone.

Outer attempts use quotas 1,2,4,…, clipping the last quota to a supplied max_round (beginning at zero if max_round is zero). Try the augmented-source binary-background counter at this term budget. After a refusal, run the complete unsaturated physical wrapper with max_round set to the same quota. Accumulate every augmented-source term and the *full nested charge totals* returned by every invocation, including failed inner and outer attempts. Thus max_round is a per-attempt primitive ceiling, not a cumulative resource ceiling. Deadline refusal returns unknown with round_limit, attempts, last_quota, and cumulative charges. Saturation discovery and all other polynomial preprocessing are uncharged. The default is uncapped. A wide delegate reports its own original round diagnostics and zero saturated terms.

For the augmented formula, let t be the retained ternary rank, f the size of the actual feedback vertex set of the augmented binary background, and h its active arity. Evaluating an inclusion–exclusion term requires counting **all** completions under the augmented binary background, including when additional pins are supplied for witness recovery. The forest counter enumerates up to 2^f feedback assignments per term and sends exact two-state messages on each residual tree. Contradictory subsets consume one term and inconsistent feedback assignments are charged. Its numeric complete ceiling including a witness is min(2^h,(h+1)2^(t+f)); infeasibility of the binary background can be detected in polynomial time. Reducing the ternary rank may increase f, so a bound based only on t would be unjustified.

Let A be the larger of one and this saturated ceiling, and let C be the larger of one and a sufficient final primitive quota for the earlier complete unsaturated counter. On a quota Q, all its nested geometric attempts cost O(1+Q) charged units per primitive, not Q times their number. The first successful outer quota is at most twice min(A,C) under the uncapped default. Summing the cost of all outer attempts gives O(1+min(A,C)) primitive charges per type up to a constant factor. Repeated polynomial preprocessing, including nested decoding and rank estimation, contributes at most polynomial overhead per attempt; quota logarithms are polynomial in the physical input length. Hence bit time is polynomial in that length times the lesser of the saturated and earlier complete numeric bounds. Polynomial space is reused. The earlier exponents include raw miss rank, active arity, unsaturated binary rank plus its feedback size and, on eligible matching inputs, affine plus Gram rank. With a finite cap the procedure can stop before either complete bound is reached.
