# Rational Gram and binary rank of missed demands

Fix the finite set Ω of actual perfect matchings in the original bipartite planar root after preprocessing. Write g_i(M)=1 if M misses demand D_i and 0 otherwise. An empty Ω can be handled immediately. The integer matrix G_ij counts matchings avoiding D_i∪D_j, including diagonal entries. With F indexed by actual matchings and columns g_i, G=FᵀF. Over the rationals, ker G=ker F because a sum of squared rational entries vanishes exactly when all vanish. Choose independent original columns via their nonsingular Gram principal minor. A new column raises rank precisely when appending its row and column to this minor gives a nonzero determinant. Every miss function is a homogeneous rational linear combination of the chosen actual miss functions. Consequently hitting all demands means just hitting their basis. This remains true on any subset of Ω during witness extraction, even if basis rank drops there. Inclusion–exclusion over the chosen ρ demands costs 2^ρ matching counts, multiplied by a separate Fourier expansion when enforcing r parity syndromes. Testing Gram entries needs at most d(d+1)/2 ordinary avoidance counts. Each Gram entry is at most 2^m for m edges; a t-by-t integer minor has O(t(m+log(t+1))) bits by Hadamard. Fraction-free elimination with pivoting, or polynomially many bordered determinant tests, therefore costs polynomial bit work outside counting.

**Direct binary compression.** It can be cheaper to recognize homogeneous pointwise dependencies over GF(2). Maintain selected actual functions g_B and actual matchings M_1,...,M_k whose k-by-k evaluation matrix is invertible. For a new function f, solve uniquely for coefficients c matching f's values on the pivots. Put h=f+Σ c_i g_i modulo two. Compute its support size from (|Ω|−sum_M (−1)^h(M))/2. Expand the sign as the product of (1−2g) over f and the selected coefficient-one basis functions. Each product of miss indicators is simply avoidance of the union, so at most 2^(k+1) ordinary matching counts suffice. If the support is empty, f is dependent. Otherwise greedily delete an edge whenever a positive support count survives, refreshing the same parity predicate on the restricted population. A remaining support matching uses every remaining edge, since that edge was necessary when tested. It gives a new pivot. Subtracting the fitted combination from the new evaluation column leaves zeros on the old pivots and one on the new pivot; the enlarged matrix is invertible. This certifies the actual binary rank, not the rank of a reduced Gram matrix. All-zero testing is again preserved under restriction. A complete pivot walk uses O((d+mk)2^(k+1)) matching counts for final binary rank k, in addition to the final inclusion–exclusion/Fourier solver and any witness replay. One may cache the size of Ω at each edge-deletion step or count it explicitly. Intermediate signed sums fit O(m+d) bits because the sum of absolute coefficients is at most 3^d. This bound counts oracle calls; count-oracle internal calibrations must be accounted for according to the actual backend.

**Strict separation with ordinary planar matching inputs.** For b≥2 take K=2^b length-three paths u−a_j−b_j−v, internally disjoint, indexed by j in GF(2)^b. Exactly one path uses its two outer edges in a perfect matching; every other path uses its middle edge. Add an independent four-cycle with phase z and fix an edge selected precisely at z=1. For every nonzero vector t include the demand consisting of this phase edge and one designated outer edge of each path with t·j=0. Its miss function is [z=0](t·j). It is not affine in raw matching incidence: any affine function on the independent components has zero XOR over every two-by-two rectangle, whereas the miss has odd rectangle parity if the two indices have opposite dot products. The nonzero t yield distinct functions with binary rank b, since the coordinate t vectors are independent on the z=0 slice. Their Gram matrix has diagonal K/2, off-diagonal K/4, thus equals (K/4)(I+J) with full rational rank K−1. Positive homogeneous rational compression can therefore be exponentially weaker in rank even when all demands are individually nonaffine. These demands may overlap, and the root has degree K at u,v. This construction isolates the rank bounds, not a running-time lower bound against other algorithms.
