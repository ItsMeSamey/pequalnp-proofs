# Fixed byte-DFA bounds on the ordered-image solver's recovered source

Start with any simple cubic graph obtained by replacing each vertex of a simple cubic base by a triangle, using each triangle vertex for exactly one external edge. Write n for the resulting vertex count. For every edge uv impose the two source clauses (z,−x_u,−x_v) and (−z,−x_u,−x_v), retaining this port order. Apply the occurrence-cycle construction in ordered-ring-planar-phase-boundary.md, also replacing z by a cycle. Call the resulting edge-variable CNF F. F itself, including every star and every conflict, is the source of both compilations. The two emitted physical objects have different formats. The source gates of the comparison are never identified with gates of either compiler.

All bridges are false in every model and each cycle alternates in one of two phases. Project onto one even-indexed edge bit per graph variable and obtain exactly the independent-set masks of the cubic graph. All these masks occur, independently of z. The FKT residual-demand count on F is exactly twice the number of cubic edges: every occurrence uses a globally private designated edge inside its own four-position block, hence the three edges of each demand are disjoint, no demand is inside one star, and no demand contains another. The stars alone delete no edges. The grouped affine fallback reconstructs the two signs of z in each pair of demands. Within each support of three distinct phases the six allowed triples are nonaffine. Width-three clauses remain, so the current two-SAT shortcut does not apply.

Feed F verbatim into stage6_source_solver.compile_source with exact_twice=True. The function accepts widths one through three and internally pads each short nonempty clause by repeating its last literal. Its byte DFA tests signed aliases to private occurrence markers and the alternating equality/OR sequence. The positive physical words project to the models of F. On successful words a source bit is an affine readout of a first-marker code in one of its equality triples: the reset DFA control is fixed there and the two possible encoded records are distinguishable. These readouts for one phase per graph vertex yield a surjective projection of positive words onto the independent sets, with affine span of dimension n.

Consequently the kernel of any complete residual affine-flat exclusion system projects to periods of that independent-set family, which has no nonzero period. A period translates zero to an independent set I; if I is nonempty, translate a singleton neighbor of I to contradict independence. Active rank is at least n. If all residual exclusions are private pairs forbidding 11 after affine closure, the legal flags through a feasible preimage of zero contain two blockwise complementary m-dimensional choices of directions, for m pairs. Each choice meets the flag-image direction space of codimension r in dimension at least m−r. Their sum has codimension at most r in that image. Each subspace projects to a linear subspace of independent sets, whose support must itself be independent (two nonzero coordinate forms can take one simultaneously). The union of two such supports contains at most two vertices per triangle. Thus n−r≤2n/3, or r≥n/3. Residual exclusions of higher ranks can instead leave the private-pair representation unavailable.

For the whole-record signed dictionary, restrict analytically to an induced matching of size k=Ω(n), obtainable by greedy deletion in maximum degree three. Pin the other graph phases, z, dummies and enable to their canonical values, and express every actual cycle edge by its phase or complement and every bridge by zero. Every structural OR arising from a vertex star or conflict now depends on at most one matching endpoint bit: at a bridge endpoint its incident cycle edges belong to one cycle and the bridge is constant. Repetitions introduced by short-row padding preserve this fact. Each remaining source-demand OR has its z port first and its endpoint ports in the original order. With the induced matching restriction it depends on at most one endpoint or on one matched pair. Reset equality triples depend on only their own bit.

Extend the actual encoded records affinely from the three legal corners 00,10,01 of each matched pair. The 3^k legal points span all 2k directions, so the entire affine section is contained in any ambient affine base containing every feasible word. At an illegal pair, the equality readouts of all actual edge-variable markers recover the assigned source bits, as these readouts are affine on the section. The phase demands preclude feasibility there. Each whole-record code fiber restricts to a rectangle under the a-versus-b matching split: unary records have unary fibers; the two-endpoint ORs have the same byte layout as in paired-source-coupled-obstruction.md. Before the last endpoint arrives a record sees at most the first endpoint; every byte that sees both retains the final endpoint bit. The three codes on legal corners thus give either an injective affine extension or a function of that final endpoint alone. Fibers and their intersections are rectangles. A signed dictionary for the specified intersections of whole record codes consequently needs at least 2^k nonzero entries, by the rational rank of the tensor power of [[1,1],[1,0]].

These simultaneous obstructions refer to the exposed rank parameters, residual group test, and explicit dictionary. Sizes of F, the byte compiler output and the ordered ring image are polynomial in n. The all-zero graph phase is feasible and supplies a simple model. The bounds therefore do not extend to arbitrary decision algorithms, to semantic preprocessing of F, or to unrelated Fourier representations.
