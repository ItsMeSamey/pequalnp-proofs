# Deterministic branch and functional-cone counting at n log n gates

Consider a bounded-fanin circuit on n primary bits in topological order, possibly of size B n log2 n. Replace portions by m<=Mn single-output functional cones, leaving at most C0 n binary gates intact. Each cone has a known set S_i of raw input bits and at most a other predecessor signals. Input sharing and fanout are unrestricted; the incidence sets and cone descriptions must be computable in polynomial time. Assume L=max |S_i|=o(sqrt(log n)), with fixed M,C0,a. The deterministic exact count on the original n inputs, and recovery of an accepting physical input, take poly(description) 2^{n-Omega(n/sqrt(log n))}, using the pinned October fixed-density 3CNF bound.

Put k=floor(c n/sqrt(log2 n)) with a sufficiently large fixed c. For a uniform k-subset U of the raw inputs, 2^{|U intersect S|} counts subsets of S contained in U. Each j-subset is contained with probability at most (k/n)^j. Thus the expected sum of truth-table lengths is at most m 2^a (1+k/n)^L=(1+o(1))m 2^a. Fix U deterministically by conditional expectations, retaining exactly k inputs. With some choices fixed, each remaining intersection has a hypergeometric distribution; exact binomial integer sums share a denominator bounded by 2^n and can be compared in polynomial time. The construction depends on the scopes, not the truth values.

Branch on the n-k other raw inputs. In each branch tabulate every cone on its surviving raw and formal predecessor inputs. Evaluate the supplied cone to fill its table. A binary Shannon decision tree using constant-cost multiplexers realizes a table on r bits in O(2^r) gates. Reconnect predecessor signals in topological order, retaining the core. The total leaf gate count is O(n) uniformly over branches with an eventual constant independent of the selected fixed c. Use Tseitin definition clauses and an output pin; each original assignment has one extension before the output pin. There are V<=C1 n variables and at most 5V clauses; V>=k. The source clock poly(V) 2^{A V/sqrt(log2 V)} thus costs at most poly(n) 2^{(A C1+o(1)) n/sqrt(log2 n)}. Choose fixed c>2AC1+2. The little-o scope promise supplies the same C1 after choosing c. The integer counts sum over disjoint branches. For witness recovery choose a positive leaf and self-restrict raw bits with unit clauses, retaining the same fixed density up to an absolute constant, or substitute bits and keep the original variable list with pins. One can allow the larger density constant in the choice of c. The fixed-density source is https://github.com/openai/math/blob/92b516ef8e/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/build/quantitative-circuits.tex .

Automatic partition for one-output cones without buried shared gates: remove output-irrelevant gates and mark every gate with at least two outgoing wire occurrences, and the final output. Replace each use of a marked gate in another producer's body by a leaf labeled with its shared port. Each marked producer heads a tree of remaining fanout-one gates, with possibly repeated raw and shared-port leaves. For each tree vertex v compute by bottom-up DP the minimum number of valid cones covering its subtree. Enumerate all antichains of at most a optional internal cone roots strictly below v. The subtree left at v, stopped at those cuts, must use at most L distinct raw bits, and at most a distinct predecessor signals (the cut vertices and shared labels still present). On success cost 1 plus the sum of optimal costs of the cut subtrees. The empty cut is included. The recurrence considers the actual immediate boundary of every legal covering; the cut subtrees are disjoint. Summing the marked producer-root optima pays each computed value once. This runs in s^{a+O(1)} time and finds an optimum for this precise partition model; take no extra core, or first set aside a supplied core. Cone-size hypotheses are checked on this partition, not inferred from s. For L=omega(sqrt(log n)), n cyclic scopes of length L have every raw input in L scopes; Jensen yields sum 2^{|S_i intersect U|}>=n 2^{kL/n}, blocking the linear table-length estimate in this model. This does not bound the best representation of those particular functions.

Joint cut optimization for a supplied acyclic cone network (no extra core, or represent each core gate as another cone): put all n raw signals and all m cone-output IDs in one universe. The scope T_i of a cone comprises its distinct raw and predecessor-port inputs. Solve the LP maximizing sum z_j with 0<=z_j<=1 and sum_{j in T_i} z_j<=a0 for each cone, a0 fixed. If the optimum is at least m+c n/sqrt(log n), with sufficient constant slack, take a feasible solution and decrease entries until its total is an integer (lose <1). Its potential Phi=sum_i product_{j in T_i}(1+z_j) is at most m exp(a0). Moving two fractional entries oppositely at fixed sum makes Phi concave along the exchange; choose an integralizing endpoint with nonincreasing Phi, even if individual LP row caps then cease to hold. Exact rational arithmetic takes polynomial time (the initial LP solution can be of polynomial bit size, and final fractional entries are sums/differences of those values and integers). At termination the live subset U has sum_i 2^{|T_i intersect U|}<=m exp(a0). Guess every other signal, a total of n+m-|U| bits. A guessed producer must still be evaluated and constrained equal to its guessed bit; only its uses are replaced by the guess. Each original assignment is consistent with exactly one complete tuple of guesses. With m=O(n) the leaf has O(n) gates/variables and O(n) additional unit constraints, padded to V>=n if needed with uniquely forced zero variables to keep fixed clause density. This yields the same clock for sufficiently large c and can exploit high raw and port fanout without independent read-load assumptions.
