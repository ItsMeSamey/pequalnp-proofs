# A bank of pair inequalities cannot refute in 127 lines

Suppose the shared derived vertices of a trimmed refutation have distinct falsity predicates x_i=x_j, indexed by the edges of a simple graph G on the five pigeons. Write k for the number of edges. All axiom sharing, interlemma wiring, unary weakenings, and presentations of these flats are allowed.

Cut into components at the k shared derived vertices. In each pair component choose four exclusive assignments, one per hole, each with this pair as its only collision. They are in its root's falsity flat and no other pair exit can be false there. Material raw inputs reached by the four samples are distinct. The immediate derived user of each reached raw is false on that sample only among the four: weakening can only shrink falsity, and resolution leaves at least three of the four raw bit literals. The four distinct such singleton nodes and the three branching nodes joining these four paths are disjoint. Thus every pair component has ≥7 internal (binary or unary) lines regardless of its cut exits.

In the root component, each unique-collision input reaches an exit naming its pair or a material leaf naming its pair and hole. Restrict separately to each duplicated hole and minimize a weighted parity tree for identifying the pair. A label in G costs 1 per leaf and a label outside G costs 4. The average total cost of the four restricted trees is at most the total root leaf count: an exit/material incidence for a pair in G appears at most four times with cost 1, and a raw incidence for a pair outside G appears only for its own hole with cost 4. Translation makes the optimum L(G) identical for all four holes. Hence the root has at least L(G) leaves. With its W_root unary steps, it has at least L(G)−1+W_root internal vertices. Including forty mandatory axiom vertices gives N≥39+7k+L(G)+W_root.

For nonempty G at least one cut exit is used by the root component. Any presentation of a pair-equality falsity flat includes a non-coordinate literal; otherwise the flat would be a coordinate subcube. Every literal in such a presentation has positive sign because the zero assignment falsifies it. The raw inputs only contain coordinate literals. Without a root-component weakening, no negative non-coordinate literal could appear anywhere in that component. A positive non-coordinate literal introduced at a cut exit could then never be erased on any path to the syntactically empty root. Consequently W_root≥1.

weighted.cpp enumerates the 34 unlabeled graphs and computes optimal weighted fixed-hole parity leaf cost by budgeted recursion over all 255 nonconstant parity partitions. All bounds are inclusive; by a restriction of the 25-leaf unweighted tree the initial upper bound is 100. Over all graphs with k>0 the minimum of 39+7k+L(G) is 127, attained at graph masks 222 and 237 with k=6. Therefore N≥128 throughout this class. At k=0 the original 100-leaf bound applies. This result does not handle other shared derived predicates or repeated distinct vertices with the same falsity flat.
