# Nonpromise axiom cost for specified reused falsity flats

Cut a trimmed proof DAG at each reused derived vertex and materialize axiom uses as separate leaves. Component r, with root falsity flat F_r (the whole assignment space at the empty root), has a set J_r of available cut exits. Define D_r as F_r minus the union of the exit falsity flats. Every assignment in D_r must reach a false material axiom in this component. Thus the material leaf count is at least the sum, over holes h, of the minimum parity-tree cost on D_r intersected with the uniquely colliding assignments at h. Materials reached on that restricted promise have labels in the set G_r of unique falsifiers occurring there. On every other assignment in D_r whose false axioms are disjoint from G_r a leaf with a different label is needed. Add to the previous bound the hitting number of this last family of false-axiom sets. This is an additive cost even if the original proof shares the underlying axiom lines.

Example: suppose the only reused derived falsity flats are S_i given by x_i equal to the coordinatewise XOR of all five two-bit words, for i=0,1. Assume i=0 comes first by symmetry. The first component has no cut exit. Each fixed-hole unique-collision slice has 24 inputs in S_0 and needs 9 leaves. All their labels involve pigeon 0. But S_0 also contains each assignment where the other four pigeons form two equal pairs at distinct holes h,g, and pigeon 0 occupies neither. Only the two pair/hole axioms are false, and neither involves pigeon 0. For each of three perfect matchings of the four pigeons these constraints require at least four distinct axiom labels: they form the edges of K4,4 minus a diagonal, which contains a perfect matching. Different matchings use disjoint labels. Thus at least twelve more material leaves belong to component S_0.

The second component must solve at least the inputs in S_1 outside S_0 (six leaves per hole); the root component must solve the complement of their union (five per hole). These counts come from the exact parity-tree dynamic program. Therefore there are at least 4(9+6+5)+12=92 physical axiom incidences. With two shared derived vertices, at least 40 input vertices, and B=p−1+s_d, the total length is at least 133. If the two components are incomparable the second one needs at least nine leaves per hole and the bound rises by twelve. This is a bound for the displayed pair of reusable falsity flats; it does not classify arbitrary pairs.
