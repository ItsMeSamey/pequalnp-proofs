# Arbitrary codimension with full hole symmetry

An affine falsity flat is hole-symmetric here if it is fixed setwise by all simultaneous permutations of the four hole words. Suppose exactly two derived vertices are shared and both of their flats are hole-symmetric. Then at least 129 physical lines are needed.

To classify these flats, let V consist of pairs (x,y) of five-bit column vectors (the two coordinate rows of the pigeon words), and take a nonempty invariant flat with direction L. All permutations of four hole words are affine maps of the two-bit word space. The linear part GL₂(F₂) preserves L. For (x,y) in L, applying the elementary shear (x,y)↦(x+y,y) and subtracting gives (y,0) in L. The coordinate interchange gives (0,y), and the other shear supplies the same for x. Thus L=D×D for a binary subspace D of the five-bit space. In the quotient by L the invariant coset must be fixed by both shears, which forces both coordinates zero. The flat equals L. Invariance under simultaneous translations requires the all-ones vector in D. Equivalently the flat imposes XOR=00 on the words indexed by each mask in W=D-perp, a subspace of the even-weight five-bit masks.

On an assignment with sole collision ij, the kernel of the word-sum map on even masks is precisely the two-space K_ij spanned by the masks omitting i and omitting j. Both vanish because the XOR of the five words is the repeated value. Even masks map onto the full two-bit space using differences among the four distinct hole words, so the kernel has dimension two. Each K_ij has the three nonzero masks omitting i, omitting j, and selecting just i,j. Consequently a nontrivial test W⊆K_ij, over the ten possible labels, accepts either a single pair, a star of the four pairs containing a fixed pigeon, or no labels. In the star case the flat necessarily is the full earlier relative-XOR predicate W=span(mask omitting that pigeon). The whole-space case is constant.
