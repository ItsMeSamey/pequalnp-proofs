# Two reused two-bit XOR tests on even subsets of pigeons

Assume the only two multiply used derived vertices in the empty-clause ancestor DAG have distinct falsity predicates of the form XOR of the words in I equals 00, with |I| equal to two or four. Let I precede J. The three components must solve respectively the event I, the event J outside I, and the complement of both. Each component's physical material leaf count has the bound in nonpromise.md. The independent parity costs can be multiplied by four because each event is invariant under translation of the hole names. The checked script natural-pairs.py computes the nonpromise hitting cost over the full 1024 inputs. Each recursive hitting branch selects one label from a still-uncovered set, so all covers are considered. The ordered size pair and intersection cardinality determine the orbit under permutation of pigeons.

For sizes/overlap (2,2,1), (2,2,0), (2,4,2), (2,4,1), (4,2,2), (4,2,1), (4,4,3), the resulting material-incidence bounds p are respectively 84,84,92,96,96,108,116. Physical line count is at least p+41+W, since there are at least forty distinct input lines, at least two derived reference excesses, and W unary inferences.

When I has size two the first component actually satisfies p_first+W_first≥8. Pick four distinct assignments, one per hole h, placing I at h and assigning the other three pigeons bijectively to the remaining holes. The matching pair/hole input is uniquely false on its respective assignment. The root of this component is false on all four. Along a false traversal going backwards from each sample to its raw input, the first derived parent of that raw input (in forward derivation order) is false on that sample and on none of the other three: weakening preserves falsity inclusion, and a resolvent retains at least three of the four signed coordinate literals on I, one of the two coordinates still fixed at both pigeons and both hole bits still specified in total. Thus these give four distinct derived tree nodes each false on exactly one of the samples. In the union of the four selected root-to-input paths there are at least three distinct binary branching nodes; each is false on at least two samples and hence is additional. With no earlier shared derived vertex this component is a tree with p_first−1+W_first internal nodes. This proves the assertion. For the first region the charged leaf lower bound for I of size two was just four, so this adds four when combining incidences and unary counts. The minimum total over all seven types is at least 129. Unrestricted proofs may use different predicates or additional reused derived vertices.
