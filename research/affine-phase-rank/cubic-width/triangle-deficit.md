# A randomized fixed-deficiency decision and witness algorithm

Work over binary vectors, with independent local choices from ternary sets S_i (a plane's three points other than its missing corner). Translate each missing corner to zero, adjusting the target y. Reject if the translated target is outside the total span; otherwise express it in span coordinates. Let m be total output span dimension, and t the maximum number of linearly independent directions using one per plane, found by matroid intersection. Write k=m−t.

Rado's rank formula gives t=min_J(rank(U_J)+p−|J|), U_J the sum of planes indexed by J and p the original group count. Find a minimizing J via a dual certificate of matroid intersection or submodular minimization of the displayed cost. On J, the maximum rainbow rank is rank(U_J), or a better subset would improve the full minimum. This means J alone attains its entire output span: choose any local point in each block, select a rainbow basis from the differences to its two other points (these span the local plane), and realize the desired sum by binary elimination. Save this lifting data. Remove J and quotient outputs by its span. The remaining p' groups have a full independent transversal by Rado's formula applied in the quotient, with residual output dimension p'+k. A plane projecting to a line has both outputs attainable; split off that group and quotient by its line until only proper planes remain. Such contractions preserve independent representatives and k. Keep lifts so any quotient witness extends in reverse. Degenerate zero-group cases are checked directly.

Write p for the remaining groups and m=p+k. Choose bases of their planes. The syndrome equations on their 2p local coordinates have rank m and every target fiber is an affine space with d=p−k free binary coordinates. The local forbidden point is zero. Introduce two independent random coefficients per group over K=F_{2^e} and form
    P(y)=Σ_{z in binary syndrome fiber} ∏_i (a_i z_{i,1}+b_i z_{i,2}).
For infeasible y this is identically zero. Conversely at any valid tuple, the two available differences to the other local points are a basis of each local plane. Rado yields p independent choices, one per group. For each choose the binary linear form on its two coordinates equal to one at both endpoints of the local line through the witness. These affine lines contain only allowed points. Their global direct sum maps injectively into outputs, hence the original witness is the unique contributing tuple. At these binary weight values P(y)=1. Thus P is formally nonzero exactly at feasible y.

On the parametrized binary fiber (variables t₁,...,t_d), each factor is an affine form over K. Summing monomials over the binary cube in characteristic two keeps exactly those containing every free coordinate at least once. Their degree excess is k. Enumerate the c rows assigned to constants, and the e variables used more than once, together with their mutually disjoint row sets of sizes l_j≥2. Require c+Σ_j(l_j−1)=k and assign all remaining rows bijectively to the remaining variables. Each enumeration contributes the supplied constants and multiple-occurrence entries times the determinant of the remaining square matrix (permanent equals determinant in characteristic two). Canonical ordering by variable and row index avoids duplicates. At most 2k total exceptional rows and at most k exceptional variables are specified; this is p^{O(k)} polynomial field operations. At k=1 there is either one constant or one doubled variable. The case d=0 also follows directly from the parameterization.

For one target the failure probability is ≤p/|K| by Schwartz–Zippel; choose extension degree logarithmic in p plus desired failure bits. An irreducible field polynomial may be constructed by sampling and deterministic verification with expected polynomial time, or deterministically at small fixed error by exhaustive enumeration of degree-O(log p) polynomials. There is no false YES. From any nonzero evaluation, specialize each local form to one of its two binary coordinate forms preserving a nonzero value (multilinearity); at the end the selected fibers give an odd number of solutions to affine equations, hence exactly one. Solve it, then restore all contracted choices. Total bit time for constant failure probability is p^{O(k)}poly(input), and polynomial space using streamed enumeration. A fixed nonzero error can be reduced by independent repetitions. This is polynomial for fixed k, not a general SAT algorithm.
