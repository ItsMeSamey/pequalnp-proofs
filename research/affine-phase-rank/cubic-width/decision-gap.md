# Deciding zero through high polar rank

Represent the CNF origin by a uniquely extended Boolean quadratic system q(z)=u with m outputs and n free inputs. Let B≤F₂^m have dimension b, and suppose every s outside B gives a quadratic s·q of polar rank at least R. Compute H=Σ_{s∈B}(−1)^{s·u}Σ_z(−1)^{s·q(z)} with 2^b quadratic Gauss evaluations, polynomial memory and O(m+n)-bit arithmetic. H is 2^b times the model count of the projected b constraints. If H=0 the full system is infeasible. If H>(2^m−2^b)2^{n−R/2}, then the omitted signed sums cannot cancel H and the system is feasible. Polar ranks are even.

If R≥2(m+b), these tests decide every syndrome. For a feasible projection its indicator is a nonzero Boolean polynomial of degree at most 2b, the product of b degree-two indicators. Every nonzero degree-d Boolean polynomial has weight at least 2^{n−d}: choose a maximum-degree monomial, fixing all outside variables leaves its coefficient one for all such choices. Thus H≥2^{n−b}, strictly exceeding the omitted-sum bound. For b=0 surjectivity follows already from R≥2m. No reconstruction of the exact unprojected count or assignment is needed for this decision guarantee.

A deterministic verifiable sufficient certificate of the rank bound uses two disjoint k-bit coordinate blocks X,Y in supplied affine coordinates and a representation of F_{2^k}. For every output j verify its mixed X,Y coefficient block is Tr(Y L_j(X)), with L_j(X)=Σ_{i=0}^d a_ji X^{2^i}, d<k. Fit or verify on basis elements by binary linear algebra and the nondegenerate trace pairing. B is the kernel of the binary coefficient map s↦(Σ_j s_j a_ji)_i. Outside B a nonzero polynomial of degree ≤2^d has at most 2^d roots; its binary rank is ≥k−d, and it is a submatrix of the polar matrix. No rank doubling is justified in the presence of arbitrary same-block quadratic terms. Irreducibility, trace calculations, coefficient fits and kernel computations cost polynomial bit time. Thus certifying k−d≥2(m+b) gives a 2^b poly(input) uniform algorithm given these coordinates. Discovery of arbitrary bases is not claimed.

For example take m independent binary functionals of the field product XY with k≥2m, adding unrestricted same-block quadratics in X alone and Y alone; every syndrome is attainable. Deterministic AND/XOR gate encodings realize all these constraints as CNF with unique gates; a supplied gate presentation can be checked locally and eliminated. The rank condition need not hold after arbitrary CNF compilation, nor at nonzero Cauchy nodes.

For a negative control, a constant sign-reversing translation of f(s,z)=s·(q(z)+u) exists exactly when a binary linear combination of q_j+u_j is identically one. Indeed D_(a,h)f=s·D_hq(z)+a·(q(z+h)+u), so constant one forces D_hq=0 and then such a contradiction. Conversely a contradiction gives translation (a,0). A triangular multiplier shear s↦s+L(z) generalizes this to exactly the Boolean identity Σ L_j(z)(q_j(z)+u_j)=1. An unrestricted circuit for this shear does not automatically give efficient identity verification.
