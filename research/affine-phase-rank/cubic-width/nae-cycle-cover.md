# Inconsistent cycle words and exact regular deficit

Use t variable-disjoint loose cycles of fixed odd length s in positive NAE: cyclic vertices v_i, distinct private tips u_i, and clauses on v_i,v_{i+1},u_i. Other clauses may be arbitrary. Signed cycles work when requiring the two spine literals of every clause to differ has inconsistent parity. Each satisfying assignment allows exactly two of three opposite-pair choices per clause. Of the 3^s words selecting one equation per clause, the all-spine word is inconsistent. All other words select s independent equations: a dependency selects an even subgraph of chosen pairs, so cannot use a private tip; the sole possible cycle is the full spine. The union has st independent chosen equations. For affine ports verify the selected planes have one joint dependence per cycle consisting of its spine directions and no other dependencies. Enumerating fixed-size variable cycles and greedily selecting vertex-disjoint ones takes polynomial time.

Group b=floor(log(d+2)) cycles at a time, d the global rank of input differences. Every feasible pattern is hit by 2^(sb) of the (3^s−1)^b words. On both sides of the local coverage table there are exp(O(sb)) elements, polynomial for fixed s. Greedy set cover, by averaging, costs at most ((3^s−1)/2^s)^b times one plus the log of the number of patterns. Enumerating products of batch covers incurs exp(O((t/b+1)log(b+2)))=exp(o(d)) overhead. Apply the general deterministic affine cover solver to the residual rank at most d−st. Total decision and witness time is poly(input) times (3/2)^d (1−3^(−s))^t exp(o(d)). Branches overlap. Linear t gives a strict improvement relative to the affine-rank baseline. Regular degree four alone guarantees no bounded cycle length. Ordinary literal inputs also have stronger algorithms measured in variable count.

If the incidence girth exceeds 2r, any set of at most r planes is independent: each nonzero plane direction picks a pair of variables, and canceling all contributions would give an even incidence subgraph containing a cycle. Conversely each incidence cycle gives a dependence. Thus the local map on each such collection is surjective. Nonzero pairs affinely span the two-bit output; pullback of the spanning product, including the full kernel, has full affine hull. Bounded local hull closure gains no dimensions there.

For connected d-regular 3-uniform hypergraphs supplied as d perfect matchings, d>=3, k=m−n+1 exactly. For any partition into p>=2 nonempty parts each part has at least three boundary incidences. When its size is 0 mod 3, connectivity gives a crossing matching, and its count of vertices not covered by internal matching edges is a positive multiple of 3. Otherwise every matching contributes at least one incidence. Therefore at least p crossing edges exist. Apply this to the partition into components of a selected edge set I including all isolates. Then rank deficit of that set is |I|−n+p ≤m−n; the full connected set achieves m−n+1. This applies directly to the known hard four-perfect-matching subclass (and gives n/3+1). The proof uses a supplied matching decomposition as a structural promise.
