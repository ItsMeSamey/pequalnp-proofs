# Incidence expansion of a direct private-AND CNF

For each vertex v of the graph in expander-scalar.md take the full truth table of its parity constraint on the private pairs x_e,y_e adjacent to v. For every forbidden assignment add the clause false exactly at that assignment. Such clauses all contain every local bit. At fixed maximum degree D this is width at most 2D and occurrence at most 2·4^D. The count is unchanged, with no auxiliaries. Choose a clause c_v of each vertex; at native syndrome one there is always a forbidden assignment.

For every edge e=uv, the incidence paths c_u–x_e–c_v are edge-disjoint. Consequently any physical incidence cut has boundary L at least the base boundary induced by the representatives c_v. Assign variables to either endpoint's representative, and additional local clauses to c_v at their vertex. Every assigned object is at distance ≤2 from its representative via local variables. These paths have constant congestion and representatives have constant load because all degrees and local table sizes are bounded. At most O(L) objects lie opposite their owner. Hence the smaller incidence shore is bounded by a constant times the smaller representative shore plus O(L), itself O(L) by base expansion. Thus the exact-scalar reduction can output an expanding bounded-occurrence CNF incidence graph with uniform fixed constants. Polynomial time here permits the large factor from exhaustive local truth tables at fixed D.
