# Two single GHZ profiles and five ordinary products

Suppose two three-party-entangled stabilizers each consist, up to local Cliffords and fixed local stabilizers, of one GHZ factor. The local Cliffords may be entirely different. Together with five product stabilizers, suppose they span g⊗g⊗g. The known seven-ray lower bound forces every coefficient nonzero. We use the local three-space certificate and product splitting theorem as in [the single-profile proof](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md).

Expand each GHZ into its two logical product terms. The target plus nine products have a relation with all coefficients nonzero and form a connected vector matroid. Indeed projection onto the target component gives a product decomposition needing at least eight terms, so at most one vector could lie outside; the full-coefficient relation rules out any remaining singleton. Span rank is at most nine. The connected splitting bound gives Σ_i d_i≤11. Every local dimension is at least three since it contains g outside the two-dimensional local support code of a GHZ. Some block A therefore has dimension exactly three. Both GHZ codes inside it must coincide: two distinct dimension-two codes each have six logical stabilizer rays and share at most one, whereas the reviewed local census bounds the total in this three-space through g by eight.

At least four ordinary terms have A factors outside this common code C_A, since otherwise a covector annihilating C_A and nonzero on g would express two copies with at most three stabilizers. The local three-space certificate gives another covector nonzero on g and annihilating every exterior local stabilizer type. Applying it leaves at most one ordinary product and two products per GHZ. At least four terms must survive. If exactly four survive, at least one GHZ contributes both logical products. Their supports on B and C each span a dimension-two code excluding g. The minimum four-product representation gives a rank-four circuit whose two local dimensions sum to at most five, contradicting the required sum at least six.

Consequently all five terms survive, including both complete GHZ pairs. The target plus these five B,C product vectors form a connected collection: the target component needs at least four products, leaving at most one vector, which cannot support another projected relation. Rank at most five gives d_B+d_C≤6; each dimension is at least three. The two GHZ codes in each of B and C now coincide by the same local three-space certificate. After the A contraction, kill the common B code by a covector nonzero on g. Only the one ordinary product remains, forcing g on C to be proportional to a stabilizer. This excludes the profile.

The argument does not assume any alignment of the codes before deriving it. No new numerical census is used.
