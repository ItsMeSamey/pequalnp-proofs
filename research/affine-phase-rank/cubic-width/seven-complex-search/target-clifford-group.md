# Target-preserving Cliffords and 343 native dictionaries

Let g(x)=2^(−3/2)(−1)^{x_1 x_2 x_3} on three bits. Up to phase every Clifford fixing g^⊗t is a permutation of whole blocks followed by local affine permutations x↦Ax+b with unique diagonal correction g(x)/g(Ax+b).

On one block, the nonidentity Pauli labels (a,z) of absolute expectation 1/2 are exactly a nonzero, a·z=0. Every other nonidentity label has zero expectation: for a nonzero shift the cubic derivative has rank-two quadratic polar with radical span(a). Its sign sum with z·x vanishes for a·z=1 and has magnitude 4 before normalization otherwise. Nonidentity pure Z sums vanish. By multiplication the global nonidentity labels of maximal absolute expectation 1/2 are precisely the local ones. Their anticommutation graph has one connected component per block: a mixed label (a,z) with nonzero z anticommutes with some pure X=(v,0). For different nonzero v,w choose z with z·v=z·w=1 and nonzero a perpendicular to z; the mixed vertex joins both. Each component spans the full local Pauli label space since (a,z)+(a,0)=(0,z). Consequently a target-preserving Clifford permutes the local Pauli spaces. Undo the block permutation and choose local Clifford representatives of the induced symplectic maps; the remaining unitary is a Pauli up to phase and is itself a product. Equality of product rays implies each local unitary fixes g projectively.

The nonidentity local Paulis for which both outcomes on g are stabilizer rays are exactly pure Z. A real shifted Pauli has projected support sizes two and six, ruling out the six-point outcome. For an imaginary shifted Pauli the outcomes have full support and phase parity, up to constant gauge, equal to the quadratic bit D_a(x_1 x_2 x_3)+z·x: the Pauli ratio to g is ±i times its sign, and one plus the two possible ratios gives phases differing by a fourth root of odd exponent. The resulting parity is nonaffine, whereas every full stabilizer chirp has affine parity. Pure Z gives two planes on which the cubic restricts to degree at most two. Thus a local fixing Clifford preserves the computational Z space, inducing a computational affine permutation. The everywhere nonzero amplitudes fix its correction uniquely. Conversely the prescribed correction is quadratic since the cubic part after substitution is multiplied by det A=1, so every such map is Clifford.

Any orthogonal two-stabilizer decomposition of g separates its rays by a common Pauli label with opposite signs, by the stabilizer trace formula. Its two components must therefore be g restricted to complementary planes of a computational Z parity. There are seven directions. For three blocks the 343 resulting product dictionaries of eight rays are all related by target-preserving Cliffords. Any seven-ray list can contain at most two rays from each individual dictionary by the four-external support theorem. This also tests lists entirely exterior to the initial dictionary. Assign every eligible product ray its tuple of local plane directions; three identical tuples rule out a list. Distinct tuples or noneligible rays are not excluded by this test.
