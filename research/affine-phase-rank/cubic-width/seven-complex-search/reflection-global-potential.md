# A decreasing potential for the reflection transfer

Consider a minimal seven-term complex stabilizer representation of the tensor cube of CCZ with five block-product rays and exactly two exceptional rays E,F. Define the integer potential to be the sum over E,F and the three cuts of the base-two logarithm of the corresponding Schmidt ranks. It is between zero and eighteen. Apply the audited encoder-reflection-transfer.md at any cut X to which it applies. Both resulting terms are nonzero by minimality. The earlier exclusion of six block-product terms also shows both must remain exceptional.

Write the replacement terms as E′ and F′. The subcode projector L in the smaller correction F′=2LF commutes with the orthogonal projection onto im F. Since F is a scalar multiple of a partial isometry, L can be pushed through F to its input as an orthogonal projector (zero on the kernel). In vectorization this is a local filter on party X, transposed in the chosen computational basis. It cannot increase Schmidt rank at any cut. In the nontrivial first-term formula E′=(P−S)E P_Q J, the right factors also act locally on party X. Each of the two left Paulis is a tensor product on the two remaining parties. At either other cut the resulting rank is therefore at most twice the corresponding rank of E. In the special case E′=E P_{Q-perp}, this bound still holds. Thus the two other cuts contribute at most two units of increase in total. On X itself the logarithmic sum falls by at least three units, since (8,4) becomes at most (4,1), or (8,8) becomes at most (4,2). Each transfer decreases the potential by at least one. This argument includes arbitrary encoders, physical phase conventions and complex coefficients under the hypotheses of the transfer.

Any putative representation in this class therefore has one reachable in at most eighteen transfers for which every cut bearing a rank-eight ray has two rank-eight rays with identical remote code. Earlier rank constraints exclude pairing rank eight with ranks below four. Equality of the full-rank cut sets on the two rays is a necessary profile condition for a terminal representative. Equality of the output codes is an additional encoder condition. This reduction preserves existence, not a pointwise exclusion of nonterminal representations.
