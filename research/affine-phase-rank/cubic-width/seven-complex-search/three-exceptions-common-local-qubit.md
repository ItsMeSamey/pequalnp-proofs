# Three exceptional rays cannot share a local encoded-qubit support

Assume G₃=g⊗g⊗g is a sum of four ordinary products of single-block stabilizer rays and three arbitrary stabilizer rays. On every original party the three exceptional local Schmidt supports cannot all lie inside a single two-dimensional stabilizer code C. This uses physical block products, regardless of any internal Fourier chart.

Suppose C on X contains them. The local target g is outside C. For each of the four ordinary X factors a, we must have g∈C+span(a); otherwise a covector killing C and a, retaining g, represents G₂ by at most three stabilizers. Hence all these a are exterior to C and lie in U=C+span(g). The reviewed local three-space certificate, as explained in single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md, supplies at most two exterior stabilizer rays in U, and their span excludes g.

We record a secant fact for the two-exterior-ray case. Let a,b be distinct exterior stabilizer rays in U. Their plane meets C in a line l since their images modulo C are nonzero and proportional. Decode C by a Clifford into the zero-syndrome subspace of two syndrome qubits with one logical qubit free. Rescale a,b so their nonzero collections of exterior syndrome fibers agree exactly. There is a common nonzero exterior fiber. For each stabilizer seed its nonempty logical fibers have fixed unsigned logical stabilizer basis: an affine section gives Pauli translates of one logical stabilizer as the syndrome changes. Equality on the common exterior fiber forces the same basis for both seeds. Apply a single fixed logical Clifford taking this basis to computational. Now each nonempty syndrome of either seed has one nonzero logical amplitude, a constant per seed times a fourth root of unity. Because they agree at one exterior entry, the constants can be taken the same. Subtract the two seeds. This difference is nonzero, is supported at syndrome zero, and represents l. If only one seed occurs there, or the two seeds have the same logical label, the result is a basis ray. If the two labels differ it is an equal-magnitude logical superposition of the two basis elements with fourth-root relative phase, also a stabilizer. Undo the common Clifford. Thus l is an encoded stabilizer ray.

A covector on U killing a,b and retaining g exists by the three-space certificate. Its restriction to C is nonzero (U=C+span(a)) and annihilates l. In a logical qubit this restriction is proportional to a stabilizer bra. If instead only one exterior type a is used among the four ordinary rays, any covector on C extends uniquely to U by killing a. The logical stabilizer bras span the dual of C, so one of these extensions retains g, since g is not proportional to a.

Extend the resulting covector arbitrarily to the full X space. It kills all four ordinary terms. On each exceptional tensor only its restriction to C contributes, hence the contraction equals a scalar times contraction with an encoded stabilizer bra on X. Each nonzero result is a stabilizer ray on the two remaining parties. This would express a nonzero multiple of G₂ with three or fewer stabilizer rays, contradicting its rank four.
