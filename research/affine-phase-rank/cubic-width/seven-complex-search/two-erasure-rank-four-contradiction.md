# Two erased parties cannot occur with four product and three exceptional stabilizers

Assume g lies outside the sum of the three exceptional local supports at both X and Y. By [two-erasure bin isolation](two-erasures-exact-secants.md), a stabilizer bra on Z gives a nonzero product u⊗v of two three-qubit Pauli-nullity-three interior rays as the sum of three projected exceptional stabilizers. No term vanishes by [the rank-two product lower bound](nullity-two-product-rank.md). Each projected local Schmidt rank is at most four since each original local stabilizer support at X,Y was proper. Use the usual matrix flattening, conjugating the Y factor and its code when viewing them as input Hilbert vectors.

**All three projected ranks are four.** A lone rank-four term has proper column and input codes excluding the two target factors. Subtracting it from the target has rank five, which exceeds the other two ranks when these are each at most two. Suppose all ranks are at most two. A unique rank-two term similarly leaves rank three for two rank-one terms. If two terms A,B have rank two, their sum is the target minus the third term C, of rank 1+rank C≤3. Its column and transposed-row spans contain the respective target factors, because each is outside C's proper code. If the respective two-dimensional codes of A,B on either side intersect nontrivially, their two-dimensional check spaces share a signed nonidentity Pauli: a common nonzero vector forces compatible commuting checks on three qubits. Their sum on that side then excludes the full-nullity factor. So both pairs of codes are disjoint, making rank(A+B)=4, impossible.

Three ordinary products also cannot work. Sylvester on a three-product expression of a rank-one matrix bounds the sum of local span dimensions by four. Since neither factor is a stabilizer, both spans have dimension two. A line through a nullity-three ray has at most two stabilizers: three distinct normalized stabilizers on the line would give a singular Gram matrix, so some pair has nonzero overlap at least 1/2 by diagonal dominance. The stabilizer overlap formula then gives at least one common signed Pauli on the three qubits. It fixes the entire line, impossible. Hence each side has only two stabilizer types and the target product needs all four product types.

Take two rank-four terms A,B. On X their dimension-four codes cannot be disjoint, else they span the whole party contrary to erasure. Nor can they coincide: the column span of A+B contains u by rank-increasing subtraction of the third term C from the target. The two signed Pauli sectors must have distinct independent commuting checks and intersection dimension two. Likewise on Y. Sylvester applied to the concatenated Schmidt frames gives rank(A+B)≥8−2−2=4. Since this equals 1+rank C, the third term must also have rank four. Repeating for each pair, the three signed checks on each party have pairwise distinct commuting labels. Each projected four-space equals its proper original marginal support.

**Membership syndromes.** On each party record membership bits for the three signed sectors, with one indicating inclusion. If the three labels were independent, a joint eigenbra in the pattern 100 would annihilate two terms and retain a nonzero contraction of the first (it has full rank on its local code). The target contraction is either zero or proportional to the other nullity-three factor outside this term's opposite code. Both are impossible. Thus both sides have binary check rank two. The 000 syndrome exists by erasure (else the union of the sectors spans the whole space). The patterns are precisely 000,110,101,011, each with a two-dimensional group. The target has zero component at 000 by the tensor identity. The target has a nonzero component in each of the other three groups: any two syndrome labels in the two-bit space belong to an affine line, so their groups are contained together in a proper Pauli sector.

**Logical Pauli blocks.** Index the three nonzero groups on each side by their missing atom. For row and column groups r,s with r≠s there is exactly one active atom i. Its block must equal the nonzero target block u_r v_sᵀ up to weighting, and has rank one and column direction u_r. Within atom i's four-dimensional input code, the two allowed groups s and r are complementary outcomes of a nonidentity logical Pauli. The row group r is likewise a logical Pauli sector of its output code (restrict an independent commuting check to the code). By bipartite stabilizer normal form, the matrix of i is a scalar Clifford isometry between the two logical codes. It carries the source check to a logical Pauli in the output.

The row check and the transported check commute independently. If equal up to sign their projector product has rank zero or two. If anticommuting, the row projector has rank two on the transported two-dimensional eigenspace, since projection back is half the identity there. Both alternatives contradict the rank-one off-diagonal block. Hence within the row group the two transported outcomes give orthogonal one-dimensional directions. The (r,s) block has column direction u_r. The same atom's (r,r) block, with the opposite source outcome, is confined to its perpendicular.

This applies to both atoms present on (r,r), using their respective unique off-diagonal groups, while the missing atom vanishes. Their diagonal blocks cannot sum to the nonzero u_r v_rᵀ. The contradiction proves at most one locally erasable party for a four-product/three-exception list. No new enumeration or coefficient restriction is used.
