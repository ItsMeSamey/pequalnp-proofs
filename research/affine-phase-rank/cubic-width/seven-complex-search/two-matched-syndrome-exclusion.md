# Two independent matched remote syndromes rule out five ordinary products

Let two exceptional stabilizers in a seven-term list share two commuting computational signed checks supported on two original blocks Y,Z. Suppose their two-dimensional label span projects injectively onto each of Y,Z. Jointly satisfying the checks means the two binary syndrome bits on Y match those on Z (absorb the fixed signs into either affine syndrome map). Each of the four local syndrome classes has two computational positions. Both exceptions lie in X tensored with the space D of remote matrices supported on the four matched two-by-two blocks.

The image of G2 in the quotient remote space modulo D needs at least three images of YZ product stabilizers. Indeed if M is a sum of at most two such products and equals G2 outside D, then Δ=M−G2 is block diagonal with rank at most three. Some diagonal block is zero, since the four block ranks add. As g has full computational support, it lies outside both the column and row supports of Δ, yielding rank M=1+rank Δ. Thus Δ has rank at most one, and if nonzero is supported on one single matched block. If zero the rank-four two-copy theorem already rules out M.

For nonzero Δ, the two terms of M must be independent on both sides. Their local span on either side is span(g,d), where d is the local factor of the rank-one perturbation, supported in the altered two-point class. Each local stabilizer factor in this span either is supported there or equals a nonzero multiple of g on all the six other coordinates, which makes its affine support full. Both local basis factors cannot be full, since their span contains g and two full stabilizer chirps cannot represent g (the reviewed one-copy full-twist lemma in ../g3-complex-two-copy-pending/proof.md). Nor can both be supported in the class. There is therefore one full and one confined factor on each side. Unless the full factors are paired in one term, M is zero outside-class × outside-class. If paired, the confined term is wholly inside the altered block. The full stabilizer product would have to equal G2 everywhere outside that single block, forcing both local factors proportional to g by inspecting an outside row and column. This is impossible.

Apply the local five-set certificate of five-product-remote-erasure.md to the quotient identity g⊗[G2]=Σ_i x_i⊗[p_i], where p_i are remote stabilizer products. This use does not require the quotient map to preserve stabilizer atoms: the proof uses only that [G2] requires ≥3 terms from the chosen remote dictionary, proved above. More explicitly, discarding zero terms leaves k≤5, and the dimension of their remote span is at least 3 (any basis extracted is from the dictionary). Sylvester gives d_X≤k−2≤3. At d_X≤2 there are at most two local stabilizer directions through g, and grouping by both would require ≥3 terms each. At d_X=3 it takes k=5, all distinct, with every three local factors spanning g, exactly the excluded local five-set. This contradiction proves the two-matched-syndrome test.

**Consequences for full cuts.** At any full-Schmidt cut of one exception the remote codes of the two exceptions overlap in at least dimension four by two-copy-basis-support.md; the companion has rank at least four. They commute and share at least two independent signed checks (three in the containment case of companion rank four; three if the two full codes agree, two otherwise). If the designated exception has local ranks eight at both partner parties as well, there can be no shared check supported on only one partner. The shared-check theorem in five-product-remote-erasure.md makes every nonlocal check pure Z. Any two independent shared checks now satisfy the matching hypothesis. Consequently no exceptional stabilizer of local ranks (8,8,8) occurs in a seven-list with five product rays, irrespective of the other profile or encoders.

More generally on a cut of ranks (8,4), the companion remote code is contained in the full exception's remote code, so the latter's whole three-dimensional check space is shared. If the two other local ranks of the full exception are each at least four, each purely local shared label subspace has dimension at most one. Choose a two-dimensional subspace avoiding both such subspaces: use the kernel of a nonzero functional taking value one on each of their nonzero generators when present (distinct generators are independent). All its nonidentity elements are nonlocal and pure Z, so the same contradiction applies.
