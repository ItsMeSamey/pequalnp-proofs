# A double GHZ with a two-Bell path reduces to a simple GHZ on that path

Take the middle party of the Bell path F to be A. In its two peripheral logical qubit codes B and C choose a Schmidt basis on each Bell link avoiding all four local coordinate rays of the double GHZ E there. Each code contains at most two of the four orthogonal E rays, and when two occur they define the same logical Pauli axis. Thus at most one of three axes is forbidden per endpoint. Expand F using these independent choices; the four A factors and the four BC product factors are independent. A pair of the four coordinate labels differing in only one bit gives a simple Bell on its corresponding link.

Let t,e,f count retained ordinary, E and F entries in the target component as in no-full-double-ghz-simple.md. As before 2e+1≤t+f, so e=4 forces t=5,f=4. With all terms together, each local dimension is five by the splitting bound. E and F have four-dimensional A codes in this five-space and hence the same code. Erasing that code gives d_B+d_C≤7 for all ordinary factors as in no-full-double-ghz-overlap.md. However the same note proves H=E+F (with weights absorbed) has rank at least four on both peripheral cuts since F has rank two there. Both local supports of H are in the ordinary spans containing the corresponding targets, a contradiction.

If t=5 but some exceptional entries are omitted, the two omitted subtensors alone must cancel. Each omitted subset has A rank equal to its size, so the counts agree and are positive. For the E subtensor those same counts give both peripheral ranks. The omitted F entries must therefore have independent factors separately on B and C as well. Since both A families are independent, their corresponding remote spans are equal. In the E matched BC span all product rays are its coordinate products by diagonal matrix rank. The chosen F factors avoid those coordinates, a contradiction.

Now t≤4 and e≤3. If e≤2 the retained E entries give at most one simple GHZ or product. If f=4 the F terms restore the path atom, so at most six terms remain, impossible. If f=3 at least two of its three labels agree on one peripheral bit and combine into a simple Bell, leaving one product. If f≤2 retain the entries as products. In each of these latter cases there are at most seven terms with only simple resources; the established exclusions apply. For e=3 one needs 7≤t+f. If f=3 this forces t=4, leaving one E and one F entry plus one ordinary product outside the target component. A weighted sum of two products that is a nonzero product requires proportional factors on at least two parties, contrary to the avoidance on B and C. The only remaining case has f=4. Three E entries split into one simple GHZ on an affine line of the double copy and one product. Together with the restored F they give t+3 terms. The unrestricted lower bound forces t=4, hence a representation with five ordinary products, one simple GHZ and the same two-Bell path.

This is an implication for existence: a candidate with a double GHZ and a two-Bell path yields a candidate with a simple GHZ and the same path. The count of double GHZ exceptions drops from one to zero, so using the reduction in a minimum-count search introduces no cycle. The latter profile remains open under this argument.
