# Exact finite decision problem for seven arbitrary complex stabilizer atoms

Let V be the nine-dimensional binary vector space and let G(x) be the product of three CCZ signs on disjoint triples. Enumerate seven supports by arbitrary nine-by-nine binary matrices H_j and nine-bit offsets b_j, testing H_j x=b_j. On each support allow every phase i raised to the exponent
    sum_u l_ju x_u + 2 sum_{u<v} q_juv x_u x_v (mod 4),
where l ranges over Z/4 and q over F2. Extend by zero off support. Form their 512-by-7 value matrix A. Constant phase and normalization are absorbed in the scalar of the column. Arbitrary affine supports and their intrinsic quadratic stabilizer phases occur: on each support take free original coordinate positions for intrinsic coordinates and extend the intrinsic phase polynomial using only those positions. Padding a list with zero coefficients permits dependent or unused columns. No permutation symmetry, conjugation condition, or product structure is required.

**Exact field reduction.** The prime 4099 is 3 modulo 4, so F=F_4099[t]/(t²+1) is a field. Reduce Gaussian integers into F by sending i to t. Every minor of size d≤8 in A or [A|G] is a Gaussian integer of modulus at most d^(d/2)≤4096 by the row-norm determinant bound. It vanishes modulo 4099 exactly when both of its integer components vanish. Thus for every candidate list, and every subset of rows, both matrix ranks agree over F and C. A solution of A c=G exists over C exactly when one exists over F. To lift a successful list, select an independent column basis, take a nonsingular square row minor on that basis, solve those rows over Q(i), and verify the full 512-row equality. Modular coefficients need not be lifted directly.

**Finite counterexample-guided construction.** Maintain a sampled row set S. Ask for H,b,l,q and seven field coefficients satisfying all sampled equalities. A failure of satisfiability rules out the entire family. For a model keep its columns, evaluate all 512 entries, and test consistency by linear algebra (refitting coefficients). If consistent return its exact lift. Otherwise let r=rank(A), choose r independent rows of A and a row where [A|G] exceeds their augmented span. This produces ≤8 rows already inconsistent for these columns. Add the rows to S and repeat. At least one row is new because the sampled equations were satisfied. The number of unsuccessful full-candidate checks is bounded by 512 minus the initial sample size. Solver search per iteration can still be expensive. This is a complete decision encoding, not a runtime claim or a positive or negative answer.

**Alternative for bit-vector backends.** A Gaussian minor vanishes modulo both square roots of -1 in each of F_17 and F_257 exactly when both components are divisible by 4369, hence zero under the same bound. Use roots ±4 and ±16 respectively. The complex rank of either matrix equals its maximum rank among these four images. Compare the two maxima; checking consistency separately in each small field can incorrectly reject a true representation whose column rank drops there.
