# Five-product remote erasure

If χ(v)≥3, g⊗v needs more than five stabilizers factoring across the cut. Indeed for k≤5 such terms Sylvester gives d_X+d_R≤k+1 and d_R≥3. If d_X≤2 the line through g contains at most two stabilizer rays; grouping by their independent directions costs ≥3 terms per group. Otherwise k=5 and d_X=3. Every three local rays must span g, or a covector leaves ≤2 terms for v. No repeated ray is possible: two copies plus any other must span g, forcing all into one line through g.

The companion five-local-triples.py reuses the 1080 rays, exact quotient algorithm and orbit representatives of product-plane-control.py. In each three-dimensional space through g containing ≥5 local rays it tests each five-subset. A triple fails precisely if it lies on a projective line missing g, since three independent rays span the ambient space. Quotienting by each first ray identifies every line; groups containing g are not forbidden. Exactness and plane coverage are as in seven-product-exclusion.md. The locked one-thread run returned 49/82/8/21 plane representatives of sizes 5/6/7/8 and no robust five-set.

If K is a remote stabilizer operation killing both exceptions in a seven-list with five ordinary products, its target KG2 consequently has rank ≤2 or vanishes. Apply this to a shared signed remote Pauli's opposite outcome. The two-copy analogue of pauli-outcome-four.md gives exact rank two iff all active labels are pure Z, or only one block is active and a real shifted label is projected to its rare outcome. Indeed rank is at least two by the separating local bra leaving g. Project a two-term representation into the P outcome. For any stabilizer Q of either survivor, a separated local P_i,Q_i would give a contraction of the opposite Q outcome to g, impossible with at most one atom. The local-pair catalog therefore forces factorization on each active block and local definite signs exactly as in the three-copy proof. In the nonlocal case both permitted patterns appear, one atom per pattern, so every local outcome is a stabilizer. In the local case the untouched factors must be independent, forcing the active factor to equal the projected target. The known local-outcome classification and product constructions finish the equivalence.

Thus every shared nonlocal remote Pauli is pure Z on both blocks. Any subgroup containing a nonlocal element is wholly pure Z: multiplying a nonlocal pure Z by a shifted local label would make a forbidden shifted nonlocal label. An equal eight-dimensional remote code for two locally full exceptions has three independent checks. If all are local they lie on one partner or their product would be nonlocal. Three checks there fix a single ray, forcing the two exceptions to factor across that same original cut, previously excluded. Hence equal full remote codes are computational, and cannot carry a remote Bell resource, whose two check restrictions anticommute at each endpoint.
