# One equal remote matching excludes two full-rank exceptions

Assume that on the X cut both exceptional atoms have full local rank and the same remote code D, and that D has full local supports on both Y and Z. These hypotheses include two full-rank atoms on all three cuts sharing their remote code on even one cut. By five-product-remote-erasure.md D is computational. Its eight positions form a matching (y_r,z_r) between the two eight-coordinate alphabets. Choose affine three-bit indices r such that both y_r,z_r are affine functions of r. Affine Clifford decoding of D removes its three fixed ancilla bits. The resulting maximally entangled three-qubit stabilizer across X|r is the Choi state of a three-qubit Clifford up to scalar: its stabilizer labels give the graph of a symplectic isomorphism, with signs fixed by a Pauli. Thus for the first and second exceptional atoms the X columns in matching order are respectively U|r> and V|r> up to global scalar, for Cliffords U,V.

Write h_r=αU|r>+βV|r>, let H be the weighted sum of the two exceptions, and let m count nonzero h_r. Across the Y cut, H has rank m: its active local coordinates are distinct, and the opposite columns h_r⊗|z_r> are nonzero and independent. For m=8, subtracting the product target leaves rank ≥7. For m<8, the target local and remote factors lie outside both respective spaces, since Y and Z have missing coordinates. The rank of G3−H is then m+1 and is at most five. Consequently m≤4.

If m=4, the remote space of G3−H across Y is exactly the five-dimensional span of g_X⊗g_Z and the four h_r⊗|z_r>. Choose a covector on Z zero on all four active |z_r> and nonzero on g. On this space the contraction has nonzero image contained in span(g_X). The five ordinary product stabilizers spanning the matrix G3−H must span this entire remote space with their XZ product factors, because the matrix rank is five. Each such factor contracts either to zero or to an X stabilizer; it must therefore contract to zero, contradicting the nonzero image.

Thus m≤3. At least five distinct indices r satisfy U*V|r>=−α/β |r>. Already one shared computational eigenray makes U*V preserve the computational unsigned Pauli stabilizer group, since it maps the stabilizer space of that ray to itself. The induced action on basis labels is affine and fixes at least five points, hence fixes all eight. The relative Clifford is diagonal, with phases a common scalar times a fourth-root Clifford chirp on three bits. Thus each nonzero h_r is a multiple of U|r>, a stabilizer, and H expands into m ordinary block products. The eight-product lower bound forces m≥3. Five cancellations and three discrepancies are impossible: constant relative phase on five points forces constant imaginary parity and leaves a binary quadratic sign whose discrepancy has even weight. This proves exclusion without requiring equal remote codes on the other two cuts.
