# Limits on a sole entangled atom

Suppose a seven-term list for g⊗g⊗g has six block-product stabilizer atoms and one entangled stabilizer E. Across each original block vs the other two the Schmidt rank of E is at most seven, since E is the difference between a product target and six product atoms. Stabilizer Schmidt ranks are powers of two, so each block has Schmidt rank at most four. Its local support C_i is a proper stabilizer code in eight dimensions. The local target g lies outside C_i because g has no nonidentity Pauli stabilizer. There exists a linear projection onto C_i that kills g: extend a basis of C_i by g and then to the full block space. Applying this projection to the tensor representation of E kills the target term, preserves E, and leaves at most six ordinary product tensors. Hence the ordinary complex tensor rank of E is at most six. These projections need only preserve ordinary tensor products.

The tripartite stabilizer normal form under local Cliffords is a tensor product of local stabilizers, Bell pairs on party pairs, and three-party GHZ states. Let m_AB,m_BC,m_CA be Bell counts and z the GHZ count. Each incident Bell count plus z contributes one to the logarithm of the corresponding local Schmidt rank; the sum at each party is at most two. If z=2 there are no Bell pairs. If z=1 at most one Bell pair is present. If z=0 the degree-at-most-two multigraph on three vertices has at most two edges unless it is the triangle with one Bell pair on each side. The triangle is excluded: its tensor has coordinates sum_{i,j,k}|ij>|jk>|ki>, the two-by-two matrix multiplication tensor of ordinary complex rank seven, even after adjoining local fixed ancillas. Thus in every allowed case E is locally Clifford-equivalent, up to local stabilizer ancillas, to at most two elementary Bell/GHZ factors. In particular E can be expanded as at most four block-product stabilizer rays. [Bravyi, Fattal and Gottesman](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0504208) · [Winograd](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0024379571900097)

There is also a necessary local incidence condition specific to the six product atoms. In every block i and for every triple J of these six atoms, g lies in C_i plus the span of their three local factors. Otherwise choose a covector vanishing on that sum but not on g. It kills E and the indicated three product atoms, leaving at most three stabilizer rays for g⊗g, contradicting its rank four. Therefore in the quotient by C_i every three projected factors span the nonzero projected g. The projected factor span has dimension at most three: four linearly independent projected factors would give four different three-dimensional coordinate subspaces with trivial intersection. No sole-entangled construction is asserted for the remaining profiles.
