# Overlap bounds for a double GHZ exception

Let E be a double GHZ and let F have local rank r≤4 on a chosen cut. Write l and j for the dimensions of the intersections of their local and remote Schmidt support codes. When j>0, all remote checks commute and agree on common signs. Their common signed subgroup has dimension 4−log₂ r+log₂ j: the remote dimension is 64 and the code dimensions are 4 and r. The remote checks of E have local-only subspaces of dimension one on each partner because the corresponding marginal ranks of E are both four. If the common subgroup has dimension at least three, it contains a two-dimensional subspace avoiding both local-only axes. To see this, within a three-dimensional subspace take the kernel of a functional evaluating to one on every nonzero local-only axis present, possible since axes on opposite parties are independent. All three nonidentity checks in the kernel are nonlocal. The erasure theorem forces them to be computational, so their projections are injective on both partners, in contradiction with two-matched-syndrome-exclusion.md. Therefore j=0 for r=1,2 and j≤1 for r=4.

Write H for the weighted sum of the two exceptions. Factoring its flattening via the direct sum of the two Schmidt spaces gives rank H≥4+r−l−j by Sylvester's inequality: the combined local matrix has rank 4+r−l and the combined remote matrix has rank 4+r−j, with intermediate dimension 4+r and invertible weights. In particular H has local rank at least four for r≤2. It has rank at least three for r=4, rising to at least five when the two local rank-four codes differ, since then l≤2.

**Two equal rank-four local codes are impossible.** Suppose E and F share their four-dimensional local codes on B and C. Erasing B's common proper code while retaining g_B expresses G_AC in k=4 or 5 ordinary products. The full ordinary factor spans on A and C contain their targets and have d_A+d_C≤11−k≤7 by the rank-one Sylvester argument in equal-full-remote-terminal.md. Erasing C gives d_A+d_B≤7. Each B,C span contains the local H support of dimension at least three and its target outside the common code, so d_B,d_C≥4. Thus d_A≤3, and H has A rank at most three. The preceding bound on A forces r=4 and equal local A codes. Erasing that third common code gives d_B+d_C≤7, a contradiction.
