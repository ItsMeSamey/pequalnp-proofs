# Exact physical lifting for a matched five-point configuration

Fix the three exceptional nine-qubit stabilizer rays and a matched labelled configuration satisfying three-exception-projective-erasure.md. Use raw fourth-root representatives and Gaussian-rational quotient maps computed by elimination from the assigned support codes. In each quotient normalize every local projective point by its first nonzero coordinate. Let v_j be the resulting four projected product vectors. Solve for their unique coefficients c_j in the quotient target. List every local stabilizer ray projecting to each prescribed point, scaling the representative to have exactly the normalized image. Let U_j consist of every product of the three candidate lifts with the jth label, and write N_j for its size.

Compute a matrix K whose kernel equals the span of the three original exceptional vectors. Form image lists D_j={K(c_j u):u in U_j}, retaining the preimages. The configuration has a physical lift with those three exceptional rays if and only if one can choose d_j in each D_j so that their sum is K(G3). Necessity follows by applying K. For sufficiency recover product preimages and solve the three-column residual system. Verify the equality at all 512 rows. The known rank-seven lower bound ensures all seven coefficients are nonzero. The formula checks the exceptional rays including their phases, beyond their support information.

Try the three pair partitions, choosing ij|kl minimizing N_i N_j+N_k N_l. Sort the smaller list of pair sums exactly and look up complements from the other side. One preimage per identical sum suffices to find a witness. This takes O((N_i N_j+N_k N_l) log(2+min(N_i N_j,N_k N_l))) vector comparisons after O(sum N_j) matrix-vector products, storing the smaller pair list and the individual image lists. Each comparison uses at most 512 Gaussian-rational coordinate comparisons in this application. Denominators and numerators throughout have polynomial bit length in input size and coordinate dimension by the determinant bound for rational linear systems. No common denominator across entire pair lists is required. The bound is per fixed physical triple and matched configuration; generating such triples, finding configurations and handling failed erasure premises incur separate costs.
