# Finitely many rank-three products on two physical CCZ secants

Fix two local secants L₁,L₂ from the [fifteen native pairs](local-code-secants.md). A ray other than the two stabilizer endpoints is interior. Each such ray has nullity three, as proved in [the two-erasure lifting note](two-erasures-exact-secants.md). The only stabilizer rays in the four-dimensional L₁⊗L₂ are its four native endpoint products. Indeed an entangled atom there would have Schmidt rank two and marginal supports L₁,L₂, impossible since these contain g outside every proper stabilizer code. The product rays follow by the local secant certificate.

Any independent stabilizer triple accounts for at most two interior projective pairs u,v with u⊗v in its span. If the triple spans a subspace of L₁⊗L₂, its three endpoint products miss a nonzero coordinate of an interior product. Otherwise the intersection is at most a projective line. A line meets the two-by-two determinant quadric of product vectors in at most two points unless it is a ruling (two independent products summing to a product must share one factor). A ruling with a fixed endpoint has no interior pair. If it has fixed interior u, parametrize it as u⊗w for all w on the other secant. Its three linear coefficient functions in the independent triple are nonzero since [interior products require rank at least three](nullity-two-product-rank.md). Their projective roots are distinct, since two coincident zeroes would give a single stabilizer u⊗w. At each root, [rank-two product classification](nonstabilizer-product-rank-two.md) then forces w to be stabilizer, because u has nullity three. The secant has only two stabilizer rays, contradicting three roots. Thus only finitely many rank-three interior pairs occur globally.

For a canonical branch with common weight parameter, write the vector up to scalar as (G₀+tG₁)⊗(H₀+tH₁), where the two canonical weighted endpoint pairs each sum to g and t=−p/(1−p)≠0 is finite. Each endpoint entry is Gaussian integer of modulus at most two. The preceding theorem implies a fixed independent stabilizer triple cannot contain this entire conic. Some four-row determinant of its three independently unit-gauged stabilizer columns and the appended branch column is therefore a nonzero polynomial A+Bt+Ct² vanishing at an admissible t. By the three-by-three Hadamard bound 3^(3/2), expanding in the last column gives |A|,|C|≤16·3^(3/2)<84 and |B|≤32·3^(3/2)<167. For any Gaussian rational solution, strip zero end coefficients and apply unique factorization in ℤ[i]: the primitive numerator and denominator of t have moduli at most 167. In the linked two-erasure application the quotient coordinate systems and the individual g decompositions are Gaussian rational, so p and t are Gaussian rational. These universal bounds do not assert realization of any candidate.
