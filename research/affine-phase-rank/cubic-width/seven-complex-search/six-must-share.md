# Every complex six-atom representation must have a shared signed Pauli

Assume six normalized stabilizer atoms represent G3 and no two share a signed nonidentity stabilizer. Their overlaps are zero or have modulus 1/sqrt(512). Choose the fourth-root gauge on each support of size 2^{d_i}. A nonzero overlap before support normalization is a Gaussian integer of norm 2^{d_i+d_j−9}. Its eighth-root phase has parity d_i+d_j−9 modulo two by unique factorization at 1+i. Multiplying each column by exp(i pi (d_i mod 2)/4) therefore converts every nonzero overlap to an odd eighth-root phase. The weighted cosine bound in odd-phase-gram.md applies for arbitrary supports, yielding lambda_min >=1−[cot(pi/24)−1]/32 >3241/4096 (use cot x<1/x and pi>3.14).

For local three-qubit fidelity let H denote the maximizers (fidelity 9/16), each a full real sign differing from g at its own point after orientation, and M the next-highest (fidelity 1/2), namely the 14 normalized restrictions of g to affine planes. Every other local stabilizer has fidelity <=5/16. Indeed a flat of dimension <=1 has mass <=1/4, and on a plane g restricts to a stabilizer sign, so a distinct local ray there has at most half of that plane's mass. For a full real chirp, the eight Walsh coefficients of a cubic sign on three bits are odd multiples of 2; Parseval gives one of magnitude 6 and seven of magnitude 2. A genuinely nonreal full chirp averages two orthogonal such real fidelities with the same quadratic polar, hence at most (36+4)/128=5/16.

Every nonproduct atom has fidelity <=125/1024. If a one-block entangled cut has Schmidt rank 4 or 8, the aligned-code bounds in rank-six-lower.md give at most (3/8)(9/16)^2 or (1/sqrt(8))(9/16)^2, both smaller. Otherwise all local support projectors have rank 1 or 2. Project each local g onto its Schmidt support, whose squared mass is <=9/16 or <=5/8, respectively. The product of these three squared norms is at most (5/8)^3. The three normalized projected vectors form a product, so their squared overlap with the atom is at most 1/2 from a nontrivial (flat) Schmidt cut. This gives the claimed bound without classifying entangled triples.

A fully product atom of fidelity >1/8 must consist solely of H and M with at least one H. The only product atoms of fidelity exactly 1/8 are MMM: any other local factor gives fidelity at most (5/16)(9/16)^2<125/1024. Two H rays at a local position share a signed nonidentity stabilizer because their overlap is 1 or 1/2. An H and M share such a stabilizer unless the exceptional H point lies on the M plane, since their overlap magnitudes are 1/sqrt(2) and 1/sqrt(8), respectively. For two M rays, any equal or transverse planes give local overlap 1 or 1/2, so only complementary planes can avoid local sharing. In that case the signed plane-normal stabilizers have opposite signs. Two candidate atoms with complementary M planes at two positions then share the product of the two plane-normal Paulis, impossible.

Thus at most one MMM atom occurs (any second requires three complementary positions), and the H-position sets of the high product atoms are disjoint. If HHH occurs there is no other high product. Otherwise at most two high products occur: three would require one H at each distinct position across HMM atoms; at any position the two M planes must be complementary while both would have to contain the exceptional point of the third atom's H. Among two high products at most one is HHM.

In the HHH case the total of the six squared overlaps with the target is at most (729+512+4*500)/4096=3241/4096. Otherwise it is at most (648+576+512+3*500)/4096=3236/4096, allowing one HHM, one HMM and one MMM and padding missing high terms with the same numerical bounds. Both are below the minimum Gram eigenvalue. The projection identity therefore contradicts membership in the span.

Consequently any six-term complex representation must contain a pair sharing a signed nonidentity Pauli. The local pair catalog independently forces any such Pauli to be real in each active block and each of the other four atoms to have Schmidt rank <=2 there. These combined necessary conditions do not rule out all six-term representations.
