# One physical Z with four active terms

The conditional targets have the same norm and are proportional to s_b tensor G₂ with unit spectators s_0=|++> and s_1=CZ|++>. Each gives all four outcomes of the other's stabilizer basis with absolute amplitude 1/2. G₂ has rank four.

Every term in a minimal expansion with a stabilizer spectator factors that spectator. For a violating term take a spectator generator Z whose required sign it lacks. If it has the opposite sign then the correct projection kills it. Otherwise its stabilizer contains K anticommuting with Z. Project onto the K sign opposite the term and contract the spectator against its target ray. The K component vanishes on the target by anticommutation. The target data survives up to scalar while the violating term vanishes, contradicting minimality.

If four terms occur in branch b, they therefore all factor s_b. A shared term's opposite slice is a Pauli translate of equal norm (parallel stabilizer fibers under a computational-bit measurement). Its spectator lies in the same basis as s_b. Postselect the opposite branch on each of the four vectors of this basis: the target data survives with rank four, and only three previously inactive terms are available. Thus each postselection needs its own shared term. All four shared terms must occur in the opposite branch in different spectator sectors, and all three formerly inactive terms must survive every such sector. Only (4,7) and its reverse can have a four-active branch.

Normalize each conditional target and each data ray. If α_j is the original data coefficient, its opposite-sector data coefficient has modulus 2|α_j|: its shared slice keeps the same norm and deterministic spectator, whereas the target is attenuated by 1/2. Phases are unrestricted. The three opposite-only terms have constant conditional magnitude across the four sectors, by stabilizer flatness.

For any four-product expansion of G₂ across original blocks, both factor matrices have rank at least two, and their rank sum is at most five by the rank inequality for their matrix product of rank one. One side therefore lies on a secant line containing g. By local-code-secants.md there are just two stabilizer rays on this line. Group by them: each opposite group must span g and uses at least two terms, hence exactly two. The resulting unit-ray coefficient modulus is the product of its local secant moduli. A full chirp has modulus one and a point or plane has modulus 1/√2, from the explicit normalized local secant identities. These support sizes are unchanged by Pauli translation. Thus doubling is impossible if both compared minimum G₂ frames are product-only; this includes non-Cartesian groupings and repeated local rays. No analogous coefficient invariant for entangled data frames is assumed.

For a five-active branch any two contributors' reduced spectator supports cannot both be orthogonal to a stabilizer bra overlapping the target spectator: this would leave at most three rays for G₂. Consequently two factored spectators from the same basis mutually unbiased to the target are forbidden even if equal. Five contributors alone are locally possible, by splitting one term of a minimal expansion with complementary stabilizer projections that both survive. The count argument excludes (4,5) and (5,4), without excluding general (5,5), (5,6) or (5,7).
