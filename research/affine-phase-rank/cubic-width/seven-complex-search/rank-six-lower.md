# Unrestricted complex stabilizer rank of the three-copy cubic is at least six

Let g have normalized amplitudes 2^{-3/2}(-1)^{xyz}. We use the independently verified chi(g tensor g)=4. All atoms below are normalized pure qubit stabilizer states. Their coefficients may be arbitrary complex numbers.

**Outcome lemma.** For every Hermitian nonidentity Pauli P on the nine qubits, both (I±P)g^3 have rank at least four, with powers denoting tensor products. Choose a nonidentity local three-bit factor R of P. Every nonidentity Pauli has expectation magnitude at most 1/2 on g: a pure nontrivial Z averages to zero, and flipping a nonzero bit string reverses exactly two entries of the unnormalized sign, yielding magnitude 1/2 or zero after applying a Z character. Thus Rg is not proportional to g. Choose two positions with unequal pointwise ratios Rg/g. A two-point normalized stabilizer bra, with relative fourth-root phase chosen to annihilate Rg, has nonzero overlap with g. Contraction in this block takes either projected target to a nonzero multiple of g^2 and maps each atom to a scalar times a stabilizer state or zero. This proves the lemma. An at-most-four-term list is impossible: take a nonidentity Pauli stabilizing any listed atom and use its opposite outcome, leaving at most three atoms.

**Near orthogonality at length five.** In a five-term list representing g^3 no two atoms can share a signed nonidentity stabilizer, since projection onto its opposite outcome would leave at most three. For stabilizer states s,t, the projector identity |s><s|=2^{-9}sum_{P in S_s} P gives squared overlap 2^{-9} times the signed character sum over their common unsigned Pauli labels. If their signs disagree on any common label the sum is zero; otherwise it equals the number of common labels. Without a common signed nonidentity Pauli the only nonzero possibility is 1/512. The five-column Gram matrix therefore satisfies lambda_min>=1−4/sqrt(512) by Gershgorin.

**Fidelity gap.** For a local three-qubit stabilizer state, support size at most four yields squared overlap with g at most 1/2 by Cauchy-Schwarz. For a full-support chirp with a nonconstant imaginary parity, its unnormalized overlap has at most four real and four imaginary summands, so squared normalized overlap is at most 32/64=1/2. A projectively real full chirp is a quadratic sign; it cannot agree exactly with the nonquadratic g. Its largest overlap squared is 36/64=9/16, attained (orienting the sign) precisely when it differs from the cubic sign at one position. This upper value is attained by the constant sign. All remaining local fidelities are <=1/2. Two local maximizing rays have overlap modulus 1 if equal or 1/2 if distinct, since then their oriented signs differ on two coordinates.

For an atom fully product across the three blocks, fidelity is at most (9/16)^3, and at most (1/2)(9/16)^2 unless all three factors maximize. Two all-maximizing product atoms have mutual overlap modulus at least (1/2)^3>1/sqrt(512), hence at most one can occur.

For every nonproduct atom, some one-block versus two-block cut has Schmidt rank R=2^e, e=1,2 or 3. Stabilizer Schmidt coefficients are flat and both Schmidt support projectors are stabilizer codes of rank R (e.g. compute the reduced stabilizer projector by tracing its Pauli expansion). Thus its fidelity with g tensor g^2 is at most P_e(g)P_e(g^2)/R by Cauchy-Schwarz, where P_e is the largest probability mass on a rank-2^e stabilizer code. Bravyi et al., Theorem 5 and Corollary 3, imply g is stabilizer-aligned, g^2 is stabilizer-aligned, F_0(g^2)=(9/16)^2, and P_e(g^2)<=sqrt(R)(9/16)^2. Their F_e definition uses precisely the factor 1/sqrt(R). See https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.00128 . On g itself the Pauli projector expansion and the local expectation bound give P_1(g)<=(1+3/2)/4=5/8 and P_2(g)<=(1+1/2)/2=3/4; P_3(g)=1. Each of (5/8)/sqrt(2), (3/4)/2, and 1/sqrt(8) is strictly below 1/2. Hence every nonproduct fidelity is strictly below (1/2)(9/16)^2. This treats arbitrary entangled atoms without a support or phase-pattern restriction.

**Contradiction.** For A with the five atom columns and b=A* g^3, the sum of the five fidelities is ||b||²<=(9/16)^3+4(1/2)(9/16)^2=3321/4096. This is strictly below 1−4/sqrt(512), since 775²>524288. If g^3 belonged to their span, 1=b*(A*A)^{-1}b<=||b||²/lambda_min<1. This excludes five. Together with the eight-term product upper bound it gives 6<=chi(g^3)<=8. It does not decide six versus seven versus eight. No claim of publication priority.
