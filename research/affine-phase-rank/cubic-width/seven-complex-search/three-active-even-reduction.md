# Three active block controls after a shared signed Z

Use an affine coordinate change fixing the exceptional CCZ point in each block to take its nonzero Z normal to one coordinate. In a coordinate system (t_i,x_i,y_i) the target sign in block i agrees, up to a common Clifford quadratic, with (-1)^(t_i x_i y_i). Choose the sign sector shared by two atoms and call it E; its four-point affine complement is O. The two shared atoms restrict to arbitrary eight-variable stabilizer columns A,B on E (write two independent control coordinates and the six free fiber bits u). The four other atoms project individually onto the four O sectors by the sector-rigidity lemma. Choose their column gauges so that the weighted atom at its own o in O equals the target sign q_o(u).

The support of such a weighted atom contains the entire six-dimensional fiber at o. Its controls form an affine flat intersecting O only at o; consequently the control set is either the singleton o or a pair {o,e} with e in E. On the second fiber its weight is i^h (-1)^(l·u) q_o(u), for h mod 4 and l a six-bit vector, since the mixed terms of a stabilizer phase are doubled. Conversely every such extension is obtained by making the binary selector of the second fiber an affine coordinate and using it for the linear phase h and the bilinear term with l. Thus each of the four lifts has exactly 1025 possible even contributions, including the absent one.

For fixed lifts define R on E by subtracting the four contributions from the target sign. Its entries are Gaussian integers of modulus at most 5. The remaining condition is exactly R=aA+bB. When A,B are independent choose a nonsingular two-row minor. Its determinant is a unit, twice a unit, or (1+i) times a unit, since its products are in D={0,1,-1,i,-i}. Cramer's rule gives 2a,2b Gaussian integers of modulus at most 20. If a single column suffices its scalar is an entry of R times a unit; zero unused coefficients handle this case.

This yields an exact finite constraint problem with four 1025-way lift choices, two scalar Gaussian integers in the radius-20 disk (representing twice the weights), and two arbitrary eight-variable stabilizer atoms in the affine-support/quadratic-phase encoding of reduction.md. For every even input require 2R=(2a)A+(2b)B, with ordinary signed small-integer components. A model lifts to the 512-entry identity. No runtime bound or resolution is inferred.

For checking a proposed set of lifts plus weights, enumerate the 25 local symbol pairs in D². If both weights are nonzero and a/b is outside {(v'-v)/(u-u'): u,u',v,v' in D, u≠u'}, at every coordinate the observed R entry uniquely determines the two symbols, or has no solution. This generic-weight test recovers both full columns in linear time; verify affine supports and fourth-root quadratic phases separately. Exceptional ratios require retaining the local alternatives.
