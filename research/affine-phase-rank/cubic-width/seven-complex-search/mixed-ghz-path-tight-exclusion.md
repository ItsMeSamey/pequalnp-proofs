# Closing the tight mixed-GHZ and two-link path orientation

Continue mixed-ghz-bell-path.md (61974eb559b78b3655e264fa7c5cbdea7620324b9908c6ac94b58d581e7fdca1). In the remaining (0011,0110) orientation A is the center of the Bell path and one endpoint of the mixed GHZ state's additional Bell link AB. The tight dimension bound makes the two four-dimensional A Schmidt codes identical. Call the weighted exceptional tensors E (mixed) and F (path), and H=E+F. Write U_B for the mixed four-dimensional B code, V_B for the path two-dimensional B code, and U_C,V_C for their respective two-dimensional C codes.

The common A code supplies a shared signed local Pauli. By the two-local-marker exclusion in two-local-shared-markers.md there is no shared signed local Pauli on B or C. Thus V_B is not contained in U_B: the unique nontrivial signed defining check of U_B would otherwise also fix V_B. In addition U_C and V_C have zero vector-space intersection. Indeed if a nonzero vector lies in both, their signed stabilizers are jointly compatible; the union of their two rank-two check groups generates an abelian Pauli label space of rank at most three on the three physical qubits. Hence they share at least one nonidentity signed check, already excluded.

Across B the remote AC images of the two exceptional flattenings lie in disjoint spaces, because their C supports are disjoint. The input kernel of H is consequently the intersection of the two input kernels, and its B support is U_B+V_B (conjugating both sides if using a transpose convention). Thus H has Schmidt rank at least five across B.

Across C the mixed remote image on AB lies wholly in the subspace with B support U_B. Every nonzero vector of the path remote AB image has B support exactly V_B: in path decoding coordinates it consists of a fixed AB Bell pair tensored with a nonzero vector in the second A link register and fixed ancillary states. Therefore the two remote images are disjoint since V_B is not contained in U_B. The input kernel is again the intersection, so H has C support U_C+V_C of dimension four.

Let b,c be the marginal B and C dimensions of the five ordinary product factors alone, including factors of any ordinary term not retained in the next test. A covector killing the common A code and retaining g exists. It erases both exceptions, leaving a nonzero g⊗g on BC represented by k=4 or 5 of the ordinary products (at least four by the two-copy theorem). The two surviving marginal dimensions sum to at most k+1 by Sylvester. Restoring the omitted 5−k ordinary factors increases the dimension sum by at most 2(5−k), so b+c≤11−k≤7. Both ordinary marginal spans contain g, as it belongs to each surviving marginal span. The original identity therefore places H=G₃ minus the ordinary terms inside the ordinary marginal spans on B and on C. Its two rank lower bounds force b+c≥5+4=9, a contradiction.

Together with the companion's spectator-center exclusion this rules out both (0011,0110) and (0011,1100) with arbitrary independent encoders. The terminal criterion using two distinct shared local checks is specific to five ordinary block products and two exceptions.
