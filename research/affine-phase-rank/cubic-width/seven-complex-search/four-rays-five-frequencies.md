# Four stabilizer rays have at most four active indices in W₃

The data target at an active control s is a nonzero multiple of the three-block product of q_{s_i}, where q_b(x,y)=(-1)^{bxy}. No four stabilizer rays with arbitrary complex coefficients can represent a W₃ vector with five or more nonzero coefficients. Use the two-block theorem that four arbitrary nonzero amplitudes in W₂ require four stabilizer rays (w2-proper-data.md and w2-full-data.md).

## Local product from a zero-free plane

Fix an affine plane P of active controls. A dropped block j is compatible if the remaining two coordinates parametrize P, equivalently j occurs in its normal. The unsigned local stabilizer spaces L₀,L₁ of q₀,q₁ are transverse graphs of zero and of a nonsingular alternating form. Their eigenbases are mutually unbiased. For each atom its nonempty data fibers have a fixed unsigned stabilizer label space L. For a fixed Hermitian Pauli p in L the eigenvalue ε(s) on these fibers is an affine sign. Indeed use an affine section of the support and fixed basis for the fiber direction. In the resulting coordinates the Clifford phase has even mixed control–data quadratic terms in its mod-four exponent. Varying controls moves the section by an affine shift and changes the relative data phase by a character with control-affine label. Extending that character to ambient data gives control-affine Pauli displacements relating fibers up to global phase. The corresponding conjugation sign against p is affine on the occurrence flat and extends affinely to all controls.

Suppose p_j lies outside the union of the L_b for the values of s_j attained on P. There exists a local stabilizer bra η overlapping every such q_b but orthogonal to every p_j q_b. For a single b take η=q_b. Otherwise identify L₀ and L₁ with dual binary 2-spaces under the symplectic pairing and write p_j=(u,v) with both coordinates nonzero. Among the two a∈L₀ with a·v=1 choose one different from u. The unique nonzero b'∈L₁ annihilating a has b'·u=1. The commuting a,b' both anticommute with p_j. Take η their joint eigenstate matching the sign of q₀ on a and q₁ on b'. Its stabilizer intersects each L_b in exactly the prescribed line, with matching sign, ensuring both overlaps. Anticommutation gives the two zero overlaps.

Apply the projector proportional to I−ε(s)p and then η on j. This kills the chosen ray. The first step is one Pauli postselection because ε(s)p is a signed joint control–data Pauli; the second is a stabilizer bra. On P the p term vanishes against the local target, whereas the identity term has nonzero amplitude at all four controls. Restricting to P and decoding leaves a W₂ vector supported on all four indices expressed with at most three rays, impossible.

Thus π_j L is contained in the union, hence lies in one of its at most two subspaces by linearity. Its symplectic orthogonal within the local four-dimensional label space equals the local-only part of L since L=L^⊥. This local-only part lies in π_j L. As π_j L lies inside a two-dimensional Lagrangian, its dimension is exactly two and it equals that Lagrangian. The two local stabilizers force every nonempty data fiber to split as a product across j, with its local ray in a fixed basis L_b. Its signed label may change with s. If s_j is constant on P the basis must be exactly that control's L_b.

## Excluding five points

Pick five active indices. Among the ten pair sums two agree (seven nonzero differences); their distinct pairs must be disjoint and determine a plane P. This plane is unique since two different planes have union size at least six. Call the fifth point the outsider. On the typed blocks (those in the normal) attach to every atom the tuple of its fixed basis bits. Ray types are global; occurrence can vary.

At a control whose typed bits w differ from every present ray's type, assign each present ray to one mismatched block. On a block assigned at most three rays an unused basis vector in the opposite local basis kills all assigned rays and overlaps q_{w_j}. On an unassigned block use the target bra. If no bin holds four, this product bra kills all rays while preserving the target. Thus four present rays are mandatory and every assignment must use a single block, forcing all four mismatch sets to be the same singleton (otherwise assign two rays to distinct blocks). The four local rays on that block must then exhaust the four distinct opposite basis elements. Projecting onto each separately isolates the corresponding ray and preserves a nonzero target on the remaining blocks.

**Weight three.** All blocks are typed. Some one of the five controls differs from all four types. The preceding argument forces four identical types, differing from this control on exactly one bit. Every active control must now equal that common type or one of its three neighbors, too few.

**Weight two.** Each ray splits across both typed blocks and hence has a fixed local unsigned space on the remaining third block. The plane contains two different typed pairs w,w', each present with both values of the third bit. Each needs at least two matching rays. If exactly one matches, kill all at most three mismatching rays by the same assignment with opposite-basis bras, separately at either third bit. The target survives so the single match must be present and must have third-block space equal to both L₀ and L₁, impossible. If none matches, isolating each of the four rays by its mismatched local outcome as above gives the same contradiction. Thus w and w' consume exactly two rays each. The outsider matches neither pair, while the exceptional four-mismatch alternative requires all four types identical.

**Weight one.** All four rays have the same basis on the single compatible block j, constant on P. At the outsider all four mismatch on j. Isolating each separately forces its fixed remaining-data space to equal the label space of the outsider's remaining product. On P choose the index with both remaining bits opposite the outsider's. Its remaining target occupies all sixteen outcomes in the common remaining basis, whereas each ray occupies just one.

Four distinct native rays attain the bound. The proof concerns three controls and four rays; it does not determine unrestricted rank seven.
