# Four ordinary products and three exceptions cannot be locally erased at all three parties

For a sum of four ordinary block-product stabilizers and three arbitrary stabilizer exceptions equaling G₃, let S_X,S_Y,S_Z be the respective sums of the three exceptional local Schmidt supports. At least one local CCZ target g belongs to the corresponding S. These are complex linear spans, not necessarily stabilizer codes.

Suppose otherwise. At every party p and for each ordinary local ray a, g∈S_p+span(a), since otherwise a covector killing S_p and that a would leave at most three stabilizers for G₂. Thus each a is outside S_p and can be scaled uniquely as g+s with s∈S_p. Absorb the local scalings into four nonzero weights w_i. A covector killing S_p and taking value one on g then takes value one on every scaled local ray. Applying this covector on each one of the three parties gives the three exact two-party identities: the weighted sum of the four remaining ordinary product terms equals g⊗g. Applying it on all parties gives sum w_i=1.

In each such two-party identity the marginal factor spans have dimensions at least two (g is nonstabilizer) and sum at most five by Sylvester. On at least one side their dimension is exactly two. That line contains g and at most two stabilizer rays (reviewed local certificate in seven-product-exclusion.md), so exactly two local types occur. Both coordinates of g in those two independent directions are nonzero, and each group of terms must span g on the opposite party. Thus each type appears twice. It follows that at least two of the three parties, say X,Y, have two local types. The four XY types all occur: expressing g⊗g in the corresponding two bases has four nonzero entries. Label the ordinary terms by their distinct types (i,j)∈{0,1}².

At X, the row sums of w are the coordinates of g in the two scaled types (apply the erasure covector on Y in the XY identity). In the XZ identity, coefficient comparison then gives Σ_j w_ij s^Z_ij=0 on each row. The same comparison in YZ gives Σ_i w_ij s^Z_ij=0 on each column. Hence all four weighted Z residual vectors equal a common vector up to sign. The Z types therefore lie in a two-space through g and form exactly two stabilizer rays there. All pairwise type combinations appear by the same nonzero-coordinate argument. Relabel the Z bit so the four rows have types (i,j,i+j mod 2).

For each pair of bits its joint weights are the products of their marginal weights by the two-party identities in independent scaled bases. Because the third bit equals the sum of the first two, its conditional distribution in the first row is the second marginal and in the other row is that marginal reversed. Independence forces that marginal to have both entries equal to 1/2; denominators in these conditional equations are nonzero. By symmetry the same holds for each bit, and w_ij=1/4. The two scaled types on party p are therefore g+h_p and g−h_p, with h_p half their difference. The uniform parity average of their products is g⊗g⊗g+h_X⊗h_Y⊗h_Z. Consequently the three exceptions sum to a nonzero multiple of the latter product.

Each h_p has Pauli nullity three. By local-code-secants.md and the local line bound, the only secants through g are the complementary-plane restrictions of g or the pairs consisting of a full chirp and a point mass. For the plane alternatives the difference of the two weighted components giving g is a linear sign times g, so it has no Pauli stabilizer. For a point p, the full chirp equals g off p and equals −g(p) at p. The difference of the two weighted components hence has full support, with one amplitude of magnitude three and all others of magnitude one, up to a common scalar. A nonzero shift cannot preserve this unique magnitude; no nonidentity pure Z fixes a full-support ray. Reversing the order of the two types only changes the global sign.

Finally, if a local h_p is outside the local Schmidt code of any one exception, there is a stabilizer bra killing that code and retaining h_p: extend its defining checks to a full stabilizer basis and pick a nonzero exterior component. Contracting the three-term identity above then expresses the nonzero product of the other two h factors with at most two stabilizer rays, impossible by nullity-two-product-rank.md. Thus every h_p is in every corresponding exceptional Schmidt code. Nullity three forces every such code to be the full local space, contradicting the initial erasure assumption.
