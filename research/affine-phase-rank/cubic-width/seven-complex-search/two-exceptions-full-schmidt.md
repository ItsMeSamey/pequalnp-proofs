# A full-Schmidt exception yields at most four relative coefficient candidates

In a seven-list with five block-product stabilizers write α,β for the two exception coefficients, using unnormalized columns with entries zero or fourth roots. Minimal rank seven implies independent columns and therefore nonzero Gaussian-rational coefficients. Across any cut where the first exception has local Schmidt rank eight write its matrix A and the second B as maps from eight to 64 dimensions. Then A* A=ηI with η a positive power of two since the Schmidt coefficients are equal and its support size is a power of two.

The matrix H=A+tB, t=β/α, has rank at most six since αH equals the product target minus five product matrices. Thus λ=−η/t is an eigenvalue of K=A*B with geometric multiplicity at least two. There are at most four possible such eigenvalues. Each permitted λ is a Gaussian rational integral over Z[i], hence a Gaussian integer. Multiplication by A* can enlarge the kernel; test H itself before accepting a ratio.

Every power of K is a contraction of stabilizer tensors, hence up to scalar either zero or a stabilizer amplitude on the remaining input/output bits. Its entries are quadratic fourth-root Gauss sums over affine binary sets, so each nonzero entry is a Gaussian unit times a nonnegative power of 1+i. There are finitely many stabilizer rays on the fixed number of bits. A nonzero eigenvalue ensures K^j never vanishes, so K^(q+l)=c K^q for some positive q,l and a quotient of two nonzero entries c. Applying an eigenvector gives λ^l=c. Gaussian unique factorization shows that the required ratio t has only 1+i as a possible Gaussian prime in numerator or denominator.

If H has rank six on any cut, write the target as u v^T. Reducing rank to at most five by subtracting αH requires u in im H and v^T in its row space. For any solution Hx=u the scale then must equal α=v^T x by the rank-one update identity. This is independent of solution choice and must agree across rank-six cuts. These are necessary pair filters for different encoders, not a profile-wide exclusion.
