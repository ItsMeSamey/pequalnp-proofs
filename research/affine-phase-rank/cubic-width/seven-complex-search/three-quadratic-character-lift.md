# Three quadratic sign generators cannot lift seven graph states

Let D be an arbitrary common diagonal Clifford, including entangling gates, and Q a map from nine bits to three bits whose three component functions have Boolean degree at most two. Form the eight full-support stabilizer rays with amplitudes proportional to D(x)(−1)^{s·Q(x)} for binary s. If a real sign target f lies in the complex span of any seven of these rays, its stabilizer rank is at most four. In particular this family cannot give seven rays for three CCZ copies.

Remove global phase and common normalization so D has fourth-root diagonal entries. On the attained fibers write f(x)/D(x)=h(Q(x)); the specified linear combination gives this factorization. Twist h by a missing character, absorbing that character into D, which remains a Clifford. On attained points write h=i^a(−1)^b uniquely with binary a,b. The parity a(Q(x)) is affine in x because f is real and the exponent of D modulo two is affine. Therefore i^{a(Q(x))} is a diagonal Clifford: the fourth-root phase of an affine parity has only linear terms and even quadratic terms modulo four.

If all eight points are attained, the missing Fourier coefficient gives sum h=0 after the twist. Thus the counts of positive and negative real entries agree, as do the counts of positive and negative imaginary entries. Exactly four entries have b=1. If some points are unhit, choose their b values to give even total parity. In either case the full Boolean table b has degree at most two, since its cubic coefficient is the parity of the table. On three bits its alternating polar form has rank zero or two, so b is affine plus a product of two affine forms (including the identically zero product). Pulling back by Q, the affine term becomes a quadratic Boolean phase. The remaining sign of a product of two quadratic Boolean functions U,V equals [1+(−1)^U+(−1)^V−(−1)^{U+V}]/2. Each of these four signs times the common Clifford phases is a stabilizer amplitude. No surjectivity, coefficient reality, or independence of the chosen signs is needed.
