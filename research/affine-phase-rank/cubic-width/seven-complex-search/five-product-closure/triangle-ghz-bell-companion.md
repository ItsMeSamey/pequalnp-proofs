# A Bell triangle cannot have a single-GHZ plus single-Bell companion

Let E be the encoded 2×2 matrix multiplication tensor (one Bell on each edge). Suppose its sole other exception F has local ranks (2,4,4) at A,B,C. The tripartite stabilizer normal-form equations force F to consist of one GHZ shared by all parties and one Bell on B,C, up to local Cliffords and ancillas. By triangle-local-kernel.md its A code is inside E's. There is a map at A that fixes E's code and kills g; this also fixes F. At neither B nor C can the two codes coincide, since their shared signed local marker there and at A would violate two-local-shared-markers.md. Their local code intersections are exactly two-dimensional. The physical E marker commutes with F's local stabilizer and hence restricts to a signed nonidentity logical Pauli J on F's two-qubit local space, with J-positive space the intersection.

Every retraction of the F support onto this intersection (identity on the intersection) extends to a linear map fixing the E support pointwise, by extending the compatible definitions on the sum of the two subspaces. In particular we may postselect any logical Pauli M anticommuting with J to any prescribed sign: its outcome projector restricts to a scaled isometry between the J-positive space and the chosen M-outcome space of equal dimensions. Follow the projector by the inverse of this restricted map. For M equal up to sign to J one can instead postselect the J-positive outcome. Each resulting local map has rank two on the F support and preserves E.

Let G denote logical Z of the GHZ qubit on each of B,C, in the normal-form basis. If at either party G equals up to sign or anticommutes with its J, postselect that party's compatible G outcome; use the J-positive restriction at the other. The GHZ parity relation leaves A in one fixed ray or the output vanishes. Otherwise both J commute with G and neither is proportional to G. Thus each J is, up to sign, a power of G times a nonidentity Pauli on its Bell qubit. Among the three correlated Bell axes choose one whose local Pauli K at each end differs from that end's J Bell component (at most two of the three forbidden). At B postselect G times K_B; at C postselect K_C. Both local observables anticommute with their J's. Their joint outcome determines the GHZ Z on A because K_B K_C is a signed Bell stabilizer and G_B equals G_A on the GHZ. Postcomposition by the local inverse maps does not change A's fixed ray.

In all cases F's surviving A factor is fixed and B,C each have resulting dimension at most two. Its ordinary product rank is at most two, and when it is two there is a two-term expansion with repeated A direction. The projected seven-list expresses E using this output and at most five other ordinary products, with g killed. Fewer than seven products contradict ordinary matrix multiplication rank; exactly seven with repeated A factor contradict de Groote uniqueness as in triangle-local-kernel.md. The conclusion allows arbitrary independent local encoders.
