# A rectangular remote span excludes the simple GHZ and two-link path pair

Use the reviewed reduction simple-ghz-path-endpoints.md. It leaves only a connected product expansion with local dimensions (d_X,d_Y,d_Z)=(5,4,4), with X the common endpoint of the two Bell links. The expansion has eleven products (five ordinary, two simple GHZ, four path) plus the target g⊗g⊗g. Its relation has nonzero coefficients on all twelve vectors.

Let R be the span of the eleven YZ product factors. The target's nonzero YZ factor is in R by the matrix identity across X|YZ. Write this identity as a product of a matrix of X columns, an invertible diagonal weight matrix, and a matrix of YZ rows. Their outer ranks are respectively 5 and dim R, and the product has rank one. Sylvester's inequality gives dim R≤11+1−5=7. The twelve remote factors, including the target, form a connected vector collection: a nontrivial partition with disjoint remote spans would give disjoint full tensor spans after adjoining arbitrary X factors, disconnecting the original collection. The remote local dimensions are four and four. Because path branches have endpoint factors indexed separately by i and j, R contains the full tensor product C_Y⊗C_Z of two two-dimensional subspaces.

Choose a generic complex line L in C_Y and apply the quotient Y→Y/L to the remote factors. For each nontrivial bipartition of the finite remote collection fix a nonzero intersection vector of its two spans. A nonzero vector lies in L⊗Z for at most one line L because these kernels for distinct lines intersect trivially. Avoid all finitely many forbidden lines, and all lines killing any individual factor. Every bipartition of the projected collection still has nonzero span intersection. Its local dimensions are three on Y and four on Z, since L is inside the local span and no term is killed. Connected splitting for bipartite products gives projected span dimension at least 3+4−1=6. But the quotient kills L⊗C_Z⊆R, of dimension two. Thus the projected span has dimension at most 7−2=5. This completes exclusion of the entire (0001,0110) pair for independent encoders.

More generally let a finite connected collection of bipartite complex product vectors have marginal dimensions d,e and span R containing a rectangle U⊗V, with u=dim U≥1 and v=dim V≥1. Repeat the generic-line quotient on U for u−1 steps, preserving connectedness. At least v dimensions of R disappear per step while d falls by one and e stays fixed. Connected splitting in the final quotient gives dim R≥d+e−1+(u−1)(v−1). For several rectangles with all left spaces U_i in direct sum the proof gives the sum of their penalties: quotienting in one U_i preserves the dimensions of the others. These quotients are dimension arguments over the complex numbers.
