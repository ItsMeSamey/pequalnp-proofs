# A single GHZ and a single Bell exception

Assume five ordinary products plus a simple three-party GHZ and a simple Bell on one link represent G₃. The two exceptions may have unrelated local Clifford frames. Expand each into two ordinary products. As in [the two-GHZ proof](two-simple-ghz-exclusion.md), the ten vectors including the target form a connected collection, giving sum of the three local dimensions at most eleven. Each local dimension is at least three because each contains the GHZ code and g outside it. Choose a party A of dimension three. Its span is U_A=C_A+span(g) for the dimension-two GHZ code C_A. The [reviewed local certificate](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md) permits a covector retaining g and annihilating all stabilizer types exterior to C_A in U_A.

If A is the Bell spectator with ray h outside C_A, killing C_A while retaining g leaves the Bell plus the exterior ordinary factors; hence at least three ordinary factors are exterior by the two-copy stabilizer rank four. Killing all exterior factors instead kills the Bell and leaves at most two ordinary terms plus the GHZ pair. All four must survive. The resulting rank-four product circuit requires the sum of the two local dimensions at most five, while the full GHZ pair and g require at least three on each side.

If the Bell spectator h lies in C_A, at least four ordinary factors are exterior, and killing the exterior leaves at most one ordinary term. On the remaining two parties each exceptional pair consists of two stabilizer products whose local factors on both sides are independent bases of the respective codes, whenever both are present. The four- and five-survivor argument from the two-GHZ proof applies. Four survivors would include an entire pair and violate the rank-four circuit bound. Five survivors give local dimensions three on both sides; the two codes on each side must coincide, since two distinct two-dimensional codes would supply at least eleven stabilizer rays in a three-space through g containing at most eight. Killing the two aligned exceptions leaves at most one product, impossible.

Finally suppose the Bell link is AB, so A is active. Its local code coincides with C_A by the same eleven-versus-eight criterion. Kill the exterior factors, leaving at most one ordinary product a⊗b, up to two GHZ products a_i⊗c_i, and a Bell residual u⊗h where u lies in the proper Bell code on B and h is the fixed C stabilizer. Project B by a covector killing the GHZ code and retaining g_B. This forces g_C into span(b,h); thus the ordinary term exists, b,h are distinct rays and the two coefficients of g_C in that basis are nonzero. Quotient C by span(b,h). Since the GHZ a_i are independent, each surviving c_i lies in this line. Both cannot survive, since then their code would contain g_C. If one survives it equals b or h projectively: the reviewed local certificate allows only two stabilizer rays on the line through g_C. If it equals h, or neither survives, the b coefficient forces g_B proportional to the ordinary stabilizer a. If it equals b the h coefficient forces g_B into the Bell code containing u. Both are impossible.

This exclusion uses no new enumeration.
