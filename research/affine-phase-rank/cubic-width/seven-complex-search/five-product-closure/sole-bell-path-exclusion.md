# Excluding the two-Bell path as the sole exceptional stabilizer ray

Suppose six block-product stabilizer rays P_i and a sole entangled stabilizer E give g⊗g⊗g in seven terms. Use the remaining normal form: Bell pairs on AB and BC, up to local Cliffords and ancillas. The local Schmidt supports C_A,C_B,C_C have dimensions 2,4,2. They are stabilizer codes and each excludes g. All coefficients in the proposed list are nonzero.

Expand each Bell pair in either the X,Y, or Z eigenbasis of its outer qubit and the conjugate matching basis at the center. This supplies nine distinct choices of four block-product stabilizers D_uv spanning the Bell path decomposition, after the given encoders. A specified P_i can coincide as a ray with some D_uv in at most one choice: its A factor fixes the first encoded basis if it is an eigenray, and its C factor fixes the second, since distinct single-qubit Pauli eigenbases have no common ray. Choose one of the nine choices avoiding all six P_i. The D_uv are independent, as are the P_i. No D_uv or P_i equals the nonstabilizer target.

The list of eleven product tensors consisting of the target, six P_i, and four D_uv has a linear dependence with every coefficient nonzero. If the list split, restriction of the dependence to each direct-sum component would give a full-support dependence. The target component has at least nine entries by the exact eight-term block-product rank. Each other component would need at least two. Thus a proportional pair would be present as the other component, contrary to the basis choice. Therefore the list is connected. By the Lovitz–Petrov splitting theorem its span dimension (at most ten) strictly exceeds sum_i(d_i−1), where d_i is its local factor span dimension. Each d_i≥dim C_i+1 since the local D_uv span C_i and g lies outside it. The baseline sum of d_i−1 is eight, and at most one unit of excess is possible. At least one outer block, call it A, must have d_A=3, so its ambient factor span is exactly U=C_A+span(g).

Here U contains at most two distinct stabilizer rays outside C_A: the two-dimensional code contains six encoded one-qubit stabilizer rays, and the reviewed local plane certificate in seven-product-exclusion.md gives at most eight in U. Their span misses g. Indeed a single stabilizer does not span g. If two outside rays u,v spanned g, the local seven-multiset u,u,v,v plus three distinct rays in C_A would evade the same certificate: any line missing g contains either at most the three code rays or at most one doubled outside ray and one code ray. This contradicts the zero surviving multiset count.

At least four of the six P_i have their A factor outside C_A: a covector annihilating C_A and nonzero on g eliminates E and otherwise would leave at most three product stabilizers representing G2. Now choose a covector f on A that annihilates all outside stabilizer types in U but is nonzero on g. Its restriction to C_A is nonzero, since any one occurring outside type together with C_A spans U. Applying f leaves at most two P_i. Contracting E on outer A instead leaves a nonzero vector on the first encoded center qubit and the other Bell pair, with fixed ancillas and center/C local Clifford encodings. Thus E_f has Schmidt rank exactly two on B|C and local supports in C_B and C_C. The contracted target is a nonzero scalar times g⊗g. Since g lies outside both of those local supports, the difference of the target and the nonzero scaled E_f has ordinary matrix rank three (disjoint row and column spaces). It cannot equal a sum of at most two product tensors. This rules out the Bell path.

With the previously reviewed normal-form exclusions, every seven-term candidate must therefore contain at least two atoms entangled across the original blocks. This result neither excludes two exceptions nor gives a seven-term list.
