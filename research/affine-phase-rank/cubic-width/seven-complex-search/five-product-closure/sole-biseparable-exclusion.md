# A sole entangled atom must entangle all three original blocks

Assume seven terms, six block-product stabilizers and an exceptional stabilizer E that factors across X|YZ but entangles Y and Z. All seven factor across X. In this cut their X-factor span has dimension at least four: every four X factors span g by the rank-four G2 contraction argument, and the exact small-span profile certificate in seven-product-exclusion.md excludes seven local rays of span dimension at most three with this property. Write the seven-term equation as a matrix of rank one on X|YZ. Sylvester's rank inequality gives d_X+d_YZ≤8 for the two factor-span dimensions (all seven weights nonzero). In particular d_YZ≤4.

Choose a covector on X zero on the X factor of E but nonzero on g. This eliminates E and expresses G2 as a combination of the six remaining YZ-product stabilizers. They span at least four dimensions by the rank-four theorem. Consequently their span equals the full four-dimensional YZ-factor space and contains E_YZ. Choose four independent product stabilizers spanning it. The expansion of G2 in that basis uses all four, making the five vectors including G2 a product-tensor circuit of dimension four. By the Lovitz–Petrov splitting theorem their local span dimensions satisfy d_Y+d_Z≤5. One is at most two and includes g. Since E_YZ is in their joint span, its local support is contained in this at-most-two-dimensional space. But the local support of the entangled bipartite stabilizer E_YZ is a stabilizer code of dimension at least two. It must equal that space and contain g. This is impossible because g has no nonidentity stabilizing Pauli and every such two-dimensional local code on three qubits has nontrivial Pauli stabilizers. Thus no sole biseparable exception is possible.

More generally let E entangle all three cuts, and let V_YZ be the span of the six YZ products from the remaining terms. The local support C_X of E is proper and excludes g by single-entangled-profile.md. A covector killing it but retaining g shows G2∈V_YZ. If dim V_YZ=4 then the same five-product circuit proof yields a local space S of dimension at most two, on Y or Z, containing g and all six corresponding local factors. The original equation places the corresponding local support of E in S, contradicting entanglement as above. Hence every two-block projected product span has dimension five or six. Modulo C_X, the six product terms sum to the rank-one tensor [g]⊗G2. Their projected X-factor span consequently has dimension at most 7−dim V_YZ, which is two if dim V_YZ=5 and one if it is six. This holds on each of the three cuts.
