# Bell triangle versus two encoded GHZs

Assume the five-product, two-exception setting with E a Bell triangle (M₂) and F two GHZs. At each party both codes have dimension four. Two coincident local codes are forbidden by two-local-shared-markers.md. At every site of distinct codes, triangle-local-kernel.md forces intersection dimension two, cut out on F by its nonidentity signed logical Pauli J (the restriction of E's physical marker). Use the retraction construction of triangle-ghz-bell-companion.md. Postselection on F of an observable anticommuting with J, or of J itself in the prescribed outcome, followed by a local inverse into the intersection, extends to fix E pointwise. More generally any arbitrary map on the exterior two dimensions of the F code, agreeing with the identity on the intersection, extends together with the identity on the E code.

Write F, after absorbing its coefficient, as a nonzero scalar times the sum of four products labeled r in F₂², one fixed encoded computational basis at each party, all labels equal across parties. Call Z(u) the logical diagonal character of label u. Select two different-code sites B,C. If their J's are not both pure diagonal with the same unsigned label, proceed as follows. At B select an allowed measurement of a nonidentity diagonal Z(u): for a diagonal J_B use its own, for a nondiagonal J_B there are two distinct u anticommuting with it. If J_C is diagonal, choose u different from its label (possible in the stated case) and postselect J_C itself. Independent bits at B and C fix r. If J_C is nondiagonal, let t be the nonzero vector in ker u, so the two remaining values after the B measurement differ by t. Measure a Pauli of shift t on C with a Z part chosen to anticommute with J_C; this is possible since J_C has nonzero shift. Its outcome projector sends the two basis labels to proportional rays. Thus F's projected output is either product, or product on C times a matrix of rank at most two on A,B. Local inverse maps preserve this or annihilate it. On A now choose any linear map fixing E's code and killing g. The surviving E would have at most five ordinary products plus a biseparable tensor of rank ≤2. Fewer than seven contradict rank seven of M₂; exactly seven would give two proportional single-party directions, forbidden by de Groote as in triangle-local-kernel.md. If J_B,J_C are identical diagonal labels with opposite prescribed logical signs, selecting those outcomes kills F altogether and yields the same contradiction.

Thus all different-code sites have the same signed logical diagonal, with two labels s,t in the kept half and p,q in the discarded half (labels are identified via F's GHZ encoding). At least two sites B,C are different-code. Their maps can send the two discarded basis states to arbitrary vectors, in particular chosen multiples of either kept basis state or zero, while fixing the E code. Distinguish the remaining party A.

If A also has different codes, then the same signed logical diagonal applies there. Its map must fix the E code and kill g, neither of which fixes the two discarded F basis images separately. If g is outside the sum of the two codes, there is no constraint on these two images since the map can be extended on g. Otherwise write g=e+γp_A+δq_A with e in the E code; γ and δ are not both zero because g is outside that code. The only condition on the images of p_A,q_A is γL(p_A)+δL(q_A)=−e. One image is freely assignable (choose an index such that the other has nonzero coefficient). Assign it −s_A, choose the other as required, send its matching freely assigned label at B,C to s_B and s_C and kill the other discarded label at B. The surviving s term cancels against the routed label, leaving only the single product t. This contradicts rank seven of E.

If A has coincident codes, a map fixing this code and killing g preserves all four independent A basis vectors. At B,C route both discarded labels to scalar multiples of s_B,s_C, independently controlling both product scalars λ,μ. Then the output consists of the terms with local data
    (s_A+λp_A+μq_A, s_B, s_C),   (t_A,t_B,t_C).
Choose λ,μ to make the first local factor invertible as a 2×2 matrix in E's matrix multiplication coordinates. A three-dimensional vector space of 2×2 matrices cannot consist of singular matrices: the determinant polar is a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form and a totally isotropic linear subspace has dimension at most two. If every vector in the indicated affine patch were singular, homogeneity and polynomial identity would make the entire three-space singular. Thus the choice exists. The resulting seven-product list must be optimal for M₂; no terms vanish by rank seven. In every optimal decomposition the seven triples of local matrix ranks consist of one (2,2,2) and six (1,1,1). This follows from de Groote's equivalence with Strassen, whose matrix multiplication isotropies act by invertible left/right multiplications and permutations/transposes of matrix factors, preserving these ranks. Consequently s_B,s_C must both have rank two. Now route p,q instead to t_B,t_C, forcing t_B,t_C also rank two by the same argument on span(t_A,p_A,q_A). These are properties of the fixed kept vectors. Either optimal list then contains two rank-two B factors, contradicting the rank pattern. [de Groote, optimal matrix multiplication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304397578900452) [Chiantini et al., geometry of Strassen decompositions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.08364)

This excludes the pair (1110,0002) with arbitrary local encoders. In conjunction with the previous local-rank and normal-form exclusions, a Bell triangle admits no no-full companion in this two-exception setting.
