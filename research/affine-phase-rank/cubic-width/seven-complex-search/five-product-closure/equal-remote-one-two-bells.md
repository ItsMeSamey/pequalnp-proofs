# Exclusion of the equal computational code with one AB and two AC Bells

Use the (p,q,k)=(1,2,0) shape of equal-full-remote-affine-classes.md. Let H be the weighted sum of the two exceptions. On B and C it is supported in proper local computational codes B₀,C₀ of dimensions two and four. Write the five ordinary products as a_j⊗b_j⊗c_j with weights absorbed. The target is g_A⊗g_B⊗g_C, with every g outside every proper stabilizer code.

First, any two full encoders sharing their remote code must give H of A-flattening rank at least three in a seven-term list. Otherwise H is, up to isometries and a nonzero scale, I−W for a scalar times a three-qubit Clifford W with fixed subspace of dimension at least six. The scalar has modulus one since W has a fixed vector. Its trace has modulus at least 6−2=4. In the unsigned Pauli space let D be the fixed space of its symplectic map. The trace of conjugation by W, calculated in the Hermitian Pauli basis, is the sum of the signs on D. It also equals |tr W|². A fixed unsigned Pauli cannot change sign, since conjugation by that Pauli would then negate W and force zero trace. Thus 2^dim(D)=|tr W|²≥16. W commutes with every Pauli in D, so its Pauli expansion uses only labels in D-perp, of dimension at most two.

For dimension zero or one this algebra is diagonal with sectors of dimension at least four. If the dimension is two and the generators anticommute, they are conjugate by a Clifford to one qubit's X,Z, so all ranks in their algebra are multiples of four. Otherwise the two independent commuting generators have four joint eigenspaces of dimension two. The rank bound forces I−W either to vanish or to be a scalar times one joint stabilizer projector. After the original Clifford encoding and input Clifford, this difference therefore has stabilizer vectorization or vanishes. Replacing the two exceptions gives at most six stabilizers, impossible.

Next, erasing B₀ by a covector retaining g_B puts g_A⊗g_C in the span V of the five products a_j⊗c_j. Thus dim V≥4 by the two-copy rank theorem. If dim V=5, its five generators are independent. In the B flattening, taking dual coordinates on V shows that every b_j belongs to the three-space B₀+span(g_B). At least four b_j are exterior to B₀, by the same erasure and the two-copy bound. The local three-space lemma from two-different-simple-bells.md supplies a covector retaining g_B while killing all exterior stabilizer rays of this space. Apply it together with a C covector erasing C₀ while retaining g_C. The exceptions vanish and at most one ordinary stabilizer remains for g_A, impossible.

It follows that dim V=4. Choose four independent ordinary product generators as its basis; expressing g_A⊗g_C uses all four by the two-copy bound. Sylvester's inequality gives d_A+d_C≤5 for the spans of their local factors. All five original products lie in this tensor product of local spans, as does the target. Both local spans contain g, and both have dimension at least two. If d_A≤2 then H is supported there too, contradicting the full-encoder rank bound.

Otherwise d_C=2. This line through g_C contains only two distinct stabilizer rays by the local census, and both occur. Call them r,t. Their coefficients in g_C are both nonzero. Since g_C lies outside C₀, the intersection of span(r,t) and C₀ has dimension at most one. The C support of H lies there. It cannot vanish, so H=h⊗K with h in this intersection and K on AB. Group the five ordinary terms by r,t; one group contains at most two terms. In that coordinate the relation expresses a nonzero multiple of g_A⊗g_B as the sum of those products and a multiple of K. That multiple is nonzero by the two-copy rank bound. Erasing B₀ removes K, and shows g_A lies in the span of the at most two A factors of the group. Returning to the same coordinate equation now puts the A support of K in that same span. Thus H again has A-flattening rank at most two, a contradiction.

This excludes the entire equal-remote-code (1,2,0) class with arbitrary independent input Cliffords, together with its B,C exchange. It does not assume equal remote codes on the other cuts.
