# A sole pair of Bell links sharing a center

Write the links as AB and AC. In Bell bases the purified expansion has four product terms indexed by (i,j), with local basis vectors indexed by (i,j) at A, i at B and j at C. Fixed local stabilizer ancillas and local Cliffords are allowed. Use the inputs and the reviewed three-space local certificate from [the companion note](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md).

First one leaf has full local span of dimension three, counting all six ordinary products, the four expansion products and the target. Indeed if the eleven-vector collection is connected, its rank is at most ten, hence its local dimensions sum to at most twelve. The center dimension is at least five and the leaf dimensions at least three, since each includes its code and g outside the code. Both leaf dimensions cannot reach four. In the disconnected case, as in [the mixed profile](single-mixed-ghz-bell-exclusion.md), one proportional pair comprising an ordinary and an expansion product lies outside the target component. The target component is a minimum eight-product circuit, with local dimensions summing to at most ten. Its three surviving expansion products span dimensions three, two and two. Including the target gives lower bounds four, three and three; both leaves therefore have dimension three in that component. The removed pair has leaf factors in the same code spans, so the full leaf dimensions remain three.

Call this leaf B. Its two-dimensional code plus g is the entire local three-space. The reviewed local certificate implies g lies outside the span of all exterior stabilizer types in that space. At least four ordinary products must have exterior B factors, since killing the code while retaining g otherwise expresses two copies with fewer than four stabilizers. Choose a covector annihilating all exterior types while retaining g. Contract and absorb the resulting nonzero scalar: on A,C the target g⊗g becomes a sum of at most two ordinary stabilizer products and a residual R from the two Bell links. If R vanished this would contradict the two-copy rank. Otherwise R has Schmidt rank exactly two. In the Bell basis on A it is a nonzero vector on the first logical qubit tensored with an intact Bell pair on the second A qubit and C. Its C marginal support is exactly the two-dimensional code C_C, and its A marginal support lies in the original four-dimensional code C_A.

Now kill C_A with a covector retaining g_A. The resulting equation puts g_C in the span of the at most two ordinary C factors. That same span therefore contains the C marginal support of R, by subtracting the ordinary products from the target. Since R has Schmidt rank two, this span is exactly C_C. This would put g_C inside its proper stabilizer code, a contradiction.

Together with the preceding notes, the tripartite stabilizer normal form and the exclusion of the three-Bell triangle, this rules out a seven-term list with precisely one entangled stabilizer atom. It does not address lists with two or more entangled atoms.
