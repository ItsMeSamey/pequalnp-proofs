# Transferring a logical reflection between exceptional encoders

Use encoder-threefold-eigenvalue.md. Let E,F be the two exceptional flattenings with their original complex weights absorbed. For ranks (8,4) or ranks (8,8) with distinct remote output codes the earlier theorem supplies a four-dimensional signed Pauli input code Q. Both E|Q and F|Q are equally scaled Clifford isometries onto the same code D₀. In the rank-four case Q is the inverse image of im F under E; the initial support B of F is Q or the eigenspace of a Pauli anticommuting with Q's defining Pauli. In the two rank-eight case Q is the common inverse image of the remote intersection. On Q the kernel of E+F has dimension at least three.

The unitary on D₀ carrying F|Q to −E|Q is exactly the identity or I−2|φ⟩⟨φ| for a logical stabilizer φ. Its phase is fixed since it acts identically on the image of the kernel. Extend it to a physical Clifford R preserving im F: choose nested Clifford code coordinates on im F with two logical qubits and, for rank F=8, one additional logical syndrome bit specifying D₀. Change basis on the two logical qubits to send φ to |11⟩, apply CZ, and undo the change, independently of the syndrome and of encoding ancillas. These coordinates exist by symplectic completion of nested stabilizer generators. Choose the +1 phase on the unaffected states; use R=I for the identity case. Thus E+RF vanishes on Q.

Now E+F=(E+RF)+(I−R)F. In the reflection case (I−R)F is twice F projected onto the code obtained by pinning the two logical qubits to φ and leaving the optional syndrome free. Thus it has stabilizer vectorization and Schmidt rank one or two, according as rank F is four or eight. For R=I the term vanishes.

For the other term choose a Hermitian input Pauli J interchanging Q and Q^⊥. Full encoder E intertwines it with some Hermitian output Pauli P, so EJ=PE. If RF is full it likewise obeys RFJ=S RF for a Hermitian output Pauli S. The scalar weights do not affect intertwining. For rank four with B=Q we have E+RF=E P_{Q^⊥} directly. If B is defined by a Pauli anticommuting with Q's generator take J to be its signed defining Pauli, fixed to +1 on B; then RFJ=RF, so use S=I. In each case with S, for x∈Q one obtains (E+RF)Jx=(P−S)Ex. Hence on the entire domain E+RF=(P−S) E P_Q J. Write P−S=P(I−PS). For Hermitian PS the latter factor is twice a Pauli projector, possibly zero or scalar. For anti-Hermitian PS it is √2 times a Clifford quarter-turn about a Hermitian Pauli. Thus the map has stabilizer vectorization or vanishes. It has rank at most four since its input is supported on Q^⊥. No equality between the original encoders on Q^⊥ is assumed.

The replacement therefore has at most two stabilizer rays of cut Schmidt ranks bounded by (4,1) or (4,2), respectively, and preserves the five original product terms. Other cuts are not controlled. Equal full-rank remote codes are outside this argument.
