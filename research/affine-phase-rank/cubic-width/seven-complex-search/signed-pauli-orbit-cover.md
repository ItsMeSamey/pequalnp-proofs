# Signed measurement representatives for the unrestricted seven-ray search

Use target-clifford-group.md and seven-must-share.md. Classify local unsigned labels (a,b) by shift and phase: identity I, nonidentity pure Z, real shifted R (a nonzero and a dot b=0), and imaginary shifted J (a dot b=1). Choose I,Z,X,Y on the first qubit as representatives, orienting R by positive expectation.

The real affine Clifford group fixing the local cubic sign g is transitive on each unsigned type. A linear computational relabeling with its compensating quadratic sign moves a nonzero shift arbitrarily while preserving imaginary parity. For fixed shift a, a translation t with compensating sign (-1)^(f(x+t)+f(x)), f=x1 x2 x3, adds to b the linear part of the second derivative along a,t. This is the functional x mapping to det(a,t,x); it ranges over the plane perpendicular to a. For pure Z, invertible linear maps act transitively directly. Translation flips any pure-Z sign by choosing b dot t=1. For an imaginary label choose t=a: the compensating sign is invariant under a, while translation contributes (-1)^(a dot b)=-1. A real shifted sign cannot be flipped at fixed unsigned label because its target expectation is nonzero.

Under the full target-preserving group, global unsigned orbits are the multisets of three local types, excluding all identities: 19 cases. For the three multisets with only identities and at least one R, the two signs have different target expectations. For every other multiset a Z or J can flip the global sign. This gives exactly 22 signed representatives, including block permutations. Require at least two rays fixed by the selected positive signed operator. Any seven-list, if it exists, can be transported in full to one of these cases by the shared-Pauli theorem. No invariance of the list is required. The branch states must still be decomposed using compatible frames and coefficients.
