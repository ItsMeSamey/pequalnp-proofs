# A diagonal-space proof against two exceptions across the same cut

Suppose a seven-term representation of g⊗G2 has five block-product atoms and two exceptions with X factors a,b that entangle YZ. As in sole-biseparable-exclusion.md the X-factor span has dimension at least four and the opposing total span V has dimension exactly four by Sylvester and stabilizer rank(G2)=4.

Let W be the span of the five ordinary YZ products. If it has dimension four, four product rays form a basis of V representing G2 with all terms needed. Sylvester across Y|Z bounds their two local span dimensions in sum by five. A space of dimension at most two on one side contains g and the local Schmidt support of the entangled exceptional matrix. The latter is a stabilizer code of dimension at least two, contradicting absence of Pauli stabilizers of g. Therefore dim W≤3 and G2 lies outside W.

Quotient by W on YZ. The two exceptional X factors span g and are independent since g is not a stabilizer. Write g=αa+βb with αβ nonzero. Coefficient comparison shows that the exceptional matrices E,F (absorbing scalar weights) equal αG2 and βG2 modulo W. Consequently V=W+span(G2) and dim W=3. Each exceptional matrix has rank at most four, being a combination of three products and G2. Its local stabilizer Schmidt supports thus exclude g. Each difference E−αG2 and F−βG2 has rank at least three by disjoint row and column spaces, and exactly three since W is spanned by three products.

Choose three independent ordinary products B_j=y_j⊗z_j spanning W. A rank-three matrix in W forces the y_j independent and z_j independent. In these two bases W consists of diagonal matrices. Its only product rays are the three basis rays, and each rank-three matrix in W uses all three. Group the five ordinary atoms by B_j. Comparing coefficients in the original identity shows that the combined X coefficient x_j for each B_j is a combination of a,b with both components nonzero, because the two rank-three differences have all three diagonal entries nonzero. The line through a,b and g contains only the two stabilizer rays a,b by the reviewed local certificate. Thus x_j is not a stabilizer ray. Each of three groups needs at least two atoms, but only five are available.
