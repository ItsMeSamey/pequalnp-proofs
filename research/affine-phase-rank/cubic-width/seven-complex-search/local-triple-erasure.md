# Stabilizer-bra separation of any local triple from CCZ

Given three arbitrary three-qubit stabilizer rays a,b,c, including repetitions, there is a three-qubit stabilizer bra orthogonal to all three but nonorthogonal to g if and only if g is outside their complex linear span. Necessity is linearity. The converse is certified by local-triple-erasure.py using the reviewed 1080-ray generator and target-preserving subgroup of g3-complex-conic-search/search.py.

For each local ray r form its exact 1080-bit nonorthogonality mask N_r from Gaussian integer inner products of the unit-gauged entries. Each real and imaginary dot product fits in signed 16-bit arithmetic. Define D_r as the complement restricted to the 926 rays nonorthogonal to g. For fixed a,b the infeasible choices of c are exactly the intersection of N_tau over tau in D_a intersect D_b, initialized to the full mask for an empty intersection. This tests all c simultaneously. The deterministic seeded permutation only changes the order of intersecting masks and preserves membership. By target-preserving Clifford symmetry we select each of the 15 first-ray orbit representatives and keep every b, including duplicates. For every infeasible c check the rank of (a,b,c,g) against (a,b,c), or skip individually when the pair already spans g and all c correctly fail. Rank reduction modulo 65537 maps i to 256; the absolute modulus of any order-at-most-four Gaussian minor is at most 16 by Hadamard, whereas a nonzero entry in the reduction kernel has modulus at least sqrt(65537). Thus these rank tests are exact over the complex numbers.

The locked one-thread run covers 16,200 representative pairs, three of which already span g. For other pairs all 5,811 failures had g in the triple's span and none had g outside. These are symmetry-reduced ordered incidences, not counts of distinct global triples. The checker supplies the physical witness for a given feasible triple by any set bit of D_a intersect D_b intersect D_c, in the same coordinate order.

For a seven-list with three product atoms, every block on which the three local factors fail to span g therefore enforces the four-survivor Schmidt/fidelity criterion of four-survivor-schmidt-test.md on all separating bras. No assumption of a shared local basis is necessary. This does not exclude cases in which all three triples span g.
