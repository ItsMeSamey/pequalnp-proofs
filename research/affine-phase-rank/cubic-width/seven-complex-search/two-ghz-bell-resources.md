# Two independently encoded GHZ-plus-Bell exceptions

Assume the target g⊗g⊗g is a nonzero weighted sum of five ordinary stabilizer products and two exceptional stabilizers E,F, each of profile one GHZ plus one Bell. Absorb nonzero coefficients and write H=E+F, R=target−H. The Bell links can coincide or differ. On every party all individual local Schmidt codes are proper. We use the two-copy stabilizer minimum four, the [local three-space certificate](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md), the [two-local-marker exclusion](two-local-shared-markers.md), the [matched remote checks exclusion](two-matched-syndrome-exclusion.md), and its signed-check classification from [remote erasure](five-product-remote-erasure.md).

**Marker alignment.** On every cut the remote target g⊗g lies outside the sum of the two remote Schmidt codes: quotient one partner by E's local code and the other by F's; the exceptions vanish and the two projected target factors are nonzero. Therefore rank R≥rank H on that cut (a rank-one perturbation with its remote direction outside cannot reduce rank). If a remote partner has a common signed local check, in fact rank R≤4. If it were five, the five ordinary remote product vectors would span its remote range and hence belong to the span of the remote target and the two exception codes. Contract that partner with a covector annihilating the common local sector and retaining g. On the displayed span the image is contained in the line through g at the third party. Each ordinary basis product must therefore map to zero, since its third factor is a stabilizer. But the target survives and both exceptions vanish, so the contraction of R is nonzero.

Consider a cut X where both exception ranks are four. Their remote codes cannot be equal, because equality aligns the proper marginal stabilizer supports on both remote parties, giving forbidden shared local markers on two parties. Let u,v denote the dimensions of the local and remote code intersections. Always rank H≥8−u−v, by the matrix product of the concatenated Schmidt frames and Sylvester's rank inequality. Remote intersections of stabilizer codes of the same dimension four are zero or a power of two; thus v≤2 here. If u=0 then rank H≥6, impossible. Each local dimension-four code is a Pauli sector, so either u=4 and the sector is shared, or u=2 for independent commuting checks. In the latter case v≥1; the remote codes have compatible commuting check groups, as follows from a common nonzero eigenvector. Each has four independent checks on six qubits. Their shared signed check space therefore has dimension 2+log₂ v. If it contained no local check, two independent checks would project injectively onto both remote parties; all nonlocal shared checks are computational, forbidden by the matched-check test. Thus some party has a shared local marker. Exactly one party can have any shared local markers.

At a four-by-four cut distinct from this unique party, u=2 and the remote shared local marker forces rank H≤rank R≤4 by the first paragraph. Hence v≥2 and the shared remote check space has dimension at least three. If its local subgroup on the unique shared party had dimension at most one, it would admit a complementary two-dimensional subspace with no local elements, again forbidden. The unique party must therefore share at least two independent local checks whenever a four-by-four cut occurs elsewhere.

**Coincident links BC.** Parties B,C are both four-by-four cuts. The unique shared party cannot be either: it would need two shared checks while having local rank four, which permits only one. Thus A shares both of its checks, and its two dimension-two exception codes are the same K. At B,C one has u=2 and v≤2, so rank H≥4 on each.

An A covector killing K while retaining g expresses g⊗g in the span of the five ordinary BC products. If this span had dimension four, any four independent members would give a minimum four-product representation. The [connected product splitting theorem](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md) forces the local span dimensions including g to sum to at most five, hence one is at most two. All five ordinary BC products belong to this four-space and therefore have their corresponding local factors in that same at-most-two-dimensional span. That would give rank H≤2 on this party. Dimension below four is also impossible, so the five products are independent. Their dual functionals show all ordinary A factors belong to K+span(g), by the seven-term identity. This is the familiar local three-space: at least four ordinary A factors are exterior to K because killing K leaves two copies, and a second covector can kill every exterior type while retaining g.

After this second contraction there is at most one ordinary product, plus the two contracted exceptions. Each contracted exception is a BC matrix equal to zero, one stabilizer Bell term, or a rank-four matrix with the original local supports. Indeed its two logical GHZ labels separate independent Bell blocks, and the A covector supplies their scalar coefficients. If both have rank four, their local intersections on B and C have dimension two each, giving rank of their sum at least 8−2−2=4, even though target minus the one ordinary term has rank at most two. If exactly one has rank four, that matrix is a sum of at most four products (target, one ordinary, and a rank-at-most-two other exception). Rank equality would force its proper local Schmidt code to contain g. If neither has rank four, at most three stabilizer terms remain. These contradictions exclude coincident links.

**Different links BC and AC.** Now C is the only four-by-four cut. The unique shared party must be C, since either other party has a rank-four exception and cannot supply two independent shared checks. Thus the center codes coincide at a dimension-four sector K. On either leaf the local code intersection has dimension at most one: containment of the smaller dimension-two code in the dimension-four code would give another shared signed local marker. The remote intersection also has dimension at most one: the remote dimension-two code has marginal dimension four on the other leaf, whereas the remote dimension-four code has marginal dimension two there, so the former cannot be contained in the latter. Sylvester now gives rank H≥6−1−1=4 on each leaf.

Projecting C to the opposite sector, or contracting with a covector killing K and retaining g, leaves at least four exterior ordinary products. If exactly four were exterior, their minimum four-product representation of g⊗g would have one leaf span of dimension at most two including g; adding the fifth product would increase that bound to at most three, contrary to rank H≥4. All five ordinary factors are therefore exterior. Their five AB products are independent, since a dimension-four span would give the same four-product argument as for coincident links. Dual functionals put all ordinary C factors in K+span(g).

Let P be the signed check of K. All five exterior factors project in the opposite sector to the same ray χ, proportional to the opposite projection of g. This is a stabilizer because the projection of each exterior stabilizer is nonzero. Each exterior stabilizer has either zero component in K or a K component on a Pauli translate of χ: if not definite for P, it has a stabilizer anticommuting with P that interchanges its two projections. All Pauli translates of χ belong to one orthonormal stabilizer basis up to scalars. This basis refines both P sectors, with χ as the only occupied opposite-sector position of g. The full target g has at least four occupied positions in any stabilizer basis, since fewer labels in the three-dimensional binary label space lie in a proper affine hyperplane, giving a Pauli eigenlabel. Hence at least three inside positions are occupied. Each of the five ordinary rays meets at most one inside position; there is an occupied inside position met by at most one ordinary ray. Its stabilizer bra retains g and leaves at most three stabilizers for two copies: one ordinary product and at most one stabilizer per projected exception. This contradicts the two-copy minimum four.

No local frame agreement or coefficient restriction was assumed. The exclusions cover profile pairs 0011+0011 and 0011+0101 up to simultaneous party permutation.
