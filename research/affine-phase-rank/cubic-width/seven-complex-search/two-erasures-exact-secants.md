# Exact lifting of four ordinary products from two local erasures

Fix three supplied exceptional stabilizer rays on X,Y,Z. Suppose g_X and g_Y lie outside the sums S_X,S_Y of their corresponding local Schmidt supports. We test whether four block-product stabilizer terms complete a seven-ray representation of g⊗g⊗g. The test below is complete for these two erasures.

**The interior of a CCZ secant.** Every non-endpoint ray of any physical two-stabilizer secant through g has Pauli nullity three. Use the fifteen secants of local-code-secants.md and the at-most-two rays per line from seven-product-exclusion.md. For a point-mass/full-chirp pair, divide by the full quadratic chirp. An interior vector is constant nonzero off one point and different at that point, possibly zero. For a nonzero Pauli shift the eigenvalue equation off the point and its shift forces its linear character times scalar to equal one on six of eight points, hence to equal one everywhere. Translation invariance then fails at the defect. No nonidentity pure Z stabilizes at least seven nonzero coordinates. For a complementary-plane secant write the vector as g multiplied by two nonzero constants on the two affine halves. A shift preserving these halves would act on g with the same eigenvalue, impossible. For a shift a interchanging them, the derivative of the cubic along a, restricted to either plane, would have to be affine. Its alternating polar is the volume form contracted with a, of rank two and radical span(a); the common plane direction complements span(a), so the restricted polar is nonzero. Pure Z is impossible by full support.

**The surviving party uses one secant.** For each ordinary X ray the quotient modulo S_X must be nonzero and proportional to the quotient of g, since otherwise a covector killing the ray and S_X and retaining g leaves at most three stabilizers for G₂. Normalize it as a_i=g mod S_X, and similarly b_i=g mod S_Y. Absorb weights into the Z vectors. Erasing X and Y separately gives four-product representations of g⊗g on YZ and XZ. Each local factor span contains g. In a four-product representation its two marginal dimensions sum to at most five, so one is two. The two local stabilizer types spanning g on such a line each occur twice: in their independent basis each group must represent the other nonstabilizer target.

If the Z vectors span at least three dimensions, X and Y each have two types occurring twice. Each grouping into two bins pairs Z rays that span g. A participating Z ray has a unique partner, since its line through g contains at most two stabilizer rays. If the X and Y groupings differ, the two perfect matchings form one connected four-cycle, and alternating through the partner relation gives only two Z types. If they coincide, let the two paired XY types be (a₀,b₀) and (a₁,b₁). In either erasure their grouped Z sums must be p g and (1−p)g, with both scalars nonzero and common across erasures. The weighted means of a and b are the corresponding local targets. Subtracting the four ordinary terms from the target leaves a nonzero scalar times (a₀−a₁)⊗(b₀−b₁)⊗g. Each factor has nullity three by the interior-secant fact. If any exceptional local Schmidt code fails to contain the corresponding factor, a stabilizer bra vanishing on the code but retaining that factor leaves its two-factor product represented by at most two stabilizer rays. This violates nullity-two-product-rank.md. Hence all three exceptional local supports must be full on each party, contradicting erasure. This proves that Z uses exactly two different stabilizer rays z₀,z₁ on one secant, each twice.

**Weights before and after lifting.** Expand g_Z=d₀z₀+d₁z₁. Inside each bin k, the two X types form a secant pair and the two Y types form a secant pair, by coefficient comparison in the Z basis after erasing the other party. The ordinary weights in bin k sum to d_k (erase both X and Y). Their ratios p_k and 1−p_k are nonzero and give BOTH identities g_X=p_k a_k₀+(1−p_k)a_k₁ and g_Y=p_k b_k₀+(1−p_k)b_k₁. Thus the four ordinary terms together are exactly

O = Σ_k d_k [p_k a_k₀⊗b_k₀ + (1−p_k)a_k₁⊗b_k₁] ⊗ z_k.

Their full residual, before projection, is

G₃−O = −Σ_k d_k p_k(1−p_k)(a_k₀−a_k₁)⊗(b_k₀−b_k₁)⊗z_k.

A stabilizer bra orthogonal to z_{1−k} but overlapping z_k always exists by completing z_{1−k} to a stabilizer basis. It isolates each branch, so both difference-factor products must have rank at most three. In particular the two secants within a bin cannot both be plane secants. Indeed their weighted differences are two CCZ states with nonzero weights depending only on their respective plane bits. Choose each bit as the first coordinate by an affine change: the cubic sign is zxy up to a binary quadratic, removable by a Clifford. The branch is then exactly the four-nonzero-coefficient W₂ state, of rank at least four by w2-proper-data.md and w2-full-data.md.

**Decision for supplied exceptions.** Enumerate an ordered secant on Z and an ordered secant on each of X,Y for each of its two bins: at most 30⁵=24,300,000 choices. Check each proposed endpoint has a unique scalar normalization to g modulo its given S and that the X/Y weights p_k from the two exact secant expansions coincide. Compute O as above and test whether G₃−O lies in the span of the three supplied exceptional rays. This final test is on the full 512 amplitudes and recovers the three coefficients; in a seven-term minimum all coefficients are nonzero. Gaussian-rational stabilizer gauges permit exact elimination throughout. Every successful test gives the actual four ordinary terms and a physical seven-ray identity, and any completing list appears in the enumeration. The bound counts choices for one supplied exceptional triple and does not claim all such triples have been tested.
