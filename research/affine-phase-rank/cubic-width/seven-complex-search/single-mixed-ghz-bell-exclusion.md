# A sole mixed GHZ and Bell profile

[The companion proof](single-bell-ghz-exclusions.md) records the product-rank, local-code and connected-splitting inputs used here. Expand one GHZ shared across all blocks and one Bell pair shared by A,B into four products indexed by (l,j). The local factors on A,B are four independent code vectors indexed by (l,j), and the two independent C vectors are indexed by l. Thus the local dimensions in the full collection of target, six ordinary products and four expansion products sum to at least 5+5+3=13: g lies outside each proper stabilizer code.

These eleven vectors carry a relation with every coefficient nonzero. If connected, their rank is at most ten, so the connected splitting inequality gives sum of local dimensions at most twelve. If disconnected, projecting the relation to the target's direct-sum component gives a representation using at least eight products. At most two vectors remain outside, and each additional component must carry a relation. Consequently exactly one proportional pair lies outside. One member belongs to the four expansion products and the other to the six ordinary products, since each group is independent. The target component gives a minimum eight-product decomposition, hence a circuit of rank eight, whose sum of local dimensions is at most ten. After removing any one of the four expansion products, their local spans have dimensions three, three and two. Adding g forces dimensions at least four, four and three, which already sum to eleven. This excludes the mixed profile.

The profile consisting of one Bell pair on each of two different links still requires a separate argument.
