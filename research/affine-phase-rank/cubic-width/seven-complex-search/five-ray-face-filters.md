# Necessary face and product-frame conditions for five rays in W₃

Use the four-nonzero-coefficient W₂ lower bound and the notation of four-rays-five-frequencies.md. Let P be a control plane on which all four amplitudes are nonzero, and j a coordinate in its normal. Deleting coordinate j gives a chart on P. L_b denotes the stabilizer basis of q_b on block j. All statements allow arbitrary complex coefficients and conditional occurrence.

**Common signed Pauli.** If two seeds have a common data stabilizer p with an affine sign ε(s) consistent with both on their occurrences in P, then p_j must lie in the union of the L_b attained on P. Otherwise the projector proportional to I−ε(s)p kills both. Contract j with the detecting bra η from four-rays-five-frequencies.md, which overlaps q_b but kills p_j q_b. Restricting to P yields W₂ with four nonzero amplitudes expressed by at most three rays. The same contradiction holds when one seed has this signed stabilizer and two other restricted rays are interchanged projectively by the joint Pauli ε(s)p with one global scalar on P: postselection kills the first and identifies the other two.

**Two local generators.** Suppose one seed has a local-only conditional data stabilizer a on j and a different seed has a local-only conditional stabilizer d there. They cannot be independent commuting labels spanning a local Lagrangian M transverse to each attained L_b. Indeed postselect the M-basis outcome on j with a-sign opposite that of the first seed and d-sign opposite that of the second. These are affine signs on P after extending from occurrence flats. A fixed Clifford basis change followed by controlled affine postselection implements this bra and kills both seeds. Mutual unbiasedness gives nonzero overlap with each local target, preserving four nonzero W₂ amplitudes, again impossible. In particular on an active coordinate face s_j=b at most one seed can factor locally in the opposite basis L_{1−b}. If a seed is absent on P, the other four must satisfy the full local product conclusion for j of four-rays-five-frequencies.md.

**Fully typed data fibers.** If each of five seeds factors on all three data blocks, each local factor in one of the two fixed bases L₀,L₁ with its basis choice independent of controls, the active control set has size at most five. Otherwise choose an active s different from all five types. For each present seed try to assign it to one block at which its type disagrees with s, with capacity three per block. If possible an unused opposite-basis outcome on each used block, and the target bra on unused blocks, gives a product bra killing all present rays and preserving the target. If impossible, Hall's theorem on three copies of each block supplies a subset with more rays than capacity of its neighborhood. With at most five rays, this means at least four rays with the same single available mismatched block. These rays all have one common type b neighboring s. Since there can be only one such heavy type, every active s unequal to a listed type lies at Hamming distance one from b. The star of b contains four vertices, and there is at most one other listed type. This proves the bound. The hypothesis does not follow solely from the five-ray representation.
