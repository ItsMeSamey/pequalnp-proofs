# A GHZ with an AB Bell cannot accompany a Bell path centered at A

Let E consist of a GHZ with a Bell on AB, and let F have Bell links AB and AC. The E local support codes U_A,U_B,U_C have dimensions 4,4,2; the F codes V_A,V_B,V_C have dimensions 4,2,2. The coefficients are arbitrary and absorbed into H=E+F. We use the remote erasures and rank inequality from double-bell-with-path-or-mixed.md: the full ordinary span on X contains g_X and the X support of H; erasing a shared signed local marker gives an ordinary dimension sum at most seven on the other parties. The two-marker exclusion prohibits shared signed local markers on two different parties. Nonzero intersection of two qubit codes on a three-qubit party entails a shared signed check. For cut ranks r,s and local and remote intersection dimensions l,j, Sylvester gives rank H_X≥r+s−l−j.

Suppose first U_C and V_C meet. Then their C marker prevents both equality U_A=V_A and containment V_B⊂U_B. Consequently l_A≤2 (the intersection of distinct dimension-four stabilizer codes has dimension at most two), and l_B≤1. Across A the two remote codes lie in U_B⊗U_C and V_B⊗V_C. These rectangles intersect in (U_B∩V_B)⊗(U_C∩V_C), of dimension at most two, as seen in compatible local vector-space bases. Thus rank H_A≥8−2−2=4. Across B the path has remote dimension two. If that entire remote code were inside the mixed ray's remote code, its local A support V_A would lie inside U_A, forcing the prohibited equality. Thus j_B≤1 and rank H_B≥6−1−1=4. Erasing the shared C marker contradicts the sum bound seven.

Now U_C∩V_C=0. The remote codes across A are disjoint by their C supports. Thus H's local A support equals U_A+V_A by independent extraction. Dimension at most five forces the two rank-four codes to coincide. This common A marker rules out V_B⊂U_B. Across B the disjoint C supports likewise force H's B support to be U_B+V_B, of dimension at least five. Across C every nonzero vector in the path remote code retains the intact AB Bell and has B support exactly V_B, even after restoring the local Clifford encoders. A vector in E's remote code has B support contained in U_B. Therefore the C-cut remote codes cannot intersect nontrivially, and H's C support is U_C+V_C of dimension four. Erasing the equal A code now contradicts the sum bound on B and C. This excludes the simultaneous-permutation orbit (0011,0110) without a relation between the two encoders.
