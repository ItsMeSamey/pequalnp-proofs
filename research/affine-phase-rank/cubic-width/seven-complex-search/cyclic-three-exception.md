# Four product terms and three cyclic entangled terms

Let the target be g_A tensor g_B tensor g_C and let the three additional terms be E_AB tensor c0, F_AC tensor b0, and H_BC tensor a0, with nonzero spectators on lines distinct from the respective target factors. Allow arbitrary complex coefficients, absorbing them into the terms. Define k_AB as the matrix rank of E after quotienting the endpoints by the lines of a0 and b0. Define the other ranks cyclically. If a decomposition with four ordinary product terms exists, each pair of distinct residual edge ranks has sum at most five whenever both are positive.

To see this, write c_j for the four ordinary C factors. Contract C by any covector killing g_C but retaining c0, and quotient A and B by a0 and b0. Only the AB exception survives on the entangled side, so at least k_AB ordinary factors survive. In particular k_AB is at most four. If k_AB=k>0, for every subset of 5-k ordinary C factors their span together with g_C must contain c0. Otherwise a covector as above kills the entire subset and at most k-1 ordinary terms survive.

Quotient C by the plane spanned by g_C and c0. Every subset of 5-k ordinary factors has dependent images there: expressing c0 using g_C and these factors gives a relation with at least one nonzero ordinary coefficient. Hence the four images together span dimension at most 4-k. In the quotient by c0 alone the ordinary C factors span dimension at most 5-k (restoring at most the target direction). Applying the same contraction and endpoint quotients to AC or BC thus gives that edge rank at most 5-k. This proves the claim without any stabilizer assumptions beyond distinct spectator and target lines.

In particular a residual rank four excludes an adjacent residual rank at least two, and even two residual ranks three exclude the decomposition. This excludes the entire cyclic (3,3,3) residual sector. These ranks are measured after the spectator quotients, independently of the ranks of the original entangled pairs.
