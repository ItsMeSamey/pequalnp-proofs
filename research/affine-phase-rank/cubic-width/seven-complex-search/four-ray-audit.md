One direct proof of the affine sign used for postselection is through the unsigned stabilizer tableau before control measurement. Let S be the vector space of global labels, Z the pure control-Z measurement space. For each joint computational outcome with nonzero probability the updated full label space is (S∩Z-perp)+Z. Removing the controls therefore always gives the same data space L. For p in L there is an original signed stabilizer ±Z^a tensor p. Its eigenvalue equation on control s gives the conditional p sign ±(-1)^(a·s). The complementary projector kills that original ray identically, including fibers absent on the selected plane.

For each compatible j, a data label with local part outside both target Lagrangians admits the claimed single bra. In dual two-dimensional spaces, a pairs nontrivially with v, b' pairs trivially with a and nontrivially with u. The joint signed eigenstate exists since the generators commute independently. It has nonzero overlap with each local target by the stabilizer overlap formula, while anticommutation annihilates the translated overlap. The one-basis version follows from the zero expectation of a Pauli outside the target label space. Projecting, contracting and affine decoding are stabilizer operations and may return zero on other rays. On the target the surviving multiplier is a nonzero scalar at each of the four plane points, so every such operation leaves a forbidden W₂ representation with at most three rays. No equality between these four scalars is needed. The needed W₂ result is the weighted one in w2-proper-data.md and w2-full-data.md, both of which I read.

Thus every atom has a fixed unsigned local Lagrangian for each compatible block. The key dimension identity is (π_j L)^perp = L∩E_j within the local label space E_j, using the Lagrangian property of L. This intersection embeds in π_j L, forcing dimension at least two; containment in the union of two subspaces forces containment in one of them, hence dimension at most two. The signed local basis outcome can vary across control fibers. Conditioning the atom on its local basis outcome preserves its other factors whenever the fiber is present.

At a point where every present type mismatches, up to three assigned local rays can be killed in any bin by choosing an unused element of the unique opposite basis. Mutual unbiasedness preserves the target. At most three present rays are therefore impossible. With four present rays any two distinguishable mismatch choices on distinct rays also lead to a killing assignment. Hence the four mismatch sets equal the same singleton and all four outcomes on it must be distinct. Isolating each term now forces the remaining factor to be proportional to the remaining target. Neither cancellation nor complex coefficients weaken this pointwise implication.

For weight two, if only one matching type exists it must occur at both values of the third bit: killing the other rays separately at each control preserves the target. The isolated ray has fixed third-block label space and would need two transverse spaces. With zero matching types the four-outcome isolation gives the same contradiction for each ray. Thus the two typed pairs need two rays each; a fifth point has a different typed pair and cannot invoke the identical-type exception. For weight one the outsider fixes the remaining two-block Lagrangian for all four rays by isolation. The plane point opposite on both remaining bits occupies all 16 basis outcomes; the four rays occupy at most four. For weight three the unmatched point forces a single type for all four rays, whose radius-one ball contains only four points. Extra active points outside the chosen five are immaterial. Native rays give sharpness.
