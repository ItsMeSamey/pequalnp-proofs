# Pauli marker covers against four ordinary products

Let the target be g⊗g⊗g with three original three-qubit parties, and suppose four ordinary block-product stabilizers and three arbitrary stabilizer exceptions represent it. All claims allow arbitrary complex weights and independently chosen encoders.

**Four directions on a line.** In any number n of qubits a complex line in projective space through a state u of Pauli nullity at least two contains at most three stabilizer rays. A normalized stabilizer pair with nonzero overlap above 1/2 shares at least n−1 independent signed checks by the usual overlap formula. Its full span therefore has nullity at most one. Thus any distinct stabilizers on the given line have squared overlap at most 1/4. Four unit vectors in complex dimension two have Gram matrix of rank at most two, whence tr(G²)≥(tr G)²/2=8. The twelve off-diagonal squared magnitudes must average at least one third, a contradiction.

**Local marker cover.** Pick a stabilizer projection on each party, allowing identities. Suppose every exceptional atom is annihilated by at least one of these local maps. If all three retained local target factors are nonstabilizers and at least one has Pauli nullity at least two, the original representation is impossible. Every surviving ordinary factor is still a stabilizer. If at most three products survived, kill each via a covector on its own distinct party retaining the corresponding target. For four products, on every party any two of their factors must span the local target, since otherwise one covector kills both and the other parties kill the other two individually. Repeated local types are impossible, as the target is nonstabilizer. Fixing one of the four types now places the other three and the target on a single line. This contradicts the three-ray bound at the party of nullity at least two.

In particular form a bipartite graph on the three exceptions and the three parties, with an edge when that local Schmidt code is proper and contains none of the [thirty local secant endpoints](local-code-secants.md). Such an edge supplies a signed check whose opposite projection kills that exception and retains a nonstabilizer local CCZ outcome of nullity two, by [the individual outcome classification](nullity-two-product-rank.md). A size-three matching is forbidden. Two exceptions covered by a common such local check on one party and the third by a check on a different party are also excluded (the untouched g has nullity three). For more general covers only projectors of codimension zero, one or two in the stabilizer-check sense need be considered here: imposing three independent checks leaves a single stabilizer ray or zero.

**A shared nonlocal link.** Suppose two exceptions share a signed nonlocal Pauli on Y,Z, with both labels nonidentity and at least one label not a pure computational Z. Its opposite outcome w on g_Y⊗g_Z has stabilizer rank at least three by [the two-copy outcome classification](five-product-remote-erasure.md). If the third exception has a proper X code containing none of the thirty secant rays, choose an opposite local projector killing it, leaving a nullity-two u_X. Project the other two exceptions away with the opposite YZ Pauli projector. Every ordinary term retains a stabilizer X factor and a stabilizer YZ factor, the latter possibly entangled. The target becomes u_X⊗w.

For k≤4 surviving cut products the dimensions of their two factor spans sum to at most k+1 by Sylvester. The remote span dimension is at least three (a basis extracted from its stabilizer generators would otherwise represent w with two stabilizers). The local span therefore has dimension at most two and must include nonstabilizer u_X. Thus k=4 and the local span is a line with at most three stabilizer directions. Some type repeats. A covector killing that type and retaining u_X leaves at most two stabilizers for w, impossible. Consequently a shared noncomputational link check on two exceptions forces the third exception's opposite-party Schmidt code to be full or contain a native secant endpoint. The argument also applies to any stabilizer projector on YZ killing the pair and leaving a target of stabilizer rank at least three.

These criteria apply whether or not any single party can erase all three exceptions. They are necessary encoder tests, not a complete exclusion of the four-product class.
