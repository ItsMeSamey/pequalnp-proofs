# Pauli outcome tests for six rays and seven arbitrary active indices in W₃

Let h be the sole missing index, and q_b the two-bit sign (-1)^(bxy). The local unsigned Pauli spaces L_b of these chirps are transverse two-dimensional Lagrangians. Each basis vector for L_0 overlaps every vector of the L_1 basis.

Suppose a signed Pauli P fixes three atoms from a six-atom representation. In the control-Walsh chart write d for its control X-shift and p_j for its data label on block j. Project the target onto the opposite outcome of P. At most three stabilizer rays remain. Fix the active face s_j=b=1−h_j. If d_j=1 the local factors of the identity and P terms are respectively q_b and p_j q_(1−b). A bra taken from the L_(1−b) basis distinct from the latter ray annihilates precisely the latter of these two contributions. If d=0 and p_j is outside L_b, the bra q_b does the same. The other two controls freely parametrize the face; the surviving identity term gives a W₂ vector with four nonzero coefficients, impossible by the weighted two-block rank-four theorem. The order matters: first project by P, then restrict the control and apply the local bra. Thus any shared signed triple Pauli has d=0 and every p_j belongs to L_(1−h_j). All such labels lie in one fixed nine-dimensional commuting Pauli subspace. If all eight controls are active, either face can be used and the data label must be zero. Any purely control-Z nonidentity Pauli then has a fully active opposite-outcome plane: decode one redundant control, pin its two data bits to zero, and again use W₂. There are no shared signed triples in the eight-active case.

A joint-outcome variant for the seven-active case permits different Pauli labels in the three killed atoms. Fix j and b=1−h_j. Let S be an unsigned commuting subgroup with a compatible choice of signs, with Z-only control factors and injective data projection to j disjoint from L_b except at the origin. The dimension is at most two. Every joint signed outcome must preserve at least four of the six list entries. Expand its stabilizer projector: after the face restriction and contraction with q_b only the identity term survives, giving full active W₂. For dimension two and k_r atoms having label intersection with S of dimension r, each such atom survives exactly 2^(2−r) outcomes. Summation yields 2 k_1+3 k_2 ≤8. These are necessary conditions, independent of the seven nonzero coefficients, and do not assert rank seven.
