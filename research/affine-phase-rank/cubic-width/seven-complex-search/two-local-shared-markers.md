# Two local shared markers on distinct parties exclude a seven-list

Suppose the two exceptions have a shared signed local Pauli on Y and a shared signed local Pauli on Z. Their product is a shared nonlocal remote Pauli, so both labels must be computational Z by five-product-remote-erasure.md. On Y,Z their support is confined to one rectangle C_Y×C_Z whose sides are affine computational planes (four positions each). Consider the remote product dictionary in the quotient by all matrices supported inside this rectangle.

If M is a sum of at most two product stabilizers representing G2 there, Δ=M−G2 is confined to the rectangle. The full-support target factors g lie outside both the row and column spaces of Δ, so rank M=1+rank Δ. The case Δ=0 is ruled out by χ(G2)=4. Otherwise Δ has rank one, both pairs of stabilizer factors are independent, and their spans on each side have the form span(g,d), d supported in the inside plane.

On either side every stabilizer basis factor not confined to the inside plane agrees with a nonzero multiple of g on the entire outside plane. Its affine support is then either full or exactly the outside plane, in which case it is proportional to the zero-extended restriction of g there. Both basis factors cannot be full, since g lies in their span and cannot be represented by two full stabilizer chirps (the one-copy twist lemma). They cannot both be outside-only, as they would be proportional. If one is outside-only and the other full, the full stabilizer must also agree up to a nonzero scalar with g on the entire inside plane, by the representation of g in this basis. Its phase scales on the two planes differ by a fourth root; that two-valued phase is Clifford because it is a fourth root raised to an affine control bit. This would make g a stabilizer. Hence one of the two factors is wholly inside, on each side. The nonconfined factors must be paired in the same product term to cover outside×outside. Thus the other term is wholly in the erased rectangle, and a single product stabilizer agrees with G2 everywhere outside; an outside row and column force its factors to be g up to scale, impossible.

The quotient image of G2 accordingly requires at least three product dictionary terms. The five-product local certificate, which applies to arbitrary remote dictionaries by the proof in two-matched-syndrome-exclusion.md, excludes the seven-list. In particular with a Bell triangle, triangle-local-kernel.md excludes a companion with local rank one anywhere or at most two at two or more parties.

Another check requiring no shared markers: choose a local marker of E at Y and a local marker of F at a different Z and project to their opposite outcomes. If both opposite local outcomes of g are nonstabilizers, the surviving remote target u⊗v needs at least three local product stabilizers (a rank-one sum of two products must have a common local direction, which would be u or v). The five-product local certificate again excludes the list. This is a necessary condition even when signs and local axes differ between the two exceptions.
