# An equal full remote code cannot occur with five ordinary products

Suppose a seven-ray representation has five block-product stabilizers and its two exceptions have rank eight with identical remote image across A. Use the computational affine classification in equal-full-remote-affine-classes.md and denote their coefficient-weighted sum by H. We first strengthen the rank-two difference lemma of equal-remote-one-two-bells.md.

**Every cut full for both exceptions has flattening rank at least four for H.** If the rank were at most three, the cut kernel would have dimension at least five. Either injective encoder maps it into the remote code intersection, whose dimension is a power of two. The two eight-dimensional remote codes therefore coincide. In common encoding coordinates H is proportional, up to Clifford isometries, to I−W with W a scalar times a three-qubit Clifford. The scalar has unit modulus, since W has fixed vectors. Ranks zero through two were already excluded by the rank-two difference lemma (zero or a single stabilizer replacement).

Rank three would give five eigenvalues equal to one. The trace has modulus at least two. As in the rank-two lemma, every fixed unsigned Pauli is fixed with positive sign, and the symplectic fixed space D has dimension d satisfying 2^d=|tr W|²≥4. If D contains anticommuting labels, W commutes with both generators of a logical one-qubit matrix algebra, so W is an identity on that qubit tensored with a unitary on the rest. The rank of I−W is then even. Otherwise D is isotropic and 2≤d≤3. For d=2 the trace bound is attained, so the other three eigenvalues all equal −1. Thus W²=I, and over the binary field its induced F satisfies (F−I)²=0. Its image under F−I lies in its kernel, forcing d≥3. For d=3 choose a stabilizer basis for D. W is diagonal in it, so its phases relative to any one eigenphase have affine binary parity and, when this parity is constant zero, are a quadratic sign. Five fixed entries force that parity to be constant zero. A binary quadratic on three bits has even weight and cannot differ on the other three entries. This proves the rank bound.

**Erasing a proper support bounds two local dimensions.** Whenever H is supported in a proper local stabilizer code on B, take a covector killing that code and retaining g_B. This expresses g_A⊗g_C in k of the five ordinary products on AC, with all retained weights nonzero. The two-copy theorem gives k=4 or 5. Across A versus C the resulting product matrix has rank one. Sylvester's inequality applied to the two factor matrices and the invertible diagonal weights gives d'_A+d'_C≤k+1 for the retained factors. The other 5−k products add at most one dimension on each side apiece. Consequently the spaces spanned by all five ordinary factors on A and C contain g_A,g_C and obey d_A+d_C≤11−k≤7. The corresponding local supports of H also lie in these spaces by subtracting the five products from the target. The same holds after permuting parties.

For (p,q,k)=(0,1,2) or (0,2,1), H is supported on a proper coordinate code on B, while both exceptions are full on A and C. The two lower bounds of four contradict the sum bound of seven.

For (1,1,1), both B and C have proper four-dimensional coordinate codes. Erasing first B and then C gives d_A+d_C≤7 and d_A+d_B≤7. Fullness on A gives d_A≥4. Hence d_B,d_C≤3. Both ordinary spans contain their respective target g, which lies outside the corresponding coordinate code. The local support S_B of H lies in the intersection of that ordinary span and coordinate code, so dim S_B≤2; likewise dim S_C≤2. Since H has rank at least four across A, its remote image must be all of S_B⊗S_C, with both local dimensions exactly two. This product space is contained in the matching code D. The union of its computational supports is the Cartesian product of the B and C support unions. Equality of the copy bit on D forces both unions onto one common value of that bit. Each resulting coordinate sector has just two points (one private bit per party), so both S_B and S_C equal the corresponding two-dimensional computational code.

Now apply the erasure and grouping argument of equal-remote-one-two-bells.md from its paragraph beginning “Next, erasing”. Its only support hypotheses are that the weighted sum H lies in a two-dimensional code on B and a proper code on C, and that rank H_A≥3. These conditions were just obtained. Independence of five ordinary AC products would put every B factor in span(g_B,S_B); killing its exterior rays and erasing S_C leaves at most one product for g_A. If the AC span instead has dimension four, its four-product basis gives a local span of dimension at most two on A or C. The former contradicts the A rank. In the latter, H has C rank at most one since g_C is outside S_C. Grouping by the two available C stabilizer rays and erasing S_B in the smaller group again gives A rank at most two. Thus (1,1,1) is impossible.

For the (0,0,3) graph, write H as the sum of h_x⊗|x,x⟩ over m active pairs, with arbitrary nonzero A vectors h_x. Its B rank is m. The target minus H has B rank at least m−1, so m≤6. Now g_B and g_C both lie outside the active coordinate subspaces, since m<8. Adding the target to −H increases its B rank by one, so m≤4. The full-cut rank bound forces m=4. The B flattening of the five ordinary products has rank five. Its remote span is therefore generated both by their five AC product directions and by the four independent h_x⊗|x⟩ together with g_A⊗g_C (the B factors are independent). Quotient C by the four active coordinates. The image of that span is exactly the line of g_A⊗[g_C]. Every ordinary AC product with a nonzero image would thus have A factor proportional to g_A, impossible for a stabilizer. This contradiction excludes the graph directly for independent input encoders.

The (1,2,0) case was excluded in equal-remote-one-two-bells.md. The one-sided case and party exchanges were accounted for in the affine classification. This completes the equal-full-remote-code exclusion with independently chosen input encoders. Distinct full remote codes and cuts on which neither exception is full are outside this statement.
