# Elementary exclusion of six nonsharing atoms

For a pure stabilizer atom with local Schmidt ranks 2^{e_A},2^{e_B},2^{e_C}, its rank-2^{e_A} Schmidt support on BC has a signed stabilizer group of size N=2^{6−e_A}. Exactly u_B=2^{3−e_B}−1 nonidentities are B-only and u_C=2^{3−e_C}−1 are C-only. These are precisely the original local stabilizers. Their expectations on g^2 have moduli at most 1/2, while a Pauli active on both blocks has modulus at most 1/4. Thus that BC support has probability <=[1+(3+u_B+u_C)/N]/4.

Flat Schmidt coefficients bound squared fidelity globally by 2^(−e_A)P_{e_A}(g)[1+(3+u_B+u_C)/N]/4. For e_A=1 use P_1(g)<=5/8; since at least one of B,C is entangled, u_B+u_C<=10. The result is <=225/2048. For e_A=2 use P_2(g)<=3/4 and u_B+u_C<=14, giving <=99/1024. For e_A=3 use P_3(g)=1 and the same u bound, giving <=25/256. Thus 225/2048 bounds every nonproduct atom, without an alignment theorem. The local P_e bounds here follow directly from the Pauli projector and the G1 local expectation bound.

The elementary local Walsh classification from six-must-share.md gives H fidelity 9/16, M fidelity 1/2 (the fourteen target plane restrictions), all others <=5/16. If six atoms share no signed nonidentity Pauli between any pair, any product atom of fidelity >1/8 consists of H and M, with nonempty H-position set. Those sets are disjoint across these high atoms since H-H overlaps 1 or 1/2 force local sharing. At most one MMM atom occurs: noncomplementary local planes share a signed stabilizer, while complementary planes in two positions share the product of their opposite normals. Other product atoms have fidelity <=(5/16)(9/16)^2<225/2048.

With one high atom at most HHH, one MMM and four remaining contributions sum to at most 3041/4096. Two high ones are bounded by HHM and HMM because of disjoint H positions; one MMM and three remaining give at most 3086/4096. Three high ones must all be HMM, giving (3*576+512+2*450)/4096=3140/4096. With no high atom the sum is smaller. Hence ||A*G3||²<=3140/4096 <1−5/sqrt(512), since 956²>819200. By signed stabilizer projector trace all off-diagonal Gram magnitudes are <=1/sqrt(512). Ordinary Gershgorin and the projection identity contradict span membership. This recovers the six-list sharing theorem without the cotangent bound or stabilizer alignment. The improved nonproduct bound also suffices in the five-list proof.
