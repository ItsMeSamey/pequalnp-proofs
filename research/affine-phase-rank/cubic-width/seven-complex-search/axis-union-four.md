# Exact rank four for the three-axis union

Translate the elementary CCZ sign into 1−2δ where δ marks 000. Subtract the four product terms 1−2(δ_A+δ_B+δ_C) from its tensor cube. On each of all 512 positions the residual equals four times the indicator of the union U of the three coordinate 3-spaces. Its rank is at most four by writing this indicator as the sum of the axis indicators minus twice the origin indicator.

Use the common diagonal Clifford with local exponents a x+b y+c z+2(h xy+k xz+l yz) on the little-endian bits of each block. In A,B,C order the coefficient tuples are (2,0,1,0,0,1), (2,1,3,0,0,1), (2,2,2,0,0,1). Apply the reversible linear change (A,B,C)→(A,B,A xor B xor C). Measure the first two registers in the X basis, retaining separately the all-plus branch and the branch differing only on the low bit of A. Ignore the common scalar. The verifier calculates both branch vectors by literal summation on all 512 rows.

On each input stabilizer ray any two nonzero conditional rays from these fixed Pauli measurements are Pauli translates. Indeed, in the basis diagonalizing the measurements, its affine support fibers are parallel translates. The quadratic phase restricted to parallel fibers differs, after translation, by a constant and an even linear phase, which gives the asserted Pauli relation. Hence if U had a three-stabilizer expansion and both branch vectors require three terms, the multiset of three unsigned output stabilizer groups would occur for both.

The finite certificate rules this out. The dictionary uses every affine support in three bits and every stabilizer phase polynomial in independent affine coordinates. It has 1080 rays. Their unsigned group is identified by the smallest dictionary index among all Pauli translates. Reduce Gaussian integers to F_65537 by i→256. Independent lists of up to three dictionary columns remain independent: a nonzero minor of order at most three is a Gaussian integer of modulus at most 6 by determinant expansion, hence its norm is positive and less than 65537. Complex target membership in such a span implies modular membership by the vanishing of augmented minors.

For fixed target v, choose a normalized pivot coordinate j. Project atoms modulo v, retaining their original j-coordinate as a tag. Fix one anchor atom. Normalize its projected pivot k and eliminate it from all subsequent projected atoms, applying the same operation and subsequent normalization to each tag. A zero remaining vector with nonzero tag detects two-term membership. Matching nonzero vectors with unequal tags detect exactly the triples containing v, since their difference represents a nonzero multiple of v after adjusting the anchor. Group the triples by unordered Pauli labels. The all-plus and flipped outcomes yield 67 and 25 distinct modular group triples and none in common. Neither outcome has a one- or two-term expansion. Therefore the rank of the indicator is exactly four. The conclusion excludes arbitrary complex coefficients and arbitrary off-union cancellations for this particular four-product residual.

Reproduce with C++17 at optimization level 2: compile axis-union-planes.cpp and pipe the output of python axis-union-branches.py into the binary. Each output row gives branch index, number of modular triples of group labels, and running intersection size.
