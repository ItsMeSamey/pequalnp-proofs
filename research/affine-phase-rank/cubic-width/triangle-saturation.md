# Smallest obstruction and exact saturation

A smallest obstruction after affine-row and common-radical closure is ((1+x)y,xz). Its image is {00,10,01}; the two independent polars have rank two, common radical zero, no nonsingular combination and no kernel split. A single nonzero polar is nonsingular after common radical closure; input dimension at most two permits at most one independent polar.

For a verified independent bit block, solve λ∧P_j=0 for a common nonzero λ. Adapt coordinates to λ=x₁. Every quadratic top monomial involves x₁, so both branches have affine images and computable preimages. Test if their union fills its affine hull by cardinalities and affine intersection. Such blocks may be found as coordinate-interaction components or verified common polar orthogonal parts.

For each independent local image S_i compute U_i=span(S_i−S_i) from differences to known reachable points, keeping their preimages. Two affine image pieces only require origins and translated basis points. Matroid intersection finds the maximum t independent differences using at most one per group. By Rado this depends only on the U_i. If t=m=dim ΣU_i, every target in the global affine hull is attainable: ordinary elimination determines which endpoint of each selected direction to use. This is a deterministic polynomial witness algorithm. At t=m−1 every attainable target similarly lies on an attainable affine hyperplane by starting at a witness. The complement of their union is affine or empty.

Normalize triangles as planes minus zero. A deterministic subclass attaches one plane at a time along an existing direction. Initially the hole set C={0}. For a plane inside the current span V update C to the intersection of its three translates. For a new plane meeting V in a line <d>, choose e outside V on that plane. New holes on V are C+d; new holes in e+V are e+(C∩(C+d)). The total hole set is affine or empty. Try each starting plane and greedily attach eligible groups, since eligibility persists. Backtrack the three possible predecessors through stored prefixes to recover an actual witness. This is polynomial for every recognized order, including unboundedly many groups.
