# Bounded local affine closure in the 3-core

On a binary affine parameter space, enumerate each subset of at most six two-bit guards. Compute the affine image of the local coordinates by Gaussian elimination. Enumerate the at most 4096 image points, discard forbidden entries, reject if empty, and pull the affine hull of the surviving local points back to the parameter space. Restricting to the inverse image preserves every feasible assignment. Delete tautologies and merge identical forbidden flats after substitution. Recompute after changes. Every proper shrink costs one dimension or guard, giving deterministic polynomial bit and space bounds including basis changes. Original coordinates can be reconstructed by retaining the substitutions.

For proper three-coloring with colors the three nonzero pairs, any triangle forces its three colors to sum to zero. This follows from its three vertex and three edge guards, and is invariant under invertible latent-coordinate changes. Retaining inferred equations, the six local guards reduce to three distinct ones. In K4 every triple has sum zero, forcing all four colors equal and then zero, hence infeasibility.

In each edge uv of a finite connected simple graph H of minimum degree at least two substitute a diamond: private adjacent vertices a,b both joined to u and v, with no uv edge. Every new vertex has degree at least three. The resulting graph has |V|+2|E| vertices and 5|E| edges. Its native deficit is 3|E|−|V|. It contains H as a minor. Both triangles in each diamond force u=v=c and b=a+c. Connectivity identifies all tips. After substitutions and duplicate deletion the guards are c != 0 and, per diamond, a != 0 and a+c != 0. For each nonzero c each private a has two choices, independently. There are exactly 3 times 2^|E| colorings. More generally, even if local hull equations are added in another order, every globally feasible tuple survives, and the displayed equations still hold at closure. For these instances the displayed affine equations span all affine consequences: differences between the two choices at a single private site span that site's two bits as c ranges over the three possible values, and projecting to c gives full affine hull. Thus no hidden affine restriction obstructs independent elimination. The expanding core construction gives arbitrarily large treewidth by choosing H to be complete, while every individual hull computation uses at most six guards. This is a structured family and not a runtime guarantee on general coloring.
