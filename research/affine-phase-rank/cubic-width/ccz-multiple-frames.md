# Several CCZ frames

Use the notation and Gaussian valuation lemma in [the one-orbit proof](ccz-code-orbit-audit.md). Set γ=1/2+log₂(4/3).

**Equal weights at fixed codimension.** Suppose k arbitrary stabilizer atoms have affine supports of common codimension d, and their coefficients are one common scalar times fourth roots of unity. Absorb the units, obtaining Σφ_j=aG_t. The amplitudes give a nonzero Gaussian integer a. The same degree-3t valuation bound gives |a|≥2^{(t−d)/2}; termwise overlap with the normalized target, using stabilizer fidelity and the triangle inequality, gives |a|≤k·2^{−d/2}(3/4)^t. Hence k≥2^{γt}, allowing distinct directions, phase functions and cancellation. If supports are parallel and d>0, in each of the 2^d cosets the number of terms is a modulo 1−i; thus their total k is even. If d=0 and t>0, all terms occupy every point, and a is divisible by 1−i, again forcing k even. In particular seven equal-unit terms with parallel supports cannot work for three triples.

**Affine restrictions.** Any nonempty codimension-h affine slice of the 3t bits contains an affine sub-slice parametrized by all coordinates of s≥t−h original intact triples. There f restricts to the sum of their cubic monomials plus a polynomial of degree at most two. To show this, parametrize by solving for h pivot bits. In every partially pivoted triple set its free bits to constants. Each such product is at most quadratic. For q fully pivoted triples choose one pivot bit in each and fix its solved affine form to jointly attainable values; at most q new affine conditions result on the still intact triples. Their solution space is nonempty by construction, and all removed products now have degree at most two. Recurse; q≤h/3 and the number of triples lost at this stage plus q is at most h, since a fully pivoted triple used three pivots. If q=0 recursion terminates. The resulting parametrization is affine, and precomposition preserves degree at most two.

**Disjoint aggregate supports, arbitrary orbit coefficients.** Take complete possibly proper translation-code orbits O_j with seeds φ_j and codes C_j. Assume their aggregate affine supports D_j=C_j+supp φ_j are pairwise disjoint. If their joint span contains G_t, these supports partition the cube. On D_j the j-th representation must give the target. Its piece G_t·1_{D_j} is invariant under its own K_u for u∈C_j, so averaging yields the nonzero equal-weight sum of k_j distinct orbit terms proportional to that piece, as in the one-orbit proof. Here k_j is a power of two. With h_j=codim D_j, pull back to the sub-slice furnished by the previous paragraph and absorb the common quadratic sign. Every nonzero restricted term is a stabilizer atom on a translate of the same restricted support direction. The fixed-codimension bound gives k_j≥2^{γ(t−h_j)} when h_j<t, and k_j≥1 otherwise. The partition identity Σ_j 2^{-h_j}=1 therefore yields Σ_j k_j≥2^{γt}. For t≤11, rounding to powers of two improves the local bound to k_j≥2^{t−h_j}, since ceil(γs)=s for 0≤s≤11. Hence Σ_j k_j≥2^t in this range. The coordinate branching expansion attains 2^t.

The restriction on aggregate supports is material: overlapping aggregates may cancel after summing across distinct codes, invalidating the orbitwise projection onto the target piece. Bounds on entire orbit sizes do not bound sparse subsets in a larger orbit.
