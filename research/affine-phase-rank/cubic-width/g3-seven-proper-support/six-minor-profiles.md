# Six-term full-support coefficient constraints on an odd-top six-bit cubic (pending review)

Use the six-dimensional twisted Gauss valuation two, the at-most-four simultaneous zero lemma, and the six-phase independence argument from one-proper-support.md. The common primitive Cramer denominator is at most six. It must be even because an integral-at-two list is incompatible with valuation two. For D=2,6 at least five numerators must be odd, otherwise twist away all negative-valuation terms. On an evaluation row an even denominator requires an even number of odd numerators, so all six are odd. For D=4 the checker allows all positive numerators up to six coprime jointly with D.

Enumerate the projective evaluation rows s_1=1, |sum a_i s_i|=D. Their vector space rank must be six. Let R be the unknown set of actual rows on the 64 inputs; it spans six and lies in this enumerated set. An affine parity of the relative phases is a quadratic bit. If its zero-row set has rank below six, it is nonzero somewhere on R, hence has weight at least 16. A parity plus the normalized output, with its quadratic normalization absorbed, has weight at least 14 by the six-bit minimum-weight argument in one-proper-support.md. If an output-twisted parity vanishes on the entire allowed set the profile is impossible.

Associate a mask of admissible rows with each such bit. Disjoint masks describe disjoint subsets of the real input domain, so their certified weight bounds add. The standard-library checker check_six_minor_profiles.py finds the maximum such sum by enumerating disjoint collections using a union-mask table. For the six D=4 profiles the bounds are 84,76,84,76,76,88, all above 64. The only remaining D=2 profiles are (1,1,1,1,1,1) and (1,1,1,1,1,3). These are necessary candidates only.

For seven G3 atoms all of whose proper support normals coincide, a hyperplane coset requires at least six active full signs by a six-dimensional minor there. Thus there are at most two proper terms. When there are two, they live on opposite hyperplanes. Each six-term restriction has denominator with valuation at most one by the profile sieve. The five globally full coefficients therefore have valuation at least minus one. A quadratic/linear twist simultaneously nulls the two hyperplane terms, either by the independent-direction test or by choosing two compatible independent vectors in the common eight-dimensional direction space. The five remaining Gauss sums are divisible by 16 at the valuation ring, contradicting the twisted cubic valuation three. One proper term is treated separately in one-proper-support.md. Hence seven atoms with a single shared hyperplane normal are excluded.
