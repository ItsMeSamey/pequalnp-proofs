# A lower bound on the union of proper supports, pending independent review

Any representation of the three-block cubic sign by at most seven real supported quadratic signs, with at least one proper support, must have at least 16 points in the union E of all proper supports.

Suppose |E|<16. On the complement, only r≤6 full-support terms remain. Remove columns linearly dependent there and determine new coefficients by Cramer's rule on rows there. For r≥1 use all common denominators from one up to the Hadamard cap, and all positive numerator multisets up to the same bound, normalized by their common gcd with the denominator. The caps for r=1,...,6 are 1,1,1,2,3,6. Absorb coefficient signs into phases. Zero remaining terms cannot produce a sign on the complement.

The exact row test in main.py is the test of the full-support proof with this enlarged coefficient enumeration. The only lists where the test cannot identify a forcing pair are r=1 with weight 1 and r=6 with all weights 1/2. In every other case the pair signs are nonconstant on the complement by linear independence of the columns there. For a witnessed pair-sign fiber, its indicator b is nonzero and quadratic. The value of the cubic bit agrees with a quadratic q on that fiber outside E. Thus the product (p+q)b is supported on E. Its ANF is nonzero by the monomial argument of the full-support proof, and its degree is at most five. A nonzero degree-d binary polynomial on n bits has weight at least 2^(n-d), by induction splitting on a variable of a top-degree monomial. The product therefore has at least 16 nonzero entries. The r=1 exception gives p+q nonzero of degree three supported on E, with weight at least 64.

The r=6 exception requires six of the original terms to have full support. They are independent on the complement, so their coefficients in the original list already have absolute value 1/2. There is exactly one proper atom. Extend its quadratic to all inputs and twist the entire equation by that quadratic sign. The residual is a constant multiple of the affine indicator. Every unnormalized Walsh coefficient of the twisted cubic sign has 2-adic valuation exactly three: expand the integer product over monomials and observe that only the three disjoint cubic monomials covering all nine variables can contribute at valuation three. Every Walsh coefficient of a full quadratic sign is divisible by 32, by the same expansion counting coverage after summing over all input bits: selecting j monomials covering v variables contributes valuation j+9-v, which is at least five since v≤min(2j,9). Half their signed sum thus has all Walsh coefficients divisible by 16. The residual has nonzero Walsh coefficient at every frequency, of valuation exactly three. A nonempty positive-dimensional affine flat indicator has vanishing Walsh coefficients. For a singleton the constant's absolute value is at most four from the pointwise bound on the six half-chirps and target, contradicting valuation three. An empty support has identically zero residual and is excluded as well.

The exact row-pattern check is the only finite computation. This extension leaves seven-atom lists whose proper supports have union size at least 16 unresolved.
