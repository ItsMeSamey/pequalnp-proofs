# Independent audit of the seven-term full-support exclusion

**Verdict.** For p=x₁y₁z₁+x₂y₂z₂+x₃y₃z₃ over F₂, an exact real linear combination representing (−1)^p requires at least eight full-support binary quadratic signs. The lower bound leaves the full-support upper bound open. Lists containing affine-supported signs are outside its scope.

Any real representation has a linearly independent sublist spanning the target by elementary column elimination. Choose a nonsingular sign evaluation matrix from r input rows of that sublist. Both coefficient numerators and their common denominator are determinants of sign matrices, with the target column in each numerator. This requires no prior bound on the magnitudes of real coefficients. Every determinant is a multiple of 2^(r−1) by elementary row subtraction and has magnitude at most r^(r/2). Divide by the common power of two, reduce the joint gcd, absorb coefficient signs into the phases, remove zero coefficients and sort. This gives precisely the positive integer magnitude bounds used by the frozen enumeration.

The real multilinear degree-nine coefficient of the target equals (−2)^3. Each chirp's degree-nine coefficient is a multiple of 32 since each contribution uses at least five distinct quadratic-or-lower monomial factors, even after multilinear reduction. Multiplying the representation by its reduced common denominator D and comparing top coefficients gives 32 dividing 8D. Only D=4,8,12 within the relevant Hadamard bounds remain. Modulo four, the sum of odd-coefficient phases is constant over F₂. The number of odd coefficients is even and nonzero by reduction of the joint gcd. A count of two would force proportional sign columns. Hence four or six coefficients are odd.

Finally a nonempty fiber supplies a nonzero Boolean polynomial b of degree d≤2, and agreement on it would imply (p+q)b=0 for a quadratic q. Given a top monomial m of b choose a cubic block disjoint from m. Their union has d+3 variables. It contains no other full cubic block because m has at most two variables. For the chosen cubic block only m can supply that top term, since any overlapping or lower-degree multiplier has degree less than d+3 after reduction. Thus pb has degree d+3 and qb has smaller degree, a contradiction.
