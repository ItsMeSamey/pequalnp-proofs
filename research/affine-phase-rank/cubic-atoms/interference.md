# Seven cubic atoms require three interfering supports

Partial Walsh transformation on z maps G_t to the scaled graph indicator Γ={(x,y,w):w=x⊙y}. Stabilizer atoms remain affine-supported fourth-root phases. The local graph Γ₁ is a four-point affine simplex, so its six pair differences are distinct, and a k-flat for k=0,1,2,3 contains ≤k+1 genuine points. Successive projections of a d-flat U give fiber dimensions k_i summing to d, hence |U∩Γ|≤∏(1+k_i). For t=3 the upper bounds at d=3,...,9 are 8,12,18,27,36,48,64. A clean flat (wholly in Γ) has dimension ≤3 by its block projections.

With seven atoms and at most one leaking support, no false point can cancel. Seven clean flats cover at most 56 of the 64 genuine points. With two leakers U,V their sets of false points must coincide, and at least 24 true points must survive their sum because five clean terms cover at most 40.

If U,V are distinct, the larger flat contains a hyperplane disjoint from the other, and that hyperplane must be clean. So both dimensions are at most four. Both must have exactly 12 genuine points in four dimensions. On each such flat its four false points lie in some affine hyperplane (four points span dimension at most three), leaving a parallel clean three-flat H. This H must be the product of three local simplex edges, by the block projection dimension bound. Its opposite translate inside U meets Γ in four points, so its three local edge intersection sizes are 2,2,1. A translated edge with two true points equals the original local edge by uniqueness of pair differences. Consequently U is a product of two simplex edges and a two-flat P containing three true points on the remaining coordinate. Its four false points are the product of these two edges and the unique invalid point p of P. They determine both edges, and p determines which three simplex points span P (their XOR is p; all four possible triples have distinct XOR). This gives U=V, a contradiction. The independent checker leakage.py lists 216 clean three-flats and 432 extremal four-flats, each with its own false four-point set.

If U=V their sum survives at ≥24 true points only, so d≥6. The relative scalar-normalized fourth-root phase equals one outside Γ. If its parity were a nonconstant affine function it would have ≥2^{d−1} exceptions, exceeding the overlap bound. Thus it is a real quadratic sign after removing a constant; its exceptional set is the support of a Boolean quadratic on U. The minimum nonzero weight 2^{d−2} excludes d≥8. At d=7 only weight32 fits, a minimum-weight quadratic supported on a five-flat, impossible inside Γ. At d=6 only weight24 fits, giving a nonbalanced rank-four quadratic preserved by its two-dimensional polar radical. These weights follow directly from symplectic elimination. Projecting this radical onto any block gives dimension at most one, or a local two-flat would lie inside Γ₁. A nonzero projected direction forces that block to its unique local pair with this difference. At least two blocks have nonzero projections. Hence the exceptional set has at most 2·2·4=16 points, contradiction.

This gives a necessary interference pattern even with arbitrary coefficients and unrelated complex stabilizer atoms. It leaves unrestricted seven-term rank open.
