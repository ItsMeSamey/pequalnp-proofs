# Real supported quadratic atoms for independent cubic blocks

For the sign of a sum of t disjoint binary cubic monomials, the minimum total absolute real coefficient in an exact expansion by unnormalized supported quadratic atoms is (4/3)^t. After identical atoms are consolidated and signs oriented, the minimizing expansion uses all 8^t distinguished atoms. Every nonmaximizing atom has absolute correlation at most 8/9 of the maximum. These conclusions concern coefficient norm; the shortest exact expansion can use larger coefficients. [Proof](atom-norm.md) · [Stability](atom-gap.md)

Closest full quadratic approximants and the corresponding normalized stabilizer extent have prior treatments. [Kolokotronis, Limniotis and Kalouptsidis](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2009.2030452) · [Bravyi and collaborators](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2019-09-02-181)
