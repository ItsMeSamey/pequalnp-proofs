# Exact real supported-quadratic rank of two disjoint cubic signs

On V=GF(2)^6 set p=x₁x₂x₃+x₄x₅x₆ and G=(-1)^p. Its minimum real linear combination of atoms 1_U(-1)^q with affine U and binary quadratic q has exactly four terms. The upper bound is the two-bit coset decomposition (fix x₁,x₄). This theorem is specific to real signs and two cubic blocks.

**Top-form facts.** Let T be the alternating trilinear third derivative of p, i.e. the sum of the two block volume forms. Its partition parity P(T), the sum over unordered partitions of six coordinate indices into two triples of the product of the two entries, is 1. This is basis-independent over GF(2): the polarized six-vector expression is multilinear and alternating by the label-swap argument of cubic-orbits.md. On a 5-space a degree drop of p to ≤2 is impossible: extend five of its basis vectors to a basis; each partition has an all-interior triple. Translations do not affect T. Thus G on every affine hyperplane is a genuinely nonquadratic sign. The cubic part of a codimension-three affine indicator is the decomposable 3-form made by wedging its three independent normals. Any decomposable 3-form D has P(D)=0. Any form M=ell∧Q with linear ell and 2-form Q likewise has P(M)=0 (take coordinates with ell first). For arbitrary two 3-forms, P(M+D)=P(M)+P(D)+[vol](M∧D), by expanding partitions including both orders of the mixed choice.

**Two-atom observation.** If two nonzero-weight atoms represent an everywhere-defined nonquadratic sign on a positive-dimensional binary affine domain, the unordered absolute coefficients must be {1,1} or {1,2}. If both supports are full, both signs must have constant relative sign: |a+b|≠|a-b| for ab≠0, so the output is one quadratic sign. If neither is full, their union covers the domain only if they are complementary hyperplanes, in which case both coefficients have magnitude one. If exactly one is full, it has magnitude one by the complement of the other support, and the second coefficient must have magnitude two to flip a ±1 to the opposite sign on each point of its support. A zero restricted atom leaves just a quadratic sign if an everywhere nonzero representation exists. Consequently G itself cannot have rank ≤2: each of the last two mechanisms forces G to be quadratic on at least one full hyperplane of V.

Suppose there are exactly three terms. Discard dependence (which would give rank ≤2) and absorb coefficient signs into phases. For any term with proper support, choose a hyperplane disjoint from it. The other two restricted atoms must pass the preceding observation on that hyperplane. Hence a deleted coefficient may have proper support only if the two remaining magnitudes are {1,1} or {1,2}.

**Finite coefficient reduction.** At each point divide the row of three atom evaluations by G(x). It becomes a vector in {-1,0,1}³ with dot product one against the coefficients. Three independent rows suffice to solve for those coefficients. Enumerating all triples of distinct ternary rows, discarding determinant zero, taking absolute values and sorting gives precisely:
(1/3,2/3,2/3), (1/3,2/3,4/3), (1/2,1/2,1/2), (1/2,1/2,1), (1/2,1/2,3/2), (1/2,1,3/2),
(1,1,1), (1,1,2), (1,1,3), (1,2,2), (1,2,3), (1,2,4), (2,2,3), (2,3,4).
This is a small exact computation, reproducible with the patterns() function in search_two_block_cubic.py or the standalone exact checker. If all three supports are full then the same enumeration with rows restricted to {-1,1}³ leaves only (1,1,1). This full-support integer case is impossible: an integer sum of quadratic signs represents, modulo four, a constant plus twice a degree-≤2 Boolean polynomial, whereas G=1-2p with p cubic. The deletion rule therefore eliminates every fractional triple and the final two triples without a remaining case.

**All three weights odd, (1,1,1) or (1,1,3).** The XOR of the three support indicators equals one. If one support is full then the other two are identical; unless all are full, their common complement contains a hyperplane on which the one term would have to equal G. If all are proper and all have codimension one, the integer Möbius coefficient of each atom on all six variables is divisible by 8, by cubic-dyadic.md (d=6,k=1); the coefficient for G is 4. For completeness this divisibility follows by expanding its single affine indicator times products of (1-2x^M), |M|≤2; a monomial with valuation l covers at most 2l+1 variables. If exactly two are hyperplanes, XOR forces the third indicator to be affine as a Boolean function, contradicting codimension ≥2. If at most one is a hyperplane then covering all 64 points requires exactly one half and two quarters forming a disjoint partition. G would be quadratic on the half, impossible.

**Weights (1,2,3) and (1,2,4).** In the former case the odd-weight supports must be complementary by parity. The weight-one support cannot be proper since the remaining pair {2,3} is forbidden; if full it forces the weight-three support empty. In the latter case the weight-one support is full by parity, and the weight-two support must be full by the deletion rule applied to {1,4}. Modulo four the weight-four term disappears, and twice the full weight-two sign is 2 everywhere, forcing G to equal the negated weight-one quadratic sign globally.

**Weights (1,1,2).** Parity forces the two unit supports to be complementary halfspaces H₀,H₁. The weight-two atom has support W. At any point in W, an output of absolute value one forces its sign to be opposite the unit term, and G equals the sign of the weight-two atom. Outside W the output is the active unit term. It follows that p+1_W is quadratic on each H_i. Each slice W∩H_i must be nonempty, since p itself cannot be quadratic on H_i. In fact 1_W restricted to H_i must have degree precisely three: p is cubic there and p+1_W is quadratic. A nonempty affine-space indicator has degree exactly its codimension, so codim(W∩H_i in H_i)=3. Since W meets both parallel halfspaces, codim W=3.

Let D be the 3-form for 1_W and let ell be the normal of the two H_i. The cubic form M of p+1_W has shape ell∧Q, since a polynomial quadratic on each slice can be written q₀+ell(q₀+q₁) after extending its two quadratic restrictions. As T=M+D, the top-form identity gives [vol](M∧D)=1. In coordinates where D uses the first three directions, this means M is nonzero on the remaining three-dimensional direction space of W. There D vanishes, so T is nonzero there too. This contradicts G equaling a quadratic sign on W.

**Weights (1,2,2).** The unit support is full. Let q be its phase and let U,V be the other supports. Reduction modulo four gives p=q+1_U+1_V over GF(2). A codimension-k indicator has highest exterior form equal to the wedge of its k normals, encoding its direction space as the radical of that k-form. If either codimension exceeds three then the two high degrees must coincide and the top forms must agree, so U,V are parallel. Equal cosets cancel, while two distinct parallel cosets have affine union. In either case the resulting cubic part is either zero or one decomposable 3-form (or its degree exceeds three, conflicting with the equation). This contradicts P(T)=1. If at least one codimension is below three and both are ≤3, the cubic part is again at most one decomposable 3-form. Thus U,V both have codimension three, and T=D_U+D_V. Since P(T)=1 their two triples of normals together have rank six by the top-form identity. Hence U∩V is exactly one point.

On U outside that point the weight-two sign must negate the full unit sign, since the other weight-two term is absent. The two phases giving this required identity are quadratic on a 3-space. Agreement on seven of its eight points forces agreement on the eighth: a nonzero Boolean function supported at a single point of a 3-space has degree three. The same applies to V. At the intersection both weight-two signs therefore negate the full unit sign, yielding magnitude three rather than one. This excludes the last triple.

The bounded SAT search is supplementary and inconclusive on some triples. The proof relies on the finite coefficient enumeration and the preceding mathematical eliminations, not on any inconclusive SAT output.
