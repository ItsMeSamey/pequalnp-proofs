# Distinct long heads with short payloads

Let K be a bounded-width core. Partition the remaining normalized clauses into p groups with a distinct signed head H_g shared by every clause of group g and at most k additional literals per clause. Shapes, signs and overlaps between heads are arbitrary. Let B_g be the k-CNF of the additional payload clauses and let M_g mean that every literal of H_g is false. The group's predicate is 1+[M_g]([B_g]−1). Exhausting this expression gives at most 3^p restricted bounded-width counts regardless of how many clauses share each head.

A stronger implementation computes the exact answer by TWO bounded-width counts on at most n+3p variables, independently of the sum of head lengths. Add u_g,v_g and forbid both being one. Imply every falsifying head literal from each of u_g and v_g, using binary clauses. For each payload clause D include (¬u_g OR D). Give assignment weight (−1)^{sum v_g}. Holding x fixed, a mismatched head allows only the neutral choice and gives 1. A matched head allows the neutral choice, the negative v choice and an additional positive u choice exactly if B_g is satisfied. Its local signed sum is [B_g]. The auxiliary choices for distinct heads are independent at fixed x, so their joint signed count times the core filter equals the desired solution indicator. Encode the global v parity by a uniquely extending XOR chain and subtract odd count from even count, both of width at most max(3,k+1,width(K)). The construction is polynomial in the explicit input. With the manuscript's fixed-width exponent O(N(log log N)^2/sqrt(log N)), p=o(n sqrt(log n)/(log log n)^2) suffices for subexponential variable-time. The same conclusion holds unconditionally as a reduction to two bounded-width calls, independently of the status of the fixed-width theorem.

For fixed k, enumerate candidates by deleting zero through k literals of each long clause. Every useful head occurs in this polynomial-size list. Cover clauses by those containing each candidate with ≤k remaining literals. Greedy set cover determines an actual grouping and uses ≤(1+ln m) times the minimum number of heads, by standard uncovered-set charging. This ensures a subexponential instance algorithm whenever that resulting p meets the bound. A supplied grouping can always be checked without this factor.

For groups with a shared selector scope S but arbitrary signs, the relevant heads correspond to distinct falsifying words on S. Every clause assigning all of S contributes to a single such group. If a clause omits at most s selectors, enumerate the at most 2^s compatible completions of its partial word and insert the payload into each complete word's group. Mutually disjoint complete selector words exactly realize the original partial-match semantics. The reduction then depends on the number of distinct generated words across all groups. Empty payloads and overlapping groups are allowed throughout.

The signed cancellation is a specialization of the general long-clause selector technique. Equivalently, a head output variable can be forced by the bounded forward implications and one exceptional converse clause per head, to which the ordinary signed-selector reduction applies. The grouping parameter and its recognition criterion relate that existing reduction to dense families with few distinct long heads. [Wu and Wang, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.02560v1#S4).
