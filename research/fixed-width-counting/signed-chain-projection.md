# Discovering eliminable signed occurrence chains

Preprocess tautological and duplicate clauses, and count unused variables externally. For a used variable v write S_v for its incident clause rows and give each such row its literal sign. Put v below w when S_v is contained in S_w and the two signs on S_v agree up to a single common flip. This is transitive by composition of flips. Break ties within equal-support equivalent columns by a fixed order. The resulting poset is computable directly from the explicit CNF. On any chain gauge each variable against the top column. A row's literals on this chain are empty or a monochromatic suffix. Consistent unions of clause-falsifying assignments therefore have at most 1+2h_i restrictions on a chain of length h_i. A union of t chains with h coordinates can be eliminated by at most product_i(1+2h_i)<=(1+2h/t)^t ordinary counts on n-h visible variables as in hidden-closure-projection.md. Clauses can meet any number of chains. A largest union of at most t disjoint chains is found by min-cost flow: split each vertex by a unit-capacity arc of cost -1, link comparable vertices forward with zero-cost arcs, and send t paths from a source to sink (allow empty paths). Integral optima give precisely maximum covered vertices. Additional residual-width promises are checked on the resulting chains; this flow only optimizes the covered vertex count.

## Bulk evaluation without enumerating cross-chain states

For each nonempty selected chain take the Boolean value of its top gauged variable and the length l in [1,h_i] of its longest top monochromatic run. If l<h_i, the predecessor must be opposite and all earlier bits are free, giving multiplicity 2^{h_i-l-1}; if l=h_i the multiplicity is 1. A suffix of length r is falsified exactly when the top value matches its forbidden bit and l>=r. Encode a profile for each chain and compute the total J=sum_i max(h_i-l_i-1,0), a number between zero and n. Summing 2^j times the ordinary count of profiles and visible inputs having J=j gives the exact original count in at most n+1 calls.

Fix d>=2. For each chain of length h encode l-1 by d one-hot base-B digits, B a power of two with B^d>=h and B=O(h^{1/d}) (B>=2). Enforce one selected value per digit using a parsimonious sequential prefix-OR plus at-most-one clauses with prefix bits. Convert each selected digit to binary by OR trees, concatenate to get l-1, reject out-of-range codes, compute the local exponent and total J by ordinary bounded-fanin arithmetic. This takes O_d(sum_i (h_i^{1/d} log(h_i+1))+log(n+1)) variables beyond the visible bits: compress triples columnwise to accumulate local binary numbers and finish by binary addition. The sign bit per chain is included.

For a clause touching a chains, write the joint antecedent that every chain suffix is false. Each comparison l>=r is a union of at most dB+1 conjunctions, by the first position of inequality in lexicographic base-B comparison, treating equality separately; each conjunction mentions at most d one-hot digit literals. Include the sign match for that chain. Distribute over the a products and forbid every combined antecedent unless a visible literal of the clause holds. No per-clause auxiliary variables are needed. With at most k visible literals and bounded a these clauses have width <=k+a(d+1), and their total length is polynomial for fixed a,d. The resulting bounded-width solver uses the visible bits plus the displayed overhead even if there are many chains and their clauses cross. Large chains imply wide source clauses because the bottom variable appears in a clause containing its entire chain. The construction gives no unconditional better worst-case bound for native constant-width SAT.

## Sparse overload and automatic chain selection

Fix visible-width bound k and chain-arity bound a. For each clause exceeding either, replace its direct expansion with one negative cancellation switch u. If selected, u forces all visible literals false and all matched chain signs by binary clauses; it also forces l>=r in each chain by forbidding each low digit-prefix cell representing l-1<r-1 under u (width <=d+1). Thus the signed switch contributes the original clause's satisfaction indicator without any arity bound. With E overloaded clauses the total added variables increase by O(E), global switch parity is counted even-minus-odd, and at most 2(n+1) queries suffice. Polynomial output construction follows for fixed a,k,d. Other source clauses retain their ordinary bounded-width encoding.

Write deg(v) for its number of incident clauses. A chain touches a clause precisely when its top variable does. Therefore E is at most sum deg(top)/(a+1) plus sum deg(v)/(k+1) over unselected variables v. For each fixed allowable path count t, minimize n-h plus this upper bound by assigning each selected vertex flow reward 1+deg(v)/(k+1), each selected path a terminal cost deg(top)/(a+1), and allowing empty paths. Constants can be scaled to integers. The additional radix cost is bounded by O_d(t(n/t)^{1/d} log(n+1)+log(n+1)) for t>=1 by concavity. This supplies a polynomially optimized measurable surrogate on arbitrary inputs. Inspecting the actual overload count and chain sizes can improve the final choice.

The logarithmic multiplier on the radix overhead can be removed. Build a complete dyadic interval OR tree on the B one-hot value bits in each digit. For output bit j, OR the B/2^{j+1} tree nodes for length-2^j aligned intervals in which bit j is one. The total number of additional OR inputs over all output bits is <B, so all binary bits cost O(B) gates including the interval tree. Each chain's arithmetic costs O_d(log(h_i+1))=O_d(h_i^{1/d}). This sharpens the oracle variable count to |V|+O_d(sum_i h_i^{1/d}+E+log(n+1)), and sharpens the uniform overhead for t paths to O_d(t(n/t)^{1/d}+log(n+1)).
