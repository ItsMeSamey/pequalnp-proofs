# Native measurement fibers and the cost of two Taylor pins

The group sum in eq:group-sum of the October manuscript fixes measurements and pins E to one. Choose pivot columns of its measurement matrix C, enumerate Boolean nonpivots, and solve for pivots, rejecting inconsistent or non-Boolean solutions. In a group of size s, finding a representative costs at most 2^{s−rank C} iterations. The pinned sum requires 2^{s−|E|−rank C_free} iterations after subtracting the pinned columns from the target. Rational elimination and minors give polynomial bit complexity per iteration. These group bounds replace the two scans for each tuple without changing the original measurement or Taylor multiplicities; no rank lower bound is assumed.

Suppose the local evaluator exposes a private signed factor with B=I+N, where N is strictly upper triangular with constant or flag-labeled arcs. Its sign is (−1)^{yᵀBz} over binary vectors y,z of dimension d. All other factors and measurement rows must ignore these private variables and private flags once exterior variables are fixed. Set pinned flags first. Let I,J be the coordinates of y,z pinned to one by the Taylor query. Expand the pin indicators as sums of characters with masks a⊆I,b⊆J. Each character gives 2^d(−1)^{bᵀB⁻¹a} after summing y,z. Its scalar weight is (−1)^{|a|+|b|}/2^{|I|+|J|}. If every directed path from a b site to an a site traverses at most two unfixed flag arcs, the character exponent is quadratic in the remaining flags, and its complete signed sum follows by polynomial-time binary Gauss elimination (see private-feedback.md). Thus h exterior bits and k free private flags cost at most 2^{h+|I|+|J|}poly(input) rather than a raw 2^{h+2d+k} scan. For zero private measurement columns any exterior measurement representative extends by zeros on private blocks. The rule requires the stated identity at the actual substituted factors in the truncated coefficient ring. Recognition of this structure in a generic generated SAT instance remains open.

Remove the return arc of the private-arc #BIS construction in private-feedback.md. The forward support now has zero feedback. Two ordinary pins y_t=z_s=1 give 2^{d−2}((−1)^p−1) after summing the hidden vectors, by the four possible masks. Thus summing flags gives −2^{d−1} times the number of flags with p=1, which recovers exact #BIS. A pin on one shore alone yields zero; with no pins the value is always 2^d per flag assignment. This hardness applies to the general local interface, with no claimed realization by a particular October sparse-SAT layout.
