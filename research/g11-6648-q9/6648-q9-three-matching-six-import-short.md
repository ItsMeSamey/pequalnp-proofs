# Short six-import exclusion

Use the original ledger and raw-reader facts in 6648-q9-three-matching-closure.md, independently of its later m≤5 invocation. If m≥6 the number of nonimport paid OR slots is at most six. The 5−r nonmandatory exterior labels reserve 2(5−r) slots, with r≤2; hence m=6,r=2 and the three nonmandatory exterior labels Y reserve all six slots. The two mandatory exterior labels cannot occur on those ORs and thus each has a sole dirty AND reader; no dirty gate has two raw pins.

Each of the three ORs is either private to a sole Y label with singleton-negative sibling, or has two distinct multiply read Y labels as its actual raw pins. Multiple-reader labels have degree exactly two within the graph of paired raw ORs. Duplicate gates computing a raw pair OR are redundant. With three labels, either every reader is private or the three labels form a triangle of raw OR gates.

In the all-private case no pretop OR can precede the earliest dirty AND. Every endpoint import requires a raw-empty second inlet, which has a dirty AND ancestor by TopForm; every private exterior OR needs the same since its second inlet is singleton-negative. Remaining pretop ORs are Lambda, and before a dirty AND their two positive raw arms can only union identical singleton endpoint labels in this matching, a redundant wire. A clean gate cannot precede the first dirty AND in its ancestry by TopForm. Thus this dirty AND has two direct raw pins, impossible by the raw-pin lemma.

In the cycle case no edge can have both endpoints in Y. Such a pair would need two active dirty ANDs entering opposite sides of an ordinary top: a shared dirty AND is impossible by its independent witness, and the two other dirty ANDs need their respective exterior literals outside Y. All pairs of Y give the same complete three-OR code. Hence an outside neighbor of Y must hit at least two vertices in Y (fix it as background and compare the pairs), since Y itself is independent. For each outside neighbor with d hits, d≤2 binom(d,2); four-cycle-freeness gives at most three shared-neighbor incidences when counting unordered pairs of Y. The total degrees of the three Y vertices are therefore at most six and at least six. Equality entails that all three have degree two, all neighbors hit exactly two, and all three pairs of Y share a neighbor. Among lows 0,1,2,3 the only pair sharing a neighbor is 0,2. This contradiction excludes six imports without a triangle-table or a sole-OR prohibition for vertex 8.
