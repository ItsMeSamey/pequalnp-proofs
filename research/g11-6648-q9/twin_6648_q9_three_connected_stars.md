# Three connected starred edges at q=9, j=3, c=0

Use the issued G11 least TopForm hypotheses and let W be the endpoints of the three original starred edges, O its complement. Count q outside Lambda. The only connected simple three-edge shapes are a triangle, K1,3, and P4. Every shape is impossible.

| Shape | Exterior labels | Necessary paid ORs | Obstruction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Triangle | 8 | ≥9 exterior proper unions and ≥2 W-only imports | Exceeds q=9 |
| K1,3 | 7 | ≥7 proper unions and ≥2 W-only imports | Four cover traces require ≥14 singleton occurrences, only 12 available |
| P4 | 7 | ≥7 proper unions and ≥2 W-only imports | Same four-cover obstruction |

An exterior proper union physically merges two nonempty incomparable original raw traces on O. All such ORs are paid: a Lambda anchor stars every raw label it reaches. Arbitrarily many physical Lambda gates merging W-labels are allowed and give no exterior proper union.

## Seven exterior labels need at least seven proper unions

Take the six original junk-AND pin traces on O from 6648-star-exterior-separation.md. As in 6648-exterior-union-cost.md, let d be the count of distinct nonempty selected traces, h the number of nonsingleton traces, and k the number of labels missing a singleton trace. Then k−h=7−d≥1, the k words are incomparable on h coordinates, and a pruned union DAG with m proper unions obeys m≥2k−h. Each missing-singleton label enters at least two distinct effective gates as a singleton operand. Each union gate other than a target must in turn occupy a later union operand slot.

Five missing labels cannot have four-coordinate codes. They would be five of the six 2-subsets of four coordinates by Sperner (a singleton or triple leaves room for at most three other words). Call the words 12=v,13=A,14=B,23=C,24=D. For any omitted vertex p of degree ≥3, a nonadjacent pair r,s of other selected labels extends to an original maximal independent set omitting p: if neither neighbors p, its ≥3 neighbors cannot all be adjacent to r or s, because each has at most one common neighbor with p. Consequently whenever word(p) is covered by word(r) union word(s), r,s must be adjacent. The same implication holds even for low p if r or s already neighbors it.

Vertex v must be low, since otherwise {A,B} versus {C,D} force all four cross edges. A high vertex A forces adjacency vC by its word cover; symmetrically C forces vA, B forces vD and D forces vB. The only low vertices are 0,1,2,3 and they form an independent set. Therefore high vertices among A,B,C,D occur in mate pairs {A,C} and {B,D}; each high label forces a distinct high neighbor of v. Exactly one pair is high, since five labels cannot all be low and v has degree two. By symmetry take A,C high. Then vA,vC are edges. The pairs (A,C), (B,C), (A,D) each cover v with a neighbor of v present, forcing edges AC, BC, AD. Thus v lies on a triangle with A,C, each of which has another low neighbor (D or B). The only low triangle vertices are 1 and 3; their other triangle vertices 4,8 or 6,7 respectively have no other low neighbor. Contradiction. The six-word, four-coordinate case is impossible by the same degree-three/K2,2 test (6648-two-touching-stars-eight-merges.md).

If m≤6, k−h≥1 and m≥2k−h imply h≤4; Sperner leaves only the five-label/four-coordinate case, excluded above. Thus m≥7. If m=7, h≤5, and the exclusions just proved force h=5, k=6, d=6. Equality m=2k−h exhausts all 14 input slots: twelve are singleton occurrences and the remaining two are precisely the required uses of the two nontarget union gates. No output union feeds another, and no union result is shared. The five output union expressions therefore have total support cardinality at most twelve (unfolding the disjoint binary trees uses just those twelve leaves). This conclusion accommodates repeated labels and any original gates that disappear in the exterior-trace projection.

## Two W-only imports force four large pin traces

The induced exterior graph has vertex-cover number at least 7−|W| because a cover there extends by adjoining W. In particular it has an edge. Every ordinary top has both raw sides nonempty and entirely in W. On an accepted exterior edge the actual winning ordinary top needs a nonraw contribution on each side. Trace a live OR-only path backwards on each side to a true dirty AND (TopForm excludes upstream clean AND gates). In the forward direction take the first OR along this path whose original raw support becomes nonempty. It has a raw-empty operand and W-only positive raw support; it is paid, and is not an exterior proper union. The two paths cannot share any such import: its original raw leaf alone would activate both sides of the supposed clean top. Hence there are at least two distinct W-only imports.

For a triangle the exterior size is eight; 6648-two-touching-stars-eight-merges.md derives m≥9 using only eight exterior labels, original maximal independent sets and the six-pin cap, irrespective of the edges within W. This already contradicts the two additional imports.

For K1,3 or P4 the budget forces exactly seven exterior proper unions and exactly two W-only imports, with no other paid gate. No pretop raw-empty OR exists (the earliest such OR would have a dirty AND inlet, hence be dirty and paid). Both imports therefore directly take dirty ANDs. On every exterior graph edge an actual winning top must receive both distinct donors, one on each side: every OR-only dirty-to-top path enters one of the two imports, and the same donor on both sides would activate that top on an independent positive donor witness. In particular both donors are true on every exterior edge. This argument allows arbitrary propagation and reuse of imports amongst W-only ORs.

Every one of the six physical junk pins has nonempty exterior raw trace since d=6. Such a pin is physically pure: an OR-only route from a dirty AND to it would enter a W-only import, then have a first OR whose exterior trace becomes nonempty. That gate has an operand with empty exterior trace, so is not a proper exterior union. Its exterior raw vertex precludes Lambda, and it differs from both W-only imports, exceeding q. TopForm precludes a route from a clean AND. Thus each of the four operand wires of the two distinct donors is a raw disjunction. Its support restricted to O must cover every exterior edge, requiring at least three exterior labels since |W|=4. All four pin traces are distinct. Four traces of size ≥3 and the fifth nonsingleton trace of size ≥2 give total size ≥14, contradicting twelve. This proves both four-endpoint cases without restrictions on overlaps in the original W-only raw cones.

This argument concerns connected three-edge masks only. Disconnected masks have at least five endpoints and require a separate analysis.
