# Two touching stars require nine effective exterior unions

Use the original G11 graph, two distinct starred edges sharing one endpoint, j=3 and c=0. Put W equal to the three star endpoints and O=V minus W. There are eight exterior labels. The ≤6 physical junk pin supports restricted to O separate every omitted exterior label from every original maximal independent set, by 6648-star-exterior-separation.md. The argument concerns support traces and never substitutes W=false into the graph.

Remove duplicate and empty traces. Let d be their remaining number, h the number of nonsingleton traces and k the number of exterior labels without a singleton trace. Then δ=8−d=k−h≥2. Let m be the number of effective proper binary unions in a construction of the nonsingleton traces by the original raw DAG. The exterior-union cost lemma gives m≥h+2δ. Suppose m≤8. A deficit ≥3 is impossible since it needs ≥11. Therefore δ=2 and h≤4. The k=h+2 distinct missing-singleton codes are incomparable on h coordinates (each is an exterior vertex's pin membership). At h≤3 Sperner width is ≤h, so h=4 and k=6. All six words must be the six 2-subsets: a six-element antichain on four coordinates attains equality in the maximal-chain bound only at size two.

The six exterior labels include some v of original degree ≥3 since only 1,2,3,0 have degree two in G11. Relabel the four coordinates so v has code 12. For a,b different from v and from each other, if a,b are nonadjacent and exterior, an original maximal independent set contains both and omits v. Indeed, if either neighbors v then extend {a,b}; otherwise each has at most one common neighbor with v by four-cycle-freeness, so a neighbor of v avoids both and can be adjoined before extension. Thus the original separating supports ensure the code of v is not contained in the union of the codes of any two nonadjacent a,b. Singleton trace coordinates discarded for these six labels were zero on all six, so they cannot alter this obstruction. Each choice of a from {13,14} and b from {23,24} covers 12. All four cross pairs must then be graph edges, contradicting four-cycle-freeness. Hence m≥9.

For q=10 at b≥2 original direct dirty-AND-reading ORs, m≤q−b≤8, impossible for every sole reader. If b=1 and the sole reader is M=x∨E with E a raw-empty OR, M is paid and nondirect, but copies singleton {x} (or empty) on exterior raw traces. Thus both the unique direct entrance and M are unavailable for proper unions, again giving m≤8. If b=1 and M=x∨D has a dirty AND sibling, the unique direct entrance is M. This is the all-q unique direct sole-OR branch of singleton_6648_all_q_single_entrance.md; already its private-donor escape gives c≥1 since D's external AND-only route has no other direct OR exit and cannot return to either fixed pin of M. This last branch is independently excluded at c=0 for both touching and disjoint masks. These arguments do not claim the disjoint mask impossible from the nine-union lower bound.
