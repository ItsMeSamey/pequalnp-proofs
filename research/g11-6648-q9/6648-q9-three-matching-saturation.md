# Six endpoint imports are impossible

Use the assumptions and ledger in 6648-q9-three-matching-endpoint.md. Six imports leave only three paid ORs for the five exterior readers, hence two dirty ANDs have respective exterior raw literals a,b. First allow a=b. Set a,b false. Only one dirty AND D can survive and its two input functions reduce to raw disjunctions. On every top the dirty alternative can occur at only one pin, since a shared D would violate the original top's cleanliness. Thus all surviving nonstar edges belong to a single clean three-clause product, consisting of the D pins and the union U of the opposite singleton raw labels. U uses only endpoints.

The edges of such a product are covered by a clique of at most three. Singleton negativity means each vertex hits at most two clauses. Every positive pair includes a vertex with a doubleton type, and different doubleton classes fully cross. Three nonempty classes must be singletons by four-cycle-freeness. With two classes the two representatives work if neither is duplicated; a duplicated class has only one common neighbor, in the other class, which then covers everything. With one class the graph is a four-cycle-free biclique and has a single center. Empty products need no center.

Remaining exterior vertices are independent because an exterior edge needs two distinct active dirty ANDs. Cover the original graph by a,b, the clique, and one representative of each matched group still uncovered. If a=b, a seven-vertex cover would require three exterior clique vertices; impossible. For distinct a,b a two-clique would need both ends exterior to attain seven; impossible. A triangle must hit at most one matched group; since at most one member is exterior it consists of the entire matched edge and one exterior vertex. Deleting it and a,b leaves six vertices with exactly two disjoint edges.

The three triangles are 148,367,589. Complements of 148 and 367 have ten and eleven edges of respective maximum degree three and four, so two deletions cannot leave two edges. The complement of 589 has edges 0–10,14,2–10,36,37,4–10,67,6–10. Removing six edges forces 10 and k in {3,6,7}. Thus two matched groups are 14 and the edge on the other two triangle-367 vertices. Both 0 and 2 are exterior; they must attach to 9 and 5 respectively in the clean product, and to no other triangle vertex. Their singleton types are distinct and neither carries U. Thus 9 and 5 miss the first two coordinates in some order, and 8, missing U, is the exterior triangle vertex. The third matching edge is 59.

The exterior reader ledger is saturated: every multiply read label has two readers, each containing two distinct raw exterior inputs, while sole readers are private. Neither of the two dirty ANDs can have a second exterior pin: choosing it as the mandatory literal gives either a different pair from the uniquely forced {10,k} or an excluded coincident pair. Thus 10,k have sole AND readers. Of the other exterior vertices 0,2,8, vertex 8 cannot have a sole OR reader by the original forbidden-sole-OR lemma in freeze-6648-p14-free-neighbor/THEOREM.md (use its neighbor 1). Its two OR readers have distinct multiple-reader partners 0,2 (identical ORs are redundant); their degree of two forces the last raw reader to be 0∨2. All three raw ORs have identical values on exterior sets {0,2} and {0,8}. Adding the same background {1} gives one independent input and one containing edge 18, a contradiction. Consequently all matching masks have at most five endpoint imports.
For t=3 there is a single final pin per endpoint. A singleton-label OR trace cannot realize all seven nonempty physical donor subsets: each of the three two-element subset wires would have to reach the sole pin by a strictly increasing OR path, hence all three would feed the unique full-set wire directly. Duplicate same-label frontier subsets are semantically equal and excluded by leastness. Thus λ_v≤min(2^{m_v}−1,6)−m_v. With λ=4 and at most five imports only (m_u,m_v)=(3,2) is possible, giving λ_u=3 and λ_v=1 and zero imports elsewhere. All low endpoints must be among u,v by the six-AND floor.
The 3-import endpoint realizes six nonempty physical donor subsets. The sole terminal pin receives them all along OR paths and consequently contains all three donors. Its opposite endpoint cannot contain any donor: a common dirty donor would force a false positive at a clean top. Thus the two imported endpoints are on distinct groups, and every top has a pure raw endpoint. The five exterior vertices contain an edge since the independence number is four, and no top can accept it. This rules out t=3.
