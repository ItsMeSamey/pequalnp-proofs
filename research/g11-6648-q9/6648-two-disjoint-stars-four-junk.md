# Two disjoint original star edges, four junk ANDs and no centreless tops

With the issued G11 least size-21 TopForm premises and c=0,j=4, suppose the two starred edges are disjoint. Then U=14≤4+q. We exclude failure q≤9. Reuse the original-gate notation of 6648-two-disjoint-stars-three-junk.md: four starred endpoints, seven exterior raw variables, t ordinary tops, l Lambda gates, each group with one singleton raw endpoint on either side of every top. The size identity here is 2t+l=18−q; disjoint stars require t≥2. At any exterior graph edge at least two different dirty AND suppliers activate. The same assertion holds for edges avoiding the two endpoints of one group whenever all other groups are inactive. A single supplier entering both OR-only inlets of one ordinary top would contradict its cleanliness on an independent witness. TopForm excludes clean AND ancestors of these inlets.

Let r count dirty ANDs directly reading an exterior raw input. Every dirty AND reads at most one raw input in a least circuit: a raw pair edge computes a clean AND, a raw nonedge pair never occurs on a graph minterm and can be suppressed globally, and duplicate raw pins are redundant. If all four dirty gates were exterior-raw gated, every exterior edge would have to activate two. With four distinct gate literals, every exterior edge is supported on these four vertices, whose induced graph has a vertex cover of at most two since it is not a clique (G11 is C4-free). With repeated literals, select three dirty gates including two on that same literal; their at most two different raw literals cover the exterior edges since every edge activates at least two of four gates. Both outcomes contradict the fact that the exterior graph has cover number at least 7−4=3. Hence r≤3. Likewise, when r=3 its three literals must be distinct.

At least seven original gates directly read exterior variables: privately read vertices with a sole reader cannot have another raw pin there (the AND neighbor-sensitivity or OR singleton-masking argument), and all multiply-read exterior variables use two or more incidences of capacity two per remaining gate. Thus at least 7−r of these gates are paid ORs. None is an endpoint importer. As before, m_v counts the singleton-v paid imports in an ordinary endpoint's raw-positive OR trace, m their sum; l_v≤2^{m_v}−1−m_v, and each group multiplicity is at most 2 raised to its smaller endpoint import count. In particular m≤q−7+r.

**If r=3**, every one of the four endpoints needs a positive import. If an endpoint v had none, its entire group would be gated by raw v. Any graph edge avoiding v and the other group's two endpoints would then activate at least two dirty suppliers, necessarily including one of the three exterior-raw gates. The three chosen endpoints and three exterior literals would cover all G11, contradicting cover number seven. Thus m≥4. At q≤8 we have m≤4, so every m_v=1, l=0, each group multiplicity ≤2 and 2t+l≤8<18−q. At q=9 there are at most five imports. Four nonzero m_v permit at most one Lambda, so 2t+l=9 forces exactly one Lambda, t=4 and m=(2,1,1,1) in some order. Both groups have multiplicity two. Five endpoint imports and four exterior ORs spend the entire charge budget.

Now all seven exterior readers are the three raw-gated dirty ANDs and these four ORs. Equality in the incidence count forces every multiply-read exterior variable to have exactly two readers, each carrying two multiply-read exterior raw pins. The three dirty ANDs cannot directly read two raw inputs and therefore are respectively sole readers of three exterior variables w1,w2,w3; call their set V. The remaining four exterior vertices Z are independent, since every exterior edge activates at least one of the three gates with a literal in V. Private exterior ORs have singleton-negative siblings. Every multiple-reader exterior OR is a pure two-raw OR on vertices of Z.

Before the earliest dirty AND, the only available nontrivial pure ORs below a top are these two-raw ORs on Z: an endpoint import needs a dirty source, a singleton-label Lambda without a previous import is redundant, and an exterior private OR cannot have a pure singleton-negative sibling. Clean AND ancestry is forbidden. If the earliest dirty AND were w_i∧T with w_i its sole reader variable, T must accept every neighbor singleton of w_i. Since T is raw or a pure OR on at most two vertices, and degree is at least two, T consists of exactly the two neighbors, making this AND clean (or the required coverage is impossible). Thus the fourth dirty gate F is earliest. If F had a raw pin on an interior endpoint, it might be raw-gated there without contributing to r. This is impossible: the three exterior gates and F would then all have raw pins, and on the exterior slice F is always false. Exterior edges would have both endpoints in V and be covered by two vertices, contradicting exterior cover≥3. Hence F has no raw pin. Its two inputs are distinct pairs of Z-vertices (identical supports violate leastness); it accepts at most one singleton of Z. Every edge between Z and V must activate F since only one exterior-raw gate can be true there, and F ignores the V endpoint. Thus every such edge hits the same possible singleton p in Z. The graph on V, if not a triangle, has a cover of size ≤1. Together with p this covers the exterior. If V is a triangle, p has at most one neighbor in it by C4-freeness, so the triangle plus its possible pendant edge also has a two-vertex cover. This contradiction eliminates r=3.

**If r≤2**, m≤q−5. For q≤8 this is at most three: the all-concentrated partition gives t≤2,l≤4, a 2+1 split gives t≤3,l≤1, and three singles give t≤3,l=0, so 2t+l≤8<18−q. Finally q=9 gives m≤4 and 2t+l=9. At t=2,l=5, all four imports would have to be at one endpoint (if not, 3+1 gives at most four Lambda). Therefore each group has a pure endpoint; those two vertices would cover the entire graph. At t=3,l=3, multiplicities 2+1 require at least one import at either endpoint of the repeated group. The only possible distribution achieving three Lambda with at most four imports is (3,1,0,0) on the repeated endpoints; all Lambda are on the rich label. At t=4,l=1, two groups of multiplicity two would each need positive imports at both endpoints, plus a second import at the Lambda label. Hence multiplicities are 3+1, with imports (2,2,0,0) on the repeated endpoints. In both cases the singleton group cd is the physical clean AND c∧d, and the other edge is ab.

The four imports and at least five exterior ORs again saturate the budget. This forces r=2 and precisely seven exterior reader gates. The two raw-gated donor literals are therefore private exterior vertices w1,w2; a duplicate gate or repeated same variable cannot attain the incidence equality. Every exterior-reader OR either reads two exterior raw pins or is the sole reader of an exterior variable with singleton-negative sibling. Thus no such OR directly reads c. The remaining paid imports and all Lambda are confined to singleton traces of a,b; they cannot directly read c either. Since c has a neighbor other than d, sensing its variable cannot proceed solely through c∧d. It must also directly enter a dirty AND, distinct from the two exterior-raw gates (no two raw pins). For an edge avoiding a,b,c the pure cd group is off and a repeated ab root must activate using two distinct dirty ANDs. Of four possible donors at least one of these two is among the three just identified, forcing w1,w2 or c true. This gives a five-vertex cover {a,b,c,w1,w2} of G11, impossible. All q are covered, without an incidence scan or cofactor classification.
