# Three matched stars, q=9, c=0, j=3

All three matched edges give nonempty groups of ordinary tops; their singleton label pin groups and imports m_v obey the endpoint ledger in 6648-two-disjoint-stars-three-junk.md. Here q+2t+λ=19 gives possibilities (t,λ)=(3,4),(4,2),(5,0); group profiles are respectively (1,1,1),(2,1,1), and (3,1,1) or (2,2,1). Each group of k tops needs k distinct v-pins at each endpoint. Each endpoint contributes λ_v≤min(2^{m_v},8)−1−m_v, by the subset bound on imports and by the three physical dirty outputs. In particular m_v≤7.

At most two dirty ANDs can have a mandatory raw literal. Otherwise choose one such literal per dirty gate. Every residual edge activates a dirty AND (earliest positive AND would otherwise give a raw clean star), and the three literals meet them all. One representative from each starred edge then gives a vertex cover of size at most six. G11 needs seven. In particular at most two dirty ANDs are direct exterior readers. The five exterior labels need at least five physical readers by the private sole-reader argument in the two-disjoint-star proof, so at least three exterior ORs are paid. These are disjoint from all singleton endpoint imports, giving ∑m_v≤6. For the two size-five profiles this implies at least four imports, independently of λ.

A low endpoint in a repeated k-group cannot use its raw label directly as an AND pin. Some other k-pin then requires a different initial raw reader to build it. Setting that low false deletes the direct AND, an OR in the clean output aggregation, and the distinct additional raw reader, contradicting the 19 floor. Hence at such a low k≤2^{m_v}−1. A bare singleton group (both endpoints have zero imports) cannot touch a low either: its only ordinary term is the plain edge AND, so minimum degree two forces another raw reader, giving the same three losses. In the t=3 case there are exactly six ANDs; every low has only OR readers by the established six-AND low-false floor. Thus every low endpoint in that case has m_v≥1.

For each independent transversal I of the three matched edges apply the pin-core flattening argument of 6648-p13-disjoint-endpoint-cuts.md. Setting I true kills all t ordinary ANDs and at least ∑_{i∈I}(λ_i+m_i) singleton-label ORs. The other three opposite-label raw cones are disjoint connected cones. They contribute the remaining λ by their internal cycle ranks, and attachment to the t-term upper clean tree adds t−3 independent cycles. Flattening the output OR region as in the cited proof gives size(true I)≤21−2t−λ+3−∑_{i∈I}m_i=14−∑_{i∈I}m_i. Thus each available lower floor f for that true cofactor gives ∑_{i∈I}m_i≤14−f. This inequality imposes no restriction for transversals that contain a graph edge. It retains arbitrary donor sharing, direct-OR reader forms, and the possibility that some original raw OR cones feed additional dirty gates.

These are necessary ledger conditions. They have not excluded all masks or produced a size-21 circuit.
