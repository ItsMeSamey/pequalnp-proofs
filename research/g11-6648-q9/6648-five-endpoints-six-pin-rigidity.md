# Five star endpoints require every one of six exterior pin traces

Use original G11, c=0,j=3, q≤9, W the union of endpoints of all starred edges, |W|=5 and O its six-vertex complement. Write H=G[O]. Then all six physical dirty-AND pin traces on O are nonempty and pairwise distinct. In particular a duplicate, empty or W-only trace excludes the P3+K2 three-star case at q=8 and 9. The result is a necessary pin condition on an actual circuit, not an exclusion of every such mask.

The exterior separator (6648-star-exterior-separation.md) uses original maximal independent sets. The induced H is used solely to pick exterior edge and independent inputs. It has a positive edge and τ(H)≥2 since α(G)=4. By the two-import argument of twin_6648_q9_three_connected_stars.md, any winning ordinary top on an H edge gets its two positives through distinct first paid W-only positive ORs with raw-empty dirty inlets. Call these endpoint imports. They cannot be exterior proper unions. If d≤5 distinct nonempty traces survive, Δ=6−d≥1 requires at least seven proper unions by 6648-global-exterior-thresholds.md. Thus q≤8 is impossible. At q=9, seven exterior proper unions and two endpoint imports exhaust q.

Let h be the number of nonsingleton traces, k the number of labels without a singleton trace. The bounds m≥h+2(k−h), k−h=6−d and Sperner show d=5,h≤5. The only possibility at h≤4 is the five-code/four-coordinate pattern, impossible on G11 by the cited original MIS argument. Hence h=5,k=6. In the pruned effective DAG, at least 12 singleton input slots and at least 7−5=2 gate input slots are needed. All 14 slots are accounted for. Every label occurs twice as a singleton input, each of the two nonoutput gates feeds exactly one later gate, and none of the five target unions feeds another gate. The output trees therefore have no gate fanout. Every label must occur in at least two target supports (otherwise its sole containing nonsingleton target has a partner dominating its code). It occurs exactly twice, once in each of two different target supports. The six label codes are distinct 2-subsets on five coordinates. All five traces have size at least two and total size twelve, hence their sizes are 4,2,2,2,2 or 3,3,2,2,2.

Only the two endpoint imports can receive dirty ANDs at ORs directly. There is no raw-empty OR: such a pre-top gate would be another paid gate. Each import's raw-empty pin therefore takes a literal dirty AND. At a winning ordinary top, both import gates must occur on separate OR-only sides. They cannot share the same OR-only side with an OR-only dependency between the imports, since the earlier import's raw leaf would activate both sides of that top. Thus on exterior inputs these two imports each derive their value from their own raw-empty pin (the positive pin is W-only and cannot carry the other import through ORs). The suppliers are distinct since a common supplier's independent positive witness would activate both inlets of the clean top. For every H edge the same two suppliers must both be positive. Each of their nonempty exterior pins is physically pure: any OR-only path to such a pin from a dirty AND would cross an endpoint import and then a first OR obtaining nonempty exterior support along this path. This OR would have an empty-exterior inlet, could not be a proper exterior union, and could not be Lambda, whose raw leaves lie in W. TopForm excludes earlier clean ANDs. Consequently each such pin's exterior trace is a vertex cover of H. Since only one of six physical pins can be empty or duplicated, the four pins of these two suppliers contain at least three distinct nonempty covers. At most two target supports have size three or more. This excludes τ(H)≥3.

For τ(H)=2 choose a minimum cover C and put I=O minus C. Every vertex of C has a neighbor in I by inclusion-minimality. An original maximal independent extension of I therefore omits both vertices of C. To separate either one requires a trace contained in C, so C itself is one target (all five are nonsingletons). The four independent labels of I now have four distinct pair codes on the other four coordinates. View them as four edges of a simple graph on four coordinate vertices. If these edges include a triangle then each label of that triple is covered by the other two codes, which is impossible for an independent G11 triple by 6648-global-exterior-thresholds.md. In the remaining case the four code edges form a four-cycle. Each edge is covered by the two adjacent edges of this code cycle. All their G11 vertices lie in the independent I. If a corresponding G11 vertex v had degree at least three, one of its original neighbors would be nonadjacent to both covering vertices (codegree ≤1); extending those two together with that neighbor would contradict coverage. The four G11 vertices must therefore be 0,1,2,3. Even for vertex 1 there is such a neighbor: 4 touches none of 0,2,3. This again contradicts its two adjacent code edges covering it. The cycle here concerns the hypothetical codes, and its labels are independent in G11. No original MIS was replaced by a maximal independent set of H.

The arithmetic one-loss cost of seven remains sharp only before these graph constraints. In the stated circuit setting the proof gives q≥10 whenever a pin trace is lost; it does not construct a q=10 example. No numerical run was used.
