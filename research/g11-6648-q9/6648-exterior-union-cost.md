# Exterior pin supports and the number of paid ORs that can merge them

Use the notation and original maximal independent set matrix of 6648-star-exterior-separation.md. Intersect the kept pin supports with O=V minus W, dropping empty and duplicate traces, and let d be their number. Thus d≤2j+c; empty junk pins, doubly empty centreless tops, coincident traces and W-only supports can improve this cap. Retain the original maximal independent set columns throughout. A separator missing an original independent set still misses its exterior trace, so the trace rectangles cover all omitted-vertex entries and contain only ones. This only relaxes their admissibility; it is not a circuit restriction that sets the W variables false.

For exterior v define its code of membership in the d retained traces. These codes are pairwise incomparable. For every ordered pair v,u there exists an original maximal independent I with u in I and v outside I. If adjacent extend {u}; otherwise some neighbor w of v is nonadjacent to u, by minimum degree two and absence of four-cycles, and one extends {u,w}. A kept pin separating v from I contains v and misses u. This argument does not require the exterior-induced graph to have positive minimum degree.

Of the exterior vertices, let k lack a singleton retained trace, and let h be the number of distinct nonsingleton retained traces. Writing n=|O| gives d=n−k+h. The k missing-singleton codes are incomparable over just h bits, so k≤binom(h,floor(h/2)). On raw exterior traces a positive direct dirty-AND-reading OR can only copy its other pin. A Lambda OR has empty exterior support by its original ordinary witness. Every genuine union of two incomparable nonempty traces on OR-only paths to the pins therefore costs an original nondirect paid OR. Build a union DAG for the h retained nonsingleton traces by bypassing empty, contained or equal inlets, pruning dead unions. Its m distinct binary union gates satisfy m≤q−b. No OR gate has been charged twice.

For each vertex v missing a singleton pin, at least two distinct surviving union gates receive singleton {v} as an input. If none does, v occurs in no output. If exactly one does, the other input contains at least one u≠v and all retained outputs containing v also contain u, contradicting incomparability. At least 2k singleton inlet occurrences are therefore required. At least m−h of the 2m inlet occurrences come from earlier unions, since every union other than a designated output must feed a later union. Consequently

    q−b ≥ m ≥ 2k−h = h+2(n−d).

For a deficit δ=n−d>0, one must have h+δ≤binom(h,floor(h/2)). In particular δ=1 requires ≥6 nondirect paid OR gates, δ=2 requires ≥8 and δ≥3 requires ≥11. Replacing q−b by the actual number of eligible proper unions strengthens every bound. The lower thresholds 6 and 8 are exact for the code/union relaxation: take five or all six edges of K4 as exterior labels and four incident edge sets as the output traces. The output codes are distinct pairs. The four supports have sizes 3,3,2,2 or 3,3,3,3 and cost six or eight binary unions. This constructs incidence codes only and makes no assertion that they realize G11.

For example, at j=3,c=0 and two starred edges, d≤6 while n≥7. Disjoint stars require q−b≥6; touching stars give n=8 and require q−b≥8. At q≤6 every such two-star signature with b≥1 is excluded, independently of the sole reader at 3. At q=10 touching stars require b≤2, disjoint stars b≤4. A lost kept pin in the touching case increases the deficit to at least three and excludes every q≤10. These statements apply to the original graph with no cofactor or leastness assumption beyond the previously derived scalar range.

## An exact five-row rank family

Suppose the exterior contains the three vertices of a triangle and the two endpoints of a vertex-disjoint graph edge. The Boolean rectangle rank restricted to these five rows and ALL original maximal independent sets is exactly five. Each endpoint of the edge sees at most one triangle vertex by four-cycle-freeness. Thus at least four distinct cross pairs are independent. Each pair dominates the other three selected vertices and extends to an original maximal independent set whose trace on these five rows is exactly that pair.

If four rectangles sufficed, the five incomparable row codes in their four coordinates would all be 2-sets. Indeed a singleton leaves at most three mutually incomparable codes avoiding that singleton's coordinate (and complements give the dual triple case). Hence the five row codes are the edges of K4 with one edge removed. A column's zero rows are exactly the codes avoiding its incident rectangle coordinates, so a two-zero trace requires two coded edges whose vertex union contains no third coded edge. Disjoint coded edges span all four coordinates; adjacent coded edges span a triangle unless their nonshared endpoints form the sole missing edge. There are precisely two pairs of the latter form, fewer than the four distinct original column traces. Thus rank exceeds four, and five singleton rectangles give equality. For example the G11 exterior {3,6,7,5,9} is of this type. Its complementary endpoint set is supported by the edge mask {0–10,2–10,1–8,4–8}. This last example is a graph mask and does not assert a realizing least circuit.
