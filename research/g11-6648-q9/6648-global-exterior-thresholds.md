# G11 exterior support thresholds in the residual table

In the original least TopForm circuit write W for the endpoints of s starred graph edges, O for its complement, n=11−|W|, and D=2j+c. Let b count paid ORs reading a dirty AND directly. There are at most D kept junk or centreless raw pins entering the exterior rectangle separator. Drop empty or duplicate traces on O to give d≤D retained traces. The bound below is uniform in W. It concerns all original G11 maximal independent sets, with no assignment fixed on W.

Let h count nonsingleton traces and k exterior vertices lacking singleton traces. Then δ=n−d=k−h. On the h coordinates the k corresponding membership codes form an antichain by original MIS separation. With m effective proper unions of exterior traces, 6648-exterior-union-cost.md gives m≥h+2δ and k≤binom(h,floor(h/2)); also m≤q−b. A code covered by the union of the codes of two other retained vertices a,b cannot have an original maximal independent set containing a,b but omitting its vertex v: all other selected singleton coordinates have value zero at v. These statements retain every original MIS column.

**A deficit of one requires at least seven unions.** Suppose δ≥1 and m≤6. The arithmetic forces δ=1,h=4,k=5: h≤3 has width at most h, and δ≥2 with h≥4 costs at least eight. The five codes on four bits are all distinct 2-sets. With a singleton code, the four other codes would form an antichain on the three remaining bits, of width three. Complementation excludes triples likewise. Relabel the missing pair as 34.

In each of the triples of labels (12,13,23) and (12,14,24), every word is covered by the other two. If some two labels are nonadjacent, extend them to an original maximal independent set. It must also contain the third label, proving that all three are pairwise nonadjacent. Each triple therefore induces either a triangle or an independent triple. In the independent case each label v has degree at most two: otherwise the other two vertices exclude at most two of its original neighbors, by four-cycle-freeness, so choose a neighbor compatible with both and extend that independent triple to a maximal independent set omitting v. The only degree-two vertices in G11 are 0,1,2,3. Every three of these include 1 or 3. The neighborhoods of 1 and 3 are respectively {4,8} and {6,7}; either is disjoint from all other degree-two neighborhoods ({9,10} at 0 and {5,10} at 2). Even for that degree-two vertex a neighbor can be adjoined to the other two. Thus the independent configuration is impossible.

Both triples must be graph triangles. Their shared code 12 has four distinct graph neighbors. Each pair selecting one of 13,14 and one of 23,24 covers code 12. If such a pair were independent, extend it to an original maximal independent set. Its vertices already neighbor 12, so this set omits 12. This contradicts coverage. All four cross edges would form a graph four-cycle. This proves the seven-union lower bound without a choice of mask.

**A deficit of two requires at least nine unions.** At δ≥2,m≤8 the same arithmetic forces δ=2,h=4,k=6. The six words are the whole middle layer. At least one corresponding label v has degree ≥3. Rename it 12. Every pair choosing one of 13,14 and one of 23,24 covers it. A nonadjacent pair could extend to a maximal independent set omitting v: if either is a neighbor of v, extend the pair; otherwise adjoin a neighbor of v compatible with both by the codegree bound. Thus all four cross edges would be present, a contradiction. This uses only original graph degrees and codes and applies for arbitrary W, as in the touching-star specialization 6648-two-touching-stars-eight-merges.md.

**Three or more.** At δ≥3 Sperner forces h≥5, hence m≥11. These are lower bounds; equality is not asserted for G11.

For table use put Δ=11−|W|−(2j+c). For Δ=1,2,≥3 require respectively q−b≥7,9,11. For Δ≤0 there is no exclusion from these scalar thresholds. If a checked placement loses l kept pins via empty or repeated traces, replace 2j+c by 2j+c−l; subtract separately any provably unusable nondirect paid gates from the merge allowance. When b≥1, every q=4,5,6,7 row requires |W|≥11−2j−c. The same holds at q=8 except b=1, and q=9 except b=1,2; these exceptional entries require |W|≥10−2j−c. Intersect with |W|≤min(11,2s), existing graph-mask counts, b≤q and the other signed tests. Physical b=0 is already handled by the all-pure result. This is an overlay per (q,j,c,s,W,b), not a rejection of every mask in a surviving scalar signature.
