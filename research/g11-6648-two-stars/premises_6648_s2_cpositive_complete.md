# Complete s=2 exclusion for c≥1, q=4,…,8

Use the original least size-21 TopForm hypotheses, P=q+j+2c≤13, j≥3 and 2t+l=22−P≥9. For an original two-star mask let Z be the star endpoints, n=11−|Z|, and m0=4 for disjoint stars, 3 for touching stars. The endpoint ledger 4edf9847… proves at least m0 physical endpoint import ORs are necessary; these have no exterior raw pin. In both shapes n+m0=11. Denote by k the number of paid ORs directly reading an exterior raw variable. Thus k≤q−m0. Every exterior variable is relevant; every non-OR direct reader is a dirty or centreless AND. Such an AND can directly read at most one exterior raw label: a two-raw nonedge AND would be deletable on all graph minterms, and a two-raw edge AND would be an ordinary top starring its exterior raw edge. The private sole-reader and binary multi-reader bound gives at least n distinct exterior readers.

Write X for the exterior raw labels with an OR reader and B⊆X for those with only OR readers. X covers the induced exterior graph. Otherwise start at an exterior graph-edge assignment with neither label entering any OR. The earliest true AND would need both raw labels directly: every nonraw OR positive before that gate would need a true AND or a raw OR reader for one of the labels. This would be an exterior ordinary star, impossible. Therefore |X|≥7−|Z| by the original vertex-cover number. Also |X|≤2k. Counting the sole and multiple readers just for B gives |B|≤k. At equality each OR is either the private sole reader of a B vertex (its sibling cannot be a second raw input by the singleton toggle/C4 lemma), or its pins are two different multiply-read B vertices. Thus no exterior raw label outside B can enter any OR at equality. In particular |X|>k implies |B|≤k−1.

If all j+c dirty/centreless ANDs directly read DISTINCT exterior mandatory labels, any exterior label v outside that list has no graph neighbor. On a pair with v and a star endpoint, every dirty gate is false and the raw stars are false. On a pair with v and another exterior label, either all dirty gates are false (then centreless empty arms are all false as well), or only the one dirty gate attached to that other label can fire. In the latter event all centreless mandatory labels are missing and no ordinary top can fire through both sides from the same dirty gate. This proves the assertion.

The physical counts give n≤j+c+k≤j+c+q−m0, or P≥11+c. At equality all j+c gates participate as distinct exterior readers. Equality for the full n-label reader count forces each raw-reading AND to be a sole raw reader of its own exterior variable (it cannot take two raw labels), so the mandatory labels are distinct. A leftover exterior label would have no neighbors by the preceding paragraph. If every exterior label is assigned, then n=j+c and k=0, contradicting |X|≥7−|Z|. Consequently P≥12+c, so c=1 and P=13 exclusively. In particular j=11−q.

q=4 is impossible since k≤1 and exterior vertex cover needs ≥3 labels when disjoint and ≥4 when touching. At q=5 the disjoint case has k≤1 and needs three labels. For touching q=5 and both shapes at q=6 we have |X|>k, hence at least n−k+1 exterior labels have an AND reader. This number is at least n−(q−m0)+1=12−q=j+c. Distinct AND readers for distinct such labels are necessary and therefore all j+c mandatory labels are distinct. There are leftover exterior labels because n>j+c in those three cases. The no-neighbor conclusion contradicts relevance.

For q=8 both shapes have j=3; q=7 touching has j=4. These are excluded by 4edf9847…, using the arbitrary-mask one-donor cut 27160c35… with a size-two/size-one original star cover, respectively.

Finally at q=7 for disjoint stars there are j+c=5, n=7, k≤3. If |B|≤2 all five ANDs again have distinct exterior labels and a leftover label is impossible. Hence |B|=k=3. The seven paid gates are precisely the three exterior readers and four endpoint imports. No exterior OR has any raw pin outside B by equality of its reader bound. At least four distinct dirty/centreless ANDs read exterior raw labels. Now 2t+l=9 with t between 2 and 4. At t=2,l=5 a split of four imports between multiple labels permits at most four Lambda gates, so they are concentrated at one label. At t=3,l=3 the two-root group needs imports on both ends: two labels with positive Lambda counts would need five imports, and a three-Lambda label requires three imports at a repeated endpoint. At t=4,l=1 a 2+2 split needs five imports, while a 3+1 split needs two at each repeated endpoint. Thus there is always a pure original ordinary group consisting solely of g AND h, with no endpoint imports or Lambda gates on either label. Neither g nor h enters an OR, since the exterior ORs have only B raw inputs. Each has degree at least two and therefore needs a direct reader besides this pure top. No other ordinary top can read these labels, and no single AND can account for both or for one of them together with one of the four exterior raw readers (that would be a deletable nonedge, a new star, or a copy of the same clean top). At least six dirty/centreless readers are required in total, but only five exist. This closes the final case.

No numerical enumeration or new cofactor floor is used.
