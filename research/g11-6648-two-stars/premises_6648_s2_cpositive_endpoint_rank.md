# Two original stars with centreless tops: endpoint accounting

Work on the original counterexample table q≥4, P=q+j+2c≤13, size 21, and 2t+l=22−P≥9. Assume s=2 and c≥1. For either pattern let h=τ(stars), n=11−|endpoints(stars)|. Every exterior vertex has degree at least two and is relevant. A sole exterior reader cannot have a second raw input: for AND this masks edges to a different neighbor; for OR the sibling must reject singletons by the C4 toggle argument in 6648-two-disjoint-stars-three-junk.md. Lone readers are therefore private among the exterior reader set, and multiply-read vertices consume at least two incidences with at most two per gate. There are ≥n distinct exterior readers. None is Lambda or an ordinary top. At most c such gates are centreless ANDs. Any directly raw-reading dirty AND mandates a raw variable. If j−1≤4−h, at most j−2 dirty gates can be raw readers: otherwise choose one mandatory label from each of j−1 of them, append a size-h star cover, and apply premises_6648_one_surviving_donor_all_stars.md (27160c35…). Hence in that range at most P−c−n−2 paid ORs remain outside the exterior reader set. Endpoint imports (positive raw pin plus an empty pin on an ordinary operand trace) are always among these remaining paid ORs, since their own raw supports contain endpoints only.

For disjoint stars h=2,n=7,j=3 the total number of endpoint imports is ≤P−c−9≤3. Each of the two endpoint-pair groups is nonempty; physically distinct input functions at each side are required by original leastness and clean-top fusion. Each singleton-v trace with m_v imports admits at most 2^{m_v} functions including v, hence at most 2^{m_v}−1−m_v Lambda gates. For total m≤3, concentrating all imports gives t≤2,l≤4; a 2+1 split gives t≤3,l≤1; three singleton imports give t≤3,l=0. All contradict 2t+l≥9. Thus disjoint s=2 is excluded for j=3 and every c≥1 in range.

For touching stars hu,hv, h as a vertex here denotes the hub; the cover number is one, n=8. For j=3 or 4 the remaining paid budget is ≤P−c−10≤2, hence ≤2 endpoint imports. Each ordinary top has a singleton hub arm and a nonempty leaf arm supported on {u,v}; both are physical terminal wires distinct across all tops by the same fusion. Every Lambda gate is in at least one of those two blocks. Denote import counts by m_H,m_L. At the hub the singleton-label count above applies. At the leaf side, with no imports there are ≤3 functions including raw u,v. For nonempty import subset T the union expression must include the raw support of the positive arm of each physical import contributing its empty input. This forces at least one specific raw leaf in the expression for each such T; at most two raw subsets remain possible. Counting formal expressions is an upper bound even when imported signals semantically coincide or depend on earlier traces. At m_L=0,1,2 the maximum number of leaf functions is thus 3,5,9. Subtract the two raw inputs and m_L distinct physical import outputs by leastness to bound Lambda counts (raw labels not actually used as standalone terminals may still be counted as distinct original input functions).

If both imports are at leaves, the hub has one possible terminal, so t≤1,l≤5. One import each gives t≤2,l≤2. Both at the hub gives t≤3 by the leaf count, l≤1+1=2. With fewer imports the same upper bound follows by monotonicity. Thus 2t+l≤8, a contradiction. This closes touching s=2 for j=3 or 4 and all c≥1 in range.

Without the one-surviving-donor restriction the same argument gives an unconditional placement condition. Let d be the number of dirty ANDs directly reading ANY exterior raw input. For disjoint stars m≥4 is necessary because 2t+l≥9, so n−d−c≤q−4, equivalently d≥11−c−q. For touching stars m≥3 is necessary, hence d≥11−c−q as well. Here d counts distinct dirty gates; a shared raw label need not kill distinct gates independently. Both conditions can be combined with the more general C∪R cotest even at larger j. These bounds alone do not settle all larger-j cases. No numerical enumeration was performed.
