# Two adjacent stars with no centreless top

Source proof for a least 21-gate G11 TopForm candidate with c=0, s=2 and P=q+j≤13. Write the stars ab,ac and O for the eight other labels. The exterior graph has cover number at least four since the whole graph has cover number seven. A guard of size d for a wire means that on exterior edge inputs every positive evaluation meets some fixed set of at most d labels. In particular a wire positive on every exterior edge cannot have a guard of size three.

Trace both ordinary pins at every top backwards through ORs to raw and dirty AND leaves. Raw-positive values in these traces have raw support contained in {a} or {b,c}, as the Cartesian product of the two raw supports contributes complete bipartite starred edges. Let k count distinct dirty AND leaves in this union. At an exterior edge some clean top fires using two such leaves on opposite pins. They are distinct, because a shared dirty leaf has an independent positive witness. Let m count traced ORs joining one raw-positive pin and one raw-empty pin, and z count traced raw-empty ORs. Every dirty leaf lies below one of the m raw-empty inlet incidences. In the union of those inlet cones, the z binary vertices supply 2z edges, and up to m root incidences complete the bound z+k≤2z+m. These m+z ORs are paid and none directly reads an exterior raw.

Let r count dirty ANDs reading an exterior raw directly, n=j−r, and b count ORs directly reading an exterior raw. Such ORs are paid, since every Lambda gate lies in an ordinary raw trace. A dirty AND cannot have two raw pins: an edge pair is clean and a nonedge pair can be cut false on all minimal positives, with constant folding saving a gate. For a sole raw OR reader v∨D the sibling is independent of v and singleton-negative: a positive singleton p masks v, forcing p nonadjacent to v and adjacent to every neighbor of v by sensitivity on {p,v,w}. Two neighbors then give a forbidden four-cycle. Thus the sole gate for a private exterior label cannot read another raw label. Other exterior labels need at least two raw-reader slots each and a gate has at most two slots. Consequently b+r≥8. The three nonnegative slacks b+r−8, q−b−m−z and m+z−k sum to P−8−n−k≤5−n−k.

Each raw-gated visible donor implies its exterior literal. If all k visible donors are raw-gated then k≤5. Pick the labels appearing repeatedly. Outside them an exterior edge must contain two distinct labels from the remaining singleton donor positions. Without repetitions this gives a C4-free graph on at most five nonisolates, with cover at most three (some two are independent when there are five). With one repeated label choose it and cover the at most three singleton labels' edges with ≤2 more. With two repeats choose both. If exactly one visible donor is nongated, n≥1 and k≤4, so the other ≤3 raw labels cover all exterior edges. Thus at least two nongated visible donors are necessary, leaving (n,k)=(2,3),(3,2),(2,2).

Before the earliest visible donor V every ordinary OR trace is pure and every endpoint raw-empty OR is unavailable: following ORs to any earlier dirty leaf would make that leaf visible. With zero exterior-reader slack each exterior OR either directly joins two multiple-reader exterior raws or is a private reader u∨D. Before V, with no extra paid OR, the private sibling is a dirty AND: it is singleton-negative and any raw-empty OR would have to be in the endpoint forest. Before the first dirty gate, therefore, available pure supports have size ≤2 (ordinary traces, raws or raw pairs). An exterior private raw-gated AND cannot be first: by sole-reader sensitivity its pure sibling must contain every neighbor, hence exactly its two neighbors, making the AND clean. These assertions use zero slack in both the reader and extra-paid counts.

For (2,3) all slacks vanish. The earliest dirty gate F must be one of the two nongated visible donors, and it has a size-two guard from either pure pin. Every exterior edge needs two distinct visible donors, so must activate F or the sole visible raw-gated donor. Their union has a size-three guard.

For (3,2) all slacks vanish. Both visible donors fire on every exterior edge. Neither can be the first dirty gate, whose guard has size ≤2. The first gate F must be the sole other nongated gate. Earlier than V, any dirty wire is therefore guarded by ≤2 labels. Each private OR u∨D adds at most one label. Every possible pin at V now has guard ≤3.

For (2,2) the total slack is ≤1. All dirty gates earlier than V have a one-label guard. Again V must fire on every exterior edge.

* With zero exterior-reader slack, the private/pair classification holds, and there is at most one extra paid OR E outside the exterior readers and endpoint forest. If E is raw-empty and precedes V, each pin is a direct earlier dirty AND (there is no other earlier raw-empty OR), so its guard has size ≤2. Every private OR consequently has guard ≤3. If E is raw-positive, no private sibling can read E directly since it is singleton-positive. With no earlier raw-empty OR, each private OR has guard ≤2. Only E itself can require four or more labels. The same bounds apply when E is absent or occurs later.
* With one exterior-reader slack there is no extra paid OR, hence no pre-V raw-empty OR. A private OR's singleton-negative sibling is a direct dirty AND and its guard has size ≤2. After reserving private readers, if there are u multiply-read labels, their remaining u+1 reader gates have at most 2u+2 slots, of which at least 2u directly read those labels. Thus at most two exterior multiple-reader ORs can have a pin other than a raw multiple-reader label. Each such OR extends one earlier guard by at most one. All base wires (raw, ordinary trace, dirty AND, raw pair, private OR) have guards of size ≤2. Only the later of the two extension gates can require a four-label guard.

In either subcase V would need both its pins to be the unique exceptional wide wire, which makes V redundant. This excludes every adjacent two-star signature in the stated regime. The argument counts physical wires even when donors or private OR outputs are reused.
