# Independent replay of the six-gate, eight-pair branch

The 11 columns are the issued 40-bit values in L-meta-18272 (statement SHA-256 `9ec4bd7fce25977e79676fd87f567c30812b34f80a264e9df1a9aeb6d972f84b`). At least three of six gates are ANDs, so at most three are ORs. The relaxation gives unbounded ANDs and drops physical-gate usage constraints. A negative result for this larger family excludes the issued branch.

The bank contains every nonempty intersection of the inputs and all OR outputs already chosen. On admitting a generator, add that generator and its intersection with each old bank member. Keep every distinct 40-bit mask, including zero and masks supported solely on negative rows. For the next physical OR, each operand is either an input or a prior physical gate, hence must have both positive and negative rows in its support. Try every unordered pair of distinct eligible masks. Equal operands, and ORs whose output already belongs to the bank, add nothing to this relaxation. Only equal outputs at the same prefix are merged. There is no canonical ordering of sibling OR outputs.

Every terminal product is an intersection in the bank. An admissible tap has no negative bit. Every positive singleton is present from the original inputs; the checker verifies this. Enumerate all subsets of the available multirow taps, filling uncovered rows with singleton taps. A tap contributing at most one currently missing row can be replaced by a singleton. If the remaining multirow union misses as many rows as there are slots, all slots would be consumed by singleton taps, so the earlier direct singleton-success test decides that case. These facts justify the only two subset-search cutoffs. An exact eight-pair witness would pass this at-most-eight test.

Each receipt row records shard index, first OR output mask, then (visited states, positive cover decisions) for one, two and three productive ORs. The startup controls include a zero-OR test and a naive ordered-union closure comparison on 128 four-row tap families at five budgets.
