# Strong regularity backdoor reduction

For a represented binary matroid M call X strong if every choice of deleting or contracting each coordinate of X produces a regular matroid. This is equivalent to intersecting the seven-element survivor set of every F₇ or F₇* minor. A nonregular choice has such a further minor; conversely if a forbidden-minor witness misses X, perform its prescribed operations on X before the remaining operations. Minors on distinct labels commute. [Tutte's binary excluded-minor characterization in Truemper](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/EMIS/monographs/md/matroiddecompositionbook.pdf) · [Regularity recognition](https://discopt.github.io/cmr/regular.html)

A decision-only regularity recognizer can extract seven surviving labels. Repeatedly scan single-element deletions and contractions and retain a nonregular one, recording its label and operation. When all single-element minors are regular, minor-minimality means the current binary matroid is F₇ or F₇*. At most quadratically many recognition calls suffice. To find X, test every one of the 2^i prescriptions for a candidate of size i. From a failing prescription take a forbidden minor; any successful extension must hit one of its seven remaining labels. Branch on these seven choices to depth k. This takes O*(14^k) time and polynomial space, and iterative deepening finds the minimum size.

Write a_f,b_f for cycle and cocycle labels. On a nonloop noncoloop e, delete it with c_e=0 or contract it with d_e=0. The branches overlap when both bits vanish, which is harmless for decision. For deletion choose a cycle z with z_e=1. Each remaining cocycle extends uniquely by d_e=∑_{f≠e} z_f d_f, by orthogonality and restriction of the dual space. Set b_f←b_f+z_f b_e. The remaining cycle lifts with c_e zero and a unchanged. For contraction reverse roles, choose a cocycle z through e and set a_f←a_f+z_f a_e. At a coloop c_e is always zero, while its independent free d_e contributes either zero or b_e. Delete and project all remaining scores and target modulo the span of b_e. A witness lifts by choosing d_e to restore the target. At a loop contract and use the analogous quotient by a_e. All transformations are elementary binary linear algebra; marker dimension does not increase. A strong X thus reduces a disjoint cycle/cocycle decision to at most 2^k regular instances with at most t markers. Apply the homogeneous theorem to each regular instance. Reverse the recorded operations to recover a witness.


## Prescribed sparse affine offsets

Let U be the kernel and V its orthogonal space. Given supplied offsets h,j with union of supports K of size k, seek disjoint c=h+x,d=j+y for x in U and y in V, with the original t-bit score. Translate the score target by the constant score of h,j. Enumerate the three disjoint actual input states at each element of K. These prescribe x,y there, potentially including 11. The residual system requires ordinary disjointness everywhere else. Offsets with sparse representatives modulo U,V may use those representatives if supplied.

Root the same decomposition. Call a subtree dirty if it contains an element of K among its genuine coordinates. On a dirty singleton boundary store all four traces. On a dirty triangle boundary store the class of x modulo 111 and the even trace of y, allowing odd pairing. Equivalently store the weight-at-most-one representative of x with that even y, and permit them to overlap. Adding 111 to x is still a zero-real-coordinate cycle, so this choice is available at each incident piece. On a clean boundary keep the ordinary table. The distinction is necessary because an odd number of prescribed overlaps inside a subtree changes its outgoing orthogonality identity.

At a node use the full table for every dirty child, guessing its boundary and attained score. There are at most k such children because their real elements are disjoint. In the remaining clean children choose baselines and at most t deviations by the same discrepancy-span argument, conditional on the dirty children and all boundary masks. Prescribe the real exceptional bits at this node directly. On graphic and cographic torsos the combined outgoing, dirty-child and real exceptional coordinates form O(k+t+1) pinned edges. Perform the forced-even-edge removal and endpoint-color enumeration as in the main proof, including a pinned 11 edge even when its endpoints have different colors. Its contribution is fixed and its endpoints supply the usual residual join boundary. K4 default slots are all clean, so the signed cographic identity retains its original hypotheses. The bounded-rank calculation imposes the pins through its per-block lists. Each successful choice glues along equal raw traces after independent triangle toggles. This is also complete by restriction from a global witness, without imposing orthogonality on a dirty outgoing pair. The resulting algorithm runs in polynomial time for every fixed k,t, with the same n^{O(k+t+1)} enumeration and parameter-dependent linkage costs. Undo the offsets after recovering x,y.

If sparse representatives are not supplied, enumerate candidate supports of size at most k. For each support solve the two independent binary linear systems asking for representatives of the given primal and dual cosets vanishing on the complement. A successful support yields the offsets required above in n^{O(k+1)} time. The accompanying offset_check.py compares complete pair sets on 30 randomized mixed sum trees and backdoor_check.py checks score images on 600 binary representations.
