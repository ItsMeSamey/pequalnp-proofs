# Cut 199, two left gates in a 6+7 tree

Assume a 12-mixed-gate tree with six distinct physical left leaves and seven right leaves, at most 21 gates total. With two paid left gates the right budget is at most seven. Left raw slots are 0,1,2,6,7.

Enumerate up to two paid left gates including equal pins and duplicate functions. Choose every six of the available physical wires. The left-cofactor cotree test on all distinct right contexts leaves 4 minimum-cost-one multisets and 15 minimum-cost-two multisets. The signed truth masks and representative programs are in cut199-m12-six-left-two-gates.json, generated by cut199-m12-six-left-two-gates.cjs. A one-gate representation of the same multiset can replace an unused or redundant two-gate representation without altering the mixed tree, so classify by the least producer cost for these six outputs.

A hit is a right port true on singleton 3. At least one is needed; nine profiles above singleton 3 require four varying right coordinates and give at most three hits.

* One hit: the cross edges (6,3) and (7,3) use exactly the same right KW leaf since some column forces right in both rows. Their left-colored completed rows coincide, and six left rectangles with rank six have independent column indicators. Their left masks therefore coincide and are nonempty. Some left port accepts both singleton 6 and singleton 7. No minimum-cost-two survivor does. Of the four minimum-cost-one portfolios only the 6 OR 7 extra passes this necessity.
* Three hits: fixing 3 to one leaves at most four varying right leaf occurrences, at least four by the nine profiles on just the eight left settings varying 0,1,2. All four must be distinct. At least two hit gates disappear. The four-port shared read-once cost is at least nine via cut199-tree-two-hit/verify.cjs, independently of left cost. This exceeds the right budget.
* Two hits: fix 3 to one and 6,7 to zero. Every listed low-cost left portfolio now consists of exactly the three raw left variables 0,1,2 appearing singly, plus constants. There are at most five varying right leaf occurrences and at most six right producer gates remain (at most seven for a one-gate left).

The projected function is the OR of the interior F_R and x_i Q_i for i=0,1,2. The queries are the raw disjunctions over {9,10}, {4,8}, and {5,10}. In a pruned tree, contract maximally pure-right subtrees to ports. Nine profiles force at least four ports. If only four remain, at most one new binary gate is needed to build them from the five original projected leaves. Cost is then at most seven (eight for the one-left branch), contradicting the same four-port cost of nine. Hence five ports remain and all surviving right subtrees are individual leaves. No pure-left cherry can merge two distinct raw left variables: either AND loses their distinct singleton responses or OR identifies them. All projected internal nodes are mixed.

Every pair of left variables has OR as its LCA. Take an independent right assignment hitting both queries, for example {8,10} for the 0/1 and 1/2 pairs, and {10} for 0/2. With other left bits zero both respective singletons accept and empty rejects. Each AND node therefore has a single pure-right child (a guard). Right leaves not serving as AND children are bare OR leaves. For a left variable i on an independent right assignment the conjunction of guards on its root path equals Q_i. Every center has a guard private to it: if its deepest guard includes another center j, all its path guards also guard j, forcing Q_j to imply Q_i contrary to the raw singleton values. With no guard its query would be constant. In particular there are at least three distinct guards. There must be a bare OR leaf to generate the nonempty F_R at empty left.

Three guards and two bare leaves: there is one guard per center. Its function on independent inputs must equal Q_i. Setting left to zero expresses F_R as the OR of at most two terms. Each is either a bare signal or a bare signal AND one guard. Two different guard nodes cannot nest since their left clusters are disjoint. cut199-m12-face-three-guards.cjs enumerates all physically realizable truth banks containing these three queries on independent inputs and checks all such term pairs. Distinct actual functions are retained even when agreeing on independent rows. Repeated equal functions can be collapsed inside the producer DAG and bare signal reuse is freely permitted. At budget 6 there are 86,466 tested states, 24,193 goal banks and zero hits. At budget 7 there are 1,569,329 tested states, 512,001 goal banks and zero hits.

Four guards and one bare leaf: F_R equals the conjunction of the bare signal and the guards on its root path. Thus the physical bank qualifies for the free-conjunction lower bound six. At budget six the archived 18 six-gate factor banks suffice. cut199-m12-face-five-port-meet.cjs enumerates all five-port multisets from them, allowing repeated physical truth values. All 1,628 portfolios satisfying the F_R meet fail the joint fixed 3+5 leaf wedge. At budget seven see the independent cut199-m12-face-four-guards-seven.cjs from the one-left lane.

The two-gate enumeration lists minimum functional portfolio costs and does not assume global leastness of the target graph circuit. Other physical port profiles and other mixed topologies require separate checks.
