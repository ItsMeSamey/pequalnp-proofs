Internal proof draft for independent replay. The graph is issued18272. Assume fan-in-two monotone AND/OR, total size at most 21. Each of the thirteen input occurrences of the mixed tree has a distinct physical source. Equal truth functions at distinct sources are allowed. Let l be the minimum pure gate count realizing its six left ports with their physical multiplicities. Rebuilding the left bank preserves the mixed decoder and its six logical inputs. The original right bank has at most 9−l gates. Since the left side has five raw wires, l≥1. No proof step requires that 21 be a global lower bound.

A hit is a right port true at the raw singleton 3. A hit exists since adding 3 to singleton 6 changes the graph value. On the face 3=1, 6=7=0, varying left inputs 0,1,2 gives nine distinct right response profiles. At least four varying right leaf occurrences are necessary, hence at most three hits. Substituting constants at every occurrence establishes the gate saving even in that case.

**Low costs.** See cut199-m12-six-left-cost-two.md for the physical enumeration and guard proof. Cost-one raw copies of 6 or 7 are included. A portfolio achievable more cheaply is classified under its cheapest physical realization, with the same six distinct port sources. The three-guard checker grants repeated bare values freely. The four-guard checker enumerates occurrence multisets. The four-port common-tree lower bound accepts unrestricted left responses, so a pure-left restriction can only make the task harder.

**High costs.** For the common-right-tree filter, fixing each of the eight left contexts separately prunes a single original right tree, allowing subtrees to be replaced by constants. On the upper face the five raw inputs are ordered 4,5,8,9,10. Enumerating distinct semantic banks is valid because interning equal local functions in the producer is free on this face. Distinct read-once occurrences of one signal remain distinct in the portfolio multiset. Four distinct functions are required to obtain nine profiles. The high-left five- and six-face enumerators allow every multiplicity pattern consistent with that minimum. Select portfolios containing the sinks of the enumerated producer, or delete unused gates to obtain a cheaper enumerated producer. The six-face search at cost four has zero common-tree witnesses in 8,652 tested portfolios. At cost five, seven of 52,395 survive. The left one-hit necessary condition includes the exact port OR(6,7), from KW independence of six left column indicators and the negative extending {0,1,2,3}. Among 17 qualifying portfolios with l≤4, 16 admit a background anchor, including the cost-one portfolio. The exceptional portfolio has l=4. Its five right gates must all survive pinning. Any gate fed raw 3 would contract at that pinning, so raw 3 is isolated and is the hit. The other six ports consequently have the same truth functions on both faces. The joint checker uses all 16 positive and 24 negative rows, preserving occurrence multiplicities for the two repeated-function portfolios.
