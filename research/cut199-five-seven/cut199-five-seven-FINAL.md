# Cut 199 with five left ports and seven right ports

No monotone binary AND/OR circuit with twelve mixed gates, five distinct physical left ports, and seven distinct physical right ports computes the issued graph in at most 21 total gates. The proof concerns this physical port profile and this cut. Right producers may share intermediate gates, and distinct physical outputs may compute identical functions.

## Reduction to the eighth projected gate

The encoder and donor argument is in cut199-five-seven-donor.md. Its frontier is settled below. All 504 compatible left banks force a singleton left donor anchor, either 6 or 7. Extracting its OR guards gives a read-once expression with two occurrences of that raw anchor. Pinning 3 high and 6,7 low leaves three singly occurring raw left variables. The four-right common-tree bound and the five-right seven-gate bound force a raw left bank and an eight-gate right bank on the pinned face. The full right bank consists of those eight gates and the single terminal hit 3 OR H. Raw 3 is another port. A copy of raw 3 is allowed by taking H zero. Five right outputs remain unchanged between faces.

The imported four-port bound is in cut199-tree-two-hit/PROOF.md and its verifier binds the graph incidence words to issued18272.txt. For the five-right bound only the projected three-left/five-right sections of cut199-m12-two-hit-seven-right.md are used. The seven-gate three-guard and four-guard checkers, the seven-gate meet-bank generator and the lower six-gate free-conjunction check are included. These imports do not assume the five original left ports are raw.

## Favorable right-port substitution

Write F for the edge predicate on right vertices 4,5,8,9,10. For a five-variable right function w let s contain the vertices whose singleton assignments it accepts. Replace w by F OR the disjunction of s. This preserves singleton and empty values. On an internal right edge it sets the output to one, and on any independent assignment it can only decrease the output by monotonicity. Thus every positive edge and every maximal negative independent assignment remain correct for the very same monotone mixed tree. This applies also to H inside the terminal 3 OR H. Raw 3 itself remains literal. The favorable functions are granted without cost.

After fixing 3=1 and 6=7=0, nine distinct profiles require at least four effective right inputs. Fewer than five varying physical right occurrences cannot occur because four uncontracted signals would then be available in eight gates, violating the four-port bound. No pure-left subtree can compress two early literals, whose neighborhoods distinguish their singleton assignments. There are two possibilities for the five varying occurrences.

## Five effective right inputs

Each pair among the three raw left leaves has OR as its LCA, witnessed on an independent right input at which both respective singletons accept and empty left rejects. Thus every AND has a single right-port guard. A private guard is necessary for each query on independent inputs. A bare right port is required to accept an internal right edge when all left inputs are zero. This leaves three guards plus two bare ports, or four guards plus one bare port.

With three guards, their singleton supports are exactly the three query neighborhoods. A bare term occurs alone or under at most one private guard. In the latter case any singleton on which the bare port is true would have to form a right edge with each member of that guard neighborhood; there is no such common neighbor for any of the three neighborhoods. An unguarded bare term also cannot accept a singleton. The favorable substitutions give one support multiset. Both anchors and all 32 H supports yield 64 negative normalized-tree checks.

With four guards, choose three private guards and let the fourth cover a nonempty subset J of the centers. Exactly the guard paths containing the bare leaf constrain its contribution to F. Subtree leaf sets must be laminar. Enumerating these subsets produces 38 templates. On singleton inputs the guard-path intersections equal the appropriate query supports. On all independent inputs the disjunctions of these singleton supports are necessary lower bounds on the original functions, so a false query must remain rejectable. The bare path must also stay false there. These constraints produce 429 ordered assignments and 26 different support multisets. Both anchors and all H supports yield 1,664 negative normalized-tree checks.

## Four effective right inputs after one contraction

The five active right occurrences must all survive constant folding. Exactly one pure-right gate combines two of them: any further combination would leave at most three effective signals. Charge that gate to the upper bank to obtain a nine-gate quartet. The imported common-tree catalogue gives 180 possible quartets requiring at least nine gates. For four effective right inputs the projected tree must have three private guards and one bare port. Each guard agrees with its own query on independent inputs, and the bare port either equals F or meets one private guard to give F. The catalogue has a unique word for each query in every quartet, as checked by the quartet verifier; all 180 pass.

Split any quartet word w back into two port occurrences with singleton supports a,b. An OR split requires a OR b equal the singleton support of w. An AND split requires a AND b equal that support, and every right assignment meeting both support disjunctions must be accepted by w. Both operations are included, including absorbing or coincident semantic values. Across all four choices of split word these necessary conditions yield 86 multisets. The normalized-tree feasibility test on favorable words, retaining two distinct anchor occurrences, initially leaves four out of 5,504 combinations. Their supports in the variable order 4,5,8,9,10 are [0,5,8,18,24] with H=16 and [0,5,16,18,24] with H=8, at either anchor.

The original normalized tree still has a necessary syntactic upper restriction after simply substituting constants for the three anchor occurrences and the two hits. Its eight active leaves survive. Replacing the right functions by favorable ones preserves this formal topology, even if a favorable function could be eliminated by extra semantic simplification. Enumerate the operation at the LCA of each pair of active leaves from each allowed private-guard/bare topology and each admissible split. This gives 20 different formal templates including both split operations.

The final separator search uses all 16 positive edges and 24 maximal negatives, as well as the prescribed formal topology. At an OR it partitions the positive rows between the children and passes every negative to both; AND is dual. A nonempty active subtree must survive the upper setting. When it combines with an inactive child, the latter must be the identity for the operation. When both children are inactive, their two constants must combine to the requested constant. Two active children must have the correct LCA operation. Mandatory singleton-difference pairs from each positive row or negative column supply additional valid LCA constraints. All 40 template/anchor checks fail. The positive syntax control grafts five constant leaves onto a formal template, synthesizes a compatible 13-leaf tree on synthetic care rows, and verifies that its upper restriction is the prescribed eight-variable function.
