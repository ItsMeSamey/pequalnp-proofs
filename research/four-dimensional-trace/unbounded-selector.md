# Unbounded separator degree for a connected graphic one-hole trace

For every n≥4 there is a graphic rank-four boundary instance with one missing flux whose covector separator selectors have Boolean degree at least n. Both the ordinary matroid and its projection modulo the boundary are connected.

Represent an edge ab by the binary vector a+b on vertex coordinates. Let T be the even-weight vectors on five terminals L,R,Z,U,D. The boundary functional prescribes potentials red (0) on L,R,Z and blue (1) on U,D. A dual extension has a unique normalized potential with L=0. For every coloring of the remaining vertices there is such an extension. An eligible edge has endpoints of the same color. A boundary vector is attainable at that coloring exactly when each monochromatic connected component contains an even number of its indicated terminals, by the edge-span characterization.

Start with a path L,v1,...,vn,R. Join each vi to U and D. Join Z to v2 and v3. Add a vertex wL adjacent to L,U,v1, and wR adjacent to R,U,vn. There are no edges between terminals. The core on U,D and the vi contains the biconnected graph K(2,n). Insert the paths v1,L,wL,U and vn,R,wR,U as ears, add the extra edges wL-v1,wR-vn, and insert the ear v2,Z,v3. Hence the resulting graph is biconnected. Projection modulo T identifies the five terminals to one hub. Deleting this hub leaves the vi path with the two w vertices attached. Deleting any other vertex leaves every remaining vertex adjacent to the hub. The quotient multigraph has no loops and is biconnected. Both cycle matroids are therefore connected.

Write A=L+R and B=U+D, and set p=A+B. At any coloring, U and D are connected by eligible edges exactly when at least one vi is blue. Indeed D has only the vi as neighbors. In the red subgraph, L and R are connected exactly when every vi is red. The Z detour joins two consecutive path vertices, and the end detours can only rejoin the red path at its first or last vertex. Thus exactly one of A,B is attainable at every coloring and p is always missing.

The other seven fluxes in ker λ are all attainable. With every vi red, all even subsets of the three red terminals are attainable. A blue v1 alone realizes B together with Z+R if desired, since v2 through vn remain red. A blue vn alone realizes B together with L+Z if desired. This covers the kernel except p.

A separator selector assigns a functional c(d) annihilating the span of the eligible edges and satisfying c(d)(p)=1 at every coloring. If all vi are red, A lies in the eligible span, so c(d)(A)=0. Otherwise B lies in that span, so c(d)(A)=1. On the affine subspace where the ear vertices are fixed red, the scalar c(d)(A) is exactly the Boolean OR of the n unconstrained vi bits. Its unique multilinear polynomial over the binary field is 1+∏(1+xi), of degree n. Evaluation on A is linear in c, and affine restriction cannot increase degree. Hence any polynomial selector has degree at least n, in any affine coordinates on the space of extensions. This lower bound concerns the selector certificate. The graph itself gives a short proof of absence.
