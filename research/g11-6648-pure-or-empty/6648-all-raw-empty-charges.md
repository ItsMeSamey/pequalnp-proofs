# Arbitrarily nested raw-empty exceptional ORs with no stars at vertex 3

Assume the issued least size-21 TopForm hypotheses, j≥3 and q≥3, and that EVERY OR outside Lambda below the clean aggregation has empty raw support. Suppose the sole raw reader of x=3 is an AND g=x∧S, either dirty or clean and centreless. Then U≤j+2c+q. This result permits all reconvergences, unrestricted multiplicative sharing and charged ORs reading other charged ORs directly.

1. No star contains x. A clean sole g is centreless by hypothesis; a dirty sole g cannot witness a raw star. An ordinary clean top's two raw-positive input pins are both pure disjunctions: a first dirty AND entering any OR path would force a charged gate, and an empty-raw paid gate cannot regain a raw input downstream through an OR without making that OR another charge with nonempty raw support. Clean ANDs cannot precede a clean top by TopForm. Thus ordinary tops accept only original stars. If there are no centreless tops the claim holds immediately. For U≥8 use the no-x-star double-footprint lemma: in an exclusive allocation to any number of clean products of original Lambda-anchored/singleton clauses, each batch has at most two residuals and at most one batch globally gets two. Scope and finite lookup are as in premises' no-x-star footprint: a nonhomogeneous double uses at least seven stars and the only ≤8-star footprints avoiding x are the three distinct seven-star H8 patterns. They mutually star a marked edge of any other nonhomogeneous pair and either star 59 or use 59 as their own mark, preventing a second, homogeneous double. The sole homogeneous double is 14 and 59. Thus N batches account for at most N+1 allocated residuals. A previously discharged edge can simply be omitted in this exclusive allocation. If U≤7 the claim already follows (c≥1,j,q≥3).

2. Each dirty AND indexed i supplies one formal atom a_i, representing the conjunction of the pure raw-positive direct pins of that gate. If it has no such pin the atom is the constant true and can be omitted. A raw-positive nonraw pin is a pure anchored OR by the previous paragraph. The remainder of the gate is the conjunction of its raw-empty pin functions, which are earlier dirty AND functions or binary unions of them (iterate through all raw-empty charged ORs). The clean top T itself has an analogous fixed pure raw guard. It therefore computes this guard times a monotone Boolean function of at most j formal, provisionally independent atoms. Substituting back actual pure guards gives the exact original top under all valuations. For each inclusion-minimal true support of that formal function, multiply all its atoms together with the fixed guard. This is a pure anchored conjunction and implies T, hence is clean. Their union gives T even if distinct atoms agree under actual input valuations. The supports form an antichain on n≤j atoms; its size is ≤binom(n,floor(n/2)) by Sperner (count maximal chains passing through each support). Independently at most 2^q pure anchored conjunctions suffice by choosing one arm simultaneously at every charged OR, preserving the original value for at least one choice at every input and only decreasing other values. Constant-false roots never require a batch.

3. With four available dirty AND indices at most FIVE minimal support batches suffice per root. If any index has no nonconstant atom, three atoms suffice and give at most three terms. Otherwise each dirty AND has ≥1 pure raw pin. Its formal output is either the fresh atom by itself (source) or that atom multiplied by an OR of earlier dirty outputs, since at most one raw-empty input remains. Any zero or repeated paths only reduce supports. If six minimal supports existed, by the maximal-chain proof of Sperner all six must be the six two-element subsets of the four atoms (all layers except the middle have strictly fewer than six subsets). If one of the first three wires is not a source, choose its index i and a later k. Its fresh atom cannot contribute on assignment {i,k}: that gate also needs a strictly earlier true atom. The top would already accept {k}, contradiction. Hence the first three wires are sources. Set atom four false; its gate output vanishes. The remaining formal top on the three sources is the conjunction of at most two ORs of source atoms (one per raw-empty top pin, after removing constant-false inputs), with fixed raw guard retained externally. Such a conjunction cannot realize the three two-element minimal supports: to reject each singleton it needs a clause omitting that singleton, and that clause must contain both other atoms to hit every accepted pair. All three distinct clauses are required. This rules out six. Equally if fewer than four surviving gate indices remain the usual Sperner bound suffices.

4. Define B(n,r)=min(2^r,binom(n,floor(n/2))) with the second bound replaced by 5 at n=4. In the clean case pay at most two edges for A=g since all its true minterms involve x. Each of the other c−1 centreless tops has ≤B(j,q) batches. In the dirty case pay both incident edges xy,xz first, then set g=false, preserving evaluations on every other graph pair and decreasing every signal. The 19-card argument gives g's unique reader M=g∨D, which is raw-empty and charged, so the projection M→D removes one binary choice and does not introduce any additional primitive clauses. The projected products are still clean and assessed against original stars. They have at most j−1 surviving dirty AND indices and q−1 paid binary choices. If a surviving index has a dead false pin, eliminate it or keep its formal output false; neither introduces a new gate/atom. Pure raw guards and binary top capacities are preserved. For U≥8 the two resulting bounds are respectively 3+(c−1)B(j,q) and 3+cB(j−1,q−1).

5. By 6648-regime-index.md, the only open numerical region has c≥1, 3≤q≤10, 3≤j≤13−2c−q (the arithmetic outside this region is already covered by other graph results). In this range BOTH bounds in (4) are ≤j+2c+q. The accompanying integer check 6648-all-raw-empty-charge-bounds.py directly evaluates the two expressions at every such triple. This proves the result without making any assumptions on which junk gates are selected. The issued balance transfers U≤j+2c+q to the selected inequality.

This proof does not cover a charged OR with a nonempty raw side, or a purely raw charged OR. It is a mathematical argument and finite arithmetic check without a combined Lean certificate.
