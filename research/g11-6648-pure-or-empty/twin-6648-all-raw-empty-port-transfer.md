# All raw-empty charges force the no-3-star port

Use the established unique-reader theorem for x=3 in the size-21 least TopForm G11 regime. Assume every pre-top exceptional OR has empty raw support. Any raw-positive pre-top OR fires at a singleton and is dirty, hence lies in Lambda. Both pins thus have positive raw support. Downward induction shows the entire cone is a pure raw disjunction: an AND has no raw reach.

A sole x∨D reader is dirty on singleton x and necessarily precedes some AND on its live route to output. By the preceding observation it is pure. Let t occur in D; D is independent of x and nonempty by leastness. If t neighbors x, singleton t and edge xt are indistinguishable. Otherwise adding x to {t,6} or {t,7} changes no wire beyond its already-true sole channel. Thus both pairs must be graph edges, impossible since x is the unique common neighbor of 6 and 7. This rules out every sole OR.

Let the sole reader be g=x∧S. Dirty g or clean g with raw-empty S cannot star an x edge. For clean g with positive-raw S, S is pure by the first observation. Its support is exactly {6,7}: both singletons must allow x to toggle the output, and adding x to any other singleton is a nonedge. Leastness excludes duplicate single- or two-vertex pure signals, so S is the physical gate h=6∨7.

Setting x false removes g and its sole OR reader T=g∨D, leaving a least 19-card. The common raw gate case of 6648-size21-sole-and.md gives a surviving OR reader B of h. It genuinely reads h in the original circuit: D cannot equal h since D must be false on both neighbor singletons for x to be decisive. Thus bypassing T to D cannot newly expose a pin reading h. Original B has positive raw support including 6,7. A Lambda witness for B needs opposite singleton x, their only common neighbor. As x's sole raw use is the AND g, the witness top must be g itself, whose pins x and h cannot OR-reach the strictly later gate B. Therefore B is paid raw-positive, a contradiction.

Thus the source theorem 6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md applies without an additional no-x-star assumption, conditional on the established unique-reader and cofactor inputs. Its integer region has 34 triples: 21 for c=1, 10 for c=2, 3 for c=3. In its stated two batch inequalities the only zero margins are dirty (c,j,q)=(1,6,5),(2,5,4) and clean (2,5,4),(3,4,3). The finite footprint and regime inputs remain those of the source theorem.
