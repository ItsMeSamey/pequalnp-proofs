# Area and overlap in the 31-by-31 plane

Let M be the complement incidence matrix. A cover by r rectangles has count matrix S of rank at most r (sum of outer products), overlap E=S−M≥0 supported on M, and total area 775+T where T is the sum of E. Each row has 25 ones, distinct rows have 20 common ones, and the column degrees also equal 25. Thus MMᵀ=5I+20J and M⁻¹=Mᵀ/5−4J/25. The inverse column Gram matrix (MMᵀ)⁻¹=I/5−4J/625 gives norm 11/25 and distinct-column inner product −4/625.

Write B=M⁻¹E and h=nullity(I+B)≥31−r. Let P project orthogonally onto that kernel and let Q=I−2P. In an adapted orthonormal basis tr(PB)=−h. The allowed cells have inverse entries 1/25 in the transposed positions, so tr B=T/25. Therefore T/25+2h=tr(QB)=Σ Eᵢⱼ(QM⁻¹)ⱼᵢ≤11T/25, using orthogonality of Q.

At r=17 this gives T≥70. Equality forces QM⁻¹eᵢ=(11/25)eⱼ for every positive Eᵢⱼ. Rank E≥31−r by M=S−E; a nonzero two-by-two minor furnishes positive entries in distinct rows and columns. Their inverse columns would be orthogonal after reflection, contradicting the nonzero Gram entry. Integrality gives T≥71 and area≥846. With the independently enumerated maximum area 60 and absent areas 46,47, five boxes of area≥48 and twelve of area≤45 yield at most 840. At least six boxes have area≥48.

For any nonnegative F≤E of entry sum f, the contribution tr(QM⁻¹F) is at most the nuclear norm of M⁻¹F (singular value decomposition). Bounding the residual T−f separately gives 10T≥50h+11f−25‖M⁻¹F‖_*. In particular two fixed boxes intersecting in u rows and v columns force F to contain that block in the sense that one can choose F equal to its indicator. This rank-one choice gives correction 11uv−√(v(125u−4u²)) using the Gram matrix. Transposition yields the same bound with u,v exchanged. For certified pruning use exact arithmetic or an upper bound on the square root. For more fixed boxes take F equal to their multiplicity minus the indicator of their union. All unfixed boxes remain unrestricted.
