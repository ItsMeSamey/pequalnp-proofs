# Necessary tests on split-defect margins at area 851

Use B, R and r from finite-field-area.md and repeated-classes.md. The following tests do not exclude area 851.

**Sparse binary triple relations.** For a zero-sum triple of distinct pure binary row indices, each with one odd defect line, every active point outside those three indices must lie on an odd number of the three lines. If any lines repeat this permits at most six points. Three distinct nonconcurrent lines permit twelve (four on each line). Thus for r≥18 the three lines must be concurrent, giving an odd-incidence footprint of sixteen points. At r≥20 no such triple exists.

For r=18 or 19 two distinct zero-sum triples are impossible. All active points outside both triples (at least twelve) must belong to both three-line pencils. With zero, one or two common lines the union intersections have size at most nine, ten, or eleven: for two common lines the center is shared, and their union has eleven points. If all lines coincide as an unordered set, equality of the two diagonal-unit-vector sums forces identical row-index triples. The number of additive triples among distinct pure row vectors is consequently at most one. Among D distinct nonzero vectors in a binary space of dimension d≤r−14 there are at least D(2D−2^d)/6 unordered additive triples by intersecting the set with each of its translates: all three members of a relation are distinct and each relation is counted six times. Test this using the same class multiplicities as repeated-classes.md.

**Ternary avoiding rows.** Let h count mod-3 rows having just one nonzero residue equal to one, and m those with just one equal to two. When r≥15, each residue-one row vector has at most seven occurrences of two (at most six incident points plus the diagonal). No such vector can be another one's negative. Equal-row classes have size at most three by the four-line argument, applied to the entries with value two. Hence at least ceil(h/3) projectively distinct linear forms represent these rows in a rank factorization of B. Any q≤3 distinct projective linear forms have independent symmetric squares over F₃: for each use a product of two linear functionals vanishing on the other directions and nonzero on the chosen one, to separate its symmetric-square tensor. All these squares evaluate to the same all-ones vector on the column family. Thus the evaluation map from quadratics has kernel dimension at least min(3,ceil(h/3))−1 when h>0. The all-ones row and the m distinguished rows give image rank at least m+1. Consequently m+min(3,ceil(h/3))≤d(d+1)/2.

**Joining the two shores.** Pair candidate histograms only when their positive entry multisets agree. Write P=Σ_cells binom(W,2), and on each shore D=Σ_rows (sum of binom(W,2) minus its largest value on that row). A pair of rectangles with nonempty intersection a by b has ab−1 repeated intersections after its first. This is at most a(b−1)+b(a−1), which counts repetitions within rows and columns. On any row the number of repeated pair occurrences is at most its contribution to D, since a maximum-multiplicity cell already accounts for that many distinct pairs. Hence P≤136+D_rows+D_columns with no partial-permutation assumption.

At any cell of defect v=m−1, summing h_i−1 over the m rectangles through it gives at most 24 plus the column excess outside the cell. The dual bound holds for w_i−1. Thus Σ(h_i+w_i−2)≤48+e_row+e_col−2v, where both e values are total row or column excesses. For maximalized covers only, the shape census bounds the maximum possible combined area at a given cost. The remaining rectangles contribute at most 60 each. For a fixed v this imposes one minimum threshold on e_row+e_col−2v. Pair the two lists of outside excesses in opposite sorted order to maximize the minimum of the pair sums. If this fails no valid pairing exists. The consistency of pairings across different v is relaxed.

split_margin.py starts from 75 necessary row profiles at excess 76, finds 44 after the binary triple test and 31 after the ternary test. The two-shore entry and repetition checks leave 26 ordered pairs. For maximal-rectangle covers the cell bounds leave 18. These are necessary profiles and do not prove any cover exists.
