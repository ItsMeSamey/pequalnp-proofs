# The last mixed gate

Let H be the single retained private point slot and J its ordinary raw label, both in the root region. Assume the only mixed gate that can have two boundary operands anywhere in a minimum separator is H AND J. Both boundary wires are unshared. Every live mixed gate consequently descends from this unique source. A fanout on any descendant of the source would place H and J upstream of a cut endpoint and hence outside the root region. Thus the live mixed subgraph cannot fork. No gate can have two distinct mixed parents, since both paths would originate at the same source and would have to fork. Repeating an identical parent gives a removable gate. The last mixed gate therefore has a boundary operand. A final AND would require that operand to be one on every positive sample; a final OR would require that it be zero on every negative sample. Every displayed boundary wire violates both requirements. This excludes a separator.
