# Three singleton-resident lines on three point features

With three singleton-resident nonroot regions, no line-free nonroot region, and four ordinary residents elsewhere, use the three unit-line envelopes as in the two-arm construction. Each equals its star on point singletons: for an outside point the all-six-neighborhood witness in verify-all-neighbors.py meets the four ordinary residents. The three target neighborhoods have exclusive points; their pairwise intersections are either three distinct points or one common point.

Write b for the number of point leaves in the envelopes, disjoint from the four ordinary private expressions. Take one exclusive point for each special target. The three b-bit feature vectors are pairwise incomparable by monotonicity of the envelopes. Thus b is at least three. When b=3, the three vectors must be the three atoms or the three coatoms of the Boolean cube.

In the coatom case any nontop vector is below some assigned coatom, which accepts only its own target. An exclusive valuation for one target cannot lie below any other assigned coatom or equal the top, so it must equal its own coatom. A point on multiple targets must receive top, which activates all three. Thus the stars are concurrent, and each target can be replaced by the two-input AND testing its coatom, which is below its envelope and covers its positives.

In the atom case an exclusive point of target i has its atom vector, since a nonzero vector avoiding both opposite atoms equals its own atom. No envelope accepts zero. In the triangle case the i,j intersection cannot contain the remaining atom and cannot be zero or an atom, so it is precisely the i,j pair. The three coordinate projections suffice. In the concurrent case the common point is either top or a pair. For top use the three coordinate projections. For a pair, the target whose atom is missing uses its own coordinate OR the AND of the other two. The others use their coordinates. This replacement is below the envelope since each added upset has its minimum at a target positive. Thus three gates suffice jointly.

At P=11 the four ordinary privates together use at least four different point leaves, so b≤7. If b=7 each uses exactly one original boundary point leaf and its singleton mask equals the target star, giving four literal singleton groups and contradicting the fourteen-paid-gate bound at width eleven (twenty mixed gates leave only thirteen paid point gates). The remaining b values are four, five and six.
