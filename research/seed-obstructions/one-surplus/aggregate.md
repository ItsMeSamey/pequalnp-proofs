# Seven-line fanout regions with no singleton resident region

Allow any surplus k in the seven-line mixed cone, decomposed as in multiple-surpluses.md. Every repeated endpoint with at least one resident raw line has zero singleton point signature at all lines zero, by the zero-line lemma in README.md. All effects of a resident T pass through the endpoint even in a larger network, and fixing its value to one gives the same monotonicity proof using a zero-line point pair.

Suppose no nonroot repeated endpoint has exactly one resident raw line. Inductively substitute constant zero at every outgoing occurrence of an endpoint with resident lines. At an endpoint with no resident line, build a point-only cofactor from its original point leaf occurrences and the already substituted preceding endpoints. The cofactor is below the original value with all lines zero and agrees with it on point singletons. If its singleton signature is empty, substitute zero and pay nothing. Otherwise retain it as a shared point wire. Its constant-simplified tree is nonconstant and costs at most p+u−1 extra point gates, where p is the number of original point leaf occurrences in its region and u is its incoming occurrence count from retained point cofactors. This also covers a repeated raw point leaf at cost zero.

For a region with e resident lines these substitutions preserve its resident positive singleton behavior, since no earlier supplier depends on those lines. All ordinary negative comparisons only decrease. For a nonroot with e≥2, the usual second-resident-line negative extension extracts each target's pair-safe private point conjunction, exactly as in the one-surplus proof. In the root, whenever it has any resident lines, its own rejection of negatives suffices. The e nonconstant expressions have disjoint constituent point occurrences and together cost at most p+u−e extra point gates, including any formerly mixed subtrees which become purely point after substitution. A root with zero resident lines contributes no required expression.

Write d_H for the original fanout of a retained nonroot point cofactor H with no resident lines. Sum over regions. The original point leaf counts contribute at most P, the number of distinct point boundary inputs (a repeated original point leaf has already been cut as its own region). Incoming uses of retained point cofactors contribute at most the sum of their d_H. Each retained cofactor subtracts one gate, and the seven target expressions subtract seven. Producing all seven targets therefore uses at most P−7+sum_H(d_H−1) extra point gates. Their point-gate lower bound is 21, and the original mixed cone has P+6+k gates. Hence the original total is at least 34+k−sum_H(d_H−1), which is at least 34 since the sum is bounded by k.

Consequently any seven-line cone at total size 33 must have a repeated endpoint with exactly one resident original line. This structural condition applies to any number of extra reads. It does not classify cones having such a singleton region.
