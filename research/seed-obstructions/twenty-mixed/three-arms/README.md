# Four or five point features for three exceptional lines

Use the seven-line raw setting with three distinct singleton-resident regions T,U,V. Their four remaining labels are ordinary. Assume the four private ordinary outputs use disjoint original point occurrences, requiring at most P−b−4 gates. The special regions and ordinary OR sides use at most b other point features. Ordinary incoming endpoints can, for example, be zero-pinned at their singleton positives when every nonroot repeated endpoint has a resident raw line. Additional nonzero zero-resident cofactors require their own accounting.

Keep each special region intact, set its raw flag according to the desired special line, and replace every ordinary region by the disjunction of its unary OR sides; keep a zero-resident root intact. This yields a monotone envelope on the same b features for each special line. At an incident singleton the appropriate envelope is true by trace domination. On a negative that activates that special line and meets every ordinary neighborhood, evaluating the envelope to true on a feature vector dominated by that negative would force actual root acceptance. In ordinary regions, ordinary raw splits are ORs and all ancestor ANDs on the chosen OR-side path pass on the selected negative through a resident singleton and monotonicity; in the intact regions monotonicity suffices. These are the same region rules as for the one-special and two-special envelope arguments.

Let W be the union of the three special neighborhoods. For each special target T, point p on T, and sampled negative n activating T and meeting all four ordinary neighborhoods, **some feature contains p and is zero on every singleton point of n**. Otherwise the actual feature vector on n dominates the vector at p, and the envelope would force acceptance. This remains necessary after projecting n to W. Treat the b signatures restricted to W as arbitrary masks. The search finds all multisets of b masks satisfying the projected obligations. It chooses an unsatisfied obligation and branches on every signature satisfying it. When all are met, unused slots can take any signature, including zero and duplicates. Only maximal negative subsets for each p need be tested in that search.

For lifting the projection, use every original obligation, including dominated ones. If only feature j distinguishes p from n within W, then j must reject every off-union singleton in n. Test these forced rejections against every off-union point. A feature confined to T within W whose rejection is certified on all off-union points is a valid original one-wire partial T output. Recover T by ORing in its missing raw point bits; direct four-point reconstruction costs three gates. All three targets may reuse features. This suffices in every four-feature case with at most three added gates. For five features all but 44 representative cases suffice within five gates.

For each remaining case, fix an outside point v and consider each hypothetical valuation z of the b features there. For an obligation (p,n) with v in n, let y be the componentwise union of the known feature values at n's points within W. If the valuation at p is dominated by y OR z, that valuation z at v is impossible. Test every original obligation and retain the union of all still-possible valuations over the off-union points. Enumerate a single AND or OR of two original features. Keep a proposed output for T only if it rejects every possible off-union valuation and every known valuation at a point of W outside T. Complete its missing target points using raw ORs. All 44 cases now cost at most five gates including the shared new gate.

The verifier derives raw obligations from the original theorem and checks the 24 simultaneous permutations of raw points and lines preserving its positive and negative rows. The 35 choices of three arms have five orbits. Across their representatives there are 1,306,982 five-feature profiles and 3,470 four-feature profiles. At P=11 three arm outputs costing at most b added gates, together with the four ordinary outputs costing at most P−b−4, yield the seven exact singleton signatures within seven added gates. Thus at least 14 original point gates are required and 20 mixed gates give total size at least 34.
