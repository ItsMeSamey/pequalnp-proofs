# Promoting a root line with a line-free repeated endpoint

Let the three fanout-two endpoints be D(T), E(U), and line-free H. Five ordinary labels reside at the root. Suppose exactly one of their original private groups contains H and call its label W. If none contains H the original two-arm proof applies.

In the original five-line root skeleton freeze the nonroots at their zero-line cofactors. D and E may instead be set to zero by the zero-line singleton lemma; H with earlier D,E zero still agrees at singletons and only decreases on negatives. Every resident is pivotal at each incident singleton; the common-point test therefore makes every original splitting gate OR. Standard target-active witnesses dropping shared guards give pair-safe private conjunctions.

Keep the four original private conjunctions other than W's. By hypothesis none contains H; with D,E zero they depend only on their disjoint original point leaves. If these comprise p leaves they cost at most p−4 gates.

For each special unit test T,U,W keep the three nonroots intact with their original inter-region links, setting T,U to their test values. Reduce the root skeleton to its four kept labels and form the OR of all sides at unary OR attachments. Set W to its unit-test bit and substitute the nonroot outputs in those sides. All reduced splits were original splits and are OR. Every original private AND attachment of a kept line remains an AND attachment on that kept leaf's path, so none of its point leaves enters the new OR sides. Count the four original private expressions, even if changing the skeleton changes other shared attachments. Nonroot leaves are also absent from the kept expressions. Thus these three envelopes depend on b other original features with p+b≤11.

At each special positive all four ordinary leaves are zero, so any true trace reaches a true OR side. Conversely take a negative with the tested special line active and meeting the four ordinary neighborhoods. If a feature valuation below the negative passes the envelope, its selected OR side passes in the actual network by monotonicity of the intact nonroots and raw W bit. Every ancestor split is OR. At an ancestor AND take an ordinary resident L beneath the ordinary child and a point q of the negative on L. On q with just L active the root is pivotal in L, so that gate's side guard is true. The side contains no L; all prior nonroot values on the negative dominate their values at q with just L active, since the nonroots are independent of L. Thus all ancestor ANDs pass. The resulting root contradiction requires the negative to meet only the four remaining ordinary neighborhoods, not W.

For b≤3 use the three-arm Boolean-lattice lemma. For b=4,5 the projected-family checker uses only three neighborhoods, monotone envelopes and this four-neighborhood negative condition; it does not depend on which region contains W. The arm cost ≤b gives at most p−4+b≤7 new point gates. For b=6 the four ordinary expressions use at most five original slots, are disjoint and reject every outside pair. The remaining slots distinguish all three unit positives from every relevant negative meeting the four ordinary neighborhoods. This satisfies even the stronger three-special point-role verifier, which allows up to six ordinary slots. At b=7 four private outputs are one-point-wire literals; larger b is impossible.
