# Clean products without an OR surcharge

Maintain a core and previously isolated clean terminals. Choose the earliest original topological clean AND H still live in the core. Earlier terminals cannot depend on H or its readers. While a direct AND reader H AND W exists in the core, bypass all of its outgoing occurrences to H. Every negative internal value stays fixed since both H and its reader are zero there. Positive values weakly increase. Each bypass removes an AND and preserves topological order.

If the core output becomes H, it supplies the final terminal and needs no further compensated collector input. If H becomes core-dead, skip it. Otherwise a live direct reader of H in the core is an OR. Cut H to zero inside the core and retain the original H as an independent terminal. At least one such OR physically disappears. It cannot be needed by an earlier extracted terminal, which precedes H in the original order. Pay at most one additional collector OR. The core retains any positive with H=0, while H=1 is accepted by the collected term. Negative values remain unchanged. Repeating the operation never requires an upstream gate copy. Only globally dead logic is pruned.

Each terminal input cone contains no previous clean product. A nonconstant native core without clean products cannot finish the computation, as an OR-only path from its output would expose a raw signal or dirty AND high on a genuine negative. In a general promise safe raw residuals may remain. Both individual paid counts, AND and OR, are nonincreasing. There is no assertion of preserving arbitrary original layouts, off-promise truth, or singleton traces.

Choose lexicographically least OR count, number of dirty ANDs, and then total AND count. A dirty gate covering only one required incidence p,t is replaced in place by its clean p AND t; one with no required positive is cut zero. This cleanup also applies to the normalized representative. Primitive transversality then implies that the first noncollector OR is raw/raw, and dirty sites before the second have actual value O AND r, with r a native primitive, exactly as in physical-prefix-products.md. This strengthens the representative restriction to OR minimization with no AND budget.
