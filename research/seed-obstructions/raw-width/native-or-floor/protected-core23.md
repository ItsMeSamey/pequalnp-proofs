# At least 23 OR gates on all four native banks

The richest bank retains both optional point-pair inputs. Four signed deletions refund one physical OR each. On the remaining core, the lower bounds for selected literal deletions restrict where those literals can occur in a minimum circuit. At the first OR that uses one of them, the proof grants arbitrary monotone functions of the other inputs. The signed rows rule out every pair of operands even with that extra freedom.

The generator-independent checker verifies 2,760 support states directly on the original rows, including 243 such first-OR exclusions. Extract the archive and run `python -B checkpartial.py` to reproduce the 19-OR core bound. With the four refunds, every bank needs at least 23 ORs, with unrestricted ANDs and fanout. Area 33 permits at most 10 ANDs; existence at that area remains open.
