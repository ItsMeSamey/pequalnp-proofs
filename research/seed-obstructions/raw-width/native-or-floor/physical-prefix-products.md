# Paid AND inputs to a native OR prefix

Choose minimum signed area, then minimum AND count, then fewest dirty ANDs. A dirty AND covering only (p,t) can be reassigned at its own site to the native p AND t, preserving all required positive evaluations and only decreasing genuine negatives, at any fanout. An AND covering no incidence can be pinned zero and simplified. Thus dirty gates cover at least two incidences. Isolated-clean normalization cuts old clean ANDs internally and collects selected terminals. It does not increase area or add ANDs. These prices remain equal by the chosen ties, and the number of dirty gates cannot increase. If a surviving dirty gate's new positive support were reduced to a singleton, the same replacement would improve the minimum dirty count. Thus the hypotheses coexist. Postpone collector ORs topologically.

For each dirty AND, stop its fanin cone at raw primitives and preceding noncollector OR outputs. Its value is the conjunction of these boundary factors. The raw factors alone must be high on two different required incidences. If present their conjunction equals one native primitive on the full cube: different tags are mutually exclusive on positives; a tag with any point primitive meets at most one incidence, by transversality of the optional native pairs; a nonempty intersection of different point primitives on positives must involve one native singleton contained in one supplied pair, whose conjunction equals that singleton globally. The two supplied pairs are disjoint. Repetitions do not matter.

Before any noncollector OR no dirty gate survives because such a monomial reduces to an available raw. Thus the first noncollector OR O has raw operands. Their positive supports must be incomparable, or bypass to the larger support to preserve positives and lower negatives. Between O and the second noncollector OR, every dirty site is globally equal to O AND r for one native raw r. The next OR may use raw, O, or these paid products with their actual native negative vectors. Distinct product signals used at its inputs cost distinct paid AND sites; sharing with future consumers remains possible. At later prefixes any dirty monomial may depend on several earlier ORs together with at most one effective raw. The general identity does not presume disjoint cones.

This is an existential restriction for a minimum representative; no original fixed physical layout or arbitrary fixed AND count is asserted to be preserved by all normalizations. Numerical first-OR domains in the companion script use original signed primitive values rather than free-AND closures.
