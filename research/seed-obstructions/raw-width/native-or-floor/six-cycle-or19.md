# At least 19 OR gates on each native bank

The 14 recorded literal deletions each refund a distinct physical OR gate. At each step a signed positive and negative force a live path from the removed input or one of its duplicate compound inputs. A retained positive on which those inputs vanish forces that path to contain an OR. Clamping the inputs removes its first OR while retaining a separator for the projected rows.

The remaining six-cycle has six required positives and five maximal negatives. Its minimum is five OR gates with unrestricted ANDs. The source archive contains the input statement, all deletion witnesses, the signed cycle and the SAT encoding. Both deletion logs and the signed cycle were regenerated from the original rows. The four-OR instance returned UNSAT and the five-OR instance SAT. Independent assembly review also checked the deletion proof and solved both instances using Glucose4 and CaDiCaL195.

Thus a size-33 separator on any of the four banks could use at most 14 AND gates. This result leaves all four banks open at size 33.
