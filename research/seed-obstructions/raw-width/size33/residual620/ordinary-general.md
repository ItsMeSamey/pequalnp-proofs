The all-zero-unpinned evaluation is dominated by the native q-positive (which additionally sets u high). Existing unary absorption and the signed supplied-chart floor force exactly one unary absorber, all constant wires single-use, and no additional dead paid gate.

The companion B of H=q OR B is low on the native q-positive. Otherwise the unique raw q could be dropped on its sole positive, preserving all other positives and lowering negatives. B cannot therefore pin high. To rule out B pinning low, every paid syntactically low gate has an ancestral low path from u. For a low AND follow a low child, which cannot be a raw degree point since its unique entry is OR. For a low OR both children are low and cannot both be raw degree points since their heads are distinct. The remaining child is therefore u or an earlier low paid gate. B cannot itself be another raw degree point (distinct heads). The u-to-B path through low constants would consume their only sockets, giving u no route except through H. This contradicts original tag pivotality with q high. This reasoning does not assume that the high alias is pivotal. Thus H alone sacrifices a nonconstant operand.

Some originally distinct outgoing socket must therefore have been discarded at B, through neutral gates only. Every such neutral OR transmits D high on the original q-positive. So does a neutral AND, since its pin-high side is also high at the original q-positive by monotonicity. This makes B high, a contradiction. Hence u is ordinary.
