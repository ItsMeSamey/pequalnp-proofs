# Disjoint AND counts for a common donor

The [proof and signed witnesses](upstream-matching-and.zip) identify four distinct heads, each the only reader of its private point. A common donor reaches each head's other operand through OR gates alone. The donor must be structurally unreachable from all four private points. In the four-head case this follows from acyclicity; when only three heads are specified it is an additional hypothesis on the fourth point.

If the common donor accepts the 18 specific nonprivate positive masks in the final witness record, its cone needs seven AND gates. At least four other AND gates are reachable from the private points. These disjoint sets require at least eleven AND gates. The bound applies without an OR limit. The specified head wiring and acceptance of those 18 rows are required; the general nine- and ten-AND cases remain open.
