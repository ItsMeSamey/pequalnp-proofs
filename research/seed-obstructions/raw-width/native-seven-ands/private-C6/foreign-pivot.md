Use the same extremal order and isolated-clean representation as cleanpivot.md, SHA 669b4b8b0f3a9f099a6b17edfc29a564884a4bfe6dc97108492fa8ed8c61109d. Let H be a private site contaminated by one or more foreign tags; q,u are its own private point and tag. Its clean exposed exterior backup K has a u-exclusive singleton tag socket opposite the non-u socket. There must be an original positive incidence p+v with v different from u on which K is the only accepting isolated clean terminal.

Otherwise set q zero throughout the circuit, preserving K as a paid site, replace K's u-facing socket itself by the primitive u, and feed q OR the rebuilt non-u operand to its other socket. These are local edge changes even if the two old operand wires have other readers. On all native q=0 positives with u present, K's former u-facing arm was already high and its other arm is unchanged. On q=0 foreign positives either K was low or some other isolated clean product already accepted, and those outputs are unchanged. The final OR collector contains no other input sources: an OR-only primitive route would accept a bare atom, and an OR-only dirty input would accept a negative. The only native positive with q high is q+u, which the new K accepts. On negatives all old signals outside K weakly decrease from the q clamp, and K can become newly high due to q only when u is high, itself a positive incidence. Thus the repair is signed correct.

At least one original paid q reader, distinct from K which was exterior, has been refunded; adding q into the new arm has cost at most one OR. Although some nonprivate bare point marks on K's u-facing socket can disappear when replacing the socket by the literal, no bare tag mark there changes (it was u-exclusive), and no other private point had marked K. K remains clean, with no AND descendants. At bare q only the new K socket is marked. Hence the total singleton-high AND count does not increase, all four private points remain unsaturated, the three other private owners are unchanged, and exactly one contaminated owner is removed. The requirement concerns foreign positives exclusive to K on the original signed rows, not arbitrary off-promise values.

In the eight-AND, exactly-two-dirty sector, after excluding the possibility that H and its own backup are those two dirty sites, denote by L the other dirty AND. Assume at least two ordinary tags cross: they all arrive at the common H, their three exterior marks are separate, and K is the spare site. For each guest x the u-facing arm of K is dark on all original negatives above q+x, so its raw support lies within {u} union the intersection of all guest point neighborhoods. If L has an OR-only path into that arm, L cannot also enter the opposite arm since K is exposed clean and L is dirty. The causal input comparison at q+u and bare u forces H into K's non-u arm along ORs (q first reaches H and the only other possible preterminal AND is L). Thus H and L enter opposite sockets of K through OR-only paths.

If L has no OR-only path into K's u arm, H cannot enter it directly either since H and the other K arm are high on q+x while K is low. That arm is therefore raw u or u OR w, where w lies in every guest neighborhood. Raw u is impossible by the private move or by the exclusive foreign positive. For two ordinary guests w is their unique common point, has degree two with exactly those two tags, and is outside N(u); for three ordinary guests the intersection is empty. Thus this branch can only have two ordinary guests and no additional guests. An exclusive foreign positive for K must be w+v for one of those two tags. Its distinct exterior matched gate E_v is high on w+v by the signed crossing first-witness theorem (w lies outside N(u)). If E_v were clean and exposed, this row would have another accepting terminal. Hence E_v=L. The tag identities, uniqueness of the common degree-two point and degree follow directly from the native 28-edge table. Thus with at least two ordinary guests, either both dirty sites enter opposite K sockets, or there are precisely two guests and L is one of their exterior matches, with an exclusive foreign row w+v at K.
