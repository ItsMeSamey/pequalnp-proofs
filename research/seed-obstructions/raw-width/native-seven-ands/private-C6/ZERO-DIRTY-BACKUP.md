Let H be a dirty private site with singleton point q and own tag u on the opposite arm; q marks only H. The tag u must have a second mark: otherwise every q or u path to the output crosses H and the accepted q+u would imply acceptance of every input with H=1. No OR-only input-to-output path is possible on these rejected bare inputs. By the tag cap and zero guests, this second site B is unique and exterior. We prove B is clean.

Suppose B=E is the other dirty gate. E(q+u)=1. Otherwise any negative on which H is high dominates the q+u positive beyond the cut H,E, since all paths from q and u hit that cut. Equivalently clamp both physical signals before comparing the remaining circuit. With both H and E high on q+u, the same cut shows H AND E is safe on all native negatives. Since E is low at bare u, some q path into E rises at q+u. Its first AND must be H, the unique q mark. Therefore H strictly precedes E. With isolated clean outputs, H has raw inputs, and its physical function is A AND (u OR C). The point mask A contains q and is contained in U=N(u), by the single q cut: if any p outside U were in A, p+u would have H=1 and dominate the positive q+u beyond H. No other tags mark either arm, and C contains only nonprivate points. It is nonempty, or H would be clean. Both primitive optional compounds expand as point disjunctions; neither contains a private point.

The E socket not marked by u receives H via ORs: it is high at q+u yet no raw q or u enters it.
