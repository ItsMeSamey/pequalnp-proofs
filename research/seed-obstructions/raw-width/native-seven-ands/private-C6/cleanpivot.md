# Transferring a private point to a clean backup

Start with the extremal native area-at-most-33 and at-most-eight-AND representative of native-no-saturated-private.zip. In order, minimize area, total singleton-high AND count, number of saturated private points, and finally the number of private matched sites having at least one guest tag. Take the last tie inside the family with the preceding objectives minimized, in which the earlier theorem gives four different private sites, each with its private point alone at one site opposite its own tag. Isolated-clean normalization preserves this last statistic: cutting internal clean outputs to zero changes no operand value on bare atoms, and no AND can disappear without yielding a native separator of at most seven ANDs at area at most 33. This conclusion is about an extremal representative.

Let H hold private q and its own tag u together with a guest on that tag-facing arm. Its distinct exterior backup K has an exclusive u socket. Suppose K is an exposed clean terminal and its u-facing operand is functionally the literal u after q is set to zero. A raw u socket suffices. Set q zero throughout the circuit, but retain K and its two sockets even if its temporarily zero-point socket would have folded. No supplied compound contains q. OR the original q into K's non-u socket alone (simply wire q there if that socket folded to zero). The q=0 behavior is unchanged. At the sole positive q+u using this private point, K is high and reaches the output along ORs. On each q-active negative u is zero, so the u-facing operand stays zero and K rejects. Other signals not downstream of K have only decreased. The result satisfies all native signed rows and K remains clean.

At least one old paid direct reader of q folds on replacing q by zero. None can be K, which had no q mark on either arm before the operation. Constant and alias propagation pays for the at most one added OR even if the former direct reader had arbitrary fanout. If an AND disappears after the repair, the resulting separator contradicts the prior at-most-seven floor. Thus in a successful candidate all eight physical AND sites persist up to reindexing. An optional unused OR simplification only lowers area further.

At every singleton other than q the old value q=0 and the new q edge is zero. All AND outputs and all operand marks on those probes are preserved. At bare q the pre-K circuit is all zero: the only live q entry is the fresh K socket, its opposite operand u is zero, and K is an exposed terminal with no AND descendants. Hence q has exactly one mark, at K, with no high AND output. All three other private points remain at their original sites, because K formerly had no private point. The area and total singleton-high objectives do not increase; if either improves there is already a contradiction. The number of saturated private points stays zero. Bare tag marks at all sites are unchanged. The newly matched K has only u on its u-facing arm by exclusivity. No tag on its other arm is possible since u plus that tag is rejected and K is exposed. H is no longer a private matched site. Thus one contaminated private site is removed and none created, contradicting the final tie.

In particular, if the intersection of the guest neighborhoods is empty, the u-facing socket of any such clean backup must receive a further dirty AND through OR-only gates. The signed q+guest darkness in native-two-dirty-multiguest-backup.zip bounds the raw singleton support there by {u} together with that intersection, and bare u has a direct OR prefix because all AND outputs are zero on bare u. H cannot enter the u-facing socket by an OR-only path: both H and the opposite socket are high on q+guest, on which K is zero. In the isolated-clean representation no earlier clean AND feeds K. With no distinct dirty contributor the operand would therefore be exactly u, contrary to the move. The raw-literal move works for any dirty count. No original owner prescription is retained.
