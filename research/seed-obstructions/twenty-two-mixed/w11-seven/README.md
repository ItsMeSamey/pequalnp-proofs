# W11, seven raw lines, 22 mixed gates

The seven raw lines start with no exceptional labels. The region allocation inequality for five extra uses gives r+δ≤5. For r=0,1,2 ordinary reconstruction (and the one- or two-star replacement as applicable) yields total point cost at most 20. For r=3 the effective allowance is 25−(g+11)−δ≥1. For r=4 it is at least 2. Apply the established three-special and four-special results, with the latter covering exterior width b≤7. At b≥8 there are at most three private leaves for three pair-safe ordinary stars. At δ≤1 at least two stars are literal. If the third requires one binary gate, it cannot take either literal star as operand, because their singleton signatures are incomparable with its own. It requires two more leaves. Three literal stars are excluded in the inclusive bank list.

For r=5, equality gives two root-resident ordinary labels. The five-special certificate applies at b≤7 and allowance≥3. For b≥8, the two private blocks are unary plus unary, or unary plus binary. A binary OR requires union equal to the target. A binary AND requires intersection equal to the target, and one operand must equal it: otherwise an exterior point from each operand gives a forbidden exterior pair. For every positive belonging to one of the five remaining labels, one retained ordinary label can be chosen for which all negatives containing the positive's line and meeting that retained star must be separated by an outside point leaf. Each physical point wire accepts every set meeting its singleton signature, by monotonicity. This gives the necessary favorable row test in roles.py.

Run python roles.py --output survivors.json. The replay uses the complete 2668-bank W11 star-local list, without subtracting cheaper banks, and acts on the bank alone by the raw symmetry group.
