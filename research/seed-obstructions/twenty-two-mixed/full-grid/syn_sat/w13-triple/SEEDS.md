# A four-point clean target requires four proper partial exterior gates

Let T be a four-point ordinary line. Suppose a monotone union/intersection signature DAG reaches singleton mask T, that every free primitive hits T in at most one point, and that no earlier gate has a strict nonsingleton submask of T. Prior to the first mask T, a proper partial gate is one hitting T in two or three points. Each such gate contains a point outside T. If the underlying masks lie on the projective plane, these gates are exterior: the unique projective line through two of their T points is T. We show there must be at least four.

Otherwise, retain T together with one outside representative from each of the at most three proper partial gates. Restrict every mask to these at most seven coordinates. Every earlier two- or three-point partial retains an outside witness. A full gate may lose an outside witness in projection, which can only bring the projected exact target earlier. The four T coordinates are distinguished, and the three possible exterior witness coordinates may be padded arbitrarily. Grant as free every mask hitting at most one T coordinate, totaling 40. From those inputs and any previously admitted proper partials, saturate all results of AND/OR which hit all four T coordinates, stopping with a counterexample if the exact target appears. Test every obtainable further proper partial which carries one of the outside witnesses, and allow it as the next retained intermediate. Distinct occurrences with an equal projected mask can be merged. The finite induction visits 1, 42, 1089 and 20138 distinct sets of marked partials at depths zero to three. No exact target occurs.

Here a T charge is a gate whose singleton mask is a nonsingleton submask of T. At least two are required for a pair-safe full output. A first charged OR has at most two points, since its parents are both contained in T and each has at most one point. A first charged AND either lacks a second T point, or both parents have exterior singleton witnesses; choosing one per parent gives acceptance on an exterior pair (extend a repeated chosen point by any other exterior point). Under a budget of two charges, the full output cannot end in OR: any inherited first charged AND would leak its pair, and the union of an available first charged OR and one singleton has at most three points. If the full output ends in AND, both children contain T. At least one must have exactly T since two proper supersets accept an exterior pair. That child is a previous full charge, necessarily X=A AND B with A,B strictly containing T and intersecting in T. The other child C must strictly contain T: reusing X would inherit its exterior pair. If C and A share any exterior point x, combine x with an exterior point in B; then X AND C accepts their pair. The same argument applies to C and B. Hence A, B, C are distinct exterior gates with pairwise intersections exactly T. None can be a free primitive or a charge for the other ordinary star, by the singleton intersection of the ordinary stars. Combining this with four proper partials gives at least seven exterior gates. For two target stars at augmented price at most eleven, if just one has two charges then 5+h≤11, contrary to h≥7. If both have two charges, 4+h≤11, so their two edge-disjoint exterior-parent triangles fit on at most h−2≤5 eligible vertices. The first two exterior gates are ineligible: before either target gets a first full mask, every available non-exterior input hits that target in at most one point, so the first exterior gate has at most two target points and the second at most three. A prior full charged gate for either star would already require four exterior partials by the preceding result. Two edge-disjoint triples on five vertices must share exactly one C. For target U, its two exclusive parents each meet C in U. Since C contains both stars, both parents intersect the other target T in at most its single common point with U. For T the three own parents are full, the two U-exclusive parents hit at most one point, and at most two further exterior gates exist. This contradicts four required partials. Therefore two ordinary pair-safe targets each receive at least three charges at every augmented price up to eleven.
