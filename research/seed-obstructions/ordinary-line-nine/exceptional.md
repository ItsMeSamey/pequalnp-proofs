# Punctured lines and exceptional raw-line splits

Use the setting of README.md, with seven raw singleton leaves and two distinct additional nonsingleton line masks A,B. The seven-signature point-gate theorem requires 21 gates even for singleton signatures alone.

Write the two-bit loose pattern of line j as τ(j). A point p on j is certified pivotal when some sampled negative containing p hits every loose mask whose bit is set in τ(j). With raw lines zero and loose leaves set to τ(j) the output then rejects p. The j-positive accepts, so every AND sibling on the unique raw-j path accepts that positive. Write Q_j for these certified points. An omitted j-incidence can be restored by ORing its raw singleton into the recovered point signal, preserving all exterior singleton rejections.

With both extras pinned, a negative hitting both masks certifies rejection of every zero-raw point subset of its point set. For L,K, a negative hitting both masks and containing points p of L and q of K certifies the pair and makes both singletons jointly pivotal. Rejection on such pairs propagates at OR splits; through a raw-skeleton AND guard the guard is true on the jointly pivotal point of its resident label. An AND separating L,K would force its two children true at the opposite point with zero raw lines and hence accept their union. Thus every cross pair of the first exceptional raw-splitting AND is a missing edge. With exactly one missing edge {t,u} such an AND has singleton raw children. With two disjoint missing edges the only possible AND blocks are the corresponding two-label blocks, in separate branches. The same induction continues above and outside each isolated block.

For ordinary targets outside all blocks, use the original private-guard/extra-cofactor construction of README.md, now on Q_j. Guard acceptance follows from raw-leaf pivotality even at a point where unrelated loose patterns can leak. For the special companions restrict resident sets to outside labels; their inclusion relations stay laminar. To reject an exterior singleton x, use a j-active negative containing x and points on Q_v for the shared ordinary guards and Q_w for the dropped special attachments. The smallest shared ordinary group contains either both members of an exceptional block or neither, so either endpoint of an included block works. If v,w have the same loose pattern, one common companion suffices: compare the smallest ordinary group and the intersection of the required special groups. From the smaller group choose its companion; its identical bits make it admissible in the other group. The checker tests these witness requirements. For the no-AND case all seven labels are ordinary. No deletion of OR attachments on unverified positive cells is required; passing all AND sides on a raw-j path suffices to pass every OR ancestor on a negative.

For t in a block {t,u}, take the entire opposite child containing u, set u zero and the loose leaves to τ(t). This cofactor accepts Q_t by pivotality. For each exterior x seek a t-active negative containing x and a point q of Q_u, and hitting all bits of τ(u). On this negative the child containing t must accept by monotonicity from the u-positive at q where it had raw t zero. Every AND sibling above the block accepts for the same reason. Therefore the opposite cofactor must reject x. Swap t,u for the other target. Its resources are disjoint from those needed for t. If the witness fails, construct the target directly in three raw-point OR gates. The opposite child has at least one point leaf, since an all-line subtree in the mixed tree would be the raw u leaf itself, contradicting acceptance at any point of Q_t. The checked Q_t are nonempty. Thus the fallback costs at most three above disjoint accounting, and this target needs no subsequent repairs.

Inside a special attachment with two loose leaves, the two exceptional retained target patterns, if present, are 10 and 01. If their repair counts are r_i,r_j, the excess over P−7 plus all repairs is at most min(e,8−e−r_i−r_j) as in README.md, since the two direct stars cover their repairs. In an exceptional-AND configuration where one loose mask is a block pair, that bit is zero for every outside target, so two incomparable targets cannot occur.

At P=11 and 19 mixed gates, the total exceeds 33 if the defect repairs plus excess cost at most c+2. Here c is the minimum pure line cost: the cardinality of the union of the two distinct loose masks minus two, plus one when they intersect. Two disconnected components attain the disjoint case; overlapping outputs depend on a common raw line, and the union of their cones is connected. Computing the intersection mask once and extending separately attains the connected lower bound.
