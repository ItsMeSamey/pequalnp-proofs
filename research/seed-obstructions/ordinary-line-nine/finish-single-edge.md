# Closing the remaining one-edge cases

After exceptional.md only 108 line banks remain. They have a two-line mask S and a concurrent three-line mask R sharing exactly one raw label t. Let u be the other label of S. The only exceptional raw AND block is {t,u}. Outside targets are the remaining five. All three-line masks under discussion cost three gates together with S.

For the no-AND branch with two retained incomparable cofactors in one special AND-side attachment J, improve the pivotal sets using its resident group. The outer context K with J pinned to one rejects (p,raw=0) whenever a raw negative contains p and contains a certified pivotal point q on a resident label j and has loose bits at least τ(j). At that negative J is one by its value at q. Thus K at p is zero independently of the loose pattern. Grant every such p as pivotal on all its incident lines. If any actual zero-raw leakage remains, a true trace on zero raw lines needs a point leaf at a raw-skeleton OR attachment. Both loose leaves are in J, so that OR side is wholly point and unused by the point recovery. This saves one original point occurrence. With no actual leakage the repair count is zero. joint.py checks every resident group. On the 108 formerly unresolved one-edge banks 36 have no arithmetic overrun. In the other 72 each overrun is one, with three potential defective incidences, hence is covered by the unused-leaf discount.

For the exceptional AND consider the two child regions G(t), H(u), and their exterior K. Every loose leaf belongs to exactly one of these three parts. At an exterior OR attachment a bare loose leaf is impossible. Choose a jointly pivotal point q on any resident raw label at the attachment, with a sampled negative containing q activating both loose bits. At the positive q all ancestor AND guards accept. These guards contain no external raw line and their values on the chosen negative are at least those at the positive. A bare loose leaf would now force the root to accept the negative. The finite checker verifies availability for every choice of resident. Thus each exterior OR attachment supplies an unused point occurrence. If none exists, K with all outside raw bits and the block output zero is identically zero: every path to truth in this ordinary skeleton must reach either an active raw leaf or the block.

Represent G, H, and the one-input context K as arbitrary monotone functions of the point set, their own raw labels, and the loose coordinates assigned to them. The context additionally sees the output of G AND H. compartments.py indexes each observed argument to a Boolean variable and enforces order on every comparable pair. The exact root value is K(0) OR (G AND H AND K(1)), where the context's argument in parentheses is the block value. The model enforces all positive rows and all maximal negative rows. For each outside incidence charge one whenever the root at its singleton point and loose pattern accepts with all raw bits zero. For t,u inspect the opposite-child cofactor directly on all point singletons. Charge three when it accepts an exterior singleton; otherwise charge its number of missed incident points capped at three. The disjoint-factors argument bounds extra point gates by P−7 plus these charges: a three-charge target can be replaced by its raw star. Outside targets use at most one retained cofactor per special attachment since the S-bit is zero throughout the outside labels. Those factors share no points with either child region.

The four raw-geometry orbits of the 72 hard banks are checked independently. At each of the nine placements a sum of at least six is UNSAT when K has no exterior OR attachment. At the eight placements other than both loose leaves exterior, a sum of at least seven is also UNSAT without this assumption. In that branch a sixth charge is absorbed by the unused point occurrence of an exterior OR attachment. These 68 UNSAT results use no bound on circuit complexity within a region.

If both loose leaves are exterior, G and H depend on no loose bits. Recover t as usual. For u the only obstacle to the opposite-child negative test is an R-sensitive exterior AND guard above the block: every other possible loose bit there is S, which is true whenever u is active. If no such guard exists, the usual opposite-child cofactor works using a negative containing a pivotal t point. If such a guard J exists, retain its cofactor at τ(u) as an additional factor in the u output. The other child and every other ancestor AND pass by that same pivotal t point; forcing the retained J factor forbids any exterior false positive.

When J contains R alone, assign its point occurrences only to u. It may be dropped for every outside target in its scope using u as companion because the R-bit on u is zero. A separate S guard has the same zero bit on every outside target. If its outside group is a singleton retain it there. Otherwise its companion and the ordinary shared-guard companion can be chosen as a common outside label whenever both groups have another outside resident, by nestedness. If the ordinary group only has the block as its other residents, use u there. The requisite negatives meeting u and one outside companion are all checked. Thus all factors use disjoint point occurrences and at most five pivotal defects need restoration.

If J contains both loose leaves and has no outside residents, retain it only for u, again using disjoint occurrences. If J also has any outside resident, the pinned-context test above certifies the point shared by t and u regardless of the loose pattern. Then at most two outside defects, one t defect, and three u fallback gates remain. Six charges suffice; if an exterior OR attachment exists its unused point occurrence saves one, and the no-OR case has already been excluded. This exhausts all placements and proves the nineteen-mixed exclusion for all 2,079 one-missing-edge banks.
