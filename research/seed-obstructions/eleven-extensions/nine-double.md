# Eleven point wires containing an irreducible nine-wire basis at point cost 15

Unconditional singleton reconstruction leaves 15 possible nine-wire bases. Each has seven nonprimitive masks, one in each of the seven projective target lines. The required sizes are 4,4,3,3,3,3,2. Their individual OR trees cost 15 gates in total.

Call a paid gate exterior when its mask is contained in none of the thirteen projective lines. For a target T call a gate charged when its mask lies in T and has size at least two. Charge classes are disjoint. Let k be the size of the required mask in T. If fewer than k−1 gates are charged to T, call T economical. The pair mask cannot be economical, and an economical triple must be the first charged gate on T and must intersect two exterior masks. Before this gate the first exterior mask meets T in at most two points since every earlier nonexterior signal meets T in at most one point. This exterior cannot participate in the intersection producing the triple.

An economical full target has one or two charges. If its first charge has size at least three, that charge intersects two exterior masks other than the first, by the same argument. If its first charge has size two, the second charge cannot reach four by OR since it has only one available clean nonsingleton. A clean operand in an AND cannot be enlarged. Hence the second charge is the intersection of two exterior masks, both containing the full target. Even if the first charged pair preceded the first exterior, that exterior meets T in at most three points (one earlier pair and at most one additional singleton), so again it cannot participate. An exterior pair claimed for T yields a clean intersection specific to T, and cannot be claimed for any other target.

With h exterior gates the savings over the baseline 15 must be at least h, before counting any other uncharged gates. For 1≤h≤3 there is at most one pair of exterior gates omitting the first; it saves at most two gates on a single full target. For h=1 or 2 there is no such pair. This excludes all three values. If h=0 there are no economical targets. To fit 15, all seven targets use exactly their baseline charge and no other gate is paid. Without two exterior parents, a clean AND never enlarges a clean parent. Producing k required points in k−1 charges therefore uses a single OR tree on precisely those k singletons. Every nonprimitive extra mask must be a subtree inside a required mask; extra masks inside one tree must be laminar.

For each pair of arbitrary extra masks double_inputs.py and double_filter.cpp recompute which original masks retain private-negative witnesses on every outside pair. Covering a target T with c safe masks or input singletons gives a full pair-safe star with c−1 appended gates, hence charge floor b=21−sum(c−1). The augmented circuit has size at most 21+d for d=15−b. For d≤3 the established inequality gives h−q≤3 and 3q≤choose(max(h−2,0),2), where q counts full pair-safe targets with just two charges. Since the original nine-mask basis requires seven distinct nonexterior paid gates, h≤8. These inequalities rule out 4≤h≤7. For h=8 they force q=5 and partition all 15 pairs among the final six exterior gates into triangles, impossible because their complete graph has odd vertex degree. Thus whenever b≥12 we have h≤3 and the preceding argument forces the original circuit to consist of seven OR trees.

The filter retains all unordered extra pairs with repetitions when b≤11, and checks the required subtree property otherwise. It includes every zero or repeated signature for which the original relaxed circuit might work.
