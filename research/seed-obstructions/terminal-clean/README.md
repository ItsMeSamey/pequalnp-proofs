# Terminal gates of a size-33 residual separator

In the terminal-clean normal form for L-meta-18968 at size at most 33, there must be at least three dirty mixed gates in the prefix and at most fourteen clean terminal AND gates. The [two-gate normalization](two-dirty-proofs.zip) covers independent gates and both possible first operations in a serial pair. The [prefix and terminal count](four-terminal-proof.zip) bounds how many positive samples can share a multi-positive terminal. The [three-clause certificate](full-three-clause.zip) separately proves exact size 34 for the stated three-clause architecture. These restrictions leave unrestricted existence at size 33 open.

For a globally minimum separator of size at most 33, the [terminal matching argument](minimum-terminal-matching.md) improves the cap to thirteen. A minimum with a terminal-clean representation must also have one dirty mixed gate feeding another. [Independent-layer proof](independent-layers.md)
