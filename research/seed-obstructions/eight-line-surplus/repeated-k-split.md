## E is inside a child

Suppose E is in X; the opposite case is symmetric. Every source of T,U,K enters B, so all three labels' positives force B=1 and make the exterior context with B=1 true with ordinary lines zero. On T-positives the Y cofactor at U=0,K=0, denoted Y₀, is true. On U-positives X at T=0,K=0,E=1, denoted X_U, is true. On K-positives X at T=0,K=1,E=0, denoted X_K, is true.

If Y₀ accepts p outside T, take a T-active negative covering p and a U point q outside T. X is true via the U-positive at q because E is on, and the exterior pinned context passes via q. This contradiction proves T-safety. Symmetrically X_U rejects outside U, using a T point to activate Y. For X_K, take a K-active negative containing the tested outside singleton p and any T point q outside K. Y passes because its K=1 value dominates Y₀(q), and the exterior context passes via q. In the guarded version use G AND X_K, so the temporary K=1 cofactor is valid whenever this test is needed.

Let X have e point leaves. Its two cofactors X_K,X_U cost at most e gates for e≤4 by ../one-surplus/prune4.py. For e≥5 build both full raw stars in six gates, at most e+1.
