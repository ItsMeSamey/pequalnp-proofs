# K,T,U inside D and E=T OR U outside

In a P=11, eight-line, 19-mixed cone let D be the sole repeated wire. Assume its residents are one ordinary raw line K and both raw arms T,U, with the loose E=T OR U exterior. The other four ordinary raw lines are exterior. This configuration needs at least 35 total gates.

Suppress D,E at zero for the four exterior ordinary stars. Since K has no bypass around D, D vanishes on zero-line singletons. The ordinary private AND guards recover these four stars. In their exterior skeleton let f be the OR of all OR-side attachment formulas, evaluated at arguments E,D. On a K-positive f(0,1) must pass. Follow the unique K path inside D. At a K-positive every AND side clamped to T=U=0 is true, otherwise the positive must bypass K and D would accept that point at zero lines. Their conjunction G suffices for D acceptance with K active. Hence G AND f(0,1) recovers the K star: an offending exterior singleton would pass a K-active negative meeting any ordinary V beneath the passing outer attachment. These K inputs come only from inner AND sides and outer OR sides, disjoint from the four ordinary expressions.

Let C be the OR of the inner K-path OR-side formulas, with i point occurrences. On a T-exclusive singleton f(1,0) must be false, since the U-unit exterior cofactor dominates it and cannot accept an exterior singleton of U. That cofactor safety follows by a U-active negative meeting an ordinary V at the chosen outer attachment. Thus real D and therefore C with T active must pass every T-exclusive singleton. Similarly C_U passes U exclusives. Exterior f(1,1) passes all six exclusive points.

If C_T passes a singleton p outside T, choose a U-exclusive point q. Choose a passing exterior OR attachment for f(1,1) at q and an ordinary V resident there. A sampled T-active negative contains p,q and meets K and V (verify_split.py). The real inner OR side passes via p and every inner AND ancestor via K, yielding D=1. The outer side passes via q and all outer AND ancestors via V. This contradiction proves C_T singleton-safe. Similarly C_U is safe and the baseline C₀₀ is false on all six exclusive singletons.

Both once-occurring T,U must therefore appear in C. Their least common ancestor has only pure point sides above it. At every exclusive positive its ancestor ANDs pass, and none of their OR siblings can bypass the ancestor, since a bypass would also accept with both arms zero. If the least common ancestor is OR, use each arm's own child cofactor at one. If it is AND, use the opposite child cofactor at zero. These functions accept their corresponding three exclusive points and use disjoint point leaves. To prove safety for outside p, use the same negative containing p,q and meeting K and V. At OR, the chosen branch on p propagates via q through the shared ancestors. At AND the opposite zero cofactor passing p combines with the own branch, since that branch was true with its own line zero at the q positive. The same q activates shared ancestors and the outer f(1,1) attachment. A synthetic OR in C merely forwards one real inner OR-side attachment, and K activates its inner AND ancestors independently of which side it was.

The two nonconstant disjoint point expressions cost at most i−2 gates, and adding the common raw point to both costs at most two. The five other nonconstant outputs cost at most P−i−5 gates from disjoint point occurrences. Thus all seven target singleton masks are recovered with ≤P−5 gates, giving original cost ≥21−(P−5)+19+1=35.
