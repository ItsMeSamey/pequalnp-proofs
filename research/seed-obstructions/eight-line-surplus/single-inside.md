# Inner and outer envelopes with one ordinary resident

D is the unique fanout-two endpoint in the eight-line cone with extra mask E=T OR U. Its unique ordinary resident outside T,U is K. Denote the original special source occurrences entering D by I⊆{T,U,E}; the others enter the exterior. Suppress D and special flags to recover the four exterior ordinary stars from their private point guards.

Inside D follow the unique K path. Its AND sides clamped at zero special flags pass every K-positive point, as K forces D on while D on zero-line singletons vanishes. Their conjunction G suffices for D with K active. Let Y_T and Y_U be ORs of K-path OR-side cofactors evaluated at T and U unit flags. In the exterior ordinary skeleton let O_T,O_U be ORs of its OR-side cofactors at the exterior unit flags and D=0. Let W_K be that OR with exterior flags zero and D=1. W_K passes every K-positive. The K signature equals G AND W_K: if an exterior singleton passed both, a K-active negative meeting the chosen outer side's ordinary V would pass D and the exterior.

O_T is singleton-safe for T by the same exterior extension. Y_T is also safe. If it accepts q outside T, choose p on K outside T. At p one outer side passes W_K, with ordinary V at its attachment. A sampled T-active negative contains p,q and meets K,V; for coincident p,q use any second exterior point. Real D accepts via the passing inner side at q and all inner path guards via K. The chosen exterior side sees D=1, the point p and flags dominating zero; its ordinary ancestors pass via V. This contradiction proves safety. At every T-positive either O_T passes or original D is true, forcing Y_T since raw K is zero. Hence T is exactly Y_T OR O_T on singletons, and likewise U. The inner zero-special envelope is empty on singletons since the analogous argument makes it a subset of all six non-K neighborhoods.

Write a for inner AND-side point leaves, i for inner OR-side leaves, o for exterior OR-side leaves. Four ordinary targets cost at most P−a−i−o−4 gates.

## I consists of raw T alone

At T the exterior flag is E, while U activates E and U. Thus O_T≤O_U and O_T is supported only at c=T∩U. The inner U envelope has zero signature. Recover U=O_U and T from Y_T OR c, the latter in at most i gates since Y_T accepts all three exclusives and is nonconstant.

If o≤4, W_K,O_U are two prunings of the same o-point formula and jointly cost at most o by ../one-surplus/prune4.py. G and the final K AND cost at most a gates. Total augmentation ≤P−4=7 yields ≥34 original gates. If o≥5 and a+o≥6 build K,U directly in six gates instead. The last case o=5,a=0 has G=1 and W_K itself is the full K output. In the outer OR envelope, up to two D occurrences are active for K alone and up to two E,U occurrences for U alone. It is precisely the unit-cofactor problem of ../parallel-algebra/README.md with five point occurrences. W_K and O_U reject every exterior pair by a target-active extension meeting V; K forces D≥G=1, U only needs D≥0. The OR-side zero baseline vanishes on all singletons. When moving a marker for K, D is forced by active K; for U the lower D=0 always suffices. Thus the finite five-point construction applies, using five gates.

## I consists of E alone

Y_T=Y_U is supported on at most c. Replace D by K AND G. This lowers negatives since K with G suffices for real D. K positives retain D=1. On other positives the real D was zero except potentially the two c positives: at T,U, D=1 implies Y_T or Y_U; at other labels it implies the inner zero envelope. The exterior is now a tree with six ordinary raw labels and two guarded K occurrences, potentially missing c only for T and U.

The repeated-one-label.md construction applies to these punctured incident sets. At each split choose any surviving point per child; zero-line pair rejection forces OR. To drop shared ordinary guards on an exterior singleton p choose another surviving point q on their common other line outside the target. Each punctured star still has at least three points and distinct stars meet in one. A sampled target-active negative covers p,q. The K OR-side cofactors are similarly safe via any surviving incident point on their resident ordinary label. Factor G once outside K's output. Seven punctured signatures thus cost at most P−7 new gates, and restoring c on T,U costs two. This gives ≥35 total.

The I={T,U} case is two-raw-inner.md. For I={T,U,E} all three K,T,U send their effects through D, so the exterior pinned W_K accepts all of their positive points. Pin E=1 in the inner OR-side formula C for the T and U cofactor argument in two-raw-inner.md. Both unit cofactors remain safe and therefore their common baseline can accept only at c. The same opposite-arm q activates the pinned exterior, giving the same disjoint two-arm construction.

## I consists of E and raw T, leaving raw U exterior

All effects of T and K pass D. The exterior pinned at D=1 and all exterior raw lines zero therefore accepts every point on T or K. On the K path, G accepts K and suffices for D acceptance with K active. If G passed p outside K, a K-active negative containing p and a point q on T outside K would activate real D and the exterior via q. Thus G alone recovers K. Likewise Y_T is the full T mask: all T positives pass D, and its singleton safety was proved above. These two outputs use disjoint interior point leaves for at most pD−2 additional gates.

At a U-positive only E is active in D. If D activates then Y_T does: K is zero, and enabling the internal T alongside E only increases the OR-side values. Thus clamping D to zero can lose only U∩T=c. The exterior now has just five once-occurring ordinary line leaves, including U possibly punctured at c. Apply the read-once private guard argument on its surviving positives, as above, then restore c with one raw OR. Its five outputs cost at most pOut−5+1 gates, so at most P−6 extra gates suffice overall. The mirror case follows by interchanging T and U.
