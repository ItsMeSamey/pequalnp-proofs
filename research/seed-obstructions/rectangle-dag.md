# ORs of point-by-line products need at least 34 residual gates

Allow arbitrary shared monotone AND/OR computation separately on the 13 points and seven remaining lines, including all free point features. Each mixed product ANDs one signal from each side. The final circuit ORs the products. A pure source feeding that OR can be pruned, since every singleton point and every singleton line is dominated on its side by a sampled negative. The bound does not require all product stars to have centers on the same side.

On singleton points and singleton lines, every useful product accepts a nonempty rectangle. Every singleton nonedge is dominated by a sampled negative and hence rejected. The incidence graph has no four-cycle, so each rectangle is a star. Assign each product to the side of its center, choosing either endpoint for a singleton edge. Prune products covering no positive. Let t be the number of distinct useful products and c the number of chosen centers.

For every chosen line center T, OR the point factors of its products. Their singleton union lies inside T and contains k_T distinct incident edges. Each factor rejects every pair of points outside T: a sampled negative dominates that pair and has T active, so the product's line factor is active on the negative. The OR inherits these rejections. For a chosen point center p, perform the symmetric construction on line factors. Every exterior line pair is dominated by a negative with p active. Building all such unions takes at most t−c extra pure gates. The k values sum to at least 28 because the stars cover all incidence edges.

The multioutput charge bound in equality.md holds for partial neighborhoods. A pair-safe output with k singleton points in T needs zero charges for k=1, at least one for k=2, and at least two for k=3. For k=3 a first charged OR could contain at most two points, and a first charged AND of that size would have dirty operands and accept an outside pair. A k=4 target needs three charges except for the two-charge exception that is paid by distinct exterior pairs. For any subset of at most seven targets the same inequality q≤h accounts for every exception. Thus the joint point circuit costs at least the sum of k_T−1 over line centers. On the line side, point neighborhoods have size at most three and intersect pairwise in at most one line. The same k=1,2,3 arguments apply. Its raw primitives are singleton lines. The combined pure circuits, including the new ORs, therefore cost at least 28−c.

Subtracting the t−c new gates, original pure cost is at least 28−t. The t mixed products and at least t−1 output OR gates give at least 27+t gates. Each star covers at most four incidence edges, so t≥7. The separate seven four-point ORs, seven products and six ORs give equality. The proof applies to arbitrary pure sharing and to shared final OR subgraphs, but requires the specified point-by-line structure of the products.
