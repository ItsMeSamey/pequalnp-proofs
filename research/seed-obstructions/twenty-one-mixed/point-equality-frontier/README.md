# Minimum-cost point banks with 21 mixed gates

The total residual budget is 33. For P point inputs and L line inputs entering a connected binary mixed cone, there are k=22−P−L extra operand reads and no more than 12 paid gates on both pure sides together. At P=11 the point projection has minimum 9 and 27 equality banks. At P=12 its minimum is 5 and there are five equality banks. Those point-domain classifications are prior inputs (point-pareto and twenty-ten-lines); their exact lists are included. The mixed behavior is checked directly against the pinned statement.

With eleven point and ten line wires all three paid line gates are mandatory, giving exactly the seven raw line wires and the three successive gate outputs. The checker generates each possible three-gate singleton-signature program using either union or intersection and all pairs of available operands, including equal operands. It keeps duplicate signatures as separate wires. The 8,275 line multisets and 27 point banks yield 223,425 labeled cases, reduced after cost filtering to 9,938 joint symmetry representatives. Every one-surplus mixed frontier exhausts. This excludes the P11/L10 plane.

For width twelve, five paid point gates cannot suffice at any possible L from seven to ten. For L=7 use the raw line set. For L=8 append any one seven-bit line mask. For L=9 take seven raw singletons and any pair of extra masks (including zeros and repeats), or one of the 24 nine-input minimal banks. At L=10 the necessary raw line cover has 437 irreducible ten-mask sets, plus extensions of the seven- and nine-mask sets. The checker finds the 24 nine-mask and 437 ten-mask sets afresh: for each point-dominated positive and negative, a selected line mask must meet the positive singleton label and avoid the negative line set. Under a width limit, exclude all previously found minimal sets and enumerate the remaining SAT models. At both widths every model has the full current cardinality. Consequently all covers of size ten contain one of the enumerated sets or the raw seven. This gives 361,245 distinct ten-wire multisets when redundant occurrences and zero are retained. Tests at width twelve permit arbitrary original line gate cost.

For L at least nine and P=12, use of a point-only cotree obstruction first leaves three of the five point banks. In every row pair where the negative contains the positive line singleton, the difference between the normalized inputs is confined to the point inputs. If a single input distinguishes them it is sensitive on both rows for every separator. Two simultaneous sensitivities at a positive force an AND least common ancestor in the same read-once region, and at a negative they force OR. With at most one surplus use, the inputs can be split into at most two regions. At every partial read-once tree some possible root label must have no forced crossing edge of the opposite label. Equivalently at least one forced-label graph is disconnected. Its components can be checked independently, since any completed tree restricts to a valid tree on each component. No bipartition works on two equality point banks, and the remaining three lie in one joint-sample orbit. All their seven-, eight-, nine- and ten-input line cases exhaust. This argument permits the original pure wires to have extra true values on nonsingleton rows, since lowering each wire to the OR of its singleton signature preserves every positive and can only decrease every negative.

Across widths eleven and twelve, all eight feasible boundary pairs at point-cost equality are excluded by the frontier. At P11/L8, nine paid point gates leave room for at most three line gates. At P11/L9 the same budget gives 2,069 line multisets. For two distinct genuine outputs A,B the necessary and attainable line cost is |A∪B|−1 when they overlap and |A|+|B|−2 otherwise. ## Mixed frontier

Each live wire stores its exact sample truth table and its side (point, line, or already mixed). One step chooses an AND or OR of two distinct live positions, with the ancestral sides required to make the gate mixed, retires zero, one or both operands, and adds the new result. Each retained operand spends a surplus use. Equal projected signals can occur as different positions. A binary gate with identical operand wires would be removable. Wire multisets with the same live truth tables and side tags represent equivalent separating continuations.

Every positive/negative pair needs to be distinguished somewhere on the live frontier. If a single frontier position distinguishes it, retiring the position must retain its distinction in the newly produced wire. The additional cotree bound is used as a necessary condition. Whenever a pair of positions would force opposing least-common-ancestor operations, they cannot reside in one read-once region. With r remaining surplus uses, at most r+1 regions are needed. If exactly one use remains and a gate retains an operand, deleting this retained position must remove every current conflict: apart from that position the other positions must all reside on the one unrepeated side of the resulting formula. Likewise if there are exactly two uses and both operands are retained, every conflict must touch at least one of those positions. At zero remaining surplus any other conflict is impossible. The C++ checker enumerates both retained and retired choices with these pruning rules and fails immediately on a surviving final state.

Run python verify.py with python-sat providing CaDiCaL 1.9.5 and a C++17 compiler. Specific cells may be selected with arguments such as 11/10 or 12/8. The output shows point/line widths, surplus, representative count, corresponding labeled count, then the histogram of the first empty frontier depth. Point-only filtering is reflected in the labeled counts.

For the 354 star-local point banks of width eleven and price at most ten, run `python local_ten.py 7 8 9`. The bank list comes from the independently enumerated star-local point domain. At line widths seven through nine a two-paid line budget permits 1, 64, and 540 signature multisets. The corresponding simultaneous orbit counts are 23, 1,070, and 8,466. Each run uses the surplus for its own width and stops on the first surviving final state.

At L=9 all 8,466 ten-paid representatives exhaust. Together with the nine-paid replay and the independent ten-paid point theorem excluding non-star-local banks, this excludes P11/L9 at 21 mixed gates and total cost at most 33. The recorded depths are in verification-ten-local-l9.log.
