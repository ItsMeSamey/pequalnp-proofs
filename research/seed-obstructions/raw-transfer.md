# Exact transfer from the raw suffix to the residual separator

Let B be the minimum number of binary AND/OR gates computing the exact 676-bit output in the back half of L-meta-18968, using the allowed inputs w0 through w43 and w54. Let R be the minimum number of such gates over the 38 signals w0 through w12 and w19 through w43 which accept the target bits outside the front and reject every negative bit. The positive bits of w54 are the front. Then **B = R + 1**. The raw circuit can be chosen with its final gate an OR with w54 at optimal size.

For the upper bound, append OR with w54 to any residual separator. Every required positive either belongs to the front or is accepted by the separator, and w54 is zero at every negative.

For the lower bound, bit 0 is a front positive and bit 52 is negative. Their projections on the 38 live signals, encoded in increasing wire order as integers, are 74089234433 and 239451881479 respectively. Every set bit of the first integer is set in the second. A monotone circuit using only the live signals cannot accept bit 0 while rejecting bit 52. Consequently, some omitted wire among w13 through w18 and w54 is used in the output cone of every raw circuit. All seven omitted wires vanish at every residual positive bit. Substitute zero for all seven. The output values at those positive bits are unchanged, and lowering inputs at negatives preserves rejection. The resulting output is nonconstant on the residual rows. A gate receiving one of the zero operands in the output cone simplifies by the identities x AND 0 = 0 and x OR 0 = x. Simplify constants and discard unused gates. At least one gate disappears, giving R ≤ B−1. A zero-gate raw circuit is impossible since no live signal separates bits 0 and 52 and every omitted wire vanishes on residual positives.

For verification, run `python3 verify_transfer.py`. It decodes the adjacent pinned formal statement and checks the two bit projections and the vanishing conditions. A componentwise comparison on the live rows also permits the set of all negatives to be replaced by its 245 maximal projected rows. There are 28 distinct residual positive rows. Thus a 34-gate raw suffix exists exactly when a 33-gate residual separator exists, allowing padding by repeated-operand idempotent gates.

The [25-gate residual lower bound](../residual-gate-bound/README.md) consequently gives a 26-gate lower bound for the unrestricted raw suffix.
