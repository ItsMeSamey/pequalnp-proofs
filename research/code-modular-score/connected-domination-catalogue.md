# All named kernels from bounded connected dominating sets in witness parts

Let G be a connected finite graph with vertex charges in a fixed finite binary space V of dimension p and d separately protected vertices. Suppose every nonempty connected induced subgraph of G has a connected dominating set of at most h vertices. Here a dominating set contains or has a neighbor at every vertex of that subgraph. There is a deterministic polynomial-time construction, for fixed h,p,d, of a computably bounded conservative spanning minor preserving all component-sum subspaces and all protected vertices in distinct branches.

A partition attaining a subspace W has a witness with k≤dim W+1 connected parts. In the matrix of part sums, merging adjacent parts restricts the coefficient domain to equal coordinates on that pair. If none of the merges preserves rank then the independent pair indicators of a spanning tree of the connected part quotient lie in the row space, giving k−1≤dim W.

For each k≤p+1 enumerate ordered disjoint nonempty connected vertex sets S₁,…,Sₖ of sizes at most h. Preassign each seed to its own part. Each remaining vertex may be assigned to any part whose seed it touches by a physical edge. Dynamic programming on the vertices records the vector of k accumulated V-sums and recovers assignments by traceback. Each recovered part is connected via its seed. Conversely each bounded-part witness has such connected dominating seeds and appears in this enumeration. Collect one realization per resulting span. Obtain also a single connected dominating set for G by the same enumeration with k=1. Running time is n^{O(h(p+1))} times a function of p,h and a polynomial for verification and traceback. No algorithm for recognizing the host promise is required.

Let Z contain this global seed and all chosen witness seeds. With s(p) the number of subspaces, |Z|≤h(1+(p+1)s(p)). Delete every edge not incident to Z. Each chosen partition stays connected part by part: its seed's internal edges and all its domination contacts survive. The global seed keeps the support connected. Other catalogue entries cannot appear by edge deletion. Z is now a vertex cover. Use a central bag Z and a bag Z∪{v} per v outside Z, yielding width at most |Z|. The bounded-treewidth conservative spanning-minor theorem (bounded-treewidth-additive-catalogue.md) completes the construction and separates the protected vertices with private marker bits. All vertices survive the initial deletion step. No support bound is placed on vertex charges. In the supplied private-cographic trace presentation, cut labels are the sums of the incident edge-label charges over one shore and a cut avoids the complement exactly when its shore is a union of complement components. Thus the entire catalogue gives all exact private traces, with the retained ordinary edge set the complement of a spanning forest of a realizing partition. For an ordinary incidence matrix with arbitrary named columns in its span, the usual even-component criterion gives the orthogonal kernels directly in the contracted forest. A row basis change and named columns outside the ordinary span can be handled separately by computing the eligible coefficient subspace and its basis before this incidence calculation.

The host condition includes bounded independence number α: start in any connected induced graph with one vertex; while there is an undominated vertex, take a distance-two vertex and its predecessor and append both to the current connected set. The distance-two vertices and the initial vertex are independent, so at most 2α−1 vertices are added in total including the initial vertex. It also includes every independent-twin blowup of a co-bipartite graph with h=3. A connected induced graph on multiple occupied twin classes projects to a connected graph with independence at most two. Lift a size-at-most-three connected dominating seed; when the seed is a singleton add any neighbor to dominate its other twins. For a nonsingleton connected seed every seed class has a neighbor among the other chosen classes. A connected single-class graph has one vertex. Base complements of square grids supply unbounded rank-width (every balanced grid cut has unbounded binary rank by bounded degree and grid bisection; complementation changes every cut rank by at most one) and arbitrarily large cliques. Unbounded twin sizes allow arbitrary independence number. The vertex-cover support result only needs existence of appropriately dominated witnesses for every attained span and of a global seed; the hereditary host condition makes the search complete.
