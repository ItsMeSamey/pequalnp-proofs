# Universal translation periods from binary kernel switches

Let D be a binary check matrix and w_e physical weights in a finite abelian group G of order g. A period within a binary switch orbit translates its score set into itself. A subgroup that acts as periods on every orbit also acts on the full score image for each right-hand side, since each affine check fiber is a union of orbits.

## Disjoint supports, exact recognition

Given pairwise support-disjoint kernel vectors C_i, compute E_i={sum_{e in C_i} s_e w_e : s_e ∈ {−1,1}} with finite-group subset-sum DP. For a fixed base word x, flipping switch i changes its score by δ_i=sum_{e in C_i}(1−2x_e)w_e. Flips on disjoint supports give independent binary increments, so the orbit score set is the base score plus A=sum_i {0,δ_i}. Conversely every tuple δ_i∈E_i is represented by some x, hence by some affine right-hand side. This converse addresses simultaneous guarantees for all right-hand sides; the fixed-right-hand-side answer can be stronger.

Start a family of masks at {{0}}. For i in sequence replace each mask S by all S∪(S+δ) for δ∈E_i, deduplicating. The resulting family is exactly the possible offset sets A. Intersect their translation stabilizers. The intersection is the largest subgroup of G acting as periods within EVERY orbit generated by the supplied switches. Time O(2^g g² times number of switches), plus signed-sum computation and final intersection. In particular the full group is attained on each orbit exactly when every final mask equals G. For any fixed base word, a targeted switch subset can be recovered by ordinary subset-sum traceback on its actual increments.

## Faster sufficient criteria

Assume E_i⊆H for the switches used, where H is the desired subgroup.

* If for each proper subgroup L of H at least [H:L]−1 switches have E_i disjoint from L, every orbit contains a full coset of H. For actual increments let L be the stabilizer in H of their subset sum. In the quotient by L, each partial sumset has trivial stabilizer since a period persists on adding further summands and the full quotient sumset has trivial stabilizer. Each increment outside L increases cardinality by at least one. The assumed lower bound therefore fills the quotient, contradicting trivial stabilizer if the quotient is nontrivial.
* For each nontrivial complex character χ of H let c_{χi}=max_{δ∈E_i}|(1+χ(δ))/2|. If the sum over χ≠1 of the products over i of c_{χi} is strictly less than one, Fourier inversion on independent uniform switches gives positive mass at every residue of H. This condition is sufficient. Exact mask recognition avoids complex arithmetic and strict numerical comparisons.
* Given a flag 0=H_0 < ... < H_s=H with prime indices p_j, reserve p_j−1 distinct switches for step j with E_i⊆H_j and E_i disjoint from H_{j−1}. The increments are nonzero in the cyclic prime quotient, so each addition grows its proper subset sums by at least one. Starting with a full H_{j−1} and adding the batch thus fills H_j. A chain of prime-step certificates requires only sum_j(p_j−1) switches when the corresponding directions are present.

For example in G=F₅^t place four disjoint unit triangles along each basis direction (all three edges on a triangle have that direction as weight). Every signed sum of its edges is ±1 or ±3 times the direction, always nonzero. The flag certificate fills G for every feasible affine check, even after adjoining an arbitrary disjoint or interacting high-rank block that preserves the supplied triangle switches. The 2-torsion-only quotient produces no nonzero period in this odd-order example.

## Intersecting switches of bounded span

For any given k-dimensional W⊆ker D select a basis and give each physical coordinate e its column of evaluations b_e∈F₂^k on that basis. Positions with b_e=0 cannot affect increments. For each other type b compute all possible v_b=sum_{e:b_e=b} s_e w_e, independently over signs. The sign sets are independent across types. For every combination of v_b, compute the mask
{sum_{b:a·b=1} v_b : a∈F₂^k}.
The same stabilizer intersection now computes the exact universal within-orbit periods for W. This is deterministic FPT in (k,g), with at most g^(2^k−1) combinations and 2^k evaluated switches per mask. It handles overlapping supports because every shared coordinate is represented by its single linear bit a·b. For witness reconstruction compute v_b from the actual base word and retain the switch a for each desired increment.

This extension is strict. Take D=[1,1,1,0], weights all one mod 3. The first three bits have possible scores {0,2} for even parity and {0,1} for odd parity; the last independent bit adds {0,1}. Both check fibers attain all three residues. No family of pairwise disjoint kernel vectors certifies this: any nonzero use of the first three positions takes two, so at most one switch does so. If it also uses the fourth position, its alone has at most two outcomes; otherwise choose opposite signs on its two positions, leaving at most one effective switch. Taking the full dimension-three kernel in the overlapping test certifies all three residues.

Recognition above is for the supplied supports or subspace. Candidate supports can be checked directly by elimination and disjointness tests. A negative result for that family alone gives no negative conclusion about other orbit partitions or global score periods.
