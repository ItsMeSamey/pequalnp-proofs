# Conservative charge minors on connected block graphs

Represent a connected block graph by its bipartite incidence tree of all physical vertices and one connector for each maximal clique with at least two members (including bridges). Root it at a physical vertex. Adjacent physical vertices are exactly distinct members of a common clique. Give every downward edge the sum of physical charges in its child subtree. Stabilize the whole sum along an infinite sequence. Record node type and ordinary representative labels and order siblings arbitrarily.

These finite-labelled rooted trees are WQO by embeddings that may dive, but when coupling two nodes must match their selected outgoing child edges in order and with the same colour *at the first departing edge*. Recursive child embeddings may dive again. For completeness, take a minimal bad sequence by tree size. Its immediate subtrees together form a WQO, since otherwise a bad sequence of such subtrees from strictly increasing parent indices splices after the prefix preceding its first parent, violating minimality (every immediate subtree embeds in its parent by diving). Increasing indices can be obtained since bounded prefixes contain finitely many subtrees. Higman's lemma on ordered lists of pairs (edge colour, child subtree), after stabilizing the finite root label, contradicts badness by coupling. Transitivity follows by composing paths: the first departing intermediate edge in each child direction again maps to a path with matching first edge.

In a forward embedding, for every target nonroot physical vertex remove the first downward source edge from the image of its parent connector toward its image. These distinct cuts separate every pair of physical representatives by the target paths and yield exactly one region per representative. Each region is physically connected: successive physical vertices on an incidence path share a clique. For a nonroot region subtract its outgoing cut-subtree totals from the incoming total; at the top start with the whole source sum. Matching first-edge colours identify all operands with the target, proving conservation including all skipped source nodes. At a matched connector, the physical vertex immediately toward the parent representative, and the distinct physical vertices immediately toward its child representatives, belong to their respective regions. They lie in a single source clique. All target edges are thus available after contraction. Fixed protected ports can have separate binary unit charge bits.

For fixed binary named dimension the full-catalogue library proof in modular-width-conservative.md applies on the incidence representation. Ordered unranked trees admit a first-child/next-sibling binary encoding. Alternation, physical root, connector degree at least two and graph adjacency via a shared connector are MSO-definable. The finite catalogue of component charge spans and the fixed conservative models therefore have effective counting MSO automata. Iterated emptiness and witness extraction terminate by the WQO just proved, giving a computable physical library that preserves arbitrary exterior dense-name queries at the protected ports.
