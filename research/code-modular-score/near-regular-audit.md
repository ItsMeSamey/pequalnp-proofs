# Recognition audits and one-sided perturbations

The spectral subspace branch DP in walsh-branchwidth.md uses W_L∩W_R⊆B_L∩B_R and B_parent⊆B_L+B_R. A pair of boundary functionals agreeing on the intersection of child boundaries determines the parent functional; actual child witnesses agree on the intersection of their full spaces and glue. At a leaf fixed boundary values merely combine odd-field Walsh terms after multiplying by signs. Nonzero dictionary is exact by invertibility; sequential restriction yields a witness. JKO's constructive subspace arrangement decomposition gives f(w)poly(input) on the presented spectra. [Jeong, Kim and Oum](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1285895).

The host-width regular-reference recognition formula in near-regular-recognition.md is correct, including seven distinct survivors for F7/F7* and quantification of both the contracted set and deleted zeros. A binary cycle is precisely a set orthogonal to every cocircuit. The two distance budgets are met by cutting a host subspace by a bounded list of linear equations and adding a bounded list of shifts. [Strozecki](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2011.02.005) states the finite-field branch-width model-checking algorithm and its cardinality-modulo extension. With an enhanced parse tree, pinning the finitely many free membership labels on one coordinate at a time adds only polynomial overhead and yields the actual reference by elimination.

Common projection or intersection with a fixed coordinate subspace cannot increase dim(U)−dim(U∩V) in either direction; use the quotient map, or inject the smaller quotient into the larger for restriction. For a proper 2-sum regular pendant, fix a circuit through its virtual element, delete other pendant elements and contract all but one real element of that circuit. A copy of the virtual port now survives on the physical ground. Any candidate reference goes through these same minors. Conversely gluing with the original regular piece equalizes a single port and projects; both distances are nonincreasing. At an ordinary port this preserves regularity by regular 2-sum closure. When the candidate port is a coloop it enforces the zero bit, giving deletion on both sides, and for a loop it permits a free bit, giving puncturing on the pendant side. Thus feasibility regions of the two budgets agree exactly, and reversing the tree reconstructs a full reference.

## Why three-port gluing needs additional structure

Take a regular rank-two pendant consisting of a triangle T and three real parallel copies of its elements. Equalizing the T bits of an arbitrary candidate with this pendant and eliminating them yields the original candidate cycle space enlarged by the span of 111 on its former port coordinates (identified with the copies). Let the candidate be the cycle code of K5, with reduced incidence vectors in four coordinates. Designate edges 01,02,34 so the port columns are e1,e2,e3+e4. Enlarging the cycle code is quotienting the column representation by their sum h=1111. Modulo h, the four singleton columns remain distinct; the six pair columns give three extra classes by complementary pairs. These seven are all nonzero points of F₂³, so the simplification is F7. Regularity can therefore fail for an otherwise regular candidate if its ports have lost the triangle condition. The example concerns the reconstruction operation, not nonexistence of every possible three-interface recognition theorem.

## Fano-guided quotients without a width bound

For the pure supercode case C⊆R, write C=ker H with H full row rank. All supercodes with at most b extra dimensions are ker(πH), with π the quotient of the column ambient space by a subspace K of dimension at most b; explicitly K=H(R). Regularity is tested on the projected binary matroid. Suppose a nonregular intermediate matroid has a Fano or dual-Fano minor with survivors S and contracted-set column span U (all other columns are deleted). Every successful continuation kernel K contains a direction v in span(U,H_S) outside U. Otherwise (U+K)/U intersects the surviving span in the minor trivially, so that forbidden minor is unchanged. Branch on all such v, projecting by its span and decreasing the remaining budget by one. For a literal seven-element restriction, U=0 and there are at most fifteen choices; find one if present in time O(n^7 poly(input)) by enumerating the seven-element restrictions.

A guaranteed full-width-independent procedure for hosts with a literal Fano or dual Fano restriction and b=1 is therefore to test these at most fifteen quotients and reconstruct the successful reference. More generally allow an inconclusive leaf when no such restriction is available before budget exhaustion. This cost is 15^b poly(input), gives certified yes/no when every needed branch resolves, and never mistakes a nonregular obstruction-free restriction search for a regularity test. An input with a promised literal restriction at every nonregular intermediate node before the budget runs out receives a complete decision. A supplied minor with rank(U)≤t has at most 2^(t+4) candidate directions at a node; finding small-contraction witnesses on arbitrary hosts is separate. For one-sided subcodes apply the dual procedure. All reconstruction retains the original ground set and solves an ordinary kernel at the accepted leaf.

Series extension explains one limit of the pivot search. Replace each element of F7 by a series class of length m, interpreted as copying an original code bit onto m coordinates. The resulting matroid remains distance one from a regular supercode by performing the same copying on a one-dimensional regular quotient of F7. Every nonregular minor must retain a representative from every series class: deleting one element from a class forces its original bit zero, giving a series extension of a deletion of F7 plus coloops, which is regular. Omitting an entire class by contraction gives a minor of a series extension of F7/e, also regular (contract it first). A seven-element nonregular minor therefore uses one representative per class and contracts every other element; these 7(m−1) contracted columns are independent since no nonzero original cycle is supported there. Thus small perturbation distance need not supply a small contraction witness in the original presentation. Series simplification or proper pendant pruning can remove this particular obstacle.
