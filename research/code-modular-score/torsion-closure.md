# Iterating the 2-torsion quotient

For a binary check matrix D and unsigned weights w in a finite abelian group G, let S_b be the set of all scores achieved on Dx=b. A subgroup K is a period if S_b+K=S_b for every b, including infeasible fibers. Start at K=0. In the current quotient Q=G/K take T={i:2w_i=0 in Q}. The sum of weights on T is a binary homomorphism into Q[2]. Compute H, the image of ker D_T under this homomorphism, by binary elimination and subgroup generation. Enlarge K to the inverse image of H under G→Q. For every assignment and every h in H, adding a suitable T-supported kernel vector adds h to its score modulo K, independent of its old bits. Thus every nonempty fiber image modulo K is H-periodic. Its full preimage is the original S_b by the induction hypothesis, proving that the new K is again a period. Repeat until H=0. The growing chain has at most log₂|G_(2)| strict steps as each quotient increment is an elementary 2-group. T increases monotonically. The resulting K_* is the least fixed point of this rule: if K⊆K' then T(K)⊆T(K'), and images of all previously available unsigned kernel-vector sums persist modulo K'. The procedure requires only polynomially many binary elimination and explicit finite-group operations.

In G/K_* run the contraction algorithm from derandomization.md, with T_*={i:2w_i in K_*}. Its exponential term is 2^(rank D−rank D_T*). To lift a returned witness store the successive kernels as well as the generating vectors for H in each round. Going backwards, correct its current difference from the target modulo the previous quotient by the corresponding T-supported kernel shift. Every subsequent stage already fixed is unaffected. The final witness has the exact requested G-score.

For G=Z/(2^a) × Z/3 take disjoint graphic triangles indexed by j=0,…,a−1 with all three weights (2^j,0) on triangle j. Modulo the current K, an order-two triangle contributes its nonzero weight through its all-edge binary cycle. Exactly one new weight layer is admitted per round. Add a disjoint forest of arbitrarily large rank with edge weights (1,0) or any graph block all of whose weights are (1,0). Initial residual check rank is arbitrarily large for a≥2; after all rounds the entire block is admitted to T. Add one final triangle weighted (0,1),(0,0),(0,0). Its two zero edges span its local incidence space and its possible residual scores are exactly 0 and 1 mod 3. The final residual check rank is zero, while the global residual target constraint is nonvacuous.

More generally, fixing a local region U with small rank of D_U, enumerate in G × im D_U all possible pairs (score, check sum) using independent-item subset-sum. For each check value b let S^U_b denote its score set. Every h in the intersection of their stabilizers is a global period for all affine checks, because the local assignment can be replaced preserving its local check. Such periods may have odd order. For example, l identical nonzero check columns all weighted w contribute at parity ε the values (ε+2j)w for 0≤ε+2j≤l. If l≥2 ord(2w)−1 this is the full coset εw+<2w> for either ε, allowing quotient by <2w>. For reconstruction retain witnessing local assignments indexed by local check and desired score.
