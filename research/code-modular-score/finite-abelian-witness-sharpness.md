# Sharpness of the connected-part witness bound

For every finite abelian group Γ and each prime ℓ dividing its order, choose one generator g_{ℓ,i} from each cyclic factor of its ℓ-primary subgroup. Build a star with one leaf of charge g_{ℓ,i} per such factor and center charge minus the sum of all leaves. The singleton partition generates Γ. In any connected partition of a star, a part avoiding the center has only one vertex. By total charge zero, the center-containing part contributes minus the sum of the remaining singleton leaves. Thus the generated subgroup is generated exactly by the leaves left outside the center part. Omitting any one loses the corresponding independent coordinate modulo ℓ, so every leaf must stay singleton. This forces exactly 1+Σ dim_Fℓ Γ/ℓΓ parts. For any desired subgroup U of a larger ambient group the same construction using the charges in U gives the bound for U. For Γ=0 a single vertex attains the one-part bound.
