# Joint odd scores with an auxiliary coprime group

Let A be a finite odd abelian group and T a finite abelian group of order coprime to |A|. Strengthen the all-high theorem of all-odd-packets.md: when every odd Frattini scalar of A has sufficiently large nonzero cycle packing, the joint even-support spectrum for any A×T edge labels contains A×{0}. Thresholds depend on A and T. Induct on |A|, keeping T fixed. For trivial A the empty support suffices. Pigeonhole the full gains (a,b) of many disjoint cycles, with a nonzero on the chosen A scalar; reserve packets of exp(A)exp(T) copies. If a packet deletion is all-high in A/⟨a⟩, apply the strengthened induction there with the unchanged auxiliary group. The residual witness has T score zero. Toggle a number of untouched packet cycles divisible by ord(b) to lift the residual A error through ⟨a⟩, possible because ord(b) is coprime to ord(a). If every deletion is low, the identical quotient-character sunflower argument of all-odd-packets.md gives four disjoint packets separately killing an odd scalar on A. Its global packing bound contradicts the hypothesis, irrespective of T labels. No assertion here that all T target values occur.

For arbitrary targets, suppose T-score feasibility and witnesses with elementary binary side equations are available in deterministic FPT. First obtain an even support at the requested T score and any current binary pin values, or reject. Translate all labels by its physical signs and subtract the full base score. The residual target is (a',0), including zero differences on the pins. For T a 2-group absorb all binary pins into the auxiliary T factor, still coprime. The preceding high theorem now directly verifies this joint target. In the low branch, quiet-recognition.md finds the physical odd scalar cover, and the usual quiet-ports.md transport and recursion on ker χ handle arbitrary translated A and T targets. At a simple R torso subtract the enumerated full child signatures in A×T, check the odd target's projected scalar vanishes, and recurse on ker χ with unchanged T and extra elementary binary pins. The purely auxiliary solve at each call uses its own root target and does not call the joint solver. Strict decrease of |A| and additive torso sizes keep one uniform polynomial exponent. Outer child hashing adds elementary binary coordinates and is used once. One outer decision self-reduction gives a witness. For 2-primary T of exponent at most four the auxiliary oracle is the exact affine-code Gauss-sum procedure in fourier-four-eight.md. Thus arbitrary graphical odd scores plus order-four and binary coordinates have joint deterministic FPT feasibility and witnesses. No mixed-group counting algorithm follows.
