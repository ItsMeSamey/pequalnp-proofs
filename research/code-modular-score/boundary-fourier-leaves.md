## Fourier messages for leaves solved by affine conditioning

Parameterize the explicit input by d binary coordinates after enforcing its affine equations. On a supplied binary tree, group affine constraints into leaves of possibly unbounded internal row rank. For a leaf let U be its row span, B its intersection with the exterior row span, b=dim B. Suppose a known space W≤U of dimension t has the property that fixing each of its 2^t evaluations either rejects or leaves one efficiently constructible affine solution flat in U*. Rank-one residual clauses provide this condition by Gaussian elimination. The projective-layer recognizer constructs W on its stated fixed-decomposition class. Put a=dim(W∩B), q=b−a.

Choose a basis of B beginning with a basis of W∩B, extend the latter to a basis of W, and write each boundary evaluation as (u,y) with u of length a and y of length q. An evaluation α of W fixes u. For a nonempty solution flat Lα, Gaussian elimination projects its boundary evaluations onto one affine set c+D in the y-coordinates. Put s=dim D. Every such y has the same multiplicity |Lα|/2^s. Maintain a primal and an unnormalized Fourier accumulator on the q bits for each bucket u. If s≤q−s, add the multiplicity to the primal accumulator on the points of c+D. Otherwise enumerate the 2^(q−s) frequencies v of D⊥ and add (−1)^(v·c)|Lα| to the Fourier accumulator. This follows by summing the sign over the coset; it vanishes off D⊥. Empty fibers add zero. Each leaf assignment belongs to exactly one α.

Apply a Walsh transform to each primal bucket, add its Fourier accumulator, then transform in u for every v. The result is the signed sum over the leaf's models in U* at each boundary frequency. Dividing by 2^dim U gives the normalized Fourier leaf coefficient on the original seeds, since restriction to U* is surjective with equal fiber size. The complete message costs poly(input)·[2^(t+floor((b−a)/2))+2^b] time and poly(input)·2^b space. All intermediate integers have polynomial bit length. The existing three-space fork algorithm then costs poly(input)·2^(3w/2), with w the maximum boundary dimension of the supplied tree, plus the sum of leaf construction costs. Multiplying the normalized root coefficient by 2^d returns the exact count. This treats Boolean leaf indicator products. A finite comparison with seed 458109 checked all coefficients against direct counting on 450 cases, exercising 2734 primal and 839 dual accumulations.

## Witness recovery from boundary Fourier tables

For the new affine-fiber macro-leaf theorem (source SHA 99463c5e), each node of row span U stores normalized Fourier coefficients on its boundary B. For a valuation β on B, the sum over boundary frequencies v of the node coefficient times (−1)^(v·β), multiplied by 2^(dim U−dim B), equals its number of satisfying U-valuations restricting to β. This follows from Fourier orthogonality. Obtain all these positive count tables by one Walsh transform per node.

At a fork let A,C be the child row spans and E exterior. Set L=A∩(C+E), R=C∩(A+E), P=(A+C)∩E, K=A∩C. Then L∩R=K and P⊆L+R: if p=x+y lies in P with x∈A,y∈C, then x=p+y∈L and y=p+x∈R. For any given parent valuation β on P, valuations on L+R extending β index exactly the compatible child boundary pairs. Their number is 2^(dim L+dim R−dim K−dim P), at most 2^w. Indeed each x∈L is the A projection of a pair (x,y) with x+y∈P; the space of these pairs has dimension dim P+dim K. Thus dim L≤dim P+dim K and the exponent is at most dim R. Use Gaussian elimination to enumerate those valuations, choosing any whose two child counts are positive. Every chosen pair of child models agrees on K, hence glues uniquely to an A+C valuation inducing β. Recurse. At a leaf search the at most 2^t conditioning fibers with the required boundary equations imposed. Extend the resulting root valuation to the original seed space using the original affine parametrization. From a positive root count this recovers a witness within the same stated time and polynomial times 2^w space, retaining the tables across tree nodes. No new restrictions need be added to the instance tree.
