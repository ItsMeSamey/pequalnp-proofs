# A fully charged square wall outside the two simple alternatives

For odd L≥21 take an n by n square grid, n=8L+4. Give every vertex the fifth bit e5. At every second vertex of the top row, starting with its left corner, add four-bit labels 9,2, then L occurrences each of 1,4,6,8. There is one pure-e5 spacer after each marker. Keep the left corner as the single exterior port. All five demands are even and independent. Every nonzero combination of the first four bits has support ≥L as the four repeated types form a basis. Every combination using the fifth bit is nonzero at every off-top vertex. No vertex is uncharged.

The four-bit projection has 31 planes and misses {0,7,11,12}, exactly as in port-four-dense-disk.md. Noncrossing three-block assignments along the top markers route using nested horizontal arcs, and other vertices may attach freely when only the projection is observed. Thus no full five-bit subspace reservoir is present. The 160 previously defined tests for the projected four-bit target all pass.

Deleting at most b<n vertices leaves every clean row and column in a common component. Each remaining vertex outside it belongs to a dirty row and a dirty column, so at most b² vertices lie outside it. Every order-b separation therefore has one strict side of size ≤b². Any strictly advancing nested chain of vertex shores with boundary ≤b has length at most 2(b²+b+1), allowing separator vertices in the nested sets. This rules out unbounded strict chains of the bounded interface type in nested-physical-charge-pump.md, while permitting disjoint local reductions.

The whole five-bit table nevertheless has a bounded physical carrier. Normalize a row-rank j≤5 witness to k=j+1 parts. If k>1 their sole dependence is the total zero. A part containing no projected marker necessarily has sum e5. At most one such part occurs: a duplicate would give a proper dependence unless k=2, when at least one part still contains markers. The marker-containing parts induce a noncrossing partition on the top markers. For k=1 use the entire grid.

Conversely route any noncrossing marker partition on ≤6 colors, connecting successive occurrences by vertical segments and horizontal arcs at depths ordered by strict interval nesting. Intervals of the same color never nest, so at most six rows of depth suffice. Optionally reserve one deep bottom corner as a singleton pure-e5 part. In the linear order of markers, adjacent unequal color pairs form a connected transition graph on the marker colors. Select transitions making a spanning tree and temporarily remove their intervening pure-e5 top spacers. The remaining grid, without those spacers and the optional corner, is connected and contains all the marker trees. Extend those trees to a spanning partition. Place each reserved spacer into either adjoining color using its horizontal edge. These tree-edge switches realize every fifth-bit assignment on marker colors with the required fixed total XOR. A color carrying markers is allowed to have projected sum zero. All produced subspaces are actually attainable; for the reverse direction the normalized original witness supplies at most six parts in total.

In a noncrossing cyclic word on ≤6 colors there are at most ten transitions. Within each odd repeated group there are at most eleven positive monochromatic segments. Shorten each to one or two copies according to parity, giving an odd total ≤21, then pad one segment by pairs to 21. Conversely pad to any odd L≥21. This preserves block sums, colors and noncrossing order. By the fifth-bit switches and optional singleton rule, the entire table stabilizes at L=21.

In each long group merge its first 2(L−21)+1 columns into one and keep its other columns as singletons. This removes an even number L−21 of extra identical projected labels and preserves spacers. Reduce the number of rows to 172 by putting all surplus into the last row interval. All row and column intervals have odd length. Their Cartesian rectangles are disjoint connected branch sets; contract each and simplify extra edges. Every cell retains its fifth bit and the top labels and corner port are precisely the 172 by 172 L=21 model. This is a physical minor preserving the entire augmented table under gluing to any exterior at the corner. No universal size bound for arbitrarily labelled walls follows.
