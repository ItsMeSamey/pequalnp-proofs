# A physical catalogue representative from bounded tree-independence

Let G be a nonempty connected graph whose vertices carry charges in F₂ᵖ. The catalogue consists of the subspaces W generated by the part sums of a partition into nonempty connected parts. A conservative spanning minor partitions all vertices into connected branches, sums their charges and may delete surplus edges. Specify d protected vertices which must remain in distinct, identifiable branches; they need not keep their original numerical charge. Suppose G admits a tree decomposition in which the subgraph of G induced by every bag has independence number at most α. This is the [tree-independence parameter of Dallard, Milanič and Štorgel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04543). Bag edges in this condition are physical edges of G.

For each attained W, take one global witnessing partition with k_W≤1+dim W parts. To obtain this from any witness, merge adjacent parts whenever the dimension of the span of their sums stays unchanged (a merger can only decrease that span). At a stall the coordinate difference of each adjacent pair belongs to the row space of the part-charge matrix: its equal-coordinate hyperplane otherwise still maps onto the original span. Differences along a spanning tree of the touching-parts quotient are independent over F₂, so k_W−1≤dim W. For charge rank at most three an explicit list and witnesses can be computed on G by the primal/dual binary three-elements reduction in rank-three-three-minor-list.md, as detailed for all ranks and total charges in separator-component-pruning.md (SHA-256 6516a3de4e0bf81cb159b3fbe244a3232becab93f3fb099e59a08f6de54c5a00). The matrix algorithm treats arbitrary binary matroids ([Geelen, Gerards and Whittle, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20960v1#S2)). For larger rank the construction below requires a supplied complete list with witnesses; it does not infer completeness from a list of positive certificates.

Give every vertex the tuple of the names of its parts in these witnesses. There are at most K=∏_W k_W possible signatures. For rank at most three one may use K≤2⁷·3⁷·4 by counting the subspaces by dimension. Set the protected vertices aside as singleton branches. In the remaining graph take as branches the connected components of the subgraphs induced by individual signatures. Contract each branch physically and retain every edge between distinct branches in the quotient Q, suppressing duplicates. This can be done by traversals and spanning trees. Every branch is contained in a single part of each witness. Thus the images of the old parts are disjoint connected sets covering Q with their original part sums. Every listed W remains attainable. Conversely, every connected partition of Q lifts through the connected branches to a partition of G with exactly its part sums. The two catalogues coincide.

Given any tree decomposition of the stipulated independence bound, replace each bag X by the set of branch images it meets. Every quotient edge is covered by the image of a bag containing the ends of a witnessing physical edge. The bags meeting a connected branch form a connected subtree, since the occurrence subtrees of the ends of each of its internal edges intersect. These image bags therefore give a tree decomposition of Q. For one signature, distinct ordinary branch images meeting X have pairwise nonadjacent representatives in X: an actual edge between same-signature ordinary representatives would unite their branches. There are at most α of these images per signature, plus at most d protected singleton images. Consequently

    tw(Q) ≤ αK+d−1.

Neither the original decomposition nor the value of α is needed to construct Q. With a supplied decomposition its image gives bags directly. Otherwise run a constructive fixed-parameter treewidth algorithm on Q, increasing the tested integer width until it returns a decomposition ([Bodlaender, SIAM J. Comput. 1996](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539793251219)). The width guarantee is conditional on the host promise; this procedure does not recognize the host's tree-independence number. The promise bounds the running time by a computable function of α,p,d times a polynomial in the input size, including witness-list size when supplied.

Finally apply the empty-template case of bounded-treewidth-additive-catalogue.md (SHA-256 be0ff664287ff5acb5025674a911e554d22f5db696d30885c42b4e6e103b8350) to Q with the full fixed catalogue tests and connectedness. The queries are CMSO1 sentences by enumerating bounded-length lists of generating part sums and checking connectedness and parity counts. Carry the d private one-hot flags for the protected vertices in the conservative minor stage, leaving them outside the score queries. Composing physical branch models yields a connected conservative spanning minor of G of computably bounded order in α,p,d, identical catalogue and distinct protected branches. One never asks an enlarged-charge witness oracle. The conclusion bounds a representative for these catalogue queries, not a well-quasi-order or the preservation of all CMSO1 sentences under the initial contractions.
