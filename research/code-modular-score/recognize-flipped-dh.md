# Recognizing the finite-template distance-hereditary presentation

Fix a finite symmetric binary type template F of binary matrix rank s. Given just a connected finite graph G, one can decide in polynomial time at fixed F whether some assignment of its vertices to the template types makes H=G XOR F connected and distance-hereditary. If a witness exists, use a width-one tree for H. At any cut the template matrix is a product of left one-hot type incidence, F and transposed right incidence, hence has rank at most s. The rank-width of G is therefore at most 1+s. Use Hliněný and Oum's fixed-bound rank-decomposition algorithm (https://doi.org/10.1137/070685920) to obtain a bounded-width representation or reject. On a supplied width-two decomposition this preliminary search can be omitted.

Convert the rank decomposition to a bounded clique-width expression, or equivalently use its finite labelled graph algebra (Oum and Seymour, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2005.10.006). Existentially quantify the finite unary colour partition (empty colours allowed) and use the quantifier-free signed interpretation for H. Require connectedness and demand that every induced connected subgraph with at least two vertices contain a pendant vertex or a pair with identical neighborhoods outside the pair. This is an MSO1 formula: quantify the induced vertex set, use bipartitions to express connectedness, and compare adjacency within that set. The characterization follows from the pendant/twin construction of Bandelt and Mulder (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0095895686900432): necessity follows by heredity on connected induced subgraphs; for sufficiency delete the pendant or one twin, preserving connectedness, and induct. Two-vertex and singleton cases obey the same rule. The MSO expression on a bounded-width term has a finite bottom-up tree automaton; projection guesses the colours at vertex creation and traceback recovers them. Form H explicitly and obtain the ordinary split tree using the constructive split decomposition of Gioan and Paul (https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.1823).

This recognizes the stated presentation class even when its type assignment and all graph decompositions were initially hidden. It does not assert that every rank-width-two graph belongs to that class. If only a fixed number of colours is specified, enumerate all finitely many symmetric templates of that order, retaining the same fixed-parameter polynomial-time conclusion.
