# Mixing compact port reductions

For each proper regular pendant branch construct both its primal K4-or-unary reduction and the dual reduction on the exchanged dual summand, as in synchronized-port-repair.md. Either gives a fixed minor operation on that branch's physical (nonvirtual) coordinates. Choices can be made independently on disjoint branches. Each compact output has at most three coordinates per port. Each candidate compact relation is invariant under the input virtual 111 gauge: the primal reduction can use a chosen raw triangle representative and includes any template gauge; the dual reduction has this property on input central cycles by orthogonal projection of the corresponding local relation. Equivalently use the physical branch's compact minor directly: for each of the eight raw virtual patterns, its possible physical outputs are the cycle traces of that minor, computable by binary elimination. This avoids conventions for labeling star spokes or parallel representatives.

The orientation choice need not be enumerated exponentially in the number of ports. Use the central triangle-bundled branch parse tree of width O(w), retaining the three original element positions per port, viewed also as slots for the possible padded compact coordinates. At each port annotate the fixed pair of constant-sized binary relations, the possible dummy-coloop positions, and whether the compact representation needs a cycle constraint, a cocycle constraint or no repair. Choose one monadic bit at the designated representative of each port. Membership of a physical set X in the resulting compact code C* is then a monadic predicate: existentially choose an original central cycle Y, agree on real outside elements, and check the locally selected finite table on each complete port for (Y,X). Padded unused coordinates are zero. The available bounded-width automata for marked triples are supplied by synchronized-port-repair.md.

Quantify a prospective reference S via the parity restrictions and added generators of near-regular-recognition.md, testing regularity via the fixed Fano minor formulas. Require zero on dummy physical positions, harmless since any actual reference on the unpadded compact minor can be padded with coloops. To bound triangle discrepancy rank d_T select at most d_T named cycle constraints whose indicators generate all cycle-constraint indicators modulo S; each is expressible by a constant-size disjunction in these parameter-many chosen indicators, testing membership in S. Dually test triad discrepancy rank d_Y using S-orthogonality defined by even intersections. Enumerate budget upper bounds a,b,d_T,d_Y to optimize the certified total a+b+d_T+d_Y, using stored global orientation choices and witnesses. Reconstruct the regular reference via the selected local reductions and simultaneous repair. This computes the best such upper-bound certificate, including the prior global orientation guarantees; it need not find the actual optimum. The recognition cost is fixed-parameter in the tested budgets and central width; the number of port relation types is constant since each maps three input bits to at most three output bits.

## Connected mixed-direction stress test

Let N_n be the quotient of the K_n incidence column matroid by the six-terminal vector h for three vertex-disjoint edges, as in synchronized-port-repair.md, n≥9. Its minimum regular subcode deficit is 1 and its minimum regular supercode excess is 2. Its dual has these numbers switched. Form the proper 2-sum of N_n and N_n dual at an edge e=vw between two nonterminal vertices. These constituents are connected: connectivity at cuts of a subset of size at least two on each side decreases by at most one under the rank-one quotient of the 3-connected K_n. Each singleton remains a nonloop and noncoloop. Thus the 2-sum is connected.

The result has a physical minor N_(n−1) direct sum with N_(n−1) dual. On the N_n side delete all other edges at v. The old vertex-star cocycle, which avoids the six-terminal syndrome, forces the virtual bit e=0. The first physical remainder is exactly N_(n−1), the second is N_n dual with e deleted. The latter is dual to the quotient matroid N_n/e, whose simplification is N_(n−1) by merging v,w and removing duplicates. Take the dual of this simplification minor on the second side.

On direct sums, minimum one-sided regular distances add: for a regular subcode project to each side, noting its dimension is no greater than the sum of projected dimensions. For a regular supercode shorten to each side; those two shortenings are disjoint subspaces of the supercode and contain the respective input constituents. Both projections and shortenings preserve regularity. Because distances cannot increase under the common minor, in the connected example each one-sided optimum is at least three. Conversely join the appropriate individually optimal one-sided references at e; equality gluing cannot increase either directional sum. This gives three in both directions. The closest mixed references have total distance two: one can glue the two closest regular references, using opposite directions, and no total-one solution can be anything but a one-sided solution. The example separates everywhere-monotone reference choices; it does not show that the synchronized 3/2 approximation is tight.

## Matrix-rank metric and primary-source scope

On a fixed ordered ground set, permit equivalent binary representations (row operations and zero-row padding). The minimum rank of the difference of parity-check matrices representing two codes equals max(a,b) for their two directional subspace distances. Indeed the difference rows span the first row space modulo the other, and vice versa, so both relative row codimensions are lower bounds. Conversely choose aligned bases of the intersection and pair the remaining basis rows, padding by zero; only max(a,b) rows can differ. Orthogonal complementation swaps the directional codimensions, retaining this maximum. Thus the subspace-distance sum and the rank-perturbation metric are distinct objectives. A factor 3/2 for the sum alone guarantees at most factor 3 for matrix rank, with no optimality implication.

[Heo's thesis](https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/items/b86f4198-90ba-4d72-bb51-ca51fb162962) and [Guenin–Heo](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-023-01944-6) concern recognition of even-cycle and even-cut binary matroids, important one-dimensional graphic and cographic specializations. [Covering Vectors by Spaces in Perturbed Graphic Matroids and Their Duals](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06957) formulates its parameterized Space Cover algorithm on an input containing the graph and perturbation (Section 2). Such an algorithm does not by itself supply a nearest regular reference from an arbitrary represented binary matroid.

Direct GF₂ basis computations on the connected K_n example at n=9,10,11 produced directional distances (1,1), (3,0), (0,3) for the three advertised repaired candidates. The direct-sum-minor lower bounds certify optimality only for these example objectives.

For a certified two-sided lower bound take the MAXIMUM across orientations of the optimum compact distance. Every physical regular reference yields a regular reference at no greater distance on each compact minor, so L=max_O min_S d(C*_O,S)≤OPT. Testing L≤k uses the alternating bounded-width formula “for every valid orientation there exists a regular compact reference of total distance at most k”. The repaired upper uses existential quantifiers and a MINIMUM over orientations. Their equality certifies the true optimum and its witness. Coordinatewise, intersect the necessary feasible budget upsets across all orientations and union the sufficient repaired budget upsets. This is an instance-specific certificate, without a better uniform factor.

For an additional exhaustive small-code check, enumerate the 60 labeled F7 and F7 dual cycle codes on seven elements by permuting the seven nonzero binary rank-three columns. By excluded minors this is the complete list of nonregular binary codes on that ground set. For a fixed F7 cycle code and one actual named triangle, enumeration of all codimension-one subcodes and all one-dimensional extensions gives 21 nearest regular references of distance one. Fourteen already contain the triangle. The remaining seven all produce direction (0,2) under the literal column-span upward repair. Thus an optimum-preserving candidate exists even though a blindly repaired nearest candidate can cost two.

There is a tighter upper bound than summing preliminary distances and discrepancy ranks. Search directly for a regular compact S' meeting every constraint required by the chosen orientation, optimizing its actual distance. Restoration is isometric on this compatible domain: it is nonexpansive, and the original specified common physical minor is a nonexpansive left inverse. In a star template the full virtual preimage followed by the pair-XOR map returns each even physical triple; in its dual the only possible enlargement is the 111 gauge, already present. Unary factorizations have the same inverse minor by construction, and other ports are untouched. Thus the resulting upper bound U=min_O min_(S' compatible,regular) d(C*_O,S') is exactly the best physical distance among references obtainable through all such mutually local regular restorations. It is at most the synchronized-repair cost and is definable with the same local tables and bounded-width candidate search. Comparison with L proves unrestricted optimality whenever they coincide. There is no claim that all regular physical references factor through one of the choices.
