# Subgroup catalogues for finite abelian vertex charges

For a connected graph with charges in a fixed finite abelian group Γ, consider all subgroups U≤Γ generated by the block sums of a connected partition. The bounded-rank-width physical full-catalogue theorem extends to this catalogue and, equivalently, to its annihilators in the character group. The subgroup here is generated by the block sums; this is a different query from merely realizing a specific element as one cut value.

Let M be the exponent of Γ. For a witnessing partition into k parts, send D=(Z/M)^k onto U by the coefficient map, and write K for its kernel. Merging adjacent vertices of the partition quotient restricts D to the kernel of the coordinate difference δ_ij. This preserves the image precisely when D=ker δ_ij+K, equivalently δ_ij(K)=Z/M. A proper subgroup of Z/M is contained in ℓ(Z/M) for some prime ℓ|M. If no edge can merge, choose a spanning tree of the connected quotient and give each tree edge one prime ℓ forcing δ_ij(K)=0 modulo ℓ. For any fixed ℓ these edges form a forest; its coordinate differences over F_ℓ are linearly independent by removing leaves. They annihilate (K+ℓD)/ℓD in the k-dimensional coefficient space D/ℓD. Its codimension is dim_Fℓ D/(K+ℓD)=dim_Fℓ U/ℓU. Summing over primes gives k≤1+Σ_{ℓ|M}dim_Fℓ U/ℓU. For trivial Γ the whole graph is a one-part witness. Repeated preserving mergers produce the bound from any partition.
