# Two odd primary factors and a quiet edge modulator

Let edges have scores in P × Q. Assume P is a finite abelian group of odd order and Q is a cyclic odd-prime-power group, optionally with elementary binary coordinates handled by the pure Q-plus-binary oracle. A supplied set F of k physical edges has the property that every even support of H=G−F has zero P-score. This promise can be tested in polynomial time by the fundamental-cycle/pair score lemma. Then joint even-support image membership and witness recovery take deterministic f(|P|,|Q|,k)poly(input) time. There is no size or treewidth bound on H and the P labels may be nonzero on arbitrarily many edges of H.

Enumerate the F bits. Their incidence syndrome on H specifies at most 2k terminal vertices T. If it is feasible, pair these terminals within connected components and choose at most k simple paths whose XOR x₀ has the required parity. Translate the two scores by the signs given by x₀, subtract its baseline score and that of the fixed F edges. The remaining problem is an even support z on H. It splits additively over the original graph blocks. For each block, the odd-degree vertices of the restriction of x₀ number at most 2k, since each of the k simple paths visits a block in a single possibly empty segment. Denote those vertices by T_B.

Use the ordinary SPQR decomposition of a biconnected graph block as a tree of polygon, parallel-edge and 3-connected graphic torsos with two-ended virtual edges (loops, bridges and trivial blocks are handled directly). Adjacent pieces glue by the binary equality of their unary virtual-edge bits. The pertinent graph of any child edge meets the rest only at the two ends of its virtual edge, and different child interiors of a bag are vertex-disjoint. The local tables on the translated instance store all pairs of full scores and the parent bit; all arithmetic uses physical scores only once.

First consider the original untranslated P scores on H. At a proper unary port each side realizes both traces. Fix a complementary state of each trace p. Every physical child state of that trace glues with the same chosen complement to a physical even support of H, of score zero. Hence its P score is uniquely determined by p, equal to zero for p=0 and to some w_S for p=1. For each torso the linear sum of its real P edge weights and virtual child w_S weights equals the parent default w_parent times its port bit, for every even torso cycle; subtract the parent contribution. At a root the right hand side is zero. In a 3-connected torso this forces each individual coefficient, hence each w_S, to vanish. To check this assertion choose cycle-space basis variables. Every edge bit is a nonzero linear form and no two coincide since the torso has no bridges or two-edge cuts. For a particular form l, take the signed sum over all basis vectors of the claimed zero identity with Walsh character (−1)^l. The l coefficient contributes −2^{d−1} times that coefficient, while every other coefficient contributes zero. Multiplication by two is invertible in P. Thus each coefficient is zero.

In a 3-connected torso, any child with no T_B in its strict interior has x₀ restricted to its physical edges even at every interior vertex. Its two boundary parities are equal by the handshake identity. That restriction is therefore itself a child state at its actual untranslated port trace. Adding any residual child state by XOR produces another actual untranslated state, possibly at a different port trace. Both have P-score zero by the preceding conclusion at the parent. Hence even after translation, *every* child state has P-score zero. The original real edges of the central torso also have zero P weights. At most |T_B| children have an internal terminal, including across nested decomposition levels because the bound is per bag.

Enumerate the trace and full score of each of these at most 2k exceptional children from its table. Subtract those contributions and enforce their virtual bits by binary pins. The remaining children have arbitrary Q tables on one trace bit and no P effect. Their optional Q deviations from an attainable default require at most |Q|−1 different children, by the prefix-sum deletion argument. The colored small-bias occurrence construction of child-hashing.md enforces them without guessing child indices, adding only elementary binary coordinates since each trace indicator is unary. Apply the unrestricted graphic odd-prime-power-plus-binary algorithm from prime-graphic-fpt.md to the torso, pinning the parent trace as well. A polygon torso has just two even states and can convolve all its child tables for each. A parallel-edge torso performs a direct subset-sum table with one binary parity bit enforcing its even constraint. Such elementary bags allow arbitrarily many nontrivial P children. Combine blocks by score convolution; traceback through local table predecessors and undo the translation and F choices. All graph-polynomial exponents are independent of group and port parameters. The theorem assumes that the edge modulator is provided; it does not supply an FPT algorithm to find one of minimum size.

## Why changing the base can exchange the primary constraints

For a concrete Z/15 example use coordinates F₃ × F₅ and vertices v₀,…,v_n. Between consecutive vertices put two parallel edges of weights a=(1/2,−1/2) and b=(1/2,1/2); add a return edge v_n v₀ of weight −na. For n≥4, if the return edge is absent an even set takes both or neither in each pair, giving exactly F₃ × {0}. If the return edge is present the set takes exactly one in each pair, giving exactly {0} × F₅. The overall image is the union of these axes and misses (1,1). At base zero the second-coordinate nonzero cycles all use the return edge, whereas the first coordinate has arbitrarily many vertex-disjoint nonzero pair cycles. Translating by the cycle consisting of all a edges and the return edge reverses the roles: b−a=(0,1), and the translated first coordinate is zero on every support omitting the return. Fixing the return edge bit resolves the example; imposing a primary cover without controlling its boundary bit does not make it survive translation.
