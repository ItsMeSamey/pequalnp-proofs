# Conservative charge WQO at fixed elimination distance to forests

For h=0 let the connected base class be trees. At level h+1 permit any connected graph for which deleting one vertex gives components each in the level-h class; admit lower levels and the one-vertex graph. Fix any finite abelian vertex-charge group and any finite set of additional vertex colors. In a conservative spanning minor, each target vertex is a connected branch whose total charge must match; also require at least one representative vertex in that branch with the target's additional color. For each fixed h this is a WQO on connected simple graphs in the class. Distinguished vertex names are encoded by additional one-hot charge bits.

At level zero use rooted trees with finite labels comprising the descendant charge sum and the extra ordinary color. The rooted Kruskal and matched-subtree-cut proof in xor-spanning-minors.md gives the result, with the matched tree node in each physical branch witnessing its color. For the inductive step choose one deletion vertex v for each graph, and pass to an infinite subsequence with the same color and charge at v and the same sum of charges of all other vertices. Give each vertex of a component of G−v the additional finite bit for its actual adjacency to v. Classify each component by its total charge as a finite extra attribute. By the inductive theorem these decorated components are WQO under conservative spanning minors requiring a matching representative color at every target vertex. List components in any order; Higman's lemma gives an earlier graph with its entire component sequence matched into a later one. Keep the deletion vertex as its own representative. Every target edge to it lifts by the adjacency bit at its matched representative. Every surplus component touches v by connectedness, and all surplus components have aggregate charge zero because the total of all components and the individual matched totals agree. Contract those components into v and discard surplus edges to get the target, preserving every source vertex's charge contribution. This proves the induction. The usual finite-subsequence argument handles sequences attaining a lower level or the trivial singleton graph.

This strictly extends the bounded-feedback-number application: one hub adjacent to arbitrarily many otherwise disjoint triangles gives elimination distance at most two and unbounded feedback number. For fixed h the class has treewidth at most h+1 by adding each chosen root to all bags of the separate child decompositions. Membership at fixed h is definable by a recursive MSO formula on vertex sets for connected induced subgraphs, quantifying a deletion vertex and checking each connected component left behind, with acyclicity at level zero. Therefore the finite-tree-automaton emptiness construction in physical-kernel-cores.md provides a computable exact-named-catalogue minor size bound, and physical branch sets can be recovered, on this whole connected class.
