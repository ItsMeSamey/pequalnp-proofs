# A physical reduction for annular disk patches with dense cofacial demands

Consider the connected annular disk graphs of port-four-disk-mask.md, or rectangular concentric-cycle grids with one radial column for each outer boundary vertex and with arbitrary additional planar edges inside the disk. All local vertex labels and all b attachment vertices lie on the outer cycle. The exterior is unrestricted. There are h=q+b−1 augmented label bits as in planar-physical-patch.md. Require at least h+1 routing rings inside and a boundary of length at least three. The reduction below gives a physically realized equivalent patch with size bounded in q and b, even for arbitrarily dense outer labels.

An attained augmented row image of dimension j is realized by a forest with j+1 components. Start from any witnessing forest. Its component-label sums have rank j. Whenever there are more than j+1 components, there is an edge merging two components while retaining this rank: algebraically extend the ordinary support inside the quotient by j independent virtual completion vectors chosen from the augmented named span, as in port-four-common-basis.md. Equivalently, if current incidence span has rank less than r−j, choose an ordinary column outside its sum with the full named span. The extra column preserves exactly the original kernel. This argument permits j=0 and dependent names. If j>0 all j+1 sums are nonzero since their sum is zero and they have rank j. Thus all components meet the boundary. For j=0 a single spanning component suffices.

Every such partition restricted to the boundary is noncrossing by planarity. Conversely the graph realizes all noncrossing partitions into at most h+1 components. Order the boundary vertices from a fixed start and join successive occurrences of each color by intervals, without wrapping. Distinct-color intervals are disjoint or strictly nested. Nested intervals have different colors, since no two same-color intervals strictly nest. Assign outer intervals greater depth than inner ones; at most h+1 interior rings suffice. Connect interval endpoints radially, then along the assigned ring in the boundary order. Different parts are vertex-disjoint. Extend these connected pieces to a spanning forest. The complete augmented table is therefore determined by the cyclic label sequence via noncrossing colorings on at most h+1 colors.

This sequence has a computable finite summary. Fix a linear order starting at port s0. A nondeterministic finite-state automaton colors each incoming vertex with one of h+1 colors, XORs its h-bit label into the corresponding accumulator, and records used colors. For every pair of distinct colors track the longest prefix (length truncated at four) of the subsequence pattern a,b,a,b seen so far, tracking both orders. Discard a run when it reaches four. At the end each surviving coloring gives the span of the color accumulators, including zero sums for unused colors. Absence of either alternating subsequence is exactly cyclic noncrossing. Conversely these computed spans are attainable, whether or not they have the maximum rank compatible with their color count. The deterministic subset construction taking all simultaneous nondeterministic states has a finite computable size M(h). Adjoin the prefix XOR to this deterministic state. Equality after two prefixes allows removal of the intervening segment with no effect on the final table, and that segment has total XOR zero.

Protect each of the b actual ports and at least three boundary positions total, declaring extra positions protected if necessary. Between successive protected positions, delete a segment whenever two prefix summaries in that gap agree. Restart or update after each removal. At most O((b+3)M(h)) unprotected positions survive, taking M to include the XOR register. Cyclic ordering starts at a protected position, so every removed interval can be contracted along the boundary to its surviving predecessor. Each step sums its zero aggregate into that predecessor and never merges ports.

For a physical minor, retain the boundary cycle, radial columns from the final surviving positions, and the first h+1 routing rings. On a doubled first ring use the radial edge to angular position 2i for surviving outer index i. Give each remaining unlabelled vertex a path to that skeleton by extending a forest rooted at skeleton vertices; contract the forest, then discard the extraneous edges. On each routing ring contract the angular intervals between retained columns to single edges. Contract the removed boundary intervals as above (or contract each maximal gap with zero aggregate at the end). Each ring still has at least three surviving positions. The resulting annular grid realizes exactly the same noncrossing score table, as proved above, and has O((h+1)(b+3)M(h)) vertices and edges. Internal contracted vertices are unlabelled; attachments and port charges remain intact. Any connected exterior, including a nonplanar one, gives the same exact global kernels, by the signed transfer formula in planar-physical-patch.md.

This establishes safe physical reduction even on the dense 31-plane controls. Labels off the outer cycle or unboundedly many distinct attachment ports are outside the statement.
