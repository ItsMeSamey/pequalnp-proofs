# Prefix matrices at a nonzero residual intersection

Let P(i,j) be the total charge in the rectangle from the upper left corner through (i,j), padding a zero row and column at index zero. The vertex charge is the XOR of the four corner entries of P around that vertex. Contracting consecutive full rows and columns with every charge conserved deletes intermediate prefix indices, so precisely takes an ordered submatrix of P keeping both ends of each axis. For port-preserving minor constructions tag the before/after cuts of every port row and column, as in physical-grid-matrix-rank.md.

**A finite common-word class.** Suppose the row and column indices of P admit a single interleaving respecting their internal orders. Each index carries a letter from a fixed finite alphabet. A fixed decoder returns P(i,j) from the row letter, column letter and the order of those two indices in the interleaving. The same physical greedy minor algorithm yields size bounded in the alphabet size, h,b. Indeed the row/column flag, port tags and endpoint tags can be treated as extra letters. Higman's lemma on the single word gives conservative rectangular comparability by its induced ordered submatrix; taking a subsequence preserves the decoder. There are only finitely many decoders on a bounded alphabet. A promised representation suffices for the size argument and the polynomial greedy procedure itself need not know it. This proof gives no effective numerical bound in the parameters.

For a recognizable example, the interior of a binary prefix matrix can be a staircase: in each row its ones occupy an initial segment of column indices, and these endpoints are nondecreasing in the row order. Insert each row item after precisely its last one-column, with tie order following the rows. The order test alone supplies its bit. Exceptional border types can be given separate letters when their prescribed outputs depend only on the other index letter. This covers lower triangular all-ones interior matrices, whose algebraic ranks grow without bound, with zero borders. Bounded-rank extra coordinates may be added by incorporating their finite row/column prefix features into the letters. In particular this positive class can contain grids with proper one-dimensional residual intersections and unbounded bit matrix rank.

**Counterexample to an unrestricted rectangular well-quasi-order.** For odd k≥5 make the (k+2) by (k+2) prefix matrix zero on its entire border; its k by k interior is the bipartite adjacency matrix of the chordless cycle C_(2k), in any fixed cyclic index order. The physical (k+1) by (k+1) scalar charge grid is its mixed difference and has even total. If a larger such grid conservatively coarsened by consecutive rows and columns to a smaller one, deleting interior prefix indices would produce a proper induced subgraph of the larger cycle that was itself a cycle. This is impossible. Thus even one-dimensional residual charge values do not imply the required ordered-submatrix well-quasi-order. This antichain says nothing about minimality for a particular kernel table; in fact each scalar example has a two by two equivalent minor from any interior nonzero prefix entry and the zero borders.

Optionally add a second bit at two opposite corners, and a third unit bit at every vertex, on these even side lengths. All vertex charges are nonzero and all three totals are zero. The second bit is invisible to the interior scan of either orientation, so neither scan can attain all three independent directions. Independence holds for k sufficiently large: the first bit is nonzero away from the corners and does not occupy the whole interior, by the cycle construction; the second is confined to the corners and the third is everywhere. Projection to the first bit preserves the conservative rectangular antichain. The residual span in this fully charged variant has dimension at most two; the scalar and two-coordinate variants give residual dimension at most one.
