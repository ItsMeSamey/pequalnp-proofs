# Simultaneous physical contractions on long faces of a charged patch

Use the augmented local table of planar-physical-patch.md, with dimension h=q+b−1 and N attainable exact kernels. The patch is connected and plane. Interior charges may be arbitrary. Compute one witnessing forest for each kernel by the planar routing solver, normalizing it to j+1 forest components when its augmented row image has dimension j. For this normalization, if the current ordinary witness span has rank <r−j, its sum with the full named span has rank <r. Add an ordinary column outside that sum without changing the named intersection, and repeat. Include all isolated vertices in component counts.

For each original vertex record its component in each of the N forests. Contract a spanning forest inside each connected region of vertices sharing the same entire record. These contractions preserve every augmented kernel simultaneously: within each witness, every contraction joins vertices of the same connected part, and sums their labels by XOR. The resulting connected parts give exactly the same row image. Conversely every choice on the minor lifts with the contracted edges included. No two ports merge by the pure-transfer test of planar-physical-patch.md. The same graph serves as a physical patch against any exterior.

On every supplied simple facial cycle this leaves at most 2hN transition edges, irrespective of its original length. In each witness the cyclic word of connected parts is noncrossing by the disk Jordan-curve separation property. A cyclic noncrossing word on k≥2 different colors has at most 2k−2 runs. Indeed, choose an innermost interval block (one color occupying a single run); its existence follows by looking inside a nonempty gap between consecutive occurrences of any color that is not such a block and descending. Remove the block, coalescing its neighbors if equal. For k>2 this drops the run count by at most two, while for k=2 the word has exactly two runs. With one color the number of transition edges is zero. Since each witness has at most h+1 colors, at most 2h edges change record in any one witness. On a simple face, every edge outside the union of these change sets has endpoints identified in the contracted graph. Parallel edges or loops may be discarded when appropriate; the statement about transition edges counts original edges with unequal records. The same vertex-record construction works on all selected faces at once.

In particular, if every nonzero local vertex label and every port lies on f specified simple facial cycles, their support after contraction occupies at most f(2hN+1) vertices in total. Thus even with arbitrarily dense original labels on those faces, the interior becomes an uncharged routing problem with a bounded number of distinguished vertices. On an arbitrary labelled triangulated wall the per-face inequality does not imply a contraction exists. No global bound on the reduced size for such patches follows from this argument.
