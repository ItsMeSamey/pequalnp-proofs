# Four widespread charge types with a geometrically infeasible plane

Fix a planar 3-connected cubic prism with four distinct sites 1,2,3,4 cyclic on one face (one may take a long enough rim that the sites are pairwise nonadjacent). At each site splice in a private cubic 3-connected graph M with a vertex removed: its three former neighbors attach bijectively to the three old edge ends. The resulting graph is simple, cubic and 3-connected. To see the last assertion for a single splice, when the at most two deleted vertices lie on one side, the other punctured side stays connected, and every component of the damaged punctured side has a surviving attachment by connectivity of its unsplit original graph after the deletions. If there is one deletion on each side, both punctured sides remain connected by original 3-connectivity, and at least one of the three matched edges survives. Apply this successively.

Take M to be K_m with each vertex replaced by a port cycle of length m−1, with one link per original edge. This cubic graph is 3-connected for m≥5. When two deletions hit one cycle, the other intact cycles form a connected core and each surviving port on the damaged cycle still links to that core. For deletions in different cycles, each damaged cycle remains connected and has a port to an intact cycle. Contracting the cycles recovers K_m. In each room i label every vertex by e_i except for one zero vertex, making its multiplicity even since M has even order. Label the fixed prism remainder zero. All four types have linear multiplicity and every nonzero functional is nonzero at linearly many vertices. Even the subgraph induced by each individual charge type has a K_(m−2) minor from the intact cycles other than the at most two containing the removed or zero vertices. The zero labels form a bounded set independent of m.

Choose u=e1+e3, v=e2+e4 and w=u+v. Unrestricted disjoint sets easily realize u and v. A connected u-shore must meet rooms 1 and 3, and a v-shore must meet 2 and 4. The w-shore meets all four. From a path in the u-shore joining rooms 1 and 3 take a subpath from its last departure from 1 before its first entrance to 3. It cannot traverse room 2 or 4: a traversal consumes two of that room's three boundary edges, yet both other colors also need one distinct boundary edge to connect its vertices there to their required vertices in other rooms. The analogous v-path avoids rooms 1 and 3. Thus on contracting the four rooms to their original prism sites, the two trimmed paths are vertex-disjoint and connect alternating pairs on the same face. The planar disk separation property forbids this. The desired plane therefore fails despite algebraic feasibility and widespread charge supports of unbounded treewidth. It follows that any sufficient universally correcting three-hub certificate is absent here, including variants enlarging switches by dependent appendages. This does not rule out a useful decomposition at the genuine order-three separators or claim that every graph contraction is unsafe.

Fully nonzero variant with a planar obstruction: take a prism of outer-rim length 6L, give its three successive intervals of 2L vertices charges e1,e2,e3, and give all inner vertices e4. Put u=e1+e2+e3, v=u+e4. Both colors must meet each outer arc. Selecting one point of each per arc gives at least three cyclic color changes, hence at least four; two same-color connected sets with such alternating samples cannot be disjoint in the disk. To obtain unbounded genus and width while preserving nonzero labels and linear multiplicities, splice a truncated K_m into one inner vertex and give its punctured room charge e4 throughout, choosing L comparable to |M|. The parity of the e4 count is preserved. Paths inside this one room may be replaced by edges on its three outside attachment vertices, connecting only vertices belonging to the same original color. Those three vertices are cofacial after removing the prism vertex (equivalently use the planar star-to-triangle move). The projected sets still violate the disk separation. This second construction asserts widespread types, not necessarily unbounded treewidth for each monochromatic type.
