# Conservative physical catalogue kernels for supplied finite flips of distance-hereditary graphs

Fix a finite symmetric type template F, a finite abelian conserved group including protected ports, and finitely many vertex-set CMSO tests including connectedness and the complete binary named-kernel catalogue. Suppose a connected G is supplied as H XOR F on distinct vertices, with connected distance-hereditary H and its supplied or computed ternary split tree. Then G has a computably bounded conservative physical spanning minor with the same full catalogue. In particular a symmetric rank-one binary template toggles a clique on a coloured subset and raises the cut-rank on the supplied tree by at most one. This gives a rank-width-two application with no bound on H's original treewidth.

Follow any oriented transmitting stretch in H's ternary split tree, viewing its two exterior sides and the incident hanging tiles as an ordered sequence. At a physical vertex in a hanging tile define left/right flags for accessibility through its site into the respective spine direction. Vertices in an exterior side use their accessibility from that side's interface as the inward flag, with the unused outward flag assigned zero. For vertices x in an earlier tile or exterior side and y in a later one, H(x,y)=right(x) left(y) by transmission. The signed graph has cross-edge value right(x) left(y) XOR F(type(x),type(y)). Thus all inter-tile G edges depend on order and a finite profile (two flags and vertex type). No claim about within-tile edges is needed.

For each occurring profile choose one physical representative at its first occurrence and one at its last occurrence in this tile order. There are at most B=8 times the number of types representatives. Mark all sites carrying these anchors. In any unmarked site, each connected component of the actual graph G induced by its tile has a physical neighbor outside that tile, by connectedness. If the neighbor is earlier, replace it by its profile's first representative; if later, use its last. This preserves order, directional flags and template type, hence the edge remains a genuine G edge. Assign the whole component to one such actual anchor, recording a Γ^B charge increment per tile (padding missing anchors). The receiver sites will not be deleted.

The earlier split-tree CMSO catalogue construction (accessible-split-run-pumping.md) applies on the H presentation with the signed adjacency relation interpreted by a quantifier-free XOR with F. Use left rooting, finite one-hole prefix transformations and retain the incidence marks at their respective endpoints. In a sufficiently long transmitting stretch, the at most B marked sites leave an unmarked contiguous stretch long enough that two prefix records agree, recording both the Γ^B sums and the finite one-hole transformation. The entire interval then contracts into its permanent receivers with zero charge increment at each. Deleting extra quotient edges gives exactly the signed graph on the spliced H tree: its H graph is the original induced H on surviving leaves since all removed turns transmitted, and types of every retained vertex stay fixed. Identical prefix transformations preserve the fixed catalogue and connectedness. All physical contractions used actual edges of G. The induced residual H is connected (it is the connected accessibility graph of its ternary tree). Iterate, recomputing representatives for each stretch as necessary.

At termination there is a computable C such that no stretch of C transmitting nodes remains. Every H-edge xy between leaves follows an entirely transmitting tree path; its tree length is consequently at most C+1. For each tree node v form a bag of all physical leaves at tree distance at most C+1 from v. All occurrences of a leaf form a connected ball in this tree; and every H-edge occurs in the bag centred at either endpoint. As degree is at most three these bags have at most 1+3(2^{C+1}−1) members, giving an explicit bounded-width tree decomposition of H. Apply bounded-treewidth-flip-catalogue.md (SHA edfdf88f154984e05eebb981d096df55707910e374c5d50a0da512ccfc9f37de), using the same fixed signed template, charges, and queries. Composing its certified physical minor with the reductions above proves the claim. The final sparse layer need only be a minor of the terminal bounded-width H; the claim concerns G's physical representative and its queries. The resulting construction takes polynomial time for fixed parameters given the presentation. No recognition of a hidden flip witness or kernel for every rank-width-two graph follows from this theorem.
