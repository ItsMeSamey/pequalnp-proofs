# Dense interiors and boundedly many exceptional physical children

Let a connected graph P have h augmented vertex-label bits summing to zero, including b−1 pure transfer columns when used in planar-physical-patch.md. Assume its complete N-state kernel table is supplied with witnesses, or computable, for example on a bounded-genus patch by the established labelled dual routing. Normalize one forest per state to k=j+1 connected parts where j≤h is row-image rank. An unrestricted torso by itself gives no algorithm for finding those witnesses.

Let connected edge-disjoint children meet the rest only in nonempty contact sets of size ≤t, all carried by a common torso. Their private vertices are disjoint. Include every global patch port lying in a child among its contacts. Attribute shared-vertex charges to the torso exactly once. Private charges can be arbitrary.

For each child choose one connected physical minor retaining all contacts distinctly and preserving every ordinary uncharged partition of the contacts realizable by disjoint connected subgraphs. For fixed t the prior ordinary rooted 0-folio argument of planar-few-face-physical-minor.md constructs bounded-size baselines on plane or fixed-genus children in polynomial time. Extend the disjoint branch trees of the chosen model to cover all private vertices, contract these trees and delete extra edges. Push every private charge by XOR, keeping separate accounting for private vertices mapped onto contacts. The baseline is built disregarding scores and need not preserve the child's original charged table.

Fix a whole-patch witness. Mark every child containing a monochromatic component of its restricted forest with no contact. Such a component cannot escape the child in the original forest, so it contains its whole global color. At most k children are marked. Every other local component meets contacts and induces a block of their partition. Realize exactly this partition in the baseline, attaching remaining vertices to obtain a spanning partition. Use the global color of each block's contacts. A color may occur on several local blocks. All original connections through the child between contacts persist; substituting the local partitions preserves connectivity of the global colors.

For each unmarked child form the difference between the original and baseline charge sums of its private vertices, separately for each color, using the spanning branch trees to identify its original private mass. All k components add to zero since only the assignment changes. The vectors therefore belong to a space of dimension h(k−1). Their total is a sum of at most h(k−1) of them: take a basis from the list and expand the total in that basis. Keeping the original choices on these exception children and the marked children, using the baseline elsewhere, restores all color sums exactly.

Take the union of these exceptions over all N witnesses. It has size at most N(h²+h+1). Physically replace every other child by its fixed baseline. For any one witness, original children retained solely because of other witnesses still realize the needed baseline coloring by lifting its spanning branch trees. Thus every table state remains, with labels at shared vertices counted exactly once, and physical minor lifting forbids any new state. The connected patch minor preserves the full augmented kernel table when glued to any exterior. The reduction bounds the number of exceptional children, not the size of those children or of the torso.
