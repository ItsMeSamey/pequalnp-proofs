# Conservative spanning minors for connected graphs of linear rank-width one

Fix a finite abelian charge group and finite representative labels. All connected graphs of linear rank-width at most one are WQO by conservative spanning minors. At fixed binary query dimension and protected port count they have computable finite physical representatives for the entire named-kernel catalogue.

By the thread graph characterization of Ganian and the word description of Adler, Farley and Proskurowski (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2013.05.001 and https://is.muni.cz/www/ganian/threadgraphs.pdf), apart from a singleton the graph is a path of thread blocks B_i with consecutive joints a_0,...,a_m. Within a block its satellites are ordered between its two endpoints. An earlier vertex is adjacent to a later one exactly if the earlier looks right and the later looks left. Each satellite looks left, right, or both; the endpoints look inward and are adjacent. Blocks share only consecutive joints.

In the order joint, satellites, joint, satellites, ..., joint, stabilize the whole charge. Tag each block with its endpoints' representative labels, its inclusive prefix charge at the left joint and its strict prefix charge before the right joint. Assign each satellite to its adjacent left endpoint if possible and otherwise to its right endpoint. Also tag the block with total satellite charge for each owner. Give each satellite its charge, label and direction flags. Higman's lemma on these satellite words with equal finite block tags orders the blocks by a WQO. A second application to block sequences gives an ordered matching of whole blocks with satellite subword embeddings.

Keep matched satellites as singleton branches. Give unmatched satellites within each matched block to their owner; each side receives a total of zero by owner tags. The branch for an internal target joint includes the whole interval from the right joint of the preceding matched block to the left joint of the following matched block, inclusive. For consecutive source matches that interval is just their one shared joint. The first joint gets the entire prefix ending at the first matched left joint, and the last joint gets the suffix starting at the last matched right joint. Such intervals are connected since complete skipped blocks are connected through their successive joints. Additional satellites attach to their assigned endpoints. The internal interval charge is the inclusive prefix charge at its last vertex minus the strict prefix charge before its first. By the tags this equals the shared target joint charge. At the ends use zero and the common whole charge respectively. Each joint has its representative label at the matched endpoint. All required edges are witnessed inside matched blocks, and all source vertices were assigned exactly once. Independent charge bits protect individual ports.

For effectivity encode the presentation as the finite word of joints and satellite letters. Adjacency of an ordered pair requires no joint strictly between them and the two appropriate direction tests; a joint at the earlier or later end always looks inward to the block in question. These are MSO word predicates. All concatenations of such two-terminal words form a regular presentation class, and the proof above works even if a normalization convention for thread words is dropped. Thus the finite-catalogue emptiness and witness loop of modular-width-conservative.md applies, providing a computable upper bound and a physical representative with the same full catalogue, including all previously augmented port query columns. General rank-width one allows branching split trees beyond the linear case.
