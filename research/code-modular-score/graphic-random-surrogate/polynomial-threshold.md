# A polynomial torso threshold in the adhesion, color count and binary rank

In arbitrary-charge-constant.md (SHA256 d845ec7c7bbf53cec9161485cd4bba841a51f2e391ba594d5d992ceaf303fcee) the recognizable decomposition and ordered connected-partition algorithm hold at a threshold polynomial jointly in a,b,r. Its fixed-parameter polynomial running-time assertion is unchanged. The following substitutes for the two exponential terms in that proof.

Keep C=binom(a,2), F=b+r(b−1), p=a(F+1)+b, M=p+4br+b+3, B=p+3br+b+2, m=p+4br+b+2, and set L=aM r(r+1)/2. Start with charge subspace V=W and P empty. At a stage with dim V=j>0 use all nonparent child interiors having charge in V. Seek M successive bases of V, each charge representative supplied by a distinct interior with two distinct marked bag ports. All marks and all selected interiors within this stage must be distinct. To extend a partial basis greedily choose any unused candidate whose charge lies outside its span and has two as yet unmarked ports. Reset the partial basis span after a basis is completed, retaining the marks and used interiors.

If M bases are found, set H=V and keep their interiors with marked pairs. Otherwise let U be the span of the last incomplete basis (possibly zero). Add the full port sets of all interiors used at this stage to P. Each interior of charge in V outside U now has at most one port outside P. For an already used interior the full set was added. For an unused one, two ports outside P would have permitted another extension of the last partial basis. Replace V by U, restart the search with fresh stage marks, and retain P across stages. Stop with H=0 if V reaches zero. The dimension decreases strictly on each failed stage, which consumes fewer than Mj selected interiors. Hence |P|≤L and every charge outside the terminal H has at most one port outside P. This classification and all baseline pivots are fixed before selecting exceptional children.

Use the usual fixed pivot star for every charge outside H. Every lost edge has both ends in P. Deleting arbitrary edges with both ends in P from a graph of vertex connectivity k>|P| leaves connectivity at least k−|P|. Indeed, after removing any Z of size less than k−|P|, the old graph minus Z∪P is connected and nonempty; each surviving vertex in P has at least one neighbor outside Z∪P, by the old minimum degree at least k. These attachments and the entire surviving outside graph are unchanged. Thus these fixed stars cost at most L units of vertex connectivity. Components of charge in H have arbitrary baseline pivots and may be routed with true charges later corrected.

Carry out normalization and the projected Q=W/H bag-basis construction exactly as in the preceding theorem. For q≥2 color parts on the bag, omit all recorded charge bases that have a marked port among the initial pins, rare vertices or reserved bag-basis vertices. At most p+(q−1)r bases are lost, since their marks are disjoint across the entire collection. Every basis still available uses only nonretained children because each retained adhesion was pinned. Select q−1 distinct surviving bases, one per owner, possible since M>p+(q−1)r+(q−1). Each owner's basis of H may be different. Pin one marked endpoint of every selected interior to the owner, the other to the last color, and withhold all these interiors from routing. Those endpoints had quotient labels in the correcting bag subspace S. The private-basis argument applies first in the quotient, then independently per owner in H. For H=0 no charge bases are required. All counts of branches, pins and withheld centers from the preceding theorem are unchanged.

The relative linkage proof in zero-charge-constant.md (SHA256 c4c54fff7b845aa2c597a10a76230c9a30b45e4868cfa6dbc11b866168e58b35) produces a graph minor J on the surviving bag of density at least κ(T)/a edges per vertex, where T is the damaged torso. Bollobás and Thomason prove that a graph with at least 256t² edges per vertex contains a subdivision of K_t, hence a K_t minor. Taking t=3m+1, the same supported-branch linkage criterion applies whenever κ(T)≥256a(3m+1)² and κ(T)>C(2m−1). [Bollobás and Thomason, Proof of a conjecture of Mader, Erdős and Hajnal on topological complete subgraphs](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0195669897901888).

One sufficient polynomial threshold is therefore
```
f = 2 + a + a*C + (1+F+br)*C + L
    + B + 2*m*(a+C+1) + C*(2*m+br+1) + 256*a*(3*m+1)^2.
```
This only changes the sufficient numerical connectivity of the actual-component torsos. It produces the same deterministic decision and witness algorithm on that recognizable class.
