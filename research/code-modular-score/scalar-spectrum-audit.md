# Graphic scalar observations at a four-dimensional port

For every binary plane K, use the column of indicators indexed by triples (d,a,b) with d in K and a,b independent modulo K. Independent binary elimination on all 35 columns gave rank 24. Enumerating the 2^11 dual dependencies gave the following weight : multiplicity pairs:
0:1, 7:15, 12:105, 15:448, 16:455, 19:455, 20:448, 23:105, 28:15, 35:1. The checker graphic_spectra_three.py reproduces these values.

When the span of a forest of size r−2 induces a named kernel of dimension greater than two, every two-column completion fails. Lower dimensions are impossible with a four-dimensional reachable named space because the forest has codimension two. Thus the observation polynomials on fixed physical variables have the stated signatures. Within at most six attainable plane columns, every column can be separated from the others by a linear form since the minimum dependency size is seven. Trying every linear combination of 24 independent observational rows includes that form. In a successful evaluation, coefficient conditioning yields a single forest by contracting present nonloops and deleting absent edges, keeping the original ambient incidence frame on deletions and the original named columns projected on contractions. Check its exact kernel explicitly. Forms that do not isolate the target can return other forests and are simply skipped. One isolating form on a positive instance gives a one-sided success probability at least one half per joint batch; no union bound over the forms is necessary. The same argument works whenever the target is a coloop in the observation matrix restricted to actually attainable columns.

Complete K6 with vertex labels (10,11,12,9,1,5) in four bits has XOR zero and rank four. Enumerating three nonempty vertex blocks and taking their component-label XORs gives all 35 two-planes as the span of the sums. The orthogonal planes are precisely the attained kernels, and every partition is realized by keeping a spanning tree in each block. K5 with rank-four named map can attain at most 25 planes because there are only 25 three-block partitions. This gives the minimum possible vertex count of a connected simple graphic example attaining all planes. The checker output includes K6 and K7 controls.

For an arbitrary connected graph, the cut sums v_e on edges of any spanning tree T give simultaneous sound witnesses for every plane perpendicular to span(v_e,v_f) for two distinct edges e,f with independent cut labels: remove those two edges of T. Their cut vectors generate the same shore-sum space as the three resulting components, since the quotient tree on three component vertices has a basis of two cuts. This is deterministic and remains valid under any number of tree exchanges, but failure to find a witnessing tree leaves the target undecided. For a complete ordinary graph, unrestricted DP for all planes tracks two bin sums and the occupancy of three bins as vertices are assigned, the last sum determined by the total. This computes the whole 3-block spectrum with at most 16^2*8 states and reconstructs witnesses. It covers dense labels independent of the six-kernel promise.

The same finite-state assignment method handles all complete multipartite graphs with arbitrarily many parts. Process one part at a time. For each bin record its global class as empty, singleton, multiple vertices all from one previous part, or already spanning two parts. During a new part additionally accumulate the number assigned to each bin capped at two and the two independent total XORs. At the part boundary an empty bin inherits its new occupancy class, a nonempty old bin receiving any new vertex now spans two parts, and the other global classes persist. At termination accept bins that are singletons or span at least two parts. Those are exactly the nonempty connected induced subgraphs in a complete multipartite graph. A three-way partition gives explicit component trees and every r−2 forest gives such a partition. Backpointers reconstruct the forest for a requested plane; the bound is O(n) transitions up to a fixed four-bit constant once the parts are supplied.
