# Finding exceptional children without guessing their identities

This refines the pure unsigned regular-matroid bag algorithm in regular-score-dp.md. Write g=|G|. At most k≤g−1 children need deviate from their defaults. Guess k, their nonzero deviations δ_j in G and their desired local quotient traces p_j, including the arity of each slot j. Enumerating these values costs only a function of g.

For N children compute a family of colorings into [k] such that every k distinct children receive different colors in one coloring. For example encode their indices in binary and enumerate lists of at most k−1 bit positions and all maps from the resulting patterns to [k]. Any fixed k indices can be distinguished with k−1 coordinate refinements. The number of lists is O((1+log N)^k), at most f(k)N for N≥2, and the number of maps depends only on k. Enumerate the association of colors to slots, or equivalently include all color maps. Handle k=0 directly.

In a fixed coloring a child i is eligible for slot j when it has color j, the correct arity, and its computed table contains (p_j,default_i(p_j)+δ_j). It is usable only if the actual bag trace on i equals p_j. The goal is to certify that every slot has a usable child. Because the colors are distinct, the sets of usable children are pairwise disjoint. Enumerate small-bias binary tags z_i on children, as in exterior-types.md, with bias below 2^{-k}. For any assignment with nonempty usable sets, one tag has odd sum on every usable set. Conversely, odd sums force all sets to be nonempty. Tags are chosen from f(k)N² candidates.

These tests are implemented with additive edge labels in the existing default gadgets. A ternary child has the three wire bits x, y and x⊕y. Twice the indicator of any prescribed two-bit trace (a,b) is an integer affine function of those three bits, since 2xy=x+y−(x⊕y); replace x and/or y by its complement as required. Unary and empty traces likewise give affine indicators after multiplying by two. For eligible children of slot j with tag one, add the variable terms on their gadget edges in one auxiliary Z/4 coordinate specific to j. Keep the constant separately. Prescribe total 2 modulo four, adjusted by that constant. This enforces precisely odd tagged count. Keep all G-default edges; subtract the sum of the δ_j from the required G-score. Parent trace pins use the previous at most three binary coordinates. Bags of bounded size may alternatively enumerate their states directly.

The bag oracle now uses G × (Z/4)^k × (Z/2)^b, b≤3. Recover one usable child per slot by scanning the returned central assignment, use its recorded exceptional table state, and use all other actual-trace defaults. The search over local query instances takes f(g)poly(N), independent of torso structure or degree. In particular a decomposition with only cographic and R10 torsos is deterministic FPT in g. The same holds with bounded orientable genus γ for each postreplacement graphic torso given its embedding: enumerate the 2^(2γ) homology cosets of the facial-boundary subspace of even edge sets. Each physical edge bit in a coset is its representative bit plus the XOR of the two incident face bits, so a group quadratic image query computes its possible scores, including all auxiliary coordinates. Homology representatives may be found by binary elimination. The argument applies per connected component, or with total genus bounded. For unrestricted graphic torsos the older linear-in-g exponent remains independently available. This result does not assume uniform FPT for arbitrary unsigned graphic routing.

At a singleton interface in the mixed binary cycle/cocycle setting the allowed states (0,0),(1,0),(0,1) have indicators 1−c−d,c,d; the same colored occurrence certificate uses only additive edge scores. This observation does not supply all mixed ternary state indicators.
