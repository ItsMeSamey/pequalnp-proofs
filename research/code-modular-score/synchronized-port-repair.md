# Simultaneous ternary port repair

Write a=dim N−dim(N∩S), b=dim S−dim(N∩S) for an input code N and regular reference S. For named triangle indicators t_i in N let d be the rank of their syndromes h_i in a parity-check matrix W of S. Then d≤a. Quotient W by the space L spanned by all physical columns of d basis triples; dim L≤3d. Contract a column basis and restore contracted positions as loops, so the resulting code S' is regular. It contains S and all t_i. Its dimension increases by dim L, while its intersection with N increases by at least d. Thus its distance bounds are (a−d,b+2d), independent of the number of ports. Dually all named triad parity equations obeyed by N can be imposed by a regular subspace of S at budgets (a+2d,b−d), d≤b.

Matching a regular reference containing 111 to a regular pendant P along P's genuine triangle preserves regularity. For coindependent nondegenerate triangles use regular 3-sum; small sides may be padded with dummy coloops. If a nondegenerate triple contains a cocircuit, that cocircuit is a pair by orthogonality. Its two bits are equal, and simultaneously toggling 111 normalizes them to zero in both pieces. Delete this pair on both sides and glue along the third bit. If the reference has three loops, its trace is independent and free, giving a direct sum of minors. Otherwise a degenerate zero-sum triple contains two parallel columns and one loop. Only the XOR of the parallel bits interacts with the outside. On the reference delete the loop and one parallel element, retaining the other as unary port. In P apply a regular K4 triangle-to-star exchange, then contract the two spokes other than the one carrying that pair XOR. Glue the unary ports. All other disjoint 111 cycles survive sequential attachments by zero extension.

When every pendant P has a physical K4 minor rooted at its virtual triangle, reduce all branches to these minors. The exposed physical core has corresponding even star triples. Any nearby regular physical reference projects there with no distance increase. Conversely enforce all triad equations in a core reference by the dual repair. Invert each star triple by the full preimage under the three pairwise XORs of a triangle. This is regular under triad compatibility by the dual triangle gluing rule with K4: the orthogonal linear relation is that of the dual K4 along its triangle. The rebuilt virtual triples contain 111. Reattach all original branches. All common linear relations are restrictions and projections of direct products, so budgets are (a+2d,b−d), exact at b=0. If the roots instead have parallel-copy triangle minors, the core retains the virtual triangle as a physical triple; primal repair gives (a−d,b+2d), exact at a=0.

## Sharpness and an exact-budget obstruction

Let S be the cycle code of K_n, n≥7, with incidence columns e_u+e_v in the even vertex space. Let t be three disjoint matching edges, h their syndrome of weight six, and N=S+span(t). Then (a,b)=(1,0), and t is a genuine coindependent triangle in N. A code containing both S and t comes from quotienting the incidence columns by a space L containing h. For dim L=1 all columns remain nonzero and distinct, violating the simple regular Heller bound binom(r+1,2) for rank r=n−2. If dim L=2 without a weight-two vector, at most two weight-four elements occur, each permitting at most three pairs of columns to become identical. There are ≥binom(n,2)−6 points, greater than binom(n−2,2) at rank n−3. If L has a weight-two vector, first merge its endpoints, retaining a K_(n−1) restriction. The remaining quotient vector has weight four or six and costs at most three points, still exceeding the rank bound. Thus every upward regular repair containing S and t needs three new dimensions. Contracting the three disjoint edges realizes this exactly.

Moreover S is the unique regular codimension-at-most-one subcode of N. N itself fails Heller's bound. A codimension-one regular subcode S'' yields a simple matroid of rank n−1 with binom(n,2) points (N has no loop or parallel pair). On contracting any e, every triangle through e loses one point in simplification, and all losses arise this way since binary cosets have size two. Heller on the contraction forces at least n−2 triangles through each e. Choose v outside the six terminals. Through each e incident to v there are exactly n−2 N-triangles: an extra three-element cycle of N would have syndrome h and therefore be a perfect matching of the six terminals, missing v. All original triangles based at v therefore occur in S''. Those triangles give a fundamental cycle basis of S (use the star tree). Equal dimensions imply S''=S. Thus a regular reference feasible at (1,0) exists but none at that budget contains the required triangle. The +2 lower bound applies to upward repair; it is not asserted as a lower bound for every possible two-sided budget relaxation.

## Exact search under designated port constraints

There is a separate recognition algorithm when the reference itself must satisfy designated triangle-cycle or triad-parity constraints. Let m_e be the host binary parity-check column of element e. To each element assign a subspace spanned by (m_e,0) and, for a named port i containing e, (0,u_i) in a fresh vector space with independent port identifiers. The named triples are disjoint. The dimension of the intersection across a branch-tree cut equals the host's binary connectivity value plus the number of split port triples. [Jeong, Kim and Oum](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1285895) recognize bounded width of this subspace arrangement and return the tree. No separate bound on the number of fully internal ports is needed.

Add a predicate Port(Z) meaning exactly the three elements of one designated port to the automaton proof of near-regular-recognition.md. At most w port identities cross a cut. For a free marked set Z retain its size truncated at four and whether its selected leaves all share one port: empty, one identified crossing port, one internal port, or failure. A selection containing an unported element fails. Two internal/other nonempty portions cannot be the same port unless the identities were both active across the child cuts. Relabel the active indices at each join according to the fixed decomposition annotations. Accept at the root for count three and one common port, distinguishing types by an extra color when needed. These finite states provide the atomic tree predicate under the same products, complements and set quantifiers of the CMSO model checker. Require every named triangle to be a member of R and every named triad to intersect every member of R evenly. Traceback returns the actual regular reference S. This is exact for the port-constrained problem in f(w,a,b)poly(input) time; the example above prohibits equating it unconditionally with the unmarked problem.
For arbitrarily many disjoint discrepant triangles of rank one, replace each of the three matching edges by m parallel copies in the graphic reference, and take T_i to consist of its ith copies. The h_i are all the same six-terminal syndrome. Adding t_1 adds every t_i, as their differences lie in the graphic cycle space. Deleting all but the first copy of each returns the original sharp example; common shortening cannot increase either subspace distance. Thus both the upward lower bound and the exact-budget obstruction survive with unboundedly many synchronized ports.

For every set of disjoint actual host triangles and triads, the combined width is at most max(9w,4) for ordinary host width w. In a subcubic tree, move each entire triangle to the median of its three leaves, as an indivisible bundle. On each original cut a triangle parked on one side has two original elements there, whose sum is its third. Hence the new side spans are contained in the original ones. At a degree-three node with arm spans U_j set D=Σ_j(U_j∩Σ_(k≠j)U_k); dim D≤3w. Each triple parked there has one element per arm, so each lies in D. The U_j are independent modulo D: for a sum in D subtract its armwise boundary decomposition, yielding a zero-sum relation whose every arm contribution lies in its respective boundary. Any subcubic refinement of this node therefore has cut intersection contained in D: the parked vectors vanish in the quotient and opposite subsets of whole arms have independent spans there. This constructs width ≤3w with triangle bundles as individual leaves. Repeat in the dual for all disjoint triads (now triangles); previous bundles remain intact leaves and cuts of their index sets have the same starting connectivity by duality. Width becomes ≤9w. Finally expand each bundle locally into its three physical leaves. Internal cuts of any such expansion have host connectivity ≤3 and split only that one designated port; all other cuts split none. Consequently exact port-compatible search for actual disjoint ternaries needs parameters (w,a,b) alone.

## Mixed port types and all proper regular appendages

On pairwise disjoint triangle and triad ports, perform the two repairs simultaneously. Quotienting by the columns on J changes S precisely into S+F₂^J, and its dual analogue on K gives S∩{x_K=0}. The chosen physical sets are disjoint, so these operations commute. The upward operation adds generators invisible to the triad checks and the downward operation preserves exactly the old dependencies among named triangle indicators (they vanish on K). Thus if d_T and d_Y are their original discrepancy ranks, the simultaneous distances are bounded by (a−d_T+2d_Y,b+2d_T−d_Y), with d_T≤a and d_Y≤b. No recharging occurs even when both types are numerous.

The representative theorem in ../triangle-baselines/README.md also treats an arbitrary proper binary branch P with three named column vectors a,b,c=a+b. Since no cocircuit lies in the virtual triangle, its outside columns span the full boundary after contracting a basis modulo that boundary. Thus at least two normalized nonzero types occur. Three give a physical parallel-copy template; two types in a common normalized fundamental component give a rooted K4. The remaining case, two types a,b in different components, requires no ternary regularity repair. Partition the normalized fundamental-graph components into type a, type b, and type zero; the disjoint basis-coordinate sets ensure that the requirement of vanishing nonboundary sum q separates among them. In a triangle trace set its c bit to zero by the available virtual 111; its two remaining bits are precisely the a and b syndrome coefficients. Each group of real components of one type, together with its single matching triangle column, is a restriction of P and is regular. Its single port bit equals the sum of its type coefficients once its own q sum vanishes. Restore the two groups by independent unary-port gluings, and restore the zero-type part by direct sum. The two-copy minor supplies the two physical port coordinates. This is inverse on the original input and preserves regularity and both distance bounds for arbitrary candidate references. Consequently all proper regular ternary branches at disjoint ports of a central core are covered, conditional on the cited constructive representative theorem.

## Choosing a K4 or exact unary factorization

One can avoid any need for primal triangle repair when selecting branch reductions from scratch. Even if all three normalized nonzero types occur, test whether any fundamental-graph component contains columns of distinct nonzero types. In such a component take a shortest path between two differently typed columns, minimizing over all such pairs. Every internal nonbasis column has type zero, since otherwise one of the two endpoints has a different type and the path was not shortest. The zero-type pivots of the rooted representative proof therefore shorten this path without changing any type until two different columns share a basic neighbor. Retaining this neighbor and the two columns after contracting the other basis elements produces the rooted K4, irrespective of other types that occur elsewhere.

Otherwise all components are monochrome or zero. For each present type v select a real column of type v after the basis contraction, giving a parallel copy of v in the physical minor. Glue back the corresponding original group via its ordinary unary transfer (the restriction to this group's outside columns plus v). When all three types are present, the three group output bits p agree with the virtual trace of the original central input modulo 111 exactly when their syndrome agrees. Since the input contains 111 it realizes p itself whenever it realizes its complement. Thus directly attaching the separate unary pieces produces precisely the original physical code. This operation is regularity-preserving on ANY regular candidate, which need not realize both raw representatives. When only two types occur use the zero-missing-bit representative as above. Type-zero components always give an independent regular summand.

Consequently every proper regular ternary branch may be contracted to either a rooted K4 or an exactly restorable independent unary factorization. All nontrivial repairs in this particular selection are triad repairs on the remaining star ports, so the single simultaneous bound (a+2d,b−d), d≤b, applies to every family of proper regular branches at disjoint central ports. In particular b=0 has exact equivalence, with no premise that every branch individually has a rooted K4.

The complementary one-sided problem a=0 is also exact using a potentially different physical core. Dualize the whole input and candidate, swapping the two budgets. Equal-trace glue followed by forgetting the traces dualizes to equal-trace glue of the orthogonal summands (binary signs coincide), by the usual orthogonal-complement rules for intersection and projection. On each original proper triangular separator the orthogonal summands have an independent triad; their trace projects onto the entire even plane H because the only original pure-port cycles were 0 and 111. Replace these triads on both sides by full preimages under the same map L:F₂³→H given by the three pairwise XORs. Matching new raw traces yields exactly the same physical glued code: equal new vectors have equal images, and equal old traces have a common preimage. Each new virtual triple is a genuine coindependent triangle: old independent triad means the only new pure-port words are ker L={0,111}, and surjectivity onto H before the transform gives full raw projection afterward. These disjoint-port conversions commute. They preserve each regular pendant's regularity by dual K4 exchange. The dual physical code thus has a proper regular-triangle pendant presentation. Apply the preceding exact b_dual=0 recognition and dualize the output back. For general budgets the primal and dual routes offer opposite directional additive relaxations, not exact simultaneous equality.

The physical core required in each direction has bounded width when the central input torso does, independently of the widths and number of regular branches. Start with its triangle-bundled width ≤3w tree. On a complete triple the three-copy reduction is identity, and the two-copy or K4 reductions are local linear maps with kernel generated by 111 on that triple, already a central cycle. On a partition separating no triple, the kernel is a direct sum of local gauges supported on either side. A word whose image is supported on one side can be adjusted to vanish on the other side by subtracting these central gauges. Thus connectivity is unchanged across every such cut by the formula using the full-space and two shortened-space dimensions. Expand each bundle locally into at most three physical coordinates for constant local width. The full-preimage gauges in the dual conversion give the same equality in reverse, followed by median bundling if needed. Therefore the exact one-sided searches work with width of the central nonregular torso rather than width of the entire physical graph of summands.

For total subspace distance this gives a constructive factor-3/2 approximation on the stated central-bounded-width, regular-pendant class. Try increasing integer k and all a+b=k. If b≤a use the primal core; otherwise use the dual. Any true optimal reference with its own split induces a feasible reference on that core. At the first successful k≤OPT return the restored reference, whose total is at most k+min(a,b)≤k+floor(k/2)≤3OPT/2. The running time is fixed-parameter in OPT and central width, with no branch-count parameter. This is an approximation for the two-sided sum, not exact two-sided budget recognition.
