# A physical full-catalogue kernel for every connected distance-hereditary graph

Fix finite binary named dimension and a fixed number of protected ports. For every connected distance-hereditary graph, with arbitrary dense named columns, there is a computably bounded conservative spanning minor with the identical complete catalogue of named kernels. The algorithm returns actual physical branches, and adding the protected port columns gives equality for all exterior dense-name tests at that boundary. This is a finite-catalogue theorem, not a proof of WQO for all charged DH graphs.

Use the ternary split-tree presentation, the full-catalogue transmitting rewrite (accessible-split-run-pumping.md, SHA 97809e0ca86f0c672adda49e93868d5f66b665884a389ee1b43d386abb43b36e), and the k-lane physical rewrite (dh-bounded-treewidth-catalogue.md, SHA 1f56ca2e1b1e6ca3845dccfa8e5fcc53c9ce6bca72b4fe57f5eb87ce1a27a6c3). The latter's physical tile construction requires only exact endpoint capacities k and intermediate capacities ≥k; its treewidth assumption was used only to bound long runs of large capacities.

Let C be the transmitting rewrite threshold for the fixed conserved group and finite catalogue automaton. In a presentation admitting no such rewrite, every set of active leaves on one side of any split edge has size at most K=2^{C+1}. Indeed, fix a leaf accessible from that edge's marker on its chosen side. Its path from the split edge goes only through transmitting internal nodes. Extend it across the edge to an accessible leaf on the opposite side; one always exists by choosing an allowed continuation through each encountered clique or star. The joined leaf-to-leaf path transmits at every internal node. In particular the route into the chosen side has fewer than C internal nodes, or the transmitting prescription would give a rewrite. A ternary tree rooted at one incoming edge has at most two downward choices per step, giving the stated generous bound. This applies in particular to every spine blocker contact set S_i.

Attempt both types of rewrite, allowing every k≤K, on any spine and repeat. Intermediate presentations are allowed to have arbitrarily high contact capacity: the finite number K is needed only for the second rule and the terminal analysis. Every step strictly reduces physical leaves and preserves the full catalogue, charged spanning model and DH representation. At termination the previous paragraph bounds every contact capacity by K. For each k let M_k bound the number of k-lane prefix records (charge vectors and the finite catalogue transformation). Put L_{K+1}=0 and L_k=M_k+(M_k+1)L_{k+1} downward to k=1. In each maximal run with capacities ≥k there can be at most M_k sites of exact capacity k, since any repeated record permits the k-lane rewrite. Splitting an interval at those sites gives the stated L_k bound by induction. Consequently every spine has at most L_1 blocked sites. Its transmitting subruns each have fewer than C sites, giving a presentation diameter at most (L_1+1)C. A degree-three tree of this diameter has at most 1+3(2^{(L_1+1)C}−1) vertices. This computes a uniform bound on the remaining physical graph. Singletons and two-leaf presentations cause no exceptional large case.

A straightforward implementation on a supplied or recognized split tree enumerates leaf pairs and orientations, computes the indicated prefix records by finite tree automata and group addition, and grows the k disjoint tile branches whenever records match. At fixed parameters the loops over k and automaton sizes are constants; all remaining traversals, construction steps and iterations are polynomial in the input graph size. The proved bound and parameter dependence can be extremely large. No host diameter, treewidth, block structure, or contact-capacity promise is required.
