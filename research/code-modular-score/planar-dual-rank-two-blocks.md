# Physical compression of planar patches by dual block gain ranks

Give the plane dual the switching-invariant binary cycle-gain map from planar-physical-patch.md; h is the augmented demand dimension. Suppose every connected cycle-matroid component of the dual has gain-image dimension at most two. The connected physical patch has a table-equivalent connected minor of size bounded in h, whatever its original treewidth, nullity or interior charge support.

A component of image dimension one contains a nonzero-gain circuit, yielding both subspaces of its line. A component of image dimension two contains a theta whose three cycles have its three distinct nonzero gains. To see this, use a tree basis of the block and the connected fundamental bipartite graph between tree edges and nonbasis edges. Color each nonbasis edge by the gain of its fundamental cycle. Choose a shortest path globally between distinctly colored nonzero nonbasis edges. All internal nonbasis edges have color zero, by shortestness, and the path is induced. If its first three vertices are c0,b1,c1 and continuation starts at b2, exchange c1 for b1 in the tree. Fundamental circuits formerly meeting b1 change by the zero-gain fundamental circuit of c1; the new nonbasis b1 gets gain zero. The missing c0,b2 fundamental adjacency toggles to present; the rest of the path stays present because b1 had no later nonbasis neighbor on the induced path. Repeat until two distinct nonzero-gain chords have a common tree neighbor. Their two tree paths intersect in a nonempty path with at least one edge and share no other vertices. The intersection plus the two complementary arcs form a theta. This includes parallel chords and length-one arms. Graph loops and bridges form singleton matroid components and cannot have image dimension two. On the theta choose no cycle, exactly one of its three simple cycles, or the whole theta to realize zero, each line, or the full plane. These are supported cycle-space image equalities.

Dual blocks may share cutvertices, but no simple cycle traverses a block boundary. Their attainable image subspaces therefore combine by vector-space sums. Two blocks giving the same complete lattice of subspaces of W are redundant: each sum of two subspaces of W is itself available from just one block. Retain one certified loop-cycle per distinct nonzero line image and one theta per distinct plane image, discarding zero-image blocks. The joint carrier has cycle rank at most the number of lines plus twice the number of planes in binary h-space. It is physically realizable even with shared cutvertices. Apply the bridgeless-core suppression procedure of planar-physical-patch.md: keep all branch points of the *joint* graph and suppress only unbranched degree-two paths. The union has at most O(total cycle rank) such edges after suppression. Delete the other dual edges before suppressing. The translated primal contractions followed by coindependent deletions preserve primal connectivity and all named transfer identities, with full signature sufficient for arbitrary nonplanar exterior gluings. The result concerns arbitrary F₂-valued vector gains, without extension to general nonbinary groups.

The basis-pivot argument is also useful for abstract two-bit circuit detection; the physical statement additionally preserves the entire supported-image subspace family, with original edge-minor operations, block composition and exterior port tracking.
