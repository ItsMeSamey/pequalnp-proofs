# Adjacent proper subspaces do not compose from unported band spectra

Use a two-by-four ladder with three binary vertex-label bits. The lower row from left to right is (0,2,4,6), and the upper row is either (0,1,3,2) or (2,3,1,0). Each row has total zero. The row spans are respectively the planes generated by 2,4 and 1,2, intersecting in the line spanned by 2. The two possibilities for the upper row have identical unported full kernel catalogues and identical charge multisets, as one is the reversal of the other. The actual shared rungs retain their fixed column indices and the lower row never changes.

Exhaustively taking connected partitions generated by all edge subsets, each complete ladder attains fourteen row-image subspaces. The first alone attains the plane {0,1,4,5} (incidence mask 51) and the second alone attains {0,2,5,7} (mask 165). The remaining thirteen agree. The adjoining checker forms endpoint partitions for all choices of the ordinary rungs and horizontal edges; for each partition it takes the actual binary span of the component charge sums. Thus the discrepancy is in full image equality on one jointly chosen support.

Inserting arbitrarily many all-zero columns before the left end gives the same discrepancy at arbitrary interface length. The added ladder meets the original only at the two leftmost vertices, already joined by a physical rung. Any support inside that addition induces either a connection between those contacts, simulated by the original rung, or no connection. Every original support also extends by selecting no added edges. The added vertices have zero label. This example forbids composition using only the separate unported catalogues, the two local label spans, and charge multisets; it does not obstruct root-aware methods or physical compression.
