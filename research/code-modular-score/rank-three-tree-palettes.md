# Rank-three tree palettes and the cost of fixing witnesses

For a connected vertex-charged graph with zero total, the palette of a spanning tree is the set of its nonzero fundamental cut scores. Its catalogue consists of the spans of subsets of its palette: the cutwords supported on omitted tree edges in tree coordinates are free. The graph catalogue is the union of its spanning-tree catalogues. Indeed any vertex partition into connected parts has forests spanning those parts, extending together to one spanning tree. In rank three a spanning tree containing six of the seven colors therefore certifies every plane: each contains three nonzero points, of which at least two are present. It certifies the whole catalogue except possibly the seventh line. Contracting all but one representative tree edge per present color after deleting cotree edges produces a physical six-edge witness for these conclusions. This is a one-sided certificate.

There is a checkable sufficient condition resolving the seventh line on a single unicyclic carrier. If one cotree edge crosses two fundamental cuts of colors a and b with a+b the missing color, keep that edge along with the tree. Pivoting one of the two tree edges against this cotree edge changes the OTHER tree edge's color to a+b. The original and changed tree are both available, so all lines and planes are present. Contract tree edges except one representative of each color, choosing the two pivotal edges as their representatives, and keep the cotree edge. This at most seven-edge physical graph still has the same pivot and realizes the full catalogue. More generally find the tree after arbitrary zero-cut-score pivots, which preserve its entire palette. These are sufficient, without an unrestricted search bound for finding such a tree.

A limitation of preserving simultaneous individual witnesses is visible already for two connected bipartitions. For n≥2 take four cells, each of size n, indexed by (i,j) in {0,1}². For each fixed i put a spanning alternating path between the two j-cells on its 2n vertices. For each fixed j likewise put a spanning alternating path between its two i-cells. These four edge sets are disjoint; every edge has endpoints in two distinct cells. The two named partitions by i and by j respectively have connected sides. Any graph minor required to lift THESE VERY partitions as literal shores cannot contract any edge: contraction would mix two cells. No edge can be deleted either since it is a bridge on one of the four required shores. The connected graph has 4n vertices, 8n−4 edges and cyclomatic number 4n−3. It is biconnected: after removing a vertex, the two paths on opposite shores avoiding that vertex intersect and remain connected, and every remaining vertex in the affected cell has a neighbor along at least one path into their union. Vertices of all other cells already belong to the union. Any full-rank total-zero three-bit charges can be superimposed. This lower bound is for exact preservation of the witness partitions, not for retaining their aggregate scores or the entire catalogue. It rules out the shortcut of first fixing one witness per query and then only pruning edges and contracting their common refinements.
