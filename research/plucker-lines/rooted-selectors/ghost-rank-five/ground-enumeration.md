# Independent ground-set enumeration for rank five

Assume an original mixed weight is constant, its dual marginal varies, and a projected positive switch occurs after freely contracting q. The virtual-contraction lemma deals with a genuinely lifting endpoint circuit, so the two endpoint lift defects agree. Independent vectors s,t,i,a,q are normalized to 1,2,4,8,16. Here a is the ordinary chart contraction pivot and i is a peripheral K4 element. The six retained vectors are s,t,i,i+s+h_j,i+t+h_k,i+s+t+h_l where every h belongs to span(a,q). Their four peripheral columns sum to either zero or a; if the sum contains q, one endpoint circuit lifts genuinely and has already been handled. The objective switch condition is that the parity of the four peripheral primal labels, together with the label of a if it occurs in their sum, is one.

It suffices to set the dual moment to q, since the original moment differs by a combination of s,t and thus changes the objective only by a constant. A feasible covector d has d(s)=1,d(t)=0 and a genuine witness cycle containing t among its zero columns. For each active d, Gaussian elimination supplies one particular witness and generators of the full space of d-zero cycles avoiding t. Constant total weight equates the total of the particular witness to the common first witness, and sets the primal weight to zero on every generator. Include the projected switch inequality as an affine equation. An inconsistent system of these equations remains inconsistent under any extension of the ground set.

To preserve a hypothetical failure it suffices to keep the chart and one rooted circuit for each of the two d(q) values. Each circuit is contained in a dimension-four kernel and therefore uses at most four other columns besides t. Thus at most eight extra columns are needed. Parallel representatives may be merged when building these circuits without losing the dual covectors. A parallel copy of t immediately gives a fixed feasible primal with freely varying d(q); zero columns and copies of s are unnecessary. Distinct labels on parallel copies cannot defeat the test: restrict the original putative example to one copy per vector, choosing the fixed chart role first. Original constancy survives restriction and dual variation survives by the replacement argument. This also justifies omitting duplicate vectors from the search.

The standalone enumerate_ground.py covers all 32 chart types before label-gauge reduction and prunes only on inconsistent objectives. Its upper limit permits 12 extra columns, more than the eight needed. It returned "clear 51052 checked configurations" in the sense that 51,052 partial and full configurations were visited (the literal program output is "clear 51052"). Thus for every rank-five binary matroid with both dual marginal values, each positive projected switch implies genuine mixed variation. The result concerns soundness conditional on an existing switch, not unrestricted detection in general rank.
