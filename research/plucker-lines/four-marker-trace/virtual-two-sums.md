# Four-marker composition across virtual matroid two-sums

Let a binary matroid be presented by a tree of proper two-sums. Model assignments are a cycle c and cocycle d with disjoint supports on ordinary elements. A k-bit score is any linear function of c,d. Represent it as a sum of ordinary element weights, allowing separate weights for c_e,d_e. Each original element has exactly one owning torso.

On each two-sum the local cycle restrictions have the same bit c_e at the virtual port and the local cocycle restrictions have the same bit d_e. Every pair with these agreements glues. Ordinary disjointness forces c_e d_e=0 across the cut: the dot product of a local cycle and local cocycle is zero and all terms except the port vanish. With many interfaces this still holds at each interface by applying the identity on the assembled subtree behind that interface. Thus the torso state at each port is one of 00,10,01, and it is legal to use ordinary disjoint cycle/cocycle models on all torsos. Conversely gluing locally disjoint models with matching states yields global ordinary disjointness.

Root the decomposition. For each child limb the three conditional score sets S_m are nonempty: the virtual port is neither loop nor coloop in its assembled pointed limb, hence a pure cycle or pure cocycle through it realizes 10 or 01; the empty assignment realizes 00. Choose any actual score b_m and witness for each m. Every map on these three states is affine in c_e,d_e. In particular the baseline is b_00+(b_10+b_00)c_e+(b_01+b_00)d_e. Add these maps to the torso score, shifting the target for constants.

For any global solution compare actual limb scores to these baseline scores at the actual states. Their total deviation is the sum of at most k of the individual deviations since the score space has dimension k. Change only those limbs' baseline maps, only at their observed states, to return the actual score in that state. On every other state and limb the baseline remains realizable. Enumerating up to k exceptions and their states and scores, then solving the altered linear score instance on the torso, therefore gives exact decision and gluing of witnesses. The torso topology is unchanged.

A parent port state requires one additional affine condition: c_e=1 for 10, d_e=1 for 01, and c_e+d_e=0 for 00 because 11 is impossible on the torso. Bottom-up conditional tables thus need a torso oracle for k+1 mixed markers; only k markers are needed at the root. Sharing the computed child tables prevents exponential branching through the decomposition tree. For fixed k, each torso uses polynomially many oracle calls. With k=4, a root supporting arbitrary four-marker searches may have arbitrarily many nonregular pointed leaves of ordinary score rank at most two: each complete leaf table is computed with at most three markers including its port condition. More generally, the same construction works for fixed-budget tractable heterogeneous torso classes, with their oracle assumptions checked after the indicated score changes. A supplied decomposition is assumed. No arbitrary four-marker algorithm for irreducible binary matroids follows.

## A virtual limb giving a complete reduction to three markers

Suppose one child limb has known four-bit sets S_m in its three states. Cover each true cell (m,b) by a choice of a nonzero direction h and one pair {t_j,t_j+h} contained in each S_j, with the pair for m containing b. This is a constant-size finite test. For every such cover element the map j→t_j is affine on the three port states. Incorporate its affine score correction on the opposite pointed matroid and take all scores and the target modulo the line through h. Solving the resulting unconditioned three-marker instance suffices: if the core chooses state j and its score agrees modulo h, one of the two available limb scores fills the residual. Conversely every global model is captured if all true cells were covered. This handles a completely arbitrary opposite matroid.

In particular suppose a limb's score weights span a space of rank two, and each of its three conditional score sets contains at least three points. The tables use at most two score equations plus the one equation selecting a state. Every nonzero direction in the rank-two score space occurs as a pair difference in every set of at least three points, and every point has a distinct partner in its own set. The covering condition follows. No represented port in either ordinary restriction is needed.

Example: take the two-sum of two Fano planes with port 7 in both, each written as the rank-three vector set 1,…,7. A simultaneous global representation after removing the port has columns [1,2,3,4,5,6,8,16,24,31,23,15]. Give them primal labels [0,3,0,1,0,2,3,8,12,13,13,5] and dual labels [1,0,2,1,0,1,3,0,7,8,8,11]. The first limb's mode score sets are {0,1,2,3}, {0,1,2,3}, {0,2,3} in state order 00,10,01. The second limb's sets are {0,3,4,7}, {1,2,5,6}, {11,12,15}. Direct enumeration gives all four-bit scores except 10. All 24 single-element deletion and contraction minors are connected. In particular no pivoted fundamental graph can expose an articulation at any element. The virtual-limb algorithm nevertheless returns an exact answer for every target. Reproduce with check_fano_limb.py.

Two further cover primitives use fewer modes. On a single specified mode m, any affine plane t+H in S_m with dim H≥2 gives a query using at most three rows: quotient the four scores by H and add the single equation selecting m. A same-direction pair available in both modes 00 and 10 gives a marker-free state restriction to the contraction of the port (where d_e=0). Adjust the score linearly by c_e on that minor, which is read from the unique filling of the outside cycle since e is nonloop. Dually, deletion restricts to 00 and 01, and d_e is determined because e is not a coloop. For all these primitives use actual local scores and lift through stored conditional witnesses. Covering every entry by any of these tests suffices.

The accompanying check_virtual_sums.py independently enumerates direct raw models versus torso gluing on 160 represented sums with two simultaneous child ports and score ranks one through four.
