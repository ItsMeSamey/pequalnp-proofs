# Weak graphic deletion backdoors with locally bounded demands

Write the binary representation as [H P; 0 Q], where H is a full-row-rank reduced incidence representation on the retained edges of the supplied graph, and P,Q are on the k deleted coordinates. Row operations realize this form after basis normalization. Enumerate the disjoint deleted states c_D,d_D. The real primal J has graph boundary T=P c_D (restore the omitted component root coordinates to make every component even), and Q c_D must vanish. Choose a reference T-join J0. The real dual is an ordinary cut y=uH. For every column P_i choose F_i with H F_i=P_i. The deleted dual bits are the vector of ⟨F_i,y⟩ plus any row-space vector of Q. Thus impose at most k additional binary cut markers, quotienting their target by row Q. Subtract the ordinary score of the fixed deleted coordinates.

Partition the real edges into cycle-matroid components B. Both cycle and cut codes split as direct sums because every cycle is a sum of simple cycles contained in the individual components, and cuts form the orthogonal code. Let T_B be the graph boundary of J0 restricted to B. It is independent of the reference choice. The possible joins are exactly independent block T_B-joins. Compute every block's reachable score set and combine the sets by XOR, saving witnesses.

If |T_B|≤d, use the bounded-topology join and cut oracle of the regular manuscript. A forest T_B-join has at most d leaves and at most d branching vertices. At most t+k further fundamental cycles preserve all its cycle scores for a fixed shore. The topology enumeration includes the demand vertices as prescribed anchors. The resulting algorithm is polynomial for fixed k,t,d, even with unbounded total demand, unbounded treewidth inside individual blocks, and references with arbitrarily many edges.

There is a second test on a block of unrestricted demand size. If the subspace of its cut scores lies in the subspace of its homogeneous primal cycle scores, the attainable totals equal the latter space translated by the score of its reference join. The empty cut attains all these values and other cuts cannot add anything beyond them. Both subspaces are computed by elimination. Arbitrarily many blocks passing either test are allowed. The criterion must hold for the branches being decided: failure of both tests on one block is not an infeasibility certificate. Duality gives a cographic contraction result.
