# Conditioning binary affine clauses to hidden rank one

Given equations and clauses as in the [rank-one projection compiler](../rank-projection/README.md), let E be the span of the hidden coefficient rows of the equations. In the quotient of the hidden dual space by E, let V_i be the span of the normals from clause i. Define t as the least dimension of a subspace U such that every V_i has image of dimension at most one modulo U.

**Theorem.** In deterministic time O(3^t poly(L)), where L is the explicit input length including the variable list, one can find an optimal U and compile existential projection into a Boolean circuit of size O(2^t poly(L)). At a supplied observation one can also return a hidden witness within the stated time bound. In particular all three tasks are polynomial on families with t = O(log L).

**Search.** Maintain a Gaussian basis for the equation normals and the choices so far. If all clauses have residual rank at most one, finish. Otherwise find distinct nonzero remainders a,b in one clause. Their two-dimensional span must lose a dimension in any valid extension. Its only nonzero vectors are a,b,a+b, so branch by adjoining each in turn. Each branch raises the dimension by one. If an optimal completion extends the current space, one branch stays inside that completion. Iterative deepening therefore returns an optimum, with O(3^t) total nodes including unsuccessful depths. Offsets play no role in the search.

**Projection.** Choose independent representatives of the returned quotient basis and enumerate their binary values, adding the corresponding equations to the original system. The hidden normals after substitution are restrictions to the common kernel of the augmented equation rows. Two forms have equal restrictions precisely when their difference belongs to the augmented row space. Each fiber consequently meets the rank-one compiler's promise, even for inconsistent observed right-hand sides, which its consistency test rejects. Take the OR of the resulting circuits. Every hidden assignment lies in exactly one fiber. For witness recovery test feasibility, then successively fix a hidden bit to zero if feasible and to one otherwise. Additional equations can only decrease t, because the image of the old conditioning space is a valid choice in the further quotient.

For example, if all normals of each clause lie in the span of a common form a and a clause-specific form b_i, then t is at most one, regardless of the dimension spanned by the b_i. The parameter measures the input representation and may overestimate the effort needed for tautological clauses.

[Implementation](backdoor.py) and [independent checker](check_backdoor.py). Run `python check_backdoor.py` with this folder alongside `rank-projection`. The checker enumerates every subspace for 480 small instances, then checks projected outputs and witness recovery against direct enumeration at 1,875 observations.
