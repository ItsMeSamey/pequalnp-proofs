# Five ANDs with one diagonal-active AND

In the terminal comparison normal form for the 12-crown, suppose one internal AND is true on at least one diagonal and four terminal ANDs are false on all diagonals. Allow arbitrary sharing of the prefix OR gates and arbitrary raw inputs.

Resolve each AND to a selected operand. The 32 resulting OR circuits dominate the physical output. Each diagonal i admits a resolution preserving every false AND at zero, with both raw ports of i absent. Its positive crosses imply that every other row and column raw reaches the output on this resolution. The twelve representative resolutions are therefore distinct.

Propagate direction sets backwards. The four terminal ANDs receive the full direction set, and their eight operands receive the corresponding coordinate halves. The internal AND receives a nonempty union U of these halves. U cannot include both halves of an axis, since a hot diagonal would then make that terminal AND true. Permuting and flipping axes leaves four cases, where U is the union of the zero halves of the first t coordinates, t=1,2,3,4. Two further branch masks intersect U with the two halves of the fifth coordinate. U must be formed before those masks are available. Reverse union cost is at most (2s+1)−(24+s)=s−23≤17, summing outdegree minus one over all vertices with one final observer edge. All raw inputs are essential.

Each raw port has a receiver mask. Receivers of opposite shores at different labels together cover the whole direction space. Matching receivers miss at least their own representative. Replace comparable matching receivers by their larger mask; this preserves both conditions. With d remaining unequal pairs the 12+d distinct receiver masks form an antichain. At most one is a free branch: branches of different axes intersect in at most eight directions, whereas any two receivers share at least ten representative directions. Opposite branches of one axis are disjoint. At most 6−d paid auxiliary masks may feed the raw receivers, and no raw receiver feeds another. If d≤2, choosing opposite shores of the two unequal pairs, or either shore of one, yields twelve masks with pairwise disjoint nonempty complements. For d≥3 the same selection from two unequal pairs and all equal pairs yields 14−d masks. The script five_one_helpers.js enumerates all helpers through depth 6−d and tests every available complement. No packing of 14−d exists for d=3,4,5,6, for any t. The independent degree/one-dirty/replay.cpp gives the same exclusions.

For twelve paired choices, anchor at a receiver with minimum complement cardinality. It has size at most two. If a free branch is chosen, that branch's complement uses at least sixteen directions, and the eleven other complements include a singleton. The helper budget is five without a free receiver and six with one. In the latter case no useful helper contains the direct receiver mask, since it could only feed a receiver comparable to the direct one. Enumerate anchors and optional direct branches modulo signed permutations preserving U. Enumerate proper helper masks in two groups: subsets of the anchor, then all others. A helper in the first group needs only parents in the first group or sources. Sort each group by cardinality, breaking ties numerically. Test every count up to the budget. Each other receiver is either a source or the union of two available masks. Its complement must avoid the fixed anchor and direct complement and have size at least the anchor minimum. Subsumption, a total-volume bound and exact disjoint-set search check for the remaining receivers.

For t>1, U is a paid mask. At the full receiver-construction helper budget, every paid union is either one of the selected receivers or a helper that feeds a receiver. Hence U is a helper or a receiver. In either case no other receiver is an ancestor of U: such a receiver would be comparable to U or to a receiver downstream from U. Its pre-split derivation therefore uses only ordinary source halves and selected helper submasks of U. The program checks reachability with that restriction. If U is not a helper, anchor or direct receiver, reserve its complement as an extra required receiver. At lower helper counts, no activation-cost restriction is imposed; an unused activation or activation-only precursor is then allowed.

The checker dirty_anchor_active.cpp accepts t and a 0/1 flag for absence or presence of a direct receiver.

All eight cases returned no packing. The counts of helper families tested without and with a direct receiver were respectively 1,245,161 and 3,990,999 for t=1; 3,286,660 and 11,851,246 for t=2; 6,155,156 and 19,712,170 for t=3; and 9,912,887 and 19,534,120 for t=4. This rules out every 40-gate circuit in the stated one-dirty/four-clean normal form. The full per-case log is five-one-dirty-census.out.
