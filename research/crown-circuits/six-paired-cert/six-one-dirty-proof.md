# Six ANDs with five clean terminal comparisons

Take a minimum-size paired circuit for twelve labels, with the number of singleton-hot ANDs minimized secondarily. Assume one AND is hot on a singleton, the other five are clean terminal comparisons, no paired receiver is a direct branch, and the reverse bank has at most seventeen binary unions. The twelve distinct receivers are nonempty proper branch masks whose complements are pairwise disjoint. Each receiver charges one union. The remaining five unions pay for any auxiliary masks and the activation of the dirty comparison.

Normalize the five ordinary directions so that its activation U is the union of the zero sides of the first t ordinary coordinates. Both sides of an ordinary coordinate cannot feed U because U must omit a witness direction for a hot singleton. The twelve free features are the ten ordinary halfcubes and the two halves of U partitioned by the dirty direction. The universe has 64 full resolutions. Duplicate observations on the complement of U can be kept without changing disjointness. For t at least two, U must itself be made by a union, and is distinct from each receiver by dirty-activation cancellation. At t=3 the pre-split derivation also needs a mask that is a union of exactly two of the three halfcubes: the first nontrivial union in the cone of U is such a mask. Permute the three ordinary coordinates to fix this pair. This leaves five, four, or three unassigned helpers for t=1, 2, or 3 respectively.

A receiver cannot lie in a proper available helper or free feature of a size-minimal bank. Its definition would be redundant upon directing the label to the available superset. Its outgoing uses cannot be essential elsewhere: a path into a different receiver contradicts receiver incomparability, and a path into a dirty activation contradicts the activation's inclusion in a hot receiver or its inequality to that receiver. Composing the rewiring with reverse-to-forward reconstruction gives a smaller realization. If the superset is free, the enlargement gives the excluded direct case. If it is the activation or a pre-split intermediate contained in the activation, enlarge directly to the activation and apply dirty-activation cancellation.

The minimum-deficit census enumerates an anchor receiver of complement size at most five, since twelve disjoint nonempty complements fit in 64 resolutions. Enumerate unions of free features, keeping one orbit representative under symmetries of the activation, and of the marked pair at t=3. For each anchor enumerate constructible helper families by first adding strict submasks of the anchor in increasing cardinality, then exterior helpers in increasing cardinality. This order respects all proper union dependencies. At each attainable family, form all binary unions of available masks, enforce receiver maximality and the anchor size constraint, and test whether eleven other pairwise disjoint complements avoid the anchor complement. Within the candidate list a smaller available complement dominates a larger one. The sum of the eleven smallest complement sizes gives an additional necessary capacity test. The clique recursion is exhaustive. A full-budget bank must be able to generate U using ordinary features and any paid helpers that are available before the split. Allowing extra derivations when under budget is a relaxation.

The separate hot-anchor census uses a different necessary anchor. A hot receiver uses both dirty branches, so its deficit is the complement of the union of T and its other ordinary signed literals B, independent of the dirty direction. If every hot label has at most one ordinary signed literal outside T, replace each hot receiver by U or one union of U and that literal; interpreting the two dirty branches of cold masks as independent opposite ordinary literals then gives the previously excluded clean paired bank on six axes. Thus some hot receiver fixes at least two of the 5−t axes outside T in its rejecting subcube. This rules out t=4 and t=5 immediately. For the other t, the hot deficit is disjoint from U and has full-resolution size at most 2^(4−t). The hot-anchor census imposes these two conditions without requiring its deficit to be smallest.

All computations apply to the stated normalized paired case. The all-clean bank exclusion, no-direct reduction, activation cancellation, and signed conversion are separate inputs to the argument.

For width one, an independent census uses the 48 observable points as four binary coordinates times a three-element coordinate (inactive, active-left, active-right). With k of the four outside binary coordinates fixed, the hot deficit has one orbit, represented by setting these k coordinates to zero in the inactive state. The required values of k are 2, 3, and 4. The actual OR cone of the hot receiver contains both dirty branches and at least k distinct ordinary sources. A connected fan-in-two cone with k+2 distinct sources contains at least k+1 unions, including the receiver itself. Thus at least k of the five helpers must lie inside the anchor. Duplicate observable masks can be identified: at width one the activation is a free ordinary branch, and every other nonreceiver union with a duplicate observable mask can reuse its earlier copy. The implementation six_one_hot_fast.js enumerates inside helpers then outside helpers, and uses a precomputed binary-union table with a count of the available recipes for each mask. Its terminal test compares the minimum possible deficit volume against 48 and checks the remaining exact packing condition.

The minimum-deficit searches for widths two and three examined 828,671 and 170,272 libraries respectively. The corresponding hot-anchor searches examined 372,034 and 10,842. At width one with k=2,3,4 the 48-point census examined 1,827,589, 673,699, and 208,326 libraries respectively. None admitted twelve disjoint receiver deficits. Together with the signed-bank transfer at widths four and five, this removes the one-dirty case from a minimum paired, no-direct six-AND representative.

The width-one minimum-deficit C++ backend also found zero packings across nine anchor orbits, 112,107 constructible anchor cases and 6,734,492 helper libraries. It uses the 64 full resolutions and does not use the hot-anchor or physical ancestry count. The native hot-cone replay counted exactly 2,709,614 libraries, matching the sum of the three JavaScript arity counts.
