# Six AND gates with one clean terminal comparison

Consider a size-minimal paired threshold-two circuit on twelve labels whose tail has at most 28 gates. Minimize the number of singleton-hot ANDs secondarily, and impose the previously established no-direct-receiver and terminal normal forms. If the representative has six ANDs, it cannot have only one clean terminal AND and five singleton-hot ANDs.

In the transposed network there are at most seventeen binary unions. The twelve distinct, paid receiver masks have pairwise disjoint nonempty complements on the 64 complete resolutions of the six AND choices. The remaining five unions pay for auxiliary masks and dirty-switch activations. Each comparison splits its activation mask by a fresh direction bit. The sole terminal comparison has full activation. Every other activation is a nonempty proper union of already available branches. Record all directions having identical membership in the twelve eventual branches as one trace atom. At least twelve atoms are needed.

Generate chronological profiles, quotienting input-pin flips and rejecting profiles with fewer than twelve atoms. This gives 50,735 profiles. Quotient by all signed permutations of the six axes, using an invariant order from pairwise activation intersections and taking a canonical minimum in the remaining tied axes and orientations. There are 17,945 orbits. Two sparse cases transfer to a different representative. If one root half is a single trace atom, a label missing that atom can be enlarged to the direct opposite half. If none misses it, its root operand accepts every singleton and pair, so the terminal AND can be bypassed. If a dirty activation misses a single atom, a receiver hot there must equal the activation, contrary to the equal-activation cancellation lemma.

Let k count distinct nonfull activation masks outside the set of free branches. They require k nonreceiver unions. All twelve receivers are paid and cannot be predecessors of other receivers or useful activations. Enlarge a receiver contained in any available proper helper to that helper and delete its original dedicated union, or use the corresponding direct/equal-activation transfer. Thus no receiver in a minimal bank can be contained in an available proper helper or branch. Grant the branches and paid activations without a chronology constraint. At most 5−k more helpers are available, and a receiver must then be the union of two available masks.

For k=1,2 also apply the single-branch cancellation. In the minimal representative, each dirty switch has a hot receiver that cannot be replaced by its activation plus one free branch while retaining a negative witness. In particular a dirty activation cannot miss exactly two atoms. The filter removes 160 and 1,368 cases in these respective strata. The argument is in paired-single-branch-cancellation.md.

For k=3,4 enumerate all constructible helper families in increasing support size and test whether twelve receiver deficits are disjoint. For k=0 choose a smallest nonempty receiver deficit as anchor, using a fixed tie break. It has at most floor(L/12) atoms, where L is the number of atoms. At least 24−L singletons are necessary when L is below 24. The earliest selected singleton therefore cannot have more than 2L−24 smaller atoms. Enumerate helpers inside the anchor first and the other helpers second. A strict union dependency respects cardinality; no outside mask feeds an inside mask. At the phase boundary the anchor must be realizable by one union. At each completed library remove deficits containing smaller eligible deficits, check the volume bound, and test eleven disjoint deficits avoiding the anchor.

For k=1,2 choose an activation whose mandatory hot receiver has the fewest possible strict supersets, and fix that receiver. Other deficits need not exceed the anchor in size. For a chosen demand, an activation whose mask varies with selector j must follow switch j in every timeline. Take the transitive closure of these forced successions. Grant as possible precursors all branch masks except those of the demand and its forced successors, restricted to submasks of the demand, together with any other paid activation in their closure. If the original heuristic predecessor set differs from this invariant source set, omit the precursor restrictions and perform the receiver-anchor search on the unrestricted helper bank. Otherwise enumerate constructible precursor subfamilies until the demand can be made by one union and give the remaining helpers arbitrary chronology. The extensions and fixed-helper symmetries are relaxations for every chronological order in a signed orbit. The executable order audit in six_causal_audit.js checks that every hard-anchor profile either uses these invariant sources or requires no precursor shortcut. The final census checks 41,620,974 libraries for k=1 and 29,353,789 for k=2.

All five strata return zero packings. This is a reduction on minimal paired six-AND representatives and uses the existing five-AND bound in the terminal-bypass case. Other six-AND terminal counts and asymmetric unpaired circuits require separate arguments.
