# Paired six-AND profiles with two, three, or four terminal comparisons

Fix p full terminal AND activations and 6−p diagonal-active comparisons in the paired twelve-label threshold tail. The no-direct and terminal normalization assumptions are as in six-single-terminal-proof.md. All p full activations can be placed first in the reverse topological order. Each receives the initial full resolution signal independently, so this reordering preserves every branch and every chronology restriction on the dirty comparisons. Subsequent activations are proper nonempty unions of previously available branch masks. Every direction uses a fresh coordinate for each active split.

Generate all chronological profiles up to input-pin flips and keep those with at least twelve trace atoms. Quotient by signed coordinate permutations and retain an original chronological representative from each orbit. A dirty activation omitting one atom forces equality with a hot receiver and is removed. The single-branch cancellation lemma further requires, for each dirty activation U, at least one receiver strictly containing U which cannot be enlarged to any proper mask U union B for a free branch B. A surviving minimal receiver also cannot be contained in an available proper helper or branch. These necessary conditions are checked on the entire closure of the branch library, so dropping a profile here cannot discard a minimal solution. The root-half shortcut used in the single-terminal calculation is omitted.

Let k count the paid proper activation masks outside the free branch set. The required budget is twelve receiver unions, k activation unions and at most 5−k auxiliary unions. At k≥3 the search directly enumerates helper families and checks twelve disjoint receiver deficits. At lower k choose the dirty activation with fewest hard hot receiver choices and fix one such receiver as an anchor. Enumerate helpers inside its mask before arbitrary exterior helpers. The fixed activation may also require preparatory helpers before its split. For one such demand, grant all branches outside its forced successor cone that lie inside the activation, as well as other paid activations in their closure. Essential selector dependence forces the successor relation in every order. If the representative-only source set differs from this invariant grant, omit the precursor search entirely. Otherwise enumerate an initial helper subfamily from the invariant closure. As soon as the activation is available in one union, grant it and search the remaining budget. Additional fixed helpers reduce the budget, enlarge the source library, and restrict the anchor symmetry group. This is a relaxation at every stopping point; any real precursor cone is covered by its first achievable stopping point. The final exact packing test is identical to the one-terminal model.

The 64 complete resolutions can produce more than 32 trace atoms in these profiles. The widened implementation stores an atom mask of up to 53 bits as an exactly represented JavaScript integer. It computes unions by separate 32-bit low and high pieces and tests disjointness on the two pieces. Beyond 53 atoms the four-terminal two-paid stratum uses BigInt mask construction, with 32-bit low and high deficit halves in the packing recursion. All variants calculate family availability and dominance by the same precomputed OR table. The independent C++ sampling uses all 64 original directions without compression.
