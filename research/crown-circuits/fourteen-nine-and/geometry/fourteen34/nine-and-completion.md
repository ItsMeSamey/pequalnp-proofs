# Completing the nine-AND case at size 34

Use nine-and-quartets.md, the unique-quartic refinement in sole-quartic.md, remote-fork.md, and degree/t14-34. There is at most one branching group U, which if present is the only degree-four vertex. For its component A, at least two detached components must each have a pure flag of degree at least three. At three components remote-fork.md excludes the host. Assume at least four components and let e be the number of edges in A.

All detached pure flags are private. Doubletons are clean because both AND shores need at least two exclusive singletons: resolving an AND and erasing a single exceptional label saves three gates, contradicting C(13)=32. Distinct private clean groups have distinct products. If disjoint groups shared a clean product, deleting one group's k labels would remove its 2k−1 bank/external ORs (including any erased mixed leaf injectors), the product AND, a collector OR and the other group's still-intact OR root. For k=2,3 this contradicts the residual size floor; for k=4 the residual has at most sixteen ORs and eight ANDs on ten inputs, below the secondary AND floor. Two overlapping private trees feeding the same product factor out a common raw and save a gate.

At most one edge joins two private pure groups. On inputs supported on these edges, all mixed flags and all branching pure flags are zero. Every private pure group supplies only its product, whose other input cannot be another private pure group by the preceding common-product argument. Inductively every product and all other decoder signals outside the private raw-only flags remain zero. Thus two different such edges give a falsely rejected positive pair. Four remote private dirty triples would require at least 12−1=11 remote labels.

Let h be the number of remote dirty private flags and s the number of remote vertices. Set all remote flags zero. This removes at least s−h ORs: one distinct dedicated injection per mixed flag and one collector OR per private clean product. The collector can be associated to peel those products, as some terminal must remain for central-only positive pairs. No central raw injector is included.

For e=4 the center is a bare four-edge star. For e=5 there is one subdivided arm or one chord between two leaves. In either case any central noncenter pure flag is a private clean doubleton; neither it nor its product feeds a precollector mixed injector. Erase the single far mixed leaf in the subdivided case, charging its own distinct dedicated OR. Now set U=true. Every nonempty choice of its four neighbor flags is realized using the corresponding star edge labels only. At least two neighbors are mixed because at least two are pendant leaves. The first two mixed neighbor injectors have zero controls. Each control must reject every other singleton, or adding its own bit to that singleton leaves its sole reader unchanged and fails to distinguish the pair. The first control depends on no surviving variable; the second depends on at most the first mixed flag, because private products only enter the collector. Each is therefore zero, by monotonicity and the rejecting singletons. Their controls are already zero before setting U=true. The two corresponding OR deletions are distinct from remote and far-leaf charges. The residual threshold on four neighbors needs at least four further ORs; the all-zero neighbor pattern rejects by monotonicity from rejecting singletons. With a central vertices, the decoder thus needs at least (s−h)+(a−5)+2+4 ORs against q−3=s+a−3. It follows h≥4, whereas at most ten remote labels remain, contradicting the eleven required.

For e≥6 the two remote high-pure components need at least three edges each and another component needs one, so e≤7. The high pair has sizes (3,3) or (3,4); one is a bare cubic star, and all nonleaves in the other form a clique. Any all-mixed extras can be deleted first: associate each mixed group to put its raw flag last. Bypassing these distinct ORs forwards their nonraw side and preserves every syntactic pure path. Remove the remaining remote mixed flags in the same way. For k nontrivial edge-containing remote pure components and h isolated pure flags, the available slack is k+h−[h>0]. A nontrivial component saves all but one flag by reverse peeling and a final anchor edge; if h>0 at least one isolated pure flag saves an OR along its retained output path. The degree-four center requires slack at least two. Here at most two pure components survive and at least one is isolated if both survive, so slack is at most one.

The only extra component with a pure flag would give 6+3+3+2, with a pure doubleton center in the last two-edge path. Remove its two actual labels, saving the path bank OR, its two mixed injectors, the private clean AND and a collector OR. Exactly five gates vanish by C(12)=29. All retained pure flags still have output paths, since a dead bank OR could be removed below this minimum. The graph has three fewer flags and loses exactly three decoder ORs besides the bank OR, so its budget remains q'−3. The two detached bare stars now have at most two isolated pure flags after bypassing remote mixed injectors (preserving the nonraw sides as above). The same pure slack contradiction applies.

With no branching group, there cannot be a first AND. Before it, any earliest shared OR would be a branching pure group. Otherwise its two operands are single-use pure OR trees. Their overlap yields the factoring saving, while disjoint private groups cannot share a clean terminal as shown above. This excludes size 34 with nine ANDs.
