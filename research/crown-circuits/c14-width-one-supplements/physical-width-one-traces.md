# Physical bridge at any AND count for a terminal-clean width-one representative

Take a live fourteen-input monotone fan-in-two threshold-two circuit with N≤34 gates, each raw feeding exactly two OR occurrences. Assume exactly one dirty AND D, and every other AND has its output observed through an OR-only path to the output and does not feed another AND. Suppose D reaches exactly one clean AND port through OR-only paths. This is the terminal-clean width-one physical stratum. No bound of five on the number of ANDs is needed.

A direction chooses one operand at each AND. Replace every AND by its chosen operand; the output is an OR of a set of raw labels. The original function is the conjunction of all these directed outputs. Each chosen output dominates the original by monotonicity. Conversely on any rejecting input, choose a false operand at every false AND, arbitrarily at true ones. An induction preserves all original wire evaluations for that direction. Hence the deficit directions of a fixed raw singleton are nonempty and different singletons have disjoint deficits (otherwise their positive pair rejects too).

All the clean selectors are observable in every direction by the terminal assumption. D is observable exactly when the receiving terminal selects its distinguished port T. There is no direct OR-only path from the dirty output to the observer, since that would accept a hot singleton. The same argument excludes an all-OR raw-to-observer path. When T is unselected the dirty selector is invisible, giving state I; when T is selected the dirty selector contributes one of the two active states. For every other clean AND its two pins are independent outside literals. In the backwards OR-only port mask of a raw, the continuation pin accepts both active states, its opposite accepts I, and each dirty pin accepts only its own active state. Taking unions therefore gives exactly the state-product form in width-one-arity-independent-minor.md. Directions with the same state and outside choices have identical observed raw sets.

At every live wire, taking the union of its outgoing occurrence labels costs at most its outdegree minus one. Label the unique observer by the empty set; an AND-input occurrence supplies its port's primitive acceptance; an OR-input occurrence supplies the destination wire's previously computed union. The g+14 live wire nodes have 2g+1 outgoing occurrences including that observer. The union budget is g−13, including fourteen distinct final unions at the two-read raws; these are never used to form other unions. For any fourteen-input minimum of the stated physical form, the conditional arity-independent state minor consequently applies directly, including the six-AND case.

The conclusion is restricted to the displayed physical hypotheses. The bridge does not assert that converting an arbitrary nonregular or nonterminal circuit preserves its original dirty width.
