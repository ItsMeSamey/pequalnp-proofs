# Existence of a terminal-clean regular representative under an AND and dirtiness cap

Assume C(13)≥32. Given a fourteen-input threshold-two circuit of size ≤34, choose minimum size subject to its upper bounds on AND count and number of singleton-dirty ANDs. Remove dead gates and gate identities. The following operations preserve the size cap and both resource caps. Three or more deletions upon pinning a raw to zero are impossible by the thirteen-input bound.

First take any singleton-clean AND C feeding another AND along a path. In the entire current circuit replace its old outgoing occurrences with constant zero, simplify, and append the OR of C with the modified output. Keep C's original input cone. When C is false the old output is preserved, and when C is true the original input has at least two raws, so the new output is correctly true. At least one original direct reader vanishes by constant propagation, paying for the new OR. These readers are downstream of C and cannot belong to its retained input cone. The singleton evaluations of remaining old gates are unchanged since C was already false on them. No new AND is introduced. The retained C now reaches the observer by ORs alone and feeds no further AND. A previously terminal-only clean gate cannot acquire a path to any AND: all rewiring among old gates only removes paths or bypasses former identities, and the new outgoing connection ends at the output OR. Repeat; the number of surviving clean gates that reach an AND strictly decreases. All remaining clean gates are live, so each reaches the output through ORs alone.

Now apply raw relocation from normalization.md. Its low-arity deletion contradictions hold without singleton-clean assumptions. The sole-OR relocation only decreases old AND signals on singleton inputs; it may cool dirty gates, and never heats a clean one. It introduces no gate path to an AND from a previously terminal-only clean signal: among old wires it removes paths or bypasses them, and the new gate is an output OR. It preserves AND count or lowers it. Minimum size within the caps rules out any simplification saving gates. The per-raw finite forward traversal and invariance of the other raw fanouts give a two-OR-reader representative as in normalization.md.

If a dirty gate cooled or disappeared, redo terminalization and regularization. Any need to repeat arises only on strict decrease of dirty-gate count, so after finitely many rounds all clean gates are terminal-only and every raw has two OR readers. Taking unrestricted resource caps gives C(14)≥34 since specializing any regular raw saves at least two gates. Thus any circuit initially of size ≤34 is actually minimum globally. This process may reduce AND count or dirtiness and may change the set of clean ports reached by a surviving dirty AND. Width strata classify the final representative.
