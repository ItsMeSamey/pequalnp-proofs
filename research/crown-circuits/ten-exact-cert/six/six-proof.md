# Six switches for ten-input threshold two at size 23

Ten essential raw variables and at most 23 gates give at most fourteen reverse binary unions. A direct receiver specializes to size at most twenty on nine inputs, contrary to the 21-gate lower bound. In minimum-dirty normal form ten distinct raw sinks require ten joins and cannot feed mandatory nonfull activations. Every such activation W not equal to a branch requires its own join, so there are q=4−|W| free helper joins. See a5-cert/PROOF.md and the general cancellation argument in six-paired-cert/paired-single-branch-cancellation.md.

profile6.cpp 10 4 generates all chronological switch activation sequences with at least ten distinct observable atoms. canon6.cpp canonicalizes simultaneous signed permutations of the six coordinates and their activations. There are 206,310 chronological representatives and 41,719 signed orbits. At q=0,1,2,3,4 the counts are 16,118, 18,089, 6,400, 1,027, 85. At q=0 and 1, sixcheck.cpp tests all 16,118 and 1,171,903 helper libraries respectively. Six relaxed hits remain in each case. The all-axis least-fixed-point chronology and dirty-hot checks of sixexact.cpp remove all twelve. The q=0 exact ledger independently tests every profile.

For q=2 and 3, each dirty activation U needs a hot receiver R which contains U and is not contained in any proper union of U and one branch B. Otherwise reattach each hot receiver to one union of U and the appropriate B at no additional cost. Each raw deficit remains nonempty and only shrinks, and singleton activation at the selected dirty split disappears. This physical cancellation applies at any arity. In particular U cannot omit just one or two trace atoms. For two omitted atoms, any proper hot R unequal to U includes exactly one of them, and R is itself a union of branches, one of which separates these two atoms in the required orientation. Equal-activation cancellation handles the one-atom case. filter-ten.py leaves 1,657 hard q=2 orbits and 283 hard q=3 orbits.

six_anchor.js forms the union closure of all branch masks using BigInt and compresses the 64 directions only by joint branch membership. It chooses for an anchor the smallest candidate class among the mandatory hard receiver for each dirty activation or an ordinary receiver of minimum deficit, which misses at most floor(L/10) of L observable atoms. An anchor cannot be contained in a branch, since it could be enlarged to produce a direct raw, nor in a nonfull activation, by enlargement and equal-activation cancellation. The helper subsets of the anchor can all be formed first, and the anchor must then be obtainable in one join. Other helpers come after, with masks of increasing cardinality and then integer order within each phase. No strict supermask of the anchor can serve as a useful helper, by incomparability on paths to another raw and the preceding subset test on paths to an activation. For each library the checker packs nine further nonempty pairwise disjoint deficits disjoint from the anchor, all formed by final binary joins. It grants the charged activations without chronological tests, a relaxation.

When q=4, every activation is full or a branch. Each resulting forest has six nodes, with unordered roots, an unordered pair of signs at each node and an unordered list of children on each sign. forest_profiles.js regenerates all 85 forest shapes with ten or more observable atoms. six_forest.js restricts the anchor/helper search to orbits of signed physical forest automorphisms preserving the chosen anchor class, then pointwise fixing the anchor and prior helpers. No precursor ordering is used. The 85 forest rows contain 3,493,930 visits and zero hits. The q=2 hard ledger contains 1,407,266 visits and zero hits. The six-q*.tsv static scans without hard cuts provide additional checks independently of that threshold-specific transfer.

The final q=3 ledger contains 283 rows and 10,203,877 candidate libraries with zero hits. This completes all forty-one thousand seven hundred nineteen six-switch orbits. Together with the four-, five-, and seven-AND lower bounds and the sixteen-OR lower bound excluding eight or more ANDs, no circuit of size 23 exists. The explicit 24-gate upper certificate therefore fixes the minimum exactly.
