# Nine-input unrestricted monotone threshold two

Assume a monotone fan-in-two AND/OR circuit computes threshold two on nine variables using at most twenty gates. The physical lower bound of fourteen OR gates permits at most six AND gates. Apply the reverse normal form and direct-input removal from threshold-six-minors/TEN.md at arity nine. The direct-input alternative using a stronger lower bound on the smaller arity is unnecessary. A direct raw can be specialized away at saving three and an AND, after which splitting a direct of the smaller circuit (or any input, if none is direct) reconstructs the original arity with no direct raw, no increase in size or dirty AND count. Full existing activations may be padded to six switches. There are at most twelve transposed binary unions, of which nine pay the distinct receivers and three remain for nonfree activations and extra helpers.

The switched-mask enumeration with six chronological splits, at least nine distinct directional traces, and three paid nonfree activations gives 161,917 chronological bit-translation representatives. Their signed coordinate-permutation orbits number 25,680. Ordered by zero, one, two, or three extra helper unions, their counts are 18,089, 6,411, 1,089, and 91. The plateaus are independently reproduced by refining trace partitions as coordinates are introduced and identifying chronological profiles via adjacent swaps of independent switches.

For each orbit, grant all switch branches and mandatory nonfree activation masks as sources. After adding the spare helpers, a final one-join receiver cannot itself be used to construct a different receiver since the proper receiver masks pairwise cover the cube and are therefore incomparable. No proper receiver can be used on a union-only path constructing a nonfull activation in this normalization. Indeed that activation is contained in a receiver of a singleton activating its physical dirty gate; any such ancestor receiver would therefore coincide with that activation, which the minimizing redirection excludes. Thus the extra helper libraries from the ten-input checker apply with demand nine and with exactly three minus the number of distinct mandatory activations as the spare-helper allowance.

The chronological test for any remaining zero-spare-helper profiles is elementary. Their last three switch activations have sizes 56, 60, 60 out of 64 directions. They are all mandatory, so a single binary union must construct the first. The later two masks of size 60 cannot feed this union. Branches of distinct earlier switches lie in distinct coordinate halves, whose union has size at most 48. The two branches of the same switch unite to its exact activation, of size 16, 32 or 64 at each of the first three switches. The full source cannot contribute to a proper mask. No operands therefore produce the required size 56.

All 24,500 profiles with at most one spare helper are checked by nine-direct.cpp. Precisely six (all with no spare helper) pass the relaxed packing test and are excluded as above. All 1,180 profiles with two or three spare helpers fail the relaxed packing test in nine-dense.cpp. The checker verify-nine-chronology.py verifies the six profile IDs, their chronological mask sizes, the full profile index coverage, and the ledger totals. The independent orbit computation verifies exact membership of each canonical row by legal adjacent swaps.

The matching 21-gate upper bound places the inputs in a 3×3 array. Compute all three row disjunctions and all three column disjunctions at cost twelve. Apply the four-gate threshold-two-of-three circuit to each family and OR the results. Any two distinct cells occupy distinct rows or distinct columns, so the circuit is correct. The executable nine-upper.py checks all 512 assignments. Thus the unrestricted monotone fan-in-two minimum at arity nine is exactly 21 gates. By splitting a variable at a cost of three gates, arities eight and seven require at least 18 and 15 gates, respectively.

The 56-direction obstruction is invariant under arbitrary switch reorderings. Even granting branches of every switch in advance, any two noncomplementary signs on distinct axes cover at most 48 directions. Complementary signs of the same switch reproduce only that switch's activation. The only switch of size 56 is the very one whose activation is needed, and its own branches cannot precede its activation. Every other paid mask has size 60. Thus the check applies to the entire signed-permutation orbit regardless of which topological ordering realizes a circuit.
