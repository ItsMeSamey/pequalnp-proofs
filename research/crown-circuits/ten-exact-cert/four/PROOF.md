# Ten-input threshold two at four ANDs

A monotone binary circuit computing threshold two on ten inputs and using at most four AND gates needs at least 24 total gates. Fewer than four ANDs supply fewer than ten rejecting representatives. At size at most 23 with four ANDs the transposed union budget is 23−10+1=14. Every raw receiver covers at least nine of the distinct witnesses and therefore cannot be one of the branch sources, each of which covers at most eight of the sixteen resolution directions. There are at most four additional helper joins beyond the ten paid receivers.

For a smallest such circuit, terminal-clean normalization gives d dirty ANDs and p clean terminal ANDs (d+p=4) which are joined by a final OR. Here clean means false on all singletons. Normalization preserves the AND cap: bypassing an AND to a clean parent, moving clean disjuncts out of mixed ORs at no net OR cost, and rebalancing the final OR of clean product gates never increases the count. The changes preserve or reduce total size.

Represent each AND pin as a union of its formal raw contribution and a subset of earlier dirty outputs. mk4.py enumerates every such skeleton up to interchanging the two input pins. For each of the sixteen global parent selections, its set of reached formal pin contributions is a monotone clause. The AND/OR output on pin contributions is true precisely when every clause is hit, by the simultaneous false-parent selection at a rejecting input. It suffices to retain its inclusion-minimal clauses. Each singleton misses a minimal clause and these ten witnesses are distinct (each input pair is accepted). A dirty gate must be true at the componentwise complement of at least one such clause, by monotonicity from a singleton where it is hot.

For each profile project its eight pin-branch masks to the retained minimal clauses, keeping one actual direction per clause. Projection preserves coverage and the witnesses. A single free branch still contains at most eight of these actual directions, fewer than the other nine receivers' witnesses. The receivers still form an antichain, since they have nonempty pairwise disjoint deficits. Beginning from the free sources, enumerate up to four additional helper unions, sorted on population count and numerical value. A helper with singleton deficit cannot feed a strictly larger proper receiver. Once the helper bank is fixed, list every output available by one more join (allowing identical operands). The checker tests for ten mutually disjoint nonempty deficits. All available singleton deficits can be taken by replacing an overlapping larger choice or being added where unused, and a larger available region containing a smaller region can be discarded.

Exactly 36 basis families remain: 28 with two clean terminals, seven with three, one with four, and none with one. None yields ten pairwise covering receivers. Compile ten23.cpp with a C++20 compiler, generate its input with python mk4.py, and run the binary on the generated text. Each pass line records a completed exhaustive family and prints its three-helper census; the source fixes the maximum search depth at four.

An independent chronological profile generation avoids terminal normalization altogether. chron4.py enumerates the activation of each switch as either the entire sixteen-direction mask or a union of earlier branches, then splits by the new coordinate. It keeps one representative for each trace atom and discards a profile only when there are fewer than ten atoms. It lists 229 distinct resulting basis systems. All fail in the same four-helper checker. Run python chron4.py followed by ten23 on this second generated input. chron4.log records the full replay.
