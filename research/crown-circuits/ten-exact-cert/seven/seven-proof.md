# Exclusion of seven ANDs at size 23 for ten-input threshold two

Assume T₂¹⁰ has a minimum 23-gate circuit with seven ANDs. Clean-terminal normalization, the T₂⁹ lower bound of 21, and the direct-input three-gate specialization give exactly two OR readers for every raw. Flatten single-use ORs into their OR consumers. The ten labels become edges of a simple graph on the resulting OR groups. A pure group has only raw operands. No pure group has arity one. For arity two, delete both raw variables. Their four raw incidences account for the full budget by size(T₂⁸)≥19. A branching pure output would cost an extra fanout; a single-use pure group instead feeds an AND, whose other operand stays nonzero on the deletion, since otherwise it implies the pure output and the AND was redundant. This incurs an extra one-sided AND. Hence every pure group has degree at least three.

Let p denote the number of pure groups. The sixteen ORs and twenty raw incidences give r=p−4=Σ(t−1) over mixed groups with t nonraw operands. Thus 4≤p≤6. The star argument of verification/threshold-twelve/eight-and-star.md yields k+max(d,2)≤r+4 for each pure group of degree k with d pure neighbors. On retaining only the edges whose two endpoints are pure, every mixed group loses its raw operands. Its residual costs at most t−1 ORs. Thus the resulting edge-threshold function uses at most r ORs on the formal pure-vertex indicators, with arbitrary ANDs. Each choice of one input per OR in the dual resolution of a monotone circuit gives a conjunction. The original function is the disjunction of these at most 2^r conjunctions, since on any accepting input one true input can be chosen at each true OR. Hence the edge function has a DNF of at most 2^r monomials.

If p=4, the star bound contradicts k≥3. At p=5 all five pure vertices have k=3 and d≤2. Their total degree of 15 gives at least five internal edges since all remaining incidences belong to at most ten edges. Thus each d=2, and simplicity makes the internal graph a five-cycle. Each consecutive triple is the support of two incident edges, and is therefore accepted. Every acceptable monomial avoids being contained in a single edge. A two-literal monomial is therefore a diagonal of the cycle, contained in at most one of the five consecutive triples. A larger monomial also covers at most one. Thus five are needed, exceeding two.

For p=6 each k≤4, and any degree-four vertex has d≤2 and therefore at least two incidences at mixed endpoints. Its degree and the other five minima give total pure degree at least 19, leaving only one incidence at a mixed endpoint. Thus every pure group has k=3. The two remaining mixed incidences permit eight or nine internal edges, with maximum internal degree three. For these graphs, seven_graphs.py generates every singleton-edge rejection set and every pair-of-edges acceptance set. It retains every monomial not contained in a singleton edge and enumerates covers with four such monomials. There are 990 labelled eight-edge graphs and 70 nine-edge graphs under consideration. None admits a four-term cover. A separate JavaScript enumeration traversing the complete 2^15 edge mask space yielded the same 1,060 negatives.
