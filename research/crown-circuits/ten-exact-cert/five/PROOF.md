# Five ANDs do not suffice at size 23 for ten-input threshold two

With ten essential variables, 23 physical AND/OR gates give at most 14 transposed joins. Choose a cleaned size-minimal circuit with the smallest dirty count. In the standard direct specialization, a direct receiver would give a circuit on nine variables with at most twenty gates, excluded by the 21-gate lower bound. Thus the ten pairwise incomparable receivers all require distinct final joins, leaving at most four other joins. The existing equal-activation cancellation lemma rules out a receiver coinciding with a nonfull dirty activation. No receiver can feed a nonfull activation: the activation is contained in the receiver of a singleton which fires its gate, and distinct receivers are incomparable. Remaining free masks are the two branch masks per switch.

profiles.cpp enumerates five-step chronological activations from unions of branches already generated or the full mask, with signed translations preserving earlier activations. Each receiver must miss a different observable atom, so at least ten atoms are required. There are 2,435 chronological representatives and canonicalfive.cpp selects 703 signed-coordinate-permutation representatives. They have 22/120/321/240 with 0/1/2/3 distinct charged activations, respectively.

exact.cpp grants all branch masks and mandatory nonfree activations, then enumerates up to four minus the number of mandatory activations additional binary joins in strict increasing population-count/bit-pattern order. At each library it builds all proper final binary-join receivers. Minimal nonempty deficit sets suffice: shrinking a disjoint deficit leaves it nonempty and preserves pair cover, and can only improve the required dirty hot witnesses. For every candidate ten-packing it checks all nonfull activations are hot and performs an order-independent least-fixed-point replay from the full activation, generating branches when enabled and permitting joins only to selected helpers or charged activations. This replay remains a necessary condition under signed coordinate permutations. Every library was rejected: 5,590,625 visited, zero hits. Each a5.tsv row records orbit ID, hit flag, mandatory count, library count, and packing-frontier count.

Compile the three C++ sources with a C++20 compiler, generate chron.txt with profiles, pipe it through canonicalfive to obtain canon.txt, then run exact 0 4 on canon.txt. Sort order of the profile files is part of the row index. This bounds five ANDs; the four-AND exclusion is threshold-ten-23/PROOF.md. A separate graph argument for seven ANDs is included beside this file. Six ANDs require a different argument.
