# Paying the full order of a shore with at most one exception

Pay the dedicated raw-last OR injector of every mixed flag and one clean collector OR for each private clean doubleton with its distinct dedicated product. For such a product D∧P the opposite partner P is shared, since erasing both labels of D already saturates the five-gate size gap. Distinct clean private products have distinct partners by factoring inside the collector. The same property for clean private triples follows by the seven-gate arity gap.

If one exceptional pure flag B is left, B has a path to the output avoiding all these products. Otherwise B must enter a product through its partner (the other input is a private raw-only group). Take the latest reachable such partner in topological order. Its other outgoing occurrence gives a path avoiding all the products: re-entering one would require a later partner as the preceding vertex, since no such path can enter a raw-only group. Equal partners for two products have been excluded. Bypassing the mixed raw injectors preserves the chosen path because it uses their nonraw operands. Collector bypasses preserve it because the paid products feed only the clean collector. With the other flags erased B thus still has a path, and erasing B must disable at least one OR since a positive survives on the opposite graph. This pays the last flag. Excess dead gates or repeated operands can only strengthen the bound.

The finite test eleven-role-debits.py uses this lemma conservatively with every private group of size above two designated exceptional. It enumerates mixed, private and branching assignments, checks the previously established admissible branching positions and adjacency restrictions, and allows at most one private-private edge and one branching doubleton. A branching doubleton or triple has two direct OR readers. Neither can be a clean collector or paid mixed injector within distance two. A path along ORs from B to a mixed flag at distance one absorbs their shared raw. At distance two along B–W–V, the first raw heats both W and V, hiding addition of the second raw. Distinct mixed injectors can absorb at most one direct B-reader credit each. Forty-nine of 71 rows fail. The G44 rows are addressed in eleven-bipartite-shore.md.
