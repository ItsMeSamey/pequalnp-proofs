# Eleven-flag exclusion at fourteen raw inputs and eight ANDs

The claim is that no regular size-34 monotone threshold-two host with fourteen inputs and exactly eight ANDs has eleven flags. It does not exclude larger flag counts. Its decoder has at most seventeen gates and nine ORs.

* The general incidence and physical role prerequisites are in the research fork at commit 06d8b2eb1dc87dd05e000e879ce86616be735835, under research/crown-circuits (including fourteen-eight-high-degree, fourteen-host-portals/four-plus-six and the earlier threshold exact archives). Threshold thirteen needs 32 gates in the established arity ledger. The proof-checker provenance for the initial graph-cut SAT/DRAT ledger is f66f5c73f2a5e471ce4cc9a588fc2fbdb2f533d5/research/crown-circuits/thirteen-exact/degree/t13-seven/graph-cut. These are historical prerequisites; the lightweight replay does not rerun these SAT proofs or the lower arity searches.

For 2+9 and 3+8 see degree/t14-34/eleven-small-components.md plus eleven-small-remotes.py, which checks all 23, 5 and 19 dense subcubic shapes in the three relevant order/edge strata against the archived obstructions.
