# Occurrence-star debits at a physical quartet hub

Use a globally size-minimal regular 34-gate circuit on fourteen inputs. Let U be a shared OR group with four distinct raw labels. On the occurrence-star slice retain the four opposite occurrences as distinct input variables, clamp one U-side pin true and the other raw pins false. This computes threshold two on four inputs and fixes 24 raw input pins. A hot shared OR hub is one whose wire becomes true when U alone is forced true with all raw inputs false. Write t for the number of such hubs other than U. Physical hubs are shared gates, not arbitrary receiver flags.

Classify each gate and each of its input occurrences as fixed (constant on the entire slice) or variable. If a gate's output is fixed, at least one input is fixed: on the monotone Boolean cube this follows by evaluating the two extremal assignments. A gate with one fixed input and nonconstant output can be bypassed. A fixed gate can be removed. Let I count gates with exactly one fixed input and fixed output. Let S sum fanout minus one over all fixed gates, counting input slots rather than distinct destination gates. A fixed gate has positive fanout, since the original circuit is minimum and its output on this slice is nonconstant.

The pin conservation identity gives fixed input slots = 24 plus the sum of fanouts of fixed gate outputs. Counting the removed gates by whether they have one or two fixed inputs gives exactly 24+S+I removals (before any extra simplifications). The remaining four-input circuit requires at least seven gates, so S+I≤3. The hub U and the t heated hubs each consume one unit of S. Every other branching pure group that is not a distinct raw neighbor of U is cold. In particular, t=2 forces exactly three fixed shared gates U,H1,H2, each with fanout two; all three are hot. There are no one-sided fixed gates, and no fixed gate can be reached from a variable pin. The published Lean star_saving statement itself takes a LexMinimum witness. The direct conservation argument here needs only a globally minimal regular circuit and a correct occurrence-star slice, not lexicographic choice of its AND count.

Each nonneighbor private pure doubleton or triple V has a shared opposite operand P in its dedicated AND. For a clean product, erase V's k=2 or 3 labels. The 2k−1 bank/external ORs, the AND, and its collector OR exhaust the size gap 2k+1 to C(12) or C(11). If P were single-use its last gate would die too. Its root cannot have been a counted external OR because a raw from V feeding such a root makes the product dirty. For a dirty triple, its unique hot label t0 leaves two cold labels by the two-exclusive-singleton bound. Its product has a proper OR reader and no such reader can contain a V raw: the hot raw is redundant, while factoring a cold raw z via (V∧P)∨z=V∧(P∨z) leaves only one exclusive singleton on the V side. The size bound again allows no single-use P to die as an extra gate. If P's root is instead part of a counted external contraction then its OR group contains t0; reassociating both private input trees to place t0 last lets their common raw factor out, saving one gate. Thus P is shared. Two clean private products cannot share P, since their two terms factor inside the collector.

Let m count the nonneighbor private pure flags of size two or three, b the nonneighbor branching pure flags of those sizes, and v the number of the m partners that are variable on this slice. Their v product ANDs are fixed zero with variable partner and count in I. Every other such product takes a slot from a fixed shared partner. Small branching groups have two OR readers and are fixed zero here, so they use 2b additional slots from fixed shared producers. None of these is one of the product AND slots.

There are at most 2S slots out of fixed shared gates since every fanout f≥2 has f≤2(f−1). At least t of these slots relay the U-only activation to further hot hubs: take an active path to each reachable hub and partition at the traversed shared hot gates. The subpath into each new hub starts with a distinct outgoing shared slot, since intermediate gates of fanout one cannot split paths before that hub. If an active shared AND lies on a path, it can itself be a splitting vertex; counting it only increases the lower bound on used slots. The relay slots can neither feed the product ANDs above (whose other input is cold) nor leave cold branching groups. Therefore m−v+2b+t≤2S and S+v≤3, yielding m+2b+t+v≤6.

Each nonneighbor private clean flag alone consumes one distinct debit, either its shared partner or its one-sided fixed product. Thus at most three such flags can exist, and at t=2 their distinct partners all belong to U,H1,H2. At t=2 there are in total at most four nonneighbor private flags of small size, even when dirty partners repeat.

The source arithmetic expands the rows used by ThresholdOccurrenceDebit.telescope. This note is an analytic consequence; it does not assert that a new Lean theorem has been checked.
