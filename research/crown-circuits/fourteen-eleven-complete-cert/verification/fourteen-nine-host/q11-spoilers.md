# Star spoiler tests for the eleven-vertex two-component hosts

For opposite components R,L, call a flag y of R a spoiler of an edge e of L if the decoder accepts y together with the two endpoints of e. To prove a quota on the number of spoiled edges, suppose a candidate set S of edges is entirely safe. Let t be its size and v the number of endpoints it covers. Build those v flags from t independent raw edge labels in 2t−v ORs. Pin y true, with its d distinct neighbors driven by d independent virtual star-edge labels; all other R flags are zero. The result computes threshold two on t+d inputs. Every singleton rejects by the safe assumption or a real star edge. Every pair contains the union of distinct real edges, and zero rejects by monotonicity.

A connected set of at least two retained flags permits peeling any others farthest-first, saving one OR per removed flag. The witness is a surviving edge at the predecessor versus its union with the predecessor-to-deleted edge. On L retain a smallest connected superset of V(S); a subset dynamic program computes its size. On R either keep the component or first peel to y and its neighbors. Claim the additional one-gate saving from y=true only if the remaining R-only tests demonstrate essentiality: some y-neighbor a has another neighbor b, so ab versus ab+ay witnesses the need for y. Within the peeled star this requires an edge among its neighbors. Even when there is no such witness, the star restriction remains valid without the one-gate deduction.

Starting budgets are 17 total gates and 9 ORs. Compare the constructed costs against known total lower bounds, the 2n−4 OR floor, and the seventeen-OR requirement at n=9,size=21. Any impossible S also makes its supersets impossible. The largest remaining candidate safe set gives the individual minimum spoil count. One can erase a connected component of size s at cost s−1 in ORs. If it contains two adjacent guaranteed-spoiler vertices, retain that edge during peeling, then remove each endpoint using its own cross-edge witness, since witnesses involve no other vertex of that component. This pays s ORs. Add the surviving opposite component's archived local OR floor.

Sources q11_peeled_spoilers.py and degree/t14-34/eleven-atlas.tsv generate q11-peeled-spoilers.tsv. On that input, the necessary test excludes 205 of 281 (5,6) hosts and 269 of 411 (4,7) hosts. These counts are standalone and overlap exclusions from other arguments. The calculation enumerates subsets of the small edge sets; no SAT solver is used.
