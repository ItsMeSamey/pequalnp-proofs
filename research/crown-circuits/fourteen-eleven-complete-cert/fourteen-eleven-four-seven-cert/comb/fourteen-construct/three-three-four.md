# The last two marked four-plus-seven hosts

Take the residual decoder after deleting the marked branching flag from G447 at vertex 1 or G450 at vertex 6. The decoder has at most fifteen gates, including seven ORs. In the first case the components are K4, a triangle, and a path on three vertices. In the second case they are K4 and two triangles. The second branching flag has degree two: its residual has twelve real edges, not eleven. The argument below uses only the residual gate budgets and the edge-union tests.

Select a triangle and let its other two components be L. Every one of its vertices v must spoil some L edge, meaning that the isolated flag v together with the flags for that edge is accepted. Otherwise set v to one, encode its two neighboring flags by two independent virtual star edges, and keep all L edges as real inputs. Every nonempty virtual selection realizes exactly the flags of the chosen edges. With only real inputs, each real singleton rejects by the no-spoiling assumption, every pair accepts by monotonicity, and zero rejects by monotonicity. The pin removes at least one gate since v is essential: adding v to the triangle edge on its other two vertices changes the output. In G447 there are eight L edges and seven L flags, costing 16−7=9 OR gates; this constructs threshold two on ten inputs at size at most 9+14=23, below 24. In G450 there are nine L edges and seven flags, costing eleven ORs; threshold two on eleven inputs would have size at most 11+14=25, below 27.

Erasing the selected triangle consequently removes at least three ORs. Classify gates as live or cold at all L flags true and all triangle flags zero. If k ORs have one cold operand and m have two, trace from the k cold operands, keeping one arbitrary cold child of each cold AND and both children of a cold OR. Each spoiled singleton is a distinct leaf in the resulting DAG: some exit operand is true on that singleton with L all true, and tracing its true branches reaches that raw. The number of distinct leaves is at most k+m. Thus k+m≥3 and no more than four ORs remain on L.

On the other hand K4 together with a three-vertex path or triangle requires at least five ORs, even with arbitrary ANDs. Suppose there are at most four. Each K4 flag must spoil an edge of the three-vertex graph; otherwise its three virtual star edges together with the path's two or triangle's three real edges give a threshold circuit on five or six inputs. The three-flag edge bank needs one or three ORs respectively. The totals at most five or seven violate the six- and eight-OR floors. All four K4 flags therefore spoil. By k4-four-loss-forest.md all four ORs are distinct singleton entries from the K4 side, and there is no internal cold OR. On the K4-only projection, the first three entry ORs bypass, by the chronology argument in k4-six-or.md: when all earlier raws are true, later raws can fill the prefix to exactly two, so the incoming flagged raw must affect the output. The projected K4 function, threshold three on four flags, would have at most one OR. Its four minimal triples cannot be obtained by one OR amid ANDs, since that gives at most two distinct minimal terms. This contradicts the four-OR upper bound on L, excluding both marked hosts.

The isolated-edge packet excludes the other three disconnected marks; the connected residuals were previously excluded by the cubic, path and cycle lower bounds. Together these exhaust the 28 marks in the q=11 two-component 4+7 stratum. This last case calculation is local to that stratum.
