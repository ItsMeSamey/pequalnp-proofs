# Four spoiled K4 flags and at most four lost ORs

Classify wires by whether they vanish (cold) or survive (live) when R is zero. Let k count exit ORs with one cold operand and m the ORs with both operands cold. Assume k+m≤4. Since each K4 flag spoils an L edge, for each small raw a some exit cold operand is true when all L raws and just a are set: some exit must change in the spoiling test, and monotonicity enlarges the L input.

Expand backwards from the k exit operands, choosing one cold operand of each cold AND visited and both operands of each cold OR. Use a common choice if a gate is reached more than once. Every resulting traced DAG contains all four distinct raw leaves: the exit that is true at the particular small singleton a traces to that raw on some branch. With one super-root having k outgoing occurrences, every other nonleaf node has one child or is one of at most m binary splits. The edge-versus-vertex count in this connected DAG bounds the number of leaves by k+m, with strict inequality if any node has multiple incoming occurrences. Thus k+m=4, all cold ORs occur, and every trace is a forest (under the super-root) with no shared occurrences.

If H is the earliest cold OR, its two operand expressions have no earlier cold ORs and each is an AND of raw flags and prior live factors. Both operands must be true on a small singleton when L is all true: in any traced forest their leaf is unique and needed as witness to the corresponding exit singleton, so the whole route to that leaf must be true. Each therefore contains precisely one distinct raw, say a or b, because an AND of two distinct raws is never singleton-true. At least one raw occurs in each since the signal is cold. The two labels are distinct by the forest count.

If a has a cold path to an exit that bypasses H, keep the indicated cold conjunct at every AND of that path. The resulting trace still contains H and hence its a leaf, so it would reach a along both a bypass and the path through H. This contradicts the tree count. The argument also applies to b. All full paths to output from either flag must reach a first exit, hence pass H. No earlier live factor can depend on a or b. Fix L=0 and precisely one of the other two flags true. The bit H is now one of 0,a,b,a∨b, and is the only route by which a,b can influence the output. Threshold three on the K4 flags requires a∧b on this slice, a contradiction. It follows that m=0 and k=4, with a distinct obligatory singleton raw at each exit.
