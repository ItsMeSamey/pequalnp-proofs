# Earlier four-group continuation templates

There were 181 proposed chronological four-dirty-AND continuation templates at protected costs zero, one and two, in counts 1, 5 and 175. A template records an ID, cost, atom count and four dirty continuations. It leaves open the four pure-root reach sets, raw endpoint graph and branching decorations. A template can have zero or multiple physical completions.

The author checked the saved ledgers. In the graph-mask-32 family, a physical completion must have one of three six-column decorations in verification/thirteen-four/rows, at lines 9, 10 and 13. These are (4,32,8,7,0,0), (4,32,4,11,0,0) and (4,32,0,15,0,0). The first two have three branches and one private triple. The third has four branches. Each belongs to the exit-count class of 80 in the 351-row crosswalk. Consequently the four-group proof excludes all physical completions of the earlier templates. The result uses no solver verdict on those 181 templates.
