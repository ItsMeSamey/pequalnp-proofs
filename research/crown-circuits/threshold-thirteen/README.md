# Necessary conditions at 13 inputs and 31 gates

A 31-gate realization of threshold two on 13 inputs would use at most eight AND gates. In its normalized representative, deleting any two raw inputs removes exactly four OR gates and preserves all ANDs. Each signed selector half must contain at least three complete singleton deficits.
