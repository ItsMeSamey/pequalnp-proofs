# Restrictions of the full raw-incidence graph

A size-g circuit with n twice-used raw inputs (both readers OR gates) and a ANDs has p pure OR groups after flattening, and total mixed nonraw excess r=g−a−2n+p. Introduce a formal Boolean input for every OR group with at least one raw operand. For a pure group this is its entire output. At a mixed group it is the disjunction of all its original raw operands, which is combined with its original nonraw inputs. Let q count the mixed groups with raw operands. In this abstract circuit at most r+q binary ORs remain. For every physical edge assignment, its incident group indicators reproduce the original circuit exactly. The group graph has N=p+q vertices and no isolates by construction. Mixed groups with no raw label retain only their original nonraw operands and do not appear as graph vertices.

Let K be any connected subgraph on s≥2 vertices and at least one edge. It need not be induced. Set other group pins to zero. This abstract restriction still rejects each individual K edge and accepts the unions of the endpoints of every two different K edges. In K's full component, peel exterior vertices farthest first using a tree rooted at K. For each peeled V take two consecutive edges VW,WZ toward K, choosing an internal neighbor of W when W is already in K. WZ and its pair with VW differ only on V. In any other component keep an anchor edge, eliminate other vertices similarly, and then pay one loss for the two joint anchor pins: a K singleton compared to itself plus the anchor edge differs on these pins. An essential pin has a downstream OR frontier, since an AND-only route would kill the entire still-nonzero target (choose K with at least two distinct edges for this case). Each elimination therefore pays at least one OR. If the full graph has C components, at least N−s−(C−1) ORs are saved.

The partial edge-threshold task on K thus has an implementation using no more than r−p+s+C−1 ORs with free ANDs. For T2 on twelve raw inputs at size 28 this is s+3−a+C. Taking a star of k≥2 edges and finally hardwiring its center indicator true gives threshold two on k variables, including when the center is a mixed group. Monotonicity and singleton rejection handle the empty assignment. Its OR lower bound 2k−4 proves k ≤ r−p+C+4 = 8−a+C. On a connected six-AND graph every connected K must meet the sharper |V(K)|−2 OR budget.
