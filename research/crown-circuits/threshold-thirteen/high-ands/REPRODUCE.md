# Reproduction and source map

From this directory, run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`, `python3 verify.py` and `python3 component_check.py`. No solver or external library is needed. `audit.out` gives the expected output. The structural arguments are proofs in prose; the witness and final graph arithmetic are independently executable.

The thirteen-input upper witness comes from the sibling research package `research/crown-circuits/threshold-thirteen/`. The unrestricted eleven-input minimum is established in commit `8ec367161de84c7aa0562391dcfb8ce39e52e19e`, `research/crown-circuits/eleven-unrestricted-27-cert/`. The twelve-input minimum is in `769947641e531fd0c3915263a7c58a734e9a224d`, `research/crown-circuits/threshold-twelve-final-cert/`; the same revision contains the general physical OR-floor theorem at `lean/Research/WegenerThreshold.lean`. The unrestricted ten-input minimum is in `8954e3c63e98f288bcaa14d9d6aae477165a834e`, `research/crown-circuits/ten-exact-cert/`. The eight- and nine-input minima use `2111f5f62b25a858972abddd75ee7d8f134eebd2`, `research/crown-circuits/threshold-eight/` and `research/crown-circuits/threshold-nine/` in the ItsMeSamey research fork.

The original supporting notes retain a few paths from the research worktree. Inside this package the general OR-tight theorem and sole-OR relocation are `lemmas/or-tight.md` and `lemmas/raw-normalization.md`. The general component and star arguments are `lemmas/component-star.md`, `lemmas/eight-star-audit.md` and `lemmas/full-group-restriction.md`. The local small-group count is `lemmas/small-pure-groups.md`. The AND-pair and branching-triple arguments received independent review; the thirteen-input exact minimum remains open between 31 and 32.
