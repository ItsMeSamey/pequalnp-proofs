# Three universal flags in mask 511

Write a,b,c for the three degree-four flags of K5 minus uv. Its decoder must reject each true edge and accept every triple, irrespective of its behavior on the missing pair uv. Fixing any universal flag to true realizes threshold two on its four neighbors. This threshold needs seven gates, including at least four ORs.

The 511 decoder needs at least nine gates. Suppose it had eight. Each universal flag could have only one reader since pinning it true erases all readers. Its reader would have to be an AND: a sole OR would be forced true, removing a further gate downstream, with the remaining function still nonconstant. The three sole AND readers must differ. If two flags shared a sole AND, then while the second flag was false, adding the first would fail to distinguish an edge on two other flags from its positive extension to a triple. In the earliest universal reader a∧P, the partner cannot depend on a,b,c. At u=v=0 that partner is zero, so adding a fails to distinguish bc from abc.

Any decoder with exactly four ORs also obeys an incidence constraint regardless of its AND count. Every reader of a universal flag must be an AND, or pinning the flag true would leave at most three ORs on its four-neighbor star. In a reduced circuit no derived wire can therefore be true on the singleton assignment containing only that universal flag: the first positive gate would require two occurrences of that same raw flag at a redundant AND.

Suppose two universal flags b,c had single readers. Their readers are distinct by the edge-versus-triple test. If b's reader b∧P is earlier, P is independent of b,c. Changing b distinguishes ac from abc, so P is true on the formal singleton a. Changing b likewise distinguishes cu from bcu, so P is true on the singleton u. The first condition makes P the raw flag a by the preceding observation, contradicting the second. Therefore at most one universal flag is singly read; the three flags occupy at least five AND input slots. This concerns the four-OR local decoder and does not itself exclude the joint host.
