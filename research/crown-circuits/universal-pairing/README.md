# When a size-40 crown circuit exists

A binary monotone 12-crown circuit on the one-hot row and column promise has size at most 40 if and only if twelve-input threshold two has a binary monotone circuit of size at most 28. Both existence questions remain open.

The forward direction combines the independently reviewed pairing and coordinate restriction proofs with the unrestricted ten-input threshold-two lower bound of 23. A fresh full replay of the published search reproduced its ledger byte for byte over 25,601 profiles and 4,824,777 helper libraries. The source and its applicability to the reduction received independent review. The general equivalence is a mathematical proof with an executable finite prerequisite. [Pairing](universal-pairing.md) · [Coordinate restriction](direct-coordinate-restriction.md) · [Ten-input proof](TEN.md)

For the reverse direction, supply the threshold circuit with twelve inputs, each the OR of one label’s row and column wires. There are exactly two active labels when the selected row and column differ, and one when they agree. These twelve gates take the size bound from 28 to 40.

Run `bash replay-ten.sh` in this directory with a C++20 compiler. It regenerates the census and compares it with `ten-six-census.txt`.
