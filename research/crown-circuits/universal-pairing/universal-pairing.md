# Universal pairing for the size-40 twelve-label crown

Assume unrestricted monotone size(T₂¹⁰) ≥ 23. Then a size-40 monotone binary circuit for crown inequality on twelve labels induces a size-28 monotone circuit for T₂¹². In fact the normalized full-direction network has at most two incomparable row/column pairs.

Use the transposed switch network with full source, one coordinate per AND, and at most 17 binary unions. Each label i has a rejecting direction where its two masks are false and every other row and column mask is true. At different labels every opposite-shore pair has full union. Comparable twins may both observe the larger mask. All distinct raw masks are then incomparable. Pair equal masks at one physical observer. A raw equals a direct branch if its full-cube mask equals that branch mask. Observe it on the branch in that case. All other raw masks need distinct paid union outputs.

Suppose d≥3 labels have incomparable twins. Delete k=max(2,d−2) exceptional labels. The n=12−k retained labels number between two and ten, with at most two incomparable pairs. The deleted labels have 2k distinct raw masks, of which s are direct. Replace each of the 2k−s nondirect masks with the full source at every use, removing distinct paid union gates. At each retained representative those masks were already true, so every wire value stays unchanged. At all other directions the masks only enlarge. The original opposite-shore full unions survive, including those involving the direct branches to be removed, which may also enlarge while remaining in their coordinate halves. Physically shared retained pairs stay shared.

Fix the s direct branches to their matching coordinate signs, giving a subcube Q containing all n retained representatives. These coordinates are distinct since all designated branches contain every retained representative and different designated original masks cannot use a single branch. Each incoming activation is still nonzero on Q. If s=0 no further restriction is required. Otherwise choose a retained receiver M having full union with every designated direct branch. If all are on the same shore, any opposite-shore raw works. If both shores occur they are at a single exceptional label: two opposite-shore branches at different labels would need full union, impossible for halves on distinct axes. The opposite signs of the same switch lack a common retained representative. Furthermore in this case there were at most two direct raw masks in the original network because any direct observer at a different label would face the same obstruction. Therefore 12+d≤17+2, hence d≤7; choose M at any of the at least five originally paired labels, all retained. In all cases M is nonempty and nonfull on Q by the different retained representatives.

The finite-forest restriction lemma in direct-coordinate-restriction.md removes at least s further distinct union gates. Its hypotheses apply to the postreplacement network, so its savings are disjoint from the first-stage removals. At most 17−2k unions survive. This is already impossible if the number is negative. The retained labels with at most two incomparable twins pair by choosing opposite shores at those twins. The resulting n single observers have pairwise full union and each has a nonempty deficit on Q. Transposition therefore gives a size-(17−2k+n−1) = (28−3k) circuit for T₂ⁿ.

Splitting one input z into z₁∨z₂ and OR-ing the original output with z₁∧z₂ lifts T₂ᵐ to T₂ᵐ⁺¹ in three new gates. The ten-input lower bound thus gives size(T₂ⁿ) ≥ 23−3(10−n) = 29−3k, a contradiction. Hence d≤2 and original pairing gives size(T₂¹²)≤17+12−1=28. A full lower bound for twelve-input threshold is a separate question.
