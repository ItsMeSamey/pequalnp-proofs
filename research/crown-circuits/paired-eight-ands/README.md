# Eight AND gates in a paired threshold tail

A normalized paired twelve-input threshold-two tail of size at most 28 using eight AND gates must contain at least three singleton-active ANDs. The all-clean case follows from the earlier clean-bank theorem. The one- and two-active proofs use mathematical reductions and finite censuses with checked manifests. Cases with three or more singleton-active AND gates remain open in general.
