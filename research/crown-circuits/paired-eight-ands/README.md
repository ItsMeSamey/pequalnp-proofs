# Eight AND gates in a paired threshold tail

A normalized paired twelve-input threshold-two tail of size at most 28 using eight AND gates must contain at least three singleton-active ANDs. The all-clean case follows from the earlier clean-bank theorem. The one- and two-active proofs use mathematical reductions and finite censuses with checked manifests. Cases with three or more singleton-active AND gates remain open in general. [One-active proof](one-active/proof.md) · [Two-active proof](two-active/README.md) · [Seven-AND prerequisite](../paired-seven-ands/two-dirty.md)

A further structural argument excludes eight AND gates altogether in a **minimum clean-terminal twelve-input threshold-two circuit of size at most 28**. It uses the unrestricted 26-gate lower bound at arity eleven and the established smaller-arity bounds. The seven-AND case remains open in general.
