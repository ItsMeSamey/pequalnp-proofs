# Eight AND gates and one dirty comparison

Consider the binary monotone threshold-two tail on twelve paired variables in the size-40 crown model. A tail of at most twenty-eight gates with eight ANDs and seven clean terminal comparisons yields at most seventeen binary unions in the transposed physical bank. Apply direct-split normalization as in reductions/seven/direct-split.md, decreasing the number of dirty gates if needed. The all-clean case is already excluded by the general signed-bank bound. With no direct receivers, the twelve distinct raw receivers are paid sinks and leave at most five auxiliary unions.

A raw receiver cannot feed another raw receiver: syntactic containment implies containment of its reach event, incompatible with nonempty disjoint missed events. Nor can a raw receiver feed T: its syntactic mask would then be contained in T, and its missed event would contain the nonempty missed event of the hot raw below.

There are seven clean selector axes and one dirty axis. The dirty continuation T uses t positive terminal signs after signed coordinate symmetry. A positive and negative sign together makes the continuation semantically full and makes the comparison terminal; the empty continuation is dead. A hot raw sink K contains both dirty pins, so its missed directions are the nonempty ordinary signed subcube specified by T and its terminal literal component. Every cold receiver has at least its ordinary signed rejection cube treating the dirty axis as independent. If every hot sink has at most one sign outside T, replace it by T or T union that single sign, and select the cold receiver ordinary cubes. This gives twelve disjoint cubes and at most seventeen unions, forbidden by the clean signed-bank theorem. Thus select a hot K with r≥2 signs outside T and b signs within T, all consistent.

The O(1) parameters (t,b,r) characterize K,T up to signed terminal permutations. Their used union graph contains t+2+r distinct source literals. It has at most two components, at most one if b>0, and at most six joins (five helpers and K). Thus r≤6−t. Widths five to seven are already impossible; width four has two disjoint complete trees on four leaves each, and the previous width-four-eight-axes.out checks all 225 tree pairs for eight axes. This leaves the twenty-five profiles in jobs.

hot_anchor.cpp and one_dirty.cpp lift the seven-axis verifier to 16 physical pins and 256 directions. They enumerate helpers contained in T or K first; no outside helper can feed an inside demand. Within each phase all unions of two available signals are taken in increasing mask weight and then integer order, with smaller proper subsets necessarily earlier. The test requires T already available and K available with one union. It then packs eleven other distinct, nonsingleton raw receivers with pairwise-disjoint nonempty deficits disjoint from K. Exact set inclusion, volume, and a component bound discard impossible libraries before exhaustive packing. All packings are tested on the full 256 resolution directions. hot_sym.cpp repeats with generators fixing the earlier helper chain, terminal support T and chosen K. Selecting the least sorted representative of each full helper family justifies skipping a node whose candidate helper can be decreased by such a generator. Raw results use no symmetry of the helper enumeration. The separate unused-axis lemma is unnecessary for either computation.
