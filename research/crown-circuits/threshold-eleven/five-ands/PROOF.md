# Exact size of threshold two on eleven inputs with at most five AND gates

The minimum is **27 gates**. The explicit circuit in control27.json uses four ANDs and passes every one of the 2,048 Boolean assignments. The finite lower bound below applies to all monotone binary AND/OR circuits with arbitrary depth and fanout.

## A bound on the ten-input minor

Resolving each AND to one input produces an OR circuit which dominates the original. For any fixed rejecting assignment, selecting false parents at all false ANDs yields a resolution that preserves every wire value. Each input variable i has a receiver mask R_i of the resolution directions in which the output depends on it. Its mask misses at least one direction, since its singleton rejects, and the union R_i ∪ R_j is full when i ≠ j, since each two-true input accepts. The n labels therefore have n distinct omitted witness directions.

A monotone circuit for threshold two on ten variables with at most four ANDs requires at least 24 gates. Fewer than four ANDs give at most eight directions. With four ANDs the sixteen directions form a Boolean cube. Read the circuit backwards from its output: a forward OR passes its activation to both inputs and a forward AND splits activation according to its switch. At each earlier wire, join the activation masks of all uses. A pruned forward circuit with g gates and n essential primary variables requires at most g−n+1 reverse joins since there are 2g+1 outgoing uses including the output tap and g+n nonempty use lists. Constant, repeated-input and unary operations can first be simplified.

At size 23, ten inputs leave at most 14 joins. Each raw receiver hits the representatives of the other nine labels. A free switch branch has at most eight directions and cannot be a receiver. The ten distinct paid receivers leave at most four additional union masks. The receivers are incomparable and cannot be ancestors of one another in ordinary union expressions stopping at the free branches. Thus each receiver is a union of two bases or helpers, after at most four paid nonreceiver helpers. Grant every switch branch before its chronological turn and omit the cost of forming activations, both relaxations.

chron4.py enumerates every nonzero activation in chronological switch order as either full or any union of the earlier branches before splitting it by the next independent coordinate. Identical membership traces are compressed to atoms. Fewer than ten atoms cannot distinguish ten labels. The resulting 229 distinct basis families are all exhausted by ten.cpp. It enumerates at most four helper masks in increasing support size. A helper missing just one atom cannot contribute to a different proper receiver. Once the library is fixed, select all available singleton receiver deficits, since each can replace an overlapping member in any proposed disjoint packing. Search the remaining possible two-operand unions for a disjoint deficit packing of size ten. Every family fails. A separate terminal-normalized skeleton enumeration also excluded the same size limit.

## Direct receivers and equal activations

Eleven representatives require at least four actual ANDs. With four switches an actual branch contains at most eight directions, so it cannot equal a raw receiver, which covers ten other representatives. For five actual ANDs the direct-receiver specialization in direct-minor.md applies with eleven inputs instead of twelve. A receiver equal to a switch branch can be physically redirected to that branch without additional gates. A singleton-hot (dirty) AND has activation U contained in the receiver of its hot singleton. That AND cannot have a receiver as a branch, since the branch is a proper subset of U and no distinct raw masks are comparable. Thus the direct receiver feeds a singleton-clean terminal AND. The opposite arm must compute the disjunction of the ten remaining variables, because adding the direct variable to any other singleton changes the output. Setting the direct variable to zero eliminates at least three gates and one AND (the direct-arm OR and terminal product, or the product and a later gate if the arm was exactly the variable, plus a gate computing the opposite-arm disjunction). This produces a size-at-most-23 circuit with at most four ANDs for ten inputs, already excluded. The argument uses only singleton and pair tests, which determine threshold two by monotonicity.

Choose a minimum-size counterexample within the stated bounds, breaking ties by the number of dirty ANDs. Terminal-clean normalization applies under the AND cap: bypassing an AND with a clean parent preserves singleton rejections and can only grow pair values; moving clean disjuncts out of mixed ORs into the final disjunction preserves every singleton wire value without increasing size; and compacting the final clean OR network does not add ANDs. Every clean AND then feeds the final disjunction and has full reverse activation.

In this representative no dirty activation can equal a raw receiver. Move an equal receiver tap to the corresponding incoming activation, preserving its full mask and all other masks. In the forward circuit its raw is now downstream of this AND, which therefore becomes false on that singleton. It cannot fire on any other singleton, by incomparability. Previously clean ANDs, all with full activation, cannot become singleton-hot. Normalizing again does not increase the dirty count, contradicting minimality. The same transfer for paired labels is detailed in activation.md.

## Complete five-AND census

To include a four-AND circuit in the same five-direction census, append a formal split of full activation along a fresh coordinate and leave its two halves unused. All real masks extend constantly along this coordinate, no receiver equals a new half, and no paid unions or nonfull activations are added. The chronological enumerator explicitly permits this activation and its extra freely available branches only relax the test. No prior eleven-input lower bound is required.

The eleven distinct nonfree raw receivers cost eleven of at most sixteen joins. The distinct nonfull activations other than free branch masks form a set W. All are dirty, paid and different from receivers. Grant the ten branches and all masks in W before checking if the raw outputs can be built; this relaxes their physical chronological requirements. At most q=5−|W| additional joins may construct helpers. In any unsplit union expression for one receiver, another receiver cannot be an ancestor by mask incomparability. Every output must therefore be the union of two available masks after building its helpers.

profiles11.cpp enumerates the chronological five-switch activation sequences up to sign translations, requiring at least eleven distinct branch traces. canonical.cpp identifies signed coordinate permutations, under which the relaxed branch/activation/receiver packing condition is invariant; it retains an actual generated representative of each orbit. families.cpp compresses switch words only if all branch values agree on them. It enumerates available helper masks in increasing population count, testing generation from pairs of currently available masks. A helper whose deficit consists of one atom cannot contribute to a different proper larger mask. The eleven candidate receiver deficits must be nonempty and disjoint. For fixed helpers, any available singleton deficit can always replace the packing member containing it (or be added when unused). Among other available deficits a containing set can be replaced by a smaller one. The checker uses these reductions and bounds on the remaining atom volume to test every library.

The 2,313 generated five-switch activation profiles reduce to 660 signed-permutation orbits. The numbers with q=2,3,4,5 are 240,298,106,16 respectively. Every orbit is excluded. The all-full five-axis case was also checked independently by fixing a largest receiver width: widths below four cannot accommodate eleven disjoint rejected cubes, and the two remaining widths four and five give 45,245 and 3,494 anchored systems respectively, all rejected.

Run replay.sh with Python and a C++20 compiler. It checks the explicit upper control, rebuilds and reruns the ten-input profile generator and the complete five-switch census, and compares the generated results to their ledgers. No SAT solver is needed for this replay.

The uniform 660-orbit pass also completed numerically: 61,010,113 helper banks were tested; profile 659 alone tested 6,019,539. This independently reproduces the final Q5 anchored exclusion.
