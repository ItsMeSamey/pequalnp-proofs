# Eleven-input threshold two with six AND gates

A monotone fan-in-two circuit computing threshold two on eleven inputs with at most six AND gates needs at least 26 total gates. A known circuit uses 27 total gates and four AND gates. The seven-AND case at size 25 remains separate.

The transposition, signed-coordinate canonicalization, trace compression and exhaustive library tests are those documented in TEN.md. For eleven inputs and 25 gates the transposed circuit uses at most 15 binary unions. A direct receiver would specialize to a circuit of size at most 22 on ten inputs, excluded by TEN.md. The eleven incomparable proper receivers therefore consume eleven distinct joins. After minimizing dirty gates, each distinct nonfree activation consumes one additional join, leaving q = 4 minus the number of such activations. Granting all charged activations as sources relaxes the remaining first-stage and receiver constructions.

The generator produces 205,517 chronological profiles before signed-coordinate canonicalization. There are 41,565 resulting profiles. The numbers with q equal to 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 are respectively 16,118, 18,089, 6,314, 964 and 80. The replay script regenerates the profile census and exhaustively tests each library in each profile against the archived ledger. Its records contain profile ID, trace-atom count, remaining helper budget, number of libraries visited and existence of a receiver packing. The Boolean tests operate on all distinguishable direction atoms. No floating-point or solver assumptions are needed.

The nonordinary ledger contains the first 41,564 profiles and 105,209,281 helper libraries, all rejected. The final profile consists of six full activations. Each of its eleven receiver deficits is an ordinary subcube; a maximum-width receiver has at least four literals because eleven boxes of relative size at least one eighth cannot be disjoint. The separate ordinary-box checker general11.cpp anchors a maximum-width receiver and checks widths four through six with four available helpers. Its internal circuit-budget argument is 16, from which it subtracts twelve to obtain the helper count. The three widths exhaust 1,892, 168 and 6 systems, respectively. All three searches reject. replay-eleven.sh verifies the nonordinary ledger and the three ordinary output files.
