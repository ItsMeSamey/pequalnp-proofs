# Mandatory receiver groups in the signed search

For each hot dirty activation the established single-branch cancellation yields a list of admissible hard hot raw receivers. The minimum-volume raw has deficit size at most floor(L/13) on L trace atoms. Besides these lists, each signed port event must contain three pairwise-disjoint whole raw deficits. Hence it must contain one whole deficit of size at most floor(number of atoms in the event / 3). A port with exactly three atoms forces each individual singleton-atom deficit. All these mandatory existence lists may be used to choose the anchor.

After fixing an available anchor, every remaining mandatory list not already satisfied by the anchor must meet the available set of raw joins whose deficits avoid the anchor. Counts are maintained when a pair of sources first makes a mask attainable or ceases to do so. At the final helper, a missing list must be met by joining this new helper with a previously active source. This is necessary even before checking that the twelve additional chosen deficits can be packed disjointly. The additional histogram rejects when the twelve smallest available volumes, counted before dominance elimination, exceed the area outside the anchor. With h attainable singleton atoms, that volume is at least 24−h. None of these tests assumes arbitrary auxiliaries are available physically; granting protected signals initially only relaxes the search.

The parent signed enumerator examines every source family by putting subsets of the anchor first and sorting other masks by increasing cardinality. A helper containing the entire anchor cannot contribute to a different receiver, since receiver masks are incomparable. A supplied protected base is stabilized setwise by every symmetry used during anchored traversal. group-anchor.js implements the additional conditions. port-anchor.js implements only the anchor and forced-singleton version.
