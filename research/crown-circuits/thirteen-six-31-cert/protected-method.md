# Charging and reconstructing protected continuation unions

A nonflat six-comparator candidate has a forward earliest dirty comparator. By the shared opposite-pin witnesses for an earliest comparator, its two pin-specific shared OR outputs correspond to two distinct paid nonraw reverse helpers outside all physical continuation cones. Thus at most four of the six helper unions are physically used in constructing continuations. For e earliest comparators, two signed pins per root require at least e external unions, since one union can first introduce at most two such pins. When e=2 and the protected cost is four, both outside unions must pair two distinct new raw pin sources. They cannot pair opposite signs of the same root, which cannot supply its two exclusive witnesses. The two pairs must then connect the same two earliest comparators and give the forbidden two-cycle. These are the arity-independent matching and two-cycle results in reductions/seven/higher-matching.md. The individually paid raw sinks are never protected.

Let U be the six semantic activation masks and B the twelve signed branch masks on all 64 resolution directions. Let W be the distinct nonfull U values not present in B. Each W consumes one physical protected union. Look for additional semantic masks H of cardinality at most 4−|W|. Any additional mask on an actual protected cone lies inside some nonfull U. Masks already equal to B or any U need not be charged again. The following relaxed reachability test deliberately allows such masks to be formed by binary union without budget charge, even if the switch which normally generates that B is unavailable when it is needed.

Begin with the full direction mask. Whenever U_j is reachable, emit both actual signed branches for j. Whenever two reachable masks have union in B, U, or H, allow that union. Iterate to the least fixed point and check that all U and H are reachable. A real protected computation passes by induction. When enumerating potential H, grant all B and U as generators from the start and rank H by cardinality then mask value. This lists any subset of H needed to build U, since a nontrivial union has larger cardinality than either strict operand. Once a valid helper subset is obtained, its supersets need not be separately listed: the ordinary signed checker can spend the balance of six helpers freely. Each listed H lies inside a dirty activation; no raw receiver can be contained in it in the normalized representative. The symmetry reduction for the subsequent signed search must stabilize the entire granted base set, including H.

The final ledger retains the general four-protected-unions bound and does not use the stronger earliest-root counting or two-cycle cuts. Every such preliminary exclusion selected during the working search was also rejected by a direct unconditioned signed solver. An independent forward breadth-first enumeration in bfs-audit.js starts from the full mask, emits only actual reachable split branches, and adds an auxiliary mask only from already reached operands. It returned exactly the same minimal basis sets on all 9,347 profiles as the separate candidate-enumeration/fixed-point method protected.js. Both methods grant the identical semantic duplicate-value relaxation.
