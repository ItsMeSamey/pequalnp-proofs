Resolve the six AND gates to a choice of operand in each of 64 directions. Each resolved output is an OR on the raw bits and dominates the monotone circuit. Every singleton has a rejecting resolution, since a rejected evaluation can preserve all six actual AND values by choosing a rejecting operand at each false AND. Positive pairs are accepted in every direction. Consequently the thirteen singleton deficits (sets of rejecting directions) are nonempty and pairwise disjoint.
