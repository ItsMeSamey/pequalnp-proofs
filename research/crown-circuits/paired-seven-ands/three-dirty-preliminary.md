To count the minimum union cost of three specified continuation masks, coalesce identical outputs and let d be the number of distinct remaining masks, n the number of literal inputs covered. Transpose their subnetwork; each input literal demands its pattern of incidence among the d≤3 outputs. Nonliteral patterns are paid. With three transpose inputs, a required three-literal pattern needs one auxiliary if and only if no required pair-literal pattern is present. All other required nonliteral patterns are obtained directly from literals. Thus the minimum shared forward cost is n−d plus the number of distinct required nonliteral patterns and this optional one auxiliary.
