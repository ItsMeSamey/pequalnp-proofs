# Seven comparisons with two dirty gates

There are five clean terminal comparisons, two older dirty comparisons, fourteen split literals and 128 directions. The second dirty continuation T1 is a nonempty consistent signed terminal mask. The earlier T0 has a consistent terminal part plus zero, one or two input bits of the later dirty gate. If it has no such bits its terminal part must be nonempty. If it carries both then its terminal part is jointly consistent with T1, because the first dirty gate is hot on a rejected singleton. Terminal signed permutations and flips, dirty input sign interchange and swapping independent dirty gates leave 244 continuation templates. two_dirty.cpp builds this list directly. For each singleton literal it computes the 128-bit set of selector directions that reject, and it obtains other masks' rejection events by intersection. A valid raw pairing requires twelve disjoint nonempty events.

The raw-helper search allows arbitrary direct receivers. At each helper count the search canonicalizes a matrix whose first two labeled rows encode T0,T1; remaining rows encode the helpers, permuted within equal cardinalities. For each axis its two column codes may be interchanged. The dirty axis columns are retained in position and the five terminal-axis column pairs may be reordered. Equality of the resulting keys preserves the ordered continuation masks and helper family up to legal symmetries. The next helper may be any available strict union, so memoizing that canonical family is sound. The completed table two-dirty-two-helpers.tsv records 244, 6,972 and 196,291 canonical libraries at zero, one and two helpers, with no twelve-packing; 3,645 two-helper libraries passed the area test.

There is also a reduction from two dirty gates to one. Replace the earliest dirty gate by a clean terminal comparison. A cold raw mask has at most one literal of that gate. When its new selector evaluation is zero it chooses away from this literal, hence did not trigger the earlier continuation in the old evaluation. Its new rejection set is contained in its old one and remains nonempty: in any old rejection witness select away from the possible earliest literal, which only removes downstream contributions. A hot raw mask has both earlier dirty literals. Rebuild it without those bits and with the full original continuation T0, which gives exactly its old rejection set even when the second dirty gate depends on the first. If all hot raw masks have at most one literal beyond T0 and the two earlier dirty bits, this costs at most one new union per old paid hot receiver. Such a receiver cannot feed any other raw receiver or either chronological continuation. The result is a one-dirty paired solution of at most the original size. Moreover no raw may feed T0, since its rejection set would contain Z(T0), which contains the hot label's nonempty rejection set. In a five-helper no-direct library this forces |T0|+r≤6, where r is the number of distinct remaining raw signs outside T0. Therefore |T0|≥5 reduces to one dirty; with a direct receiver and six helpers, |T0|≥6 does so.

The no-direct one-dirty case is excluded across every continuation width. In the earliest-dirty reduction above, when |T0|≥5 every rebuilt raw remains nonliteral. This excludes all 80 templates with the wider earliest continuation. The generic paired direct-split normalization in direct-split.md then allows the two-dirty search to concentrate on nonliteral raw outputs. When |T0|=4, a hot K with r≥2 saturates the source count: r=2, K and T0 are disjoint, and every helper belongs to one of the two separate four-literal trees. two_saturated.cpp checks every valid such input choice for all 54 topologies, even without charging T1. Across 279 anchors it checks 62,775 tree pairs, 2,554 passing volume and none admitting the remaining eleven disjoint events. The table is two-dirty-width-four.tsv.

No raw receiver can feed T1 either. An original hot label for the second dirty gate has rejection event contained in Z(T1), and a receiver included in T1 has a larger rejection event. Disjointness would force them to be the same label, but a mask contained in T1 contains only terminal literals and cannot activate either dirty AND. Thus both continuations may be charged as helpers/literals in the original no-direct search.

At |T0|=3 and r=3 the same equality forces disjointness and two separate optimal trees, now on three and five literals. This slice is also excluded: 52 templates, 550 hot raw anchors and 173,250 tree pairs, of which 13,678 passed the area test and none admitted the remaining packing. Thus candidates at width three require r=2.

The full fixed-anchor pass also excludes all 52 earliest-width-three templates. There were 212 canonical possible initial hot masks and 8,419 volume-passing helper systems, with no packing. two_anchor.cpp first transposes the demanded sublibrary of T0,T1,K. For d distinct demanded masks covering n literals, five helpers plus the single raw gate K allow at most 6+d−n binary unions after transposition. Each distinct literal incidence pattern of cardinality at least two consumes one such union. Check this necessary bound, then identify K masks equivalent under simultaneous signed terminal permutations preserving the two continuations and under legal input flips. Enumerate helpers inside any of T0,T1,K before outside helpers, requiring both continuations and one-step availability of K at the boundary. At the end, test eleven other nonsingleton raw events avoiding K. The reusable proof of the phase ordering is the same as in the one-dirty fixed-target check. The totals are in two-dirty-width-three.tsv.

Independent dirty gates can be renamed in either order. A further inexpensive redirection applies when the earliest continuation includes both input literals of the later dirty gate. Writing A for its terminal part, the semantic receiver of T0 in this case equals the union receiver of A∪T1: either selected later input carries the same continuation T1. If |A minus T1|≤1, replace old T0 globally, including as a helper used by raw masks, by the already demanded T1 plus that optional literal. The old T0 needed a gate, and T1's construction has no dirty literals, so this replacement fits the budget and introduces no cycle. All direction events are unchanged. The resulting dirties are independent. With the width-three-and-up exclusion, two-semantic-cases.tsv counts 186 original templates eliminated by earliest width, 22 by independent interchange and 3 by this redirection. This leaves 33 profiles for the numerical workers without counting lower-width search results.

Earliest continuation width two is also excluded. two_sym.cpp first filters each candidate raw q by transposing the union program for T0,T1,K,q: it has at most seven paid gates, so its incidence patterns must fit the budget 7+d−n. With no spare union every nonliteral incidence pattern must be a strict union of two available subpatterns. A raw cannot be included in either continuation by the hot-label argument. Superset removal and the exact disjointness search on all these candidate events screen anchor roots. Helpers not contained in any protected demand or remaining raw target may be omitted. During exhaustive construction use the subgroup fixing T0,T1,K and each previously chosen helper to discard a newly proposed mask whose equivalent integer is smaller. The lexicographically least representation of any family in its two ordered phases survives these tests. Out of all 39 width-two templates, 562 initial anchors, 139 postfilter anchors and 28,570 volume-passing canonical systems gave zero packings. The evidence is in two-dirty-width-two.tsv.

## Completion at earliest width one

A single earlier literal either feeds one input of the second dirty gate or is a terminal literal. The former produces five templates, one for each width of the second gate's consistent terminal continuation. The latter means the two gates are independent, so the case with a wider second continuation can be reordered to a solved larger earliest width. When both continuations are terminal literals, they may be equal, opposite, or on different terminal axes. All eight remaining templates are listed in two-dirty-width-one-core.tsv. The exact checker finds no twelve-packing for any of them. The last independent, distinct-terminal-axis case, ID 10, had 48 canonical hard-hot roots, 37 after candidate feasibility filtering and 453,822 volume-passing canonical libraries. Its exact packing count was zero.

Combined with the paired direct-split normalization and the one-dirty and all-clean cases, a paired twelve-input size-40 candidate with seven ANDs requires at least three diagonal-active ANDs once clean terminals have been collected. This is a restricted architecture lower bound.

A second table two-dirty-width-one.tsv directly checks all nineteen physical earliest-width-one templates, even those interchangeable with wider independent templates: 563 initial roots, 265 postfilter roots and 802,552 volume-passing canonical libraries, with zero packings. replay_two_final.sh uses this full table, so the finite certificate does not require the optional interchange or semantic redirection of equivalent templates.

As a further independent permissive check, two-dirty-three-helpers.tsv allows direct receivers and grants the continuations at no cost. All 244 templates were tested through three generic raw helpers. Across 5,440,334 canonical three-helper libraries, 1,151,967 passed the area bound and none admitted twelve disjoint raw events.
