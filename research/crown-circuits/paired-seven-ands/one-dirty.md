# Seven comparisons, one diagonal-active AND

Consider a paired twelve-label crown of size at most forty whose paired threshold-two tail has at most twenty-eight gates, seven AND gates and exactly one diagonal-active AND before six clean terminals. Cut the nonterminal AND. The prefix has at most 16 OR gates, 13 sources and 14 taps, hence at most 17 transposed binary unions. The dirty continuation T is a nonempty consistent set of terminal literals. Up to signed permutations of the six terminal axes there is one template for each width 1 through 6.

For a hot raw variable, its receiver contains both dirty operand literals. If B is its terminal part, its rejection event over the seven selectors is exactly the signed cube specified by B∪T, with the dirty coordinate unrestricted. The union B∪T is consistent, since the singleton input is rejected. Every cold raw receiver contains at most one dirty literal and its own ordinary signed cube lies in its rejection event.

Suppose that each hot raw receiver has at most one terminal literal outside T. In the reverse library T is an existing signal. Replace each hot receiver by T or by the union of T and its extra literal. Each such receiver previously required a union gate. It cannot be an ancestor of any other named raw receiver, since raw receivers are incomparable; nor can it feed T, which has no dirty literal. Thus each replacement costs at most what it frees. The twelve new signed cubes are nonempty and pairwise disjoint, and their masks are computed in at most 17 unions. This contradicts the previously verified clean seven-axis paired-bank lower bound. Consequently some hot receiver must have at least two terminal literals outside T. Since those literals must be compatible with T, widths five and six are impossible.

For the remaining widths, one_dirty.cpp enumerates small raw-helper families. A raw helper set is represented up to swaps of the dirty axis and permutations and legal sign flips of terminal axes preserving T. For each fixed ordering of the helpers, encode their two literal-incidence columns on every axis, reorder each symmetric axis group, and minimize the code over helper orders of nondecreasing mask cardinality. Iterating every subsequent binary union with no numerical order requirement makes this canonical set representative sufficient. Every target may use one last union or be present in the bank. All receiver types, including literal receivers, are allowed. A 128-bit event encodes each full selector fiber. Superset removal, the exact cell-volume bound and an exhaustive disjoint-set search test the twelve-label condition.

For width four without a direct raw receiver, let K be a hot disputed mask requiring two terminal literals outside T. The union of T and K has eight source literals. No raw receiver can feed T: if its mask were contained in T, its zero event would contain Z(T), which contains the hot receiver's nonempty event. The hot receiver itself contains dirty bits and cannot be contained in T. The used graph realizing T and K has at most five helper unions plus K's final union. If T and K overlap, it is connected, and eight source literals require at least seven binary unions. Thus T,K are disjoint. The six available gates are all consumed by two separate binary trees, each on four distinct literal leaves. There are fifteen possible distinct libraries per four-leaf tree. width_four.py constructs all 225 pairs and finds zero 12-packings, even when its other labels are freely chosen from the full set of available one-step unions. The output is in width_four.out.

The fixed-mask search handles widths two and three without direct receivers. Choose a hot receiver K whose terminal part contains at least two signs outside T. Write r for their number and b for the number of signs shared with T. Because the two sign sets are consistent, terminal axis symmetries make (|T|,b,r) a complete description of the pair T,K. The used union graph for T and K has at least |T|+2+r−c unions, where c=2 when b=0 and c=1 otherwise. Only the final K is charged as a named raw signal. hot_anchor.cpp enumerates at most five helpers for all nonsingleton receivers: first those contained in K or T, then all outside helpers. An outside helper cannot feed either inside demand. The inside phase may finish only if T is available and K is available in one additional union. Within each phase enumerate masks by increasing cardinality and integer encoding. Every partial external library is tested. A histogram bounds the total size of eleven other receiver zero events; the exact disjoint-packing search then decides the cases not excluded by volume. The checked width-two and width-three tables contain zero packings.

The source count generalizes independently of the number of clean terminal axes. A candidate without a literal receiver satisfies |T|+r≤6 in the disjoint case, and |T|+r≤5 if T meets K. Since r≥2, widths at least five are impossible regardless of the number of extra terminal axes (whenever the corresponding clean-bank lower bound applies). For eight comparison axes and width four, the same saturated two-tree search admits one unused axis and still reports no packing, as shown by width-four-eight-axes.out.

The no-direct exclusion is complete for width one as well. no-direct-width-one.tsv contains all eight (b,r) profiles and 1,023,916 volume-passing libraries with zero packings. The partially constructed K,T ancestor graphs give a safe additional bound: with g remaining helper slots, the number of connected components among K's bits under already present K-subset helpers is at most g+2; for T it is at most g+1. The same argument on K∪T allows one extra component when the demands are disjoint. replay_one.sh runs all four finite no-direct widths; widths five and six follow from hot replacement above.

In a paired instance there can be at most one literal receiver. Its event contains the corresponding selector halfcube. If a second literal receiver existed, the only way their halfcubes could be disjoint would be opposite sides of one axis, leaving no room for any other rejection event. In a one-dirty instance the literal cannot lie on the dirty axis, since a hot receiver's event is contained in Z(T), itself contained in each dirty literal's avoidance event. A terminal literal receiver must oppose a sign of T∪B for the mandatory hot raw. Symmetries fixing T and K leave at most three possible placements: one in their common terminals, one in T alone, and one in K alone. For width four, direct-width-four.tsv covers the thirteen resulting profiles with one direct receiver, up to six helpers, 3,350 volume-passing libraries and no solutions. Thus for every width four through six the direct branch is excluded as well.

The independent direct-split normalization in direct-split.md removes the no-direct qualification from the seven-AND one-dirty exclusion. If the eleven-input specialization has no dirty gate remaining, its clean split is excluded by the clean-bank theorem instead.
