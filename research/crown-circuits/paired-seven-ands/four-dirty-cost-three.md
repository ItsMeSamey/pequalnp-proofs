# Earliest gates and two tagged helpers

Work with a globally minimum paired seven-comparator circuit subject to the five-helper reverse budget. An earliest dirty comparator has two input pins that occur in no protected continuation. Multiple dirty comparators may have this property, and each can be moved first without renaming which port it feeds. Each needs at least two nonraw helper masks containing its pins, by the earlier single-helper cancellation lemma. If the continuation masks cost c unions, at most h=5−c helpers contain any of the earliest pins. Every earliest comparator must have introduced at least one of its pins by helper h−1. Each helper can introduce at most two previously unseen comparator coordinates. Thus their number e is at most 2(h−1), or 8−2c. In particular cost three leaves at most two earliest comparators. The three pure helpers are all in the ancestry of the protected continuations, and therefore are proper or equal submasks of those continuations.

At cost three with two earliest comparators, both remaining helpers must touch both coordinates. The first is precisely one literal from each, up to pin swaps. higher_two_earliest.cpp enumerates the second as a one-step union touching both; it checks all hard witnesses on the two coordinates and then all label packings. It allows a single chosen witness to cover both comparators.

At cost three with only one earliest comparator, write its pins as a and b and its continuation as T. Let H1 and H2 be the two tagged helpers in topological order. If H1=a∨b, replace H1 by T. If H2 was independently formed using just a (or b) and a pure operand P, any additional hot receiver lacking H1 is a∨b or H2∨b, and can be switched to T or T∨P individually. A descendant that used the old H1 gets the same pure part and T. Its other operand can retain at most one original pin. Its new deficit is therefore the old deficit, or its half obtained by choosing the opposite direction at the now-clean earliest comparator. Both are nonempty and contained in the old deficit.

Otherwise write H1=a∨P. If H2 has both signs, it is either a∨b, treated as above, or H1∨b. Replace the latter by T∨P. Remaining hot receivers can only be a∨b or H1∨b and are individually representable. If H2 carries only a and is independently formed, the only hot receivers are individual joins of b with a or one helper, again representable.

The remaining possibilities use pure available operands P,Q. If H2 has the b sign, then H2=b∨Q is independent and the only possible hard raw is K=H1∨H2. If H2 carries a and depends on H1, then H2=H1∨Q and the only possible hard raw is K=H2∨b. In each case K=a∨b∨P∨Q. The other hot receivers need at most T plus one available pure operand. Swapping the two original pins permits fixing the orientation of H1. For the independent case one may additionally order P,Q. Both shapes are implemented in higher_two_tags.cpp. Its replacements require a nonempty deficit contained in the old one, so proper singleton behavior and pair coverage are preserved. Other dirty gates may cease to fire.

For four dirty gates, 3,257 of the 11,623 cost-three templates have too many earliest comparators. The 6,163 templates with two earliest comparators were examined by higher_two_earliest.cpp. Across 1,287,432 eligible helper libraries there were 55,352 compatible hard-root pairs (allowing the same raw twice), zero volume passes and zero packings. The full data are in higher-four-two-earliest.tsv. The other 2,203 have a single earliest comparator.

The 2,203 single-earliest templates also returned zero packings; higher-four-one-earliest.tsv records every visited case. This completes all 11,623 four-dirty physical templates with continuation cost three. Lower continuation costs remain a separate search.
