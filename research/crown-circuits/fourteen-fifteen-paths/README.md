# Local OR counts for the fifteen-flag classification

The three candidate interfaces generated here are C3+P4 (triangle plus four-vertex path), P4+P4 and P4+P5. The last remains a staged query only. In each component combine its local positive-pair detector with a raw cover OR testing whether that component has any active label. The final output is the OR of both local detectors and the AND of their two cover signals. The conjunction of all three triangle flags is its positive detector; P4 uses ac∨bd; P5 uses c(a∨e)∨bd. In the triangle a∨b is a cover; P4 and P5 use the cover from prepare.py. These yield respectively 5, 6 and 7 OR upper bounds. The script checks each one on every raw edge subset, then captures the archived graph-cut encoder’s CNF for one fewer OR.

The C3+P4 lower bound of five has an independently accepted binary DRAT certificate, as recorded in triangle-p4.sha256 and triangle-p4-checker.log. Consequently the residual C3+P3+P4+P4 costs at least twelve ORs by retaining C3+P4 and charging each of the other components its full order with the independent-raw increment. Only eleven are available at fifteen flags.

For any frozen CNF run receipt.py INPUT.cnf DEST with TMPDIR, TMP, TEMP set to DEST. For UNSAT it preserves CaDiCaL’s native proof without text expansion. Verify with drat-trim INPUT.cnf DEST/proof.dratb -i. For SAT the selector model is stored instead. Lower bounds here refer to unrestricted numbers of ANDs and arbitrary fanout; flattening AND cones gives the complete selector model as described in the adjacent triangle-path-sat proof.

The two-path P4+P4 minimum is six ORs. The five-OR CNF and native proof are pinned in p4-p4.sha256; p4-p4-checker.log records the independent verified result in 37.546 seconds using 20,086,309 resolution steps. For the remaining P4+P5+P5 fifteen-flag class, remove one endpoint of one P5 at cost one OR, keeping a P4+P4 base and an untouched P5. The latter costs five additional ORs, so at least twelve were needed before the endpoint removal, above the budget of eleven. Combined with duality/uniform/fourteen-fifteen-conditional.md this excludes the entire fifteen-flag case.

More generally, in a post-branch residual with N vertices, OR cap N−3, and only nontrivial components, at most one component can be a nonstar. Any connected nonstar contains as a connected subgraph either a triangle or a four-vertex path: a spanning tree of diameter at least three contains a path, and a star spanning tree plus a noncentral edge contains a triangle. Retain one such subgraph within each of two purported nonstars, deleting their other vertices by descending distance and charging one OR apiece. The three possible base pairs C3+C3, C3+P4 and P4+P4 need respectively four, five and six ORs, each equal to their combined order less two. The base bank has no common flag covering all edge singletons. Every other entire component therefore adds its full order by the independent-raw increment. This totals N−2 ORs, a contradiction.
