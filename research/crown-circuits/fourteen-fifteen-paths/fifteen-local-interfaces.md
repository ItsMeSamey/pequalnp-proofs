# Exclusion of the fifteen-flag eight-AND host

The residual arithmetic in fourteen-fifteen-conditional.md uses the checked six-OR floors for C3+P5 and C4+P4. It leaves C3+P3+P4+P4 or P4+P5+P5, on fourteen residual flags and eleven ORs.

For the first family a five-OR floor on C3+P4 suffices: its fixed bank has no flag covering all edge singletons; the disjoint P3 and P4 increase the threshold decoder minimum by their three and four vertices, giving twelve. For the second, a seven-OR floor on P4+P5 similarly gives twelve by charging the remaining P5 at five. These are two local lower-bound hypotheses, not consequences of the shape count itself.

The matching upper formulas use unrestricted binary ANDs. On ordered path flags a,b,c,d, its local pair indicator is ac∨bd, and b∨c covers every edge. On ordered path flags p,q,r,s,t the local pair indicator is r(p∨t)∨qs, and q∨s covers every edge. On a triangle u,v,w the local indicator is uvw and u∨v covers every edge. Combine the two local indicators with the conjunction of their cover signals, taking an OR of the three terms. This uses five ORs for C3+P4 and seven for P4+P5, checked on all 64 and 128 underlying edge sets respectively.

There are useful restrictions even prior to any lower-bound replay. In a cap-four C3+P4 decoder the two P4 end flags cannot be joined by a direct OR. Such a join permits replacing P4 by a triangle on the same three independent labels while saving that gate. The two interior path flags are triangle flags, endpoint indicators are singleton-exact overapproximations obtained by ANDing the two adjacent triangle flags, and the third triangle flag supplies the exact end-indicator union. Substitution dominates the intended computation on positives and stays exact on negative inputs. This would give three ORs for two triangles, below their four-OR minimum. For identical reasons a cap-six P4+P5 decoder cannot directly join the P4 ends or the P5 ends: closing those paths would save one OR, contradicting respectively the certified six-OR floors for C3+P5 and C4+P4.

A single smaller alternative query suffices for both families. If P4+P4 needs six ORs, then retaining these paths in C3+P3+P4+P4 and charging the remotes gives 6+3+3=12. In P4+P5+P5 prune one end of the first P5 (one OR by the path edge-extension witness) and charge the remaining P5 to the two P4s, giving 1+6+5=12. The two-path upper formula above with the P4 formula on both sides uses six ORs.

The P4+P4 cap-five certificate in geometry/fourteen34/fifteen-path-sat passed independent DRAT replay (20,086,309 resolution steps). Its six-OR construction is given above. The preceding two applications therefore exclude both residual families and all regular fifteen-flag fourteen-edge hosts at size 34 with eight ANDs, regardless of connectivity. The separate C3+P4 cap-four query also passed independent DRAT replay (1,006,338 resolution steps). No P4+P5 cap-six replay is required.
