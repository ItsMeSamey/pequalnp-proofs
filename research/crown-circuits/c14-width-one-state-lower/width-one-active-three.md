# At least three exclusive receivers on each active panel

Use the notation and hypotheses of one-dirty-width-one-state-minors.md and the twelve-receiver classification in width-one-twelve-grid.md. Suppose exactly two receivers have deficit confined to the positive panel. Delete this panel. The unique direct use of the positive literal P was a helper J=P∨X, with X a nonempty disjunction of outside literals. The other six helper projections are the six distinct signed pair helpers of the twelve-grid classification. On the remaining two panels the state axis c has signs O and M, and T projects to M. Every projected pair helper involving c pairs it with the same outside coordinate ζ, since the canonical bank uses two fixed complementary pairs of axes.

A discarded receiver accepts both I and the negative panel at every point on its nonempty outside face. It must use O and M and cannot use T or P. In particular its ancestry avoids J. No surviving helper contains both opposite c signs. The two inputs to the receiver must therefore separately carry the two opposite signs. Every outside literal of either input is on ζ, since the outside coordinates survive projection unchanged and these inputs are primitives or surviving pair helpers. The two discarded receivers have nonempty disjoint deficits on the positive panel, each fixing at most ζ. They fix its two opposite polarities, and their union is the whole panel.

Consequently every surviving receiver accepts the positive state on its nonempty outside face. A receiver with a deficit on the negative panel omits T and must use P, hence uses J and carries X. All its negative-panel deficit points therefore lie in the proper outside face on which X vanishes. But the twelve-grid projected deficits tile the complete negative panel. This contradiction excludes two positive-exclusive receivers. Exchanging signs excludes two negative-exclusive receivers. Together with the preceding lower bounds and the inactive-panel exclusion, each of the three panels has at least three exclusive receivers.

The claim concerns one dirty AND with one-literal continuation at the stated gate budget. It does not exclude that circuit class.
