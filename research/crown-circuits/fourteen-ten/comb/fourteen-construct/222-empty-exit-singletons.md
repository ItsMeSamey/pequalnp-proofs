# K2,3: one empty exit and five singleton slots

In the two-exit, two-cold-OR stratum use the established boundary classification with L the eight-edge component and R=K2,3 with parts X={x1,x2}, Y={y1,y2,y3}. Each exit has a designated conjunction of R raws multiplying any earlier cold-OR and live factors; each cold OR has two such designated conjunctions. Suppose precisely one exit conjunction is empty and the other five slots are the five distinct singleton raws. This note rules out that stratum.

If the nonempty exit is gated by x2, fix x2=0,x1=1; exchanging the two x flags covers the other choice. The nonempty exit's cold operand is zero even with L active. Before the empty exit every live signal is independent of R, because only cold contributions to earlier exits could introduce such dependence. On L=0 these live signals vanish. The small function on Y is threshold two: choosing a singleton Y gives one edge and any pair of Y gives two edges. The live output as a function of the empty exit's cold operand on this slice is monotone unary and nonconstant, so equals that operand. The three minimal terms of threshold two require two ORs; both cold ORs therefore precede the empty exit and survive on this slice. No pre-exit live factor enters the contributing cold AND cone: it would vanish at L=0, darkening that AND, bypassing a required cold OR along a path, or annihilating the entire exit operand. With x1=1,x2=0 the operand accordingly remains independent of L. It vanishes on every singleton Y. For a fixed L edge, the empty exit contributes zero on an empty R input as well: monotonicity bounds that contribution by its zero value on x1 plus a singleton Y. Both exits therefore transmit exactly as on empty R, contradicting cross-edge acceptance.

Suppose instead the nonempty exit is gated by y3; permuting Y suffices. Pin L=0 and y3=0. This leaves a K2,2 subproblem on a four-set W={x1,x2,y1,y2}. Every edge is negative and every triple positive. Every flag is essential: for a desired flag a choose a genuine edge avoiding it and add a. The four singleton slots on the two cold ORs are exactly W. Before the earliest cold OR all live signals are constant on this subcube: the nonempty exit's obligatory y3 is zero, and the empty exit, if already present, has neither a raw factor nor any earlier cold OR. Hence the earliest cold OR is a disjunction of two distinct raws a,b in W with constant masking on each. All later raw dependence on a,b passes through this single bit; neither has another designated slot. A constant or singleton output from the OR contradicts essentiality. If it outputs a∨b, choose a neighbor z of b different from a, possible since K2,2 is 2-regular. The edge {b,z} rejects while the triple {a,b,z} accepts. These assignments agree on a∨b and the other two flags, contradiction.

This argument completes the five-singleton, one-empty-exit case only. The remaining slot classifications and other exit counts are separate prerequisites for excluding both complete hosts.
