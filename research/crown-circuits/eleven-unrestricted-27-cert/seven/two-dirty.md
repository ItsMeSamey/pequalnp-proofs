# Eleven inputs, 26 gates, seven AND slots and two dirty ANDs

Suppose a minimum-size monotone threshold-two circuit on eleven inputs has size 26, at most seven ANDs, and is chosen to minimize dirty ANDs under that bound. Its size is globally minimum because the established lower bound is 26. The independent ten-input minimum of 24 excludes a direct receiver: specialization of a direct input would leave at most 23 gates. Terminal collection puts its singleton-negative ANDs at the output; unused clean selector coordinates can pad a smaller number of ANDs. This argument is conditional on the existing specialization and clean-terminal results.

With two dirty and five clean AND positions, the 26-gate circuit gives at most 16 reverse union gates. Eleven distinct raw receivers are paid sinks, leaving at most five auxiliary binary unions. At least two shared nonraw helper masks carry pins of the earliest dirty comparator and no protected continuation can use those pins. The joint cost of the protected continuations is therefore at most three. Enumerate these two chronological continuation masks on the fourteen signed input ports, retaining simultaneous physical gate permutations and independent pin swaps. The seven-axis template generators in ../seven/higher_templates.cpp and ../seven/higher_templates_reference.cpp produce identical catalogs. There are four, nine, twenty-one and thirty-four physical classes at joint costs zero, one, two and three.

full11.cpp uses the independently reviewed seven-coordinate formal helper checker with eleven raw receivers. A raw must have a nonempty deficit of size at most 118 of the 128 selector directions. The hard-anchor check requires ten other disjoint deficits. Protected synthesis, input-port witnesses, the source-count budget, phase ordering, stabilizers and hard-singleton reductions have the same proofs as in the twelve-input checker. Every listed template is searched with five available helper unions, including the matching-equality classes.

The four cost files contain a complete zero-hit row for every catalog ID. The count is 68 of 68. This excludes a minimum-dirty two-dirty configuration at eleven inputs, size 26 and at most seven AND positions; the other dirty counts require independent arguments.

The positive control lifts the included ten-input witness to 27 gates on eleven inputs. It evaluates the circuit on all 2,048 assignments, reconstructs the six auxiliary unions, and passes the same eleven-receiver packing predicate used for the exclusion. Removing a receiver makes that predicate reject. Run `sh replay_two.sh /dev/shm/eleven-replay` in this directory to regenerate the catalog, positive control and all four result files.
