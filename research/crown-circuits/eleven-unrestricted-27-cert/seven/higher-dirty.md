# Seven genuine ANDs on eleven inputs

Suppose an eleven-input monotone threshold-two circuit has 26 gates, exactly seven physical ANDs, and is in the minimum-dirty normal form used in two-dirty.md. The bare-gate and raw-regularity hypotheses refer to a globally minimum circuit. Every signed selector half contains at least two whole singleton deficits. To see this, fix a sign and retain the opposite half. If at most one receiver loses its entire deficit, let y be this label, choosing an arbitrary label when none is lost. Setting y=0 removes its two distinct OR readers. The remaining ten labels still compute threshold two, so no AND was removed, since a ten-label circuit of size 23 contradicts the ten-input minimum of 24. Resolve the designated surviving AND to its chosen parent. Every remaining deficit meets the selected half and pair acceptance still holds there. The resulting ten-input circuit has at most 23 gates, a contradiction.

A deficit wholly inside a signed half has its corresponding port observed at each of its points. Otherwise flipping that selector, which does not change behavior at an inactive comparator, would leave the point in the deficit. Consequently each signed port must occur in at least two distinct observable atoms. A family of six pairwise-disjoint signed port events would require twelve distinct labels. project11.cpp tests these conditions and the eleven-atom lower bound before the physical helper partition. This step uses genuine ANDs.

For a chronological physical template let c count the least joint continuation cost and e count earliest dirty gates. Each earliest gate requires two shared opposite-pin witnesses, so c+e≤5. At equality the extra helpers pair all root pins. A two-cycle of paired roots admits a clean realization. cycles11.cpp handles the other equality cases, two_tags11.cpp the c=3 single-root case, and residual11.cpp the strict inequality for c≤2. verify_higher.py compares every result's ID to its uniquely determined class and requires a zero hit count. The seven-selector representation has 128 directions; every search must find eleven nonempty disjoint deficits, including any prescribed anchor.

The six-dirty complete results cover 313,318 chronological templates. The verified partition is in six/verification-6.out and the corresponding files are registered in six-dirty.sha256.
