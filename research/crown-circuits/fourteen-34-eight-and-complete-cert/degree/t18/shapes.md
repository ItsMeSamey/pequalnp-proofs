# Seventeen post-branch shapes at q=18

Consider the regular fourteen-input 34-gate eight-AND candidate with eighteen raw group vertices. Its original relative OR budget is 16; the branch reduction gives N−3 ORs on N=17−t remaining flags when the degree-d branch has t leaf neighbors. This inherits the same eight-AND upper bound, and the surviving edge count is 14−d, independent of t. Use the original q16 structural dependencies for the branch reduction. All arguments here cover disconnected hosts as well as connected ones.

A quartic flag forces its component to be a bare four-star and every remote component to be a star by the earlier quartic/full-order nonstar transfer. Higher degree is already excluded at this budget. A star forest on eighteen vertices and fourteen edges has four components. At least three of these stars have degree at least three: two with degree at most four and two with degree at most two could account for only twelve edges. Deleting a branching flag and its adjacent leaves affects only one component. Two intact stars of degree at least three remain. Peel excess leaves, one OR each by a surviving local sibling-pair witness. Their remaining two three-stars alone require six ORs, so these components together cost at least their combined order minus two. This two-component base has no universal singleton flag. All remaining nontrivial components charge full order by peeling to an independent raw. No unaccounted isolates occur: in the affected star a branching pure flag has degree at least two, hence is its center and the branch procedure removes all its leaves. This contradicts N−3. Thus no quartic is possible.

Consequently d is two or three. The same component-floor tests as at q16 allow only K2, P3, K1,3, P4, P5, C3 and C4, with at most one item outside K2/P3: two three-stars are strong, two of the nonstar shapes are strong, and a three-star plus any nonstar is strong. Write e=14−d. There are N−e=3+d−t tree components by Euler. Summing orders and edges over these allowed types gives the complete table in census.tsv, reproduced by the short census.py. It enumerates 17 rows.

At (d,t)=(2,0), delete one leaf of a P3 (at least one OR), identify the two remaining endpoint flags and use the resulting independent raw. The three rows then coincide with q17 P5+3P3+K2, C4+2P3+3K2 and C3+3P3+2K2 at thirteen ORs/eight ANDs. The (2,1) branch is q17 C4+4P3. The five (3,1) rows are the five q17 branches with (3,0), at the identical gate caps. Both (3,2) rows are the q16 cyclic cases at twelve ORs and eight ANDs. These eleven transfers use their source results and do not claim that a smaller arity by itself forces the required composition.

The remaining six shapes have d=3,t=0, eleven labels and fourteen decoder ORs. They are P5+2P3+3K2, K1,3+3P3+2K2, P4+3P3+2K2, 5P3+K2, C4+P3+5K2 and C3+2P3+4K2.
