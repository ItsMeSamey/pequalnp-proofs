# Four new eleven-label nonstar rows at eighteen flags

The four cases are C3+2P3+4K2, C4+P3+5K2, P4+3P3+2K2, and P5+2P3+3K2, each with **fourteen** decoder ORs and eight ANDs. They arise from the corresponding q17 row by replacing one P3 with two independent edge raws. Write G for the retained nonstar, h for its order, k for the number of short paths and j for the number of independent raws. Thus h+3k+2j=17, and restoring the five genuine bank ORs yields 27 gates on eleven independent raws. In what follows all OR allowances are fourteen.

**Reader reductions.** There are two neighboring internal raw bank ORs in each G. Thus the T10 forest transfer of degree/t16-cyclic-build/ten-forest.md applies to every independent raw and all short paths. Exactly as in degree/t17/cyclic-extra-edges.md and path-five.md, every independent raw has two OR readers and all short leaves are single-read into ORs. Each short center is single-read and ordinary. The numerical changes from the matching q17 row are +1 available OR, −1 short path and +2 independent raws: the exterior charge 3k+2j also rises by one. Every lower bound used to restrict an outside leaf or center therefore rises by the same one. The local base G has no universal singleton flag, with OR floors respectively 0,1,1,2 for C3,C4,P4,P5. When two short centers share a clean product the same deletion of one path and splitting the other path into two independent raws contradicts the same numerical deficit. For the square case with one P3 there is no second center to consider.

For the cycles the false-pin composition is T9 starting at 24 gates and sixteen ORs before simplification: count fourteen initial ORs plus two restored banks, either both remaining short banks for C3, or the one short bank and reduced-cycle middle bank for C4. For the triangle two-port upper count, use the current 22 decoder gates plus two outside banks, yielding the same sum 24 as in the original autonomous-port argument. All its restrictions, the no-pure-cycle-OR lemmas, and at most one triangle AND reader remain valid. The square dirty-AND erasure still retains the adjacent two unaltered banks, so every direct square AND reader is clean. In the harmless-vertex and cycle-branch counting arguments the additional independent raws raise the outside floor by one, as before. More explicitly, pin the harmless triangle vertex true and add the two short banks for sixteen ORs on T10; for a harmless square vertex first erase its opposite, and add the single short bank for fourteen ORs on T9 before the remaining mandatory pin. To bound branching the outside floors are respectively 10 and 9 (erase k leaves, leaving k+j independent bits). Hence each cycle has b≤1, using exclusion of the pure cycle-flag OR groups. The internal graph G=P4 or P5 has no cycle argument: its endpoint readers obey exactly the q17 bounds, determined by the unchanged C11→C10 gap and the physical counterexample in degree/t17/path-four.md or path-five.md. Both P5 endpoints have a single OR reader. A P4 endpoint has a single OR or a single clean AND reader.

**Incidence graph.** Each physical raw has two effective occurrences, including an endpoint with one direct AND and one bank OR in the P4 case. Represent this direct raw-to-AND occurrence by a dedicated degree-one flag (an identity, zero bank cost). This is a permissible fixed-bank representation; it does not assert that the physical gate was an OR. Every other occurrence is fed into the usual flattened OR groups. If several private AND occurrences of a single raw existed, separate flags might need care; there is at most one here. The relative OR allowance is 19−(22−v)=v−3. The graph is simple: two output groups observing the same two distinct raw labels together cannot distinguish the corresponding singleton from their pair, and a repeated raw within an OR tree saves a gate. A dedicated degree-one endpoint cannot create a duplicated incidence.

The retained core G is certified nonstar: two neighboring bank roots are distinct and mark opposite ends of their common raw edge. Consequently the host has no degree three anywhere, by ten-forest.md's general high-degree-component plus nonstar bound. Each traced cycle thus stays an isolated simple cycle. A traced path keeps its interior vertices distinct (for P5 identifying the first and third internal vertices would create parallel middle edges). End-to-end closure of P4 or P5 would allow regrouping the two sole OR endpoints, then identifying them to obtain respectively C3 or C4 with the same remaining paths and independent raws at one fewer OR. These are preceding q17 cyclic exclusions. An AND-read endpoint is a dedicated singleton flag and cannot coalesce.

If two independent raws occupy a common OR group, regroup their OR and supply it as a free short-path center while retaining the two independent leaves. This eliminates one paid OR, yielding the q17 instance of the same G at its thirteen-OR/eight-AND cap, already excluded. Hence their endpoints cannot coalesce with one another.

No independent raw or outside flag can attach to G except possibly one producing a longer P4 as follows. On a cycle such attachment exceeds degree two. Extending P5 yields P6, or closing a six-or-more-edge trace into a cycle yields C5 or a longer cycle, all strong by their local floors and peeling. Extending P4 to a path of six vertices is similarly impossible; closure into C5 or a longer cycle is strong. Extending P4 into exactly P5 uses just one more edge. This edge must be an independent raw since another P3 adds at least two. In that case any additional attachment elsewhere making a second nonstar gives a strong pair with P5, or a degree-three vertex. The remaining independent raw cannot form a two-edge path with a different independent raw (there is only one left), so it must remain isolated. The resulting graph is precisely P5+3P3+K2, a q17 exclusion at its intrinsic v−3 OR budget and eight ANDs.

For completeness, a K2 edge can also join the two P4 endpoints directly and form C5, already excluded; two P3s joined by flag fusion give a second nonstar, as does an independent raw extending or closing any P3. The known strong pairs for all two nonstars of orders three through five and their extensions exclude each possibility. A component of two independent edges has already been excluded by the common-group argument. The only physical shape left is G with all k short paths unchanged and j isolated independent edges.

**Terminal budgets and local endpoints.** Those j isolated edges cost 2j distinct mixed injection ORs; their endpoints cannot occur together with short-path flags or G flags in the same raw group. The k ordinary paths cost 3k separate injections/collector ORs as in the matching q17 file. A surviving core positive permits full collector debit when path terms are removed. This leaves exactly h−3 ORs for G, namely 0,1,1,2 in the four respective cases. The proofs now specialize without changing a local count.

- For C3, at most one vertex branches and at most one has an AND reader, so there is an additional distinct sole OR beyond zero.
- For C4, the at least three single-read vertices cost three additional ORs: each pays an injection or a dedicated clean terminal. An independent raw plus the square edge missed by all clean square vertices supplies an extra surviving terminal, hence no collector offset. Even granting one offset would exceed one.
- For P4, the two endpoint charges contradict one OR. Shared endpoint OR is the excluded triangle conversion. Different ORs cost two. One AND and one OR also cost two since the other local pair survives the endpoint's clean term. Two different ANDs would violate physical direct-AND exclusion at T10 by erasing one endpoint, while a common AND conceals its addition when the other endpoint is absent. See degree/t17/path-four.md.
- For P5, two distinct endpoint ORs exhaust the two remaining slots. Setting the ordinary paths and independent edges zero bypasses their charged ORs syntactically as before, so the exact unrestricted five-flag expression is (a∨cd)(e∨bc). Each of b,c,d must reach an endpoint OR. A sole AND reader would be hot by clean-terminal normalization, and deletion of the other incident raw yields the globally forbidden T10/24/A7, independent of any absorbed earlier OR factor. The three two-reader banks, each OR-only, now follow from the same physical T11-to-T9 counts as in the corrected degree/t17/path-five.md. Zeroing them and then a costs seven ORs. The remaining kP3+jK2 require at least k+2(k+j)−4=3k+2j−4, while only 14−7=7 are available (the requirement here is eight).

All four new q18 nonstar rows are thereby excluded.
