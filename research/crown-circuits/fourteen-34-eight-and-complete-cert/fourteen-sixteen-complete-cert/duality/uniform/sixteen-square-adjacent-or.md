# Adjacent square flags cannot be joined by an OR

Use the twelve-OR, eight-AND decoder on C4+3P3+K2. Suppose H=A∨B reads adjacent square flags. Both A and B require another user: if A had only H then singleton BC and its union with AB would be indistinguishable, and the symmetric test applies to B. The cycle-reader lemma gives exactly two OR readers each; call their second readers L_A,L_B. Neither can equal the other, which would duplicate H, or directly read H, which dominates both A and B. H is live and is not the output.

Pin C false. Keep only edges AB and AD, as independent labels x,y, so B=x,D=y,A=x∨y=F, and bypass H to F. The untouched three P3s and K2 provide seven further independent labels and need three bank ORs. Together with the one OR for F the bare nine-input circuit has sixteen ORs and eight ANDs before decoder savings. Erasure of C costs at least one OR using its local extension witness; that first OR cannot be H, whose only operands are A,B. More than two OR losses violate the fourteen-OR floor. If C has an AND reader, its AND and a subsequent first OR both disappear, alongside H: at most twenty-one gates remain, with at least seven ANDs if precisely three gates were removed, contradicting the four-AND size-21 composition. Additional gate savings violate the size floor. Thus C has one OR reader L_C. The symmetric pin D false yields the same result for D; denote its sole OR reader by L_D. Neither singly used flag can share its OR with a bare other cycle vertex: every other vertex is present at some square singleton where adding the singly used vertex creates a second edge. In particular L_C and L_D are distinct from L_A and L_B and from one another.

Return to the C-false minor. Eliminate L_C by passing its other operand, and eliminate H by passing F. Now T2(9) has fourteen ORs and eight ANDs, the joint optimum at the OR floor. Its raw x feeds F and L_B and its raw y feeds F and L_D. These external OR readers are distinct. F has the user L_A and a distinct user inherited from H: if any H user other than L_C exists, that original user is distinct from L_A by absorption. Otherwise L_C was H's sole user; replacing L_C by its surviving operand passes H's uses onward. No user of L_C can be L_A, because originally L_C≥H≥A would absorb the direct A input of L_A. All surviving uses may be kept through these two substitutions; any further simplification would exceed the primary or secondary floor. Apply the shared pure-cherry obstruction in degree/t16-cyclic-build/square-no-nonempty.md to F and its two external raw readers. This is impossible.

The opposite-pair obstruction in that separate note now forbids every OR of two raw square flags. In a flattened group exclusively containing branching square flags one could associate the first two as such an OR. Thus the pure branch-group count P is zero, and sixteen-cycle-readers.md gives at most one branching square vertex.
