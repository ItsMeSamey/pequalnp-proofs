# Five-vertex path, three two-edge paths and an independent raw

Write x,y,z,w for the consecutive P5 edge labels and a=x, b=x∨y, c=y∨z, d=z∨w, e=w for its flags. Together with three short paths and an independent edge, restoring the six bank ORs yields T11/27 from the candidate 13-OR/eight-AND decoder.

**Regularity.** The physical b,c pair gives the protected-bank obstruction ten-forest.md when any outside specialization removes two ORs and one AND. As in square-regularity.md, independent s has two OR readers, and an AND-read short leaf is impossible. A short leaf has one reader: after erasing it the remaining P5, two P3s and two independent raws need 2+6+4=12 ORs, so only one first OR can disappear (multiple AND readers factor at that frontier). Each short center is single-read: the T11-to-T9 debit in sixteen-center-branch.md forces two OR readers when branching, but then removing the full path costs four distinct ORs against a remaining 2+6+2=10 floor. A local hot center AND would save three gates by erasing the sibling, keeping b,c. Thus all short paths are ordinary.

Endpoint x must have a decoder reader besides its bank b, else adding it to the singleton y is invisible. Two additional OR readers would leave T10 with sixteen ORs and eight ANDs, forbidden at the OR floor. Any AND reader removes that reader, its first proper downstream OR, and b upon x=0, leaving the two neighboring banks c,d intact, impossible by ten-forest.md. The endpoint a therefore reads one OR, as does e. All physical raws now have exactly two OR readers.

**Physical shape.** The resulting simple incidence graph permits v−3 ORs. Its neighboring b,c banks force a nonstar. Therefore it cannot contain a vertex of degree at least three, by the local star bound and full-order nonstar transfer in ten-forest.md. Trace its four distinct raw edges x,y,z,w. The successive intermediate banks cannot coincide by raw duplication, and b=d would make parallel y,z. Thus the trace embeds a P5, unless its endpoint flags a,e enter one group to form C4. The latter permits reassociation to a∨e then identification of these two flags, saving one OR and giving the excluded q16 square. No other vertices can attach to the P5: any extension forms at least P6 or a cycle of length at least five, both strong (P6 and C5 floors, with longer paths/cycles obtained by peeling). The three short paths also remain separate, since gluing them or attaching s to one forms a second nonstar (or degree three), and P5 jointly with P4/P5/C3/C4 is strong. A P3 cannot become two parallel raw edges by identifying its leaves. Thus s is isolated, costing two distinct mixed OR groups.

**OR allocation.** The ordinary short flags cost nine distinct ORs (injections or dedicated clean collector terms, retaining a local P5 positive). To justify distinct center products, a putative common product permits erasing one short path for three ORs: its two leaf readers plus a proper OR downstream of that product, choosing the erased path to avoid the latter being its leaf reader. The retained path now contributes two independent raws; the P5, one short path and three independent raws need 2+3+6=11 against ten left. The two isolated s users and two distinct endpoint OR groups use the four remaining ORs. Each hot group is exactly one physical gate containing its assigned flag. In particular no OR reads two of b,c,d.

**The isolated local expression.** Set all outside flags to zero, keeping the five formal P5 bits unrestricted. Outside injection ORs bypass (including both s users), and designated path terms vanish from the collector. These are syntactic identities on all five-bit inputs. Only the two OR sites on a and e remain. Neither vanishes since the P5 needs two ORs. No raw flag occurs on every positive edge pair; therefore the output, an AND monomial in raws and OR results, contains no raw factor. It must use both OR gates since each individually accepts its endpoint singleton. Its expression is (a∨M)(e∨N), where M,N are raw monomials: if the later operand depends on the earlier OR, erase that factor using the final product. All pairs among y,z,w force M to use only c,d. All pairs among x,y,z force N to use only b,c. Singleton w forces c in M, and x forces c in N. Then singleton y forces d in M and z forces b in N. Thus on all formal assignments the restricted result is (a∨cd)(e∨bc).

**All three internal banks branch.** Each of b,c,d must reach one of the two endpoint ORs in the original decoder: these flags occur as raw factors in cd or bc after simplification, even if the physical later operand also carries the earlier OR. A sole AND reader on that route cannot be singleton-clean, because clean-terminal normalization forbids a clean signal from reaching a hot gate, and both endpoint ORs are hot. For any of the three bank flags its sole AND is therefore hot on some incident singleton. Delete its other incident raw. If this is an internal raw y or z, two distinct neighboring bank ORs disappear. If it is an endpoint x or w, the bank and its separate leaf OR disappear. In every case the marked bank becomes its surviving singleton raw, and its AND aliases that raw by monotonicity. The three losses yield T10/24/A7, excluded without any preserved-bank assumption by duality/uniform/ten-unrestricted-seven-and.md (SHA 6cf4bc42d1b4816b6ee49ffbd643f4f3f5e0f8be492979fe23d282cd4ebe07f7). This argument does not infer the value of the physical reader from an absorbed monomial. A sole OR reader is also impossible: the bank's two physical raws would merge with the assigned raw tag of that OR, exceeding degree two; if that tag is already in the bank the gate is redundant. Thus all three internal banks branch.

Each branching bank has exactly two OR readers. For c the two other uses of y,z enter b,d, which remain live on erasure; use sixteen-cycle-readers.md. For b, erase x,y. Dark-edge accounting charges four raw incidences, f(b)−1 surplus and the number of one-sided dark ANDs. Six losses require four ANDs by the T9 optimum and further losses violate its size. If f(b)≥3 no one-sided AND can occur, and no first nonredundant both-dark AND can be formed: after the bank the only available source values are x and b with x≤b. For f(b)=2 an AND reader would be one-sided (another dark operand would imply b). Its extra debit leaves no further dark fanout. Only three dark outgoing occurrences remain after the bank and the other y read into surviving c: two from b and one from x. Each both-dark merger consumes one such occurrence and at least one must exit. This gives at most two both-dark ANDs plus the single one-sided AND, short of four. Symmetry covers d.

Erase b,c,d as formal flags, saving six distinct direct ORs. The local expression on outside-empty inputs is now ae, so a is essential when e is present. Some outside positive survives a=0, and deleting a therefore loses another OR. Three P3s and one independent raw still need seven (erase three leaves then threshold four needs four). Only six remain.
