# Excluding physical cubic raw vertices at size 27 with eight ANDs on eleven labels

A size-24 ten-input minimum uses at most six ANDs: eight would leave sixteen ORs where nine ANDs are required; seven is excluded by ten-unrestricted-seven-and.md, and more would violate the OR floor. Hence at size 27 and eight ANDs on eleven inputs no raw deletion can save three gates including two ORs, nor four gates of any kind.

The standard regularization in degree/t13-31/or-optimal.md now preserves this composition. Three OR readers are impossible. A raw with an AND reader and an OR reader loses the AND, the OR and a distinct first proper OR reached through that AND (absorption rules out equality). If all readers are ANDs, two first ORs yield the same impossible deletion; two or more initial ANDs and only one frontier OR allow single-frontier factoring with a gate saving. A sole AND reader has the full disjunction of all other raws as its partner, and erasure saves that AND, a downstream OR and the partner's maximum OR root. A sole OR reader's other operand is singleton-clean and may be forwarded to the collector at the same composition, moving the raw past this reader until it has two OR readers (direct ANDs having been excluded). Clean-terminal normalization preserves regularity as in degree/t16-cyclic-build/ten-forest.md. Each AND side has at least two exclusive singleton labels: otherwise resolve to that side, erasing the lone false-positive raw (any raw if absent). Its two regular ORs go with the resolved AND, an impossible ten-input minor.

The eleven-edge simple regular graph has relative decoder budget 19−(22−v)=v−3. A high vertex (degree d≥3) in a nonstar component needs a local order-minus-two bound, then all remote components cost full order. A high bare star and a remote nonstar likewise need joint order minus two by full-order nonstar transfer. Hence any high vertex forces a star forest. A d-star costs 2d−4, and its first remote costs order minus one with full order thereafter, excluding d≥4. Two three-stars cost six with all remotes at full order. Thus the only possible high graph is precisely one three-star, p short paths and s independent edges, where 2p+s=8.

The L=3+2p+2s degree-one vertices charge L distinct mixed ORs, leaving p−2 relative ORs. Each mixed short center costs its own OR beyond its bank. If p≤3, a pure doubleton cannot branch: sixteen-center-branch.md gives two OR exits to a branching center; the two leaf groups and these exits debit four ORs when deleting that path. Both leaf injection gates are distinct from both exits since a center exit feeding its own leaf group absorbs its raw. The remaining triple and remote components (s≥2) need at least their order minus three, permitting only three OR losses. Every private pure doubleton heads a clean product by the two-exclusive-shore condition. At most two centers share each such AND; an isolated three-star pair is positive without them, so each costs a collector OR. The centers therefore cost at least ceil(p/2)>p−2. For p=4 the graph is exactly the separately excluded K1,3+4P3 at 13 ORs and eight ANDs (seventeen-star-four-paths.md). Consequently any normalized regular physical T11 at this composition has maximum degree at most two.

In particular the q18 interface K1,3+3P3+2K2 (14 decoder ORs, eight ANDs, five bank ORs) is impossible. Its two-gate three-star bank survives regularization: every star raw has its bank use and another use by the sibling-pair test. Sole-raw relocations cannot bypass either bank gate or add an exit to the private partial join, whose sole reader is the full bank. Neither protected gate has a clean operand. Size minimality preserves liveness. Therefore all three raws share a flattened vertex, contradicting maximum degree two.
