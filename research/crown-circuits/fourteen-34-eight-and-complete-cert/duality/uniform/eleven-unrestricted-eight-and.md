# Excluding eight ANDs at 27 gates for threshold two on eleven independent inputs

Assume a binary monotone circuit computes T2(11) with at most 27 gates and eight ANDs. The known minimum is 27, hence its composition is nineteen ORs and eight ANDs.

1. The unconditional T10/24 seven-AND exclusion is ten-unrestricted-seven-and.md (6cf4bc42). At ten, eight ANDs with 24 gates would realize the OR minimum with one too few ANDs, and nine or more ANDs violate the OR lower bound. A three-gate raw deletion from our witness losing at least two ORs is therefore impossible; larger deletions contradict minimum size at ten.
2. By eleven-cubic.md (0733db47), direct ANDs and third OR readers of a raw are impossible, sole OR readers can be relocated at fixed composition, and clean-terminal normalization preserves the regular representation. Every AND side has at least two exclusive singleton labels. The full simple raw-incidence graph has eleven edges and v−3 decoder ORs on its v vertices. All vertices have degree at most two. The proof of this step uses the q17 K1,3+4P3 fixed-bank exclusion (049669c5); that exclusion uses only smaller-arity theorems and direct conditional counts at eleven. Thus these steps do not depend on the present unconditional conclusion.
3. By eleven-nonstar.md (1b8a36dc) there can be no nonstar component. Its proof covers every exterior composition for C3,C4,P4,P5, and uses strong floors for larger components and pairs. It depends only on the preceding regularity and fixed-interface results. In particular it counts all eleven raw edges before specialization.
4. It remains to rule out pP3+sK2 with 2p+s=11. Here v=3p+2s, and the L=2p+2s degree-one vertices require distinct mixed injections, leaving p−3 other decoder ORs. If p<3 this already fails. The argument below is the intrinsic-graph part of degree/t18/five-short.md (60b9ebfb), with p arbitrary. A mixed short center uses its own extra decoder OR. A private pure short center reaches a clean AND since both its labels must be exclusive; two centers cannot share that AND. Indeed erasing one bank uses its own bank, its two leaf injections, the product and a collector OR, and also makes the other bank unused. This removes five ORs and one AND and contradicts the exact T9/21 four-AND composition. A positive survives on another raw pair, and the collector OR is clean and distinct from the leaf injections.
5. If there are no branching pure centers the p mixed/private centers cost at least p−1 after the maximum one collector offset. This exceeds p−3. Otherwise a positive on a branching pair survives when all private paths are zero. Writing b for the number of branching pure centers, the p−b mixed/private centers therefore require p−b distinct extra ORs. Hence b≥3.
6. Each branching center has two OR exits by sixteen-center-branch.md. Reassociate the singleton-bearing mixed groups raw-last. Deleting a branching pair i eliminates its bank, its two leaf injections, and its two center exits (an exit into its own leaf group would absorb that raw). The surviving T9 circuit has fourteen ORs and eight ANDs, simultaneously at their primary and secondary OR-optimum floors. All other gates are live and irredundant. The four removed decoder ORs bypass with exactly one live operand: a fully dark center exit is absorbed by the center; a fully dark leaf injection would use another dark signal, with the only initial dark generators the two raws and their bank, yielding absorption/duplication or another dark gate loss. As detailed in degree/t18/five-short.md, another branching bank j retains multiple consumer slots: each formerly bypassed slot traces forward to a live one, and two routes cannot first coalesce in a bypass with only one live operand. A repeated operand at a surviving gate would violate secondary optimality. If j's two raw external ORs both still exist, the OR-tight secondary-minimal local cherry lemma (degree/t16-cyclic-build/square-no-nonempty.md) contradicts j's branching. Thus i's two outgoing ORs must hit a distinct leaf injection in every other branching pair. It follows that b≤3.
7. Equality b=3 also saturates the L+p−3 OR budget. Restrict every nonbranching pair and independent raw to false. Their singly-raw groups bypass, while all nonbranching-center mixed ORs or collector charges vanish, with a positive on a branching pair surviving. Exactly six branch-leaf injections remain in the decoder, one from each branching center to a leaf in each of the other two pairs. These are direct ORs of a two-raw bank and a leaf in a different pair, with no extra operand. They are true on just three of the six surviving singleton labels. All surviving signals available to the downstream AND-only computation are raws, pair-bank ORs, or these injections. Each is false on some pair of distinct raws outside its support. Hence no output monomial can be true on every pair, contradicting T2(6).

These alternatives exhaust all graphs from every original circuit of the specified composition. No size-27 T2(11) circuit uses eight ANDs. The OR minimum eighteen at eleven inputs requires at least ten ANDs, so any size-27 circuit uses at most seven ANDs. These conclusions concern the composition of minima; the numerical size minimum 27 is unchanged.
