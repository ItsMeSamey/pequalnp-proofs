# Two early products reaching one common input

For five ordered ANDs in a threshold-two circuit, assume the first D reaches exactly one input of the second B through ORs only. Write D=XY and B=K(D∨J), with X,Y,K,J raw disjunctions. Resolving the three later products gives L_s∨δ_sD∨ε_sB for every tail assignment s. Thus δ,ε and L can depend on s, but the physical membership signature of every raw in X,Y,K,J is fixed.

Let t=δ+2ε and evaluate f_t(v)=δD(v)∨εB(v) for every four-bit signature. Each raw has a rejecting tail assignment: on its singleton, resolve every false AND to a false operand, preserving every wire value. Assign each raw to one such assignment. Any two assigned raws have f_t(v)=f_t(w)=0 and f_t(v∨w)=1, since neither activates the raw-linear bypass. Their signatures therefore differ. Conversely, granting independent bypass ORs at each tail state, these clique constraints suffice: set L_s to exactly the unassigned raws, so all pairs remain positive under every resolution and every raw has a negative resolution. The histogram DP in prefix.py decides exact feasibility in this relaxation for a specified multiset of fourteen raw signatures. The four maximal clique sizes are respectively 1,2,3,4.

Zero D in the physical prefix, preserving its two input cones and the two resulting B-pin producers, to obtain a simultaneous OR-only network for X,Y,K,J. Its cost lower-bounds the original prefix cost when minimized. Discard empty/duplicate outputs and unused raws. With n active raws, m distinct outputs and s binary joins, the network has 2s+m uses, counting observers. Transposition aggregates the uses at each wire with s+m−n binary joins, generating the transposed relation. Applying both ways proves equality of optima. On ≤4 reverse primitives, at most eleven nontrivial masks exist. Require every nontrivial raw signature in the bank; enumerate subsets of optional nontrivial masks, and in increasing size test that each is the union of two already available smaller masks. A redundant same-mask use can be bypassed. This yields the exact OR-only price implemented in price().

The census parameterizes all reverse tail activation tuples on three ordered oriented axes, without signed-permutation quotient. The latest tail gate is observed, and each earlier live gate has activation equal to a union of later pins or a direct output. δ and ε are unions of tail pins: when D and B are dirty, neither can be connected to the observer via only ORs. Each tail clique adds its cardinality and its observed singleton-hot D/B indicators. Summing to 14 and attaining both indicators is necessary. It is sufficient only in the indicated independent-bypass relaxation. census.py initially grants a separate bypass even for duplicate tail observations. visible.py additionally merges assignments with the same vector of observable tail pins, since these have identical physical bypasses.
