# The four B traces forming a column plus one cell

Use ternary-rows.md and its saturated (2,0,4,0) histogram. If the two bypass-only cells are in different ternary rows of one H column, the four B cells consist of a complete ternary column (rename its sign H0) and the cell of one ternary row r in H1. B must reach H0 and the primitive corresponding to exactly r. No other primitive can hit only these four cells: the remaining H sign includes both bypass cells and a ternary sign containing another row hits a bypass cell. The ternary sign T covers two rows and cannot be used. Thus B's own formal continuation costs a helper; none of the eight mandatory B-only observers can use that helper since it covers the observer's designated rejected cell. At most six helpers are available for the eight sinks.

The required sinks have precisely X or Y and K on early pins, with J excluded. Each uses tail signs whose union is exactly the five traces outside its own designated cell, since its D,B singleton values are both false. On I there are five possible formal tail masks; each other cell has one. No assumption that two raws in the same tail class choose the same decoration is made. The duplicated row r is either I or an active row (P and M are symmetric). The two helper-bank feasibility instances are generated by ternary_sat.py.

For each of eight sinks, list every semantically permissible full formal mask. Candidate helper masks are all nonprimitive proper subsets of any permissible target. This is complete: a useful helper union can only feed supersets, and equal masks can be bypassed down to their first strict double-source union. More explicitly, if a terminal mask is also a helper and has no strictly larger target descendant, move its defining union to its unique corresponding terminal sink and discard the helper; different receivers never have the same target. One can simultaneously construct selected helpers in increasing mask cardinality. For each selected helper and for each of the eight sinks the CNF requires a pair of distinctly valued strictly smaller available masks whose union is the mask. Singletons are free. A pair of helpers in a witness conjunction is enabled only if both are selected; this is sufficient for an existential CNF. CardEnc enforces at most six selected helpers. Both negative cases admit checkable DRAT, and the two cap-twelve positive controls rebuild all eight outputs from the selected banks.

Together with the two-complete-row argument this covers all 54 chronological tuples in the histogram, subject to the stated normalization and prior small-arity lower bounds. No other histogram is decided here.
