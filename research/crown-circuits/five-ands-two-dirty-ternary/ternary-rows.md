# Four B-only triples and two bypass-only traces

Take the scoped chronological histogram (2,0,4,0) of visible.py, with D dirty, B dirty, and D feeding one B pin. Six tail traces on three selectors have the product form {I,P,M}×{+,−}. Indeed the latest tail is always observed. If the middle tail is full there are four observable two-selector states; the third adds two traces, so its activation comprises two of those states. A union of literal signs of size two must be a single sign. If the middle tail activates only on a literal, there are three two-selector traces; obtaining six requires the third active on each. In both cases the available primitive tail pins correspond to I alone (O), P and M together (T), P alone, M alone, and the two independent signs H±. The singleton trace I carries O but neither P nor M. The active states carry T plus their respective singleton pin.

The four B-only triples and two bypass-only singleton classes saturate capacity 14. They partition the raw labels. Each B-only triple contains one exclusive raw on each D side with K present and J absent, as in quarter.md. Such a raw has B=D=0 on its singleton and therefore its direct bypass mask must accept exactly the five traces other than its assigned trace. Its formal pins contain exactly X or Y and K in the early prefix.

Suppose B occupies two full ternary rows. If the rows are P,M, resolving the controlling primitive to O (its inactive sign) leaves two bypass-only states. Hence at most two singleton negatives remain. On every singleton cold on the chosen pin, replacing an AND by that pin leaves all wire values unchanged. By all-wire-ten.md at least four singletons are cold there, a contradiction.

Otherwise rename the active signs so that B occupies rows I and P. Its formal continuation requires O and P, and cannot contain another primitive: H± and T also hit the excluded row M, and the M pin hits that row. Thus producing O∨P consumes a nonobserver helper unusable in all eight exclusive receiver masks. At I the receivers omit O; at P they omit P. For each raw in row P the exact tail mask consists of O, M, and the opposite H sign. For each in row I the opposite H sign is mandatory; its other pins are a subset of {P,M,T} covering P and M, hence contain T or both P and M. Set the M literal to zero and identify T and P as one variable. Rename O as the other row variable. The eight projected outputs are exactly {K, X_i, H_j, S_k}. At least one OR disappears on setting M to zero, since every row-P receiver uses M along with other distinct variables. Initially the eight-sink subnetwork had at most 14 joins: eight mandatory sinks and at most six eligible helpers after removing the B continuation charge from the seven-helper budget. The projected at-most-13 network contradicts cube-face-price.md by the same transposition and removal of the all-eight output as in quarter.md.

This covers all placements with two B-only rows and both independent H signs on each. A remaining placement has the entire column of one H sign plus one cell of the opposite column: the two bypass-only cells form a product, because an OR of tail pins rejects a product of a ternary subset and a binary subset. It is outside the two-row exclusion.
