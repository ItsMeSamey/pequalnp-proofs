# A remote five-edge path is impossible

Apply ten-detached-doubletons.md to a two-component ten-edge host with a triple center and a detached path on six vertices. Its two middle doubletons B and D both branch and have a common physical reader B OR D. Their underlying raw sets are {x,y} and {y,z}. Setting x false bypasses B's own raw OR, and also the separate raw OR in the other group containing x. The latter group retains its other raw label in the path. The common reader now has operands y and y OR z, and hence bypasses to D. None of these three rewrites removes an AND. The remaining circuit computes threshold two on nine inputs with at most 21 gates. At fewer than 21 this contradicts the minimum. At 21 it has seven ANDs, whereas the certified nine-input optimum always has four. Thus a remote path in the tight two-component case has at least six edges.
