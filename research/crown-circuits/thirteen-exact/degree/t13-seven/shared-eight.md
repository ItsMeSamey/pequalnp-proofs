# A shared wire accepts at most eight singleton inputs

Assume a regular seven-AND, 31-gate thirteen-input threshold-two circuit. Each shared internal wire is either an AND or the output of a maximal flattened OR group. Reassociation inside the groups preserves that wire and its outgoing occurrences.

Suppose wire W with fanout f≥2 accepts nine selected singleton inputs. Set the four other inputs to zero and clamp W to true. Every nonempty subinput has W true, by monotonicity. The value at the empty input is forced false by the values at singletons. The shared-wire telescope in five-four-branching.md gives a nine-input circuit with at most 5+2·9−f≤21 gates.

It also gives a circuit with at most sixteen ORs. To see this count on the same clamp, first mark which gates are forced constant by the four raw deletions alone. The group contraction in census.md removes at least eight ORs using constant simplification. If one pure group is fully dark, the eighth loss can be recovered at the first surviving OR along a path from that group, since this OR has a zero nonraw input in addition to its lost raw inputs. Now W was not constant under partial evaluation: its zero value at the zero input and its true singleton values remain. Clamping it only refines the assignment of known constants, so every other originally forced constant stays forced at the same value. In particular all eight ORs with a constant operand remain removable, including W itself if the marked set includes its root, in which case clamping removes it directly. Run the simultaneous constant simplification underlying the telescope. The result has at most 24−8=16 ORs. This contradicts the composition of every 21-gate nine-input optimum, which has exactly seventeen ORs.

For three branching pure groups (all triples), the raw and pure-group contributions to the singleton mass are 26+9=35. Only three further fanout-surplus units are available. If an AND branches, one of those units is canceled by that AND's own hot-singleton debit (or has zero credit if the AND is clean). The remaining two units give at most 16, and at least d−1 other dirty ANDs debit one each. For d≥5 the resulting exclusive mass is at most 35+16−4=47, below the required 49. All seven ANDs therefore have fanout one in the remaining three-group cases.
