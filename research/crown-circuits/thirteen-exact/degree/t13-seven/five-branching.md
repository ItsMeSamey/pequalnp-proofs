# Five pure groups all branching are impossible

In a regular 31-gate T₂¹³ with seven ANDs, let b be the number of branching pure OR groups. For any pure group U of size k let d count its branching pure neighbors. On the star restriction keep the k opposite occurrences, force one U slot true, and set all other raw slots zero. This computes T₂ᵏ. The 26−k constant raw pins delete at least 26−k gates. Each of the b−d branching roots that is not a distinct neighbor is constant (U itself true if branching, all others false), contributing ≥1 additional saving by the constant-pin telescope since it has fanout two. Consequently C(k)≤5+k−b+d. For b=5 every pure triple needs ≥1 branching neighbor and every quartet ≥3 (C(3)=4, C(4)=7). A quartet forces at least two pure components by k≤H+2. Its three neighbors leave an isolated fifth pure vertex, impossible. All five groups are triples.

The five branching groups use five of the six internal surplus units, leaving a single further fanout-two wire W. The seven AND singleton bicliques cover all pairs, so total exclusive-shore incidence is at least ceil(13 log₂13)=49. The support identity credits 26 raw occurrences and 15 from the triples. If W were an AND, its hot support would debit its own credit. Thus W is a shared mixed OR. It accepts at most 12 singleton labels since a full-disjunction wire is redundant in minimum threshold two. The total of AND hot incidences and OR input overlaps is therefore at most four. The clean-terminal collector has c≤4 terminals (three mixed nonraw merges), hence at least three dirty ANDs and c=3 or 4.

W has at most four raw operands: otherwise retain exactly five opposite raw occurrences and override W true. The raw-constant telescope plus W's fanout credit yields C(5)≤5+5−1, contradicting C(5)=10. Its nonraw operands can only be branching triples or AND outputs (private OR chains flatten, and W is the only other shared OR). Each has singleton support at most three, since total AND hot incidence is at most four over ≥3 dirty ANDs. The collector uses c−1 ordinary ORs, so W can have at most 1+(4−c) nonraw inputs. For c=4 its support is ≤7, whereas ≥49−41+3=11 is needed. For c=3 it is ≤10, whereas ≥49−41+4=12 is needed.
