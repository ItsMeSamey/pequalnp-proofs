# No seven-edge path beside a three-edge star

Consider the subcubic ten-input size-24 seven-AND regular representative. If the component sizes are seven and three and the seven-edge component contains a degree-three vertex, its small detached component is either a path or cycle excluded by ten-detached-doubletons.md, or a three-edge star with its at most one eligible pure vertex, excluded by the isolated-pure-flag correction in the hereditary layer bound. Otherwise the seven-edge component has maximum degree two. A detached seven-cycle is excluded by ten-detached-doubletons.md. We treat the remaining three-edge star and seven-edge path.

The star center is the unique degree-three vertex, so must be pure by ten-doubletons.md. Let b,u be the branching and private doubletons in the path, which has s=8 vertices and m=8−b−u mixed vertices. Erase all path flags, and for a moment set the star center true. The threshold on the remaining three star leaves must be majority three. Each mixed leaf bit x enters its own OR x OR P once. Its control is independent of x, and it rejects the other two singletons, since otherwise adding x to such a singleton would leave x's sole reader unchanged. Thus P is zero or the product of the other two leaves. Two such products at different leaves would make each injection depend on the other. At least two controls are zero, and by monotonicity they are already zero under the path erasure before setting the star center true. Those two central ORs disappear in addition to the m remote injection ORs and u distinct collector ORs for the private products. Since the entire path can cost at most s−1 ORs, b≥3.

Write p=1+b+u, c for the clean terminals and d=7−c for the dirty gates. The b doubletons and u separate shared partners of the private doubletons consume b+u≤5 distinct fanout surplus units. Thus p≤6, c≤p−2≤4 and d≥3. If b≥4 the singleton incidence mass is at most 20+2b+7(5−b)−d≤32, below the required 34. With b=3 and the center branching it is at most 20+6+3+7−3=33. Hence b=3 and the center is private.

Now m+u+2=s−1. All six OR readers of the branching path flags must already be among the charged mixed injections. They cannot be collector ORs, since their input is singleton-hot. Reassociate mixed OR groups with the raw flag last: each charged injection can be a branch reader only if it has just one nonraw term, the branch itself. Thus these are six distinct groups. At most two are central, because a third central zero injection would overspend. At least four are remote, including at least two internal vertices of the path, as only its two endpoints have raw degree one. Consequently m≥4 gives u≤1. The six groups use at least eight mixed raw occurrences in total, each internal path vertex contributing two. Originally there were 20−3−2(b+u)=11−2u mixed raw occurrences, leaving at most 3−2u.

Two surplus units remain beyond the three branching doubletons. Neither can be on an AND: its support debit cancels that unit and the other contributes at most seven, leaving at most 26+7−(d−1)<34. Both are on nonpure ORs, either on two sites or on one site of fanout three. Write C for their singleton support credit with multiplicity and H for dirty singleton mass. Then C≥8+H≥11. In fact p=4+u≤5 gives d≥4, hence C≥12. Because each site covers at most seven labels, H≤6. Each of the six branch-supplied groups covers at most four labels, being a doubleton plus one or two raws. It cannot be one of these shared sites: in the two-site case 4+7<12 and in the one-site case 2·4<12.

Any maximal OR group which is unshared feeds an AND, so none of the six groups can be a source in an OR-only ancestry of the shared sites. Likewise the central and other private pure flags feed their dedicated ANDs. Consequently each shared OR's remaining sources come from dirty ANDs and from the at most 3−2u unspent raw occurrences. The number of available nonraw joins before the collector is j=p−2−c≤1, since c≥u+1 from the private terminals and a star-only pair. An OR-only ancestry can therefore have at most 1+j different dirty sources, totaling support at most H−(d−1−j)≤7−d+j. With d=7−c and j=p−2−c this is at most p−2=2+u. Even adding every unspent raw gives support at most 5−u. Thus C≤10, a contradiction.
