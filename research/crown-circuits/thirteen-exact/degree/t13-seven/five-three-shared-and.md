# Three pure branches, a fanout-two mixed OR and a fanout-three AND

Let R be the mixed OR (support m≤8) and G the AND (support h≤7). Write q for the number of branching quartets, at most two by five-three-mixed-roots.md. Singleton mass has baseline 35+q, and R and G contribute m and 2h. Debiting h at G and at least 6−c from the remaining dirty gates gives m+h≥14−q+6−c. This remains necessary for a clean G and rules out h=0. Thus c≥3. At c=3 necessarily m=8,h=7,q=2 and all three other dirty gates have support one. At c=4 necessarily q≥1, m+2h≥20, and every other dirty gate has support at most two. Each G operand has support at least h+3. G cannot be a dedicated product with a private pure operand of size at most four.

Consider three physical union trees ending at R and at the two operands of G. Cut at shared roots, ANDs and pure branches. A bare operand is a tree with one nonraw term and zero raws. These trees are disjoint apart from formal source occurrences; a repeated source in one tree is redundant. An internal dependency is R feeding one or both G operands, or G feeding R. Let t count internal source occurrences, so t≤2. The remaining terms are branches of support at most four or other dirty ANDs of support at most two. Clean ANDs feed the collector. The three trees have total output support at least m+2h+6.

At c=4, there are precisely three nonraw terms including the t internal terms. Inside the trees at least 26−8t−4(3−t)=14−4t raw occurrences are needed. At least 6−(3−t)=3+t unused branch exits each need another raw since there are no interior joins outside the trees. Thus at least 17−3t≥11 raw occurrences are required, and q≥1 leaves at most ten.

At c=3, R cannot feed both G operands since m>h. Thus t≤1. If e interior joins occur outside the trees there are at most 4−e nonraw terms in the trees. Their raw slots number at least 28−8t−4(4−e−t)=12+4e−4t. Remaining branch exits need at least 6−(4−e−t)−2e=2+t−e additional raws. At least 14+3e−3t≥11 raw occurrences are needed, while q=2 leaves nine.
