# High branching at six pure groups

Let b be the number of branching pure groups, d the number of dirty private quartet products, x the number of mixed raw incidences, and t the number of clean terminal ANDs. Every branching pure output has two OR readers. Every dirty private-quartet product has exactly one OR reader: on deleting its quartet the one-sided AND already consumes all surplus, so neither that product nor any subsequent dark gate branches. A first further dark AND cannot have two distinct dark inputs, as all nonraw dark flow outside the private tree has passed through this unique path; no raw enters an AND. The output is nonzero after the deletion. Thus all 2b+d indicated wires into OR groups enter nonfinal mixed groups, since each signal is true on some singleton. A group with z raw and u nonraw operands satisfies u≤z+2(u−1): if z=0 then u≥2. Summing the nonfinal OR slots gives

    2b+d ≤ x + 2(r−t+1).

If all six pure groups branch, they exhaust the six units of internal fanout surplus. The signed singleton mass is at most 26+24−2=48: each pure group has at most four labels, and t≤5 forces at least two hot singleton incidences among seven ANDs. Thirteen incompatible partial codes require at least 49 occurrences, excluding the case.

If five groups branch, the sole private group has a shared partner. All six surplus units are exhausted at fanout-two wires. For every candidate singleton support of that partner, its known private AND gives a signed coordinate, and the per-label identity bounds an available residual capacity cᵢ on the other six coordinates. At least 7−t−d unknown ANDs are dirty. Assign one hot witness to each, allowing the same label for different axes. If label i witnesses h of these gates, its unknown exclusive length is at most min(cᵢ,6)−h: every hot event costs one in the raw accounting and the label cannot be hot and exclusive at the same gate. Its minimum associated volume in each compatible fiber doubles at every such debit. A necessary test is whether any placement of these compulsory debits fits all fibers.

p6_small.py generates the 60 clean and 28 dirty private-group incidence patterns directly from the full quartet_models.cpp census, before the induced-graph cuts. p6_debit.cpp enumerates the possible partner supports and hot-toll distributions. All 45,530 clean and 52,192 dirty cases fail. No five-branching case remains. The same code can screen four branching groups with two private products.
