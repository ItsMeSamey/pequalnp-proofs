# Receiver components after deleting a branching triple

Delete a branching pure triple U in a regular size-31, seven-AND circuit. U is the only dark group because an additional first dark mixed OR could only join U and deleted labels, giving U again in the original unrestricted circuit. No AND reads U directly. Removing U saves its two internal ORs, three other raw occurrences and its two nonraw output occurrences. In the resulting ten-input circuit the seven ANDs and seventeen ORs give exact minimum size 24. Every original surviving raw has at least two actual readers: bypass paths starting at its two original slots cannot coalesce before a retained gate, as a unary bypass has only one live input. A duplicate at a retained gate or an orphan would permit another size reduction. By the certified nine-input composition and the physical deletion lemma in ten-minor.md, such a raw has exactly two OR readers.

The other three original pure groups remain positive and keep one fewer raw if they were adjacent to U. Keep their group names, along with the names of any one-input group formed by bypassing zero operands. A newly positive pure group must have taken U as its only nonraw operand; at most two such groups form. Each must keep at least two distinct surviving mixed raw slots. If only x survived and the original group was shared, x would gain its other raw read plus two inherited uses. If it was private, its sole immediate reader was an AND by original flattening and that AND survives. Both options violate the preceding two-OR reader property. The group cannot have been the output, since its restriction would be a raw. No original pure group becomes a singleton: the originals had size at least three and shared at most one raw with U.

Introduce one formal flag for each residual group with a raw slot. The grouped OR count is 17, the surviving raw incidences total 20, and parameterizing the batches saves 20−v ORs, giving v−3 decoding ORs. The pin for each remaining mixed raw group has its dedicated OR. For a center of degree k≥2, contract all mixed flags outside the closed neighborhood. The center and its neighbors plus all remaining pure pins are present. In the center's connected component peel every pure vertex outside the star farthest first: an edge to its parent and the edge from that parent to its own predecessor witness essentiality after preceding deletions. In every other component of size t≥2 root at an anchor edge; use the same argument until two endpoints remain. Compare a star singleton edge with its union with that anchor edge. At least one of the two endpoint pins is essential before both are set to zero, saving one OR, so each such component costs at least t−1. If an isolated remote pure component exists, begin instead by setting one such pin false before all peels. Its original syntactic path survived the mixed-flag contractions and forces an OR removal since the retained star is nonconstant. This does not impair any peels or anchor tests, which avoid that isolated pin. The concluding star requires 2k−4 ORs, giving

    k−2 ≤ number of remote components − indicator of any isolated remote component.

For a necessary test, taking only pure-to-pure incidences overestimates the right side. All retained mixed vertices neighbor the center and already lie in its component. Additional edges can merge other components with each other or the center. A previous singleton component ceasing to be isolated in such a merge decreases the component count at least once, compensating for loss of the indicator.

The finite test tries every branching triple as U and every assignment of zero, one or two fresh pure groups, reserving two different previously mixed raw labels for each. A mixed-only label may supply its two distinct occurrences to different groups. No actual configuration is lost when choosing two representatives in each nonempty group of at least two. Ignoring further incidences can only increase the estimated number of remote components after the singleton correction. The degrees used for the original center vertices still count all their raw labels. From the 44 prior records, hcomponent.py excludes 30, leaving 14. If one ignores the lower bound of two slots and reserves only one instead, 17 remain. All 14 remaining records have four triple groups, including two with one clean-private group.
