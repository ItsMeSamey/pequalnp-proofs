# Chronological templates for three dirty comparisons in the eight-input six-AND profile

There are three clean terminal comparisons and three earlier-or-later dirty comparisons, six axes in all. List the dirty comparisons in a topological order. Each of their continuations may use none, one, or both signs of any later dirty comparator; it may use at most one sign of any clean comparator (it actually fires on some rejected singleton). For a fixed clean axis, record the three digit column of its uses by the dirty continuations. Each digit is absent, positive, or negative. Identify a column with its simultaneous sign flip, then choose the multiset of three clean columns to account for axis permutations. The middle dirty comparison has a one-entry column of uses by its predecessor; identify the two nonempty single signs. The last dirty comparison has a two-entry column of such uses, modulo its simultaneous sign flip, also allowing both signs. Require each continuation nonempty. Forward-propagating a continuation must not itself activate a clean comparator: monotonicity would then contradict the rejected singleton at which the source dirty comparator fires. These choices give the 8,873 rows of t8-three-profiles.tsv; three-profiles.py generates them without assuming independent dirty activation labels.

The 13 OR gates include two terminal collector ORs, leaving an 11-OR front from 11 sources to 12 operands. The transpose has at most 12 joins, eight reserved for nonliteral raw receiver joins, hence only four for the helper bank containing all three continuations. The same one-pin deletion/resplitting lemma as t8-one-dirty.md reduces to nondirect raw masks. Joining the pinned signed literals in each mandatory set requires that the helper union graph connect them. On the union of those sets this graph requires at least the number of distinct literals minus the number of connected components of their overlap hypergraph. This rank exceeds four for 4,468 rows. For the remaining 4,405, enumerate every auxiliary submask of the mandatory sets, keeping all mandatory sets of size at least two and at most four total joins. A library is realizable precisely when each included mask is a proper union of two earlier masks, starting with signed literals, in increasing size. No helper outside a mandatory target is needed to build it. Computing the minimum by set-system isomorphism leaves 4,212 profiles with costs 0/1/2/3/4 distributed 23/149/542/1,310/2,188. The rank and minimum gates are lower bounds that do not require any globally minimal template orientation.

For a given profile, t8-dirty-exact.cpp tests every canonical bank up to four proper unions using the chronological forward acceptance and backward blocking certificate arguments of t8-two-dirty.md. The ordering of a prospective helper m first compares the bitset of mandatory masks that fail to contain m (lexicographically in the fixed target order), then the cardinality and numeric value of m. Along an inclusion chain this key strictly increases. An as-yet-unbuilt mandatory mask with key smaller than m could never be built later in the sorted order. The old exact prefix stabilizer now permutes only the first three axes, flips signs independently, and keeps both the chronological axes and all three targets fixed. After every bank supplying the protected targets, all consistent maximal raw unions of two bank wires are considered; eight must pairwise conflict, use all signs, and activate each of the three dirty axes at least once.

The singletons must also witness the necessity of both operands of every AND: for each choice of a comparison j and one operand, there must be an input singleton on which that operand is true, the opposite one false, and replacing the comparison output by true activates the final collector. Otherwise resolving comparison j to that operand preserves rejection on zero and singletons and can only increase acceptance on pairs, producing threshold two in at most 18 gates. Thus each consistent candidate raw mask is tagged with up to six exclusive, propagated-critical signs, and the candidate pool at a feasible front must cover all twelve signs. Testing these tags after replacing raw candidates by maximal consistent supersets is sound: if some enlarged pair-conflicting family realizes the truth table, its gate count is still at most nineteen and the absolute lower bound forces this necessity property.

three-control.py tests full threshold-four Boolean behavior on a nonoptimal semantic positive with three independently singleton-hot comparisons and compares direct chronological acceptance to backward certificates for 2,000 independent test masks. Any putative positive eight-input bank must be reconstructed by lift-dirty.py and checked on all 256 input assignments at size 19 and six ANDs before being used. Template counts alone make no circuit-exclusion claim.

## Completed receiver census

All 4,212 mandatory-admissible profiles are now excluded. Of these, 4,201 have completed negative receiver-search receipts (9,743,895 helper-bank nodes and 3,727,096 fronts). For the other eleven, dirty-permute.py explicitly reindexes the three dirty comparisons by every permutation that is a valid topological order, renames their corresponding input columns, and canonicalizes the free clean-axis permutations and all independent sign flips. The marked representative has identical canonical coordinates and a completed negative search receipt. Both circuits and bank libraries transport by these literal permutations in either direction. The eleven uses are limited to the cost-zero sector. three-ledger.py verifies the exact-or-alias dispositions; the final t8-three-exact-ledger.tsv SHA-256 is cc34c886e5171107ec483c8397169d1c7ca5bfc20b3995f66cad462e6e4b1345. The solver t8-dirty-exact.cpp SHA-256 is a94e4e52900a4643471ceb1a5b701c9acf5eb0082ea1ce1e18c3162587ce53ef.

Thus no size-19 six-AND T2(8) has exactly three dirty ANDs. If a one-pin candidate existed, its clean direct comparator and two ORs would delete to the exact T7/16/five-AND profile without losing a dirty singleton. Splitting that smaller circuit's sole one-pin raw, or any raw if all are nonliteral, would give the excluded nondirect T8 profile with all three dirty gates preserved. The one-pin uniqueness and gate accounting are proved in t8-one-dirty.md.

Combined with the separate clean covering argument and the one-/two-dirty exclusions, the tight eight-input minor of any branching pure doubleton in a regular T10/24/six-AND candidate must retain at least four dirty ANDs. This leaves at most two clean terminal products. The four- and five-dirty eight-input profiles have not been excluded by this census.
