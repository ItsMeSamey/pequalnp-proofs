# Four and five dirty comparators in the six-AND threshold-eight profile

At four or five dirty comparisons the collected circuit has respectively two or one clean terminal terms. At six dirty comparisons even its output collector would have no singleton-clean AND output, so a nonzero threshold function cannot be realized. Use six axes, order the clean ones first and order the dirty ones topologically.

The same four-helper budget applies to all dirty counts d. The 13 ORs include 5−d collector ORs, leaving 8+d front joins. After transposition from 8+d source signals to twelve operands these become at most 12 reverse joins. Reserve eight final joins for nondirect mutually incomparable raw outputs; no raw can be contained in a dirty continuation. For direct masks, the arity-seven delete/resplit argument of t8-one-dirty.md applies unchanged as both clean gate erasure and raw splitting preserve the original dirty comparators and their singleton witnesses.

For each clean signed axis, its d-entry column of memberships in the dirty continuations has absent, positive or negative entries. Simultaneous sign flip on that axis is free; clean-axis permutations are free. In a dirty-axis column all rows at or after its dirty comparison are zero, and earlier entries may additionally contain both signs. Again simultaneous column sign flip is free. profiles-general.cpp enumerates these columns, pruning when the rank needed to connect literals within overlapping protected masks already exceeds four. The rank is monotone upon assigning further signed-axis columns. Require all protected outputs nonempty. For each dirty continuation evaluate the forward closure in chronological order; it may not itself accept because an actual singleton activates that comparison.

large-partition.cpp computes minimum mandatory helper cost as in mandatory.py: all nonliteral targets themselves must appear, and any other join necessary to synthesize such a target is a submask of it. Enumerate the possible collections of at most four target/auxiliary masks and test for a two-parent realization in increasing cardinality. The cache key counts literal membership patterns for the sorted list of distinct nonliteral targets, which preserves exact unlabelled set realization cost. For each valid profile, swapping two consecutive dirty gates with no edge between them gives an equivalent chronological representation. Every linear extension of the protected dependency order can be reached by such adjacent swaps. Relabel all dirty rows and columns together and normalize the clean-column ordering/signs and dirty-column signs. DSU computes the resulting connected classes; retaining one representative per class preserves every realizable circuit and its helper budget. The code checks that the transformed key is present in the repertoire before unioning.

t8-fixed.cpp is the specialized cost-four backend: enumerate every constructible set of four mandatory/submask helpers, then test its consistent maximal receiver masks exactly as in t8-dirty-exact.cpp. No helper outside all targets can occur because then three would suffice for the targets. t8-seeded.cpp generalizes this to every cost: enumerate every inclusion-minimal realizing mandatory library of size at most four, in increasing size. Once such a library is built, any remaining helper is a freely chosen union; all selected seeds precede free joins. Every concrete realizing bank contains such a subset achievable independently of the remaining gates. The original maximal-receiver, critical-singleton, actual-hot and disjoint-volume tests carry over unchanged. Apply lift-dirty.py to any positive semantic bank before treating it as a physical seed.
