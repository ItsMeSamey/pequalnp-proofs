# Two dirty comparisons at threshold eight, size nineteen, six ANDs

The chronological transposed-bank enumeration found no size-19 T2(8) circuit having six binary ANDs of which exactly two are true on some singleton. A singleton can fire both dirty sites; distinct hot witnesses are not assumed. The one-dirty result and its direct deletion/resplitting lemma are in t8-one-dirty.md. This argument does not enumerate three or more dirty sites.

Normalize clean ANDs into the terminal collector as in geometry/ten24-six/RESULT.md. At a total minimum this preserves all retained AND singleton values. Number four clean axes 0 to 3 and the two dirty axes 4 then 5 in a topological order. Use the mask bits 2j,2j+1 for the two slots of axis j. The second dirty output has a nonempty signed continuation Q among the clean slots. The first has a nonempty continuation W among the clean slots and/or both slots of the second dirty comparison. No clean axis has both signs in Q or in W, since each protected output actually fires on a rejected singleton. When W contains both second-dirty slots, W union Q must also be consistent on clean slots. Each inclusion is tested by the solver.

Permute and flip the four clean axes so Q has its positive sign on an initial block of 1,2,3,4 axes. On that block W has some initial agreements, some opposites, and the rest absent. Off that block only the number of W slots matters; all can be positive. On dirty axis 5, W contains neither, one chosen sign, or both. These choices exhaust the 156 valid profiles in t8-two-profiles.tsv, produced by t8-two-profiles.py. Dirty-axis dependencies do not impose an additional distinctness requirement on the two continuations.

Assume first every raw mask is nonliteral. There are thirteen OR gates; three collect the four clean terms. The ten front ORs feed twelve pins from ten sources (eight raw inputs and the two dirty outputs). Transposition thus uses at most 10+12−10=12 reverse joins. The eight raw masks cannot contain one another and none is contained in a protected continuation: such a raw would be invisible beside a different singleton activating that continuation (its own future pins cannot activate that source). Each may take its own final join, leaving at most four helpers. Both W and Q must already be bank wires. The initial bank consists of the twelve signed literals.

On any raw mask m first add W if m activates both axis-4 signs; then add Q if the resulting mask activates axis 5. The output is true exactly when both signs of a clean axis are present after this propagation. Singleton raw masks must reject, every pair of distinct masks must accept on union, all signs must be used, and each dirty comparison must actually fire on some singleton. The search uses superset-maximal consistent candidate receivers, since enlarging a receiver only adds possible pair conflicts, actual dirty triggers and occurrences. Two originally conflicting masks cannot be enlarged to the same consistent mask. Its secondary pruning is the minimum volume of certificates: select one blocked sign per clean comparator, then one at axis 5 if a Q target is blocked, then one at axis 4 if a W target is blocked. The avoiding assignments of different pair-conflicting raw masks are disjoint, giving total volume at most 64. semantic-control.py compares the 64 backward certificates against direct chronological Boolean propagation for 2,496 profile/mask combinations.

t8-two-exact.cpp enumerates up to four helper unions of distinct existing wires, retaining only proper unions passing the singleton test. Every helper can be topologically scheduled by first taking subsets of both W and Q, then subsets of W alone, subsets of Q alone, and the rest; within each class use increasing size and numeric mask. Proper containment never decreases this key. W may already be constructed before the third class and Q before the fourth: restrict each protected construction to its own subset helpers and order independent joins first. At a bank node, exact stabilizer signatures for the ordered W,Q,prior helpers,and next helper identify clean-axis permutations and independent sign flips that fix the entire prefix; dirty axes are not permuted. Among choices with one signature take the smallest numeric mask. Completeness with sorted helper order follows by choosing, over the symmetry orbit of an entire admissible remaining library, the lexicographically first sorted sequence; its next member cannot be discarded in favor of a smaller equivalent prefix extension. The solver tests every bank supporting W,Q and need not spend every helper slot. The C++ main prints a positive bank immediately and otherwise prints the completed-node and compatibility-front counts.

All 156 keys completed, with 689,043 bank nodes and 535,940 compatibility fronts and no positive. The ordered per-profile ledger is t8-two-exact-ledger.tsv. Compile t8-two-exact.cpp with C++20, then run it on each (W,Q) in t8-two-profiles.tsv for independent replay. For a direct mask, the proof in t8-one-dirty.md applies with both dirty ANDs unchanged: the direct singleton fires no AND, deleting it drops exactly a clean comparator and two ORs to reach C(7)=16, and resplitting the smaller circuit's unique direct raw if present (any raw otherwise) restores eight nondirect receivers without changing the dirty count. This proves the candidate exclusion for arbitrary direct and nondirect originals.

With the clean signed-cover obstruction and the one-dirty result, the five-OR branching-doubleton minor of a regular T10/24/A6 candidate would have at least three genuinely singleton-hot ANDs. This conclusion is restricted to the stated projection of an actual larger circuit; size-19 six-AND eight-input circuits with zero dirty gates are known.
