# Rectangular small-component cases at ten flags

Write L for an eight-edge five-vertex component of mask 255 or 507, and R for K2,3 with degree-three vertices x1,x2 and degree-two vertices Y={a,b,c}. There are eight decoder ORs and sixteen decoder gates; L requires four ORs. Use the exits k, cold ORs m and fixed raw-monomial expansion from comb/fourteen-construct/four-flag-three-lost-ors.md. Thus k+m≤4, and the R-dependence of every L-live wire factors through the k+2m raw monomials, even at mixed assignments.

Each x spoils an L edge. Otherwise clamp x true, build the five large flags from eight labels using eleven ORs, and use three additional raw flags on the independent small star neighbors. Each singleton rejects, and every two distinct labels form a positive pair by the original edge tests. The pinned essential x saves a gate (some small edge at the other x changes from negative to positive upon adding x). This gives size at most 26 against C11=27. Thus each x is a required singleton features. On both slices (x1,x2)=(1,0),(0,1) the small function on Y is threshold two on three labels. Its three label membership columns in the active y-feature bank must be pairwise incomparable (toggle a second y on a singleton). At least three y-bearing features survive on each slice. With precisely five nonempty slots, the three y-features survive both and hence use no x. The three incomparable columns in the three-cube form its complete first or second level. The y-features are therefore either the three singleton y flags or all three pair products.

Every permissible local F on K2,3 uses at least three ORs. For each homogeneous Y pair, either that pair is a minterm or its extensions by x1 and x2 are distinct minterms. Similarly either x1x2 is a minterm or its three extensions by a y are distinct minterms. This gives at least four minterms; equality forces x1x2 and the three Y pairs. Resolving each OR of a two-OR network gives at most four monomials. To attain four distinct minterms both ORs must be encountered in every resolved output. Expanding AND trees down to the next OR or raw leaf thus writes the four resolved sets as Q union A_i union B_j, with each index independently choosing an OR branch; repeated occurrences give the same set by idempotence. The cell x1x2 is disjoint from both neighbors, forcing its Q,A_i,B_j all empty, impossible.

If k=1, at most three cold ORs are available. The sole cold exit operand equals local F on L=0; all three cold ORs are therefore indispensable there. Any pre-exit live factor in its cone is pure L and would zero an AND on this slice, either bypassing one indispensable OR or annihilating the operand. Thus the exported function is purely small and rejects every single small edge. It cannot transmit the cross-pair signal.

If k≥2 and m=0 there are too few slots. If k=3,m=1, the five slots are distinct and nonempty and the two x singletons must each guard an exit by spoiling. For singleton Y features let a guard the third exit. Fix L=0,x=10,a=0. Before the cold OR, live wires see neither b nor c, so that OR computes only 0,b,c or b∨c. After it the live output depends on b,c only via that bit, incapable of bc.

Whenever the entire Y feature bank consists of its three pair products, pin Y=0. Every small edge x_i+y has the same signature as x_i alone. Thus the eight L edges and the two x flags act as ten distinct threshold labels: pairs of x labels accept by comparison with their positive extension by one y. For each pair monomial, an AND inside its cold expansion first joins the two distinct raw Y occurrences. No such joining gate can be shared between different pair monomials because its raw-factor set contains both labels. All three gates vanish, as does at least one distinct OR receiving a y-pair-bearing operand. The resulting ten-input threshold circuit costs at most eleven bank ORs plus twelve surviving decoder gates, contrary to C10=24.

For k=2,m=2 with both exit monomials nonempty, spoiling forces these monomials to be x1 and x2, respectively. On either single-x slice with L=0, the only possible exit's cold operand computes threshold two on Y, since all other live computation can use only that one bit. Both cold ORs are indispensable within BOTH operand cones, so both precede either exit. No live pre-exit factor can enter these cold OR cones: on L=0 this would disable an indispensable OR or annihilate the operand. Nor can either exit's remaining factors include a live factor: on its own x slice the other exit is disabled and every earlier live signal is zero. Hence both exits export pure functions of R, both zero at each single small edge. Again the cross tests fail.

The outstanding case in this argument is k=2,m=2 with an empty exit monomial and the five distinct singleton flag features. It is being studied separately.
