# Two deletions exclude two exceptional P3 components

In a minimum physical T2(11) circuit obtained from either cyclic q16 residual, call a P3 exceptional if a leaf's single decoder reader is an AND, or its center is read by an AND hot on one of its two local singleton labels. At most one P3 is exceptional.

Suppose two different components have such witnesses. From each choose a raw x to erase. Prefer an AND-read leaf x if available (type I). Otherwise choose the sibling x of a local label u accepted by an AND reading that center (type II). The two erased raws each have one use in their bank center and one direct decoder use. The two bank centers remain nonzero after erasure and reduce to their surviving siblings.

For type I, the opposite input to x's sole decoder AND must be true at the surviving sibling singleton. On that singleton the center is already true, so adding x can affect the circuit only at its AND. Its other operand cannot depend on the changed leaf before that gate, and adding x must cause acceptance. The witness persists under the other raw deletion. Hence this AND is one-sided dark. For type II, the marked AND becomes redundant after changing the center to its surviving raw u: its other operand still accepts singleton u and therefore dominates u. This AND is live after erasure. Marked ANDs are distinct: a cross-component leaf/leaf AND cannot distinguish either local pair, two disjoint centers cannot feed a locally hot product, and a different component's bare leaf cannot be true on a marked center's local label.

The ordinary dark-edge telescope charges four raw uses and one additional gate per type-I one-sided AND. The subsequent type-II live AND bypasses are distinct further savings, totaling at least six. Bypassing one equivalent live wire cannot destroy the equivalence for another. The nine-input minimum 21 implies equality with the original 27 gates. Its known size-21 composition requires exactly four of the original eight ANDs to be removed. No unmarked one-sided dark AND and no dark internal fanout surplus can occur, or the total savings would exceed six. Two of the four raw uses already exit to the still-live bank centers. The two remaining dark occurrences cannot branch: a one-sided AND passes one dark occurrence onward, and a both-dark gate consumes two occurrences and returns one. Thus at most one both-dark AND can occur. All removed ANDs are either the two marked ANDs or such a both-dark product. There are at most three, a contradiction. Root counting may defer idempotence and further dead-gate simplification until after this explicit sequence; any further saving also contradicts the size floor.

Consequently at least three P3s in the triangle residual and at least two in the square residual have two OR-read leaves and no AND directly reading their center hot on their local singletons.
