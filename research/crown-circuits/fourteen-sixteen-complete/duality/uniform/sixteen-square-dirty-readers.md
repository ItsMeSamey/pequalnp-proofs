# At most one square flag has a dirty AND reader

In the remaining C4+3P3+K2 interface, label the square vertices a,b,c,d cyclically. By sixteen-cycle-readers.md each vertex with an AND reader is read once. A dirty such reader is true on exactly one of its two incident singleton edges: no other label activates the vertex and truth on both would make the vertex imply the other operand, making the gate redundant.

Two adjacent vertices cannot feed one common product ab. On the negative edge cd versus its positive union with da the only added flag is a, and b is false in both tests. Thus a would be invisible. Two distinct ANDs on a,b both reject their shared singleton ab: on that input the only active flags are a and b and each feeds a different AND, so there can be no first true gate. If both were dirty, each would therefore be true on its exterior incident edge. Delete the shared edge ab. Its two incident bank ORs reduce to the exterior raw labels, and the two products equal those respective raw labels by their surviving hot singleton witnesses. Four gates disappear, reducing physical T2(11) of size 27 to T2(10) of size at most 23, below 24.

For opposite dirty vertices delete the cold incident raw at each. The hot witnesses lie on distinct surviving edges. The two bank flags now equal their respective hot raws, and their two distinct ANDs can be bypassed. If the erased edges are disjoint then all four incident bank ORs disappear. If the erased edges meet at another vertex, three bank ORs disappear and this third vertex becomes constantly zero. Its direct decoder reader also disappears and cannot be either marked dirty AND, since that AND remains positive on its surviving hot singleton where the erased vertex is zero. In both cases six distinct gates can be removed, at most three being ANDs. This gives 21 gates on nine inputs, where the established composition requires four ANDs, whereas at least five of the original eight ANDs remain. Further saving violates the size floor. Thus a second dirty square vertex is impossible.
