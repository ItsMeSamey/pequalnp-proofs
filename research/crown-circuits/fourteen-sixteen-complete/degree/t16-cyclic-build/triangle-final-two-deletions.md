# The triangle and four two-edge paths exceed the twelve-OR/eight-AND budget

Use the normalized residual classification in triangle-ordinary-budget.md. All eight leaves have OR readers. Exactly three ordinary centers are each either OR-read or the input of a dedicated clean terminal. The exceptional center F=x∨y has a dirty product H=F∧K hot on the singleton x. One triangle vertex branches, another has a sole OR reader, and C has a sole AND reader W=C∧P.

Let c be the number of ordinary centers read by ANDs. The eight leaf OR groups, the 3−c ordinary center OR groups, and the OR group of the single-read triangle vertex are pairwise distinct and singleton-hot. These give 12−c ORs. There are exactly c dedicated clean products at the ordinary AND-read centers, and some further terminal accepts a doubled triangle. A collector combining at least c+1 distinct terminals needs at least c ORs, separate from the hot groups. The twelve-OR cap therefore permits exactly one further terminal T.

The product W cannot be one of the ordinary dedicated products: if its other operand were an ordinary center, the doubled triangle would reject just as the singleton on the opposite edge does, since that center stays false and C is single-read. Nor can W be T: the pair of the two exceptional path edges, with no triangle or ordinary path edge, would then activate no terminal. Thus W is dirty. Since C has nonempty singleton support only at its two incident triangle edges, W is true on some edge v incident to C. H and W are distinct: H is bounded by F and cannot be true on any isolated triangle edge.

Return to the physical threshold-two circuit on eleven independent edge labels, of size 27 with eight ANDs. Delete the other edge w incident to C and delete the path leaf y. Four distinct ORs simplify: both triangle endpoint bank ORs of w, the exceptional path bank OR F, and the direct decoder OR reader of y. The reader is a decoder gate and both triangle bank outputs remain nonzero. The three bank ORs use disjoint gate positions. The surviving signal C equals raw v, and P is true on its singleton v even under the deletions. By monotonicity W therefore equals v on the entire restricted cube. Likewise F equals raw x and K is still true on the singleton x, so H equals x throughout. Bypass both distinct ANDs. All replacements preserve the outputs of the retained gates; any overlap in ancestry changes neither equality.

These four OR and two AND deletions leave a 21-gate threshold-two circuit on nine independent raws with six ANDs. This contradicts the established composition theorem that every size-21 threshold-nine circuit has exactly four ANDs. Keeping any other redundant gates is permitted, or deleting them would violate the size minimum. Hence no decoder of this cyclic triangle shape fits twelve ORs and eight ANDs. The cyclic square case is separate.
