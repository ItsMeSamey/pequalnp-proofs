# Raw regularity in 24-gate ten-input minors

In either the six- or seven-AND stratum, there is a size-preserving representative in which every raw input feeds exactly two ORs, and each of the two exclusive singleton shores of every AND has at least two labels. This uses the rigid 21-gate nine-input composition of four ANDs and seventeen ORs.

Fix a ten-input minimum and a raw x. Three OR readers all vanish under x=0, leaving at most 21 gates while keeping at least six ANDs (the total loss budget is three, so no additional gate vanishes). Three readers including an AND would lose a fourth successor outside these readers by absorption. If one OR and one AND read x, erasure loses both and a first proper OR downstream of the AND, distinct from the direct OR. The residual nine-input minimum would have at least five ANDs. Two direct ANDs and no OR force an immediate common single OR successor by the three-gate loss budget, allowing a one-gate factoring. A sole AND reader x∧P forces P to be the full disjunction of all other raws; erasing x then deletes this AND, a downstream OR, and P's own OR root, yielding the same contradiction. These are the ten-tight.md arguments with the stronger nine-input composition substituted. Sole OR readers may be moved forward by the usual raw relocation lemma; their intermediate circuits obey the same gate-count prohibitions.

Now replacing any AND by one operand preserves all pair positives and the zero. If at most one singleton newly accepted, remove that raw (or any raw when none), saving its two direct OR readers together with the replaced AND. This again produces a forbidden 21-gate nine-input circuit with five or more ANDs. Every physical signed port therefore has at least two wholly contained singleton deficits in the switched-mask model.

In the thirteen-input, seven-AND regular candidate, any dirty private pure quartet U feeds a product U∧P hot on exactly one singleton x. Resolving that product to U only changes the remaining three quartet singleton labels at most. Erase those three labels: the regular graph has no wholly erased pure group, because a different group shares at most one raw with U. Group reassociation bypasses six ORs along with the resolved AND. The ten-input bound forces exactly a 24-gate six-AND minor. A branching pure triple instead produces a 24-gate seven-AND minor as in census.md.
