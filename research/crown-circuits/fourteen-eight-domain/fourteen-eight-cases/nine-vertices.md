# The nine-vertex, eight-AND boundary

A connected simple host on nine vertices of maximum degree three has at most thirteen edges; hence a fourteen-edge host here is disconnected and has a quartic vertex. Three edge-bearing components on nine vertices could have at most 1+1+10=12 edges. There are exactly two. The degree-four center needs at least one unit of remote-component slack in the q−2 decoder bound.

Two pure doubletons cannot share a label. Delete the three distinct labels in their union. The six removed raw incidences are supplemented by one dark-fanout charge per branching doubleton and one one-sided AND per private doubleton. A private doubleton's opposite operand accepts at least two outside labels, of which at most one is deleted. The corresponding ANDs are distinct, since two overlapping private groups feeding one AND factor their common input at a gate saving. Eight eliminated gates leave at most 26 for eleven inputs, contradicting C(11)=27.

A remote component on two vertices has only mixed flags, giving no slack. In a three-vertex connected component (a two-edge path or triangle) every pure flag is a doubleton. At most one can be pure, by the preceding independence. Bypass its mixed injections first. If the remaining isolated pure flag exists, pinning it false saves one more OR: it has a retained path and the large component contains a surviving positive pair. This gives full remote cost and no slack. Thus the component orders must be 4 and 5.

A four-vertex component has four to six edges, since the other holds at most ten. Up to isomorphism the only pairs are the four-cycle or paw plus K5; the diamond plus K5 with one edge missing; or K4 plus K5 with two edges missing (adjacent or disjoint). In either K5 case, restricting the four-vertex component away costs at least three of the seven decoder ORs, by the general connected-component peeling bound. The remaining five-vertex decoder must reject all pairs and accept all triples. The published graph-cut certificate for (5,1023,4) excludes this with only four ORs and any number of ANDs; see thirteen-exact/degree/t13-seven/graph-cut/initial-drat.tsv at fork commit f66f5c73f2a5e471ce4cc9a588fc2fbdb2f533d5. The archived CNF digest is e934d3594914f5d0464dd181607bc41a0cebb1a0669bb43bcd9145677474b930 and the proof digest is 29a03bd560516cd6f64135f5e23d65ea077d5c3ccc2c395c7ae5552f1666974b.

This leaves precisely three graph shapes for further checking: diamond plus K5 minus one edge, or K4 plus K5 minus two adjacent or two disjoint edges. Role labels and decoder instances within all three remain to be decided.
