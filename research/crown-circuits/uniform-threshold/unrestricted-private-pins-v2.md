# Private receiver gates and dormant components at the limiting coefficient

Let λ denote the liminf of (C(n)−2n−√(2n))/n^(3/10). The established regular-or-delete transfer takes any sequence attaining λ to regular minima also attaining λ, with the number of inputs asymptotically unchanged. Use those regular minima and the notation of unrestricted-shared-cover.md.

## No shared AND reader for two retained private columns

Let η=1/100. Discard selected rows missing more than h^(3/5+η) raw neighbors among H, losing o(h) rows since total missing pairs are O(hE). Start from the receiver graph with no private–private row. Its sum of squared degrees is O(h^(7/5)): the sum of codegrees T satisfies T=O(E²+E√L)=O(h^(7/5)) as in unrestricted-block-moment.md. Delete the edges incident to any column of degree above h^(2/5+η), losing o(h) edges. Now peel vertices of degree below d₀=floor(h^(2/5−η)); this removes at most O(E d₀)=o(h) edges. Thus g∼h edges remain and every surviving column has degree at least d₀.

For a private column P, for every neighbor column S consider the edges at S other than A=(P,S). Their union T(P) has at least d₀(d₀−1)/2 rows, since each edge can be counted at most twice. For each row B in T(P) fix one witnessing A=A(B) and common column S. Suppose two surviving private columns P,Q feed the same AND. For F in T(Q) analogously write D(F)=(Q,R) with F sharing R but distinct from D(F). There exist B∈T(P), F∈T(Q) such that BF,A(B)F,D(F)B are all actual raw edges. Indeed for each fixed B at most 2(h^(3/5+η)+1) choices of F fail the first two requirements; for fixed F at most h^(3/5+η)+1 choices of B fail the third. These are negligible fractions since d₀²≫h^(3/5+η).

Singleton BF activates S and R. Adding raw A(B)F can only cause a downstream change through P: F and S are already true, and A(B) feeds only P,S. Since P is private its only reader is the alleged AND P∧Q. If P was true or Q false, this addition cannot change the output (in the latter case Q cannot change via P before the AND does). Thus P must be false and Q true. Adding D(F)B instead requires P true and Q false by the same argument. The contradiction proves that every retained private column occupies a distinct AND gate.

## Almost all remote components can be peeled at full price

The number r of raw labels in components other than the giant is O(E²), as their total vertex count is O(E). In particular m=n−r gives √(2n)−√(2m)=o(E) and m/n→1. Call a remote component dormant if no vertex in it is activated on any actual giant raw singleton. At most four components have no shared raw-bearing group. In each remaining dormant component choose one shared group W. Restrict all r remote raw labels false. At every chosen W its signal on the remaining inputs is singleton-clean (and false at empty).

Simultaneously force these selected outputs false in the downstream circuit and append their computed pre-override signals to the final output as terminal disjuncts. Those signals can only have decreased and stay singleton-clean. On any lost positive choose the earliest selected gate whose original true output was overridden: its inputs are unchanged, so its terminal disjunct restores acceptance. The resulting circuit computes threshold two on the m retained labels.

For the size bound, propagate constants on the original physical pins, with the downstream pins of a selected root W forced zero even if its pre-override expression is nonconstant. In the constant-flow telescope from star-third-order.md a nonconstant promoted W has c≤1 constant inputs; when c=1 its original gate is eliminated as an alias and when c=0 retain its original gate to compute its terminal expression. Its local credit (eliminated gate indicator)+(number of outgoing constant pins)−c is at least f_W. Adding one terminal OR leaves net credit at least f_W−1≥1. When its pre-override expression is constant it must be false, so omit its terminal OR and again credit at least f_W−1. All other nonoutput gates contribute nonnegative credit. The old output before the terminal disjuncts may simplify to false and has zero fanout, so allow one unit of negative credit there. Thus the circuit has at most C(n)−2r−(#dormant components)+5 gates. Along a sequence attaining λ this implies that the number of dormant components is o(E), since C(m)≥2m+√(2m)+(λ−o(1))m^(3/10).

Peel remote components before specializing any giant formal input in the component/star OR argument. In each nondormant remote component choose a vertex W activated by a giant singleton x and a raw neighbor V. Then Wformal∨T retains its nonraw T true on x. The two anchor OR savings are exactly as in unrestricted-coefficient-final.md, now using x instead of XB; x's two giant pins remain free at every remote step. Thus for every center X∈H the final OR count gives k_X≤s−a+O(1)+o(E). Averaging degrees over H yields a≤(1+z)E+o(E).

The private and shared columns of the resulting g∼h edge receiver graph can therefore be bounded separately by (1+z+o(1))E and (1−z+o(1))E. The block-moment lower bound applies with π=1+z and σ=1−z, padding with isolates independently. Neither this argument nor the induced graph assumes a layered circuit.
