# Six intact labels require four surviving ANDs

Restrict to twelve raw inputs, a row and a column literal per label. Assume the output represents the 6-crown on the one-row, one-column promise, that all but at most three ANDs have a false operand on the whole intact subcube, and that at most three physical ORs fire on each intact raw singleton. These hypotheses are inconsistent. The finite enumeration uses only Python’s standard library. Run `python certify.py`.

**Diagonal profiles.** Replace dead ANDs by false leaves. Two surviving ANDs cannot suffice: the first-mixed-AND witness for every pair of different false diagonals and a positive cross implies their left-operand codes differ, so six labels would inject into four words. Therefore three ordered ANDs survive; each must participate as a witness, since otherwise two coordinates suffice to distinguish all labels. In particular each has two genuine operands. At each gate encode a label’s diagonal as 0/1 if just that side is hot, 2 if both are cold, or 3 if both are hot. The witness lemma guarantees every two labels have a coordinate of opposite pure sides. Regarding 2 and 3 as wildcards makes these six extension subcubes pairwise disjoint in the binary 3-cube. Each has dimension at most one (one four-point subcube and five more would require nine vertices). Enumerating six disjoint point/edge subcubes up to label permutation, then splitting each wildcard into its two physical polarities, gives 340 profiles.

**Forced raw leaves.** The shadow of each operand is its OR tree unrolled with all earlier AND outputs replaced by false. A raw leaf there reaches the physical target along ORs only. If earlier ANDs are cold on a particular label’s diagonal, then they are cold on its two singleton inputs by monotonicity. On these three inputs the shadow and the target agree. If the target is hot on the diagonal its shadow must therefore have at least one of that label’s raw ports as a leaf.

Suppose a pair of labels has only one coordinate k of opposite pure sides and every earlier gate has a common cold operand on their two diagonals. The two positive crosses must both first mix at k. All earlier ANDs vanish on both crosses: induct along the earlier gates. At each its common cold operand equals its shadow on those two diagonals and on the crosses after all preceding ANDs have vanished. Its shadow contains none of the four ports, so that operand rejects both crosses. Each k-operand is then its shadow on these samples. It rejects one diagonal but accepts both crosses; each of the two ports of the other label must be in it. The enumeration records these double-leaf witnesses in `known`.

**External ORs and Kraft.** Consider an operand with at least two hot diagonal labels and a diagonal vector different from each earlier surviving AND output. It must be a physical OR: no intact raw has two hot diagonal labels, and every discarded AND is cold. If this operand is hot on a target-cold diagonal, it cannot lie on a path of ORs to the target, hence is external to the target’s shadow. Distinct diagonal vectors certify distinct physical ORs. For a source with both its ports forced into such an external OR, either chosen port gains one external activation. The check counts up to two distinct external ORs this way. For each chosen raw leaf with e external activations its path through the shadow has depth at most 3−e, since all ORs along that path fire on its singleton. Thus it contributes at least 2^e/8 to the binary-tree Kraft sum. Choose distinct leaves, one or two per label according to the preceding rules. Some operand exceeds one in 338 profiles. This argument permits shared gates because the unrolled occurrences chosen for different raw identities are distinct and no path repeats a physical gate.

**The two remaining profiles.** In either, the first AND is true on two diagonal labels on which the bits of both later gates differ. Every later operand therefore rejects one of those diagonals; the final output rejects both. Cut all AND outputs into independent leaves in the OR-only expressions for the later operands and final output. None of these expressions can contain the first AND, since its true value would propagate to a false root on the corresponding diagonal. Delete the first AND. Two surviving ANDs cannot distinguish the six labels. The finite checker tests this removal criterion independently for each residual profile.

The argument is a mathematical proof with executable finite verification. Integration with the physical-circuit definitions in Lean remains separate.
