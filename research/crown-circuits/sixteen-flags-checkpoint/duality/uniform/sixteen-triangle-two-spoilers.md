# At least two remote singleton spoilers for every triangle vertex

Consider the sixteen-flag triangle plus four three-vertex paths interface with twelve OR and eight AND gates. Each of the eight path edges enters its center bank OR and has a separate use as the corresponding leaf flag. Write the three triangle flags A,B,C. A singleton edge lights two flags and a pair of distinct triangle edges lights all three. A spoiler for A is a remote singleton y whose path-bank encoding together with A alone makes the decoder accept.

Fix A to one and supply B and C as two independent raw labels. Rebuild only the four path bank ORs. Before propagation this costs 24 gates. One active local label now gives a valid triangle singleton. Two active local labels give a valid positive. With zero local labels every original positive stays positive by monotonicity, and the empty remote input is rejected because A alone lies below a valid negative triangle singleton. The only possible errors are precisely the remote singleton spoilers.

If there are at most one, delete a path raw y covering all spoilers. This now computes threshold two on nine raw labels. Both direct readers of y simplify, as do all direct readers of A. These cover at least three distinct gates unless the only reader of A is y's outside reader. If that reader is an AND, A is invisible on the isolated triangle, whereas the valid negative BC and valid positive ABC must differ. If it is an OR, y already sets the reader to one on its singleton, so adding A cannot spoil y. If there are zero spoilers, choose any y whose reader differs from the sole reader of A (such a binary reader can directly receive at most one of the eight path flags). Thus at least three distinct gates disappear.

The nine-input lower bound of 21 forces exactly three deletions. But every 21-gate circuit on nine inputs has exactly four ANDs, and deleting only three gates cannot lower eight ANDs to four. Each of the three triangle flags therefore has at least two distinct remote singleton spoilers. Both cyclic sixteen-flag host questions remain open.
