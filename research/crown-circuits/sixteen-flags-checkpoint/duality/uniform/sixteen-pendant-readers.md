# Pendant readers in the cyclic sixteen-flag residuals

In either cyclic residual write R for its twelve-OR, eight-AND decoder. Its edge bank on eleven independent labels has seven ORs, so any size improvement would contradict the ordinary minimum C(11)=27. We can use a minimum-size representative under the twelve-OR cap while retaining the eight-AND composition: the component lower bound already forces twelve ORs, and the 27-gate floor then forces eight ANDs.

Each leaf of a P3 flag component has one reader in that representative. Erasing the leaf retains a single edge; its two endpoint flags may be identified as one independent raw. Relative to the fixed cycle bank this independent raw costs two ORs, each remaining P3 costs three and any K2 costs two. These and the cycle floor exhaust eleven ORs after the selected erasure, so the leaf cannot have two distinct first ORs. The single-reader factorization applies. No two such leaves can feed one OR: siblings already have their common center as their disjunction, and two singly-read leaves in different P3s would permit a one-gate-saving formal vertex identification. The resulting P5, with the still-intact cycle, is a strong base; its floor and full-order remote charges exceed the reduced budget. A cross-component leaf–leaf AND also destroys the ability of each chosen leaf to distinguish its own local pair from its sibling singleton when the opposite component is absent.

At least one P3 has both of its leaf readers OR gates. Otherwise erase one AND-read leaf per P3. The erased ANDs are distinct by the preceding observation. Choose to propagate zeros only and postpone other simplifications; each P3 erasure also contracts at least one distinct OR along a path witnessing local essentiality. Equivalently those ORs must be lost via the successive individual leaf tests even with all intervening constant propagation: later P3 leaf distinctions persist. With C3+4P3 the remaining triangle plus four independent raws gives an ordinary seven-input circuit using at most three triangle bank ORs plus eight decoder ORs and four ANDs, below C(7)=16. With C4+3P3+K2 the remaining square plus four independent raws gives eight inputs with at most four bank ORs plus nine decoder ORs and five ANDs, below C(8)=19. This proves the stated disjunction of reader types without excluding either residual.

Every cycle vertex z spoils some remote singleton in both families. In C3+4P3, if z were harmless its two neighbors would become two independent virtual raws on pinning z true. The four P3 banks use four ORs, giving ordinary T2 on ten inputs with at most sixteen ORs and eight ANDs. At this OR floor nine ANDs are necessary. In C4+3P3+K2, first erase the cycle vertex z' opposite z, losing at least one decoder OR by the local edge-extension witness. Pinning harmless z gives two independent virtual raws alongside the seven remote labels. The three P3 banks cost three ORs, hence the ordinary nine-input circuit would use at most fourteen ORs and eight ANDs. These are respectively its OR floor and its minimum AND count at the floor. No additional OR and no single AND may be lost. In particular no AND was removed during zero propagation at z'. Under that assumption a physical path from z survives: a dark OR has only dark operands, whereas a one-dark OR redirects its live operand along every original outgoing slot. An AND with a dark operand would have disappeared. Keep live gates without discretionary functional simplification, so z still has a reader unless it is the output, which is impossible on the remaining positive/negative tests. Fixing z true simplifies that reader, an additional gate loss and a contradiction.
