# No triangle-only gate in the cyclic sixteen-flag decoder

Use the triangle-plus-four-paths residual with twelve decoder ORs and eight ANDs, and the pin-false bound from triangle-ports.md. The reconstructed eleven-variable threshold circuit costs 27, so the decoder is size-minimal. By triangle-readers.md a direct vertex-pair AND is impossible, and an AND reading any triangle vertex is its sole reader.

**At most one vertex has an AND reader.** Suppose A and B do, with the A reader A∧P earlier. P depends on neither A nor B. At the triangle-only singleton BC, adding A changes the verdict, so P is true. It remains true at triangle-only AC since only C and the outside paths are visible to it. Pin B false and identify A=C=z. The product A∧P equals z by monotonicity from that last singleton. This removes a distinct third gate beyond B's reader and its first downstream OR, contrary to the pin-false bound.

**A common OR is impossible.** Suppose N=A∨B exists. On every allowed assignment N is triangle nonemptiness. Each of A and B requires a second reader, since otherwise adding that vertex to its opposite triangle edge would be invisible. These other readers L and M are ORs by the reader lemma. They are distinct since a second OR reading both A and B duplicates N. Neither reads N since N absorbs A and B. Neither reads C since every OR of two distinct triangle vertices equals N.

Pin C false and identify A=B=z. Bypassing N saves one gate. At least one direct reader of C also simplifies. An AND reader would lose a further downstream OR distinct from N, which has only raw operands. Two direct readers of C would exceed the two-gate allowance as well. Thus C has a sole OR reader E, different from L,M. It cannot read N since N absorbs C. Bypass E and N. Any further gate simplification or dead gate would contradict the two-gate allowance. After rebuilding the four path-center ORs the threshold-nine circuit has fourteen ORs and eight ANDs.

Now z directly feeds L and M and at least one distinct reader inherited from N. Such a reader existed because N was live and cannot be the output. If the third reader is an OR, deleting z removes three ORs. If it is an AND, follow a path from it to the first OR. Such an OR occurs since some threshold-eight positive survives z=0. If this first OR were L or M, its operands would be z and an AND-only descendant bounded by z, making it redundant in the threshold-nine circuit. The two-gate allowance excludes this. Thus a third OR simplifies in addition to L and M. In either case the threshold-eight minor uses at most eleven ORs, contradicting the 2n−4 floor at n=8.

The first triangle-only gate would be an AND or OR of triangle vertices. Duplicate operands are redundant and both distinct-vertex cases are impossible.
