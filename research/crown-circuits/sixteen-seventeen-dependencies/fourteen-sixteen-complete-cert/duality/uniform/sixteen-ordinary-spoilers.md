# Spoilers of ordinary P3 flags

A formal flag spoils an external singleton when adding that flag alone to the singleton makes the decoder accept. In the cyclic q16 candidate every P3 center, and both leaves of each ordinary P3, spoil a cycle edge or the K2 singleton, or else spoil both raw singleton labels of some other P3. If none of these alternatives held, select a harmless representative in each other P3. Delete its sibling at the cost of one OR and identify its remaining bank flags as one independent raw. For a proposed harmless center, set it true and make its two leaf flags independent raws. For an ordinary leaf x with sibling y, set x true and y false and make the center an independent raw. This latter substitution eliminates the two distinct direct leaf OR gates. In both cases a nonempty virtual selection represents genuine star edges; when virtual-empty, external singletons reject by the selected harmlessness and all positive pairs still accept by monotonicity. Counting the erased ORs and the independent-raw increments against the retained cycle gives four ORs for the designated path, instead of the original three. The cap is exceeded.

Each P3 center in the triangle case spoils at least two external raw labels. This also holds in the square case except for a prospective sole independent K2 spoiler. With a unique spoiler z, pin the center true and delete z to obtain ordinary T2(10). If z is a cycle raw, two neighboring bank ORs disappear in addition to the designated center bank OR and one reader of the now-true center, lowering size 27 to at most 23. If z lies in another P3, both center banks and the leaf reader of z disappear. A shared sole OR of the designated center and z would make z harmless, and a shared sole AND would hide z's local pair when the first component is empty. Thus at least one separate center reader is removable. The resulting 23-gate bound violates C(10)=24.

An ordinary leaf x likewise spoils at least two external labels, with the same K2 exception. With unique spoiler z, set x true, its sibling y false and use the center as an independent virtual raw, then delete z. This realizes T2(9). Its explicit savings include the designated center bank, both direct OR readers Ux and Uy, and either two incident OR banks of z on the cycle or the other path's bank OR and z's leaf reader. These five gates are distinct. The now-true Ux also simplifies each of its direct consumers. No such consumer is Uy, which is false at singleton x while Ux is true there. If its only consumer were z's leaf OR, x and z would be singly used terms of the same maximal OR group, contrary to the leaf fusion obstruction. If its only consumer were z's leaf AND, x could not distinguish its own positive local pair from its sibling singleton when z is false. A sixth distinct gate is therefore removable. The nine-input size floor is 21, where four original ANDs must have disappeared; at most two of the six displayed gates are ANDs. Further savings would violate the size floor. This contradiction proves the two-spoiler claim.
