# All three residual shapes at sixteen flags

For the size-34 fourteen-input threshold-two candidate with eight ANDs, the degree/pendant classification in duality/uniform/fourteen-sixteen-residuals.md gives exactly three residual shapes, each with a decoder allowance of twelve ORs and eight ANDs. The three exclusions are in this packet.

- Three three-edge stars and one two-edge star are excluded by degree/t16-pair-stars/two-three-stars.md. The small source check.py is the constructive upper control for the local two-star theorem.
- The triangle with four two-edge paths is excluded by degree/t16-cyclic-build/triangle-final-two-deletions.md and its predecessors. Its original source manifest and scope statement are in scoped-receipts/triangle.
- The square with three two-edge paths and one single edge is excluded by degree/t16-cyclic-build/square-final-host.md and its predecessors. Its original source manifest and scope statement are in scoped-receipts/square.

The tree and cyclic proofs concern physical circuits with arbitrary fanout. The older shape and path-bound notes describe the state of those questions when written; the final files above give the later conclusions. Historical gate-composition results and checked local path floors are dependencies. This packet collects their relevant texts and receipts without re-running the earlier large computations. The scope is the sixteen-flag stratum.
