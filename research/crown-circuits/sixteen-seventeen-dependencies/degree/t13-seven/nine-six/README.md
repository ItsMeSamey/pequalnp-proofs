# Nine-input, six-AND subcase

A size-21 threshold-two circuit on nine inputs, if it has exactly six ANDs, has at most thirteen reverse binary unions. In the minimizing representative there is no direct receiver: such a receiver saves three gates on specialization, whereas eight inputs require nineteen. The nine distinct paid receiver joins leave at most four other joins, for both nonfree activations and additional helpers. Each of the six physical selector signs must contain at least two whole receiver deficits. Otherwise resolving that AND and erasing the possible single lost label (padding to one when necessary) removes two distinct raw OR readers plus the resolved AND, violating the eight-input minimum. The 21-gate representative has two OR readers per variable by the standard g−2 normalization.

Generate profiles using verification/threshold-nine/profile6.cpp with arguments 9 4 and then its canon6.cpp. There are 41,798 signed coordinate orbits. quota.cpp checks a necessary trace-atom condition for every sign; 37,860 profiles remain. low.cpp and high.cpp are adaptations of the verified smaller budget solvers: four minus the number of mandatory nonbranch activations is the spare budget. Numbered profile IDs in their outputs index the retained list. Exact refinements use exact.cpp, adapted from calculus/eleven26six/sixexact.cpp, demanding nine receivers, four spare joins inclusive of mandatory activations, hot-singleton witnesses for nonfull activations, two whole deficits per sign, and simultaneous fixed-point chronology.

For the all-full profile there is a separate signed-literal proof. Nine pairwise-incompatible proper signed codes require a maximum receiver width between four and six by volume. For each width normalize an anchor, enumerate at most four nonreceiver joins and search for eight more pairwise-conflicting codes. Replacing a smaller code by a superset from the same library preserves every required signed-literal quota and disjointness of deficits. full.cpp, with arguments 6 WIDTH 0 16, rejected all three widths. The encoded budget 16 in that source means four helpers. This search accounts for all 2,066 normalized anchor libraries jointly.

The general profile test and any exact refinements need a reconciled ledger to certify the full class.

The six-AND class is completely excluded. The 1,338 relaxed hits partition by spare budget as 445, 649, 168, 46 and 30. Exact chronological search rejects all 1,337 profiles with some nonfull activation. The one remaining all-full profile is excluded by the independent anchored checker. verify.py rebuilds the source-to-endpoint calculation in a temporary directory. Compressed ledgers record the original primary run.
