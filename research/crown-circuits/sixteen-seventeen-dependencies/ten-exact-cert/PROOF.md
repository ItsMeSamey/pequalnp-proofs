# Exact monotone circuit size of threshold two on ten inputs

The least number of binary AND and OR gates in a monotone circuit of arbitrary depth and fanout computing T₂¹⁰ is **24**. The explicit upper circuit splits one cell of a nine-cell grid into two raw variables and adds their conjunction at the output. upper/ten-upper.py evaluates all 1,024 inputs.

For the lower bound, the previously established T₂¹⁰ lower bound of 23 permits choosing a size-23 minimum hypothetical counterexample. At least sixteen OR gates are needed by the 2n−4 OR lower bound; consequently at most seven ANDs are allowed. Fewer than four ANDs supply fewer than ten selector resolutions, so two rejected singleton witnesses would coincide. The subdirectories four, five, six and seven give separate proofs for four through seven ANDs.

The four-AND archive uses projected minimal clauses and binary union search. The five-AND archive checks every chronological switch profile up to signed coordinate renaming with the exact paid union budget. The six-AND archive uses the same switch transfer, incomparable raw sinks, and at most four auxiliary reverse unions. Its five budget strata cover 41,719 signed switch orbits. The seven-AND argument uses the exact lower bounds 21 and 19 at nine and eight inputs, two OR readers per raw, OR-group flattening, the star minor, and a finite DNF cover calculation for six-vertex graphs. An independent physical OR-tight seven-AND argument is included in dependencies. The exact four/five/six signed and physical prerequisites are described inside their proofs; source reviews and numerical verification details are in REVIEW.md.

Run python3 check_ledgers.py and python3 upper/ten-upper.py from this directory for the aggregate accounting and circuit check. Full finite searches are reproducible using the published C++20, JavaScript, and Python sources and the reproduction instructions. These are finite computational lower bounds, without a Lean proof of the complete circuit minimum.
