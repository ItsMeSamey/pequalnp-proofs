# Four-bit component planes and weighted groves

For a zero-total F₂⁴ vertex labeling, let Z_P sum the edge monomials of all spanning three-forests whose component label sums span the specified binary plane P. The arithmetic coefficient field may be unrelated to the label field.

In the frozen permanent-to-grove construction (SHA256 8de6e2d5a3518de6af0f605dc0c5729b6fd2be4289fea4fd33dddf4fc0ae6d0f), remove the forced unit pendants but keep z. Give the four coordinates supports {a0,aN}, {c0,cN}, {a0,z}, {c0,z}. Target span(e3,e4), indexing bits from one. The first two coordinates vanish on every component exactly when both endpoint pairs cohabit their respective components. The last two columns form the binary incidence of the projected links a0–z and c0–z. Thus rank two holds exactly when a0,c0,z occupy distinct components. This is exactly the weighted grove, with equal coefficients, preserving all three extraction tags. The instances use five nonzero vertex labels. To obtain a connected host add a formal edge incident to z, specialized to zero. The verified characteristic-zero permanent reduction carries over.

Order a forest's components by smallest vertex and let x,y be the first two sums. The third is x+y. By character orthogonality, in odd characteristic or characteristic zero the indicator of exact plane P equals the sum over distinct nonzero a,b in P and h,k in F₂⁴ of (-1)^(h·(x+a)+k·(y+b)), divided by 256. The order removes any multiplicity from permuting components. In characteristic two use F₂[(F₂⁴)²] and put the forest monomial in the (x,y) slot, then project to the six ordered bases of P. These identities require computation of weighted forests by ordered component scores. With distinct edge variables different forests cannot cancel. Efficiently constructing or testing this polynomial in characteristic two remains open. Exact characteristic-zero computation inherits the formal arithmetic lower bound of the cited permanent theorem.
