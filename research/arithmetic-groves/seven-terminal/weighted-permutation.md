# A polynomial arithmetic reduction for a weighted permutation sum

For a permutation π on N positions let Hπ be the multigraph on its N selected cells, carrying one path in row order and another in column order, with all parallel edges distinguished. Let a(π) count the ordered decompositions of E(Hπ) into two spanning trees. An empty tree on one vertex is allowed. Over any field, the polynomial
Σπ a(π) ∏i X_{i,π(i)}
is a homogeneous component of an explicit polynomial-size weighted grove Z331. These coefficients are neither the ordinary permanent coefficients nor Hamiltonian cycle indicators.

## Four-port cancellation

For four distinct ports with conductances g1,...,g4 summing to one, use a central star with these weights and add edges ij of weight −g_i g_j. In a larger graph retain the local forests with all components touching ports. The all-distinct state has weight one. Every single-pair state has weight zero, by its mixed Laplacian minor and the zero Schur complement at the star center. The all-connected state is zero by the tree cofactor of the same Schur complement. For any pair-pair partition, assigning the center to either side and expanding the two trees gives weight −g1g2g3g4. For instance, the weights when the center belongs to ports 12 are g1g2[1−g1−g2] and −g3g4 on the other side, and conversely for 34. A triple-singleton partition has the opposite weight. For triple 123 and singleton 4, assigning the center to 4 contributes (g1+g2+g3)g1g2g3g4, and assigning it to 123 contributes g4²g1g2g3. For the latter three-edge tree cofactor the matrix after deleting the center row and column is g4 diag(g1,g2,g3)+gg^T with g=(g1,g2,g3)^T. Its determinant is the claimed expression, identically even at zero conductances.

Set (g1,g2,g3,g4)=(x,1−x,1,−1). The signature is the inactive all-distinct state plus x(1−x)D, where D has value +1 at the three pair-pair partitions and −1 at the four triple-singletons. All other entries of D vanish. Internalizing any single port with no other attachment kills D: if the other three marks join, the four-port all-connected weight is zero; if a pair joins, the two compatible cuts have weights +1 and −1; if all stay separate, all compatible zero- or single-join terms vanish. Likewise D is invisible when substituted into a tree with no other specified connection condition, even with an arbitrary surrounding tree graph: summing all possible forests over local states computing a single spanning tree is exactly a Kirchhoff determinant, which depends only on the star's zero Schur complement. These are polynomial identities over the integers after the stated normalization.

## Enforcing a permutation

Make two open chains of interface vertices a0,...,aN and b0,...,bN, with no chain edges. At cell (i,j) install the four-port gadget on (a_{i−1},a_i,b_{j−1},b_j), with x=Xij and private center. Retain its individual edge variables until substituting the stated signed weights. Parallel edges are distinguishable. Request in the full assembled network the partition a0 aN | b0 bN in a spanning two-forest. For a Z331 instance attach two new pendant vertices, one via a unit edge to a0 and one to b0, include them in the corresponding triples, then add a separate isolated singleton. The pendant edges are forced. The construction uses O(N²) vertices and edges and constant arithmetic per edge.

In the local state expansion an active D contributes exactly two joins among interface vertices and an inactive gadget contributes none. To obtain a two-forest on 2N+2 interface vertices requires exactly N active cells (the number of joins is additive if the union has no cycle). Suppose an unmarked interface vertex is incident to exactly one active cell. It can be internalized in just that D, giving zero by the previous identity. If it has no active incident cell it is an unmarked component, giving zero. Both marked endpoints on each chain must be incident to at least one active cell or their requested connections fail. In the row chain the sum of all these incidences is 2N. Thus each internal interface vertex has exactly two and each endpoint exactly one. For the row active counts k_i this gives k_1=1 and k_{i−1}+k_i=2, forcing k_i=1 throughout. The column argument is identical. The only contributing active sets are permutations.

## The remaining coefficient

For a selected permutation identify its cells with vertices of Hπ. Its internal interface vertices are the labelled edges of Hπ. The four exterior endpoints are dangling halfedges with the prescribed two tree colors. For a fixed coloring of all interface vertices by the two final trees, if all four ports of any active D are in one tree the total contribution vanishes. Indeed, with the other color and other local choices fixed, the monochrome part of the sum is the difference of two spanning-tree sums obtained by inserting the four-port network and its electrically equivalent inactive network, divided formally by x(1−x). Both tree sums agree by Schur complementation, even in a disconnected surrounding graph. The free center of the gadget belongs to that same tree because it has no neighbor of the other color.

At any remaining active cell both colors occur. D has two blocks, so they must be its two color classes. Collapsing each local tree of a given color to that cell gives the subgraph of Hπ with edges of that color and the corresponding marked halfedges. Its connectivity and acyclicity are precisely the global tree requirement. Thus each color must give a spanning tree of Hπ. Conversely for N≥2 any ordered complementary pair of spanning trees gives both colors at every cell, and defines the unique local partition at each. For N=1 the fixed halfedges themselves give both colors. The local sign is negative exactly when the number of incident halfedges of the first color is odd. Summed over cells that degree is twice the number of first-color internal edges plus two marked halfedges, hence even. All valid spanning-tree decompositions contribute +1. This gives the exact weight a(π)∏i X_{i,π(i)}(1−X_{i,π(i)}). Its degree-N part is the polynomial stated above; it has degree at most 2N, so homogeneous component extraction costs polynomially many arithmetic gates per original gate by standard coefficient propagation over any field.

When N≥2 the exchange of complementary trees is a fixed-point-free involution, so all a(π) vanish in characteristic two. In characteristic zero the coefficient does vary with π: for N=4, the identity has a doubled path, giving a=8. For π=2413 the two paths together form K4, giving a=12 since the four stars have non-tree complements among the 16 spanning trees of K4. This disproves division by a universal scalar. No reduction from the ordinary permanent or HC follows from this construction. A further procedure fixing the complementary tree coloring or removing its π-dependent multiplicity would be needed.
