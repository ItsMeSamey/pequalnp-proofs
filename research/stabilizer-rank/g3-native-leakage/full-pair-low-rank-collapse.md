# A mixed triangle exclusion by coherent collapse of its full pair

Let U,V be isometric stabilizer encoders from three qubits to the same register, and a,b nonzero complex numbers. If rank(aU+bV)≤2, its vectorization is zero or a scaled stabilizer ray.

The kernel has dimension at least six, so the eight-dimensional stabilizer image codes meet in dimension at least six. Nonempty stabilizer code intersection has power-of-two dimension. Thus their images coincide. The relative C=U†V is Clifford up to phase and has eigenvalue −a/b with multiplicity at least six. Divide out this eigenvalue. The defect becomes I−C and |tr C|≥4.

The coefficients tr(PC)/8 over Hermitian Pauli representatives are the normalized stabilizer Choi state's coordinates in the Bell stabilizer basis, with harmless phase conventions. Thus their nonzero magnitudes are equal on affine support. The identity coefficient has magnitude ≥1/2, so support size is 1, 2 or 4. Size 1 gives a scalar. Size 2 makes C a linear combination of I and one nonidentity Pauli, with two distinct eigenvalues of multiplicity four, impossible. Size 4 gives |tr C|=4. Equality in the triangle inequality forces exactly six eigenvalues 1 and two others −1. Then Π=(I−C)/2 has rank two, trace two, and Pauli expansion (I±P±Q±R)/4 because C is Hermitian and the three nonidentity coefficients have magnitude 1/2. The state Π/2 has expectation modulus one on each of P,Q,R: their appropriately signed representatives stabilize its support. Two independent commuting labels cut out exactly dimension two, so Π is their stabilizer-code projector. Composing it with U has stabilizer Choi vector up to scale by stabilizer projection of the encoder Choi state.

In a minimum seven-term G3 list no pair can collapse. On any original cut a remote-orthogonal component sum has matrix rank ≤1, from projecting the true target. If two full parents in that component account for all but an aggregate map of rank at most one, their weighted pair sum has rank at most two. This excludes in particular an entire triangle of local Schmidt ranks (8,8,1), even though it has no degree-one edge. Several remaining parents may share one input or remote line. Arbitrary collections of rank-one terms need not have rank-one aggregate. No conclusion on three full parents follows.
