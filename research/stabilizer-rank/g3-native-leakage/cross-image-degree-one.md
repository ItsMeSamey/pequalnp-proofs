# Degree-one full parents impose an exact coefficient ratio

Keep normalized flattenings from actual-list-cross-image-rank.md. At every full parent i of a seven-list, at least one of its six potential neighbors has cross-map rank ≥2, since their ranks sum to at least seven. If exactly one neighbor j has a nonzero cross map M=A_i†A_j, the displayed identity gives rank M≥7. Its Choi vector is either zero or a scaled stabilizer by contraction of the two parent stabilizers with Bell bras (complex conjugation preserves stabilizerness). Nonzero singular values of a stabilizer flattening are equal and its rank is a power of two. M therefore has rank eight and is proportional to a unitary Clifford, by the maximal-entanglement stabilizer Choi correspondence.

On the seven-dimensional kernel of the target bra the map c_j M equals −c_i I/8. A nonscalar three-qubit Clifford cannot have an eigenvalue of multiplicity seven: such a multiplicity forces trace magnitude at least 6. Yet |tr C|² is the trace of conjugation by C, a signed permutation on 64 Pauli labels. If its symplectic action is nonidentity at most 32 labels can be fixed. If the action is identity a nonscalar Clifford is a nonidentity Pauli up to phase and has trace zero. Thus M=μI, A_i†G2=0 and c_i=−8μ c_j. In particular if the remote G2 has a nonzero projection into the image of full parent i, then this parent has at least two distinct neighbors with nonorthogonal remote images. Each degree-one constraint uses the same physical coefficients on all three cuts. Contradictory ratios rule out that candidate list even with unknown nonzero complex weights.
