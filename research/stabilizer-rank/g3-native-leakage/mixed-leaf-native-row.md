# Native input excludes homogeneous trees of Schmidt rank two or four

Use the leaf-folding setup of full-component-forest-exclusion.md. The component sum is T=h g^T by remote projection of the true target; g is real and has no nonidentity signed Pauli stabilizer. Put r=2 or 4. At a leaf let its self Gram be αΠ_S, α>0, for a dimension-r input stabilizer code S. For the original sole-neighbor cross map M=A_i†A_j one has c_i αΠ_S+c_j M=B_i†T with row space in span(g). Since g is outside S, Π_S maps g-perpendicular space onto S. The identity on this hyperplane forces rank M≥r. Its rank is at most r via the original rank-r first factor, hence equals r.

The row space S' of M is another dimension-r stabilizer code by its stabilizer Choi vector. The rank-one perturbation implies dim(S+S')≤r+1 since the row space of one map lies in the sum of the other's row space and the difference's row space. Thus their intersection has dimension at least r−1. For r=4 distinct such stabilizer hyperplanes intersect in dimension at most two, so S=S'. For r=2 a common nonzero vector forces all defining signed Pauli checks to commute with consistent signs. Extend the union to a maximal commuting basis; S+S' is spanned by at most three basis strings. Any three strings in a three-bit stabilizer basis lie in an affine plane, so g cannot lie in their span since g has no Pauli check. For both ranks g∉S+S'; the row space of B_i†T lies in both S+S' and span(g), hence vanishes. In particular c_j M=−c_i αΠ_S. This precludes a lower-rank sole neighbor.

In a component of homogeneous rank r the neighbor's original input support necessarily equals S (it contains row M and has dimension r). This applies also to a folded neighbor retaining its original input support. Fold i into j with the original coefficient ratio as before. The new self Gram remains a positive scalar times Π_S by independence. Cross Grams to remaining vertices remain original. At the final node one gets rank r>1, contradiction. Hence every homogeneous rank-two or rank-four component must contain a cycle. Folding a mixed leaf into a higher-rank parent may yield a nonuniform Gram, so unrestricted mixed-rank tree exclusion does not follow.
