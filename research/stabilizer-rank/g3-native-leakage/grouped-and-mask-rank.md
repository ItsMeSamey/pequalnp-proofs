# Partial AND substitution with a mask-dependent rank

Normalize an original circuit to m products z_i=a_i b_i and affine output o. Choose S whose gate operands read no z_s for s∈S (raw-only gates are one directly discoverable choice). Let R be the remaining gates and C_j the 2×|S| coefficient matrix on z_S in j's operands. Exact count and original witness take polynomial space and time bounded by
    poly(size) Σ_{U⊆R} 2^{rank(stack(C_j:j∈U))}.

Compute the signed physical sum (−1)^o, subtract from 2^n and halve. Expand only R using [z=ab]=(1−z)+(2z−1)ab. Selecting second terms for U sets z_j=0 outside U and a_j=b_j=1 inside U, with sign (−1)^{|U|+Σ_{j∈U}z_j}. Substitute z_s=q_s=a_s b_s for all s. Each q_s is quadratic in remaining x,z_R, hence so is the readout. Each selected operand pin is an affine function plus one row of the stacked C_U applied to q. Reduce C_U, applying the same row operations to the whole equations. Zero coefficient rows are affine pins. Fourier expand the d=rank(C_U) remaining quadratic pins and evaluate 2^d signed quadratic Gauss sums on the pinned affine space. Normalize by 2^d for that mask. Unique original gate traces and direct substitution introduce no multiplicity. Raw pins do not increase d; at most n additional counts recover an original witness. All numerators and common power-of-two denominators have polynomial bit length since d≤m and the domain dimension is ≤n+m.

Checkable bounds without listing all masks include ∏_{j∈R}(1+2^{rank C_j}) and 2^{|R|+rank C_R}. Gates in R may have arbitrary dependencies on earlier R gates, and S may fan out densely. Further savings are possible by recomputing the rank of the actual quadratic polar forms on the affine slice and imposing any linear equations obtained.

Example: n=m=2k for even k. First set s_i=x_i y_i. Then r_j=L_j(s)(x_j+r_{j−1}), starting r_0=0, output r_k. Use L=J+I, invertible for even k over F2. The raw coefficient matrix has rank n from the first layer. Its column space intersects the nonlinear producer column space trivially since the latter vanishes on first-layer rows and the former restricts there injectively. L, the predecessor-copy rows and the output readout give full producer-column rank m. Hence the earlier quotient parameters are ρ=n, τ=m. For S the first layer, each receiver reads one independent dense row on S while its other operand reads none. Thus d(U)=|U|, giving deterministic exact time 3^k=2^{0.792481…n} times a polynomial, also under arbitrary raw pins. This is a guarantee for the measured class, not an optimality or unrestricted circuit bound.
