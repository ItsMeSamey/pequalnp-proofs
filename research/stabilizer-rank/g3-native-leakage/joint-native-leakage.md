# Wrong-outcome incidences across all three native axes

Consider one CCZ block of a stabilizer ray, possibly entangled with external qubits. In chart i measure X on i and Z on the other two bits. Call a cell wrong if its X sign bit differs from the product of those two bits. Count nonzero outcomes without probability weights. Every ray incurs at least three wrong cells summed over the three charts. If it entangles the block with the exterior it incurs at least five.

The original three-Z marginal support is an affine flat A of dimension k, by the stabilizer measurement rule. If e_i is outside its direction then deleting bit i is injective on A. At every projected support point the computational bit is fixed, so both X signs occur. This gives exactly 2^k wrong cells. At most k unit directions belong to the flat direction. For k=0,1,2 this alone gives at least (3−k)2^k≥3. For k=3 every chart projects onto all four unmeasured-in-X bit strings. Its support is affine and cannot be contained in the nonlinear good graph, so every chart incurs at least one wrong cell.

For the entangled refinement k=0 is impossible. When k is one or two and fewer than k unit directions are present, absent directions alone contribute at least six. Otherwise there are k independent unit directions, including the k=3 case. For a present unit direction the chart projects onto 2^(k−1) two-bit strings. By affineness either both signs occur over every string, or the sign is an affine function on the projected flat. In the latter case there is a signed block-local stabilizer X_i times a product of Zs on the other two sites, extending the affine function arbitrarily to all strings. If all k directions had this property, those k checks together with the 3−k local Z support checks would form three independent checks on the block, forcing a product across its boundary. Hence at least one present axis pays 2^(k−1). Together with absent axes this gives at least five for k=1 and six for k=2. For k=3 the two remaining axes each pay at least one, giving six. A Bell pair joining one block bit to a remote bit, with the remaining block bits fixed to zero, attains five.

For a seven-ray list with e rays entangling the block, the total wrong incidence count is at least 21+2e. The floor 21 can coexist with at least four per good cell and no singleton wrong cell. Let D=CCZ and take block rays D X^v D |+++> for v=000,011,101 with multiplicities 3,2,2. This monomial Clifford stabilizes the CCZ target and permutes the cells in each native chart, translating the measured Z bits by the corresponding entries of v. In any given chart the unshifted plus state covers three good cells and one wrong cell; its missing good and unique wrong occur over the same Z pair 11. The three translations have distinct projections upon deleting any coordinate. Thus the four good multiplicities are 4,5,5,7 and the wrong multiplicities 3,2,2,0, in corresponding order. Distinct remote factors may distinguish the repeated local rays. These figures concern incidences only.
