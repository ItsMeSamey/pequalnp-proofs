# Exact lifting of prescribed native Pauli quarters

Use one coherent Clifford chart for two measured bits a,b. For four proposed nonzero data vectors v_ab, retain their proposed coefficients, up to one common scalar. Their coherent sum is a stabilizer ray iff the following tests pass. The data rays have the same unsigned maximal stabilizer L. Relative eigenvalue sign shifts on L satisfy δ11=δ10+δ01, taking 00 as origin. Choose data Paulis U,V with symplectic pairing against L equal to δ10,δ01. These always exist. Undo those shifts by U controlled by a followed by V controlled by b. These Clifford gates leave four proportional vectors c_ab times a fixed ket. Require c10/c00,c01/c00 to be fourth roots of unity and c00 c11/(c10 c01) to be a sign. The remaining syndrome ket is then precisely a full-support quadratic two-qubit stabilizer. Undoing the gates proves sufficiency.

For necessity the X-syndrome image of the joint stabilizer group is all F2²: a proper image would yield a nonzero pure syndrome Z commuting with all stabilizers, forcing support on a line. Thus the subgroup commuting with both measured Zs has dimension n for n data qubits. Its restriction to data is injective (no pure syndrome Z), giving the maximal data group L. Its elements Z^u⊗P induce signs differing by (−1)^{u·(a,b)}, proving the character test. Controlled correction reduces the remaining test to the two-qubit quadratic phase formula.

Apply this to the actual G3 quarters under the two local Z measurements on the first two bits of one CCZ block. The four vectors are Z^{ab}|+>⊗G2, up to a common scalar. Expand G2 by any fixed four-term stabilizer decomposition in every quarter. Split one term into nonzero proportional parts to obtain five occurrences per quarter. Each one-check line, including both diagonals, lifts termwise by a single controlled-Pauli, since the phase coefficients are constant and the two data vectors differ only by Z on the remaining first-block bit. Yet NO permutation of occurrences can yield four-way columns: EVERY candidate occurrence in quarter ab has the same X eigenvalue (−1)^{ab} on that bit, independently of the G2 term. This sign function is nonlinear on the four outcomes. Every full column consequently fails. Equally sized lists matched bijectively cannot lift with the same number of parent columns. This proves a failure after all relabelings of the prescribed decompositions. It does not exclude changing the quarter decompositions or allowing additional columns with partial support.

In fact the obstruction survives allowing seven parent columns of arbitrary partial support. When every occurrence carries the prescribed target X sign, no column can meet all four quarters. The syndrome supports of stabilizers are affine, so every column then meets at most two. Seven columns have at most fourteen occurrences, whereas every quarter requires at least four by contraction with its correct X eigenbra and χ(G2)=4. This gives at least sixteen occurrences, or twenty for the five-term lists. Consequently any actual seven-ray decomposition must escape the prescribed target X eigenspace in at least one quarter. This argument concerns the target-matched eigenray hypothesis on all nonzero occurrences, not just one chosen labeling.
