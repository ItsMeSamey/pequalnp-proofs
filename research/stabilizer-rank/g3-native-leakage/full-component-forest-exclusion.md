# Exact seven-ray lists cannot have an all-full tree component

Fix one original block of a candidate exact seven-term representation of G3. On the seven parents draw undirected ij when the original normalized flattenings have A_i†A_j≠0. Consider any connected component consisting entirely of full-Schmidt parents. It must contain a cycle. The assertion holds regardless of ranks and coefficients outside the component. All original parents are independent by χ(G3)≥7; equivalently one may apply this theorem to any independent representation of a target of Schmidt rank one across an eight-dimensional input.

Suppose such a component is a tree, including possibly an isolated vertex. Distinct components have orthogonal remote image spans, hence projecting the total map onto the component gives T=Σ c_i A_i of rank at most one. It is nonzero by independence. Start with B_i=A_i and repeatedly fold leaves. Maintain B_i†B_i=α_i I with α_i>0 and B_i†B_j=A_i†A_j for any distinct surviving labels. Maintain Σ c_i B_i=T with the ORIGINAL coefficients of surviving nodes. Supports of the linear combinations of original parents representing each B_i are disjoint, with its own original parent having coefficient one.

For a leaf i adjacent to j, B_i†T=c_i α_i I+c_j A_i†A_j has rank at most one. Thus M=A_i†A_j has rank at least seven. Its Choi vector is a nonzero stabilizer contraction, so its rank is eight, with equal singular values and M proportional to a Clifford. On a subspace of dimension at least seven this Clifford is scalar with a single eigenvalue. A nonscalar three-qubit Clifford cannot have such an eigenvalue: trace magnitude would be ≥6, but the signed Pauli permutation trace bound gives |tr C|≤√32 for nonidentity symplectic action, and a nonidentity Pauli has trace zero. Consequently M is scalar and c_i α_i I+c_j M=0. This applies to the ORIGINAL cross map even though B_i may have ceased to be a stabilizer.

Replace B_j by B_j+(c_i/c_j)B_i and delete i. The new self Gram is scalar because both self Grams and the cross map are scalar. The new map is nonzero by the disjoint-support invariant and independence, so this scalar is positive. Cross Grams to other surviving nodes are unchanged since i was a leaf. All invariants hold and the weighted sum is unchanged. At the final node T has rank eight, contradicting its rank at most one.

This rules out every tree topology for such a component, including stars whose pairwise arrow constraints were compatible. A cycle is a necessary condition, not a construction. The graph uses the actual signed rays; unsigned symplectic frames by themselves may not determine nonzero cross maps.
