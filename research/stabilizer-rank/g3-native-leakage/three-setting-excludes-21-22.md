# Seven terms require at least 23 wrong incidences across the three native settings on a block

Use the reviewed local signed enumeration three-setting-leakage-exact.py. The minima for signed local group dimensions 0 through 3 are 12,8,5,3. The sixteen groups attaining three are pure. Write e_s for each computational basis vector, g for the CCZ phase vector, and h_s=g-2g_s e_s. The e_s each contribute one wrong incidence in each setting; the h_s have that property because reversing the sign at s reverses the X outcome only on the single axis-parallel edge through s. Each h_s is a stabilizer since adding a single point indicator to the Boolean cubic cancels its cubic coefficient. These sixteen rays are distinct, exhausting the minimum.

For total 21 all columns have one of these local pure factors. Group their remote weighted stabilizer contributions as A_s for e_s and B_s for h_s. Write D=G2-sum B_s. Pointwise the identity gives A_s=g_s(D+2B_s). Some index is unused by seven atoms on eight indices, hence D=0. Taking stabilizer rank with rank(0)=0 gives a term count at least twice the sum of the ranks of B_s, and hence at least eight.

For total 22 precisely six columns have minimizing factors, while the remaining column has a pure local factor k of cost four by the mixed-state minima. Denote its nonzero weighted remote stabilizer by C. Now A_s=g_s(D+2B_s)-k_s C, and an index unused by the six gives D=lambda C. In the quotient by span(C), the class of G2 needs at least three stabilizer images: otherwise lifting and including C costs at most three stabilizers. The classes of B_s sum to this class, and the classes of A_s/(2g_s) also sum to it. Thus the six columns comprise exactly three of each kind. No nonempty B_s can vanish in the quotient, as deleting its terms would leave at most two. Every nonempty B_s therefore has a matching nonempty A_s, and conversely, so there are at most three used indices. At the other five or more, k_s=lambda g_s. The scalar lambda is nonzero because the three B terms cannot sum to G2.

The affine support of k contains at least five points and is full. After rescaling, k equals g on at least five points, with constant magnitude everywhere. The parity of the fourth-root phase exponent of a full chirp is affine up to a constant; since k is real at five points it is real everywhere. Its signs are those of a Boolean quadratic up to constant. Thus its set T of differences from g has odd size, since the XOR of all values of their Boolean difference is its cubic coefficient, one. Each of the twelve cube edges yields exactly one wrong occurrence iff its endpoints have different membership in T. The boundary edge count equals 3|T| minus twice the number of edges internal to T, hence is odd. This contradicts cost four.

This bound concerns the sum of the three wrong-incidence counts on one fixed local block; no single setting is asserted to attain 23.
