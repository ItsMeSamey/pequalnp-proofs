# Physical compiler, quotient counter and original witnesses

The physical-bounded-bank-compiler produces topologically ordered functional tables w_i=f_i(y_i) with affine input normals. Treat its final bank as a producer with affine readout pinned to one. Each original assignment extends uniquely.

For an arity-b table obtain its Boolean algebraic normal form by Möbius transform in O(b 2^b) operations. All monomials of degree ≥2 are computable with at most 2^b−b−1 binary ANDs, using one smaller monomial and one input per multiplication. Recombine by XOR. Compose the new DAG and inline affine-only producers. M tables of arity at most r require at most M(2^r−r−1) fresh ANDs for r≥1 and O(M(r+1)2^r) local synthesis work plus polynomial affine-wiring costs. Apply and-affine-quotient-counter.md using ranks of this actual synthesized binary presentation. Compute both original and compiled ranks when both are available, and keep the better bound after accounting for preprocessing.

Alternatively use y=Ax+Bw+d over all native table ports and the affine readout, factoring B=AC+EV. The same inverse gives x'=x+Cw, s=Vw, y=Ax'+Es+d and τ constraints s=Vf(y). If every component after substitution is Boolean degree ≤2, direct quadratic Fourier contraction is valid with exponent τ, including cancellations between banks. For fixed arity r verify by expanding multilinear monomials, reducing repetitions and canceling coefficients mod two in (n+M+1)^{O(r)}2^{O(r)} time plus input processing. If the degree test fails the binary synthesis route remains valid.

For a witness, first count; when positive, fix the n+τ coordinates of (x',s) successively by substitution into the transformed system, choosing a branch with positive count. This costs one additional count per bit, never increases the number of quadratic equations, and uses polynomial space. Recover w=f(y), then x=x'+Cw, and verify on the original circuit. For primal enumeration, choose an accepting Ax and solve the linear system. No growing list of extra raw-input equations enters the exponential parameter.

Native τ alone fails for arbitrary arity-three tables. For any AND/affine circuit construct F=Σ_i t_i(z_i+a_i b_i)+t_o(o+1), a polynomial-size list of monomials of degree ≤3 after binary reduction. Computing its parity output by independent monomial producers has B columns supported only on the final readout, hence τ≤1. On N=n+2m+1 bits, gap(F)=2^{m+1}#SAT. One exact count of F=1 therefore recovers #SAT. This metric reduction concerns the abstract bank representation and does not assert the physical pass emits it on all inputs.

Strict decrease for the actual rank-two pass: n=2k with k,L≥2. For each pair compute z_j1=x_jy_j then z_jh=z_j,h−1 y_j through depth L; take the left-associated XOR of all k outputs. Initially ρ=2k and τ=k(L−1)+1: the predecessor-copy rows and final parity readout (all zero in A) give exactly these independent B rows. Each chain wire represents the same two-variable table x_jy_j and does not overflow. The first sum of two disjoint products has essential rank four, so the pass promotes both operands. Thereafter the accumulated parity is affine on earlier producer values. Its sum with an independent product has essential rank three, so promotes as required. Inline all affine-only banks. ANF synthesis gives k independent products and parity, with ρ'=2k, τ'=1. For L=Θ(log n), m=Θ(n log n) and the original min exponent is n for large n; the compiled count and witness are polynomial. As a direct check the answer is (4^k−2^k)/2. This family is easy by idempotence; the generic baseline ρ=n, τ=m remains possible.
