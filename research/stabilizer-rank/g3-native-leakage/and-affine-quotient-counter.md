# Original circuit count via nonlinear signals modulo original inputs

Represent the AND operands and final output of an acyclic affine/binary-AND circuit as y=Ax+Bz+d, z_i=y_{2i−1}y_{2i}, y_{2m+1}=1, by affine symbolic propagation treating the m AND outputs z as formal inputs. All matrices are over F2 and are computable in polynomial time in the explicit description. Write ρ=rank A and τ=rank[A B]−rank A. Exact original model counting takes poly(input size)·2^{min(ρ,τ)} time and polynomial space.

Choose E whose τ columns complement im A in im[A B]. Solve B=AC+EV. Introduce x'=x+Cz and s=Vz. Now y=A x'+E s+d. Define q_i(x',s)=y_{2i−1}y_{2i}. Impose s=Vq(x',s) and pin the output y_o=1. These are τ quadratic equations on n+τ bits and one affine constraint. Forward, each original satisfying x has unique topological z and yields x',s. Backward, compute z=q and x=x'+Cz; original y=Ax+Bz+d and every gate equation hold, so this is an inverse. Invertibility of x↦x' alone is unnecessary. Average the signs of all 2^τ Fourier characters of the residual equations. Each is a quadratic Gauss sum subject to an affine pin, computable by polynomial-time binary elimination. Term counts and total numerators have O(n+2τ) bits; exact division by 2^τ follows accumulation.

Alternatively enumerate all 2^ρ images Ax, each with 2^{n−ρ} preimages, and evaluate the circuit in topological order. Independent dual derivation: Fourier expand the port equations and sum original x. Only λ∈ker A^T remains; for κ=B^Tλ each AND contributes 4[λ_p=λ_q=0] if κ_i=0, or 2(−1)^{λ_pλ_q} otherwise. The κ image has dimension τ. Each fiber has linear constraints, fixed magnitude and quadratic sign. Full dual normalization is 2^{n−(2m+1)} after accumulating the signed sum.

This is polynomial even at m=Θ(n log n) if τ or ρ is logarithmic. One example is a circuit whose uses of prior AND outputs in all ports pass through logarithmically many independent affine aggregates. Conversely take a chain p_i=z_{i−1} for i≥2 and output z_m, with exogenous q_i and p_1 spanning all n inputs. The m copy/output rows have zero A and select every z_i, so τ=m while ρ=n, possible at m=Θ(n log n). The bound then guarantees only input enumeration even though this example is easy by other means. No unrestricted circuit-SAT conclusion follows.
