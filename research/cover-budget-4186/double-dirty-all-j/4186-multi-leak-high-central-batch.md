# One leaking arm with at least three assigned residuals

Let a,b,w be leaf slots of the two arm and common guard trees before accounting for external sharing. For the star of leaks from A, each product L_i shares its entire private dirty tree between A and precisely one external top E_i. Let h_i≥2 count its leaf slots and p_i≥1 count remaining slots at E_i. All L_i dirty cones are disjoint; E_i has no other shared dirty factor. The combined allowance is a+b+w + Σ(p_i+1), since the effective extra external AND count is p_i, one of which is its clean top. Other private top costs can be handled separately. Let r≥3 be the exclusive batch size assigned to AW, let s be the exclusive batch size of BW and let e_i be external batch sizes.

All endpoints of the A batch and all its primitive clauses (including those of every L_i and of W) lie in N(v) of one ordinary star center v, by C4 and the transversal cube lemma. G restricted to N(v) is a matching together with isolated vertices. Any external batch contains only edges meeting N(v) because L_i accepts every assigned external edge. An external batch of size at least three is contained entirely inside N(v): it too has a common center, and any shared L_i clause contains at least two of its endpoints and has center v. Every positive batch at one projected pure conjunction is an induced matching.

An external root with p_i+1≥2e_i pays its full endpoint charge while leaving all L_i costs available to A. Only double or larger external batches can fail this test. Those entirely inside N(v) are handled together below. For a remaining mixed batch there are exactly two assigned edges.

* If both edges leave N(v), their respective inside endpoints x,y occur in every L_i clause. Three of the four independent transversals select x or y and hence satisfy L_i. Each requires one of the other p_i clauses to be false, and each such clause can fail on at most one transversal. Thus p_i≥3.
* Otherwise the batch comprises one interior edge f and one boundary edge xz, x∈N(v). The outside endpoint z differs from v: v is connected to every endpoint of the A batch by ordinary star edges, but one must also exclude z=v for this specific residual. In that case x appears in every L_i clause and v is its star center, so vx is already a star edge, a contradiction. Thus z is outside the closed neighborhood and has x as its unique neighbor in N(v), by C4-freeness. Every L_i clause contains x. The two independent transversals selecting x force at least two other clauses, both omitting x if p_i=2, hence both containing z. For p_i≥3 the local charge is paid already.

Suppose some mixed double has p_i=2. Every independent selection of endpoints from the r A-owned internal edges and f that avoids x can be extended by z without creating an edge. All non-L_i factors are true on the extension, so E_i's cleanliness forces L_i to be false. All L_i clauses lie inside N(v), so z does not affect them. The r+1 distinct internal edges form an induced matching; at most half its 2^(r+1) orientations select x. Each L_i clause hits every one of these edges, so it can fail on at most one orientation, giving h_i≥2^r. Allocate every other shared product entirely to its external top, whose full leaf count pays all its edges. Replacing these products and L_i by atomic factors in A leaves at least one boundary slot d_A. The remaining allowance for A and this E_i is d_A+(h_i−1)+(p_i+1)=d_A+h_i+2. It covers 2r+4 since h_i≥2^r and r≥3. The b+w slots independently cover BW. This completes the case if any such deficient mixed external exists.

Otherwise pay every noninternal external from p_i+1 alone. Pool all remaining external batches (each internal) with A. Their union is an induced matching. With independent uniform endpoint orientations, each projected root rejects each orientation. A given primitive clause shared among some roots meets every edge assigned to those roots, so is false with probability at most 2 raised to the negative sum of their batch sizes. Weighting the root rejection for batch e by 2^e charges each distinct primitive leaf at most one in aggregate (the sum of the 2^e is at most their product when there are at least two positive batches). Thus 2^r+Σ2^e_i is at most the number of distinct primitive leaves, itself at most a+w+Σp_i. Zero batches are paid separately. If s≤2 then 2^r+b≥2r+2s (r≥3,b≥2), giving the desired bound. If s≥3, any W clause pins the common B center to v, so add B to the same matching pool. The primitive-leaf count in the enlarged union is at most a+b+w+Σp_i because all w guard occurrences are shared. Thus 2r+2s+Σ2e_i ≤ 2^r+2^s+Σ2^e_i ≤ a+b+w+Σp_i, within available cost.

This proves the desired cover charge for the one-sided leaking star at r≥3, independently of the number and sizes of its external batches. The comparison uses the ordinary centres and one endpoint per residual. It does not claim a result for two-sided leakage.
