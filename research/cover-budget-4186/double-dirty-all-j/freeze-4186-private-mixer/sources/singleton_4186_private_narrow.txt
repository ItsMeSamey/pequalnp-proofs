In the private two-dirty-arm setting, the profiles (3,3;1), (2,3;2) and (2,2;3) all have private budget seven. Their only excess case is four distinct assigned residuals, two per arm. Fix global disjoint ownership: unaffected q=0 roots are charged two per owned residual, allowing any one endpoint as its cover vertex.

If two marked residuals touch, choose their common endpoint for both, saving one vertex. Otherwise all four are disjoint. A cross edge xy between endpoints of distinct marked edges differs from all four. If it is star-covered, a chosen center of an ordinary star containing xy is x or y; choose that same endpoint for the corresponding marked residual. If xy is residual, it is owned by an unaffected root, so choose x for xy and also for the marked edge through x. Both cases save one in the cardinality of the union of the chosen cover vertices. In the latter case the saving is global, with no need to charge the external root below its budget. The remaining case is a globally induced four-edge matching, impossible since 16 orientations would need at least 16 distinct rejecting guard clauses or clause unions, whereas w+ab is respectively 10,8,7. All three profiles close even if an ambient cross edge is uncovered by all ordinary stars.
