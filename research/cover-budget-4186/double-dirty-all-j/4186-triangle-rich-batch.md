# Three projected roots with a rich batch

Use three pairwise-disjoint shared subtree blocks linking the three roots in a triangle. The two projected arms share w>=1 guard leaves; each shares k>=2 or l>=2 leaves with the external root. The three sets of private slots have sizes x,y,z>=0. Thus their clause counts are n1=w+k+x, n2=w+l+y, n3=k+l+z. The previously established gate accounting gives a combined allowance B=w+k+l+x+y+z+2, counting the shared dirty subtrees once. Assign distinct residuals exclusively; root i is responsible for an induced matching of r_i edges. Its 2^(r_i) independent transversals force at least that many slots when r_i>0.

Let M=max r_i. If M>=5 then B>=2^M+2>=6M, paying the edge and cover additions. If 3<=M<=4 and there is a positive deficit relative to two tokens per residual, then B<=23 since 2 sum r_i<=24. All six slot counts therefore sum to at most 21.

For each possible integer profile apply the two-root necessary conditions in bounds_4186_shared_defects.md separately to the three choices of two roots. These conditions use only the two selected batches, their total slot counts n_i,n_j and the number of their common occurrences; extra equalities of support among independently paid slots are allowed. A pair's cover saving extends to the full three roots by adding one endpoint per residual of the third. Thus the greatest of the separate guaranteed pairwise savings is valid, without requiring simultaneous compatible endpoint choices. An empty option set excludes that profile. When one batch is empty the positive-rich test on an empty internal batch has harmless one-slot overhead: the other shared block at the empty root provides at least one slot outside the selected pair's common block.

Each remaining 58 has a pair whose smallest admissible saving covers the deficit. No unresolved rich profile remains.
