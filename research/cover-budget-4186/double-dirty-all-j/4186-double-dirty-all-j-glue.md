# Double-dirty mixer with one exceptional OR, arbitrary gate counts

**Mathematical claim.** Under the original four positive hypotheses in L-meta-4186, suppose q=|orN minus lam|=1 and the exceptional OR M reads two dirty AND gates. For every j=|junk| and c=|centreless|, a vertex cover K satisfies |K|+u≤t+2c+j+1. Here u=|Ustar|, t counts the other tops. The argument concerns this slice of the original positive statement. The original least circuit supplies the gate budget throughout.

**Physical reduction.** The two AND wires give M empty raw OR-reach. An OR directly reading M would be dirty and have an empty-raw pin, forcing another exceptional OR. The same applies to any other OR directly reading a dirty AND. Thus before reaching a clean AND, a path after M traverses only ANDs. Factor each such dirty wire out of its affected first clean tops: substituting symbolic true removes every distinct direct AND reader, and one restoration AND suffices after aggregating the affected projected tops. A true cofactor at a clean top would make that top equal to the dirty factor. Leastness and liveness force a single distinct reader, and so there is one affected top, necessarily centreless. In particular c=0 is impossible under these premises. Ordinary tops have only pure raw OR operands, because a dirty AND or M at their pin has empty raw side, and neither can feed a raw OR other than M. Their cross products are stars by singleton rejection and four-cycle freeness, so one chosen center per ordinary top covers all ordinary contributions.

Write the affected top as (A∨B)W, with the two dirty arms A,B and the common conjunctive guard W. Acyclicity excludes M from either arm. The guard has at least one primitive clause; otherwise the top would equal dirty M. The arms cannot contain one another as AND factors, by absorption at M. If a dirty ancestor Z occurred in both, both arms would be products of Z. Every influenced clean top then factors Z: paths through M retain Z in both disjuncts, all others retain it along AND paths. Substitution and one restoration rule out branching toward both arms by leastness. A guard factor cannot escape to other clean tops, by the same original-circuit factor extraction. See 4186-two-dirty-private-guard.md and 4186-two-dirty-external-shares.md.

**Split and incidence.** Form projected roots P=AW, Q=BW. They are clean conjunctions on all inputs. The competitor realizing these roots costs at most one more than the least circuit: remove M and simplify its direct AND reader by substituting true to obtain W, then use two new ANDs and one aggregation OR. This gives one unit of size slack solely for proving structural restrictions. In the split AND regions, the one- and two-factor extraction, common-reader and comparable-branch cases in 4186-two-dirty-external-shares.md give incomparable branching dirty gates, at most two readers each, distinct two-root pairs and pairwise-intersecting reachable-root sets. Their upstream dirty trees are disjoint; shared ancestors before divergence would themselves branch.

Two remaining exclusions use **original** leastness. If a branch in an arm reaches only its arm boundary and no external root or guard, substitute true through its descendant AND region up to the arm boundary, restore the wire once at that boundary, and leave M unchanged. Two distinct direct readers would save a gate; an identically true projected arm would just require the original factor. The case of a branching wire equal to the arm output and having M as a direct reader requires an additional external use to be branching. If a branch reaches only external roots, it is outside both arms and guard: group precisely those external tops and restore the factor once in the original circuit to save a gate. This excludes both external–external pairs and external-only reconvergence. Duplicate identical pins can be bypassed in a least circuit. Analogous guard-only reconvergence is removed by pulling the factor at the guard boundary or by original global extraction through the guard. Hence the only external leak pairs are P–E or Q–E with E an unaffected centreless top. Repeated pairs are excluded by the split structural lemma. If both arms leak, their external endpoints coincide by pairwise intersection, with exactly one leak on each arm. The physical W region is always counted once, even when W is a single primitive clause without any branching gate.

**Counting convention.** Assign every residual edge to one accepting P,Q or external centreless root. Ordinary tops cannot accept residuals. In each projected pure conjunction the owned edges form an induced matching. A nonempty batch of h edges needs at least 2^h primitive clauses by independent transversal rejection. A root with no owned edges costs zero edges and zero cover additions; no orientation argument or probabilistic weight is needed for it. Primitive occurrence counts include repeated supports. Each remaining private external root with d dirty ancestors has at most d+2 primitive leaves, so all its owned edges and one freely selectable endpoint per edge are paid by its original d+2 budget. These regions are disjoint from the incidence component in question; their number is unrestricted. Original primitive budget for the merged pair is a+b+w. Each external root has its stand-alone primitive budget; subtract k−1 per shared dirty subtree with k leaves. This counts disjoint dirty gates once, the merged centreless top twice, and M once. Combining chosen vertices by union can only improve this bound.

**Exhaustion of components.**

1. No external leak. Apply freeze-4186-private-mixer/THEOREM.md, including singleton and empty batches and the tight private cases.
2. One external leak, with k≥2 shared leaves between P and E. Write x for P-arm private leaves, z≥1 for additional E leaves, b≥2 for Q-arm leaves and w≥1 for guard leaves. The local allowance is k+x+z+b+w+1. At k=2 use 4186-single-shared-z-all-sizes.md; for k≥3 use bounds_4186_shared_defects.md and its finite checker. The assigned counts can be zero.
3. At least two leaks confined to one arm. Write a,b,w for arm and guard sizes and z_i≥1 for the external private leaves. The local allowance is a+b+w+Σ(z_i+1). Use 4186-multi-leak-low-central-batch.md for central batch r≤2 (including zero), and 4186-multi-leak-high-central-batch.md for r≥3. Partner and external counts are arbitrary. Zero external batches require no clause weighting.
4. Both arms leak into one external E. This includes the external-centered two-edge star when there is no physical W branching AND. Treat W as a shared block of w≥1 occurrences regardless. Let x,y≥2 be the other shared blocks, and i,j′,k≥0 the three private counts. Combined allowance is x+y+w+i+j′+k+2. If all three batches are ≤2 use 4186-small-triangle.md and 4186-small-triangle.py, which explicitly include 0. If a batch is ≥3 use 4186-triangle-rich-batch.md and quantifiers_4186_triangle.py, with the two-batch filters in bounds_4186_shared_defects.py. Only one pairwise cover saving is used at a time. An empty partner costs zero and its other shared block supplies the harmless one-slot margin when the finite filter uses it.

Summing component charges, the external private portions and t ordinary centers yields the stated inequality. No general q=1 claim about a mixer with a nonempty pure operand is required.
