# A sole shared two-clause dirty AND

Use the private double-arm setting and notation of 4186-two-dirty-private-guard.md, except that one two-leaf dirty wire Z is shared between the projected A arm and an external centreless top Y. All other dirty wires are private; a,b,w count leaves in the unfolded private trees and y=d_Y+2 is Y's stand-alone allowance, with y≥3. Assign residual edges exclusively. Write r,s,h for the batches at AW,BW,Y. Their private total allowance is a+b+w+y; the actual allowance subtracts one for the shared gate.

The external conjunction accepts an induced matching of h assigned edges. For h>0 its 2^h rejecting transversals require that many distinct primitive clauses, so 2h≤2^h≤y. Since y≥3, equality 2h=y is possible only for h=2,y=4. Outside that case Y has at least one unit of slack (also for h=0), so the established private double-arm estimate proves the combined bound even after deducting the shared gate.

If h=2,y=4, every primitive clause of Y is exactly a pair consisting of one endpoint per assigned edge, by the unique-rejection and outsider-C4 argument. For the two Z clauses the supports are either disjoint or share a vertex v. The former makes all Z-satisfying graph edges lie within the four endpoints and hence only the assigned edges can occur. In the latter case every fresh Z-satisfying edge contains v. Thus r≤1 and when r=1 its edge can be jointly covered with a Y edge by choosing v. The simple endpoint accounting satisfies 2r≤2≤a and 2s≤b+w, the latter by the induced matching/transversal bound for BW (trivially for s=0). If r=0 the a allowance leaves at least two units unused; otherwise the vertex v saves the required one. This settles every external batch profile without imposing leastness on either projected circuit.

For clarity, the formerly remaining private profile (2,2;3) has a local cover of size two when r=s=2. The five primitive leaf occurrences of AW accommodate four distinct mandatory rejection patterns. At most two occurrences are not the unique falsifier of some transversal: if one pattern has multiplicity two these are the two in that pattern, otherwise there is at most one superfluous occurrence. Among three guard occurrences at least one is a unique falsifier. Its support is exactly its two designated endpoints by outsider-C4. This guard hits all four assigned edges, so its two vertices cover all of them.
