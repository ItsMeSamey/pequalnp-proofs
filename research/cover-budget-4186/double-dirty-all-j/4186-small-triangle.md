# Three pairwise shared conjunctions with batches of size at most two

Let X,Y,W have x,y,w primitive leaf occurrences, shared respectively by (AW,E), (BW,E), (AW,BW). Let i,j,k be remaining private occurrences at AW,BW,E respectively. We have x,y≥2, w≥1; all three private counts are nonnegative. Each root is a conjunction of anchored primitive clauses, so its assigned edges form an induced matching, and the three assigned batches are pairwise disjoint as edge sets. The uncoupled merged-root budget is its primitive leaf count, and the stand-alone E budget is its occurrence count. Subtract x−1 and y−1 for the two shared dirty trees. The resulting allowance is N+2 where N=x+y+w+i+j+k.

Let r=(r_A,r_B,r_E) have entries 0,1,2 and L=(x+w+i,y+w+j,x+y+k). An endpoint cover costs at most 2 sum r including edge charges. Nontrivial cases have N≤9. Necessary feasibility is 2^r_n≤L_n at each root (trivial for zero edges). When r_n=2, the four endpoint-choice assignments each require at least one distinct falsifying primitive occurrence. If L_n=4+e, there are at most 2e occurrences which do not uniquely falsify an endpoint choice. Indeed start with one occurrence for each of four classes; each additional occurrence can render at most two occurrences nonunique. Counts include superfluous slots. Each unique falsifier is literally a two-vertex transversal of the root's matching, by the outsider-C4 argument. Choosing that transversal as the local two-vertex cover also covers the entire batch at every root sharing that occurrence.

For m=1,2, compare the total number of shared occurrences at root n whose neighbor has at least m assigned edges against 2(L_n−4). If the total is larger, at least one of those slots is a unique falsifier. The above cover then saves at least m compared with separate endpoint bounds. A second feasibility test: a root with two edges and four slots has all slots exact. If two slots are shared with a neighboring two-edge root, their distinct two-element supports have at least three vertices in their union, forced to belong to both batches' endpoint sets. On any three such vertices inducedness forces a common edge in both batches, contrary to disjoint assignment.

The adjacent script exhausts N≤9. The cover saving leaves 11 count profiles. All have r=(2,2,2) and fail the second feasibility test. Thus the small triangle satisfies the desired budget throughout. This only uses graph structure at the three projections; it does not assume any projected circuit is least.
