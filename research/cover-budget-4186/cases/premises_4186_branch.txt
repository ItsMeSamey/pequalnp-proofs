Under the least-circuit output-cone reduction and TopForm, in the branch (u,j,c,q)=(2,1,1,0), let D be the unique dirty AND. Neither clean AND gates nor D itself can occur upstream of D. Its operands therefore compute disjunctions A,B of raw inputs. An OR directly reading D would be dirty (take a false-F witness activating D), and one operand would have empty OR-reachable input set, excluding it from lambda and contradicting q=0. Every direct AND reader of D is clean and centreless. It must be the unique centreless top H; both operands cannot equal D, which would make H dirty. The other operand of H is a pure OR S: any path there from D would first use H, forming a cycle, and a clean AND ancestor violates TopForm. Other tops compute ordinary two-clause cross products. Minimality places all gates in the output cone and the output is the union of tops, so both residual edges activate H=A AND B AND S.

If the two residual edges intersect, one vertex covers both. Otherwise, on their four endpoints there are four transversals picking one endpoint of each edge. Each of A,B,S meets each residual edge, hence can miss at most one transversal. Some transversal meets all three and consequently activates H. Its endpoints form a graph edge by cleanliness. This third edge is outside the two residuals, so is represented by a noncentreless star. In a C4-free graph the opposite sides of that star cannot both have at least two vertices, and cleanliness makes them disjoint. Choose its singleton-side vertex as centre; it belongs to the transversal. Taking one centre per noncentreless top thus covers all nonresidual edges and at least one residual edge. Add one endpoint of the other. In either case tau <= t+1 and tau+u <= t+3 = t+2c+j+q.
