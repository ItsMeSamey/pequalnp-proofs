For j=1,u=2, assume TopForm (other gate and graph hypotheses as in issued 4186). Always tau<=t+1. See 4186-last-branch.txt for definitions. Beneath each clean AND no other clean AND is an ancestor. Write sole junk J=(OR A) AND (OR B). A top reaching J in one operand computes (J OR (OR R_i)) AND (OR S_i); the other operand cannot also reach J since then the top would inherit a dirty witness. This representation permits R_i empty. All residual edges activate J and some S_i, since every accepted input activates a top and ordinary cross-products are exactly the star edges. The 3-clause term (OR A) AND (OR B) AND (OR of union of S_i) is below F, since whenever it is true one of the affected tops is true. Both residual pairs satisfy the three clauses. If they meet, add one vertex to centers of all ordinary stars. If disjoint, every clause hits both residual pairs and hence misses at most one of the four transversal endpoint pairs. Some transversal then satisfies all 3 clauses, making a graph edge. It is distinct from both residuals, so a star edge of an ordinary top. Choose a star center there, which must be one of its endpoints; this covers one residual edge. Add one vertex for the other, achieving tau<=t+1.
