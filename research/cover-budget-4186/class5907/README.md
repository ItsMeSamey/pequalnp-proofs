# Cover budget on the six-vertex graph of canonical edge mask 5907

Let G consist of triangles 015 and 234 joined by edge 02. For each globally minimum binary AND/OR circuit computing its edge function on the full Boolean cube and satisfying the issued TopForm predicate, the available cover budget Q − |U★| equals the minimum cover size. In the notation of 4183, this is Q=4+|U★|.

## Minimum size and the budget inequality

The graph has seven edges and requires four vertices in a cover because it contains two vertex-disjoint triangles. The cover {0,1,2,3} suffices. Four stars with centre and leaf sets 0 with {1,2,5}, 1 with {5}, 2 with {3,4}, and 3 with {4} compute the graph using three leaf ORs, four ANDs and three collector ORs. The [six-vertex census](prior/connected_n6.log) reached no circuit for this class through size nine. It used the [connected circuit enumerator](prior/connected_n.cpp), with the [prior independent replay](prior/replay.txt). All six vertices are essential, so no connected binary circuit of fewer than five gates is possible. The minimum size is ten.

Write u=|U★|. The issued 4183 inequality applied at size ten gives 7+Q ≤ 10+u+1, hence Q ≤ 4+u. To obtain equality it suffices to rule out strict failure of the other inequality. Let j count dirty AND gates, meaning gates that accept an input rejected by the graph. The prior [zero-junk argument](prior/4186-audit.txt) and [one-junk incidence analysis](prior/4186-one-junk-incidence.txt), including [overlapping](prior/4186-one-junk-overlap.md) and [disjoint](prior/4186-one-junk-disjoint.txt) supports, give 4+u ≤ Q when j ≤ 1. These are reviewed mathematical arguments. The remaining cases have j ≥ 2.

## Gate counts

In any such least circuit, choose an earliest internal gate in the ancestor cone of a dirty AND. It has only raw inputs. A clean AND cannot be an ancestor of this dirty AND by TopForm. An AND on two distinct nonadjacent raw vertices vanishes on every minimal positive assignment. Replacing it by false preserves all edge positives and can only decrease the output elsewhere. Simplifying the constant gives a smaller binary circuit for the same graph. Repeated raw pins are also redundant. Thus a binary OR on two distinct raw vertices can be placed first without changing the dependencies.

The [raw-first OR check](five-or5907-first-raw.cjs) excludes at most five total ORs even with arbitrary shared ANDs. Around the last live OR, write the output as K(A ∨ B) by flattening subsequent ANDs. Distribute to (KA) ∨ (KB). Each conjunction can use any available signal from the same bank of up to four preceding ORs. The raw-first ordering supplies five first-OR symmetry classes. Both terminal conjunctions are unrestricted and explicit omission or duplication handles spare gates. On the seven edges and eight maximal independent pairs the checker considers all 271 canonical two-OR banks and all subsequent choices. The two runs report 3,765 and 15,758 third-bank checks respectively, allowing overlap across runs, with zero covers. These 15 rows suffice by monotonicity because each singleton is in a nonedge pair and every triple contains an edge.

There are at least four ANDs independently of the OR count. In the Boolean dual, the eight minimal true sets are the complements of the eight nonedge pairs. Three OR gates yield at most eight expanded conjunctions, by choosing a single arm consistently for every use of each OR. Each choice must realize a different minimal true set. Every OR must therefore be active for every choice, since flipping an inactive selector would repeat the conjunction. For a fixed raw vertex, its absence is an intersection of selector conditions after flattening AND-only paths. The number of choices in that intersection is zero or a power of two. Vertex 1 is absent from exactly three minimal covers, those complementary to pairs 12, 13, and 14, a contradiction. The [selector checker](check5907-dual-three-or-choices.cjs) provides a finite cross-check.

Consequently a ten-gate circuit with dirty ANDs has exactly six total ORs and four ANDs. Let t be the number of clean AND tops. Then t+j=4, and t ≥ 1 because the edge function is nonzero. TopForm prevents a clean AND from feeding any AND. The output is the union of the clean tops. Minimality gives exactly t-1 collector ORs, since their union can be rebuilt using that many gates. Thus the number o of ORs in the dirty precursor bank is 6 − (t−1). The only triples (t,j,o) with j ≥ 2 are (1,3,6) and (2,2,5). The latter uses one further OR as its collector.

## The two finite checks

* For (1,3,6), the [dual checker](check5907-sole-three-dirty-dual.cjs) treats the three dirty ANDs as precursor ORs and the only top as the final OR. The last precursor misses a dual positive, so it can occur in at most one terminal conjunction. Expanding that conjunction gives three clean terms over the first two precursors. Among 141,057 precursor pairs, 11 survive this relaxation; they give 896 formal operand systems. Every system requires more than six binary conjunctions.
* For (2,2,5), the [corrected source, inputs, and regression tests](frozen-5907-t2j2o5-v2.zip) give 238 first-signal representatives, 346,898 ordered semantic banks, 176 viable banks and 1,708 concrete operand systems. Their shared-OR lower bounds are six for 39 systems, seven for 505, eight for 842, and nine for 322. A formal footprint on k source atoms requires at least k-1 binary OR ancestors even with reconvergence. Any other demanded footprint outside that footprint requires a separate gate. The smallest bound exceeds the five-OR budget. The earlier 77-bank list omitted valid submasks and is superseded. The corrected archive uses direct subset membership and tests every mask from 0 through 511.

These exclusions establish the equality for graph 5907. The [independent replay receipts](verification.md) describe each verified scope. The finite computations are external to Lean. The formal 4183 inequality, global minimum premise, and reviewed one-junk argument have separate provenance.
