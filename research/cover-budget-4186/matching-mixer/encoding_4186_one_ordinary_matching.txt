Full ambient-induced-matching class. Assume exact 4186 hypotheses with q=1, a unique dirty-AND-reading OR M=D OR P having nonempty pure raw operand P, and exactly one affected ordinary top. Its actual pure opposite operand wire H has a nonempty raw support, disjoint from P, with every P-H edge ordinary. Assign residuals to the virtual clean terms O=D H, V_i=D Q_i, W_i=Q_i S_i for each genuinely mixed centreless top, and just the raw pure conjunction for each unmixed centreless top. Q_i,S_i are conjunctions of raw primitive clauses; each S factor contains all P. D is a conjunction of pure clauses. All nonsingleton primitive supports are anchored by ordinary star edges: nontrivial pure and non-mixer mixed-root ORs below a top are Lambda, M projects to its pure P, and H is anchored by any p in P. The total number of distinct supports across the projections is <=B=j+2c+2 by the cut count (2(j+c) pins at dirty ANDs and centreless tops, at least j-1 used by dirty ANDs other than D, plus the synthetic ordinary guard). Each projected ordinary/star-cross term can be omitted from R.

Guard privacy. H is not a D clause since D would then imply H, hence would equal the clean term D H despite being dirty. Nor can H equal a mixed S factor because P is nonempty and disjoint from H. H cannot be a pure Q_i factor or an unmixed centreless primitive. A pure boundary wire with that support is the very same physical wire H by global leastness, since equivalent earlier wires may replace later ones. Trace all direct AND uses of H (retaining its OR uses) through ANDs to their first clean tops. This AND-only region cannot reach D, or D would imply H throughout that path and hence be clean. No other dirty AND can enter an OR, so there is no OR exit to restore before clean tops. Substitute symbolic true for H in the traced direct AND positions, processing affected ANDs in order. Each direct reader disappears while affected tops equal H times their projected versions. No such top projects to constant true, as then it equals dirty H (every nonempty raw disjunction is true on a singleton). Regroup the original clean OR aggregation and reinsert H once around the affected top projections. Two distinct direct AND readers would thus strictly shorten the circuit. The one affected ordinary top is already a direct AND reader, excluding H from any centreless pure boundary AND. This excludes primitive equality only, not use within other raw ORs.

Suppose all u residual edges form an induced matching in the ambient graph. Select one endpoint per edge independently and uniformly, set all other inputs false. This assignment is always graph-negative. For each projected root with r>=1 assigned edges, it falsifies at least one clause. Weight its rejection by 2^r. A primitive support common to roots with assigned counts r_1,...,r_k hits every one of their distinct matching edges: its probability of being missed is at most 2^(-sum r_i), irrespective of any extra vertices. Summing charge over these roots therefore costs at most one per genuinely distinct support, since sum 2^r_i <= product 2^r_i when k>=2. For k=1 the cap is also one. In particular 2u<=sum 2^r<=number of active supports<=B. The desired allowance for u plus cover additions is B-1. If inequality is strict then one endpoint per edge suffices.

If 2u=B, equality throughout forces each positive root to have r=1 or 2, all counted supports to be used, and each to supply its full unit. Every shared active support must occur at exactly two roots, both with r=1, paying 1/2 at each; every exclusive support pays one at its only root. Equality also forces each root to receive exactly 2^r units, since its received weight is at least its obligation and all global inequalities are equalities.

If O inactive its private H is absent from the active pool, which has size <=B-1. If H singleton and O active choose H as the centre of its ordinary star, thereby already covering one O edge and saving the required unit. If r_O=2, no O support can be shared at equality, so O has exactly four distinct clauses. Four-clause rigidity makes H exactly the two mates of a transversal of the O edges. Indeed each of the four independent orientations of the two edges misses a unique clause containing its opposite mates. An additional vertex in that clause would activate the term together with the rejected orientation and so neighbor one chosen endpoint; the clause anchor and that endpoint then share both the extra vertex and the endpoint's omitted mate, forcing the residual edge to be ordinary. Choose H's two endpoints as the O cover; they cover the full ordinary star at this top, so omit that centre and save one.

Finally r_O=1 and H nonsingleton. H is exclusive and pays one of the two required charge units. D needs at least two distinct pure factors, otherwise the dirty gate D equals an earlier raw input/OR wire, violating leastness. Every D support contributes either one unit or half a unit to O at equality. There must consequently be exactly two distinct D supports, each shared with another singleton root. A clean product of three anchored clauses accepting a residual edge must have a singleton factor: one endpoint hits at least two of the three, cannot hit all by singleton rejection, and is adjacent to all of the remaining clause by cleanliness. A nonsingleton remaining clause would force its anchor to equal the endpoint, making the marked edge ordinary. Since H is nonsingleton, one of the shared D supports is a singleton. Its vertex would be present on the assigned edge of both O and its sharing root, impossible in a matching. This completes the stated class for arbitrary j,c.

Regardless of inducedness, every vertex has residual degree at most c+1. At x with D(x)=false there is at most one edge from O and the V_i together, because two residuals at x satisfying D force every D clause to contain x (the two mates cannot occupy the same anchored clause). Each W_i contributes at most one. At x with D(x)=true, the two colors V_i,W_i cannot both have x incidences for one arm: the same argument forces Q_i(x)=true, violating cleanliness of V_i on a singleton. Thus c arms plus O give the upper bound. A simultaneous V_i/W_i incidence requires x adjacent to every p in P since Q_i(x)=true and every S_i contains P. No conclusion about all non-induced residuals follows solely from this degree bound.

Extension to arbitrary number of affected ordinary tops (r09 observation). Define H as the union of all their pure guards. It satisfies the same anchor and star-edge properties. The only use of physical guard privacy above is to ensure H is exclusive when 2u=B. There are <=B-1 distinct actual cut supports before adding H, so equality itself forces H to differ from every cut support and be used by O. This replacement deletes the factor-and-restore lemma from the proof and eliminates the assumption that the affected ordinary top is unique. If H is singleton, choose it as centre of any affected ordinary top; if H consists of two O residual endpoints, selecting them covers all star edges of any affected ordinary top, saving its one allocated centre. If there is no affected ordinary top, H need not be added at all and the bound follows immediately. The degree bound likewise uses only the aggregate clean term. Consequently the ambient-induced-matching theorem holds for arbitrary top count in the nonempty-raw-mixer slice.
