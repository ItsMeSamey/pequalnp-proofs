At q=0 and arbitrary j, assign vertex-disjoint residual edges exclusively to witnessing centreless tops. Each root H owning r_H>=2 sees an induced matching, even though edges owned by different roots need not induce a matching together. Each primitive clause contains distinct endpoints of its >=2 owned edges, so its lambda star center is unique and its support is in that open neighborhood.

For every orientation selecting one endpoint per owned edge of H, the selected transversal is independent, hence misses a clause. All omitted mates belong to that clause and have a unique common neighbor s by C4 (there are >=2 distinct mates). Group orientations by s. Let k_Hs count owned edges entirely within N(s). Each other owned edge has one forced mate h in N(s) and outside endpoint y. None of these y equals s: a rejecting clause for this eligible s contains h and its lambda witnessing star has center s, making sh a star edge. For each of the 2^k_Hs choices of omitted mates on the internal edges all mates lie in N(s), forcing the orientation's unique center to be s. Therefore sum_s 2^k_Hs=2^r_H.

For one s choose a random independent set I in N(s): choose one endpoint per edge in G[N(s)] uniformly, and independent fair coins on isolates. C4 implies G[N(s)] has degree <=1. The forced mates of H are mutually nonadjacent by the owned induced matching, so I omits all of them with probability 2^{-(r_H-k_Hs)}. On this event add the outside endpoints y to I. These y are mutually independent, are distinct from s as above, and have only one neighbor h in N(s) (common-neighbor uniqueness). Thus the augmented assignment is independent and H false. Any missed clause contains the omitted mates of the transversal inside this assignment (one from each owned edge); they all lie in N(s), so its lambda center is s. Hence I already misses a leaf of center s belonging to H.

Weight by 2^r_H, giving expected obligation at least 2^k_Hs. Any distinct primitive clause L with center s seen by roots T contains at least one vertex of each of their globally vertex-disjoint assigned residual edges. These are distinct vertices in N(s). If L contains both endpoints of an edge there, its miss probability is zero; otherwise the vertices yield distinct independent fair coins, and P(L missed)<=2^{-(sum_{H in T} r_H)}. As sum 2^r_H<=2^{sum r_H}, L supplies expected weighted charge at most 1. Sum across centers and roots: #distinct primitive leaf wires >= sum_H 2^r_H. Binary DAG connectivity gives leaf count <= #distinct dirty AND ancestors + twice the number of chosen roots. Roots assigned one residual pay their own two top credits, omitted roots need none. Hence if Ustar is a matching, 2u<=j+2c and no defect. More generally this accounts for any selected residual matching and arbitrary sharing of ancestor ANDs.
