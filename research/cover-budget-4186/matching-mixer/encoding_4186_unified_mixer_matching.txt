Theorem (mathematical draft). Under the four positive hypotheses internal to 4186, suppose the unique excluded OR is a mixer M=D OR P with nonempty pure raw support P and a dirty AND operand D. Assume all residual edges U form a matching, with arbitrary ambient crosses. Then tau(G)+|U|<=t+2c+j+1. No bound on the number of affected ordinary tops or on j,c is imposed.

Structure and budget. The pure AND cone of D has d>=1 dirty gates and at most a=d+1 distinct raw primitive supports. Every raw support below a clean top is singleton or ordinarily star-anchored, including mixed raw projections; the nonempty support P is included in each mixed boundary support. Use one accounting slot per distinct D primitive and leave the unused portion of a blank. A dirty gate outside the D cone has no OR exit. Factoring its AND-only descendant uses proves it has at most one distinct direct AND reader (remove all direct readers and restore once around the aggregated projected clean tops). Thus outside-cone dirty regions of centreless tops are disjoint. At an actual centreless top i having n_i such outside dirty gates, its private credits n_i+2 dominate its distinct boundary slots: collapse the region to at most n_i+2 leaves, counting a pure raw pin, a mixed boundary D OR S, or an escaped D-ancestral dirty wire Z as one slot each. For each proxy Z use its original D-cone slots when expanding its pure clauses, not the proxy slot; the latter can remain blank. Count private boundary occurrences at distinct actual roots separately even if their raw supports agree. The same primitive repeated within the same root need only contribute once to a given slot. The total capacity including one synthetic slot H for the union of ordinary guards is B=(d+1)+sum_i(n_i+2)+1<=j+2c+2. There is no need to remove H's slot if ordinary corrections are absent.

Each mixed centreless arm has the clean terms V_i=D Q_i and W_i=Q_i times all mixed raw S factors, with Q_i the conjunction of pure pins and expanded proxy factors. Its physical private input-count parameters are k_i ordinary pure slots, m_i>=1 mixed slots, l_i proxy slots. There must be k_i>=1: D implies all proxies and all inputs D OR S, so lacking a pure factor a positive dirty witness for D would activate the clean top. At least one outside dirty gate exists for each genuinely mixed arm: if all its dirty ancestors were D ancestors, the top's only possible mixed operand would be a pure-reaching operand at the top itself, with opposite empty operand an ancestor of D, again propagating D. Thus n_i+2>=3. H is ordinarily anchored by any p in P, as P times every pure guard is an ordinary star. The clean ordinary correction is O=D H. Assign each residual exclusively to an activating projected root. Unmixed arms contribute only one pure conjunction.

Rich-root ledger. A rich root with r>=2 owned edges imposes obligation 2^r. For a distinct support L of that root the standard local-centre proof gives charge at most 2^r times w(L), where w(L)=0 if L contains an ambient edge and otherwise 2^{-|L|}; the charges sum to at least 2^r. This uses only that the owned edges at this root are induced, as holds for each clean conjunction of anchored clauses. Across roots the residual matching ensures distinct assigned endpoints even if there are ambient crosses. At each physical slot, sum over the different rich users of the represented support, once per root per slot. The combined charge is <=1 since L hits all their disjoint marked edges and sum_r 2^r<=2^(sum r). If L additionally hits b distinct uncharged singleton-batch edges, the cap is 2^{-b}. One can charge repeated copies in different private slots without hurting these upper bounds; every rich root has at least one slot for every distinct raw clause. The D slots include the original subfactors of its proxies and are shared by all relevant arms. Private slots only serve their own arm; the synthetic H slot serves O.

Elementary obligations. Unmixed or mixed arms with only one nonempty singleton batch use two private centreless credits and need no stochastic charge. Arms with only rich batches receive >=2r per batch. If a singleton has a sibling of size r>=3, use that sibling's existing surplus 2^r-2r>=2; do not run a lottery for the singleton. Arms with neither batch assigned cost nothing. For ordinary O of size >=2 use the standard rich charge on D and H. If O is a singleton, spend one synthetic H unit and request one D reserve unit; denote this case by o=1, otherwise o=0. Do not use any cover savings at this stage.

The 1+2 obligations (bounds_4186_one_two_arms.md). When V owns two edges and W one, each private pure slot hits all three and spends at most half in V's rich charge. No mixed slot or proxy slot is needed by V. At least m_i+l_i+k_i/2 units (plus blank allowance) remain privately. This is >=2 if l_i>=1; if l_i=0, m_i>=1 and total allowance >=3 give the same bound. Thus pay the entire singleton.

When W owns two edges and V one, first consider l_i=0. Each K clause (pure slot) meets all three edges. In a clean term accepting two residuals, the four endpoint transversals are independent and each distinct clause rejects at most one. A unique rejecting clause for one transversal must consist exactly of its two omitted mates: any extra z added to the rejected transversal activates the term, forcing adjacency z-x to a selected endpoint x; both z and x's omitted mate then neighbor the clause anchor and x, forcing the residual mate edge to be an ordinary star. Therefore no K clause can be a unique rejector. Four or five distinct W clauses would require respectively four or at least three unique rejectors. All those would be S clauses and hence contain the same p, impossible for three different two-vertex transversal mate pairs. Thus the private budget contains at least six slots and pays both batches directly. Omit this rich W from the global ledger. This argument also excludes fewer than four slots.

If l_i>=1 and neither W edge contains p, each S and K clause has at least three vertices, all shares in the private rich charge are <=1/2. Reserve at least l_i+(k_i+m_i)/2>=2. If a W edge contains p there is at most one such arm globally. The l_i+k_i/2 private reserve suffices except l_i=k_i=1. In the latter case at least 1.5 remains. If D activates the p edge, give that edge to V instead: it already activates Q_i, so V then owns two, W one and the earlier fully private payment applies. Otherwise some D clause misses both p-edge endpoints. It is not needed by this rich W, whose clauses all hit that edge. It does hit the singleton V edge. Its residual reserve of at least 1/2 pays the shortfall. Let e=1 if this prescribed D half is needed, zero otherwise. This exceptional arm is unique; its W owns the unique matching edge incident to the fixed p in P. Perform this reassignment before the final rich ledger.

The 1+1 obligations. If n_i>=2 pay all four units privately. Otherwise n_i=1 and the true two-AND private arm has at most three boundary slots. Give it its three private units and request one from D. Let k count these requests, irrespective of where ordinary centres lie. If there is no proxy, W uses at most three raw clauses and accepts a residual, hence has a singleton raw clause: an endpoint hitting two of three can activate the term with every element of the remaining clause; if the latter had >=2 elements, C4 forces its ordinary anchor to be this endpoint and makes the pair ordinary. Repeating clauses handles fewer than three. No Q clause can be singleton as it hits BOTH disjoint assigned edges at V and W. Thus a mixed S clause is singleton, P={p}, and this W edge owns p. At most one of the k arms is proxy-free, and none is proxy-free if e=1. An arm with a proxy cannot use D itself as a pure boundary: all its mixed factors D OR S are then redundant in the product. Set all such boundary factors symbolically true and simplify the two private ANDs; at least one gate disappears, the output is unchanged, and no removed private dirty gate has an exterior consumer. This contradicts leastness. Each proxy-using arm therefore uses a proper dirty ancestor of D.

Exit-head justification even with reconvergence. A fixed proper gate Z in the D cone can feed at most one distinct outside-cone direct AND reader. Freeze all D-cone wires and the mixer (as well as any later mixed OR values). On the AND-only paths starting with Z's direct pins at outside-cone readers, substitute true in those pins, while leaving unrelated uses of Z and frozen inputs intact. Any affected original wire equals Z times its new version; all its pre-top readers are inside these AND paths because no such dirty outside wire exits to OR or re-enters the D cone. At each first clean top the projected output cannot be identically true since Z is dirty. Two distinct direct AND readers disappear, and regrouping affected top projections then restoring their common factor Z costs one AND and no added aggregation OR. Hence two such readers are impossible. A single outside direct dirty reader cannot reach two centreless roots, by uniqueness of its succeeding outside AND readers. Thus distinct proxy-using arms among the k require distinct proper D gates, even if the input cones of those gates overlap. Including the final gate D itself gives d>=k when e=0 and d>=k+1 when e=1. Always a=d+1>=max(2,k+1+e).

Pooled D reserve and integrality. Every D primitive meets all k singleton V edges from the short 1+1 arms, the O edge if o=1, and the exceptional V edge if e=1, even if the rich root using it is elsewhere. All k+o+e edges are distinct and excluded from the rich lottery. Thus each of the a slots spends at most 2^{-k-o-e} on rich roots (zero on a blank slot). The reserve in this pool is at least a(1-2^{-k-o-e}). Requests are k+o+e/2. If e=0 then a>=max(2,k+1)>=k+o, and the reserve exceeds k+o-1: for t=k+o>=1 use t/2^t<1 and if t=0 there is no request. Hence total obligations 2u are strictly less than capacity B+1, so 2u<=B by integrality. If e=1, set t=k+o+1>=1, a>=k+2. For o=1, this yields t<=a and t/2^t<=1/2 (t>=2), giving reserve >=t-1/2=k+o+1/2. For o=0 the same inequality with a>=t+1 is at least as strong. For k=1,o=1 the reserve is at least 3(1-1/8)=2.625, exceeding the 2.5 requests. The prescribed exceptional D clause has its own >=1/2 available, so reserve it first; the other requests can use any remaining D capacity. There is no circular transfer of fractional charges. The pooled calculation therefore gives 2u<=j+2c+2 for every matching.

If the marked graph has any ambient cross, that edge is nonresidual and every ordinary star cover covers at least one of its marked endpoints. Choose one centre per ordinary top and then one endpoint for every uncovered residual. The size plus u is at most t+2u-1<=t+j+2c+1. No earlier step has spent that cover gain. With no ambient cross the reviewed induced-matching saturation argument in encoding_4186_one_ordinary_matching.txt closes the possible last unit. This is an argument only for the stated branch; it does not establish the official negation or global leastness of any example.
