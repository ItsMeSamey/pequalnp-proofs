Theorem. Under the exact hypotheses inside the negation in L-meta-4186, the positive cover inequality holds for q=|(orN minus lam)|=0, for every j=|junk|, c=|centreless|, and graph order. No assumption about overlap of residuals from different tops is used.

Every gate of a least circuit reaches the output, by deleting a dead gate and renumbering. The output computes the nonempty simple graph's nonconstant function, so is a genuine gate. All below-F ORs have only below-F operands; no raw projection is below F by singleton rejection. Expanding the output through such ORs gives an OR of clean ANDs (tops). Conversely, no top has an AND descendant by TopForm. Every downstream path from a top thus consists of ORs to the clean output and stays below F. So the clean aggregation above m tops is purely OR, with at least m-1 ORs by binary connected-DAG counting. It can be rebuilt at cost m-1 in any desired grouping.

At q=0 no OR directly reads a dirty AND g: it would inherit g's false-F positive witness, and its pin reading an AND has empty orIn, disqualifying it from lambda. Consequently along any path starting at g, up to its first clean AND, only AND gates occur. The first clean AND is necessarily centreless, since it directly reads a dirty AND wire. A pure OR region before these ANDs is purely a raw disjunction, since a clean AND ancestor of another AND is forbidden. Its nontrivial root belongs to lambda by q=0, being outside F on a singleton. Its entire raw support lies on the leaf side of some star with opposite singleton center v, hence v is adjacent *by star edges* to every such support vertex.

Distinct-node lemma. Let g be any dirty AND. Let d be the number of distinct gates directly reading its output. They are all ANDs. Collect its descendant ANDs through its first clean ANDs inclusive, and let T be the nonempty set of clean tops so reached. For each of these affected AND gates compute a projected version obtained by substituting symbolic true for the wire g throughout this descendant AND region. Inputs from outside continue to denote their original wires; no affected dirty wire can have a consumer outside this region before a clean top, because that consumer would be an AND and thus would belong to the region. Keep g and all its own ancestors for use below. Process the affected AND gates in their original order. Simplify AND(true,w) to w; every direct reader of g is consequently deleted. If both projected pins are symbolic true, propagate that marker without generating a physical constant. Other gates require at most one binary AND each. Any affected top H projected to symbolic true would have computed exactly g originally, impossible because H is below F and g is not. All resulting projected top wires H' therefore exist in the exact earlier-wire binary AO model.

Induction on the affected region, or factoring g out of an AND of its primitive inputs, gives on the full cube H=g AND H' at each H in T. Unaffected tops remain unchanged. Replace the old clean OR aggregation by first combining all |T| projected outputs with |T|-1 ORs, then AND the result with the preserved g, then combine that result with all m-|T| unaffected tops with m-|T| ORs. This uses m-1 ORs and one extra AND. It computes precisely the old function by distributivity, including when T contains a single top or all tops. A topological ordering followed by wire-index renumbering implements the construction with valid exact binary gates. This competitor need not be TopForm. If d>=2 its size is strictly smaller (at least d ANDs deleted, one added), contradicting IsLeastAO. Since nothing is dead, every dirty AND has exactly one distinct reader. In particular the dirty ancestor sets of distinct centreless tops are disjoint: following any dirty wire forward to its first clean top yields a unique result. Repeated pins produce no extra branching.

Local residual accounting. A centreless H with d_H distinct dirty ancestors is the conjunction of at most d_H+2 primitive raw clauses: its connected binary AND DAG has d_H+1 internal gates including H and at most one more leaf than internal gates. Its accepted residual edges form an induced matching. To see this, two such edges xy,xz would require some clause missing x (singleton rejection) to contain both y,z. Its lambda center and x share both neighbors, forcing the center to be x by C4, so both edges would already be star edges. For disjoint accepted residuals xx',yy' with xy an ambient graph edge, no clause can contain x' and y: the lambda center shares x',y with x, implying xx' is a star edge. Similarly no clause contains x and y'. Each clause hits both residuals, and thus either contains x,y or x',y'. Consequently both xy' and x'y activate H, giving a four-cycle together with xx',yy'. Such a cross edge is impossible.

For r_H>=1 residual edges accepted by H, the 2^{r_H} transversals choosing one endpoint per edge are independent and H rejects each. Each clause can be missed by at most one transversal because it hits every residual edge. Thus 2r_H<=2^{r_H}<=d_H+2; r_H=0 needs no charge. Every residual edge activates a centreless top, since under q=0 noncentreless tops have purely raw OR sides and all their pair activations are star edges. By the distinct-node lemma, summing yields 2u<=2 sum_H r_H<=j+2c without counting any dirty gate twice. Choose one singleton-side center for each of the t noncentreless stars (C4-freeness and singleton rejection), then one endpoint per residual. The resulting cover has size <=t+u, giving cover size + u<=t+2u<=t+j+2c as required. This is a mathematical exclusion of one parameter slice, not a Lean proof of the issued negation.
