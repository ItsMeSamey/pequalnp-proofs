# Private costs for a single nonempty mixer

Assume sole charged AND-reading OR M=D OR P, with P nonempty and pure, and that all d dirty ANDs in the backward AND cone of D have no outside consumer except M reading D. By the dirty-fanout lemma, every dirty AND outside that cone has exactly one reader, which is an AND. These remaining ANDs form disjoint ancestor regions at centreless tops. With n_T dirty ANDs at top T its AND-only conjunction has at most n_T+2 boundary pins, and their aggregate over all T is at most j−d+2c. Mixed boundary pins compute D OR S with P contained in S, and nontrivial raw supports are Lambda-anchored. Pure boundary pins are ordinary anchored clauses.

First consider raw projections at every centreless top: replace every mixed pin D OR S by S. The resulting clean conjunction on each top accepts an induced matching of residual edges. For r>0 the 2^r independent transversals require at least 2^r distinct clauses. Thus the union of these raw-projection residuals costs at most j−d+2c in residual count plus extra cover vertices (one endpoint per edge).

For a mixed centreless top, write K_T for the conjunction of its pure pins, and H_T for the set of vertices activating K_T on their own. K_T cannot be empty: otherwise the top would propagate an independent positive witness of D. Define H as the union of the H_T and all ordinary affected raw guards. D AND H is clean. Fix p in P. Each h in H differs from p by singleton rejection, and p-h is a graph edge: it is an ordinary star edge for ordinary guards, or it activates the raw projection of the corresponding centreless top, since p activates all mixed raw supports. Let E be the residuals outside every raw projection. Then none of these p-h edges belongs to E. The proofs that anchored clean conjunctions accept an induced matching and require 2^r clauses only use, for the assigned edges, that an anchor is adjacent to every clause vertex and the resulting anchor edges are not assigned. Hence the virtual H is an adequate anchor relative to E. If every e in E has an endpoint in H and activates D, the single conjunction D AND H accounts for all of E with at most d+2 clauses. For d=1 or d≥3 the resulting cost 2r≤d+1 fits after paying the raw projections. In particular this applies if every mixed centreless top has at most one distinct pure boundary factor. For d=2 the same proof suffices if r≤1 or the raw-side accounting saves one unit.

There is a second bound without the singleton-guard condition. Fix p in P and let W be the complement of the closed graph neighborhood N[p]. Every independent assignment in W remains independent after adjoining p. On all these extended assignments every mixed boundary pin is true. For each centreless top use its conjunction of pure pins, discarding clauses containing p and restricting the remaining supports to W. This conjunction, also valid for a pure top, is clean on the induced graph: on any independent support in W it agrees with the original top on the extended independent assignment. Every edge originally accepted by this top and contained in W is accepted by the restricted conjunction, by monotonicity. If the conjunction has an empty clause it accepts no such edge; the empty conjunction cannot be true on the extended empty support by singleton rejection. Retained supports still have their original anchors for the local induced-matching proof, which needs adjacency and star membership in the ambient graph but only tests assignments in W. Every residual in W is accepted by some centreless top, since any ordinary affected guard lies in N(p). Consequently the number u_out of residuals in W satisfies 2u_out≤j−d+2c by the same sum over private pins.

Let b be the number of residuals meeting N[p] and let tau_near be the minimum cover of just those edges. Combining t ordinary centres, one endpoint per edge in W, and a cover of the near edges gives the target whenever b+tau_near≤d+1. One may select the best p in P or use the exact per-root cofactor clause counts to gain further savings. These are sufficient conditions; neither unrestricted leakage from D's cone nor unrestricted residual placement is covered.
