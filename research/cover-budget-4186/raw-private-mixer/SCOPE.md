# Arbitrary-size private dirty cone with a nonsingleton raw mixer partner

Under the four hypotheses of the positive statement inside L-meta-4186, suppose q=1 and the sole exceptional OR is M = D OR P, where D is a dirty AND and the other operand is a pure raw disjunction with at least two distinct input vertices. Require at least one affected ordinary top. Require that no dirty AND in D's upstream cone has any consumer outside that cone except D's use by M. Allow arbitrary j,c and arbitrary residual placement. Then a vertex cover has size at most t+2c+j+1−u (in integers). Here t counts ordinary tops, c centreless tops, j dirty ANDs and u the uncovered edges in Ustar.

Every affected ordinary top has singleton guard a: its mixed operand contains the entire raw set P, forcing the opposite pure operand to be singleton by C4-freeness; two guards would share every neighbor in P. Choose a as the ordinary center. Write S* for the union of the raw supports on the mixed sides of all affected ordinary tops. Every post-M mixed OR is dirty and belongs to Lambda, so some ordinary top operand OR-reaches it. That operand is the mixed side; both sides of the witness are nonempty. Hence each mixed pin D OR S_i has P contained in S_i contained in S* contained in N(a). The input M itself has raw support P. Every a-to-S* edge is star-covered, and aD is clean on the full cube. The proof only uses these inclusions and implications; S* need not name a gate or a largest individual support.

For |P|≥3 use proofs/4186-common-guard-large-support.md. For |P|=2 use proofs/singleton_4186_common_guard_two.md, which supplies the missed boundary case in the first proof. The private-region counting and projection conventions are also given in proofs/4186-one-mixer-private-cofactors.md. In the latter file the more general sufficient conditions alone are not the claimed theorem.

The result excludes this specified slice of the negated universal statement. It provides neither a counterexample nor a resolution for dirty leakage, singleton P, absent affected ordinary tops, or unrestricted q. This is a mathematical proof bundle without a Lean certificate.
