# Empty raw S, clean sole x-AND, three positive direct receivers

Assume the original G11 least size-21 TopForm hypotheses, j≥3, q=3, x=3 has sole reader A=x∧S clean, raw(S) empty, and all three exceptional pretop ORs have positive raw support and a direct dirty AND pin. We prove U≤2c+j+3. Write y=6,z=7 and H=G11−{x,y,z}; c≥1 since A is centreless. All labels, guard witnesses and physical receiver counts are original.

## Original interface

There is no raw-empty pretop OR: the earliest such OR would read dirty ANDs and be a paid empty receiver. Raw S is empty, so S is a dirty AND (a clean ancestor of the later AND A violates TopForm). It is positive on both singleton y,z; S restricted to H is clean by adjoining x to independent H inputs. Any pretop OR with both singleton positives is paid: if it OR-reached an ordinary top, its opposite raw leaf would have to be adjacent to y,z, hence x, which can reach only the centreless A. These arguments use the actual full-cube singleton values, not raw support. The tight-card interface in 6648-three-direct-clean-empty-s-index.md, section Exact card hypotheses and S_H, applies without the at-least-one-empty premise: a common y∨z is a paid pure OR, impossible here too; x=false preserves S; the remaining 6,7 reader deletion leaves the least 15-gate H core and keeps S as a clean nonconstant physical wire. An extra original AND reader of S would survive there and could be deleted by bypassing it to clean S_H. Hence S has an actual external OR reader F, not merely an AND path to F. Its raw-empty input is precisely S, so F=S∨R3 with raw-positive other arm R3.

The AND frontier of S consists exclusively of bivalent paid ORs. At least two incomparable effective frontier wires are necessary, since otherwise S duplicates an earlier OR. With F strictly downstream, exactly two causal receivers E1,E2 occur, and S=E1∧E2 on the full cube. Repeating a factor cannot alter this equality. All three receivers now inherit both singleton positives, so none can OR-reach an ordinary top or a Lambda gate. Every ordinary top therefore provides only original stars, and every Lambda support used as a primitive is pure. This is the original guard justification for all projections below.

## Three or more dirty ANDs with a shared causal donor

Suppose E1=K∨R1 and E2=K∨R2 share the same physical dirty AND. The R_i are pure positive raw supports: a dirt-carrying OR-only path into either raw arm must inherit one of the other two receivers, impossible from later F; an OR-only route from the other E would give absorption and contradict their incomparable frontier roles. A dirt-containing path with an intermediate AND and a further OR must pass a charged gate already included in this argument. The pre-E1,E2 donor K itself is a conjunction of ordinary pure clauses, as it precedes both. R_i as primitive wires are literals or originally Lambda-anchored. The identity S=K∨(R1∧R2) is pointwise, and K≤S. Thus K and R1∧R2 are two H-clean anchored batches containing all S-positive H residuals. This uses actual wires in the original circuit regardless of other fanout.

On the remaining H pairs, globally set both S and K false. Monotonicity ensures the resulting terms are clean; evaluations agree on those pairs because K≤S. E1,E2 reduce to R1,R2 everywhere and F reduces to its other arm, which likewise becomes its original raw support. R3 itself cannot be a bivalent causal wire or OR-inherit one (as S≤Ei would make F redundant), but even with shared OR paths the global substitution keeps the raw support of each surviving wire. The positive arm at an exceptional gate is a literal or an original pure Lambda support, and no new unanchored guard is invented by pooling different exits. Direct uses of suppressed dirty wires only reduce terms. Each of the c−1 original centreless roots besides A gives at most one additional conjunction of anchored raw clauses. Ordinary tops supply no residuals. Counting exclusively gives at most c+1 H batches, at most five near edges, and at most one extra edge across all batches when U≥8 by the no-x-star anchored-footprint lemma (6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md §1). Thus U≤c+7≤2c+j+3. Smaller U is automatic. This handles shared K even at j=3 regardless of any spare dirty wire downstream of F.

## Distinct causal donors

Each E_i has an original dirty AND donor K_i accepting at least one of y,z: its raw-facing pin cannot itself be bivalent since a bivalent noninput would be the other earlier receiver, causing absorption. Assume j=3. The dirty ANDs K1,K2,S are distinct (S follows both); all are earlier than F. F cannot feed an OR before the next clean top, since it is bivalent and all three paid gates are already used. Its next AND must be clean. Its opposite pin must reject both singleton y,z. If it has a raw leaf w, cleanliness at that top forces w adjacent to both, so w=x, unavailable since A is x's only reader and precedes any such top. If it has empty raw reach, it must be a dirty AND (no raw-empty pretop OR, no clean AND ancestors by TopForm). The only options K1,K2,S all accept at least one forbidden singleton. Hence F has no possible live route and j=3 is impossible.

At j≥4 the previous 6648-three-direct-clean-empty-s-dirty-and.md section j≥4 accounting applies verbatim; none of its counting hypotheses demands a raw-empty exit once all three receivers are bivalent. For c=1 only A can add residuals, at most its two x edges. For c=2 one other centreless root has ≤8 anchored batches, from the three positive direct donor flags in causal order. In each raw branch substitute its physical projected raw-facing pin at that mixer; in a true branch retain the projected actual donor and set that mixer true. The root-wide substitutions are global and guards incorporate all earlier choices, so every term implies the original clean root. A true upstream flag removes satisfied OR clauses; in all-raw portions every Lambda remains within the same original anchor. Each positive raw-facing pin is physically pure by bivalence (a causal predecessor carrying both singletons would make the receiving bivalent OR redundant only when S≤that predecessor, or incomparable at the two causal exits); in particular R3 cannot inherit an Ei since S≤Ei. Thus the surviving primitive disjunctions are originally anchored/literal. If U≥12 then at most four original stars, permitting only one global homogeneous double 14,59. This yields U≤2+8+1≤2c+j+3. For c≥3 a failure would force at least 14 residuals, hence ≤2 ordinary stars. Pure ordinary tops each require a private star by leastness, so t≤2; pure Lambda cannot compute a duplicate or singleton function and there is at most one nonsingleton anchored support over two star edges. Thus λ≤1. The size bound from regrouping the aggregation is 21≤2t+(2c+j+3)−1+λ. Strict failure implies 2c+j+3≤15, and the right side is then ≤19, contradiction. These arguments cover all c. This is a source proof under the established 19/15-card and graph-footprint results; no numerical run or combined Lean verification was made.
