# Visible S and three positive direct entrances, zero or three taps

Take the original size-21 least TopForm graph circuit, q=3, j≥3, x=3's sole reader A=x∧S clean, and raw(S) nonempty. Each paid OR Q_i directly takes a dirty AND J_i and a raw-positive pin. Count taps by physical Q_i outputs that reach S through ORs only, including S itself. Let H=G−{3,6,7}. The conclusion in these two cases is U≤2c+j+3.

With zero taps S is pure as a wire function: its OR-only cone has no AND input, because a first OR following such an AND would be a paid entrance. Both singleton labels y,z must activate S, hence S=y∨z, physically the common gate by leastness. The original 19/15 card and common-OR results in premises_6648_q3_full_S_zero_taps.md apply independently of how many of the three paid ORs directly read ANDs. Its paid raw gates B=(y∨z)∨W,Ry,Rz collapse to their other pins on H. No downstream Lambda OR can receive any of them and a mixed ordinary term, if present on H, is the single Ry·Rz correction. Thus the bound there gives U≤2(c+1)+3. A single visible label is impossible with zero taps.

With three taps each full donor J_i implies S. S is clean on H: an independent H input positive at S remains independent on adjoining x but would be accepted at A. Simultaneously cut every direct dirty-AND-to-OR pin (retaining all gates for this semantic accounting). For any edge accepted at some original donor, its earliest positive donor in topological order stays positive under these cuts. Other edge evaluations are unchanged; the joint cut only decreases signals. In the cut circuit each donor has an AND-only expansion into anchored raw clauses, singleton clauses or constants. Indeed the paid ORs are bypassed to their original raw-facing pin and every other relevant OR is an original Lambda support, with its raw support intact. Positive direct exits cannot have a second dirty AND pin. A donor's projected concurrence is clean on H since its original function implies S, and it keeps the original star certificates. Each cut centreless top similarly defines a clean conjunction; every residual from a cut ordinary top would already be starred. These facts give at most c+3 clean, fully anchored H batches without a private-cone assumption.

There are at least two original starred edges incident to y or z. If both labels are visible this holds at A. With one visible label u the x-u edge is starred, and the common raw AND case contributes yz. Otherwise under the original four-reader classification an individual raw port of the other label v cannot be paid: all three paid outputs OR-reach S, so any raw v path to one would put v in raw(S). Its Lambda witness stars an edge incident to v, different from x-u. A physical common OR y∨z cannot be a direct entrance and cannot be anchored with only one visible neighbor at A. Thus at most three near residuals remain.

Suppose (c,j) differs from (0,3) and the proposed bound fails. Then U≥8 and at most eight original stars exist, including at least two near stars. At most six are internal to H. Each clean anchored H batch owns at most two marked edges, and two are a disjoint induced pair. All four orientations of that pair must be rejected by different guarded clauses whose original anchors witness the four complementary corner pairs. Outside H no anchor has two H neighbors. If all four choices do not share one anchor, at least seven internal H stars are required; in particular two distinct anchors cannot share the same two marked endpoints, by C4-freeness. Thus only the homogeneous pair can occur, necessarily 14,59 at 8. In disjoint allocation at most one batch receives two. Hence U≤c+3+1+3=c+7≤2c+j+3, a contradiction.

For c=0,j=3 there are only donor batches. Work with distinct physical donor gates, and discard every donor possessing another donor in its AND-only expansion, because its cut conjunction implies that ancestor's cut conjunction (repeated discards terminate). If one effective donor remains, at most two H residuals are possible. If at least two remain, the expansion of each omits every other remaining donor. All its ANDs are dirty by original TopForm and among the three available gates. Its connected binary AND DAG therefore uses at most two distinct ANDs and at most three primitive inputs. The raw projection of a primitive OR is a disjunction (all three receivers have positive raw partners), and the H restriction does not increase the number of clauses. Two residuals would give an induced matching; each of its four independent endpoint orientations must miss a distinct clause. This precludes double ownership for all effective donors. At most three H and three near edges are residual, as required.

No numerical check or formalization was used for the three-tap case. One and two physical taps are outside this argument.
