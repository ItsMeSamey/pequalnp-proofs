# Two tapped receivers in the clean visible-S case

Use the hypotheses and original star accounting of 6648-three-positive-visible-zero-three-taps.md. Of the three raw-positive direct receivers exactly two OR-reach S. Call their AND suppliers J1,J2 and the remaining receiver Q. No distinctness of suppliers is assumed.

In the exact 19-card (x false) S remains live. The two removals are A and its OR reader, whose other inlet cannot be S by their different singleton values. Set y,z false and all eight H inputs true. With four individual raw OR ports, every gate is true by induction: each port has a different, non-near input. With a shared h=y⋆z, the only false gate is h. Each remaining raw port is an individual OR; its sibling cannot be h since that would be an absorption or duplicate on the least 19-card. The sole reader of h is an OR whose other inlet cannot be y,z or h by the same argument (if h is AND, h∨y=y). Induction now gives the assertion. S differs physically from h because two receivers OR-reach S; the x-card introduces no change to S's ancestry. Thus its image S_H is nonzero. It implies the H graph function, by adjoining x to any independent H input. Any direct AND reader other than A survives both cards with its S_H inlet, since only deleted ANDs can be A and the raw gate h. Replacing that reader by S_H on H can enlarge output only on graph-positive inputs and saves a gate from the exact 15. Thus every other direct reader of S is OR. If one were Lambda its anchor would neighbor both y,z (semantic singleton positivity propagates). The only common neighbor is x, whose only ordinary star is A, impossible for a strict descendant of S. Neither can a reader lie in the clean suffix since S is singleton-positive. The external reader required by the exact 19 is therefore Q, with Q=J∨S.

The same anchor argument excludes a Lambda OR immediately downstream of Q and any OR-only path from Q to an ordinary top leaf. The other paid ORs precede Q. On H project the two tapped receivers to their positive raw-facing pins. Then S is zero (all its original raw labels lie among y,z). Q aliases J. For an original edge activating either tapped donor choose its earliest positive donor; it remains positive in the projection because all earlier tapped injections were already false. Each projected tapped donor defines a clean H conjunction: it implies original S, and all ORs remaining inside its primitive clauses carry just original raw supports. For edges activating neither donor, both projections preserve evaluation. Projected centreless tops are likewise clean anchored conjunctions: Q aliases its AND pin and has no Lambda successor, and every other pretop OR primitive reduces to its original raw support. Ordinary root raw paths cannot involve Q, so every projected ordinary edge was originally starred. These give at most c+2 clean anchored H batches.

At least two near edges are starred. For one visible label the common AND stars yz, and a common OR could only be anchored at A, which would make both visible. In the four-individual case a raw port of the invisible label cannot be either tap (else visible), nor Q (its pins are J and S). Its Lambda witness supplies the second near star. With two visible labels A alone supplies two.

Hence U≤c+2+1+3≤2c+j+3. If c=0,j=3 discard repeated donor batches and any whose projected AND expansion includes the other donor. One remaining batch has ≤2 edges. With two remaining batches each uses at most two of the three original dirty AND gates, whence at most three primitive clauses and no double. Thus U≤2+3.
