# Empty raw S with one centreless top and two distinct donors

Assume the size-21 least original TopForm circuit of L-meta-6648, q=3, j≥3, sole x reader A=x∧S clean, raw(S) empty, c=1. Exactly two physical ORs M1,M2 read dirty ANDs, with distinct direct donors D1,D2 and one raw-positive other pin apiece. All reasoning uses original residuals. H deletes x=3,y=6,z=7. Use the original raw-reader classification and its excluded common-OR branch at empty S. We prove U≤j+5.

Every top except A is ordinary. On H, A is false. On an ordinary input side the nonraw alternatives are precisely the donors that reach it through their physical exits along ORs; the other alternative is the original raw support. Multiplying the two sides and aggregating tops gives the original starred pairs plus D1·H1, D2·H2, and possibly D1·D2. Here Hi is the union of opposite raw supports, all anchored by any raw leaf of Mi since that leaf reaches the same sides. A top with one dirty donor on both sides would imply a dirty signal is below a clean output, impossible. Each displayed term individually implies the original output. Projection to H preserves cleanliness.

D1 is a product of pure clauses. If D2 is nested through the first exit, expand its primitive OR-only frontiers and write D2=D20∨D1K, with D20 the full raw product and K the product of guards unaffected by the first exit. A direct multiplicative D1 factor can instead be kept explicitly (equivalently use zero base and retain D1 in the positive mode). D20⇒K. If the joint term is present it simplifies to D1K and absorbs D1K·H2; without the joint, use D1·H1, D20·H2, D1K·H2. Otherwise the independent donors are pure products, each allowed to have shared ancestors. Thus at most three clean conjunctions cover all original H residuals, with at most one common binary exceptional projected support from the third paid OR Q. After projection, each injector's raw-mode contribution is its raw-positive partner, an existing support; Q is a union of two guarded supports after tracing through such projected aliases. Its presence in dirty-positive modes merely removes satisfied clauses. No projected leastness is used.

F(1,3)≤5 gives at most five H residuals. There is at least one near star: common AND h=yz stars yz, or one of four individual ORs is lambda. Thus at most four near residuals, U≤9. Only j=3,U=9 could violate, and requires five H residuals plus exactly four near residuals. There is exactly one near star.

For common AND this star is yz. Both individual raw readers Ry,Rz must be paid: an individual lambda OR needs a distinct raw leaf on its other pin (otherwise absorption), forcing a second near star, since the only anchor that could star its own raw input is the opposite mate and all leaves of such a star are adjacent to that mate. In the four individual OR case a unique yz star similarly forbids any individual lambda OR, impossible at q=3. If the only star is a private spoke, both readers of the near label untouched by this star are paid. A star at x cannot occur since x has only A and raw S is empty. Thus two distinct paid raw readers have been identified.

If one is Q, its raw support on H collapses to its other input, and that input's raw mode has no Q ancestry. Thus no exceptional support is needed by any projected batch. At U=9 there are at most six internal H stars. The original anchored-corner footprint lemma excludes every nonhomogeneous double (at least seven internal H stars); only 14,59 can double. After exclusive assignment the three batches cover at most four H residuals.

If both named readers are the two physical exits, each exit has a direct near-label raw input with no starred H neighbor (both labels for common AND, the untouched label for four readers). Thus neither Hi contains an H vertex, since the named raw input anchors Hi. Only the joint term survives on H. Whether independent or nested, this is one clean pure product, with at most one common exception. F(1,1)≤2 contradicts five H residuals.
