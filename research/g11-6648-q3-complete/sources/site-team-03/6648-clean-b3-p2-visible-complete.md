# Clean sole AND at 3 with visible sibling and two positive direct entrances

Assume the original least size-21 TopForm G11 circuit, q=b=3, j≥3, and sole clean reader A=x∧S at x=3 with nonempty raw(S). Write y=6,z=7, H=G−{x,y,z}. The paid receivers are P1,P2 with positive raw support and E with empty raw support, all three directly reading dirty ANDs. We prove U≤2c+j+3.

E has two distinct dirty AND pins: an earlier empty OR would itself be paid, and the other paid gates are positive. No pre-top OR reads E: such an OR would be paid on account of its empty dirty inlet, and neither positive receiver can take E while still having a dirty AND pin and a positive-raw pin. Thus E cannot OR-reach S. Count the P_i outputs reaching S through ORs only, including equality.

With no tap S is pure and must be y∨z by singleton positivity at both neighbors. The common-OR result in premises_6648_q3_full_S_zero_taps.md then forces all three paid ORs B,Ry,Rz to have positive raw support, a contradiction.

With at least one tap use the external-reader lemma of 6648-three-positive-visible-two-taps.md and its one-tap application twin_6648_three_positive_visible_one_tap.md. For precision, the x=false exact 19-card keeps S and an external reader. When y,z are also set false, only the shared raw gate h (if present) is false on the all-true H input, by the raw-port induction. S cannot be h since a paid direct receiver OR-reaches S. Hence S_H is nonzero and implies the H graph function by adjoining x on independent inputs. Any further AND reader survives on the exact 15-card with its S_H inlet, so forwarding it to S_H could enlarge output only on graph-positive inputs while saving a gate, impossible. An external OR reader must be paid: an ordinary Lambda witness would require a raw opposite leaf adjacent to both y,z by S's singleton values, necessarily x, available only at A upstream. The external reader cannot be E by raw positivity, and cannot be any tapped P_i by acyclicity. These facts concern the exact 19/15 cards before any dirty-pin cuts. They rule out two taps.

Thus exactly one receiver P_t=T∨R taps, and the other is P_u=J∨S directly. P_u accepts both y,z singleton inputs. It has no pre-top Lambda successor or OR-only path to another ordinary top: the same common-neighbor obstruction forces the opposite leaf x and hence A, inaccessible downstream of S. It also cannot feed E on an OR-only path because E is raw-empty. The entire raw OR support of P_t is contained in raw(S)⊆{y,z}, so R is false on H. Cut the dirty pin of P_t to false. On H, S becomes false and P_u forwards J. Every original T-positive H edge remains positive at T by precedence; the cut preserves all T-negative rows. T implies S and is clean on H. In the resulting circuit E is the only genuine disjunction between dirty signals. Its surviving arms are pure anchored products: any earlier positive receiver was forwarded, and any path out of the forwarded J into a Lambda raw clause via P_u was excluded in the original graph. Each other primitive raw OR support is a singleton or has its original Lambda anchor after restriction; forwarding P_t retains its original raw support and forwarding P_u does not create an OR reader of J. E occurs only multiplicatively, including in the cone of J if applicable. Thus T and each cut centreless top yield at most two pure anchored H conjunctions by splitting this shared E factor. All nonzero products are clean on H. Cut ordinary tops give only original starred pairs. This allocation permits E upstream, downstream, or sharing dirty ancestry with the positive donors.

At least two original stars are near. If both labels are visible A supplies both. If only one is visible the common AND gives yz; a common OR would require both labels visible by the common-OR result. In the four-individual case a raw reader of the hidden label cannot be P_t (else the label is visible), P_u (its pins J,S), or E (empty). Its original Lambda witness supplies a star incident to the hidden label, separate from the visible x edge. Hence ≤3 near edges are residual. By the H corner lemma in premises_6648_q3_visible_two_positive_addendum.md and the H footprints cited therein, whenever U≥7 the clean anchored H products admit at most one exclusive double globally. They receive at most 2(c+1)+1 H residuals. Therefore U≤2c+6≤2c+j+3. At U≤6 the bound holds directly.
