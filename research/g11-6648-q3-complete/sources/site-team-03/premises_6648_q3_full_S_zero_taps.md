# No OR-only tap into full-raw clean S at q=3

If no dirty-reading OR has an OR-only path into S, its raw closure consists of pure input unions. With full raw {y,z}, leastness makes S the physical h=y∨z. By the common-OR theorem the entire paid set then consists of B=h∨W and the two individual Ry=y∨V,Rz=z∨V'. The original y–10 and z–9 are residual. No uncharged OR below an AND can directly read one of those three paid outputs, regardless of the number of dirty pins at the three gates. For B a lambda witness would require raw centre x at upstream A. For Ry, lambda centre x is unavailable (A has only h); centre 10 would star y–10; centre z permits only the raw leaf y because x is unavailable and z–9 is residual. A direct lambda OR reader must have a second raw-positive input. To have raw support contained in {y}, this second input must itself OR-reach Ry, the only channel out of raw y besides h which also contains z. The direct reader would be absorbable. The argument for Rz is symmetric. No direct read among the three paid wires is possible either: B's W rejects y10 and z9 by the 19-card necessity, so cannot equal Ry or Rz; each individual reader would absorb its raw input if it also read B; and each individual reader rejects the other raw singleton by yz toggling and so cannot read its mate's output.

On H all three paid ORs collapse to their other original operands, because the inputs h,y,z vanish. Every surviving OR below a top is accordingly a pure anchored raw disjunction on H. In fact no surviving uncharged OR had an OR-only path through any paid wire, so a nonpure donor cannot re-emerge below such an OR even if originally fed through a sequence of dirty ANDs and additional paid wires. Every centreless top supplies one clean pure anchored H-conjunction, covering at most two original residuals.

In an ordinary top with H behavior beyond the original star product a nonpure operand must be a direct paid wire: every uncharged OR above these was excluded. Wire B cannot be such an ordinary input because it carries y,z and x only goes to A. If Ry is an ordinary input, its opposite raw leaves must all be z (10 would star y–10 and x only feeds A); symmetrically for Rz. A pure opposite raw union on z vanishes on H. The only nonpure alternative with exactly raw z is Rz: B has both leaves and Ry contains y. Thus the entire ordinary correction on H, if any, is the single clean product Ry∧Rz restricted to H, again a conjunction of anchored clauses. Adding at most three near residual edges gives U≤2(c+1)+3, sufficient for j≥3, without a numerical replay. This proof permits either or both individual readers to be genuine dirty exits, and in particular covers the zero-tap branch of the two-exit classification.
