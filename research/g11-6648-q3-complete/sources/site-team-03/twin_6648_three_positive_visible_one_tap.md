# Three positive direct receivers, visible S, exactly one tap

Use the original size-21 least TopForm hypotheses of 6648-three-positive-visible-two-taps.md with q=3, j≥3, clean sole x-reader A=x∧S and nonempty raw(S). Exactly one of the three direct positive receivers, M=D∨R0, reaches S by ORs. H deletes x=3,y=6,z=7. This treats one or both visible labels and arbitrary sharing. We prove U≤2c+j+3.

**External reader.** On H, S equals D as a function: all raw leaves in S, including those reached from R0, lie in {y,z}; any dirty AND met on an OR-only path must be immediately preceded in that backwards traversal by a direct receiver, necessarily M. D is clean on H by adjoining x. Use the exact 19/15-card argument of the two-tap note: S survives the first two deletions with an external use, is different from the common raw gate h (it has M in its OR closure), and S_H is nonzero. On the all-true H assignment the four-individual configuration keeps every wire true; with common h it alone becomes false, as proved in that note. Thus any further AND reader with S_H as pin would survive and could be bypassed to S_H on the exact 15, a contradiction. An external OR reader is dirty by singleton positivity, and cannot be Lambda: its opposite raw witness would neighbor both y,z and thus be x, unavailable outside A. It must be a second distinct direct receiver F=J∨S. F is bivalent, meaning true on both singleton y,z. The same witness argument precludes any Lambda OR successor or OR-only path to an ordinary top (F is downstream of S and cannot return to A). Write P=K∨R for the third distinct receiver. These names specify physical gates, not necessarily distinct suppliers. J=D would give F=S by D≤S and hence violate leastness.

At least two original near edges are stars. With two visible labels A gives both. With one visible label, a common AND supplies yz. A common OR y∨z is neither a direct entrance nor Lambda unless both visible at A. In the remaining four-individual configuration at least one of the four distinct raw OR ports is Lambda, as q=3. Its second pin has a raw leaf different from its literal pin (otherwise the second pin already dominates the literal and the gate can be removed). Its witness cannot be A at center x with just one visible label. Thus it stars a second near edge. We may pay three near residuals.

**No direct F pin at P.** No OR can now read F: a Lambda reader is impossible, M is earlier, and the only other paid receiver is P. All its forward paths begin with AND until another dirty injection or a first clean top. Separate the D-positive H edges first. If P does not lie in D's strict cone, D is one anchored pure conjunction on H. If P does lie there, D has at most two anchored H batches: set P to its original raw pin in one branch, and multiply the P=true branch by its upstream dirty supplier K in the other. The raw clauses inherit original Lambda anchors or are raw literals; the positive pin of an earlier paid receiver is followed to its original raw support, and neither M nor F can precede D. Both batches imply S and are clean on H.

On the other H pairs set D=false globally and M to its projected raw pin. Then S=0 and F=J (all occurrences consistently projected). Because no OR reads F, this alias inserts only AND products, possibly in the donor of P. There is now just one genuine selector, P, with its dirty donor and its raw-facing support. If a raw-facing pin of P inherits M, all such paths project to actual original raw sets (on H); any zero raw-facing support is allowed. A dirty supplier beneath P has no P ancestry and is a pure conjunction after the aliases: a multiplicative F occurrence merely inserts J, whose ancestors are also projected. If J is downstream of P instead, apply the same single P mode consistently inside J. Every centreless root has ≤2 clean anchored terms by the joint-mode lemma. If the root multiplicatively requires K after projection, the raw mode is covered by the dirty-side mode. Every ordinary all-raw contribution is an original star. The pooled ordinary correction is the projected supplier K times the union of opposite original raw guards, anchored by any original raw vertex of R (possibly outside H). No ordinary side can OR-reach F, and P cannot OR-reach both sides of an original ordinary top. Thus there are ≤2c+2 H batches nonnested and ≤2c+3 nested. This counts S/D explicitly rather than using leastness in the projected circuit.

For a putative violation U≥8, there are at most six internal H stars after the two near stars. Only the homogeneous double survives, uniquely {14,59}, by the four-corner lemma of 6648-three-positive-visible-zero-three-taps.md. Thus U≤N+1+3 for N H batches; at U≥11 there are at most three H stars, excluding even that double. These estimates close the nonnested case for c≥1, and the nested case for c≥1,j≥4 or c≥2,j=3. In the nonnested c=0 case at most two batches exist. At U≥7 there are at most seven internal stars, where two exclusive doubles are still impossible by premises_6648_q3_full_S_one_tap.md, final paragraph (including the seven-star nonhomogeneous footprint list). Hence U≤2+1+3=6.

In the nested case F is last. It cannot reach a further OR before the next clean top, so it multiplicatively reaches a centreless destination; c=0 is impossible. For c=1,j=3 its sole destination must save one of the five batches. If J=K that top multiplicatively requires K on D-negative H pairs because F=J. Otherwise J,K,D are distinct: K=D is impossible by nesting, J=D gives the redundancy above. All three dirty gates precede F. F thus directly reads into a clean AND (its successor cannot be OR). The other pin of that AND has no raw leaf: otherwise that leaf neighbors both y,z, so is x, which only A reads. Therefore that pin is a dirty AND since no raw-empty pretop OR exists and no clean AND can precede an AND. It cannot be J, since (J∨S)∧J is J. If D it kills the root; if K it forces the dirty mode. This leaves at most four batches, sufficient for U≤8.

**P directly reads F.** Now P=K∨F is bivalent and last; neither F nor P can reach an ordinary top through ORs. D has no upstream receiver, so contributes one anchored H batch. On D-negative pairs F aliases J, P to K∨J. The projected suppliers are pure products: a multiplicative F dependency in K inserts J, and P cannot precede either. P has only AND continuations to its first clean top. The usual last-empty factoring proof applies to this physical continuation, independent of P's originally positive raw support: two direct AND readers on the AND-only continuation can be deleted by cofactoring P=true, aggregating the affected tops and restoring P once. The same applies to every dirty AND on that continuation. Hence there is one centreless destination; c≥1. It costs ≤2 anchored batches, all other centreless roots ≤1, and no ordinary correction survives. With the one D batch N≤c+2, proving the bound by the same footprint estimate.

All arguments are source-level and use the established exact-card and original-star footprint lemmas.
