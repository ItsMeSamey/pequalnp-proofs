# Mixed raw signs, only the empty entrance feeds visible S along ORs

Keep the original clean sole A size-21 assumptions with q=3,j≥3. Let E be the physical OR with empty raw support, P the physical OR with a direct dirty AND and positive raw partner, and Q the spare paid OR. Only E feeds positive-raw S by an OR-only path. The first empty OR must have two direct dirty AND operands, since any distinct earlier empty OR would itself require a distinct physical dirty-reading OR of empty raw support. Thus E=J1∨J2. The first raw-positive OR on the E-to-S path must be Q: it has an empty inlet, hence is paid, and cannot be P because P already has one direct dirty AND pin and a raw-positive other pin. There is no intervening raw-empty OR: no other empty signal without a direct AND input is available except E itself, and E∨E is redundant. Write Q=E∨R. R is pure, since it cannot OR-reach P without giving P a tap. Its raw labels are confined to the visible near labels.

On H the tapped E and both donor arms are clean since they imply S and x can be added to any independent H input. Unfold each Ji on H. Before E, only P can enter a raw OR region. Let D be its dirty pin. When P contributes a mixed mode to a Ji, the pure base product and D times the unaffected guard suffice; otherwise one pure product suffices. If P is downstream of E, the Ji precede P and hence are pure before E. On H rows negative for E, cut both Ji at their OR pins. Q collapses at the same time; E and its positive raw entry now give no exception. If D was downstream of E, it is a pure product after this cut, with Q's original near raw arm zero on H. In the remaining circuit at most two products per centreless top, and one ordinary product D times an aggregated guard anchored by a raw leaf of P, suffice. All clauses are fully guarded: the single spare Q is accounted for on both sides of the cut.

If r≤2 counts Ji arms having the two modes, this yields at most n=2c+3+r H batches. Two near stars hold as in the positive entrance index. At U≥7 anchored H footprints permit at most one extra allocated double. Thus j≥4+r suffices. At U≥11 no double is possible (four internal stars plus two near stars would force U≤10), so j≥3+r suffices at this threshold. These bounds alone are partial.

## One visible label, exactly two near stars

The original named-reader argument in premises_6648_q3_visible_two_positive_addendum.md uses only q=3 and the original supports. It gives two distinct paid individual near-input readers; since E is raw-empty these must be Q and P. Hence Q=E∨u for the visible label u, and P=D∨w, with w a near label having no private starred spoke. In the common-AND case the only near stars are xu and yz; in the four-individual case the second star is the private spoke of the other label, and both named readers have the untouched label. No Q occurs downstream of P on an OR-only path.

Every OR-only lambda extension of P has no raw H leaf. Any anchor of such an extension stars w, so it must be x (whose only raw star is xu) or the opposite near label in the yz case (which has no starred H neighbor). In the private-spoke case w has no possible anchor except x if it is visible. Thus on H all P-carrying OR-only signals equal D. After restricting H, Q equals E. The remaining circuit leading to clean tops consists of anchored pure clauses and ANDs except for E=J1∨J2. The two Ji are pure conjunctions since both precede E and P, if present in their ancestry, now aliases D. Allocate their clean H positives first. After zeroing E, each remaining centreless output gives at most one anchored conjunction. No ordinary top can contribute a D correction via P on H because every opposite raw guard leaf is originally starred from w and w has no starred H neighbor. Also Q eliminates E on this slice. At most c+2 batches remain, yielding U≤c+2+1+3 whenever U≥7, and smaller U is harmless. This closes this original-card subcase at every c,j.
