The retention argument of 6648-three-positive-visible-two-taps.md needs only one tap: S cannot equal the common raw gate h if present. At all H bits true and y,z false the only possibly false nonraw wire in the 19-card is h. The core image S_H is therefore nonzero; it implies H by adjoining x to independent inputs. An external direct AND reader would survive both cards with its inlet, and enlarging this reader to S_H would save a gate in the least 15-card. The direct reader cannot be a clean OR (S accepts both singleton neighbors), and a Lambda OR would have to reach an ordinary top whose opposite leaf shares an anchor of both semantic singleton neighbors, namely x. That top could only be the earlier A. E has no positive pin and P is upstream. Consequently Q=J∨S.

Q cannot feed a Lambda OR or OR-reach an ordinary top by the same singleton argument. Cut just the dirty pin of P, leaving T unchanged. T implies original S and is clean on H. On pairs negative at T no downstream value changes. On H after the cut S is zero, since every OR-only raw label of S is near and the sole dirty entrance into that OR cone was P. Thus Q aliases J. Similarly Q cannot feed a pretop OR after the cut: before the cut it had no Lambda successor, P was earlier and E accepts dirty ANDs only.
