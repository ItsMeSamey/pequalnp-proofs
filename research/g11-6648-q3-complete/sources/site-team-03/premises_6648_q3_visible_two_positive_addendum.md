# Single-visible single-tap reduction to nested j=3

Its negative slice leaves only original ordinary stars and c pure centreless batches. At c=0,1 the resulting c+1 one-exception batches suffice immediately. If at least three near stars exist, the numerical bounds in the index save one near edge and close every nonnested case and every nested j≥4 case. At least four near stars also close nested j=3.

Suppose there are exactly two near stars. One is xu at the visible label u; the other x edge cannot be starred, so the second star is yz or a private spoke. The common-AND case stars yz. An individual lambda OR reader cannot use x as its anchor: its other raw-positive pin must supply a distinct raw label by absorption, whereas x's sole A stars only xu. If the other star is yz, the only remaining possible anchor of an individual reader is the other near mate. Its second distinct raw leaf then stars a third near edge (x cannot enter any OR). Thus in the common-AND case both individual readers are paid; four individual readers with only additional yz would all be paid and are impossible. If the second star is a private spoke the common AND is impossible, and both individual readers of the label without a private star must be paid. In all possible cases two named individual readers are paid.

If Q (the only paid OR without a direct dirty pin) is a named reader, its near raw label disappears on H and its contribution aliases the other pin or is satisfied in a positive flag mode. No exceptional clause remains. Once U≥7 at most seven internal H stars exist, since at least two near stars are original. The three nonhomogeneous anchored footprint rows at this threshold exclude any second allocated nonhomogeneous double and any separate allocated homogeneous double. Thus the n batches cover at most n+1 H residuals. Since at most three near residuals remain, this yields U≤2c+6 nonnested and ≤2c+7 nested, closing j≥3 and j≥4 respectively.

If both named readers are the two exits, the untapped exit's direct raw label has no starred H neighbor. This holds for either label in the common-AND case and for the label of both named readers in the four-reader case. Its entire opposite ordinary guard therefore vanishes on H. The H-batch count loses one. For c=0 the counts 1 and 2 give ≤2 and ≤4 H edges, respectively. For c=1 the counts 3 and 4 give ≤5 and ≤6 H edges. Adding at most three near residuals again closes nonnested j≥3 and nested j≥4.

The only original cases left by these arguments have distinct donors, the untapped exit feeding the tapped dirty donor, j=3, c=0 or 1, and exactly two or three near stars. This is a necessary parameter description, not an existence assertion.
