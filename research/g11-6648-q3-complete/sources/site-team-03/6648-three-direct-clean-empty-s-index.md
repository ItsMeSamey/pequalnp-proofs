# Complete source dispatch for clean sole AND, empty S, three direct entrances, at least one empty entrance

Assume the issued least size-21 TopForm G11 circuit with j≥3 and q=3. Its sole x=3 reader is the clean A=x∧S, raw(S) empty. All three paid ORs directly read a dirty AND, and at least one has empty raw support. The target is U≤2c+j+3. The H slice deletes x=3,y=6,z=7. Counts use original stars. Three empty entrances are already covered, with arbitrary reconvergence, by 6648-all-raw-empty-charges.md. Below we also address mixtures with one or two empty entrances.

**Exact card hypotheses and S_H.** Use the original clean unique-AND interface in 6648-critical-and-cofactors.md and the 19/15 reader classification of 6648-size21-sole-and.md. At x=false delete A and its sole OR reader, leaving a least 19-gate circuit. All remaining physical gates are live. S cannot be the port's forwarded sibling: that sibling rejects both y,z singletons and S accepts both. None of the subsequently deleted raw readers can have received a signal via A, since the clean A has no later AND and those readers accept singletons and must have a later AND. In the 19 card, setting y=z=false uses exactly four deletions and leaves a least 15-gate H circuit. These are either four distinct raw OR readers with no shared raw gate, or h=y∧z, its unique card OR reader, and the two separate raw OR ports. A shared raw y∨z would be a pure paid OR: only x could anchor both, and A is centreless. This contradicts all paid gates being direct entrances.

The raw-empty S cannot be one of the deleted raw OR readers, nor h, which is clean and would be a predecessor of A; nor h's OR reader, for the same TopForm reason. No further gate can be deleted or pruned without violating the 15 floor. In particular S remains a physical, nonconstant gate. Explicitly, in the four-OR case, forwarding every raw port through its other pin ends at a retained wire or core input; otherwise some earlier port would have both inputs y,z. In the common-AND case h becomes zero, its sole OR reader forwards its other pin, and h has no path to S. In S's ancestor cone all individual ports likewise have a nonzero core source on the all-ones H input. All surviving gates reject the empty input. The actual S_H is clean: an independent H input could be extended by x without creating an edge. If any original AND reader of S other than A existed, its original index would survive both restrictions, since it is not any deleted raw OR, h, or h's OR reader. It would directly read the actual S_H wire. Replacing that AND by S_H can only enlarge signals where the correct H output is true. This deletes a gate from a least 15-gate circuit, impossible. Thus all external readers of S are ORs. At least one exists by liveness in the 19 card. Such a reader is dirty on the singleton inputs y,z and paid because it reads raw-empty S. This uses leastness only on the original and the two explicitly tight physical cards.

**S an AND.** The complete proof is 6648-three-direct-clean-empty-s-dirty-and.md (SHA256 c8c6fe51a8d0336a24db029d9467f5982acb9953c58845b70a92d99718b6942a). Every AND-frontier primitive of S accepts both singleton y,z; it must be a paid OR because a Lambda witness would require an ordinary top with opposite x. Thus two incomparable bivalent receivers feed S, and the remaining receiver directly reads S. No ordinary top receives any of these three through OR-only paths. The j=3 terminal obstruction and the c=1, c=2, c≥3 counts there prove the target. This resolves in particular the exactly-one-empty-entrance configuration.

**S an OR.** S is a paid raw-empty entrance. Each external OR reader F must be raw-empty with a dirty AND K as its other pin: it must be a direct entrance, and S is an OR. None of these F gates is a raw y,z reader or h's clean-descendant OR child, so its index remains an OR reader of the same S_H in the 15 card. There is at most one such F by the usual clean-wire extraction: cut the direct uses of S_H to false before restoring it once by a final OR; original positives with S_H false remain exact and those with S_H true are safe. Thus F=S∨K is unique, so at least two entrances are raw-empty. The three-empty case is the cited earlier theorem. With exactly two empty entrances let P=J∨R be the remaining raw-positive entrance with one dirty donor and nonempty raw-facing pin. Then S=B1∨B2 for two distinct dirty AND inputs. An earlier raw-empty OR pin is impossible: the only other raw-empty entrance F is later, and a raw-empty pre-top OR is always paid. Both arms imply S and are H-clean. The only possible additional entrance causal to S is P, with no assumption that it has private uses. If P is not an ancestor, the two arms have pure anchored AND expansions and cannot accept both near singletons, since x could not supply an anchor. The two arms must therefore split the singleton positives, one each. If P is an ancestor an arm can receive its contribution, so the count below uses both flag modes instead of asserting such a split.

There are at least two original near stars (edges touching y or z). Here x has no star. If the only near star were yz, each individual Lambda raw reader would have a nonempty other raw pin providing a distinct leaf (else absorption); under its opposite-mate anchor that other leaf gives a second near star. No other anchor is then possible. Thus the common-AND case needs both individual readers paid, and the four-reader case all four paid. There is only one raw-positive paid OR. If the only near star were a private spoke, the common AND h cannot exist, and both readers of the label untouched by that star would have to be paid. Again impossible. With no near star, all individual readers would have to be paid. Thus at most three of the five near edges are residual.

If j=3, B1,B2,K exhaust the dirty ANDs and all precede F (K distinct from both since otherwise F duplicates S). Every one implies F. F cannot be output or pass only through ORs, as F is bivalent on the original singletons. It cannot have an OR reader below a top: an uncharged reader cannot take the empty-raw F pin, and the sole remaining charged P cannot take it either since its other pin must be a direct dirty AND and its output has positive raw support. Thus the first AND reached from F must be a direct clean reader. An opposite pin with a raw leaf would force that leaf adjacent to both y,z, i.e. x, unavailable anywhere except A. An opposite dirty AND or raw-empty OR therefore supplies the other pin. It is B1,B2,K,S or F, all implying F, so the proposed clean product equals that dirty wire. This excludes j=3.

For j≥4, expand each B_i on H using P (if ancestral) as the sole switch. The two products are the P-raw setting and J times the P-true setting. Apply substitutions also in any shared multiplicative subgraph. Each product implies B_i and hence is H-clean, and the two together equal B_i on H. All surviving raw clauses have original star anchors or are raw/constant; when P is upstream, its raw partner has no preceding dirty entrance. There are at most four protected H batches in total. Assign all edges accepted by either B_i first. Set both B_i false globally on remaining H pairs, retaining exact values there; S=0 and F forwards K, which can itself simplify. Ignore the vanishing A. There remains at most one raw-positive switch P. Each of the c−1 other centreless tops yields at most two clean anchored products. The ordinary base is originally starred; any ordinary affected side contains J through P and its opposite raw guard is anchored to a raw leaf of P (all original raw supports are retained under the injections' raw modes). If J becomes H-clean on the restricted computation, both sides seeing it might be possible functionally there, but new OR-only connections from P to ordinary sides cannot occur through the forwarded empty F: K is an AND unless it has become false, and a raw-empty F could never directly feed an ordinary raw OR below a top. Any original top seeing P on both sides would be dirty. Thus the ordinary corrections cost at most one pooled clean anchored product. Together there are at most 4+2(c−1)+1=2c+3 H batches over the whole allocation.

At a violation for j≥4,c≥1, U≥10, so at most six original star edges exist. Each anchored clean H batch owns at most two residuals, forming an induced matching when there are two. The four corner anchors for a disjoint marked double require at least seven star edges unless one anchor sees all four marks. Outside H a vertex sees at most one H vertex, and inside H only 8 has the necessary degree, with sole matched pair 14,59. In disjoint ownership at most one batch can therefore receive a second edge. Add at most three near residuals: U≤(2c+3)+1+3=2c+7≤2c+j+3. This proves the OR case, independent of P's position relative to S or F.
