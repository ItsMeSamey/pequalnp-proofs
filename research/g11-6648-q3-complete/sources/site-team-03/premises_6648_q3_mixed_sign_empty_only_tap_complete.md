# Completion of mixed-sign E-only tap at q=3

Use premises_6648_q3_mixed_sign_empty_only_tap.md, SHA256 5f32443f9fcb64b3f2ac075636b7c6167769558fc55f2463f9b6b06373a120cb. The paid wires are E=J1∨J2 raw-empty, P with direct dirty pin D and positive raw arm, and Q=E∨R carrying E into the positive-raw OR cone of S. Visibility may be one or both neighbors. No P-to-S path is entirely ORs. The earlier note gives completely anchored H batches. At U≥7 their total coverage exceeds their number by at most one; at U≥11 there is no double because even a homogeneous one costs four internal stars, besides at least two near stars. The two arms of E differ by leastness.

If D coincides with a Ji, allocate this donor first; it is pure before P. On its negative rows all P contributions collapse. The other Ji then gives one clean pure projected product, and on the jointly negative slice there is no ordinary correction and at most c remaining pure products. At most c+2 batches suffice, giving U≤c+6 at U≥7. Thus assume all three donors distinct.

Let r be the number of Ji cones with a mixed P input that needs its D-positive mode. For r>0, P precedes E, and the entire paid set precedes or equals Q. The baseline is 2+r donor products, one ordinary correction, and two modes per remaining centreless root. All dirty donor terms are allocated on H only. Monotone cuts of signals known false on those rows may be made at all uses, even with shared ancestors.

For full raw S, the exact x-false 19-gate card requires an external live use of S. Any OR directly consuming S would be paid because its full raw labels could only be anchored at x via the earlier A. There is no later paid OR. Therefore this external path begins with AND and stays multiplicative to a centreless top. There is no later dirty-AND-to-OR exit. On the Ji-negative slice S vanishes on H, so that centreless top loses both of its modes. In particular c≥1.

For solitary visible label u, the untouched raw arm R has Boolean value u. The only raw alternatives in S are u, and the only dirty alternatives entering its OR cone arise via E, so S=Q=E∨u and Q accepts both original singleton neighbors, including the hidden v. An ordinary top seeing Q by ORs must therefore have opposite raw label adjacent to both, hence x; this is A alone. A lambda OR on such an OR-only path must itself feed A, contains only raw u, and cannot receive P by ORs, as that would create a second tap. Every such extension is consequently equal to Q. Any OR directly reading E has an empty pin, requiring a charge; Q is its only such reader when r>0. Once an E-to-top path enters an AND there is no later OR reading a dirty AND. Therefore any centreless top that depends on E must multiplicatively require E or Q, which are equal on H. It loses both modes on jointly Ji-negative H rows. If no centreless top depends on E, skip all Ji-positive allocations: ordinary tops other than A can see only P's dirty correction, and unaffected centreless tops use at most two P-modes each. The resulting 2c+1 batches suffice even allowing one double and three near residuals at any U≥7.

In either visibility, when a centreless root vanishes this gives n≤2c+1+r. For c≥2 any violation has U≥11 and n+3≤2c+6. At c=1, j≥4 gives n+1+3≤9. At c=1,j=3,r=1 one has n≤4 and the same estimate is eight. If c=1,j=3,r=2, the three dirty ANDs are D,J1,J2. A multiplicatively required Ji in the other would make E redundant; a multiplicatively required D in a Ji kills its raw base. More generally a directly required D contributes only the positive product D times at most one other factor. D has two pure factors. Each Ji raw base that remains has at most two primitive factors, and its D-positive product has at most three since at least one Ji pin containing the P alternative is discharged. The ordinary D times guard product also has at most three factors. There is no other centreless root. None of these ≤5 anchored batches can double, as an anchored disjoint double needs four distinct complementary-corner clauses and a touching double would require an unanchored clause. Hence U≤5+3=8.

For r=0, the baseline n≤2c+3 suffices at j≥4 or U≥11. At j=3,c=0 or 1 all dirty gates are D,J1,J2. Allocate Ji-positive pairs first. A multiplicatively required D in Ji would allow Ji a third primitive clause; it still cannot double. If either Ji has other nontrivial D ancestry involving a P alternative, use the r>0 branch, or omit its zero/discharged mode. With no such ancestry each Ji primitive cone contains at most its own binary AND and D, hence at most three primitive factors; the other Ji cannot be a required factor by irredundancy of E. After assigning both, set J1=J2=E=false at all uses on the remaining H rows. Q then has no H value. D, if not already upstream, has only its own AND left in its surviving cone and hence at most two factors. If upstream the same statement follows since the other ANDs are exactly the Ji and were cut. Thus D·H0, the sole ordinary correction, also cannot double. Every centreless root directly reads an empty signal from E,J1,J2,D: there is no other empty OR and no other dirty AND. Only a D-requiring root survives, with a single positive mode. For c=0 the three double-free H products and three near edges give six. For c=1 at most four H products, at most one double and three near edges give eight. This completes all j,c.

All estimates use original card and star hypotheses. No numerical execution was performed. This supplement concerns only mixed signs with visible S and E as its unique physical tap.
