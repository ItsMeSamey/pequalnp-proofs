# Clean sole x-AND, empty S, three direct entrances, at least one empty

Assume the original least size-21 TopForm G11 circuit with j≥3, q=3, x=3 having sole reader A=x∧S clean, raw(S) empty, and all three paid ORs directly reading a dirty AND. At least one of these three has empty raw support. Write y=6,z=7 and H=G−{x,y,z}. The following proves the entire case in which S is an AND. It also restricts the distinct case in which S is an OR.

**Original-to-core interface.** S is independent of x, accepts singleton y and singleton z (the two x edges), and becomes clean on H (extend an independent positive H input by x to contradict cleanliness of A). Call a signal bivalent when it accepts both original singleton inputs. No bivalent signal can OR-reach an ordinary top operand: every raw opposite leaf would have to be a common neighbor of y,z, hence x, and x can only feed A, which is centreless. Thus every bivalent pre-top OR is paid. In particular it is one of the three direct entrances. A common physical raw reader y∨z would itself be a pure paid OR and is excluded. Expanding a dirty AND accepting both singletons through its AND pins must terminate at bivalent ORs, never raw inputs. If only one earlier bivalent OR exists the AND would duplicate that OR as a whole-cube function and violate leastness.

The x=false card is least size 19, removing only A and its clean OR reader, and S must remain live there. On this card the y=z=false restriction is least size 15 under the established original reader classification: four distinct individual raw ORs disappear, or the common h=y∧z, its sole card OR reader, and two other individual raw OR readers disappear. S is not one of the disappearing gates. It is raw-empty; h is clean and cannot enter S, even by an OR path, since A is a later AND; the extra OR reader of h likewise cannot be S. Moreover on the all-ones H input S remains positive. In the four-port case all individual-port siblings reach some core input after following other ports (an only-y,z cycle or common reader is excluded); all retained gates are constant-free. In the common-h case no signal depending on h can reach S, and in this cone the remaining individual-port siblings likewise have a core source. Equivalently a constant fold of the retained S would give an extra deletion below 15. S therefore survives as a nonconstant clean gate. Every original direct AND reader of S except A would also survive; none is the common raw h or one of the deleted ORs. Its card output would be S_H∧K. Replacing it by S_H only enlarges signals where H is already true, contradicting least 15. No such reader exists. An external reader of S exists from least 19 and must therefore be an OR; it is dirty, with raw-empty S pin, and hence paid.

**If S is an AND.** Its original conjunctive OR frontier consists only of bivalent paid receivers. At least two incomparable effective frontier functions occur, or S duplicates one of its own ancestors. Its external reader F already uses a third charge, so there are exactly two distinct causal receivers E1,E2, both bivalent and incomparable. S physically computes their meet: every AND below the frontier either equals an earlier Ei or already computes that meet, so leastness also excludes any duplicate intermediate row. The only remaining paid receiver F directly OR-reads S, so is bivalent. In particular all ordinary tops are pure stars and every Lambda OR is pure. Any path from a dirty AND into an ordinary top OR cone would have a first direct entrance, bivalent and hence forbidden there.

**j=3 impossible.** Suppose one of the two bivalent causal receivers is raw-empty. Its two inputs are distinct dirty ANDs: a raw-empty OR input would require an earlier direct entrance via OR-only paths; the only earlier candidate is the other bivalent receiver, making the two comparable. Clean AND ancestry is excluded by TopForm. Neither input can be bivalent because each precedes the later of the two causal entrances, hence has at most one earlier bivalent OR; the AND-frontier duplication argument applies. Consequently the two inputs accept different singleton labels. With S these exhaust the three dirty ANDs and all precede F.

If both causal receivers are raw-positive, neither of their raw-facing pins can be bivalent: if an OR, it would be the other paid receiver and make the two comparable. Their dirty donor pins must each accept at least one of y,z. If the donors are distinct, with S they exhaust the three dirty ANDs, all preceding F. If they share a donor D, then D≤S; F must be the given raw-empty receiver. Its pin besides S is a dirty AND K distinct from S,D, else F duplicates S. An earlier raw-empty OR pin is impossible since neither earlier receiver is raw-empty; clean AND ancestry is forbidden. All three dirty ANDs again precede F. This last case allows a singleton-negative K, but K≤F.

In each of these cases F cannot have an OR reader before a first clean top: a Lambda reader would route the bivalent F to an ordinary top, and no later paid OR is available. Nor can F itself be the output or only feed the output aggregation, because it is positive on a singleton. Its next AND must be clean since every dirty AND precedes it. A clean such reader needs an opposite pin rejecting both singleton y,z and with empty raw support (any raw label would have to be x, unavailable). A raw-empty OR would be a bivalent paid receiver (or would contain a clean AND, forbidden before the clean reader); a raw-empty dirty AND is one of the previously identified wires. In the only case permitting a singleton-negative dirty pin it is K≤F, and the resulting AND equals dirty K. This rules out every possible live route from F.

**j≥4 accounting.** For c=1 the only nonstar source is A, which adds only its two x edges. For c=2 there is only one additional centreless top. By the exact three-direct-entrance joint projection, its three physical switches give at most eight clean conjunctions of fully anchored or singleton raw clauses even with shared inputs. More explicitly, forward one chosen input at each raw-empty OR; at each raw-positive OR forward the raw-facing input, or force the output true and require the topologically projected dirty supplier as a conjunct. Correct modes retain all positive pairs, every mode only accepts where the clean top does, and AND expansion gives the indicated anchored conjunctions. A clean anchored batch has at most two original residuals which if two form an induced matching. Three pairs would need eight independent transversal clauses, forcing an anchor with six neighbors by the cube argument. Two pairs need all four complementary corners witnessed by original stars. If U≥12 there are at most four original stars: these four corners then force the same anchor adjacent to all four marks, or at least seven star incidences are necessary (a nonhomogeneous array has at most one adjacent repeated anchor). Only 8's neighborhood in G11 admits two disjoint edges, the pair 14,59. Hence across all eight disjointly assigned batches there is at most one extra marked edge, giving U≤2+8+1=11≤2c+j+3. Otherwise U≤11 already.

For c≥3 any failure has U≥14 and at most two original starred edges. Each ordinary pure top must supply a private star among the ordinary tops, since otherwise it can be removed in the clean output aggregation. Thus t≤2. A pure Lambda OR cannot compute a singleton and has a distinct support by leastness. With ≤2 star edges there is at most one nonsingleton guarded support, so |Lambda|≤1. Rebuilding the clean OR aggregation on all t+c clean tops takes at most t+c−1 gates. The circuit size is consequently at most 2t+(2c+j+3)−1+|Lambda|. Failure gives 2c+j+3≤15, hence size≤19, contradicting 21. This proves the bound throughout the dirty-AND S case.

**If S itself is an OR.** S is a raw-empty bivalent direct receiver. Every external reader of S apart from A must be a dirty OR with a dirty AND sibling (S is an OR, so only the sibling can provide the charged reader's physical dirty pin). It is therefore raw-empty. Such readers survive the 15 card unchanged as physical readers of S_H: they are neither individual raw-port ORs nor the common h's OR child, since a clean h cannot precede this dirty OR's subsequent AND. A clean wire in a least circuit has at most one distinct OR reader: cut those uses to false, retain its cone, then restore its output once by OR at the final output. Thus exactly one external OR F directly reads S, and at least two raw-empty paid receivers exist. If exactly one receiver is causal to S, it is S; its two pure anchored dirty-AND arms must split the y,z singleton positives and each is clean on H since it implies S. Their forced private star and H edge footprints are those in team08's q1 clean sole-AND source argument. If two receivers are causal to S, the other must precede this bivalent raw-empty S and the remaining F succeeds it. This final OR case is only a structural reduction here.
