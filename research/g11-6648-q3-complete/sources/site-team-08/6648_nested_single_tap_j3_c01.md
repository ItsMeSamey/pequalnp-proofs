# Nested single tap with three dirty gates and at most one centreless top

Continue the index and addendum in site-team-03/premises_6648_q3_visible_two_positive{_index,_addendum}.md. Write E for the earlier exit with donor J, M for the tapped exit with donor D, W for the third exceptional OR. The hidden and visible labels are v and u, respectively. The other pin of M is u as a function: its raw support is {u}, and an OR-only reach from E to that pin would make E a second tap. Thus M=D∨u. It is positive on each singleton u and v.

Every ordinary top seeing M through only ORs must have each opposite raw label adjacent to both u and v (a repeated label would activate the singleton). Their only common neighbor is x; the sole reader of x is A. Consequently this top is A. Every Lambda OR on an OR-only path from M must therefore OR-reach A; it cannot import E along an OR-only path, and all its raw labels are u. Its value is precisely M, by OR flattening. This includes Lambda gates after W if W reaches A. Thus any genuine enlargement past M on an OR-only route must be W itself, whose entering M-side has value M. If both pins receive M along OR-only routes, both are M and it has no effect. Otherwise write W=M∨P. Further Lambda extensions of a genuinely enlarged W cannot exist on that route (they would have value M although they dominate W).

On H, A is zero. Every ordinary correction there is covered by the single J times shared guard from the index: no ordinary top on H receives M along an OR-only path, and passage from M through an AND cannot reach another exit in this nesting order. For c=0 this one batch suffices even with three near residuals.

For c=1 call the centreless top B. If B multiplicatively requires J or D, the index's direct-dependency saving applies also through AND-only paths: on the D-negative slice B either vanishes or has only the J-positive mode. This saves one of the five H batches. Three near stars give at most 6+2 residuals by F(1,4). At two near stars, if W is named, the anchored-only estimate gives at most 4+1+3; if both E,M are named then the H ordinary guard vanishes, leaving at most F(1,3)+3=8.

Otherwise B directly requires the remaining dirty gate T, since there is no raw-empty OR. If M never influences B, only J needs expansion there; the ordinary guard plus its two B modes give F(1,3)+3. If M influences B, the first AND on any such path is B or T: D and J precede M and an intervening clean top cannot be a dirty predecessor. An intermediate T is multiplicatively required by B. Select one path. If its OR-only prefix has value M at the first AND pin, B requires M and vanishes on the D-negative slice of H. Two upstream D batches plus the ordinary guard give F(1,3)+3.

The remaining path passes through genuine W=M∨P. On H with D disabled, M vanishes and W aliases P. This alias introduces no exceptional raw union in either ordinary or B terms: the raw part of P is a singleton, an original Lambda support, or the raw part of E (its other pin is itself singleton or Lambda since W is downstream). If P receives E through Lambda gates its raw fallback is the original anchored raw support of that gate; in positive J mode it is satisfied. Upstream D-positive batches are W-free by acyclicity. All surviving H batches therefore have only anchored or singleton supports relative to their original stars. At three near stars the five batches give at most six H residuals by the anchored-only footprint bound of the addendum under putative U≥9. Together with two near residuals this suffices. At two near stars there are two paid named readers. If both E,M are named the ordinary guard vanishes and the anchored-only four batches give ≤5 H. If W is named, P is its direct near literal (the M pin cannot be a raw literal); both M and P vanish on the D-negative H slice, so B vanishes there. Again F(1,3)+3 suffices. All counts refer to original star edges.
