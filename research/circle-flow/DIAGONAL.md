# Diagonal guards on a planar tour multigraph

Run `python -B diagonal.py`. The separate module accepts the cyclic word, a sphere rotation of its tour multigraph, one diagonal bit per vertex, and the marker labels and bit width. The rotation lists ports in cyclic order, with occurrence i (zero-based) using incoming port 2i and outgoing port 2i+1. The code checks the expanded rotation's Euler characteristic. It expects a supplied embedding.

Swap the incoming and outgoing roles at the first visit of vertex v whenever its diagonal bit is one. Local balance of the modified roles allows either no toggles or a toggle at both visits. For an active vertex with diagonal zero, the original toggles have opposite directions. For diagonal one they have the same direction, requiring odd interlacement parity. This gives the exact guard x_v((Ax)_v + d_v)=0.

Alternating port roles admit the four-cycle. For cyclic order s1,s2,t2,t1, with the s ports of one role and the t ports of the other, place a and b between the two s ports and between the two t ports respectively. The perimeter is s1,a,s2,t2,b,t1; add the internal edge ab. The local graph is bipartite, with s1,s2,b on one side and t1,t2,a on the other. After deleting ports covered by wires, an internal perfect matching requires equal numbers of remaining s and t ports. With no remaining ports, use ab. With one of each, match the remaining s to a and t to b. With all four, use ab and both s–t perimeter edges. This proves local feasibility for every balanced choice in either cyclic order. Multiplicities can depend on the exact surviving port set.

The global graph may be nonbipartite. Orient the expanded graph to give an odd number of oriented edge traversals around each bounded face, using binary elimination on face equations. Evaluate the Pfaffian of the signed skew adjacency matrix at each label character by rational skew elimination. The planar Pfaffian orientation gives all matching monomials a common sign in the trivial evaluation. Fourier inversion using this one sign yields exact numbers of matchings in the label classes. Their support recovers the guard answers.

The test exercises every diagonal on five words, including abcabc with nonbipartite interlacement, using a spherical rotation chosen from exhaustive small rotation enumeration and reproducible two-bit labels. It compares the whole output vector with direct matching enumeration and checks its support independently against guard enumeration.
