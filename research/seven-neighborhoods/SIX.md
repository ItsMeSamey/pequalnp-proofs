# Six retained lines

The five-line certificate gives 15 unmarked gates for every five-line restriction even when all transversal masks are free. The marked minimum for any four targets is at least 14. To reproduce the latter lower bound use marked_four.py 0123 13 and marked_four.py 0124 13. They represent the three-concurrent and general-position quadruples; in a quadruple two different triples cannot both be concurrent unless all four are concurrent, which never occurs here. In thirteen gates the exact twelve-marked triple bound forces every root to be full AND: deleting an OR-root removes it and its separate marked triple, leaving at most eleven. The same single deletion proves every nonoutput mask must hit at least two different targets twice. These are the additional clauses of marked_four.py. The checks are recorded in marked4fast.log.

In a six-target circuit with 16 gates, deleting any output leaves exactly the 15 needed for five. All six outputs are full intersections with two proper supersets, since no prior clean signal can be lost. Every nonoutput mask is rich on at least two targets (a rich target meets it twice or more). No full parent graph component has more edges than vertices; all components are stars or carry at most three concurrent labels. A graph with at least eight parent vertices would leave at most two additional nonoutput gates, impossible by the common-first-gate lemma of FIVE.md. No two three-line pencils have disjoint labels among these seven lines: the six pencils have the K4 description given in H.md. A sole star on six labels would force its leaves to meet only their own target twice. Consequently the only remaining graph has a concurrent triangle and a separate three-edge star. Seven concrete memberships across the two six-line incidence geometries are exhausted in roles.py and roles6.log.

For a minimum circuit of size 17 the usual exclusive-mask bound makes each root either an intersection (full four-point core) or a singleton union with a clean intersection (three-point core). At most one root can have the three-point form, since two such deleted targets would leave at most thirteen paid gates for four marked targets. Write r for the number of three-point cores. For each target e let C_e be its intersection core; the parents A_u,A_v are proper supersets and satisfy A_u ∩ A_v = C_e. The parent graph is simple because all cores are distinct. All its vertices are paid gates distinct from the six outputs and r extra clean cores. There are exactly 11-r dirty gates, comprising the graph vertices and Q additional intermediate gates.

If r=0, Q must be at least three. With two extra gates, the first two must be those extras, all other signals contain four points of a target, and the common-first-gate argument applies. If r=1, Q must be at least two: the first paid gate spans at most two points on any target, while every parent, output or clean core spans at least three. Were this the only other gate, every later signal spanning three points would contain the entire first gate, by induction on union and intersection. All six outputs have empty intersection.

role_general.py first enumerates the full-edge subgraph. Its connected components are stars or, for three concurrent edge labels, a triangle or a three-edge path (with each of the three choices for its middle label). It inserts the optional truncated edge between two existing or newly introduced vertices. Target incidence permutations and vertex renaming identify equivalent configurations. For each choice of a missing point it forms the minimum mask at each vertex (the union of its incident cores). It rejects any pair whose overlap already exceeds its intersection core. The maximum mask of a parent excludes the other parents' mandatory points outside the corresponding core. A parent forced equal to a root, or a full-universe parent, would be redundant. For r=1 removing the shortened line and any one full line leaves fourteen gates for four marked targets; hence every dirty gate is rich on two of the five full targets.

For every surviving role graph the SAT variables describe the masks of the parent and extra gates. Each is made by a binary union or intersection using a free port, an earlier role, or the output or core of a target whose two parents are already available. A transitive total order enforces causality. Extra gates may be ordered by their creation time. The first full-bearing parent needs at least two earlier extra gates when r=0, or at least one when r=1. Each mask must be nontransversal, and no extra gate can be dead. Every target has at most two exclusive gates, already including its root and any separate core. The trace-count tests for smaller retained families use the marked and unmarked bounds above.

Run python role_general.py 012345 17 0 and python role_general.py 012346 17 0 for the two six-line incidence types. The final 0 requests unlimited solver conflicts. In the two geometries there are 66 and 164 cases after the reviewed topology and mask filters.
